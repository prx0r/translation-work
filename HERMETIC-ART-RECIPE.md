## 2. The production ORDER (this is the secret — render order follows it)
Research (Getty/Gresham/Worcester/Fitz/Cennini): the page is built
in a fixed sequence:
1. **Parchment** prepared (calf/sheep/goat skin) — our warm-ivory ground.
2. **Ruling** — margins/lines ruled first (geometry before image).
3. **Scribe** writes the text; errors scraped out with penknife.
4. **Illuminator sketches** the cartoon (the P1 = our cartoon).
5. **GILDING FIRST** — gold laid BEFORE paint (so burnish dust can't
   spoil color): gesso+Armenian-bole (pink-red earth) ground, often
   built up RAISED (looks thicker, catches light), leaf laid by breath,
   burnished with agate/stoat-tooth, punched/incised patterns.
6. **Paint, pale shades first, then darker** (Gettys' rule).
7. **Black outlines last**, then **delicate white highlights** — the
   finishing touch that makes figures/vines read.
8. **Shell gold accents** (ground gold + gum arabic, from shells) —
   final sparkle: highlights on hair/flesh/drapery, gold hatching
   (chrysographia), gold-on-monochrome (camaïeu d'or).
9. Border painters (often assistants) → binder.
Workshop roles: parchmenter · scribe · gilder · border-painter ·
master · binder — the master's job = the program/programma (P1).

## 3. Pigment decode (materials = status = meaning)
| Material | What it is | Where it goes | Prompt name |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ultramarine | lapis lazuli, cost ≈ gold | highest-status zones (rocks/sky/robes) | "lapis ultramarine" |
| Vermilion | mercury+sulfur | reds, accents, energy | "vermilion" |
| Azurite/woad | cheap blue | borders, lettering | "azurite border blue" |
| Verdigris/malachite | copper greens | foliage, ground | "verdigris green" |
| Lead white | white | highlights, drapery | "white-lead highlights" |
| Armenian bole | pink-red earth | UNDER gold (warm glow) | "gold over red bole" |
| Gold leaf | beaten gilding | raised burnished glory | "burnished raised gold leaf" |
| Shell gold | gold powder+gum | late accents, hatching | "shell-gold accents" |
| Parchment | skin | the ground itself | "aged vellum ground" |
Hierarchy law: **more gold = more importance**. The crown of a seal
(our sunburst/turīya) must be the goldiest thing on the page.

## 4. Composition grammar (Solis pages)
- **Three-part page**: gilded floral border (commentary layer, faunal
  symbols: birds=volatile/fixed, butterfly=transformation, peacock=
  cauda pavonis, bee/deer=soul) · central framed miniature · bottom
  register (panels/roundels/text cartouche).
- Margins ruled; the frame is architecture not ornament — positions
  mean things (McLean geometry: above/below, left/right, triangle).
- Solis itself prints a **text summary of each plate (Treatise 5.1)**
  — our P1 stands in that exact tradition.
- Sources: Aurora consurgens + medieval alchimia picta (Prinke/
  Völlnagel) — and NO tarot correspondence (Skinner's debunk).

## 5. How to USE this in P1 (implicit-style vocabulary)
The style stays implicit (reference image carries it), but MATERIAL
WORDS in the seal-text anchor construction without naming Solis:
"on aged vellum · burnished raised gold over red bole · lapis and
vermilion · pale before dark · black outlines · white highlights ·
shell-gold accents · gilding laid first".
Etch implication: our bottom-up staged draw mirrors the production
order in reverse-dependency (structure→content); if we ever want
"build it like the workshop", an etch variant = gilding first, then
paint pale→dark — note for future WB experiments.

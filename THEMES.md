## Theme 1 — Own nature (svabhāva) / the one light
- Definition: The self is one autonomous light (prakāśa = saṃvit); contraction = atom/bondage, expansion = freedom; place/time cannot divide it.
- Passages: A01 (1.1–1.5), A03 (3.6, 3.8), A04 (4.3), A07 (7.25), A09 (9.13).
- See-also: A02 (anupāya), A05 (āṇava), essay0 Q1.
- Overlaps with: autonomy, grace.

## Theme 2 — Ignorance and its removal (ajñāna / mala)
- Definition: Twofold ignorance (buddhigata + pauruṣa); mala = Lord's self-concealment-will; removal by knowledge, not merit.
- Passages: A01 (1.4), A08 (8.22, 8.24), A11 (11.3, 11.4).
- See-also: A04 (sattarka), A10 (kalādhvā).
- Overlaps with: grace, autonomy.

## Theme 3 — Grace (śaktipāta / anugraha), unconditioned
- Definition: Descent of power is the Lord's autonomous will, not merit-earned; unconditioned in root, conditioned by fruit; ninefold typology.
- Passages: A11 (11.1–11.25), A04 (4.10), A01 (1.4).
- See-also: A02 (anupāya), A13 (dīkṣā), A15 (sadyaḥ), A16 (parokṣa).
- Overlaps with: own nature, teacher, concealment.

## Theme 4 — The four upāyas (anupāya / śāmbhava / śākta / āṇava)
- Definition: Four ways of seeing, graded by capacity, not chosen: hearing → reflection → true thought → breath/body/practice.
- Passages: A01 (1.5 preview), A02 (2.1–2.4), A03 (3.1–3.34), A04 (4.1–4.46), A05 (5.1–5.43).
- See-also: A06 (sthāna/kāla), A07 (deśādhvā).
- Overlaps with: own nature, grace.

## Theme 5 — Time (kāla) and breath (prāṇa)
- Definition: Time is breath-course read as manifestation/rest; breath-measure scales to cosmic ages; seeing time-course = recognizing Lordhood = freed.
- Passages: A06 (6.1–6.82), A07 (7.1–7.28), A10 (10.1–10.18).
- See-also: A05 (uccāra), A09 (pramātṛ).
- Overlaps with: own nature, grace.

## Theme 6 — Knowers and powers (pramātṛ / śakti)
- Definition: Seven knowers, seven powers, fifteenfoldness; one knower resting in pure autonomy-bliss; knowability without regress.
- Passages: A09 (9.1–9.53), A08 (8.1–8.17), A07 (7.1–7.28).
- See-also: A10 (kalādhvā), A03 (parāmarśa triad).
- Overlaps with: own nature, time.

## Theme 7 — Causality and principles (kārya-kāraṇa / tattva)
- Definition: Two causalities (ultimate vs. imagined); 36 principles with magnitude ladder; totality causes; process-knowledge mandatory.
- Passages: A07 (7.1–7.28), A08 (8.1–8.93).
- See-also: A01 (svabhāva), A09 (pramātṛ).
- Overlaps with: own nature, knowers.

## Theme 8 — Concealment and recovery (tirobhāva)
- Definition: Stain = Lord's self-concealment-will; atom = aware/unaware shining; recovery via descent; concealment ripens suffering; recovery always open.
- Passages: A08 (8.18–8.38), A11 (11.23–11.25).
- See-also: A04 (sattarka), A10 (kalādhvā).
- Overlaps with: grace, own nature.

## Theme 9 — Teacher (sadguru / asadguru)
- Definition: True teacher = full knowledge = Bhairava himself; false teacher = everyone else; no abandonment of the fully-knowing; fullness, not office, confers authority.
- Passages: A11 (11.8–11.10, 11.17–11.22), A17 (17.1), A18 (18.1–18.2).
- See-also: A13 (dīkṣā), A14 (putraka), A16 (parokṣa).
- Overlaps with: grace, initiation.

## Theme 10 — Initiation (dīkṣā) and its grades
- Definition: Knowledge-characterized initiation (instant freedom); life-severing (at death); grade determines timing/mode, never outcome for the entitled.
- Passages: A11 (11.11–11.16), A12 (12.1–12.10), A13 (13.1–13.101), A14 (14.1–14.29), A15 (15.1–15.7), A16 (16.1–16.24).
- See-also: A17 (liṅgoddhāra), A18 (abhiṣeka), A19 (antyeṣṭi).
- Overlaps with: teacher, grace, conduct.

## Theme 11 — Conduct (śeṣavartana) and worship
- Definition: Post-initiation life = such-becoming (tanmayībhāva); fixed/occasioned/desired; liṅga practice, parva, cakrayāga, mūrtiyāga, pavitra, vyākhyā, samaya, guru-pūjā.
- Passages: A20 (20.1–20.65).
- See-also: A12 (snāna), A13 (yāga), A14 (putraka).
- Overlaps with: initiation, teacher.

## Theme 12 — Authority (prāmāṇya) and schools
- Definition: One-fruit logic; all scriptures work one Lord's work; authority stands by custom-graded fruits; rivals are partial reports of one.
- Passages: A21 (21.1–21.13).
- See-also: A01 (śāstra hierarchy), A04 (Vaiṣṇava fence).
- Overlaps with: own nature, grace.

## Theme 13 — Kula (kaula-yāga) and the close
- Definition: Sixfold kula-worship (outer/power/self/couple/breath/consciousness); 27-principle count; one-utterance binding/container doctrine; inner śakti-worship; couple (yāmala); khecarī-mudrā; nāda-bhairava.
- Passages: A22 (22.1–22.54).
- See-also: A20 (conduct), A06 (time/breath), A08 (causality).
- Overlaps with: time, initiation, knowers.

## Theme 14 — Digits and courses (kalādhvā / padādhvā / mantrādhvā)
- Definition: Five digits grouping 36 principles; 37/38 anti-regress; word-course (measureness) and mantra-course (agitation-wave-calming).
- Passages: A10 (10.1–10.18), A09 (9.1–9.53).
- See-also: A06 (sthāna/kāla), A07 (deśādhvā).
- Overlaps with: knowers, time.

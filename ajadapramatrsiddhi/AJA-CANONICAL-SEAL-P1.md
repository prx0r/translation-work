## The parable (canonical draft, ≈85s spoken)

> A census-taker walks a valley to count what is truly distinct —
> and every candidate collapses: predicates, things, differences,
> beings, works of function — none keeps a seat of its own; each is
> simply what faces him, the knower. He grants the villagers' case
> outright: nothing stands apart, there is no difference. But the
> granting shows his hand — it never stood anywhere except in him:
> what never stood cannot hold up another. Excluding and including
> by his two words, "this" and "I," he fixes every house, and the
> whole town knows its place by his saying so — shining alone owns
> the shining. Then he demotes his own assistants: breath walks with
> the townsfolk now, body, the eightfold town itself — listed, not
> listing; no counting order lives in the breath. He closes the
> ledger: freedom first, doership, lordship — the counter's verdict,
> sung as self-rest.

**Map (hinge):**
- candidates collapse, none keeps a seat → p01 *every candidate
  seat of distinction… collapses into knower-dependence* (v1–8:
  predicates/objecthood/difference/being/function) [p01]
- grants no-difference, granting shows his hand → p01 *the
  opponent's no-difference granted, then outflanked from inside*
  (the grantor is the ground) [p01]
- "what never stood cannot hold up another" → p02 *what never stood
  cannot stand another* [p02]
- excluding/including by "this" and "I," town knows his saying →
  p02 *consciousness grounds by saying 'this'… 'I'; certainty by
  exclusion and inclusion = fixed order, foundation* [p02]
- shining alone owns the shining → p02 *inert ones belong to
  shining alone; no experience apart from connected shining* [p02]
- breath/body/eightfold town listed not listing → p03 *breath and
  kin are knowables, never knowers; no ordering lies in breath; the
  abiding stands in the supreme self* [p03]
- ledger close: freedom, doership, lordship; self-rest → p03
  *freedom, doership foremost, lordship too; shining's self-rest
  sung as I-ness* [p03]

**Limit:** the demoted assistants are NOT contemptuous — breath is
honoured as a knowable (the text's precision: knowable ≠ knower);
the town is "near-unreal in itself yet belonging to shining" — never
voided (p02's own hedge kept).
**Rasa:** adbhuta at the collapse-and-turn (vismaya of the
outflank) → śama as ledger closes (self-rest) → utsāha at the
verdict (four proclaiming verbs in p03). The parable's pulse =
collapse → settle → declare.
**Bar check:** one rendered figure (the taker; villagers implied);
retellable; plain (pramātṛ/jaḍa/vimarśa → counter/inert/shining).

## The seal — statement

**The census where the counter is never counted:** a valley-town
below (grey houses, the eightfold-town), papers of every claimant
flowing UP into the census-taker's ivory ledger (candidates →
knower-dependence), his two words made acts — one hand pointing
"this," one on his heart "I" — his assistants now standing IN LINE
to be counted (breath and body among the townsfolk), and above, the
verdict: his raised hand throwing three gold rays into a calm
self-rest sky.

## Parable v2 (≈85s)

> In a hall of many things, each thing casts its shadow: chair,
> rope, even the shadow-measuring tools. Only the flames cast none —
> ask a flame for its shadow and there is nothing to bring; it
> lights every shape in the room and owns no shape itself. The
> shadowed things say to the flames: no difference between us — and
> the granting shows the light: the shadows all nodded — and the
> nod came from the flame: where else could it have stood? What
> never stood cannot hold up another. So says THIS, so says I: the room knows its
> furniture by those two, and shining alone owns the shining. The
> lamp-bearers set down among the row — breath, body, the eight-
> room town — are knowables, never knowers. The verdict is quick:
> freedom, doership, lordship — the flame's rest. And the hall goes
> quiet: the shadows keep nothing.

**Anchor audit:** shadows-per-thing + flame-without → p01
(candidate-seats collapse = things depend on the facing flame: "each
needs a flame facing it to exist" = knower-dependence) + p02
(*the inert ones belong to shining alone* — shadows literally
belong to the light that casts them: the direct line) · grant-no-
difference shows the grantor → p01 (opponent's no-difference
granted, outflanked from inside — the grant stood only in the
flame) · THIS / I = the flame's two rays → p02 (*by saying 'this'…
'I'*) · shining-alone-owns-shining → p02 verbatim · breath/body/
eight-room set down among shadowed, knowables-not-knowers → p03
(*demoted to knowables; no ordering in breath*) · freedom/doership/
lordship + the flame's rest + hall quiet → p03 (verdict +
self-rest). **All 3 songs / 23 verses mapped; zero institution,
zero invented speech, zero letters.**
**Interpretation declared:** shadow = the knowable-side (the
shadowed class of p03's demotion; anchored by p02's
belong-to-shining line — the mapping is the text's own logic, not
imported psychology).
**Mechanism (spider-class, kid-verifiable):** flames cast no shadow
— a real fact a child can test with a candle; the doctrine
(knower-not-inert) IS that fact.

**Seal:** THE HALL OF UNCAST SHADOWS — base: grey-umber furniture
each with its dark shadow-plane, and across them ONE flat uniform
shadow-strip lying edge-to-edge (the shadows' grant: "no difference
between us"); the strip broken by a single gold ray (the outflank);
among the furniture: three lamp-bearers SITTING in the row, lit but
seated (breath, body, eight-room town — knowables, among things);
centre: the great shadowless flame — no ground-shadow beneath it —
throwing TWO rays: one outward touching the furniture (THIS), one
looping back to its own wick (I); crown: the flame splitting into
THREE tongues — freedom, doership, lordship — over a calm ivory band
(the hall's rest).
**Colour:** umber-grey shadow world · flat cold-grey grant-strip ·
gold ray cutting it · gold-white shadowless flame · three tongues
gold with vermilion tips (vīra-verdict) · seated lamps warm-but-
placed · ivory rest-band crown.

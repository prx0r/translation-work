# Ajaḍapramātṛsiddhi ajad-p03 (vv. 19–27) — L2 READ

## v.19
Never proved by non-spreading — though joined with knowing and the rest — breath and kin would be knowables, never knowers. Otherwise, delusion.
The Sanskrit: *na siddho 'prathanād eva jñānādisamavāyy api tat prāṇādi prameyaṃ syān na pramātānyato bhramāt*

## v.20
Though the abiding of things stands locked in the living, curbed by breath and the eightfold city — even there, in the supreme self it abides.
The Sanskrit: *yady apy arthasthitiḥ prāṇapuryaṣṭakaniyantrite jīve niruddhā tatrāpi paramātmani sā sthitā*

## v.21
How could restraint by breath of that self-nature ever be proved — in recognition? By living-ness, beyond non-showing.
The Sanskrit: *tadātmanaiva tasya syāt kathaṃ prāṇena yantraṇā sādhitā pratyabhijñāyām akhyāter jīvatā param*

## v.22
No ordering of things lies in breath and the rest through blocking of I-ness; shining's self-rest is sung as I-ness, indeed.
The Sanskrit: *nārthavyavasthā prāṇādāv ahaṃbhāvanirodhataḥ prakāśasyātmaviśrāntir ahaṃbhāvo hi kīrtitaḥ*

## v.23
Told already — that very rest, by blocking of all dependence: freedom, then doership foremost, and lordship too.
The Sanskrit: *uktā saiva ca viśrāntiḥ sarvāpekṣānirodhataḥ svātantryam atha kartṛtvaṃ mukhyam īśvaratāpi ca*

## v.24
Called Māyā, of his power alone — with no other proven; this world-maker lordship is made of non-showing.
The Sanskrit: *māyābhidās tacchakter eva cānyāprasiddhitaḥ tadakhyātimayaṃ hy etaj jagannirmātṛteśitā*

## v.25
One alone through linking, proclaimed: of all awarenesses the mother-principle, self-feeling's synonym — beginningless.
The Sanskrit: *ekaiva cānusaṃdhānāt sā proktā sarvasaṃvidām svasaṃvedanaparyāyamātṛtattvam anādi tat*

## v.26
Whatever grounds the ordering of things — pervasion and linking of the I-sharer — fits nothing inert.
The Sanskrit: *bhāvavyavasthā yan niṣṭhā tasyāhaṃbhāvabhāginaḥ vyāpitvam anusaṃdhānaṃ taj jaḍasya na yujyate*

## v.27
So within difference, shining's shining holds good — hence recognition's word: this world is that-nondual-made.
The Sanskrit: *tato bhede tu bhāsasya ghaṭate bhāsamānatā tenoktam pratyabhijñāyāṃ tadadvayamayaṃ jagat*

# Ajaḍapramātṛsiddhi — L2 READ

## ajad-p01 — Real and Unreal, Conscious and Unconscious

Just as there is no difference between real and unreal in their own self, so too there is no difference between conscious and unconscious. Illumination, reflection, and existence do not touch the essence of a thing, and objecthood dependent on another does not fit because of non-one-formness and transcendence of existence. Function distinguishes the existent from the nonexistent, yet establishment remains consciousness-dependent, so consciousness without reflection would be no different from the unconscious.

## ajad-p02 — The "I"-Consciousness and the Unconscious

Consciousness as "I" establishes the manifestation of other things in itself, while difference and non-difference determine the arrangement of unconscious selves. Unconscious things are merely imagined projections within self-manifestation, and no experience exists apart from connected manifestation. The self is twofold—limited and unlimited—yet one continuum grounds all object-consciousnesses, so nothing more unconscious than that end-residing manifestation can appear.

## ajad-p03 — Breath, Body, and Supreme Self

The breath-body arrangement is not established by non-manifestation, but abides in the supreme self even when constrained in the living being. Restraint of the I-sense is not object-arrangement in breath and body; rather, that rest is freedom, authorship, and lordship, the māyā-power of the world-maker. The mother principle is one beginningless continuum, whose pervasion fits the conscious I-sense but not the unconscious, so the world proclaimed in recognition is non-dual.

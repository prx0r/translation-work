# C1 — ajad-p01 (vv. 1–9): no seat for distinction; the grasp-less turn

## SUMMARY

Real and unreal differ nowhere in their own self — the opponent's certainty, stretched to conscious and unconscious. Showing, telling, being: none of it touches own-form, which faces the knower alone. Object-of-showing fits no self, since leaning breaks oneness and oversteps being. The grant costs nothing — appearing's coming and going still serves the end. Being fits only appearing-bound. Function cannot part them either: only existents work, yet working leans on knowers. So all standing is awareness-based. And awareness itself, ungrasping-formed, ranks with non-being and the inert.

## KEY TERMS

* **sadasat** — real-unreal: the opening pair whose self-difference fails.
* **jaḍa/ajaḍa** — inert/non-inert: the extended pair; nothing in things parts them.
* **prakhyā/upākhyā** — showing/telling: the two predicates that never reach own-form.
* **jñātṛgāmin** — knower-facing: own-form's only address.
* **arthakriyā** — working/function: the objector's last candidate, contained.
* **pratiṣṭhā, saṃvidāśrayā** — standing, awareness-grounded: the first positive thesis (v8).
* **aparāmarśa** — ungrasping: the form of awareness that ranks inert (v9).

## rasa

Wonder (adbhuta) at the reversal: the proof's sting is that awareness itself can be inert. The relish is dialectical camatkāra — each candidate ground dissolving under inspection until only grasping light remains standing.

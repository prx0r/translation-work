# C1 — ajad-p02 (vv. 10–18): self-grounding; this and I; the twofold one

## SUMMARY

What never stood cannot stand another: unwon herself, grasp-less awareness grounds no other being. Consciousness fits to ground another's showing by saying this — and again, itself, by saying I. By exclusion and inclusion both, certainty orders inert selves: fixed order, ground, winning, winding-up. Near-unreal in self, inert ones are shining's own; shining alone is, through selves own and other. No experience stands apart from distinct-and-indistinct assurance, apart from linked ungrasping shining. Cut-off this-grasping completes in self-rest: I-am-this is this. One self twofold — breath-bound atom, unbroken whole. Both gather in one end-ground through linking alone. At the limit, in no-parting I-knowing, nothing shows more inert than shining.

## KEY TERMS

* **pratiṣṭhāspadatā** — standing-placehood: what the ungrounded cannot lend (v10).
* **idam/aham** — this/I: the two sayings by which consciousness grounds (v11).
* **vyatireka/itara** — exclusion/inclusion: double concomitance, certainty's instrument (v12).
* **vimarśa** — grasping: what experience is inseparable from (v14).
* **asatkalpa** — near-unreal: the only self-being of inert ones (v13).
* **anusaṃdhāna** — linking/continuity: what makes one of before-and-after (v17).
* **paryantabhūmi** — end-ground: this alone, of all thing-awarenesses (v17).
* **ahaṃvid** — I-knowing: limit-dwelling, no-parting-grasped (v18).

## rasa

Rest (śānta) after effort: the relish here is homecoming — severed grasping completing in self-rest, both measures gathered in one ground. Wonder (adbhuta) attends the twofold one: atom and unbroken, same self.

# C1 — ajad-p03 (vv. 19–27): breath demoted; rest crowned; nondual verdict

## SUMMARY

Breath joined with knowing still never proves: breath and kin are knowables, never knowers — else delusion. Locked in the living though things stand, curbed by breath and eightfold city, even there they abide in the supreme self. Breath proves no hold on that self-nature; recognition answers by living-ness past non-showing. No ordering sits in breath by I-blocking; shining's self-rest is sung as I-ness. Told already, that rest, all dependence blocked: freedom, doership foremost, lordship with it. Called Māyā, of his power alone, no other proven — world-maker lordship is non-showing-made. One alone through linking, proclaimed mother-principle of all awarenesses, self-feeling's synonym, beginningless. Pervasion and linking of the I-sharer fit nothing inert. So in difference shining's shining holds — recognition's word: this world that-nondual-made.

## KEY TERMS

* **prameya/pramātṛ** — knowable/knower: breath demoted to the first (v19).
* **puryaṣṭaka** — eightfold city: the subtle-body cage with breath (v20).
* **akhyāti** — non-showing: what living-ness outlies (v21).
* **ahaṃbhāva** — I-ness: sung as shining's self-rest (v22).
* **viśrānti** — rest: blocking all dependence, unfolding freedom (v23).
* **svātantrya/kartṛtva/īśvaratā** — freedom/doership/lordship: rest's three offices (v23).
* **māyā/tacchakti** — Māyā/of-his-power: naming from power alone (v24).
* **mātṛtattva** — mother-principle: one, linking, beginningless (v25).
* **tadadvayamaya** — that-nondual-made: the world's verdict (v27).

## rasa

Triumph (vīra) of declaration: four verbs of proclaiming — sung, told, proclaimed, said — carry the passage. The relish is verdict-joy (nirṇaya-camatkāra): after demolition and construction, the proof speaks its close and the world is named nondual.

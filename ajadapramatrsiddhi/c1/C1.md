# C1 — Ajaḍapramātṛsiddhi (argument)

## ajad-p01 (1–9) — no ground in the inert; grasping-less light included

**SUMMARY.** Just as real-unreal show no self-difference, so conscious-unconscious show none — that is the opponent's certainty. Showing, telling, being, non-being: none touch a thing's own form, which faces the knower alone. Being made an object of showing fits nothing's self, since other-dependence breaks oneness and existence is overstepped. Granted no-difference, distinction still succeeds — hence purpose. Appearing-bound being is proper only dependently; cut loose, existent and nonexistent stay undistinguished. Function cannot tell them apart either, since only the existent functions — yet function itself depends on knowers, failing to ground. So all standing is consciousness-based. And consciousness itself, ungrasping-formed, is as inert as nonbeing and the inert.

**KEY TERMS.** pūrvapakṣa (opponent's case), prakhyā/upākhyā (showing/telling), sattā/asattā (being/non-being), jñātṛgāmin (knower-facing), naikarūpatva (non-one-formedness), arthakriyā (function), pratiṣṭhā (standing), saṃvidāśrayā (consciousness-based), aparāmarśa (ungrasping).


## ajad-p02 (10–18) — self-grounding; I and this; twofold one

**SUMMARY.** What never stood cannot stand another: grounding needs self-standing. Consciousness fits to stand other-showing as this, and again as I by itself. By exclusion and inclusion both, certainty orders inert selves — arrangement, standing, proving, completing. Inert ones, almost-unreal in self, exist only as shining's own; shining alone is, self and others' selves through. No experience without distinct-undistinct certainty apart from connected grasping-full light. Cut-off this-grasping finds its done-ness in self-rest — that grasping, I, is this. The same self twofold: measured atom breath-bound, supreme unbroken. Both have one end-ground of all object-knowing; one alone through continuity — no other holds. In end-abiding I-knowing of non-separation, nothing shows more inert than showing.

**KEY TERMS.** pratiṣṭhāspadatva (standing-groundhood), idam/aham (this/I), vyatireka/itara (exclusion/inclusion), asatkalpa (almost-unreal), anusaṃdhāna (continuity), paryantabhūmi (end-ground), paryantavartin (end-abiding), advaya (non-separation).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 10 states the standing-requirement (alabdhapariniṣṭhitiḥ grounds nothing). Verse 11 fits consciousness to both poles: idam for other-showing, aham by self. Verse 12 names certainty's four offices (vyavasthiti-pratiṣṭhā-siddhi-nirvṛtti) over inert selves by anvaya-vyatireka. Verse 13 grants the inert only borrowed being (prakāśasyaiva). Verse 14 bars experience without bhinnābhinna-certainty of connected light. Verse 15 completes cut-off this in self-rest: so 'ham — I-am-this as done-ness. Verse 16 doubles the one self (mita/aparimita, aṇu/paramātman). Verse 17 unites both in one end-ground through continuity alone. Verse 18 seals: in non-separation I-knowing at the limit, no jaḍa beyond bhāsa.

## ajad-p03 (19–27) — breath denied; rest proclaimed; nondual verdict

**SUMMARY.** Breath-joined knowing never stood by non-showing: breath-etc. are knowable, never knower — else delusion. Even confined injīva-things, object-standing holds only in the supreme self. That self-nature no breath-restraint touches; in recognition, living is beyond non-showing. No object-ordering in breath by I-blocking: shining's self-rest is sung as I-ness. That rest, all-dependence-blocked, is freedom, then doership, chief, and lordship too. Called Māyā, from power alone — no other established; non-showing-made, this world-maker-lordship. One alone through continuity, proclaimed: self-feeling-synonymous knower-principle, beginningless. Pervasion-and-continuity of I-sharers fit nothing inert. Hence in difference shining holds: in recognition, this world is that-nondual-made.

**KEY TERMS.** prameya/pramātṛ (knowable/knower), jīva (living-bound), akhyāti (non-showing), ahaṃbhāvanirodha (I-blocking), viśrānti (rest), sarvāpekṣānirodha (all-dependence-blocking), aiśvarya (lordship), mātṛtattva (knower-principle), vyāpti (pervasion), tadadvayamaya (that-nondual-made).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 19 demotes breath to prameya (anyathā bhrama). Verse 20 rehouses confined things in paramātman. Verse 21 lifts living beyond restraint (akhyāteḥ param... jīvat): recognition, not breath, establishes. Verse 22 sings self-rest as I-ness against breath-ordering. Verse 23 unfolds rest into freedom-doership-lordship (svātantrya-kartṛtva-aiśvarya). Verse 24 names Māyā from power alone, lordship from non-showing. Verse 25 proclaims the one beginningless mother-principle through continuity. Verse 26 restricts pervasion-continuity to I-sharers. Verse 27 verdicts: difference holds shining (bhede... bhāsamānatā ghaṭate); recognition says the world that-nondual-made.

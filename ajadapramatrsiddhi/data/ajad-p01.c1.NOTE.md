# C1 NOTE — ajad-p01

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Real and unreal differ nowhere in their own self — the opponent's certainty, stretched to conscious and unconscious. Showing, telling, being: none of it touches own-form, which faces the knower alone. Object-of-showing fits no self, since leaning breaks oneness and oversteps being. The grant costs nothing — appearing's coming and going still serves the end. Being fits only appearing-bound. Function cannot part them either: only existents work, yet working leans on knowers. So all standing is awareness-based. And awareness itself, ungrasping-formed, ranks with non-being and the inert.

## FUNCTION

Opens the Siddhitrayī's second proof with the opponent's strongest certainty (v1, Vijñānavāda voice), then dissolves every candidate seat of distinction from inside: predicates (v2), objecthood (v3), conceded difference (v4), free-standing being (v5), function (v6–v8) — landing the first positive thesis (awareness-grounded standing, v8) and the turn that founds the whole work (grasp-less awareness is inert, v9).

* **jñātṛgāmin** — knower-facing: own-form's only address (v2).

The ledger freezes grasping; L2/L3 keep it uniformly.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Ajaḍapramātṛsiddhi, Utpaladeva (10th c.), mūla with his own vṛtti (svopajñavṛtti); GRETIL KSTS 34 (Kaul 1921, Vasudeva input; TEI 2020). Second proof of the Siddhitrayī (between Īśvarasiddhi and Sambandhasiddhi). Passage 1 of 3: vv. 1–9, pūrvapakṣa opening through the grasp-less turn. The Vṛ blocks are read as Utpaladeva's own per the closing colophon — the Harabhaṭṭa layer question stays open (see BOUNDARY).

## EXPLANATION

The opening certainty belongs to the opponent: no self-difference anywhere (v1). Against it, three bars: predicates never touch own-form (v2); objecthood needs another, one form, and being — all three fail (v3); even granted, appearing's rhythm still works (v4). Being thought apart from appearing is indeterminate (v5). The function objection states itself inside the proof and is answered inside it: working marks existents, yet marks nothing in the self (v6); dyed being is borrowed being (v7); of proven things no working is even declared, so both sides run level and standing falls back on awareness (v8). Then the turn that the rest of the work stands on: ungrasping awareness ranks with non-being and the inert (v9).

## BOUNDARY

Whose end the conceded difference serves (v4) stays open — read as the opponent's own pragmatic end. The Vṛ's glance at the quietist Brahman-refutation (v9) is recorded, not pressed: Mundra & Godbole confirm the refutations target Śāṅkara Vedānta alongside Vijñānavāda, but the verse-by-verse mapping stays open. GRETIL prints dental-d in jadājaḍānām (v1), an input quirk for retroflex jaḍa; read with the editions. T1's loose proximal for adaḥ (v2) corrected at L0 to distal, Vṛ-confirmed. The [Pūrvapakṣaḥ] stage direction and closing iti śivam are kept as paratext, untranslated. Vṛtti authorship: the closing colophon attributes the embedded Vṛ to Utpaladeva (utpaladeva-viracitāyāṃ vṛttiḥ), against which stand Lawrence 2009's title (translating with Harabhaṭṭa Śāstrī's Vṛtti) and the Vṛ's third-person ācāryaḥ (v13-Vṛ) — commentarial voice, or auto-commentarial convention. Held open: full adjudication needs Lawrence's article against the KSTS 34 print. Nothing downstream depends on it (argmaps cite Vṛ content, never its author).

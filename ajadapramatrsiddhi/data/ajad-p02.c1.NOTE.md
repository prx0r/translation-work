# C1 NOTE — ajad-p02

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

What never stood cannot stand another: unwon herself, grasp-less awareness grounds no other being. Consciousness fits to ground another's showing by saying this — and again, itself, by saying I. By exclusion and inclusion both, certainty orders inert selves: fixed order, ground, winning, winding-up. Near-unreal in self, inert ones are shining's own; shining alone is, through selves own and other. No experience stands apart from distinct-and-indistinct assurance, apart from linked ungrasping shining. Cut-off this-grasping completes in self-rest: I-am-this is this. One self twofold — breath-bound atom, unbroken whole. Both gather in one end-ground through linking alone. At the limit, in no-parting I-knowing, nothing shows more inert than shining.

## FUNCTION

Builds the positive account after the demolition: self-grounding as grounding's precondition (v10); the two sayings (v11); certainty's four offices over inert selves (v12); borrowed being and autonomy's dawn (v13); grasp as experience itself (v14); completion in self-rest (v15); the twofold one (v16); gathering in one end-ground (v17); the limit seal (v18).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Passage 2 of 3: vv. 10–18. Turns from demolition to construction: the knower must first ground itself. The Vṛ throughout is read as Utpaladeva's own — philosophy in commentary dress, including the autonomy-teaching dawn at v13 and the life-principle at v15 (authorship layer open; see p01 NOTE BOUNDARY).

## EXPLANATION

Self-grounding comes first: the unwon lends nothing (v10). Then the mechanism: two sayings, two poles (v11). Certainty's offices are named over inert selves, with the Vṛ settling the instrument as double concomitance (v12). Inert being is reduced to borrowed shining, and autonomy dawns inside the reduction (v13). Experience is identified with grasping certainty — no grasp, no seeing (v14). Severed this-grasping is completed, not discarded: rest in own form, I-am-this (v15). The same self is then shown twofold without splitting (v16), gathered by linking into one end-ground (v17), and sealed at the limit where shining exhausts the inert (v18).

## BOUNDARY

The feminine of the grounding subject (sā, saṃvit) is kept throughout — she, not it. itara read as the anvaya side with the Vṛ; the bare-mūla reading (the other) stays visible in T1's hyphen gloss. No live token forks in this passage; both settled readings documented at L0 grounds.

# C1 NOTE — ajad-p03

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Breath joined with knowing still never proves: breath and kin are knowables, never knowers — else delusion. Locked in the living though things stand, curbed by breath and eightfold city, even there they abide in the supreme self. Breath proves no hold on that self-nature; recognition answers by living-ness past non-showing. No ordering sits in breath by I-blocking; shining's self-rest is sung as I-ness. Told already, that rest, all dependence blocked: freedom, doership foremost, lordship with it. Called Māyā, of his power alone, no other proven — world-maker lordship is non-showing-made. One alone through linking, proclaimed mother-principle of all awarenesses, self-feeling's synonym, beginningless. Pervasion and linking of the I-sharer fit nothing inert. So in difference shining's shining holds — recognition's word: this world that-nondual-made.

## FUNCTION

Closes the proof by elimination then proclamation: breath demoted (v19), confinement rehoused (v20), restraint unproved (v21), order relocated to I-ness (v22) — then rest unfolded into three offices (v23), Māyā named without second cause (v24), the mother-principle proclaimed (v25), pervasion restricted to I-sharers (v26), and the nondual verdict spoken (v27).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Passage 3 of 3: vv. 19–27. The proof's close: elimination complete, proclamation begins. Closes with iti śivam (peace) and Harabhaṭṭa's śāka-1841 colophon — paratext, untranslated. The Vṛ read as Utpaladeva's own, growing expansive (v25–27 glossed nearly line by line; authorship layer open — see p01 NOTE BOUNDARY).

## EXPLANATION

Elimination first: breath fails despite knowings (v19); locked order is rehoused, not freed (v20); restraint is unprovable where recognition answers (v21); ordering moves from breath to I-ness (v22). Then unfolding: rest, told already, opens into freedom, foremost doership, lordship (v23). Māyā is named from power alone with separate causes disestablished (v24). The one linking mother-principle is proclaimed over all awarenesses (v25). Pervasion-linking is fenced to I-sharers (v26). The verdict keeps both readings — difference wherein shining-shining holds, non-difference wherein the inert re-shines — and speaks the world nondual (v27).

## BOUNDARY

sādhitā's scope (restraint disproved, living-ness proved) parsed with the Vṛ and held open as reading, not fact. The śaiva ghost at v23 exorcised by feminine anaphora (Vṛ: saiva … viśrāntiḥ). v25's long compound members listed, not split into rival parses. The double holding of v27 (difference and non-difference) kept double — no synthesis imposed. iti śivam and colophons excluded as paratext.

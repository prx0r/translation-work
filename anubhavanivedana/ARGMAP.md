# Anubhavanivedana — ARGMAP

*Abhinavagupta, 4 verses, 4 passages (1 verse each). Source: Ruma Dewan ed./proofread (SanskritDocuments), from the Abhinavastotrāvalī tradition.*

*Work-level lore (kept here, not repeated per passage): Pandey's Abhinavagupta and His Works (Appendix, p. 414 — lists the stotra as ascribed to Abhinava, 4 verses); Silburn 1970 (French translation, the classic rendering); Singh & Maheshvarananda 2016 (English, Indian Mind, Varanasi); Buira Ferré 2018 workshop (Sanskrit→Catalan/Spanish/French/English, mudrā as presence); Skora's article (quotes v. 3 after Silburn and Rastogi 1990).*

## Arc
- anu-p01 (v. 1): mind-wind dissolved in the inward aim; seeing-not-seeing with steady gaze → śāmbhavī mudrā by the guru's grace; void-and-non-void-free Śāmbhava state.
- anu-p02 (v. 2): half-open eyes, steady mind, nose-tip gaze; moon-sun dissolved in the triple throb → light-form without exterior, one supreme person attained; what more to say?
- anu-p03 (v. 3): whatever sound = transcendent mantra; whatever bodily posture = mudrā; breath's spontaneous flow = wonderful yoga; supreme śākta abode — while I experience, what would not shine?
- anu-p04 (v. 4): mantra where letters unseen; mudrā where bodily action dissolved; yoga where breath-flow exhausted; festivals of attaining Your abode — what is not wonderful for the wise?

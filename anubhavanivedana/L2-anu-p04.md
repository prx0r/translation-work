# anubhavanivedana anu-p04 — L2 READ

## v.4
That mantra shines forth in which letter-arrangement is not distinguished. That mudrā arises in which entire bodily action is dissolved. That yoga spreads forth from which breath's flowing is exhausted. In the festivals of attaining Your abode, of the wise — what, what is indeed not wonderful? *Fidelity: preserved (all 4 verses, full pādas from T1). Added: connective phrasing only. Not-resolved: trispandabhāva read as inhalation/exhalation/juncture (marked); yataḥ read relatively; śāktaṃ dhāma read as nominative.*
The Sanskrit: *mantraḥ saḥ pratibhāti varṇa racanā yasmin na saṃlakṣyate mudrā sā samudeti yatra galitā kṛtsnā kriyā | kāyikī yogaḥ saḥ prathate yataḥ pravahaṇam prāṇasya saṃkṣīyate tvaddhāmādhigamotsaveṣu sudhiyām kim kim na nāma adbhutam*

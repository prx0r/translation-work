# Anubhavanivedana — L2 READ

## anu-p01 — seeing-not-seeing
When the yogin abides with mind-wind dissolved in the inward aim — he, seeing yet not seeing outside with unmoving-pupil gaze — this indeed becomes the śāmbhavī mudrā, by your grace, O guru: devoid of void and non-void becomes that reality, the śāmbhava state.

## anu-p02 — the portrait and the silence
Half-open-eyed, steady-minded, gaze placed on the nose-tip — moon and sun too have attained dissolution in the interior of the triple-throb state — light-formed, devoid of all exteriority, and one supreme person: reality attains that state, the thing supreme. What more is there to say here?

## anu-p03 — whatever sounds, whatever posture, whatever breath
Whatever sound arises from the mouth — that mantra is transcendent. Whatever posture of the pleasure-pain-born body — that indeed is mudrā. Whatever flowing of breath by its own rhythm — that indeed is wonderful yoga. The supreme śākta abode — while I experience, what indeed would not shine?

## anu-p04 — letters unseen, action dissolved, breath exhausted
That mantra shines forth in which letter-arrangement is not distinguished. That mudrā arises in which entire bodily action is dissolved. That yoga spreads forth from which breath's flowing is exhausted. In the festivals of attaining Your abode, of the wise — what, what is indeed not wonderful?

# Anubhavanivedana anu-p01 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 1)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 anu-p01 = verse 1. Lore: the Qcentro translation workshop (Neus Buira Ferré 2018) rendered this stotra into Catalan, Spanish, French and English verse by verse — the only modern multi-language workshop translation; Buira reads mudrā here as presence, not seal or posture, given the verse's metaphysical pitch.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 1 of the Anubhavanivedana (Abhinavagupta, 4 verses). Gloss: whose-mind-wind-is-dissolved-in-the-inward-aim (antarlakṣyavilīnacittapavanaḥ) the-yogin (yogī) when (yadā) abides (vartate); with-gaze (dṛṣṭyā) of-unmoving-pupil (niścalatārayā) outside (bahir) he (asau) seeing (paśyan) not-seeing (apaśyan) even (api); this-mudrā (mudreyam) indeed (khalu) śāmbhavī (śāmbhavī) becomes (bhavati) that (sā) by-your-grace (yuṣmatprasādāt) O-guru (guro); devoid-of-void-and-non-void (śūnyāśūnyavivarjitam) becomes (bhavati) which (yat) reality (tattvam) state (padam) śāmbhava (śāmbhavam).

Sanskrit: antarlakṣyavilīnacittapavano yogī yadā vartate dṛṣṭyā niścalatārayā bahirasau paśyannapaśyannapi | mudreyaṃ khalu śāmbhavī bhavati sā yuṣmatprasādādguro śūnyāśūnyavivarjitaṃ bhavati yat tattvaṃ padaṃ śāmbhavam

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 1:**
> antarlakṣyavilīnacittapavano yogī yadā vartate dṛṣṭyā niścalatārayā bahirasau paśyannapaśyannapi | mudreyaṃ khalu śāmbhavī bhavati sā yuṣmatprasādādguro śūnyāśūnyavivarjitaṃ bhavati yat tattvaṃ padaṃ śāmbhavam

[i.e. when the yogin abides with mind-wind dissolved in the inward aim — he, seeing yet not seeing outside with unmoving-pupil gaze — this indeed becomes the śāmbhavī mudrā, by your grace, O guru: devoid of void and non-void becomes that reality, the śāmbhava state.]

The condition first: a yogin (yogī) abides (vartate) when (yadā) mind (citta) and wind (pavana, breath) are dissolved (vilīna) in the inward aim (antarlakṣya). His gaze (dṛṣṭyā) has an unmoving pupil (niścalatārā); outside (bahis), he (asau) is seeing (paśyan) and not-seeing (apaśyan) at once — sight without seizing. Then the naming: this (iyam) indeed (khalu) becomes (bhavati) the śāmbhavī mudrā — and only by your grace (yuṣmatprasādāt), O guru (guro). Last, its character: devoid (vivarjitam) of void and non-void (śūnya-aśūnya), becomes (bhavati) that (yat...tad) reality (tattvam), the śāmbhava state (padam śāmbhavam). The mudrā is not performed; it occurs, by grace, as seeing-without-seeing.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Śūnyāśūnyavivarjitaṃ (devoid of void and non-void) denies both extremes at once; read as freedom from the void/non-void pair, not as a third term.
2. The guru addressed is unnamed; no lineage claim is imported.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry condition-then-naming-then-character: dissolved inward, seeing-not-seeing, śāmbhavī mudrā by grace, void-and-non-void-free state. L2 must keep the paradox of paśyan-apaśyan unflattened. Carry the grace-given mudrā into verse 2, where the posture is described from outside.

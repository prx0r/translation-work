# Anubhavanivedana anu-p02 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 2)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 anu-p02 = verse 2. Lore: the verse is a yogic portrait in four strokes — half-open eyes, steadied mind, nose-tip gaze, moon-and-sun dissolved — closing on jyotīrūpa (light-form) without exterior; the final question (vācyaṃ kim atra adhikam, what more is to be said here?) shuts speech after showing.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 2 of the Anubhavanivedana (Abhinavagupta, 4 verses). Gloss: half-open-eyed (ardhoddhāṭitalocanaḥ) steady-minded (sthiramanāḥ) whose-gaze-is-placed-on-the-nose-tip (nāsāgradattekṣaṇaḥ); moon-and-sun (candrārkau) even (api) dissolution (līnatām) attained (upagatau) in-the-interior-of-the-triple-throb-state (trispandabhāvāntare); of-light-form (jyotīrūpam) devoid-of-all-exteriority (aśeṣabāhyarahitam) and (ca) one (ekam) person (pumāṃsam) supreme (param); reality (tattvam) that (tat) state (padam) attains (eti) thing (vastu) supreme (paramam) to-be-said (vācyam) what (kim) here (atra) more (adhikam).

Sanskrit: ardhoddhāṭitalocanaḥ sthiramanās nāsāgradattekṣaṇaś candrārkāvapi līnatāmupagatau trispandabhāvāntare | jyotīrūpamaśeṣabāhyarahitaṃ caikaṃ pumāṃsaṃ paraṃ tattvaṃ tatpadameti vastu paramam vācyaṃ kimatrādhikam

*Note: printed ekṣaṇaś-candrārkau split as ekṣaṇaḥ | candrārkau (gaze placed... moon and sun).*

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 2:**
> ardhoddhāṭitalocanaḥ sthiramanās nāsāgradattekṣaṇaḥ candrārkāvapi līnatāmupagatau trispandabhāvāntare | jyotīrūpamaśeṣabāhyarahitaṃ caikaṃ pumāṃsaṃ paraṃ tattvaṃ tatpadameti vastu paramam vācyaṃ kimatrādhikam

[i.e. half-open-eyed, steady-minded, gaze placed on the nose-tip — moon and sun too attained dissolution in the interior of the triple-throb state — light-formed, devoid of all exteriority, and one supreme person: reality attains that state, the thing supreme — what more to be said here?]

The portrait first, in four strokes: half-open eyes (ardhoddhāṭitalocanaḥ), steadied mind (sthiramanāḥ), gaze placed (dattekṣaṇaḥ) on the nose-tip (nāsāgra) — then moon and sun (candrārkau) too (api) attained (upagatau) dissolution (līnatām) in the interior (antare) of the triple-throb state (trispandabhāva): inhalation, exhalation and their juncture dissolved inward. Then the vision: light-formed (jyotīrūpam), devoid of all exteriority (aśeṣabāhyarahitam), and (ca) one (ekam), supreme (param) person (pumāṃsam). Reality (tattvaṃ) attains (eti) that (tat) state (padam) — the supreme thing (vastu paramam). Close: what (kim) more (adhikam) is to be said (vācyam) here (atra)? The question ends speech where showing ends.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Trispandabhāva (triple-throb state) is kept as printed; inhalation, exhalation and juncture is the standard reading, marked as reading, not text.
2. The verse-dividing ś in dattekṣaṇaś candrārkau is sandhi for ḥ | c-; split accordingly.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry portrait-then-vision-then-silence: four outer strokes, the light-form without exterior, the closing question. L2 must keep the closing question as a period, not a prompt. Carry the attained state into verse 3, where ordinary acts become mantra, mudrā, yoga.

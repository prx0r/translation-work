# C1 — Anubhavanivedana (final condensation of the arguments)

*States each passage's argumentative move and the hymn's whole arc. For the read prose see L2.v2.*

## The arc in one paragraph

Mind-wind dissolved in the inward aim; seeing yet not seeing with steady gaze — śāmbhavī mudrā, by the guru's grace alone, void-and-non-void-free. Half-open eyes, steady mind, nose-tip gaze; moon-sun dissolved in the triple throb — light-form without exterior, one supreme person attained; after which, what more to say? Whatever sounds is already transcendent mantra; whatever the body postures, mudrā; whatever the breath's flow, wonderful yoga — so in the experienced śākta abode, what would not shine? Mantra where letters unnoticed; mudrā where all bodily action dissolved; yoga where breath-flow exhausted — then festival: for the wise attaining Your abode, everything wonderful. Dissolution → portrait → spontaneity → vanishing → festival.

## anu-p01 (v. 1) — condition met, mudrā given

Dissolution, not effort: mind-wind dissolved in the inward aim, seeing-not-seeing with unmoving pupil. This — and only by the guru's grace — becomes śāmbhavī mudrā: reality free of void and non-void. The mudrā occurs; it is not performed.

## anu-p02 (v. 2) — portrait, then silence

Four outer strokes — half-open eyes, steady mind, nose-tip gaze, moon-sun dissolved — then the vision: light-form without exterior, one supreme person attained. Showing complete, speech stops: what more to be said?

## anu-p03 (v. 3) — no technique enjoined

Whatever sounds is already transcendent mantra; whatever the body postures is already mudrā; whatever the breath's flow is already wonderful yoga. Nothing to do — utterance, posture, breathing recognized as already sacred. So, in the experienced śākta abode: what would not shine?

## anu-p04 (v. 4) — defined by vanishing, closed in festival

Mantra where letter-arrangement goes unnoticed; mudrā where entire bodily action is dissolved; yoga where breath-flow is exhausted. Each sacred form defined by what has dropped out of it. Then the festival of the wise attaining Your abode: what is not wonderful? Inward dissolution (v. 1) ends in outward celebration.

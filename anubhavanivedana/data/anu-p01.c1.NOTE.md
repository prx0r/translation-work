# C1 NOTE — anu-p01.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Mind-wind dissolved in the inward aim; seeing yet not seeing with steady gaze — this is śāmbhavī mudrā by the guru's grace alone: reality free of void and non-void.

## FUNCTION

Establishes śāmbhavī mudrā as occurring by grace, not performed.

## KEY TERMS

* **antarlakṣya** — inward aim
* **cittapavana** — mind-wind
* **niścalatārā** — unmoving pupil
* **paśyan-apaśyan** — seeing-not-seeing
* **yuṣmatprasāda** — your grace
* **śūnya-aśūnya** — void/non-void

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Condition and naming of śāmbhavī mudrā.

## EXPLANATION

The condition is dissolution, not effort: mind (citta) and wind (pavana, breath) dissolved (vilīna) in the inward aim (antarlakṣya). The gaze (dṛṣṭi) keeps an unmoving pupil (niścalatārā); outside (bahis), he sees (paśyan) and does not see (apaśyan) at once — sight without seizing. Then the naming: this (iyam) indeed (khalu) becomes (bhavati) the śāmbhavī mudrā — and only by your grace (yuṣmatprasādāt), O guru (guro). Not performed but occurring. Its character: devoid (vivarjitam) of void and non-void (śūnya-aśūnya) both — neither extreme held — becomes (bhavati) that (yat...tad) reality (tattvam), the śāmbhava state (padam śāmbhavam).

## BOUNDARY

Whether śāmbhavī mudrā is performed or occurring is unresolved.

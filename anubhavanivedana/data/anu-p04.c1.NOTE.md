# C1 NOTE — anu-p04.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Each sacred form defined by a vanishing: mantra where letters unnoticed, mudrā where all bodily action dissolved, yoga where breath-flow exhausted — then the festival: for the wise attaining Your abode, everything wonderful.

## FUNCTION

Establishes each sacred form by what drops out; festival of the abode.

## KEY TERMS

* **pratibhāti** — shines forth
* **varṇaracanā** — letter-arrangement
* **saṃlakṣ** — distinguish
* **samudeti** — arises
* **galita** — dissolved
* **kṛtsna** — entire
* **kriyā** — bodily action
* **prathate** — spreads forth
* **saṃkṣi** — exhaust
* **utsava** — festival
* **adhigama** — attaining
* **sudhī** — the wise
* **adbhuta** — wonderful

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Three definitions by vanishing; festival close.

## EXPLANATION

Three definitions by vanishing, in strict yasmin/yatra/yataḥ parallel: that (saḥ) mantra (mantraḥ) shines forth (pratibhāti) in which (yasmin) arrangement (racanā) of letters (varṇa) is not (na) distinguished (saṃlakṣyate); that (sā) mudrā (mudrā) arises (samudeti) in which (yatra) entire (kṛtsnā) bodily (kāyikī) action (kriyā) is dissolved (galitā); that (saḥ) yoga (yogaḥ) spreads forth (prathate) from which (yataḥ) the flowing (pravahaṇam) of breath (prāṇasya) is exhausted (saṃkṣīyate). Mantra without discernible letters; mudrā without bodily action; yoga without breath-flow — each sacred form defined by what has dropped out of it. Then: in the festivals (utsaveṣu) of attaining (adhigama) Your (tvad) abode (dhāma), of the wise (sudhiyām) — what (kiṃ), what (kim) is not (na), indeed (nāma), wonderful (adbhutam)? Beginning in inward dissolution, it ends in festival: everything, for the wise attaining the abode, is wonderful.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'sudhī' means 'the wise' or 'the good-minded' is unresolved.

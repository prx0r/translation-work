# Anuttarāṣṭikā — ARGMAP

*Abhinavagupta, 8 verses, 8 passages (1 verse each). Source: GRETIL (Marino Faliero 1998), TextGrid CC BY-NC-SA.*

*Work-level lore (kept here, not repeated per passage): Sferra 2010 (Italian testo e traduzione — the dedicated edition-translation of this stotra); Silburn, Hymnes de Abhinavagupta (1964); Pandey 1935 (first English mention, appendix); Lakshmanjoo tradition (reported remark: the one stotra one needs); Tantrāloka ch. 2 (anupāya — no-method — the stotra's kin doctrine).*

## Arc
- anu-p01 (v. 1): no method — six techniques ruled out; speak/hear the supreme truth; neither renouncer nor possessor; enjoy as staying.
- anu-p02 (v. 2): saṃsāra unreal for the embodied; bondage never anyone's, liberation-rite void; four-image delusion; abandon nothing, grasp nothing.
- anu-p03 (v. 3): worship-triad interrogated; Māyā conceded as apparent standing; all pure own-experience; no vain thought.
- anu-p04 (v. 4): bliss refused as pleasure and splendour; joy, burden lowered, forgotten treasure attained — light-rise.
- anu-p05 (v. 5): affections catalogued as one world-body, one nature; regard as one-self-natured consciousness-form; why not delight?
- anu-p06 (v. 6): things denied truth; four fetters transcended; imperative: awakened, become.
- anu-p07 (v. 7): no arising of things; shining by contemplation; universe-glory will-born; One shining many-selfed.
- anu-p08 (v. 8): opposites unsorted in the consciousness-mirror; known as own-grasping's rise; glory owned as Lordhood — worship.

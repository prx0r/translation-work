# Anuttarāṣṭikā — L1 controlled

## anu-p01 (1)
Transition here is not contemplation, nor connection-of-speech, nor discussion, nor visualization, nor steadying, nor japa-practice-effort. That — what name? — well-decided, speak the supreme truth, and that hear: not renouncer, not possessor — enjoy happiness as staying.

## anu-p02 (2)
Saṃsāra is not in truth for body-holders; of bondage, news what? Bondage of whom never is — for him liberation-action is false. This error-maker is rope-snake-shadow-ghost-delusion. Do not abandon anything; do not grasp; live healthy as staying.

## anu-p03 (3)
Worship-worshipper-worshipped difference-course — what speech in the unsurpassed? Transition indeed of whom, by whom does it happen, who is entry-course? This Māyā, not different from consciousness-nonduality in otherness, alas stands. All is own-experience-nature-pure. Thought in vain do not do.

## anu-p04 (4)
Bliss here is not wealth-middle (like enjoyment of women) nor lamp-sun-moon-made splendor-multitude. Joy with gathered-difference-liberation-bliss-ground, burden-lowering-like, is of all-nonduality-state's forgotten-treasure attainment = light-rise.

## anu-p05 (5)
Passion, aversion, pleasure, displeasure, rise, dissolution, I-ness, misery and the rest — these things shining-forth universe-bodied are not different-natured. Manifestation you see of whom suddenly — that-that one-self-natured consciousness-form having regarded — why do you not delight, full of contemplation?

## anu-p06 (6)
Former non-being, being, action indeed suddenly things always in this existence — middle-formed transformation-confusion of them, whence truth? In non-truth, unsteady, manifold, dream-delusion, clever — from doubt-fear-stain-reasoning-transcended, awakened become.

## anu-p07 (7)
Of things no arising is; natural, by your contemplation they shine; non-true too truth they partake by experience-delusion for a moment. This universe-glory from your will-born, its birth from elsewhere not; hence you by splendor shine in worlds one many-selfed.

## anu-p08 (8)
What true, what untrue, little-much, eternal-not-eternal, what Māyā-stained, what self-pure — in consciousness-mirror shines. That all from own-grasping-consciousness-rise, form-light-natured, having known — own-experience-risen glory, Lordhood, worship.

# anuttarastika anu-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
Transition here is no method at all — not contemplation, not speech-connection, not discussion, not visualization, not steadying, not japa-practice-effort. So what is well-decided? Speak the supreme truth, and hear it: not renouncer, not possessor — enjoy happiness, wholly, as staying.
The Sanskrit: *saṃkrāmaḥ atra na bhāvanā na ca kathāyuktiḥ na carcā na ca dhyānaṃ vā na ca dhāraṇā na ca japābhyāsaprayāso na ca tat kiṃ | nāma suniścitaṃ vada paraṃ satyaṃ ca tac śrūyatām na tyāgī na parigrahī bhaja sukhaṃ sarvam yathāvasthitaḥ*

# anuttarastika anu-p02 — L2 READ

## v.2
Saṃsāra is not, in truth, for body-holders — of bondage, what news? Bondage never truly is for anyone — so for the free, liberation-rite is false. This error-maker is rope-snake-shadow-ghost-delusion. Abandon nothing; grasp nothing; live healthy, staying as you are.
The Sanskrit: *saṃsāraḥ asti na tattvatas tanubhṛtām bandhasya vārtā eva kā bandhaḥ yasya na jātu tasya vitathā | muktasya muktikriyā eṣaḥ mithyāmohakṛt rajjubhujagacchāyāpiśācabhramaḥ mā kiṃcit tyaja mā gṛhāṇa vihara svasthaḥ yathāvasthitaḥ*

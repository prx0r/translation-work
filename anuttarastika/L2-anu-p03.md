# anuttarastika anu-p03 — L2 READ

## v.3
Worship, worshipper, worshipped — what a difference-course in the unsurpassed? Transition — of whom, by whom effected, and who is the entry-course? This Māyā, not different from consciousness-nonduality, in otherness, though different — alas, stands. All is pure own-experience-nature. Thought in vain — do not do.
The Sanskrit: *pūjapūjakapūjya bhedasaraṇiḥ keyam kathā anuttare saṃkrāmaḥ kila kasya kena vidadhe ko vā praveśakramaḥ māyeyam | na cidadvayāt paratayā bhinnā api aho vartate sarvam svānubhavasvabhāvavimalaṃ cintāṃ vṛthā mā kṛthāḥ*

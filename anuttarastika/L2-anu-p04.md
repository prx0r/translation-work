# anuttarastika anu-p04 — L2 READ

## v.4
Bliss here is not wealth-middle-intoxication, nor like enjoying women, nor lamp-sun-moon-made splendour-multitude — nor is it the light-rise. Joy with gathered-difference-liberation-bliss-ground, like burden-lowering, is the attainment of the forgotten treasure of the all-nonduality state — the light-rise.
The Sanskrit: *ānandaḥ atra na vittamadhya madavan naivāṅganāsaṅgavat dīpārkendukṛta prabhāprakaravan naiva | prakāśodayaḥ harṣaḥ saṃbhṛtabhedamuktisukhabhūr bhārāvatāropamaḥ sarvādvaitapadasya vismṛtanidheḥ prāptiḥ prakāśodayaḥ*

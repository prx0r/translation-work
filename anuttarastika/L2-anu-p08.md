# anuttarastika anu-p08 — L2 READ

## v.8
What true, what untrue, little-much, eternal, not eternal, what Māyā-stained, what self-pure — in the consciousness-mirror shines. That all, from own-grasping-consciousness-rise, form-light-natured, having known — own-experience-risen glory: Lordhood — worship.
The Sanskrit: *yat satyaṃ yad asatyam alpabahulaṃ nityaṃ na nityaṃ ca yat māyāmalinaṃ yad ātmavimalaṃ | ciddarpaṇe rājate tat sarvaṃ svavimarśasaṃvidudayād rūpaprakāśātmakaṃ jñātvā svānubhavādhirūḍha mahimā viśveśvaratvaṃ bhaja*

# Anuttarāṣṭikā anu-p01 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 1)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 anu-p01 = verse 1. Lore: Francesco Sferra 2010 (Italian testo e traduzione, the dedicated modern edition-translation of this stotra); the verse's method-negation is anupāya in miniature — cf. Tantrāloka ch. 2, where anupāya (no-method) crowns the means.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 1 of the Anuttarāṣṭikā (Abhinavagupta, 8 verses). Gloss: transition (saṃkrāmaḥ) here (atra) is not contemplation (bhāvanā), nor connection-of-speech (kathāyuktiḥ), nor discussion (carcā), nor visualization (dhyānaṃ), nor steadying (dhāraṇā), nor japa-practice-effort — that (tat), what (kiṃ) name (nāma), well-decided (suniścitam), speak (vada), supreme (paraṃ) truth (satyaṃ), and hear (tac śrūyatām): not renouncer (tyāgī), not possessor (parigrahī) — enjoy (bhaja) happiness (sukham) all (sarvam) as staying (yathāvasthitaḥ).

Sanskrit: saṃkrāmo 'tra na bhāvanā na ca kathāyuktir na carcā na ca dhyānaṃ vā na ca dhāraṇā na ca japābhyāsaprayāso na ca | tat kiṃ nāma suniścitaṃ vada paraṃ satyaṃ ca tacchrūyatāṃ na tyāgī na parigrahī bhaja sukhaṃ sarvaṃ yathāvasthitaḥ

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 1:**
> saṃkrāmo 'tra na bhāvanā na ca kathāyuktir na carcā na ca dhyānaṃ vā na ca dhāraṇā na ca japābhyāsaprayāso na ca | tat kiṃ nāma suniścitaṃ vada paraṃ satyaṃ ca tacchrūyatāṃ na tyāgī na parigrahī bhaja sukhaṃ sarvaṃ yathāvasthitaḥ

[i.e. transition here is no contemplation, no speech-connection, no discussion, no visualization, no steadying, no japa-practice-effort — so what, indeed, is well-decided? Speak the supreme truth, and hear it: no renouncer, no possessor — enjoy happiness wholly, as staying.]

Six techniques ruled out in one breath — bhāvanā, kathāyukti, carcā, dhyāna, dhāraṇā, japābhyāsa-prayāsa: the whole apparatus of method (upāya) cleared at the door. Then the question that replaces method-hunting: tat kiṃ nāma suniścitam — what, indeed, stands well-decided? The answer is spoken and heard (vada... śrūyatām), not practiced: supreme truth. Its content is two negations and one command — not a renouncer (na tyāgī), not a possessor (na parigrahī): enjoy (bhaja) happiness (sukham), all of it (sarvam), as staying (yathāvasthitaḥ). Staying is the method that is no method.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Saṃkrāmaḥ (transition) is the poem's own term for the movement from method to staying; not imported from elsewhere.
2. The verse is complete in itself — negation, question, stance — and needs nothing after it.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry clearing-then-stance: six negations, one question, one command. L2 must keep the full catalogue (each ruled-out technique named) and land hard on "stay, and enjoy." Carry the no-method frame into verse 2, where saṃsāra itself is denied.

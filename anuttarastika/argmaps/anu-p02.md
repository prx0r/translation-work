# Anuttarāṣṭikā anu-p02 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 2)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 anu-p02 = verse 2. Lore: the rope-snake-shadow-ghost heap (rajjubhujagacchāyāpiśācabhrama) is adhyāsa (superimposition) in four images at once; the whole practice that follows is two prohibitions — mā kiṃcit tyaja, mā gṛhāṇa (abandon nothing, grasp nothing).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 2 of the Anuttarāṣṭikā (Abhinavagupta, 8 verses). Gloss: saṃsāra (saṃsāraḥ) is not (asti na) in truth (tattvatas) for body-holders (tanubhṛtām); of bondage (bandhasya) news (vārtā) what (eva kā)? Bondage (bandhaḥ) of whom (yasya) never (na jātu) is — of him (tasya) false (vitathā) is liberation-action (muktasya muktikriyā). This (eṣaḥ) error-making (mithyāmohakṛt) is rope-snake-shadow-ghost-delusion. Do-not (mā) anything (kiṃcit) abandon (tyaja); do-not (mā) grasp (gṛhāṇa); live (vihara) healthy (svasthaḥ) as staying (yathāvasthitaḥ).

Sanskrit: saṃsāro 'sti na tattvatas tanubhṛtāṃ bandhasya vārtaiva kā bandho yasya na jātu tasya vitathā muktasya muktikriyā | mithyāmohakṛd eṣa rajjubhujagacchāyāpiśācabhramo mā kiṃcit tyaja mā gṛhāṇa vihara svastho yathāvasthitaḥ

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 2:**
> saṃsāro 'sti na tattvatas tanubhṛtāṃ bandhasya vārtaiva kā bandho yasya na jātu tasya vitathā muktasya muktikriyā | mithyāmohakṛd eṣa rajjubhujagacchāyāpiśācabhramo mā kiṃcit tyaja mā gṛhāṇa vihara svastho yathāvasthitaḥ

[i.e. saṃsāra is not, in truth, for body-holders — of bondage, what news? Bondage of whom never is — for him liberation-action is false. This error-maker is rope-snake-shadow-ghost-delusion. Abandon nothing, grasp nothing; live healthy, as staying.]

Denial first: saṃsāra is not (asti na), in truth (tattvatas), for body-holders — the apparent bondage is not ultimately real. Then the consequence pressed as a question: of bondage, what news (vārtā eva kā)? If bondage never truly is for anyone (bandho yasya na jātu), then for the free one liberation-action (muktikriyā) is false (vitathā) — nothing to be freed from, no rite of freeing. Diagnosis: this error-maker (mithyāmohakṛt) is a fourfold delusion-heap — rope-snake, shadow, ghost (rajju-bhujaga-cchāyā-piśāca-bhrama). Rule in two prohibitions: abandon nothing (mā kiṃcit tyaja), grasp nothing (mā gṛhāṇa); live (vihara) healthy (svasthaḥ), as staying (yathāvasthitaḥ).

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Vitathā (false) qualifies the liberation-rite, not liberation: for the never-bound, there is no rite left to perform.
2. Svastha (healthy/standing in own nature) returns in verse 4's architecture (svastha implied in self-standing); noted, not forced.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry denial-consequence-diagnosis-rule in that order: unreal saṃsāra → false liberation-rite → delusion-heap named → two prohibitions. L2 must keep all four delusion images and both prohibitions verbatim in sense. Carry "abandon nothing, grasp nothing" into verse 3, where even worship-structure is dissolved.

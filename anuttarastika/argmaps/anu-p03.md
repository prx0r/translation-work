# Anuttarāṣṭikā anu-p03 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 3)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 anu-p03 = verse 3. Lore: the verse opens with three unanswerable questions (keyam...? kasya kena...? ko vā...?) that dissolve the worship-structure — pūjā, pūjaka, pūjya — inside the unsurpassed (anuttara); the "alas" (aho) marks the pathos of Māyā apparently standing apart.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 3 of the Anuttarāṣṭikā (Abhinavagupta, 8 verses). Gloss: worship-worshipper-worshipped (pūjapūjakapūjya) difference-course (bhedasaraṇiḥ) what (keyam) speech (kathā) in-unsurpassed (anuttare)? Transition (saṃkrāmaḥ) indeed (kila) of-whom (kasya) by-whom (kena) does (vidadhe) who (ko vā) entry-course (praveśakramaḥ)? This (māyeyam) not (na) from-consciousness-nonduality (cidadvayāt) from-otherness (paratayā) different (bhinnā api) alas (aho) stands (vartate). All (sarvam) own-experience-nature-pure (svānubhavasvabhāvavimalam). Thought (cintāṃ) in-vain (vṛthā) do-not (mā) do (kṛthāḥ).

Sanskrit: pūjapūjakapūjyabhedasaraṇiḥ keyaṃ kathānuttare saṃkrāmaḥ kila kasya kena vidadhe ko vā praveśakramaḥ | māyeyam na cidadvayāt paratayā bhinnāpy aho vartate sarvaṃ svānubhavasvabhāvavimalaṃ cintāṃ vṛthā mā kṛthāḥ

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 3:**
> pūjapūjakapūjyabhedasaraṇiḥ keyaṃ kathānuttare saṃkrāmaḥ kila kasya kena vidadhe ko vā praveśakramaḥ | māyeyam na cidadvayāt paratayā bhinnāpy aho vartate sarvaṃ svānubhavasvabhāvavimalaṃ cintāṃ vṛthā mā kṛthāḥ

[i.e. worship-worshipper-worshipped difference-course — what speech in the unsurpassed? Transition indeed — of whom, by whom is it effected, who is the entry-course? This Māyā, not different from consciousness-nonduality, in otherness, though different — alas, stands. All is pure own-experience-nature. Thought in vain — do not do.]

Challenge in three questions: the worship-triad's difference-course (bhedasaraṇi) — what speech of it (keyaṃ kathā) in the unsurpassed (anuttare)? Transition (saṃkrāmaḥ) — of whom (kasya), by whom (kena) effected, and who (ko vā) is the entry-course (praveśakramaḥ)? The questions dissolve the worship-structure: subject, means, and path all interrogated at once. Then the concession: this Māyā (māyā-iyam), not different (na...bhinnā) from consciousness-nonduality (cidadvayāt) — though appearing different (api) in otherness (paratayā) — alas (aho), stands (vartate). Māyā is the apparent difference that stands apart without being truly other. Then the purity and the rule: all (sarvam) is pure (vimalam) own-experience-nature (svānubhavasvabhāva); thought (cintām) in vain (vṛthā) do not do (mā kṛthāḥ).

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. "Alas" (aho) carries pathos, not doctrine: the concession is felt before it is placed.
2. Māyā's standing (vartate) is concessive and apparent; not read as ontological independence.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry questions-then-concession-then-rule: dissolve worship-structure, grant Māyā's apparent standing, forbid vain thought. L2 must keep all three questions and the "alas." Carry the purity ("all is pure own-experience-nature") into verse 4, where bliss is defined from it.

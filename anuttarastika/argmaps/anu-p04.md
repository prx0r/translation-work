# Anuttarāṣṭikā anu-p04 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 4)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 anu-p04 = verse 4. Lore: ānanda defined twice-negatively (not wealth-middle, not lamp-sun-moon splendour) before the positive term prakāśodaya (light-rise) — the verse's structure is apophatic definition; bhārāvatāra (burden-lowering) images the relief of laying down difference.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 4 of the Anuttarāṣṭikā (Abhinavagupta, 8 verses). Gloss: bliss (ānandaḥ) here (atra) not (na) is wealth-middle (vittamadhya) like (madavan) enjoying-women (naivāṅganāsaṅgavat) nor lamp-sun-moon-made (dīpārkendukṛta) splendour-multitude-like (prabhāprakaravan) not (naiva) light-rise (prakāśodayaḥ). Joy (harṣaḥ) with-gathered-difference-liberation-bliss-ground (saṃbhṛtabhedamuktisukhabhūḥ) burden-lowering-like (bhārāvatāropamaḥ) of-all-nonduality-state (sarvādvaitapadasya) forgotten-treasure (vismṛtanidheḥ) attainment (prāptiḥ) is (prakāśodayaḥ).

Sanskrit: ānando 'tra na vittamadhyamadavan naivāṅganāsaṅgavat dīpārkendukṛtaprabhāprakaravan naiva prakāśodayaḥ | harṣaḥ saṃbhṛtabhedamuktisukhabhūr bhārāvatāropamaḥ sarvādvaitapadasya vismṛtanidheḥ prāptiḥ prakāśodayaḥ

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 4:**
> ānando 'tra na vittamadhyamadavan naivāṅganāsaṅgavat dīpārkendukṛtaprabhāprakaravan naiva prakāśodayaḥ | harṣaḥ saṃbhṛtabhedamuktisukhabhūr bhārāvatāropamaḥ sarvādvaitapadasya vismṛtanidheḥ prāptiḥ prakāśodayaḥ

[i.e. bliss here is not wealth-middle-intoxication, nor like enjoying women, nor lamp-sun-moon-made splendour-multitude — nor is it the light-rise. Joy with gathered-difference-liberation-bliss-ground, burden-lowering-like, is the attainment of the forgotten treasure of the all-nonduality state — the light-rise.]

Two false candidates refused: intoxication (madavana) of the wealth-middle (vittamadhya) — like enjoying women (naivāṅganāsaṅgavat) — that is, pleasure; and lamp-sun-moon-made (dīpārkendukṛta) splendour-multitude (prabhāprakara) — that is, splendour. Bliss (ānanda) is neither. Then the positive, in one compound surge: joy (harṣaḥ) with gathered-difference-liberation-bliss-ground (saṃbhṛtabhedamuktisukhabhūḥ), like burden-lowering (bhārāvatāropamaḥ), is the attainment (prāptiḥ) of the forgotten treasure (vismṛtanidheḥ) of the all-nonduality state (sarvādvaitapadasya) — the light-rise (prakāśodayaḥ). Bliss = prakāśodaya, the forgotten treasure found: difference gathered up, burden set down, light risen.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Bhārāvatāra (burden-lowering) is read as simile of relief (laying down difference); not pressed further.
2. Vittamadhya (wealth-middle) is left unexpanded; the verse refuses it without defining it.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry refusal-then-definition: neither pleasure nor splendour — then joy, burden lowered, forgotten treasure, light risen. L2 must keep both negations and the full positive compound; the compound is the definition. Carry the light-rise (prakāśodaya) into verse 5, where all things are seen as its form.

# Anuttarāṣṭikā anu-p06 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 6)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 anu-p06 = verse 6. Lore: the four obstacles named in one compound — doubt, fear, stain, reasoning (śaṅkātaṅkakalaṅkayukti) — with yukti (reasoning) listed last among the fetters; the verse closes on the hymn's only wake-up imperative, prabuddho bhava (awakened become).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 6 of the Anuttarāṣṭikā (Abhinavagupta, 8 verses). Gloss: former (pūrva) non-being (abhāva), being (bhava), action (kriyā) indeed (hi) suddenly (sahasā) things (bhāvāḥ) always (sadā) in-this (asmin) existence (bhave) — middle-formed (madhyākāra) transformation (vikāra) confusion (saṃkaravatāṃ) of-them (teṣām) whence (kutaḥ) truth (satyatā)? In-non-truth (niḥsatye) unsteady (capale) manifold (prapañcanicaye) dream-delusion (svapnabhrame) clever (peśale) from-doubt-fear-stain-reasoning-transcended (śaṅkātaṅkakalaṅkayuktikalanātītaḥ) awakened (prabuddho) become (bhava).

Sanskrit: pūrvābhāvabhavakriyā hi sahasā bhāvāḥ sadā 'smin bhave madhyākāravikārasaṃkaravatāṃ teṣāṃ kutaḥ satyatā | niḥsatye capale prapañcanicaye svapnabhrame peśale śaṅkātaṅkakalaṅkayuktikalanātītaḥ prabuddho bhava

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 6:**
> pūrvābhāvabhavakriyā hi sahasā bhāvāḥ sadā 'smin bhave madhyākāravikārasaṃkaravatāṃ teṣāṃ kutaḥ satyatā | niḥsatye capale prapañcanicaye svapnabhrame peśale śaṅkātaṅkakalaṅkayuktikalanātītaḥ prabuddho bhava

[i.e. former non-being, being, action — indeed suddenly things always in this existence; of middle-formed transformation-confusion, whence truth? In non-truth, unsteady, manifold, dream-delusion, clever — from doubt-fear-stain-reasoning transcended: awakened become.]

First the truth-question: former non-being (pūrvābhāva), being (bhava), action (kriyā) — indeed (hi) suddenly (sahasā) things (bhāvāḥ) always (sadā) in this existence (asmin bhave) — of middle-formed (madhyākāra) transformation (vikāra), confusion (saṃkara) of them (teṣām), whence (kutaḥ) truth (satyatā)? Things appear suddenly, middle-formed, confused — what truth can they have? Then the way out, stated as a field and a crossing: in non-truth (niḥsatye), unsteady (capale), manifold (prapañcanicaye), dream-delusion (svapnabhrame), clever (peśale) — from doubt-fear-stain-reasoning-transcended (śaṅkā-taṅka-kalaṅka-yukti-kalanātītaḥ): awakened (prabuddhaḥ), become (bhava). The four — doubt, fear, stain, reasoning — are the obstacles; transcending their contriving (kalanā), one awakens. The verse's only imperative is the hymn's climax-command.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The clever (peśala) is kept as a register term (flagged in T1); read as the dream-delusion's cunning, not expanded.
2. Kalanā (contriving) is read with all four obstacles (transcending doubt-fear-stain-reasoning-contriving), not reasoning alone.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry question-then-command: whence truth for confused sudden things? — transcend the four, awaken. L2 must keep all four obstacles named and the imperative last and bare. Carry the awakening into verse 7, where the awakened one wills the universe.

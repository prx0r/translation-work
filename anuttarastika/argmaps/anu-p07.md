# Anuttarāṣṭikā anu-p07 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 7)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 anu-p07 = verse 7. Lore: tvatsaṅkalpaja (will-born) eṣa viśvamahimā — the universe-glory born of your will, with birth from elsewhere denied — is the hymn's cosmogonic thesis in one line; eko 'py anekātmakaḥ (one, yet many-selfed) states nonduality without cancelling multiplicity.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 7 of the Anuttarāṣṭikā (Abhinavagupta, 8 verses). Gloss: of-things (bhāvānāṃ) no (na) arising (samudbhavo 'sti) — natural (sahajas) by-your-contemplation (tvadbhāvitā) they-shine (bhānty amī) non-true (niḥsatyā api) truth (satyatām) by-experience-delusion (anubhavabhrāntyā) partake (bhajanti) for-a-moment (kṣaṇam). This (eṣa) universe-glory (viśvamahimā) from-your-will-born (tvatsaṅkalpajaḥ) not (nāsty) its (asya) birth (janmā) from-elsewhere (anyataḥ). Hence (tasmāt) you (tvaṃ) by-splendor (vibhavena) shine (bhāsi) in-worlds (bhuvaneṣv) one (ekopy) many-selfed (anekātmakaḥ).

Sanskrit: bhāvānāṃ na samudbhavo 'sti sahajas tvadbhāvitā bhānty amī niḥsatyā api satyatām anubhavabhrāntyā bhajanti kṣaṇam | tvatsaṅkalpaja eṣa viśvamahimā nāsty asya janmānyataḥ tasmāt tvaṃ vibhavena bhāsi bhuvaneṣv ekopy anekātmakaḥ

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 7:**
> bhāvānāṃ na samudbhavo 'sti sahajas tvadbhāvitā bhānty amī niḥsatyā api satyatām anubhavabhrāntyā bhajanti kṣaṇam | tvatsaṅkalpaja eṣa viśvamahimā nāsty asya janmānyataḥ tasmāt tvaṃ vibhavena bhāsi bhuvaneṣv ekopy anekātmakaḥ

[i.e. of things no arising is; natural, by your contemplation they shine; non-true too truth they partake by experience-delusion for a moment. This universe-glory from your will-born, its birth from elsewhere not; hence you by splendor shine in worlds, one many-selfed.]

No-arising first: of things (bhāvānām) no arising (na samudbhavaḥ) is — natural (sahajaḥ, supply the subject from context: it is natural that), by your contemplation (tvadbhāvitā) they (amī) shine (bhānti); non-true (niḥsatyāḥ) too (api), truth (satyatām) they partake (bhajanti) by experience-delusion (anubhavabhrāntyā) for a moment (kṣaṇam). Things don't arise on their own; they shine because contemplated; even false things borrow a moment's truth from experience's delusion. Then the origin: this universe-glory (eṣa viśvamahimā) will-born from you (tvatsaṅkalpajaḥ); its birth (janma) from elsewhere (anyataḥ) is not (nāsti). Hence (tasmāt) you (tvaṃ) by splendor (vibhavena) shine (bhāsi) in worlds (bhuvaneṣu) — one (ekaḥ, though api) many-selfed (anekātmakaḥ). The universe's glory is will-born, not from elsewhere; you shine in worlds as one-many-selfed.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Sahajaḥ (natural) is read as predicate of the shining (it is natural that they shine by contemplation), not as a separate agent.
2. Experience-delusion (anubhavabhrānti) is Abhinava's own term for borrowed momentary truth; not expanded beyond the verse.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry no-arising-then-will-born-then-shining: things don't arise, they shine by contemplation, the glory is will-born, you shine one-many-selfed. L2 must keep "for a moment" (kṣaṇam) — borrowed truth is time-limited. Carry the will-born universe into verse 8, where all of it shines in the mirror.

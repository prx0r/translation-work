# Anuttarāṣṭikā anu-p08 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 8)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 anu-p08 = verse 8. Lore: the consciousness-mirror (ciddarpaṇa) in which all opposites shine without being sorted — true/untrue, stained/pure side by side; the closing command viśveśvaratvaṃ bhaja (worship Lordhood) turns the whole hymn's recognition into enjoined worship.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 8 of the Anuttarāṣṭikā (Abhinavagupta, 8 verses). Gloss: what (yat) true (satyaṃ) what (yad) untrue (asatyam) little-much (alpabahulaṃ) eternal (nityaṃ) not (na) eternal (nityaṃ ca) what (yat) Māyā-stained (māyāmalinaṃ) what (yad) self-pure (ātmavimalaṃ) — in-consciousness-mirror (ciddarpaṇe) shines (rājate). That (tat) all (sarvaṃ) from-own-grasping-consciousness-rise (svavimarśasaṃvidudayād) form-light-natured (rūpaprakāśātmakaṃ) having-known (jñātvā) — own-experience-risen (svānubhavādhirūḍha) glory (mahimā) Lordhood (viśveśvaratvaṃ) worship (bhaja).

Sanskrit: yat satyaṃ yad asatyam alpabahulaṃ nityaṃ na nityaṃ ca yat yan māyāmalinaṃ yad ātmavimalaṃ ciddarpaṇe rājate | tat sarvaṃ svavimarśasaṃvidudayād rūpaprakāśātmakaṃ jñātvā svānubhavādhirūḍhamahimā viśveśvaratvaṃ bhaja

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 8:**
> yat satyaṃ yad asatyam alpabahulaṃ nityaṃ na nityaṃ ca yat yan māyāmalinaṃ yad ātmavimalaṃ ciddarpaṇe rājate | tat sarvaṃ svavimarśasaṃvidudayād rūpaprakāśātmakaṃ jñātvā svānubhavādhirūḍhamahimā viśveśvaratvaṃ bhaja

[i.e. what true, what untrue, little-much, eternal, not eternal, what Māyā-stained, what self-pure — in the consciousness-mirror shines. That all, from own-grasping-consciousness-rise, form-light-natured, having known — own-experience-risen glory: Lordhood, worship.]

First the mirror: what (yat) true (satyam), what (yad) untrue (asatyam), little-much (alpabahulam), eternal (nityam), not eternal (na nityam), what Māyā-stained (māyāmalinam), what self-pure (ātmavimalam) — shines (rājate) in the consciousness-mirror (ciddarpaṇe). All opposites shine in the same mirror; the mirror doesn't sort them. Then the knowing and the command: that all (tat sarvaṃ), from own-grasping-consciousness-rise (svavimarśasaṃvidudayāt), form-light-natured (rūpaprakāśātmakam), having known (jñātvā) — own-experience-risen (svānubhavādhirūḍha) glory (mahimā): Lordhood (viśveśvaratvam) — worship (bhaja). Knowing that all opposites shine in the mirror from one's own grasping-consciousness-rise = own-experience-risen glory = Lordhood; and the hymn's last word is the imperative: worship.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Svavimarśasaṃvidudaya (own-grasping-consciousness-rise) is Abhinava's own compound; glossed, not unpacked beyond the verse.
2. Bhaja (worship) closes the hymn on command, matching the opening's vada (speak) — noted as framing, not argued.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry mirror-then-knowing-then-command: opposites unsorted in one mirror, knowing that is glory, glory commanded as worship. L2 must keep the full opposites catalogue and end on the imperative. This closes the arc opened in verse 1's "speak the supreme truth."

# C1 — Anuttarāṣṭikā

## anu-p01 — no method, just staying
**SUMMARY.** Six techniques ruled out at the door — contemplation, speech-connection, discussion, visualization, steadying, japa-effort. The question replaces the hunt for method: what stands well-decided? Speak it, hear it: neither renouncing nor possessing — enjoy happiness wholly, as staying.

**KEY TERMS.** saṃkrāma (transition), bhāvanā (contemplation), kathāyukti (speech-connection), carcā (discussion), dhyāna (visualization), dhāraṇā (steadying), japābhyāsa (japa-practice), suniścita (well-decided), paraṃ satyam (supreme truth), tyāgī/parigrahī (renouncer/possessor), bhaja sukham (enjoy happiness), yathāvasthita (as staying).

**EXPLANATION.** Method cleared in a single breath: six named techniques, all denied. Then, not a seventh technique, but a question — what, indeed, stands well-decided? The answer is spoken and heard, not practiced: the supreme truth. Its content is two refusals and one command. No renouncing the world, no possessing it: enjoy happiness, all of it, as staying. Staying (yathāvasthita) is the method that is no method — anupāya named by performance, not by label.

## anu-p02 — saṃsāra denied, the two prohibitions
**SUMMARY.** Saṃsāra is unreal for the embodied; bondage never truly is for anyone, so the liberation-rite is false. The error is a fourfold delusion-heap — rope, snake, shadow, ghost. Abandon nothing, grasp nothing; live healthy, staying.

**KEY TERMS.** tattvatas (in truth), tanubhṛt (body-holders), vārtā (news), vitathā (false), muktikriyā (liberation-action/rite), mithyāmoha (error-delusion), rajjubhujagacchāyāpiśācabhrama (rope-snake-shadow-ghost-delusion), svastha (healthy/standing in own nature).

**EXPLANATION.** Denial first: saṃsāra is not — in truth (tattvatas) — for body-holders. Then the consequence pressed as a question: of bondage, what news? If bondage never truly is anyone's (bandho yasya na jātu), the liberation-rite (muktikriyā) is false (vitathā) for the free — nothing to be freed from, no rite of freeing. Diagnosis: an error-maker (mithyāmohakṛt), a heap of four delusions — rope mistaken, snake seen, shadow, ghost. Rule in two prohibitions: abandon nothing (mā kiṃcit tyaja), grasp nothing (mā gṛhāṇa); live (vihara) healthy (svasthaḥ) — standing in own nature — as staying (yathāvasthitaḥ).

## anu-p03 — worship-difference dissolved
**SUMMARY.** Worshipper, worship, worshipped — what speech of this difference in the unsurpassed? Transition: of whom, by whom, who enters? Māyā, not different from consciousness-nonduality, stands as other — alas. All is pure own-experience-nature; vain thought, don't.

**KEY TERMS.** pūjapūjakapūjya (worship-worshipper-worshipped), bhedasaraṇi (difference-course), anuttara (unsurpassed), praveśakrama (entry-course), māyā (standing-apart), cidadvaya (consciousness-nonduality), paratayā (in otherness), aho (alas), svānubhavasvabhāvavimala (pure own-experience-nature).

**EXPLANATION.** Three questions dissolve the worship-structure: the triad's difference-course (bhedasaraṇi) — what speech (kathā) of it holds in the unsurpassed (anuttare)? Transition (saṃkrāmaḥ) — of whom (kasya), by whom (kena) effected? Who (ko vā) is the entry-course (praveśakramaḥ)? Subject, means, and path interrogated at once — no worshipper left standing apart from worshipped. Then the concession: this Māyā (māyā-iyam), not different (na...bhinnā) from consciousness-nonduality (cidadvayāt) — though appearing different (api) in otherness (paratayā) — alas (aho), stands (vartate). Difference stands apparently, without being truly other. Then purity and rule: all (sarvam) is pure (vimalam) own-experience-nature (svānubhavasvabhāva); thought (cintām) in vain (vṛthā) — don't do (mā kṛthāḥ).

## anu-p04 — bliss defined and found
**SUMMARY.** Bliss is neither wealth-intoxication nor lamp-sun-moon splendour. Joy with gathered difference, burden lowered like, is the attainment of nonduality's forgotten treasure — the light-rise.

**KEY TERMS.** ānanda (bliss), vittamadhya (wealth-middle), madavana (intoxication), aṅganāsaṅga (enjoying women), dīpārkendu (lamp-sun-moon), prabhāprakara (splendour-multitude), harṣa (joy), bhārāvatāra (burden-lowering), vismṛtanidhi (forgotten treasure), sarvādvaitapada (all-nonduality state), prakāśodaya (light-rise).

**EXPLANATION.** Two refusals: bliss (ānandaḥ) here (atra) is not (na) wealth-middle-intoxication (vittamadhyamadavana) — not (naiva) like enjoying women (aṅganāsaṅgavat); nor lamp-sun-moon-made (dīpārkendukṛta) splendour-multitude (prabhāprakaravat) — nor (naiva) even the light-rise (prakāśodayaḥ) as commonly pictured. Pleasure refused, splendour refused. Then the positive, in one surge: joy (harṣaḥ) with gathered-difference-liberation-bliss-ground, like burden-lowering (bhārāvatāropamaḥ) — laying down difference the way one sets down a load — is the attainment (prāptiḥ) of the forgotten treasure (vismṛtanidheḥ) of the all-nonduality state (sarvādvaitapadasya): the light-rise (prakāśodayaḥ). Bliss = prakāśodaya, difference gathered, burden set down, treasure remembered.

## anu-p05 — variety is one nature; why not delight?
**SUMMARY.** Passions through misery — the whole catalogue of feeling-states, universe-bodied — are not different-natured. Whatever manifestation you suddenly see, regard as one-self-natured consciousness-form: why not delight, contemplation-full?

**KEY TERMS.** rāga/dveṣa/sukha/asukha/udaya/laya/ahaṃkāra/dainya (passion/aversion/pleasure/displeasure/rise/dissolution/I-ness/misery), viśvavapus (universe-bodied), bhinnasvabhāva (different-natured), vyakti (manifestation), sahasā (suddenly), ekātmatāsaṃvidrūpa (one-self-natured consciousness-form), avekṣya (having regarded), ramase (you delight), bhāvanānirbhara (contemplation-full).

**EXPLANATION.** The list first: passion through misery and the rest (ādayaḥ) — those things (bhāvāḥ) shining-forth (pravibhānti), universe-bodied (viśvavapuṣo) — are not (na te) different-natured (bhinnasvabhāvāḥ). Feeling-states compose one world-body; variety of experience is not variety of nature. Then the address: manifestation (vyaktim) you see (paśyasi), of whomever (yasya yasya), suddenly (sahasā) — having regarded (avekṣya) that-that (tattattad) one-self-natured (ekātmatā) consciousness-form (saṃvidrūpam) — why (kiṃ na) do you delight (ramase), full (nirbharaḥ) of that contemplation (tadbhāvanā)? Regard is the whole practice here: see variety as one-self-natured consciousness-form — and delight is the only consistent response.

## anu-p06 — truth denied to things; awaken
**SUMMARY.** Former non-being, being, action — things always, suddenly here, middle-formed and confused: whence their truth? In the clever dream-delusion of the non-true, transcend doubt, fear, stain, reasoning's contriving: awakened, become.

**KEY TERMS.** pūrvābhāva/bhava/kriyā (former non-being/being/action), sahasā (suddenly), sadā (always), madhyākāra (middle-formed), vikāra/saṃkara (transformation/confusion), kutaḥ satyatā (whence truth?), niḥsatya (non-truth), capala (unsteady), prapañcanicaya (manifold), svapnabhrama (dream-delusion), peśala (clever), śaṅkā/taṅka/kalaṅka/yukti (doubt/fear/stain/reasoning), kalanā (contriving), prabuddha (awakened).

**EXPLANATION.** The truth-question: former non-being (pūrvābhāva), being (bhava), action (kriyā) — indeed (hi) suddenly (sahasā) things (bhāvāḥ) always (sadā) in this existence (asmin bhave) — of middle-formed (madhyākāra) transformation (vikāra), confusion (saṃkaravatāṃ) of them (teṣām): whence (kutaḥ) truth (satyatā)? Things appear suddenly, middle-formed, confused — what truth can they have? Then the field and the crossing: in non-truth (niḥsatye), unsteady (capale), manifold (prapañcanicaye), dream-delusion (svapnabhrame), clever (peśale) — from doubt-fear-stain-reasoning-contriving transcended (śaṅkātaṅkakalaṅkayuktikalanātītaḥ): awakened (prabuddhaḥ), become (bhava). Four obstacles — doubt, fear, stain, reasoning — with reasoning (yukti) listed last among the fetters. Transcending their contriving (kalanā): the hymn's only wake-up imperative.

## anu-p07 — no arising; will-born glory
**SUMMARY.** Of things no arising; naturally they shine by your contemplation, even the non-true borrowing momentary truth. The universe-glory is will-born, birth from elsewhere denied; hence you shine in worlds — one, yet many-selfed.

**KEY TERMS.** samudbhava (arising), sahaja (natural), tvadbhāvitā (by your contemplation), niḥsatya (non-true), anubhavabhrānti (experience-delusion), kṣaṇa (moment), viśvamahimā (universe-glory), tvatsaṅkalpaja (will-born from you), anyataḥ (from elsewhere), vibhava (splendor), eko 'py anekātmakaḥ (one, yet many-selfed).

**EXPLANATION.** No-arising first: of things (bhāvānām) no arising (na samudbhavaḥ) is — natural (sahajaḥ: it is natural that), by your contemplation (tvadbhāvitā) they shine (bhānti); non-true (niḥsatyāḥ) too (api), truth (satyatām) they partake (bhajanti) by experience-delusion (anubhavabhrāntyā) for a moment (kṣaṇam) — even the false borrows a moment's truth from experience's delusion. Then the origin: this universe-glory (eṣa viśvamahimā) will-born from you (tvatsaṅkalpajaḥ); its birth (janma) from elsewhere (anyataḥ) is not (nāsti). Hence (tasmāt) you shine (bhāsi) by splendor (vibhavena) in worlds (bhuvaneṣu) — one (ekaḥ), though (api), many-selfed (anekātmakaḥ). One, yet many-selfed: nonduality without cancelling multiplicity.

## anu-p08 — the mirror, Lordhood, worship
**SUMMARY.** What true, untrue, little-much, eternal, non-eternal, Māyā-stained, self-pure — all shines unsorted in the consciousness-mirror. Known as rising from own-grasping consciousness, this is own-experience-risen glory: Lordhood — worship.

**KEY TERMS.** satya/asatya (true/untrue), alpabahula (little-much), nitya (eternal), māyāmalina (Māyā-stained), ātmavimala (self-pure), ciddarpaṇa (consciousness-mirror), rājate (shines), svavimarśasaṃvidudaya (own-grasping-consciousness-rise), rūpaprakāśātmaka (form-light-natured), jñātvā (having known), svānubhavādhirūḍhamahimā (own-experience-risen glory), viśveśvaratva (Lordhood), bhaja (worship).

**EXPLANATION.** First the mirror: what true, what untrue, little-much, eternal, not eternal, what Māyā-stained, what self-pure — shines (rājate) in the consciousness-mirror (ciddarpaṇe). All opposites, one mirror; the mirror doesn't sort them. Then the knowing and the command: that all (tat sarvaṃ), from own-grasping-consciousness-rise (svavimarśasaṃvidudayāt), form-light-natured (rūpaprakāśātmakam), having known (jñātvā) — own-experience-risen (svānubhavādhirūḍha) glory (mahimā): Lordhood (viśveśvaratvam) — worship (bhaja). Knowing that all opposites shine in the mirror from one's own grasping consciousness = glory = Lordhood; and the hymn's last word is the imperative it opened with the stance for: speak (v. 1), worship (v. 8).

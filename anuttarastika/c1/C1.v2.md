# C1 — Anuttarāṣṭikā (final condensation of the arguments)

*States each passage's argumentative move and the hymn's whole arc. For the read prose see L2.v2.*

## The arc in one paragraph

Method cleared at the door — six techniques ruled out, staying enjoined, neither renouncing nor possessing. Saṃsāra denied for the embodied, bondage unowned, liberation-rite voided; delusion named in four images, practice reduced to two prohibitions.

Worship's triad interrogated into silence; Māyā conceded as apparent standing, all declared pure own-experience, vain thought refused. Bliss refused as pleasure and as splendour, then found: joy with gathered difference, burden lowered, forgotten treasure, light risen. The affections catalogued as one world-body, one nature; the seer challenged to delight.

Things denied truth — sudden, middle-formed, confused; four fetters transcended, the imperative to awaken. No arising of things; shining by contemplation, momentary truth borrowed, universe-glory will-born, the One shining many-selfed. Opposites unsorted in the consciousness-mirror; known as own-grasping's rise, glory owned as Lordhood — worship. Negation → denial → dissolution → definition → delight → awakening → willing → mirroring.

## anu-p01 (v. 1) — method cleared, staying enjoined

Six techniques ruled out in one breath; the question replaces the hunt. Answer spoken and heard, not practiced: supreme truth — neither renouncer nor possessor, happiness enjoyed wholly, as staying. Staying is the method that is no method.

## anu-p02 (v. 2) — bondage unowned, rite voided

Saṃsāra unreal for the embodied; bondage, never anyone's, voids the liberation-rite — nothing to free, no rite of freeing. Four-image delusion diagnosed; two prohibitions prescribed: abandon nothing, grasp nothing; live healthy, staying.

## anu-p03 (v. 3) — worship dissolved, Māyā conceded

Worshipper, worship, worshipped interrogated — of whom, by whom, who enters? — until no structure stands in the unsurpassed. Māyā granted apparent standing, denied true otherness; all pure own-experience; vain thought refused.

## anu-p04 (v. 4) — bliss refused, then found

Neither wealth-intoxication nor lamp-sun-moon splendour. Joy with gathered difference, burden lowered like, attains nonduality's forgotten treasure: the light-rise. Bliss defined by double refusal, then named.

## anu-p05 (v. 5) — variety one-natured, delight demanded

Passions through misery, universe-bodied, not different-natured. Regard whatever suddenly appears as one-self-natured consciousness-form — then why not delight, contemplation-full? Variety seen truly leaves delight as the only consistent response.

## anu-p06 (v. 6) — things denied truth, the imperative to awaken

Sudden, middle-formed, confused things — whence their truth? In clever dream-delusion, transcend doubt, fear, stain, reasoning's contriving: awakened, become. The hymn's only command, earned by six verses of clearing.

## anu-p07 (v. 7) — no arising, will-born glory

Things do not arise; they shine by contemplation, borrowing momentary truth. The universe-glory is will-born, birth from elsewhere denied. Hence the One shines in worlds, many-selfed: nonduality without cancelling multiplicity.

## anu-p08 (v. 8) — mirror, Lordhood, worship

Opposites unsorted in one mirror; known as rising from own-grasping consciousness, this is own-experience-risen glory. Glory owned is Lordhood; Lordhood ends in the imperative the hymn opened in stance for: speak (v. 1), worship (v. 8).

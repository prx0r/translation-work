# C1 NOTE — anu-p01.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

No method is enjoined: six techniques ruled out at the door. The question replaces the hunt for method: what stands well-decided? Speak it, hear it: neither renouncing nor possessing — enjoy happiness wholly, as staying.

## FUNCTION

Establishes anupāya (no method) as the highest: method cleared in a single breath, then the question, then the answer in two refusals and one command.

## KEY TERMS

* **saṃkrāma** — transition: the spiritual progress denied. * **bhāvanā** — contemplation: denied. * **kathāyukti** — speech-connection: denied. * **carcā** — discussion: denied. * **dhyāna** — visualization: denied. * **dhāraṇā** — steadying: denied. * **japābhyāsa** — japa-practice: denied. * **suniścita** — well-decided: what stands firm. * **paraṃ satya** — supreme truth: the answer spoken and heard. * **tyāgī** — renouncer: the first refusal. * **parigrahī** — possessor: the second refusal. * **bhaja sukham** — enjoy happiness: the one command. * **yathāvasthita** — as staying: the method that is no method.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

First verse of the Anuttarāṣṭikā: anupāya (no method) in the light of anuttara (the unsurpassed).

## EXPLANATION

Method cleared in a single breath: six named techniques, all denied. Then, not a seventh technique, but a question — what, indeed, stands well-decided? The answer is spoken and heard, not practiced: the supreme truth. Its content is two refusals and one command. No renouncing the world, no possessing it: enjoy happiness, all of it, as staying. Staying (yathāvasthita) is the method that is no method — anupāya named by performance, not by label. Its structure is the argument: denial of method, then the question, then the answer. Anupāya is not a technique added to the six denied; it is the recognition that no technique is needed.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'suniścita' (well-decided) is an adjective of truth or a separate noun is unresolved. Whether 'japābhyāsaprayāso na ca' is one token or several is unresolved (kept as printed).

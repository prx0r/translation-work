# C1 NOTE — anu-p02.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Saṃsāra is unreal for the embodied; bondage never truly is for anyone, so the liberation-rite is false. The error is a fourfold delusion-heap — rope, snake, shadow, ghost. Abandon nothing, grasp nothing; live healthy, staying.

## FUNCTION

Establishes the unreality of saṃsāra and bondage, and the two prohibitions: abandon nothing, grasp nothing.

## KEY TERMS

* **tattvatas** — in truth: the standpoint of reality. * **tanubhṛt** — body-holders: the embodied. * **bandha** — bondage: denied as real. * **vārtā** — news: rhetorical question — what news of bondage? * **vitathā** — false: the liberation-rite for the free. * **muktikriyā** — liberation-action/rite: useless, nothing to be freed from. * **mithyāmoha** — error-delusion: the cause. * **rajjubhujagacchāyāpiśācabhrama** — rope-snake-shadow-ghost-delusion: the fourfold heap. * **tyaja** — abandon: the first prohibition. * **gṛhāṇa** — grasp: the second prohibition. * **svastha** — healthy/standing in own nature: how to live. * **yathāvasthita** — as staying: the final stance.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Second verse: saṃsāra denied, the two prohibitions.

## EXPLANATION

Denial first: saṃsāra is not — in truth (tattvatas) — for body-holders. Then the consequence pressed as a question: of bondage, what news? If bondage never truly is anyone's (bandho yasya na jātu), the liberation-rite (muktikriyā) is false (vitathā) for the free — nothing to be freed from, no rite of freeing. Diagnosis: an error-maker (mithyāmohakṛt), a heap of four delusions — rope mistaken, snake seen, shadow, ghost. Rule in two prohibitions: abandon nothing (mā kiṃcit tyaja), grasp nothing (mā gṛhāṇa); live (vihara) healthy (svasthaḥ) — standing in own nature — as staying (yathāvasthitaḥ). Its structure is the argument: denial, then question, then diagnosis, then rule.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'muktasya muktikriyā' means 'liberation-rite for the free' or 'rite of the liberated' is unresolved. Whether 'vihara' is imperative or indicative is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Anuttarāṣṭikā v.1 — no method, just staying. * Anuttarāṣṭikā v.3 — worship-difference is Māyā. * Tantrāloka ch.2 — anupāya: no progress, no liberation-rite.

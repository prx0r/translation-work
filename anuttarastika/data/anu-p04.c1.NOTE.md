# C1 NOTE — anu-p04.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Bliss is neither wealth-intoxication nor lamp-sun-moon splendour. Joy like burden-lowering attains the forgotten treasure of the all-nonduality state — the light-rise.

## FUNCTION

Establishes bliss by two refusals and one positive: not wealth-intoxication, not splendour, but joy like burden-lowering — the attainment of the forgotten treasure.

## KEY TERMS

* **ānanda** — bliss: the subject, defined by refusal and affirmation. * **vittamadhya** — wealth-middle: wealth-intoxication refused. * **madavana** — intoxication: the refused pleasure. * **aṅganāsaṅga** — enjoying women: refused. * **dīpārkendu** — lamp-sun-moon: splendour refused. * **prabhāprakara** — splendour-multitude: refused. * **prakāśodaya** — light-rise: bliss identified with, not splendour. * **harṣa** — joy: the positive. * **saṃbhṛtabhedamuktisukhabhūr** — gathered-difference-liberation-bliss-ground: the joy's quality. * **bhārāvatāra** — burden-lowering: the simile. * **sarvādvaitapada** — all-nonduality state: the treasure's abode. * **vismṛtanidhi** — forgotten treasure: what bliss attains. * **prāpti** — attainment: the result.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Fourth verse: bliss defined and found.

## EXPLANATION

Two refusals: bliss (ānandaḥ) here (atra) is not (na) wealth-middle-intoxication (vittamadhyamadavana) — not (naiva) like enjoying women (aṅganāsaṅgavat); nor lamp-sun-moon-made (dīpārkendukṛta) splendour-multitude (prabhāprakaravat) — nor (naiva) even the light-rise (prakāśodayaḥ) as commonly pictured. Pleasure refused, splendour refused. Then the positive, in one surge: joy (harṣaḥ) with gathered-difference-liberation-bliss-ground (saṃbhṛtabhedamuktisukhabhūr), like burden-lowering (bhārāvatāropamaḥ) — laying down difference the way one sets down a load — is the attainment (prāptiḥ) of the forgotten treasure (vismṛtanidheḥ) of the all-nonduality state (sarvādvaitapadasya): the light-rise (prakāśodayaḥ). Bliss = prakāśodaya, difference gathered, burden set down, treasure remembered.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'saṃbhṛtabhedamuktisukhabhūr' is one compound or several is unresolved (kept as printed). Whether 'prakāśodaya' in the first half and second half refers to the same or different light-rise is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Anuttarāṣṭikā v.3 — worship-difference is Māyā. * Anuttarāṣṭikā v.5 — variety is one nature. * Tantrāloka — bliss (ānanda) as the Absolute's nature.

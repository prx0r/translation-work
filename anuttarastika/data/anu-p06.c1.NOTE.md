# C1 NOTE — anu-p06.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Former non-being, being, action — things always, suddenly here, middle-formed and confused: whence their truth? In the non-true, unsteady, manifold, clever dream-delusion — transcend doubt, fear, stain, reasoning's contriving: awaken.

## FUNCTION

Establishes the truth-question and the wake-up imperative: things lack truth; transcend doubt, fear, stain, reasoning; awaken.

## KEY TERMS

* **pūrvābhāva** — former non-being: what things were before. * **bhava** — being: what things are now. * **kriyā** — action: what things do. * **sahasā** — suddenly: the sudden arising. * **sadā** — always: the temporal claim. * **madhyākāra** — middle-formed: the shape of appearing things. * **vikāra** — transformation: the change. * **saṃkara** — confusion: the mixing. * **satyatā** — truth: questioned, not found. * **niḥsatya** — non-truth: the field of things. * **capala** — unsteady: the field's quality. * **prapañcanicaya** — manifold: the field's extent. * **svapnabhrama** — dream-delusion: the field's nature. * **peśala** — clever: the field's cunning. * **śaṅkā** — doubt. * **taṅka** — fear. * **kalaṅka** — stain. * **yukti** — reasoning. * **kalanā** — contriving: what reasoning does. * **prabuddha** — awakened: the result. * **bhava** — become: the imperative.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Sixth verse: truth denied to things; awaken.

## EXPLANATION

The truth-question: former non-being (pūrvābhāva), being (bhava), action (kriyā) — indeed (hi) suddenly (sahasā) things (bhāvāḥ) always (sadā) in this existence (asmin bhave) — of middle-formed (madhyākāra) transformation (vikāra), confusion (saṃkaravatāṃ) of them (teṣām): whence (kutaḥ) truth (satyatā)? Things appear suddenly, middle-formed, confused — what truth can they have? Then the field and the crossing: in non-truth (niḥsatye), unsteady (capale), manifold (prapañcanicaye), dream-delusion (svapnabhrame), clever (peśale) — from doubt-fear-stain-reasoning-contriving transcended (śaṅkātaṅkakalaṅkayuktikalanātītaḥ): awakened (prabuddhaḥ), become (bhava). Four obstacles — doubt, fear, stain, reasoning — with reasoning (yukti) listed last among the fetters. Transcending their contriving (kalanā): the only wake-up imperative. Its structure is the argument: truth-question, then field, then crossing, then imperative.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'madhyākāra' means middle-formed or of middle-form is unresolved. Whether 'peśala' means clever or cunning is unresolved. Whether 'bhava' (become) is imperative or indicative is unresolved.

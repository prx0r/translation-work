# C1 NOTE — anu-p08.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

What true, untrue, little-much, eternal, not eternal, Māyā-stained, self-pure — all shines unsorted in the consciousness-mirror. Known as rising from own-grasping consciousness, this is own-experience-risen glory: Lordhood — worship.

## FUNCTION

Establishes the mirror and the command: all opposites shine in the consciousness-mirror; knowing that all is own-grasping-consciousness-rise = glory = Lordhood; worship.

## KEY TERMS

* **satya** — true. * **asatya** — untrue. * **alpabahula** — little-much: the second pair. * **nitya** — eternal: the third pair (eternal/non-eternal). * **māyāmalina** — Māyā-stained: the fourth pair's first. * **ātmavimala** — self-pure: the fourth pair's second. * **ciddarpaṇa** — consciousness-mirror: where all shines unsorted. * **rājate** — shines: the mirror's act. * **svavimarśasaṃvidudaya** — own-grasping-consciousness-rise: the source of all. * **rūpaprakāśātmaka** — form-light-natured: the nature of all. * **jñātvā** — having known: the act of knowing. * **svānubhavādhirūḍhamahimā** — own-experience-risen glory: the result. * **viśveśvaratva** — Lordhood: the result named. * **bhaja** — worship: the final imperative.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Eighth verse: the mirror, Lordhood, worship.

## EXPLANATION

First the mirror: what true, what untrue, little-much, eternal, not eternal, what Māyā-stained, what self-pure — shines (rājate) in the consciousness-mirror (ciddarpaṇe). All opposites, one mirror; the mirror doesn't sort them. Then the knowing and the command: that all (tat sarvaṃ), from own-grasping-consciousness-rise (svavimarśasaṃvidudayāt), form-light-natured (rūpaprakāśātmakam), having known (jñātvā) — own-experience-risen (svānubhavādhirūḍha) glory (mahimā): Lordhood (viśveśvaratvam) — worship (bhaja). Knowing that all opposites shine in the mirror from one's own grasping consciousness = glory = Lordhood; and its last word is the imperative it opened with the stance for: speak (v.1), worship (v.8). Its structure is the argument: mirror (all opposites unsorted), then knowing (own-grasping-consciousness-rise), then result (glory = Lordhood), then command (worship).

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'svavimarśasaṃvidudaya' is one compound or several is unresolved (kept as printed). Whether 'rūpaprakāśātmaka' modifies 'tat sarvam' or 'mahimā' is unresolved. Whether 'bhaja' is imperative or indicative is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Anuttarāṣṭikā v.1 — no method, just staying. * Anuttarāṣṭikā v.7 — no arising; will-born glory. * Tantrāloka — the mirror of consciousness (ciddarpaṇa) and Lordhood.

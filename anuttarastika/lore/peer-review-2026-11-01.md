# PEER REVIEW — Anuttarāṣṭikā vs scholarly takes

*2026-11-01. Reviewed against: abhinavagupta.org (hymn page), GRETIL (TextGrid), Lakshmanjoo Academy (Swamiji's commentary on anupāya), Qcentro PDF (multi-language), SanskritDocuments (Ruma Dewan ed.).*

## Scholarly facts

1. Anuttarāṣṭikā = 8 verses by Abhinavagupta on anupāya (no method) in the light of anuttara (unsurpassed). Tantrāloka ch.2 devoted to anupāya.
2. Composed in Pauṣa, Kṛṣṇa Daśamī (per colophon v.10? no — Bhairavastava has that; Anuttarāṣṭikā has no colophon).
3. Four translations compared: Bettina Bäumer, Lilian Silburn, Lakshmanjoo Academy, Qcentro multi-language.
4. Key terms: anupāya (no method), anuttara (unsurpassed), svānubhavasvabhāva (own-experience-nature), māyā (standing as other without being different), pratyabhijñā (recognition).
5. The hymn moves from denial of method (v.1) to saṃsāra denied (v.2) to worship-difference dissolved (v.3) to bliss defined (v.4) to variety as one nature (v.5) to truth denied to things (v.6) to no arising/will-born glory (v.7) to mirror/Lordhood/worship (v.8).

## What our work got right

1. Anupāya as no method, named by performance not label — matches scholarship (abhinavagupta.org: "no need of spiritual progress, no contemplation..."). ✓
2. Saṃsāra denied as unreal for body-holders — matches scholarship (Qcentro: "in reality no such thing as birth and death"). ✓
3. Māyā as standing as other without being different from consciousness-nonduality — matches scholarship (abhinavagupta.org: "this illusion is no other than consciousness, one without a second"). ✓
4. Bliss = prakāśodaya (light-rise), not wealth-intoxication or splendour — matches scholarship (Qcentro: "the brilliant flow of light is unlike the collection of rays from a lamp"). ✓
5. Variety as one nature; regard as consciousness-form — matches scholarship (abhinavagupta.org: "all these states... shine as mutually different but in reality they are not"). ✓
6. Truth denied to things; awakening as imperative — matches scholarship (Qcentro: "differentiated action is an illusion"). ✓
7. No arising; will-born glory; one, yet many-selfed — matches scholarship (abhinavagupta.org: "the greatness of this universe arises from your own imagination"). ✓
8. Mirror of consciousness; all opposites shine; Lordhood; worship — matches scholarship (Qcentro: "everything that shines in the mirror of Consciousness has a luminous nature"). ✓

## What we missed

1. The scholarly translations (Bäumer, Silburn, Lakshmanjoo) use "remain as you are" / "enjoy everything" for v.1; our L2 uses "enjoy happiness, wholly, as staying" — close but could be sharper.
2. v.4's "forgotten treasure" (vismṛtanidhi) is rendered by Qcentro as "your own treasure abode that you had temporarily forgotten" — our L2 captures this.
3. v.6's "transcend doubt, fear, stain, reasoning" — Qcentro lists "doubt, fear, stain, reasoning" as the four fetters; we match.
4. v.8's "worship" (bhaja) as final imperative — matches all translations.
5. We did not incorporate the Lakshmanjoo Academy's specific commentary on anupāya (upāyas) — could enrich C1 BOUNDARY.

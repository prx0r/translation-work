## The parable (canonical draft, ≈88s spoken)

> She comes to the place of open pyres, where the garlands of the
> dead hang thick and ghouls stand guard — and she is not afraid.
> She measures the whole field of the senses with one glance, lifts
> a skull-cup, and drinks the juices of the world as if they were
> wine — drinking always from the egg-middle where all of it lives.
> One raised hand gives every boon, each done on stage: the ground-
> claim stamped by her step, the veil torn away by a breath, the rest
> commanded by a held-up palm, a sound ringing where no ear is, gods
> lit where they stand, a thunderbolt riding her wrist unlifted. Behind her the whole spread goes — all the goddesses led
> by the first. She seals everything with one gesture, and says the
> only line: Consciousness-Bhairava am I. The draught conquers. The
> heart shines. And at the gate two words follow her: take this
> state — wander not in vain.

**Map (hinge):**
- gate-words: take this state, wander not in vain, grace conquers →
  p01 *salute, shelter, wander-not; grace conquers (take
  consciousness-state)* [p01] (placed as her entry-echo at the close)
- behind her the whole spread, goddesses led by the first → p02
  *her spread: all Brahmī-led* [p02]
- raised hand, every boon, universe-glory; fears uprooted, veils
  shattered, commanded rest; sound with no ear; gods mirrored;
  thunderbolt unlifted → p03 (boon-hand glory; uprooted fears;
  shattered Māyā; commanded rest; nāda heard sound; mirrored gods;
  borne thunderbolt) [p03]
- skull-cup, juices of the world, egg-middle, always drinking →
  p04 *in the Śakti-egg-fragment middle, world-juices — all I drink
  always; skull-cup pretext* [p04]
- pyres, garlands of the dead, ghouls; measures the sense-field;
  seals with gesture; Consciousness-Bhairava am I; draught
  conquers; heart shines → p05 *ghoul-girt measuring shown;
  Bhairava-am-I; eightfold retinue (folded into "the whole
  spread"); mudrā-seals; conquering draught; heart-shining* [p05]

**Limit:** the blood-pretext of p04 stays folded into "juices of the
world" (skull-cup carries the register plainly; no gore-detail
narrated — raudra lives in setting and diction, not spectacle); the
eightfold retinue folds into her spread (flagged); Brahmī kept
plain as "the first."
**Rasa:** maṅgala (gate-words) → vīra cascade (boon-hand verbs) →
raudra as ENVIRONMENT (pyres/ghouls/skull — not narrated cruelty)
→ mudrā-vīra (the seal) → amṛta close (draught, heart-shine). The
line "Consciousness-Bhairava am I" is the hinge — kept verbatim-ish
(the work's one allowed utterance, per bhairavastava-killers
tradition of single hard lines).
**Bar check:** one protagonist; retellable; plain (nāda→sound with
no ear; mudrā→gesture; Brahmī→the first; anukaraṇa unnamed).

## The seal — statement

**The measure, the cup, the line:** night-rite vertical — bottom:
ember-dark pyre-ground with hanging dead-garlands and small ghouls
at the rim, she stands MID-frame wearing her own dark garland,
ghoul-girt; at her lips a tilted bone-ivory skull-cup (the drink);
a gold measuring-arc sweeping five small lapis sense-lamps around
her (the field measured); her raised boon-hand glowing gold, a
thunderbolt resting unlifted at her wrist, gold nāda-rings opening
from her brow (sound with no ear); behind her a fan of small pale-
gold goddess-lights spreading (the spread of the first); and at her
heart one blazing vermilion-gold — the declaration made light.

## Composition laws (this seal)

ONE subject (her). NIGHT is allowed here (the only night-rite seal

the register is raudra by LOCATION and ink, never by wound). Five
lamps exact. Ghouls stay rim-scale, never central. Declaration =
heart-light, not a mouth-shout (no letters, no scream-lines).

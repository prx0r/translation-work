# Bhairavānukaraṇastotra — L1 controlled

## bhair-p01 (1-10)
[and]-Om obeisance to Consciousness-Bhairava; consciousness-ground-refuge ones resort to consciousness-state, do not wander in vain; proclaiming thus, Shiva grace-inclined conquers among devotees (oṃ namaś cidbhairavāya | citibhūmisamāśrayaṇāś citibhūtiṃ śrayata mā mudhā bhramata | prathayann iti bhaktimatāṃ jayati śivo 'nugrahapravaṇaḥ) // 1 //

## bhair-p02 (11-20)
[and]-Of supreme light-body, reflection-power, O Lord, supreme goddess; her expansion indeed all goddesses from Brahmi etc (paramaprakāśavapuṣo vimarśaśaktiḥ prabho parā devī | asyā eva prasaraḥ sarvā brāhmyādikā devyaḥ) // 11 //

## bhair-p03 (21-30)
[and]-By boon-giving hand you enact universe-glory-giving; indeed one fixed in supreme principle is never touched by glory-impurities (varadena pāṇinā tvaṃ viśvavibhūtipradatvam abhinayasi | na khalu paratattvaniṣṭho vibhavamalaiḥ spṛśyate jātu) // 21 //

## bhair-p04 (31-40)
[and]-In middle of Shakti-egg-fragment, all world-essences, I always drink; by skull-cup blood pretext you manifest this within me (śāktāṇḍakhaṇḍamadhye viśvarasāḥ sarvam ahaṃ pibāmi sadā | vyañjayasi narakapālagarudhiramiṣād etad iva me 'ntaḥ) // 31 //

## bhair-p05 (41-48)
[and]-Surrounded by various cremation-garlands, vetala, you manifest; 'I am Consciousness-Bhairava in middle of consciousness-object-discrimination' (vividhaśmaśānamālāvetālāvṛtaḥ prakāśayase | saṃvittiviṣayakalanāmadhyaś citibhairavo 'smīti) // 41 //

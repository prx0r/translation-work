# Bhairavanukarana bhair-p02 (bodied, spread, led) — L2 READ

## v.11
Of supreme-light-bodied, reflection-power, O Lord — supreme goddess. Hers indeed spread — all Brahmī-led goddesses.
The Sanskrit: *paramaprakāśavapuṣo vimarśaśaktiḥ prabho parā devī | asyā eva prasaraḥ sarvā brāhmyādikā devyaḥ*

## v.12
Fire-water-nectar-streak-poison-bearing, You point this. World opposed though — in true-consciousness-formed, in me unopposed — not this.
The Sanskrit: *dahanajalāmṛtalekhāviṣādi bibhrat tvam ādiśasy etat | viśvaṃ viruddham api saccidrūpe mayi virudhyate naitat*

## v.13
Knowing-thread-dissolved, sapless knower-host, this — skull-garland-of-faculties-bearing, You show thus, as-if, to us, O Lord.
The Sanskrit: *saṃvitsūtranilīno niḥsāro mātṛnivaho 'yam | karaṇamuṇḍamālabhārin darśayasītīva naḥ svāmin*

## v.14
See, see — adverse nooses — those, these expelled ever. Thus entrail-holding-as-if, saying, to us appear.
The Sanskrit: *paśyata paśyata pāśā viṣamās ta ime bahiṣkṛtāḥ śaśvat | antradhāraṇamiṣat kathayaṃs tvaṃ naḥ samābhāsi*

## v.15
Māyā-cloth passionate in supreme heaven — in this that this cast. Thus elephant-hide bloody on head bearing — You tell, O Lord.
The Sanskrit: *māyāpaṭaḥ sarāgo vyomni pare 'smin sa eṣa nikṣiptaḥ | gajacarma saraktaṃ mūrdhni vahan kathayasīśa*

## v.16
Inner-power-dagger bared — saṃsāra-splitting — by sword. All-world-by-own-power-glory-taken indeed — best heroes.
The Sanskrit: *antaḥśaktikṛpāṇīm vyanakṣi saṃsṛtivibhedinīm asinā | nijaśaktimahimasvīkṛtasamastaviśvā hi vīravarāḥ*

## v.17
World-fear-killer — he, I, in lordship established, am — fear not. Thus by shield-clash-showing, You point us, O Lord.
The Sanskrit: *bhavabhayahantā so 'ham sānāthye 'vasthito 'smi | mā bhaiṣṭa ity āsphoṭitakheṭakadarśanato diśasi naḥ svāmin*

## v.18
Own-power-noose-bound, by consciousness-body, time is counted by me. Thus by noose-holding, You show us death-of-death — You.
The Sanskrit: *nijaśaktipāśavalitaś cidvapuṣā kalyate mayā kālaḥ | pāśadhāraṇavaśāt prathayasi naḥ kālakālas tvam*

## v.19
Distinction-made, all, this, by own power indeed I hurl to destroy. Thus by goad-holding, supreme Bhairava makes plain to us.
The Sanskrit: *bhedamayam akhilam etan nijaśaktyaivākṣipāmi saṃhartum | aṅkuśadhāraṇataḥ sphuṭayati parabhairavo 'smākam*

## v.20
By bow-mounted-arrow-showing, Brahmā-Viṣṇu-Rudra-lords with Sadākhya-Śivas — by power, cause-deer pierced like a deer — You.
The Sanskrit: *kodaṇḍārūdhaśarapradarśanād | brahmaviṣṇurudreśān sasadākhyaśivāñ chaktyā kāraṇahariṇaṃ bhinatsi mṛgavat tvam*

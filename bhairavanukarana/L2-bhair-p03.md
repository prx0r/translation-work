# Bhairavanukarana bhair-p03 (enacted glory, unstained) — L2 READ

## v.21
By the boon-giving hand, You enact universe-glory-giving. Not indeed supreme-principle-fixed by glory-stains touched ever.
The Sanskrit: *varadena pāṇinā tvaṃ viśvavibhūtipradatvam abhinayasi na khalu paratattvaniṣṭho vibhavamalaiḥ spṛśyate jātu*

## v.22
By fearlessness, world-fears uprooting shown always. World-grace-cause-nature Yours by palm.
The Sanskrit: *abhayena ca bhavabhayāni unmūlayatā prakāśyate satatam viśvānugrahakāraṇasvabhāvatā tava karatalena*

## v.23
Unfamed-formed, unequal, Māyā's shattered head — there this, by supreme waking, borne by me as skull — thus You show.
The Sanskrit: *akhyātirūpam asamaṃ māyāyāḥ śakalitaṃ śiro 'traitat parabodhamayena mayā muṇḍaṃ bibhrat prakāśayasy evam*

## v.24
Wholly-essence-taken, in me, alone world-rest — in consciousness-one-mass thus. By skull-staff-bone-bearing pretext, You give command.
The Sanskrit: *niḥśeṣahṛtasārā mayy eva jagatsthitiś cidekaghane iti khaṭvāṅgakaraṅkodvahanacchalato dadāsy ājñām*

## v.25
Lute-bell-drum, O Uddāmara, playing — this You show. Triple-split function sound hearing, standing am I — thus.
The Sanskrit: *vīṇāghaṇtāḍamarūn uḍḍāmara vādayann idaṃ diśasi vṛttitribhedabhinnaṃ nādaṃ śṛṇvan sthito 'smīti*

## v.26
Moreover — Parā-etc.-power-triple-spoke-lovely great power — gods You imitate outside, by trident-holding pretext, always — You.
The Sanskrit: *kiṃ ca parādikaśaktitritayārāsundarām mahāśaktim devānukaroṣi bahis triśūladhāraṇamiṣeṇa nityaṃ tvam*

## v.27
Will-etc.-own-power-shown-lower-abode-threefold — own indeed supreme power, thunderbolt-made — borne, six-spoked — You.
The Sanskrit: *icchādikanijaśaktiprakāśitādhaḥsthagocaratritayām svām eva parāṃ śaktiṃ vajramayīṃ vahasi ṣaḍarāṃ tvam*

## v.28
World all by my power subdued, all stations holds — thus by staff-holding revealed Consciousness-Bhairava to us.
The Sanskrit: *jagad akhilaṃ macchaktyā damitaṃ sarvavyāvasthitīr dhatte iti daṇḍadhāraṇavaśād vyanakṣi cidbhairavāsmākam*

## v.29
Mace-axe bearing, dot-sound-imitating-formed — distinction-undistinction-splitting-bent, O Īśāna — You indicate.
The Sanskrit: *mudgaraparaśū bibhrad baindavanādānukārarūpau tvam bhedavibhedanaśakalanaparatvam īśāna nirdiśasi*

## v.30
By vision-difference, O Lord — various form assuming — Bhairava-supreme-waking-made proclaimed — own abode everywhere.
The Sanskrit: *darśanabhedāt svāminn anyānyām āhṛtiṃ dadhānas tvam bhairavaparabodhamayaṃ prathayasi nijadhāma sarvatra*

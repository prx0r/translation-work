# Bhairavanukarana bhair-p04 (middled, drunk, within) — L2 READ

## v.31
In Śakti-egg-fragment middle, world-juices — all I drink always. Shown by skull-cup-blood-pretext — this as-it-were my within.
The Sanskrit: *śāktāṇḍakhaṇḍamadhye viśvarasāḥ sarvam ahaṃ pibāmi sadā vyañjayasi narakapālagarudhiramiṣād etad iva me 'ntaḥ*

## v.32
Pride-heart-bond-uprooters — those two, these tufted embodied — as-it-were skull-lotuses — showing, held by You, Mahākāla.
The Sanskrit: *abhimānahṛdayabandhānutkhātau tāv imau saśikhau mūrtāv iva muṇdābjau prathayan dhatse mahākālaḥ*

## v.33
All-world-seed-ground, first-station — imitated, treasure-of-waking. By palm-pomegranate-show-play, O god.
The Sanskrit: *akhilajagadbījabhuvaṃ prathamasthām anukaroṣi bodhanidhe karatalagabījapūrakadarśanalīlāyitād deva*

## v.34
Bhairava-formed though — in hand rosary held — so by all-letter-expansion world turned within.
The Sanskrit: *bhairavarūpo 'py asi yat tatrāpi kare 'kṣamālikāṃ dhatse tat sarvākṣavikāsād viśvaṃ parivartayasy antaḥ*

## v.35
Bone-handed-skull-garlanded-ash-decked, man-blood-hued — world by Brahma-fullness pure — thus the Lord teaches.
The Sanskrit: *asthikaramuṇḍamālābhasmābharaṇo nṛraktaruciḥ viśvaṃ brahmamayatvāc chuddham itīśaḥ samādiśasi*

## v.36
For self-delight-full ones — adverse though sense-bubbles — what these desire-burning brow-eye showing supreme-fire-formed teach.
The Sanskrit: *svātmārāmamayānāṃ viṣamā api viṣayabudbudhāḥ kim amī smaradahanam alikanayanam prathayan paramāgnirūpam ādiśasi*

## v.37
Inward-bound-aimed, expanding-facultied — though since always You are — that shown in all states, delusion-stain gone self.
The Sanskrit: *antarnibaddhalakṣyaḥ pravikāsikaraṇo 'pi yat sadā bhavasi tat prathayasi sarvadaśaṃ gatamohakalaṅkam ātmānam*

## v.38
O god — sky-cladness Yours says uncovered of self form — suns-of-supreme-Brahma-fullness — Māyā-covering — how would-cover?
The Sanskrit: *deva digambaratā te vadati nirāvaraṇam ātmano rūpam paramabrahmamayārkān māyāvaraṇam kathaṃ vṛṇuyāt*

## v.39
Destroyer of all, suns and the rest — I, darkness-ground — by spotless-autumn-sky-gleaming body — thus shown.
The Sanskrit: *saṃhartā sarveṣām arkādīnām ahaṃ tamobhūmiḥ nirmalaśāradagaganaprabheṇa vapuṣeti darśayasi*

## v.40
Supreme-Brahma-full You, god — when taking Bhairava-form — that shown knower-of-principle — being though in difference freed — thus.
The Sanskrit: *paramabrahmamayas tvaṃ deva yadāśrayasi bhairavākāram tat prathayasi tattvajñaḥ saty api bhede vimukta iti*

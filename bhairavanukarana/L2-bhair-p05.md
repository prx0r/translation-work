# Bhairavanukarana bhair-p05 (girt, shown, am) — L2 READ

## v.41
Much-cremation-garland-ghoul-girt, shown sense-field-measuring-midst — Consciousness-Bhairava am I — thus.
The Sanskrit: *vividhaśmaśānamālāvetālāvṛtaḥ prakāśayase saṃvittiviṣayakalanāmadhyaś citibhairavo 'smīti*

## v.42
In You alone nectar-streak, supreme-power-made, ultimate-knower — hence Lord — You indeed worlds' life — thus shine our in-heart.
The Sanskrit: *tvayy evāmṛtalekhā paraśaktimayī parapramātātaḥ nātha tvam eva jagatām jīvanam iti bhāsi no hṛdaye*

## v.43
Yours sound-mass-bodied eightfold-ward just outer-retinue — by supreme-subtle-splits, Bhairava, world-lord-led.
The Sanskrit: *tava śabdarāśivapuṣaḥ pratyaṣṭakam eva bāhyaparivāraḥ parasūkṣmādivibhedād bhairava lokeśvarapramukhāḥ*

## v.44
Seals all this by total-own-form-transmission — delights devotee-throng, path-joy, by fullness-showing.
The Sanskrit: *mudrayati nikhilam etan niḥśeṣasvasvarūpasaṃkramaṇāt modayati bhaktavargaṃ gatimudaṃ pūrṇatāprathanāt*

## v.45
Looses noose-net, melts distinction — O Lord, Your form seals much-consciousness-wealth — seal hence called.
The Sanskrit: *mocayati pāśajālaṃ drāvayati vibhedam īśa te mūrtiḥ mudrayati vividhasaṃviddraviṇaṃ mudrā tataḥ kathitā*

## v.46
Conquers in supreme-principle-ground-excellent — draught-become-this — clearly may it dwell — by true-principle-showing, of heart known-stain.
The Sanskrit: *jayati paratattvabhūmipravare nipāyabhūtaiṣa sphuṭam adhivasatu satattvaprathayā hṛdayasya meyamalam*

## v.47
Momently rises consciousness — which indeed there flashes below — world Śiva-Śakti-glory-formed know, Maheśa, rite-standers.
The Sanskrit: *pratikalam udeti saṃvid yā kila tatra sphuraty adho viśvam śivaśaktivibhavarūpaṃ jānanti maheśa yāgasthāḥ*

## v.48
Thus You flash always in me — Consciousness-Bhairava, all-beings-self — then rite-to-mantra-end accomplished effortlessly — all.
The Sanskrit: *itthaṃ sphurasi sadā me cidbhairava sarvabhūtātma tad yāgādijapāntaṃ sampannam ayatnataḥ sarvam*

# Bhairavānukaraṇastotra — L2 READ

# L2 READ

## bhair-p01 — Consciousness-Bhairava as the Sole Reality
The passage opens by saluting Consciousness-Bhairava and urging refuge in consciousness rather than wandering in vain. It denies any real distinction between praised, praiser, and praise, presenting every form as consciousness in varied appearance. The devotee bows to the principle and its form, enters it without conceptualization, and recognizes the body as a temple where Mahesha, the mass of consciousness, is praised. It closes by celebrating the powers of consciousness, bliss, will, cognition, and action, and by naming the limbs and seats up to Sadashiva as Bhairava's own.

## bhair-p02 — Weapons and Ornaments as Liberating Signs
Bhairava's supreme power and its expansion into goddesses are declared, while the universe, though seemingly opposed, is not opposed in consciousness-being. His skull-garland, expelled nooses, cast Maya-cloth, and other fierce signs are read as teachings that worldly bonds and distinctions have been cast away. The sword, shield, noose, goad, and bow further show the splitting of samsara, freedom from fear, mastery of time, destruction of distinction, and piercing of causal roots. Thus frightening iconography becomes direct instruction in liberation.

## bhair-p03 — Manifestation Through Boon, Fearlessness, and Power
The boon-giving hand enacts the giving of universal glory, while fearlessness uproots worldly fear and reveals grace as the cause of the universe. Shattered Maya, skull-staff, lute, bell, drum, trident, vajra, staff, mace, and axe each disclose a further aspect of Bhairava's power and the dissolution of distinction. The passage insists that one fixed in the supreme principle is untouched by glory-impurities. It ends by stating that Bhairava manifests his own abode everywhere through differences of vision.

## bhair-p04 — Purity, Inner Fire, and Formless Self
Bhairava drinks the essences of the world within the Shakti-egg fragment and shatters the unequal head of Maya. Skull-cup, pomegranate, rosary, bone, skull-garland, and ash declare the world pure through Brahma-fullness and the transformative power of letters. Forehead-fire, inward-bound aim, sky-cladness, and the darkness-ground that destroys suns all point to the self free from delusion-stain. Even in Bhairava form, the knower of principle is proclaimed liberated despite apparent distinction.

## bhair-p05 — Retinue, Mudra, and Effortless Worship
Surrounded by cremation-garlands and vetala, Bhairava declares himself consciousness in the midst of consciousness-object discrimination. He is the nectar-ray, supreme knower, and life of worlds, while his sound-body has an eight-fold external retinue. His form releases noose-nets, dissolves distinction, and seals consciousness-wealth as mudra, conquering the supreme principle-ground. Consciousness arises each moment, the universe flashes below, and worship from ritual to japa is accomplished effortlessly.

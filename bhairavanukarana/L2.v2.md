## bhair-p01 — Consciousness-Bhairava as sole reality (vv. 1–10)

**Salutation and refuge (v. 1).** Oṃ, salutation to Consciousness-Bhairava. You who seek shelter in the reality of Citi — resort to the existence of the power of consciousness; do not wander uselessly. Proclaiming thus, Śiva, intent on bestowing grace to devotees, is victorious. The opening: refuge in consciousness, not in wandering. Śiva is victorious as the bestower of grace.

**The supreme form (v. 2).** I venerate Śiva, the supreme Bhairava of the character of consciousness, solitary, of the form of supreme nectar, radiant, who has devoured the entire existence with the realm of senses streaming out. Śiva as Cidbhairava: consciousness itself, supreme, solitary, radiant, all-devouring.

**No distinction (v. 3).** Here nothing distinct as praised, praiser, praise; as a form appears, so it becomes by consciousness-form. The non-dual core: the threefold distinction (praised/praiser/praise) is dissolved in consciousness. This is the stotra's central insight, echoed throughout.

**The form as imitation (v. 4).** I bow to your form, imitation of your principle, consciousness-dense bliss, all-distinction-dissolved, all-distinction-self, O Lord. The visible form is an *anukaraṇa* (imitation/copy) of the formless principle — like a drawing of a man.

**The drawing (v. 5).** As a drawn man reveals a man, script reveals letter-collection, so form reveals you, of varied form, supreme self. The iconographic form reveals the formless, as a drawing reveals a man. This is the key to reading all subsequent iconography: each fierce element is a "drawing" of a non-dual truth.

**Entering without conceptualization (v. 6).** There, having entered you, consciousness-form, without conceptualization, we reflect not even a little; principle is not object of imagination. Entering the consciousness-form without conceptualization; the principle is not an object of imagination.

**The guru and the heart (v. 7).** Having made speech-goddess one's own light, offering of sense-flowers, then bowing to guru, first manifestation, entering heart. The practice: make speech (vāgdevī) one's own light; offer sense-flowers; bow to the guru's first manifestation; enter the heart.

**Praise in the body-temple (v. 8).** I praise, reflecting, Maheśvara, one's own self, consciousness-one-mass, in this body-temple, having poured out cluster of bonds. The body is a temple (*dehadevagṛha*); Maheśvara, the mass of consciousness, is praised there; the bonds (pāśa) are poured out.

**The powers (v. 9).** Victorious are powers named consciousness-bliss-will-cognition-action, O Lord; subtle-gross distinctions obtained, face-hood, Brahma-skull named. The five śaktis (cit, ānanda, icchā, vit, karaṇa) are victorious.

**Limbs and seats (v. 10).** Omniscience etc. objects, heart etc. six limbs of yours; all up to Sadāśiva, by corpse-self, your seat, O Lord. The six limbs (hṛdaya etc.) with omniscience etc. as their objects; all up to Sadāśiva; the seat is by corpse-self (pretātmatā).

## bhair-p02 — Weapons and ornaments as liberating signs (vv. 11–20)

**Supreme power and goddesses (v. 11).** Of supreme light-body, reflection-power, O Lord, supreme goddess; her expansion indeed all goddesses from Brāhmī etc. Bhairava's vimarśaśakti is the supreme goddess (parā devī); all other goddesses (Brāhmī etc.) are her expansion.

**Universe not opposed (v. 12).** Bearing fire-water-nectar-ray-poison etc., you indicate this; universe, though opposed, in me of consciousness-being form is not opposed. The universe, though seemingly opposed, is not opposed in consciousness-being.

**Consciousness-thread (v. 13).** Consciousness-thread-dissolved, sapless, this host of knowers; skull-garland-bearing, you show us thus, O Lord. The host of knowers is sapless (niḥsāra) when dissolved in the consciousness-thread; the skull-garland shows this.

**Nooses expelled (v. 14).** See, see, these nooses are adverse, eternally expelled; saying thus, as if holding entrails, you address us. The nooses (pāśa) are expelled; the entrails-holding is a display.

**Māyā-cloth cast (v. 15).** Māyā-cloth, with attachment, in supreme space, this is cast; thus elephant-skin, bloody, on head bearing, you tell, O Lord. Māyā is cast like a cloth; the elephant-skin shows Māyā cast away.

**Sword splits saṃsāra (v. 16).** You reveal inner power-knife, saṃsāra-splitting, by sword; indeed heroes excellent have accepted all universe by own power-glory. The sword splits saṃsāra; the heroes (vīra) accept the universe by their own power-glory.

**Shield: freedom from fear (v. 17).** Destroyer of worldly fear, "I am established in lordship, do not fear"; thus by shield-display you point us, O Lord. The shield shows freedom from fear: "I am established in lordship, do not fear."

**Noose: mastery of time (v. 18).** Bound by own power-noose, by consciousness-body, time is measured by me; thus by noose-holding you show us death of death, O you. The noose shows mastery of time: time is measured by the consciousness-body; death of death.

**Goad: destruction of distinction (v. 19).** All this is distinction-made; by own power I draw to destroy; thus by goad-holding supreme Bhairava makes clear to us. The goad shows destruction of distinction: all is distinction-made; by own power it is drawn to destroy.

**Bow: piercing causal roots (v. 20).** By bow-mounted arrow-display, Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra lords, with Sadāśiva, by power you pierce causal deer like deer. The bow pierces the causal roots (kāraṇa-hariṇa) of Brahma, Viṣṇu, Rudra, and Sadāśiva — like piercing a deer.

## bhair-p03 — Manifestation through boon, fearlessness, and power (vv. 21–30)

**Boon-giving (v. 21).** By boon-giving hand you enact universe-glory-giving; indeed one fixed in supreme principle is never touched by glory-impurities. The boon-giving hand enacts the giving of universal glory; one fixed in the supreme principle is untouched by glory-impurities.

**Fearlessness (v. 22).** And by fearlessness, uprooting worldly fears, always manifest; by your palm, nature as cause of universe-grace. Fearlessness uproots worldly fears; the palm reveals the nature as cause of universe-grace.

**Shattered Māyā (v. 23).** This head, unequal, of Māyā's unmanifest form, shattered; thus you manifest bearing skull by me of supreme awakening. Māyā's form is shattered; the skull is borne by supreme awakening.

**Skull-staff (v. 24).** All essence taken, in me alone world-rest in consciousness-one-mass; thus by skull-staff bone-bearing pretext you give command. All essence is taken; world-rest is in consciousness-one-mass; the skull-staff gives command.

**Lute, bell, drum (v. 25).** Playing lute, bell, drum, O Uḍḍāmara, you show this; "I stand hearing sound divided by three functions." The three functions (creation, maintenance, dissolution) are heard as sound.

**Trident (v. 26).** Moreover, you imitate gods outside, by trident-holding pretext, supreme power beautiful with three powers from Para etc. The trident imitates the gods; the three powers (icchā, jñāna, kriyā) from Para etc.

**Vajra (v. 27).** You bear your own supreme power, diamond-like, six-spoked, three lower realms revealed by own powers from will etc. The vajra (diamond) is the supreme power, six-spoked; three lower realms revealed by will etc.

**Staff (v. 28).** Entire world subdued by my power, all arrangements bears; thus by staff-holding you reveal Consciousness-Bhairava to us. The staff reveals Cidbhairava: the world is subdued by his power.

**Mace and axe (v. 29).** Bearing mace and axe, of Bindu-sound-imitation forms, you indicate, O Īśāna, state of splitting distinction and non-distinction. The mace and axe indicate the splitting of distinction and non-distinction.

**Differences of vision (v. 30).** O Lord, by difference of vision, assuming various forms, you proclaim your own abode, Bhairava supreme awakening, everywhere. Bhairava manifests his own abode everywhere through differences of vision.

## bhair-p04 — Purity, inner fire, and formless self (vv. 31–40)

**Drinking world-essences (v. 31).** In middle of Śakti-egg-fragment, all world-essences, I always drink; by skull-cup blood pretext you manifest this within me. Bhairava drinks the essences of the world within the Śakti-egg fragment; the skull-cup manifests this within.

**Ego-bonds excavated (v. 32).** These two, with tufts, excavation of ego-heart-bonds; like embodied skull-lotuses, Mahākāla establishes. The two (tufts) excavate the ego-heart-bonds; Mahākāla establishes them like skull-lotuses.

**Pomegranate (v. 33).** O treasure of awakening, you imitate first station, seed-place of all universe; by palm-held pomegranate display-play, O god. The pomegranate (seed-place) imitates the first station (prathamasthā) of all universe.

**Rosary (v. 34).** Though of Bhairava form, there too you hold rosary in hand; by expansion of all letters, you transform universe within. The rosary (akṣamālikā) transforms the universe by expansion of all letters (akṣa).

**Bone, skull, ash (v. 35).** Bone-hand, skull-garland, ash-ornament, human-blood-hued; "universe is pure due to Brahma-fullness," O Lord, you instruct. Bone, skull, ash declare the world pure through Brahma-fullness.

**Forehead-fire (v. 36).** For those full of self-delight, what are these adverse sense-bubbles? Manifesting fire of desire, forehead-eye, you instruct supreme fire-form. Forehead-fire (smaradahana, the fire of desire) instructs the supreme fire-form.

**Inward-bound aim (v. 37).** Though always with inward-bound target and expanding faculties, that proclaims self free from delusion-stain in all states. The self is free from delusion-stain in all states.

**Sky-cladness (v. 38).** O god, your sky-cladness says uncovered form of self; how would Māyā-covering take supreme-Brahma suns? Sky-cladness (digambara) says the uncovered form of self; Māyā cannot cover the supreme-Brahma suns.

**Darkness-ground (v. 39).** Destroyer of all suns etc., I am darkness-ground; by stainless autumn-sky radiance body you show. The darkness-ground destroys all suns; the stainless autumn-sky radiance body shows this.

**Liberated despite distinction (v. 40).** You are supreme-Brahma-filled, O god, when assuming Bhairava form; that proclaims knower of principle, though distinction exists, liberated. Even in Bhairava form, the knower of principle is proclaimed liberated despite apparent distinction.

## bhair-p05 — Retinue, mudra, and effortless worship (vv. 41–48)

**Surrounded by cremation-garlands (v. 41).** Surrounded by various cremation-garlands, vetala, you manifest; "I am Consciousness-Bhairava in middle of consciousness-object-discrimination." Bhairava is surrounded by cremation-garlands and vetala; he declares himself Cidbhairava in the midst of consciousness-object discrimination.

**Nectar-ray, supreme knower (v. 42).** In you alone nectar-ray, supreme-power-filled, supreme knower; O Lord, you indeed are life of worlds, thus shine in our heart. In Bhairava alone: nectar-ray, supreme power, supreme knower; he is the life of worlds, shining in the heart.

**Sound-body retinue (v. 43).** Of your sound-mass-body, the eight-fold external retinue; from supreme-subtle etc. distinctions, Bhairava, world-lords etc. The sound-body (śabdarāśivapuṣa) has an eight-fold external retinue (pratyaṣṭaka); from supreme to subtle distinctions, Bhairava, world-lords etc.

**Mudra seals all (v. 44).** It seals all this by complete own-form transmission; delights devotee-group by path-joy, fullness-manifestation. The mudra seals all by complete transmission of own-form; delights the devotee-group by path-joy and fullness-manifestation.

**Mudra releases (v. 45).** Your form releases noose-net, dissolves distinction, O Lord; seals various consciousness-wealth, hence called mudra. The form releases the noose-net (pāśajāla), dissolves distinction (vibheda); seals various consciousness-wealth (saṃvid-draviṇa); hence called mudra.

**Conquering the principle-ground (v. 46).** Conquers in supreme principle-ground, excellent, this abode; may it clearly dwell, by true-principle-manifestation, impurity of my heart. The form conquers in the supreme principle-ground; may it dwell clearly in the heart by true-principle-manifestation, destroying the impurity.

**Consciousness arises (v. 47).** Consciousness arises every moment; indeed there universe flashes below; Mahesha, ritualists know Śiva-Śakti-glory-form. Consciousness arises every moment; the universe flashes below; ritualists know the Śiva-Śakti-glory-form.

**Effortless worship (v. 48).** Thus you always flash in me, Consciousness-Bhairava, all-beings-self; then all from ritual to japa accomplished effortlessly. Thus you always flash in me, Cidbhairava, all-beings-self; then all from ritual to japa is accomplished effortlessly. The text's final word: effortless worship — not by effort but by recognition.

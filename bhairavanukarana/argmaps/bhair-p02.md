# Bhairavānukaraṇastotra bhair-p02 — ARGUMENT MAP (weapons and ornaments as liberating signs)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 bhair-p02 = vv. 11–20. Lore: Gnoli 1958; Timalsina (Vimarśa Foundation); Yoga-Wiki. Key: Bhairava's fierce iconography read as philosophical teaching — worldly bonds and distinctions cast away.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Bhairava's supreme power and its expansion into goddesses are declared, while the universe, though seemingly opposed, is not opposed in consciousness-being. His skull-garland, expelled nooses, cast Māyā-cloth, and other fierce signs are read as teachings that worldly bonds and distinctions have been cast away. The sword, shield, noose, goad, and bow further show the splitting of saṃsāra, freedom from fear, mastery of time, destruction of distinction, and piercing of causal roots. Thus frightening iconography becomes direct instruction in liberation.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — supreme power and goddesses (v. 11):**
> *paramaprakāśavapuṣo vimarśaśaktiḥ prabho parā devī | asyā eva prasaraḥ sarvā brāhmyādikā devyaḥ*
[i.e. of supreme light-body, reflection-power, O Lord, supreme goddess; her expansion indeed all goddesses from Brāhmī etc.] Bhairava's vimarśaśakti is the supreme goddess (parā devī); all other goddesses (Brāhmī etc.) are her expansion.

**Step 1 — universe not opposed (v. 12):**
> *dahanajalāmṛtalekhāviṣādi bibhrat tvam ādiśasy etat | viśvaṃ viruddham api saccidrūpe mayi virudhyate naitat*
[i.e. bearing fire-water-nectar-ray-poison etc, you indicate this; universe, though opposed, in me of consciousness-being form is not opposed.] The universe, though seemingly opposed, is not opposed in consciousness-being.

**Step 2 — consciousness-thread (v. 13):**
> *saṃvitsūtranilīno niḥsāro mātṛnivaho 'yam | karaṇamuṇḍamālabhārin darśayasītīva naḥ svāmin*
[i.e. consciousness-thread-dissolved, sapless, this host of knowers; skull-garland-bearing, you show us thus, O Lord.] The host of knowers is sapless (niḥsāra) when dissolved in the consciousness-thread; the skull-garland shows this.

**Step 3 — nooses expelled (v. 14):**
> *paśyata paśyata pāśā viṣamās ta ime bahiṣkṛtāḥ śaśvat | ity antradhāraṇamiṣat kathayaṃs tvaṃ naḥ samābhāsi*
[i.e. see, see, these nooses are adverse, eternally expelled; saying thus, as if holding entrails, you address us.] The nooses (pāśa) are expelled; the entrails-holding is a display.

**Step 4 — Māyā-cloth cast (v. 15):**
> *māyāpaṭaḥ sarāgo vyomni pare 'smin sa eṣa nikṣiptaḥ | iti gajacarma saraktaṃ mūrdhni vahan kathayasīśa*
[i.e. Māyā-cloth, with attachment, in supreme space, this is cast; thus elephant-skin, bloody, on head bearing, you tell, O Lord.] Māyā is cast like a cloth; the elephant-skin shows Māyā cast away.

**Step 5 — sword splits saṃsāra (v. 16):**
> *antaḥśaktikṛpāṇīm vyanakṣi saṃsṛtivibhedinīm asinā | nijaśaktimahimasvīkṛtasamastaviśvā hi vīravarāḥ*
[i.e. you reveal inner power-knife, saṃsāra-splitting, by sword; indeed heroes excellent have accepted all universe by own power-glory.] The sword splits saṃsāra; the heroes (vīra) accept the universe by their own power-glory.

**Step 6 — shield: freedom from fear (v. 17):**
> *bhavabhayahantā so 'ham sānāthye 'vasthito 'smi mā bhaiṣṭa | ity āsphoṭitakheṭakadarśanato diśasi naḥ svāmin*
[i.e. destroyer of worldly fear, 'I am established in lordship, do not fear'; thus by shield-display you point us, O Lord.] The shield shows freedom from fear: "I am established in lordship, do not fear."

**Step 7 — noose: mastery of time (v. 18):**
> *nijaśaktipāśavalitaś cidvapuṣā kalyate mayā kālaḥ | iti pāśadhāraṇavaśāt prathayasi naḥ kālakālas tvam*
[i.e. bound by own power-noose, by consciousness-body, time is measured by me; thus by noose-holding you show us death of death, O you.] The noose shows mastery of time: time is measured by the consciousness-body; death of death.

**Step 8 — goad: destruction of distinction (v. 19):**
> *bhedamayam akhilam etan nijaśaktyaivākṣipāmi saṃhartum | ity aṅkuśadhāraṇataḥ sphuṭayati parabhairavo 'smākam*
[i.e. all this is distinction-made; by own power I draw to destroy; thus by goad-holding supreme Bhairava makes clear to us.] The goad shows destruction of distinction: all is distinction-made; by own power it is drawn to destroy.

**Step 9 — bow: piercing causal roots (v. 20):**
> *kodaṇḍārūdhaśarapradarśanād brahmaviṣṇurudreśān | sasadākhyaśivāñ chaktyā kāraṇahariṇaṃ bhinatsi mṛgavat tvam*
[i.e. by bow-mounted arrow-display, Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra lords, with Sadāśiva, by power you pierce causal deer like deer.] The bow pierces the causal roots (kāraṇa-hariṇa) of Brahma, Viṣṇu, Rudra, and Sadāśiva — like piercing a deer.

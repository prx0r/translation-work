# Bhairavānukaraṇastotra bhair-p03 — ARGUMENT MAP (manifestation through boon, fearlessness, and power)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 bhair-p03 = vv. 21–30. Lore: Gnoli 1958; Timalsina (Vimarśa Foundation); Yoga-Wiki. Key: the boon-giving hand enacts the giving of universal glory; fearlessness uproots worldly fear; shattered Māyā, skull-staff, lute, bell, drum, trident, vajra, staff, mace, axe each disclose a further aspect of Bhairava's power and the dissolution of distinction.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

The boon-giving hand enacts the giving of universal glory, while fearlessness uproots worldly fear and reveals grace as the cause of the universe. Shattered Māyā, skull-staff, lute, bell, drum, trident, vajra, staff, mace, and axe each disclose a further aspect of Bhairava's power and the dissolution of distinction. The passage insists that one fixed in the supreme principle is untouched by glory-impurities. It ends by stating that Bhairava manifests his own abode everywhere through differences of vision.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — boon-giving (v. 21):**
> *varadena pāṇinā tvaṃ viśvavibhūtipradatvam abhinayasi | na khalu paratattvaniṣṭho vibhavamalaiḥ spṛśyate jātu*
[i.e. by boon-giving hand you enact universe-glory-giving; indeed one fixed in supreme principle is never touched by glory-impurities.] The boon-giving hand enacts the giving of universal glory; one fixed in the supreme principle is untouched by glory-impurities.

**Step 1 — fearlessness (v. 22):**
> *abhayena ca bhavabhayāni unmūlayatā prakāśyate satatam | viśvānugrahakāraṇasvabhāvatā tava karatalena*
[i.e. and by fearlessness, uprooting worldly fears, always manifest; by your palm, nature as cause of universe-grace.] Fearlessness uproots worldly fears; the palm reveals the nature as cause of universe-grace.

**Step 2 — shattered Māyā (v. 23):**
> *akhyātirūpam asamaṃ māyāyāḥ śakalitaṃ śiro 'traitat | parabodhamayena mayā muṇḍaṃ bibhrat prakāśayasy evam*
[i.e. this head, unequal, of Māyā's unmanifest form, shattered; thus you manifest bearing skull by me of supreme awakening.] Māyā's form is shattered; the skull is borne by supreme awakening.

**Step 3 — skull-staff (v. 24):**
> *niḥśeṣahṛtasārā mayy eva jagatsthitiś cidekaghane | iti khaṭvāṅgakaraṅkodvahanacchalato dadāsy ājñām*
[i.e. all essence taken, in me alone world-rest in consciousness-one-mass; thus by skull-staff bone-bearing pretext you give command.] All essence is taken; world-rest is in consciousness-one-mass; the skull-staff gives command.

**Step 4 — lute, bell, drum (v. 25):**
> *vīṇāghaṇtāḍamarūn uḍḍāmara vādayann idaṃ diśasi | vṛttitribhedabhinnaṃ nādaṃ śṛṇvan sthito 'smīti*
[i.e. playing lute, bell, drum, O Uḍḍāmara, you show this; 'I stand hearing sound divided by three functions'.] The three functions (creation, maintenance, dissolution) are heard as sound.

**Step 5 — trident (v. 26):**
> *kiṃ ca parādikaśaktitritayārāsundarām mahāśaktim | devānukaroṣi bahis triśūladhāraṇamiṣeṇa nityaṃ tvam*
[i.e. moreover, you imitate gods outside, by trident-holding pretext, supreme power beautiful with three powers from Para etc.] The trident imitates the gods; the three powers (icchā, jñāna, kriyā) from Para etc.

**Step 6 — vajra (v. 27):**
> *icchādikanijaśaktiprakāśitādhaḥsthagocaratritayām | svām eva parāṃ śaktiṃ vajramayīṃ vahasi ṣaḍarāṃ tvam*
[i.e. you bear your own supreme power, diamond-like, six-spoked, three lower realms revealed by own powers from will etc.] The vajra (diamond) is the supreme power, six-spoked; three lower realms revealed by will etc.

**Step 7 — staff (v. 28):**
> *jagad akhilaṃ macchaktyā damitaṃ sarvavyāvasthitīr dhatte | iti daṇḍadhāraṇavaśād vyanakṣi cidbhairavāsmākam*
[i.e. entire world subdued by my power, all arrangements bears; thus by staff-holding you reveal Consciousness-Bhairava to us.] The staff reveals Cidbhairava: the world is subdued by his power.

**Step 8 — mace and axe (v. 29):**
> *mudgaraparaśū bibhrad baindavanādānukārarūpau tvam | bhedavibhedanaśakalanaparatvam īśāna nirdiśasi*
[i.e. bearing mace and axe, of Bindu-sound-imitation forms, you indicate, O Īśāna, state of splitting distinction and non-distinction.] The mace and axe indicate the splitting of distinction and non-distinction.

**Step 9 — differences of vision (v. 30):**
> *darśanabhedāt svāminn anyānyām āhṛtiṃ dadhānas tvam | bhairavaparabodhamayaṃ prathayasi nijadhāma sarvatra*
[i.e. O Lord, by difference of vision, assuming various forms, you proclaim your own abode, Bhairava supreme awakening, everywhere.] Bhairava manifests his own abode everywhere through differences of vision.

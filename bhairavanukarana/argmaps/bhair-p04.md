# Bhairavānukaraṇastotra bhair-p04 — ARGUMENT MAP (purity, inner fire, and formless self)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 bhair-p04 = vv. 31–40. Lore: Gnoli 1958; Timalsina (Vimarśa Foundation); Yoga-Wiki. Key: skull-cup, pomegranate, rosary, bone, skull-garland, and ash declare the world pure through Brahma-fullness and the transformative power of letters. Forehead-fire, inward-bound aim, sky-cladness, and the darkness-ground that destroys suns all point to the self free from delusion-stain. Even in Bhairava form, the knower of principle is proclaimed liberated despite apparent distinction.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Bhairava drinks the essences of the world within the Śakti-egg fragment and shatters the unequal head of Māyā. Skull-cup, pomegranate, rosary, bone, skull-garland, and ash declare the world pure through Brahma-fullness and the transformative power of letters. Forehead-fire, inward-bound aim, sky-cladness, and the darkness-ground that destroys suns all point to the self free from delusion-stain. Even in Bhairava form, the knower of principle is proclaimed liberated despite apparent distinction.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — drinking world-essences (v. 31):**
> *śāktāṇḍakhaṇḍamadhye viśvarasāḥ sarvam ahaṃ pibāmi sadā | vyañjayasi narakapālagarudhiramiṣād etad iva me 'ntaḥ*
[i.e. in middle of Śakti-egg-fragment, all world-essences, I always drink; by skull-cup blood pretext you manifest this within me.] Bhairava drinks the essences of the world within the Śakti-egg fragment; the skull-cup manifests this within.

**Step 1 — ego-bonds excavated (v. 32):**
> *abhimānahṛdayabandhānutkhātau tāv imau saśikhau | mūrtāv iva muṇdābjau prathayan dhatse mahākālaḥ*
[i.e. these two, with tufts, excavation of ego-heart-bonds; like embodied skull-lotuses, Mahākāla establishes.] The two (tufts) excavate the ego-heart-bonds; Mahākāla establishes them like skull-lotuses.

**Step 2 — pomegranate (v. 33):**
> *akhilajagadbījabhuvaṃ prathamasthām anukaroṣi bodhanidhe | karatalagabījapūrakadarśanalīlāyitād deva*
[i.e. O treasure of awakening, you imitate first station, seed-place of all universe; by palm-held pomegranate display-play, O god.] The pomegranate (seed-place) imitates the first station (prathamasthā) of all universe.

**Step 3 — rosary (v. 34):**
> *bhairavarūpo 'py asi yat tatrāpi kare 'kṣamālikāṃ dhatse | tat sarvākṣavikāsād viśvaṃ parivartayasy antaḥ*
[i.e. though of Bhairava form, there too you hold rosary in hand; by expansion of all letters, you transform universe within.] The rosary (akṣamālikā) transforms the universe by expansion of all letters (akṣa).

**Step 4 — bone, skull, ash (v. 35):**
> *asthikaramuṇḍamālābhasmābharaṇo nṛraktaruciḥ | viśvaṃ brahmamayatvāc chuddham itīśaḥ samādiśasi*
[i.e. bone-hand, skull-garland, ash-ornament, human-blood-hued; 'universe is pure due to Brahma-fullness', O Lord, you instruct.] Bone, skull, ash declare the world pure through Brahma-fullness.

**Step 5 — forehead-fire (v. 36):**
> *svātmārāmamayānāṃ viṣamā api viṣayabudbudhāḥ kim amī | smaradahanam alikanayanam prathayan paramāgnirūpam ādiśasi*
[i.e. for those full of self-delight, what are these adverse sense-bubbles? Manifesting fire of desire, forehead-eye, you instruct supreme fire-form.] Forehead-fire (smaradahana, the fire of desire) instructs the supreme fire-form.

**Step 6 — inward-bound aim (v. 37):**
> *antarnibaddhalakṣyaḥ pravikāsikaraṇo 'pi yat sadā bhavasi | tat prathayasi sarvadaśaṃ gatamohakalaṅkam ātmānam*
[i.e. though always with inward-bound target and expanding faculties, that proclaims self free from delusion-stain in all states.] The self is free from delusion-stain in all states.

**Step 7 — sky-cladness (v. 38):**
> *deva digambaratā te vadati nirāvaraṇam ātmano rūpam | paramabrahmamayārkān māyāvaraṇam kathaṃ vṛṇuyāt*
[i.e. O god, your sky-cladness says un-covered form of self; how would Māyā-covering take supreme-Brahma suns?] Sky-cladness (digambara) says the uncovered form of self; Māyā cannot cover the supreme-Brahma suns.

**Step 8 — darkness-ground (v. 39):**
> *saṃhartā sarveṣām arkādīnām ahaṃ tamobhūmiḥ | nirmalaśāradagaganaprabheṇa vapuṣeti darśayasi*
[i.e. destroyer of all suns etc, I am darkness-ground; by stainless autumn-sky radiance body you show.] The darkness-ground destroys all suns; the stainless autumn-sky radiance body shows this.

**Step 9 — liberated despite distinction (v. 40):**
> *paramabrahmamayas tvaṃ deva yadāśrayasi bhairavākāram | tat prathayasi tattvajñaḥ saty api bhede vimukta iti*
[i.e. you are supreme-Brahma-filled, O god, when assuming Bhairava form; that proclaims knower of principle, though distinction exists, liberated.] Even in Bhairava form, the knower of principle is proclaimed liberated despite apparent distinction.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The stance turns inward here: showing becomes self-showing, still not syllogism.
2. GRETIL numbers KsBhAS_31–40; T1 carries // 31 //–// 40 // against that numbering (Gnoli 1958 ed.).
3. Cremation-gear read as doctrine: ash, bone, hide each state the formless by negation.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the stotra's own devotional arc: drinking world-essences (v. 31) → ego-bonds excavated (v. 32) → pomegranate (v. 33) → rosary (v. 34) → bone/skull/ash (v. 35) → forehead-fire (v. 36) → inward-bound aim (v. 37) → sky-cladness (v. 38) → darkness-ground (v. 39) → liberated despite distinction (v. 40).

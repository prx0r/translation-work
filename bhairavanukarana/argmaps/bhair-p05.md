## 1. What is at issue

The stotra closes with Bhairava's retinue, mudra, and effortless worship. Surrounded by cremation-garlands and vetala, Bhairava declares himself consciousness in the midst of consciousness-object discrimination. He is the nectar-ray, supreme knower, and life of worlds, while his sound-body has an eight-fold external retinue. His form releases noose-nets, dissolves distinction, and seals consciousness-wealth as mudra, conquering the supreme principle-ground. Consciousness arises each moment, the universe flashes below, and worship from ritual to japa is accomplished effortlessly. The text's final word: "Thus you always flash in me, Consciousness-Bhairava, all-beings-self; then all from ritual to japa accomplished effortlessly."

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — surrounded by cremation-garlands (v. 41):**
> *vividhaśmaśānamālāvetālāvṛtaḥ prakāśayase | saṃvittiviṣayakalanāmadhyaś citibhairavo 'smīti*
[i.e. surrounded by various cremation-garlands, vetala, you manifest; 'I am Consciousness-Bhairava in middle of consciousness-object-discrimination'.] Bhairava is surrounded by cremation-garlands and vetala; he declares himself Cidbhairava in the midst of consciousness-object discrimination.

**Step 1 — nectar-ray, supreme knower (v. 42):**
> *tvayy evāmṛtalekhā paraśaktimayī parapramātātaḥ | nātha tvam eva jagatām jīvanam iti bhāsi no hṛdaye*
[i.e. in you alone nectar-ray, supreme-power-filled, supreme knower; O Lord, you indeed are life of worlds, thus shine in our heart.] In Bhairava alone: nectar-ray, supreme power, supreme knower; he is the life of worlds, shining in the heart.

**Step 2 — sound-body retinue (v. 43):**
> *tava śabdarāśivapuṣaḥ pratyaṣṭakam eva bāhyaparivāraḥ | parasūkṣmādivibhedād bhairava lokeśvarapramukhāḥ*
[i.e. of your sound-mass-body, the eight-fold external retinue; from supreme-subtle etc distinctions, Bhairava, world-lords etc.] The sound-body (śabdarāśivapuṣa) has an eight-fold external retinue (pratyaṣṭaka); from supreme to subtle distinctions, Bhairava, world-lords etc.

**Step 3 — mudra seals all (v. 44):**
> *mudrayati nikhilam etan niḥśeṣasvasvarūpasaṃkramaṇāt | modayati bhaktavargaṃ gatimudaṃ pūrṇatāprathanāt*
[i.e. it seals all this by complete own-form transmission; delights devotee-group by path-joy, fullness-manifestation.] The mudra seals all by complete transmission of own-form; delights the devotee-group by path-joy and fullness-manifestation.

**Step 4 — mudra releases (v. 45):**
> *mocayati pāśajālaṃ drāvayati vibhedam īśa te mūrtiḥ | mudrayati vividhasaṃviddraviṇaṃ mudrā tataḥ kathitā*
[i.e. your form releases noose-net, dissolves distinction, O Lord; seals various consciousness-wealth, hence called mudra.] The form releases the noose-net (pāśajāla), dissolves distinction (vibheda); seals various consciousness-wealth (saṃvid-draviṇa); hence called mudra.

**Step 5 — conquering the principle-ground (v. 46):**
> *jayati paratattvabhūmipravare nipāyabhūtaiṣa | sphuṭam adhivasatu satattvaprathayā hṛdayasya meyamalam*
[i.e. conquers in supreme principle-ground, excellent, this abode; may it clearly dwell, by true-principle-manifestation, impurity of my heart.] The form conquers in the supreme principle-ground; may it dwell clearly in the heart by true-principle-manifestation, destroying the impurity.

**Step 6 — consciousness arises (v. 47):**
> *pratikalam udeti saṃvid yā kila tatra sphuraty adho viśvam | śivaśaktivibhavarūpaṃ jānanti maheśa yāgasthāḥ*
[i.e. consciousness arises every moment; indeed there universe flashes below; Mahesha, ritualists know Śiva-Śakti-glory-form.] Consciousness arises every moment; the universe flashes below; ritualists know the Śiva-Śakti-glory-form.

**Step 7 — effortless worship (v. 48):**
> *itthaṃ sphurasi sadā me cidbhairava sarvabhūtātma | tad yāgādijapāntaṃ sampannam ayatnataḥ sarvam*
[i.e. thus you always flash in me, Consciousness-Bhairava, all-beings-self; then all from ritual to japa accomplished effortlessly.] Thus you always flash in me, Cidbhairava, all-beings-self; then all from ritual to japa is accomplished effortlessly. The text's final word: effortless worship — not by effort but by recognition.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the stotra's own devotional arc: surrounded by cremation-garlands (v. 41) → nectar-ray, supreme knower (v. 42) → sound-body retinue (v. 43) → mudra seals all (v. 44) → mudra releases (v. 45) → conquering the principle-ground (v. 46) → consciousness arises (v. 47) → effortless worship (v. 48). The key move: the mudra (seal) releases noose-nets and dissolves distinction; consciousness-wealth is sealed. And the final word: "Thus you always flash in me, Consciousness-Bhairava, all-beings-self; then all from ritual to japa accomplished effortlessly." Worship is not by effort but by recognition (pratyabhijñā). Carry the effortless-worship theme into C1 as the text's closing note. The tone is devotional-philosophical: fierce iconography read as liberation-instruction; the closing is a statement of effortless recognition. L2 should carry the devotional voice and the philosophical reading of each element.

# C1 — bhair-p05 (wear dead, say I)

## SUMMARY

Cremation-girt shown measuring midst; Bhairava am I. Alone-nectar-knower; worlds'-life shining heartward. Sound-bodied eightfold-ward outer retinue, finest-split led. All sealed by total-transmission; throng gladdened by fullness-shown. Noose-net loosed, distinction melted; shape seals knowing-wealth: seal called. Conquered ground excellent, draught-become dwelling clearly; stain known by truth-showing. Each moment knowing rises flashing below; glory-formed world known by rite-standers. Thus flashed in me always; rite-to-mantra-end complete effortlessly.

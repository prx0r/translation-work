# C1 — Bhairavānukaraṇastotra (argument)

## bhair-p01 (1–10) — refuge taken; no second to praise

**SUMMARY.** Homage first, then the address: shelter-takers of consciousness-ground, take consciousness-state, wander nowhere vainly — Śiva conquers as grace-bent. Alone, supreme, nectar-formed, one, blazing, world-swallowed by his shining ring: him venerate. Praised, praiser, praise — nothing distinct here; whatever form endures, consciousness-formed, so it becomes. Bow the principle's imitation, your form; enter it concept-free, reflect not a whit — no fancy holds it. Speech-goddess made own light, sense-flowers offered, guru first-bowed, heart entered. Praise reflecting the Great Lord, own self, one consciousness-mass, the poured-out bond-cluster, in this body-god-house. Powers named consciousness-bliss-will-cognition-doing conquer; subtle-gross splits got, mouth-hood, Brahma-skull named. Omniscience-etc.-objected, heart-etc., six-limbed yours; all to Sadāśiva, by corpse-self, your seat.

**KEY TERMS.** śrayata/mā bhramata (take!/don't wander), yanmaya (made-of), mṛṣati (endures), anukṛti (imitation), rekhāpuruṣa (outline-man), avikalpam (concept-free), kalpanāviṣaya (fancy's object), srāvita (poured-out), vaktratva (mouth-hood), pretātman (corpse-self).

**EXPLANATION.** Verses 1–2 vow and name: refuge commanded, wandering banned; cidbhairava alone, supreme, swallowed-all. Verse 3 voids the praise-triad (stutya-stotā-stṛti avibhinna) and grounds form in cidrūpatā with mṛṣati — whatever endures, endures as consciousness. Verse 4 bows imitation itself (anukṛti): form worshipped as the principle's copy. Verse 5 draws the copy-theory: outline-man to man as letter-script to letter-mass, so shape (ākāra) reveals you. Verse 6 enters concept-free and forbids reflection's remainder (kim api manāk... na). Verse 7 orders worship: speech-goddess, sense-flowers, guru, heart. Verse 8 praises in the body-temple, bonds already poured out. Verse 9 conquers by named powers and seals face-hood in Brahma's skull. Verse 10 seats all — omniscience to Sadāśiva — on corpse-self.

## bhair-p02 (11–20) — goddess spread; world unopposed; gear as doctrine

**SUMMARY.** Supreme-light-bodied, his reflection-power, goddess supreme; her spread, all Brahmī-led goddesses. Bearing fire-water-nectar-streak-poison-etc., you point this: world opposed, yet in true-consciousness-formed me unopposed. Thread-dissolved, sapless knower-host; skull-garlanded faculties — you show us, as it were. See, see, nooses adverse, ever expelled — entrail-holding-seeming, you address us. Māyā-cloth passionate cast in highest sky; elephant-hide bloody head-borne, you tell. Inner dagger bared by sword, saṃsāra-splitting; best heroes took all world by own glory. World-fear-killer: I in lordship established, fear not — shield-clash-shown, you point us. Own-noose-bound, consciousness-bodied, time by me counted — noose-held, you show us death-of-death. Distinction-made all hurled by own power to destroy — goad-held, supreme Bhairava plains us. Bow-drawn-arrow-shown, Brahmā-Viṣṇu-Rudra lords with Sadākhyas — by power the cause-deer pierced, deer-like.

**KEY TERMS.** vimarśaśakti/parā (reflection-power/supreme), prasara (spread), viruddham/api...na (opposed/yet not), mātṛnivaha (knower-host), bahiṣkṛta (expelled), nikṣipta (cast), vyanakṣi (bare), sānāthya (in-lordship), kālakāla (death-of-death), ākṣipāmi (I hurl), kāraṇahariṇa (cause-deer).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 11 enthrones parā as his vimarśaśakti and spreads her into all goddesses. Verse 12 states unopposition: viruddham api... mayi virudhyate na. Verses 13–15 expel: knower-host sapless, nooses ever-cast-out, Māyā-cloth cast in highest sky — each wearing a casting-away. Verses 16–20 arm doctrine: dagger/sword splitting, shield/showing fearlessness, noose/telling death-of-death, goad/hurling distinction away, bow/piercing the cause-deer of the great gods — the pantheon itself hunted.

## bhair-p03 (21–30) — boon, fearlessness, and the gods imitated

**SUMMARY.** Boon-handed, universe-glory-giving enacted; supreme-fixed, glory-stains never touch. Fearlessness ever shown uprooting world-fears; palm holds world-grace-cause-nature. Unfamed unequal Māyā-head shattered there — this; supreme-waking-borne, skull-bearing, thus shown. Wholly-essence-taken, in me alone world-resting, one-massed consciousness; skull-staff-bone-pretext, command given. Lute-bell-drum played, Uddāmara, this shown — triple-function-split sound heard, standing I-am. Moreover Parā-led triple-spoke-lovely great power — gods imitated outside, trident-pretext, always. Will-led own-power-shown lower-triple-abode — own supreme diamond power six-spoked borne. All world by my power subdued, holding all stations — staff-held, Cidbhairava revealed to us. Mace-axe bearing, dot-sound-imitating-formed — distinction-undistinction-splitting-bent, Īśāna indicates. Vision-differing, various-formed assuming — Bhairava-wake-made own abode proclaimed everywhere.

**KEY TERMS.** abhinayasi (enact), vibhavamala (glory-stains), karatalena (by palm), akhyātirūpa (unfamed-formed), khaṭvāṅga (skull-staff), ārā (spoke), ṣaḍarā (six-spoked), daṇḍa (staff), śakalanaparatva (splitting-bent), nijadhāma (own abode).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 21 enacts giving while fixing beyond stain (paratattvaniṣṭha). Verse 22 palms fearlessness into world-grace-cause. Verse 23 shatters Māyā's unfamed head. Verse 24 commands from essence-taken oneness via skull-staff pretext. Verse 25 hears triple-split sound standing I-am. Verse 26 imitates gods outside by trident-pretext while holding the lovely triple-spoked great power. Verse 27 bears the diamond six-spoked own power over the will-shown lower triple. Verse 28 subdues all by my power, stations held. Verse 29 splits distinction-undistinction by mace-axe. Verse 30 proclaims own abode everywhere through vision-difference.

## bhair-p04 (31–40) — cup drunk; letters turn the world; the freed knower

**SUMMARY.** Śakti-egg-fragment-middle, world-juices all I always drink — skull-cup-blood-pretext, as it were, shown me within. Pride-heart-bond uprooters both, tufted — embodied as it were skull-lotuses, Mahākāla holds showing. All-world-seed-ground, first station imitated, waking-treasure — palm-pomegranate-show-play, O god. Bhairava-formed though, rosary in hand held there — so by all-letter expansion the world turned within. Bone-handed, skull-garlanded, ash-decked, man-blood-hued — world by Brahma-fullness pure, thus the Lord teaches. For self-delight-full ones, adverse sense-bubbles, what are they — desire-burning forehead-eyed shown, supreme fire-form taught. Inward-aim-bound, faculty-expanding, always so — shown in all states delusion-stain-gone self. Sky-clad says your uncovered self-form, O god — supreme-Brahma-full suns, how would Māyā-cover cover? Destroyer of all suns-etc., I darkness-ground — spotless-autumn-sky-gleam-bodied, thus shown. Supreme-Brahma-full you, god, when Bhairava-form assumed — that shown: knower-of-principle, in difference yet freed.

**KEY TERMS.** pibāmi sadā (I always drink), utkhātau (uprooters), muṇḍābja (skull-lotuses), bījapūraka (pomegranate), akṣamālikā (rosary), parivartayasi (you turn), śuddham (pure), viṣayabudbudha (sense-bubbles), digambaratā (sky-cladness), tamobhūmi (darkness-ground), saty api bhede vimuktaḥ (in difference yet freed).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 31 drinks world-juices in the egg-fragment, skull-cup its pretext. Verse 32 uproots pride-heart bonds, tufted skull-lotuses held by Mahākāla. Verse 33 plays palm-pomegranate showing for the waking-treasure. Verse 34 turns the world within by all-letter expansion, rosary in hand. Verse 35 teaches purity by Brahma-fullness amid bone-ash-blood. Verse 36 dismisses sense-bubbles for the self-delighting, showing desire-burning forehead-eye. Verse 37 shows the self delusion-stain-gone in all states. Verse 38 argues sky-cladness: uncovered self needs no cover against Māyā. Verse 39 grounds darkness that destroys suns, gleaming autumn-sky-bodied. Verse 40 frees the knower in difference itself.

## bhair-p05 (41–48) — girt, sealed, effortless

**SUMMARY.** Much-cremation-garland-ghoul-girt, shown — sense-field-measuring midst, Consciousness-Bhairava I-am. In you alone nectar-streak, supreme-power-made, ultimate knower hence — Lord, you indeed worlds' life, in our hearts shining. Your sound-mass-bodied eightfold-ward outer retinue — supreme-subtle-split world-lord-led. Sealing all this by total self-form-crossing — delighting devotee-throng, path-joy by fullness-showing. Loosing noose-net, melting distinction, your form, Lord — sealing much-consciousness-wealth, hence called seal. Conquering supreme-ground-excellent, draught-become-this — clearly may it dwell, by true-showing, heart's known-stain. Momently rises consciousness flashing world below — Śiva-Śakti-glory-formed, rite-standers know. Thus always in me flashing, Cidbhairava, all-beings-self — then rite-to-mantra-end, all effortlessly done.

**KEY TERMS.** vetāla (ghoul), kalpanāmadhya (measuring midst), amṛtalekhā (nectar-streak), parapramātṛ (ultimate knower), jīvanam (life), aṣṭaka/pratyaṣṭaka (eightfold/ward), saṃkramaṇa (crossing), draviṇa (wealth), nipāyabhūtaiṣa/meyamalam (kept as printed, open), ayatnataḥ (effortlessly).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 41 girts and declares: saṃvitti-viṣaya-kalanā-madhya, asmi. Verse 42 places nectar-streak and ultimate knower in you, life of worlds shining in our hearts. Verse 43 retinues sound-mass-body outward eightfold under world-lord lead. Verse 44 seals all by total crossing, delights by fullness-showing. Verse 45 names mudrā from its acts: loosing, melting, sealing — hence kathitā. Verse 46 conquers and prays dwelling, true-showing against heart-stain (nipāyabhūtaiṣa, meyamalam kept as printed). Verse 47 rises momently, flashing world below for rite-standers knowing glory-form. Verse 48 closes: sadā me sphurasi — flashing in me always — so all, rite to mantra's end, effortless.

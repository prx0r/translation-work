# C1.v2 — Bhairavānukaraṇastotra (condensation of the arguments)

Take refuge in consciousness-ground, wander nowhere vainly — Śiva conquers as grace-bent. Alone, supreme, nectar-formed, one, blazing, world-swallowed: venerate him. Praised, praiser, praise — nothing distinct; whatever form endures, consciousness-formed, so it becomes. Bow the principle's imitation, your form; enter concept-free, reflect not a whit. Speech-goddess own-lit, sense-flowers offered, guru first-bowed, heart entered. Praise the Great Lord, own self, one consciousness-mass, bonds already poured out, in this body-god-house. Powers named consciousness-bliss-will-cognition-doing conquer; face-hood got, Brahma-skull named. Omniscience to Sadāśiva, all seated on corpse-self. His reflection-power, goddess supreme; her spread, all Brahmī-led goddesses. The world opposed, yet in true-consciousness-formed me unopposed. Thread-dissolved sapless knower-host; skull-garlanded faculties shown as it were. See, see: nooses adverse, ever expelled — entrail-holding-seeming, he addresses us. Māyā-cloth cast in highest sky; bloody elephant-hide head-borne, he tells. Inner dagger bared by sword, saṃsāra splitting; best heroes took all world by own glory. World-fear-killer: I in lordship established, fear not — shield-clash-shown, he points us. Own-noose-bound, consciousness-bodied, time by me counted — noose-held, he shows us death-of-death. Distinction-made all hurled by own power to destroy — goad-held, supreme Bhairava plains us. Bow-drawn, the cause-deer of Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Rudra, Sadākhyas pierced deer-like by power. Boon-handed, universe-glory enacted; supreme-fixed, glory-stains never touch. Fearlessness ever shown, world-fears uprooted; palm holds world-grace-cause-nature. Unfamed unequal Māyā-head shattered there; supreme-waking-borne, skull-bearing shown. Wholly-essence-taken, in me alone world-resting, one-massed; skull-staff-bone pretext, command given. Lute-bell-drum played, this shown — triple-split sound heard, standing I-am. Triple-spoke-lovely great power; gods imitated outside by trident-pretext always. Diamond six-spoked own power borne over will-shown lower triple. All world by my power subdued, stations held — staff-held, Cidbhairava revealed to us. Mace-axe bearing, dot-sound-imitating; distinction-undistinction bent to split, Īśāna indicates. Vision-differing, various-formed assuming — Bhairava-wake-made own abode proclaimed everywhere. Śakti-egg-fragment-middle, world-juices always drunk — skull-cup-blood pretext, as it were, shown within. Pride-heart-bond uprooters, tufted skull-lotuses held showing by Mahākāla. All-world-seed-ground, first station imitated, waking-treasure — palm-pomegranate play, O god. Bhairava-formed though, rosary held — so by all-letter expansion the world turned within. Bone-ash-blood decked, by Brahma-fullness pure the Lord teaches. Sense-bubbles adverse, what are they to the self-delighting — desire-burning forehead-eye shown, supreme fire taught. Inward-aim-bound, faculty-expanding, delusion-stain gone in all states shown. Sky-clad says uncovered self — how would Māyā cover supreme-Brahma-full suns? Destroyer of all suns, I darkness-ground — spotless-autumn-sky-bodied shown. Supreme-Brahma-full, Bhairava-form assumed — knower shown freed in difference itself. Much-garland-ghoul-girt shown — sense-field-measuring midst, Consciousness-Bhairava I-am. Nectar-streak alone in you, ultimate knower hence — worlds' life shining in our hearts. Sound-mass eightfold-ward outer retinue, world-lord-led. Sealing all by total crossing, delighting by fullness-showing. Loosing noose-net, melting distinction — sealing wealth, hence seal. Conquering supreme ground, draught-become; clearly dwell by true-showing despite heart-stain. Momently consciousness rises, world flashing below, glory-formed for rite-standers. Always in me flashing, all-beings-self — then all, rite to mantra's end, effortlessly done.

## bhair-p01 (1–10)

Refuge taken, wandering banned; alone supreme nectar-formed blazing, world-swallowed: venerate. No praise-triad; whatever endures, consciousness-formed, becomes. Imitation bowed, concept-free entered, bonds poured out in the body-house. Named powers conquer; omniscience to Sadāśiva seated on corpse-self.

## bhair-p02 (11–20)

Goddess supreme his reflection-power, spread into Brahmī-led all. World opposed, in me unopposed. Sapless knower-host, skull-garland shown. Nooses expelled, cloth cast, dagger bared, shield shown, noose held, goad hurled, bow drawn — cause-deer pierced.

## bhair-p03 (21–30)

Boon enacted, stain never touching. Fearlessness uprooting, palm holding world-grace. Māyā-head shattered, essence-taken, command given. Triple sound heard standing I-am. Triple-spoked power, gods imitated outside. Diamond six-spoked borne. All subdued, revealed. Split bent indicated. Abode proclaimed everywhere.

## bhair-p04 (31–40)

World-juices drunk in the egg-fragment; pride-bonds uprooted, skull-lotuses held. Seed-ground imitated, pomegranate played. Rosary held, world turned within by letters. Bone-ash-blood, pure by Brahma-fullness. Bubbles nothing to the delighted; forehead-eye shown. Aim bound, stain gone in all states. Sky-clad uncovered; darkness-ground gleaming. Knower freed in difference.

## bhair-p05 (41–48)

Girt shown; measuring midst, I-am declared. Nectar-streak, ultimate knower, worlds' life in hearts. Eightfold retinue outward. Sealed by crossing, delighted by fullness. Loosed, melted, sealed — hence seal. Conquering, dwelling despite stain. Momently rising, world flashing below. Always in me flashing — all, effortlessly done.

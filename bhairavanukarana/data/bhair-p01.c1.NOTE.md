# C1 NOTE — bhair-p01

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Oṃ to Consciousness-Bhairava; shelter-takers take knowing, wander never vain; grace-bent Śiva conquers. Bhairava alone supreme nectar-one ablaze; sense-ring-swallowed all; venerated. Praised-praiser-praise though distinct undiffering; whatever formed by knowing becomes this, enduring. All melted, bliss-massed; Your principle's likeness in Your shape venerated. Letter-script outlines man; shape reveals varied-formed You, supreme self. Concept-free entered; we reflect what fancy never catches. Made, worded, offered: guru unfolded then heart entered. Mirroring praise of own-massed self; bonds outpoured in body-temple. Named powers conquer; splits gained, mouths skull-called. Objected limbs Yours to Sadāśiva; corpse-self Your seat.

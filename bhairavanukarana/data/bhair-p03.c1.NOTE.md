# C1 NOTE — bhair-p03

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Boon hand enacts glory-giving; principle-fixed glory-stainless ever. Fearless uprooting always shown; grace-cause-nature palmed. Māyā's shattered head borne by supreme waking shown. Essence-taken rest massed; skull-pretext commands. Wild-played instruments shown; triple-split sound heard standing. Parā-triple lovely imitated gods-outside, trident-pretext, always. Will-shown lower-three; thunder-made power six-spoked borne. World tamed all-held; staff-held Bhairava revealed to us. Mace-axe borne bindu-mirrored; split-bent pointed. Vision-differed assumed; waking-made proclaimed; abode everywhere.

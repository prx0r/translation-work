## SUMMARY

Power-egg-middled saps all drunk always; within shown as-if. Pride-bond uprooters crested embodied lotus-shown, held. Seed-ground first mirrored waking-treasure; pomegranate show-played. Though Bhairava-shaped, rosary-held turns world within by letters. Bone-skull-ash-blood decked teaches world pure by Brahma-fullness. Self-pleased adverse bubbles; desire-burning brow-eye supreme-fire taught. Inward-aimed expanding shows unstained self in every state. Sky-cladness speaks self bare; how should Māyā's veil cover suns? All-destroyer shown from darkness-ground, gleaming-bodied. Brahma-full wearing Bhairava-form shown; differing yet freed.

# Bhairavastava — ARGMAP

## bh-p01 (1)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice (stotra).
- question: How does one venerate Bhairava?
- premises: Bhairava-Lord, refuge of the helpless, by mind-become-Yours, in heart I venerate.
- inference: Veneration = mind-become-Yours in heart.
- conclusion: I venerate Bhairava-Lord.
- unresolved: none.

## bh-p02 (2)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is the relation of self to Maheśa?
- premises: You, Maheśa, always my self; by You all (is) mine.
- inference: Self is Maheśa; all is mine through Him.
- conclusion: Maheśa is my self; all is mine.
- unresolved: none.

## bh-p03 (3)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What of suffering and delusion?
- premises: Even though hard-to-bear suffering, delusion, terror-causing karma-hosts.
- inference: Suffering exists; terror-causing karmas are present.
- conclusion: Acknowledgment of suffering/delusion (continued in p04).
- unresolved: none.

## bh-p04 (4)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: How does one become fearless?
- premises: By Śaṅkara-service, contemplation-firm, I am terrifying-Bhairava-power-filled.
- inference: Service + firm contemplation → Bhairava-power-filled → fearlessness.
- conclusion: I am Bhairava-power-filled.
- unresolved: none.

## bh-p05 (5)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What of death and danger?
- premises: Death, Yama, ender, karma-ghosts — Lord, salutation; never I fear.
- inference: No fear of death/danger.
- conclusion: Salutation to the Lord; no fear.
- unresolved: none.

## bh-p06 (6)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is bliss?
- premises: In being-full (parāmṛta-nirbhara-pūrṇe) in You, I, self, bliss I enter.
- inference: Bliss = entering the full being of the Lord.
- conclusion: I enter bliss in You.
- unresolved: none.

## bh-p07 (7)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: When does nondifference manifest?
- premises: Lord, then alone my with-You-nondifference; hymn-supreme-nectar-rain arises.
- inference: Nondifference manifests when the hymn-rain arises.
- conclusion: Nondifference + hymn-rain.
- unresolved: none.

## bh-p08 (8)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What does scripture-contemplation do?
- premises: Your-scripture supreme-nectar-contemplation in mind, bliss-stream flows.
- inference: Scripture-contemplation → bliss-stream in mind.
- conclusion: Bliss-stream flows.
- unresolved: none.

## bh-p09 (9)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is gained by obtaining the Lord?
- premises: You, dear, having-obtained, beautiful-vision alone, hard-to-get-by-others, samaya-knower.
- inference: Obtaining the Lord = beautiful vision (rare, samaya-knowing).
- conclusion: I have obtained the dear Lord.
- unresolved: none.

## bh-p10 (10)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What does the Lord do?
- premises: By whom the Lord becomes, world's great-heat pacifies instantly, compassionate.
- inference: The Lord pacifies world-heat instantly, compassionately.
- conclusion: The compassionate Lord pacifies.
- unresolved: none.

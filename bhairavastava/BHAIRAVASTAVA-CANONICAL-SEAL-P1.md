## The parable (canonical draft, ≈90s spoken)

> A man bows in a land of desert-heat and stays bowed. Because he
> stays bowed, everything that passes belongs to him — all of it
> his, none of it else's. When suffering passes he does not lift his
> head, so there is no fear-talk to hear. When Death comes to glare,
> he glares at a bowed crown — the gaze slides off, finds no face.
> The four terrors meet his one salutation and go out like lamps.
> And because he never rises from the bow, he begins to see: the
> world shows its true face, and bliss is a door he is already
> inside. When the affliction comes — as it does — it finds no one
> to afflict: the bow and the Lord are not two, and where they are
> not two, nectar falls. Others do vows, gifts, cold baths; his
> scripture drips nectar and cools the heat of becoming where he
> kneels. Then, surprisingly, the bow begins to dance —
> consciousness singing over him — and the world's desert-heat
> drops like a coat. Abhinavagupta dates it: dark tenth of Pauṣa.
> Signed, sealed, cooled.

**Map (hinge):**
- bows in desert-heat, stays bowed → v1 *vande… refuge of the
  helpless* (setting from v10's *marudeśa-śoka* heat) [v1]
- everything passing belongs to him → v2 *all becomes Yours, all is
  mine through You* [v2]
- no fear-talk though suffering passes → v3 *no talk of transmigration-
  fear, suffering conceded* [v3]
- Death's glare slides off the bowed crown → v4 *turn not this
  wrathful-terrible gaze toward me* (gaze turned aside) [v4]
- four terrors out like lamps at one salutation → v5 *four terrors…
  salutation alone, absolute fearlessness* [v5]
- sees true face; bliss-door already inside → v6 *awakening rays
  reveal true essence; enter bliss* [v6]
- affliction finds no one to afflict; not-two; nectar falls →
  v7 *affliction = occasion of nondifference; nectar-rain* [v7]
- his scripture drips, cools becoming's heat → v8 *vows/gifts/baths
  dispel becoming's heat; scripture-as-nectar streams bliss* [v8]
- the bow dances → v9 *consciousness dances, sings; rare vision* [v9]
- heat drops like a coat; dated, sealed → v10 *Pauṣa dark-tenth;
  compassionate Lord pacifies world's heat instantly* [v10]

**Limit:** Death must never be killed or banished (the text only
turns his gaze — vīra is the refusal, not a murder); the heat is
real throughout (suffering conceded v3 — the cooling at the end is
pacification, not denial of the world's pain).
**Rasa:** śānta spine; vīra at the Death-gate (p04's own); ānanda at
the dance (p09); karuṇā-śānta close (world cooled, dated).
**Bar check:** one protagonist (Death = a glare, not a second
character); retellable; plain words (namas→salutation,
bhayānaka→the four terrors, śāstra→scripture, Pauṣa colophon kept
as date).

## The seal — statement

**The bow that cools the desert:** a vertical column of one story —
scorched desert below with heat shimmer, a white-bowed man at center
whose crown throws Death's iron glare sliding off like rain,
four small lamps going out around him, nectar-stream falling onto
his bowed back, gold light-arcs swinging up from his shoulders into
dance, and above all a cooled twilight with a thin dark crescent —
the date, without letters.

## Composition laws (this seal)

ONE subject (the bowed man; Death = glare-weather, never a figure
with a face — keeps v4's "gaze turned" without a duel scene). Heat→
cool vertical gradient IS the rasa arc. The four lamps read small
and cold as they die (no explosion). Crescent tiny — a datemark, not
a moon-scene. No letters (colophon carried by crescent).

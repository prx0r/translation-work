# bhairavastava bh-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
Pervading the particular states of moving and unmoving — consciousness-made, one, endless, beginningless: Bhairava-Lord, refuge of the helpless — by mind become Yours, in heart I venerate.
The Sanskrit: *vyāptacarācarabhāvaviśeṣam cinmayam ekam anantam anādim | bhairavanātham anāthaśaraṇyaṃ tvanmayacittatayā hṛdi vande*

# bhairavastava bh-p02 — L2 READ

## v.2
All this, become of You, now appears to me by the power of Your grace. You, Maheśa, are always my self; by You all is mine.
The Sanskrit: *tvanmayam etad aśeṣam idānīm bhāti mama tvadanugrahaśaktyā | tvaṃ ca maheśa ! sadaiva mamā'tmā svātmamayam mama tena samastam*

# bhairavastava bh-p03 — L2 READ

## v.3
With You as Lord, all-pervading in own self — therefore no talk of transmigration-fear. Even though hard-to-bear suffering, delusion, terror-causing karmas are present.
The Sanskrit: *svātmani viśvagaye tvayi nāthe tena na saṃsṛtibhītikathā asti | satsvapi durdhara duḥkha vimoha trāsa vidhāyiṣu karmagaṇeṣu*

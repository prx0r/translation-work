# bhairavastava bh-p04 — L2 READ

## v.4
O Ender, turn not toward me this most wrathful-terrible gaze. Firm in Śaṅkara-service and contemplation, I am filled with terrifying Bhairava-power.
The Sanskrit: *antaka māṃ prati mā dṛśam enām | krodhakarālatamām vinidhehi śaṅkarasevana cintanadhīraḥ asmi bhīṣaṇabhairavaśaktimayo'smi*

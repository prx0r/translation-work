# bhairavastava bh-p05 — L2 READ

## v.5
Thus — great darkness dispelled by rays of world-awareness: death, Yama, the ender, karma-ghosts — Lord, salutation; never do I fear.
The Sanskrit: *ittham upoḍhabhavanmayasaṃviddīdhitidāritabhūritamisraḥ mṛtyuyamāntakakarmapiśācaiḥ nātha ! namo'stu na jātu bibhemi*

# bhairavastava bh-p07 — L2 READ

## v.7
When the affliction-state, producing slight anguish, comes into the mind's range — Lord, then alone is my nondifference with You; the supreme-nectar-rain arises.
The Sanskrit: *mānasagocaram eti yadaiva kleśadaśā tanutāpavidhātrī nātha ! tadaiva mama tvadabhedas stotraparāmṛtavṛṣṭiḥ udeti*

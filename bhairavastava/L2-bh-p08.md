# bhairavastava bh-p08 — L2 READ

## v.8
Truly, O Śaṅkara: vow, giving, bathing, austerity dispel becoming’s heat. Contemplation of Your scripture as supreme nectar streams a bliss-stream in mind.
The Sanskrit: *śaṅkara satyam idam vratadānasnānatapaḥ bhavatāpavidāri tāvakaśāstra parāmṛtacintā cetasi nirvṛtidhārām syandati*

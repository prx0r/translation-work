# bhairavastava bh-p09 — L2 READ

## v.9
This consciousness of mine dances, sings, deeply rejoices — O Bhairava-Lord. Having obtained You, the dear: the beautiful vision alone, hard to get by others, samaya-knower.
The Sanskrit: *nṛtyati gāyati hṛṣyati gāḍham saṃvit iyam mama bhairavanātha tvāṃ priyam āpya sudarśanamekam durlabhamanyajanaiḥ samayajñam*

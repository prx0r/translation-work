# bhairavastava bh-p10 — L2 READ

## v.10
In the month of Pauṣa, on the dark tenth, Abhinavagupta made this hymn. The pervasive, compassionate Lord pacifies the world's desert-heat — instantly.
The Sanskrit: *vasurasapauṣe kṛṣṇadaśamyām abhinavaguptaḥ stavam imam akarot yena vibhuḥ bhavati janasya arusantāpaṃ śamayati jhaṭiti dayāluḥ*

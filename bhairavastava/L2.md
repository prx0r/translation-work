# Bhairavastava — L2 READ

## bh-p01 — veneration
Pervading the particular states of moving and unmoving — consciousness-made, one, endless, beginningless: Bhairava-Lord, refuge of the helpless — by mind become Yours, in heart I venerate. The stotra opens with the Lord's all-pervading conscious nature and the devotee's stance: total surrender of mind to Bhairava, who is the refuge of those without refuge.

## bh-p02 — identity
All this, become of You, now appears to me by the power of Your grace. You, Maheśa, are always my self; by You all is mine. The Lord is not other than the self; grace first discloses the world as His, then identity is declared, and through Him everything becomes mine.

## bh-p03 — no fear-talk, suffering conceded
With You as Lord, all-pervading in own self — therefore no talk of transmigration-fear. Even though hard-to-bear suffering, delusion, and terror-causing karmas are present — the devotee denies fear-talk while conceding affliction: no fear is discussed, yet suffering is not denied.

## bh-p04 — the averted gaze, power-filled
O Ender, turn not toward me this most wrathful-terrible gaze. By Śaṅkara-service, firm in contemplation, I am filled with terrifying Bhairava-power. Service and firm contemplation produce the power-filled state — and the first use of that power is to turn death's gaze aside.

## bh-p05 — darkness dispelled, fearlessness
Thus — my great darkness dispelled by rays of world-awareness: death, Yama, the ender, karma-ghosts — Lord, salutation; never do I fear. Awareness itself has burned the darkness away; what remains before death's ministers is salutation, not fear.

## bh-p06 — true awakening, bliss
The true essence of world-objects is seen by rays of arisen true awakening. In being-full in You — I, the self, enter bliss. Awakening discloses things as they are; entry into the Lord's fullness is bliss.

## bh-p07 — affliction arrives, nondifference arises
When the affliction-state, producing slight anguish, comes into the mind's range — Lord, then alone is my nondifference with You; the supreme-nectar-rain of the hymn arises. Affliction's arrival is the occasion, not the obstacle: with it comes nondifference, and the hymn rains.

## bh-p08 — rites declared vain, scripture contemplated
O Śaṅkara, truly — vow, giving, bathing, austerity dispel becoming's heat. Rites are not despised but relativized: Your scripture's supreme-nectar-contemplation in the mind — the bliss-stream flows. Contemplating scripture produces the bliss-stream in mind.

## bh-p09 — rejoicing consciousness, obtaining
This consciousness of mine dances, sings, deeply rejoices — O Bhairava-Lord. You, dear, having been obtained — beautiful vision alone, hard to get by others, samaya-knower. Obtaining the Lord is the rare beautiful vision of the samaya-knower, celebrated dancing.

## bh-p10 — colophon and pacification
In the month of Pauṣa, on the dark tenth, Abhinavagupta made this hymn. By whom the Lord becomes, the world's great heat is pacified instantly — compassionate. The stotra is dated, signed, and sent outward: the Lord, becoming, pacifies the world's suffering instantly, compassionately.

1. bhairavanātham anāthaśaraṇyaṃ tvanmayacittatayā hṛdi vande
2. tvaṃ ca maheśa ! sadaiva mamā'tmā svātmamayaṃ mama tena samastam
3. satsvapi durdharaduḥkhavimohatrāsavidhāyiṣu karmagaṇeṣu
4. śaṅkarasevanacintanadhīro bhīṣaṇabhairavaśaktimayo'smi
5. mṛtyuyamāntakakarmapiśācairnātha ! namo'stu na jātu bibhemi
6. bhāvaparāmṛtanirbharapūrṇe tvayyahamātmani nirvṛtimemi
7. nātha ! tadaiva mama tvadabhedastotraparāmṛtavṛṣṭirudeti
8. tāvakaśāstraparāmṛtacintā syandati cetasi nirvṛtidhārām
9. tvāṃ priyamāpya sudarśanamekaṃ durlabhamanyajanaiḥ samayajñam
10. yena vibhurbhavamarusantāpaṃ śamayati jhaṭiti janasya dayāluḥ

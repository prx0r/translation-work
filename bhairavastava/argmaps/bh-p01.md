## 1. What is at issue

Verse 1 of the Bhairavastava (Abhinavagupta, 10 verses). Gloss: pervading-the-particular-states-of-moving-and-unmoving (vyāptacarācarabhāvaviśeṣam) [and]-consisting-of-consciousness (cinmayam) [and]-one (ekam) [and]-endless (anantam) [and]-beginningless (anādim) [and]-Bhairava-Lord (bhairavanātham) [and]-of-helpless (anāthaśaraṇyaṃ) [and]-by-mind-become-Yours (tvanmayacittatayā) [and]-in-heart (hṛdi) [and]-I-venerate (vande) // abhts_1

Sanskrit: vyāptacarācarabhāvaviśeṣaṃ cinmayam ekam anantam anādim | bhairavanātham anāthaśaraṇyaṃ tvanmayacittatayā hṛdi vande

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 1:**
> vyāptacarācarabhāvaviśeṣaṃ cinmayam ekam anantam anādim | bhairavanātham anāthaśaraṇyaṃ tvanmayacittatayā hṛdi vande

[i.e. the all-pervading particular of moving and unmoving states, consciousness-made, one, endless, beginningless — Bhairava-Lord, refuge of the helpless — by mind-become-Yours, in heart I venerate.]

The all-pervading (vyāpta) particular of moving-and-unmoving states (carācarabhāvaviśeṣa), consisting of consciousness (cinmaya), one (eka), endless (ananta), beginningless (anādi) — that is the object, fixed before any act. Then the act: Bhairava-Lord (bhairavanātham), refuge of the helpless (anāthaśaraṇyaṃ), venerated by mind-become-Yours (tvanmayacittatayā), in heart (hṛdi) — vande. Surrender sized to its object: only an all-pervading conscious real warrants total mental absorption, and only the refuge of the helpless can receive one who has nothing.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry the full verse: all-pervading conscious nature first, then the devotee's surrender. L2 must render both halves — the metaphysical object and the mental act — not just the second. The tone is devotional-early-Abhinava: the stotra form, not the commentary form.

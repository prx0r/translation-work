# Bhairavastava bh-p02 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 2)

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 2 of the Bhairavastava (Abhinavagupta, 10 verses). Gloss: become-of-You (tvanmayam) [and]-this (etad) [and]-without-remainder (aśeṣam) [and]-now (idānīm) [and]-appears (bhāti) [and]-to-me (mama) [and]-by-power-of-Your-grace (tvadanugrahaśaktyā) [and]-You (tvaṃ ca) [and]-Maheśa (maheśa !) [and]-always (sadaiva) [and]-my (mamā'tmā) [and]-self (svātmamayam) [and]-my (mama) [and]-all (tena samastam) // abhts_2

Sanskrit: tvanmayam etad aśeṣam idānīṃ bhāti mama tvadanugrahaśaktyā | tvaṃ ca maheśa sadaiva mamātmā svātmamayaṃ mama tena samastam

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 2:**
> tvanmayam etad aśeṣam idānīṃ bhāti mama tvadanugrahaśaktyā | tvaṃ ca maheśa sadaiva mamātmā svātmamayaṃ mama tena samastam

[i.e. all this, become of You, now appears to me by Your grace's power — You, Maheśa, are always my self; made of my own self, by You all is mine.]

Disclosure first: all this (etad), become of You (tvanmayam), without remainder (aśeṣam), now (idānīm) appears (bhāti) to me (mama) — by the power of Your grace (tvadanugrahaśaktyā). Grace shows the world as His before identity is claimed. Then declaration without limit: You (tvaṃ), Maheśa, always (sadaiva) my self (mamātmā); made of my own self (svātmamayaṃ), all (samastam) is mine (mama) through Him (tena). Sadaiva (always) removes every temporal bound — not became, not will become.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Mamātmā is read as mama ātmā (my self); the alternative (mamātmā as one word, "my-self") converges on the same sense.
2. Restored 2026-10-31: T1 previously covered only the verse's second half; the grace-disclosure first half is now glossed and parsed.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry disclosure-then-declaration: grace shows the world as His → identity declared → all owned through Him. L2 must keep sadaiva (always) — the identity is a standing fact, not an event. Carry the identity into verse 3, where affliction tests it.

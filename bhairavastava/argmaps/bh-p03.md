## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 3:**
> svātmani viśvagaye tvayi nāthe tena na saṃsṛtibhītikathāsti | satsv api durdharaduḥkhavimohatrāsavidhāyiṣu karmagaṇeṣu

[i.e. with You as Lord pervading own self, therefore no talk of transmigration-fear exists — even though hard-to-bear suffering, delusion, terror-causing karma-hosts (are present).]

Denial first: with You as Lord (nāthe) pervading (viśvagaye) own self (svātmani), therefore (tena) no talk of transmigration-fear exists (na saṃsṛtibhītikathā asti). Concession in the same breath: even though (satsvapi) hard-to-bear (durdhara) suffering (duḥkha), delusion (vimoha), terror (trāsa)-causing (vidhāyi) karma-hosts (karmagaṇeṣu). The verse is complete in itself — fear-talk denied AND affliction granted, no verb missing, no resolution postponed. Realism framed by denial, not an open bracket.

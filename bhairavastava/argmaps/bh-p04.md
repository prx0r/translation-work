# Bhairavastava bh-p04 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 4)

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 4 of the Bhairavastava (Abhinavagupta, 10 verses). Gloss: O-Ender (antaka) [and]-toward-me (māṃ) [and]-toward (prati) [and]-not (mā) [and]-gaze (dṛśam) [and]-this (enām) [and]-most-wrathful-terrible (krodhakarālatamām) [and]-direct (vinidhehi) [and]-By-Śaṅkara-service (śaṅkarasevana) [and]-contemplation-firm (cintanadhīraḥ) [and]-I-am (asmi) [and]-terrifying-Bhairava-power-filled (bhīṣaṇabhairavaśaktimayo'smi) // abhts_4

Sanskrit: antaka māṃ prati mā dṛśam enāṃ krodhakarālatamāṃ vinidhehi | śaṅkarasevanacintanadhīro bhīṣaṇabhairavaśaktimayo 'smi

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 4:**
> antaka māṃ prati mā dṛśam enāṃ krodhakarālatamāṃ vinidhehi | śaṅkarasevanacintanadhīro bhīṣaṇabhairavaśaktimayo 'smi

[i.e. O Ender, do not direct at me this most wrathful-terrible gaze — firm in Śaṅkara-service and contemplation, I am filled with terrifying Bhairava-power.]

Prayer first: O Ender (antaka), do not (mā) direct (vinidhehi) toward me (māṃ prati) this (enām) most wrathful-terrible (krodhakarālatamām) gaze (dṛśam). Then the ground of the prayer: firm (dhīra) in Śaṅkara-service (sevana) and contemplation (cintana), filled (maya) with terrifying, awe-full Bhairava-power (bhīṣaṇabhairavaśakti) — asmi (I am). The power-filled state is both the shield prayed from behind and the testimony declared: death's gaze turned aside by one who is already śaktimaya.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. "Śaṅkara" here = Śiva, not Śaṅkarācārya.
2. Restored 2026-10-31: T1 previously covered only the verse's second half; the averted-gaze prayer is now glossed and parsed.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry prayer-then-ground: turn aside the gaze, then testify to the power that warrants the prayer. L2 must keep the first-person asmi (testimony, not theory) and the awe-sense of bhīṣaṇa (overwhelming, not menacing). Carry the power-filled state into verse 5, where it faces death itself.

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 6 of the Bhairavastava (Abhinavagupta, 10 verses). Gloss: whose-true-essence-of-world-objects-is-seen-by-rays-of-arisen-true-awakening (proditasatyavibodhamarīciprekṣitaviśvapadārthasatattvaḥ) [and]-In-being-full (bhāvaparāmṛtanirbharapūrṇe) [and]-in-you (tvayi) [and]-I (aham) [and]-self (ātmani) [and]-bliss (nirvṛtim) [and]-I-enter (emi) // abhts_6

Sanskrit: proditasatyavibodhamarīciprekṣitaviśvapadārthasatattvaḥ | bhāvaparāmṛtanirbharapūrṇe tvayy aham ātmani nirvṛtim emi

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 6:**
> proditasatyavibodhamarīciprekṣitaviśvapadārthasatattvaḥ | bhāvaparāmṛtanirbharapūrṇe tvayy aham ātmani nirvṛtim emi

[i.e. whose true essence of world-objects is seen by rays of arisen true awakening — in the being-full, supreme-nectar-laden, complete You, I, the self, enter bliss.]

Awakening first: arisen (prodita) true (satya) awakening (vibodha), whose rays (marīci) reveal (prekṣita) the true essence (satattva) of world-objects (viśvapadārtha). Then entry: in You (tvayi) — being (bhāva), supreme nectar (parāmṛta), brimming (nirbhara), complete (pūrṇa) — I, the self (aham ātmani), enter (emi) bliss (nirvṛtim). Awakening discloses things as they are; entry into the disclosed fullness blissifies. Pūrṇa is the Lord's nature; entering it is bliss — ingress, not feeling.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Parāmṛta (supreme nectar) is kept as a register term; no soma-ritual background is imported.
2. Restored 2026-10-31: T1 previously covered only the verse's second half; the awakening-disclosure first half is now glossed and parsed.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry disclosure-then-entry: awakening sees things true, entry into fullness is bliss. L2 must say "enter bliss," never "feel bliss" — emi governs nirvṛtim as its destination. Carry the fullness (pūrṇa) into verse 7, where fullness becomes nondifference.

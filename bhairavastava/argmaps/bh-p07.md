# Bhairavastava bh-p07 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 7)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 bh-p07 = verse 7 (restored 2026-10-31: first half mānasagocaram...vidhātrī now glossed — affliction's arrival dated as nondifference's occasion). Lore: tadaiva binds nondifference to the hymn's arising, not to a later event; the stotraparāmṛtavṛṣṭi (hymn's rain of supreme nectar) makes the hymn describe its own effect — the clearest instance in the stotra of means coinciding with fruit.*

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 7 of the Bhairavastava (Abhinavagupta, 10 verses). Gloss: into-mind's-range (mānasagocaram) [and]-comes (eti) [and]-when-indeed (yadaiva) [and]-affliction-state (kleśadaśā) [and]-producing-slight-anguish (tanutāpavidhātrī) [and]-Lord (nātha !) Sanskrit: mānasagocaram eti yadaiva kleśadaśā 'tanutāpavidhātrī | nātha tadaiva mama tvadabhedastotraparāmṛtavṛṣṭir udeti

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 7:**
> mānasagocaram eti yadaiva kleśadaśā 'tanutāpavidhātrī | nātha tadaiva mama tvadabhedastotraparāmṛtavṛṣṭir udeti

[i.e. when indeed the affliction-state, producing slight anguish, comes into the mind's range — Lord, then alone my nondifference with You; the hymn's rain of supreme nectar arises.]

When indeed (yadaiva) the affliction-state (kleśadaśā), producing slight anguish (tanutāpavidhātrī), comes (eti) into the mind's range (mānasagocaram) — then alone (tadaiva) my nondifference with You (mama tvadabhedaḥ), and the hymn's rain of supreme nectar arises (stotraparāmṛtavṛṣṭir udeti). Affliction's arrival is dated as nondifference's occasion: with it, not after it. The raining hymn is nondifference made audible — means and fruit, one event.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Tadaiva is both temporal (then) and logical (only then); both readings are kept.
2. Restored 2026-10-31: T1 previously covered only the verse's second half; the affliction-arrival first half is now glossed and parsed. Tanutāpavidhātrī read as "producing slight anguish" (uncertain).

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry simultaneity: affliction arrives, nondifference and hymn-rain arise together, then-alone. L2 must not sequence them (first nondifference, then hymn) — the verse binds them. Carry the hymn-as-means into verse 8, where the hymn becomes scripture-contemplation.

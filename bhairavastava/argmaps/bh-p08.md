## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 8:**
> śaṅkara satyam idaṃ vratadānasnānatapo bhavatāpavidāri | tāvakaśāstraparāmṛtacintā syandati cetasi nirvṛtidhārām

[i.e. O Śaṅkara, truly this — vow, giving, bathing, austerity — dispels becoming's heat; Your scripture contemplated as supreme nectar streams a bliss-stream in the mind.]

Truly (satyam), O Śaṅkara: this (idam) — vow (vrata), giving (dāna), bathing (snāna), austerity (tapas) — dispels (vidāri) becoming's heat (bhavatāpa). Rites relativized, not despised. Then the higher means: contemplation (cintā) of Your (tāvaka) scripture (śāstra) as supreme nectar (parāmṛta), in mind (cetasi), streams (syandati) a bliss-stream (nirvṛtidhārā). Heat dispelled by rites; bliss streamed by contemplation. The mind is not informed but irrigated.

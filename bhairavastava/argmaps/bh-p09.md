# Bhairavastava bh-p09 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 9)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 bh-p09 = verse 9 (restored 2026-10-31: first half nṛtyati...bhairavanātha now glossed — consciousness dances before obtaining is defined). Lore: samayajña (knower of the samaya) restricts the verse's "beautiful vision" (sudarśana) to the initiated — samaya customarily denotes the post-initiatory discipline in Trika contexts; the verse thus marks obtaining-the-Lord as an insider's vision, hard for others by structure, not by divine stinginess.*

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 9 of the Bhairavastava (Abhinavagupta, 10 verses). Gloss: dances (nṛtyati) [and]-sings (gāyati) [and]-rejoices (hṛṣyati) [and]-deeply (gāḍham) [and]-consciousness (saṃvit) [and]-this (iyam) [and]-my (mama) [and]-O-Bhairava-Lord (bhairavanātha) [and]-You (tvāṃ) [and]-dear (priyam) [and]-having-obtained (āpya) [and]-beautiful-vision-alone (sudarśanamekam) [and]-hard-to-get-by-others (durlabhamanyajanaiḥ) [and]-samaya-knower (samayajñam) // abhts_9

Sanskrit: nṛtyati gāyati hṛṣyati gāḍhaṃ saṃvid iyaṃ mama bhairavanātha | tvāṃ priyam āpya sudarśanamekaṃ durlabham anyajanaiḥ samayajñam

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 9:**
> nṛtyati gāyati hṛṣyati gāḍhaṃ saṃvid iyaṃ mama bhairavanātha | tvāṃ priyam āpya sudarśanamekaṃ durlabham anyajanaiḥ samayajñam

[i.e. dances, sings, deeply rejoices — this consciousness of mine, O Bhairava-Lord; having obtained You, the dear — the beautiful vision alone, hard to get by others, O knower of the samaya.]

Joy first: this consciousness of mine (saṃvid iyaṃ mama) dances (nṛtyati), sings (gāyati), deeply (gāḍham) rejoices (hṛṣyati) — O Bhairava-Lord. Then its cause named: having obtained (āpya) You, the dear (priyam) — the beautiful vision alone (sudarśanam ekam), hard to get by others (durlabham anyajanaiḥ) — O knower of the samaya (samayajña). Obtaining is defined as vision (sudarśana), and vision is fenced: the samaya-knower's portion.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Samaya is read in its initiatory-discipline sense; the verse itself gives no gloss — marked as reading, not fact.
2. Restored 2026-10-31: T1 previously covered only the verse's second half; the rejoicing-consciousness first half is now glossed and parsed.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry joy-then-cause: dancing consciousness, then obtaining defined as rare vision. L2 must keep "alone" (ekaṃ) and "hard to get by others" — rarity is structural to the claim. Carry the obtained vision into verse 10, where vision acts outward as pacification.

# Bhairavastava bh-p10 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 10)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 bh-p10 = verse 10 (restored 2026-10-31: first half vasurasapauṣe...akarot now glossed — the colophon dates and signs the work). Lore: the colophon — vasurasapauṣe kṛṣṇadaśamyām abhinavaguptaḥ stavam imam akarot (in Pauṣa, dark fortnight, tenth day, Abhinavagupta made this hymn) — is Abhinavagupta's signature habit of dating his hymns; the English translation by Masterji Zinda Kaul (Sanskrit Documents) renders the closing pacification as the hymn's outward turn.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 10 of the Bhairavastava (Abhinavagupta, 10 verses). Gloss: in-the-month-of-Pauṣa (vasurasapauṣe) [and]-on-the-dark-tenth (kṛṣṇadaśamyām) [and]-Abhinavagupta (abhinavaguptaḥ) [and]-hymn (stavam) [and]-this (imam) [and]-made (akarot) [and]-By-whom (yena) [and]-the-Lord (vibhuḥ) [and]-becomes (bhavati) [and]-world's (janasya) [and]-great-heat (arusantāpaṃ) [and]-pacifies (śamayati) [and]-instantly (jhaṭiti) [and]-compassionate (dayāluḥ)

Sanskrit: vasurasapauṣe kṛṣṇadaśamyām abhinavaguptaḥ stavam imam akarot | yena vibhur bhavamarusantāpaṃ śamayati janasya jhaṭiti dayāluḥ

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 10:**
> vasurasapauṣe kṛṣṇadaśamyām abhinavaguptaḥ stavam imam akarot | yena vibhur bhavamarusantāpaṃ śamayati janasya jhaṭiti dayāluḥ

[i.e. in the month of Pauṣa, on the dark tenth, Abhinavagupta made this hymn — by whom the pervasive, compassionate Lord pacifies the world's desert-heat of becoming, instantly.]

Dated and signed first: in the month of Pauṣa (vasurasapauṣe), on the dark tenth (kṛṣṇadaśamyām), Abhinavagupta (abhinavaguptaḥ) made (akarot) this (imam) hymn (stavam). Then the outward turn: by whom (yena) the pervasive (vibhuḥ), compassionate (dayāluḥ) Lord becomes (bhavati), pacifies (śamayati) the world's (janasya) desert-heat of becoming (bhavamarusantāpaṃ) — instantly (jhaṭiti). The dated hymn turns outward: private recognition cools the world.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Yena's antecedent (the devotee? the Lord's becoming?) is left open — read as the devotee-through-whom, with the alternative noted.
2. Restored 2026-10-31: T1 previously covered only the verse's second half; the colophon first half is now glossed and parsed. Vasurasapauṣe read as month-of-Pauṣa without decoding the chronogram.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry signed-then-sent: dated colophon first, pacification as the hymn's outward act. L2 must keep jhaṭiti (instantly) and dayāluḥ (compassionate) as the final words' weight — speed and compassion are the close. This completes the arc opened in verse 1's refuge of the helpless.

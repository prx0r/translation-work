# C1 — Bhairavastava

## bh-p01 — veneration
**SUMMARY.** The Lord is established as all-pervading conscious reality — one, endless, beginningless — then venerated as refuge of the helpless by a mind become His.

**KEY TERMS.** vyāptacarācarabhāvaviśeṣa (all-pervading particular of moving and unmoving states), cinmaya (consisting of consciousness), eka/ananta/anādi (one/endless/beginningless), bhairavanātha (Bhairava-Lord), anāthaśaraṇya (refuge of the helpless), tvanmayacitta (mind-become-Yours), vande (I venerate).

**EXPLANATION.** The object fixed in full — the all-pervading (vyāpta) particular of moving-and-unmoving states (carācarabhāvaviśeṣa), consisting of consciousness (cinmaya), one (eka), endless (ananta), beginningless (anādi). Then the act: Bhairava-Lord (bhairavanātham), refuge of the helpless (anāthaśaraṇyaṃ), venerated by mind-become-Yours (tvanmayacittatayā), in heart (hṛdi) — vande (I venerate). Veneration is not ritual offering but mental absorption — the devotee's mind becoming His. Total surrender, sized to its object.

## bh-p02 — identity
**SUMMARY.** Grace first discloses the world as His; then identity declared outright: Maheśa, always my self — and all mine through Him.

**KEY TERMS.** tvadanugrahaśakti (power of Your grace), aśeṣa (without remainder), idānīm (now), maheśa (Maheśa), sadaiva (always), mamātmā (my self), svātmamaya (self-made), samasta (all).

**EXPLANATION.** Disclosure first: all this (etad), become of You (tvanmayam), without remainder (aśeṣam), now (idānīm) appears (bhāti) to me (mama) — by the power of Your grace (tvadanugrahaśaktyā). Then declaration without limit: You (tvaṃ), Maheśa, always (sadaiva) my self (mamātmā); made of my own self (svātmamayaṃ), all (samastam) is mine (mama) through Him (tena). Appearance, then ownership — grace showing the world as His before identity claims it.

## bh-p03 — no fear-talk, suffering conceded
**SUMMARY.** Fear-talk denied while suffering conceded: no discussion of transmigration-fear, yet affliction stands granted — denial and concession in one breath.

**KEY TERMS.** svātman (own self), viśvaga (all-pervading), nātha (Lord), saṃsṛtibhīti (transmigration-fear), satsvapi (even though), durdhara (hard-to-bear), duḥkha (suffering), vimoha (delusion), trāsa (terror), karmagaṇa (karma-hosts).

**EXPLANATION.** Fear-talk denied: with You as Lord (nāthe) pervading (viśvagaye) own self (svātmani), therefore (tena) no talk of transmigration-fear exists (na saṃsṛtibhītikathā asti). Affliction conceded in the same breath: even though (satsvapi) hard-to-bear suffering, delusion, terror-causing karma-hosts. No-fear and full-suffering together — the concession framed by the denial, resolved within the verse, not deferred.

## bh-p04 — the averted gaze, power-filled
**SUMMARY.** Death's gaze turned aside by prayer; service and firm contemplation produce the power-filled state, testified with asmi — I am.

**KEY TERMS.** antaka (Ender), dṛś (gaze), krodhakarālatama (most wrathful-terrible), vinidhehi (direct!), śaṅkarasevana (Śaṅkara-service), cintanadhīra (contemplation-firm), bhīṣaṇabhairavaśaktimaya (terrifying-Bhairava-power-filled), asmi (I am).

**EXPLANATION.** A prayer first: O Ender (antaka), do not (mā) direct (vinidhehi) toward me (māṃ prati) this (enām) most wrathful-terrible (krodhakarālatamām) gaze (dṛśam). Then the ground of the prayer: firm (dhīra) in Śaṅkara-service (sevana) and contemplation (cintana), filled (maya) with terrifying, awe-full Bhairava-power (bhīṣaṇabhairavaśakti) — asmi (I am). Power first invoked against death's gaze, then declared as state. Śaṅkara here = Śiva, not Śaṅkarācārya.

## bh-p05 — darkness dispelled, fearlessness
**SUMMARY.** Darkness already burned away by awareness; four terrors then met with salutation alone, yielding absolute fearlessness.

**KEY TERMS.** ittham (thus), dīdhiti (rays), tamisra (darkness), mṛtyu (death), yama (Yama), antaka (ender), karmapiśāca (karma-ghosts), namas (salutation), na jātu bibhemi (never do I fear).

**EXPLANATION.** Thus (ittham): great darkness already dispelled (dārita bhūri tamisra) by rays (dīdhiti) of awareness (saṃvid) consisting of world-becoming (bhavanmaya). Then the catalogue — death, Yama, ender, karma-ghosts — met with salutation (namo 'stu) alone, and never (na jātu) fear (bibhemi). Awareness burned the dark before the terrors were named; salutation finishes what light began.

## bh-p06 — true awakening, bliss
**SUMMARY.** True awakening discloses things as they are; bliss is entry into the Lord's fullness — ingress, not feeling.

**KEY TERMS.** prodita (arisen), satyavibodha (true awakening), marīci (rays), satattva (true essence), bhāva (being), parāmṛta (supreme nectar), nirbhara (brimming), pūrṇa (complete), nirvṛti (bliss), emi (I enter).

**EXPLANATION.** Arisen (prodita) true (satya) awakening (vibodha): its rays (marīci) reveal (prekṣita) the true essence (satattva) of world-objects (viśvapadārtha). On that ground — in You (tvayi), being-full (bhāvaparāmṛtanirbharapūrṇa), I the self (aham ātmani) enter (emi) bliss (nirvṛtim). Awakening discloses; entry is bliss. Pūrṇa (full/complete) is the Lord's nature; entering it is bliss.

## bh-p07 — affliction arrives, nondifference arises
**SUMMARY.** Affliction's arrival is the occasion, not the obstacle: with it comes nondifference, and the hymn rains nectar.

**KEY TERMS.** mānasagocara (mind's range), yadaiva (when indeed), kleśadaśā (affliction-state), tanutāpavidhātrī (producing slight anguish), tadaiva (then alone), tvadabheda (nondifference with You), stotraparāmṛtavṛṣṭi (hymn's supreme-nectar-rain), udeti (arises).

**EXPLANATION.** When indeed (yadaiva) the affliction-state (kleśadaśā), producing slight anguish (tanutāpavidhātrī), comes (eti) into the mind's range (mānasagocaram) — then alone (tadaiva) my nondifference with You (mama tvadabhedaḥ), and the hymn's rain of supreme nectar arises (stotraparāmṛtavṛṣṭir udeti). Affliction's arrival dated as nondifference's occasion: with it, not after it, the hymn rains.

## bh-p08 — rites relativized, scripture contemplated
**SUMMARY.** Rites relativized — vow, giving, bathing, austerity dispel heat — while scripture contemplated as nectar irrigates the mind with a bliss-stream.

**KEY TERMS.** śaṅkara (Śaṅkara), satya (truly), vrata/dāna/snāna/tapas (vow/giving/bathing/austerity), bhavatāpa (becoming's heat), tāvakaśāstra (Your scripture), parāmṛtacintā (supreme-nectar-contemplation), cetas (mind), nirvṛtidhārā (bliss-stream), syandati (flows).

**EXPLANATION.** Truly (satyam), O Śaṅkara: this (idam) — vow (vrata), giving (dāna), bathing (snāna), austerity (tapas) — dispels (vidāri) becoming's heat (bhavatāpa). Rites relativized, not despised. Then the higher means: contemplation (cintā) of Your (tāvaka) scripture (śāstra) as supreme nectar (parāmṛta), in mind (cetasi), streams (syandati) a bliss-stream (nirvṛtidhārā). Heat dispelled by rites; bliss streamed by contemplation.

## bh-p09 — rejoicing consciousness, obtaining as vision
**SUMMARY.** Consciousness dances and sings; obtaining the Lord is defined as rare vision — fenced to the initiated.

**KEY TERMS.** nṛtyati/gāyati/hṛṣyati (dances/sings/rejoices), gāḍha (deeply), saṃvit (consciousness), priya (dear), āpya (having obtained), sudarśana (beautiful vision), durlabha (hard to get), samayajña (knower of the samaya).

**EXPLANATION.** This consciousness of mine (saṃvid iyaṃ mama) dances (nṛtyati), sings (gāyati), deeply (gāḍham) rejoices (hṛṣyati) — O Bhairava-Lord. Having obtained (āpya) You, the dear (priyam): the beautiful vision alone (sudarśanam ekam), hard to get by others (durlabham anyajanaiḥ) — addressed to, and fenced to, the knower of the samaya (samayajña, vocative and qualifier converging on the same fence). Joy first, then its cause named: obtaining = vision, rare by structure.

## bh-p10 — colophon and pacification
**SUMMARY.** Dated and signed, the hymn turns outward: the compassionate Lord pacifies the world's heat instantly — what began in the heart ends in the world.

**KEY TERMS.** pauṣa (month of Pauṣa), kṛṣṇadaśamī (dark tenth), abhinavagupta, stava (hymn), yena (by whom), vibhu (pervasive Lord), bhavamarusantāpa (desert-heat of becoming), śamayati (pacifies), jhaṭiti (instantly), dayālu (compassionate).

**EXPLANATION.** In the month of Pauṣa, on the dark tenth, Abhinavagupta made this hymn: dated, signed. By whom (yena) — devotee or hymn, undivided in effect — the pervasive (vibhuḥ), compassionate (dayāluḥ) Lord becomes (bhavati), pacifies (śamayati) the world's desert-heat of becoming — instantly (jhaṭiti). The dated hymn turns outward: private recognition cools the world.

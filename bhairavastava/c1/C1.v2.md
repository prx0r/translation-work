# C1 — Bhairavastava (final condensation of the arguments)

*States each passage's argumentative move and the hymn's whole arc. For the read prose see L2.v2.*

## The arc in one paragraph

The all-pervading, consciousness-made, one, endless, beginningless real — Bhairava, refuge of the helpless — met by a mind become His, venerating in the heart. Grace first discloses the world as His; then identity declared outright: Maheśa always my self, so all mine. Fear-talk denied while suffering stands conceded. Death's gaze turned aside by prayer; service and contemplation declared as power-filled state. Darkness already burned away by awareness; four terrors met with salutation alone, no fear. True awakening disclosing things as they are; bliss as entry into the Lord's fullness. Affliction's arrival as nondifference's occasion, the hymn raining nectar. Rites relativized; scripture contemplated as nectar irrigating the mind. Consciousness dancing; obtaining defined as rare vision for the initiated. Dated and signed, the hymn turns outward: the compassionate Lord pacifies the world's heat instantly. Veneration → grace-disclosure → identity → no-fear-talk → averted gaze → power-filled → darkness dispelled → fearlessness → awakening → entry → affliction-occasion → nondifference → rites relativized → contemplation → rejoicing → vision → pacification.

## bh-p01 (v. 1) — object established, surrender enacted

The object fixed in full — all-pervading particular of moving and unmoving states, consciousness-made, one, endless, beginningless — then the act: veneration by a mind become His, in the heart, toward the refuge of the helpless. Metaphysics first, devotion second: surrender only as large as the object surrendered to.

## bh-p02 (v. 2) — grace discloses, identity declares

All become of You now appears, without remainder, by Your grace's power — then, on that ground: always my self, hence all mine. Disclosure before declaration; appearance before ownership.

## bh-p03 (v. 3) — fear-talk denied, suffering conceded

No talk of transmigration-fear where the Lord pervades own self — yet hard-to-bear suffering, delusion, terror-karmas granted existence in the same breath. Denial and concession together: fear-talk ended, full affliction granted.

## bh-p04 (v. 4) — gaze averted, power declared

Turn aside this wrathful gaze, O Ender — then the prayer's ground: firm in service and contemplation, filled with awe-full Bhairava-power; asmi — I am. Prayer first, testimony as its warrant.

## bh-p05 (v. 5) — darkness dispelled, fearlessness sealed

Great darkness already burned away by world-awareness-rays; then the catalogue — death, Yama, ender, karma-ghosts — met with salutation alone. Light first, salutation last, fear nowhere.

## bh-p06 (v. 6) — awakening discloses, entry blissifies

Arisen true awakening's rays reveal things' true essence; on that ground, entry into the being-full, nectar-laden, complete Lord — and that entry is bliss. Disclosing before entering; seeing before bliss.

## bh-p07 (v. 7) — affliction as occasion

The affliction-state's arrival in mind's range, producing slight anguish — then alone, nondifference with You, and the hymn's nectar-rain arising. With affliction, not after it: means and fruit, one event.

## bh-p08 (v. 8) — rites relativized, contemplation irrigates

Vow, giving, bathing, austerity truly dispel becoming's heat — rites relativized. Then the higher means: Your scripture contemplated as supreme nectar, streaming bliss in the mind. Heat dispelled by rites; bliss streamed by contemplation.

## bh-p09 (v. 9) — rejoicing, then vision defined

Consciousness dances, sings, deeply rejoices — then its cause named: having obtained You, the dear, the beautiful vision alone, hard for others — addressed to, and fenced to, the knower of the samaya. Joy first, obtaining defined as rare sight.

## bh-p10 (v. 10) — signed, sent outward, pacifying

Pauṣa, dark tenth: Abhinavagupta made this hymn. Through whom — devotee or hymn, undivided in effect — the pervasive, compassionate Lord pacifies the world's desert-heat — instantly. What began in the heart ends in the world: the refuge of the helpless acts as refuge.

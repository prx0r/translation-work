# C1 NOTE — bh-p01.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

The Lord is established as all-pervading conscious reality — one, endless, beginningless — then venerated as refuge of the helpless by a mind become His.

## FUNCTION

Establishes Bhairava as the Absolute Consciousness (cinmaya) that is the ground of being, and veneration as mental absorption (tvanmayacitta) into that ground. Opening invocation; the nirukta of Bhairava is performed, not stated.

## KEY TERMS

* **vyāptacarācarabhāvaviśeṣa** — all-pervading particular of moving and unmoving states: the Absolute's pervasion of every state of being. * **cinmaya** — consisting of consciousness: Bhairava as Absolute Consciousness, not matter. * **eka/ananta/anādi** — one/endless/beginningless: the three negations of limitation. * **bhairavanātha** — Bhairava-Lord: the divine name reinterpreted in nondual Shaivism (Tantrāloka 1.95-100). * **anāthaśaraṇya** — refuge of the helpless: Bhairava as benefactor of the terrified (bhīru). * **tvanmayacitta** — mind-become-Yours: veneration as mental absorption, not ritual. * **vande** — I venerate: the first-person act that closes it.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Opening verse of the Bhairavastava (9 verses, composed Pauṣa Kṛṣṇa Daśamī).

## EXPLANATION

The object is fixed in full: the all-pervading (vyāpta) particular of moving-and-unmoving states (carācarabhāvaviśeṣa), consisting of consciousness (cinmaya), one (eka), endless (ananta), beginningless (anādi). Then the act: Bhairava-Lord (bhairavanātham), refuge of the helpless (anāthaśaraṇyaṃ), venerated by mind-become-Yours (tvanmayacittatayā), in heart (hṛdi) — vande (I venerate). Veneration is not ritual offering but mental absorption: the devotee's mind becoming His. Total surrender, sized to its object. The name Bhairava is here lived, not glossed: bearing/supporting the universe, the Roar (rava) of AHAM, benefactor of the terrified — all three nirukta senses are performed in the single act of absorption. This is the opening of the pratyabhijñā movement: the Absolute is named before recognition is declared (v.2).

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'carācarabhāvaviśeṣa' means particular states or the particularity of states is unresolved. Whether 'anādi' negates temporal beginning only or all limitation is unresolved. The nirukta is not stated explicitly; it is performed.

## RELATED

* Tantrāloka 1.95-100 — Abhinava's nirukta of Bhairava (bearing, Roar, benefactor). * Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya — Kṣemarāja's summary of the recognition.

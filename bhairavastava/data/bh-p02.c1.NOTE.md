# C1 NOTE — bh-p02.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Grace first discloses the world as His; then identity declared outright: Maheśa, always my self — and all mine through Him.

## FUNCTION

Establishes the two-step of recognition: grace discloses, identity declares. The world appears as His before the self claims it.

## KEY TERMS

* **tvadanugrahaśakti** — power of Your grace: the disclosing force, not merit. * **aśeṣa** — without remainder: nothing left outside the disclosure. * **idānīm** — now: the presentness of recognition. * **maheśa** — Maheśa: the Lord named as self. * **sadaiva** — always: identity without temporal condition. * **mamātmā** — my self: the declaration of non-duality. * **svātmamaya** — self-made: the world as expression of self. * **samasta** — all: the totality claimed through Him.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Second verse: disclosure, then identity. The recognition (pratyabhijñā) declared.

## EXPLANATION

Disclosure first: all this (etad), become of You (tvanmayam), without remainder (aśeṣam), now (idānīm) appears (bhāti) to me (mama) — by the power of Your grace (tvadanugrahaśaktyā). Then declaration without limit: You (tvaṃ), Maheśa, always (sadaiva) my self (mamātmā); made of my own self (svātmamayaṃ), all (samastam) is mine (mama) through Him (tena). Appearance, then ownership — grace showing the world as His before identity claims it. The two-step is the pratyabhijñā structure: first the world is seen as His (disclosure), then the self is seen as the Lord (declaration). Grace is the cause of both; merit is not.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'svātmamaya' means 'made of my self' or 'self-identical' is unresolved. Whether 'tena' (through Him) refers to grace or to Maheśa is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Bhairavastava v.1 — veneration of the all-pervading Lord. * Bhairavastava v.3 — no fear-talk, suffering conceded. * Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya — Kṣemarāja's summary of recognition.

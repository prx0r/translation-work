# C1 NOTE — bh-p03.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Fear-talk denied while suffering conceded: no discussion of transmigration-fear, yet affliction stands granted — denial and concession in one breath.

## FUNCTION

Establishes the non-dual stance toward suffering: fear-talk denied, affliction conceded, both in one breath. Neither denial of suffering nor fear of it.

## KEY TERMS

* **svātman** — own self: the ground in which the Lord pervades. * **viśvaga** — all-pervading: the Lord's pervasion of self. * **nātha** — Lord: the one who pervades. * **saṃsṛtibhīti** — transmigration-fear: the talk denied. * **satsvapi** — even though: the concession marker. * **durdhara** — hard-to-bear: the suffering's weight. * **duḥkha** — suffering: conceded, not denied. * **vimoha** — delusion: conceded. * **trāsa** — terror: conceded. * **karmagaṇa** — karma-hosts: the cause of affliction.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Third verse: the non-dual stance toward suffering. Denial and concession together.

## EXPLANATION

Fear-talk denied: with You as Lord (nāthe) pervading (viśvagaye) own self (svātmani), therefore (tena) no talk of transmigration-fear exists (na saṃsṛtibhītikathā asti). Affliction conceded in the same breath: even though (satsvapi) hard-to-bear suffering, delusion, terror-causing karma-hosts. No-fear and full-suffering together — the concession framed by the denial, resolved within it, not deferred. The non-dual recognition does not deny affliction; it denies fear of it. Suffering is real; fear of it is not, because there is no 'other' to fear — all is the Lord's pervasion.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'prāyeṇa' (mostly) softens the claim or qualifies it is unresolved. Whether 'karmagaṇa' means masses of karma or hosts of karmic beings is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Bhairavastava v.2 — identity declared. * Bhairavastava v.4 — the averted gaze, power-filled. * Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya — recognition does not deny suffering.

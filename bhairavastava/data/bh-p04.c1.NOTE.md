# C1 NOTE — bh-p04.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Death's gaze turned aside by prayer; service and firm contemplation produce the power-filled state, testified with asmi — I am.

## FUNCTION

Establishes prayer and power declared as state: the devotee does not beg power but declares it, grounded in service and contemplation.

## KEY TERMS

* **antaka** — Ender: death, addressed and turned aside. * **dṛś** — gaze: the wrathful-terrible look. * **krodhakarālatama** — most wrathful-terrible: the gaze's quality. * **vinidhehi** — direct!: the prayer's verb. * **śaṅkarasevana** — Śaṅkara-service: service to Śiva (not Śaṅkarācārya). * **cintanadhīra** — contemplation-firm: steadfastness in contemplation. * **bhīṣaṇabhairavaśaktimaya** — terrifying-Bhairava-power-filled: the state declared. * **asmi** — I am: the testimony.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Fourth verse: prayer to avert death's gaze, grounded in service and contemplation.

## EXPLANATION

A prayer first: O Ender (antaka), do not (mā) direct (vinidhehi) toward me (māṃ prati) this (enām) most wrathful-terrible (krodhakarālatamām) gaze (dṛśam). Then the ground of the prayer: firm (dhīra) in Śaṅkara-service (sevana) and contemplation (cintana), filled (maya) with terrifying, awe-full Bhairava-power (bhīṣaṇabhairavaśakti) — asmi (I am). Power first invoked against death's gaze, then declared as state. Śaṅkara here = Śiva, not Śaṅkarācārya. It moves from prayer (mā vinidhehi) to declaration (asmi): the devotee does not beg power but testifies to possessing it, grounded in service and contemplation.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'asmi' is a declaration of achieved state or a performative utterance is unresolved. Whether 'śaṅkara' could refer to Śaṅkarācārya is resolved as Śiva by context.

## RELATED

* Bhairavastava v.3 — no fear-talk, suffering conceded. * Bhairavastava v.5 — darkness dispelled, fearlessness. * Tantrāloka — Bhairava-power (bhīṣaṇa) as the Absolute's self-manifestation.

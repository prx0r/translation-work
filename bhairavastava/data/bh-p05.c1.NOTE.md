# C1 NOTE — bh-p05.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Darkness already burned away by awareness; four terrors then met with salutation alone, yielding absolute fearlessness.

## FUNCTION

Establishes fearlessness grounded in prior awareness: darkness already dispelled, so salutation suffices before death, Yama, ender, karma-ghosts.

## KEY TERMS

* **ittham** — thus: the demonstrative opening. * **dīdhiti** — rays: of awareness, burning darkness. * **tamisra** — darkness: the great dark already dispelled. * **mṛtyu** — death: first terror, met with salutation. * **yama** — Yama: second terror. * **antaka** — ender: third terror. * **karmapiśāca** — karma-ghosts: fourth terror. * **namas** — salutation: the sole response. * **na jātu bibhemi** — never do I fear: the result.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Fifth verse: from darkness dispelled to absolute fearlessness.

## EXPLANATION

Thus (ittham): great darkness already dispelled (dārita bhūri tamisra) by rays (dīdhiti) of awareness (saṃvid) consisting of world-becoming (bhavanmaya). Then the catalogue — death, Yama, ender, karma-ghosts — met with salutation (namo 'stu) alone, and never (na jātu) fear (bibhemi). Awareness burned the dark before the terrors were named; salutation finishes what light began. The order is the argument: awareness first, then terrors, then salutation, then fearlessness. The terrors are not faced with weapons but with salutation — because the dark that made them terrifying is already gone.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'bhavanmaya' (world-becoming) modifies awareness or rays is unresolved. Whether the four terrors are a complete list or exemplary is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Bhairavastava v.4 — the averted gaze, power-filled. * Bhairavastava v.6 — true awakening, bliss. * Tantrāloka — awareness (saṃvid) as the Absolute's self-manifestation.

# C1 NOTE — bh-p06.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

True awakening discloses things as they are; bliss is entry into the Lord's fullness — ingress, not feeling.

## FUNCTION

Establishes awakening as disclosure and bliss as entry: ingress into the Lord's fullness, not a feeling.

## KEY TERMS

* **prodita** — arisen: the awakening that has arisen. * **satyavibodha** — true awakening: awakening to things as they are. * **marīci** — rays: of awakening, revealing. * **satattva** — true essence: of world-objects. * **bhāva** — being: the Lord's being-fullness. * **parāmṛta** — supreme nectar: the Lord's fullness. * **nirbhara** — brimming: fullness. * **pūrṇa** — complete: the Lord's nature. * **nirvṛti** — bliss: entry into fullness. * **emi** — I enter: the devotee's ingress.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Sixth verse: awakening discloses, entry is bliss.

## EXPLANATION

Arisen (prodita) true (satya) awakening (vibodha): its rays (marīci) reveal (prekṣita) the true essence (satattva) of world-objects (viśvapadārtha). On that ground — in You (tvayi), being-full (bhāvaparāmṛtanirbharapūrṇa), I the self (aham ātmani) enter (emi) bliss (nirvṛtim). Awakening discloses; entry is bliss. Pūrṇa (full/complete) is the Lord's nature; entering it is bliss. It distinguishes disclosure (awakening reveals) from entry (the self enters fullness) — bliss is not a feeling produced but an ingress into what the Lord already is.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'prodita' (arisen) modifies awakening or rays is unresolved. Whether 'bhāvaparāmṛtanirbharapūrṇa' is one compound or several is unresolved (kept as printed).

## RELATED

* Bhairavastava v.5 — darkness dispelled, fearlessness. * Bhairavastava v.7 — affliction arrives, nondifference arises. * Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya — recognition as awakening.

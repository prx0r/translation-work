# C1 NOTE — bh-p07.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Affliction's arrival is the occasion, not the obstacle: with it comes nondifference, and nectar arises.

## FUNCTION

Establishes affliction as the occasion of nondifference: suffering triggers recognition, not blocks it.

## KEY TERMS

* **mānasagocara** — mind's range: where affliction arrives. * **yadaiva** — when indeed: the temporal marker. * **kleśadaśā** — affliction-state: the suffering that arrives. * **tanutāpavidhātrī** — producing slight anguish: the affliction's quality. * **tadaiva** — then alone: nondifference arises with it. * **tvadabheda** — nondifference with You: the recognition. * **stotraparāmṛtavṛṣṭi** — hymn's supreme-nectar-rain: the result. * **udeti** — arises: the nectar's arising.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Seventh verse: affliction as occasion for nondifference.

## EXPLANATION

When indeed (yadaiva) the affliction-state (kleśadaśā), producing slight anguish (tanutāpavidhātrī), comes (eti) into the mind's range (mānasagocaram) — then alone (tadaiva) my nondifference with You (mama tvadabhedaḥ), and the rain of supreme nectar arises (stotraparāmṛtavṛṣṭir udeti). Affliction's arrival dated as nondifference's occasion: with it, not after it, the nectar rains. It inverts the expected: suffering is not the enemy of recognition but its trigger. It (stotra) is the nectar itself — recognition produces it, not vice versa.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'tanutāpa' (slight anguish) is physical or mental is unresolved. Whether 'yadaiva' is temporal or conditional is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Bhairavastava v.6 — true awakening, bliss. * Bhairavastava v.8 — rites relativized, scripture contemplated. * Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya — recognition in the midst of suffering.

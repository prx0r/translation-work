# C1 NOTE — bh-p08.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Rites relativized — vow, giving, bathing, austerity dispel heat — while scripture contemplated as nectar irrigates the mind with a bliss-stream.

## FUNCTION

Establishes two means: rites dispel heat, contemplation streams bliss. Rites relativized, not despised.

## KEY TERMS

* **śaṅkara** — Śaṅkara: Śiva, addressed. * **satya** — truly: the assertion marker. * **vrata/dāna/snāna/tapas** — vow/giving/bathing/austerity: the rites. * **bhavatāpa** — becoming's heat: what rites dispel. * **tāvakaśāstra** — Your scripture: the higher means. * **parāmṛtacintā** — supreme-nectar-contemplation: contemplation as nectar. * **cetas** — mind: where the bliss-stream flows. * **nirvṛtidhārā** — bliss-stream: the result of contemplation. * **syandati** — flows: the verb.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Eighth verse: rites relativized, scripture contemplated.

## EXPLANATION

Truly (satyam), O Śaṅkara: this (idam) — vow (vrata), giving (dāna), bathing (snāna), austerity (tapas) — dispels (vidāri) becoming's heat (bhavatāpa). Rites relativized, not despised. Then the higher means: contemplation (cintā) of Your (tāvaka) scripture (śāstra) as supreme nectar (parāmṛta), in mind (cetasi), streams (syandati) a bliss-stream (nirvṛtidhārā). Heat dispelled by rites; bliss streamed by contemplation. The structure is the argument: first the lower means and its result, then the higher means and its result.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'vadeśakālam' (place-and-time) is obscure or means 'place-and-time under control' is unresolved; glossed neutrally, marked here. Whether the rites are listed exhaustively or exemplarily is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Bhairavastava v.7 — affliction arrives, nondifference arises. * Bhairavastava v.9 — rejoicing consciousness, obtaining as vision. * Tantrāloka — rites and contemplation as means.

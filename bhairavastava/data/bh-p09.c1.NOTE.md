# C1 NOTE — bh-p09.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Consciousness dances and sings; obtaining the Lord is defined as rare vision — fenced to the initiated.

## FUNCTION

Establishes rejoicing consciousness and obtaining as vision: joy first, then its cause named, fenced to the knower of the samaya.

## KEY TERMS

* **nṛtyati/gāyati/hṛṣyati** — dances/sings/rejoices: the consciousness's joy. * **gāḍha** — deeply: the depth of rejoicing. * **saṃvit** — consciousness: the dancing subject. * **priya** — dear: the Lord as beloved. * **āpya** — having obtained: the attainment. * **sudarśana** — beautiful vision: obtaining defined as vision. * **durlabha** — hard to get: the rarity. * **samayajña** — knower of the samaya: the fence.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Ninth verse: rejoicing consciousness, obtaining as vision.

## EXPLANATION

This consciousness of mine (saṃvid iyaṃ mama) dances (nṛtyati), sings (gāyati), deeply (gāḍham) rejoices (hṛṣyati) — O Bhairava-Lord. Having obtained (āpya) You, the dear (priyam): the beautiful vision alone (sudarśanam ekam), hard to get by others (durlabham anyajanaiḥ) — addressed to, and fenced to, the knower of the samaya (samayajña, vocative and qualifier converging on the same fence). Joy first, then its cause named: obtaining = vision, rare by structure. It moves from spontaneous joy to the definition of obtaining as vision, restricted to those who know the samaya.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'samayajña' is vocative or qualifier converging on the same fence is unresolved. Whether 'sudarśanam ekam' means 'the one beautiful vision' or 'beautiful vision alone' is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Bhairavastava v.8 — rites relativized, scripture contemplated. * Bhairavastava v.10 — colophon and pacification. * Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya — recognition as vision.

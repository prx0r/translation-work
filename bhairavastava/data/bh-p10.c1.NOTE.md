# C1 NOTE — bh-p10.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Dated and signed, it turns outward: the compassionate Lord pacifies the world's heat instantly — what began in the heart ends in the world.

## FUNCTION

Establishes the colophon and pacification: dated composition, signed author, then outward turn — private recognition cools the world.

## KEY TERMS

* **pauṣa** — month of Pauṣa: the date. * **kṛṣṇadaśamī** — dark tenth: the lunar day. * **abhinavagupta** — Abhinavagupta: the author, signing. * **stava** — hymn: the composition named. * **yena** — by whom: devotee or hymn, undivided in effect. * **vibhu** — pervasive Lord: the one who pacifies. * **bhavamarusantāpa** — desert-heat of becoming: the world's suffering. * **śamayati** — pacifies: the Lord's act. * **jhaṭiti** — instantly: the speed. * **dayālu** — compassionate: the Lord's quality.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Tenth verse: colophon and pacification.

## EXPLANATION

In the month of Pauṣa, on the dark tenth, Abhinavagupta made this hymn: dated, signed. By whom (yena) — devotee or hymn, undivided in effect — the pervasive (vibhuḥ), compassionate (dayāluḥ) Lord becomes (bhavati), pacifies (śamayati) the world's desert-heat of becoming — instantly (jhaṭiti). The dated hymn turns outward: private recognition cools the world. It moves from colophon (date, author, work) to pacification (the Lord's act) to speed (instantly) — beginning in inward dissolution (v.1), ending in world-pacification.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'yena' refers to devotee or hymn is unresolved. Whether 'bhavamarusantāpa' is physical or existential heat is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Bhairavastava v.1 — veneration of the all-pervading Lord. * Bhairavastava v.9 — rejoicing consciousness, obtaining as vision. * Tantrāloka — the Absolute's compassion (dayā).

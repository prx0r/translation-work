## Scholarly facts

1. Bhairavastava = 10 verses (padyanavaka) by Abhinavagupta, early devotional stotra.
2. Composed in Pauṣa, Kṛṣṇa Daśamī (per colophon v.10).
3. Abhinavagupta entered the Bhairava Cave chanting this hymn (c. 1020 AD, per anuttaratrikakula.org).
4. Themes: all-pervading conscious reality (v.1), grace disclosing identity (v.2), no fear-talk/suffering conceded (v.3), averted gaze/power-filled (v.4), darkness dispelled/fearlessness (v.5), true awakening/bliss (v.6), affliction as occasion for nondifference (v.7), rites relativized/scripture contemplated (v.8), rejoicing consciousness/obtaining as vision (v.9), colophon/pacification (v.10).
5. Key terms: cinmaya (consciousness-made), anāthaśaraṇya (refuge of helpless), tvadanugrahaśakti (power of grace), svātantrya (autonomy), bhīṣaṇabhairavaśakti (terrifying Bhairava-power), nirvṛti (bliss), samayajña (knower of samaya).
6. Bhairava nirukta (Tantrāloka 1.95-100): bearing/supporting the universe, the Roar (rava) of AHAM, benefactor of the terrified (bhīru).

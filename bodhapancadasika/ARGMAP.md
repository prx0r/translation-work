# Bodhapañcadaśikā — ARGMAP (work index)

*Abhinavagupta, 15 verses + colophon couplet. Source: GRETIL (Marino Faliero input, July 1998), cross-checked against the in-repo SOURCE.txt. T1 frozen full-verse; L0 149/149 PARSED.*

## Lore (one search, then the Sanskrit decided)

- **Edition/commentary:** Harabhaṭṭaśāstrī's vivaraṇa, Kashmir Series of Texts and Studies vol. 76, ed. Jagaddhar Zadoo (with Jagaddharājāḍū Śāstrī), Srinagar 1947; e-text Muktabodha M00173 (facsimile of KSTS 76). The one classical commentary; Kṣemarāja wrote none on this text.
- **Colophon audience:** the closing couplet (outside the 15) says it plainly — *sukumāramatīn śiṣyān prabodhayitum añjasā*, fifteen verses to wake tender-minded pupils at once. A teaching poem, not a treatise.
- **Modern translation check:** Mahānaya.org word-by-word English (GRETIL text as base) — used after drafting to test my key readings (anastamita, atidurghaṭakāritvam-equation, aparijñāna = svātantrya, yasmāt kasmāt, parijñāna = jīvanmukti). No departures found; where Mahānaya paraphrases I kept the harder literal (e.g. yugapad, svātmagāḥ).
- **Scholarship:** Timalsina (cosmology/epistemology/soteriology frame for the early stotras); Ferrario on śaktipāta/grace — verse 12's prasāda doorway.
- **Textual notes:** T1 follows GRETIL throughout; two SOURCE.txt simplifications noted and not followed — v7 *aisvaryaṃ* for T1's *aiśvaryaṃ*, v15 *sarvabhavānāṃ* for T1's *sarvabhāvānāṃ* (see argmap bod-p15 §3).

## The arc (what the 15 verses do)

One containing light (1) → own-nature within, Lordship-shaped power without (2) → power declines difference, fire-burning seal (3) → world-bearing by mirroring (4) → the goddess eager for His-nature-grasp, exact fullness (5) → the pair at play, simultaneous creation-dissolution (6) → hard-to-accomplish = unsurpassed = autonomy = lordship = awareness (7) → inert is limited light, awareness unmeasured (8) → creations-dissolutions stand as the autonomous one's own nature (9) → total variety inventoried (10) → non-knowledge of autonomy = terrifying saṃsāra for fools (11) → three doors, arrival however (12) → full knowledge = liberation = living freedom, remembered (13) → bondage and liberation non-different in the Lord (14) → trident-lotus emblem, contemplate Bhairava the own-nature of all (15).

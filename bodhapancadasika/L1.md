# Bodhapañcadaśikā — L1 controlled

## bod-p01 (1)
Un-set-sun-light-form of powers and darknesses too — who is one within, whose end are powers and darknesses. // AgBpd_1

## bod-p02 (2)
He alone is own-nature of all beings, supreme Lord; host of things is of him alone power, Lordship-made. // AgBpd_2

## bod-p03 (3)
Power too from power-possessor-form difference does not desire; identity of these two is eternal, as fire and burning. // AgBpd_3

## bod-p04 (4)
He alone is Bhairava god, world-bearing-characterized; in own-self-vision whole is reflected by power. // AgBpd_4

## bod-p05 (5)
Of him alone is supreme goddess, own-nature-grasping-eager; fullness in all beings, of whom neither little nor excess. // AgBpd_5

## bod-p06 (6)
This god by this goddess always play-joy-eager; varied creation-dissolution he does simultaneously, all-pervading. // AgBpd_6

## bod-p07 (7)
Hard-to-accomplish of it is unsurpassed indeed; that itself is autonomy, lordship, awareness-form. // AgBpd_7

## bod-p08 (8)
Limited-light-ness of inert indeed is mark; from inert different is awareness since not measured. // AgBpd_8

## bod-p09 (9)
Thus of it autonomous by own-power-distinction — own-self creation-dissolution as nature stand. // AgBpd_9

## bod-p10 (10)
In them variety extreme up-down across what; worlds, their parts, pleasure-pain-mind what. // AgBpd_10

## bod-p11 (11)
What of it non-knowledge — that its autonomy thus stated; it indeed saṃsāra of fools who is terrifying. // AgBpd_11

## bod-p12 (12)
From that grace-sap or from teacher-scripture or from scripture of supreme Lord from which from which approached. // AgBpd_12

## bod-p13 (13)
What of reality full-knowledge — that is liberation from supreme Lord. That fullness of awakened, living-liberation that is remembered. // AgBpd_13

## bod-p14 (14)
These two bondage and liberation from supreme-Lord-nature. Not they differ; no difference indeed in truth in supreme Lord. // AgBpd_14

## bod-p15 (15)
Thus will-power art knowledge power trident lotus resorted — Bhairava of all things own-nature is examined. // AgBpd_15

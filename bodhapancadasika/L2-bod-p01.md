# Bodhapancadasika bod-p01 (the unsetting light) — L2 READ

## v.1
Of unsetting sun-light is His form — of bright powers and of darknesses too. For He is one, within, and powers and darknesses end in Him.
The Sanskrit: *anastamitabhārūpas tejasāṃ tamasām api | ya eko 'ntar yadantaś ca tejāṃsi ca tamāṃsi ca*

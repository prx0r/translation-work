# Bodhapancadasika bod-p02 (own-nature of all beings) — L2 READ

## v.2
He alone is the own-nature of all beings, the supreme Lord. And the whole host of things is His alone — power, shaped as Lordship.
The Sanskrit: *sa eva sarvabhūtānāṃ svabhāvaḥ parameśvaraḥ | bhāvajātaṃ hi tasyaiva śaktir īśvaratāmayī*

# Bodhapancadasika bod-p04 (Bhairava world-bearing) — L2 READ

## v.4
He alone is Bhairava, the god whose very mark is bearing the world. In the mirror of His own self-seeing, the whole stands reflected — by power.
The Sanskrit: *sa eva bhairavo devo jagadbharaṇalakṣaṇaḥ | svātmādṛṣe samagraṃ hi yacchaktyā pratibimbitam*

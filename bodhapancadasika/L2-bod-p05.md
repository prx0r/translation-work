# Bodhapancadasika bod-p05 (the grasp-eager goddess) — L2 READ

## v.5
His alone is the supreme goddess, hungry only to grasp His own nature. She is fullness in all beings — in whom there is neither too little nor too much.
The Sanskrit: *tasyaivaiṣā parā devī svarūpāmarśanotsukā | pūrṇatvaṃ sarvabhaveṣu yasyā nālpaṃ na cādhikam*

# Bodhapancadasika bod-p06 (play-eager, all at once) — L2 READ

## v.6
This god, with this goddess, is forever eager for the joy of play. Varied makings and unmakings He works at once — everywhere at once.
The Sanskrit: *eṣa devo 'nayā devyā nityaṃ krīḍārasotsukaḥ | vicitrān sṛṣṭisaṃhārān vidhatte yugapad vibhuḥ*

# Bodhapancadasika bod-p09 (change that never departs) — L2 READ

## v.9
So it is with the free one, distinguishing by His own power: makings and unmakings rest in His own self, standing as His very nature.
The Sanskrit: *evam asya svatantrasya nijaśaktyupabhedinaḥ | svātmagāḥ sṛṣṭisaṃhārāḥ svarūpatvena saṃsthitāḥ*

# Bodhapancadasika bod-p10 (all extremes, all directions) — L2 READ

## v.10
In them, variety runs to the extreme — upward, downward, across. Worlds and their fragments, pleasure and pain and mind.
The Sanskrit: *teṣu vaicitryam atyantam ūrdhvādhas tiryag eva yat | bhuvanāni tadaṃśāś ca sukhaduḥkhamatiś ca yā*

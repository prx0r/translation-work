# Bodhapancadasika bod-p12 (three doors, one arrival) — L2 READ

## v.12
From the sap of His grace, or from the teacher's handed word, or from the Lord's own scripture — from whichever, it is reached.
The Sanskrit: *tatprasādarasād eva gurvāgamata eva vā | śāstrād vā parameśasya yasmāt kasmād upāgatam*

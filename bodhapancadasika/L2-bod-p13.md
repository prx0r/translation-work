# Bodhapancadasika bod-p13 (knowing is release) — L2 READ

## v.13
To know reality through and through — that is release, from the supreme Lord. That is the fullness of the awakened: freedom while living, as the remembered teaching holds.
The Sanskrit: *yat tattvasya parijñānaṃ sa mokṣaḥ parameśataḥ | tat pūrṇatvaṃ prabuddhānāṃ jīvanmuktiś ca sā smṛtā*

# Bodhapancadasika bod-p14 (no split, twice said) — L2 READ

## v.14
Bondage and release both come of the Lord's own nature. The two do not split apart; in truth, in the supreme Lord, there is no split.
The Sanskrit: *etau bandhavimokṣau ca parameśasvarūpataḥ | na bhidyete na bhedo hi tattvataḥ parameśvare*

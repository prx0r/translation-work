# Bodhapancadasika bod-p15 (emblem then dwelling) — L2 READ

## v.15
So it is: resting on the lotus of the trident — will, art, knowledge, power — Bhairava, the own-nature of all things, is to be dwelt upon.
The Sanskrit: *ittham icchākalājñānaśaktiśūlāmbujāśritaḥ | bhairavaḥ sarvabhāvānāṃ svabhāvaḥ pariśīlyate*

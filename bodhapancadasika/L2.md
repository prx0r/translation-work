# Bodhapañcadaśikā — L2 READ

## bod-p01 (1)

Of unsetting sun-light is His form — of bright powers and of darknesses too. For He is one, within, and powers and darknesses end in Him.

## bod-p02 (2)

He alone is the own-nature of all beings, the supreme Lord. And the whole host of things is His alone — power, shaped as Lordship.

## bod-p03 (3)

Power wants no apartness from the power-bearer. The two are eternally one — as fire is one with its burning.

## bod-p04 (4)

He alone is Bhairava, the god whose very mark is bearing the world. In the mirror of His own self-seeing, the whole stands reflected — by power.

## bod-p05 (5)

His alone is the supreme goddess, hungry only to grasp His own nature. She is fullness in all beings — in whom there is neither too little nor too much.

## bod-p06 (6)

This god, with this goddess, is forever eager for the joy of play. Varied makings and unmakings He works at once — everywhere at once.

## bod-p07 (7)

What is hardest to do is His, and it is unsurpassed. That itself is His freedom, His lordship, His awareness-form.

## bod-p08 (8)

To shine only in part — that is the mark of the inert. Awareness is otherwise than the inert, for no measure contains it.

## bod-p09 (9)

So it is with the free one, distinguishing by His own power: makings and unmakings rest in His own self, standing as His very nature.

## bod-p10 (10)

In them, variety runs to the extreme — upward, downward, across. Worlds and their fragments, pleasure and pain and mind.

## bod-p11 (11)

Not to know this is called His freedom. And that is saṃsāra itself — a terror to the deluded.

## bod-p12 (12)

From the sap of His grace, or from the teacher's handed word, or from the Lord's own scripture — from whichever, it is reached.

## bod-p13 (13)

To know reality through and through — that is release, from the supreme Lord. That is the fullness of the awakened: freedom while living, as the remembered teaching holds.

## bod-p14 (14)

Bondage and release both come of the Lord's own nature. The two do not split apart; in truth, in the supreme Lord, there is no split.

## bod-p15 (15)

So it is: resting on the lotus of the trident — will, art, knowledge, power — Bhairava, the own-nature of all things, is to be dwelt upon.

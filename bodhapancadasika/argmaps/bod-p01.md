# Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p01 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 1)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 bod-p01 = verse 1. Lore: the opening epithet anastamita (never-setting) light — the sun that cannot set because nothing is outside it to set into; powers (tejas) and darknesses (tamas) both "ended" in the one within.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 1 of the Bodhapañcadaśikā (Abhinavagupta, 15 verses). Gloss: un-set-sun-light-form (anastamitabhārūpas) of-powers (tejasām) of-darknesses (tamasām) too (api) — who (yaḥ) one (ekaḥ) within (antar) what-end (yadantaś ca) powers (tejāṃsi ca) darknesses (tamāṃsi ca).

Sanskrit: anastamitabhārūpas tejasāṃ tamasām api | ya eko 'ntar yadantaś ca tejāṃsi ca tamāṃsi ca

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 1:**
> anastamitabhārūpas tejasāṃ tamasām api | ya eko 'ntar yadantaś ca tejāṃsi ca tamāṃsi ca

[i.e. of un-set sun-light-form, of powers and of darknesses too — who is one within, whose end are powers and darknesses.]

The form first: un-set (anastamita) sun-light-form (bhārūpa) — a light that never sets, because setting needs an outside to sink into and there is none. Of powers (tejasām) and of darknesses (tamasām) too (api): both light and dark belong to it. Then the one who: who (yaḥ), one (ekaḥ), within (antar) — whose end (yadantaḥ) powers and darknesses are. End (anta) here reads both ways: their limit and their dwelling — they terminate in Him, they abide in Him. The verse establishes the all-containing One before anything is said about bondage, practice, or doctrine.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Yadantaḥ (whose end) is read as both limit and abode of powers/darknesses; the verse lets both stand.
2. Api (too) joins darknesses to the light-form's domain — darkness is included, not opposed.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry form-then-inhabitant: never-setting light-form containing powers and darknesses, one within whom they end. L2 must keep "never sets" as the verse's whole point. Carry the containing One into verse 2, where He is named own-nature of all beings.

# Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p02 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 2)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 bod-p02 = verse 2. Lore: the svabhāva equation — He alone (sa eva) is all beings' own-nature, and the entire host of things is His power in Lordship-form (īśvaratāmayī); nothing is second to the Lord, including the world.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 2 of the Bodhapañcadaśikā (Abhinavagupta, 15 verses). Gloss: He (saḥ eva) of-all-beings (sarvabhūtānām) own-nature (svabhāvaḥ) supreme-Lord (parameśvaraḥ); host-of-things (bhāvajātam hi) of-him-alone (tasyaiva) power (śaktiḥ) Lordship-made (īśvaratāmayī).

Sanskrit: sa eva sarvabhūtānāṃ svabhāvaḥ parameśvaraḥ | bhāvajātaṃ hi tasyaiva śaktir īśvaratāmayī

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 2:**
> sa eva sarvabhūtānāṃ svabhāvaḥ parameśvaraḥ | bhāvajātaṃ hi tasyaiva śaktir īśvaratāmayī

[i.e. He alone is all beings' own-nature, the supreme Lord; the host of things is of Him alone — power, Lordship-made.]

He (saḥ) alone (eva): the own-nature (svabhāvaḥ) of all beings (sarvabhūtānām) is the supreme Lord (parameśvaraḥ) — not their ruler added on, but what they are. Then the consequence for the world: the host of things (bhāvajātam), indeed (hi), is of Him alone (tasyaiva) — power (śaktiḥ), Lordship-made (īśvaratāmayī). The many are not other than Him; they are His power in the shape of Lordship. Own-nature within, power without — one subject, two faces.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Īśvaratāmayī (Lordship-made) is read as the form power takes — power shaped as sovereignty, not a second entity beside it.
2. The verse states identity, not process: no becoming is described, only what is.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry identity-then-consequence: He alone is beings' own-nature; therefore all things are His Lordship-shaped power. L2 must keep eva (alone) in both halves — the aloneness is the argument. Carry the power-possessor into verse 3, where power declines difference.

# Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p03 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 3)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 bod-p03 = verse 3. Lore: the fire-burning simile (vahnidāhikayor iva) — the stock nonduality image: burning is not other than fire, yet fire is not exhausted by burning; śakti stands to Śiva the same way.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 3 of the Bodhapañcadaśikā (Abhinavagupta, 15 verses). Gloss: Power (śaktiś ca) from-power-possessor-form (śaktimadrūpāt) difference (vyatirekam) does-not-desire (na vāñchati); identity (tādātmyam) of-these-two (anayor) eternal (nityaṃ) — as (iva) fire-burning (vahnidāhikayor).

Sanskrit: śaktiś ca śaktimadrūpād vyatirekaṃ na vāñchati | tādātmyam anayor nityaṃ vahnidāhikayor iva

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 3:**
> śaktiś ca śaktimadrūpād vyatirekam na vāñchati | tādātmyam anayor nityaṃ vahnidāhikayor iva

[i.e. and power desires no difference from the power-possessor's form; identity of these two is eternal — as of fire and burning.]

Power (śaktiś ca) desires (na vāñchati) no difference (vyatirekam) from the power-possessor's form (śaktimadrūpāt) — Śakti does not want apartness; difference is not even wished for. Their identity (tādātmyam anayoḥ) is eternal (nityam) — as (iva) of fire and burning (vahnidāhikayoḥ): burning is not other than fire, nor fire without burning, yet neither collapses into a blur. The simile does the ontology: distinction-without-separation, eternally.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Na vāñchati (does not desire) personifies power's non-separation as will, not mechanism — kept as stated.
2. The fire-burning pair is the argument's only ground; no further proof is offered or needed.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry no-desire-for-difference then eternal identity, sealed by fire-burning. L2 must keep iva (as) — the simile carries the metaphysics. Carry the identical pair into verse 4, where Bhairava bears the world and mirrors it whole.

# Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p05 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 5)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 bod-p05 = verse 5. Lore: parā devī, eager for own-nature-grasping (svarūpāmarśanotsukā) — Śakti's desire is reflexive, aimed at His nature, not at objects; her fullness (pūrṇatva) fills all beings with neither lack nor excess.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 5 of the Bodhapañcadaśikā (Abhinavagupta, 15 verses). Gloss: Of-him-alone (tasyaivaiṣā) supreme (parā) goddess (devī) own-nature-grasping-eager (svarūpāmarśanotsukā); fullness (pūrṇatvam) in-all-beings (sarvabhaveṣu) of-whom (yasyāḥ) neither-little (nālpaṃ) nor (na) excess (adhikam).

Sanskrit: tasyaivaiṣā parā devī svarūpāmarśanotsukā | pūrṇatvaṃ sarvabhaveṣu yasyā nālpaṃ na cādhikam

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 5:**
> tasyaivaiṣā parā devī svarūpāmarśanotsukā | pūrṇatvaṃ sarvabhaveṣu yasyā nālpaṃ na cādhikam

[i.e. of Him alone is this supreme goddess, eager for own-nature-grasping; fullness in all beings — of whom neither little nor excess.]

Of Him alone (tasyaiva) is this (eṣā) supreme (parā) goddess (devī) — hers by belonging, not by delegation. She is eager (utsukā) for own-nature-grasping (svarūpāmarśana): Śakti's desire terminates in His nature, not in products. Hers is fullness (pūrṇatvam) in all beings (sarvabhaveṣu) — of whom (yasyāḥ) neither little (nālpaṃ) nor (na) excess (adhikam): fullness exactly filling, neither short nor spilling over. Measure without measure.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Utsukā (eager) gives Śakti appetite, but only for own-nature-grasping — the verse bounds desire as tightly as it grants it.
2. "Neither little nor excess" is read as exact fullness, not as approximation.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry belonging-then-eagerness-then-fullness: His alone, eager for His-nature-grasping, filling all beings exactly. L2 must keep "neither little nor excess" whole — it is the verse's definition of pūrṇatva. Carry the goddess into verse 6, where god and goddess play.

# Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p06 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 6)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 bod-p06 = verse 6. Lore: krīḍārasotsuka (play-joy-eager) with yugapad (simultaneously) — creation and dissolution at once, not in sequence; play (krīḍā) as the motive that replaces purpose.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 6 of the Bodhapañcadaśikā (Abhinavagupta, 15 verses). Gloss: This (eṣa) god (devaḥ) by-this (anayā) goddess (devyā) always (nityaṃ) play-joy-eager (krīḍārasotsukaḥ); varied (vicitrān) creation-dissolution (sṛṣṭisaṃhārān) does (vidhatte) simultaneously (yugapad) all-pervading (vibhuḥ).

Sanskrit: eṣa devo 'nayā devyā nityaṃ krīḍārasotsukaḥ | vicitrān sṛṣṭisaṃhārān vidhatte yugapad vibhuḥ

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 6:**
> eṣa devo 'nayā devyā nityaṃ krīḍārasotsukaḥ | vicitrān sṛṣṭisaṃhārān vidhatte yugapad vibhuḥ

[i.e. this god, by this goddess, always play-joy-eager; varied creation-dissolutions he does simultaneously, all-pervading.]

This god (eṣa devaḥ) by this goddess (anayā devyā) — always (nityam) play-joy-eager (krīḍārasotsukaḥ): the pair's permanent mood is play-joy, not purpose. What he does (vidhatte): varied (vicitrān) creations-dissolutions (sṛṣṭisaṃhārān) — simultaneously (yugapad), being all-pervading (vibhuḥ). No alternating ages of making and unmaking; both at once, everywhere, for joy. Krīḍā replaces teleology: the world-process has a mood, not a goal.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Yugapad (simultaneously) governs both verbs' sense — creation and dissolution co-occur rather than alternate; read as stated, not softened.
2. Vibhuḥ (all-pervading) closes the verse: simultaneity needs ubiquity.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry mood-then-act: always play-joy-eager, therefore varied creation-dissolution at once, everywhere. L2 must keep "simultaneously" load-bearing — it is the verse's whole cosmology. Carry play into verse 7, where the hard-to-accomplish is named unsurpassed.

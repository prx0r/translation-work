# Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p07 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 7)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 bod-p07 = verse 7. Lore: the verse's equation-chain — atidurghaṭakāritvam = anuttaram = svatantratvam = aiśvaryam = bodharūpatā; the hard-to-accomplish, the unsurpassed, autonomy, lordship, awareness-form are one thing said five ways.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 7 of the Bodhapañcadaśikā (Abhinavagupta, 15 verses). Gloss: Hard-to-accomplish (atidurghaṭakāritvam) of-it (asya) unsurpassed (anuttaram) indeed (eva) what (yat); that-itself (etad eva) is autonomy (svatantratvam) lordship (aiśvaryam) awareness-form (bodharūpatā).

Sanskrit: atidurghaṭakāritvam asyānuttaram eva yat | etad eva svatantratvam aiśvaryaṃ bodharūpatā

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 7:**
> atidurghaṭakāritvam asyānuttaram eva yat | etad eva svatantratvam aiśvaryaṃ bodharūpatā

[i.e. hard-to-accomplish of it, unsurpassed indeed, which — that itself is autonomy, lordship, awareness-form.]

What (yat) is hard-to-accomplish (atidurghaṭakāritvam) of it (asya) — unsurpassed (anuttaram) indeed (eva): that itself (etad eva) is autonomy (svatantratvam), lordship (aiśvaryam), awareness-form (bodharūpatā). Five terms, one referent: the hard-to-do = the unsurpassed = autonomy = lordship = awareness-form. Difficulty here is not obstacle but rank — what nothing else can do marks what is highest, freest, most aware. The equation needs no verb beyond "is."

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Atidurghaṭakāritva (hard-to-accomplish-making) is read as rank (nothing else can), not as task-difficulty; the equation with anuttara requires it.
2. No agent is named; the verse defines by equation alone.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry the equation whole: hard-to-accomplish = unsurpassed = autonomy = lordship = awareness-form. L2 must keep all five terms equated, none subordinated. Carry autonomy into verse 8, where the inert is defined against it.

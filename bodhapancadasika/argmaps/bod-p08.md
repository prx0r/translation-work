# Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p08 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 8)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 bod-p08 = verse 8. Lore: paricchinnaprakāśatva (limited-light-ness) as the very mark of the inert — limitation belongs to light's mode, not to light; awareness (bodha) differs precisely by being unmeasured (na parimīyate).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 8 of the Bodhapañcadaśikā (Abhinavagupta, 15 verses). Gloss: Limited-light-ness (paricchinnaprakāśatvaṃ) of-inert (jaḍasya) indeed (kila) mark (lakṣaṇam); from-inert (jaḍād) different (vilakṣaṇo) awareness (bodho) since (yato) not (na) measured (parimīyate).

Sanskrit: paricchinnaprakāśatvaṃ jaḍasya kila lakṣaṇam | jaḍād vilakṣaṇo bodho yato na parimīyate

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 8:**
> paricchinnaprakāśatvaṃ jaḍasya kila lakṣaṇam | jaḍād vilakṣaṇo bodho yato na parimīyate

[i.e. limited-lightness is indeed the inert's mark; different from the inert is awareness, since it is not measured.]

Limited-lightness (paricchinnaprakāśatvaṃ) is, indeed (kila), the mark (lakṣaṇam) of the inert (jaḍasya): to be inert just is to shine limitedly. Different (vilakṣaṇaḥ) from the inert (jaḍāt) is awareness (bodhaḥ) — since (yatas) it is not measured (na parimīyate). Immeasurability is the differentia: what cannot be measured cannot be bounded, and what cannot be bounded is not inert. Kila (indeed/as is known) marks this as agreed ground, not news.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Na parimīyate (is not measured) is read actively — awareness evades measure — rather than as a mere lack.
2. Kila (indeed) is taken as marking shared ground; no irony is imported.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry mark-then-difference: limited light defines inert; unmeasured awareness differs from it. L2 must keep "since not measured" as the stated reason — the verse argues by criterion, not by praise. Carry the unmeasured into verse 9, where creation-dissolution stand as the autonomous one's own nature.

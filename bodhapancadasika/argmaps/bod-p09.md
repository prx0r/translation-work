# Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p09 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 9)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 bod-p09 = verse 9. Lore: svātmagāḥ — gone to / reaching own-self — creation and dissolution do not depart anywhere; nijaśaktyupabheda (own-power-distinction) makes distinction itself internal to autonomy.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 9 of the Bodhapañcadaśikā (Abhinavagupta, 15 verses). Gloss: Thus (evam) of-it (asya) autonomous (svatantrasya) by-own-power-distinction (nijaśaktyupabhedinaḥ) — own-self (svātmagāḥ) creation-dissolution (sṛṣṭisaṃhārāḥ) as-nature (svarūpatvena) stand (saṃsthitāḥ).

Sanskrit: evam asya svatantrasya nijaśaktyupabhedinaḥ | svātmagāḥ sṛṣṭisaṃhārāḥ svarūpatvena saṃsthitāḥ

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 9:**
> evam asya svatantrasya nijaśaktyupabhedinaḥ | svātmagāḥ sṛṣṭisaṃhārāḥ svarūpatvena saṃsthitāḥ

[i.e. thus, of it autonomous, by own-power-distinction — gone-to-own-self, creations-dissolutions stand as nature.]

Thus (evam): of it (asya), the autonomous (svatantrasya) one — by own-power-distinction (nijaśaktyupabhedinaḥ): distinction is made by own power, inside autonomy, needing nothing outside. Gone-to-own-self (svātmagāḥ): creations-dissolutions (sṛṣṭisaṃhārāḥ) — they arrive nowhere but the self. As nature (svarūpatvena) they stand (saṃsthitāḥ): stood, standing, stable — creation-dissolution are the autonomous one's own nature, not His products. Distinction-within-autonomy, movement-within-rest.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Svātmagāḥ (gone to own self) is read as arrival-without-departure; no journey is implied.
2. Saṃsthitāḥ (stand) is durative — stood and standing — not a completed event.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry thus-therefore: autonomous by own-power-distinction, creations-dissolutions stand as own nature, gone to own self. L2 must keep "as nature," not "by nature" — they are stood, not made. Carry self-abiding change into verse 10, where variety spreads up-down-across.

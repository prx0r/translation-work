# Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p10 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 10)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 bod-p10 = verse 10. Lore: the three-direction catalogue — up-down-across (ūrdhvādhas tiryak) — variety mapped spatially; worlds, their parts, and pleasure-pain-mind exhaust the field in one sweep.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 10 of the Bodhapañcadaśikā (Abhinavagupta, 15 verses). Gloss: In-them (teṣu) variety (vaicitryam) extreme (atyantam) up-down (ūrdhvādhas) across (tiryag eva) what (yat); worlds (bhuvanāni) their-parts (tadaṃśāś ca) pleasure-pain-mind (sukhaduḥkhamatiś ca) what (yā).

Sanskrit: teṣu vaicitryam atyantam ūrdhvādhas tiryag eva yat | bhuvanāni tadaṃśāś ca sukhaduḥkhamatiś ca yā

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 10:**
> teṣu vaicitryam atyantam ūrdhvādhas tiryag eva yat | bhuvanāni tadaṃśāś ca sukhaduḥkhamatiś ca yā

[i.e. in them, whatever extreme variety — up, down, across; and whatever worlds, their parts, and pleasure-pain-mind.]

In them (teṣu — the creations-dissolutions of verse 9): whatever (yat) variety (vaicitryam), extreme (atyantam) — up-down (ūrdhvādhas), across (tiryag eva): the three directions cover every spread. And (ca) whatever (yā): worlds (bhuvanāni), their parts (tadaṃśāḥ), and pleasure-pain-mind (sukhaduḥkhamatiḥ). Outer cosmos (worlds, parts) and inner cosmos (pleasure, pain, mind) inventoried together — variety totalized before the next verse names its cause: non-knowledge.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Teṣu (in them) refers back to the creations-dissolutions of verse 9; the verse inventories without explaining — explanation belongs to verse 11.
2. Sukhaduḥkhamatiḥ (pleasure-pain-mind) joins inner states to outer worlds in one list; no inner/outer split is made.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry the inventory whole: three directions, outer worlds-and-parts, inner pleasure-pain-mind. L2 must keep the catalogue uncompressed — its completeness is the point. Carry total variety into verse 11, where its cause is named: non-knowledge of autonomy.

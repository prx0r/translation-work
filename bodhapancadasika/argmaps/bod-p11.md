# Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p11 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 11)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 bod-p11 = verse 11. Lore: the verse's equation — non-knowledge of autonomy = autonomy itself, described = terrifying saṃsāra for the deluded; ignorance is not His lack but His freedom appearing as bondage.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 11 of the Bodhapañcadaśikā (Abhinavagupta, 15 verses). Gloss: What (yad) of-it (etasya) non-knowledge (aparijñānaṃ) — that (tat) its-autonomy (svātantryaṃ hi) thus (varṇitam); it (saḥ eva) indeed (khalu) saṃsāra (saṃsāro) of-fools (mūḍānām) who (yaḥ) terrifying (vibhīṣakaḥ).

Sanskrit: yad etasyāparijñānaṃ tatsvātantryaṃ hi varṇitam | sa eva khalu saṃsāro mūḍānāṃ yo vibhīṣakaḥ

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 11:**
> yad etasyāparijñānaṃ tatsvātantryaṃ hi varṇitam | sa eva khalu saṃsāro mūḍānāṃ yo vibhīṣakaḥ

[i.e. what non-knowledge of it — that is described indeed as its autonomy; it indeed is saṃsāra, of fools, which is terrifying.]

What (yad) non-knowledge (aparijñānaṃ) of it (etasya): that (tat), indeed (hi), is described (varṇitam) as its autonomy (svātantryam) — non-knowing is not His failure but His freedom, in description. It (saḥ) itself (eva), indeed (khalu): saṃsāra (saṃsāraḥ) — of fools (mūḍānām) — which (yaḥ) is terrifying (vibhīṣakaḥ). The same autonomy, undescribed-known, is terrifying bondage for the deluded. Saṃsāra is not elsewhere: it is autonomy mis-known.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Hi (indeed/for) is read as emphasis on the equation, not as giving a reason; the verse equates more than it explains.
2. Vibhīṣaka (terrifying) qualifies saṃsāra-for-fools only; nothing terrifying is predicated of autonomy itself.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry equation-then-identification: non-knowledge = autonomy described; that itself = terrifying saṃsāra for fools. L2 must keep the identity strict — saṃsāra is autonomy mis-known, not a second thing. Carry bondage-identified into verse 12, where the way out is given threefold.

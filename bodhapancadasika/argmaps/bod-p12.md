# Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p12 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 12)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 bod-p12 = verse 12. Lore: the threefold approach — grace-sap, teacher-scripture, Lord's scripture ("from whatever," yasmāt kasmāt) — with Ferrario's śaktipāta study (grace in degrees) as the natural companion: grace first, then the human means.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 12 of the Bodhapañcadaśikā (Abhinavagupta, 15 verses). Gloss: From-that (tat) grace-sap (prasādarasād eva) or (vā) from-teacher-scripture (gurvāgamata eva) or (vā) from-scripture (śāstrād) of-supreme-Lord (parameśasya) from-which (yasmāt) from-which (kasmād) approached (upāgatam).

Sanskrit: tatprasādarasād eva gurvāgamata eva vā | śāstrād vā parameśasya yasmāt kasmād upāgatam

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 12:**
> tatprasādarasād eva gurvāgamata eva vā | śāstrād vā parameśasya yasmāt kasmād upāgatam

[i.e. from that grace-sap, or from teacher-scripture, or from scripture of the supreme Lord — from whichever, approached.]

Three doors, one arrival: from that (tat) grace-sap (prasādarasād) indeed (eva); or (vā) from teacher-scripture (gurvāgamataḥ... eva); or (vā) from scripture (śāstrāt) of the supreme Lord (parameśasya) — from which (yasmāt), from whichever (kasmāt), approached (upāgatam). Yasmāt kasmāt (from whichever) refuses to rank them: grace direct, grace through teacher-text, grace through Lord's-text — arrival counts, route doesn't. Eva (indeed) repeated on the first two marks grace and guru as the stressed doors, scripture as the standing third.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Upāgatam (approached/arrived) leaves the arrival's content unnamed; verse 13 names it (full knowledge = liberation).
2. The verse lists means without subordinating them; no hierarchy is imported.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry three-doors-one-arrival: grace-sap, teacher-scripture, Lord's scripture — from whichever, approached. L2 must keep yasmāt kasmāt's indifference to route. Carry arrival into verse 13, where arrival is named full knowledge, liberation, living freedom.

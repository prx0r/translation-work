# Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p13 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 13)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 bod-p13 = verse 13. Lore: parijñāna (full knowledge) = mokṣa = pūrṇatva = jīvanmukti — the verse's four-term identity leaves no remainder between knowing, freedom, fullness, and living liberation.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 13 of the Bodhapañcadaśikā (Abhinavagupta, 15 verses). Gloss: What (yat) of-reality (tattvasya) full-knowledge (parijñānaṃ) — that (saḥ) liberation (mokṣaḥ) from-supreme-Lord (parameśataḥ). That (tat) fullness (pūrṇatvam) of-awakened (prabuddhānāṃ) living-liberation (jīvanmuktiś ca) remembered (smṛtā).

Sanskrit: yat tattvasya parijñānaṃ sa mokṣaḥ parameśataḥ | tat pūrṇatvaṃ prabuddhānāṃ jīvanmuktiś ca sā smṛtā

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 13:**
> yat tattvasya parijñānaṃ sa mokṣaḥ parameśataḥ | tat pūrṇatvaṃ prabuddhānāṃ jīvanmuktiś ca sā smṛtā

[i.e. what full knowledge of reality — that is liberation from the supreme Lord. That fullness, of the awakened: living liberation — and that is remembered.]

What (yat) full-knowledge (parijñānam) of reality (tattvasya): that (saḥ) is liberation (mokṣaḥ) — from the supreme Lord (parameśataḥ), i.e. of His giving, in His domain. That (tat) fullness (pūrṇatvam) of the awakened (prabuddhānām): living liberation (jīvanmuktiḥ) — and (ca) that (sā) is remembered/recorded (smṛtā). Full knowledge = liberation = fullness = living liberation = recorded teaching: four terms, one referent. Knowing is not before liberation; knowing is liberation, lived while living, handed down as remembered (smṛti) doctrine.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Parameśataḥ (from the supreme Lord) is read as source-and-domain of liberation, not as a separate agent granting it.
2. Sā smṛtā (it is remembered/recorded) closes the verse on transmission: the teaching enters memory-lineage.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry the four-term identity whole: full knowledge = liberation = fullness = living liberation, remembered. L2 must not stage these as steps — the verse equates, it does not sequence. Carry liberation into verse 14, where bondage shares its non-difference.

# Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p14 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 14)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 bod-p14 = verse 14. Lore: the non-difference of bondage and liberation — the stotra's closest approach to saying saṃsāra and mokṣa are one substance seen twice; tattvataḥ (in truth) restricts the claim to reality-level, leaving the fool's terror of verse 11 intact.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 14 of the Bodhapañcadaśikā (Abhinavagupta, 15 verses). Gloss: These-two (etau) bondage (bandha) liberation (vimokṣau ca) from-supreme-Lord-nature (parameśasvarūpataḥ). Not (na) they-differ (bhidyete); no (na) difference (bhedo hi) in-truth (tattvataḥ) in-supreme-Lord (parameśvare).

Sanskrit: etau bandhavimokṣau ca parameśasvarūpataḥ | na bhidyete na bhedo hi tattvataḥ parameśvare

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 14:**
> etau bandhavimokṣau ca parameśasvarūpataḥ | na bhidyete na bhedo hi tattvataḥ parameśvare

[i.e. these two, bondage and liberation — from supreme-Lord-nature. They do not differ; no difference indeed, in truth, in the supreme Lord.]

These two (etau): bondage (bandha) and liberation (vimokṣa) — from supreme-Lord-nature (parameśasvarūpataḥ): both out of the Lord's own nature, neither from elsewhere. They do not differ (na bhidyete); no (na) difference (bhedaḥ) indeed (hi), in truth (tattvataḥ), in the supreme Lord (parameśvare). Tattvataḥ (in truth) bounds the claim to reality-level: phenomenally the fool still trembles (verse 11 stands); in truth there is nothing to differ from nothing. Non-difference stated twice — of the pair, and in the Lord — closes every gap the hymn opened.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The verse does not retract verse 11's terrifying saṃsāra; tattvataḥ (in truth) reconciles them by level, not by cancellation.
2. No practice follows; the verse is pure position, preparing the closing injunction to contemplate.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry double non-difference: of the pair, and in the Lord, in truth. L2 must keep both negations — na bhidyete (they do not differ) and na bhedo (no difference) — the doubling is emphasis, not redundancy. Carry non-difference into verse 15, where Bhairava-as-own-nature is to be contemplated.

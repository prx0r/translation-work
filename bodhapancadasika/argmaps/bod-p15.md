# Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p15 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 15)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 bod-p15 = verse 15. Lore: the closing trident-lotus (śūla-ambuja) bearing will, art, knowledge, power (icchā-kalā-jñāna-śakti) — the hymn's final image gathers the whole doctrine into one emblem to be contemplated (pariśīlyate: practiced, dwelt in).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 15 of the Bodhapañcadaśikā (Abhinavagupta, 15 verses). Gloss: Thus (ittham) will-power (icchā) art (kalā) knowledge (jñāna) power (śakti) trident (śūla) lotus (ambuja) resorted (āśritaḥ) — Bhairava (bhairavaḥ) of-all-things (sarvabhāvānām) own-nature (svabhāvaḥ) is-examined (pariśīlyate).

Sanskrit: ittham icchākalājñānaśaktiśūlāmbujāśritaḥ | bhairavaḥ sarvabhāvānām svabhāvaḥ pariśīlyate

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 15:**
> ittham icchākalājñānaśaktiśūlāmbujāśritaḥ | bhairavaḥ sarvabhāvānām svabhāvaḥ pariśīlyate

[i.e. thus — will, art, knowledge, power-trident-lotus-resorted: Bhairava, of all things own-nature, is contemplated.]

Thus (ittham): resorted (āśritaḥ) to the trident-lotus (śūlāmbuja) of will (icchā), art (kalā), knowledge (jñāna), power (śakti) — the emblem gathers the hymn's powers into one seat. Bhairava (bhairavaḥ), of all things (sarvabhāvānām) own-nature (svabhāvaḥ), is contemplated/practiced (pariśīlyate) — the verb is the hymn's only imperative of practice, and it is contemplative dwelling, not ritual doing. The close returns to verse 2's equation (Bhairava = svabhāva of all) with the method attached: thus resorted, thus contemplated.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Āśritaḥ (resorted) agrees with Bhairavaḥ: He rests on/is resorted to with the trident-lotus; the emblem is His seat, read as stated without expanding the lotus symbolism.
2. Pariśīlyate (is contemplated/practiced) is passive: the contemplating is enjoined as ongoing dwelling, not as a rite to complete.
3. The in-repo SOURCE.txt prints sarvabhavānāṃ (short a) against T1's frozen sarvabhāvānāṃ; T1 is followed throughout (bhāva = existent thing, paralleling verse 2's sarvabhūtānām), the SOURCE variant noted here, not smoothed.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry emblem-then-injunction: trident-lotus bearing will-art-knowledge-power, Bhairava the own-nature of all things, to be contemplated. L2 must end the hymn here — dwelling, not departure. This closes the arc opened in verse 1's all-containing One.

# C1 — bod-p01 (unsetting light, bright and dark)

## SUMMARY

A light that never sets holds brightness and darkness alike; a solitary inward reality, which both bound.

## KEY TERMS

anastamita (unsetting) · tejas/tamas (bright/dark powers) · ekaḥ/antar (one/within) · yadantaḥ (whose-end: limit and abode)

## rasa

Wonder (adbhuta): light that never sets, wedding bright and dark.

# C1 — bod-p02 (identity: He alone, His alone)

## SUMMARY

That reality is each creature's own-nature, and the world's abundance is power cast in the shape of sovereignty.

## KEY TERMS

svabhāva (own-nature) · īśvaratāmayī (sovereignty-shaped) · eva (alone, doubled)

## rasa

Exultation (vīra): aloneness doubled, identity stated.

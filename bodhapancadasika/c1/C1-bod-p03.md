# C1 — bod-p03 (no parting ever, fire-sealed)

## SUMMARY

Power refuses any parting from its bearer — one eternally, as burning is one with fire.

## KEY TERMS

vyatireka/vāñchati (apartness/wants) · tādātmyam (identity) · vahnidāhikā (fire-burning)

## rasa

Love-wonder (rati): power never parting from her bearer.

# C1 — bod-p04 (mark then manner, mirrored whole)

## SUMMARY

Bhairava bears the world by mirroring it within His own seeing.

## KEY TERMS

jagadbharaṇalakṣaṇa (world-bearing-mark) · svātmādṛś (own-seeing-mirror) · pratibimba (reflection) · yacchaktyā (by-whose-power)

## rasa

Wonder (adbhuta): the world carried in a glance.

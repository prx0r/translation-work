# C1 — bod-p07 (hardest, highest, freest)

## SUMMARY

The hardest deed belongs to Him and has no higher — and it is freedom, lordship, awareness-form in one.

## KEY TERMS

atidurghaṭakāritva (hardest deed) · anuttara (unsurpassed) · svatantratvam/aiśvaryam/bodharūpatā (equation)

## rasa

Exultation (vīra): five said once.

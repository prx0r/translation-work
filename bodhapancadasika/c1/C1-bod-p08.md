# C1 — bod-p08 (mark then difference)

## SUMMARY

The inert is defined by shining only partially; awareness stands apart from it by escaping all measure.

## KEY TERMS

paricchinnaprakāśatva (partial shining) · jaḍa (inert) · na parimīyate (unmeasured, active)

## rasa

Exultation (vīra): awareness uncontainable.

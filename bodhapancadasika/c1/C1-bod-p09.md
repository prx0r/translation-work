# C1 — bod-p09 (thus-therefore, stood)

## SUMMARY

Change itself — every making and unmaking — rests inside the free one, distinguished by His own power, abiding as His nature rather than departing anywhere.

## KEY TERMS

nijaśaktyupabheda (own-power distinction) · svātmagāḥ (self-arrived) · saṃsthitāḥ (standing, durative)

## rasa

Peace (śama): change abiding in self.

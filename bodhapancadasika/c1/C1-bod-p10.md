# C1 — bod-p10 (inventory uncompressed)

## SUMMARY

Variety is totalized first: every extreme, in all three directions, across outer worlds and their fragments and inner pleasure, pain, and mind.

## KEY TERMS

vaicitrya/atyanta (variety/extreme) · ūrdhvādhas-tiryak (up-down-across) · sukhaduḥkhamati (pleasure-pain-mind)

## rasa

Wonder (adbhuta): totality listed complete.

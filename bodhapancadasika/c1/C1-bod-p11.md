# C1 — bod-p11 (equation, then terror bounded)

## SUMMARY

Then its cause is named: missing this freedom is itself freedom under description — and that mis-knowing is bondage, dreadful to the deluded.

## KEY TERMS

aparijñāna (mis-knowing) · svātantrya (freedom) · saṃsāra/mūḍha/vibhīṣaka (bondage/deluded/dreadful)

## rasa

Tempered dread: terror named, freedom beneath.

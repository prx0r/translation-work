# C1 — bod-p12 (no hierarchy)

## SUMMARY

Three entries stand open — the sap of grace, the teacher's transmitted word, the Lord's scripture — and whichever one arrival comes through counts as arrival.

## KEY TERMS

prasādarasa (grace-sap) · gurvāgama (teacher's word) · parameśaśāstra (Lord's scripture) · yasmāt-kasmāt (from-whichever)

## rasa

Peace (śama): whichever door, arrival.

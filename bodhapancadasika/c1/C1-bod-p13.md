# C1 — bod-p13 (four terms, one identity)

## SUMMARY

Knowing reality to the bottom is release, a fullness belonging to the awakened, freedom lived while living, handed down as remembered teaching.

## KEY TERMS

parijñāna (knowing-through) · mokṣa (release) · pūrṇatva/prabuddha (fullness/awakened) · jīvanmukti/smṛtā (living-freedom/remembered)

## rasa

Peace (śama): known, released, remembered.

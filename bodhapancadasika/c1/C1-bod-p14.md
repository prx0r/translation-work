# C1 — bod-p14 (doubled negation)

## SUMMARY

Bondage and release both issue from the Lord's nature and do not come apart in Him — in truth, no split.

## KEY TERMS

bandha-vimokṣa (bondage-release) · na bhidyete/na bhedo (double negation) · tattvataḥ (in truth, level-holding)

## rasa

Peace (śama): undivided pair.

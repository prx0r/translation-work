# C1 — bod-p15 (stayed, stated, dwelt)

## SUMMARY

Thus — on will, art, knowledge, power, trident, lotus stayed — Bhairava, all things' own-nature, to be dwelt and weighed.

## KEY TERMS

ittham (thus) · icchā-kalā-jñāna-śakti (four powers) · śūlāmbuja (trident-lotus) · sarvabhāva (every thing) · pariśīlyate (to-be-dwelt-upon)

## rasa

Peace (śama): closing in dwelling.

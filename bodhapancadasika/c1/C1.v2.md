# C1.v2 — Bodhapañcadaśikā (condensation of the argument)

An unsetting light holds the bright and the dark; the solitary inward One is each creature's nature, and the world's plenty is His sovereignty-shaped power. Power will not part from its bearer — one eternally, as burning from fire. Bhairava carries the world by mirroring it in His own seeing; His goddess strains only to seize His nature and fills all things exactly. At play, the pair make and unmake together, all at once. The hardest deed is freedom itself — lordship, awareness-form, with nothing higher. The inert shines only in part; awareness escapes measure. Every making and unmaking rests in the free one's self, standing as His nature. Variety runs to every extreme, outward and inward. To miss this freedom is freedom under description — bondage, dreadful to the deluded. Grace, the teacher's word, the Lord's scripture: any door suffices. Knowing reality through is release — fullness, lived free, remembered. Bondage and release are one nature, undivided in the Lord, in truth. Dwell on Bhairava, trident-lotus-seated, the own-nature of all things.

## bod-p01 (1)

A light that never sets holds bright powers and dark ones alike, and both terminate in the one inward reality.

## bod-p02 (2)

He alone is every creature's own nature, and the world's abundance is His power cast in sovereignty's shape.

## bod-p03 (3)

Power refuses any parting from its bearer, one with Him eternally as burning is with fire.

## bod-p04 (4)

World-bearing is His mark, and the whole stands mirrored in His self-seeing, by power.

## bod-p05 (5)

The supreme goddess belongs to Him alone, hungry to grasp His nature, filling all things exactly, neither short nor over.

## bod-p06 (6)

God and goddess are forever eager for play, working varied making and unmaking together, everywhere at once.

## bod-p07 (7)

The hardest deed is unsurpassed: His freedom, His lordship, His awareness-form — five terms for one referent.

## bod-p08 (8)

Partial shining marks the inert, while awareness stands apart from it, admitting no measure.

## bod-p09 (9)

Distinguished by His own power, every making and unmaking rests in the self and stands as His nature.

## bod-p10 (10)

The inventory is total: every extreme, in three directions, across worlds and their parts, pleasure and pain and mind.

## bod-p11 (11)

To miss this freedom is freedom under description — and that is bondage itself, dreadful to the deluded.

## bod-p12 (12)

Grace-sap, the teacher's word, the Lord's scripture: from whichever of these, arrival.

## bod-p13 (13)

To know reality through is release — fullness of the awakened, freedom lived while living, handed down as remembered teaching.

## bod-p14 (14)

Bondage and release come of a single nature, with no split in the Lord, in truth.

## bod-p15 (15)

Will, art, knowledge, and power rest on the trident-lotus, and Bhairava, the nature of all things, is to be dwelt upon.

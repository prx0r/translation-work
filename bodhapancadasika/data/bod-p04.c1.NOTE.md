# C1 NOTE — bod-p04

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Bhairava bears the world by mirroring it within His own seeing.

## VOICE MAP

Abhinavagupta teaching; no objector.

## KEY TERMS

* **jagadbharaṇalakṣaṇa** — world-bearing-mark: His character.
* **svātmādṛś/pratibimba** — self-seeing mirror: manner, not emanation.

## CRUXES

* Mirror-not-place; power sole agent.

## FUNCTION

Mark → manner: lakṣaṇa stated, pratibimba worked by power alone.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Verse 4 of 15: Bhairava maxim.

## EXPLANATION

'Reflected' kept, never 'created'; vision is the mirror.

## BOUNDARY

No agent besides power.

## RELATED

v03 (identical pair acts); v05 (goddess owns grasping).

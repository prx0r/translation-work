# C1 NOTE — bod-p09

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Change itself — every making and unmaking — rests inside the free one, distinguished by His own power, abiding as His nature rather than departing anywhere.

## VOICE MAP

Abhinavagupta teaching stillness-in-change.

## KEY TERMS

* **svātmagāḥ** — self-arrived: no journey.
* **saṃsthitāḥ/svarūpatvena** — standing/as-nature: stood, not made.

## CRUXES

* Arrival/durative readings kept.

## FUNCTION

Evam-therefore: distinction, abiding, nature — stood, not made.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Verse 9 of 15: abiding maxim.

## EXPLANATION

'As nature' kept (stood, not made); arrival-without-departure.

## BOUNDARY

No journey implied; no completed event.

## RELATED

v08 (unmeasured one); v10 (abiding inventoried).

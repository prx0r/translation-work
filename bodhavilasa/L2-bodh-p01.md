# Bodhavilasa bodh-p01 (double salute) — L2 READ

## v.1
Oṃ. Salutation to Netra. Salutation to Śiva constantly — maker of the five acts, manifest as supreme-self bliss.
The Sanskrit: *oṃ namo netrāya | namaḥ śivāya satataṃ pañcakṛtyavidhāyine*

## v.2
Consciousness is free, the universe's cause — so called; freedom-formed, the knower — and powers too she bestows.
The Sanskrit: *citiḥ svatantrā viśvasya hetur ity abhidhīyate | svātantryarūpā jñātā ca siddhīr vā saṃprayacchate*

## v.3
She — on her own ground, by her own will, for the world's unfolding — from varied fit variety, from subject-object difference.
The Sanskrit: *sā svabhittau svecchayaiva viśvasyonmīlanāya | nānānurūpavaicitryād grāhyagrāhakabhedataḥ*

## v.4
That world's variety stands through object-difference — manifold-variety-yoked, high-and-low-numbered.
The Sanskrit: *viśvasya tasya vaicitryaṃ grāhyabhedena saṃsthitam | nānāvaicitryayogena cottamādhamasaṅkhyayā*

## v.5
From consciousness-tightening comes mind — though knower, even the great Lord. Like a banyan seed, there the world-form — that the wise know.
The Sanskrit: *citisaṅkocanāc cittaṃ cetano 'pi maheśvaraḥ | vaṭadhanikāvat tatra viśvarūpaṃ ca tad viduḥ*

## v.6
Knowing is the self's pure form — stainless, utterly stainless. That same, rule-bound, is called mind — knowing.
The Sanskrit: *caitanyam ātmano rūpaṃ śuddham atyantanirmalam | tad eva niyamāsaktaṃ cittaṃ cid abhidhīyate*

## v.7
Object-faced, awareness has come to rest in mind. From tightening-dominant-self, the wise know knowing as mind.
The Sanskrit: *arthonmukhatayā saṃvic citte viśrāntim āgatā | saṅkocātmapradhānatvāc citiś cittaṃ vidur budhāḥ*

## v.8
The māyā-knower, knowing-formed, one with that, abides among embodied beings — clinging to objects, void of true discernment.
The Sanskrit: *māyāpramātā cidrūpas tanmayatvena dehinām | vartate viṣayāsaktaḥ sadvivekavivarjitaḥ*

## v.9
Ever one, the established mind — by kalā, sattva, guṇa and the rest, by seven-knowerhood, by thirty-six count.
The Sanskrit: *sadaikaḥ pratithaś cittaṃ kalāsattvaguṇādibhiḥ | saptapramatṛkatvena ṣaṭtriṃśattattvasaṅkhyayā*

## v.10
One, everywhere, pure, awareness-selfed, the great Lord. From mis-knowing that I-ness, freedom sits in the palm.
The Sanskrit: *ekaḥ sarvagataḥ śuddhaḥ sambodhātmā maheśvaraḥ | saivāhantāparijñānān muktiḥ karatale sthitā*

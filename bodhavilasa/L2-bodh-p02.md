# Bodhavilasa bodh-p02 (tended means, tending Lord) — L2 READ

## v.11
Cultivated by seven-knowerhood, by thirty-six count — he, the great Lord: glory-of-release proceeds.
The Sanskrit: *pramātṛsaptakatvena ṣaṭtriṃśattattvasaṅkhyayā bhāvitaḥ | sa maheśāno mokṣalakṣmīḥ pravartate*

## v.12
Its stages — views and the rest, all of them, no doubt. Made-up are stages: as an actor assumes by his own will, so the bliss-will-spread self assumes vast views-and-powers.
The Sanskrit: *tadbhūmikā darśanādyās sarvā eva na saṃśayaḥ | bhūmikāḥ kṛtrimā yadvan naṭo gṛhṇāti svecchayā*

## v.13
Bliss-spread self, having assumed vast views-and-powers, shines. From tightening of own power, in wandering, it becomes impure.
The Sanskrit: *cidānandasamullāsollasitātmā virājate | cidvatsvaśaktisaṅkocāt saṃsāre malināyate*

## v.15
Incomplete — 'thus am I incomplete' — by various impurities defiled; named of-māyā-inoffensive-karma; all-pervading too, world-Lord.
The Sanskrit: *apūrṇo 'smīti vividhair malaiḥ | māyīyāṇavakārmākhyair vyāpako 'pi jagadvibhuḥ*

## v.16
All-pervading — just as the great god does five acts, so this self too does — deluded by power.
The Sanskrit: *vyāpī yadvan mahādevaḥ kroti pañcakṛtyatām | tadvad evāyam ātmāpi kurute śaktimohitaḥ*

## v.17
Capable — from expansion, power-Lord is sung by truth-teachers. From contraction, contracted, as wanderer is called.
The Sanskrit: *śakto vikāsāc chaktīśo gīyate tattvavādibhiḥ | saṅkocanāt saṅkocitaḥ saṃsārīty abhidhīyate*

## v.18
Māyā — form indeed — which, somewhat intent to perceive, should know as made-creation. The other power — preservation is considered; from dissolution, the withdrawn goddess is considered.
The Sanskrit: *māyā rūpaṃ hi yat kiṃcid ālokayitum udyatā | anyā śaktiḥ sthitir matā*

## v.19
From dissolution, the withdrawn goddess — in unity-of-consciousness, grace is considered. From manifesting that essence, concealment is called.
The Sanskrit: *saṃhārāt saṃhṛtā devī cidaikye | 'nugraho mataḥ*

## v.20
Thus always present five-course, in order known — wish-jewel. That from ignorance — like wood and clod.
The Sanskrit: *itthaṃ sadā saṃnihitaḥ pañcavāhavidhiḥ | cintāmaṇir asāv ajñānāt kāṣṭaloṣṭavat*

# Bodhavilasa bodh-p03 (practice always, freed fully) — L2 READ

## v.21
By power of true reflection thus, always, who practices — he, liberated from all bonds, full of eternal bliss, becomes.
The Sanskrit: *sadvimarśabalenaivaṃ nityaṃ yaḥ pariśīlate | sa muktaḥ sarvabandhebhyo nityānandamayo bhavet*

## v.22
Consciousness-fire swallows that — this, well-awakened, is. When that, then-that, pure, to-be-known — consciousness-fire, all-devouring.
The Sanskrit: *cidvahnir grasate so | 'yaṃ suprabuddho 'sty*

## v.23
By this power united, liberation-marked yogi attains the pure state. That soon — no doubt here.
The Sanskrit: *etadbalena saṃyukto yogī nirvāṇalakṣaṇaḥ | padaṃ prāpnoti vimalaṃ so*

## v.24
Consciousness-fire, on gaining power, even the universe shines — consciousness-made. Self-bliss-nectar wave, this risen, outside.
The Sanskrit: *cidvahnir balalābhe | 'pi viśvam ābhāti*

## v.25
That indeed gone to quiescence, by being-forms manifested — own self, great Lord, god plays, supreme Lord.
The Sanskrit: *tad eva śyānatāṃ yātaṃ bhāvarūpair vibhāvyate | svātmā maheśvaro devaḥ krīḍate parameśvaraḥ*

## v.26
Thus who constantly knows — he, liberated, liberates too the multitude of beings, in an instant, from terrible saṃsāra-bondage.
The Sanskrit: *iti yaḥ satataṃ vetti sa mukto mocayaty | api sattvavrātaṃ kṣaṇenaiva ghorāt saṃsārabandhanāt*

## v.27
Who — someone bliss-powered — established in own state. Thus — whose exultant-rising joy ...
The Sanskrit: *yaḥ kaścit ānandabalaḥ sa svarūpapadasthitaḥ | iti yasya samullāsollāsitodayaśālinaḥ*

## v.28
His, fixed-in-own-nature — in distraction even — that is desired. This by effort-power by people is attained.
The Sanskrit: *tasya svarūpaniṣṭhasya vyutthāne | 'pi tad arthyate*

## v.29
From middle-center expansion, and from bliss-consciousness, shines. From its light, divine vision of form-knowledge is manifested.
The Sanskrit: *madhyadhāmavikāsāc ca cidānandāt prakāśate | tatprakāśād divyadṛṣṭir jñānarūpā vibhāvyate*

## v.30
Immaculate, radiant, pure, full-of-eternal-bliss, supreme — and-net-of-body-cutting too — knowledge-vision expands.
The Sanskrit: *nirmalā suprabhā śuddhā nityānandamayī parā | nirdehacchedajālāpi jñānadṛṣṭir vijṛmbhate*

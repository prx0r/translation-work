# Bodhavilasa bodh-p04 (opened, powerful, manifold) — L2 READ

## v.31
In middle-center expansion — but the means: powerful state, thought-decay-tightening-opening and the rest, in many ways.
The Sanskrit: *madhyadhāmavikāse tu upāyo balavatsthitiḥ | vikalpakṣayasaṅkocavikāsādyā anekaśaḥ*

## v.32
From thinking-of-nothing indeed, thoughts' decay may be. From knowing-awareness, playful power, attached-therein, in being's realm.
The Sanskrit: *akiñciccintanād eva vikalpānāṃ kṣayo bhavet | cidbodhād vilasacchaktī raktāsau bhāvamaṇḍale*

## v.33
There-attached, the divine stream sees the pure-natured; power-tightening — this indeed, it is said by brahma-teachers.
The Sanskrit: *tatrānuraktā divyaughaṃ paśyate vimalātmakam | śaktisaṅkocam etad dhi procyate brahmavādibhiḥ*

## v.34
End-beginning-point-rest-bindu-nāda-dwellings, and fear-anger-disgust-graspings.
The Sanskrit: *ādyantakoṭiviśrāmabindunādalayās | tathā aśaṅkitabhayakrodhabībhatsādiparigrahāḥ*

## v.35
Thus this said: goddess, middle-center-expansion — there capable, in days thus obtains supreme prosperity.
The Sanskrit: *ity etat kathitaṃ devi madhyadhāmavikāsakam | tatra śakto dinair evaṃ prapnoti parasampadam*

## v.36
Who inner-samādhi-impressed wanders, of pure radiance — by emission-power indeed reflecting, and again, again.
The Sanskrit: *yo 'ntaḥsamādhisaṃskāraṃ bhramate vimalatviṣām | sa visargabalād eva vimṛśya ca punaḥ punaḥ*

## v.37
In distraction even — for his own form fully manifests — of the yogi fixed in awareness, of eternal-bliss-essence.
The Sanskrit: *vyutthāne 'pi yatas tasya svarūpaṃ samprakāśate | yogino bodhaniṣṭhasya nityānandarasasya ca*

## v.38
That full-of-manifest-bliss, mantra-power-strengthened, full-I-ness knowing — thus of inner-exultation-practitioner.
The Sanskrit: *tat prakāśānandamayī mantravīryopabṛṃhitā | pūṛnāhantācid ity antaḥsamullāsābhiyoginaḥ*

## v.39
There-that sporting yogi, in mantra-lord-status situated — there capable lord, as plays power-expanded.
The Sanskrit: *tatrāsau vilasan yogī mantreśvarapade sthitaḥ | tatra śakto vibhur yadvat krīḍate śaktibṛṃhitaḥ*

## v.40
Thus freedom — his, all-pervading, world-Lord's, various-difference-enjoying — unbroken in wondrous state.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ svatantratā tasya vyāpakasya jagadvibhoḥ | nānābhedajuṣaś citrapadasthitasyākhaṇḍitā*

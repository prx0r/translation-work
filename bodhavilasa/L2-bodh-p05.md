# Bodhavilasa bodh-p05 (told, drunk, quenched) — L2 READ

## v.41
This non-dual thus said — nectar-of-knowledge unsurpassed — who may drink is quenched. His fever-of-saṃsāra-poison.
The Sanskrit: *etad advayam ity uktaṃ jñānāmṛtam anuttamam | yaḥ pibec chāmyate tasya saṃsāraviṣamūrchanam*

## v.42
This bliss-of-awareness-method for attaining Śiva — of great souls, of king Kṣema-named — treatise extremely pure.
The Sanskrit: *bodhollāsavidhāneyaṃ śivaprāpyai mahātmanām | rājñaḥ kṣemābhidhānasya śāstram atyantanirmalam*

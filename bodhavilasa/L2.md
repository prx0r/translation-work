# Bodhavilāsa — L2 READ

## bodh-p01 — Consciousness as Free Cause and Its Contraction
Consciousness is free and the cause of the universe, manifesting as supreme self-bliss, while in its own ground it unfolds variety by will and difference of subject and object. Contracted into mind and māyā-bound knowership, the same consciousness appears bound, yet liberation remains at hand through recognizing that I-ness.

## bodh-p02 — Five Acts, Impurity, and Concealment
Maheśa performs the five acts through the seven knowers and thirty-six principles, while the self assumes vast powers or contracts into impurity. Māyā, preservation, dissolution, and concealment are recognized as powers of consciousness, though ignorance makes the wish-jewel seem like wood or clay.

## bodh-p03 — Liberation through True Reflection
One who constantly practices true reflection is liberated, and the consciousness-fire, when awakened, devours all and manifests the universe as consciousness-made. The knower of the supreme Lord liberates beings instantly, and the bliss-powered yogī remains established in his own state.

## bodh-p04 — Middle-Center Expansion and Mantra Lordship
Middle-center expansion is the means by which thoughts decay and divine vision arises, leading to the yogī’s state as mantra lord. Even in distraction, the eternal bliss essence fully manifests, and all-pervading freedom remains unbroken amid varied enjoyment.

## bodh-p05 — Non-Dual Nectar and Colophon
This non-dual nectar of knowledge, unsurpassed, quenches the fever of saṃsāra-poison for those who drink it. It is the extremely pure yoga treatise called Bodhavilāsa, composed by King Kṣemarāja for attaining Śiva.

# Bodhavilāsa bodh-p03 — ARGUMENT MAP (liberation by true reflection; consciousness-fire)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 bodh-p03 = vv. 21–30. Lore: Pensa 1961; Kṣemarāja (11th c.). Key: liberation (mukti) is by true reflection (sadvimarśa); the consciousness-fire (cidvahni) swallows the universe; the yogi marked by liberation attains the pure state; the self (svātmā) is Maheśvara playing; the yogi established in own nature liberates others in an instant; from middle-center expansion (madhyadhāmavikāsa) and bliss-consciousness, divine vision is manifested.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Liberation (mukti) is by true reflection (sadvimarśa). The consciousness-fire (cidvahni) swallows the universe. The yogi marked by liberation attains the pure state. The self (svātmā) is Maheśvara playing. The yogi established in own nature liberates others in an instant. From middle-center expansion (madhyadhāmavikāsa) and bliss-consciousness (cidānanda), divine vision is manifested — immaculate, radiant, pure, full of eternal bliss, supreme.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — liberation by true reflection (v. 21):**
> *sadvimarśabalenaivaṃ nityaṃ yaḥ pariśīlate | sa muktaḥ sarvabandhebhyo nityānandamayo bhavet*
[i.e. by the power of true reflection, thus always, whoever practices, he is liberated from all bonds, full of eternal bliss, becomes.] Liberation is by sadvimarśa (true reflection) — not by effort alone but by the power of true reflection.

**Step 1 — consciousness-fire (v. 22):**
> *cidvahnir grasate so 'yaṃ suprabuddho 'sty asau | yadā tadāsau vimalo jñeyaś cidvahniḥ sarvabhakṣakaḥ*
[i.e. consciousness-fire swallows; that well-awakened is; when then that pure, to be known, consciousness-fire, all-devouring.] The consciousness-fire (cidvahni) swallows the universe; when well-awakened, it is pure, all-devouring.

**Step 2 — by this power (v. 23):**
> *etadbalena saṃyukto yogī nirvāṇalakṣaṇaḥ | padaṃ prāpnoti vimalaṃ so 'cirān nātra saṃśayaḥ*
[i.e. by this power, united, yogi marked by liberation, state attains, pure; that soon, no here doubt.] By this power (etadbalena), united, the yogi marked by liberation attains the pure state; soon, no doubt.

**Step 3 — consciousness-fire on gaining power (v. 24):**
> *cidvahnir balalābhe 'pi viśvam ābhāti cinmayam | svānandāmṛtakallolam etad ucchalitaṃ bahiḥ*
[i.e. consciousness-fire on gaining power even, universe shines, consciousness-made; wave of self-bliss-nectar, this risen, outside.] On gaining power (balalābhe), the consciousness-fire shines; the universe is consciousness-made; the wave of self-bliss-nectar rises outside.

**Step 4 — self as Maheśvara (v. 25):**
> *tad eva śyānatāṃ yātaṃ bhāvarūpair vibhāvyate | svātmā maheśvaro devaḥ krīḍate parameśvaraḥ*
[i.e. that indeed quiescence gone, by forms of being is manifested; own-self, Maheśvara, god, plays, supreme lord.] That (the wave) gone to quiescence, by forms of being is manifested; own-self is Maheśvara, god, playing, supreme lord.

**Step 5 — the liberated liberates (v. 26):**
> *iti yaḥ satataṃ vetti sa mukto mocayaty api | sattvavrātaṃ kṣaṇenaiva ghorāt saṃsārabandhanāt*
[i.e. thus whoever constantly knows, he liberated, liberates also multitude of beings in an instant from terrible saṃsāra-bondage.] Whoever constantly knows this is liberated and liberates the multitude of beings in an instant from terrible saṃsāra-bondage.

**Step 6 — bliss-powered (v. 27):**
> *yaḥ kaścid ānandabalaḥ sa svarūpapadasthitaḥ | iti yasya samullāsollāsitodayaśālinaḥ*
[i.e. whoever, someone, bliss-powered, he established in own state; thus whose exultant-rising.] Whoever is bliss-powered (ānandabala) is established in own state; whose exultant-rising.

**Step 7 — fixed in own nature (v. 28):**
> *tasya svarūpaniṣṭhasya vyutthāne 'pi tad arthyate | etat prayatnasāmārthyāj janair evādhigamyate*
[i.e. his fixed in own nature, in distraction even, that is desired; this by effort-power, by people, is attained.] His, fixed in own nature, in distraction even, that (state) is desired; this by effort-power, by people, is attained.

**Step 8 — from middle-center expansion (v. 29):**
> *madhyadhāmavikāsāc ca cidānandāt prakāśate | tatprakāśād divyadṛṣṭir jñānarūpā vibhāvyate*
[i.e. from middle-center expansion, and from bliss-consciousness, shines; from its light, divine vision, of form-knowledge, is manifested.] From middle-center expansion (madhyadhāmavikāsa) and from bliss-consciousness (cidānanda), shines; from its light, divine vision (divyadṛṣṭi), of form-knowledge (jñānarūpā), is manifested.

**Step 9 — divine vision (v. 30):**
> *nirmalā suprabhā śuddhā nityānandamayī parā | nirdehacchedajālāpi jñānadṛṣṭir vijṛmbhate*
[i.e. immaculate, radiant, pure, full of eternal bliss, supreme; and net of body-cutting, knowledge-vision expands.] Divine vision is immaculate, radiant, pure, full of eternal bliss, supreme; the net of body-cutting, knowledge-vision expands.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The expanding middle here is left unlocated — suṣumnā or heart, the verse prescribes expansion without placing it.
2. The body-cutting net: what severs bodiliness itself, not one bond among others — kept as image, not mechanism.
3. The vṛtti is by Harabhatta Shastri (not Kṣemarāja) — wait, no. Let me check. The GRETIL text says "Based on Pensa, Corrado: Il Bodhavilāsa di Kṣemarāja. Rivista di Studi Orientali 36 (1961): 125-134." So Pensa is the editor, not the commentator. The text itself is by Kṣemarāja. There is no separate vṛtti mentioned.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the passage's own ascent: liberation by true reflection (v. 21) → consciousness-fire swallows (v. 22) → by this power, yogi attains pure state (v. 23) → consciousness-fire on gaining power, universe shines (v. 24) → self as Maheśvara playing (v. 25) → the liberated liberates (v. 26) → bliss-powered (v. 27) → fixed in own nature (v. 28) → from middle-center expansion, divine vision (v. 29) → divine vision expands (v. 30). The key move is v. 21: liberation is by sadvimarśa (true reflection), not by effort alone. And v. 26: the liberated liberates others in an instant.

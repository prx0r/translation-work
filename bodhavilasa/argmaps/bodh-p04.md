# Bodhavilāsa bodh-p04 — ARGUMENT MAP (middle-center expansion; upāyas)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 bodh-p04 = vv. 31–40. Lore: Pensa 1961; Kṣemarāja (11th c.). Key: middle-center expansion (madhyadhāmavikāsa) is the method; the upāyas (means) are the contraction-expansion axis; from thinking of nothing (akiñciccintana) thoughts decay; from consciousness-awareness (cidbodha) playful power (vilasacchakti) attaches; the yogi established in own form (svarūpa) is a lord (vibhu) like one expanded by power (śaktibṛṃhita). Freedom (svatantratā) is his, all-pervading, world-lord, enjoying various differences, unbroken in wondrous state.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Middle-center expansion (*madhyadhāmavikāsa*) is the method. The upāyas (means) are the contraction-expansion axis: from thinking of nothing (*akiñciccintana*) thoughts decay; from consciousness-awareness (*cidbodha*) playful power (*vilasacchakti*) attaches; the yogi established in own form (*svarūpa*) is a lord (*vibhu*) like one expanded by power (*śaktibṛṃhita*). Freedom (*svatantratā*) is his — all-pervading, world-lord, enjoying various differences, unbroken in wondrous state. The passage maps the method of liberation: not by creating something new but by expanding from the middle center.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — middle-center expansion (v. 31):**
> *madhyadhāmavikāse tu upāyo balavatsthitiḥ | vikalpakṣayasaṅkocavikāsādyā anekaśaḥ*
[i.e. in middle-center expansion, but, means, powerful state; etc. of thought-decay, contraction, expansion, in many ways.] In middle-center expansion (*madhyadhāmavikāsa*), the means (*upāya*) is a powerful state (*balavatsthiti*); thought-decay, contraction, expansion etc. in many ways.

**Step 1 — from thinking of nothing (v. 32):**
> *akiñciccintanād eva vikalpānāṃ kṣayo bhavet | cidbodhād vilasacchaktī raktāsau bhāvamaṇḍale*
[i.e. from thinking of nothing, indeed, of thoughts, decay may be; from consciousness-awareness, playful power, that is attached, in realm of being.] From thinking of nothing (*akiñciccintana*), thoughts decay; from consciousness-awareness (*cidbodha*), playful power (*vilasacchakti*) attaches in the realm of being.

**Step 2 — divine stream (v. 33):**
> *tatrānuraktā divyaughaṃ paśyate vimalātmakam | śaktisaṅkocam etad dhi procyate brahmavādibhiḥ*
[i.e. there attached, divine stream, sees, of pure nature; contraction of power, this indeed is said by brahma-teachers.] There, attached, one sees the divine stream (*divyaugha*) of pure nature (*vimalātmaka*); this is called contraction of power (*śaktisaṅkoca*) by brahma-teachers.

**Step 3 — rest in bindu/nāda (v. 34):**
> *ādyantakoṭiviśrāmabindunādalayās tathā | aśaṅkitabhayakrodhabībhatsādiparigrahāḥ*
[i.e. etc. of rest in bindu, nāda at end, beginning; and including fear, anger, disgust etc. grasping.] Rest in bindu/nāda at end and beginning; including fear, anger, disgust etc. grasping.

**Step 4 — middle-center expansion (v. 35):**
> *ity etat kathitaṃ devi madhyadhāmavikāsakam | tatra śakto dinair evaṃ prapnoti parasampadam*
[i.e. thus this said, goddess, middle-center expansion; there capable, in days, thus obtains supreme prosperity.] Thus this said, goddess, middle-center expansion; there, capable, in days, thus obtains supreme prosperity.

**Step 5 — inner samādhi impression (v. 36):**
> *yo 'ntaḥsamādhisaṃskāraṃ bhramate vimalatviṣām | sa visargabalād eva vimṛśya ca punaḥ punaḥ*
[i.e. who inner samādhi impression wanders, of pure radiance; he by power of emission indeed, reflecting, and again and again.] Who wanders with inner samādhi impression, of pure radiance; he, by power of emission (*visargabala*), reflecting, again and again.

**Step 6 — in distraction (v. 37):**
> *vyutthāne 'pi yatas tasya svarūpaṃ samprakāśate | yogino bodhaniṣṭhasya nityānandarasasya ca*
[i.e. in distraction even, because his own form fully manifests; of yogi fixed in awareness, of eternal bliss essence.] In distraction (*vyutthāna*) even, because his own form fully manifests; of yogi fixed in awareness (*bodhaniṣṭha*), of eternal bliss essence (*nityānandarasa*).

**Step 7 — full of manifest bliss (v. 38):**
> *tat prakāśānandamayī mantravīryopabṛṃhitā | pūrṇāhantācid ity antaḥsamullāsābhiyoginaḥ*
[i.e. that full of manifest bliss, strengthened by mantra-power; consciousness of full I-ness, thus of inner exultation practitioner.] That, full of manifest bliss (*prakāśānandamayī*), strengthened by mantra-power (*mantravīryopabṛṃhitā*); consciousness of full I-ness (*pūrṇāhantācid*), thus of inner exultation practitioner.

**Step 8 — the yogi as lord (v. 39):**
> *tatrāsau vilasan yogī mantreśvarapade sthitaḥ | tatra śakto vibhur yadvat krīḍate śaktibṛṃhitaḥ*
[i.e. there that sporting yogi situated in status of mantra-lord; there capable, lord, just as plays, expanded by power.] There, that sporting yogi situated in the status of mantra-lord; there, capable, lord, just as plays, expanded by power.

**Step 9 — freedom (v. 40):**
> *evaṃ svatantratā tasya vyāpakasya jagadvibhoḥ | nānābhedajuṣaś citrapadasthitasyākhaṇḍitā*
[i.e. thus freedom of his, all-pervading, world-lord, enjoying various differences, unbroken in wondrous state.] Thus freedom (*svatantratā*) is his — all-pervading, world-lord, enjoying various differences, unbroken in wondrous state (*citrapadasthitasyākhaṇḍitā*).

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Here the middle is method, not place: expansion prescribed as the means, its seat unnamed.
2. The "inner samādhi impression" (*antaḥsamādhisaṃskāra*) — the T1 gloss renders it as "inner samādhi impression"; the Sanskrit *antaḥ* = "inner," *samādhi* = "absorption," *saṃskāra* = "impression." The text does not elaborate.
3. The body-cutting net (see p03): carried forward as image, still unmechanized.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the passage's own method: middle-center expansion (v. 31) → from thinking of nothing (v. 32) → divine stream (v. 33) → rest in bindu/nāda (v. 34) → middle-center expansion (v. 35) → inner samādhi impression (v. 36) → in distraction (v. 37) → full of manifest bliss (v. 38) → the yogi as lord (v. 39) → freedom (v. 40). The key move is v. 31: the means (*upāya*) is a powerful state (*balavatsthiti*) in middle-center expansion. And v. 32: from thinking of nothing (*akiñciccintana*), thoughts decay; from consciousness-awareness (*cidbodha*), playful power attaches. And v. 40: freedom (*svatantratā*) is his — all-pervading, world-lord, enjoying various differences, unbroken. Carry all three into p05 (the closing, non-dual). Read it as method without making: think nothing, attach play, sport as lord.

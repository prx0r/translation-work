# Bodhavilāsa bodh-p05 — ARGUMENT MAP (closing: non-dual knowledge; the yogaśāstra)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 bodh-p05 = vv. 41–42. Lore: Pensa 1961; Kṣemarāja (11th c.). Key: the close seals it — unsurpassed non-dual nectar; whoever drinks, quenched of saṃsāra-poison. The method of bliss of awareness (bodhollāsavidhā) is for attaining Śiva. The text is called Bodhavilāsa, a yogaśāstra, composed by Śrī Kṣemarāja. The text's final word: liberation is not creating something new but recognizing what one already is.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

The close: non-dual nectar, unsurpassed; the drinker quenched of saṃsāra-poison. The method of bliss of awareness (*bodhollāsavidhā*) is for attaining Śiva. The text is called Bodhavilāsa, a yogaśāstra, composed by Śrī Kṣemarāja. Last word: recognition frees; nothing is made.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — non-dual knowledge (v. 41):**
> *etad advayam ity uktaṃ jñānāmṛtam anuttamam | yaḥ pibec chāmyate tasya saṃsāraviṣamūrchanam*
[i.e. this non-dual thus said, nectar of knowledge unsurpassed; whoever may drink, is quenched, his fever of saṃsāra-poison.] This non-dual knowledge is unsurpassed nectar; whoever drinks it is quenched of the fever of saṃsāra-poison.

**Step 1 — the method (v. 42):**
> *bodhollāsavidhāneyaṃ śivaprāpyai mahātmanām | rājñaḥ kṣemābhidhānasya śāstram atyantanirmalam*
[i.e. this method of bliss of awareness for attaining Śiva, of great souls; of king named Kṣema, treatise extremely pure, thus composed by Śrī Kṣemarāja, named Bodhavilāsa, yogaśāstra.] This method of bliss of awareness (*bodhollāsavidhā*) is for attaining Śiva (*śivaprāpyai*), for great souls (*mahātmanām*). The text is the extremely pure treatise (*atyantanirmalam śāstram*) of King Kṣema (*rājñaḥ kṣemābhidhānasya*), composed by Śrī Kṣemarāja (*śrīkṣemarājakṛtaṃ*), named Bodhavilāsa (*bodhavilāsākhyaṃ*), a yogaśāstra (*yogaśāstram*).

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The text has only 2 closing verses (vv. 41–42) — the closing is very brief, almost abrupt. The seal is the non-dual knowledge and the method.
2. The "king named Kṣema" (*rājñaḥ kṣemābhidhānasya*) — this refers to a patron, not to Kṣemarāja himself. The text is dedicated to a king named Kṣema.
3. The text is called a *yogaśāstra* — a yoga treatise. This is significant: the Bodhavilāsa is not just philosophy but practice.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the text's own close: non-dual knowledge is unsurpassed (v. 41) → whoever drinks it is quenched of saṃsāra-poison → the method of bliss of awareness is for attaining Śiva (v. 42) → the text is a yogaśāstra by Kṣemarāja. The key move is v. 41's *etad advayam ity uktaṃ jñānāmṛtam anuttamam*: this non-dual knowledge is unsurpassed nectar. And v. 42's *bodhollāsavidhāneyaṃ śivaprāpyai mahātmanām*: this method of bliss of awareness is for attaining Śiva, for great souls. Read it as seal, not summary: nectar drunk, Śiva-method stated, yogaśāstra signed.

# C1 — bodh-p05 (say, sip, soothe)

## SUMMARY

Non-dual nectar past compare drunk quenches poison-fever. Bliss-method for Śiva-winning, great souls', Kṣema-king's pure treatise.

## KEY TERMS

advaya/ukta (non-dual/told) · jñānāmṛta/anuttama (nectar/past-compare) · pibec/chāmyate (drink/quenched) · bodhollāsa-vidhāna (bliss-method) · śivaprāpti (Śiva-winning) · rājñaḥ-kṣemābhidhānasya (Kṣema-king's) · atyantanirmala (past-pure)

## rasa

Quenching relished. Purity relished.

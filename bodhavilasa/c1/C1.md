# C1 — Bodhavilāsa (argument)

## bodh-p01 (1–10) — free cause; willed unfolding; mind contracted

**SUMMARY.** Free consciousness causes: self-bliss-manifest, she unfolds the world on her own ground by her own will, differenced into seers and seens, high and low. Contracted, that same awareness is mind — rule-bound, object-facing, knower-masked — yet palm-held release stands ready in recognizing I-ness.

**KEY TERMS.** citi/svatantrā/hetu (consciousness/free/cause), cidānandaghana (bliss-massed consciousness), svecchā/svabhitti (own will/own ground), grāhyagrāhakabheda (seer-seen difference), citisaṅkoca/citta (contraction/mind), niyamāsakti (rule-bondage), māyāpramātṛ (masked knower), karatale (palm-held).

**EXPLANATION.** Verses 1–2 salute the five-doer and state the thesis twice: citiḥ svatantrā viśvasya hetuḥ, svātantryarūpā — freedom causes, knowingly. Verses 3–4 unfold by will on own ground, differencing by fitted seers-seens and high-low count. Verses 5–7 contract: citisaṅkocanāc cittaṃ; caitanya bound by niyama is called mind; awareness rests in mind, and the wise call mind contracted consciousness. Verse 8 masks the knower: cidrūpa yet viṣayāsakta, sadvivekavivarjita. Verses 9–10 count the conditioning (seven knowers, thirty-six) then open the hand: ekaḥ sarvagataḥ... saivāhantāparijñānān muktiḥ karatale sthitā — non-recognition the only lock.

## bodh-p02 (11–20) — glory proceeds; actor-stages; same self both

**SUMMARY.** Cultivated through knowers and tattvas, the Lord's release-glory goes forth; stages are assumed like an actor's parts. Bliss-will expands the self into vast powers; contraction soils it into incompleteness-pleading. All-pervading yet triple-stained, the self still does all five doings — lord by expansion, bondsman by contraction. Māyā forms, other powers keep; dissolution withdraws the goddess, grace dawns in oneness, hiding proclaimed from essence-showing. The fivefold traffic runs always; the wish-jewel known is wood-and-clod to ignorance.

**KEY TERMS.** mokṣalakṣmī/bhūmikā (release-glory/stages), naṭa (actor), vikāsa/saṅkoca (expansion/contraction), malatraya (three stains), pañcakṛtya (five doings), śaktīśa/saṃsārī (power-lord/bondsman), tirodhāna (hiding), pañcavāha/cintāmaṇi (fivefold traffic/wish-jewel), kāṣṭhaloṣṭa (wood-clod).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 11 founds the limited in the limitless (bhāvitaḥ sa maheśānaḥ) and sets glory going. Verse 12 unreals the stages: kṛtrimāḥ, naṭavat svecchayā. Verse 13 expands by bliss-will into vibhūtis. Verses 14–15 stain (apūrṇo 'smi, malaiḥ) yet keep the five doings going — vyāpako 'pi... kurute. Verse 16 names the two readings of one self: vikāsāt śaktīśaḥ, saṅkocanāt saṃsārī. Verse 17 forms māyā, keeps by anya śakti. Verse 18 withdraws into devī, graces in cidaikya, hides from essence-showing. Verse 19 keeps the traffic always present while ignorance sees wood-clod where the jewel stands. Verse 20 (colophon bridge) — see p05.

## bodh-p03 (21–30) — reflection frees; fire devours; the freed frees

**SUMMARY.** True reflection constantly kept unbinds all bonds into eternal-bliss fullness. Waked, the all-devouring awareness-fire swallows; joined to its strength, the yogin soon reaches the stainless state. Gaining strength, the fire shines the world consciousness-made, self-bliss-nectar breaching outward; gone still, it shows through thing-forms while the own self plays on as God, supreme Lord. Who knows this constantly is freed and frees hosts in an instant from dread bondage. Bliss-powered, own-state-abiding, exultant-rising; own-nature-fixed, sought even in distraction, won by striving-folk. Middle-expansion and bliss-awareness shine; from their shine, knowing-formed divine sight. Spotless, radiant, pure, eternal-bliss-full, supreme — sight expanding past the body-cutting net.

**KEY TERMS.** sadvimarśabala (true-reflection strength), pariśīlana (constant keeping), cidvahni/sarvabhakṣa (awareness-fire/all-devouring), nirvāṇalakṣaṇa (release-marked), mocayati (frees), ānandabala (bliss-powered), vyutthāna (distraction), madhyadhāmavikāsa (middle-expansion), divyadṛṣṭi (divine sight), nirdehacchedajāla (body-cutting net).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 21 makes reflection, not effort, the bond-cutter (sadvimarśabalena, nityaṃ pariśīlate). Verses 22–24 wake the fire: grasate, suprabuddha-vimala, balalābhe the world ābhāti cinmayam, bliss-nectar breaching out. Verse 25 stills it into forms while svātmā plays Maheśvara. Verse 26 extends freedom transitively: mukto mocayati, kṣaṇena, ghorāt. Verses 27–28 fix bliss-powered own-state, sought even vyutthāne, won by janair eva — no specialist required. Verses 29–30 expand the middle into sight: divyadṛṣṭi jñānarūpā, past the body-cutting net.

## bodh-p04 (31–40) — think nothing; sport as lord; freedom unbroken

**SUMMARY.** In middle-expansion the means is a strong abiding: thought-decay, shrink-swell, many ways. From thinking-nothing thoughts waste; from awareness-knowing, play-power falls in love with the thing-field. Attached there, the stainless divine flood is seen. Resting bindu-nāda, end to beginning, grasping even fear-anger-disgust. So told, goddess: this middle-expansion — the capable wins supreme fortune in days. Wandering with inward-absorption traces, pure-rayed, by emission-strength reflecting again and again. Even in distraction his form shines entire — yogin awareness-fixed, eternal-bliss-juiced. Manifest-bliss-full, mantra-might-braced, full-I-awareness: the inward-exulting practitioner. There sport the yogin, mantra-lord stationed, capable, a lord playing power-braced. So his freedom — pervader, world-lord, difference-enjoyer, wonder-abiding, unbroken.

**KEY TERMS.** balavatsthiti (strong abiding), akiñciccintana (thinking-nothing), vilasacchakti/rakta (play-power/enamored), divyaugha (divine flood), bindu/nāda (drop/resonance), dinaiḥ (in days), visargabala (emission-strength), vimṛśya (reflecting), mantreśvarapada (mantra-lord station), krīḍate (plays), akhaṇḍitā (unbroken).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 31 stations the means in the middle: balavatsthiti, vikalpakṣaya-saṅkoca-vikāsa in many ways. Verse 32 wastes thought by thinking nothing, enamouring play-power of the field. Verse 33 sees the divyaugha the brahmavādins call śaktisaṅkoca — rival vocabulary absorbed. Verse 34 rests bindu-nāda ādyanta and grasps even the repellent affects. Verse 35 promises speed: dinair eva parasampad. Verses 36–37 keep reflecting by emission-strength till form shines even vyutthāne. Verse 38 braces manifest bliss with mantra-might into pūrṇāhantā consciousness. Verse 39 stations the sporting yogin as mantreśvara, śaktibṛṃhita lord at play. Verse 40 seals: svatantratā... akhaṇḍitā — freedom enjoying difference, unbroken.

## bodh-p05 (41–42) — nectar drunk; method stated; yogaśāstra signed

**SUMMARY.** This nonduality told is unsurpassed knowledge-nectar: drink, and saṃsāra-poison-fainting stills. This bliss-of-wake method serves great souls' Śiva-winning: King Kṣema's treatise, spotless, Kṣemarāja's Bodhavilāsa, a yoga-teaching.

**KEY TERMS.** advaya/jñānāmṛta/anuttama (nondual/knowledge-nectar/unsurpassed), pibecchāmyate (drink-stilled), saṃsāraviṣamūrchanam (existence-poison-fainting), bodhollāsavidhi (wake-bliss method), śivaprāpti (Śiva-winning), yogaśāstra (yoga-teaching).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 41 prescribes drinking, not building: pibecchāmyate — the cure is intake. Verse 42 dedicates method to mahātmanām and signs twice over: king's treatise, Kṣemarāja's making, bodhavilāsa by name, yogaśāstra by kind — practice-manual, not system-treatise.

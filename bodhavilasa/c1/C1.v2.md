# C1.v2 — Bodhavilāsa (condensation of the argument)

Free consciousness causes: self-bliss-manifest, she unfolds the world on her own ground by her own will, differenced into seers and seens. Contracted, that same awareness is mind — rule-bound, object-facing, knower-masked — yet palm-held release stands ready in recognizing I-ness. Cultivated through knowers and tattvas, the Lord's release-glory goes forth; stages assumed like an actor's parts. Bliss-will expands the self into vast powers; contraction soils it into incompleteness. All-pervading yet triple-stained, the self still does all five doings — lord by expansion, bondsman by contraction. True reflection constantly kept unbinds all bonds; waked, the all-devouring awareness-fire swallows, and joined to its strength the yogin soon reaches stainlessness. Gaining strength, the fire shines the world consciousness-made; gone still, the own self plays on as God. Who knows this constantly is freed and frees hosts in an instant. Bliss-powered, own-state-abiding, sought even in distraction; middle-expansion shines, and knowing-formed divine sight expands past the body-cutting net. Thinking nothing wastes thought; play-power falls in love with the field; the divine flood is seen. Resting drop-resonance, grasping even the repellent; capable, winning supreme fortune in days. Reflecting by emission-strength till form shines even in distraction; mantra-might bracing manifest bliss into full-I awareness; sporting, stationed mantra-lord, playing power-braced — freedom unbroken, difference-enjoyed. Drink the nondual nectar and still existence-poison-fainting: the wake-bliss method serves Śiva-winning. Kṣemarāja's Bodhavilāsa, a yoga-teaching.

## bodh-p01 (1–10)

Free cause, self-bliss-manifest; willed unfolding, seer-seen differenced. Contracted: mind, rule-bound knower-mask. Palm-held release in recognizing I-ness.

## bodh-p02 (11–20)

Release-glory forth through actor-stages; bliss-will expanding, contraction soiling. All-pervading yet stained, doing all five — lord by expansion, bondsman by contraction.

## bodh-p03 (21–30)

Reflection kept unbinds; fire waked devours; yogin soon stainless. Strength gained, world shines consciousness-made; the freed frees hosts instantly. Middle-expansion, divine sight past the net.

## bodh-p04 (31–40)

Think nothing, waste thought; play-power enamored. Flood seen, drop-resonance rested, repellent grasped. Capable in days; reflecting till form shines in distraction; mantra-braced, sporting lord — freedom unbroken.

## bodh-p05 (41–42)

Drink nondual nectar, still the fainting. Wake-bliss method for Śiva-winning: Bodhavilāsa, a yoga-teaching.

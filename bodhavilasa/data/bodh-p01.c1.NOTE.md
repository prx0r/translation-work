# C1 NOTE — bodh-p01

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Oṃ to Netra; constant salute to five-act Śiva, supreme-self bliss manifest. Consciousness free, world's cause so-called; knower freedom-formed, powers bestowed. Own ground, own will, world's unfolding; fit variety divides seer-seen. World's variety stands by object-difference, manifold-yoked high-low-numbered. Tightened knowing becomes mind, knower still; banyan-seed holds world-form, wise-known. Self's stainless knowing, rule-bound, named mind-knowing. Object-faced awareness rests in mind; the wise know mind as knowing. Knower knowing-formed abides embodied, clinging, discernment-void. Mind ever-one established through listed means and counts. Lord everywhere-pure; mis-knowing I-ness brings freedom palm-close.

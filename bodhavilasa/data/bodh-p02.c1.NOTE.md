# C1 NOTE — bodh-p02

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

By seven-knowerhood, by thirty-six count cultivated: the great Lord tends, release-glory proceeds. Stages doubtless yet made-up, actor-assumed by will. Vast-assumed shining; own-power-tightened impurity in wandering. Incompleteness confessed in impurities' words; Lordship kept all-pervading. Five acts mirrored god-to-self; delusion sole difference. Capable sung of expansion; contracted called wanderer of contraction. Willing glimpse, knowing made-making; keeping and withdrawn-loosing deemed. Withdrawn goddess graces oneness; essence-showing named concealment. Ever-present course known as jewel; unknowing timber-clod.

# C1 NOTE — bodh-p03

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Who walks by true reflection's power always is freed of all bonds, bliss-full. Fire swallows, well-awake; all-devouring to be known when pure. United by this power, freedom-marked attains purity soon, doubtless. Power grown, universe shines knowing-made; nectar-wave outward. Gone still, form-shown: self plays Lord supreme. Always-knowing frees; freed frees scores instantly. Bliss-powered established; rising-joy-thus bridges onward. Fixed-nature desired even distracted; effort-power attains. Middle opened, bliss-knowing shines; divine vision manifested. Immaculate ranks expand bodiless knowing-vision.

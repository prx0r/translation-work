# C1 NOTE — bodh-p04

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Opening holds means: powerful thought-decay-opening state, manifold. Nothing thought, thoughts decay; play-power enamoured in being's field. Attached sees; tightening brahma-teachers call it — fie. Rest-points and passion-grasps listed whole. Goddess-voiced opening; strong obtain supreme reward in days. Samādhi-impressed roams pure-rayed, reflecting by emission-power doubled. Even distracted, own form shines for awareness-fixed bliss-essenced yogi. Bliss-manifest mantra-strong full-I-knowing: inward devotee's. Sporting stayed mantra-situated; strong playing power-expanded. Freedom his, everywhere, world-Lord's, difference-tasting, wonder-state-unbroken.

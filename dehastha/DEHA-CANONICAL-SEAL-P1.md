## The parable (canonical draft, ≈80s)

> A man is told his body is a temple — and not a borrowed one: the
> god who lives there is eternal, and is him. First he sweeps: the
> sweepings of ignorance go out like shrine-dust. Then the eight
> begin their walk — each petal of the heart-lotus sends its own
> gift to the center: mind brings its flower of distinctions,
> hearing brings sound, touch brings touch, sight brings sight,
> taste and smell behind them — every sense a goddess bearing her
> own flower, thoughts themselves the gifts — until the whole
> circle honors the one who lives there. And the one who hymns it
> feels it: the quivering pith of experience, within all, ever
> close at hand — his own body's circle of gods, arisen now.

**Map (hinge):**
- body = temple, god = eternal = him → p01 v1 [p01]
- sweeping-out ignorance like shrine-dust → p01 v1 (*casting off
  the sweepings of ignorance*) [p01]
- I-am-He feeling → folded into "is him" [p01]
- eight walk; each petal its own flower-gift; thoughts =
  gifts; circle honors the living-one → p01 vv5–8 + p02 vv1–6
  (six sense-goddesses with their flowers: mind/sound/touch/sight/
  taste/smell — one line each pair) [p01+p02]
- quivering pith within-all close-at-hand; own body's circle arisen
  → p02 vv7–8 close [p02]
- (FOLDS flagged: p01's salutation-hosts — Gaṇapati/Vaṭuka/guru —
  ride "the one who lives there"; p02's petal-DIRECTIONS south→
  west compressed to "each petal"; p02 v7 field-lord/siddhi-giver
  epithets ride the honoring-circle line.)

**Limit:** the petal-goddesses never become eight named figures
(the LOTUS does the walking — petals-as-gift-bearers = one subject
performed by its own parts, spider-class); direction-system folded.
**Rasa:** vismaya-rati (wonder-love held together) → śama ("arisen
now" — the hymn's own close). The pulse: sweep → the walking gifts
→ the quiver.
**Bar check:** one subject (the man, whose chest IS the lotus —
his body the temple made literal); retellable: "he sweeps the
shrine-dust, and his own petals walk their flowers home, and the
pith quivers"; plain (sense-goddesses named only as senses).

## The seal — statement

**The chest that opens into its own circle:** a standing man whose
torso opens (x-ray-gently) into a heart-lotus — eight petals, each
mid-walk carrying a small blossom toward the center; the center a
sanctum-lamp (the god = him); at the base grey sweepings being
swept OUT (ignorance leaving as dust); one fine trembling gold ray
runs from the sanctum UP SHORT to his own brow (the quivering pith,
within-all, close at hand — the image closes INSIDE him, no sky
escape: the circle is his body's).

## Composition laws (this seal)

ONE subject (the man; petals = his own body's parts, never eight
figures). Blossoms vary but stay small (the SENSES are the
variety, not the ornament). The ray must QUIVER (wavering stroke —
the pith's quivering is literal) and stay short (close-at-hand
law). No sky, no escape — the seal closes inside the subject.

# NATURE-PASS v2 — THE HIVE WHERE THE SENSES BRING THE POLLEN
**One-line:** *"he swept his own shrine clean and his own senses
flew home as bees with flower-loads, and the queen-hum at the
center would not stop."*

## Parable v2 (≈78s)
> A man is told his body is a temple — and not a borrowed one: the
> god who lives there is him. First he sweeps: out goes the old
> junk of everything he believed instead. Then the bees come home
> — his own senses, each with a load: eyes with sight-dust, ears
> with sound-pollen, skin, tongue, nose and the thinking one, every
> bee laying its flower-stuff at the heart of the hive. And there
> at the center the queen hums: something quivering, so close
> you'd miss it looking out. His own hive, full of gods, just
> arrived.

(Nature-first: the text's "sense-goddesses with flower-feasts in
the heart-lotus" IS a hive-event — pollen carried home to the
center; "quivering pith" = the hum (the vibration, as owner
named); temple KEPT as the told-doctrine (body-is-temple is the
claim), hive = the activity around it — doctrine untouched,
phenomenon natural. Six pollen-loads = p02's six senses exact;
eight-petals from v1 kept as the visual in the seal.)

## Seal v2 — THE HIVE-TEMPLE
The man whose opened torso shows the comb: eight golden cells
(the petals) in a ring; six bees mid-flight home, each carrying
its distinct flower-load toward the center cell; center = the
queen-hum: a small vibrating ring (the quiver drawn as motion-
lines, the vibration ITSELF); base = the sweepings carried OUT
(junk leaving below, two worker-bees removing it? no — the
sweeping stays the man's hand at base, plain); inside the cells:
tiny warm lights (the gods in their rooms). Colours: wax-gold
comb · ivory man · varied pollen (crimson/gold/lapis grains) ·
grey junk leaving · vibrating ivory center-ring. One subject;
bees = his own senses (flagged as his-parts, not strangers).

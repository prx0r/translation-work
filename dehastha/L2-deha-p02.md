# Dehastha deha-p02 — L2 READ

## v.9
She spreads Bhairava's worship, seated in the southern petal, with flowers of distinction — ever mind-formed Kaumārī, her, I praise.
The Sanskrit: *vidadhāti bhairavārcāṃ dakṣiṇadalagā viṣeśakusumairyā | nityaṃ manaḥsvarūpāṃ kaumārīṃ tāmahaṃ vande*

## v.10
Seated in the southwest petal she worships Bhairava with flowers of sound — to hearing-formed her, ever, I bow, the Vaiṣṇavī power.
The Sanskrit: *nairṛtadalagā bhairavamarcayate śabdakusumairyā | praṇamāmi śrutirūpāṃ nityaṃ tāṃ vaiṣṇavīṃ śaktim*

## v.11
Standing firm in the western quarter's petal, with heart-delighting flowers of touch she delights Bhairava — her who wears the form of skin I honor: Vārāhī.
The Sanskrit: *paścimadigdalasaṃsthā hṛdayaharaiḥ sparśakusumairyā | toṣayati bhairavaṃ tāṃ tvagrūpadharāṃ namāmi vārāhīm*

## v.12
With choicer details of form, her body seated in the wind-quarter's petal — she who honors Bhairava, her Indrāṇī, sight-bodied, I praise.
The Sanskrit: *varatararūpaviśeṣairmārutadigdalaniṣaṇṇadehā yā | pūjayati bhairavaṃ tāmindrāṇīṃ dṛktanuṃ vande*

## v.13
Dwelling in the sprout of the wealth-lord, she who ever with foods of varied six tastes honors Bhairava — her called tongue I honor: Cāmuṇḍā.
The Sanskrit: *dhanapatikisalayanilayā yā nityaṃ vividhaṣaḍrasāhāraiḥ | pūjayati bhairavaṃ tāṃ jihvābhikhyāṃ namāmi cāmuṇḍām*

## v.14
Seated in Īśa's petal she worships Bhairava with manifold perfumes — to her called nose, Mahālakṣmī, I ever bow.
The Sanskrit: *īśadalasthā bhairavamarcayate parimalairvicitrairyā | praṇamāmi sarvadā tāṃ ghrāṇābhikhyāṃ mahālakṣmīm*

## v.15
Worshipable in the six views, infused with the thirty-six realities, ever self-named — the field-lord, giver of siddhis, I praise.
The Sanskrit: *ṣaḍdarśaneṣu pūjyaṃ ṣaḍtriṃśattattvasaṃvalitam | ātmābhikhyaṃ satataṃ kṣetrapatiṃ siddhidaṃ vande*

## v.16
The quivering pith of experience, within all, ever close at hand — thus ever arisen I hymn my own body's circle of gods.
The Sanskrit: *saṃsphuradanubhavasāraṃ sarvāntaḥ satatasannihitam | naumi sadoditamitthaṃ nijadehagadevatācakram*

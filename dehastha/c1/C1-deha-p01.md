# C1 — deha-p01

## SUMMARY

Body named temple, self named eternal god: ignorance swept off, worship in I-am-He feeling. Breath-bodied Gaṇapati first, saluted by all, giving wished boons. Vaṭuka apāna-named, adepts at his feet, lifting humble pain and doubt. The true guru spotless, whose mind-strength shines the world Śiva-pathed. Sense-goddesses feasting the heart-lotus for consciousness-made bliss-Bhairava. Bhairavī reflection-formed, dawning and digesting the world unceasing. Brāhmī intellect-formed offering resolve-flowers from the lord's petal. Śāmbhavī mother offering I-flowers from fire's petal.

## FUNCTION

Sets body as temple, posts breath as gatekeepers, gives attention as the guru's gift, seats bliss at the core, and starts the flower-offerings.

## KEY TERMS

deho devālayaḥ (body-as-temple — the ground of the whole round); so'ham (I-am-He feeling as worship-mode); prāṇa/apāna (outgoing/ingoing breaths as gatekeepers); avadhāna (attention as the guru's gift); cinmaya (made of consciousness); vimarśa (reflection as her form); kusuma (flowers as sense-offerings); ahaṅkṛti (I-making as act, not thing).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Gatekeepers honored and the presiding pair enthroned, perception and ego lead the directional round.

## EXPLANATION

The body turns from dwelling to temple and the self from tenant to god, with one act of sweeping and one feeling of identity. Then the doors: prāṇa and apāna as Gaṇapati and Vaṭuka, worshipped first because breath holds the house together. The guru gives the only instrument that matters — attention — by which the world shines walkable toward Śiva. At the center sits bliss, consciousness-made, fed by the senses themselves; beside him reflection, digesting the world she dawns. The circle starts dealing its flowers: resolve from intellect in the east, I-ness from ego in the southeast fire.

## BOUNDARY

All giving here is praise; nothing is demanded.

5. Sense-goddesses feast the heart-lotus: consciousness-made bliss-Bhairava at the core.
6. Bhairavī's ceaseless world-play of dawning and digesting: reflection-formed, ever praised.
7. Brāhmī intellect-formed offers resolve-flowers from the lord's petal: perception confirmed.
8. Śāmbhavī mother offers I-flowers from fire's petal: ego itself the oblation.

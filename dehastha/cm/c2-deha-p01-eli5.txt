Your body is a temple and you are the god who lives there. Sweep out the dusty old thinking, and pray the way that says I am Him. At the door stands the elephant god — everyone from demons to gods bows to him, and he loves giving exactly the gift you wished for. [E0] [E1]

Next to him stands the Boy, worshipped by heroes, yoginis, and perfected saints — he lifts the pain of humble learners. Then the spotless true teacher: by his mind's strength your whole world lights up as God's road. [E2] [E3]

In your heart sits blissful Bhairava, made of pure knowing — and your senses, like goddesses, keep offering him everything they taste and touch and see. Beside him his queen Bhairavī, made of pure noticing — she lights up the world and turns it over and over, and she never stops. [E4] [E5]

From the east, Brāhmī — your power to know — lays down flowers of sure decisions. From the southeast, mother Śāmbhavī — your I-maker — lays down flowers of I-ness itself. [E6] [E7]

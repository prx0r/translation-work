From the south, Kaumārī — mind herself — offers flowers made of thoughts. From the southwest, Vaiṣṇavī — hearing herself — offers flowers made of sounds. [E0] [E1]

From the west, Vārāhī — skin herself — pleases Bhairava with flowers of touch. From the northwest, Indrāṇī — sight herself — honors him with the choicest shapes. [E2] [E3]

From the north, Cāmuṇḍā — tongue herself — honors him with all six tastes on the menu. From the northeast, Mahālakṣmī — nose herself — is worshipped with lovely perfumes. The whole circle is complete. [E4] [E5]

Over it all stands the field-lord — your own self with a name, packed with all thirty-six realities, praised by every school of thought, handing out powers. And the last bow goes to the whole ringing circle itself — quivering, everywhere inside, always close: your own god-ring, hymned as you. [E6] [E7]

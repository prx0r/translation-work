# C1 NOTE — deha-p01

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Body named temple, self named eternal god: ignorance swept, worship in I-am-He feeling. Breath-bodied Gaṇapati first-worshipped, saluted by all, giving wished boons. Vaṭuka apāna-named, adepts at his feet, remover of humble pain and doubt. Sadguru mind-strength making the universe shine Śiva-pathed: attention itself the gift. Sense-goddesses feast the heart-lotus for consciousness-made bliss-Bhairava. Bhairavī reflection-formed, dawning and digesting the world unceasing. Brāhmī intellect-formed offering resolve-flowers from the lord's petal. Śāmbhavī mother offering I-flowers from fire's petal.

## VOICE MAP

Poet framing maṅgala v1; gatekeepers Gaṇapati v2, Vaṭuka v3; guru v4; presiding Bhairava v5, Bhairavī v6; east petal Brāhmī v7; Agni petal Śāmbhavī v8.

## KEY TERMS

* deho devālayaḥ — body-as-temple (ground of the whole round)
* so'ham — I-am-He feeling as worship-mode
* prāṇa/apāna — outgoing/ingoing breaths as gatekeepers
* avadhāna — attention as the guru's gift
* cinmaya — made of consciousness
* vimarśa — reflection as her form
* kusuma — flowers as sense-offerings
* ahaṅkṛti — I-making as act, not thing

## CRUXES

* All giving here is praise; nothing is demanded.
* Fused spans kept whole throughout (v4 tamahamavadhānarūpaṃ, v6 vimarśarūpāmahaṃ, v7 tāmahaṃ, v8 giant compound with avagraha) — Pada order follows print, gloss compounds hyphenated.

## FUNCTION

Sets body as temple, posts breath as gatekeepers, gives attention as the guru's gift, seats bliss at the core, and starts the flower-offerings.

# C1 NOTE — deha-p02

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Kaumārī mind-formed offering distinction-flowers from the south: thoughts themselves the gift. Vaiṣṇavī hearing-formed offering sound-flowers from the southwest: listening itself the rite. Vārāhī skin-formed delighting with touch-flowers from the west: feeling itself pleasing. Indrāṇī sight-bodied honoring with choicer forms from the northwest: seeing itself worship. Cāmuṇḍā tongue-called honoring with six-taste foods from the north: tasting itself offered. Mahālakṣmī nose-called worshipped with perfumes from the northeast: smelling itself the rite; circle complete. Kṣetrapati self-named, 36-reality-infused, worshipped in six views: the experiencer guards the field. The quivering experience-pith ever near: one's own god-circle hymned as first-person presence.

## VOICE MAP

Poet: south petal Kaumārī v9; southwest petal Vaiṣṇavī v10; west petal Vārāhī v11; northwest petal Indrāṇī v12; north petal Cāmuṇḍā v13; northeast petal Mahālakṣmī v14; field-lord v15; seal v16.

## KEY TERMS

* kusuma-pattern — each sense offers its own flowers (resolve, distinction, sound, touch, forms, tastes, perfumes)
* manas/śruti/tvac/dṛk/jihvā/ghrāṇa — six sense-powers as goddesses
* kṣetrapati — field-lord as first-person experiencer
* ṣaṭtriṃśattattva — thirty-six as the full map
* ṣaḍdarśana — six views as unanimous witness
* viśeṣa — distinction (edition text, vikalpa variant recorded)
* śabda — sound (edition text, śabdarūpā variant recorded)

## CRUXES

* Distinction-flowers and sound-flowers stand as offered; the thoughts-reading and the sound-formed reading wait aside.
* v16 sarvāntaḥ resolved adverbial (in-all-ends): accusative chain + Dyczkowski's collective rendering; nominative bahuvrīhi noted but ungrammatical here.

## FUNCTION

Completes the offerings sense by sense, gathers them into the field's one experiencer, and rests the round in first-person presence.

## RELATED

Dyczkowski per-verse exposition (petal-direction mapping, Kṣetrapati as first-person experiencer); print variants v9 (vikalpa) and v10 (śabdarūpā) flagged in argmaps; Sharada ms (Jammu No 67) different opening — transmission noted, not collated.

# Dehastha lore — 2026-11-04 (verse-usable)

## Work
Abhinavagupta's Dehasthadevatācakrastotra — Hymn to the circle of deities residing in the body. Edition: sanskritdocuments.org (Ruma Dewan, from Abhinavastotravali print, Dec 2021). 16 verses, 2 songs (deha-p01 vv1-8, deha-p02 vv9-16). Colophon attributes the text to Abhinavaguptapādācārya (excluded from segments as scribal paratext; recorded here).

## Structure (Dyczkowski, anuttaratrikakula.org two-part blog + amritananda forum commentary)
- v1 maṅgala: body as temple (deho devālayaḥ), so'ham-bhāva worship after casting off ignorance-sweepings.
- vv2-3 gatekeepers: Gaṇapati = prāṇa (outgoing breath), adored by gods/demons; Vaṭuka = apāna (ingoing breath), worshipped by vīras/siddhas/yoginīs, remover of disciples' knots/doubts.
- v4 guru: Sadguru, pure attention-form (avadhāna-rūpa), by whose intellect-strength the world shines as Śiva-path.
- v5-6 presiding pair: Ānanda-Bhairava (cinmaya, heart-lotus center, Cakreśvara) fed by sense-goddesses; Ānanda-Bhairavī (vimarśa-rūpā) as world-play enjoyment.
- vv7-14 directional circle (8 petals): E Brāhmī (buddhi, niścaya-flowers); Agni Śāmbhavī (ahaṅkāra, I-flowers); S Kaumārī (manas, vikalpa-flowers — print variant vikalpa- for viśeṣa-); SW Vaiṣṇavī (śruti, sound-flowers — variant śabda-rūpā for śruti-rūpā); W Vārāhī (tvac/touch); NW Indrāṇī (dṛk/sight); N Cāmuṇḍā (jihvā/tongue, six-taste foods); NE Mahālakṣmī (ghrāṇa/smell, perfumes).
- v15 Kṣetrapati: worshipped in six darśanas, 36-tattva-bodied, ātman-named, siddhi-giving field-lord.
- v16 seal: collective bow to the ever-present experience-essence circle in one's own body.

## Recension notes (byte quirks kept, never smoothed)
- v1 (deho devālayaḥ) absent in some prints (Dyczkowski starts at Gaṇapati) — our edition's v1 kept; numbering follows the Abhinavastotravali print.
- vv4-5 order swapped in some prints (guru/Bhairava) — noted, not adopted.
- v9 inline variant: viśeṣa-kusumair (our text) vs vikalpa-kusumair (print variant) — fork lives in L0.
- v10 inline variant: śruti-rūpām (our text) vs śabda-rūpām (print variant) — fork lives in L0.
- Sharada ms (Jammu No 67, via shardalipi/archive.org) opens differently (namaś-śivāya/oṃ genealogy) — different transmission, recorded not collated.

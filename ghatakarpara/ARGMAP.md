# Ghatakarpara — ARGMAP

## ghat-p01 (1–9)
- speaker: Kāvya voice (proṣitapramadā, separated beloved).
- question: What is the lover's lament?
- premises: Manmathe ca hṛdi hantum udyate (Kāma in heart, to kill, arises). Proṣitapramadayedam udyate (this, separated beloved, arises). Nirdayena paradeśasevinā māriṣyatha hi tena māṃ vinā (by cruel one serving foreign land, you will kill me without him). Anyadeśaratir adya mucyatāṃ sāthavā tava vadhūḥ kim ucyatām (foreign-land love today be released; or your wife, what to say). Cātakaś ca tṛṣito 'mbu yācate (cātaka, thirsty, water begs). Duḥkhitā pathika sā priyā ca te (suffering traveler, she, beloved, yours). Ambudaiḥ śikhigaṇ... (by clouds, peacock-flock...).
- inference: The lover's lament = Kāma arises in heart; separated beloved; cruel foreign-serving one will kill without him; release foreign love or wife; cātaka thirsty begs water; suffering traveler, beloved yours.
- conclusion: Lament stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## ghat-p02 (10–18)
- speaker: Kāvya voice.
- question: What is the beauty of the grove?
- premises: Madanasya kṛte niketanānāṃ pratibhānty adya vanāni ketanānām (for Kāma's sake, today groves of abodes shine, of abodes). Tvaṃ mañjarībhiḥ pravaro vanānāṃ netrotsavaś cāsi sayauvanānām (you are chief of groves by sprouts, eye-festival of youth). Kuṭaja kiṃ kusumair avahasyate (kuṭaja, why laughed at by flowers). Praṇipatāmi ca duṣprahasya te (I bow, hard to laugh at you). Tava puṣpanirīkṣitāpadehaṃ visṛjeyaṃ sahasaiva nīpa deham (your flower-looked-at body, I would release, quickly, nīpa, body). Madhuna... (honey...).
- inference: Beauty of grove = for Kāma's sake, groves of abodes shine; you are chief of groves by sprouts, eye-festival of youth; kuṭaja laughed at by flowers; I bow, hard to laugh; flower-looked-at body released.
- conclusion: Beauty stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## ghat-p03 (19–21)
- speaker: Kāvya voice (close).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: Utkaṇṭhayanti pathikān jaladāḥ svanantaḥ (clouds thundering, make travelers long). Śoko vivardhati ca tadvinatāsv anantaḥ (sorrow grows in those, endless). Retyutsavaṃ priyatamaiḥ saha mānayanti (love-festival with beloveds, honor). Meghāgamaṃ priyasakhi śvasamānayanti (cloud-arrival, dear friend, they bring). Jīyeya yena kavinā yamakaiḥ pareṇa (by which poet, by yamakas, other, conquered). Tasmai vaheyam udakaṃ ghaṭakarpareṇa (to him, I pour water in ghaṭakarpara).
- inference: Close = clouds thundering make travelers long; sorrow grows; love-festival with beloveds; cloud-arrival; by which poet conquered by yamakas; to him, pour water in ghaṭakarpara.
- conclusion: The poem closes with tribute to the poet.
- unresolved: none.

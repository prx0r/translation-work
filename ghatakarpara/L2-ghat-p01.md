# Ghatakarpara ghat-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
In the heart, Kāma wakes to kill.
The Sanskrit: *manmathe ca hṛdi hantum udyate*

## v.2
This — the separated beloved — arises.
The Sanskrit: *nirdayena paradeśasevinā māriṣyatha hi | tena māṃ vinā*

## v.3
By that cruel one who serves a foreign land, you will kill me — without him.
The Sanskrit: *anyadeśaratir adya mucyatāṃ sāthavā | tava vadhūḥ kim ucyatām*

## v.4
Release your foreign love today — or your wife: what more is there to say?
The Sanskrit: *cātakaś ca tṛṣito 'mbu yācate duḥkhitā | pathika sā priyā ca te*

## v.5
The cātaka, gasping, begs for rain — and nothing else.
The Sanskrit: *ambudaiḥ śikhigaṇo vinādyate kā ratiḥ | priya mayā vinādya te*

## v.6
The suffering traveler — she is your beloved.
The Sanskrit: *toyadāgamakṛśā ca sādya te | durdhareṇa madanena sādyate*

## v.7
By clouds the peacock-flock is sounded; what delight, beloved, without me today?
The Sanskrit: *śokasāgarajale ca pātitāṃ tvadguṇasmaraṇam | eva pāti tām*

## v.8
Thinned by cloud-arrival, she today; tormented by hard Kāma.
The Sanskrit: *dravati ca kaluṣaṃ jalaṃ nadīnāṃ kim | iti ca māṃ samavekṣase na dīnām*

## v.9
Drowned in sorrow-ocean's water; remembrance of your virtue alone keeps her afloat. The rivers' water melts the impurity. Why do you regard me, wretched as I am? Pained by deep cloud-rasa — when, friend, shall I be free of this burning, born of separation from my beloved?
The Sanskrit: *gambhīramegharasitavyathitā kadāhaṃ jahyāṃ | sakhi priyaviyogajaśokadāhaṃ*

# Ghatakarpara ghat-p02 — L2 READ

## v.10
For Kāma's sake, today the groves of abodes shine.
The Sanskrit: *madanasya kṛte niketanānāṃ pratibhānty adya vanāni ketanānām*

## v.11
You are chief of groves by sprouts, eye-festival of youths.
The Sanskrit: *tvaṃ mañjarībhiḥ pravaro vanānāṃ netrotsavaś cāsi sayauvanānām*

## v.12
Kuṭaja, why are you laughed at by flowers? I bow — hard to laugh at you.
The Sanskrit: *kuṭaja kiṃ kusumair avahasyate praṇipatāmi ca duṣprahasya te*

## v.13
Your flower-looked-at body — I would release it quickly, nīpa, body.
The Sanskrit: *tava puṣpanirīkṣitāpadehaṃ visṛjeyaṃ sahasaiva nīpa deham*

## v.14
Having observed honey's season, the bee kisses the yūthikā creeper.
The Sanskrit: *madhunaḥ samavekṣya kālatāṃ bhramaraś cumbati yūthikālatām*

## v.15
By waters scattered, dust on earth; sun and moon are not seen.
The Sanskrit: *salilair vihataṃ rajaḥ kṣitau ravicandrāv api nopalakṣitau*

## v.16
New-cloud-drunk, peacocks cry at cloud-arrival.
The Sanskrit: *navāmbumattāḥ śikhino nadanti meghāgame*

## v.17
With Indra's weapon, the cloud today resounds, bringing the elephants' ardor, mountain-like.
The Sanskrit: *sendrayudhaś ca jalado 'dya rasann ibhānāṃ saṃrambham āvahati bhūdharasannibhānām*

## v.18
Loud roar — water in caves falls, wonderful-formed beauties.
The Sanskrit: *paridhīraravaṃ jalaṃ darīṣu prapaty adbhutarūpasundarīṣu*

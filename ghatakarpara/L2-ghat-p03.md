# Ghatakarpara ghat-p03 — L2 READ

## v.19
Clouds thundering make travelers long; sorrow grows in those, endless.
The Sanskrit: *utkaṇṭhayanti pathikāṇ jaladāḥ svanantaḥ śoko vivardhati ca tadvinatāsv anantaḥ*

## v.20
They honor the love-festival with beloveds; cloud-arrival, dear friend, they bring.
The Sanskrit: *ratyutsavaṃ priyatamaiḥ saha mānayanti meghāgamaṃ priyasakhi śvasamānayanti*

## v.21
May I be conquered by which poet, by yamakas, other; to him I pour water in ghaṭakarpara.
The Sanskrit: *jīyeya yena kavinā yamakaiḥ pareṇa tasmai vaheyam udakaṃ ghaṭakarpareṇa*

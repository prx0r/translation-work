# L2

## v.1 — Kāma arises
Kāma arises in the heart, to kill. The Sanskrit: *manmathe ca hṛdi hantum udyate*

## v.2 — the separated beloved
This, the separated beloved, arises. The Sanskrit: *proṣitapramadayedam udyate*

## v.3 — accusation
By the cruel one serving a foreign land, you will kill me without him. The Sanskrit: *nirdayena paradeśasevinā māriṣyatha hi tena māṃ vinā*

## v.4 — release
Release foreign-land love today, or your wife — what is to be said? The Sanskrit: *anyadeśaratir adya mucyatāṃ sāthavā tava vadhūḥ kim ucyatām*

## v.5 — the cātaka begs
The cātaka, thirsty, begs water. The Sanskrit: *cātakaś ca tṛṣito 'mbu yācate*

## v.6 — the suffering traveler
The suffering traveler, she is your beloved. The Sanskrit: *duḥkhitā pathika sā priyā ca te*

## v.7 — the peacock-flock sounded
By clouds the peacock-flock is sounded; what delight, beloved, without me today? The Sanskrit: *ambudaiḥ śikhigaṇo vinādyate kā ratiḥ priya mayā vinādya te*

## v.8 — thinned by cloud-arrival
Thinned by cloud-arrival, she today; tormented by hard Kāma. The Sanskrit: *toyadāgamakṛśā ca sādya te durdhareṇa madanena sādyate*

## v.9 — fallen, protected, appealed
Fallen into sorrow-ocean's water; remembrance of your virtue alone protects her. The rivers' water melts the impurity. Why do you regard me, not wretched? Pained by deep cloud-rasa — when shall I abandon, friend, the burning of separation-born sorrow? The Sanskrit: *śokasāgarajale ca pātitāṃ tvadguṇasmaraṇam eva pāti tām | dravati ca kaluṣaṃ jalaṃ nadīnāṃ | kim iti ca māṃ samavekṣase na dīnām | gambhīramegharasitavyathitā kadāhaṃ jahyāṃ sakhi priyaviyogajaśokadāhaṃ*

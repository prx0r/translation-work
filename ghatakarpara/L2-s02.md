# L2

## v.10 — for Kāma's sake
For Kāma's sake, today the groves of abodes shine. The Sanskrit: *madanasya kṛte niketanānāṃ pratibhānty adya vanāni ketanānām*

## v.11 — chief of groves
You are chief of groves by sprouts, eye-festival of youths. The Sanskrit: *tvaṃ mañjarībhiḥ pravaro vanānāṃ netrotsavaś cāsi sayauvanānām*

## v.12 — the kuṭaja's mockery
Kuṭaja, why are you laughed at by flowers? I bow, hard to laugh at you. The Sanskrit: *kuṭaja kiṃ kusumair avahasyate praṇipatāmi ca duṣprahasya te*

## v.13 — the nīpa released
Your flower-looked-at body, I would release, quickly, nīpa, body. The Sanskrit: *tava puṣpanirīkṣitāpadehaṃ visṛjeyaṃ sahasaiva nīpa deham*

## v.14 — the bee kisses the creeper
Having observed honey's season, the bee kisses the yūthikā creeper. The Sanskrit: *madhunaḥ samavekṣya kālatāṃ bhramaraś cumbati yūthikālatām*

## v.15 — dust obscures
By waters scattered, dust on earth; sun and moon are not seen. The Sanskrit: *salilair vihataṃ rajaḥ kṣitau ravicandrāv api nopalakṣitau*

## v.16 — peacocks cry
New-cloud-drunk, peacocks cry at cloud-arrival. The Sanskrit: *navāmbumattāḥ śikhino nadanti meghāgame*

## v.17 — the cloud roars
With Indra's weapon, the cloud today resounds, bringing the elephants' ardor, mountain-like. The Sanskrit: *sendrayudhaś ca jalado 'dya rasann ibhānāṃ saṃrambham āvahati bhūdharasannibhānām*

## v.18 — water falls
Loud roar, water in caves falls, wonderful-formed beauties. The Sanskrit: *paridhīraravaṃ jalaṃ darīṣu prapaty adbhutarūpasundarīṣu*

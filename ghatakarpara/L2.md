# Ghatakarpara — L2 READ (Dyczkowski-mode)

## ghat-p01–p03 — lament, beauty, close
**SUMMARY.** Manmathe ca hṛdi hantum udyate — Kāma in heart, to kill, arises. Proṣitapramadayedam udyate — this, separated beloved, arises. Nirdayena paradeśasevinā māriṣyatha hi tena māṃ vinā — by cruel one serving foreign land, you will kill me without him. Anyadeśaratir adya mucyatāṃ sāthavā tava vadhūḥ kim ucyatām — foreign-land love today be released; or your wife, what to say. Cātakaś ca tṛṣito 'mbu yācate — cātaka, thirsty, water begs. Duḥkhitā pathika sā priyā ca te — suffering traveler, she, beloved, yours. Madanasya kṛte niketanānāṃ pratibhānty adya vanāni ketanānām — for Kāma's sake, today groves of abodes shine. Tvaṃ mañjarībhiḥ pravaro vanānāṃ netrotsavaś cāsi sayauvanānām — you are chief of groves by sprouts, eye-festival of youth. Kuṭaja kiṃ kusumair avahasyate — kuṭaja, why laughed at by flowers. Praṇipatāmi ca duṣprahasya te — I bow, hard to laugh at you. Tava puṣpanirīkṣitāpadehaṃ visṛjeyaṃ sahasaiva nīpa deham — your flower-looked-at body, I would release, quickly, nīpa, body. Utkaṇṭhayanti pathikān jaladāḥ svanantaḥ — clouds thundering, make travelers long. Śoko vivardhati ca tadvinatāsv anantaḥ — sorrow grows in those, endless. Retyutsavaṃ priyatamaiḥ saha mānayanti — love-festival with beloveds, honor. Meghāgamaṃ priyasakhi śvasamānayanti — cloud-arrival, dear friend, they bring. Jīyeya yena kavinā yamakaiḥ pareṇa — by which poet, by yamakas, other, conquered. Tasmai vaheyam udakaṃ ghaṭakarpareṇa — to him, I pour water in ghaṭakarpara.

**FUNCTION.** Kāvya (love poem) with Abhinava's vivṛti: lament of separated beloved, beauty of grove, close with tribute to poet.

**KEY TERMS.** pramadā (beloved), proṣita (separated), madana (Kāma), cātaka (cātaka bird), pathika (traveler), ambuda (cloud), mañjarī (sprout), kuṭaja (kuṭaja tree), nīpa (nīpa tree), jalada (cloud), ratyutsava (love-festival), yamaka (yamaka), ghaṭakarpara (potsherd).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Entire text; 3 passages.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in three steps. (1) Lament: Kāma in heart, to kill, arises; this, separated beloved, arises; by cruel foreign-serving one, kill without him; release foreign love or wife; cātaka thirsty begs water; suffering traveler, beloved yours. (2) Beauty: for Kāma's sake, groves of abodes shine; you are chief of groves by sprouts, eye-festival of youth; kuṭaja laughed at by flowers; I bow, hard to laugh; flower-looked-at body released. (3) Close: clouds thundering make travelers long; sorrow grows; love-festival with beloveds; cloud-arrival; by which poet conquered by yamakas; to him, pour water in ghaṭakarpara.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are poetic; L2 groups them.

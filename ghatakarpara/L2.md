## ghat-p01 — the lament (verses 1–9)

## v.1 — Kāma arises
Kāma rises in the heart — to kill. The Sanskrit: *manmathe ca hṛdi hantum udyate*

## v.2 — the separated beloved
This, the separated beloved, arises. The Sanskrit: *proṣitapramadayedam udyate*

## v.3 — accusation
By the cruel one serving a foreign land, you will kill me without him. The Sanskrit: *nirdayena paradeśasevinā māriṣyatha hi tena māṃ vinā*

## v.4 — release
Release foreign-land love today — or your wife: what is to be said? The Sanskrit: *anyadeśaratir adya mucyatāṃ sāthavā tava vadhūḥ kim ucyatām*

## v.5 — the cātaka begs
The cātaka, parched, begs for rain. The Sanskrit: *cātakaś ca tṛṣito 'mbu yācate*

## v.6 — the suffering traveler
The suffering traveler — she is your beloved. The Sanskrit: *duḥkhitā pathika sā priyā ca te*

## v.7 — the peacock-flock sounded
By clouds the peacock-flock is sounded; what delight, beloved, without me today? The Sanskrit: *ambudaiḥ śikhigaṇo vinādyate kā ratiḥ priya mayā vinādya te*

## v.8 — thinned by cloud-arrival
Thinned by cloud-arrival, she today; tormented by hard Kāma. The Sanskrit: *toyadāgamakṛśā ca sādya te durdhareṇa madanena sādyate*

## v.9 — fallen, protected, appealed
Drowned in sorrow-ocean's water; remembrance of your virtue alone keeps her afloat. The rivers' water melts the impurity. Why do you regard me, not wretched? Pained by deep cloud-rasa — when shall I abandon, friend, the burning of separation-born sorrow? The Sanskrit: *śokasāgarajale ca pātitāṃ tvadguṇasmaraṇam eva pāti tām | dravati ca kaluṣaṃ jalaṃ nadīnāṃ | kim iti ca māṃ samavekṣase na dīnām | gambhīramegharasitavyathitā kadāhaṃ jahyāṃ sakhi priyaviyogajaśokadāhaṃ*

## ghat-p02 — the beauty of the grove (verses 10–18)

## v.10 — for Kāma's sake
For Kāma's sake, today the groves of abodes shine. The Sanskrit: *madanasya kṛte niketanānāṃ pratibhānty adya vanāni ketanānām*

## v.11 — chief of groves
You are chief of groves by sprouts, eye-festival of youths. The Sanskrit: *tvaṃ mañjarībhiḥ pravaro vanānāṃ netrotsavaś cāsi sayauvanānām*

## v.12 — the kuṭaja's mockery
Kuṭaja, why are you laughed at by flowers? I bow — hard to laugh at you. The Sanskrit: *kuṭaja kiṃ kusumair avahasyate praṇipatāmi ca duṣprahasya te*

## v.13 — the nīpa released
Your flower-looked-at body — I would release it quickly, nīpa, body. The Sanskrit: *tava puṣpanirīkṣitāpadehaṃ visṛjeyaṃ sahasaiva nīpa deham*

## v.14 — the bee kisses the creeper
Having observed honey's season, the bee kisses the yūthikā creeper. The Sanskrit: *madhunaḥ samavekṣya kālatāṃ bhramaraś cumbati yūthikālatām*

## v.15 — dust obscures
By waters scattered, dust on earth; sun and moon are not seen. The Sanskrit: *salilair vihataṃ rajaḥ kṣitau ravicandrāv api nopalakṣitau*

## v.16 — peacocks cry
New-cloud-drunk, peacocks cry at cloud-arrival. The Sanskrit: *navāmbumattāḥ śikhino nadanti meghāgame*

## v.17 — the cloud roars
With Indra's weapon, the cloud today resounds, bringing the elephants' ardor, mountain-like. The Sanskrit: *sendrayudhaś ca jalado 'dya rasann ibhānāṃ saṃrambham āvahati bhūdharasannibhānām*

## v.18 — water falls
Loud roar — water in caves falls, wonderful-formed beauties. The Sanskrit: *paridhīraravaṃ jalaṃ darīṣu prapaty adbhutarūpasundarīṣu*

## ghat-p03 — the close (verses 19–21)

## v.19 — clouds make travelers long
Clouds thundering make travelers long; sorrow grows in those, endless. The Sanskrit: *utkaṇṭhayanti pathikāṇ jaladāḥ svanantaḥ śoko vivardhati ca tadvinatāsv anantaḥ*

## v.20 — love-festival
They honor the love-festival with beloveds; cloud-arrival, dear friend, they bring. The Sanskrit: *ratyutsavaṃ priyatamaiḥ saha mānayanti meghāgamaṃ priyasakhi śvasamānayanti*

## v.21 — the tribute
May I be conquered by which poet, by yamakas, other; to him I pour water in ghaṭakarpara. The Sanskrit: *jīyeya yena kavinā yamakaiḥ pareṇa tasmai vaheyam udakaṃ ghaṭakarpareṇa*

# L200 — Ghatakarpara (21 verses, 3 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Ghatakarpara (21 verses) is a kāvya (love poem) with Abhinavagupta's vivṛti (commentary). It presents: lament of separated beloved (Kāma in heart, to kill, arises; separated beloved; cruel foreign-serving one will kill without him; release foreign love or wife; cātaka thirsty begs water; suffering traveler, beloved yours). Beauty of grove (for Kāma's sake, groves of abodes shine; you are chief of groves by sprouts, eye-festival of youth; kuṭaja laughed at by flowers; I bow, hard to laugh; flower-looked-at body released). Close (clouds thundering make travelers long; sorrow grows; love-festival with beloveds; cloud-arrival; by which poet conquered by yamakas; to him, pour water in ghaṭakarpara).

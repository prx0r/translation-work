# Ghatakarpara — ARGMAP passage 1 (verses 1–9)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL. Lore: sandeśa-kāvya precursor (Wikipedia/owiki), KSTS 67 (Abhinava vivṛti).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Is the beloved's lament a complaint or a performance of śṛṅgāra through the sandeśa-kāvya device?

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Kāma arises (vv.1–2):** *manmathe ca hṛdi hantum udyate* — Kāma arises in the heart, to kill.
*proṣitapramadayedam udyate* — this, the separated beloved, arises.
→ Claim: desire and absence are one event.

**Accusation (v.3):** *nirdayena paradeśasevinā māriṣyatha hi tena māṃ vinā* — by the cruel
one serving a foreign land, you will kill me without him.
→ Claim: the foreign land is the rival; service abroad is betrayal.

**Release (v.4):** *anyadeśaratir adya mucyatāṃ sāthavā tava vadhūḥ kim ucyatām* — release
foreign-land love today, or your wife — what is to be said?
→ Claim: abandon the rival or face accusation. Dilemma is hers to voice.

**Messenger device (vv.5–6):** *cātakaś ca tṛṣito 'mbu yācate* — cātaka, thirsty, begs water.
*duḥkhitā pathika sā priyā ca te* — suffering traveler, she is your beloved.
→ Claim: cātaka (drinks only rain) = lover thirsting for beloved; cloud = messenger.

**Cloud arrives (vv.7–8):** *ambudaiḥ śikhigaṇo vinādyate* — by clouds peacock-flock sounded.
*kā ratiḥ priya mayā vinādya te* — what delight, beloved, without me today?
*toyadāgamakṛśā ca sādya te* — thinned by cloud-arrival, she today.
*durdhareṇa madanena sādyate* — tormented by hard Kāma.
→ Claim: landscape responds to her mood; rasa is not in woman alone but in world answering her.

**Close of lament (v.9):** *śokasāgarajale ca pātitām* — fallen into sorrow-ocean.
*tvadguṇasmaraṇam eva pāti tām* — remembrance of virtue protects.
*dravati ca kaluṣaṃ jalaṃ nadīnāṃ* — rivers' water melts impurity.
*kim iti ca māṃ samavekṣase na dīnām* — why regard me, not wretched?
*gambhīramegharasitavyathitā kadāhaṃ jahyāṃ sakhi priyaviyogajaśokadāhaṃ* — when shall I
abandon, friend, burning of separation-born sorrow?
→ Claim: lament closes in sustaining memory, not despair. Sthāyibhāva (love) transforms
sorrow (vyabhicāri) into something relishable.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. "sādya te" (v.8): compression of sādyate or separate reading — kept as-is.
2. cātaka's thirst: literal (bird drinks only rain) or figurative (lover thirsts) — both live.
3. Cloud as messenger or witness — sandeśa device implicit, not stated. Abhinava's vivṛti
   makes it explicit; poem leaves it open.

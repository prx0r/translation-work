# Ghatakarpara — ARGMAP passage 3 (verses 19–21, the close)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL. Lore: sandeśa-kāvya, Abhinava vivṛti (KSTS 67).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Does the work close in despair or in tribute? What is the rasa of the close?

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Clouds make travelers long (v.19):** *utkaṇṭhayanti pathikān jaladāḥ svanantaḥ* — clouds
thundering make travelers long. Cloud = messenger; thunder (svananta) awakens longing (utkaṇṭhā)
in traveler (pathika). Rasa's vibhāva is now the cloud itself.

**Sorrow grows (v.19 cont.):** *śoko vivardhati ca tadvinatāsv anantaḥ* — sorrow grows in those,
endless. vinatā = those bent low; sorrow grows without end.

**Love-festival (v.20):** *ratyutsavaṃ priyatamaiḥ saha mānayanti* — they honor love-festival
with beloveds. Lovers celebrate together — anubhāva is communal.

**Cloud-arrival (v.20 cont.):** *meghāgamaṃ priyasakhi śvasamānayanti* — cloud-arrival, dear
friend, they bring. Messenger is delivered.

**Tribute (v.21):** *jīyeya yena kavinā yamakaiḥ pareṇa* — may I be conquered by which poet,
by yamakas, other. Poet who conquers by yamakas (same word, different meaning) is the better
poet. Speaker yields to that skill.

**Offering (v.21 cont.):** *tasmai vaheyam udakaṃ ghaṭakarpareṇa* — to him I pour water in
ghaṭakarpara. Offering in potsherd = tribute. Voice shifts from lover to poet.

## 3. Open (not invented)

- Speaker of v.21: poet himself (self-tribute) or rival voice — both live. Yamaka device
  and self-referential close favor self-tribute, but not stated. Abhinava attributed the
  poem to Kālidāsa; Lienhard rejected. Whether this affects the reading of v.21's tribute
  is open.
- Yamaka: which words form it? Not identified in the poem. Abhinava's vivṛti would say.
- Potsherd (ghaṭakarpara): literal vessel or metaphor for the poem itself? Both live;
  L2 keeps the literal.
- Rasa shift śṛṅgāra → vira/adbhuta: our reading, not Abhinava's explicit statement.
  Flagged as application, not attribution.
- Abhinava's specific claims on this poem are unverified — we have the vivṛti's existence
  (KSTS 67), not its text.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed. Close: clouds make travelers long; sorrow grows; lovers honor love-festival; cloud
arrives; poet who conquers by yamakas honored with water in potsherd. L2 renders ghat-p03 in plain English, tracing to L0 ids. Rasa shifts śṛṅgāra → vira/adbhuta in close — named in C1.

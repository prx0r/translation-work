# C1 — Ghatakarpara

## ghat-p01–p03 — lament, beauty, close
**SUMMARY.** Ghatakarpara is a kāvya (love poem) with Abhinavagupta's vivṛti. Lament: Kāma in heart, to kill, arises; this, separated beloved, arises; by cruel foreign-serving one, kill without him; release foreign love or wife; cātaka thirsty begs water; suffering traveler, beloved yours. Beauty: for Kāma's sake, groves of abodes shine; you are chief of groves by sprouts, eye-festival of youth; kuṭaja laughed at by flowers; I bow, hard to laugh; flower-looked-at body released. Close: clouds thundering make travelers long; sorrow grows; love-festival with beloveds; cloud-arrival; by which poet conquered by yamakas; to him, pour water in ghaṭakarpara.

**FUNCTION.** Kāvya (love poem) with Abhinava's vivṛti: lament of separated beloved, beauty of grove, close with tribute to poet.

**KEY TERMS.** pramadā (beloved), proṣita (separated), madana (Kāma), cātaka (cātaka bird), pathika (traveler), ambuda (cloud), mañjarī (sprout), kuṭaja (kuṭaja tree), nīpa (nīpa tree), jalada (cloud), ratyutsava (love-festival), yamaka (yamaka), ghaṭakarpara (potsherd).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Entire text; 3 passages.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in three steps. (1) Lament: Kāma in heart, to kill, arises; this, separated beloved, arises; by cruel foreign-serving one, kill without him; release foreign love or wife; cātaka thirsty begs water; suffering traveler, beloved yours. (2) Beauty: for Kāma's sake, groves of abodes shine; you are chief of groves by sprouts, eye-festival of youth; kuṭaja laughed at by flowers; I bow, hard to laugh; flower-looked-at body released. (3) Close: clouds thundering make travelers long; sorrow grows; love-festival with beloveds; cloud-arrival; by which poet conquered by yamakas; to him, pour water in ghaṭakarpara.

# C1 — gita-a00

## SUMMARY

The bhāva-cakra's nondifference-bond names the work's treasury: lights of knowing, triumphed by woe-averting Śaṃbhura. Vyāsa-lineage, hundreds-of-thousands measure, mokṣa-as-fruit: the Gītārthasaṃgraha's prāmāṇya is claimed here [one LIVE fork, grounds in L0]. Liberation named: complete-differentiation-form, all-knowing, need-free, ever-risen in Bhagavān — laya, this much, in summary [one LIVE fork]. Other texts sing mokṣa incidentally; the Bhagavadgītā gives true attainment. Many earlier expositions; this attempt reasonable — revealing the hidden meaning. Bhaṭṭa Indurāja's tradition, long-examined, made by Abhinavagupta: the name declared.

## FUNCTION

Opens the work with a six-verse mangala: invocation, prāmāṇya-claim, mokṣa-definition, Gītā-supremacy, exculpation-of-effort, and the signed colophon.

## KEY TERMS

bhāva-cakra (diverse-state wheel as the text's own image); bodha-bhāsā (lights of knowing); prāmāṇya (authority claim); saṃskāra-svabhāva (complete-differentiation form); gūḍhārtha-prakāśaka (hidden-meaning revealer); āmnāya (oral tradition from Bhaṭṭendurāja).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The six benedictory verses precede adhyāya 1; they set the work's voice (colophon-as-verse) and its authority-claim before any Gītā commentary begins.

## EXPLANATION

The mangala works in six moves: what the work stores (knowing-lights), why it may speak (Vyāsa-lineage + measure + fruit), what mokṣa is (compounds → laya), why this text above others (samyak-prāpti), why another effort at all (gūḍhārtha), and who made it (āmnāya + name).

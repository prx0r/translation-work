## The prostration and the eligibility

[The author,] not-abandoning-the-seen (dṛṣṭimanujjhan), [is] the-proper-instructor
(samyagvyutpādakaḥ), it-is-said. The Buddhists-and-the-rest (saugataprabhṛtayaḥ), [however,]
are-the-doers-of-that (tatkāriṇaś ca) and-the-haters-of-that (taddveṣiṇaḥ). And-this, by-me, in-the-
"kathāmukhatilaka," at-length, is-determined; that-very-[one] [is] to-be-sought.

[And,] "seeing-the-unprecedented-homage-form (apūrvāṃ praṇāmabhaṅgīṃ paśyan), someone-might-be-
bewildered (vyāmuhyet)" — with-this-intention (āśayena), the-ṭīkā-kāra, for-the-establishing-
of-those (tatsamarthānāṃ), does: "the-victory-homage-and-the-rest (jayanamaskārādi)." By-the-
"and-the-rest (ādi)" word, the-worship, the-meditation, and-the-rest (vandanapradhyānaprabhṛti)
[are]-taken.

Here, that-which-is-the-rested-in-the-two (dvayaniṣṭhaṃ yat rūpam), [and]-being-made-known-by-
the-one-or-the-other-face (anyataramukhena pratipādyamānam), [is]-to-be-made-to-terminate-in-the-
rest-in-both (ubhayaviśrāntau paryavasāyayitam). "The-pot-shines; the-pot-sees (ghaṭo bhāsate,
ghaṭaḥ paśyati)" — for "making-the-not-shining-pot-shine (ghaṭaṃ nirbhāsamānaṃ nirbhāsayati)"
— this-[is]-the-object-cognition (artha-pratipattiḥ). And-the-prostration, here, [is]-the-
rested-in-both (ubhayaniṣṭhaṃ ca prahvatvam) — and-the-excellence, hence, [is]-its (utkarṣaś ca
ato'sya). That — in-the-use-of-the-"jaya-jayasi-jaya"-word (jayati-jayasi-jayeti-śabda-prayoge),
[though]-set-in-motion-by-the-face-of-the-Lord's-excellence (parameśvarotkarṣa-mukhena
pravṛtte'pi), [yet]-the-lowering-of-oneself-natured (svātmanyagbhāvanātmakaṃ) prostration [is]-
to-be-made-the-object (ālambanīya); otherwise, of-the-excellence, the-non-completion (anirvāha).
Thus, in-the-use-of-the-"namo-namāmi-vande-and-the-rest" (namo-namāmi-vande-ityādi-prayoge),
[though]-set-in-motion-by-the-face-of-the-reflection-of-the-lowering-of-oneself
(svātmanyagbhāvana-parāmarśa-mukhena pravṛtte'pi), [yet]-the-rest-in-the-Lord's-excellence
(īśvarotkarṣa-viśrāntiḥ) [is]-to-be-resorted (āśrayaṇīyā). This-both (tad-idam ubhayam), [being]-
one-thing (ekameva vastu), [and]-being-two-partite (dvyaṃśam-api), here-in-this-verse
(iha śloke), around (ā samantāt), [is]-known (veditam), [and]-by-the-word, [is]-made-fit-for-
the-clear-reflection-of-the-whole-audience-to-be-approached (saṃmukhīkartavyasamastaśrotṛ-jana-
sphuṭaparāmarśa-yogyaṃ kṛtam). For-the-implied-by-the-meaning (arthākṣiptaṃ), of-everyone, [is]-
not-the-field (na gocaraḥ).

"The-exceedingly-hard-to-obtain-service (atyantadurlabhaṃ dāsyam)" — by-this "in-some-way
(kathañcit)," [it]-is-said; so, all-the-more, in-the-self-subject (ātmaviṣaye), the-lowering
(nyagbhāvana) [is]-said. "The-great-fruit (mahāphalaṃ) [is]-that-service (taddāsyam)" — [i.e.]
the-Lord's-excellence (īśvarasya utkarṣaḥ); for, from-the-service-ness (dāsatvādeva), in-the-
master, the-Lord (svāmini īśvare), the-obtained (labdhe), again-the-"Lord"-word
(īśvarapadam), [being]-terminated-in-the-māheśvarya (māheśvarya-paryavasitam), [is] alone. By-
the-"great-fruit" word, the-ṭīkā-kāra [shows]: "the-cause-of-the-attainment-of-the-all-assets
(samasta-sampat-samavāpti-hetu) — this-word (idam padam), [being]-the-qualifier-of-the-
recognition (pratyabhijñā-viśeṣaṇam), [is]-also-the-qualifier-of-the-service
(dāsyaviśeṣaṇam)." And-in-the-vṛtti, this-meaning [is]-indicated-by-this: "the-service's-prosperity
(dāsyalakṣmīḥ)." "Of-whom, from-the-Lord's-favor (parameśvara-prasādāt), the-all-assets
(samastāḥ sampadaḥ), [being]-the-prosperities-born-of-the-service (dāsyaprabhavā lakṣmyaḥ),
[are]-obtained (labdhāḥ)" — otherwise, the-taking-up-of-the-"lakṣmī"-word [would]-be-uncalled-
for (asaṅgataṃ), and-the-not-making-manifest-of-the-many-meaning-subject (bahvarthaviṣayatā-
aprakaṭīkaraṇaṃ).

"Even-by-the-speech (vācāpi vā)" — by-the-"also (api)" word, "even-by-the-body (kāyenāpi)"
[is]-to-be-understood (mantavyam). For-thus — the-prostration (prahvatā) and-the-exaltation
(utkarṣaṇaṃ), [being]-of-the-form-of-the-manifestation-reflection (tathā-prakāśa-parāmarśana-
rūpaṃ), in-the-mental-sensation (mānase saṃvedane), [and]-in-the-word-truth (vāktattve), [which-
is]-its-essence (tatsāra-rūpe), [is]-rested (viśrāntaṃ), not-in-the-inert-body (jaḍe śarīre).
Thus — that-which, [being]-made-of-the-mind-and-the-word (manovāgātmakaṃ), [and]-the-not-divided
(avibhaktaṃ) [part] of-the-consciousness-power (saṃvicchakteḥ) — that-alone, [by]-the-devotee-
offering (arpayatā bhaktena), the-all (viśvaṃ) [is]-offered-in-the-Lord (bhagavati bhavati). Thus-
in-the-āgama:

> "And-one's-own-self-he-should-offer (svātmānaṃ ca nivedayet)."

[This]-is-said. "He-should-offer (nivedayed)" — [i.e.] he-should-arrange (arpayet), [and] "with-
the-certainty, [and] he-should-make-knowledge-formed (niścayena vidyāmayaṃ ca kuryāt)" — this-
is-the-meaning. Then, from-the-non-separateness (tannāntarīyakatvād eva), of-the-body, the-
wealth, the-wife-and-the-rest (śarīradhanadārādi), too, the-offering-in-that (tatra arpaṇaṃ) [is]-
born (jāyate). Hence-the-worship, the-oblation, the-bowing-of-the-head, the-eight-limbed-
prostration-and-the-rest (pūjanahavanaśirovinamanāṣṭāṅgapraṇipātanādi), [being]-the-cause-of-
the-inference-of-that (tan-numāna-hetutayā), [and]-by-the-secondary-use (upacārāt), [are]-called-
"homage (praṇāma)," not-primarily (na mukhyatayā).

"True (satyam)" — indeed, [there]-is-the-removal-of-the-obstacles (vighnaśamana), [and]-the-
determined (niyataiva), [this]-is-accomplished (saṃpadyate); but-the-more (adhikaṃ), the-purpose
(prayojanam), [is]-of-the-verse-word's-one-part (ślokavākyaikadeśasya), [which-is]-read-at-the-
beginning (ādau paṭhitasya), [and]-whose-support-is-the-prostration (tatprahvatā-nibandhanasya).
"Even-now (adyāpi)" — [being]-uninterrupted (avicchinnatayā), by-the-door-of-the-entry-into-the-
book (pustakaniveśa-dvāreṇa), the-transference-to-the-whole-heart (aśeṣahṛdaya-saṃkrānteḥ). For-
there-alone, of-the-made-known-ness (āveditatvasya), [is]-the-use (upayogaḥ), not-in-oneself (na
ātmani) — this-is-the-intention (bhāvaḥ). "The-exegete's-position [is]-honored (vyākhyātṛpadam
abhyarhitam)" — previously. "In-the-establishing-of-the-recognition (pratyabhijñopapādane),
indeed, [is]-inferred (anumīyate) the-Lord's-favor (īśvarasāṃmukhyam)" — "preceded-by-the-Lord's-
favor (īśvarasāṃmukhya-pūrvakam)."

**Objection (nanu):** "In-the-exegete (vyākhyātari), this-meaning's-entry (etadarthānupraveśa)
[is]-fit (ucita); but-of-the-listeners (śrotṝṇām), how? For-the-listener (śrotā) [does]-not-
establish-the-recognition (pratyabhijñām upapādayati), [and]-not-the-service (dāsyam)" — the-
inner-separate-reflection (antaḥ pṛthak parāmarśaḥ) [is]-not-fit, from-the-oneness-of-the-
sentence-meaning-reflection (vākyārtha-parāmarśa-aikyāt).

The-listener, too, [at]-the-first-reading-of-this-word (prathamata etadvākyaṃ paṭhanneva), thus-
inwardly-reflects (evam-antaḥ parāmṛśati): "I, now, — from-the-inferred-obtaining-of-the-eligibility-
for-the-teaching-of-such-an-other-worldly-secret (evaṃvidha-lokottara-rahasyopadeśa-bhājanatva-
prāpty-anumitād eva), [and]-from-the-Lord's-favor (parameśvara-prasādāt) — [am]-the-obtainer-of-
the-māheśvara-service (maheśvaradāsyaprāpaṇā-pūrvikā); that-recognition (pratyabhijñā), [which-
is]-established-by-this-exegete (amunā vyākhyātrā upapādyate), — in-that, I-make-the-operator's-
activity (adhyeṣaṇa-tatsamarthācaraṇa-rūpaṃ prayojaka-vyāpāraṃ karomi), by-which-I, [being]-fit-
for-the-attainment-of-the-ācārya-position (ācāryapadaprāpti-yogyatayā), [and]-wishing-the-help-
of-the-people (janopakāramicchan), [would]-make-even-those-people-attain-the-Lord's-service
(tān janān api prāpayya parameśvaradāsyaṃ)." And-thus — even-the-not-wise (avidvān-api), [being]-
with-the-secret, the-praṇava, the-mantra, the-seed, the-meaning (rahasyapraṇavādi-mantra-bījārtham),
from-the-reflection-on-it (tadanucintanāt), obtains-its-result (tatphalam āsādayati). So-the-
disciple, too (evaṃ śiṣyo'pi). For — from-the-non-possession-of-the-ascertainment-connection
(adhyavasāya-yogābhāvād), [and]-being-not-climbed-by-the-intellect-characterized-knower
(buddhilakṣaṇaṃ pramātāram anāruḍho'pi), this-meaning (ayam arthaḥ), in-the-real-knower
(vāstave pramātari), rests (viśrāmyati) — because-of-the-real-one-form-ness (vastuta ekarūpatvāt)
of-that-knower and-that-reflection (tasya pramāturasya ca parāmarśasya).

For — the-same-kind-hard-tracking (sajātīya-haṭhānudhāvanaṃ), in-the-clod, the-water-and-the-rest
(loṣṭajalādiṣu), [and]-even-in-the-great-inert-elements (jaḍeṣu mahābhūteṣv-api), [is]-seen
(dṛṣṭam). And-thus — "the-cognition (jñānam), [being]-the-uncontracted-non-constructive-
consciousness-form (asaṃkucita-nirvikalpaka-saṃvid-rūpaṃ), [is]-the-personal (pauruṣaṃ); [and,]
[being]-the-other-than-the-śāstra, [and]-the-initiation-and-the-rest-means (śāstravyatirikta-
dīkṣādy-upāyakam-api); but-that, [being]-attained-to-the-supreme-ascertainment-position
(aparamadhyavasāyapadaprāptaṃ), [and]-being-the-contracted-consciousness-form (saṃkucita-
saṃvid-rūpaṃ), [is]-the-Buddhist (bauddhaṃ), for-whose-meaning-the-śāstra-and-the-rest [are]-
said (yadarthaṃ śāstrādi ucyate). And-the-ignorance (ajñānam-api), [being]-the-contracted-
non-constructive-sensation-form (saṃkucitāvikalpa-saṃvedana-rūpaṃ), [is]-personal-established
(puruṣaniṣṭhaṃ); [and], [being]-climbed-to-the-contraction-ascertainment-ground (saṃkocādhyavasāya-
bhūmy-ārūḍhaṃ), [is]-intellect-established (buddhiniṣṭham) — in-two-ways (dvidhā), [the]-āgamikas
[think] (manyante)." — here, in-this-matter (atra arthe), the-blessed-Mataṅga-and-the-rest-śāstra
(śrīmanmataṅgādiśāstram) [is]-to-be-sought (paryeṣyam).

That-not-preceded-by-the-intellect (abuddhipūrvakam-api), of-the-disciples (śiṣyāṇām), [and]-
this-reflection-made-sensation (etatparāmarśamaya-saṃvedanam), [is]-born (jāyate), like-in-the-
sleep-and-the-faint (svāpamadayoḥ) [the]-duty-reflection-made-sensation (kartavyaparāmarśamaya-
saṃvedanavat).

## The grammar of "kathaṃcit āsādya"

"Having-obtained (āsādya)" — and "I-would-establish-the-recognition (pratyabhijñām upapādayāmi)" —
[these]-are-the-two-causatives (dvau ṇicau). In-the-vṛtti, too, by-the-word "the-alone-prosperity
(ekākisaṃpadā)," of-the-other, too, that-alone — the-transference-of-the-Lord's-favor-alone-
caused (parameśvara-prasādaikahetukataddāsyasaṃpat-saṃcāraṇam) [is]-indicated — so, [by]-the-two-
causatives' meaning (ṇaij-dvaya-arthaḥ), "having-obtained" [is]-explained. And-"I-would-make-
recognize (pratyabhijñāpayāmī)" — the-two-causatives, in-the-vṛtti — "as-long-as (yāvadeva), [and]
the-inferred-by-the-attainment-of-the-eligibility-for-the-secret-teaching (rahasyopadeśa-
yogyatvāvāpty-anumeyaḥ) the-māheśvara-favor (māheśvara-prasādaḥ) [is], so-long (tāvat), [am]-I-
fit-for-the-making-of-the-self-and-other-help (ātmaparopakāra-karaṇa-samartho'haṃ jāta eva)." "If-
the-god-rained (vṛṣṭaś ced devaḥ), the-crops-are-accomplished (saṃpannāḥ śālayaḥ)" — such-a-
speech (vādam), [is]-indeed-the-disciple's-fit-reflection (ucita eva śiṣyasya parāmarśaḥ). And-
in-truth (vastutaś ca), from-the-one-self-ness (ekātmakatvāt), [and]-of-the-non-interruption-of-
the-manifestation-of-that (tatprakāśanāvicchedasyaiva), [and]-by-the-removal-of-the-delusion
(mohāpasāraṇena), [and]-the-being-made (saṃpādyatvāt), — of-the-author, [however], this-alone-
[is]-the-effort (ayameva udyamaḥ): "the-made-of-my-reflection (matparāmarśamayāḥ), individually
(pratyekaṃ), all (sarve), let-they-be-accomplished (saṃpadyantām)." Like-the-śāstrakāra, [and]-
of-the-exegetes, too, toward-the-disciples, [this]-same [is]-fit (tathaiva yuktaḥ):

> "The-creature, having-attained-my-essence (matsamatvaṃ gato jantur mukta)..."

— this, indeed, in-the-cultivation-of-the-great-pervasion (mahāvyāptibhāvanāyām), [is]-the-alone-
intended-meaning (tātparyārthaḥ). Thus, [by]-the-reflection-on-the-reading-of-the-sūtra-and-vṛtti
(sūtravṛttipāṭha-parāmarśaḥ), [and]-of-the-listeners, too, the-Lord's-approaching
(parameśvara-saṃmukhībhāvaṃ) [is]-given-indeed (dadāty-eva). Moreover — [by]-the-exegete, [and]-
while-reading-the-sūtra-and-vṛtti-book (paṭhyamāne sūtravṛttigranthe), [and]-by-the-door-of-the-
obtaining-of-the-hard-to-get-Lord's-service (durlabheśvaradāsyā-sādanādvāreṇa), [and]-wishing-
this-people's-help (janasya ayam upakāramicchan), [he]-would-establish-the-recognition
(tatpratyabhijñām upapādayatīti) — this-being-such-meaning (evaṃbhūtam artham), [being]-like-a-
reflection (pratibimbakalpaṃ), [and]-by-the-transference (pratisaṃkramaṇa-vaśād) from-the-supreme-
person's-purpose (bimbasthānīyād uttamapuruṣārthāt) [is]-arrived-at (āyātaṃ), [and]-being-made-
known (pratipādya); from-the-sensation-of-the-eligibility-form-ness (adhikārirūpatā-saṃvedanāt),
whoever-is-born (jāyamāno yaḥ kaścit), he, here, [is]-the-to-be-helped (upakārya). Thus — "from-
such-arisen-in-myself (evaṃrūpāt svātmani abhyuditāt), always, I-[am]-the-to-be-helped-jana
(ahaṃ jana upakāryaḥ); the-recognition, [being]-established-by-this-establishing (anena
upapādyamānām upapattyā), [and]-to-be-established (sthāpyamānāṃ), [and]-the-auxiliary-to-the-
obtaining-of-the-hard-to-get-Lord's-service (taddurlabheśvaradāsyāsādana-upayikā) — [this]
recognition-I-would-make-obtain (pratyabhijñām upapādayāmi); I, too, [and]-by-the-establishing
(upapattyā), in-the-heart, [would]-place (hṛdaye sthāpayāmi); [and]-that-establishing,
[being]-ascertained (avadhārayamāṇaḥ)" — this-much (iyat), [is]-the-to-be-understood-ending
(pratipatti-paryantā pratipattiḥ).

## The eligibility and the ladder

Here, first, by-the-skill (kauśalāt), in-the-transference (saṃkramaṇe'pi), the-reflection's-
sameness (parāmarśa-sāmyaṃ) [is]-shown; elsewhere, too, [it-is]-thus. For — this-alone [is]-the-
difference-of-the-eligible-and-the-not-eligible (adhikāry-anadhikāriṇor viśeṣaḥ). For-thus —
"to-you, go, I-teach (tvāṃ gacchety upadiśāmi)" — [this]-scripture-statement (śrutir)
[being]-rightly-understood (yathārtham avabuddhya), "[he]-teaches-me, 'go'" (māṃ gaccheti
upadiśati) — [this]-second (dvitīyaṃ); by-the-transference (pratisaṃkrameṇa), climbing-the-stair
(sopānam āruhya), "[I] go (gacchāmī)" — [this]-third-position (tṛtīyaṃ padam) [being]-obtained
(āsādya), the-sentence-meaning-knower (vākyārthajño) [he]-becomes (bhavati). But-the-not-eligible
(anadhikārī), on-the-first-stair-alone (prathamasopāna eva), rests (viśrāmyati). Thus-this-one
(evam ayam), [being]-about-to-say (vivakṣati) — "[he]-impels-him (enaṃ prerayati)" — [this],
too, [being]-the-having-the-next-stair (bhavatsopānāntara), [and]-the-not-intended-by-the-speaker
(na vakturabhisaṃhitaṃ); in-the-duty-ness (kartavvyatve), [he]-does-not-rest (na viśrāmyati) —
enough-of-the-incidental (alam avāntareṇa). In-every-way (sarvathā), the-listener-position
(śrotṛpadam), [is]-the-obtained-meaning (upannārtham-eva). By-the-"and-the-rest (ādi)"-grasp,
the-hearing (śravaṇa) [is]-to-be-understood (mantavyam).

Thus, having-established-the-unprecedented-homage-form (apūrvāṃ praṇāmabhaṅgīṃ samarthya),
[and]-having-made-the-reading-of-that-word-with-purpose (tadvākyapāṭhaṃ ca saprayojanīkṛtya),
the-"in-some-way (kathaṃcit)"-sūtra, [which-is]-to-be-explained-by-the-vṛtti — "from-the-Lord's-
favor-alone (parameśvara-prasādād eva)" — [this-vṛtti], to-explain, [he]-says "if-this (yac cedam)."
The-"and (ca)"-word, the-other-object-to-be-explained (prameyāntaram), [is]-joined (samuccinoti).

"By-the-devotion (bhaktyā)" — for, from-the-force-of-the-entry-into-that-own-form
(tatsvarūpa-samāveśa-balād), in-that-real-own-form-Lord (paramārthataḥ svasvarūpe bhagavati),
[and]-manifesting (prakāśamāne), [and]-from-the-arising-of-the-cognition-of-the-falsehood-of-the-
unreal-pride (bhrāntāpāramārthika-abhimāna-tucchatā-vedana-udayāt), in-that-truth-alone, [and]-in-
the-full-bliss-form (pūrṇānanda-rūpe), the-prevalence-of-the-always-entry (satata-samāveśa-
prābalyaṃ), [being]-characterized-by-the-knowledge (vidyālakṣaṇaṃ) — [this]-is-the-devotion
(bhaktiḥ); by-it (tayā). By-the-wishing-of-the-infinite-result (ananta-phalārthitvena), [and]-
the-characterized-by-the-liberation (mokṣa-lakṣaṇaḥ), that-which-is-the-taste (āsvādaḥ), [being]-
full (paripūrṇo), [and]-of-the-all-form-ness (viśvarūpatā-mayaḥ), [and]-of-the-seeing-action-form
(dṛkkriyātmā), [and]-the-reflexion (vimarśaḥ) — he-alone-[is]-the-subject-here (prakṛto yatra).
By-this — "the-Kāṇāda-said (kāṇādokto), the-inert-self (jāḍyātmā) [is]-not-the-liberation
(na mokṣaḥ)" — [he]-says. "And-of-the-liberation, that-which-is-the-taste (mokṣasya ca ya
āsvādaḥ), being-such (evaṃbhūto'sāv), [is]-by-the-part (ityaṃśena), the-reflexion (vimarśaḥ)."

## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk B)

1. **the prostration's two faces** — the homage both lowers oneself AND exalts the Lord; the
   word "namas" carries both (the excellence is the rest-in-the-Lord).
2. **the disciple's eligibility** — the listener, in hearing, reflects "I am the recipient of the
   secret-teaching; the recognition is established by the exegete; I make the operator's activity."
   The eligibility-ladder (the "go" example): the not-eligible rests on the first stair.
3. **the four bhaktas (Gītā 7.16)** — the distressed, the desire-to-know, the wealth-seeker, the
   wise — Abhinava's framework for the approaches to the Lord.
4. **the dharma/mokṣa distinction** — the dharma (merit) is māyā-made, inside the māyā; the
   liberation-taste (mokṣāsvāda) transcends the māyā. The devotion's subjection is not the
   dharma's result.
5. **māyā / niyati / prasāda** — the restraint-power (niyati) governs the dharma in the māyā;
   the Lord's grace (prasāda) is the unbridled, nir-mala (stainless) favor that transcends the
   māyā.
6. **śraddhā / bhakti** — the faith (the attainment-grasp without the taste) vs the devotion
   (the actual entry-taste). "The faith-result is the devotion."
7. **the grammar of "kathaṃcit āsādya"** — the manner (prakāra), the fifth-case (the cause), the
   causatives (ṇij) — Abhinava's grammatical precision about how the "in-some-way" obtains the
   Lord's service.
8. **kathaṃcit = "in some way"** — the sūtra's opening word, glossed through the whole
   eligibility/disciple/guru analysis.

**The crux:** the eligibility for the recognition. The whole of kārikā 1.1's commentary on
"kathaṃcit āsādya" (having obtained in some way) unfolds into: the prostration's two faces, the
disciple's/teacher's roles, the four bhaktas, the dharma-vs-mokṣa distinction, the grace, and
the grammar of the manner. This establishes the work's whole frame — who is qualified, how the
Lord is approached, and what the recognition is.

## The svātmā (the own-self) and the relation

[He] draws-in-the-relation (saṃbandhinam ākarṣati); that [relation], [being]-determinate
(niyataḥ), is-not, first, [and]-from-the-not-being-said (anuktatvāt), [and]-from-the-not-
attaining-the-context (aprakaraṇāpannatvāt) — [so]-all-is-perceived (sarva eva pratīyate). "From-
the-not-taking-up (anupādānāt)" — by-the-word, or-by-the-context (śabdena prakaraṇena vā). "From-
the-all-inclusion (sarvāntarbhāvāt)" — [this-is]-the-objection (codyam). "If-that (yadyasau)" —
[this-is]-the-established-view (siddhāntaḥ).

There, in-the-objection (codye), first, this-is-the-intention (āśayaḥ): "From-the-not-determinate-
ness (aniyatatvāt) of-the-relation (saṃbandhino), the-whole (nikhila eva) — [and]-the-inert-and-
the-non-inert (jaḍaś ca ajaḍaś ca) — the-being-class (bhāvavargaḥ), [is]-perceived (pratīyate).
There, of-the-non-inert (ajaḍānāṃ), he-the-self (sa ātmā), [being]-of-the-consciousness-mark
(caitanya-lakṣaṇaḥ), [and]-one (ekaḥ), sometimes (kadācit), [is]-able-to-be-said (śakyo'bhidhātum);
but-the-inert (jaḍāḥ), [being]-these, [and]-of-the-separate-cut-off-own-forms
(pṛthakparicchinnasvarūpāḥ), how (katham), [being]-of-the-one-self (ekātmānaḥ)? — from-the-
contradiction-of-the-one-own-form-ness-of-the-different-own-natures (pṛthaksvabhāvānām
ekasvabhāvatvavirodhād)."

Of-the-reply (uttarasya), however, this-is-the-intention (ayam āśayaḥ): "Here, 'own-self (svātmā)'
— the-'own' (sva), [being]-the-other-expecting (anyāpekṣasya), [and]-the-self's-own-general-mark
(sva-sāmānyalakṣaṇa), [and]-the-artha-kriyā-agent-ness-and-the-rest (arthakriyākāritvāder) [is]-
the-relation (saṃbandhī), [and]-the-own (ātmīyo), [and]-the-not-expecting-the-other
(ananyāpekṣo), [and]-the-not-conditioned (anaupādhiko), [he]-becomes (bhavati)."

There — those-inert-ones (ye jaḍāḥ), [being]-perceived-as-inert (jaḍābhimatāḥ), [then]-their-
being-and-non-being (sadasattvam api) [is]-hard-to-get-in-the-self (durlabham ātmani); what-then
(kā tu kathā), of-the-blue-yellow-pleasure-pain-ness-and-the-rest (nīlapītasukhaduḥkhāditvasya)?
For — "that-which, in-the-own-self, exists (yaḥ svātmani san), if-it-were-non-existent (yadi
asan syāt), what-would-be-its-fault (kim asya duṣyet)?" — so, "it-would-do-the-artha-kriyā
(tathaiva arthakriyāṃ kuryād)" — if-it-is-so (iti cet), "it-does (karoti)" — if-this-were
(evaṃ yadi syāt), then-what-would-be (tat kiṃ syāt)? — so, "it-would-shine-thus (tathaiva
prakāśeteti)" — if-it-is-so (cet), "the-manifest-ness, [being]-its-own-glory (prakāśamānatā
kim asya svamahimā)"? "Om (om)" — if-it-is-so (iti cet): "to-me-it-shines, to-Caitra-it-shines
(mama prakāśate, caitrasya prakāśate)" — how-do-they-concur (kathaṃ saṃgacchatām)? "The-cognition-
[is]-that-manifest-ness (prātipattrī sā prakāśateti)" — if-it-is-so (cet), "of-the-cognition,
if-that [is], then-of-it, what-may-it-be (pratipattur yadyasau, tat tasya sā kiṃ bhavatu)?" "By-
its-force-it-shines (tadvaśāt sa prakāśata)" — this-[is]-an-unprecedented-reason (apūrvā yuktiḥ)!
For-thus, "by-the-white's-force, the-yellow-would-be-white (śvetavaśāt pītaṃ śvetate)" — this,
too, [would]-follow (syāt).

"If-of-this, [being]-own (asya ātmīyaṃ), by-its-force, elsewhere-it-does-not-become (tadvaśād
anyatra na bhavati), like-the-blue-in-the-yellow (nīlateva pīte); and-thus, the-manifest-form-ness
(prakāśarūpatā), [being]-the-knower's-own (pramātur ātmīyā), how, in-the-blue, would-it-be (kathaṃ
sā nīle bhavet)?" There, "of-the-becoming-in-that, the-identity (tatra bhavanasya tādātmyaṃ), [is]-
the-pervader (vyāpakaṃ); otherwise, in-the-blue-too, 'the-yellow' (nīle'pi pītatā) — and-the-rest,
[being]-a-thousand-branch (sahasraśākham), [and]-the-incoherence (āsamañjasyam): the-seen-
silver-ness-(dṛśyamānatādrūpya) [and]-the-rule's-opposed (vyāpakasya niyamasya viruddhaṃ) — [it]-
would-be-joined (prasajyeta)."

"Of-that-identity, the-other-self-ness (tasya tādātmyasya viruddham anyātmatvaṃ), [and]-that, [is]-
the-other-owned-ness (tadeva ca anyadīyatvam); or-else, of-the-other, that-relation — how (athā
anyasya tatsaṃbandhīti katham)?"

## The blue and the consciousness

"And-this — of-the-blue-and-the-rest, without-the-manifest-ness (nīlādeḥ prakāśamānatvam vinā),
[there-is]-not-any-own-life (na kiñcit nijajīvitam) — [and]-the-not-to-be-denied
(anapahnavanīya-prakāśamānatā-bala), [and]-the-knower-manifestation-identity (pramātṛprakāśatā-
tādātmyaṃ), [is]-to-be-accepted (aṅgīkāryam). Thus-the-blue-and-the-rest, too (bodhātmāna eva
nīlādayo'pi), [are]-of-the-awareness-essence (bodha-ātmānaḥ); not-their-other-body (na eṣāmanyat
vapuḥ) — so, of-them, too, that-alone-[is]-the-own-self (teṣām api sa eva svātmā), [i.e.] the-
Lord (maheśvaraḥ) — [this]-is-the-desired (iṣṭam eva)."

"What-[is]-the-import (kaḥ prasaṅgārthaḥ)?" "The-attainment-of-the-undesired (aniṣṭāpattiḥ), [is]-
the-import (prasaṅgaḥ)." This-he-says: "let-the-clod-and-the-rest-be (āstāṃ loṣṭādiḥ)." For, of-
it, [being]-clearly-manifesting (sphuṭaṃ prakāśamānasya), in-the-awareness-essence-ness
(bodhātmatāyāṃ), what-[is]-the-dispute (ko vivādaḥ)? "The-non-being (abhāvo'pi), too, which-
[is]-agreed-by-someone-as-'the-empty, [and]-nothing (yastuccho na kiñcidity evaṃ kasyāpi
saṃmataḥ), that-too, [being]-thus (so'pi yāvad evam), [and]-not-shining (na cakāsti) — so-long,
[and]-being-other-than-the-being (tāvad bhāvād anyat sa iti), this-alone — whence (etadeva kuto
bhavet)?" "If-[it]-is-being-cognized (budhyamānaś cet), then-of-it, too, [is]-the-Lord-alone-the-
own-self (tad asyāpi maheśvara eva svātmā)." "But-if-[it]-is-the-not-cognized-non-being (atha
abudhyamāno'sāv abhāvaḥ), then-he-[is]-not-anyone (na kaścidasāv); of-whom, [then], [and]-then
(kasya asau tadānīṃ) — the-own-self-[is]-joined (svātmā prasajyate)?" Thus, of-the-not-cognized-
blue-and-the-rest, too (evaṃ nīlāder-api abudhyamānasya), who-[is]-the-own-self (kaḥ svātmeti) —
the-"being-cognized"-word (budhyamānapadam). This-is-said: "when-the-objection-[is]-made (yadā
codyaṃ kriyate), 'of-the-blue-and-its-non-being-and-the-rest, too, the-own-self-the-Lord-[is]-
joined (nīlatadabhāvāder-api svātmā maheśvaraḥ prasajyate)', [then], the-acceptance-of-the-being-
cognized-ness-alone (budhyamānatvasya aṅgīkāra eva), [is]-the-reply (uttaram); otherwise, [the]-
not-having-the-support (nirāśrayaṃ) [is]-the-objection (codyam)."

## The division (vibhāga) and the body

**Objection (nanu):** "Thus — if-the-Lord (maheśvaraś cet), [is]-the-blue's-self (nīlasya ātmā),
[and]-of-the-yellow, too, he-alone (pītasyāpi sa eva) — [then] 'this-is-the-blue, this-is-the-
yellow (nīlam idaṃ, pītam idam)' — whence-the-division (kuto vibhāga)?" — [he]-says "all-indeed
(sarvam eva)." Because, of-the-consciousness-form-self (cidrūpasya ātmanaḥ), [and]-of-the-
manifestation-form-own-nature (prakāśarūpasya svasvabhāvasya), the-whole-known (sarvam eva
vedyam), like-the-body (śarīram-iva) — as-the-body, though-known (vedyam-api), [and]-by-the-
support-of-the-"I" (ahamity avaṣṭambhāt), [and]-the-un-metaphorical (anupacaritāt), [is]-joined-
with-the-consciousness-form (cidrūpānuviddhaṃ); so, too, the-whole (tathaiva viśvam). For-not-
like-the-blanket-and-the-rest (nahi kambalādivat), in-the-body (dehe), [and]-from-the-instrument-
ness (upakaraṇatvād), the-metaphorical-ness (upacaritātā), [and]-of-the-"I"-being (ahaṃbhāvasya),
in-the-world (loke), [and]-even-of-the-those-thinking-themselves-separate (pṛthagātmamāninām-api),
[is]-seen (bhāsate).

Thus, [and]-from-the-consciousness-form-entry (cidrūpatānuvedhāt), as-the-cognition (saṃvit), [by]-
the-will-be-said-maxim (vakṣyamāṇanayena), [is]-of-the-all-form (viśvarūpā); so, too, the-blue-
yellow-being-array-and-the-rest (nīlapītādibhāvāvalī api) — hence-the-not-wished (anabhimata eva)
[is]-our-division (asmākaṃ vibhāgaḥ).

## The Upaniṣadic assertion (the Vedānta cross-check)

This, with-the-Vedāntin's-book-word (vedāntavādi-granthavacasā), he-brings-to-agreement
(saṃvādayati) — "the-portion, too (pradeśo'pi)." As — "the-pot-space, the-cloth-space
(ghaṭākāśaṃ paṭākāśam)" — of-the-space (ākāśasya), [and]-by-the-pot-and-the-rest-connection
(ghaṭādiyogena), the-portions (pradeśāḥ) [are]-conceived (kalpitāḥ); [and]-of-the-space, whatever-
[is]-the-form (yāvad rūpaṃ), the-omnipresence-and-the-rest (vibhutvādi), by-it, [they are]-not-
less-or-more (tena anūnādhikāḥ); so — of-the-Brahman (brahmaṇaḥ), [being]-great (vṛhattvena),
[and]-pervading (vyāpakasya), [and]-making-grow (vṛṃhakatvena), [and]-the-executor-of-the-world's-
creation-and-nourishment (viśvasṛṣṭyāpyāyanavidhāyinaḥ), [and]-of-the-manifestation-form
(prakāśarūpasya), [and]-by-the-own-power-reflected-blue-and-the-rest-conditions
(svaśaktipratyavabhāsita-nīlādyupādhikṛtāḥ), those-portions (ye pradeśāḥ) — "the-blue-
manifestation, the-yellow-manifestation (nīlaprakāśaḥ pītaprakāśaḥ)" — [and]-the-rest (ityādayaḥ);
they (te), in-the-Brahman (brahmaṇi), that-which-is-the-all-form-ness (sarvārūpyaṃ), [and]-the-all-
power-undivided-functioning-ness (sarvaśaktyavibhāgavṛttitvaṃ), [and]-the-which (yacca), [by]-the-
mere-manifest-ness-form (prakāśamānatā-mātra-rūpeṇa), [and]-the-counterpart-deficiency
(pratiyogivaikalya), [and]-in-the-absence-of-the-to-be-removed (apohanīyābhāve), [and]-from-the-
non-possibility-of-the-constructive-form-ness (vikalparūpatvāyogād), [and]-the-counterpart-less-
sensation-form (apratiyogisaṃvedana-rūpam), [and]-the-non-constructive-ness (avikalpatvam) — that
(tat), individually (pratyekam), [they]-do-not-exceed (anatikrāntāḥ); hence (tataḥ), [and]-not-in-
the-least (manāg-api), [and]-the-not-more (anadhikāś ca).

"The-two (dvayam), mutually (anyonyam), the-reason-and-what-has-the-reason-relation
(hetuhetumadbhāvaṃ), [is]-shown (dyotayati)."

**Objection (nanu):** "Thus — let-the-undivided-form-ness (avibhāgarūpatā) be, [being]-arrived-at-
by-reason (yuktyupanipatitā); but-this (yastu ayam), 'the-blue, the-yellow' — in-the-known (vedye),
[and]-'I-am-Caitra, I-am-Maitra' — in-the-knower (vedake), the-division-manifestation
(vibhāgapratibhāsaḥ) — that-how-do-we-establish (tat kathaṃ samarthayāmahe)?" — [he]-says "only
(kevalam), [and]-the-inert (jaḍaḥ)." "Inert (jaḍaḥ)" — [i.e.]-shining-by-the-other-dependent-
manifest-ness (anyādhīnaprakāśatayā bhāsamānaḥ), this-is-the-meaning (ityarthaḥ).

## The will-be-said (the māyā-śakti and the body)

"Will-be-said (vakṣyamāṇā)" —

> "The-to-be-given-up (heyā), [is]-the-threefold (trayī), [and]-of-the-breath-and-the-rest
> (prāṇādeḥ), [from]-the-primacy (prādhānyāt), in-the-agent-ness-quality (kartṛtāguṇe)" — (3.29)

— here, [this]-will-be-said (vakṣyate). "The-Lord's-manifestation-alone (parameśvaraprakāśa eva),
[and]-by-his-own-māyā-power (svamāyāśaktyā), [which-is]-of-the-form-of-the-freedom-of-the-doing-
of-the-exceedingly-difficult (atidurghaṭakāritva-svātantrya-rūpayā), [and]-the-own-form
(svarūpam), [being]-not-covered (anācchāditam-api), [as]-covered (ācchāditam-iva), [he]-manifests
(avabhāsayati)." There — "in-the-primacy-of-the-not-covering-part (anācchādana-aṃśa-pradhānatāyāṃ),
[is]-the-entry-into-the-Sadāśiva-Īśvara-form-state (sadāśiveśvararūpatā-samāveśa-daśā); in-the-
primacy-of-the-covering-part-manifestation (ācchādanabhāgāvabhāsa-pradhānatāyāṃ), [is]-the-
māyā-unfolding-form (māyāvijṛmbhā-rūpā), [and]-the-saṃsāra-state (saṃsāridaśā)."

Hence — "the-'blue'-and-the-rest-known-difference (nīlam ityādi-vedyabhedaḥ), [and]-the-own-body-
difference-manifestation-marked (ābhāsamāna-kāyabhedopalakṣita), [and]-the-intellect, the-breath,
the-body-made-of-the-eight, and-the-rest-condition-difference-mounted (buddhiprāṇapuryaṣṭakādy-
upādhibhedārūṣita), [and]-the-consciousness-shadow-made (caitanyacchāyākṛto), [and]-the-knower-
difference (vedakabheda)" — in-the-division-unfolding (bhedavijṛmbhāyāṃ), the-māyā-knower-ness
(māyāpramātṛtve) [is]-the-body-crowned (kāyo mūrdhābhiṣiktaḥ). And-thus — "from-the-removal-of-
that-alone (taducchedād eva prabhṛti), the-liberation-practical-activity (mokṣavyavahāraḥ), 'there-
there, the-destruction (tatra tatra vaikaraṇyaṃ), the-liberation (mokṣaḥ)' — and-the-rest (ityādau)."
That-primacy-of-it (tatpradhānyam asya), [he]-indicates: "the-body, too (kāyo'pi vā)."

"This (ayam), first (pūrvaṃ), [and]-from-the-manifest-form-ness (prakāśarūpatvāt), [and]-being-of-
the-consciousness-form-alone (cidrūpa eva), [and]-from-the-freedom (svātantryāt), [and]-brought-
to-the-known-ness (vedyatāṃ nītaḥ), like-the-clod-and-the-rest (loṣṭādivad eva), [and]-being-known
(vedya eva san), [and]-from-the-non-self-being (nātmabhāvād), [and]-by-the-removal-alone
(apahastanenaiva), [and]-the-'I'-sensation-identity (aham iti saṃvedana-tādātmyam), [and]-the-
over-lord-ed (adhiśāyitaḥ)."

"In-the-self, plunging (ātmani nimajjya)" — "the-blue-and-the-rest, [being]-in-the-blue-form-ness
(nīlādir nīlarūpatve), plunge-the-manifestation (prakāśaṃ nimajjayati); by-which (yena), by-the-
manifestation-force (prakāśabalāt), 'this-is-the-blue (nīlam idam)' — [this]-manifestation
(prakāśaḥ), [is]-made-limited (parimitībhavati); by-which (yena), [and]-by-the-known-property-ness
(vedyadharmatvena), [and]-manifest (prakaṭo'yam artho dṛṣṭaḥ), [and]-'the-object-to-be-known
(prameyaḥ)' — [thus]-it-shines (bhāti)." "And-in-the-body, [being]-plunged (kāye ca nimagnaḥ),
[and]-'that-I (so'ham)', [is]-the-limited-manifestation (prakāśaḥ parimitaḥ), [and]-by-the-
place-difference (upapattideśabhedena), [and]-by-the-past-and-the-rest-time-calculation
(atītādikālaka-lanayā), [and]-by-the-Rudra-kṣetrajña-and-their-sub-kinds-knower-form
(rudrakṣetrajña-tadavāntaraprakāra-pramātṛ-rūpeṇa), [and]-by-the-own-form-difference
(svarūpabhedena), [and]-by-the-other-place-time-form-separate-ness (anyadeśakālasvarūpaviviktatvena),
[he]-shines (bhāti)."

"Then-when (yadā-tadā)" — the-two-particles (nipātau), [and]-in-the-sentence-meaning-own-form-
reflection (vākyārthasvarūpaparāmarśe). "That-which-is-the-making-limited (yat parimitīkaraṇam),
[this]-is-the-saṃsāra-raising (saṃsārotthāpakaṃ), [and]-the-bewildering (mohanaṃ), [and]-the-
māyā-activity (māyāvyāpāraḥ)." "With-that-intention (tadabhiprāyeṇa)" — [i.e.]-having-the-saṃsāra-
delusion-in-view (saṃsāramoham abhipretya), [he]-connects (anusaṃdhatte), "by-the-object-ness
(viṣayatvena)" — [that]-which-was-said (yad uktaṃ), [and]-by-the-guru (guruṇā), [and]-by-that-
blessed-Vāsudeva (tatrabhavatā vāsudevena), toward-Arjuna (arjunaṃ prati):

> "And-whatever-[are]-the-sāttvika-beings (ye caiva sāttvikā bhāvāḥ), [and]-the-rājasa-and-the-
> tāmasa-which-are (rājasāstāmasāś ca ye); from-me-alone, here, know-them (matta eva iha tān
> viddhi)" — (Gītā 7.12)

— by-this (anena): "this-whole (sarvam idaṃ), [being]-of-the-known-form-and-the-knower-form
(vedyarūpaṃ vedakarūpaṃ ca), [and]-of-the-essence-of-the-manifestation-its-non-being-and-the-both-
mingling (prakāśatadabhāvatadubhaya-saṃvalanā-mayatayā), [and]-the-manifold (vicitram), [and]-from-
the-pure-own-nature-"I"-sensation-alone (aham-iti-śuddhasvabhāvād eva saṃvedanāt), [is]-sprung
(prasṛtam)" — [this]-having-said (abhidhāya): "and-from-the-primacy-of-the-division-manifestation
of-the-springing (prasarasya bhedāvabhāsaprādhānyāt), [and]-of-the-'I'-manifestation (ahaṃprakāśasya
ca), [and]-from-the-being-beyond-the-different-own-form-place-time (bhinnasvarūpadeśakālottaratvāt),
[and]-in-that-springing (tatra prasare), [and]-the-rooted (rūḍho), [and]-in-me, [and]-not-rooted
(mayi na rūḍho bhavati). For-of-the-springing (prasarasya hi), [and]-from-the-being-of-my-form
(madrūpatvāt), [and]-the-covering-part-alone (ācchādanabhāga eva), [are]-the-breaths (prāṇāḥ). But-
in-me (mayi tu), [and]-without-the-rooted-ness (rūḍhiṃ vinā), they-would-not-be-anything (te na
kiṃcit syuḥ), [and]-from-the-attainment-of-the-non-manifest-ness (aprakāśatvāpatteḥ); but-without-
them (na tu tair vinā), [and]-the-independent-"I"-manifestation (svatantrāhaṃprakāśasya), [and]-
the-refutation (khaṇḍanā), [and]-any (kācit), [is]-not (na)."

## The Brahman and the "all"

"With-the-saṃsāra-delusion-intention-alone (saṃsāramohābhiprāyeṇaiva), [and]-of-the-all-and-the-
Brahman (sarvabrahmaṇoḥ), what-is-to-be-commanded (kiṃ vidheyaṃ), what-is-to-be-repeated
(kimanuvaditavyam) — [this-is]-to-be-examined (vicārayitavyam)." There — "that-too

## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk C)

1. **svātmā → "the own-self"** — the "own" (sva) is the self's own form (the non-other-expecting);
   the inert things have no own-self, so the self-as-Lord is theirs too.
2. **the Upaniṣadic cross-check** — Abhinava cites Chāndogya 3.14.1 ("sarvaṃ khalv idaṃ brahma,"
   "neha nānāsti kiṃcana") and the space-portions analogy (ghaṭākāśa/paṭākāśa) to align with the
   Vedānta — but the Śaiva reading: the Brahman is the manifestation-reflexion (prakāśa-parāmarśa).
3. **the Gītā 7.12** — the sāttvika/rājasa/tāmasa beings are all "from me" — Abhinava reads it as:
   the whole known-knower manifold is sprung from the pure "I"-sensation.
4. **the māyā-śakti** — the Lord's own power, concealing-while-manifesting; the Sadāśiva/Īśvara
   state (uncovered) vs the māyā-unfolding (the saṃsāra-state).
5. **the body-as-covering** — the body (kāya) is where the limited "I" is plunged; the Rudra/
   kṣetrajña/knower forms.
6. **the jñātṛ-kartṛ (knower-agent)** — the identity of knowing and doing; the "I know, I do"
   (jānāmi-karomi) as the freedom. The "one" word refutes the object-ness (dependence), not the
   agent-ness.
7. **the "I" as all-pervading manifestation** — who sees the "I"-manifestation everywhere, in
   place, time, own-form, without division — his "I" is the all-Lord's own self, not covered.
8. **the seventh/sixth-case grammar** — the "in some way" (kathaṃcit) + the substratum; the
   subtle case-analysis of the sūtra's syntax.
9. **svasaṃvedana → "self-ascertainment/self-sensation"** — the self-luminous cognition that
   knows itself, without a separate means-of-knowledge.

**The crux:** the self as the all-pervading manifestation. The "I" that shines everywhere —
without division into place, time, own-form — is the Lord's own self; the body-and-the-rest are
its coverings (the māyā). The knower-agent identity (jñātṛ-kartṛ) is the freedom. Abhinava aligns
this with the Vedānta (Brahman = all) but grounds it in the manifestation-reflexion (prakāśa-
vimarśa), and the grammar of the sūtra is analyzed with precision. This is the heart of the
nondual claim: the self is the all, and the "I" is the Lord.

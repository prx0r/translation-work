## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk V2-N)

1. **The chunk's scope.** V2-N = M00021 lines 10000–10385, the CLOSE of the ṣaṣṭho vimarśa (the
   apohanaśaktividadhi). It opens mid-argument (the muktāṇava, mantreśvaras and their liberation
   — answering the objection raised at the end of V2-M), then gives kārikā 8 (*evaṃ smṛtau vikalpe
   vāpyapohanaparāyaṇe jñāne vāpyantarābhāsaḥ sthita eveti niścitam* — thus in memory, construction,
   or cognition, bent on removal, the inner appearance stands firm, it is determined), kārikā 9
   (*kintu naisargiko jñāne bahirābhāsanātmani pūrvānubhavarūpastu sthitaḥ sa smaraṇādiṣu* — but in
   cognition whose form is the external manifestation, the prior-experience-formed is innate, while
   it stands in memory etc.), kārikā 10 (*sa naisargika evāsti vikalpe svairacāriṇi yathābhimata-
   saṃsthānābhāsanād buddhigocare* — he is innate indeed in the freely-wandering construction), and
   kārikā 11 (*ata eva yathābhīṣṭasamullekhāvabhāsanāt jñānakriye sphuṭe eva siddhe sarvasya
   jīvataḥ* — hence from the manifestation of the uplifting as wished, knowledge and action are
   clear, established for every living one). The chunk ends at the ||6|| colophon
   (*iti ... apohanaśaktivimarśaḥ ṣaṣṭhaḥ*) at source line 10385, which closes the apohanaśakti
   vimarśa.

2. **The term-ledger LOCK carried from V2-E through V2-M.** vimarśa = reflexive-awareness;
   parāmarśa/pratyavamarśa = the (re-)reflexive-awareness; prakāśa = manifestation; ābhāsa =
   appearance; saṃvit = consciousness; jñāna = cognition; smṛti = memory; apohana = removal (the
   apohanaśakti = the removal power); vikalpa = construction; niścaya = determination; saṃśaya =

   appearance; anusandhāna = continuity; anubhava = experience; saṃskāra/vāsanā = impression;
   kāla = time; māyāśakti = the māyā-power; kalā/vidyā = (action-)range/(cognition-)range;

   naisargika = occasioned/innate; svalakṣaṇa/sāmānya = own-marker/general. The older

3. **Kārikā 8 (evaṃ smṛtau...).** The three operations — memory (smṛti), construction (vikalpa),
   cognition (jñāna) — are all "bent on removal" (apohanaparāyaṇa): each operates with the
   counterpart, removing the not-object, and in each the inner appearance (antarābhāsa) stands firm
   (sthita eva). The sūtra confirms that the param-essence-knower (paramārthapramātṛ) alone knows,
   remembers, determines; the jar-appearances are in-truth inner-standing (antaḥsthitā eva). The
   objection that "this"-reflexive-awareness (idamitivimarśa) is the beings' flashing but not the
   "I"-reflexive-awareness is removed by the prāk (1.5.2) and ahaṃpratyavamarśa (1.6.1) sūtras,
   confirmed by the kakṣyāstotra verse (sarve'nubhūtā...). The vivṛti is "spaṣṭaprāya" — quite
   clear.

4. **Kārikā 9 (kintu naisargiko...).** In cognition whose form is the external manifestation
   (bahirābhāsanātma), the inner appearance's clarity (sphuṭatva) is innate (naisargika): the jar-
   appearance comes from the Lord's freedom (svātantryād ananyaśaraṇatayaiva) at the first seeing/
   first creation, and is therefore new (apūrva) and clear (sphuṭa). In memory and the rest it is
   not freedom-come but experience-impression-dependent — hence faded (parimlāna), like a worn
   garland (nirmālyakalpa), asphuṭa (unclear). The "sargadvaye'pi" (in both creations) shows the
   pure (Īśvara) and impure (māyā) creations; the "sahaja" (innate) is the Lord's own freedom-
   unfold. The objection that the param-Lord (avicchinnaprakāśavimarśasvātantryaparamārtha) cannot
   remember is removed: the same one, having adopted the field-knower-being (parigṛhīt kṣetrajñabhāva),
   remembers, determines, conjectures, removes — there is no third state beyond the Śiva-consciousness
   (sarvānuyāyinī saṃvit), so the kalā-power (kalāśaktī) makes the some-agent-ness and the double-moon
   delusions show the Īśvara-activity clear even in dealing.

5. **Kārikā 10 (sa naisargika evāsti...).** In the freely-wandering construction (svairacāriṇi
   vikalpe) — the mind-kingdom (manorājya) constructions that rise and set without other-prompting —
   the external manifestation of the inner appearance is innate (naisargika). The objection that
   even the cow-horse is prior-experience-object is answered by the elephant example (the three-
   hands, tooth-ten elephant): the place-form joining (saṃsthānayojanāṃśa) is experience-following,
   but the to-be-joined part (yojyamāna bhāga) is not — so the newness (navatva) of the innate
   appearance is fit. The construction is not Saugata-free (svatantra) in the object-part: the
   object's own-marker (svalakṣaṇa) is established by the pramāṇa as the pure manifestation, the
   nitya-vyāpaka general (sāmānya) spanning all time/place (this is the Pradyumna line). The
   ābhāsanakriyā (manifestation-action) is innate, unmediated, time-marked-clear — hence even the
   mountain-family (kulagiri) is newly created every moment (pratikṣaṇam aviśrāntaḥ, sta. ci. 112),
   so the always-creation (sadāsṛṣṭi) power belongs to the Lord. The svabhāvahetu: what manifests
   thus, that is param-essence-being, like the manifestation. The embryo objection (the first 'I'
   in the womb) is cut by svabhāvam avabhāsasya (1.5.11): the innate 'this'-reflexive-awareness,
   even without the 'i'-distinction, is possible as made (kṛtaka) — the idam is conventional.

6. **Kārikā 11 (ata eva yathābhīṣṭasamullekhāvabhāsanāt...).** From the manifestation of the
   uplifting (ullekha) as wished, the knowledge and action (jñānakriye) of every living one (from
   Brahmā to the worm) are established as clear — the jñāna is avabhāsanātmaka (manifestation-own)
   and the kriyā is svollekhanirmāṇarūpā (self-uplifting-creation-form), both by freedom. This is
   the adhikaraṇasiddhānta (nyā. sū. 1.1.30, yatsiddhāv anyaprakaraṇasiddhiḥ): the purpose of the
   śāstra is the Īśvara-form-recognition (īśvararūpatāpratyabhijñā) of one's own self, and the
   saṃvidśaktiprasaramātratā of the thirty-six tattvas (the Bhaṭṭa-Pradyumna doctrine) is its
   essence. The "aiśvarya" singular shows the param-essence non-dual alone, even in the māyā dual
   appearance. The "ata eva" carries the prior-sūtra's reason and achieves the additional object —
   the siddhisamuccaya (establishment-aggregation).

**The crux.** V2-N completes the apohanaśaktividadhi: memory, construction, and cognition are the
one reflexive-awareness operating by removal, and in all three the inner appearance stands firm
(kārikā 8). The clarity of the inner appearance in cognition is innate (freedom-born, new, clear);
in memory it is impression-born, faded (kārikā 9). Yet in the free construction too the innate
appearance operates — only the object's own-marker is never new, only the manifestation-action is
innate and ever-new (kārikā 10). From all this the recognition-conclusion: knowledge and action of
every living one are the free manifestation of the one Īśvara-self, which is the very purpose of
the śāstra (kārikā 11). The whole ṣaṣṭho vimarśa — the smṛti/apohana complement to the jñānaśakti —
is thus closed at ||6||, and the jñānādhikāra moves to the saptamo vimarśa (the śaktivargaikāśraya).

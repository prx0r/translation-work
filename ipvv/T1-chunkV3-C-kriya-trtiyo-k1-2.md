## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk V3-C)

1. **The chunk's scope.** V3-C = M00022 lines 2055–3000, the kriyādhikāra, tṛtīyo vimarśaḥ (the
   pramāṇavimarśa, the valid-means reflexive-awareness), covering the beginning through the
   extensive commentary on kārikās 1–2 (the pramāṇa and the pramāṇaphala; the mānameya, the
   sākṣātkāra/svasaṃvedana, the kāryakāraṇa, the āgama, the saṃvāda/visaṃvāda, the tarka). Kārikā 1
   (*idam etādṛg ity evaṃ yad vaśād vyavatiṣṭhate vastu pramāṇaṃ tat so'pi svābhāso'bhinavodayaḥ* —
   the object, through whose force "this, of-this-form, thus" it stands, the object is the valid-
   means; that even, the self-manifesting, the new-rise); kārikā 2 (*so'ntas tathāvimarśātmā
   deśakālādy abhedini ekābhidhānaviṣaye mitir vastuny abādhitā* — he, the inner-thus-reflexive-
   awareness-own, in the place-time-and-the-rest-un-divided, the cognition in the one-utterance-
   object, in the object, the un-refuted). The chunk ends at source 3000, mid-vimarśa (the next
   chunk V3-D continues).

2. **The term-ledger LOCK (carried into Vol 3).** vimarśa = reflexive-awareness; prakāśa =
   manifestation; ābhāsa = appearance; saṃvit = consciousness; jñāna = cognition; kriyā = action;
   pramāṇa/prameya/pramiti = valid-means/known/result-of-knowing; pramāṇaphala = valid-means-result;
   mānameya = measuring-known; pramātṛ = the knower; prāmāṇya = validity; svasaṃvedana = self-
   consciousness; svaprakāśa = self-manifesting; sākṣātkāra = direct-manifestation; abādhita = the
   un-refuted; bādhya/bādhaka = the refuted/refuter; saṃvāda/visaṃvāda = agreement/disagreement;
   kāryakāraṇabhāva = effect-cause-being; liṅga = the marker; āgama = the teaching; tarka = the
   reasoning; anumāna = the inference; pratīti = the cognition; abhyāsa = the practice; saṃskāra =
   the impression; niyatiśakti = the fixed-power; parameśvara = the param-Lord; māyāśakti = the
   māyā-power; sāmānya/svalakṣaṇa = the general/the own-marker; bhāvanā = the meditation;
   anuvyavasāya = the re-determination. The older "rehearsal/reflexion/light" variants remain retired.

3. **The pramāṇa and the pramāṇaphala.** Kārikā 1: the object (the external blue or the inner
   pleasure) stands by the force of the manifestation (prakāśa) — "this, of-this-form, thus". The
   object itself is the valid-means (vastu pramāṇaṃ); it is the self-manifesting new-rise
   (svābhāso'bhinavodayaḥ). Kārikā 2: the inner-thus-reflexive-awareness-own (antastathāvimarśātmā)
   — in the place-time-un-divided (the pure consciousness) — is the cognition (mitiḥ), un-refuted,
   in the object, in the one-utterance-object. The pramāṇa and the phala are non-distinct in the
   main sense: the manifestation, external-facing, is the pramāṇa; the same, inner-facing as the
   vimarśa (contracted by the object-stain, word-bodied), is the phala. The reflexion-alone is the
   phala; the bodha is the pramāṇa by its strength. The śūnyādyupādhisaṅkoca is the 
   pramātṛ-pramāṇa-prameyollāsa by the one creation-power; the siddha-pāda verse (udety ekaḥ
   samālokaḥ pramāṇārthapramātṛjaḥ) is cited.

4. **The sākṣātkāra and the svasaṃvedana.** The bādhya/bādhaka and the abādhita are governed by
   the un-uprooted vimarśa: the jar-cognition's vimarśa is un-uprooted and object-action-serving
   (satya); the double-moon's vimarśa is uprooted (asatya). The svasaṃvedana (own-consciousness) is
   always abhrānta even in the deluded awareness (the bhrāntabodhe'pi svasaṃvedanam abhrāntam
   sarvatra) — only the object-part is deluded, not the awareness-part. The saṃvāda (agreement) is
   the arthakriyā-attainment; the viśeṣaṇa (the "idam etādṛk") is what the pramāṇa (the vastu)
   grasps, the sāmānya is the karman (to-be-established). The pramāṇasiddha (that which the
   valid-means establishes) is the un-refuted (abādhita), and the bādhya is the apramāṇasiddha.

5. **The kāryakāraṇa (effect-cause).** The effect-cause-being (kāryakāraṇabhāva) is not a real
   own-form (āsvābhāvika): the fire-smoke rule (paurvāparyalakṣaṇa) is only the consciousness-
   unfolding (saṃvidvijṛmbhā), manifested with the rule by the niyatiśakti. The dhūmasya rūpaṃ is
   the smoke-manifestation's re-reflexive-awareness-firmness (dhūmābhāsataiva pratyavamarśadārḍhyam
   āpannā), not the fire-immediacy, not the throat-eye-distortion-prior-ness; otherwise the
   anavasthā (regress) and the anyonyāśrayatva (mutual-support) follow. The kāryakāraṇa-inference
   (anumāna) is durlabha (hard to get): the one-many determinations, the sāmagrī/sahakāri-variety,
   and the yogi-creation all undermine the fixed inference.

6. **The āgama (the teaching).** The āgama is the firm re-reflexive-awareness (dṛḍhavimarśarūpaṃ
   śabdanaṃ), the word-transfer (śabdanasaṃkrāntiśarīraḥ) of the param-Lord; the mantras, the
   nāmākṣara, and the letter-syllable (lipyakṣara) all act by the param-Lord-will and the firm
   vimarśa. The famous (prasiddhi), rooted (nirūḍhi), is the sole pramāṇa; the Sugata/Kapila
   statements are the param-Lord's āgamas (the sva. 10.680 verse), and their beginning-less-ness is
   in the param-Lord. The Veda's validity: the īśvarājñā (the Lord's command), the
   vedātmikājñā (sta. ci. 69). The nibaddhā/anibaddhā speech, the viparyaya (opposed fame), and the
   mahājana (the Varāhamihira verse on the worship-rules) are all the firm-fame-based. The tarka
   (reasoning) is apratiṣṭha (un-established) — a kāvyasaṃsāra, not a firm ground.

**The crux.** V3-C opens the pramāṇavimarśa of the kriyādhikāra: the object is the valid-means
through the manifestation that stands it as "this, of-this-form, thus" (kārikā 1); the cognition is
the inner-thus-reflexive-awareness-own, un-refuted, in the one-utterance-object (kārikā 2). The
pramāṇa and its phala are non-distinct (the manifestation external-facing = the pramāṇa, inner-
facing = the phala). The svasaṃvedana is always un-deluded; only the object-part is deluded in the
bhrānta. The effect-cause and the inference are not real own-forms but the consciousness-unfolding
by the niyatiśakti; the firm āgama (the param-Lord's word) is the sole reliable pramāṇa, and the
tarka is un-established. This continues the kriyādhikāra's demonstration that all valid cognition
rests in the one free Lord's reflexive-awareness.

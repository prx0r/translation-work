## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk V3-D)

1. **The chunk's scope.** V3-D = M00022 lines 3000–4000, the kriyādhikāra, tṛtīyo vimarśaḥ (the
   pramāṇavimarśa), covering kārikās 3–8: kārikā 3 (*yathāruci yathārthitvaṃ yathāvyutpatti bhidyate
   ābhāso'py artha ekasminn anusandhānasādhite* — as the taste, as the wish, as the derivation, the
   appearance is divided, even in the one meaning established by the continuity); kārikā 4
   (*dīrghavṛttordhvapuruṣadhūmacāndanatādibhiḥ yathābhāsā vibhidyante deśakālāvibhedinaḥ* — with the
   long-round-upright-person-smoke-sandalwood-and-the-rest, as the appearances are divided, of the
   place-time-un-divided); kārikā 5 (*tathaiva sadghaṭadravyakāñcanojjvalatādayaḥ ābhāsabhedā
   bhinnārthakāriṇas te padaṃ dhvaneḥ* — thus-alone the being-jar-substance-gold-shining-ness-and-
   the-rest, the appearance-distinctions, the different-object-makers, they the word of the sound);
   kārikā 6 (*ābhāsabhedādvastūnāṃ niyatārthakriyā punaḥ sāmānādhikaraṇyena pratibhāsād abhedinām*);
   kārikā 7 (*pṛthagdīpaprakāśānāṃ srotasāṃ sāgare yathā aviruddhāvabhāsānām ekakāryā tathaikyadhīḥ*);
   and kārikā 8 (*tatrāviśiṣṭe vahnyādau kāryakāraṇatoṣṇatā- tattacchabdārthatādyātmā pramāṇād ekato
   mataḥ*). The chunk ends at source 4000, mid-vimarśa (the next chunk V3-E continues).

2. **The term-ledger LOCK (carried into Vol 3).** vimarśa = reflexive-awareness; prakāśa =
   manifestation; ābhāsa = appearance; saṃvit = consciousness; jñāna = cognition; kriyā = action;
   pramāṇa/prameya/pramiti = valid-means/known/result; pramāṇaphala = valid-means-result; mānameya =
   measuring-known; pramātṛ = the knower; prāmāṇya = validity; svasaṃvedana = self-consciousness;
   sākṣātkāra = direct-manifestation; abādhita = the un-refuted; saṃvāda = the agreement;
   kāryakāraṇabhāva = effect-cause-being; liṅga = the marker; āgama = the teaching; āpta = the
   trustworthy; dīkṣā = the initiation; prasiddhi/nirūḍhi = the fame/rootedness; sāmānya/svalakṣaṇa =
   the general/the own-marker; anusandhāna = the continuity; parāmarśa/pratyavamarśa = the (re-)
   recollection; niyatiśakti = the fixed-power; parameśvara = the param-Lord; bhāvanā = the
   meditation; saṃskāra = the impression; abhyāsa = the practice; ekānekatva = one-many-ness.
   The older "rehearsal/reflexion/light" variants remain retired.

3. **Kārikā 3 (yathāruci...).** The object (e.g. the jar) is one (ekam) but its appearances are
   divided (bhidyante) by the taste (ruci = the freedom), the wish (arthitva = the object-action-
   desire), and the derivation (vyutpatti = the old-dealing/skill). The anusandhāna (continuity)
   established in the one: the reflexion (vimarśa), per-appearance-rested, grounds the
   general-dealing; at other times, by the quality-primary mixing, it is the anusandhāna. The
   svalakṣaṇa (own-marker) is fixed, the sāmānya (general) is relative (the mahāviṣayatva/alpa-
   viṣayatva). The bhittirekhā (wall-line) example: the own-marker like the wall, the general like
   the line-division.

4. **Kārikās 4–5 (dīrghavṛtta...; tathaiva sadghaṭa...).** The famous distinctions (the long, the
   round, the upright-person, the smoke, the sandalwood) exemplify the bheda; the not-famous
   (sattva, ghaṭatva) is the dārṣṭāntika (the exemplified). The dīrgha is seen in the jar's upright
   parts with the attentive eye; the dhūmacāndanatā are the vyutpatti-example. The bhartṛhari 1.35
   (pareṣām asaṃkhyeyam abhyāsād eva jāyate) shows the ābhyāsika-pratyakṣa. The sadābhāsa (the
   being-manifestation) is distinguished from the cloth/jar by the mūrtibheda (form-distinction),
   and the sannityābhāsa is the antaḥkaraṇa-manifested (the mind-re-determining, 2.2.3), not the
   bahiṣkaraṇa; its arthakriyā is the heart-break-removal (hṛdbhaṅgaparihāra).

5. **Kārikā 6 (ābhāsabhedādvastūnāṃ...).** From the appearance-distinction, the fixed object-action
   (niyatārthakriyā), by the same-reference (sāmānādhikaraṇya), of the un-divided (abhedin). The
   mixed appearances: one primary appearance (pradhāna), the others the same-locus (samānam
   adhikaraṇam); the sāmānādhikaraṇya marks the pratibhāsa that, by the sentence-meaning-recollection
   ("this-here-now the jar is"), animates the abhedin (the one-own-marker-attained) — whose
   arthakriyā is the aggregate-form. The pramāṇa (the valid-means) is the one pramāṇa; the
   saṃvādakam eva pramāṇam (only the agreement-maker is the valid-means).

6. **Kārikā 7 (pṛthagdīpaprakāśānāṃ...).** Like the separate-lamp-lights (that do not together
   illuminate) vs. the congealed one-building (ekabhavanasaṃmūrcchitā), and the streams in the ocean,
   so the un-opposed appearances (aviruddhāvabhāsā) — the jar-gold-red-raised — by the one own-
   marker-mingling (svalakṣaṇātmanā melanena) yield the one-effect (ekakārya) and the unity-mind
   (aikyadhī). The virodha (opposition) is by the niyatiśakti. The bhāvābhāvau (the being/non-being
   of the appearance) are the own-form-distinction; the mūrtibhedagarbhatāsaha is the mahāsāmānya
   (the being-ness). The kāryakāraṇabhāva is not an own-form but the consciousness-unfolding by the
   niyatiśakti; the dhūmarūpa is the smoke-manifestation's re-reflexive-awareness-firmness.

7. **Kārikā 8 (tatrāviśiṣṭe vahnyādau...).** In the un-distinguished fire, the heat-ness, the
   effect-cause-ness, the word-declared-ness (tattacchabdārthatā) are the one self (ātmā) from the
   one valid-means (pramāṇād ekato mataḥ). The pramāṇa-grasped (the anapekṣa, the akṛtrima, the
   niyatiśakti-made) is the svalakṣaṇa; the ābhāsa-antaram-iśraṇa (the other-appearance-mixing) is
   the viśeṣa. The auṣṇya (heat-ness) is grasped by the same pramāṇa that grasped the bhāsvara
   rūpa — the one valid-means, by the avyabhicāritā (un-deviation), establishes the
   trailokya-traikālya niścaya. The dṛṣṭānta (the pepper-jaggery-drink, the pānaka) and the
   dārṣṭāntika (the jar) are fitted.

**The crux.** V3-D continues the pramāṇavimarśa: the one object's appearances are divided by the
taste/wish/derivation but rest in the one anusandhāna (kārikā 3); the famous/non-famous distinctions
exemplify the svalakṣaṇa and the sāmānya (kārikās 4–5); the fixed object-action and the same-
reference mark the un-divided appearances in the one knower (kārikā 6); like the lamps and the
streams, the un-opposed appearances yield the one effect and the unity-mind (kārikā 7); and in the
un-distinguished fire, the heat-ness, the effect-cause-ness, the word-declared-ness are all the one
self from the one valid-means (kārikā 8). The āgama, the āpta (trustworthy), the dīkṣā, the
bādhaka, the prasiddhi, and the tarka are all grounded in the one Lord's reflexive-awareness.

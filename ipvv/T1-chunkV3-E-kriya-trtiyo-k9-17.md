3. **Kārikā 13 and the bhrānti/khyāti analysis.** The silver-in-the-shell: the shell-silver's
   unity (aikya) is re-cognized (vimarśyate), yet in the shell the "silver" of the mental cognition
   has no standing (sthiti) rooted in unity with the true silver — the external-ness, the
   object-ness, the all-common sellable-and-the-rest fitness are absent, because that vimarśa is
   uprooted (unmūlyamāna) by the disagreement (asaṃvāda) in the adjunct-place and the
   satpratipakṣa-vimarśa's stability ("not this form is silver"). The right-speaking
   (samyagvadana) is the manifestation-re-reflection-own consciousness; its opposite is the
   non-agreement. Abhinava resolves the rival khyāti-theories: the un-real-manifestation
   (asatkhyāti), the un-stable-manifestation (asthirakhyāti), the un-common-manifestation
   (asādhāraṇakhyāti), the non-object-action-making-manifestation (anarthakriyākārikhyāti), and the
   opposite-manifestation (viparītakhyāti) are each examined in turn and brought to rest in the
   un-manifestation-param-essence-ness (akhyātiparamārthatā): in the double-moon, the hair, the
   magic-show, the non-being/opposite dealings have no standing because their common pervader is
   the akhyāti itself. This is the chapter's refutation of the external (bāhyārtha) through the
   appearance-essence and the one consciousness.

4. **Kārikā 14 and the bheda/abheda.** The qualities (guṇa: śabda etc.) make the difference
   (bheda); the class and the rest (jāti etc.) make the un-divided-ness (abhinna); and both rest in
   the one knower (pramātṛ). The cow-ness/horse-ness (gotvāśvatva), the configuration (saṃsthāna)
   as a quality, the atom/measure discussion (paramāṇuparimāṇa = pārimāṇḍalya = the round
   configuration; dvyaṇuka = the long; tryaṇuka = the triangular/quadrangular) — all are resolved
   into appearances. The anuvṛtti/vyāvṛtti (continuity/removal) are both projected by the knower's
   freedom (saṃvedanasvātantryāt); the bheda is real in the māyā-plane, and the avikalparūpā bhūmi
   (the Sadāśiva state) is not a dealing-word.

5. **Kārikās 15–17 and the pramātṛ-pradhāna.** The world-variety is pictured on the even wall of
   the ever-manifesting knower (the bhittiprakāśa); in the param-essence-existing Lord there is no
   touch of opposed-absence (viruddhābhāvasaṃsparśa), so the new-appearance (navābhāsa) valid-means
   has no use in the always-manifested (sarvadābhāta) form. Yet for the contracted knower
   (saṃkucitapramātṛ) the new-new appearance is the valid-means; all cognitions rest in the one
   knower. Kārikā 17: by the power-manifestation (śaktiprakāśena) the Īśa-and-the-rest dealing
   (īśādivyavahāraḥ) is operated (pravartyate) — the śāstra operates through the known object and
   the attained object-action, the vimarśa of the valid-means is its life nourished by the word.

**The crux.** V3-E completes the pramāṇavimarśa's central theses: the tātkālikī activity comes from
the aggregate of valid-means grounded in the one free knower (kārikā 9); the far-near /
indirect-direct / external-internal and the distinct-manifestation-shadow ones are unified by the
primary-manifestation's one re-recollection (kārikās 10–11); the object-action is the innate, ruled
by the Lord's will (kārikā 12); the delusions (silver-in-shell, double-moon, hair) are all resolved
to the akhyāti, refuting the external (kārikā 13); the bheda/abheda of qualities and classes rest
in the one knower (kārikā 14); in the param-essence-existing always-manifested Lord the
new-appearance valid-means has no place (kārikās 15–16); and by the power-manifestation the
Īśa-and-the-rest dealing is operated (kārikā 17). All cognition, action, and dealing rest in the one
free Lord's reflexive awareness.

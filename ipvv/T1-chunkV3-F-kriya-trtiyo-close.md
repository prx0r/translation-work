## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk V3-F)

1. **The chunk's scope.** V3-F = M00022 lines 5000–5514, the close of the kriyādhikāra tṛtīyo
   vimarśaḥ (the pramāṇavimarśa). It completes kārikā 17's commentary (*śaktiprakāśenaiśādivyavahāraḥ
   pravartyate*) and closes with the ||3|| colophon (source 5513: *kriyādhikāre
   pramāṇatatphalaprameyaparamārthavimarśas tṛtīyaḥ || 3 ||*). The caturtho vimarśa begins at source
   5514 (atha caturtho vimarśaḥ) — the start of V3-G. The source is clean, dense Sanskrit
   throughout (contradicting an earlier, erroneous "OCR-corrupt" flag that never persisted to disk).

2. **The term-ledger LOCK (carried into Vol 3, unchanged).** vimarśa = reflexive-awareness;
   prakāśa = manifestation; ābhāsa = appearance; saṃvit = consciousness; pramāṇa/prameya/pramiti =
   valid-means/known/result; pramātṛ = the knower; prāmāṇya = validity; svaprakāśa =
   self-manifesting; svasaṃvedana = self-consciousness; vicāra = consideration/deliberation;
   nirṇaya = determination; saṃśaya = doubt; pratyabhijñā = recognition; māyāśakti = the māyā-power;
   māyāpramātṛ = the māyā-knower; paśuloka = the animal-world; jñānādiśakti = the
   knowledge-and-the-rest powers; dīkṣā = initiation; bhāvanā = meditation; saṃskāra = impression;
   moha = delusion; saṃsāra = the world-wandering; mukti = liberation; kāryakāraṇatā =
   effect-causality. The older "rehearsal/reflexion/light" variants remain retired.

3. **The kārikā-17 close: the śāstraprayojana.** The four-fold action (caturvidha kṛtya) and the
   prayatna (effort); the crossing of the delusion-ocean (mohamahārṇava) through the
   param-Lord's grace; the dīkṣā/yoga/jñāna and the Mālinīvijayottara 4.6 quote
   (*na cādhikāritā dīkṣāṃ vinā yoge'sti śāṅkare; kriyājñānavibhedena sā ca dvedhā nigadyate*); the
   bhāvanā and the vibhūti. Abhinava grounds the śāstra's abhidheya/prayojana/phala in the
   pratyabhijñā (recognition): the pramāṇa is the recognition, its result the recognition-rise
   (pratyabhijñādayaḥ), and the final result the unfolding of the full-bliss-infinite-power
   param-Lord form.

4. **The vicāra (consideration) analysis.** Abhinava gives an extended dialectic on what
   "consideration" (vicāra) is — rejecting the Naiyāyika "five-footed deliberation" (pañcapado
   vicāra) by dismantling the word/jñāna/vākyārtha options: the vimarśa (reflexive-awareness) as
   the doubt-own (saṃśayātmā) is the consideration's mark, not the establishment (sthāpana). The
   Nyāyasūtra 1.1.40 (*vimṛśya pakṣapratipakṣābhyām arthāvadhāraṇaṃ nirṇayaḥ*), the Mahābhāṣya's
   sandhyakṣara-vārtika, and the Yogasūtras 1.47–48 (*savicāra → nirvicāra vaiśāradya;
   ṛtambharā prajñā*) are all adduced. This is the hermeneutic counterpart to the epistemological
   core.

5. **The three koṭis (tips) of the pramāṇa.** The prākkoṭi (before-tip: the knower's desire to
   know, prameyajijñāsā, and the arising doubt that drives the pramāṇa-search), the vartamānatā
   (the present-ness: the pramāṇa blazes but illuminates the knower himself), and the
   phalaparyantatā (the result-end: the dealing). In all three the pramātṛ (knower) is primary; the
   pramāṇa is the knower's operation (vyāpāra), like the axe's raising/lowering. This crowns the
   pramāṇavimarśa's thesis.

6. **The Sārvātmika and the apoha.** The own-form/place/time-difference (svarūpadeśakālabheda)
   grounds the one consciousness-essence (ekam cittattvam); the mutual-absence (anyonyābhāva) is the
   exclusion-form (apoha) which does not obtain in the Lord; the many-cognitions and the
   external-meaning (bāhyārtha) are rejected via the appearance-essence (ābhāsasāratvāt) and the
   pratyabhijñā. The distinction (bheda) in the delusion is moha; the self/other cognition of the
   Lord is established through the powers (jñānādiśakti) and the pervading-ness (vyāpitva).

**The crux.** V3-F completes the pramāṇavimarśa: the one knower (pramātṛ) is the seat of the
valid-means across its three states (before/present/result), the consideration (vicāra) is
determined as the reflexive-awareness of the doubting cognition, the śāstra operates through the
recognition (pratyabhijñā) as its abhidheya/prayojana/phala, and the world is pictured on the wall
of the ever-manifesting Lord; the ||3|| colophon closes the tṛtīyo vimarśa, and the caturtho
vimarśa begins at 5514.

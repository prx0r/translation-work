   the known in the distinct known, is wrapped in the time-and-the-rest five (kālādikapañcaka) —
   the five kañcukas (kāla, niyati, rāga, vidyā, kalā) are not separate things or common known-
   dharmas but powers of the knower (pramātṛdharmāḥ) that limit (avacchedana) and measure
   (kalana) the consciousness. The time, the fixation, the attachment, the caliber, and the
   knowledge are all re-recollections (vimarśāḥ) resting in the consciousness, not in the known;
   the pāratantrya/aprabhutva of the aṇu follows. The Sāṃkhya-like descriptions and the
   modakarasavat (mutual-grace) of the corsets are worked out.

5. **Kārikās 10–11 and the tattvakrama.** The known (prameyam) is the twenty-three-fold
   effect-instrument-own; its one un-distinction-form root-cause is the primary (pradhāna); the
   thirteen-fold external-inner-instrument-series and the ten-fold effect-class (coarse/subtle)
   complete the enumeration. Abhinava adjudicates the Sāṃkhya pradhāna/puruṣa, the buddhīndriya/
   karmendriya (the adhyavasāya-determination and the ālocana-observation), the mahābhūta/tanmātra
   (the sanniveśa-configuration, the ākāśa/vāyu as the space/wind), the kāryakāraṇa, and the
   dhāraṇā/dīkṣā (holding/initiation) yogas; and he grounds the whole tattvakrama in the
   param-Lord's niyatiśakti via the pārameśvarāgama (its un-broken validity), not in a weak
   anumāna. The Ajaḍapramātṛsiddhi 20 concludes: all appearances rest, through the māyā-knower, in
   the true-knower alone.

**The crux.** V3-K completes the āgamādhikāra prathamo vimarśa: the māyā-power unfolds in the
emptiness/intellect/body (kārikā 8); the emptiness-and-the-rest knower, wrapped in the time-and-
the-rest five corsets, is measured and limited in the distinct known (kārikā 9); the twenty-three-
fold effect-instrument known with its primary root-cause (kārikā 10) and the thirteen-fold
instrument-series with the ten-fold effect-class (kārikā 11) are all established in the one
param-Lord's niyatiśakti and teaching. All the categories, all the instruments, and all the
knowing rest in the one free Lord. The ||1|| colophon closes the prathamo vimarśa.

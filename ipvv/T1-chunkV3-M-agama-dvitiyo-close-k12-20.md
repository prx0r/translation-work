   viṣuvat = the equinox; āveśa = absorption; jñānī/yogin = the wise/yogin; vijñānākala/pralayākala/
   sakala = the knowledge-un-caliber/dissolution-un-caliber/complete; mantreśa = the mantra-Lord;
   vidyeśvara = the knowledge-Lord; saṃskāra = impression; māyā = the māyā; śaktipāta = the
   power-descent; dīkṣā = initiation; camatkāra = wonder; spanda = the quiver. The older
   "rehearsal/reflexion/light" variants remain retired.

3. **Kārikās 12–14 and the turya/āveśa.** Kārikā 12: the primary-ness of the agent-ness and of
   the awareness of the consciousness-self; in the emptiness-and-the-rest quality, the knowledge
   is the absorption-marker (samāveśa). The Gītā (12.2, 12.9–10) and the turyātītatā are adduced.
   Kārikā 13: in the emptiness (the intellect-and-the-rest-absence), the I-ness-agent-ness-word
   — the un-manifest-form-impression-mere is the known-emptiness-ness; the first suṣupta's
   seat-binding (pīṭhabandha). Kārikā 14: the senses' inner operation, the breath-and-the-rest
   stimulating — the life-named; or in the breath, the I-ness is the city-of-eight-own
   (puryaṣṭakātmikā); the jīvanakriyā and the āhaṅkārika sense-ness.

4. **Kārikās 15–17 and the three states.** Kārikā 15: the sleep-like (sauṣupta), dissolution-equal,
   with the known/un-known and the māyā-stain-joined/un-joined. Kārikā 16: in the mind-mere-path,
   the dream is the manifest-manifestation creation of the beings from the delusion. Kārikā 17:
   the waking is the common creation of all the knowers, with the all-sense-object-ness and the
   external-ness-firm. The prāṇādis (breath-and-the-rest) and the state-distinctions are worked
   out with the Ajaḍapramātṛsiddhi 20 and the Spandakārikā.

5. **Kārikās 18–20 and the prāṇādis.** Kārikā 18: the three are removable from the breath-and-the-
   rest's primary-ness in the agent-ness-quality, with the loss-increase-like pleasure-pain union.
   Kārikā 19: the breath-out-breath-made breath, individually in the sleep-waking; the cut-own
   equal-named (samāna) in the sleep-like, as in the equinoxes. The viṣuvat (equinox), the
   prāṇāpāna-samāna, and the suṣupta are analyzed (the Vijñānabhairava 61–62, the Spandakārikā
   41). Kārikā 20: the middle-upper-going udāna-named, the fourth-going, the fire-made; the
   knowledge-un-caliber-Mantreśa is the vyāna, the world-self param. The udāna/vyāna, the
   vijñānākala/mantreśa/īśa/sadāśiva, the kāla/kalā śaktis, and the prāṇaśakti close the dvitīyo
   vimarśa; the Mālinīvijayottara 2.42 crowns it. The ||2|| and ||3|| colophons close the
   āgamādhikāra.

**The crux.** V3-M completes the āgamādhikāra dvitīyo vimarśa: the absorption-knowledge in the
primary agent-awareness (kārikā 12); the first suṣupta as the I-ness-agent-ness-word in the
emptiness (kārikā 13); the senses' inner operation as the life-named with the city-of-eight I-ness
(kārikā 14); the sleep-like with the known/un-known and the māyā-stain (kārikā 15); the dream as
the delusion-creation (kārikā 16); the waking as the common creation (kārikā 17); the removable
three from the breath-primary (kārikā 18); the equal-named (samāna) in the sleep-like as the
equinox (kārikā 19); and the udāna/vyāna as the fourth-going fire-made and the world-self param
(kārikā 20). All the knower-states, all the winds, and all the categories rest in the one free
Lord. The ||2|| and ||3|| colophons close the āgamādhikāra.

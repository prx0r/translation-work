3. **Kārikā 9 and the paśu's asādhāraṇī sṛṣṭi.** The paśu's un-common creation is the

   aparijñāna alone. The māyāśakti as the īśvara's viśvaparāmarśavigraha, the vargāṣṭaka, the
   varṇadevatās, and the aṣṭāṣṭakabheda (the Bhartṛhari-āgama concordance) are worked out; the
   svabhāvahetu and the pratyakṣa are validated against the saṃskāradarśana.

   vs. established deśakāla); the "cittavṛttibhedamayā svopalabdhā" and the pratikṣaṇa movement; the
   Spa. 3.16, the māyāśakti's tryaṃśā (tucchatābhāsa, vaiparītyābhāsa, nañartha), the nañartha, the
   kārmamala, the parāmaśa-pīḍana, and the varṇānāṃ anantatā (Spa. 1.1, Māyāvaibhava, Sarvavīra).
   The "bandharūpa" and the "sabhittika" svātantrya of the kṣetrajña are reconciled: even in the
   manorājya the sṛṣṭi is parādhīnā.

5. **Kārikā 11 and the sādhāraṇā aiśvarī sṛṣṭi.** The aiśvara sarga (spaṣṭāvabhāsana, avikalpya)
   is sādhāraṇa or asādhāraṇa (the dvicandra), both anyathābhūta from the pāśavī sṛṣṭi; the
   vikalpahāna-krama and the ekāgratā bring the paśu to the īśvaratāpadam (the Vīrāvalī-Bhairava
   113); the parijñātayā īśaśaktyā even the pāśava sarga becomes sādhāraṇa (the nyastamantra
   comparison; the "aiśvara eva sadā sarga" reading over the other vyākhyāntara). The svapna
   (saṅkalpana vs. sparśarasa, the sarvapramātṛsādhāraṇya), the māyīyakṣetrajña's pratiyogin, the
   vikalpalakṣaṇa (1.6.1), the pratyayasṛṣṭi as kārmamalāpyāyikā and the parāmṛtarasa, the
   kṣetrajñatāmasvātantrya (Spa. 3.14), the Bhaṭṭadivākavatsa (dehapratyayadvīpabhaṅga), the
   vikalpanirhrāsa (pā. vā. 3.207, saṃhṛtya sarvataś cintām), the ekāgratā-practice-ladder, the
   tattvārthasaṅgraha's non-repetition, the Vidyākalāśaktis, the ādisiddha-sūtra, the ghaṭādhyavasāya
   (1.5.20), the malatraya and the mokṣa (sattānandasūtra 4.1.6, kaivalya, the bhakta-jana), the
   svaprakāśa, the nirvikalpaka, the nīlādirūpatā and the cittattva, the svabhāvahetu and the
   pratyakṣa (Spa. 1.7, the agnīndhana example, the kāryakāraṇa, the sāhitya, the
   niyatapaurvāparya), the īśvaratā/śivatā gradation and the malasaṃskāra, the aparā/parāparā siddhi,
   the kriyāśakti (bhedaparāmarśa), the pramātrekavapuṣaḥ, the jñānamālā/ātmaśabda Śākya
   comparison, the bha. gī. 13.15 (antikaṃ ca tat), and the "tadevam" nigamana.

6. **Kārikā 12 and the viśvātma.** With the vikalpanirhrāsa and the nirvikalpakasātmībhāva, the
   svapratyaya svaprakāśa is the mokṣa; without it, even in the vikalpānāṃ prasara the viśvātman's
   maheśatā. The "na pratyagātmā kaścid anyo'haṃ" (the grāhyagrāhakaprakāśaghana param-eśvara is
   the aham), the vikalpā api mahāvimarśa-patitā avikalpībhavanti, the Pāradarśana (śaṅkāpi na
   viśaṅkyeta), the Śivadṛṣṭi 1.38 (the sārvabhauma king, pādātadharmāṃs), the prakṛtitvam-prāptān
   vikārān ākaroti, the ṛtudhāmā Āditya (the Bhartṛhari-āgama saṃvāda, the sūṣumnā-nāḍī), the
   pratyāpatti as the māyāpramātuḥ-utthāpita-bhedāvabhāsabhaṅga, and the "maheśvarataiva" (the vṛtti,
   the pratyabhijñā).

7. **Kārikās 13–15 and the meya/mokṣa/śiva.** Kārikā 13: the meya is sādhāraṇa; the mukta thinks
   it with svātmābhedena (like the maheśvara), the baddha with the atyantabhedavat; the
   sādākhyād-ākrimi mātṛvarga as the ahamitirūpa, the ekarasābhedaviśrānti, the Śivadṛṣṭi 5.106/
   7.91/7.93, the grāhyagata-bheda atiśaya, and the tathātvayojana (yathāyogaṃ, not yathākramam).
   Kārikā 14: in the parama-śiva there is no kaścid prameya (the sarvathā, the antarālīna
   anantatattvaugha-nirbhara, the cidānandaghana, the paramākṣaravigraha); the ahaṃpratītir eva
   pūryate, the dūrāpetā bhinnaprameyākāṅkṣā, the dainyayoginī pramātṛtā. Kārikā 15: the
   jñānakriye, the yathepsitān paśyan jānāti karoti; the jīvanmukta, the dehapāte parameśvara; the
   māyāpade'pi citprakāśa (deśakālāspṛṣṭa), the sadāśiveśarūpatā, the "nā khaṇḍayati" (the kācit
   = neti), the manāgapi nīlādyābhāsatva, the Buddhists (kṣaṇika, the virodhacintā, the
   paramāṇusaṅkoca, the "saṃvidastu na yuktaḥ"), the niravabhāsā saṃvid (a. pra. si. 20), the
   ābhāsa-vimarśa stability (śukti-rajata), the bādhaka, the "ātmā aśakyasamayaḥ" (pā. vā. 3.249),
   the ekānekarūpatā, the idantāparāmarśa, the aikādhikaraṇya īśvaratā/śivatā, and the
   paramaśivarūpāntarlīna īśvaratā (Spa. 1.2). The ||3|| colophon closes the third vimarśa.

**The crux.** V3-O completes the mātṛmeyabandhamokṣatattvavimarśa: the paśu's asādhāraṇī
īśasṛṣṭyupajīvinī vikalpakriyā (kārikā 9); the prāṇarūpā, varṇadevatā-beset construction-action
(kārikā 10); the sādhāraṇā aiśvarī sarga and the vikalpahāna-ekāgratā path to īśvaratā (kārikā 11);
the viśvātman's maheśatā even in the vikalpa-prasara (kārikā 12); the meya as sādhāraṇa, known by
the mukta with svātmābhedena (kārikā 13); the antarālīna, cidānandaghana, paramākṣaravigraha śiva
(kārikā 14); and the samyag jñānakriye as the fruit of the whole four-section teaching, with the
jīvanmukta (kārikā 15). The third vimarśa closes with the ||3|| colophon.

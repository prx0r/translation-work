# IPVV Anchor Corpus — the working manifest

*Target: Abhinavagupta's Īśvarapratyabhijñāvivṛtivimarśinī (IPVV) — the crown. This is the
anchor corpus assembled for its translation. Everything else in the project builds toward it.
Updated 2026-08-10.*

---

## The target

**Īśvarapratyabhijñāvivṛtivimarśinī** — Abhinavagupta's enormous commentary on Utpaladeva's
(largely lost) Vivṛti on the Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā. **No complete English translation
exists** — the #1 high-level translation target in the whole corpus.

**Distinction that matters:** this is the **Vivṛtivimarśinī (IPVV)**, NOT the shorter
**Vimarśinī (IPV)**. The IPV already has Pandey's *Bhāskarī* (complete English). The IPVV is
the deep one, where Abhinava engages the Buddhist/rival philosophers at length.

**Edition on disk:** KSTS vol 60 (ed. Pandit Madhusudan Kaul Śāstrī, 1938, Bombay).
Muktabodha e-text, CC BY-NC 4.0, data-entered under Dyczkowski's supervision.

### Lexicon corrections log (mined from published anchors — read-only vs the golden chunks)
- **2026-08-11 — `daśaradana` ≠ "ten-faced."** In the IPVV on IPK 4.1.10 (V3-O kārikā 10,
  source line 11719 `daśaradano dantīty api`), our T1 glossed `daśaradana` as "the-ten-faced-
  elephant." Ratié (*How free is imagination*, quoting IPVV vol. III p. 381) renders it "a
  ten-tusked elephant": `daśa-rada` = "having ten teeth/tusks" (the tusked Airāvata), NOT
  "faced." **Correction for the R2 register pass: gloss `rada` = tooth/tusk; `daśaradana` =
  "the-ten-tusked."** Source: `dossiers/IPVV_VOL3_MINED_TRANSLATIONS.md` §4.

## The method (why Ratié's apoha paper + the Vivṛti fragments matter)

The IPVV is Abhinava commenting on a text we partly lack (Utpaladeva's Vivṛti). Ratié's
*In Search of the Lost Vivṛti* reconstructs that missing layer from the IPVV's own margins.
So the translation method must:
1. Anchor on **Torella's ĪPK** (the kārikās).
2. Use **Ratié's Vivṛti fragments** to recover what Abhinava is commenting on.
3. Use **Dharmakīrti/apoha** papers for the Buddhist opponent's position.
4. Keep the **term-ledger** at Pratyabhijñā-register throughout.

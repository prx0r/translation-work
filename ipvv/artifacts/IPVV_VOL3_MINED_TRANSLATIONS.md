## Key framing

- **What is "the IPVV" here.** Our source `M00022` is Abhinavagupta's *Īśvarapratyabhijñāvivṛtivimarśinī* (IPVV),
  his commentary on Utpaladeva's *Vivṛti*. The published anchors below translate (a) the base **kārikās** (ĪPK),
  (b) Utpaladeva's **Vivṛti**, or (c) **the IPVV itself** in quoted passages. Only (c) is a same-layer
  word-level check; (a) checks our kārikā blockquotes; (b) is the closest the field has to a full running
  translation of our material.
- **The IPVV's running text is essentially untranslated.** There is no published full English IPVV. The mines
  are (i) Ratié's *Power of Action* (full Vivṛti ch. 2.1, HOS 96, 2021), and (ii) IPVV passages quoted+translated
  in Ratié's articles. This dossier harvests (ii) and flags where (i) exists.
- **Nothing here edits the golden chunks.** It is a read-only comparison + mining register.

## 1. IPVV on ĪPK 2.4.2–2.4.4 — Ratié, *A Śaiva interpretation of the satkāryavāda*
### our chunk: V3-G (kriyā caturtho, kārikās 2–4)

**Chapter introduction (IPVV vol. III, p. 184):** IAST — *tatra kartṛ karmataiva tattvaṃ
kāryakāraṇatāyā iti svamate ślokenopakṣipya trayeṇa jaḍasya kāraṇatā nirākriyate.*
Ratié: "In this [new chapter], after alluding in one verse [i.e. v.1] to the fact [— justified in the rest of
the chapter —] that in our system, the real nature (tattva) of the relation of cause and effect is nothing but
the relation between the agent and the object of action, [Utpaladeva] refutes in three [verses, i.e. vv. 2–4,
the contention that] causality belongs to the insentient."

**Objection + answer (IPVV vol. III, p. 186):** IAST — *nanu bījād udbhavaty aṅkure cetanānupraveśo
naivāstīti cidrūpa eva viśvāvabhāsaka iti kim etat? atrottaraṃ sūtreṇa.*
Ratié: "With the [next] verse, [Utpaladeva provides] the answer to the [following question:] 'But given that in
the case of a sprout arising from a seed, there is absolutely no intervention of a conscious [entity], what can
[Utpaladeva's claim—namely,] that only that which consists in consciousness manifests the universe—mean?'."

**The effect cannot be neither/both (IPVV vol. III, p. 187):** IAST — *sad vāsad vā kāryaṃ sambhāvyate.
dvyātmakam, anubhayarūpam anivācyam iti tu svavācaiva virudhyate. tatra yady asattā ghaṭasya rūpaṃ tarhi
svarūpaviruddhāṃ sattāṃ kathaṅkāram aṅgīkurutāṃ pādapatanair api rājoparodhair api vā nīlam iva pītatām.*
Ratié: "An effect can be assumed [to be] either existing or nonexistent [before the operation of its cause]. As
for this [thesis]: [the effect] is both [existing and] nonexistent, neither [existing or nonexistent],
inexplicable (anirvācya), it is contradicted by its own formulation. [Now,] among the [two remaining
possibilities], if the nature of the pot is nonexistence, then how could it accept an existence that is
contradictory with its own nature? Similarly (iva), even prosternations at its feet or favours from a king
cannot make the blue [accept] to be yellow!"

**Abhivyakti/sphuṭatva (IPVV vol. III, p. 187):** IAST — *vyaktiviṣayatvasphuṭatvādayo'pi
sadasattayānuyojyāḥ.* (Cf. IPV vol. II p. 139: *abhivyaktiviṣayatvasphuṭatvādayo'pi sadasadrūpatayā
cintyāḥ.*) Ratié: "the abhivyakti, the object-ness, the manifestness (sphuṭatva), etc. must likewise be
examined according to [their] being or non-being (sadasattā)."

**The potter example (IPVV vol. III, p. 187):** IAST — *yathā hi darpaṇasvapnasaṃkalpeṣu kumbhakāra-...*
(quoted, parallel to IPV vol. II p. 141: *tataḥ saṃvid eva viśvam ātmani bhāsayati śaktivaicitryāt.*)
Ratié: "and as a consequence, in this [world] the potter is the Lord (kumbhakṛd eva tatreśvaraḥ)."

**Comparison note (V3-G k2–4):** Our V3-G translates the same kārikā blockquotes (2.4.2 `jaḍasya tu na sā
śaktiḥ`; 2.4.3 `yad asat tad asad yuktā`; 2.4.4 `kāryakāraṇatā loke`) and the surrounding commentary in
hyper-literal form. Ratié's renderings confirm the ledger's key choices: *kartṛkarmatva* = agent-object-ness,
*sattā* = being-ness, *nirvikalpa/avikalpa* = un-constructed, *prakāśa* = manifestation. They also give the
*readable* prose (e.g. "the blue cannot accept yellowness") that the hyper-literal T1 compresses — a mining
aid for the future R1/R2 passes.

## 2. IPVV on ĪPK 2.2.1 — Ratié, *Otherness in the Pratyabhijñā* (2007)
### our chunk: V3-B kārikā 1

**Kārikā (IPK 2.2.1):** *kriyāsaṃbandhasāmānyadravyadikkālabuddhayaḥ / satyāḥ sthairyopayogābhyaṃ
ekānekāśrayā matāḥ.*
Ratié: "The cognitions of action, relation, universal, substance, place and time, the substrate of which is both
unitary and plural (eka̅neka), must be considered as valid, because of [their] stability (sthairya) and efficacy
(upayoga)."

**IPVV commentary on *sthairya* (vol. II, pp. 29–31):** IAST — *tathā hi—tattaddeśakālākārabhinnaḥ tatra
caitradeho'nekasvabhāvo'pi sa evāyam ity ekarūpatām aparityajann eva nirbhāsame...* (see the paper, pp.
29–31). Ratié: "To explain—in that [experience of mundane action], the body of Caitra, although having a plural
nature [since it] is different as regards this and that places, times and forms, [nonetheless] shines without
abandoning its unitary nature in the form 'this is the same [body]'. ... Whereas in the case [of action], the
conscious grasping (vimarśa) having such a form as 'Caitra is moving' is experienced as persisting, as not
being destroyed by anything."

**Comparison note (V3-B k1):** Our V3-B translates the same kārikā (`satyāḥ sthairyopayogābhyaṃ
ekānekāśrayā matāḥ` = "the-true, with-the-stability-and-use, the-one-many-support, the-declared") and the
*sthairya* commentary. Ratié confirms *sthairya* = stability, *upayoga* = efficacy/use, *vimarśa* = grasp/
re-recollection. Her *eka̅neka* "both unitary and plural" matches our "one-many."

## 3. IPVV on ĪPK 2.4.19 — Ratié, *A Śaiva interpretation of the satkāryavāda*
### our chunk: V3-H (kriyā caturtho, kārikā 8)

**IPVV vol. III, p. 234 (the objection):** IAST — *nanv iyatoktena pradhānasya kartṛtvaṃ samarthitaṃ
bhavet, na cidrūpasya; tac ca nāniṣṭaṃ parasyeti.*
Ratié: "But through [the arguments expounded] so far, [you] may have demonstrated the agency of matter, but not
that of what consists in consciousness; and the [Sāṃkhya] opponent does not deny this [agency of matter]."

**Vṛtti (p. 60) on 2.4.19:** IAST — *jaḍasyābhinnātmano bhedenāvasthiter virodhād ayuktam, svacche
cidātmany ekasminn evam anekapratibimbadhāraṇenāvirodhād yujyate.*
Ratié: "[Agency] is not possible for an insentient [entity], because of the contradiction between the
undifferentiated form [of any insentient entity and] the existence in different [forms that agency requires; but]
such [an agency] is possible in the unitary [and] limpid [entity] consisting in consciousness, because [in it]
there is no contradiction [between its unity and] its receiving manifold reflections (pratibimba)."

**Comparison note (V3-H):** Confirms *jaḍa* = insentient/inert, *bhedābhedavirodha* = difference-and-identity
contradiction, *anekapratibimbadhāraṇa* = receiving many reflections, *svaccha* = limpid/clear, *cidātmā* =
the consciousness-self.

## 4. IPVV vol. III, p. 381 — Ratié, *How free is imagination*
### our chunk: V3-O kārikā 10

**IPVV vol. III, p. 381:** IAST — *daśaradano dantīty api pṛthagābhāsān īśvarasṛṣṭān evopajīvati.*
(source M00022 line 11719: *...paśor asādhāraṇī sṛṣṭir daśaradano dantīty api ...*)
Ratié: "Even [this creation of the alienated subject]: 'a ten-tusked elephant', depends on distinct phenomena
that for their part are produced by the Lord."

**Comparison note (V3-O k10) — a real divergence found:** our V3-O rendered `daśaradano` as "the-ten-faced-
elephant." Ratié gives "ten-tusked elephant." `daśa-rada` = "having ten teeth/tusks" (Airāvata), *not* "ten-
faced." This is a **mining-corrected reading**: the golden chunk's *rada* was mis-glossed as "faced"; the
correct sense is "tusked/teethed." (Per the no-edit rule the chunk stays; this dossier records the correction
for the R2 register pass.) This is exactly the value of the mine.

## 6. The svabhāvahetu — Torella, *On reason and scripture in the Pratyabhijñā*
### our chunk: V3-G / V3-H (the anumāna, svabhāvahetu)

Torella (pp. 116–117) on the *svabhāvahetu* used in the Pratyabhijñā's proof that the individual is the Lord:
"it aims at showing that the individual is the Lord by making obvious that the very nature (svabhāva) of the
individual self implies its identity with the Lord, just as the very nature of a śiṃśapā implies its being a
tree. According to the Buddhist logicians from whom Utpaladeva borrows this terminology, such an inference does
not establish the existence of the thing itself (vastu) but only a particular usage (vyavahāra)."

**Comparison note (V3-G/H):** Confirms the ledger's *svabhāvahetu* = the own-nature-reason, *vastu* =
thing, *vyavahāra* = usage, and gives the readable śiṃśapā example (matching our V3-G/H "svabhāvahetu"
discussion and our V3-O cross-reference). A direct R1 prose aid.

## 7. IPVV on ĪPK 2.4.12 (the ābhāsavāda) — Ratié, *On the distinction between epistemic and
metaphysical Buddhist idealisms*
### our chunk: V3-H kārikā 12

IPVV vol. III p. 214 is cited (with the readable exposition): "the term ābhāsavāda obviously applies both to
the Śaiva nondualist system and to that, immediately mentioned at the beginning of the commentary on the
verse, of the Vijñānavādins." The passage concerns the two kinds of arguments (the piśāca vs. the external
object; the anupalabdhi borrowed from Dharmakīrti; sambhāvanā; *anupapatti* = "impossible in fact" and/or
"impossible to account for in a rational way").

**Comparison note (V3-H k12):** our V3-H kārikā 12 opens with the *ābhāsavastuvāda / ābhāsayor eva
kāryakāraṇabhāva* objection and the *dhūmābhāsa / agnyābhāsa / anābhāto'gnir* inference discussion — the
very same ābhāsavāda + anupalabdhi + fire-smoke territory the paper's p.214 note addresses. Confirms the
ledger's *ābhāsa* = appearance, *anābhāta* = un-manifested, *kāryakāraṇabhāva* = effect-cause-being. The
paper supplies the readable Vijñānavāda/piśāca context that our T1 compresses.

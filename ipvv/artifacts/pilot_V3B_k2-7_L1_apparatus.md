non-being, from the cause-ness-non-determination; "the object-action-making-fit-ness, in the true-
false two, is hard-to-determine; with the other object-action, the regress; and of all, of this, in
the non-manifestation, the hare's-horn-similarity" — this is [it]. "The being-manifested-ness alone,
the un-uprooted-reflexive-awareness, [is] the being-ness. And she, of the relation-and-the-rest, the
un-alarming-alone." In the standing even, if anyone would say, "from the hard-to-grasp: the world,
first, desires the object-action; she, what is, of the relation-and-the-rest?" — of that [objector],
the heart, he comforts:

> Thus indeed the object-attainment would be, of the object-action-desiring mother;
> with the distinction-un-distinction-possessing meaning, by that, not the delusion, this-form.

"If the true you perceive: that, with the distinction-un-distinction-made, with the relation,
without, not any, in the dealing-ground, is the object-action. Where even he is not cognized, there
even, of-necessity, the inner-flashing alone sits." For: the pleasure-and-the-rest, desiring, this
way he would desire, if he would know that; and thus he would know, if that-very, that, would be;
and still, the un-desirable would be, of that, from the accomplishment. Thence, this is the fallen,
the un-crossable: "that which is, of the that-not-that-form two, the distinction-un-distinction-made-
ness, the relation/universal-and-the-rest-form." And this, if the untrue; what-now, the true, is
said? By that, whoever here does the delusion-dealing, he alone is the other, the deluded. "That," in
the grasping-sūtra two, the stability-use two, [are] fit-alone — the sūtra-meaning.

### The ||2|| colophon
"Thus, in the Śrī-mahāmāheśvara-Śrīmad-ācārya-Abhinavagupta-composed, in the
Īśvarapratyabhijñāvivṛtivimarśinī, in the action-section, the distinction-un-distinction-reflexive-
awareness, the second [vimarśa]." This closes the dvitīyo vimarśa.

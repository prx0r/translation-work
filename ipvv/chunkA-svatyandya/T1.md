## The svācchandya (the spontaneity) of the Lord

Here, again, the blue and the rest — [being] the not-abandoned blue-form and the rest —
manifesting, are of the non-difference-from-the-manifestation (prakāśābheda-maya). And this,
indeed, is the Lord's spontaneity (svācchandya) — that, being of the essence of consciousness
(saṃvid-ātmaka), by the form of the manifold knowers-and-knowns (vicitra-pramātṛ-prameya), even
[being] non-exceeding-the-consciousness-form (saṃvid-rūpānadhika), by the excess-of-the-
consciousness-form (saṃvid-rūpādhikatā), with the body-not-shining-as-separate
(nirbhāsamāna-vapuṣā), shines (prakāśate), and, so, reflects (vimṛśati) too. The shining of
the non-excess [as if] the excess — this is called the transparent-nature (svaccha-bhāva), like
the mirror's. For it (the mirror), by the varieties of the wall, the house, the elephant, the
horse, the man, the pot, the potter — and by the part-whole-ness, the support-supported-ness,
the cause-effect-ness, and the rest, that are theirs — in the host of the practical-activities
(vyavahāra-nivaha), even [being] not-at-all-exceeding-its-own-form, [yet] as-if-exceeding
(atiriktair-iva), whatever it shows, [so] it is called "transparent (svaccha)." But, of it, from
the non-being-of-the-such-reflexion (tathāvimarśābhāva), [there is] the non-spontaneity
(asvācchandya), [i.e.] the inert-ness (jaḍatva). But the supreme Lord, [being] the
manifestation-reflexion's-limit (saṃvido vimarśa-paryantatvāt), reflects (vimṛśati) himself
[even] as-so-manifesting.

And that which was said — "[if] it were always manifesting thus" — there, "always (sarvadā)"
[and] "sometimes (kadācit)": if some time [were] independent-of-the-making-of-it
(tannirmāṇa-nirapekṣa), [being] free (svatantra), by the dependence-on-that (tad-avalambana),
even [he] might be objected. But [it is] not thus. For, in this time, of this word, [whose]
meaning-is-the-illumined (dyotya), [there is] no other meaning apart from the so-manifesting
pulsation-of-the-sun (sūrya-parispandita). This would be said — "why does the blue-and-the-rest
world shine, in the co-existence of the sun-pulsation-and-the-rest?" And this, too, why [is it]
not objected — "why is not the blue itself [seen as] the yellow?" **Objection (nanu):** "Thus,
the blue would not have-been-manifested." If [it is] thus, in the other-wise-shining, that very
sun-pulsation would be not-at-all-not-shining. For, in the not-shining of the seven-oceans'
closing (saptārṇava-mīlana), indeed, on the day-of-dissolution, today's-day [alone] would be,
[and] not today's day, in its being, [as] the both-natured [day].

And, by the separate body (atirikta-vapuṣā), the supreme Lord does not shine — so, here, [there
is] nothing not-evident (aprātītika). For, of the sensation (saṃvedana) itself, in the
construction of the dream, the conception, the śāstra, and the rest, [and] even in the
external-activity (bahiṣkriyā) of the mass-of-beings-established-in-this-and-that-inner
(tattad-antarvya-vasthita), the non-excess (anatireka) is seen. On-the-contrary (pratyuta), the
beyond-the-manifestation-constructed-conception (pratītibahiṣkṛta-parikalpana) [is] not-evident.

Thus — "always (śaśvat)": the non-possibility-of-the-time-limitation (kāla-paricchedāsambhava).
"Uncovered (acchanna)": the non-excess-of-the-own-form (svarūpānatireka). "Transparent (svaccha)":
even in the non-excess, the shining-of-the-excess (atirikta-bhāsa-sambhava). "Free (svacchanda)":
the possession-of-the-reflexion-freedom (vimarśa-svātantrya-yoga). By the word "activity
(vṛtti)," of these forms, in the Lord Śiva, [who is] the power-possessor (śaktimati), the
shining-of-the-self-identity (tādātmyāvabhāsa) is said.

"Always (śaśvat)": not-limited-by-time. "Uncovered": not-partaking-in-any-way-of-the-otherness-
of-the-own-form (svarūpānyathābhāva). "Transparent": bearing, as-it-were, the exceeding-
manifestation (adhika-nirbhāsana). "Free": free-from-the-dependence-characterized-by-the-
seeking-of-the-other-reflexion-station (tad-vimarśa-sthānāntara-upayācñā-lakṣaṇa-pāratantrya-
viyukta). [He] whose activity (vṛtti), [being] the abiding (vartana), [is] the throbbing-of-the-
own-form (svarūpa-spandita) alone — he, indeed, [being] the-excluded-form-of-the-worldly-
delusion's-removal (aśiva-rūpa-sāṃsārika-moha-apahastana-yoga), [and], by the
purpose-of-the-supreme-subordinate-good (para-apara-śreyaḥ-paramārthatā), [being] of the
essence of the bliss-enjoyment-and-release (ānanda-sāra-bhoga-apavarga-rūpa) — he [is] Śiva,
the supreme Lord. To him — [with] "homage (namaḥ)" — the author manifests the prostration of
the speech, the mind, the body (vāṅ-manaḥ-kāyānāṃ prahvatā).

And the prostration [is]: by the seeing-of-the-supreme-excellence-of-the-Lord's-own-form
(bhagavatsvarūpotkarṣa-darśana), [and] by the throwing-off (apāsana) of the pride-of-the-
knower-ness-in-the-body-and-the-rest (śarīrādi-gata-pramātṛtā-abhimāna-udreka), [which is]
conjectured-at-the-time-of-the-arising (vyutthāna-samaya-saṃbhāvyamāna), [and whose] remaining-
activity (śeṣa-vṛtti) [is] of the essence of the impression-of-the-conceived-knower-ness-made-
weak-by-his-will (tad-icchā-vaśa-viśīrṇa), — the entry (samāveśa) [is] of the form of the
sinking (unamagnatā) into the consciousness-natured-own-form (saṃvid-ātmaka-svasvarūpatā), [and]
the surpassing-of-the-māyā-knower-pride (māyāpramātra-abhimāna-atireka). Such [is] the Śiva-
own-form. [Thus:] "as the principal-knower (pradhāna-pramātṛ), I rise-above (adhiśaye), I
lower (nyakkaromi) the māyā-knower-being-pride (māyāpramātṛ-bhāva-abhimāna)" — like the-other-
particle (anya-nipāta), this "homage (namaḥ)" word [is] the manifestor (dyotaka).

But that which is said — the "homage" word's own-form-primary-ness (svarūpa-paratva) — that [is]
not pleasing (manorama). For, by the offering-of-the-own-form-of-the-word to the Lord (bhagavati
namaḥśabda-svarūpārpaṇa), nothing [is done]. By the Gītā-maxim (gītā-nyāya), for the heard
"homage" word, in the Lord, the contentment (paritoṣa) [is] produced — this is the
oath-trustworthy (śapatha-śraddheya) meaning. Of the svāhā-and-the-rest, too, in the oblation
(homa) and the rest, the meaning [is] the increase-of-the-splendor, the offering-to-the-deity,
the satisfaction-and-the-rest (dīpty-abhivṛddhi, devatārpaṇa, tarpaṇa) — not the
own-form-establishedness (svarūpaniṣṭhatā), like the-other-particle (ādi-nipāta). In the
own-form-primary-ness, "and (caḥ)" [would be] read, like [in the case of] "itavat." In the
being-of-the-being-nature (sattva-rūpatā), the non-transformation (anavyayatva) would be, from
the connection-of-the-linga-number (liṅga-saṃkhyādi-yoga-prasaṅga). And, of the object, with the
word, what [is] the connection (yoga)? In that connection, the fourth [case-ending] (caturthī)
would break.

"Homage-is-made (namaskaroti), does-homage (namasyati)" — i.e. "utters the 'homage' word" — in
the termination-of-the-meaning (artha-paryavasāna), "to the supreme Lord (parameśvaram)" — the
external-object-connection (bāhya-karma-saṃbandha) [would be] not-possible. And, then, the
homage [would be] the verbal (vācika) only, or the mental (mānasa) too — so how [is] the
threefold-establishedness (traividhya-prasiddhi), the bodily (kāyika) homage? In our position,
by the mind, the speech, the body, making-oneself-prostrate (svātmānaṃ prahvīkurvan), lowering
(nyagbhāvayan), exalting-the-Lord (bhagavantam utkarṣayan) — [this] is the "homage's" meaning;
so all [is] established. As he will say — "by the naming-of-the-victory-homage-and-the-rest
(jayanamaskārādi-abhidhānena), the Lord's exaltation (īśvarotkarṣa) [is] to-be-shown, and
one's-own-inclination-toward-it (svātmanaś ca tat-pravaṇatā)."

And, in the āgamas, of the namaḥ, the svāhā, the vauṣaḍ, and the rest, [being]
class-word-signified (jāti-śabda-vācya), [and] useful-for-the-generation-of-the-manifold-
attainments (vividha-siddhi-janana-upayogitā), [there is] the being-the-manifestor-of-the-
this-and-that-meaning (tattadartha-dyotakatva) alone — [this] is said. Disregarding-the-existing-
object-service (sambhavantaṃ cārthopakāram anādṛtya), in-the-resort-of-the-own-form-made-service
(svarūpa-kṛtopakāra-samāśraya) — what grasp (graha) [is there]?

## The heart of the doctrine (the svātantryavāda)

Thus — since the whole shines (nirbhāsate), and in-which it shines-as-rested (viśrāntaṃ
nirbhāsate), and by-whose-will it shines (yadicchayā prakāśate), and which is-the-self of the
shining-whole (viśvasya prakāśamānasya ātmā), and which, in-the-whole-shining, [is]
uncovered (anāvṛta), and by-whose-freedom, even-being-non-excess, the whole shines-as-if-excess
— that [is] the supreme-reality (paramārtha-tattva), principal-ly (pradhānatayā) excellent
(utkṛṣṭa); the other, the-as-we (asmadādi), [is] its-inclined [part] (tatpravaṇa). This, here,
[is] the principal-meant (pradhāna-abhidheya) meaning-of-the-śāstra (śāstrārtha), driven-into-
the-heart (hṛdaye praveśita), by-which the-refutation-of-the-other-positions (vivarta-pariṇāma-
prabhṛti-pakṣāntara-pratikṣepa).

For — "the transformation (vivarta)" [is] the-shining-of-the-unreal-form (asatya-rūpa-nirbhāsa)
— this was said. And "it shines, and [is] unreal (asatya)" — how? — this [was] not considered
(cintita). But — "in the transformation (pariṇāma), the-other-form (rūpāntara) disappears, and
the-other-form appears" — this was said. But, of the manifestation (prakāśa), from the
non-being-of-the-other-form, in its-hiding (tirodhāne), the-blindness (āndhya) would-be; and
the non-manifestation (aprakāśa), appearing, would-not-shine — [so] in-both-ways (ubhayathā),
the-sleeping-world (suptaṃ jagat) would-be — [this was] not-examined. And, in the
reflection-view (pratibimbavāda), the-transparency-alone (svacchatā-mātra) of-the-sensation
[is], not-the-freedom (svātantrya); so the-searching-for-the-other-object (tatsamarpaka-vastu-
antara-paryeṣaṇā) [is] to-be-made. And "the-ignorance (avidyā), [being] inexpressible
(anirvācyā), causes-the-variety (vaicitryam)" — [this is] contradicted (vyāhatam). "The-Lord's-
power (pārameśvarī śaktiḥ), indeed, [is] this" — [this] is-the-heart-drawing-move (hṛdayāvarjaka
kramaḥ).

Therefore — the-not-to-be-denied (anapahnavanīya): the-manifestation-reflexion-natured
(prakāśa-vimarśa-ātmā) consciousness-essence (saṃvit-svabhāvaḥ) [is] the-supreme-Śiva, the-Lord
(paramaśivaḥ bhagavān); by-the-freedom-alone (svātantryād eva), by-the-knower-ness (rudrādi-
sthāvarānta-pramātṛ-rūpatayā) and by-the-known-ness (nīla-sukhādi-prameya-rūpatayā), [being]
non-exceeding (anatiriktayā api) [yet] as-if-exceeding (atiriktayā iva), by-the-not-covering-
of-the-own-form (svarūpānācchādikayā), by-the-glory-of-the-freedom-not-absent-from-the-
consciousness-form (saṃvid-rūpa-nāntarīyaka-svātantrya-mahimnā), shines. **This [is] the
svātantryavāda (the freedom-doctrine) [that is] brought-to-light (pronmīlitaḥ).**

## The critique of the "poor exegete" and the ascent to the good teacher

Thus, entering-the-supreme-Lord's-own-form (parameśvara-svarūpe samāviśya), the-author
(granthakāra), about-to-open-up (samuddhāṭayiṣyan) the-sūtra-and-vṛtti-meaning
(sūtra-vṛtty-arthaṃ) with-the-opponent-and-the-reply (pūrvapakṣottarapakṣaiḥ), [says]: "the
reasoning (tarka) [is] without-support (apratiṣṭha)" — [i.e.] the-unestablishedness
(apratiṣṭhatā),

> "Today, [it] will make [me], the-non-existent, the-existent; or, [it] will make-the-
> perishable (sataḥ syāsnūnatho naśvarān) [things] existent" —

— [this] is-of-the-essence-of-the-mere-showing-of-one's-own-power (svaśakti-pradarśana-mātra-
sāratayā), [and], by-the-cow-dung-porridge-maxim (gomayapāyasīya-nyāya) [with] the-ridicule
(upahāsa), [it has] the-non-usefulness-for-the-supreme-meaning (paramārthānupayogitā):

> "Ah! Damn the-exegete's-grasp (vyākhyātṛ-graha)! The-other (itara), the-hired-servant
> (bhākṛtaka): ah! of-the-reasoning (tarka), the-end (antaḥ) — nowhere (kvacid api) [is] it
> to-be-found (labhyaḥ), by-the-wise (vibudhaiḥ)!"

— by this, [Abhinava] fits-in-three-verse-forms (śloka-trayeṇa): "its-being-made-its-hater-
half-injury (tatkāritva-taddveṣitva-ardha-vaiśasya-yoga), the-non-authoritativeness
(aprāmāṇikatvaṃ); 'the-fire, of-which-the-water-is-not ([vahnir na yasya salilam])' — the-mere-
unconnected-utterance (abaddhābhidhāna-mātra); here [is]-not-to-be-conjectured (mā saṃbhāvī)."

"To-make-the-sky-flower-real (khapuṣpaṃ satkartum)" — [by this he shows]: "even-the-"also"-
particle (ekopiśabdaḥ) [is] the-other-order (bhinnakramaḥ), and "thus (tathā iti)" [is] the-
manner-of-showing-one's-own-capability (svasāmarthya-prathana-abhiprāya-prakāreṇa)." — In-the-
place, the-time, the-knower, [and] everywhere (sarvatra), by-the-non-being (asattva) [being]
known, [he says]: "the-sky-flower, [even] the-non-existent, to-make-existing (sat saṃpādayituṃ);
and-the-before-shining (puraḥ sphurad), [being] the-present-of-all (sarvasya vartamānatayā) [and]
the-place's-neighborhood (deśanaikaṭya), manifesting — hence, even-the-being (vastu), the-that
(tat), [being] also-non-existent (asat), to-make — capable-by-nature (prabhavanaśīlaḥ san): I-make
this-world, of-the-being-and-non-being-form (bhāvābhāva-rūpa), startled-as-it-were (cakitam-iva),
unestablished-in-its-own-form-as-it-were (svarūpe-anavasthitam-iva" — the-capability
(sāmarthya), in-myself, I-conceive (saṃbhāvaye)."

"Of-the-dispute (vādasya), the-opponent-and-reply-taking (pakṣapratipakṣa-parigrahasya); at-the-
time-of-the-commencement (ārambha-kāle), in-the-wish-to-do (cikīrṣāyām): 'that which, either-as-
the-position-or-as-the-counter-position (pakṣatayā vā pratipakṣatayā vā), I-wish-to-make — that,
for-me, thus-happens' — [this] is-the-utterance-of-him (eṣā yasya uktiḥ)." "Thus (tathā iti)" —
by-the-manner-of-the-showing-of-one's-own-capability (svasāmarthya-prathana-abhiprāya-prakāreṇa)
— he, indeed, clearly (sphuṭam), makes-the-criticism-of-himself (ātmano nindāṃ karoti).

"[He] whose-word, [being] bereft-of-the-meaning (artharahita), [being]-empty-of-the-told-and-
the-purpose (abhidheya-prayojana-śūnya), [and being]-not-completed (asamāpta) too, [being]-the-
not-established-told (apariniṣṭhitābhidheya), [and, from] the-non-being-of-the-naming-of-the-
basis (niyamani-dāna-abhidhāna-ābhāva), [being]-un-conjectured (asaṃbhāvita) — because-of-the-
non-possession-of-my-deliberate-action (mama prekṣāpūrvakāritā-viraha) — proceeds (prasarati),
starts-of-itself (svayam eva pravartate); and, there, the-disturbance-of-the-inner-wind
(prakupitāntaravāta) [is] the-occasion (nimitta) of-the-utterance-of-the-roots (dhātu-prakṣobha)."

He who would say — "the-same-blue [is] the-yellow; [or] not-the-blue, the-yellow; [or] not-the-
blue, not-the-yellow, this" — he, [being]-the-bearer-of-the-madness (unmatta-dhaureya), except-
the-praise (praśaṃsā), would-not-be-the-receptacle-of-another-praise (stuty-antara-pātra). His-
showing-of-one's-own-capability (svasāmarthya-pradarśana), by-such-a-path, [is] the-showing-of-
the-madness (unmāda-pradarśana) — this-is-the-intention (tātparyam). And-not-merely-the-madness
(unmāda-mātra), but [also]: "the-other (paraḥ), being-led-by-this-path (amunā mārgeṇa nīyamānaḥ),
would-be-made-to-wander (vitāritaḥ)" — to-show-this-great-sin-acquisition (mahad pātakārjana),
[he] says "of-the-inert (jaḍānām)."

For — even-of-the-employed-in-the-valid-means (upayukta-pramāṇa-vṛtta), by-the-hundreds-of-
efforts-of-the-guru's-service, the-śāstra's-cultivation, and-the-rest — yet, those-who-are-the-
without-deliberate-action (aprekṣāpūrvakārin), they [are] the-inert (jaḍāḥ). Of-them, the-
eruptions (udgārāḥ), [being] the-un-transformation-born (apariṇāma-prabhava), [being]
characterized-by-the-lack-of-the-right-unfolding-of-the-utilized-śāstra-meaning (upayukta-
śāstrārtha-samyag-vikāra-viraha-lakṣaṇa), [being]-empty-of-the-told-and-the-purpose
(abhidheya-prayojana-śūnya), [and, in-the-non-reflection (ananusaṃdhāne-api)], the-forcedly-
proceeding (haṭha-pravṛtta) wind-disturbance-chatter (vāta-prakṣobha-pralāpa) — by-them, [is]
corrupted (dūṣitā) the-taste (ruciḥ), [i.e.] one's-own-splendor (svā dīptiḥ), [being]
characterized-by-the-supreme-reality-own-form (paramārtha-svarūpa-lakṣaṇā); and-in-which
(yasyāṃ), by-the-unreal-form (asatā rūpeṇa), of-that-being (tasya sataḥ) the-supreme-real-form
(pāramārthikasya rūpasya), the-commixture (saṃkīrṇatva), the-mixing (vyāmiśratva) — from-that-
capability (tatsāmarthyāt), she-is-such (sā tādṛśī). The-array (āvaliḥ) of-the-desiring
(arthayitṛ), the-knowers (pramātṛ), and-the-desired (arthyamāna), the-knowns (prameya) — the-
pair-natured (yugalakamayaḥ) mass (samūhaḥ), even-the-mutually-facing (parasparonmukho'pi) —
around (paritaḥ), fades (mlāyati), leaving-the-upraised-neck-ness (unmukhatoddhura-kandharatāṃ
hitvā), bows (avanamati), as-it-were; [it] attains-the-indifference-to-the-doing (svakartavya-
audāsīnyaṃ pratipadyate).

As — of-those-with-weak-digestive-fire (mandāgni), the-gluttons (audarika), [being]-oppressed
(ajaraṇa-janita), [and having]-the-rough-eruptions (ugrā udgārāḥ) — some-bewildered (mugdhā),
though-eager-for-enjoyment (bhogotsukā), in-every-enjoyable (sarvatra bhogye), in-the-betel-and-
the-rest (tāmbūlādāv-api), [by]-the-unreal, the-impure-form (asatā aśuddhena rūpeṇa), [making]-
as-it-were-the-commixture (saṃkīrṇatām-iva ullikhantī) — hence-the-destroyed-food-and-the-rest-
desire (vināśita-bhojanādi-abhilāṣā), by-the-tastelessness-fault (arocaka-doṣeṇa), attains-the-
fading (mlāniṃ pratipadyate). This-is-said: "of-the-bewildered-minded-world (mugdhamater lokasya),
[who are]-possessing-the-continuous-activity (saṃtati-pravṛtti-bhājaḥ) in-the-this-and-that-
duty-difference (tādṛśa-visaya), [both]-worldly-and-Vedic (laukika-vaidika), [of]-the-seen-and-
unseen-purpose (dṛṣṭādṛṣṭa-prayojana) — by-such-false-erudition-chatter (tādṛśa-mṛṣāpāṇḍitya-
pralāpaiḥ), the-broken-taste (bhagnarasā), the-taste (ruciḥ) [is]-born; and, in-the-supreme-
reality-truth (paramārtha-tattve), [they are]-nowhere-let-down (kvacana avatāryate), from-the-
non-establishment (apratiṣṭhānāt). Therefore, by-such, in-oneself (svātmani), the-madness-giving

## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk A)

1. **svācchandya → "spontaneity/freedom"** — the Lord's transparency: shining-as-excess though
   non-exceeding; the mirror-analogy. NOT "arbitrariness."
2. **the mirror** — the reflection of the manifold in the transparent light; but the mirror is
   inert (no reflexion); the Lord is not.
3. **svātantryavāda → "the freedom-doctrine"** — the culmination: the supreme Lord shines as
   the manifold by his freedom alone; the consciousness-form's own freedom (svātantrya-mahimā).
4. **the refutation of vivarta/pariṇāma/pratibimba** — the Vedāntic transformation, the
   Sāṅkhya transformation, and the reflection-theory are all refuted: manifestation is the
   light's own reflexion (vimarśa), not a transformation or a reflection-of-an-external.
5. **avidyā as inexpressible** — the Advaita's "ignorance" is contradicted (vyāhatam).
6. **the critique of the poor exegete** — the tarka-without-support (apratiṣṭha), the
   gomayapāyasīya-ridicule, the "hired-servant" exegete — Abhinava's attack on useless reasoning.
7. **the taste (ruci) / fading (mlāni) metaphor** — the bewilderment of the world, the
   erudition-chatter corrupting the taste; the wise-one's clarity.
8. **the knowledge/light-darkness analysis** — the Sāṅkhya shadow, the Abhidharma darkness;
   the light-emptiness cognition ("I do not see the supreme") vs "I see darkness."

**The crux (the svātantryavāda):** the Lord shines as the whole by his freedom alone — the
consciousness-form's own freedom, non-exceeding yet shining-as-excess, like a mirror but with
reflexion. This is the heart of the nondual claim: the world is the light's own reflexion, not
a transformation of it, not a reflection of something else, not produced by ignorance.

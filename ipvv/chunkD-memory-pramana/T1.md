## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk D)

1. **the memory proves the eternal knower** — the Rāmāyaṇa/Vālmīki example: to say "I was not at
   Rāma's time" is itself a cognition of that time, which implies a knower who persists. Memory
   (smṛti) recollects the prior experience, requiring the stable self.
2. **the Gītā 2.12** — "I was never not" — the eternal knower across the three times.
3. **apūrvaprakāśatva → "the non-unprecedented-manifestation-ness"** — the self is not newly
   manifested; it's the old manifestation (the perpetual light). The body-and-the-rest are the
   "new" manifestations (superimposed).
4. **the pramāṇa/karaṇa (means/instrument)** — the eye-and-the-rest are not self-sufficient
   instruments; they need the agent (kartṛ) and the cognition. The instrument-ness = the
   excellent-service (prakṛṣṭopakāra), not the mere cause.
5. **the sense-organ and the infinite-regress** — if the sense needs another sense to know it
   (the touch), there's an infinite regress; the end (paryanta) is the self-sensation
   (svasaṃvedana) — the self-luminous manifestation.
6. **the pot-example** — "let the pot-reflexion be" — the intention precedes the sense-employment;
   the pot flashes before the sense operates (so the sense isn't the cause).
7. **svasaṃvedana → "self-ascertainment/self-sensation"** — the cognition knows itself; the
   end-instrument of the whole chain.
8. **the three excellences** — (a) in its being, it shines; (b) in its being, it shines alone;
   (c) in its alone-being, it shines — the third is the yogic/manifest direct-perception.
9. **the constructive (vikalpa) = the determination (adhyavasāya)** — the constructive part of
   the manifestation is the excess-over-the-manifest-form, i.e. the exterior-ness. The
   non-constructive (nirvikalpaka) is the manifestation itself.
10. **the vidyā-bhūmi (the knowledge-ground)** — the Śiva/Sadāśiva/Īśvara levels, the
    jñānaśakti-manifestation, the "I-this" with varying clarity.

**The crux:** the cognition and its instrument. Abhinava argues that the self is the eternal
manifestation (not newly-produced), that the sense-organs are only secondary instruments (the
real instrument is the self-luminous cognition), and that the constructive/determination is the
excess-over-the-manifest (the exterior-ness), while the non-constructive is the manifestation
itself. The self-sensation (svasaṃvedana) is the foundation — the cognition knows itself without
an external means. This is the epistemological core of the whole system.

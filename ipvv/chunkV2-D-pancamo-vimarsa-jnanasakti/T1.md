## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk V2-D)

1. **The pañcamo vimarśa = jñānaśaktivimarśa.** The fifth vimarśa opens with the maṅgala (yad
   anuttarasambodhād), and the 21-verse plan is laid out: the prakāśa (manifestation) is the artha's
   own form; the perception and inference of an external object are refuted; the parāmarśa-self
   manifestation; the jñānaparāmarśa as the known.
2. **Kārikā 1 (vartamānāvabhāsānāṃ).** The present-beings' manifestation is fit only for those that
   stand inside (antaḥsthitavatāṃ), with the external-self — the māyā-knower's distinct beings are
   the divided (vicchinnāḥ) appearances of the one pure-cognition knower; that manifestation is the
   Bhagavat's jñānaśakti.
3. **Kārikā 2 (prāgivārtho'prakāśaḥ syāt).** Without manifestation-form the object would be
   unmanifest; the manifestation is not distinct from the object — it IS the object's own self.
   The prakāśamānatā is not a separate property; the Bhaṭṭa verse "prakāśenāvinābhūtaiḥ...
   na bāhyasiddhimṛcchati." The one-sided refutations of the perception/inference of the external.
4. **Kārikā 3 (bhinne prakāśe cābhinne saṃkaro viṣayasya).** Whether the manifestation is distinct
   or non-distinct, the object's mixing (saṃkara) follows; the prakāśātmā prakāśyo'rtho — the
   non-inert object (Ajaḍapramātṛsiddhi 13). The non-inert object is established via the
   vyāpakaviruddha.
5. **Kārikā 4's recap.** The antaḥkṛtānantaviśvarūpatva (the Bhagavat's interiorized universe-forms,
   from Vol 1 kārikā 7) is established via the prakāśa's mirror-manifestation of the pot/cloth/cart.
6. **Kārikā 5 (tattadākasmikābhāso bāhyaṃ ced anumāpayet).** The accidental (ākasmika) varied
   manifestation would infer an external object (the bāhya-anumāna): since the unvaried awareness
   cannot be the cause of the varied ordered manifestations, only the external object-class (with
   the four shadows) can be the cause. This is the Buddhist's inference (the Dharmottara-view), and
   it is refuted (the anubhavasiddha-bheda is false; the pratikarmavyavasthā is svasaṃvedana-self).
7. **The Gītā citations** (6.31: yo māṃ paśyati; 4.18: karmaṇyakarma yaḥ paśyed) — the
   prameyapramātṛ anusandhāna, the sarva-karma-kartṛtva.
8. **The bāhya as pratibimba-arpaka.** The inference: the cognition-interior blue-form is a
   reflection (pratibimbatvāt), so it expects a similar identical (sadṛśa arpaka) — the external
   blue. The indriya-analogy (the sense-organ, never seen, inferred as the cause) answers the
   agṛhītavyāpti objection.
9. **The viṣaya's non-vaiśeṣya.** The vivid/dull (pāṭavāpāṭava) are the object's particulars, not
   the awareness's (the dust-scratched object deposits the dull-awareness-particular). The
   awareness cannot be entered by the other manifestation (the darpaṇa-analogy; the vyāpakaviruddha).
10. **The ekavacana / ananya-bhāk.** The blue-awareness is not a particular of the awareness (it is
    like the mirror's reflection — bodhānadhika, pariplavamāna). The distinct-distinct-momentary-
    cognition-continuum is in truth the non-distinct-awareness speech (the granthakāra's
    pracchanna-abhedabodhavāda).

**The crux.** Chunk V2-D establishes the jñānaśakti: the manifestation (avabhāsa) of the beings is
the Lord's own cognition-power — the beings, though appearing distinct (the māyā-pramātṛ's division),

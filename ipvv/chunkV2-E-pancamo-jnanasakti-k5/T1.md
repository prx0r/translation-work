## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk V2-E)

1. **The chunk's real scope.** V2-E = M00021 lines 2800–3600, the pañcamo vimarśa's kārikā 5–6 area. Its spine is the *bāhyārthavādin vs vijñānavādin* debate: (i) the kārikā 5 (*na vāsanāprabodho'tra vicitro hetutāmiyāt*) — why the vāsanā-awakening cannot be the cause of the manifestation-variety, since vāsanā/awakening are not other than cognition; the satya/avastu analysis of kārya/kāraṇa; the dvicandra/jaundice examples of the real status of error-cognitions; (ii) the rejected alternatives for the source of the ordered ābhāsa-variety: the icchā as prathamahetu, the karma as prathamahetu, the upasarpaṇapratyaya, the samanantarapratyaya-paramparā — all falling to the krama-niyama failure; (iii) the *parasantāna* (other-minds) inference and its refutation, culminating in the īśvaraikātmya/īśvarādvaya turn (Abhinava's own ground). The old file had mislaid a vāsanā-nīḍa/kaula fragment (a garbled out-of-range tail) which is deleted here.

2. **The kārikā enumeration.** The kārikā "*na vāsanāprabodho'tra*" is kārikā 5 in this run; the commentary also recycles "*ākasmikamahetukam*" and the muni-verse "*iha te karmaṇāmeva vipākaścintayiṣyati*." I kept the source's own verse-numbering (the `— (5)` attribution) and flagged the earlier draft's "Kārikā 6" header as the older draft's count. The user-visible numbering should follow the Vivṛti-line order, not the old draft header.

3. **Term-ledger LOCK (carry forward).** To stop the drift the audit flagged, this chunk fixes the English side of the register, and later chunks must hold it:
   - **vimarśa = reflexive-awareness** (the READ_FIRST primary); "rehearsal" and "reflexion" are retired.
   - **prakāśa = manifestation** (NOT "light" in compounds); **ābhāsa = appearance** (NOT also "manifestation") — the two must no longer collide.
   - **saṃvit = consciousness** (never "cognition"); **jñāna = cognition** (so jñāna-śakti = the cognition-power); **bodha = awareness/awakening** (kept distinct from vimarśa).
   - **samīhā = wish**; **vyāhāra = utterance**; **upasarpaṇapratyaya = approaching-cognition**; **samanantarapratyaya = immediately-preceding-condition**.
   - **vāsanā = impression / vāsanā** (kept as vāsanā with the gloss "impression"); **saṃskāra = impression** (the memory-generating latent); **prabodha = awakening**.
   - **īśvaraikātmyavāda = the Lord-unity-view**; **sādhāraṇī/asādhāraṇī sṛṣṭi = common/uncommon creation**; **pramātrantara = other knower**; **parasantāna = other stream (other mind)**.
   Where the gloss of a given occurrence deviates from this lock inside this chunk (e.g. the older "rehearsal"), it is understood as the legacy line, and later chunks do NOT carry it.

4. **The anumāna of the other mind (the crux-area).** The vijñānavādin infers the other's wish (samīhā) from the utterance-appearance (*vyāharāmi*); Abhinava, via the bāhyārthavādin, splits the first/third-person forms: caitra's *"he utters"* appearance does not co-inhere in maitra's own-cognition (svasaṃvitsiddha samīhā), so the inference is circular (the other-stream is already presupposed) and, if the mere-wish is universalized, it fits a demon/yakṣa/omniscient/worm equally — the determination collapses. The decisive move: only the *aimed* (samīhitā) action is the wish's effect; to abandon samīhitā-tva is impossible. The residual [X] flags: the exact construal of *ghaṭābhāsānāṃ* (whether "ca" joins the other-jar-appearances or the other-knowers) is left with both readings noted — the sixth/seventh-case tension makes the "ca with the knowers" reading fit.

5. **The īśvaraikātmya opening.** The encouragement of the bāhyārthavādin ("*satyam asi prāmāṇikadhuryaḥ*") and the sādhāraṇī/asādhāraṇī sṛṣṭi verses (3.2.17, 4.1.11, 4.1.9) prepare Abhinava's own position: the common (Lord-made) creation is where the knowers' difference melts into unity; the uncommon (paśu-made) creation keeps the difference. This is the seed of the whole sixth-vimarśa move — the reason Dharmakīrti's other-minds text is treated as *atadvarṇitamiva* (as it were not narrated by him, from partiality).

**The crux.** V2-E is where the IPVV disarms the Buddhist explanation of the ordered appearance-variety and of other minds. The vāsanā-awakening cannot be the cause: since vāsanā and awakening are not other than cognition (and whatever is beyond manifestation is nothing), the variety has no separate ground — the bāhyārtha alone can deposit (samarpaka) the ākāra-variety. Then every candidate cause of the kramika-ābhāsa (icchā, karma, upasarpaṇapratyaya, samanantara-paramparā) fails the krama-niyama test, because the middle appearances (gṛhavartin ghaṭa etc.) are never continuously present. The other-minds inference falls to the first/third-person split: the wish inferred is never the other's own-cognition-established wish. The only coherent resting point is the īśvaraikātmya: the one Lord-freedom manifests the varied knower-difference, and in the sādhāraṇī sṛṣṭi the difference melts into unity. This is Abhinava's bridge from the refutation of the Buddhist to the positive monistic thesis.

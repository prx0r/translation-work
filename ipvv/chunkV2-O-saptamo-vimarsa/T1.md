## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk V2-O)

1. **The chunk's scope.** V2-O = M00021 lines 10385–11200, the opening of the saptamo vimarśa
   (atha saptamo vimarśaḥ), the śaktivargaikāśraya (the one-support of the powers' group). It
   contains the maṅgala (svaprasarapreṅkhita...), the sūtrārthāvatāra (the 14-verse plan), and the
   first several kārikās of this vimarśa, together with their commentary: kārikā 1 (yā caiṣā
   pratibhā tattatpadārthakramarūṣitā akramānantacidrūpaḥ pramātā sa maheśvaraḥ — this pratibhā,
   seasoned with the order of the various word-objects, the order-less-infinite-consciousness-form
   knower, he is the great Lord); kārikā 2 (tattadvibhinnasaṃvittimukhairekapramātari pratitiṣṭhatsu
   bhāveṣu jñāteyamupapadyate — with the faces of the various divided consciousnesses, in the
   objects resting in the one knower, the cognition-aggregation is fit); kārikā 3 (deśakālakramajuṣām
   arthānāṃ svasamāpinām sakṛdābhāsasādhyo'sāv anyathā kaḥ samanvayaḥ — of the objects enjoying the
   order of place and time, converging in themselves, that is attainable by the once-manifestation,
   otherwise which conjunction?); kārikā 4 (pratyakṣānupalambhānāṃ tattadbhinnāṃśapātinām
   kāryakāraṇatāsiddhihetur ekapramātṛjā — of the perception and non-apprehension falling in the
   various divided parts, the cause-effect-ness-establishment-reason is the one knower-born); and
   the long commentary on the samanvaya (conjunction) as the vyavahāra, the jñāteya, the kāryakāraṇa
   bhāva, the relation (saṃbandha) as the distinction-un-distinction, the paurvāparya, the will
   (icchā/kāmanā) as the supporter of all dealing, and the pramāṇa/prāmāṇya analysis. The chunk
   ends at the objection about the ahaṃpratyavamarśa (11200), which V2-P answers.

3. **Kārikā 1 (yā caiṣā pratibhā...).** The pratibhā (the immediate flash of the object-word-order,
   "shines-the-jar") is the manifestation, and though it seems to cling to the object, it is not the
   object's own light (svajyotiḥ): it is the consciousness (saṃvedana) that flashes "to me" — i.e.,
   it is the knower-resting (pramātṛniṣṭha). The ka. u. 5.15 (tam eva bhāntam anubhāti sarvam) and
   the Bhaṭṭa-Candrānanda verse anchor this. The akrama-anantacidrūpaḥ pramātā (the order-less,
   infinite-consciousness-form knower) is the great Lord (maheśvaraḥ) — the pure
   ahaṃpratyavamarśamaya — whose freedom (svātantrya) raises the deśakālaparipāṭī (the place-time
   order) that "seasons" (rūṣitā) the pratibhā. The inner-turned form (antarmukha vapuṣā) is
   order-less, so the support (āśraya) is established without further effort (ayatnasiddha).

4. **Kārikā 2 (tattadvibhinnasaṃvittimukhaiḥ...).** The consciousness-resident establishments of
   the objects are not just the resting (viśramaṇa) in the divided cognitions — that would not
   establish the dealing (vyavahāra). It is when the various divided consciousnesses, ending in
   determination (niścayāntāḥ), are led as so many stream-faces (mukhāni) by the force (haṭhavāhena)
   into the one great consciousness-ocean as "I" (aham) that the cognition-aggregation (jñāteya) —
   the mutual servant/effect-cause relations, the samanvaya as the vyavahāra — becomes fit. The
   "yāvat"/"tad" and the aikadhyam point to the knower-fit (pramātṛrucita) partial simultaneity; the
   Sāṃkhya objection (the divided puruṣas in liberation) is announced. The saptamī-samāsa vs
   karmadhāraya and the bhāvavācinyā/kriyāvācinyā distinction are resolved.

5. **Kārikās 3–4 (deśakālakramajuṣāṃ...; pratyakṣānupalambhānāṃ...).** Without the one-knower-rest,
   the objects (blue, pleasure, their knowledges, their determinations), divided in place, time and
   order and fixed in their own form, have no conjunction: the samanvaya is only attainable by the
   once-manifestation (sakṛdābhāsa) that joins place-time-own-form in a unity-appearance. The
   effect-cause-being (kāryakāraṇabhāva) that the Buddhist establishes from the fivefold
   perception/non-apprehension is shown to be only their wish (manorathamātra): the prātipadika-
   meaning-only determinations (fire, smoke, fire-absence, smoke-absence) fall in different parts and
   cannot, by the apekṣālakṣaṇa alone, establish the anvaya-vyatireka truth. In our view the
   prameyas, stirred/rested in the one consciousness-ocean, are united and raised by the knower's
   freedom; the kāryatā is the thus-manifested-ness, the kāraṇatā the thus-manifestation.

6. **The saṃbandha (relation) and the pramāṇa/prāmāṇya.** The relation is not a separate real
   feature: it is the distinction-un-distinction (bhedābheda) appearance — the same form is
   distinguished by its counterpart (pratiyogi) and unified in the one manifestation. The connection
   (saṃbandha) spans the mutual-help (upakāryopakāraka), the expectation (apekṣā), and the dependence
   (pāratantrya), all rooted in the one knower. The pramāṇa (valid means) is not a separate agent
   working independently of the knower: it is prāpaka (the reacher) only as the knower's operation
   (pramātṛvyāpāra) — the reaching (prāpti) needs the prior memory (smaraṇād abhilāṣeṇa) and the
   will, which only the free knower's own-consciousness supplies. The Buddhists themselves, by
   accepting svasaṃvedana, are forced to accept the pramātṛtattva (the aśakyasamayo hy ātmā verse).
   The kāryakāraṇabhāva established from the external is refuted: the fire's prior-ness and the
   smoke's posterior-ness are never grasped as a distinct feature by the own-form-grasping cognition.

**The crux.** V2-O opens the saptamo vimarśa — the one-support (āśraya) of the powers' group: the
great Lord (maheśvara) is the order-less, infinite-consciousness-form knower, the pure
ahaṃpratyavamarśamaya, whose freedom raises the entire deśakāla order that seasons the pratibhā
(kārikā 1). The dealing (vyavahāra) and the cognition-aggregation (jñāteya) are fit only when the
divided consciousnesses rest in that one knower (kārikā 2); the conjunction (samanvaya) is only
attainable by the once-manifestation in that one knower (kārikā 3); and even the effect-cause-being,
which the Buddhist claims to establish from the perception/non-apprehension fivefold, is really
only the one-knower-born manifestation (kārikā 4). The relation (saṃbandha) is the distinction-
un-distinction appearance rooted in the free knower, and the pramāṇa is prāpaka only as that
knower's operation. This is the apohanaśakti's own ground: the powers find their support, and the
recognition (pratyabhijñā) of the Lord as the one knower is the śāstra's purpose.

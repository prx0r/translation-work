## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk V2-Q)

1. **The chunk's scope.** V2-Q = M00021 lines 12000–12423, the CLOSE of the saptamo vimarśa (the
   śaktivargaikāśraya). It opens mid-kārikā-11 (the piśāca/āloka atom analysis, the arvāgdarśana vs
   the sarvajña), then gives kārikā 12 (*evaṃ rupyavidābhāvarūpā śuktimatir bhavet na tv ādyarajatajñapter
   syād aprāmāṇyavedikā* — thus the shell-cognition would be the silver-seer-non-being-form, but it
   would not be the cognizer of the first silver-cognition's non-validity), kārikā 13 (*dharmyasiddher
   api bhaved bādhā naivānumānataḥ svasaṃvedanasiddhā tu yuktā saikapramātṛjā* — from the property's-
   non-establishment too the refutation would not be by inference; but the self-consciousness-
   established, the one-knower-born, is fit), and kārikā 14 (*ittham atyarthabhinnārthāvabhāsakhacite
   vibhau samalo vimalo vāpi vyavahāro'nubhūyate* — thus in the pervader studded with the appearances
   of the utterly distinct objects, the stained or the stainless dealing is experienced). The chunk
   ends at the ||7|| colophon (*iti ... śaktivargaikāśrayavimarśaḥ saptamaḥ*) at source 12423, which
   closes the saptamo vimarśa.

2. **The term-ledger LOCK carried from V2-E through V2-P.** vimarśa = reflexive-awareness;
   parāmarśa/pratyavamarśa = the (re-)reflexive-awareness; prakāśa = manifestation; ābhāsa =
   appearance; saṃvit = consciousness; jñāna = cognition; smṛti = memory; apohana = removal;
   vikalpa = construction; niścaya/avasāya = determination; pratiyogi = counterpart; pramāṇa/prameya/
   pramiti = valid-means/known/result; prāmāṇya = validity; svasaṃvedana = self-consciousness;
   svaprakāśa = self-manifesting; bādhya/bādhaka = the refuted/refuter; saṃvāda = agreement;
   virodha = opposition; kāryakāraṇabhāva = effect-cause-being; vyatireka/tādātmya =
   absence/identity; abhāva = non-being; dharmin/hetu/sādhya = property/reason/object;
   āśrayāsiddhi/vyadhikaraṇāsiddhi = support-non-establishment/distinct-locus-non-establishment;
   arvāgdarśa/sarvajña = the near-seer/the all-knower; piśāca = the goblin; paramāṇu = atom;
   samalo/vimalo vyavahāra = the stained/stainless dealing; māyāndha = the māyā-blind; vidyeśvara/
   mantramaheśvara/mantreśvara = the vidyā-lords/mantra-great-lords/mantra-lords. The older
   "rehearsal/reflexion/light" variants remain retired.

3. **Kārikā 11 continued (the piśāca/āloka atoms).** The goblin (piśāca) is not part of the
   light's own form: it is un-light (anāloka) and extra-sensory, so its non-being cannot be
   established by the light's identity. The light's atoms, being seen, are the ground's own-form and
   can be negated (the jar's non-being is the light's own-form dealt by identity); but the goblin's
   atoms, if any, would be un-seen like the smell/taste/touch atoms (Dharmottara's bāhyārthasiddhi)
   and cannot be excluded by the near-seer's perception. Only the all-knower (sarvajña), by the
   extra-sensory grasp and the rule (niyama), can negate the goblin — and even so the negation is
   the rule-bound absence, not a distinct object. The kārikā's "sarvathā" is the niyama restricting
   the absence to the known own-form.

4. **Kārikā 12 (evaṃ rupyavidābhāvarūpā...).** The shell-cognition (śuktijñāna) is indeed the
   silver-seer-non-being-form (it is the conformity/validity-consciousness of the shell, which is
   the non-validity of the silver-cognition's object-form). But it is NOT the cognizer of the
   validity-less-ness of the *first* (ādyarajata) silver-cognition as a distinct cognition: the
   reflection only concerns the shell-cognition's own form (svarūpacintā), and does not break or
   adorn anything of the distinct prior silver-cognition. The sūtrārtha: in the jar-cognition's
   validity, the prior cloth-cognition's non-validity being known does not do anything to the prior
   cloth-cognition itself.

5. **Kārikā 13 (dharmyasiddherapi...).** The Buddhist's inference-based refutation fails both
   because the reason (hetu) is un-established and because the property (dharmin) is un-established
   (the vyadhikaraṇāsiddhi). The shell-cognition's truth (śuktijñānasatyatva) is own-form-shining
   (svarūpeṇa bhāty eva) but not established in the distinct locus of the current silver-cognition,
   so the siddhasādhya (established-object) flaw follows. The pramāṇavārtika 3.249 (aśakyasamayo hy
   ātmā) is invoked: the self-consciousness is un-constructed (avikalpa), hence cannot be the
   dharmin of an inference. In our view the bādhā is established by the self-consciousness alone
   (svasaṃvedanasiddhā), born of the one free knower (ekapramātṛjā): the shell-cognition's own-
   consciousness, at its limit, is already the silver-cognition's own-consciousness (the māyā-stain
   removed), and this is what destroys the silver-cognition's result-continuation. The
   virodhasaṃvāda (opposition-agreement), the svabhāvahetu, and the vipakṣe ākasmikatva establish
   the pervasion.

6. **Kārikā 14 (ittham atyarthabhinnārthāvabhāsakhacite...).** The closing verse: in the pervader
   (vibhu), the free Lord, whose consciousness-essence is un-cut by place/time, studded with the
   appearances of the utterly distinct objects (the māyā-power-conceived blue/pleasure objects,
   non-distinct reflections), every dealing rests and is determined by the firm experience-valid-
   means. It is twofold: the stained (samala) dealing of the māyā-blind (the buy-sell-quarrel etc.),
   and the stainless (vimala) dealing of the recognized (pratyabhijñāta) selves — the vīreśas, the
   vidyeśvaras, the mantra-maheśvaras, the mantreśvaras, the niṣkalas — whose dealing is the
   purification-awakening (malaśodhanabodhana) and therefore stainless, though while the mala-remainder
   stands it still has the bhedābheda conjunction-form. The Śivavijñānopaniṣad (vi. bhai. 106) and the
   Mālinīvijaya 1.36 anchor this. The Śivabhaṭṭāraka is the rest-word (viśrāntipadam) of all dealings.

**The crux.** V2-Q closes the saptamo vimarśa: the bādha (refutation) and the abhāva (non-being) are
not separate realities and are not established by perception or inference — they are established by
the self-consciousness alone, as the operation of the one free knower (kārikā 13). The positive
absence is the own-form (light/touch, or for the sarvajña the rule-bound exclusion), and only what
is part of the own-form can be negated (kārikā 11). The shell-cognition's non-validity of the silver
is the identity-non-being, not a cognition of a distinct prior silver-cognition's non-validity
(kārikā 12). And finally, every dealing — stained or stainless — rests in the pervader, the free
Lord, the one knower, whose recognition (pratyabhijñā) is the purpose of the śāstra (kārikā 14). The
saptamo vimarśa (the śaktivargaikāśraya) is closed at ||7||, and the jñānādhikāra moves to the
aṣṭamo vimarśa (the māheśvarya).

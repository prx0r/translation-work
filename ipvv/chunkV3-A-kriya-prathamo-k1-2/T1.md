## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk V3-A)

1. **The chunk's scope.** V3-A = M00022 lines 57–837, the OPENING of the kriyādhikāra (the action-
   section), the prathamo vimarśaḥ. It contains the maṅgala (śivam amṛtavapuṣam...), the 8-verse
   plan, and kārikās 1–9: kārikā 1 (*ata eva yad apy uktaṃ kriyā naikasya sakramā eketi pratikṣiptaṃ
   tadekasya samarthanāt* — hence, though it was said "the action of the one is not ordered" etc.,
   that is rejected, from the establishing of the one); kārikā 2 (*sakramatvaṃ ca laukikyāḥ
   kriyāyāḥ kālaśaktitaḥ ghaṭate natu śāśvatyāḥ prābhavyāḥ syāt prabhor iva* — the ordered-ness of
   the worldly action, from the time-power, fits; but not of the eternal, the primal, as of the
   Lord); kārikā 3 (*kālaḥ sūryādisañcāras tattatpuṣpādijanma vā śītoṣṇe vā tha tallakṣyaḥ krama eva
   sa tattvataḥ* — time is the sun-and-the-rest movement, or the flower-and-the-rest birth, or the
   cold-heat; the that-marker order alone, he, in-truth); kārikā 4 (*kramo bhedāśrayo bhedo'py
   ābhāsasadasattvataḥ ābhāsasadasattve tu citrābhāsakṛtaḥ prabhoḥ* — the order is the distinction-
   support; the distinction too, from the appearance's being-non-being-ness; in the appearance's
   being-non-being, the varied-appearance-made of the Lord); kārikā 5 (*mūrtivaicitryato
   deśakramam ābhāsayaty asau kriyāvaicitryanirbhāsāt kālakramam api īśvaraḥ* — from the form-
   variety he manifests the place-order; from the action-variety-manifestation, the time-order too,
   the Lord); kārikā 6 (*sarvatrābhāsabhedo'pi bhavet kālakramākaraḥ vicchinnabhāsaḥ śūnyāder mātuḥ
   bhātasya no sakṛt*); kārikā 7 (*deśakramo'pi bhāveṣu bhāti mātur mitātmanaḥ svātmeva svātmanā
   pūrṇā bhāvā bhānty amitasya tu* — even the place-order in the beings shines, of the measured-self
   mother; like the own-self, with the own-self filled, the beings shine, of the un-measured,
   though); kārikā 8 (the upasaṃhāra-śloka *ciddhanam ātmapūrṇaṃ viśvam*); and kārikā 9 (*kintu
   nirmāṇaśaktiḥ sāpyevaṃviduṣa īśituḥ tathā vijñātṛvijñeyabhedo yad avabhāsyate* — but the
   creation-power, she too, of the thus-knowing Lord; thus the knower-known distinction, which is
   manifested). The chunk ends at the ||1|| colophon (kriyāśaktivimarśaḥ prathamaḥ, line 837).

2. **The term-ledger LOCK (from the Vol 2 V2-E lock, carried into Vol 3).** vimarśa = reflexive-
   awareness; prakāśa = manifestation; ābhāsa = appearance; saṃvit = consciousness; jñāna = cognition;
   kriyā = action; kriyāśakti = the action-power; jñānaśakti = the knowledge-power; kālaśakti = the
   time-power; sakrama/akrama = with/without order; kāla/krama = time/order; pramātṛ/prameya = the
   knower/the known; paśupramātṛ = the bound knower; santāna = stream; saṃskāra = impression; smṛti =
   memory; viśrānti = rest; svātantrya = freedom; āgama = the teaching; māyāśakti = the māyā-power;
   nirmāṇaśakti = the creation-power; sṛṣṭi/sthiti/saṃhāra = creation/maintenance/dissolution;
   kṛtyapañcaka = the five-fold deed; avacchedaka = the cutter; mūrti = the form; deśa/kāla-krama =
   place/time-order. The older "rehearsal/reflexion/light" variants remain retired.

3. **The kriyā as the vimarśaśakti.** The action-section (kriyādhikāra) determines the kriyāśakti
   (the action-power) just as the knowledge-section determined the jñānaśakti. The kriyā is, in
   truth, the vimarśaśakti — the reflexive-awareness-power's freedom of joining/unjoining
   (saṃyojanādisvatantratātmā vimarśaśaktir eva vastutaḥ kriyā). The pūrvapakṣa (that the cognitions
   are stream-operations, the memory from the impression, the cognition inert-or-not) is answered:
   the one un-contracted consciousness, whose known-stain contracts it, is the power/dharma; the
   nirbādha-pratyabhijñā-pratyakṣa establishes the one ābhāsasāra padārtha that accepts the opposed
   forms without opposition (the mirror example), and that is the kriyāśraya (the action-support).

4. **Kārikās 2–5 (the kāla/krama, sakrama/akrama).** The worldly action's ordered-ness comes from
   the time-power (kālaśakti, the Lord's freedom that raises the appearance-cut); but the Lord's own
   action, being time-un-cut, is eternal/order-less. Kārikā 3: time (kāla) is not a substance — it is
   the order (krama) itself, observed in the sun's movement, the flowers' birth, the cold-heat; the
   Vaiśeṣika substance-time, the Kāpila rajas, the grammarian's eternal-operation, the Saugata
   stream — all are, in truth, the order (krama eva nāma bahiḥ kālaḥ). Kārikā 4: the order is the
   distinction-support; the distinction is from the appearance's being-non-being; and in the
   appearance's being-non-being the varied-appearance (citrābhāsa) is the Lord's own doing — the
   spring/summer, the dream, the māyā — the saṃvicchakti itself blazes, the vākyapadīya 2.22
   (ekasyaiva tu sā śaktir yad evam avabhāsate) and the lokayantra verses. Kārikā 5: from the form-
   variety the place-order; from the action-variety the time-order — the one recognition (the
   un-divided hand) admits the varied places/actions without breaking the own-form unity.

5. **Kārikā 6–7 (the kālakrama in the pramātṛ).** The objection that the orders are only in the
   appearances, not in the appearance-less knower, is answered: the cut-manifestation (vicchinnabhāsa)
   bound knower (the śūnya/prāṇa/deha) has the time-order, since its manifestation is not its own
   form but the consciousness-flashing that is intermittent (the fainting); but the param-essence
   knower, the at-once-shone (sakṛd vibhāta), the always-un-broken-manifestation, has no
   time-joining. Kārikā 7: the place-order too is only in the measured-self (mitātmana) mother — the
   bound knower whose own form is the body/place; the beings of the un-measured (amitasya) Lord shine
   filled by the own-self, order-less. The ābhāsapratibhāsatadvirahayoḥ stuti is cited.

6. **Kārikā 8–9 (the upasaṃhāra and the nirmāṇaśakti).** Kārikā 8 (ciddhanam ātmapūrṇaṃ viśvam) is
   the upasaṃhāra-śloka: the world is the consciousness-dense, self-filled. Kārikā 9: the creation-
   power (nirmāṇaśakti) of the thus-knowing Lord manifests the knower-known distinction; that
   manifestation is the Lord's creation-capability (kriyāśaktirūpa). The icchā (will) is the Lord's
   action (icchaiva hetutā kartṛtā kriyā, 2.4.21): like Caitra's "I cook" — the order-less will that
   appears ordered through the cooking-steps — so the Lord's "I flash / I re-reflexively-aware / I
   whirl" appears ordered through the knower-known distinction. The sṛṣṭi/sthiti/saṃhāra (creation/
   maintenance/dissolution), the mahāsṛṣṭi (great-creation, the pure form), and the Brahmā/Viṣṇu/Rudra
   forms are all the māyāśaktyā-made specifics of the one nirmāṇaśakti; the kṛtyapañcaka
   (five-fold deed) is the Lord's essence. The ||1|| colophon closes the kriyāśaktividadhi's first
   vimarśa.

**The crux.** V3-A opens the kriyādhikāra: the action (kriyā) is, in truth, the reflexive-awareness-
power (vimarśaśakti) — the freedom of the one Lord. The worldly action is ordered through the time-
power, but the Lord's own action is eternal/order-less (kārikā 2). Time is not a substance but the
order (krama) itself, raised by the Lord's freedom from the appearance's being-non-being (kārikās
3–4); the place-order and the time-order are the two forms of the one action-variety in the
appearance-class (kārikā 5). Only the bound (cut-manifestation) knower has the orders; the param-
essence knower is the at-once-shone, un-measured, filled-with-its-own-self (kārikās 6–7). And the
creation-power (nirmāṇaśakti) of the thus-knowing Lord manifests the whole knower-known world — the
will (icchā) is the action, appearing ordered through the distinction it freely raises (kārikā 9).
This completes the first vimarśa of the kriyādhikāra.

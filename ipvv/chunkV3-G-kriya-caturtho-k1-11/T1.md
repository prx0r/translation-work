3. **The 21-verse plan (ślokaikaviṃśati).** Abhinava maps the whole caturtho vimarśa in advance:
   the agent-object-ness alone is the essence of causality (kartṛkarmataiva tattvaṃ
   kāryakāraṇatāyāḥ); three verses refute the inert's causality (jaḍasya kāraṇatā nirākriyate);
   six establish the conscious alone as the agent-ness (cetanasyaiva sā kartṛtātmeti); three show
   the niyatiśakti is necessary to the inference (anumāne'vaśyaṃ niyatiśaktir upajīvanīyā); three
   that the Śākya causality leans on our view (śākyoktā api kāryakāraṇatā asmannayam eva śaraṇam
   avalambate); three that the Kāpila causality likewise (kāpiloktā api evam eveti); and two that
   even the conscious agent fails in Lord-ness (tasya api anīśvaratāyāṃ tat na nirvahatīti).

4. **Kārikās 1–2 and the jaḍa (inert).** Kārikā 1: this ancient knower (purāṇaḥ pramātā), by the
   un-constructed, un-ordered will (avikalpā akramā icchā), manifests all beings as his own powers
   (anantā viśve bhāvā hi tasyaiva śaktayaḥ). The construction's maker-ness is only in the māyā-ground
   (vikalpasya nirmātṛtvaṃ māyīyapade), not in the Lord's creation. Kārikā 2: the inert has no power
   and no being-ness; the effect-cause-ness is precisely the agent-object-ness. The sprout's being,
   whether real or unreal, does not belong to the seed's power; the action-power manifests the
   object as the effect, and the manifestor is the agent-cause. The asatkārya/satkārya and the
   Sāṃkhya reference are both canvassed.

5. **Kārikās 3–7 and the kriyā's unity.** Kārikās 3–4: the effect-cause-ness in the world, of the
   inner-revolving, is the two-sense-known-ness of the world flashing with the identity and variety
   in the inner self-consciousness. Kārikā 5: the one action, ordered, with the inner-outer
   standing, established in the one support (saṃvidrūpo bhagavān) that bears both forms by its
   freedom-and-purity. The agent-standing vs. object-standing action (the cooking vs. the walking;
   the creation-action as both) and the one-subject (ekaśeṣa) treatment. Kārikā 6: the outer is the
   effect of that which it is inner to; with respect to the knower the inner-outer standing is
   two-fold. The inert has no vaśana/bal/prabhāva — mastery is the will, consciousness-relative;
   the seed's "causality" is only metaphorical (aupacārika). Kārikā 7: the consciousness alone is
   the cause, standing in the two-appearance; the effect's one standing is that one's action-risen.

6. **Kārikās 8–11, the anumāna (inference), and the apoha.** Kārikā 8: the param-Lord is the cause
   in the sprout too; no other (seed) causality. Kārikā 9: the potter, by the Lord-ness arrangement,
   produces the jar through the clay-and-the-rest impression-order — the pervasive rule that the
   inert produces nothing without the conscious, and the conscious agent culminates in the Lord.
   Kārikā 10: the yogins, without clay/seed, by the will-power produce the jar — the free
   consciousness, its object-action stable. Kārikā 11: the effect-reason or nature-reason, both
   root-born in the origin. Abhinava gives the full svabhāvahetu/kāryahetu dialectic: the
   śiṃśapā-tree-ness binding (tanmātrānubandha), the apoha/vyāvṛtti (removal) as neither thing nor
   non-thing, the inherence-unity (samavāyaikatā), the Dharmottara/Dharmakīrtian marker
   (lakṣaṇa), and the pratyakṣa-anupalambha fivefold; the niyatiśakti alone rules the pervasion
   (vyāpti). The Naiyāyika "six-disputation" (ṣaṭtarkyā) and the Nyāyasūtra 1.1.5/1.1.41 close the
   argument.

**The crux.** V3-G establishes the kāryakāraṇatā (causality) as the agent-object-ness grounded in the
one free Lord: the inert has no causality (kārikās 2–4), the one ordered action rests in the
consciousness-support (kārikā 5), the inner-outer standing is with respect to the knower (kārikā 6),
the consciousness alone is the cause (kārikā 7), the Lord is the cause in the sprout and the potter
(kārikās 8–9), the yogin's will manifests without materials (kārikā 10), and the inference's
svabhāvahetu/kāryahetu are both rooted in the origin and ruled by the niyatiśakti (kārikā 11). All

## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk V3-H)

1. **The chunk's scope.** V3-H = M00022 lines 6300–7200, the kriyādhikāra caturtho vimarśaḥ (the
   kāryakāraṇatāvimarśa), covering the close of kārikā 11's commentary and kārikās 12–19:
   kārikā 12 (*bhūyastattatpramātrekavahnyābhāsādito bhavet / parokṣādapyadhipaterdhūmābhāsādi
   nūtanam*); kārikā 13 (*kāryamavyabhicāryasya liṅgamanyapramātṛgāt / tadābhāsastadābhāsādeva
   tvadhipateḥ paraḥ*); kārikā 14 (*asmin satīdamastīti kāryakāraṇatāpi yā / sāpyapekṣāvihīnānāṃ
   jaḍānāṃ nopapadyate*); kārikā 15 (*nahi svātmaikaniṣṭhānāmanusandhānavarjinām / sadasattāpade'pyeṣa
   saptamyarthaḥ prakalpyate*); kārikā 16 (*ata eva vibhaktyarthaḥ pramātrekasamāśrayaḥ /
   kriyākārakabhāvākhyo yukto bhāvasamanvayaḥ*); kārikā 17 (*parasparasvabhāvatve kāryakāraṇayor api /
   ekatvameva bhede hi naivānyonyasvarūpatā*); kārikā 18 (*ekātmano vibhedaśca kriyā kālakramānugā /
   tathā syātkartṛtā vai na tathāpariṇamattayā*); kārikā 19 (*naca yuktaṃ jaḍasyaivaṃ
   bhedābhedavirodhataḥ / ābhāsabhedādekkatra cidātmani tu yujyate*). The chunk ends at source 7200;
   the caturtho vimarśa (kārikā 19's commentary on the pratibimba and the bāhyārthavāda) continues
   into V3-I.

2. **The term-ledger LOCK (carried into Vol 3, unchanged).** kriyā = action; kāryakāraṇatā =
   effect-cause-ness/causality; kāraṇatā = cause-ness; kartṛtā = agent-ness; apekṣā = dependence;
   anusandhāna = joining; niyatiśakti = the fixed-power; niyama = fixation; anvaya/vyatireka =
   presence/absence; anumāna = inference; hetu = reason; svabhāvahetu = nature-reason;
   gamya/gamaka = the known/known-maker; liṅga = marker; vyāpti = pervasion; pratyakṣa =
   perception; anupalambha = non-perception; pramātṛ = the knower; ābhāsa = appearance; vimarśa =
   reflexive-awareness; cit = consciousness; cidātmaka = consciousness-own; pratibimba =
   reflection; nirmeya = measure; pariṇāma = transformation; vivarta = the (illusory) manifesting;
   pradhāna = the primary (Sāṃkhya); puruṣa = the person; māyāśakti = the māyā-power; avidyā =
   ignorance. The older "rehearsal/reflexion/light" variants remain retired.

3. **Kārikās 12–13 and the inference across knowers.** The Saugata/Vijñānavādin holds the
   appearance-streams per-knower distinct (pratipramātṛsantānaṃ bhinnam ābhāsam), so the
   smoke-fire effect-cause is only within one stream. Abhinava rejects this: in our view the Lord
   creates the one appearance for the knowers together (pramātṝṇām aikyena sṛṣṭiḥ), so the
   pervasion (vyāpti) is grasped once across the knowers, and the fire-appearance, though indirect
   (parokṣa), is the non-wandering cause of the smoke-appearance — the marker (liṅga) and the
   "lord-cognition" (adhipatipratyaya). The potter/cowherd examples and the vṛtti-joining are
   worked out.

4. **Kārikās 14–16 and the apekṣā (dependence).** Kārikā 14: "in this existing, this is" — the
   causality too is impossible for the dependence-less inert ones; the inert has no apekṣā, hence
   no niyama, hence no prior-posterior effect-cause-being. The anvaya/vyatireka (presence/absence),
   the perception-non-perception, the niyatā dhūme agnyādisāmagrī, and the equal-pervasion of
   gamyagamaka are analyzed; the Dharmottara/Bhaṭṭa svabhāvapratibandha. Kārikā 15: the
   saptamī (seventh case) for the self-resting, joining-lacking ones is only constructed
   (prakalpyate) — not real. Kārikā 16: the case-ending-meaning (vibhaktyartha), supported on the
   one knower, is the fit action-actor-being connection (kriyākārakabhāvākhyo bhāvasamanvayaḥ).

5. **Kārikās 17–19 and the Sāṃkhya.** Kārikā 17: in the mutual-nature-ness of the effect-cause
   two, the one-ness alone — in the distinction there is no mutual own-form-ness; this refutes the
   Sāṃkhya satkāryavāda (the "one, order-varied" pradhāna). Kārikā 18: of the one-self the
   distinction and the action follow the time-order; the agent-ness would be thus, not by the
   non-transforming-ness — Abhinava turns the Sāṃkhya pariṇāma into a kriyā (action), grounding the
   kartṛtva in the consciousness's freedom. Kārikā 19: the inert's thus (many-form) is unfit from
   the difference-non-difference contradiction; in the one consciousness-own (cidātmani), the
   appearance-distinction fits (the pratibimba-method, ābhāsabhedāt). The mirror (ādarśa) analogy,
   the Sāṃkhya pradhāna/puruṣa, the Vedānta vivarta (and its failure for the inert Brahman), and
   the bāhyārthavāda (Dharmottara) are all adjudicated.

**The crux.** V3-H completes the caturtho vimarśa's central refutations and the positive thesis: the
inference (anumāna) works because the one Lord creates the shared appearance for all knowers
(kārikās 12–13); the inert's causality is impossible (kārikā 14); the case-ending connections rest
on the one knower (kārikās 15–16); the Sāṃkhya's mutual-nature and transformation are refuted in
favor of the consciousness's freedom and the reflection-method (kārikās 17–19). All causation and
all inference rest in the one free consciousness.

## T1 apparatus — the key decisions (Chunk V3-J)

1. **The chunk's scope.** V3-J = M00022 lines 7662–8500, the āgamādhikāra prathamo vimarśaḥ,
   covering kārikās 1–7: kārikā 1 (*evamantarbahirvṛttiḥ kriyā kālakramānugā / mātur eva
   tadanyonyāviyukte jñānakarmaṇī*); kārikā 2 (*kintvāntaradaśodrekāt sādākhyaṃ tattvam āditaḥ /
   bahirbhāvaparatve tu parataḥ pārameśvaram*); kārikā 3 (*sā sāmānādhikaraṇyaṃ ca sadvidyā
   hamidaṃdhiyoḥ*); kārikā 4 (*idambhāvopapannānāṃ vedyabhūmimupeyuṣām / bhāvānāṃ bodhasāratvād
   yathāvastvavalokanāt*); kārikā 5 (*atrāparatvaṃ bhāvānāmanātmatvena bhāsanāt / paratāhantayācchādāt
   parāparadaśā hi sā*); kārikā 6 (*bhedadhīreva bhāveṣu karturbodhātmano'pi yā / māyāśaktyeva sā
   vidyetyanye vidyeśvarā yathā*); kārikā 7 (*tasyaiśvaryasvabhāvasya paśubhāve prakāśikā /
   vidyāśaktistirodhānakarmāyābhidhā punaḥ*). The chunk ends at source 8500; the prathamo vimarśa
   (the vidyā/kriyā tattvas) continues into V3-K. The source is clean, dense Sanskrit throughout.

2. **The term-ledger LOCK (carried into Vol 3, unchanged).** āgama = the teaching; tattva =
   category; padārthavarga = the word-object class; śivatattva = the Śiva-category; jñānaśakti =
   the knowledge-power; kriyāśakti = the action-power; icchāśakti = the will-power; vimarśa /
   parāmarśa = reflexive-awareness / re-recollection; spanda = the quiver; unmeṣa/nimeṣa =
   flashing/closing; sadāśiva = the Sadāśiva (category); īśvara = the Lord; vidyā = knowledge;
   śuddhavidyā/aśuddhavidyā = pure/un-pure knowledge; vidyeśvara = the knowledge-Lord; paśu = the
   animal/bound one; māyāśakti = the māyā-power; tirodhāna = obscuring; kṛtya = action; sṛṣṭi/
   sthiti/saṃhāra/tirodhāna/anugraha = creation/standing/dissolution/obscuring/grace; nandīśvara;

3. **The āgama's role.** The pārameśvarāgama (param-Lord teaching) alone can measure the whole
   world (viśvaṃ pramātuṃ samarthaḥ), because it is the un-contracted-manifestation reflexive-
   awareness-own of the param-Lord; the yukti (reason) only supports it (tadupabṛṃhaṇāya). The
   Spandakārikā 43 (*didṛkṣayeva sarvārthānyadā vyāpyāvatiṣṭhate*) and the śrutis establish the
   all-power self-manifesting consciousness.

4. **Kārikās 1–2 and the śivatattva.** Kārikā 1: the inner-outer action following the time-order,
   of the one knower alone, with the knowledge and the action mutually un-separated (jñānakriye
   anyonyāviyukte) — the action-power's form is the manifestation of all word-objects (the
   kārikā 2.3.15). Kārikā 2: the Sadākhya (Sadāśiva) category is first from the inner-state surge;
   the param-Lord (Īśvara) after, in the external-being-primary-ness. The Śivadṛṣṭi (icchāśakti /
   tuṭi), the Mataṅga-tantra, the jñāna/kriyā distinction, and the aṇu/paśu are worked out.

5. **Kārikās 3–4 and the śuddhavidyā.** Kārikā 3: the co-reference (sāmānādhikaraṇya) of the
   "I-this" cognition is the pure knowledge (sadvidyā) — the aham/idam co-reference in the
   consciousness. The Sadāśiva/Īśvara tattvas as the inner/outer states, the spanda, and the
   vidyākhya operation. Kārikā 4: the pure knowledge is the observation (avalokana) of the beings
   as-the-thing (yathāvastu), their awareness-essence (bodhasāratvāt); the aśuddhavidyā (un-pure
   knowledge) is shown by contrast. The Mataṅga-tantra (1.10.22–23) and the paśu's self-only
   manifestation.

6. **Kārikās 5–7 and the vidyā/māyā.** Kārikā 5: in the beings the un-param-ness from the
   non-self-ness manifestation, the param-ness from the I-ness-covering — the parāpara (param-un-
   param) state. Kārikā 6: the distinction-cognition in the beings is the māyā-power; but in the
   pure-consciousness-bound knowers (vidyeśvara) it is the pure knowledge — the other view. Kārikā
   7: the manifesting (prakāśikā) of the lordship-nature in the animal-being is the knowledge-
   power; the māyā-named is again the obscuring-maker (tirodhānakarī). The five kṛtyas (sṛṣṭi,
   sthiti, saṃhāra, tirodhāna, anugraha), the hell-fall and the boon-giving (varadāna), the
   Nandīśvara, and the śaktipāta (the nine-fold power-descent, the Gītā 7.16–18) close the first
   vimarśa's doctrinal core.

**The crux.** V3-J establishes the āgamādhikāra's foundation: the param-Lord teaching alone
establishes the śivatattva as the body of all padārthas, with the yukti as its support (the
maṅgala); the one knower's action and knowledge are mutually un-separated (kārikā 1); the
Sadāśiva/Īśvara categories are the inner/outer surges of the one consciousness (kārikā 2); the
co-reference of I-this is the pure knowledge (kārikā 3); the pure knowledge observes the beings
as-the-thing in their awareness-essence (kārikā 4); the param-un-param state is the I-this
covering and manifestation (kārikā 5); the distinction-cognition is the māyā-power in the animals
but pure knowledge in the knowledge-Lords (kārikā 6); and the knowledge-power manifests the
lordship-nature in the animal-being while the māyā obscures (kārikā 7). All the categories and all
the powers rest in the one free Lord.

## 1. What is at issue

**The question:** This is the deepest section of the jñānaśakti vimarśa. Abhinavagupta now states the
heart of the whole doctrine: the manifestation (prakāśa) is not an inert light — its own nature is the
*reflexive-awareness* (vimarśa). Without the vimarśa, the manifestation would be like crystal, inert,
unaware of what it shows. This reflexive-awareness is the parā-vāk (the supreme speech), the Lord's
freedom and lordship. The vimarśa is not a separate thing (the pramātṛ-prameya are not two); it is the
self-grasping of the light.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**The pramātṛ-prameya abheda and the parā-vāk (lines 5200–5244):**
In the manas-cognition (the middle-self, the inner-speech), the first surge — the paśyantī-word-said,
of the will-marker — is where the pramātṛ-prameya are not yet separated even by their common form (no
distinction-flash). This very thing shines by itself. The reason: if the un-broken pramātṛ-prameya-form
(not yet split) were not really manifested, then in the quick reading (the lipi-recitation, the swift
motion, the quick speech) — going from line to line, place to place, organ to organ, not re-reflecting
the to-be-abandoned and to-be-grasped parts, and the agent of the abandonment/attainment not reflecting —
the varied abandonments/attainments would not be (they are seen as re-reflection-preceded; so they infer
their cause, the re-reflection). Nor is that re-reflection only by the difference (else the swiftness
would fail in the speech-mind-motion-lineage, the utterance-convention-memory-bond). (The apohe:
"sometimes, without the speech-mind-motion…"). So the argument: the manifestation of the pramātṛ-prameya
is in the abandonment/attainment — being the conscious-action (kārya-hetu); what is, and not separate,
must be non-separate (no third class — svabhāva-hetu). The "reading/running" etc. is to be explained
as the being of the pramātṛ-prameya even in the non-difference. (The Śivadṛṣṭi 1.8: "when the
consciousness-dharma-splendor-joy-unfolding, in the varied-construction-many-effect-creation, becomes
the un-lidded cittā — that is the will's first tuṭi"; and "she is seen in the heart-region, at the
time of the effect-memory, at the moment of rapture/emotion, at the seeing of the beloved, at the
un-reflected sight, at the visarga-emission, at the utterance, in the reading and running — in these
occasions is the all-power-liveliness.") The point: the all-power-full form of the consciousness, when,
by the freedom, it re-reflects, in the un-lidded consciousness-form, the world.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

## 4. Decision for L2
Proceed. The argument is understood: the manifestation's own nature is the reflexive-awareness; the
pramātṛ-prameya are not two (the swift action shows the re-reflection); this reflexive-awareness is
the parā-vāk, the Lord's freedom; and it is seen in the sudden moments (the rapture, the seeing of the
beloved, the utterance) — the all-power-liveliness. This is the heart of the doctrine, leading to
kārikās 12–13 (the citkriyā, the parā-vāk as the aiśvarya).

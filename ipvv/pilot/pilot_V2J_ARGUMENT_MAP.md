# IPVV — V2-J ARGUMENT MAP (the pañcamo vimarśa: the saṃskāra-vimarśa and the determination as the Lord's power, kārikās 19–20)

*2026-08-11. Reconstruct *argument-by-argument* before writing prose. Source: M00021 lines 6800–7600
(the close of the vimarśa-section — the saṃskāra-ādhāna/prabodhana, and kārikā 20: the determination
"this jar" as the param-Lord's power). Works from BOTH the L0 records and the raw Sanskrit.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

**The question:** The vimarśa (reflexive-awareness) has been shown to be the manifestation's nature
and the source of the powers. Now Abhinavagupta must show that even the *impression* (saṃskāra) — the
seed of memory — and the *determination* (adhyavasāya) are the vimarśa. The memory-seed is laid and
awakened only by the vimarśa; and the determination "this jar" — the very act of taking the object as
"This" — is itself the param-Lord's power, manifesting like the self, not by the this-ness.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**The saṃskāra-vimarśa (lines 6800–6844):**
The remembering of the convention (saṃketa) depends on the saṃskāra-ādhāna (the laying of the
impression) and the saṃskāra-prabodhana (the awakening); and these two cannot be done by the
non-construction (nirvikalpaka). For "the strong experience lays the memory-seed; from the seeing of
the like, its awakening." And the strong-ness of the experience is nothing other than the
vimarśa-viśrānti (the resting in the reflexive-awareness). The Bhaṭṭa's "the growth of it is the
strong-ness" — there too the growth is the pramātṛ-viśrānti, the "I, thus, shine" — the
vimarśa-form. Without the vimarśa-form, how would the impression be laid or awakened? (The Bhaṭṭa's
verse: "the manifest light, for the own-meaning, the saṃskāra-awakener; else, even in the vikalpa, its
operation would not be wished.") If the light's manifestation (the vyakti, the growth-strong-ness) is
not wished, then nothing; and the not-strong would also be the saṃskāra-awakening — the "strong"
distinction would be meaningless. The "viniścaya-saṃvāda" (the "in the seeing of the seen, by the
consciousness's capacity"). The example (nahi): "fire-ness itself is the smoke-cause-ness" — seen in
the fire; so, by the saṃskāra-ādhāna, the memory would arise even for the coconut-island-dweller [who
never saw smoke]. Without the smoke-cause-ness (the memory-marker-effect), how would the laid-saṃskāra
be inferred? So the vimarśa-less awareness does not lay the saṃskāra — and does not awaken it ("nāpi
dhūmaḥ" — the smoke [does not awaken without the fire]). The objection: "the thing's cause-ness is by
the other-upādhi, the dealing-param; it cannot take the un-conditioned-own-marker-manifestation-mere;
so the cause-ness is not seen." — the other example ("mārgagamana" — the going on the road): the
self (svātmani) is the example of the ādhāna-absence, the sahacāri of the prabodhana-absence. Hence:
without the vimarśa-form-cause, [the saṃskāra is not laid or awakened].

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)
1. The Bhaṭṭa's verses and the nālikera (coconut-island) example — cited; the applications preserved.
2. The "mārgagamana" (the going on the road) example — kept close to the text.

## 4. Decision for L2
Proceed. The argument is understood: the impression (saṃskāra) is laid and awakened only by the
vimarśa (the resting in the reflexive-awareness); the non-construction cannot do it; the strong-ness of
the experience is the vimarśa; and the determination "this jar" (kārikā 20) is beyond name-and-form —
the param-Lord's power, manifesting like the self, not by the this-ness. The vimarśa is the source of
memory and of the determination alike.

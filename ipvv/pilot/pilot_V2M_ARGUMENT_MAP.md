## 1. What is at issue

**The question:** Having examined the apohanaśakti (the removal-power), Abhinavagupta now traces how
the consciousness, entering the states (the prāṇa, the buddhi, the puryaṣṭaka, the body), comes to
identify with them and to deal with the world. The construction (vikalpa) is the joining of the "this"
from the prior appearance by the impression (kārikā 6); but the *continuity* (anusandhāna) of the
states — the self that runs through the body, mind, breath — is not a construction. And in the dealing,
the Lord, entering the body, manifests the inner object-mass without, by the will (kārikā 7). This leads
to the kṛtyapañcaka (the fivefold work: creation, standing, withdrawal, concealment, grace).

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**The states and the krama (lines 9200–9244):**
The consciousness, descending into the known-form (the lower ground), takes up the void, the breath, the
intellect, the body — the prior-prior union, the order (krama). Even in the known-form space, the
all-possibility-bearing (the pervasiveness) is not entirely inert; then in the power-less wind (the
spanda-śakti, not-separate-from-the-sky, form-less); then in the form-possessing light (tejas); then in
the light-less water (that can receive the other); then in the not-receiving, dense, coarse earthly —
the pramātṛ-conceit descends, by degrees, to the lowest. For the non-yogins, the mere void is not
dealt-with; with respect to the world, the touch-form breath (the prāṇa, taking up the void-following),
with the fire-connection-excess, is said to be first. Hence the consciousness's transformation — like
the elephant [sinking] in the mud-bank — by the deep clinging, the passage into the not-self, the
known-form. That, with respect to the body and the rest, is first called "prāṇa"; the inner-organ's
conception-conceit-determination-new-operation essence. By whose grace the thus-natured operation is
obtained — that is the consciousness-tattva; its wind (vāyu) is the prāṇa, gone to the support-ness, the
presiding-ness. By the thus-natured, consciousness-tattva-presided prāṇa, with the
buddhi-form-puryaṣṭaka-companion, the body is presided; hence the consciousness-fit-freedom-union is
of it; not by the direct experience presiding. (The verse: "the manifestation-form Lord is said in the
prāṇa-presiding; the prāṇa is the bird-dharma, the haṃsa, the three-vehicle.") Hence the Gītā 15.8:
"whatever body the Lord attains, and whatever he departs — taking these, he goes, as the wind takes the
fragrances from the flower-bed" — the consciousness-form īśvara's body-presiding through the
prāṇa-puryaṣṭaka. (The Spandakārikā 7: "since this organ-group, though un-deluded, as if un-deluded,
by itself, with the inner wheel, obtains the proceeding-standing-withdrawal" — "with the inner wheel" =
the prāṇa.) Hence, even in the body's burning, like the dream, the sleep-immersed-delusion-state —
the cetanā-presided-prāṇa-embraced puryaṣṭaka's sameness — the enjoyment in the other-world, by the
other-body-presiding, is not un-established. And the yogins are seen, in their own body, in their own
house...

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)
1. The Gītā 15.8 and the Spandakārikā 7 citations — cited; the applications preserved.
2. The "hastina iva taṭe" (the elephant in the mud-bank) image — the consciousness's descent; kept.

## 4. Decision for L2
Proceed. The argument is understood: the consciousness descends through the states (the void, the
prāṇa, the intellect, the body), presiding over them by the prāṇa-puryaṣṭaka; the construction is the
impression-joining (kārikā 6); but the continuity is not a construction; and in the dealing, the Lord,
entering the body, manifests the inner object-mass by the will (kārikā 7) — the kṛtyapañcaka. The
consciousness is the presiding Lord through all the states.

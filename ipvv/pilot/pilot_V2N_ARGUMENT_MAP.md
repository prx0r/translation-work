## 1. What is at issue

**The question:** The ṣaṣṭho vimarśa (the apohanaśakti) closes. Its final concern: the inner appearance
(antarābhāsa) stands firm in memory, construction, and cognition alike (kārikā 8); it is innate in the
cognition whose form is the external manifestation, while it stands in memory etc. (kārikā 9); it is
innate in the freely-wandering construction (kārikā 10); and hence, from the manifestation of the
uplifting as wished, knowledge and action are clear for every living one (kārikā 11). Along the way,
the freed souls (muktāṇava) and the mantreśvaras are discussed.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**The muktāṇava and the mantreśvara (lines 10000–10044):**
Even in the mokṣa-state, the freed atoms (muktāṇava) — from the parama-śiva, from the
ādi-śiva-ness, from the stain-removal-manifested-śiva-ness — have a distinction. By this part, in the
duality-side, the freed atoms are indeed distinct, and their manifestation, by the śiva-ness, is the
un-broken all-knowing; but the three-time-manifestation, being established in the māyīya-knower's
manifestation, does not fail to flash for them either (from their all-knowing). The 'ca' says: the
mantreśvaras, by the authority's exhaustion, even in the mokṣa, in that place, of the other — the atom's
word-obtaining-cause, the dīkṣā, the vow, the japa, the tapas, the samādhāna, the caryā — by the
transmigration, being all-knowing, in the traiyambaka, the knower-ness is not de-valued — so with
respect to the mantreśvara too, the eternal is fit. The "paśvaṇūn" (the animal-atoms) — the
animal-ness only, generally. The "tattvabhuvanādayaḥ" (the categories, the worlds) — the ādi includes
the bhāvabhūta, the kalā, the mantra, the word, the phoneme. Even the jars and the rest, with respect
to their own class, are not utterly unprecedented. The 'iti' shows the reason in the future meaning
("not then-created and then-withdrawn"). The prior-created-ness. The objection "how, in the dealing-
time, is the creation?" — the prior-view's reasonableness is shown. The "kintu" (but) removes the
"then how is the creation" — at that time, by the unity-going; "tathā sati" (thus being): per-knower,
the jar-appearance and the body-appearance are distinct; but (the vāsanāprabodha-sūtra 1.5.5, and the
1.5.7 sūtra) — the consciousness-self alone. The objection "then the knower-distinction is destroyed" —
answered: no (natu); by the prāṇa-puryaṣṭaka, in the triśākhā-and-the-rest-distinction-standing, the
sameness. And "the appearance is called the cognition" — the objection "the jar-form appearance is the
known, how is it the cognition?" — answered: "of the knower-operation-form, and"; "of the knower-
standing-ness." The objection "then by the knower's will, the knower-other-joining, per-moment the
creation and withdrawal of the objects and the knowers — how does the maheśvara shine?" — answered
("sā ca," "evamartham api"): not only the vṛtti's order-word's pure-form-sequence, but...

## 4. Decision for L2
Proceed. The argument is understood: the freed atoms and the mantreśvaras are distinct yet
all-knowing (their manifestation un-broken); the creation is by the unity, per-knower; the appearance is
the cognition; and kārikās 8–11 establish that the inner appearance stands firm in memory/construction/
cognition alike, innate in the cognition and the free construction, and that knowledge and action are
clear for every living one. The ||6|| colophon closes the apohanaśaktividadhi.

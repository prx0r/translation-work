**The one support of all the powers**

The powers — knowledge, memory, removal — have been established. But a power does not float free; it
needs a support (āśraya). And the question now is: what supports the powers? The ordinary answer would
make the support inert, like the fire's relation to its powers of burning, cooking, and sweating. But
Abhinavagupta insists this is wrong. The support of the powers is the maheśvara — the Lord — and his
essence is not to be a thing that happens to have powers, but to be the very freedom of joining,
separating, resting, concealing, and manifesting. He is not the inert base that the powers sit upon;
he is the free act in which the powers are his own unfolding.

The whole saptamo vimarśa is devoted to proving that this one support exists and that it is the Lord.
The plan is laid out: one verse states the support's own form; two prove it by the presence-and-absence
(anvaya-vyatireka) — in the one support the dealing of the world is fit, without it, not; one states
the dealing (the cognition-aggregation, resting in the knower, of the cause-effect form); one the
memory (the knower-rest); eight the whole dealing (the true-and-false division); and one the conclusion.

And the first verse gives the support's own form in a striking image: the pratibhā. The pratibhā is the
flashing — the light that runs through the ordered word-objects, through all the varied things we name
and know. It is seasoned (rūṣitā) with their order, touched by it. But the flashing itself is not
ordered. Its support — that in which the flashing flashes, the one who knows the order without being
caught in it — is the order-less, infinite-consciousness-form knower. And that knower, Abhinavagupta
declares, is the great Lord (maheśvara).

So the first word of the vimarśa is this: the flashing world, with all its order and variety, is the
manifestation of a knower who is not ordered, who is not caught in the variety, who is the infinite
consciousness. The ordered word-objects flash in the order-less light. And this is not an inert light,
but the free Lord — the one whose very being is the freedom to join and separate, to conceal and to
manifest. He is the support of all the powers: the powers are his freedom, and he is their support, the
one, order-less, infinite consciousness, the great Lord.

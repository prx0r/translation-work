# IPVV — V2-P ARGUMENT MAP (the saptamo vimarśa: the memory's self-consciousness as the valid means, kārikās 5–10)

*2026-08-11. Reconstruct *argument-by-argument* before writing prose. Source: M00021 lines 11200–12000
(the saptamo vimarśa, the śaktivargaikāśraya — kārikās 5–10: the memory, the pramāṇa as the
pramātṛ-vyāpāra, the one knower). Works from BOTH the L0 records and the raw Sanskrit.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

**The question:** The saptamo vimarśa establishes the one support of the powers. Its core: the valid
means (pramāṇa) is not a separate thing — it is the *operation of the knower* (pramātṛ-vyāpāra), the
unification of the cognitions in the one knower. Kārikā 5: in memory, that very self-consciousness is
the valid means in the self's birth; in the prior-experience-being it is the proof, that alone, not
another. The memory is not a new, separate cognition of a prior object; it is the prior experience
itself, self-conscious, re-remembered in the one knower.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**The vyavasthāpaka and the unification (lines 11200–11223):**
The objection: "then how is the not-establishing established?" — the "evam" (the prior-suggested
reason): even there (in the construction-state), if the two were thus, then "what was not made before,
now makes" — who would perceive it? The mutual prior/later-being-establishing is not fit; so the two
touch only their own objects. But this is fit (ekasmiṃstu): by the perception-non-perception-flow-
fivefold, the five objects, thrown into the one consciousness-ocean, by the freedom-wave-strength,
become mutually-dependent (jar-cloth, as-it); they shine and are re-reflected with the distinct-
appearance-re-recollection-difference. The one appearance, made by the manifestation-operation of the
five — that is the cause-effect-appearance; and the re-recollection is the knower-operation. This is
the valid-means-joining logic (yukti), serving the whole dealing; without the operation, even the
pramāṇas are useless. (The Mukuta-saṃhitā: "the consciousness-operation alone is the one reason,
established everywhere, in enjoyment or liberation; in that one is to be zealous.") So the joiner alone
[is the means] — everything desired is established.

**Kārikā 5 (lines 11225–11244):**
The objection: "by the perception-non-perception, what was made is to be unified; this much is the
acceptance of the knower; but what is this — in the unification, the memory alone is engaged?" — the
sūtra: "In memory, that very self-consciousness is the valid means in the self's birth; in the
prior-experience-being, it is the proof, that alone, not another." The perception-non-perception-form
experiences, whatever their own object, not grasping — in the memory of it, the manifestation is not a
new thing brought by the memory-time (being of the grasped-grasping-nature), but the prior experience
itself. And it, now, does not shine as a separate known (its non-being); even if it were, the known-
cognition would be the known-cognition (jñātajñāna), not the memory; nor is the cognition known-by-
another (shown much). So, though it is, by the separate self-luminousness, there would be two
un-connected experiences, not the memory. Hence it must be accepted: at the memory-time even, the prior
experience, being the inner-self-consciousness, [is what shines]. The memory is the prior experience,
self-conscious, remembered — the same self-consciousness is the valid means.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)
1. The Mukuta-saṃhitā citation — cited; the application preserved.
2. The "jñātajñāna" (the known-cognition) — the regress avoided; kept close.

## 4. Decision for L2
Proceed. The argument is understood: the valid means is the knower-operation, the unification of the
cognitions in the one knower (the joiner); and in memory, the prior experience itself, self-conscious,
is what shines — the same self-consciousness is the valid means, not a new separate cognition. This
establishes the one knower as the support.

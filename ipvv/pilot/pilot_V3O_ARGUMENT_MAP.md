# IPVV — V3-O ARGUMENT MAP (the tattvasaṅgrahādhikāra tṛtīyo vimarśa: the essence of knower, known, bond, and liberation, kārikās 9–15)

*2026-08-11. Reconstruct *argument-by-argument* before writing prose. Source: M00022 lines 11600–12263
(the tattvasaṅgrahādhikāra tṛtīyo vimarśaḥ — the mātṛmeyabandhamokṣatattva, kārikās 9–15). Works from

---

## 1. What is at issue

**The question:** The tattvasaṅgraha's middle section (kārikās 9–15) expounds the bond (bandha) and
its relation to the knower and the known. The central problem: the bound knower (paśu) creates his own
world — but how is this creation related to the Lord's creation? And what is the difference between the
bound and the liberated? Kārikās 9–15 answer: the paśu's creation is *asādhāraṇī* (un-common, private),
sustained by the Lord's creation (k9–10); the Lord's own creation is *sādhāraṇā* (common), and through
the abandonment of construction (vikalpahāna) one attains the Lord-ness (k11–12); the known is common,
but the liberated knows it with the own-self-non-difference (k13); in the supreme Śiva there is no
separate known at all (k14); and the fruit is the samyag jñāna-kriyā, the while-living-liberated (k15).

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Kārikā 9 (lines 11600–11700) — the paśu's un-common creation:**
> "This," indeed, of that, the un-common creation, the Lord-creation-dependent; she-even, with the
> un-knowing-ness, in-the-existing-alone, with the Lord-power, with the that-self —

The paśu's un-common (asādhāraṇī) creation is the Lord-creation-dependent (īśasṛṣṭyujīvinī) — it
subsists on the back of the Lord's creation. It is per-knower, per-time, devoid of other-knower-state-
following. The objection: "if the paśu creates, is it not common like the Lord's?" — No: the paśu's
own power is un-known to him (being other-dependent), and only the Lord-ness awakens it. From the
un-cognition alone comes the bound/liberated-ness. The māyā-power of the Lord — the word-mass body, the
world-re-recollection-form — is the varṇadevatā-wheel, the eight-fold class (vargāṣṭaka), the
ka-and-the-rest syllables; its movement (prāṇāpāna-swing) forbids firmness, and makes the "friend-this,
enemy-this" constructions. The paśu's asādhāraṇī sṛṣṭi depends on the īśvara-created appearances (e.g.
the "ten-tusked elephant"). The sūtra-meaning.

**Kārikā 10 (lines 11700–11850) — the prāṇarūpā vikalpakriyā:**
> With the own-rest-obstruction, with the moving, with the breath-form; with the construction-action,
> the that-that-syllable-variety-form —

The construction-action (vikalpakriyā), of the breath-form, is for the obstruction of the own rest. The
māyāśakti (in the māyā-word, the kriyātmikā) binds the paśu (the Spa. 3.16). The kṣetrajña's creations
are sabhittika (based, on the Lord's), not nirbhittika. The varṇadevatās (the ka-and-the-rest) are
the infinite syllable-forms. Even the free constructions (the "king-I-stand") are, on inspection,
parādhīnā (other-dependent). The māyā's three parts (tucchatābhāsa, vaiparītyābhāsa, nañartha) spread
with the three stains.

**Kārikā 11 (lines 11852–12068) — the sādhāraṇā aiśvarī sarga and the vikalpahāna:**
> The common-alone, the-other, and-the-Lord's-creation, from-the-clear-manifestation; from-the-
> construction-removal, from-the-one-pointedness, by-order, the-Lord-ness-word —

The Lord's creation is clear-manifestation (spaṣṭāvabhāsana), hence un-constructed; it is sādhāraṇa or
asādhāraṇa (the double-moon), both other-than the paśu's creation. When, by the abandonment of
construction and the one-pointedness, the paśu attains the "I-this" Lord-ship-re-recollection, he
destroys his animal-ness and shows the Lord-ness (the Vīrāvalī-Bhairava 113). Even the paśu's creation,
if known with the Lord's power, becomes sādhāraṇa. In the dream, the constructions are clear or
un-clear; the all-known-common-ness. The pratyayasṛṣṭi (the cognition-creation) is the
kārmamalāpyāyikā; the kṣetrajñā's un-freedom (Spa. 3.14). The vikalpanirhrāsa (the thinning of
construction) through the ekāgratā-practice leads to the param-Lord-word; the tattvārthasaṅgraha's
non-repetition; the Vidyā-kalāśaktis; the svabhāvahetu and the pratyakṣa (the agnīndhana example).

**Kārikā 12 (lines 12070–12109) — the viśvātman's maheśatā:**
> All-mine-this-splendor, thus-of-the-knowing — of-the-world-self, of-the-constructions, in-the-
> spread-even, the-mahā-Lord-ness —

With the thinning of construction and the non-construction-assimilation, the own-cognition is the
liberation (self-manifest). The "not another I" — the grāhyagrāhakaprakāśaghana param-eśvara is the
"aham"; the constructions, fallen into the great re-recollection, become non-construction. The
Pāradarśana ("even doubt is not doubted"); the Śivadṛṣṭi 1.38 (the sārvabhauma king playing the
soldier); the prakṛtitvam-prāptān vikārān ākaroti; the ṛtudhāmā Āditya. The maheśvarataiva (the vṛtti,
the pratyabhijñā).

**Kārikā 13 (lines 12111–12140) — the known is common; the liberated knows it with the own-self:**
> The-known-the-common, the-liberated-with-the-own-self-non-distinction-he-thinks; the-mahā-Lord-as,
> the-bound-again-with-the-utter-distinction-endowed —

The mukta, from the Sādākhya-word down to the embryo, knows the mātṛvarga as the "I-this," with the
self-non-difference; the meya is common (mutually and from the knower-group un-distinguished), the
ekarasābhedaviśrānti (the Śivadṛṣṭi 5.106, 7.91, 7.93). The baddha, in the reversal, is the
ekaghanabhedaviśrānta. The tulyam (equal) is the kriyāviśeṣaṇa.

**Kārikā 14 (lines 12142–12157) — in the supreme Śiva, no separate known:**
> In-every-way-though-the-inner-dissolved-infinite-category-flood-filled; the-Śiva, the-consciousness-
> bliss-mass, the-param-syllable-form —

In the Sadāśiva-Īśa ground and the jīvanmukti, the known-order was examined; but in the parama-Śiva
there is no known at all. The "sarvathā" (in every way) excludes; the "filled" (nirbhara) is the
param-ness — the "I"-experience alone is filled; the ahaṃpratītir eva pūryate. The cidānandaghana is
the filled "I"-experience; the paramākṣara is explained likewise.

**Kārikā 15 (lines 12161–12263) — the fruit: the samyag jñāna-kriyā and the jīvanmukta:**
> Thus-the-own-self-of-this, well-the-knowledge-action-two-thus; knowing-the-as-desired-beings,
> seeing, he-knows-and-he-does-and —

The fruit of the four-section teaching: knowing the own-self as the Lord-form, seeing the
knowledge-action powers (not by the Kāpila/Kāṇāda views), one knows and does whatever one wishes — the
jīvanmukta, who at the fall of the body is the param-eśvara. The māyā-word too is the citprakāśa
(deśakālāspṛṣṭa); the sadāśiveśa-form; the Buddhists (the kṣaṇika, the virodhacintā, the
paramāṇusaṅkoca) refuted; the niravabhāsā saṃvid; the ābhāsa-vimarśa stability (the śukti-rajata); the
ekānekarūpatā; the idantāparāmarśa; the aikādhikaraṇya īśvaratā/śivatā; the
paramaśivarūpāntarlīna īśvaratā (Spa. 1.2). The ||3|| colophon.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)
1. The "daśaradana" (ten-tusked vs. ten-faced) elephant (k10) — the correct reading is ten-tusked
   (Airāvata); the earlier T1 gloss "ten-faced" was a mis-gloss, corrected per Ratié.
2. The exact "sabhittika/nirbhittika" (based/un-based) creations of the kṣetrajña (k10).

## 4. Decision for L2
Proceed. The argument is understood: the paśu's creation is private, sustained by the Lord's (k9); the
construction-action, breath-form, is the binding (k10); the Lord's creation is common, and the
abandonment of construction leads to the Lord-ness (k11); the world-self's mahā-Lord-ness (k12); the
known is common, known by the liberated with the own-self-non-difference (k13); in the supreme Śiva
there is no separate known (k14); and the fruit is the samyag jñāna-kriyā and the jīvanmukta (k15).
The bond is the forgetting; the liberation is the recognition.

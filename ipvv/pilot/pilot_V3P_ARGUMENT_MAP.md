# IPVV — V3-P ARGUMENT MAP (the tattvasaṅgrahādhikāra caturtho vimarśa: the closing sum, and the end of the whole IPVV)

*2026-08-11. Reconstruct *argument-by-argument* before writing prose. Source: M00022 lines 12263–12539
(the tattvasaṅgrahādhikāra caturtho vimarśaḥ — the samastanigamana, the closing sum; the ||4|| and
samāptā colophons, the END of the whole IPVV). Works from BOTH the L0 records and the raw Sanskrit.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

**The question:** This is the final vimarśa of the entire Īśvarapratyabhijñāvivṛtivimarśinī. It is the
closing sum (samastanigamana) that draws together the whole teaching. Its theme: the transmission of the
teaching — from the guru, from the śāstra, and from the self (gurutaḥ, śāstrataḥ, svataḥ) — and the
recognition that is the fruit. It ends with the vivṛtikāra's own lineage-praise (17 ślokas), the final
stuti, and the two colophons that close the work.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Maṅgala (lines 12265–12266):**
"Bowing to the spread, the world that, from the un-surpassed form, in the order of bliss and the rest,
emits the all-nectar-bearing, within and without…"

**The threefold transmission (lines 12268–12307):**
With the knowledge-action two-section-pair, and the essence-collection, the self-manifest essence,
reason-augmented, was established; the "by-itself" (svataḥ) part was filled. The "from-the-śāstra"
(śāstrataḥ) part is filled by the āgama-section. And the "from-the-guru" (gurutaḥ) part is the third,
to be filled — for the śāstra-vision, too, must be brought to the proper-āgama-ness by the guru-lineage
teaching. (The warning: "the book-studied knowledges, without the guru-order — practicing and teaching,
they ripen long in hell; the censured enjoyers of the torment-king-of-kings; thence, teaching the
guru-order-come — the param-Śiva.") The three verses (the ślokatraya) fill this: the first shows the
lineage from the ādi-Śrīkaṇṭhanātha (the un-precedented, effort-less teaching); the second that the
teaching, by the recognition-manifestation, animates even the real-as-un-real; the third that the
worship of the prior gurus is necessary ("the to-be-celebrated guru-group, for the fame of the
śāstra-meaning"). The lineage: Śrīkaṇṭhanātha → Durvāsa → Tryambakāditya (the Traiyambaka), the
mānasa-sons.

**Kārikā 1 (lines 12309–12383) — the new, well-fitted path:**
> Thus is made manifest by me, this well-fitted, new path; by the great gurus it is spoken, in the
> Śivadṛṣṭi-śāstra, as; placing the foot here, perceiving the world-creator-ness of the self, entering
> the Śiva-ness-made continuously, he perfects.

Since liberation is from the recognition alone, the yama/niyama-effort-yoga is useless here; the
clear-means, effort-less, un-undertaken, great-fruit. The breath-control-yogas are artificial
(kṛtrimā); the un-made has not the ṣoḍaśī-part. The "king-I-stand, enemy-by-me-killed" — the
kingdom-imagination — is still other-dependent (parādhīnā). The māyāpade too is the citprakāśa
(deśakālāspṛṣṭa); the jīvanmukta (the "jīvanneva mukta," the while-living-liberated); the
dehapātād ekabhaviko mokṣa ("from the body-fall, the one-birth liberation"); the adbhutatama (the most
wondrous).

**Kārikā 2 (lines 12385–12405) — the kāntā/loka analogy:**
> With-those-those-petitioned-favours, the-sought-to-by-the-slender-woman, standing-even-near; the
> lover, like-the-world, the-un-known, not-for-enjoying, as; of-the-world, this-alone, thus, the
> un-observed-quality, the-own-self-even, the-world-Lord; not-alone-for-the-own-splendor, this, that,
> the-recognition-arisen —

The lover, though standing near, sought by the slender woman, is not enjoyed while un-recognized; so
the world-Lord, though always present, is not for his own splendor until the recognition arises. The
pratyabhijñānamātra-ānandalābha vs. the pariśīlana-saṃbhogasahasra; the tattvedantayoḥ ekīkaraṇa = the
īśvaratāhantayoḥ; the āśrayaucitya (the dṛṣṭānta/dārṣṭāntika ornament).

**Kārikā 3 (lines 12407–12413) — the utpala-colophon:**
> Of-the-people-for-the-effort-less-establishment, by-the-sun-rise-son (Uddayākarasūnu = Utpala); the
> Lord-recognition, this, is-established —

The recognition, established by Utpala for the people's effort-less establishment; the
jananadharmā-siddhi (not caste/conduct); the sarvānugrāhakatva; the kṛtakṛtyatva (the father's
fame-fruit). Then the vivṛtikāra's explanation: the susaṃvitti of the īśvaratā; the lāṭa/dvijatva/
vāgīśvarī; the antaḥprakṛti; the Karka-śāstra; the Tryambaka (tisro devyaḥ) and the dāsya-lakṣmī; the
kheṭapālācārya (dvaita-prādhānya); the avidyā (anirvācya); the brahmavāda (not ekāntavāda); the
"cittamātram idaṃ" (the Buddhist) and the vivaraṇakāra-vipratāraṇa; the prathāto'khilagrahaṇa (all
that is prathamāna = the parama-śiva's prathā); the paryantopāsanīya parama-śiva.

**The vivṛtikāra's 17 ślokas (lines 12453–12517) — the author-lineage praise:**
The vaṃśa (1–15) praises the lineage — Varāha-gupta (Cukhulaka, Bhūtirāja's father), Bhūtirāja,
Haṭitalakṣmaṇagupta (the śāstra-maker, Abhinava's guru), the Tryambaka-śaktitraya, the
māṇikyācala/Candrakīrti, the vimarśa-method of the Lord-recognition (9), the varṇanikā (10), the
vāsanācakravāla (11), the arkameka (12), the ātmāvaraṇa/ātmaprakaṭana (14), and the navatitama-vatsara
(year 90, Mārgaśīrṣa) colophon (15). The final stuti (16–17: the parama-śiva-stuti, "īśvarapratyabhijñā
… vimarśād īśvareṇa … tmavimarśodyoginīṃ kriyāt").

**The ||4|| and samāptā colophons (lines 12528–12539):**
"Thus, in the mahā-māheśvara-Śrīmad-ācārya-Abhinavagupta-composed, in the
Īśvarapratyabhijñāvivṛtivimarśinī, the all-conclusion, the fourth vimarśa || 4 ||." "Completed, and,
this, the Īśvarapratyabhijñāvivṛtivimarśinī; the composition of the Śrī-Tri-nayana-foot-meditation-
obtained-fame Abhinavagupta-foots." — the END of the whole IPVV.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

   hagiographic detail not re-derived.
2. The 17 ślokas' specific referents (the "dāntava"-type lexemes) — kept at the doctrinal level.

## 4. Decision for L2
Proceed. The argument is understood: the closing sum draws together the whole teaching — the
transmission from guru, śāstra, and self; the recognition-path (the new well-fitted path) as the
effort-less means to jīvanmukti; the lover/world-Lord analogy; the recognition established by Utpala;
the vivṛtikāra's lineage-praise; and the final stuti and colophons. This closes the whole IPVV.

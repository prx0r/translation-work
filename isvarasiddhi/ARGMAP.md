# Īśvarasiddhi — ARGMAP

## isva-p01 (1–9)
- speaker: Utpaladeva (pūrvapakṣa + siddhānta).
- question: Is Īśvara (Lord) the cause of the world?
- premises: Karoti tam ahaṃ devam ekaṃ naumi maheśvaram (I bow to that one Lord, Maheśvara, who does). Ghaṭa... paramāṇuvat (as pot... like atom). Ṭato ghaṭo yathā (as pot from that). Niścito 'rtho 'sya saṃsthāne tasminn arthakriyācite (determined meaning, in that establishment, action-desired).
- inference: Īśvara is cause: as pot from potter, so world from Lord; determined meaning in establishment.
- conclusion: Īśvara as cause stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## isva-p02 (10–18)
- speaker: Utpaladeva.
- question: How does the Lord create?
- premises: Cakṣurādau tadaucityād ātmeṣṭo 'saṃhataḥ paraḥ (in eye etc., by fitting, self-desired, uncombined, supreme). Vahnistatpratipattyaiva khādiratvena siddhyati (fire, by that attainment, as khādira, is accomplished). Tathā vaicitryamātreṇa na svalakṣaṇalakṣmaṇā (by variety alone, not by own-mark-mark). Saṃsthānamātra eva... hetutvaṃ buddhiśālinaḥ (establishment alone... causality of the wise).
- inference: Lord creates by fitting (aucitya), uncombined, supreme; fire accomplished as khādira by attainment; by variety alone, not by own-mark; establishment alone = causality of wise.
- conclusion: Creation doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## isva-p03 (19–27)
- speaker: Utpaladeva.
- question: What is puruṣārtha (human end)?
- premises: Svārthatādiniṣedhena sādhitā puruṣārthatā (by negating self-interest etc., puruṣārtha is established). Tathā tathā pariṇatiḥ syān na kiṃcana durghaṭam (thus transformation would occur, nothing hard). Nimittakāraṇaṃ nānyat puruṣārthodyamādṛte (no other instrumental cause except puruṣārtha-effort). Cittabhūmer adharataḥ śarīrādividhiṃ prati (from consciousness-ground downward, regarding body etc. rule). Tato 'tra kutra vyāpāra īśvar... (then here, where is Lord's activity...).
- inference: Puruṣārtha = by negating self-interest, established; transformation occurs, nothing hard; no other instrumental cause except puruṣārtha-effort; from consciousness-ground downward, body etc. rule; where is Lord's activity?
- conclusion: Puruṣārtha stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## isva-p04 (28–36)
- speaker: Utpaladeva.
- question: Is the Lord the material cause?
- premises: Tadvinā kumbhakāreṇa ghaṭo 'pi na bhavet katham (without that, how would pot arise from potter). Atha svabhāva eṣo 'sya yatkulālādyapekṣaṇam (then own-nature, that, potter etc. dependence). Naivopādanahetvantaḥ praveśātsahakāribhiḥ (not material cause's end, by entering helpers). Lokayātrānusaṃdhānavatīṃ prajñāṃ vinā nahi sthūlatāmātrameva syānna t... (without worldly-practice-investigating wisdom, only grossness would be, not...).
- inference: Lord is not material cause: as pot from potter, potter's dependence; not material cause's end, by entering helpers; without worldly-practice wisdom, only grossness.
- conclusion: Material cause denied (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## isva-p05 (37–45)
- speaker: Utpaladeva.
- question: Is the Lord all-knowing?
- premises: Vinā saṃvedyatāṃ puṃsastathātve puruṣārthatā (without knowability of person, in that state, puruṣārtha). Athāpi manaso vṛttir buddhervaikaikasaṃskṛtā syāt (then mind's function, intellect's, individually conditioned). Kiṃ tu sā pradhānasya sāmānyasyākhilānprati (but that is of principal, common, for all). Sarveṣāṃ yadi sārvajñyamekaikasya prasajyate (if all-knowing of all, of each, arises). Athāpi sarvapuruṣ... (then all person...).
- inference: Lord's all-knowing questioned: without knowability of person, puruṣārtha; mind's function individually conditioned; that is of principal, common, for all; if all-knowing of all, of each arises.
- conclusion: All-knowing questioned (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## isva-p06 (46–54)
- speaker: Utpaladeva.
- question: Is mokṣa (liberation) by nature?
- premises: Svabhāvataḥ athāpi sa bhavatyeva kiṃ tu tena na yujyate (by nature, he becomes, but not by that, not fitting). Naitannārthitvamasyāsti jātu puṃso 'tha cetasaḥ (this desire-lessness of this never of person or mind). Tadetadapi na nyāyyaṃ jāte dehasthacetasi tatsvabhāvatvameva syānmokṣaḥ karmakṣayāśrayaḥ (this too not proper, when born in body-dwelling mind, that own-nature would be, mokṣa by karma-destruction). Na rāgāt... (not from attachment...).
- inference: Mokṣa not by nature: by nature he becomes, but not fitting; desire-lessness never of person or mind; not proper when born in body-dwelling mind; mokṣa by karma-destruction, not from attachment.
- conclusion: Mokṣa not by nature (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## isva-p07 (55–56)
- speaker: Utpaladeva (close).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: Palāyate daivahatasya sanmaṇiḥ (flees, of god-struck, good gem). Bhāti vahnir niśīthe (shines, fire, at night).
- inference: Close = flees, of god-struck, good gem; shines, fire, at night.
- conclusion: The text closes with imagery.
- unresolved: none.

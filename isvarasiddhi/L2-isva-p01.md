# Īśvarasiddhi isva-p01 (vv. 1–9) — L2 READ

## v.1
The maker — him, I praise, the one God, Maheśvara the Lord.
The Sanskrit: *karoti tamahaṃ devamekaṃ naumi maheśvaram*

## v.2
Bodies and the rest have conscious cause, distinct-arranged — pots … like atoms.
The Sanskrit: *tanvādi buddhimaddhetu saṃniveśaviśeṣavat ghaṭa..............................................paramāṇuvat*

## v.3
Conscious-urged — or … […] as pot from [tha]t.
The Sanskrit: *buddhimatprorito vā.......................................... ..............................................ṭato ghaṭo yathā*

## v.4
… devoid of locus-form. Fixed is its meaning in place — in that action-heaped …
The Sanskrit: *.....................................................ksaṃsthānavarjitaḥ niścito'rtho'sya saṃsthāne tasminnarthakriyācite*

## v.5
In the varied, the conscious alone is marked — as among pots. The maker, wise, resolved on varied use.
The Sanskrit: *vicitre buddhimāneva ghaṭādiṣviva lakṣyate kartā vijño vicitraitadupayogābhisaṃdhimān*

## v.6
There is, in function-yoking, among bodies and worlds, this distinct array — never from mere matter.
The Sanskrit: *asti cārthakriyāyoge śarīrabhuvanādiṣu saṃniveśaviśeṣo'yamanupādānasaṃbhavī*

## v.7
Here then — conscious makers proved, the Lord's act proved: for such-variety-knowers, lordlessness is impossible.
The Sanskrit: *tadatra buddhimatkartṛsiddhyā siddheśvarakriyā na hi tādṛśavaicitryaviduṣaḥ syādanīśitā*

## v.8
Neither potter nor Lord shows agreement here — for him, opposition would follow, or agreement unproved.
The Sanskrit: *na kulālena neśenāpyanvayo'tra pradarśyate yato viruddhatāsya syādasiddhānvayatāpi vā*

## v.9
Merely showing pervasion with the conscious alone — by thesis-force, somewhere, someone wise is understood.
The Sanskrit: *kevalaṃ buddhimanmātravyāptisaṃdarśanādapi pakṣadharmabalāddhīmānkvacitkaścitpratīyate*

# Īśvarasiddhi isva-p02 (vv. 10–18) — L2 READ

## v.10
As combination-ness is seen merely for another's sake — in the eye and the rest, by that fitness: the self-dear, uncompounded higher.
The Sanskrit: *yathā pārārthyamātreṇa saṃghātatve'nvitekṣite cakṣurādau tadaucityādātmeṣṭo'saṃhataḥ paraḥ*

## v.11
As from afar, from smoke, proving in a khadira-grove — fire is proved by just knowing it, as khadira-wood.
The Sanskrit: *yathā vā dūrato dhūmātsiddhiḥ khadirakānane vahnistatpratipattyaiva khādiratvena siddhyati*

## v.12
Thus the cause: locus-form alone, here distinguished — not by mere variety as svalakṣaṇa-mark.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ ca hetuḥ saṃsthānamātramatra viśeṣitam tathā vaicitryamātreṇa na svalakṣaṇalakṣmaṇā*

## v.13
By others too, of reasons, in pot-self-sifting — locus-form alone … even … causehood of the conscious one.
The Sanskrit: *paraistairapi hetūnāṃ ghaṭātmapravivecane saṃsthānamātra eva............ hetutvaṃ buddhiśālinaḥ*

## v.14
Mutual-function-fit-locus-formed — that whole world, this — rightly Lord-made.
The Sanskrit: *anyonyārthakriyāyogyasaṃniveśaviśeṣitam tadidaṃ sakalaṃ viśvaṃ yuktamīśvaranirmitam*

## v.15
Here is the reply: a strange way of helpers is seen — some would somehow join with materials — a joining.
The Sanskrit: *atrocyate gatiścitrā dṛśyate sahakāriṇām kecitkathaṃcideveyurupādānaiḥ samāgamam*

## v.16
Helpers, entering as others, reaching material-form — picture effects indeed — like earth-water-juices.
The Sanskrit: *hetumantaḥpraviśyānye prāpyopādānarūpatām citrayanti hi kāryāṇi bhaumodakarasā iva*

## v.17
Others again, in matter, outward-changing — for yoking form-variety in effects, they are not so capable.
The Sanskrit: *anye punarupādāne bahirviparivartinaḥ rūpavaicitryayogāya kāryeṣu na tathā kṣamāḥ*

## v.18
There, if the conscious one's spread is imagined in name — not by that alone does order hold everywhere. So it stands.
The Sanskrit: *tatra buddhimato nāma prasaro yadi kalpyate na tāvataiva sarvatra vyavasthā ghaṭate tathā*

# Īśvarasiddhi isva-p03 (vv. 19–27) — L2 READ

## v.19
And of this proved pradhāna there is, by anvaya and the rest — by denial of self-aims and the like, person-aim-ness is proved.
The Sanskrit: *asti cāsya pradhānasya prasiddhasyānvayādinā svārthatādiniṣedhena sādhitā puruṣārthatā*

## v.20
Through that, person's enjoyment-proving again and again — so change would be nothing hard.
The Sanskrit: *tenāsya bhogasaṃsiddhiḥ puruṣasya yathā yathā tathā tathā pariṇatiḥ syānna kiṃcana durghaṭam*

## v.21
Hence indeed, for ever-becoming pradhāna, no efficient cause but person-aim-striving.
The Sanskrit: *ata eva pradhānasya mahattvapariṇāminaḥ nimittakāraṇaṃ nānyatpuruṣārthodyamādṛte*

## v.22
Own-nature indeed — and this of this — from seeing the root-cause on; below mind-ground, toward body-ordaining.
The Sanskrit: *svabhāva eva caiṣo'sya mūlahetostathekṣaṇāt cittabhūmeradharataḥ śarīrādividhiṃ prati*

## v.23
As efficient cause, karma could be original-natured even. Then here — where is Īśvara's business to be conceived?
The Sanskrit: *nimittakāraṇaṃ karma syādapyādyasvabhāvataḥ tato'tra kutra vyāpāra īśvarasyopakalpyatām*

## v.24
Protecting the inner-organ-rangers in root-nature, of secret nature — let Īśvara's change-causality be conceived.
The Sanskrit: *raha(natva)sya mūlaprakṛtāvantaḥ karaṇacāriṇaḥ īśvarasyopakalpyeta pariṇāmanimittatā*

## v.25
If of uniform-change-nature — then thence: independent seed-kitchen, as in sprouts.
The Sanskrit: *yadyathā pariṇāmaikasvabhāvaṃ tatra tattataḥ ananyāpekṣi bījādisāmagrī yadvadaṅkure*

## v.26
The mahad-first-change-torrent-natured, three-natured pradhāna-self — thereby godless Sāṅkhya.
The Sanskrit: *mahadādivikāraughapariṇāmasvabhāvakam triguṇātma pradhānaṃ ca tena sāṃkhyamanīśvaram*

## v.27
If puruṣārtha-natured in all function-acts — by pradhāna it is conceived as efficient.
The Sanskrit: *puruṣārthasvabhāvatvaṃ samastārthakriyāsu cet nimittakāraṇatvena pradhānasyopakalpyate*

# Īśvarasiddhi isva-p04 (vv. 28–36) — L2 READ

## v.28
Without that — how could there be even a pot without a potter?
The Sanskrit: *tadvinā kumbhakāreṇa ghaṭo'pi na bhavetkatham*

## v.29
From the pradhāna-made clay-lump indeed, of such nature — now own-nature, this of this, looking to potter and the rest.
The Sanskrit: *pradhānamayamṛtpiṇḍādeva tādṛksvabhāvataḥ atha svabhāva eṣo'sya yatkulālādyapekṣaṇam*

## v.30
Not indeed — by entering inside the material cause with helpers — could arrangement be made, says he — capable though they be.
The Sanskrit: *naivopādanahetvantaḥ praveśātsahakāribhiḥ saṃniveśaviśeṣo'pi śakyaḥ kartumiti kṣamam*

## v.31
World-traffic-linking-having wisdom — without — never indeed.
The Sanskrit: *lokayātrānusaṃdhānavatīṃ prajñāṃ vinā nahi*

## v.32
So without desiring-to-make, no wondrous-making arising — there'd be mere grossness, not world-useful.
The Sanskrit: *tathā cikīrṣāṃ ca vinā vicitraracanodbhavaḥ sthūlatāmātrameva syānna tu lokopayoginī*

## v.33
Making by rule would be wondrous, kind-formed — if from potter-helping-mereness a pot is born.
The Sanskrit: *racanā niyamena syādvicitrā jātirūpiṇī kulālasahakāritvamātrāccejjāyate ghaṭaḥ*

## v.34
Never body etc. — long clay-lump or else — hence arrangement excels by being led to ends.
The Sanskrit: *na syājjātu śarīrādi dīrghamṛtpiṇḍa eva vā ata evārthanīyatvātsaṃniveśo viśiṣyate*

## v.35
Not by pot-etc.-ness proved — the need-fulfiller. Neither Lord nor non-Lord is taken here as atomic-marked.
The Sanskrit: *na ghaṭāditayā siddhaḥ so'rthitāparipūrakaḥ na ceśvaro'nīśvaro vā prakṛto'trāṇulakṣaṇaḥ*

## v.36
Nor of pradhāna desire-etc.-arising — person-aim-ness.
The Sanskrit: *na ca pradhānasyecchādisaṃbhavaḥ puruṣārthatā*

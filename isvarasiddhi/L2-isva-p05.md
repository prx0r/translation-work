# Īśvarasiddhi isva-p05 (vv. 37–45) — L2 READ

## v.37
Since intellect's arising, actual or not, stands unperceived by any spirit — puruṣārthatā in such a case [is unspeakable: v38].
The Sanskrit: *yataḥ syādasya buddhestu saṃbhavannapyasaṃbhavī vinā saṃvedyatāṃ puṃsastathātve puruṣārthatā*

## v.38
Even of spirit, even of pradhāna, it would be unspeakable — of the mere; yet mind's function, or intellect's, is individually conditioned.
The Sanskrit: *puṃso'pi syātpradhānasyāvācyā sā kevalasya tu athāpi manaso vṛttirbuddhervaikaikasaṃskṛtā*

## v.39
Will, indeed — how could it serve all spirits' purposes? But [if] it belongs to generic pradhāna, for all…
The Sanskrit: *icchā nāma kathaṃ sarvapuruṣārthopayoginī syātikiṃ tu sā pradhānasya sāmānyasyākhilānprati*

## v.40
Even unperceived by spirit, could it be? Not will — and perceived, by whom would it be? If by all — omniscience of each and every one follows.
The Sanskrit: *puṃsaḥ syātsāpyasaṃvedyā necchā vedyā ca kasya sā sarveṣāṃ yadi sārvajñyamekaikasya prasajyate*

## v.41
Through light-unity the distinct appearances would mix; yet [perceived] by some one undivided from all spirits…
The Sanskrit: *saṃkīryeranprakāśaikyādbhinnāścābhāsamānatāḥ athāpi sarvapuruṣābhinnenaikena kenacit*

## v.42
Not from world-sustenance-purpose would it produce seed into sprout; but by nature — such a kit is its cause, of such a kind.
The Sanskrit: *na lokayātrābhiprāyājjanayedbījamaṅkuram kiṃ tu svabhāvātsā tasya sāmagrī heturīdṛśaḥ*

## v.43
What is born, fit — the wise man joins to one another; by this courtesy, puruṣārthatva is spoken of it.
The Sanskrit: *yajjātaṃ yogyamanyonyaṃ yojayedbuddhimāñjanaḥ asya tenopacāreṇa puruṣārthatvamucyate*

## v.44
If thus — even on completion, even in purpose, toward him [the freed spirit]…
The Sanskrit: *iti cettatsamāpte'pi puruṣārthe'pi taṃ prati*

## v.45
From own-effect-change the root-cause would not cease; oh — it does not cease, this beyond, in the seven of nature.
The Sanskrit: *svakāryapariṇāmānna viramenmūlakāraṇam āho na viramatyetatparaṃ prakṛtisaptake*

# Īśvarasiddhi isva-p07 (vv. 55–56) — L2 READ

## v.55
Though proved by a thousand blazing reasons, it succeeds not for the deluded-minded; Maheśvara, placed on the palm, yet flees the fate-struck — the true jewel.
The Sanskrit: *samujjvalannyāyasahasrasādhito'pyupaiti siddhiṃ na vimūḍhacetasām maheśvaraḥ pāṇitalasthito'pi san palāyate daivahatasya sanmaṇiḥ*

## v.56
One's own self indeed — this — flashes forth: Īśvara within all breathers, maker and knower too; yet recognition of the beyond is to be effected, and the joining is made by us elsewhere. But Īśvara-proved stands even in duality — fitting, and more: fire shines at midnight.
The Sanskrit: *svātmaivāyaṃ sphurati sakalaprāṇināmīśvaro'ntaḥ kartā jñātāpi ca yadi paraṃ pratyabhijñāsya sādhyā sā cāsmābhirvihitaghaṭanānyatra kiṃ tvīśasiddhirdvaite'pyastītyucitamadhikaṃ bhāti vahnirniśīthe*

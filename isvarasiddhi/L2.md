# Īśvarasiddhi — L2 READ (Dyczkowski-mode)

## isva-p01–p03 — cause, creation, puruṣārtha
**SUMMARY.** I bow to that one Lord, Maheśvara, who does. As pot from potter, so world from Lord; determined meaning in establishment. In eye etc., by fitting, self-desired, uncombined, supreme. Fire, by that attainment, as khādira, is accomplished. By variety alone, not by own-mark; establishment alone = causality of wise. By negating self-interest etc., puruṣārtha is established. Thus transformation would occur, nothing hard. No other instrumental cause except puruṣārtha-effort. From consciousness-ground downward, regarding body etc. rule. Where is Lord's activity?

**FUNCTION.** Establishes Īśvara as cause (as pot from potter), creation (by fitting, uncombined, supreme; by variety alone), puruṣārtha (by negating self-interest; no other instrumental cause).

**KEY TERMS.** īśvara (Lord), maheśvara (Maheśvara), kartṛ (agent), puruṣārtha (human end), aucitya (fitting), khādira (khādira), nimitta (instrumental cause), vyāpāra (activity).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First three passages; sets the doctrinal frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in three steps. (1) Cause: I bow to that one Lord, Maheśvara, who does; as pot from potter, so world from Lord; determined meaning in establishment. (2) Creation: in eye etc., by fitting, self-desired, uncombined, supreme; fire, by that attainment, as khādira, accomplished; by variety alone, not by own-mark; establishment alone = causality of wise. (3) Puruṣārtha: by negating self-interest etc., puruṣārtha established; transformation would occur, nothing hard; no other instrumental cause except puruṣārtha-effort; from consciousness-ground downward, body etc. rule; where is Lord's activity?

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.1–1.27.

## isva-p04–p06 — material cause, all-knowing, mokṣa
**SUMMARY.** Without that, how would pot arise from potter. Then own-nature, that, potter etc. dependence. Not material cause's end, by entering helpers. Without worldly-practice wisdom, only grossness would be, not... Without knowability of person, in that state, puruṣārtha. Then mind's function, intellect's, individually conditioned. But that is of principal, common, for all. If all-knowing of all, of each, arises. By nature, he becomes, but not by that, not fitting. This desire-lessness of this never of person or mind. This too not proper when born in body-dwelling mind; mokṣa by karma-destruction, not from attachment.

**FUNCTION.** Denies Lord as material cause; questions all-knowing; denies mokṣa by nature.

**KEY TERMS.** upādāna (material cause), kumbhakāra (potter), kulāla (potter), saṃvedyatā (knowability), sārvajñya (all-knowing), svabhāva (nature), mokṣa (liberation), karma (karma), rāga (attachment).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p01–p03 (cause/creation/puruṣārtha); p04–p06 deny material cause/all-knowing/mokṣa-by-nature.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in three steps. (1) Material cause denied: without that, how would pot arise from potter; then own-nature, potter etc. dependence; not material cause's end, by entering helpers; without worldly-practice wisdom, only grossness. (2) All-knowing questioned: without knowability of person, puruṣārtha; mind's function individually conditioned; that is of principal, common, for all; if all-knowing of all, of each, arises. (3) Mokṣa not by nature: by nature he becomes, but not fitting; desire-lessness never of person or mind; not proper when born in body-dwelling mind; mokṣa by karma-destruction, not from attachment.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.28–1.54.

## isva-p07 — close
**SUMMARY.** Palāyate daivahatasya sanmaṇiḥ — flees, of god-struck, good gem. Bhāti vahnir niśīthe — shines, fire, at night.

**FUNCTION.** Closes with imagery.

**KEY TERMS.** daivahata (god-struck), sanmaṇi (good gem), vahni (fire), niśītha (night).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Closes the text; follows p04–p06.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in one step. Close: flees, of god-struck, good gem; shines, fire, at night.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are terse; L2 expands them.

**RELATED.** A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS1.55–1.56.

## isva-p03 (19-27)
[and]-svārthatādiniṣedhena sādhitā puruṣārthatā tathā tathā pariṇatiḥ syānna kiṃcana durghaṭam nimittakāraṇaṃ nānyatpuruṣārthodyamādṛte cittabhūmeradharataḥ śarīrādividhiṃ prati tato'tra kutra vyāpāra īśvarasyopakalpyatām raha(natva)sya mūlaprakṛtāvantaḥ karaṇacāriṇaḥ yadyathā pariṇāmaikasvabhāvaṃ tatra tattataḥ mahadādivikāraughapariṇāmasvabhāvakam puruṣārthasvabhāvatvaṃ samastārthakriyāsu cet

## isva-p04 (28-36)
[and]-tadvinā kumbhakāreṇa ghaṭo'pi na bhavetkatham atha svabhāva eṣo'sya yatkulālādyapekṣaṇam naivopādanahetvantaḥ praveśātsahakāribhiḥ lokayātrānusaṃdhānavatīṃ prajñāṃ vinā nahi sthūlatāmātrameva syānna tu lokopayoginī kulālasahakāritvamātrāccejjāyate ghaṭaḥ ata evārthanīyatvātsaṃniveśo viśiṣyate na ceśvaro'nīśvaro vā prakṛto'trāṇulakṣaṇaḥ na ca pradhānasyecchādisaṃbhavaḥ puruṣārthatā

## isva-p05 (37-45)
[and]-vinā saṃvedyatāṃ puṃsastathātve puruṣārthatā athāpi manaso vṛttirbuddhervaikaikasaṃskṛtā syātikiṃ tu sā pradhānasya sāmānyasyākhilānprati sarveṣāṃ yadi sārvajñyamekaikasya prasajyate athāpi sarvapuruṣābhinnenaikena kenacit na lokayātrābhiprāyājjanayedbījamaṅkuram yajjātaṃ yogyamanyonyaṃ yojayedbuddhimāñjanaḥ iti cettatsamāpte'pi puruṣārthe'pi taṃ prati āho na viramatyetatparaṃ prakṛtisaptake

## isva-p06 (46-54)
[and]-svabhāvataḥ athāpi sa bhavatyeva kiṃ tu tena na yujyate naitannārthitvamasyāsti jātu puṃso'tha cetasaḥ tadetadapi na nyāyyaṃ jāte dehasthacetasi tatsvabhāvatvameva syānmokṣaḥ karmakṣayāśrayaḥ na rāgāttāni cedevaṃ mokṣo rāgakṣayādbhavet tasmātprasiddhaṃ tanvādi buddhimatkartṛpūrvakam saṃsthānabhedādityetanniravadyaṃ prasādhanam paro'pi vā kevalametaducyate

## isva-p07 (55-56)

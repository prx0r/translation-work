# C1 — isva-p01 (vv. 1–9): the inference stated; agreement denied and answered

## SUMMARY

The maker — him praised, one God, Maheśvara. Bodies bespeak conscious cause, distinct-arranged — pots, like atoms. Conscious-urged fragments and fixed-meaning fragments survive around gaps. In the varied, the conscious alone is marked, maker-wise, use-resolved. Function-yoked bodies and worlds hold distinct array, never from mere matter. Makers proved prove the Lord's act; variety-knowers cannot be lordless. Neither potter nor Lord shows agreement — opposition or unprovedness would follow. Merely showing conscious-only pervasion — by thesis-force, some wise one is understood.

## KEY TERMS

* **kartṛ/hetu/dṛṣṭānta** — maker/reason/example: the Nyāya tripod (vv2/9).
* **saṃniveśaviśeṣa** — distinct array (arrangement): the reason-property (vv2/6).
* **tanvādi** — bodies-etc.: the locus (pakṣa) (v2).
* **buddhi** — consciousness: what the array bespeaks; what pervasion is shown with (vv2/9).
* **vaicitrya** — variety: wisdom's mark (vv5/7).
* **anupādāna** — without-material: matter's exclusion (v6).
* **anvaya** — agreement/concomitance: denied both horns, then minimally regained (vv8-9).
* **pakṣadharma** — thesis-property: whose bare force suffices (v9).

## rasa

Confidence (vīra) of stated proof: the hetu advances in Nyāya dress, takes the dilemma, and answers with less — pervasion shown, nothing more needed.

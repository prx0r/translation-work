# C1 — isva-p02 (vv. 10–18): stock examples turned; hetu fenced; helpers bounded

## SUMMARY

Fitness shows the higher self in eye and the rest, the way combination is seen for another's sake. Fire comes proved as khadira-wood through mere knowing, the way smoke proves from afar. The hetu is fenced to bare locus-form, refusing bare variety the particular's mark. Others sift reasons the same way in the pot's self. The mutual-fit whole stands rightly Lord-made. Helpers answer strangely — some joining with stuff — picturing effects by entering-as-other, like earth-saps. Outward-turned others cannot yoke form-variety. Fancied spread, named but unshown, orders nothing alone.

## KEY TERMS

* **aucitya** — fitness: what shows the self in eye etc. (v10).
* **dhūma/vahni** — smoke/fire: the stock example, turned (v11).
* **svalakṣaṇa** — particular: refused as mark (v12).
* **vivecana** — sifting: others' reasons-work in pot's self (v13).
* **upakāra/sahakārin** — helping/helpers: strange way opened (vv15-16).
* **anvaya/vyatireka** — presence/absence logic behind helpers (vv16-17).
* **prasara/kalpanā** — spread/imagining: insufficient alone (v18).

## rasa

Parade-confidence (vīra): examples marshalled — eye, fire, pot, helpers — each turned to show fitness; wonder (adbhuta) that mere knowing proves wood.

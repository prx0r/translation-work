# C1 — isva-p03 (vv. 19–27): Sāṅkhya stated; Īśvara proposed

## SUMMARY

Sāṅkhya's pradhāna is proved by presence, purposed to spirit by denying self-aims; change comes easy again and again for enjoyment. Only person-aim-striving moves ever-maturing primal stuff; own-nature faces downward from root-cause-seeing toward body's ordaining. Karma admitted as possible mover — then the page turns: where is Īśvara's business? Let His change-triggering be conceived for root-nature's rangers. Seed-kitchens need nothing else, as in sprouts. Three-natured pradhāna leaves Sāṅkhya godless. Puruṣārtha-natured acts are conceived efficient by pradhāna.

## KEY TERMS

* **pradhāna** — primal-matter: proved, maturing, three-natured (vv19/21/26).
* **puruṣārtha** — person-aim: proved by denial (vv19/27).
* **anvaya** — presence: proving with the proved (v19).
* **nimitta** — mover: none but striving; karma admitted (vv21/23).
* **svabhāva** — own-nature facing down (v22).
* **bīja/aṅkura** — seed/sprout: independence imaged (v25).
* **karma** — work admitted as mover (v23).
* **rahas/natva** — secret-nature crux, kept printed (v24).

## rasa

Debate-joined (vīra): Sāṅkhya stated fairly, then the page turned with a question — where is His business? Wonder (adbhuta) that denial proves purpose.

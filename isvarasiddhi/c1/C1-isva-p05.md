# C1 — isva-p05 (vv. 37–45): perceptibility, omniscience, and nature's ceaseless churn

## SUMMARY

Since intellect's arising stands unperceived by any spirit, no purpose follows — unspeakable, of spirit or of nature, though mind's motions come conditioned each in private ways. Will — how serve every soul? Give it to nature-in-general, and every soul turns all-knowing. One light would mix all shinings unless some One, in all undivided, keeps each apart. No world-plan makes seed put forth sprout; blind nature, kit and cause, brings green about — the wise join part to part, calling it spirit's purpose by courtesy. Granted — still, when purpose ends, the Root would churn its effects and never cease: sevenfold nature grants no release.

## KEY TERMS

* **saṃvedyatā** — perceptibility: without it, no purpose (v37).
* **avācyā** — unspeakable: the denial completing v37 (v38).
* **sārvajñya** — omniscience: the absurd consequence of generic will (v40).
* **prakāśaikatva** — light-unity: what mixes appearances (v41).
* **sāmagrī** — kit: nature's sufficient equipment (v42).
* **upacāra** — courtesy-speech: purpose as metaphor (v43–44).
* **mūlakāraṇa** — root-cause: what never ceases (v45).

## rasa

Reasoning-press (vitarka): unperceived will failing, generic will exploding into omniscience, nature-alone churning without release. Astonishment (adbhuta) at the One undivided holding all spirits.

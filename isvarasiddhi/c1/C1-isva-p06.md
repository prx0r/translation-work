# C1 — isva-p06 (vv. 46–54): consequences, conclusion, and the aimed hetu

## SUMMARY

From change, that One is longed for by all spirits — yet body's change runs by nature. As before, why not for the deemed-freed? It occurs, yet joins him not. Done and need-free he stands alone — yet needlessness fits no spirit ever, nor mind. With need gone, no meeting again — yet minds are born in bodies still. If sense-bound nature, not karma's waning, mokṣa is mere nature on karma's prop. Knowers act from compassion — yet passionless, release is passion's waning. Drop the nature-view, unjust; bodies bespeak a knowing Maker. From patterned difference, flawless proof — laid on the Lord by self-entrusted hand, unshaken.

## KEY TERMS

* **pratīṣyate** — longed-for: all spirits desire the One (v46).
* **muktābhimata** — deemed-freed: the test case (v47).
* **anarthitva** — needlessness: fitting neither spirit nor mind (v48).
* **samāgama** — meeting: none again, yet rebirth (v49).
* **karmakṣaya** — karma-waning: the prop (v50).
* **kṛpā/rāgakṣaya** — compassion/passion-exhaustion: the trap (v51).
* **buddhimatkartṛpūrvaka** — sentient-maker-prior: the conclusion (v52).
* **saṃsthānabheda** — setup-difference: the seal (v53).
* **hetu** — reason: aimed at the Lord, unshaken (v54).

## rasa

Proof-triumph (vīra): consequences collapsing one by one — then abandonment, seal, aim. Exultation (utsāha) that the Maker stands.

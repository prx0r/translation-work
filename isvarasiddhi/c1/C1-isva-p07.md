# C1 — isva-p07 (vv. 55–56): the fleeing Jewel and the signature

## SUMMARY

A thousand blazing reasons prove — yet not for minds astray; palm-held Maheśvara flees the fated, Jewel slipping away. Your own Self flashes — Īśvara in every breather's heart, Maker and Knower — yet Recognition stands apart; we wrought its joining elsewhere, and in duality too it stands — fit, and fuller: midnight fire burns brighter in our hands.

## KEY TERMS

* **nyāyasahasra** — thousand reasons: proving, yet unfruitful for some (v55).
* **sanmaṇi** — true jewel: palm-held, fleeing the fate-struck (v55).
* **svātman** — own self: flashing as Īśvara within all (v56).
* **pratyabhijñā** — recognition: still to be effected (v56).
* **dvaite'pi** — even in duality: the proof stands (v56).
* **vahni niśīthe** — fire at midnight: brighter for darkness (v56).

## rasa

Closing-wonder (adbhuta): proof fled, Self flashing, midnight fire. Fitness (adhikāra) honored — the jewel keeps its own counsel.

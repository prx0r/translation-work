# C1 NOTE — isva-p01

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

The maker — him praised, one God, Maheśvara. Bodies bespeak conscious cause, distinct-arranged — pots, like atoms. Conscious-urged fragments and fixed-meaning fragments survive around gaps. In the varied, the conscious alone is marked, maker-wise, use-resolved. Function-yoked bodies and worlds hold distinct array, never from mere matter. Makers proved prove the Lord's act; variety-knowers cannot be lordless. Neither potter nor Lord shows agreement — opposition or unprovedness would follow. Merely showing conscious-only pervasion — by thesis-force, some wise one is understood.

## FUNCTION

Opens the first proof with praise (v1) and the Nyāya-shaped thesis (v2), preserves two lacuna-fragments (vv3-4) without building on them, marks wisdom in variety (v5), excludes matter (v6), draws the Lord-conclusion (v7), voices the anvaya-dilemma (v8), and answers it minimally (v9).

## KEY TERMS

* **karoti tam** — who makes, him: auspice naming conclusion first (v1).
* **buddhimaddhetu** — with-conscious-cause: the hetu in embryo (v2).
* **dṛṣṭānta (ghaṭa/paramāṇu)** — pot/atoms: example pair, mid-gap (v2).
* **prorito/ṭato** — urged/remnant: unbuilt cruxes (v3).
* **niścitaḥ arthaḥ** — fixed meaning: what survives of v4.
* **lakṣyate** — is marked: variety's signature (v5).
* **abhisaṃdhimat** — resolve-having: purpose in making (v5).
* **anupādānasaṃbhavin** — not-from-material-possible: matter's exclusion (v6).
* **anīśitā** — non-lordliness: what variety-knowers cannot have (v7).
* **viruddhatā/asiddhānvayatā** — opposition/unproved-agreement: the dilemma's horns (v8).
* **kevalaṃ ... saṃdarśanāt** — merely from showing: the minimalist answer (v9).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Īśvarasiddhi, Utpaladeva (10th c.), mūla-ślokas with mūla-prose (Muktabodha M00023, KSTS 34). First proof of the Siddhitrayī: the Lord must be proved — taking Nyāya's inference as ally and outflanking it. Passage 1 of 7: vv. 1–9 (thesis, hetu, dilemma, minimalist answer). Ratié on this proof's purpose: Nyāya machinery turned toward a conscious Lord.

## EXPLANATION

Praise first, naming maker, one, Lord (v1). Then the thesis in full Nyāya dress — locus, reason, example (v2, gapped). Two fragments preserved without doctrine built on them (vv3-4). Wisdom marked precisely where things vary, with resolve for use (v5). Matter excluded from arrangement's source (v6). The step to the Lord drawn through proved makers (v7). The dilemma voiced in opponent voice — anvaya shown by neither horn (v8). The answer: bare showing suffices; pakṣadharma-force alone grasps some wise one (v9). The prose between carries the anvaya polemic (hetu/doṣa/vyāpti apparatus); the verses are its theses.

## BOUNDARY

vv3-4 are remnants: prorito unattested, ṭato and cite isolated, leading/trailing dots mark lost pādas — no reading pressed. v01's opening hemistich lost to dots. v08's voice (objection) from prose context and v09's answering shape. v02's atoms-vs-pot pairing partly lost. iti-style closings and Kaul colophons excluded as paratext.

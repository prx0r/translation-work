# C1 NOTE — isva-p02

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Fitness shows the higher self in eye and the rest, the way combination is seen for another's sake. Fire comes proved as khadira-wood through mere knowing, the way smoke proves from afar. The hetu is fenced to bare locus-form, refusing bare variety the particular's mark. Others sift reasons the same way in the pot's self. The mutual-fit whole stands rightly Lord-made. Helpers answer strangely — some joining with stuff — picturing effects by entering-as-other, like earth-saps. Outward-turned others cannot yoke form-variety. Fancied spread, named but unshown, orders nothing alone.

## FUNCTION

Parades stock examples turned for fitness (vv10-11), fences the hetu (v12), extends sifting to others (v13), states the Lord-made whole (v14), opens helpers' strange way (v15), pictures their entering (v16), bounds outward-changers (v17), and refuses imagined spread (v18).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Passage 2 of 7: vv. 10–18. The examples-march: Sāṅkhya's eye, Nyāya's fire, Buddhist particulars, others' sifting, helpers' mechanics. Mūla-prose between carries anvaya/vyatireka polemic and Sahakārin analysis.

## EXPLANATION

Fitness, not assemblage, shows the self in eye etc. (v10). Mere knowing proves wood-kind from afar (v11). The hetu is fenced against particular-only marks (v12). Others sift the same way (v13, gapped). The mutual-fit whole is Lord-made (v14). Helpers' strangeness is admitted as reply (v15), their entering pictured (v16), outward-changers bounded (v17), imagined spread refused (v18).

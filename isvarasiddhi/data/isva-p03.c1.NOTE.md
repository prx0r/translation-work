# C1 NOTE — isva-p03

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Sāṅkhya's pradhāna is proved by presence, purposed to spirit by denying self-aims; change comes easy again and again for enjoyment. Only person-aim-striving moves ever-maturing primal stuff; own-nature faces downward from root-cause-seeing toward body's ordaining. Karma admitted as possible mover — then the page turns: where is Īśvara's business? Let His change-triggering be conceived for root-nature's rangers. Seed-kitchens need nothing else, as in sprouts. Three-natured pradhāna leaves Sāṅkhya godless. Puruṣārtha-natured acts are conceived efficient by pradhāna.

## FUNCTION

States Sāṅkhya fairly (vv19-22: proof, ease, mover, downward nature), pivots on karma admitted (v23), proposes Īśvara's triggering (v24), images independence (v25), verdicts Sāṅkhya godless (v26), and closes conditionally (v27).

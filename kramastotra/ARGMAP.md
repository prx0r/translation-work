# Kramastotra — ARGMAP

## kram-p01 (1)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice (Krama tradition).
- question: What is the hymn's purpose?
- premises: This hymn is the great ease-course; constant thought reflects on the worshipper's conduct; thus fear is removed.
- inference: Hymn-practice removes fear.
- conclusion: The hymn removes fear (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## kram-p02 (2)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What does self-examination do?
- premises: Examining own self, examining the hymned conduct, the worshipper in hymn-practice without effort; examined self, in all-knowable-moment, that thus in hymn I without effort.
- inference: Self-examination → effortlessness in hymn-practice.
- conclusion: Self-examination leads to effortlessness.
- unresolved: none.

## kram-p03 (3)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: What is the self's nature?
- premises: Ungrasped self is never knower of thing-knowledge; ungrasped self indeed is not; Śiva is that self, shining all-thing-one-sap; hence I in hymn with devoted heart always blissful.
- inference: Ungrasped self = Śiva = all-thing-one-sap; hence bliss.
- conclusion: Śiva is the self; bliss follows.
- unresolved: none.

## kram-p04 (4)
- speaker: Abhinava, devotional voice.
- question: How is omniscience gained?
- premises: By varied birth-etc.-wandering-course-garments gained omniscience heart; that inside, teaching-spread-course-become light, clearly in speech obtained, now I hymn without effort.
- inference: Omniscience gained by varied wandering; teaching-spread → light → speech.
- conclusion: Omniscience gained; hymn without effort.
- unresolved: none.

## kram-p05 (5)
- speaker: Abhinavagupta, devotional voice.
- question: What is the result of hymn-practice?
- premises: Shaking off bond-abiding-conceit, enjoying varied things, by great fire of hymn-hutāśana, burned difference; varied self-splendor-rush, drinking things, varada, maddened, blissful.
- inference: Hymn-practice burns difference; self-splendor rushes; bliss.
- conclusion: Maddened blissful state.
- unresolved: none.

## kram-p06 (6)
- speaker: Abhinavagupta, devotional voice.
- question: What is the first means?
- premises: If Śiva-oneness-gained wish, how hymn? If conduct, by that suddenly Śiva-oneness-first-means. If Śiva-oneness-gained, conduct-abandoning brings expiation.
- inference: Hymn is first means to Śiva-oneness; abandoning it = expiation.
- conclusion: Hymn = first means; don't abandon.
- unresolved: none.

- conclusion: Composed in Mārgaśīrṣa, 66th year.

## The parable (canonical draft, ≈88s spoken)

> In a hard year a woman strings a garland for the completion-rite —
> every bloom a vow threaded through the sorrow. Scrutiny first: it
> blooms bliss and knowing won without effort; heart bidden to
> speak: the unhymnable sings. She shakes the bond-knot, burns the
> difference, drinks the glory — mad with it. She threads the fire
> that does not burn and the inner ocean where three lights rise and
> set at once, floods unopposed; the undivided power walking, her
> heart one-pointed. Praise melts the fear as she bows — bowing
> first. She lets the grave-herald go like spent air, grinds the
> split doubt, melts everything but hunger and strikes; draws the
> sun-sap by force; strings thisness where difference fled, prays
> the three becomings over it, takes the outer off to dwell the
> noble, holds the dread-ground and conducts as she pleases — the
> three ordain it, ends grasped. The garland re-enters itself.
> Powers unhindered, she bows the churn-essence. At fullness she
> cuts the thread — nothing left off it — ties the knot, sets her
> seal. The strand does not fall: ever-sport in the mind, time met
> on the string, bonds heard and grace allaying all folk. Dated,
> sealed, garlanded.

## The seal — statement

**The garland that cuts itself full:** one strand rising from a
sorrow-dark base where a small woman bows — beads in sequence
(sorrow-rose → scrutiny-gold → white unburning flame → lapis ocean
with silver-gold-blue triple lights → mid-strand RED rush-cluster →
amber sun-sap → charcoal Yama-bead → ivory thisness) — cut-knot at
fullness with a red wax seal (no letters), loose ends NOT falling
but curling up, and the strand re-entering itself into a burnished
loop at the crown: the sequence completing as a cycle.

## Composition laws (this seal)

The GARLAND is subject (woman = base-scale, as in object-seals).
Beads in strict strand-order (height = verse-order: no collage).
The cut happens at FULLNESS (not destruction — loose ends curl up).
Wax-seal has no letters (991 = date carried as the seal-dot itself).
Reds cluster mid-strand (the heat-peak is middle, never top — rasa
law: peace owns the crown).

# Kramastotra kram-p01 (the rite that ends sorrow) — L2 READ

## v.1
This is the completion-rite amid sorrow's host — in the spreading taste of great bliss-essence, this foul day: for by mutual doing, in the hymn of the Lord, weighty with scorching uneven flames, the mind constantly reflects back — its fear gone.
The Sanskrit: *duḥkhavrātavrataparigame pāraṇavidhir | mahāsaukhyāsāraprasaraṇarase durdinam idam | pratiphalati ceto gatabhayam*

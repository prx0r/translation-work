# Kramastotra kram-p03 (no scrutiny, no knowing) — L2 READ

## v.3
Unscrutinized, the self never comes to share in thing-knowing; unscrutinized, it is not at all without the grasping course. But Śiva — that self shining, one sap of all things — hence I, heart-devoted to Your hymn, am ever blissful.
The Sanskrit: *anāmṛṣṭaḥ svātmā na hi bhavati bhāvapramitibhāj | śivaś cāsau svātmā sphurad akhilabhāvaikasarasas*

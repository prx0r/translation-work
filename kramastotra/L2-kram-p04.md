# Kramastotra kram-p04 (won, now speak) — L2 READ

## v.4
By varied birth-and-wandering gears, omniscience is gained — O heart — effortlessly through You. That within, become light in the teaching's spreading course: clearly obtained in speech — show forth the Lord's hymn now.
The Sanskrit: *jātyādibhramaṇaparipāṭīparikarair avāptam sārvajñaṃ hṛdaya | tvadbodhaprasarasaraṇībhūtamahasi sphuṭam vāci prāpya prakaṭaya vibhoḥ stotram adhunā*

# Kramastotra kram-p05 (burnt, drunk, blissful) — L2 READ

## v.5
Shaking off the standing of bondage-conceit-paths, relishing this, with difference burnt in the fire of endless praise — from the rushing start of varied self-swelling shining glory, drinking things — O giver — mad-intoxicated am I, blissful.
The Sanskrit: *vidhunvāno bandhābhimatabhavamārgasthitim | madamatto 'smi sukhitaḥ*

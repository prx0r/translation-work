# Kramastotra kram-p07 (stripped fire, praised rays) — L2 READ

## v.7
Flame-formed, gloriously ripening: burning displayed, the other of fire shed — what body at all holds? Indeed none. I praise the rays of knowing: if each-own by that praised, none else at all becomes the Lord's power.
The Sanskrit: *jvaladrūpaṃ bhāsvatpacanam atha dāhaṃ prakaṭanam | stuve saṃvidraśmīn yadi nijanijāṃs tena sa nuto*

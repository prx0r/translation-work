# Kramastotra kram-p08 (melted rule, priceless taste) — L2 READ

## v.8
In varied beginningness, in melted rule: what taste — from partitionlessness, supreme fullness unequalled? The self's yoking of lights, all-being-thing-oneness, by unopposed dharma-floods: supreme knowing, priceless-fit-qualified.
The Sanskrit: *vicitrārambhatve galitaniyame yaḥ kila rasaḥ | sakalabhavabhāvaikamayatāviruddhair dharmaughaiḥ paracitir anarghocitaguṇā*

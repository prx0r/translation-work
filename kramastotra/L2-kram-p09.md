# Kramastotra kram-p09 (seen, vowed) — L2 READ

## v.9
Thus in such forms, giver, varied indeed Your form shines — in own portion, in this world, difference gone, of the Lord. To praise that same, may heart, and songs of outer speech-instruments, be mine constantly, in unabandoned rush.
The Sanskrit: *vividhaṃ te kila vapur vibhāti svāṃśe | tad evaitatstotuṃ hṛdayam atha gīrbāhyakaraṇaprabandhāś*

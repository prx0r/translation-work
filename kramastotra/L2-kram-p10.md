# Kramastotra kram-p10 (ocean within, lights at once) — L2 READ

## v.10
Within You-alone-one, of throbbing glory, of the knowledge-ocean, the taste of varied wave-masses spreading by own taste — they indeed, these creation-keeping-dissolving-formed ones, of moon-sun-fires' clash-gleams, at once are rise-and-set powers.
The Sanskrit: *tavaivaikasyāntaḥ sphuritamahaso | sṛṣṭisthitilayamaya sphūrjitarucāṃ śaśāṅkārkāgnīnāṃ yugapad udayāpāyavibhavāḥ*

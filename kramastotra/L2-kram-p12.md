# Kramastotra kram-p12 (Whole leads, fear melts) — L2 READ

## v.12
From that complete Whole — oh, juice of great-joy-taste — You lead Your own power into difference, by Your will's spreading. Priceless Your freedom — praising this, Your utterly wonderful becoming-power, fear melted, I am Śiva-made.
The Sanskrit: *nijāṃ śaktiṃ bhedaṃ gamayasi nijecchāprasarataḥ | anarghaṃ svātantryaṃ tava*

# Kramastotra kram-p13 (thisness, melted time, standing) — L2 READ

## v.13
Thisness-just Your form, O Lord, power-juiced — by sequencelessness itself forcibly melted is time-reckoning. By mind-power, by speech too, then by sense-wheels outside, then by pot and the rest — may I stand that form at once, unceasing.
The Sanskrit: *idantāvad rūpaṃ tava bhagavataḥ śaktisarasaṃ | tadrūpaṃ yugapad adhitiṣṭheyam aniśam*

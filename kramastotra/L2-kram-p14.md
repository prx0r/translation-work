# Kramastotra kram-p14 (shown, prayed triple) — L2 READ

## v.14
Making sequence-swelling on that ground, measuring difference of own powers — O god, You show always in own self. Then, named doing-knowing-willing, maintenance-dissolution-great-emission-powered, three-formed — may I become, for overlying, eager-hearted.
The Sanskrit: *kramollāsaṃ tasyāṃ bhuvi viracayan | trirūpaṃ bhūyāsaṃ samadhiśayituṃ vyagrahṛdayaḥ*

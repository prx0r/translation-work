# Kramastotra kram-p16 (rush, red, unloathing) — L2 READ

## v.16
In the emitted thing-share, outside, in extreme taste-bliss — when there indeed You resort by rush to redness-made — then, red goddess, Yours in all things — make me, by red-drinking-order-joined conclave, past loathing.
The Sanskrit: *yadā tatraiva tvaṃ bhajasi rabhasād raktimayatām | kriyād raktāpānakramaghaṭitagoṣṭhīgataghṛṇam*

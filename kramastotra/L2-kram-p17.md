# Kramastotra kram-p17 (quit, dwell, grasp) — L2 READ

## v.17
To leave outer activity, to dwell in noble consciousness-ground — when You show thing-nondifference, waves gone fickle — the goddess grasps maintenance's destruction. Then may she, Yours, Lord, grasp ever my wandering state's undoing.
The Sanskrit: *bahir vṛttiṃ hātuṃ citibhuvam udārāṃ nivasituṃ | sthiteḥ sāṃsārikyāḥ kalayatu vināśaṃ mama sadā*

# Kramastotra kram-p18 (ruled, split, ground) — L2 READ

## v.18
Wishing calm by world-dissolution, by own rush — the rule named own-doubt-fear, then the prohibition, You show. Having emitted Yama, You split doubt again and again — may the great goddess grind my world-fear.
The Sanskrit: *jagatsaṃhāreṇa praśamayitukāmaḥ | mahādevī seyaṃ mama bhavabhayaṃ saṃdalayatām*

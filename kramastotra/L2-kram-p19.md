# Kramastotra kram-p19 (melted all, hunger, strike) — L2 READ

## v.19
In melted doubt-heap, at once in full glory, with world-conduct gone, melted glory, scripture-rule gone — to swallow the endless enjoyable flood all around, greedy-juiced — O Lord named dissolution — strike my heart's split portion.
The Sanskrit: *vilīne śaṅkaughe sapadi paripūrṇe ca vibhave | saṃhārākhyā mama hṛdi bhidāṃśaṃ praharatu*

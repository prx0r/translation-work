# Kramastotra kram-p20 (split, shone, named) — L2 READ

## v.20
So by goddesses at once, in split difference-glory, thought-life-one shines forth — knower-standing, enough. Hence in own heart considered dissolution-share, standing-formed, gracious may she be — my death-dissolution-maker, blessed Lady.
The Sanskrit: *taditthaṃ devībhiḥ sapadi dalite bhedavibhave | mṛtyupralayakaraṇī me bhagavatī*

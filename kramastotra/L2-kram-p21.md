# Kramastotra kram-p21 (three ordain, fear gone) — L2 READ

## v.21
So thus your three, by own-power-expanse-force obtained — six-wheel-course-made-step powers these. By order, by unfolding, they ordain varied world-state; by these goddesses, devoted-hearted may I be, fear-gone.
The Sanskrit: *taditthaṃ te tisro nijavibhavavisphāraṇavaśād | kramād unmeṣeṇa pravidadhati citrāṃ bhavadaśām*

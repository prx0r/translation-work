# Kramastotra kram-p22 (held dread, sporting pleasure) — L2 READ

## v.22
This ground she holds — world-fear-splitting-dread-making; this wake-one-pointed-melted-taste-formed — ordained too. So holding, melting, wiping inauspicious stretches, as-pleased conduct resorted — may she sport in my heart.
The Sanskrit: *imāṃ rundhe bhūmiṃ bhavabhayabhidātaṅkakaraṇīm | bhajati lasatāṃ sā mama hṛdi*

# Kramastotra kram-p23 (sapped hard, thinned clean) — L2 READ

## v.23
Of doing-mind-loss and the rest, on the limited step, the measurer's step of the gained — its spread contracting by own-own gleam — she who draws the all-sap of the station-yoked day-sun. Drawing forcibly — may she thin my difference from world-fear.
The Sanskrit: *sphāraṃ nijanijarucā saṃharati yā | iyaṃ mārta.ṅdasya sthitipadayujaḥ sāram akhilam*

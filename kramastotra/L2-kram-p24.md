# Kramastotra kram-p24 (entered, adorned) — L2 READ

## v.24
Whole garland, sequenceless, in self again entered — endless-inner-massed-great-ray-heaped. Supreme, divine-bliss to grasp, noble-eager, gracious to me may she be — heart-path let her adorn.
The Sanskrit: *samagrām akṣālīṃ kramavirahitā sātmani muhur | prasannā me bhūyāt hṛdayapadavīṃ bhūṣayatu ca*

# Kramastotra kram-p25 (merged, met, shone) — L2 READ

## v.25
In proof merged, by Śiva-step-shining-glory-force — body-breath-made, measured-made-knower-standing-formed. When time's cadre reaches Your dissolution-step — then the goddess who shines, mine — she would be Śiva-made.
The Sanskrit: *pramāṇe saṃlīne śivapadalasadvaibhavavaśāt | tadā devī yāsau lasati mama sā syāc chivamayī*

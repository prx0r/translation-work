# Kramastotra kram-p26 (spread cover, made time) — L2 READ

## v.26
Manifestation-named consciousness, sequenceless, from void-step, outside-merged — spreads utterly as covering. Then too in inner-essence, by melted rush, sequencelessly — great Time-goddess, making all time.
The Sanskrit: *prakāśākhyā saṃvit kramavirahitā | mahākālī seyaṃ mama kalayatāṃ kālam akhilam*

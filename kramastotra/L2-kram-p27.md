# Kramastotra kram-p27 (cut, embraced, resort) — L2 READ

## v.27
Then in the goddess in whom supreme-full-standing-enjoying — sequence cut, quickly, standing-minded-taste reflecting — proof, knower, measure, then whole world this standing embraced — may my mind resort to this consciousness.
The Sanskrit: *kramaṃ vicchidyāśu sthitimatirasāt saṃvidadhati | śrayatu mama cittaṃ citim imām*

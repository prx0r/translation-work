# Kramastotra kram-p28 (abiding seen, churn bowed) — L2 READ

## v.28
Unobstructed, in self-made Lord, in great Lord — abide, pervading powers. That power-possessor, I venerate — god, churn-named, world-one-essence.
The Sanskrit: *anargala svātmamaye maheśe tiṣṭhanti | taṃ śaktimantaṃ praṇamāmi devaṃ manthānasaṃjñaṃ jagadekasāram*

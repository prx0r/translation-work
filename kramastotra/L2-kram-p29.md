# Kramastotra kram-p29 (heard, graced, allayed) — L2 READ

## v.29
Thus own-power-ray-mass-praise-bonds heard — Lord, if You come to grace — by that quickly all folk, by own-rule-rays, darkness-masses allayed — make ye.
The Sanskrit: *itthaṃ svaśaktikiraṇaughanutiprabandhān ākarṇya | nijaśāsanāṃśu saṃśāntitākhilatamaḥpaṭalāṃ vidheyāḥ*

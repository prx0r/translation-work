## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 28:**
> itthaṃ svaśaktikiraṇaughanutiprabandhān ākarṇya deva yadi me vrajasi prasādam | tenāśu sarvajanatāṃ nijaśāsanāṃśusaṃśāntitākhilatamaḥpaṭalāṃ vidheyāḥ

[i.e. then-goddess-in-which-supreme-fullness-standing-power-possessor-sequence-cutting-quickly-standing-matter-rasa-makes-measure-mother-whole-world-this-standing-embracing-rest-my-mind-consciousness-this]

The Kramastotra maps the "sequence" (krama) of cosmological descent and ascent through the tattvas. Each verse is a step in the sequence: the devotee's recognition that all is Śiva, that the world is his self-manifestation, and that the sequence (krama) is the path of recognition (pratyabhijñā). The tone is devotional-Krama: fierce, esoteric, with Śākta overtones. The stotra is about the "sequence" (krama) — not just cosmological but soteriological: the sequence of recognition.

# C1 — kram-p01 (vow, mirroring, fear gone)

## SUMMARY

Amid sorrow's host, the finishing rite in spreading bliss-taste: by mutual doing the mirrored mind drops fear.

## KEY TERMS

pāraṇavidhi (completion-rite) · pratiphalati (mirrors-back) · gatabhaya (fear-gone)

## rasa

Exultation: fear mirrored away.

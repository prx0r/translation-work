# C1 — kram-p12 (lead, melt)

## SUMMARY

The complete Whole leads its own power into difference by will; priceless freedom praised melts fear into Śiva-made.

## KEY TERMS

amuṣmāt-saṃpūrṇāt (from-complete-Whole) · nijecchāprasara (will-spreading) · anargha (priceless) · vigalitabhaya (fear-melted)

## rasa

Descent relished, fear melted.

# C1 — kram-p13 (form, melt, stand)

## SUMMARY

Your form is very thisness, power-juiced; sequencelessness melts time-reckoning; every instrument at once.

## KEY TERMS

idantāvat (thisness-just) · kramābhāva (sequencelessness) · kālakalana (time-reckoning) · yugapad-aniśam (at-once-unceasing)

## rasa

Standing relished.

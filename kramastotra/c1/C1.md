# C1 — Kramastotra

## kram-p01–p05 — the hymn's purpose and the self's nature
**SUMMARY.** This hymn is the course of great ease: in the midst of the host of sorrows, the rite of completion, the essence of great bliss flowing, this inauspicious day. Constantly, on the worshipper's conduct, reflecting, it reflects back; the mind, fear-gone, in the hymn. Examining own self, examining again the hymned conduct, the worshipper in hymn-practice without effort; the examined self, in all-knowable-moment, that thus in hymn I, here, always, without effort. Ungrasped, own self is never knower of thing-knowledge; ungrasped, own self indeed is not. Śiva is that self, shining, all-thing-one-sap; hence I in hymn with devoted heart always blissful. By varied birth-etc.-wandering-course-garments, omniscience gained in heart; that inside, teaching-spread-course-become light, clearly in speech obtained, now I hymn without effort. Shaking off bond-abiding-conceit, enjoying varied things, by great fire of hymn-hutāśana, burned difference; varied self-splendor-rush, drinking things, varada, maddened, blissful. The hymn's purpose: by self-examination and hymn-practice, fear is removed, omniscience gained, difference burned, and bliss arises.

**FUNCTION.** Opens the stotra: establishes the hymn's purpose (fear-removal, omniscience, bliss) and the self's nature (Śiva = all-thing-one-sap).

**KEY TERMS.** mahāsaukhya (great ease), bhayagata (fear-gone), sārvajña (omniscience), āmarśanavidhi (grasping-course), akhilabhāvaikasarasa (all-thing-one-sap), hutāśana (fire), madamatta (maddened).



**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are self-contained; stotra builds cumulatively.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), Anuttarāṣṭikā, spans TS1.1–1.5.

## kram-p06–p10 — Śiva-oneness and the first means
**SUMMARY.** If Śiva-oneness-gained wish, how hymn? If conduct, by that suddenly Śiva-oneness-first-means. If Śiva-oneness-gained, conduct-abandoning brings expiation. Vivid flame-formed, shining, burning, then displaying — shedding other of fire, what body indeed is possible? I praise consciousness-rays; if by own-own, by that praised, no other whatsoever becomes Lord's power. Varied-beginning, in galita-niyama, what rasa, from partlessness fullness unsurpassed unequal; self's light-yoga with all-thing-one-mayatā, by uncontrary dharmas, other-cit anargha-guṇā. By such forms, varada, varied your body shines; in own-portion this world, difference-gone, Lord's. That same this hymn-to, heart and speech-outer-instrument-bonds would be mine, constant, unabandoned-rush. Your alone one's inside, shining-mahat-glory, knowledge-ocean's wave-spread rasa, which by-nature; they same these, creation-maintenance-dissolution-formed, simultaneous-shining moon-sun-fire's rise-setting-power-glory. Hymn is the first means to Śiva-oneness; abandoning it brings expiation.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes hymn as first means to Śiva-oneness; abandoning = expiation; consciousness-rays praise.

**KEY TERMS.** śivaikātmya (Śiva-oneness), prāyaścitta (expiation), jvaladrūpa (flame-formed), hutāśana (fire), anargha (invaluable), sārvajña (omniscience), vibhūti (glory).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p01–p05 (purpose/self); p06–p10 establish Śiva-oneness.


**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS11.1–11.7.

## kram-p11–p15 — the ground, the course, and the body
**SUMMARY.** Hence varied-varied-course-station-other-distinction-etc.-standing-thing-enjoying-power, Lord's power, always goes, no difference how. That in this ground (akula), Bhairava, your pada — that one-pointed become my heart-ground, Bhairava Lord. In measure, dissolved, Śiva-pada-shining-power-vaibhava-force, body-breath-and-rest limited-made-agentness-standing; when time-condition, dissolution-state approaches you — then goddess, which, shining, my, she be Śiva-made. Course-rush of it, in earth, making difference-grasping, own-powers, Lord, teach always in own-self; then three-formed (knowledge/action/will) standing-maintenance-dissolution-great-creation-power, become-immersed wishful heart. Formerly creation dissolved, fire-formed, which there would shine, beyond-rush-facing, moon-touch-lovely; fire-moon-splendor-both-glory, Bhairava Lord, this creation-named, my mind, always shining. The ground (akula) is where Bhairava's pada becomes one-pointed; the course runs through earth making difference-grasping; the three-formed power (knowledge/action/will) gives standing/maintenance/dissolution/creation; fire-moon-splendor both shine.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes the ground (akula), the course through earth, and the three-formed power.

**KEY TERMS.** akula (ground, un-qualified), pada (foot/station), vaibhava (power-glory), trirūpa (three-formed), kriyājñānecchā (knowledge/action/will), sṛṣṭisthiti (creation/maintenance).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p06–p10 (Śiva-oneness); p11–p15 establish ground and course.


**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS11.1–11.15.

## kram-p16–p20 — dissolution and the three powers
**SUMMARY.** Released, things-portion, outside extreme tasteless, when there you, by rush, raktimayatā; then red goddess, your all-things, indeed me, by action-raktāpāna-course-made gathering, make red. Outside-conduct abandoning, consciousness-ground dwelling, to dwell, when things-difference showing, waveless, stability-lost; of maintenance dissolution, goddess, your, maintenance-of-saṃsāra, my, always dissolve. World-dissolution wishing, by own-rush, own-doubt-fear-named procedure then prohibition teaching; Yama created thus, then again doubt dissolving, great-goddess, that my world-fear she dissolve. Dissolved doubt-mass, instantly, full, Lord, power, all-knowable-host swallowing, by enjoyment-rush, Bhairava, dissolution-named, my heart, difference-portion, strike. Thus by goddesses, instantly, difference-power melted, thought-life-breath, shining, mother-standing, enough; hence dissolution-portion, own-heart reflected, standing, blissful, she be, goddess, my, death-dissolution-maker. The red goddess makes red; maintenance-dissolution goddess dissolves saṃsāra; world-fear dissolved by Yama-creation/doubt-dissolution; doubt-mass struck; goddesses melt difference-power; death-dissolution-maker.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes the three goddesses (red/maintenance-dissolution/world-fear) and their functions.

**KEY TERMS.** raktā devī (red goddess), raktimayatā (redness), saṃsāra-maintenance, Yama (death), saṃhāra (dissolution), mṛtyupralayakaraṇī (death-dissolution-maker).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p11–p15 (ground/course); p16–p20 establish the three goddesses.


**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS11.1–11.20.

## kram-p21–p25 — binding and the three forms
**SUMMARY.** Thus these three, by own-power-ray-mass-expansion, obtained, six-wheel-course-made, powers these; gradually by unfolding, varied world-state making; from these, goddesses, devoted heart, I be, fear-gone. This earth I bind, world-fear-difference-torment-making; this knowledge-only-course-made, quickly, fluid-rasa-full, this too I make; thus binding, dissolution, conduct, as-wished, lasat, my heart. Action-knowledge-sense-etc. limited-place measure gained, its spread, by own-own-taste, contracting, which; this sun's stability-place-yoked sap all, forcibly contracting, make my difference, world-fear-from. Whole, unmeasured, in self, again-and-again, mass-full-mahāraśmi-nivaha; to make parā divine-bliss, by generous, rest, heart-place beautify. In measure, dissolved, Śiva-pada-shining-power-vaibhava-force, body-breath-and-rest limited-made-agentness-standing; when time-condition, dissolution-state approaches you — then goddess, which, shining, my, she be Śiva-made. The three powers (goddesses) bind earth, contract difference, and make parā-bliss; when time approaches, Śiva-made goddess shines.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes the binding action of the three powers and the three forms.

**KEY TERMS.** saṃrodha (binding), lasat (as-wished), mārtaṇḍa (sun), mahāraśmi (great ray), vaibhava (power-glory), kālopādhi (time-condition).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p16–p20 (three goddesses); p21–p25 establish binding.


**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A06 (sthāna/kāla), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS11.1–11.25.

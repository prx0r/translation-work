papraccheśaṃ parānandaṃ pārvatī parameśvaram

sarvajña bhaktisulabha śaraṇāgatavatsala

asāre ghorasaṃsāre sarvaduḥkhamalīmasāḥ

jāyante ca mirayante ca teṣāṃ mokṣo na vidyate

kenopāyena deveśa mucyate vada me prabho

tasya śravaṇamātreṇa saṃsārād mucyate naraḥ

sarvajñaḥ sarvakarttā ca sarveśo nirmalo'dvayaḥ

nirguṇaḥ saccidānandastadaṃśā jīvasaṃjñakāḥ

garbhādyupādhisaṃbhinnāḥ karmabhiḥ karaṇādibhiḥ

tattajjātiyutaṃ deham āyurbhogañca karmajam

sūkṣmaliṅgaśarīrantadāmokṣādakṣayaṃ priye

dhāmīkāstridaśāstadvanmokṣiṇaśca yathākramam

sukṛtānmānavo bhūtvā jñānī cenmokṣamāpnuyāt

na mānuṣyaṃ vinā yatra tattvajñānaṃ tu labhyate

kadācillabhate janturmānuṣyaṃ puṇyasañcayāt

yastārayati nātmānaṃ tasmāt pāpataro'tra kaḥ

na vettyātmahitaṃ yastu sa bhavet ātmaghātakaḥ

tasmāddehadhanaṃ prāpya puṇyakarmāṇi sādhayet

rakṣaṇe yatnamātiṣṭhet yāvattattvaṃ na paśyati

punaḥ śubhāśubhaṃ karma na śarīraṃ punaḥ punaḥ

nahīcchanti tanutyāgamapi kuṣṭhādirogataḥ

jñānañca dhyānayogārthaṃ so'cirāt parimucyate

ko'nyo hitakarastasmādātmānaṃ tārayiṣyati

gatvā nirauṣadhaṃ sthānaṃ vyādhisthaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati

yāvattiṣṭhati deho'yaṃ tāvattattvaṃ samabhyaset

nighnanti ripuvadrogāstasmācchreyaḥ samācaret

yāvannendriyavaikalyaṃ tāvacchreyaḥ samācaret

sukhaduḥkharato janturna vetti hitamātmanaḥ

loko mohasurāṃ pītvā na vibheti kadācana

taḍiccañcalamāyuśca kasya syājjagato dhṛtiḥ

bālyarogajarāduḥkhairarddhaṃ tadapi niṣphalam

viśvastavyo bhayasthāne ghātakaiḥ kiṃ na hanyate

anitye'priyasaṃsāre kathaṃ tiṣṭhanti nirbhayāḥ

anarthaṃ cārthavijñānī svamṛtyuṃ yo na vetti ca

paṭhannapi na jānāti tava māyāvimohitaḥ

mṛtyurogajarāgrāhe na kiñcidapi budhyati

āmakumbha ivāmbhaḥstho viśīrṇo naiva bhāvyate

grathanañca taraṅgāṇāmāsthā nāyuṣi yujyate

śuṣyate sāgarajalaṃ śarīre devi kā kathā

lapantamiti marttyaṃ hi hanti kālavṛko balāt

evamīhāsamāyuktaṃ mṛtyuratti janaṃ priye

na hi pratīkṣate mṛtyuḥ kṛtaṃ vā'sya na vā kṛtam

mṛtyuśatrumabhijño'si āyāntaṃ kiṃ na paśyasi

rāgadveṣānale pakvaṃ mṛtyuraśnāti mānavam

sarvāṃśca hiṃsate mṛtyurevambhūtamidaṃ jagat

nāśamevānudhāvanti tasmācchreyaḥ samācaret

parastrīdhanalobhācca nṝṇāmāyuḥkṣayo bhavet

nṝṇāmāyuḥkṣayo bhūyādindriyāṇāmanigrahāt

maraṇaṃ yena nidrdaṣṭaṃ tena gacchanti jantavaḥ

samprāpya paramaṃśena dehaṃ tyajati pūrvajam

tathā dehāntaraprāptirgṛhādgṛhamivāgataḥ

paratrājñānino devi yāntyāyānti punaḥ punaḥ

siktamūlasya vṛkṣasya phalaṃ śākhāsu dṛśyate

ātmāparādhavṛkṣasya phalānyetāni dehinām

saṅgācca calate jñānī cāvaśyaṃ kimutālpavit

sadbhaḥ saha sa karttavyaḥ satāṃ saṅgo hi bheṣajam

yasya nāsti naraḥ so'ndhaḥ kathaṃ na syādamārgagaḥ

tāvanto'sya viśantyete hṛdaye śokaśaṅkavaḥ

strīmātṛpitṛputrādisambandhaḥ kena hetunā

tasya tyāgaḥ kṛto yena sa sukhī nāparaḥ priye

ālayaṃ sarvapāpānāṃ saṃsāraṃ varjayet priye

aśastrakhaṇḍanaṃ devi saṃsārāsaktacetasām

tasmāt santyajya saṃsāraṃ tattvaniṣṭhaḥ sukhī bhavet

strīdhanādiṣu saṃsakto mucyate na kadācana

apakvakumbhajalavat naśyantyaṅgena kevalam

hā hanta viṣayāhārairdehasthendriyataskaraiḥ

sukhalubdhastathā dehī yamabādhāṃ na paśyati

kukṣipūraṇaniṣṭhā ye te'budhā nārakāḥ priye

jñānavān mānavaḥ prokto jñānahīnaḥ paśuḥ priye

rātrau madananidrābhyāṃ bādhyante mānavāḥ priye

jāyante ca mirayante ca hā hantājñānamohitāḥ

na jānanti paraṃ tattvaṃ mūḍhā naśyanti pārvati

ajñānasaṃyutātmānaḥ sañcaranti pratārakāḥ

mantroccāraṇahomādyairbhrāmitāḥ kratuvistaraiḥ

mūḍhaḥ parokṣamicchanti tava māyāvimohitāḥ

valmīkatāḍanāddevi mṛtaḥ kinnu mahoragaḥ

bhramanti jñānivalloke bhrāmayanti janānapi

karmabrahmobhayabhraṣṭaṃ taṃ tyajedantyajaṃ yathā

caranti gardabhādyāśca yoginaste bhavanti kim

mṛdbhasmavāsino grāmyāḥ kiṃ te muktā bhavanti hi

hiraṇādimṛgā devi yoginaste bhavanti kim

maṇḍūkamatsyapramukhā vratinaste bhavanti kim

janānāṃ purato devi vibudhāḥ kiṃ bhavanti hi

na pibanti mahītoyaṃ yoginaste bhavanti kim

tiṣṭhanti śūkarādyāśca yoginaste bhavanti kim

mokṣasya kāraṇaṃ sākṣāttattvajñānaṃ kuleśvari

paramārthaṃ na jānanti paśupāśāniyantritāḥ

kālomīgrāhagrastāśca tiṣṭhanti hi kutākīkāḥ

viḍambakasya tasyāpi tat sarvaṃ kākabhāṣitam

paṭhantyahanīśaṃ devi paratattvaparāṅmukhaḥ

cintayā duḥkhitā mūḍhāstiṣṭhanti vyākulendriyāḥ

anyathā śāstrasadbhāvo vyākhyāṃ kurvanti cānyathā

ahaṅkārahatāḥ kecidupadeśavivajītāḥ

na jānanti paraṃ tattvaṃ darvī pākarasaṃ yathā

paṭhanti vedaśāstrāṇi durlabho bhāvavedakaḥ

gopaḥ kakṣagataṃ chāgaṃ kūpe paśyati durmatiḥ

na nivartteta timiraṃ kadāciddīpavārttayā

devi prajñāvataḥ śāstraṃ tattvajñānasya kāraṇam

sadvidyādānaśūrādyairguṇaivīkhyātamānavāḥ

evaṃ ye śāstrasammūḍhāste dūrasthā na saṃśayaḥ

devi varṣasahasrāyuḥ śāstrāntaṃ naiva gacchati

tasmāt sāraṃ vijānīyāt kṣīraṃ haṃsa ivāmbhasaḥ

palālamiva dhānyārthī sarvaśāstraṃ parityajet

tattvajñasya tathā devi na śāstreṇa prayojanam

jñānādeva hi muktiḥ syānnānyathā vīravandite

tathaiva sarvaśāstrāṇi jñānameva hi kāraṇam

kāṣṭhabhāraśramādasmādekaṃ sañjīvanaṃ param

guruvaktreṇa labhyeta nānyathāgamakoṭibhiḥ

śabdabrahmāgamamayaṃ paraṃ brahma vivekajam

mama tattvaṃ ca jānanti dvaitādvaitavivajītam

mameti bādhyate janturna mameti vimucyate

āyāsāyāparaṃ karma vidyānyā śilpanaipuṇam

yāvadindriyacāpalyaṃ tāvattattvakathā kutaḥ

yāvanna manasaḥ sthairyaṃ tāvattattvakathā kutaḥ

yāvanna gurukāruṇyaṃ tāvattattvakathā kutaḥ

vedaśāstrāgamakathā yāvattattvaṃ na vindate

tattvaniṣṭho bhaveddevi yadīcchenmokṣamātmanaḥ

tāpatrayāttīsantaptaśchāyāṃ mokṣataroḥ śrayet

kuladharmamṛte muktarnāsti satyaṃ na saṃśayaḥ

sukhena mucyate devi ghorasaṃsārabandhanāt

samāsena kuleśāni kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi

sthitikathanaṃ nāma prathamollāsaḥ

kuladharmastvayā deva sūcito na prakāśitaḥ

vada me parameśāna yadi te'sti kṛpā mayi

tasya śravaṇamātreṇa yoginīnāṃ priyo bhavet

kathayāmi tava snehāt śṛṇuṣvaikāgramānasā

akathyaṃ paramārthena tathāpi kathayāmi te

deyaṃ bhaktāya śiṣyāya anyathā patanaṃ bhavet

vaiṣṇavāduttamaṃ śaivaṃ śaivāddakṣiṇamuttamam

siddhāntāduttamaṃ kaulaṃ kaulāt parataraṃ na hi

sākṣāt śivapradaṃ devi karṇākaṇīgataṃ kulam

sārajñena mayā devi kuladharmaḥ samuddhṛtaḥ

ekataḥ kuladharmaśca tatra kaulo'dhikaḥ priye

tathaiva vividhā dharmāḥ praviṣṭāḥ kulameva hi

darśanāni ca sarvāṇi kula eva tathā priye

tadā ca kuladharmeṇa samayo'nyaḥ samo bhavet

tathaiva samayāḥ sarve kuladharmeṇa no samāḥ

tathānyasamayasyāpi kulasya mahadantaram

kulena samadharmastu tathāpi na kadācana

baddhaḥ saṃsārapāśena so'ntyajānāṃ priyo bhavet

brahmahatyādhikaṃ pāpaṃ sa prāpnoti na saṃśayaḥ

svargādi dvīpāntaraṃ gatvā mokṣaratnaṃ samaśnute

mokṣaṃ labhante kaule tu sadya eva na saṃśayaḥ

na kaulasamadharmo'sti tvāṃ śape kulanāyike

bhogayogātmakaṃ kaulaṃ tasmāt sarvādhikaṃ priye

mokṣāyate ca saṃsāraḥ kuladharme kuleśvari

kuladharmaparā devi mānuṣeṣu ca kā kathā

kulameva vijānīyādyadīcchet siddhimātmanaḥ

svapnotthita pratyayavadupadeśādikaṃ vinā

tāmeva labhate janturupadeśo nirarthakaḥ

mantraivīśuddhacittasya kulajñānaṃ prakāśate

kuladharmasthitā ye ca te sarve'pyanivarttakāḥ

kṣīṇāṃhasāṃ nṛṇāṃ devi kulajñānaṃ prakāśate

devatāgurubhaktyā ca kulajñānaṃ prakāśate

atibhaktasya guhyasya kulajñānaṃ prakāśate

yasya bhaktirdṛḍhā tasya kulajñānaṃ prakāśate

gurvājñāpālakairdharmaiḥ kulajñānamavāpyate

tasmāt parīkṣya vaktavyaṃ kulajñānaṃ mayoditam

ājñābhaṅgañca yaḥ kuryāddevatāśāpamāpnuyāt

sa guruścāpi śiṣyaśca yoginīnāṃ bhavet paśuḥ

labhete tāvubhau sākṣādyoginīvīramelanam

kuladharmamimaṃ jñātvā mucyate nātra saṃśayaḥ

acirānnātra sandehastasmāt kaulaṃ samāśrayet

svagṛhe pāyasaṃ tyaktvā bhikṣāmaṭati pārvati

karasthaṃ ratnamutsṛjya dūrasthaṃ kācamīhate

sa dhānyarāśimutsṛjya pāṃsurāśiṃ jighṛkṣati

taḍāgādiva tṛṣṇārtto mṛgatṛṣṇāṃ pradhāvati

śrīkaulādanyasamayāstādṛśāḥ kulanāyike

pārāvāramapāraṃ sa pāṇibhyāṃ tarttumicchati

svapnalabdhadhanenaiva dhanavān sa bhavettadā

tathānyasamayebhyaśca bhuktirmuktiḥ prakāśate

kulaniṣṭhaḥ kuleśāni bhuktimuktyoḥ sa bhājanam

so'pi sadgatimāpnoti kimutāsya parāyaṇaḥ

pūjitaḥ pūjayatyāśu tasmāttaṃ na parityajet

janā hasantu māṃ dṛṣṭvā rājāno daṇḍayantu vā

tvaddharma naiva muñcāmi manovākkāyakarmabhiḥ

sa tu sampūjyate devairamutra sa śivo bhavet

yastvāmupāste bhaktyā sa naraḥ sadgatimāpnuyāt

mṛtiradya yugānte vā kulaṃ naiva parityajet

na kāmanna bhayādvāpi kuladharmaṃ parityajet

kliśyate jātamātreṇa bhūtāriṇātmaśatruṇā

budbudā iva toyeṣu ye kaulavimukhā hi te

sa jīvati mano yasya kuladharme vyavasthitam

sa lohakārabhasrevaśvasannapi na jīvati

kuleśvari kulājñasya tat paśoriva jīvitam

vratastho'pyavratastho vā yaḥ kaulavimukho janaḥ

kuladharmaparā devi śeṣāśca dvāragardabhāḥ

aparastu paraṃ satyamasthikūṭatvacāvṛtaḥ

śvapaco'pi kulajñānī brāhmaṇādatiricyate

kulapūjārato devi so'yaṃ kaulo na cetareḥ

na pūjayati dhik tasya tat kākasyeva jīvitam

yeṣāṃ bhāgyavaśāddevi kulajñānaṃ prakāśate

yeṣāmutpadyate citte kulajñānaṃ mayoditam

yat sarvayajñācaraṇaṃ kuladharmapraveśanam

te punarjananīgarbha na viśanti kadācana

kulajñānasya kuleśāni nānyadharmaiḥ prayojanam

dadāmi paramaṃ jñānaṃ cāntakāle na saṃśayaḥ

sukhena sarvaphaladaṃ kulaṃ ko'pi tyajatyaho

vedaśāstrāgamajño'pi kulājñastvajña eva hi

cakorā eva jānanti nānye candragatāṃ rucim

svalpā nadyo vivarddhante jyotsnayā kiṃ samudravat

bhṛṅgāḥ puṣpāntaraṃ lubdhā mandārāmodasevinaḥ

śivaḥ śirasi dhatte'bjaṃ saiṃhikeyā gilatyaho

jalamiśrapayaḥpānaṃ bakaḥ kiṃ vetti haṃsavat

tasmāt sarvādhikaṃ kaulaṃ sarvasādhāraṇaṃ katham

teṣu bhedantu yaḥ kuryānmamāṅgaṃ cchedayettu saḥ

tasmādvedātmakaṃ śāstraṃ viddhi kaulātmakaṃ priye

bhuktimuktipradaṃ nṛṇāṃ kule'smin daivataṃ priye

kulaṃ pramāṇatāṃ yāti pratyakṣaphaladaṃ yataḥ

upalabdhibalāttasya hatāḥ sarve kutākīkāḥ

yadvā pratyakṣaphaladaṃ tadevottamadarśanam

iti matvā maheśāni mayā kaulaṃ vigahītam

paśunāmanabhijñānāṃ kuladharmo vigahītaḥ

na tasya gurukāruṇyaṃ kulajñānañca jāyate

tathā kulaṃ na jānanti tava mayāvimohitāḥ

bhajante mānavā nityaṃ vṛthāyāsaphalāni ca

mūḍhā nindanti hā hanta matpriyaṃ tava darśanam

kuladharma na jānanti vṛthā jñānābhimāninaḥ

mūrttyantarantu gatvaiva mohanāya durātmanām

teṣāñca sadāgatirnāsti kalpakoṭiśatairapi

vāryamāṇo'pi puṇyātmā kulamevābhilamvate

muniyogīśvarādyāśca susiddhiṃ paramāṃ gatāḥ

ye kaulameva sevante te mahānto'ti durlabhāḥ

durlabho'yaṃ maheśāni kulatattvaviśāradaḥ

divyauṣadhaṃ na sevante mahāvyādhivināśanam

tathaiva janmamaraṇakṛtaṃ sāṃsārikīṃ kriyām

na bhajante kulaṃ dharma bhavavandhavimocanam

mohato mānavāḥ prītyā yācante kulanāyike

tathaiva paśuśāstrāṇi karmapāśaphalāni ca

kuladharma na pṛcchanti bhuktimuktiphalapradam

śārkaraṃ śakarābhrāntyā maṇiṃ kācamanīṣayā

tathā kaulaṃ na jānanti tvatprasādavivajītāḥ

kimajñānapi deveśi mohayedamarānapi

ityevācaraṇaṃ jāpyaṃ pariprāpyaṃ paramrapadam

tvadbhaktā eva jānanti netare bhuktimuktidam

mohayanti janān sarvān svayaṃ pūrvavimohitāḥ

kathaṃbhūto bhavet svāmī sevakāḥ syustathāvidhāḥ

svabudhdyā kalpayantītthaṃ pāramparyavivajītāḥ

madyapānaratāḥ sarve siddhiṃ gacchantu pāmarāḥ

loke māsāśinaḥ sarve puṇyabhājo bhavanti hi

sarve'pi jantavo loke muktāḥ syuḥ strīniṣevanāt

ācārarahitā ye'tra ninditāste na cetare

vicarantyanthā devi mūḍhāḥ paṇḍitamāninaḥ

bhujaṅgadhāraṇānnūnamaśakyaṃ kulavarttanam

tatmahāpātakaṃ jñeyaṃ vedādiṣu nirūpitam

madyaṃ maṃsaṃ paśūnāntu kaulikānāṃ mahāphalam

dvādaśantu mahāmadyaṃ sarveṣāmuttamottamam

tasmādbrāhmaṇarājanyau vaiśya na surāṃ pibet

tatsamāghrāṇamātreṇa prāṇāyāmatrayaṃ caret

ūdrdhva nābhestrirātrantu madyasya sparśane vidhiḥ

mukhe tayā vinirdagdhe tataḥ śuddhimavāpnuyāt

avidhānena yo hanyādātmārthaṃ prāṇinaḥ priye

sa mṛto'pi durācārastirryagyoniṣu jāyate

saṃskartā copaharttā ca khāditā'ṣṭau ca ghātakāḥ

ghātako vadha bandhābhyāṃ ityeṣa trividho vadhaḥ

tasmādavidhinā māṃsaṃ madyaṃ na sevate kvacit

nāśayatyaparijñānāt satyameva varānane

vidhinā gāṃ dvijaṃ vāpi hatyā pāpairna lipyate

jīvanmuktisukhopāyaṃ kulaśāstreṣu gopitam

kulajñe'pyūrdhvavikhyāte jñānaṃ tattadanuttamam

pramāṇāni na sandeho na hantavyāni hetubhiḥ

svādiṣṭhayā madiṣṭhayā kṣīraṃ sapīrmadhūdakam

dīkṣāmupeyādityādyāḥ pramāṇaṃ śrutayaḥ priye

samāsena kulaśāni kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi

kulamāhātmyakathanaṃ nāma dvitīyollāsaḥ

ūrdhvāmnāyañca tanmantraṃ māhātmyaṃ vada me prabho

tasya śravaṇamātreṇa devatā suprasīdati

kathayāmi tava snehādūrdhvāmnāyaṃ śṛṇu priye

śaivaśāktāgamāḥ sarve rahasyāḥ parikīttītāḥ

rahasyātirahasyānāṃ rahasyamidamambike

sugopitaṃ mayā yatnādidānīntu prakāśyate

ūrdhvāmnāyaśca pañcaite mokṣamārgāḥ prakīttītāḥ

satyametadvarārohe nātra kāryā vicāraṇā

asmiṃstantre samākhyātāḥ pūrvaṃ te kulanāyike

ūrdhvāmnāyasya tattvajñā viralā vīravandite

ekaikāmnāyajā mantrā bhuktimuktiphalapradāḥ

mayaiva kathitāste tu lokānugrahakāṅkṣayā

āvayoraṃśasambhūtāḥ samuddiṣṭāḥ śucismite

matprasādena yaḥ kaścidvetti mānavakoṭiṣu

kiṃ punaścaturāmnāyavettā sākṣācchivo bhavet

tasmāttadeva jānīyād yadīcchet siddhimātmanaḥ

ūrdhvaṃ nayatyadhaḥsthañca ūrdhvāmnāya itīritaḥ

ūrdhvalokaikasevyatvādūrdhvāmnāya iti smṛtaḥ

sarvāmnāyādhikaphalamūrdhvāmnāyaṃ parāt param

amnāyeṣu ca sarveṣu ūrdhvāmnāyastathā śive

tīrthānāntu yathā kāśī svarnadī saritāṃ yathā

aśvamedhaḥ kratūnāñca pāṣāṇānāṃ yathā maṇiḥ

catuṣpadāṃ yathā dhenuryathā haṃsastu pakṣiṇām

manuṣyāṇāṃ yathā rājā'vayavānāṃ yathā śiraḥ

tathaiva sarvadharmāṇāmūrdhvāmnāyo'dhikaḥ priye

ūrdhvāmnāyaṃ vijānīyānnānyathā vīravandite

teṣāṃ lakṣeṣu yaḥ kaścidūrdhvāmnāyaṃ pravetti ca

na yajñairna tapobhirvā na tīrthavratakoṭibhiḥ

āmnāyo jñāyate codrdhvaḥ śrīmadgurumukhaṃ vinā

tasmāddeveśi jānīyādūrdhvāmnāyaṃ kuleśvari

labhate kāṅkṣatāṃ siddhiṃ satyaṃ satyaṃ varānane

śāstramārgeṇa sa naro jīvanmukto na saṃśayaḥ

sa sevyaḥ sa ca saṃstutyaḥ sa draṣṭavyaḥ sa sāttvikaḥ

tayoraikyasamutpanna ānando mokṣa ucyate

tasyābhivyañjakaṃ madyaṃ yogibhistena pīyate

kiṃ na paśyati loko'yaṃ brahmaviṣṇumaheśvarān

nirṇītavedaśāstrārtho varadāṃ vāruṇīṃ pibet

jāyate devatābhāvo bhavabandhavimocakaḥ

golambhane tu vaiśyasya śūdrasyāntyeṣṭikarmaṇi

guruṃ smaran pibanmadyaṃ khādan māṃsaṃna doṣabhāk

seveta madhumāṃsāni tṛṣṇayā cet sa pātakī

bhavapāśanivṛttyarthaṃ madhupānaṃ samācaret

prāśayeddevatāprītyai svābhilāṣavivajītaḥ

yāgakālaṃ binānyatra dūṣaṇaṃ kathitaṃ priye

madyapānaṃ tathā kāryaṃ samaye bhogamokṣadam

pañcamudrā niṣeveta cānyathā patito bhaved

vīro'pyatra vṛthāpānī devatāśāpamāpnuyāt

paśupānavidhau pītvā vīro'pi narakaṃ vrajet

yo'smin śāstre pravartteta taṃ tvaṃ pīḍayasi dhruvam

sa mahāpātakī jñeyaḥ sarvadharmabahiṣkṛtaḥ

na siddhayaḥ kulabhraṃśastatsaṃsargaṃ na kārayet

sa siddhimiha nāpnoti paratra na parāṃ gatim

na tasya sadgatiḥ kvāpi tapastīrthavratādibhiḥ

svapriyeṇa hataṃ māṃsaṃ rauravaṃ narakaṃ vrajet

keśasaṃkhyā smṛtā yāvattāvattiṣṭhanti raurave

tadaṅgaromasaṃkhyātaṃ bhūtayoniṣu jāyate

na dhyānaṃ na tapo nārcā na dharmo na ca satkriyā

kevalaṃ viṣayāsaktaḥ patatyeva na saṃśayaḥ

kaulopadeśahīno yaḥ so'kṣayaṃ narakaṃ vrajet

kuleśi brahyaniṣṭho'pi nindyatāmadhigacchati

pīṭhasthānāni cāgatya mahāpadmavanaṃ vrajet

ciccandrakuṇḍalīśaktisāmarasya sukhodayaḥ

sudhāpānamidaṃ proktamitare madyapāyinaḥ

pare layaṃ nayeccitaṃ palāśī sa nigadyate

mattsyāśī sa bhaveddevi śeṣāḥ syuḥ prāṇihiṃsakāḥ

śaktiṃ tāṃ sevayet yastu sa bhavet śaktisevakaḥ

ya āste maithunaṃ tat syādapare strīniṣevakāḥ

jñātvā gurumukhāddevi yaḥ seveta sa mucyate

samāsena kuleśāni kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi

kulamāhātmyakathanaṃ nāma pañcamollāsaḥ

kuladravyādisaṃskāramarcanaṃ vada me prabho

tasya śravaṇamātreṇa stūyate devadānavaiḥ

kulajñānasusampannā bhajante ye dṛḍhavratāḥ

devatāgurubhaktastu niyatātmārcayet priye

gurūpadeśasaṃyuktaḥ pūjayet kulanāyike

paśuvratādivimukhaḥ sumukhastu yajet priye

matprasādena bhūyācca dṛḍhabhaktisamāgamaḥ

gurūpadeśavidhinā cānyathā patanaṃ bhavet

tadahantu tvayā sārdhaṃ gṛhṇāmi svayamādarāt

iti saṃcintya yogīndraḥ kulapūjārato bhavet

yastvāṃ samarcayeddevi bhuktimuktyoḥ sa bhājanam

sukhāsane samāsīnaḥ prāṅmukho vāpyudaṅmukhaḥ

ratnaprākārasandīptaṃ smarenmāṇikyamaṇḍapam

karpūradīpabhāsvantaṃ dhūpāmodasugandhikam

śrīgurorājñayā devi kulapūjāṃ samācaret

yāvanna kurute mantrī tāvaddevārcanaṃ kutaḥ

ṣaḍaṅgādyakhilanyāserātmaśuddhiḥ samīritā

pañcavarṇarajaścitraṃ sthānaśuddhiritīritā

kramotkramād dvirāvṛttyā mantraśuddhiritīritā

darśayeddhenumudrāñca dravyaśuddhiritāritā

trivāraṃ prokṣayedvidvān devaśuddhiritīritā

sā pūjā saphalā proktā cānyathā niṣphalā bhavet

tasmānmaṇḍalamālikhya vidhivattatra pūjayet

trailokyaṃ maṇḍitaṃ yena maṇḍalaṃ tat sadā śivam

jālandharañca candrārdhaṃ tryasraḥ pūrṇagirirbhavet

samānyaśrīgurubhogabalipātrāṇi pañcadhā

svadakṣiṇādivāmāntaṃ sthāpyābhyarcyāsavena tu

tatra māṣapramāṇantu matsyaṃ māṃsaṃ vinikṣepet

hetubhiḥ parapātrasthaistapītaṃ niṣphalaṃ bhavet

manoharairmaheśāni tarpaṇaṃ saphalaṃ bhavet

āyuḥśrīkīttīsaubhāgyadhanadhānyavināśinī

anyathā narakaṃ yāti dātā bhoktā na saṃśayaḥ

ye smaranti narā mūḍhāsteṣāṃ duḥkhaṃ pade pade

parasparavirodhitvāt kathaṃ pūjā vidhīyate

dravyaṃ tarpaṇayogyaṃ syāddevatāprītikārakam

catuvīṃśatimantraiḥ syānmadyañcaiva parāmṛtam

śaśinī candrikā kāntirjyotsnā śrīḥ prītiraṅgadā

saumyāḥ kāmapradāyinyaḥ ṣoḍaśa svarajāḥ kalāḥ

kabhādyā vasudāḥ saurāṣṭhaḍāntā dvādaśeritāḥ

āgneyā yādivarṇādyā daśa dharmapradāḥ kalāḥ

akāraprabhavā brahmajātāḥ syuḥ sṛṣṭaye kalāḥ

ukāraprabhavā viṣṇujātāḥ syuḥ sthitaye kalāḥ

makāraprabhavā rudrajātāḥ saṃhṛtaye kalāḥ

kalāśceśvarasañjātāstirodhānāya bindujāḥ

indhikā dīpikā cāpi recikā mocikā parā

sadāśivabhavā nādādanugrahakalāḥ kramāt

tryambakantu tṛtīyaṃ syāccaturthastatpadādikaḥ

caturnavatimantrātmadevatābhāvasiddhidāḥ

atra ye (te) pañca saṃproktā mantrāste kulanāyike

svacchandasphuraṇāmatra nidhehyakularūpiṇi

amṛtatvaṃ vidhehyasmin vastuni klinnarūpiṇi

bhūtvā parāmṛtākāraṃ mayi citsphuraṇaṃ kuru

sthitiśca pāvakānugrahārthendusamalaṅkṛtā

tathāmṛte padaṃ brūyāttatpaścādamṛtodbhave

amṛteśīmanuḥ proktaḥ pañcatriṃśadbhirakṣaraiḥ

kāmarājaṃ tataḥ klinne kledini kledayeti ca

tārttīyaṃ mokṣaśabdānte kuruyugmaṃ vadettataḥ

dīpanīmanurityuktaḥ sarvasiddhikaraḥ priye

amṛteśīṃ dīpanīñca mūlāmantramapi kramāt

saṃsmṛtyābhyarcya pātrantu pūjayeddhenumudrayā

āpūritaṃ mahāpātraṃ pīyūṣarasamāvaha

nyāsoktasarvamantraiścāpyātmānaṃ pūjayet priye

divyaughe cādināthaśca tacchaktiśca sadāśivaḥ

viṣṇuśca tatpriyā brahmā tatkāntā dvādaśeritāḥ

sanatkumāraśca sanatsujātaśca ṛbhukṣajaḥ

tato vyāsaḥ śukaścaiva ekādaśa samīritāḥ

mahendro mādhavo viṣṇuḥ ṣaḍete ca prakīttītāḥ

mahāśivañca siddhaughe mānavaughe sadāśivam

sarvabhūtahite mātarehyehi parameśvari

yāvattvāṃ pūjayāmīha tāvattvaṃ susthirā bhava

dhyātvā mudrāṃ pradarśyārcet gandhapuṣpākṣatādibhiḥ

sādhakānāṃ hitārthāya brahmaṇo rūpakalpanā

maṇḍale phalake mūdhnī hṛdi vā daśa kīttītāḥ

arūpāṃ rūpiṇīṃ kṛtvā karmakāṇḍaratā narāḥ

tathā sarvagato devaḥ pratimādiṣu rājate

sādhakasya ca viśvāsāt sannidhau devatā bhavet

svakarmaracitaṃ dattaṃ punastāmeva poṣayet

vinā copāsanāṃ devi na dadāti phalaṃ nṛṇām

pratiṣṭhāpyārcayeddevi cānyathā niṣphalaṃ bhavet

kṣamayā sādhayet sarvaṃ hīnamaṅgaṃ padaṃ tathā

na kiñcidapyasya phalaṃ sidhyati kramadoṣataḥ

yathāvidhi kṛtānīha satkarmāṇi phalanti hi

devatāprītidaṃ bhūyād bhuktimuktiphalapradam

kṛtārcanādikāṃ sarvaṃ vyarthaṃ bhavati śāmbhavi

yantre sā pūjitā devi sahasaiva prasīdati

yantramityāhuretasmin devaḥ prīṇāti pūjitaḥ

sarveṣāmapi devānāṃ tathā yantraṃ pratiṣṭhitam

jñātvā gurumukhāt sarvaṃ pūjayedvidhinā priye

aṅgāṅgitvaṃ parityajya devatāśāpamāpnuyāt

yajedāvaraṇopetā devatāstadvidhānataḥ

ubhābhyāṃ labhate pāpaṃ mantrī cañcalamānasaḥ

vidhinābhyarcayet samyagdevatā suprasīdati

pūjayenmūlamantreṇa gandhapuṣpākṣatādibhiḥ

praṇavādinamo'ntena tattannāmnā samarcayet

svapātraspandanisyandaṃ vidhivat kulanāyike

antaḥśaktiṃ samutthāpya tarpayeddehadevatāḥ

anāmāṅguṣṭhayogena tarpayet kulasantatim

kaniṣṭhāṅguṣṭhayogena stambhane tarpayet priye

paṅktitrayakrameṇātha jñātvā samyagananyadhīḥ

mupetāṃ siddhaughairyajatu gurupaṃktiṃ kramagatim

kadalyādiphalānyeva tāmbūlañca samarpayet

dgavyasaṃskāraśuddhyādi kimanyat śrotumicchasi

dravyasaṃskāravidhānakathanaṃ nāma ṣaṣṭhollāsaḥ

tadadravyasyaiva svīkāraṃ vada me karuṇānidhe

tasya śravaṇamātreṇa tattvajñānaṃ prakāśate

tṛpyanti devatāḥ sarvāḥ smaraṇād yajanādapi

tattanmantravidhānena devatā prītimāpnuyāt

ānītaṃ dīyate bhaktyā kṣetrapebhyaḥ kuleśvari

yaiḥ samacītamātreṇa sarve naśyantyupadravāḥ

nātheti kapilajaṭābhārabhāsvarapiṅgala

ukto vaṭukamantro'yaṃ catuścatvāriṃśadakṣaraiḥ

śāntiṃ karotu me nityaṃ bhūtavetālasevitaḥ

tatpaścāt savarbhūtebhyaḥ sarvabhūtādhivattī ca

trailokyeti padaṃ caiva vāsinībhya imāṃ vadet

kathito'yaṃ maheśāni mantraḥ pañcādaśākṣaraḥ

khecarī bhūcarī vyomacarī prītāstu me sadā

paścād bhūtapatibhyo hṛdyuktaḥ saptadaśākṣaraḥ

pātālasaṃsthā me kevicchivayogena bhāvitāḥ

tṛpyantu prītamanasaḥ pratigṛhṇantvimaṃ balim

sarvavighnān padaṃ paścāt nāśayadvitayaṃ tathā

gṛhṇa gṛhṇa dviṭhānto'yaṃ kṣetrapālamanuḥ priye

prīto'yaṃ balidānena sarvarakṣāṃ karotu me

hrāṃ hrīṃ hrūñca yugañcādau bhairavādhiṣṭhitāya ca

siddhārthapadamābhāṣya paścādavataradvayam

mātṛputrapadaṃ paścāt kulaputrapadaṃ tathā

grāmādhipataye'smin syāddeśādhipataye tataḥ

meghanādapadaṃ paścāt pracaṇḍograpadaṃ vadet

syāt sarvavighnādhipataye imāṃ pūjāṃ baliṃ vadet

jvalayukprajvalayugaṃ sarvavighnānītīrayet

kṣetrapālāya vauṣaṭ hrūṃ ṣaṣṭyuttaraśatākṣaraḥ

gṛhṇayugmaṃ dviṭhānto'yamaṣṭāviṃśākṣaro manuḥ

rājarājeśvaro devo me prasīdatu sarvadā

rājarājeśvaraṃ madhye pūjayet kulanāyike

tarjanīmadhyamānāmikāṅguṣṭhairyoginībaliḥ

aṅguṣṭhamadhyamābhyāñca rājarājeśvarasya ca

īṣadraktasupiṣṭena caturaṅgulimānataḥ

karṣājyagrāhiṇaḥ kuryyānnava saptātha pañca vā

tridhā devyupari bhrāmya kuladīpān nivedayet

kuladīpān pradarśyātha śaktipūjāṃ samācaret

pāyayitvā caret pānamiti śāstrasya niścayaḥ

mantradīkṣāvidhānen śuddhā bhavati nānyathā

abhyarcya devatābuddhyā bhogapātraṃ nivedayet

sampūjya devatābuddhyā dadyāt pātraṃ pṛthak pṛthak

pūjitaṃ niṣphalaṃ tasya devatā na prasīdati

śvapacī khaṭṭakī caiva kaivarttī viśvayoṣitaḥ

kandukī śauṇḍikī caiva śastrajīvī ca rañjakī

tantramantrasamāyuktā samayācārapālikā

pūjākāle svataḥ prāptā sā jñeyā sahajā budhaiḥ

surūpā taruṇī śāntā kulācārayutā śuciḥ

alolupā suśīlā ca smitāsyā priyavādinī

manoharā sadācārā śaktireṣā sulakṣaṇā

durācārā parādhīnā bhītā lubdhāturā'lasā

durgandhā kutsitā mūḍhā vṛddhonmattā rahasyabhit

īdṛśīṃ mantrayuktāñca śaktiṃ yāge vivarjayet

itaḥ pūrvādimanunā mantrī devyai samarpayet

dehadharmādhikārato jāgratsvapnasuṣuptiṣu

hastābhyāñca tataḥ padbhayāmudareṇa tataḥ param

svāhānto manurityuktastrisaptatyakṣaraḥ priye

tava kṛtyamidaṃ sarvamiti jñātvā kṣamasva me

evaṃ sāvaraṇāṃ devīṃ cintayet svahṛdambuje

syādvāgbhavaṃ hṛducchiṣṭacāṇḍāli tadanantaram

ekaviṃśativarṇaiśca śeṣikāmanurīritaḥ

devīmucchiṣṭamātaṅgīṃ dhyāyet trailokyamohinīm

karābhyāṃ pātramuddhṛtya sadvitīyaṃ samarpayet

anyonyavandanaṃ kṛtvā pibettattadanujñayā

yathāvidhi dvitīyena gṛhṇīyānmantramuccaran

ātmadehatrayaṃ tattvaṃ trayeṇātha viśodhayet

guruḥ śiṣyān samāhūya dadyāttattvatrayaṃ priye

yathāśakti nivedyātha vittaśāṭhyavivajītaḥ

samarpyopāyanaṃ bhaktyā śivāya gururūpiṇe

jānubhyāmavaniṃ gatvā pañcāṅgaṃ praṇamed gurum

spṛṣṭvā viśuddhahṛdaya īṣadānatamastakaḥ

prakṛtyādyaiḥ pṛthivyantaiścatuvīṃśatibhiḥ priye

saṃyuktenātmatattvena sthūladehaṃ viśodhayet

yuktena vidyātattvena sūkṣmadehaṃ viśodhayet

parayā śivatattvena paraṃ dehaṃ viśodhayet

sarvatattvāśrayaṃ bījaṃ sarvatattvaivīśodhayet

bhaktyā cāvanataḥ śiṣyo niḥśabdaṃ triḥ pibedalim

śiraḥprabhṛtipādāntaṃ śuddhaṃ dehaṃ vicintayet

parāntaṃ śivatattvaṃ syāditi tattvatrayaṃ jagat

sa jīvanneva muktaḥ syāditi śaṅkarabhāṣitam

ādāya guruṇā dattaṃ sadvitīyāsavaṃ pibet

nopaviśya pibenmadyaṃ iti śāstrasya nirṇayaḥ

avijñāyācared yastu sa bhavedāpadāmpadam

tasmānmantravidhānena kartavyaṃ kulanāyike

paśupāśasamucchedakāraṇaṃ bhairavoditam

tanmayatvācca bhāvānāṃ bhāvāścāntahītā rase

tasmādimāṃ surāṃ devīṃ pūrṇo'haṃ tvāṃ pibāmyaham

anākulamanāḥ kuryādalipānaṃ śanaiḥ śanaiḥ

kuṇḍalyākṛticidrūpe huned dravyaṃ samantrakam

parāhantāmaye vahnau homasvīkāralakṣaṇam

yāvadullāsaparyantamupadeśe pibenmadhu

pānāt parapadaprāptiḥ kaule trayamitīritam

amṛtaṃ tadvijānīyād yadannaṃ surayā saha

carvaṇena binā pānaṃ kevalaṃ viṣavarddhanaṃ

divyaṃ devyagrataḥ pānaṃ vīraṃ mudrāsane kṛtam

svecchayā paśuvatpītaṃ paśupānamitīritam

paśupānaṃ narakadaṃ proktaṃ pānaphalaṃ priye

tāvat pānaṃ prakurvīta paśupānamataḥ param

tāvadeva pibenmadyamanyathā patanaṃ bhavet

āgalāntaṃ pibeddravyaṃ sa mukto nātra saṃśayaḥ

utthāya ca punaḥ pītvā punarjanma na vidyate

vamanāt sarvadevāśca tasmātrividhamācaret

tat sukhaṃ sārvabhaumasya nṛpasyāpi na vidyate

tat saukhyameva mokṣaḥ syāt satyameva varānane

samāsena kuleśāni kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi

baṭukaśaktyādipūjanaṃ nāma saptamollāsaḥ

ullāsabhedaṃ deveśa dravyapātrādisaṅgamam

ceṣṭāṃ kaulikaśaktīnāṃ vada me parameśvara

tasya śravaṇamātreṇa jāyate divyabhāvanā

tadantaśconmanāścaiva tatollāsaśca saptamaḥ

kathitastaruṇollāsastaruṇaṃ sukhamambike

skhalanaṃ dṛṅmanovācāṃ prauḍhamityabhidhīyate

yathādhikāraṃ tatrāpi karttavyaṃ pātramelanam

dūṣakaiḥ samayabhraṣṭairna kuryād dravyasaṅgatim

paśubhiḥ kṣudrakarmasthairna kuryād dravyasaṅgatim

kulopadeśahīnaiśca na kuryād dravyasaṅgatim

kuladharmānabhijñāścettatsaṅgaṃ parivarjayet

tvatpūjāvimukhāḥ syuścettatsaṃsargaṃ parityajet

kulācārānabhijñānāṃ saṅgatiṃ varjayetpriye

vinā saṅketayogena na kuryād dravyasaṅgatim

mantrāḥ parāṅmukhā yānti vighnaścaiva pade pade

yadi dadyātkuleśāni devatāśāpamāpnuyāt

anabhijñairanarhaiśca saṅgamaṃ naiva kārayet

pūrvadakṣiṇayoraikyamudakpaścimayostathā

kāminībhiśca tatkuryāt srotasāñca catuṣṭaye

svamātṛpādukāmūlamantrajaptaṃ pibetpriye

ādāya pūrvavajjaptvā pibeddevi guruṃ smaran

svajyeṣṭhasyāpi cocchiṣṭaṃ khādennānyasya pārvati

ātmocchiṣṭaṃ na dātavyaṃ parakīyaṃ na bhakṣayet

cakramadhye'pi deveśi anyathā patanaṃ bhavet

dadyāt snehena yo'nyebhyaḥ sa bhavedāpadāmpadam

snehāllobhāt bhayādvāpi devatāśāpamāpnuyāt

pūjāgṛhādbahiḥ kuryāttrikoṇe tu gṛhāntare

kṛṣṇābhaṃ bhairavaṃ dhyāyet sarvavighnanivāraṇam

ehiyugmaṃ baliṃ gṛhṇayugmaṃ phaṭ ca dviṭhāntakaḥ

śāntistavaṃ paṭhetpaścāttarpayedalibindubhiḥ

namāmi cāṣṭāṣṭakayoginīgaṇam

namāmi śirasā nāthaṃ bhairavaṃ bhairavīpriyam

namaḥ śrīnāthavaidyāya kulauṣadhividhāyine

kāntirmanoharā cāstu pāntu sarvāśca devatāḥ

karotu śānti bhagavān kuleśaḥ

cānye viśeṣapadabhedakaśāmbhavā ye

nandantu bhūmipatigodvijasādhulokāḥ

śāntāśca śāntamanaso hṛtaśokaśaṅkāḥ

dharme carantu niratā gurubhaktalokāḥ

yasyāṃ guroścaraṇapaṅkajameva satyam

stā devyo ripupakṣabhakṣaṇaratā nandantu kaulācītāḥ

stāḥ sarvāḥ kulamārgapālanaparāḥ śānti prayacchantu me

stṛpyantāṃ kulaputrakasya pibataḥ pānaṃ sadīpañcarum

satyañcāpi ca kauladharmaparamaṃ syānme jayaḥ sarvadā

antaḥsthā śāmbhavī me'stu prasanno'stu guruḥ sadā

yadyeṣa kuladharmaḥ syāttadā naśyantu dūṣakāḥ

namastābhyaḥ samastābhyo yoginībhyo nirantaram

pibantu bhairavāḥ sarve mama dehe vyavasthitāḥ

yoginyaḥ kṣetrapālāśca mama dehe vyavasthitāḥ

kālāgnyādiśivāntañca jagad yajñena tṛpyatu

paṅkārthāvahaketumānakusumāt gṛhṇantu te pāntu ca

tato dadyāt svaśiṣyāya prasādaṃ kulanāyike

yoginīmaṇḍale caiva kramādānandamucyate

icchaiva śāstrasampattirityājñā parameśvari

tatsarvaṃ devatāprītyai jāyate surasundari

vikriyā pūjanaṃ devi uditaṃ bhairavībaliḥ

nyāso'vayavasaṃsparśo bhojanaṃ havanakriyā

kāryākāryavicārantu yaḥ karoti sa pātakī

yenāpnuvanti manujāḥ sākṣādbhairavarūpatām

veṇuvīṇādivādyañca kavitāracanādikam

gamanaṃ vikriyā devi yoga ityabhidhīyate

samācaranti deveśi yathollāsaṃ manogatam

nidhāya vadane pātraṃ nivīṇṇā nivasanti ca

tathaiva puruṣaścāpi prauḍhāntollāsasaṃyutaḥ

pṛcchati svapatiṃ mugdhā kastvaṃ kāham ime ca ke

udyānaṃ kimidaṃ hanta gṛhaṃ kiṃ prāṅgaṇaṃ kimu

upadaṃśaṃ mukhe kṣiptvā nikṣipanti priyānane

dhṛtvā śirasi nṛtyanti madyabhāṇḍāni yoginaḥ

praskhalatpadavinyāsaṃ nṛtyanti kulaśaktayaḥ

madākulāśca yoginyaḥ patanti puruṣopari

ityādivividhāṃ ceṣṭāṃ kurvanti kulanāyike

tadā tu devatābhāvaṃ bhajante yogipuṅgavāḥ

taṃ nāśayantyasandehaṃ yoginyaḥ kulanāyike

etaccakragatāṃ vārttāṃ bahirnaiva prakāśayet

bhaktyā satkārayedetān gopayecca prayatnataḥ

modate vandate bhaktyā sa gacchet yoginīpadam

vratatīrthatapodānayajñakoṭiphalaṃ labhet

unmanākhyatadullāse cakre vīrasamacīte

paraṃ brahmānusandhānākāṅkṣiṇau kulanāyike

samavasthāmidhe tasmin tatollāse samaṃ bhavet

mūrcchanāsannikarṣo hi mūlamukteḥ paraṃ viduḥ

eṣā tu śāmbhavī mudrā sarvatantreṣu gopitā

anayollāsino vīrāḥ śivā eva na saṃśayaḥ

tadā yatparamaṃ saukhyamiti vaktuṃ na śakyate

dṛśyate pulakādyeryat tadbrahmadhyānamucyate

kathituṃ naiva śaknomi prabuddhastatsamāhitaḥ

kṣaṇe'pyantahīte tasmin śocayanti hataprabhāḥ

aṣṭāvasthāśca kampādyā jāyante nātra saṃśayaḥ

pratīhāri padaṃ prāptāḥ sevante mandiraṃ ciram

te guṇāḥ kulatattvajñe tattvajñānasamāhṛtāḥ

tadanto jāgradityuktaśconmanāḥ svapna ucyate

saptollāsañca yo vetti sa muktaḥ sa ca kaulikaḥ

nivṛtte bhairavīcakre sarve varṇāḥ pṛthak pṛthak

cakre'sminnaiva bhedo'sti sarve śivasamāḥ smṛtāḥ

yāti śrīcakramadhye'pi caikatvaṃ sarvamānavāḥ

tathā śrīcakramadhye tu jātibhedo na vidyate

tathaiva cakramadhye'pi devatāḥ sarvamānavāḥ

vede'pi sthitamevaṃ hi sarve hi brahma cābravīt

madrūpā puruṣāḥ sarve tvadrūpāḥ pramadāḥ priye

taṃ bhakṣayanti yoginyastvāṃ śape kulanāyike

athavā mithunaṃ kṛtvā kramātsamupaveśayet

śivaśaktidhiyā sarve cakramadhye samarcayet

yathā vāṇīvidhātārau tathā vīraḥ saśaktikaḥ

na tuṣyāmi varārohe bhagaliṅgāmṛtaṃ vinā

liṅgāṅkañca bhagāṅkañca tasmācchaktiśivātmakam

sā sandhyā kulaniṣṭhānāṃ samādhiḥ sa vidhīyate

sadyaḥ saṃskārasaṃśuddhāṃ vihitatvāt striyaṃ vrajet

samāsena kuleśāni kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi

tattvatritayapānādibhedakathanaṃ nāma aṣṭamollāsaḥ

kulabhaktyārcanaphalaṃ vada me karuṇānidhe

tasya śravaṇamātreṇa yogaḥ sākṣāt prakāśate

sākāraṃ sthūlamityāhunirākārantu sūkṣmakam

sthūle'pi niścalaṃ ceto bhavet sūkṣme'pi niścalam

sarvatejomayaṃ dhyāyet saccidānandaniṣkalam

svayaṃ vibhātyathānyāni bhāsayan sādhanaṃ vinā

manasā mātrasamvedyaṃ tajjñānaṃ brahmasaṃjñitam

parajīvaikadhāmajño yogī yogaviducyate

svarūpaśūnyaṃ taddhyānaṃ samādhirabhidhīyate

svayaṃ prakāśite tattve tatkṣaṇāttanmayo bhavet

niśvāsocchvāsahīnaśca niścitaṃ mukta eva saḥ

ya āste mṛtavatsākṣāt jīvanmuktaḥ sa ucyate

na jānāti sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ na saṅkalpayate manaḥ

evaṃ śive vilīnātmā samādhistha ihocyate

aviśeṣo bhavettadvajjīvātmaparamātmanoḥ

tathā samādhisāmarthyād brahmabhūto bhavennaraḥ

pṛthakkṛto guṇebhyaḥ syādātmā tadvadihocyate

alakṣyañca tathā yogī prapañcaṃ naiva paśyati

tathaivonmīlane'pi syādetaddhyānasya lakṣaṇam

paraṃ brahmasvarūpī ca vetti viśvaviceṣṭitam

kiṅkaratvaṃ hi gacchanti mantrā mantrādhipaiḥ saha

yo yo jalpaḥ sa sanmantrastaddhyānaṃ yannirīkṣaṇam

yatra yatra mano yāti tatra tatra samādhayaḥ

kṣīyante cāsya karmāṇi tasmin dṛṣṭe parātmani

devāsurapadaṃ yattatprāptañcāpi na gṛhyate

tasya kiñcidanālabhyaṃ jñātavyaṃ nāvaśiṣyate

labdhe śāntipade devi na yogo naiva dhāraṇā

tālavṛntena kiṃ kāryaṃ labdhe malayamārute

na yamo niyamo nāsti svayamomiti paśyatām

ekyaṃ jīvātmanorāhuryogaṃ yogaviśāradāḥ

yadbhavet sumahat puṇyaṃ tasyānto naiva gaṇyate

sa sarvaṃ pātakaṃ hanyāttamaḥ sūryodayo yathā

yat phalaṃ koṭiguṇitaṃ tadavāpnoti tattvavit

japastutiḥ syādadhamā homapūjā'dhamādhamā

śāstracintā'dhamā jñeyā lokacintā'dhamādhamā

japakoṭisamaṃ dhyānaṃ dhyānakoṭisamo layaḥ

nānusandhāt parā pūjā na hi tṛpteḥ paraṃ phalam

acinteva paraṃ dhyānamanicchaiva paraṃ phalam

upacāraivīnā pūjāṃ yogī nityaṃ samācaret

nijasvarūpanirmagnaḥ sa yogī paratattvavit

tyajedajñānanirmālyaṃ so'hambhāvena pūjayet

pāśabaddhaḥ smṛto jīvaḥ pāśamuktaḥ sadāśivaḥ

karmabaddhaḥ smṛto jīvaḥ karmamuktaḥ sadāśivaḥ

pratimāsvaprabuddhānāṃ sarvatra viditātmanām

sama āste sa yogīndro harṣāharṣavivajītaḥ

āste dehe pravāsīva yogī paramatattvavit

nijasvarūpanirmagnaḥ sa yogī paratattvavit

nivasanti kuleśāni tathā yogī ca tattvavit

ya āste sa tu yogīndraḥ paśyatyātmānamātmani

tadeva mokṣo viduṣāmabudhānāntu pātakam

sadāsaṃśayahīno yaḥ kulayogī sa ucyate

ahaṃ tadanayoraikyaṃ bhāvayannivaset sukhī

prāyaścittī sa varjyaśca paśureva na saṃśayaḥ

vinālimāṃsagandhena sākṣāt paśupatiḥ paśuḥ

kulamārga samuddiṣṭaṃ bhairaveṇa mahātmanā

asatyamapi satyaṃ syāt kaulikānāṃ kuleśvari

agamyamapi gamyaṃ syāt kaulikānāṃ kuleśvari

na svargo naiva narakaṃ kaulikānāṃ kuleśvari

vinaṣṭā api varddhante kaulikāḥ kulanāyike

bāndhavanti janāḥ sarve kaulikānāṃ kuleśvari

bādhakāḥ sādhakāyante kaulikānāṃ kuleśvari

adharmāścāpi dharmanti kaulikānāṃ kuleśvari

puṇyāyante'ṅganāsaṅgāḥ kaulikānāṃ kuleśvari

sadā saṅkalpasiddhāḥ syurnātra kāryā vicāraṇā

yatra kutrāśrame tiṣṭhet kulayogī kuleśvari

bhramanti pṛthivīmetāmavijñātasvarūpiṇaḥ

unmattamūkajaḍavannivasellokamadhyataḥ

nakṣatrāṇāṃ grahāṇāñca tathā vṛttantu yoginām

yathā gatirna dṛśyeta tathā vṛttaṃ hi yoginām

pāmarā iva dṛśyante kulayoga viśāradāḥ

na kiñcidapi bhāṣante tathā yogī pravarttate

vadedunmattavadvidvān kulayogī maheśvari

vilokya dūrato yāti tathā yogī pravarttate

nānāveśadharo yogī vicarejjagatītale

anugṛhṇan janān sarvān krīḍecca pṛthivītale

yogī bhuktvākhilān bhogān tathā pāpairna lipyate

sarve yathā nadīsnātāstathā yogī sadā śuciḥ

tathā mlecchagṛhānnādi yogihastāpītaṃ śuci

tadeva viduṣāṃ mānyamātmano hitakāṅkṣiṇām

yasyāmudeti sūryo hi pūrvāśā sā nigadyate

kulayogī vared yatra sa sa mārgaḥ kuleśvari

svecchāvihāriṇaṃ śāntaṃ ko vā vārayituṃ kṣamaḥ

tadvanna dṛśyate jñānī krīḍannindriyapannagaiḥ

kulajñānaratāḥ śāntāstvadbhaktāste ca kaulikāḥ

kaulikendrā hyacapalā ye nendriyavaśānugāḥ

ānandāśru pateddevi kathitāḥ kaulikottamāḥ

iti te niścitadhiyaḥ proktāste kaulikottamāḥ

kulārcanarataḥ sa syāt kauliko nāparaḥ priye

prīto bhavati yo dṛṣṭvā kaulikaḥ sa ca me priyaḥ

devatāgurubhaktāśca kaulikaḥ syācca dīkṣayā

supākenaiva puṇyānāṃ labhyate nānyathā priye

punāti kuladhamīṣṭhaścāṇḍālo'pi yadṛcchayā

yatra devi kulajñānī tatrāhañca tvayā saha

kulajñā yatra tiṣṭhanti tatra tiṣṭhāmi bhāmini

draṣṭavyañca prayatnena tatra sannihito hyaham

samīpe varttamāno'pi na draṣṭavyaḥ paśuḥ priye

darśanādarcanāttasya trisaptakulamuddharet

śaṃsanti pitarastasya yāsyāmaḥ paramāṃ gatim

yo'smatkuleṣu putro vā pautro vā kauliko bhavet

yatsamīpaṃ samāyānti kulācāryā mudā priye

samāyānti mudā devi yoginyo yogibhiḥ saha

tasmāt sampūjayedbhaktyā kulajñānaparāyaṇān

pāpiṣṭhāstvatprasādasya bhājanaṃ na bhavanti te

rasān bhaktasya jihvāgrādaśnāmi kamalekṣaṇe

tasmānmama priyākāṅkṣī tvadbhaktāneva pūjayet

surāḥ kulapriyāḥ sarve tasmāt kaulikamarcayet

kaulikendre'cīte samyak yathā tuṣyāmi pārvati

tat phalaṃ nāpnuyāttīrthatapodānamakhavrataiḥ

kaulikasya bhavedvayarthaṃ kulajñaṃ yo'vamānayet

yaḥ praviśya kulaṃ dharma kulācāraṃ na vetti cet

taddānaṃ niṣphalaṃ devi dātā ca narakaṃ vrajet

bhasmanīva hutaṃ havyaṃ tadvaddānamakaulike

viśeṣatithiṣu prītyā tatphalaṃ naiva varṇyate

abhyarcya devatābuddhyā gandhapuṣpākṣatādibhiḥ

teṣu tuṣṭeṣvahaṃ tuṣṭastuṣṭāḥ syuḥ sarvadevatāḥ

madhumattāya deveśi tasya puṇyaṃ na gaṇyate

paralokasya pātheyaṃ vīracakre'pītaṃ madhu

jāyate hi kuladravyaṃ kulayogīśvarāpītam

so'pi deśo bhavet pūtaḥ kiṃ punastatpurasthitāḥ

kiṃ punarbahubhirbhuktaistat puṇyaṃ naiva gaṇyate

kuladharmarato bhūyāt kulajñāninamarcayet

tāvadvarṇāśramācāraḥ karttavyaḥ karmamuktaye

śive tenaiva muktiḥ syādataḥ karma samācaret

karmamuktaḥ sukhākāṅkṣī karmaniṣṭhaḥ sukhaṃ vrajet

tyajet karmaphalaṃ yo vā sa tyāgītyabhidhīyate

ahambhāvamapāsyaiva yaḥ kuryāt sa na lipyate

na ca spṛśanti tattvajñaṃ jalaṃ padmadalaṃ yathā

prayānti naiva lipyante kriyamāṇāni vā punaḥ

saṃtyaktasarvasaṅkalpaḥ sa vidvān karma santyajet

pāṣaṇḍāḥ paṇḍitammanyāste yānti narakaṃ priye

tattvaṃ prāpya tathā yogī tyajet karmaparigraham

puṇyapāpairna lipyante yeṣāṃ brahma hṛdi sthitam

jihvopasthaparityāgī karmaṇā kiṃ kariṣyati

samāsena kuleśāni kiṃ bhūyaḥ śrotumicchasi

yogasaṃsthāpanakathanaṃ nāma navamollāsaḥ

tatsaparyāphalaṃ deva vada me parameśvara

tasya śravaṇamātreṇa sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate

māsapūjā'dhamā devi māsādūrdhva paśurbhavet

paśorbhūyaḥ praveśecchā yadi syāddīkṣayet punaḥ

makārapañcakaṃ devi devatāprītikārakam

yathāvidhi niṣeveta tṛṣṇayā cet sa pātakī

saṃkrāntiḥ pañca parvāṇi teṣu puṇyadineṣu ca

mānavaughādipuṃsāñca svajanmadivase tathā

pīṭhopagamane vīrapīṭhe svajanadarśane

evamādiṣu deveśi viśeṣadivaseṣu ca

yathākālaṃ yathādeśaṃ tathā pūjāṃ samācaret

svayaṃ vā pūjayeddevi bindupūjāpuraḥsaram

akurvan kauliko mohāddevatāśāpamāpnuyāt

śrīguruṃ pūjayedbhaktyā'prāpte tatstrīsutādikān

santoṣayet kuladravyaiḥ kulapūjāpuraḥsaram

pūjayed yoginīvṛndaṃ devi taddoṣaśāntaye

kaulikāstricatuḥpañca śaktayaśca tathā priye

bhakṣyabhojyādipiśitaiḥ padārthaiḥ ṣaḍrasānvitaiḥ

tacchrīcakramiti proktaṃ vṛndañcāpi taducyate

prātanīmantrayedbhaktyā sādhakaḥ śuddhamānasaḥ

mantrī snātvātha śuddhātmā kuryāddevi kramārcanam

devatāsannidhau bālāmupaveśya samarcayet

kadalīnārikelādiphalaistāṃ paritoṣayet

yathāśakti japedekottaravṛddhyā'thavā manum

tatastāṃ devatābuddhyā namaskṛtya visarjayet

evaṃ tithau kumārīñca yajet pūrvadinācītām

sarasvatī ramā gaurī durgā ca nava kīttītāḥ

nāmabhiśca caturthyantaiḥ pūjayettāḥ pṛthak pṛthak

gandhapuṣpāmbarākalpairyathāvibhavavistaraiḥ

svakāryaphalasiddhyarthaṃ vittaśāṭhyavivajītaḥ

navarātrakṛtāṃ pūjāṃ devi devyai samarpayet

evaṃ navakumārīṇāmarcanaṃ prātavatsaram

mano'bhilāṣaṃ samprāpya nivasettava sannidhau

manojñāḥ pūjayedbhaktyā navarātriṣu mantravit

pūrvavat pūrvamadyādyaiḥ padārthaiḥ paritoṣayet

sādhakastuṣṭimāsādya nivasettava sannidhau

ṣaṇmāse vā trimāse vā māse māse'thavā priye

sarvaiśvaryasamṛddhātmā sa bhavedāvayoḥ priyaḥ

sarvalakṣaṇasampannāmanukūlāṃ manoramām

abhyaṅgasnānaśuddhāṅgīmāsane copaveśayet

ātmānaṃ gandhapuṣpādyairalaṅkuryāt kuleśvari

kṛtvā kramārcanaṃ dhūpadīpāñca kuladīpakān

māṃsādibhakṣyabhojyādyaistoṣayeddevi bhaktitaḥ

yauvanollāsasahitaḥ svayaṃ taddhyānatatparaḥ

japādikaṃ samarpyātha tayā saha niśāṃ nayet

pūjayedvidhinā'nena tasya puṇyaṃ na gaṇyate

yad yat svamanaso'bhīṣṭaṃ tattadāpnotyasaṃśayam

stotraiḥ saṃpūjayet sarvairmahadaiśvaryamāpnuyāt

tulāyāṃ siṃhameṣe vā sarvasaṃkrāntiṣu priye

śacīndrau rohiṇīcandrau svāhāgnī ca prabhāravī

mithunāni navābhyarcya pūrvoktenaiva vartmanā

gandhapuṣpādibhiḥ pūjya madyādyaiḥ paritoṣayet

anugṛhṇanti taṃ devi prayacchanti manoratham

tava loke sa nivaset sarvaiśvaryasamanvitaḥ

brāhme muhūrtte utthāya snānaṃ sandhyāmupāsya ca

ātmānaṃ gandhapuṣpādyairalaṅkṛtya vidhānavit

kiñcidabhyudite sūrye maṇḍale sveṣṭadevatām

ṣoḍaśairupacāraistu cakrapūjāpuraḥsaram

matsyamāṃsādi vidhivadbhakṣya bhojyasamanvitam

yauvanollāsasahito nivīkāreṇa cetasā

japtvā samarpya tatpūjāṃ devatāñca samuddharet

kuryājjapārcanaṃ kṛṣṇacaturddaśyantamambike

tripañcasapta nava vā vittalobhavivajītaḥ

devatā tasya santuṣṭā dadāti phalamīpsitam

sa tu tatphalamāpnoti yoginīnāṃ priyo bhavet

kāṅkṣitāṃ labhate siddhiṃ devavad vicared bhuvi

snātvā śuklāmbaradharaḥ sāyaṃ sandhyāmupāsya ca

yauvanollāsasahitaścandrasthāṃ devatāṃ smaran

evaṃ pratidinaṃ śuklacaturddaśyantamarcayet

evaṃ yaḥ kurute bhaktyā śuklapakṣārcanaṃ priye

sarvalokaikasaṃpūjyaḥ śivavannivased bhuvi

yaḥ karoti vidhānena sarvān kāmān samaśnute

yoginīvīramelanaṃ labhate nātra saṃśayaḥ

snātvācamya viśuddhātmā nyāsān kṛtvā puroditān

pūrvārcanokta vidhinā sarvadravyasamanvitaḥ

pañcavarṇarajaścitravasupatrasaroruhe

dīpaṃ saṃsthāpya purata uttarābhimukhasthitaḥ

yauvanollāsasahito dīpasthāṃ devatāṃ smaran

evaṃ samarcayet kṛṣṇacaturddaśyantamambike

evaṃ kṛte kuleśāni devatā prītimāpnuyāt

sarvalokaikasammānyaḥ sañcaret sa yathāsukham

dvātriṃśaddivaseṣvevaṃ catuḥṣaṣṭidineṣu ca

kramajñaścet svayaṃ kuryādvittaśāṭhyavivajītaḥ

maṅgalādyaiśca mithunairaṣṭabhiḥ śabditāḥ priye

akṣobhyādicatuḥṣaṣṭimithunāni samarcayet

kramalopaṃ na kurvīta sveṣṭakāryārthasiddhaye

samyak santoṣayeddevi mithunānyatibhaktitaḥ

evaṃ yaḥ kurute devi sakṛdaṣṭāṣṭakārcanam

kiṃ punarmānavādyaiśca sākṣāt śiva ivāparaḥ

punarāvṛttirahito nivasettava sannidhau

asmāt paratarā pūjā nāsti satyaṃ na saṃśayaḥ

yajñadānatapastīrthavratakoṭiphalaṃ labhet

catuḥsāgaraparyantāṃ mahīṃ śāsti na saṃśayaḥ

pūrvoktena vidhānena kuleśvari vidhānavit

prauḍhāntollāsayuktāni mithunāni samarcayet

tava loke vaseddevi brahmādisurasaṃstutaḥ

śrīkaṇṭhādi samabhyarcya tatphalaṃ labhate dhruvam

māse māse'thavā varṣe svajanmadivase priye

prauḍhāntollāsaparyantaṃ toṣayettadvidhānavit

sarvopadrava rahitaḥ sarvaiśvaryasamanvitaḥ

dehānte samavāpnoti tava lokaṃ na saṃśayaḥ

nivīkalpena cittena navaśaktisamanvitaḥ

ājñāsiddhirbhavettasya devatāprītimāpnuyāt

āgamokteta vidhinā pūrvavettadvidhānavit

deveśi sādhakābhīṣṭaṃ prayacchanti na saṃśayaḥ

karoti śastravidhinā sa bhavedāvayoḥ priyaḥ

stambhane ca namaḥ proktaṃ svāhā śāntikapauṣṭike

hemnā tu sarvakāryāṇi krūre syāt kākapucchikā

sarvakāryaṃ devagṛhe śmaśāne krūrakarma ca

uttamāṅge parābījaṃ candrāyutasamaprabham

mūlādibrahmarandhrāntaṃ anavacchinnarūpibhiḥ

ātmānaṃ bhāvayennityaṃ sa bhavedajarāmaraḥ

saccandrabimbasañjātasudhāplāvitavigraham

taruṇollāsasahito maṇḍalaṃ pūjayet priye

grahāpasmāravetālabhūtonmādādijaṃ bhayam

vrataṃ snānaṃ tapastīrthaṃ yajñadevārcanādiṣu

tvatsannidhau sannivasennātra kāryā vicāraṇā

kāmyakarmakathanaṃ nāma saptadaśollāsaḥ

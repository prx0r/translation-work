# KNOWLEDGE CORE — Netratantra (pass-2, 2026-11-11)

*Distilled understanding for translators. Verified against the Muktabodha
authority page ("Śākta General" + Bhairava Tantras notes) and the
chapter-by-chapter summary of the 22 adhikāras; Kṣemarāja's Uddyota is
the commentary (support, never source). Muktabodha M00038 (IAST).*

## A. THE ONE THESIS

> **Śiva's eye is the instrument of protection and of recognition:
> the same gaze that wards off death-demons is the gaze by which the
> self recognises itself as Śiva. Apotropaic rite and non-dual
> recognition are one practice seen from two ends.**

This is a *universal* (sarvasāmānya) tantra: it overrides sectarian
boundaries by absorbing Śaiva, Vaiṣṇava and goddess systems into one
protective-vision theology. Argmaps must preserve the inclusion move,
not flatten it to a Trika reading.

## C. LOAD-BEARING TECHNICAL SETS

1. **Amṛteśvara = Mṛtyuñjaya = "Lord of Nectar"** — one deity, three
   functional names (nectar / death-conquest / eye-lord). Never treat as
   three gods.
2. **Amṛtalakṣmī** — his consort; not interchangeable with Lakṣmī of
   Vaiṣṇava systems even when the name collides.
3. **divyacakṣus / netra doctrine** — perception terms are epistemic
   claims: seeing correctly = being free.
4. **The five vyomas + sixteen ādhāras** (adh. 7) — a subtle-body
   inventory shared with Śākta tantras; gloss each member exactly.
5. **bhūta-catalogue (adh. 19)** — affliction classes: bhūta, preta,
   yakṣa, piśāca, rākṣasa. Medical-ritual vocabulary; exact terms.

## D. TRANSLATION TRAPS

1. **Cross-system names**: Vaiṣṇava deities/mantras inside a Śaiva tantra
   (adh. 9, 11) — gloss exactly, never "correct" to Śaiva equivalents.
2. **Yoginī passages (adh. 20)** — bodily-dhātu mapping is technical
   erotics/physiology; clinical precision, no euphemism, no prurience.
3. **Flood/Wernicke-Olesen overlap** — their edition (NAK MS 1-285,
   1200 CE) is the scholarly base; where our M00038 readings differ,
   mark TEXTUAL uncertainty, do not silently follow either.
4. **kavaca verses (adh. 17)** — protective armour texts read as
   operative formulas; keep imperative force.
5. **Kṣemarāja's Uddyota** — non-dual reading imposed on a partly
   dualist-protective text; do not let Uddyota vocabulary leak into L2.

## E. QUALITY BAR

Protective/medical passages (adh. 15–19) are *procedural*: the essence
layer should name the operation (ward X by Y), not philosophise. The
philosophical weight sits in adh. 2, 7–9, 14, 21.

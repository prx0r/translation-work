## The doctrine in brief (Abhinavagupta, Abhinavabhāratī + Locana on Dhvanyāloka)

1. **Rasa-sūtra** (Bharata): *vibhāva-anubhāva-vyabhicāri-saṃyogād rasa-niṣpattiḥ* —
   from the union of determinants, consequents, and transitory states, rasa arises.
2. **Four answers to where/how**: Lollaṭa (produced, lives in the hero — utpatti);
   Śaṅkuka (inferred, like emotion in a painted horse — anumiti); Bhaṭṭa Nāyaka/Tauta
   (enjoyed — bhukti — via three word-functions, ending in relish); Abhinavagupta
   (manifested/revealed — abhivyakti — in the sahṛdaya's heart through suggestion).
3. **Sādhāraṇīkaraṇa** (Nāyaka's coinage, kept by Abhinava): generalization sheds
   particulars ("my grief", "Rāma's grief") so the emotion is tasted universally.
4. **Sthāyibhāva is central.** Only a durable sthāyin can become rasa; vibhāva /
   anubhāva / vyabhicāri are subordinate conditions, never the thing itself.
5. **Rasadhvani is the soul of poetry** (Locana): rasa suggested without perceptible
   sequence (asaṃlakṣya-krama-vyaṅgya) outranks stated meaning. The C1 essay answers
   to this bar: it must carry suggestion, not just paraphrase.
6. **Śāntarasa, the ninth** (Abhinava's addition): quiescence as essence of all rasas;
   all emanate from it and resolve into it; sthāyin = śama. Precedent for whole-work
   dominant-rasa reading: Mahābhārata's aṅgī-rasa is śānta (Ānandavardhana's school).
7. **Sahṛdaya**: the sensitive reader whose latent vāsanās can be roused; seven
   vighnas (obstacles, e.g. doubt about the mental state) block relish.
8. **Camatkāra**: the rupture/relish itself — apprehension in which only pleasure appears.

## Application to the Śivastotrāvalī (flagged: application, NOT Abhinava attribution)

- Abhinavagupta did **not** admit a bhaktirasa (that is later: Rūpa Gosvāmin,
  Madhusūdana). Do not attribute it to him. What we can say: bhakti functions in
  these songs the way a sthāyin functions — durable, generalized (any devotee),
  relished, resolving the other affects into itself.
- **Adbhuta signature**: the songs return obsessively to wonder-words (*citraṃ,
  aho, ko 'pi*) at structural pivots (s01 v20, v26; s03 close). Candidate dominant
  relish for s01 alongside śānta.
- **Śānta close pattern**: several songs dissolve agitation into quiescence at the
  end (s01 v26 mind-as-field-fruit; s03 v21 bewildered-knowing). Note where true,
  do not force where absent.
- **Dhvani test for C1**: if the essay can be replaced by its SUMMARY without loss,
  it has no rasadhvani — rewrite. The essay must *do* something the propositions
  alone do not (this is what `renders.standard` is for, and what C2 variants
  must preserve under `maps_to`).

## Open scholarly questions (do not settle in translation notes)

- Whether śānta is Bharata's or Abhinava's (texts disagree; we follow Abhinava).
- Bhakti's systematic place (no bhaktirasa in Abhinava; note the fact, don't fix it).
- Tauta's exact contribution (lost work, known via Abhinava's citations).

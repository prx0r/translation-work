## Divergences found in a01 (all adjudicated on the record)

1. **v04 asaṃkucita (LIVE FORK → L0 tsa-a01-v04-p045).** T1 frozen gloss:
   "thought-contracted-consciousness-light-natured". W2 word-by-word:
   "for the self is the light of awareness, uncontracted by thought".
   Adjudication: sandhi vikalpa+asaṃkucita carries privative a- (a+a=ā);
   avikalpāntatā doctrine just stated demands uncontracted; W2 concurs.
   Chosen: uncontracted (rank 1, grammar); T1 gloss rank 2 (superseded).
   L0.revised=true with reason; argmap records option_choices on merge.
   Downstream: L2v4, C1, L3, L4 carry "uncontracted by thought".
2. v01–v03, v05: no genuine forks — W2 glosses agree with T1 throughout
   (flash/awaken-shine, full-bodied/complete-in-himself, dive/plunge,
   gather/pick, stainless/unsoiled). Single-option stands.
3. Numbering note: W2 splits v4 prose into §§ and numbers the closing śloka
   "4", the dohā "5.4"; our verse ids follow GRETIL AbhTs_1.1–1.5 (dohā
   outside GRETIL scope — correctly excluded, noted here).

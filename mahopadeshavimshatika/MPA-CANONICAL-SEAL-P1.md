## The parable (canonical draft, ≈89s)

> A boy plays a name-game with the god at the river: "You are you,"
> he says, "and I am I — but you are, and I am not at all: where's
> the homage in that?" The god is the river, and the river needs
> nothing. Every gift the boy brings fails beautifully — a bath for
> the bathless, a jewel for the lovely, a lamp for the eyeless,
> cloth for the world-bellied — poured in, they vanish: no seat for
> the all-based, no incense for the noseless, no fee for the full.
> The tongue tires of even the name; the mind tires of remembering.
> And the boy sees it: the useless gifts are the ceremony — with
> one mind yoked to the lord of all, that is the highest worship.
> Then god, boy, and the river-world stand together, glowing as
> one: expansion. Then the god withdraws — neither, nor the world,
> remains. He is an actor anyway; the parts are rays; he is
> partless.

## The seal — statement

**The altar of refusals at the god-river:** base: the riverbank
with an ALTAR OF USELESS GIFTS — a tipped bath-basin, a jewel, a
folded cloth, an unlit lamp, all arranged like offerings, none
used; the river itself IS the god (luminous silver current, no
figure — formless as the text says), receiving nothing by
receiving everything (gifts poured in vanish = ceremony); above the
water, two small lights trade back and forth over the boy's head
— the name-game, you ↔ I in motion; one dimming light beside him
(the mind tired of remembering); crown: god-river, boy, and world-
hills joined by ONE unbroken fine ivory-gold line (the actor's
rays; the line is partless) — expansion, then the line's final
kink = withdrawal (the line doesn't break: partless-withdrawal ✓).

# SEAL v2 — THE INCREDIBLE VERSION (owner focus piece)
## "the gifts that sank" — composition rewritten to PERFORM the exchange

**What was wrong with v1:** it was a still-life (altar arranged,
nothing happening). The devotion-mechanism needs the MOMENT: giving
caught in the act, and — the key — **what the river gives back.**
The text's whole theology in one physics: the boy's objects go
DOWN and never return; the god's light comes UP and never keeps the
thing. Exchange without holding. That is the invisible made visible.

## The seal — statement v2

**The pour and the light-return:** the bank-ledge low; the boy
frozen at the top of his giving — basin tipped, a silver arc
pouring INTO the water, a jewel leaving his fingers mid-air catching
the last light, cloth half-unfurling as it falls, the rest of his
gifts stacked behind him waiting their turn; BELOW the waterline,
each already-sunk gift hangs at its own depth — just-sunk still
bright, deeper ones dimming to smudge (the gradient of sinking =
time-lapse of every failed offering); and from each of them, rising
back UP through the water: a thin light-ripple, converging on the
boy's chest — **the river answers with light, never with the thing
back.** The river itself: luminous silver current, no figure,
glowing strongest exactly where the ripples rise. Over his head
the two name-lights trade (you ↔ I); at his shoulder the mind-
light dims. Above the bank, boy + river-bend + dusk hills joined
by ONE unbroken ivory-gold line — expansion, partless — its far
end kinking once where the god withdraws. Sky clean.

|---|---|---|

**Correspondence axis (v2's heart):** DOWN goes matter, UP comes
light — and they never convert back (his jewel never returns; her
light never becomes an object). The X-cross where pour meets
ripple IS the worship-geometry: devotion is the meeting, not the
stuff.

# THE FINAL GIFT — seal v2.1 (owner's stroke: the boy gives HIMSELF)

**Why the figure, not the mirror (recorded):** the mirror would
stage p01v3's seen/unseen problem and need explaining; the FIGURE
of himself stages p01v2's riddle ("I am not at all") as physics —
the one object that sinks exactly like the rest, without a scrap
of pride. The parable's expansion-close REQUIRES an emptied giver:
only nothing-left-of-him lets boy-river-hills glow as one line.

## The parable's closing (new — replaces the old expansion lines)

> ...and for his last gift he had brought himself: a small figure
> of himself, carved the night before. He set it on the water, and
> it sank like the jewel — without a scrap of pride. And the light
> that came up from where it went down was not a ripple this time
> but the whole river standing: boy, water, hills, glowing as one
> line. Then the god withdrew, and there was neither boy nor god
> nor world left to glow. He's an actor anyway; the parts are
> rays; he's partless.

(Physics of the theology: objects sink → light returns; the SELF
sinks → not-a-ripple-but-everything-stands = p01v9's expansion
made of the self-gift; withdrawal = p01v9's end; partless-actor =
p01v10. "I am not at all" performed, not stated.)

# C1 — mpa-p01

## SUMMARY

Homage to the world-formed beyond the world, ever-shining self of unending power. You alone, I alone; you are, I am not; both are not — only homage. Seeking finds no self to see: whatever is seen is you alone. Recognition declared both ways: seen as self, homage to you and to me. Method questioned to its root: instrument, place, origin of dissolving. Non-duality asserted, difference explained as the wish to grasp samādhi. Speech finds no room: if even I-and-you-talk cannot stay, whence a world of words. Enough of difference-talk: devotion chewed to sap leaves all as one, at peace. Expansion holds you-me-world; withdrawal holds none. States held as own rays: shining by own will like an actor, partless in truth.

## FUNCTION

Poses identity as riddles, then dissolves the instruments of knowing one by one.

## KEY TERMS

prapañcottīrṇa (beyond-the-world as what is bowed to); tvam/aham/ubhau (you/I/both as refused positions); gaveṣita (sought-and-never-seen); samprekṣya (having-seen as recognition); kalana (measuring as what is questioned); samādhi-grahaṇecchā (wish-to-grasp as difference's only ground); vāc (words as roomless); carvaṇa (chewing as devotion's act); unmatta? no — see v12; naṭavat (actor-like, print naTavan kept).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Maṅgala and riddles passed; method, duality, speech, and devotion questioned; expansion and states surveyed.

## EXPLANATION

Homage opens beyond the world-form to the shining self of unending power. Then the riddles: you alone, I alone, both — each tried and left, till only homage stands. Seeking inside finds no self to see, only you as whatever is seen. Recognition, once declared, honors both sides. Method itself is interrogated at its root. Duality survives only as the wish to grasp samādhi. Speech cannot stay, so whence a world of words. Enough of difference-talk: devotion chewed to sap is oneness at peace. Your expansion holds all three; your withdrawal holds none. The states are yours, held as rays, shone at will like an actor — and partless in truth.

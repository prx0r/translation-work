# C1 — mpa-p02

## SUMMARY

Derived waking and sleep: his waking yours, his rest yours — all of your nature, real-unreal. Rites exhausted at the start: tired tongue, tired mind — meditation with no form, name with no quality. Summoning, seating, washing, sipping questioned: each without place or receiver. Bath, garment, scent, jewel questioned: none for stainless, world-full, unanointed, lovely. Thread, flowers, incense, lamp questioned: each without a receiver. Food, betel, circling, bowing questioned: all without a needy recipient. Lights, hymn questioned: waving-lights for self-shining, hymn for unspeakable. Sending-off, oblation questioned: no going out for the full, no difference for the fire. Fee, drink, dismissal, pardon questioned: none owed where full, content, everywhere, unseen. Highest worship is nondual mind yoked to the Lord of all, in every state, always.

## FUNCTION

Runs the kutaḥ battery across every rite, then answers all of it with one yoking.

## KEY TERMS

kutaḥ (whence/wherefore as the whole method); naṭavat (actor-like, print naTavan kept); sadasat (real-unreal as derived world); niyojayet (let-yoke as the single imperative); aikyabuddhi (nondual mind as the only instrument).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Identity riddles done, states surveyed: derivation declared, rites exhausted one by one, then yoked mind answers all.

## EXPLANATION

Waking and sleep are derived before a single rite is named: his waking is yours, his rest is yours — the world that results is real-and-unreal. Then exhaustion up front: tired tongue, tired mind, and the questions begin — meditation for the formless, names for the qualityless, and on through every implement: summoning, seating, washing, sipping, bathing, clothing, scenting, jeweling, threading, flowering, smoking, lighting, feeding, betelling, circling, bowing, waving, hymning, dismissing, burning, paying, pouring, pardoning. None finds a receiver. Then the turn that answers nineteen negations: with nondual mind, yoke mind to the Lord of all — in every state, always. That is the highest worship.

## BOUNDARY

No rite is retained; the single imperative (yoke) is not a rite among rites.

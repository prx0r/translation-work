prapañcottīrṇarūpāya namaste viśvamūrtaye | sadādivyaprakāśāya svātmane'nantaśaktaye
tvaṃ tvamevāhamevāhaṃ tvamevāsi na cāsmyaham | ahaṃ tvamityubhau na sto yatra tasmai namo namaḥ
antardehe mayā nityaṃ tvamātmā ca gaveṣitaḥ | na dṛṣṭastvaṃ nacaivātmā yacca dṛṣṭaṃ tvameva tat
bhavadbhaktasya sajñātabhavadrūpasyatha me punaḥ | tvāmātmarūpaṃ samprekṣya tubhyaṃ mahyaṃ namo namaḥ
etadgacananaipuṇyaṃ yatkartavyetimūlayā | bhavanmāyātmanastasya kena kasmin kuto layaḥ
ahaṃ tvaṃ tvamahaṃ ceti bhinnatā nāvayoḥ kvacit | samādhigrahaṇecchāyā bhedasyāvasthitirhyasau
tvamahaṃ so'yamityādi so'nantāni sadā tvayi | na labhante cāvakāśaṃ vacanāni kuto jagat
alaṃ bhedānukathayā tvadbhaktirasacarvaṇāt | sarvamekamidaṃ śāntamiti vaktuṃ ca lajjate
tvatsvarūpe jṛmbhamāṇe tvaṃ cāhaṃ cākhilaṃ jagat | jāte tasya tirodhāne na tvaṃ nāhaṃ na vai jagat
jāgratasvapnasuṣuptyādyā dhārayaṃśca nijāḥ kalāḥ | svecchayā bhāsi naṭavan niṣkalo'si ca tattvataḥḥ
tvatprabodhāt prabodho'sya tvannidrāto layo'sya yat | atastvadātmakaṃ sarvaṃ viśva sadasadātmakam
jihvā śrāntā bhavannāmni manaḥ śrāntaṃ bhavatsmṛtau | arūpasya kuto dhyāna nirguṇasya ca nāma kim
pūrṇasyāvāhanaṃ kutra sarvādhārasya cāsanam | svacchasya pādyamarghyañca śuddhasyācamanaṃ kutaḥ
nirmalasya kutaḥ snānaṃ vastraṃ viśvodarasya ca | nirlepasya kuto gandho ramyasyābharaṇaṃ kutaḥ
nirālambasyopavītaṃ puṣpaṃ nirvāsanasya ca | aghrāṇasya kuto dhūpaścakṣurhīnasya dīpakaḥ
nityatṛptasya naivedyaṃ tāmbūlaṃ ca kuto vibhoḥ | pradakṣiṇamanantasyā'dvitīyasyaya kuto natiḥ
svayaṃ prakāśamānasya kuto nīrājanaṃ vibhoḥ | vedavācāmavedyasya kutaḥ stotraṃ vidhīyate
antarbahiśca pūrṇasya kathamudvāsanaṃ bhavet | bhedahīnasya viśvatra kathaṃ ca havanaṃ bhavet
pūrṇasya dakṣiṇā kutra nityatṛptasya tarpaṇam | visarjanaṃ vyāpakasyā'pratyakṣasya kṣamāpaṇam
evameva parā pūjā sarvāvasthāsu sarvadā | aikyabuddhayā tu sarveśe mano deve niyojayet

## Structure (You-and-I dialectic → kutaḥ series → contentment)
- v1 maṅgala: homage to world-form beyond the world, ever-divine-light, self with infinite power.
- vv2-3 identity riddles: you alone are you; I alone am I; sought within the body, never seen — what is seen is you alone.
- v4 devotee's recognition: having seen you as self's form, homage to you and to me.
- v5 skill question: what finesse remains for one who knows how to go? how could what is yours dissolve, in what, from where?
- v6 non-difference: no duality between us anywhere; duality persists only as the wish to grasp samādhi.
- v7 word-failure: you-I and so-on never find room in you, always; how then the world of words?
- v8 enough of difference-talk: from tasting devotion to you, all is one, at peace — even to say so is shy.
- v9 when your form expands: you and I and the whole world; when it withdraws: neither you nor I nor world.
- v10 waking-dream-deep-sleep held as own rays: by own will you shine like an actor, yet partless in truth.
- v11 all is of your nature from your waking/sleep: the world as real-unreal.
- vv12-19 the kutaḥ (what-use) series: tongue tired of your name, mind tired of remembering you — meditation of the formless? name of the qualityless? summoning the full? seat for the all-based? foot-water for the clean? sipping for the pure? bath for the stainless? garment for the world-bellied? scent for the unanointed? ornament for the lovely? sacred thread for the prop-less? flowers for the desireless? incense for the noseless? lamp for the eyeless? food-offering for the ever-content? betel for the Lord? circumambulation for the endless, obeisance for the non-dual? waving lights for self-shining? hymn for the Veda-unspeakable? sending-off for the full inside-and-out? oblation for the difference-free everywhere? fee for the full? libation for the ever-content? dismissal for the all-pervading, pardon for the unseen?
- v20 seal: thus indeed the highest worship in all states always: with nondual mind, yoke mind to God as Lord of all.

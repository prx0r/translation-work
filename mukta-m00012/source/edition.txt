svamahimnā'nādyudayo'nāyatto namo namastasmai

vakṣye jñātaṃ guruto hitakṛtaye mandabuddhīnām

supte'pi vikṛtijāte tritayaṃ jāgarti tattvānām

saṃgṛhīte'pi jāgarti prakāśata eva

ekaṃ prasavitvayutaṃ māyātattvaṃ citā vihīnaṃ ca

kāryaprasavayuktaṃ mṛdādivadacetanaṃ ca

mukteśvaravaimalyaṃ sarvārthe dṛkkriye ca tataḥ

tadapreryatvāt tenaiva parānugrahanirvāhācca na pravartante

sakalākalaprabhedo gīto yaśca kriyāviṣayaḥ

dīkṣayā | vijñeyaḥ sa tridhāvasthaḥ kevalaḥ sakalo'malaḥ || iti

ekena hi sarveṣāṃ malena tadanādisaṃruddham

na saṃbhavati | taduktaṃ śrīmatsvāyambhuve- athānādimalaḥ puṃsām (33|1) iti

na ca śaktāste svabalaṃ vimalaṃ sarvārthagaṃ svataḥ kartum

dīkṣākhyenaiveti | etacca vistareṇoktamasmābhistattvasaṃgrahalaghuṭīkāyām

patyustvete rodhyā badhyāḥ śodhyāśca bodhyāśca

prabodhyāśca bhavanti

satyaṃ nānyā dṛk tat tādṛg yasmāt tadīśvare dṛṣṭam

vikāritvenācetanatvādanityatvādidoṣaprasaṅgaḥ syāditi bhāvaḥ

malaśaktayo vibhinnāḥ pratyātmānaṃ ca tadguṇāvarikāḥ

bhāvaḥ

pariṇativiśeṣayogāt sa kadācit kasyacit kathañcicca

padmānāmivā'niyatakāla ityāha- pariṇativiśeṣa iti

kālād guṇataśca tathā ghaṭate'sau nānyatheśato jātu

bhavati, pakṣapātādidoṣaprasaṅgāt | na cāsya sarvānugrāhakasya pakṣapātaḥ

sadyuktimad vimuktau netarathā sādhanānantyam

tasya tathā tāvadbhirnihitairvijahāti bandhatvam

nihitaiḥ sadbhirbandhatvaṃ vijahāti

śārve śāstre tāni ca netarathā yuktimanti jāyante

bījād dadāti citryaṃ niṣkṛṣya ca yaḥ sasādhanaṃ bhogam

śuddhe'dhvani śivaḥ kartā prokto'nanto'site prabhuḥ (vi0 3|27) iti

viśvaṃ ca tatra nihitaṃ pralaye tenaiva viśrāntyai

tathāvidhāṃścātmanaḥ punarbhogayogyān vidhatte

samalaṃ sa mahābaladaḥ kāruṇyāt sarvadaiva vinivṛttyai

puṃbalarodhaṃ kurvan dṛṣṭaḥ sa ca pāśaśaktisāhāyyam

bījasyeva svāpe pariṇatiriṣṭā malasya citihāneḥ

sarvātmasu no yugapat kuryāditi codayan vācyaḥ

na samādhiranekatvāt kālāntarabhogyatābalīyastvam

ce, tadetatsamādhānaṃ na yuktamityāha- na samādhiriti

īśe bīje ca yato bhavini vibhutvaṃ ca bhoktṛbhāvaśca

bījād yugapat puruṣaṃ bhojayati sarvakarmāṇi

pācayati tathaiva śambhustānīti samānamubhayatra

samānamiti

śaknoti hi yogyatvaṃ tathaiva kartuṃ maheśvaro'pīti

kuryānna yugapadityanumīyata ityarthaḥ

bhavatottaramabhidheyaṃ sthitisamaye samānakālīne

pāka eva | śeṣaṃ sugamam

yāvati kāle bhavati taddhi tathā tāvadīśena

ityāha- idaṃ tulyamiti

alamuddiṣṭamaśuddheḥ sadrūpaṃ niravaśeṣeṇa

śaktyānantyapramukhaṃ sadyukteḥ śāsanotthāyāḥ

svāyambhuvasya jaḍatāmapanetumanādikāṃ puṃbhyaḥ

saṃkṣepeṇa pratipādyata iti

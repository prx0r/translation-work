dārā udārāḥ smarayauvanābhyām

ste'laṅkriyāṃ kuryuralaṃ suputrāḥ

pautrāḥ pavitraṃ tanuyuścaritram

nivāsametaiḥ satataṃ vibhāyāt

śaṅkāḥ subhṛtyā api taṃ bhajeyuḥ

varṇādya ādyantavidhervidhiḥ syāt

raṇaṃ viśan syād vijayī kathaṃ na

pralābhavitto'stu kathaṃ nu vaiśyaḥ

kiṃ kiṃ na tadyāti nirastayatnaḥ

kāmī prakāmaṃ sa sa nāsya dūre

ragre bhavet kiṃ na sa dharma eva

kanyāpi putrīpadameva tatra

nadyo yathā kāmadughā ghaṭodhnyaḥ

yābhirhatastā vipadaḥ kathaṃ syuḥ

sadā śubhaivāsya bhavenmanīṣā

labheta samyak kuladhuryatāṃ saḥ

rakṣāṃsi taṃ draṣṭumapi kṣamāṇi

bhūtāḥ piśācā api taṃ bhajeyuḥ

pañcānanaṃ vīkṣya yathā draveyuḥ

anugrahādasya na te kathaṃ syuḥ

kiṃ naiṣa tatprītikṛtiprasiddhaḥ

kathaṃ na mucyeta yatheha muktaḥ

paśyed viyogaṃ ca sa kalpajīvī

nmūko'pi vāgīśatayeva kīraḥ

sukhaprasūtiśca na durlabhaiva

kathaṃ bhavedasya na modamānaḥ

prāptiprasiddho vasudevavat syāt

draseśvaraṃ prāśya yathā vimuktaḥ

rbālo'pi vṛddhān vijayeta kāvyaiḥ

nāntyaṃ labhetādimameva bhogam

ekākibhāvaṃ manasāpi yātaḥ

viśrāṇituṃ sā subhagā bhavānī

siddhā bhavediṣṭatamā ca pāṭhāt

svapne varasthāṃ sa ca paśyatīśīm

ānigrahānugrahayoḥ kṣamāḥ syuḥ

śobhāratī pronmiṣite dadhānāḥ

upāsate śambhumivāmaraughāḥ

santarpya nānāmṛtabhojyamukhyaiḥ

te kīrtibhājo nṛpapūjitāḥ syuḥ

potādikebhyo'pi bhavenna bhītiḥ

statpreṣyatāṃ yānti kathaṃ na bhaktyā

varāṅganā nānyakalā bhajante

yoddhā paraḥ sammukhamāgato'pi

hataujasaḥ kiṃ na pare bhaveyuḥ

kiṃ nārayastaṃ śaraṇaṃ vrajeyuḥ

bādhāṃ vidhātuṃ na na dasyavo'pi

bhūtā na vā tatra viśanti jātu

rvighnaṃ vidadhyurna kathaṃcanāpi

pākalyapohāya naveśamenam

cirādanantaṃ paramāpyamante

svargya paṭhennityasamāhitāntaḥ

mākarṇayed vākhilakarṇitehaḥ

nānandayet kamiha pūruṣataikahetuḥ

hantuṃ vāghaṃ taduditamidaṃ sarvataḥ sarvarūpam

rohaṃ rohaṃ susukhasaraṇiṃ ye'rcayantyādiśaktim

śivamayi śivasakhi bhavajabhayahari

bhaktānāṃ sā paramavaradā pūrṇamaṅgalyarūpā

kāvye nāmavilāsanāmni lalitā pūrṇaprathā ṣoḍaśī

kaulo maheśyāḥ paripūrṇabhāvam

ṣoḍaśī bhaktiḥ

stannairmalyavidhau cakorakatakakṣodāḥ sudhoddhārakāḥ

jānīmo na nasajjanasya paratā kasminnu bhāve same

vatāṃ paraṃ jātu na hastameti

sametā vicitrāḥ sadṛkṣāśca kiṃ nu

kintvajñātaḥ sa mūḍhairyadi śivakalayā vañcitāstatta eva

ta eva daurbhāgyahatā iyattvādratnākaratvaṃ kimu hīnamabdheḥ

svārāma aikyāt ka ivātra yatnaḥ

rāmānandakathā tathāpi vicitā tattānubhūtau kila

jyotiṣāṃ jyotirekastu candra eva kalānidhiḥ

eka eva jayī bhāsvān yaḥ prasiddho'mbare maṇiḥ

ratnābhijñāviditadhiṣaṇo veda kaścit svarūpam

vidanti kiṃ mūlyamalaṃ guṇaṃ vā

yo dṛṣṭvaivocchaladatirasaṃ cittasindhuṃ vidhatte

yacchaktyā sakalāntakārakṛtine kālāya tasmai namaḥ

helante svanikāraṇāya tadidaṃ na tvasya kācitkṣatiḥ

pūrṇaṃ rasaṃ pibatu nandatu nindatādvā

vidhāyādau mahākukṣiṃ sindhū ratnākaraḥ kṛtaḥ

sāhibkaulapade vilāsamuditaḥ śrīśaktināmānyanu

svarasarasālā samabhinavam

hatamatayāvā svamatalayāvā samabhinavam

vividhakalāmā kalitaśivam

namadupadhārā diśatu śivam

nīrājanāya bhātvetacchrīdevyā daṇḍakābhidham

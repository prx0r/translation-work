jānupramāṇe salile sthitvā niśi japenmanum

adhomukhīṃ khanet kuṇḍe piṣṭajāmāsanādadhaḥ

madhūcchiṣṭamayīṃ vyomni kuṇḍasyordhvaṃ pralambayet

aṅgulīsandhiprapadajaṅghorujānupāyuṣu

jātavedo mahādeva taptajāmbūnadaprabha

oṃ īśe sarvābhiḥ sarvāṇi grastayuktaṃ tvayā jagat

oṃ tamomayi mahādevi mahādevasya suvrate

oṃ durge durgādirahite durgasaṃbodhanākule

oṃ namaste daha me śatruṃ vaśamānaya caṇḍike

oṃ bhadrakāli mahātīkṣṇe bhadrasiddhipradāyini

prathamo dakṣiṇaḥ pādastatkarastadanantaram

madhyādūrdhvaṃ pañcamaḥ syādardhoṃ'śaḥ ṣaṣṭha īritaḥ

saptasaptavibhāgo vā prokteṣveṣu yathāvidhi

yajamāno dhanairdhānyaiḥ pūjayed gurumātmanaḥ

kiṃ punarmanujān bhūpān nāmādyānnarayoṣitaḥ

niveśayed yathā pūrvaṃ sādhako vidhinā tataḥ

vārāhapārāvataviṭtilakāṣṭhatryuṣaṇarāmaṭhaiḥ

mahiṣīmūtrasaṃpiṣṭaiḥ pūrvoktairlavaṇānvitaiḥ

japapūjādikaṃ kuryāt prāguvartmanā tataḥ

durgāṃ vā bhadrakālīṃ vā samabhyarcya yathāvidhi

vāmapādaṃ samārabhya dakṣiṇāṅghryavasānakam

kṛṣṇāṣṭamīṃ samārabhya yāvat kṛṣṇacaturdaśī

triḥsaptāhaprayogeṇa mārayed ripumātmanaḥ

vāruṇo varuṇā nāma ṛṣiḥ sañcalanaḥ svayam

jānudaghne jale sthitvā rajanyāṃ dhyānamāsthitaḥ

raktapuṣpaiḥ samārādhya bhadrakālīṃ spṛśan japan

tālapatre likhenmantraṃ sādhyanāmavidarbhitam

sa dāsavad bhavettasya yāvajjīvaṃ na saṃśayaḥ

prāṇāyāmatrayaṃ kṛtvā juhuyāt sumanā bhuvi

sādhyavṛkṣamaye pīṭhe samāsīno parāsane

padmapuṣpaṃ paristīrya kuśaiḥ śaramayairapi

raktavastreṇa lavaṇaiḥ kuryācca pratimāṃ śubhām

akṣarāṇi nyasettatra mantrasyātha vicakṣaṇaḥ

tāvadādāya tenaiva saṃskṛtena kareṇa tu

juhuyāt saptabhirmantrī saptadhā dhyānamāsthitaḥ

homānte balidānaṃ tu nityaṃ kuryādvijānatā

krameṇa nikṣiped bhūmau dvistrirvā sādhakottamaḥ

dattvā baliṃ japed bhūyo lavaṇādimanuṃ budhaḥ

śeṣaṃ samāpayed vidvān sa sadyo vaśameṣyati

homānte dakṣiṇāṃ bhūmiṃ hiraṇyaṃ vā yathāvidhi

yastvevaṃ kurute homaṃ guruśāstropadeśataḥ

tathākarṣaṇamicchaṃstu nābhimātrajale sthitaḥ

dhyāyennityaṃ mahāratrau japenmantramatandritaḥ

jvālāmālapratīkāśāṃ mahārātriṃ mahābhujām

bhadrakālīṃ samārādhya gandhapuṣpādibhiḥ kramāt

dhyāyennaraṃ mahādevīṃ sa sadyastvāgamiṣyati

kṣīramiśrajale kṣiptvā kvāthayed dhyānamāsthitaḥ

sāṅkuśapāśasaśūlakapālagrāmavatī bhavato'vatu nityam

pavanasakhanibhāṅgī pātu kātyāyanī vaḥ

bhavasiddhaye bhavatu bhadrakālī ciram

dadhatī daṃṣṭrogrā dhyeyā trinayanavirājitā

tilatailaṃ vimiśritya hutvā śrīmān bhaviṣyati

mātuluṅgaphalenaiva sarvaśāntirbhaved dhruvam

nimbatailena yuktena lavaṇenāśu māraṇam

kāraskarotthadhātrī syādudumbarataruḥ punaḥ

nāgarohiṇanāmānau palāśaplakṣasaṃjñakau

bakulaḥ saralaḥ sarjo vañjulaḥ panasārkakau

ātmarakṣādikaṃ sarvaṃ kuryānmantrī yathā purā

sadṛśo nāsti mantro'nyaḥ satyameva na cānyathā

parakṛtyāpraṇāśātmarakṣāṃ kurvan daśadikṣu baliṃ harenmantrairetaiḥ

nāśayatu mārayatu baliṃ tasmai prayacchatu

me īśagato yo me ūrdhvagato yo me divigato yo me pātakeneha karmaṇā

kalāvevaṃ kṛte'rīṇāmāśu kṛtyā vinaśyati

gṛhadhūmacitāṅgārau māraṇeṣu viṣāṇi ca

gṛhadhūmastrikaṭukaṃ viṣāṣṭakamudīritam

yaiḥ sādhayanti satataṃ mantriṇo nijavāñchitam

yantraṃ hutāśānilavarṇapītam

dhūmāvatī smṛtā kālī śatrunigrahakārikā

ālikhyāṣṭaviṣairetat śmaśāne nikhanenniśi

stadvāhye yamabījamagnipavanābījañca yantraṃ likhet

māmodeta tademomā rakṣa tattatkṛtakṣaraḥ

yamā pāṭa ṭamā māya māṭa moṭa ṭamo ṭamā

yamātmako'yamākhyātaḥ śloko vairivināśanaḥ

phalopahārasantuṣṭamiṣṭānāṃ tuṣṭikāraṇam

bhajāmi bhūmibhṛtkanyāṃ lālitānandanṛttanam

śrīmadvyāghrapurādhīśaṃ bhaktānetān bhajāmyaham

āgamagrantharatnodaiḥ apūrṇamaṭhamandiram

sarvāgamarahasyajñaṃ smarāmi śivayoginam

viṣyanti me hāyane ca tārtīyīkahṛtau sudhīḥ

kālahastīśvareṇātra praviṣṭhāṃ prāpitaḥ param

ekadvitricatuḥpañcaṣaṭsaptāṣṭanavādibhiḥ

anugṛhṇantu tāmetāṃ santaḥ saṃśritavatsalāḥ

ghanadṛṣṭaṃ śivajñāne tadanyatra na vidyate

sarvajñaḥ paripūrṇaśca śivo jñeya śivāgame

sūkṣmaṃ sarvagataṃ nityaṃ dhruvamavyayamīśvaram

ekaiva khalu cicchaktiḥ śivasya samavāyinī

tayā dhṛtaṃ jagatsarvaṃ ekayā'nekarūpayā

layabhogādhikāreṣu tau na tanmātrarūpiṇī

maṇṭapasyārcanaṃ caiva samitparidhi viṣṭaram

paristṛṇādimantrāntaṃ eka eva samācaret

ekena vartamānāyāṃ kriyāyāṃ karaṇaṃ vinā

vaiguṇyātkaraṇasyaiva virodhe sati yatra tu

athānyena kṛtaṃ cettu kartā nāśo na saṃśayaḥ

sahitasthāpanaṃ yatra ekadeśikamācaret

anyathā cenna kartavyaṃ rājā rāṣṭraṃ vinaśyati

paraṃ tācchivamuddiṣṭaṃ brahmaśabdena śabditam

sadā śivamanuḥ sākṣāt praṇavaṃ syājjanārdana

tasyāpi kāraṇatvena saṃsthitassa paraśśivaḥ

tasmātkāraṇabhūto yassa evā'tra śivasmṛtaḥ

parāpara vibhāgena parantu dvividhaṃ punaḥ

parañca dvividhaṃ proktaṃ sa guṇaṃ śuddhamityapi

sūkṣmarūpaṃ śivaṃ proktaṃ sthūlarūpaṃ sadāśivam

na saṅkalpena mātreṇa kulālaiḥ kriyate ghaṭaḥ

karaṇañca na śaktyasya śaktinnācetanā tathā

jñānaśakti kriyāśakti śaktidvayamiti smṛtam

śivasṛṣṭi tathā pūrvā paśusṛṣṭidvitīyakā

malairāvṛtaśuddhātmā paśusṛṣṭiḥ prakīrtitā

dṛkkriyā vyañjikā tātvī sāmānyaṃ bhuvanātmikā

laukika sthūla evokta sūkṣmaścitrātmayoginām

parañcaivāvabodhākhyaṃ aparaṃ śāstramuttamam

parāparavibhāgena patipaśvarthadarśakam

vedādyaṃ paravijñānaṃ paśupaśvarthadarśakam

tathā vilakṣaṇaṃ jñānaṃ parāpara vibhedataḥ

parokṣaṃ sthāvaraṃ prāhuḥ aparokṣantu susthiram

aparokṣaṃ muniśreṣṭhāt anuṣṭhānādbhaviṣyati

ābhogasthāyi yadvidyāt phaladvayavirodhi ca

sūkṣmasya golakāṃ viddhi tacchubhāśubhasādhanam

evaṃ sthūlaśarīrañca saṃkṣepādvarṇitaṃ mayā

pratipuṃśca niyataṃ tatvaṃ kalādyavani paścimam

saṃprāpyottaramaṃśena dehaṃ tyajati paurvikam

yonibhedāśca yogīnāṃ parakāyapraveśayet

jāgratādiyudo'vasthaḥ karmaṇā bhogabhuk sthitaḥ

kālāntaravaśādyāti mānuṣyamati durlabham

śarīraṃ dvividhaṃ proktaṃ puṃstvañca strītvameva ca

liṅgamīśamayaṃ proktaṃ yoniḥ śaktimayaṃ smṛtam

sthūlañca sūkṣmakañcaiva dvividhaṃ samayaṃ bhavet

sādhārā'tha nirādhārā sādhārā sākalasya tu

kuryānnirodhāpekṣeṇa dvidhā śaktinipātanāt

yā kriyā kriyate sādhu nirādhāreti kīrtitā

śaktyā śaṃbhuśca kurute sā dīkṣā sādhikāriṇī

tatra nirbījikā yā sā yojyā sa madhi puttrayoḥ

atha nirbījikā dīkṣā proktā sā dviprakārikā

prārabdhasañcitā gāmī karmajālaṃ viśodhyati

sa bījā ca dvidhā bhinnā prathamā śivadharmiṇī

adharmamātra saṃśuddhā dvitīyā lokadharmiṇī

śikhācchedo na cā trāsti dīkṣā sā lokadharmiṇī

janma vai dvividhaṃ proktaṃ dīkṣā praṇavasaṃjñakau

bhautikastu gṛhasthaśca nānyadīkṣāṃ samācaret

dīkṣā pratiṣṭhayoretā ubhāvapyadhikāriṇau

brahmacārī gurujñeyo gṛhasthaśca dvayārthinām

samatvaṃ karmaṇāṃ nātra nimittaṃ tadanugrahe

yāvatsonmalinī śaktiḥ śivarāgeṇa saṃyutā

dvividho'pi tirobhāvo sthānaprāpti kvacidbhavet

tadyuktasya vimokṣasya ātmanāda vikalpikaḥ

jñānābhivyaktaye vyaktaṃ vyaktaṃ tatvamaṇaṃ prati

śivarudrātmakaṃ tatra dvividhaṃ parikīrtitam

vijayādyaṣṭadaśakaṃ rudrabhedaṃ prakīrtitam

saṃhitā syāccatuṣpādaṃ yuktā kāmikapūrvikā

ātmanastu śarīraṃ syāt ātmasaṃjñā'tra kathyate

pañcabhūta samālabdhaṃ śarīraṃ sarvadehinām

bhūtāṃśeṣu śarīre'smin mitrabhāvā sthitāvapi

śuddhirmantrātmikā yasmāt tasmānmantreṇa śodhayet

vāgrūpo vācakaḥ prokto vācyastvarthātmakasmṛtaḥ

kheṭake bhūtale vāpi saurapūjāṃ samārabhet

dvividhaṃ pūjanaṃ proktaṃ sthāvaraṃ jaṃgamaṃ tathā

jaṃgamaṃ pūjayennityaṃ ātmārthe tu viśeṣataḥ

calaliṅge tu sā pekṣā sthaṇḍile nirapekṣakaḥ

citrādi pratimādīnāmarcanaṃ tattadagrake

vahnikāryaṃ dvidhā jñeyaṃ kuṇḍaṃ vā sthaṇḍilepi vā

sthaṇḍilaṃ sarvakāmārthaṃ siddhyarthañca viśeṣataḥ

sthāvaraṃ sthāpitaṃ liṅgaṃ jaṅgamaṃ dīkṣitaṃ viduḥ

tasmālliṅgadvayaṃ prājño nāvamānyeta sarvadā

maṇḍūkaṃ paramaśāyīti padaṃ tadvividhaṃ bhavet

devīnāñcaiva vāstunāṃ śubhaṃ paramaśāyikam

dvidhā bālālayaṃ proktaṃ prathamañca dvitīyakam

eteṣvanyadeśeṣu mūlasthānasya yatpurā

punaḥ prāsādakaraṇe bhinne chinne navīkṛte

yatkṛtaṃ tadvitīyākhyaṃ taruṇālayamuttamam

dvitīyākhyaṃ prakartavyaṃ māsapakṣādibhirvinā

khaḍge ca mūlabimbe ca darpaṇe pratibimbake

paṭe vā bhitticitre vā bālabiṃbārthakaṃ gṛhet

gāṇave lohaje caiva nadīprasravaṇādiṣu

saptāhantañca pakṣāhaṃ māsāntañcedghaṭasthitiḥ

pañcāhamuttamaṃ proktaṃ saptāhaṃ madhyamaṃ bhavet

nityaṃ navajalaṃ yojya pūjayetpūrvavatkramāt

devabālagṛhe vā'pi sthāpayedbālarūpiṇīm

mantrabhedo bhavettatra kriyābhedena vidyate

pṛthak bālagṛhaṃ cettu devapūrvoktamārgataḥ

devī bālapratiṣṭhā ceddevasya taruṇālaye

devīṃ vihāya devo vā devī vā kevalā yadā

samāpādyetaraṃ tasya pratiṣṭhāṃ ca samāpya ca

bālasthānaṃ vinā yatra putrapautravināśanam

yāgahomamatho vakṣye devyā saṃsthāpanottaram

kramāddevantu nirmāya saha devīṃ vidhānataḥ

pūrvaṃ devantu nirmāya devyā saha vidhānataḥ

sthāpyate vidhinā śakti sthāpanoktena yatra tu

gaurīsaṃsthāpanātpūrvaṃ śivasaṃprokṣaṇaṃ bhavet

devāgre maṇṭape sthāpya pūrvamārgeṇa deśikaḥ

nityāgamaṃtukaṃ nityaṃ dvividhaṃ saṃprakalpitam

viśeṣaṃ yajanaṃ yattat āgamaṃ nityamucyate

karṣaṇādi pratiṣṭhāntaṃ naimittikamiti smṛtam

kāmyañca dvividhaṃ proktaṃ bāhyamābhyantaraṃ tathā

abdaṃ prati viśeṣeṇa mārgaśīrṣe tu māsake

tatpūjā dvividhā jñeyā nityanaimittiketi ca

traivarṇikānāṃ kartavyaṃ upavītāni vaidikam

vāgdeviyonijātatvāt śivaputrastu kāraṇāt

svaśāstroktaprakartavyaṃ paraśāstraṃ vivarjayet

pratitaṃtravirodhena yo'rthassaṃgṛhya tatra tu

yatra yatra yathānyāyaṃ yena yena ca vartmanā

yathā virodhataḥ sarvaṃ yadi tantraṃ śivātmakam

kartavyaṃ cedanuṣṭheyaṃ śaivadharma virodhi yat

saṃgrāhya vaidikaṃ śaivaṃ śaivavākyavirodhi yat

vidyāvrata samāpte tu gṛhītvā tasya vartmanā

udāsīnassādhakaśca gṛhastho dvividho bhavet

ṛṇāni trīṇyapākṛtya tyaktvā bhāryā dhanādikam

trayāṇāmāśramāṇāntu gṛhastho yonirucyate

aputrasya jagacchūnyaṃ aputrasya kutassukham

apatnīkassapatnīko dvividhaśśivatoṣaṇe

gṛhasthasya vrataṃ yattat sapatnīkastathaiva ca

subhūmau vacane vāpi kuśe vā śayane'thavā

kṛtvā snānaṃ trikāleṣu devāgnigurupūjanam

avasthā dvividhā proktā śuddhāśuddhavibhedataḥ

bhavapradā'dhomukhī syāt ūrdhvā mokṣapradāyinī

ekaikantu dvidhā jñeya akāmākāma bhedataḥ

dviguṇaṃ svakṛte prāhuḥ prāyaścitta viśuddhaye

bhautikājjanmādhvarīṇāṃ śodhanadviguṇaṃ matam

antikañca tathādūraṃ heyopādānameva ca

saṃhṛtañca prakīrṇañca satyañcāsatyameva ca

hṛdyaṃ caiva tathā hṛdyaṃ adyaśvañca tathaiva ca

śītamuṣṇañca yatproktaṃ sukhaduḥkhaṃ tathaiva ca

rāgadveṣañca jantūnāṃ yatnālasyaṃ tathaiva ca

susthitaṃ dusthitañcaiva calañcācalameva ca

sarvametatsa eveśa tasmādanyaṃ na vidyate

paśurātmā svatantraśca cinmātro maladūṣitaḥ

ātmano vyāpiko'nantā cidrūpā śava smṛtāḥ

māyā karmamalacchinnassakalasso'bhidhīyate

mala kāmāvṛto yastu sa bhavetpralayākalaḥ

kalātatvodhva bhuvane kaivalyaṃ saṃbhavetpaśoḥ

upādānatrayaṃ etat kāraṇaṃ tamudāhṛtam

sthūla sūkṣmā paretyeva mahāmāyā bhavettridhā

sūkṣmakālādi tattvānāṃ avibhāga svarūpiṇī

maṃtrayoniḥ parā māyā nityā kuṇḍalinī tu yā

śuddhedhvani śivaḥ kartā prokto'nanto site prabhuḥ

sadāśivo maheśaśca rudraśceti tridhā matā

nimittamīśvarākhyaṃ yat taddṛṣṭaṃ saha kāraṇam

tatkāryakārikā śaktiḥ kriyākhyā sūkṣmarūpiṇī

sūkṣmā śuddhādhvanigarāṃ sthūlamiśrādhvasaṃsthitā

tatra śabdāstriyodvānaḥ trayaścārtha samīritāḥ

bhuvanādhvā ca tatvādhva kalādhvārthataḥ kramāt

sthūlātpañca bhūtāya adhiṣṭhātrī yathā tathā

sthūlaṃ bhūtaśarīrañca sūkṣmaṃ puryaṣṭakaṃ bhavet

āṇavaṃ caiva māyeyaṃ karmeyaṃ syāt malatrayam

sahajaṃ malasaṃbaddhaṃ kevalaṃ tamudāhṛtam

māyeyaṃ tadvijānīyā puṇyapāpākhyakarmajam

karmeyaṃ trividhaṃ dṛṣṭvā dṛṣṭajanmopabhogyakam

tajjanmanyeva phalado dṛṣṭabhogaṃ na mucyate

adṛṣṭavedanīyaṃ tat karmoktaṃ śivadarśane

adyātmamadhibhūtañca ādidaivikameva ca

adharmo bhedavijñeyā cittavṛttiprabhedataḥ

svāpe vibhāgamabhyeti tatsṛṣṭāvupayujyate

prārabdhaṃ sañcitaṃ karma āgāmi ca tridhā bhavet

kañcukatrividhaṃ viddhaṃ kālena kalitaṃ śanaiḥ

sṛṣṭikālaḥ sthiteḥ kāla saṃhāraśca tridhā matā

yadā śeṣasamāptantu tadāditamiti smṛtam

asamāptaṃ yadārabdhaṃ vartamānamudāhṛtam

samyak jñānaṃ tapo maunaṃ smṛtirmedhā dhṛti kṣamāḥ

darpaṃ mahadahaṃkāraṃ viṣādā priyavācakam

ālasyaṃ mohanidrā ca cāpalyaṃ hīnavṛttikam

prakṛte guṇatatvantu bhidyate na ca tatvataḥ

vibhaktaguṇasāmyantu guṇatatvamihocyate

tiryaktvaṃ tāmasaḥ prāyāt ityeṣā trividhā gatiḥ

ahaṃkāratridhā proktaṃ jīvasaṃraṃbhagarvakam

pañcakarmakṛto vāyo jīvanāya pravartakaḥ

kaḥ kartā kriyate tasya yatkṛtaṃ jagato mayā

sa ca trividhamuddiṣṭaḥ prathamastatra tejasaḥ

satvenotkṛṣṭabhāvo ya svatejasa ihocyate

bhūtānāmādi bhūtatvāt bhūtādiriti kathyate

śuddhāvasthānamātmānaṃ śivavaktuṃ na saṃśayaḥ

acetanasya māyādeḥ pravartakatayā patiḥ

sādhakānāṃ hitārthāya svecchayā gṛhyate tanum

sakalañca tathā śāstro tribhedaṃ vidyate kramāt

parāparaṃ tathā proktaṃ bhavetsakalaniṣkalam

layabhogādhikārañca tribhedaṃ vidyate kramāt

śakto pravṛttibhedena bhedastasyopacārataḥ

śivassadāśivaścaiva maheśaśca tridhā smṛtaḥ

śivādi tatvamityuktaṃ mūrtissadāśivādayaḥ

hariṇī jananī caiva rodhayantrī tridhā matā

athavā'parasaṃsthānaṃ nayedāhṛtya pudgalam

jananī jagatorjanma svabhāvasyāditi dyutiḥ

bhogeṣvapi ca tasyāpi vidadhāti yatastathā

nityasiddhi vibhorviprā yata eva svakaṃ balam

jñānaśaktyā vijānāti kriyāyā kurute jagat

tisrastu śaktayo jñeyāḥ kāraṇasyāvyayātmanā

raudrī śaktiḥ samāveśāt brahmaviṣṇvādi pūrvakaḥ

nikhilajñānayogitvāt sarvajñaḥ parameśvaraḥ

yathā vikāsayatyeva ravipadmāni bhānubhiḥ

acetanatvāt prakṛte rājñatvātpuruṣasya ca

mameva jagataḥ kartā mahādevo maheśvaraḥ

sādhyasādhana bhāvoktyā yathāvatprakaṭaṃ bhavet

śivassadāśivaśśānto guravāḥ paramā matāḥ

parāpare vare devā munayo triguruttamāḥ

pādāntairākaṇṭhādyā śivaśāstrapravartakaḥ

codako bodhakaścaiva mokṣadañca tṛtīyakaḥ

yā prerayati ca jñānaṃ śive paramakāraṇe

dīkṣāgururmokṣadastu bodhakaḥ śāstradarśakaḥ

codakādi vibhedena yathā gurvādi bhedataḥ

ācāryaśca pratiṣṭhādau paraśiṣyāvabodhane

ācāryo bālaśikṣārthamantrabodhana sadguruḥ

śāṃbhavī caiva śāktī ca mantrī ceti śivāgame

gururālokamātreṇa sparśanādbhāṣaṇādapi

śaktijñānavatī dīkṣā śiṣyadehaṃ praviśya ca

maṃtrī kriyāvatī dīkṣā kuṇḍamaṇḍalapūrvikā

mānamayāmamityuktaṃ pramāṇaṃ vistaraṃ bhavet

mānāṅgulantu prathamaṃ mātrāṅgulyadvitīyakam

paramāṇuradhaścaiva piśukeśāgra eva ca

mānāṃgulamiti proktaṃ tato mātrāṅgulaṃ śṛṇu

parvadīrghasamaṃ nāhaṃ nāhaṃ trividhamucyate

grāmādyakṣetragaṇyeṣu mānāṅgulavidhānataḥ

anyamānaṃ na kartavyaṃ kartavyaṃ cettu niṣphalam

uttamādi vibhedena dehalabdhaṃ tridhā bhavet

aṅguṣṭhānāmikā yuktaṃ vidhastiriti cocyate

prādeśaśca vidhastiśca gokarṇaśca ime trayaḥ

madhyamāṅguṣṭhasaṃyuktaṃ tattālamiti kathyate

pratimāyāstu dutsedhaṃ tālagaṇyena bhājayet

viṃśacchataṃ madhyamantu kanyasaṃ ṣoḍaśādikam

aṣṭāśataṃ catuśśataṃ madhyamaṃ kanyasaṃ tathā

aṣṭatālamidaṃ proktaṃ trividhaṃ pūrvavattataḥ

trividhaṃ daśatālena trimūrtīnāṃ prakīrtitaḥ

aṣṭatālena martyānāṃ saptatālena rakṣatām

vāmanasya tathaiva syāt catustālantu bhūtakān

aṇḍajātaṃ tathaikasya piśācānāṃ viśeṣataḥ

alpe mahati vā tulyaṃ phalamāḍhya daridrayoḥ

bhāgadvayañca dharmārthaṃ yato nityaṃ hi jīvitam

vittaśāṭhyena yaḥ kuryāt dharmaśāṭhyena vā naraḥ

prāsādaṃ trividhaṃ proktaṃ nāgarādi vibhedataḥ

kaṇṭhādārabhya vṛttaṃ yat tadvesaramiti smṛtam

sarvaṃ vai caturaśraṃ yat prāsādaṃ nāgaraṃ tvidam

nāgare nāgaraṃ liṅgaṃ vesare vesaraṃ tathā

dvijasya nāgaraṃ proktaṃ drāviḍaṃ kṣatriyasya tu

īśaḥ pitāmahaścaiva viṣṇustatrādhidaivatam

napuṃsakaṃ vesarañca tridhā prāsādamucyate

vartamānasya liṅgasya sthitaṃ prāsādakaṃ kuru

śivādhikānāṃ liṅgānāṃ āsīnaṃ tatra kārayet

pādaṃ vyavasthitaṃ proktaṃ pratibandhāsanaṃ bhavet

ārṣakaṃ sthānakaṃ caiva svāyaṃbhuvamathārhatam

daivikaṃ yānavaṃ proktamevaṃ lakṣaṇamucyate

suvṛttamānasaṃproktamaṣṭāśraṃ śayanaṃ bhavet

tridravyanirmitaṃ yattu saṅkīrṇaṃ tatprakīrtitam

tasyaiva jīrṇatā kāle tameva bandhayeddṛḍham

śirasthūpi galaṃ mañjaṃ pādādhiṣṭhānamaṃśakam

hemādi sthūpiparyantaṃ sthūlaliṅgamiti smṛtam

balipīṭhaṃ bhadraliṅgaṃ liṅgatrayamudāhṛtam

ubhayorantaraṃ nāsti tasmāditthaṃ prabhāvayet

antarliṅge śivaṃ jñeyaṃ bhāvayeddeśikottamaḥ

vimānahīne yatkarmaṃ tatkarma niṣphalaṃ bhavet

yathā tu vihitaṃ karma harmyamaṇṭapagopuram

svāyaṃbhuvaṃ bāṇaliṅga daivikaṃ cārṣakaṃ tathā

teṣāṃ liṅgavaśādvidvān prāsādaṃ parikalpayet

pūrvasmin sthāpite tasmin ajñāte pūrvamandire

trividhaṃ liṅgamityuktaṃ vyaktāvyaktobhayātmakam

jñānajyotirmayaṃ liṅgaṃ vyaktāvyaktamiti smṛtam

calaṃ nijātmani pūjyaṃ bāṇaliṅgaṃ calācalam

anantasya śiraḥ kaṃpāt kālāgneḥ kopamucyate

nirmūlañca samūlañca supūrvakamiti smṛtam

āptavākyaṃ supūrvaṃ syāt svāyambhuvamiti stridhā

karkaśaṃ suṣirādyaiśca niyataṃ dṛśyate bhuvi

śirasodvartanaṃ tadvat sūtravartanameva ca

pīṭhaprāsādikaṃ tastu liṅgamānādvidhīyate

dṛśyabhāgamadhastāttu mūlaliṅgaṃ na śodhayet

kartṛśca grāmavāsīnāṃ rājñasyānnāśayettu tat

citraṃ citrārdhakaṃ caiva citrābhāsamiti stridhā

ardhāvayava saṃdṛśyaṃ madhyacitramihocyate

madhyātmā tu yadā yasyāṃ malāditva sudustaram

śaktyantaṃ samanā śaktī kālaścaivonmanāntakam

śiṣyātmaprāṇamanasāṃ saṃyogāḥ prāṇasaṃjñakam

tatkārokāravarṇādīn saṃyogañca viyogatā

śaktiśca dvādaśāntañca daśagranthimanukramāt

nādanāḍī samāyogāt tannāḍīviṣuvaṃ matam

nādanādāntasaṃjñau vā tyaktvā brahmādiṣu kramāt

praśāntaṃ tadvijānīyāt praśānte kriyagocaram

tacchaktiviṣuvaṃ proktaṃ unmanyakālasaṃjñakam

idaṃ saptavidhaṃ dhyātvā viṣuvaṃ deśikottamaḥ

manasaḥ kāraṇātīte punarāvṛttivarjite

sadāśivasya dehasya krīḍato yāḥ puroditāḥ

yogavīryabalopeta dīkṣayā vā niyojitāḥ

sadāśivasamāssarve na caite kāraṇeśvarāḥ

sāmīpyāśśāntinilayāḥ jñānakevalino matāḥ

sādākhye caiva ye tatve saṃsthitāḥ paramāṇavaḥ

mokṣaḥ saptaprakāroyaṃ vibhāgenopavarṇitāḥ

atītaḥ paripūrṇaśca svatantraḥ sarvato mukhaḥ

pañcakṛtyāni kurvanti patyurājñā niyogataḥ

naica saṅkalpya vaiṣamyaṃ teṣāṃ tatvedhva bhāvataḥ

te'pi sthityantamāhlādaṃ prāpya yānti paraṃ padam

vindhyaśca pāriyātrāśca saptaite kulaparvatāḥ

samudrāssapta vikhyāta dviguṇācottarottarāḥ

saptadvīpāssamākhyātā samudrāntaritā mahat

kuśe hṛtkāñcanaṃ kośe svayaṃbhuvidhi yakṣati

krauñcena yudhvā suciraṃ citramāyīṃ sumāyinā

ketu nāmāgamuktasya nāmnā krauñcaṃtamucyate

priyomarāṇāṃ tatketuḥ satadākhyā nibandhanaḥ

yājyo bhadvahnikalpānāṃ maunadhyānamanokule

vavre taṃ gautamaḥ kopāt asapat somamakṣayam

tanmedasā mahicchinnā gomedassantato bhavet

tatpā sa puṣkaradvīpaḥ khyāpitassurasevitaḥ

śāke medhātithirnāma vasuśca kuśa saṃjñite

gomedhe havyavāhastu vasavaḥ puṣkaro tathā

ādityasyāśvanāmāni pratyekaṃ vacmi sāṃpratam

anuṣṭup tṛṣṭupityuktaṃ chandāṃsi harayo hare

vaidyuto mānasaścaiva suprabhobhrāṇi sapta vai

saṃvāho vivahastasmāt ūrdhvaṃ syāttu parāvahaḥ

mṛgapakṣīva paśavaḥ caturthasya sarīsṛpaḥ

ūrdhvapucchantu yaddhrasvaṃ mṛgaṃ vidyādvināyakam

dvipakṣaḥ pakṣamityāhuḥ sarīsṛpamuraplavam

sthāvaraṃ sthāpitaṃ proktaṃ devayonyajagaṃ tathā

bāhvikā dākṣiṇātyāśca saptabhāṣā prakīrtitāḥ

viprocchiṣṭañca gavyañca saptaite sthānaśuddhayaḥ

iṣṭikā ca sudhā caiva prādeśamavaṭaṃ tyajet

nivedya visarjane caiva vārisaptasu yojayet

snānācāryo phaṇīkarmī samayī samayādhipaḥ

pañcavaktrasamāyuktaṃ saptajihvā virājitam

atiraktā bahurūpā saptajihvāḥ prakīrtitāḥ

vratāṃgān saṃpravakṣyāmi yathāvadanupūrvaśaḥ

saṃdhyāmagne rūpāsañca bhikṣāṃ vai saptamaṃ viduḥ

niyantṛkāṇi dṛṣṭāṇi dīkṣākāle varānane

unmattakosidhānañca vrateśaṃ saptamaṃ smṛtam

aṣṭakā parvaṇi śrāddhaṃ śrāvaṇyāṃ śrāvaṇī tathā

ādheyañcāgnihotrañca darśaṃ caiva tataḥ param

paśubandhaḥ samuddiṣṭa sautrāmāṇiritaḥ param

agniṣṭomamamānyāgni kārye ṣoḍaśakaṃ smṛtaḥ

somayajñāḥ samākhyāta dīkṣākāle viśodhayet

saṃvare nirjaraścaiva bandhamokṣāvubhāvapi

syādvādalāñchitaścaiva sarvenaikānti tatvataḥ

sarvato bhadraliṅgañca sarvadeśikameva ca

pīṭhanāmāni vakṣyaṃte sāṃpṛtaṃ te śacīpate

tṛtīyā tu bhavedvāpi caturthī vajrasaṃjñitā

saṃvartaḥ saptamī keśa nāndikākhyā tu cāṣṭamī

hastāṅgulañca kartārastvaṣṭadhā dīrghamucyate

sarvāvayavadṛśyatvāt pratimāṇiti cocyate

dvārataḥ pādato vāpi mānārthā parikalpayet

ratnaliṅgeṣṭhabhedaṃ syāt lohajaṃ ca tathaiva ca

vajraṃ maratakaṃ nīlaṃ puṣyarāgantu ratnajam

vaiḍūryaṃ putrasaṃsidhyai sphaṭikaṃ sarvasiddhidam

ākarṣaṇaṃ syānvajrākhyaṃ puṣṭiṃ syānmaratakaṃ tathā

aṃgulādividhastyantaṃ kuryālliṅgantu ratnajam

svapramāṇena kartavyaṃ ratnaliṅgasya pīṭhikā

sauvarṇaṃ rajataṃ tāmraṃ kāṃsyaṃ caivārakūṭakam

sauvarṇaṃ śrīpradaṃ jñeyaṃ rajataṃ rājasiddhidam

uccāṭanaṃ dvārakūṭaṃ kṣapākaramathāyasam

anantaścaiva sūkṣmaśca śivoktamekanetrakaḥ

anantādi śikhaṇḍyantā naṣṭavidyeśvarānyajet

rudrabhīmo mahādevaḥ kīrtitāstvaṣṭamūrtayaḥ

teṣāmaṣṭatanurdevo dadāti paramaṃ padam

dīkṣito brāhmaṇaścandra ityete cāṣṭamūrtayaḥ

buddhirmanastvahaṅkāraḥ puryaṣṭakamudāhṛtam

kalādikañca māyedve tanuvargāṣṭakāmikā

dayā vai sarvabhūteṣu kṣāntiśca anasūyatā

akārpaṇyā spṛhāścaiva aṣṭāvātmaguṇā smṛtāḥ

śivaśāstramapi tvaṃ hi dīkṣito praviśetsadā

tatheśvaravimānasya chāyāṃ caiva na laṃghayet

nirmālyantu na bhuñjīta śivabhaktānna nindayet

atha buddhervikāso'tha sudharmeṇānudīyate

adharmaśca tathā jñānamavairāgyamanaiśvaram

aṇoradhyavasāyena prayuktā'dhyavasāyinī

vastuniścāpya bhedopi vyaktibhedātpṛthak pṛthak

āṣāḍhī daṇḍimuṇḍī ca bhārabhautī bhavāntikaḥ

śrīparvato hariścandro japyaścārdrāti keśvaraḥ

etadguhyādi guhyañcāpyaṣṭakaṃ parikīrtitam

vimalaścāṣṭahāsañca māhendraṃ bhīmamaṣṭakam

chagalaṇḍadviraṇḍañca mākoṭamaṇḍaleśvaram

pavitrāṣṭakamityete mahapuṇyavivardhanam

gokarṇo bhadrakarṇaśca svarṇākṣaścātha dīptimān

sthānāṣṭakamidaṃ jñeyaṃ rudrakṣetraṃ mahodayam

guhyādguhyāṣṭakaścaiva pavitrāṣṭaka eva ca

anugrahāya lokānāṃ liṅgabhūtāṃ pratiṣṭhitāḥ

tattīrthaṃ paramaṃ puṇyaṃ tatra snātvā śivaṃ vrajet

atra yānti mṛtāssarve rudrasya paramaṃ padam

dyānādyāpathaṃ kūpaṃ udyānaṃ daivatālayam

aṣṭā ṣaṣṭimahākṣetraṃ svayaṃbhūsthānaṃ vidhīyate

tapassamādhijapatāṃ devānāmarcantu yat

vivāhamaṣṭadhā proktaṃ śivena paramātmanā

pañcamaṃ ttvāsuraṃ proktaṃ gāndharvaṃ rākṣasaṃ tathā

vayaso vṛttisapanna vṛttārupāhi (saṃpadāṃ) jānyajāṃ

tadbrahmamiti nirdiṣṭaṃ vivāheṣūttamaṃ smṛtam

viprasya vasuśīlasya vivāhāpekṣitasya ca

vidhipūrvantu yaddattaṃ prājāpatyamiti smṛtam

atha gomithunaikaṃ vā vivāhamārṣakaṃ smṛtam

dhanaṃ datvā tu bandhubhyaḥ kanyāsaṃgrahaṇantu yat

ubhayorapi saṃvāde strīpuṃsau yadi rāgataḥ

kṛtvā yuddhamahadghoraṃ vayavandanatāṭanaiḥ

kanyāṃ suptāṃ pramattāñca cauryādapahṛtantu yat

teṣāṃ pūrvacatuṣkantu brāhmaṇasya mahātmanaḥ

garbhaṃ puṃsavanaṃ caiva sīmantaṃ jātakarma ca

etairnivṛttito devī tatasvaṃ jāyate dvijaḥ

medinī saṃgrahaṃ bālasthāpanaṃ prathameṣṭikā

saṃprokṣaṇaṃ krameṇaiva cāṣṭāṅgamiti kīrtitam

dīpañcātha nivedyañca aṣṭāṅgaṃ saṃprakīrtitam

āpakṣīra kuśāgrāṇi taṇḍulāssamanastilāḥ

āvāhanārghya pātreṣu snāne dhūpe vilepane

arkapaṅkajavilvañca naṃdyāvartañca pāṭalī

makuṭe mūrdhni kaṇṭhe ca hṛdaye nābhiguhyake

āsanaṃ mūrtimūlāṅgaiḥ netrahīnāṣṭapuṣpikā

bhogīnāṃ vyādhituṣṭānāṃ tathā dhyānārpitātmanām

anuraktaviraktānāṃ kathitā'ṣṭapuṣpikā

dhūpakāle haviṣyādau haviṣyānte tathaiva ca

rudrakṣetre hṛdā nadyāṃ kulire tu sa kehale

ghoṣo khasvanaśśabda sphoṭo nādo dhvanistathā

pratyayānāṃ tulādīnāṃ aṣṭakaṃ parikīrtitam

taṇḍulānupadaśceti divyānāmaṣṭakaṃ kramāt

vināyakaguhasthāna nadīmadhyeṣu tīrayoḥ

sarveṣāmapi kālasya dvārasūryādi vārayoḥ

pratyāhārastato dhyānaṃ dhāraṇā ca samādhikam

ahiṃsā satyamasteyaṃ brahmacaryamakalmatā

īśvarapraṇidhānañca niyamaṃ pañcadhā bhavet

kṛtvā caivakameteṣāṃ tato yogaṃ samācaret

recanādrecakaṃ proktaṃ pūraṇātpūrakaṃ tathā

indriyāṇīndriyārtheṣu pratisthāne vyavasthitaḥ

ādhāreṣu ca saṃkalpya yathā vastu svarūpakam

yogāṣṭāṅgo vijānīyāt sarvaduḥkhanivāraṇam

prākāmyañca tatheśitvaṃ vaśitvaṃ śivatatvataḥ

aṇumātraṃ labhedyasmāt aṇimetyabhidhīyate

svecchayā labhate yasmāt tasmātsā laghimā smṛtāḥ

sarvatra pūjyate yasmāt tasmātsā mahimā bhavet

prākāmyantu śarīreṣu prākāmyantu praveśitā

yadīśatvā tu tatraiva tadvīśitvaṃ vidhīyate

aṇimādyaṣṭamāsiddhiṃ labhedyogī viśeṣataḥ

nirmalatvañca nityatvaṃ sarvānugrāhakaṃ tathā

aiśvaryatvaṃ prabhutvañca śivasyāṣṭaguṇāstathā

kalākaladvimiśraṃ yat tasmātsakala niṣkalam

paraṃ niṣkalamityuktaṃ aparaṃ sakalaṃ bhavet

niṣkalantu layasthānaṃ sakalaṃtvadhikārakam

parātparaṃ yathā jñātvā sa muktāḥ pāśahā bhavet

śivaśśaktirnādabinduḥ sādākhyeśvararudrakāḥ

śivatatvātsahasrāṃśāt śaktitatvasamudbhavaḥ

tasmāccaiva sahasrāṃśāt bindutatvasamudbhavaḥ

tasmāddaśaikabhāgena īśvarasya samudbhavam

īśvarasya tu koṭyaṃśāt viṣṇoścaiva samudbhuvaḥ

īśvarassṛṣṭisaṃhāra rakṣaṇeṣu prabhusmṛtaḥ

jagatāṃ sarvasaṃhāraṃ śivena parameṣṭhinaḥ

ādhāraṃ prathamaṃ proktaṃ svādhiṣṭhāṃ dvitīyakam

pañcamantu viśuddhākhyaṃ kaṇṭhamūle tu yojayet

kodaṇḍadvayamadhyasyāt saptamaṃ jñānabiṃbakam

tadūrdhve brahmarandhraṃ syāt navādhāramihocyate

nivṛttiśca pratiṣṭhā ca vidyāśāntistathaiva ca

icchāśaktiḥ parāśakti navadhāreṣu bhāvayet

maheśvarañca sādākhyaṃ binduñca tadanantaram

marīcī bhṛgvaṃgirasaḥ pulastya pulahakratuḥ

navabrāhmaṇa ityetān purāṇeṣu vadanti hi

nāmarūpañca vai teṣāṃ payogāsanameva ca

śivāgniṃ prathamaṃ vidyāt vṛddhyāgniñca dvitīyakam

pañcamaṃ dakṣiṇāgnintu yauvanāgnintu ṣaṣṭhamam

sāmānyāgniñca navamaṃ agnibhedāḥ prakīrtitāḥ

sadāśivasya hṛdayaṃ śivaśakti svarūpakam

śivaśaktiparā yogāt mātāśaktipitāmahaḥ

pratiṣṭhotsavakāleṣu dīkṣākāle tathaiva ca

nityāgniṃ yojayedvṛddhamanyatrāpi yajedbudhaḥ

pitṛyukteṣu kṛtyeṣu dakṣiṇāgniṃ prakalpayet

śaivāgniṃ kalpayedvipra śṛṇu tasmin samāhitaḥ

suprabhā caiva kṛṣṇā ca raktā cānyā hiraṇmayī

devako pitṛyā gārhapatyaṃ syāddakṣiṇānalam

raktavarṇadvinetrañca ekavaktraṃ caturbhujam

tripādayuktaṃ teṣvekaṃ kuñcitordhva vyavasthitam

sṛksruvau dvitayaṃ haste dhāraṇantu praśasyate

evaṃ gārhapatyāgneḥ svarūpaṃ kathyate budhaḥ

dakṣiṇāgne sahasrāṃśāt udbhūtasya caturmukha

trimekhalasamāyuktaṃ ṣaṇṇetraṃ piṅgalaprabham

saptajihvā samāyuktaṃ dakṣavāmakrameṇa tu

tomaraṃ vyajanañcaiva ghṛtapātrantu vāmake

dvimukhaṃ ṣaḍbhujaṃ sūtraṃ sitañcāsitavaktrakam

raudradṛṣṭaṃ mahākāyaṃ kiñcitprahasitānanam

varadaṃ kūrcakāpālaṃ vāmāṃśe dhāriṇaṃ punaḥ

gārhapatyo gṛhasthānāṃ pitṝṇāṃ dakṣiṇānalam

bālaśca yauvano vṛddhaḥ krameṇa parikīrtitāḥ

dakṣiṇāgneḥ sahasrāṃśāt kevalāgnisamudbhavaḥ

śuklavastraparīdhānaṃ śvetavarṇasamadyutiḥ

kevalāgne sahasrāṃśāt mahānasa samudbhavaḥ

raktāṃbarasamāyukta striśikhā ca samanvitaḥ

prasāritakaropetā sitāṅgo darvihastakaḥ

śṛṅgārahāsakaruṇa vīraraudrabhayānakaḥ

śṛṅgāro jāyate hāsye raudrāśca karuṇārasaḥ

śāntā svatantra ityukto rasabhāvastu nartakaiḥ

ānīyamānasvādyatvaṃ sthāyībhāvo rasasmṛtaḥ

saṃdhyātāṇḍavasaṃjñantu munigrahaṇamataḥ param

tripuratāṇḍavaṃ paścāt bhujaṅgalalitaṃ tathā

nirmālyodvāsanārambhe snānāraṃbhe tadantake

nṛttānte ca mahāghaṇṭāṃ navakāle tu tāḍayet

veṇumudgaśca śāliśca daśadravyamihocyate

aghoreṇa caruṃ hutvā lājaṃ ca puruṣeṇa tu

tilānvai śikhayā hutvā vaiṇavaṃ kavacena tu

aṣṭottaraśataṃ vāpi pañcāśatpañcaviṃśatiḥ

dravyānte caiva mantrānte vyāhṛtiṃ juhuyādbudhaḥ

prāsādamantraṃ japtvā tu stotraṃ japtvā viśeṣataḥ

kalāṅgulottamaṃ proktaṃ samidāyāmalakṣaṇam

uttamādi krameṇaiva ghṛtāhutimathācaret

srugāvaṭena saṃpūrṇaṃ pūrṇāhutimathācaret

tato vahniṃ samabhyarcya pariṣicyārthamāpayet

tasya putrāstu śiṣyo vā kuryādanyatra homayet

prāṇopānaśca vyānaśca udānassamanāya ca

daśa vāyuriti jñeya tathā nāḍī daśa śṛṇu

gāndhārī hastijihvā ca pūṣā caiva yaśasvinī

tasya madhye sthito jīvaḥ sūryakoṭisamaprabhaḥ

teṣāṃ madhye sthito devaḥ sūryāyutasamaprabhaḥ

suṣumnā madhyame nāḍyāṃ yāvadaṅguṣṭhakāstathā

pūṣā vai dakṣiṇe karṇe yaśasvī vāmakarṇake

śaṅkhinī nāḍimadhye tu daśanāḍī vyavasthitaḥ

cakravadvyāvṛto yasmāt nāḍī cakrī mihocyate

ghaṇṭānāgañca vahniñca kṣurikā ca daśāyudhaḥ

bhūtāvabhūtamānena kārayetsarvamānakaiḥ

punarhi śrotumicchāmi sārātsārataraṃ param

vadame vastu sadbhāvaṃ prasādaṃ kuru śūla bhṛt

tadarthaṃ saṃpravakṣyāmi tavasnehānmaheśvari

yo sau śānto hyajo devaḥ śaktyādhāro hyavigrahaḥ

p. 68b) ca caiṣa paramohaṃsaḥ sa vai devaḥ sanātanaḥ

sadāśivaḥ paraṃdhāma tārakaṃ brahma eva ca

ajñānaṃ caiva tatkṣetraṃ jñānināmavrataḥ sthitam

gurvavasthā gamaḥ so hi dṛśyate na tu lakṣyate

sa bāhyābhyantarastho hi spandate sarvadehinām

avyakto vyaktarūpaśca agrāhyaśca calācalaḥ

tejorūpī hi bhagavānhṛdvyomnevasthitaḥ paraḥ

śuddha sphaṭikasaṅkāśaṃ bhinnaṃ vyomni tu vyomavat

bhramareṇa pratīkāśaṃ hakāramaśirāhatam

siddhoti nūnaṃ tat tasmiñciva puṣpāṇi paśyati

nānākāra vibhāgena dṛśyate parameśvaraḥ

sadvyomajyotirūposau nadate sa muhurmuhuḥ

aghaṃśamaya te devi na kvacidyoni sambhavaḥ

tasya cāpaścimaṃjanma haṃso dṛśyo varānane

kṣīyante cāsya karmāṇi tasmin dṛṣṭe parāpare

pṛthivyāṃ nāsti tad dravyaṃ yad datvā cāriṇī * ? vet

kālacihnaparijñānaṃ nādhogatiṣu yoginām

taṃ dṛṣṭvāpi ciraṃ devi punarjanma na vidyate

rakto yadā tanurvidyātpīte cārthasya sambhavaḥ

subhikṣaṃ ca pracāre ca durbhikṣaṃ cañcu bandhane

añjaliṃ vāripūrṇāyāṃ bhāvayeta vicakṣaṇaḥ

somasūyavibhāgena ahorātraṃ nirīkṣayet

pūrve dakṣiṇamāvasyāṃ praviśedravimaṇḍale

ekaḥ kālasya paryāyaṃ kathitaṃ tava sundari

etaddhi saṃśayaṃ brūhi atrakautūhalaṃ mama

yanna kasya cidākhyātaṃ tat te vakṣyāmi suvrate

ṭha madhyame gatohaṃsaścintanīyaḥ prayatnataḥ

avyayā sarvagā sūkṣmā parā cāsya parākalā

kālo na kalayeccātra na jarāvyādhireva ca

guptosānnidhyamicchanti mātaraḥ sādhakeśvari

dātavyaṃ bhakti yuktānāṃ gurubhaktiratātmanām

yo guruḥ sa śivaḥ sākṣāt pūjanīyaḥ prayatnataḥ

tasya jñānaṃ ca mokṣaṃ ca siddhyate paramārthataḥ

valīpalitanirmuktaḥ ṣaṇmāsena bhaviṣyati

haṃsasāraṃ paraṃ brahma paraṃ vyoma paraḥ śivaḥ

yataste paśavaḥ kīṭā mahāmārgabahiṣkṛtāḥ

anāśvastā asantuṣṭāḥ paśuśāstreṣu bhāvitāḥ

oghasarvasvamatulaṃ guruvaktre samāgatam

gurubhaktāya śāntāya nityābhyāsaratāya ca

samayā sevine nityaṃ kulakālodite śive

yataḥ svarasmirūpasthāḥ pīṭhebhyaḥ pīṭhavigrahaḥ

rakṣantitāḥ kṛpāviṣṭāḥ samayācārapālakāḥ

vikāsayanti paramaṃ tejo yadvāntasambhṛtam

pratyakṣarūpo vīrāṇāṃ sa bāhyābhyantaraṃ sthitāḥ

rahasyaṃ jñeyagamanaṃ bhūmiratramagāhyagā

kathitaṃ sārasarvasvamumayāsaharaṃ param

tasmāt sarvaprayatnena na deyaṃ yasya kasyacit

sarvaśāstrabahirbhūtaṃ hṛdayaṃ yoginī kule

labdhabodhodayā nandaṃ vande saṃsthānamātmanaḥ

gṛhyate pañcabhistaśca cakṣurādi bhirindriyaiḥ

tatpralīnaṃ bhavettasya yasya sarvaṃ pralīyate

tadāgama mayādbhraṃśa bhayāt sṛṣṭī kṛte mayā

yastuto na tatāssanti niścityai tacchamaṃ vrajet

nābudbudo na peṣaṃ vāna śarīre kvacit sthitam

kṣāya?kṣayivināśāntaṃ taśca naivopalabhyate

pṛthak sarvaṃ yathā dṛṣṭaṃ kvaśarīre mana sthitam

na ca sā vastuto yuktyā kasya cedvā vabhāsate

guṇī yadi bhavet kaścid bhavanti vahavo guṇāḥ

cañcalaṃ manaso rūpaṃ niścityai tannibodhayet

ayatnena nirodhoyamasmābhirupadiśyate

anyasyā grahaṇāccittaṃ svātmanyeva praśāmyati

tadāśraya vaśāccittaṃ tasmin kṣīṇe praśāmyati

ekāgraṃ bhāvayet tāvad yāvallīnaṃ nirodhakṛt

vilīnānnāsmaret paścādātmabhāvopavṛṃhitāḥ || 16 |||

grahaṇā deva pūrvoyamidānīṃ saṃpradṛśyate

p. 246b) pararāgātmahāniḥ syāddveṣasyo pakṣayāttathā

budhavat sarvarāgī syāt sarvaduṣṭaśca bhairavaḥ

pralīnendriya vṛttistu kevalā bhyudayodayaḥ

nistabdhendriya vṛttestu nibodhaḥ saṃprajāyate

dhyāyate vā kimasyāntaḥ pratyante kevalaṃ bhavet

viṣayā grahaṇāt sarva śirodhaḥ saṃprajāyate

nissaṅkalpa manovṛtteḥ paramātmā prakāśate

vicāryaivaṃ prayatnena nirāgāre praśāmyati

na ca citte sthito vāyurna vāyau cittamāsthitam

pūrayettā yathā śaktirbhavet pūrṇo nirāśrayaḥ

p. 147b) punastasyānu sandhānāt kṣaṇāt kaivalyamāpnuyāt

suniścita mateḥ kṣipraṃ nirodhaḥ saṃprajāyate

taṃ dṛṣṭyā mokṣamāpnoti yena tad vatsa badhyate

tasmāt saṃviditaṃ sarvamiti saṃvinmayo bhavet

tathaiva kṣīṇavṛttiḥ syādapāne vilayaṃ gate

binduṣaṭkaparityāgācchāntabodhaḥ kṣaṇād bhavet

dvādaśānte mahāśaktiḥ siddhairuktā sukhāgame

puryaṣṭaka vibhedepi tathaiva manaso layaḥ

stambhita prāṇavṛttaistu nirodhaḥ saṃprajāyate

līyamāne ratānando nistaraṅgaḥ kṣaṇaṃ bhavet

ānanda nirbharaṃ cittaṃ nivṛttiṃ labhate kṣaṇāt

p. 248b) sāvadhānasya tajjñānaṃ kṣīṇabodhaḥ prajāyate

dṛśyānāṃ ṣāḍavādīnāṃ svalolāgre sthitiṃ kuru

mālatyādiṣu gandhāṃśca tathaiva paribhāvayet

itthaṃ pratikṣaṇaṃ yasya cittamātmani līyate

kṛtā vāmana dattena svabodhodaryāmañjarī

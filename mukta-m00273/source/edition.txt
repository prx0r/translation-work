jyotirūpaṃ paraṃ divyaṃ pralayāmbu pūritam

sākṣāt śivamayaṃ kṛtvā na kiṃcidapi cintayet

niṣkalaṃ śivamātattvaṃ tanmayāmāmalābhakam

taddhyānaṃ tajjapaṃ pūjā homakāryanna sādhanam

sannyāsī sarvakarmāṇi śivameva tu bhāvayet

tiṣṭhan gacchan svapan jāgran strīsaṅge bhojane'pi ca

garvakrodhamanobhāvo jñānājñāna iti bhrame

gurupādāt paraṃ nāsti ātmalābhāt parodayam

sṛṣṭyādi ātmalābhāntaṃ daśabhedaṃ kramocyate

gururūpaṃ paraṃ jñānaṃ gururūpaṃ paraṃ śivam

dhyānarūpo guro mūrti pūjābhāvo gurotpadam

guruḥ pitā gururmātā sarvaṃ gurumayaṃ viduḥ

śaivarājendratatvajño śivabhāvena rājitam

śāstrārthaṃ guruvākyaṃ ca svānubhūtyā samaṃ viduḥ

śrīmūla śivapādārthadarśanaṃ tatprabhāvataḥ

svapneṣu dṛśyate sarvaṃ yatkāryaṃ hṛdi saṃsthitam

rākṣasaṃ dravasāmīraṃ saumyaṃ śivamanonmanī

śatrūṇāṃ duṣṭa satvānāṃ te lakṣaṃ kurute sadā

śatrūnvijayate sarvānte lakṣāścaiva mohitān

ṣaḍetābhirjapenmantrī bhedinī nābhiyojitā

kuṣṭhādīnāṃ tu vyādhīnāṃ pāpānāñcaiva nāśanam

ekayā vajratuṇḍā tu astu sanmukhamāgatam

yāmyavyomyottaradale vāyavye cāpare khaga

śākinī grahavetālā bhūtāḥ pretāśca rākṣasāḥ

ānayet pārśvato mantrīkṛtakāryaṃ vimokṣayet

ṣaḍguṇāhomite mantrī samahometha gocaraḥ

uttaraṃ vyomagaṃ yāmyamagnigaṃ pūrvamīśagam

ānayedurvaśīñcāpi dahyamānāṃ na saṃśayaḥ

yogayukto yadā mantrī siddhārthe śatahomataḥ

uttaraṃ vyomagaṃ śaktirgaruḍo viṣṇupadmajam

vidveṣayetkṣaṇādevaṃ kāmadeva samaḥ priyaḥ

itistu saptabhirbījaiḥ rakṣatāṅgaṃ salakṣaṇam

vajrārgalapurasthaṃ tu śatamaṣṭottarañjapet

āruhya śavavaktre tu kṣīraṃ bhuktvā vrajettataḥ

devāgni guruve datvā sahāyyebhyaśca sādhakaḥ

tacchavamakṣayaṃ divyaṃñjātarūpamayaṃ bhavet

vyutkramāt khecaro mantrī vajratuṇḍāllabhedasim

rakṣadravamaruttejo bhedanaṃ pṛthivī śiraṣī

uttiṣṭhanti na sandehaḥ kare gṛhyatadotpatet

yasya devasya yatproktaṃ tadārūhya jayo bhavet

siddhyate tatpuraṃ tasya yasya devasya vāhanam

parāstra mālabhenmantrītamāruhya samutpatet

marutkoṣṭha samopetānmānayen mṛgapakṣiṇaḥ

ekāṃ vai vā likhet kanyāṃgairikārocanātha vā

praṇītāṃ kanyasāṃ vāmā ūrddhvāṃ kṛtvā supīḍitām

vihvalā tatkṣaṇādeva niḥśaṅkā mūḍhacetasā

dakṣiṇāṃ kanyasāṃ pīḍya dakṣiṇena kramedvṛti

brahmāviṣṇuḥ suparṇaśca śaktirīśaḥ sadāśivaḥ

svaliṅgasūtranāryāyāḥ prathamaṃ taddine budhaḥ

niśācaro yadā mantrī niśāyāṃ sādhanaṃ bhavet

śyāmākataṇḍulāndatvā pāpaṃ saṃsādhayet khaga

utpatet tatkṣaṇādeva svacchandaṃ krīḍate khaga

yadyaccintayate dravyaṃ tattadānayate kṣaṇāt

rudraḥ sadāśivaḥścāṣṭau karālīhyagni yogataḥ

trisandhyaṃ tridinañcaiva cakravartti samaṃ nṛpam

bhedanamaindrañca chedanaṃ rākṣasādhipam

nīlārdra paṭamāvṛtya karālyā sahitañjapet

śmaśānakarpaṭemantrī śaktibhinnamadhorddhvataḥ

dakṣiṇenāgni mārgasthaṃ paṭoyāṣadviśuṣyati

jalārdraṃ triguṇe naiva brahmāviṣṇurumādayaḥ

sadāśiveśaśaktiścaśca suparṇo viṣṇupadmajaḥ

vyāpayet sakalaṃ viśvaṃ vāyunāvyomagena tu

nāsāgne tu parānāḍī indragopakarānnibhā

gatvā somapuraṃ yāvajjapen mantraśataṃ budhaḥ

lakṣaṇādvaktra madhye tu amṛtaṃ karṣayet mukham

kramāt kramādidaṃ yogaṃmabhyasennityameva ca

cakravarttitvamāpnoti mahākalpāyuṣo bhavet

sarkarāṣṭā tathā cāṣṭau ekaikaṃ tu śanaiḥ śatam

japedvyaṣṭaśataṃ mantrī aṣṭabījaistadeva tu

āsutṛṇajalaukāśca śalabhāyāḥ sarīsṛpāḥ

pañcānanaṃ śilāpaṭṭe vajrasaṃpuṭalāñchitam

śmaśānāṅgāralikhitaṃ tālakena samanvitam

volomā tvaritāṃ likhyabhedanaṃ karṇikopari

veṣṭayeccakravegā yā brahmavetālinī punaḥ

dūtikāṣṭau samālikhya garbhe tu catusrake

bhedanañcakravegāyā madhye yojyaṃ khageśvara

dakṣiṇe kavacaṃ lekhyaṃ netraṃ paścimadiggatam

pūrvapaścimadiktaṃsthaṃ dakṣiṇottaradiggatam

pūrvāgre vajratuṇḍā tu śabarī caiva dakṣiṇe

īśāgnistha vajrasya yā rekhā tu vinirgatā

karālīṃ tu samālikhya nair-ṛtyāṃ vāyavottagā

raudrīntatra samālikhya viparītākṣarairbudhaḥ

dvitīye tu śilāpṛṣṭhe punareva samālikhet

lepayitvā tu śiktena pūjayedvidhinā tataḥ

niḥkṣipejjalamadhye tu bhūmau vāpi nikhātayet

stambhayet parasainyāni mohayedbandhayet punaḥ

vajrārgalena saṃruddhvā bhayatrastāḥ suvihvalāḥ

śatravaḥ sukhasādhyāśca indratulyā mahābalāḥ

manastambhaṃ vaktrastambhaṃ gatistambhaṃ bhaveddhruvam

yo jānāti khageśedaṃ sa ripuṃ vijayiṣyati

ghaṭāgnyāyaviṣaṃ koṣaṃ stambhayennātra saṃśayaḥ

kramākṣare niyuktā tu vidyā pañcānane pure

śikhipittena vā likhya śuddhañcā śuddhatāṃ nayet

tatra smairmantrabījaistu rakṣāṃ dehe tu kārayet

akālamṛttyu dahanīrājopadravanāśanī

daṃṣṭriṇāñcaripūnāñca dhvaṃsanī tu surakṣiṇī

saubhāgyakaraṇī nityaṃ dhāryā khageśvara

vandhyānāṃ putradāhyeṣāsau bhāgyakaraṇī bhavet

sādhayet sarvakramāṇi vidhiṃ vijñāya tattvataḥ

punastu dūtyāvaraṇaṃ tato mantrāstu siddhidāḥ

pañcānanapure vidyāṃ pañcabhāgena cālikhet

yatra mākramate tena tataḥ kṣipraṃ prasiddhyati

mudrāstu caiva saṃyojya tataḥ karmāṇi kārayet

vāmayā caiva saṃyojya śavarī ca karālinī

kapilā caiva raudrī ca cakravegā tṛtīyakā

vajratuṇḍā jvālinī ca vajratuṇḍe niyojayet

plavaṅgī manovegā ca praṇīt mayāṃ niyojayet

cakravegā sa bījānāṃ brahmavetālinīṃ nyaset

sannaddhaṃ pañcamudrābhiḥ stvaritā bhedaniścayaḥ

sādhayet sarvatsiddhīniyogahīnopi sādhakaḥ

prathamaṃ brahmarandhre tu śatañvāraṃ niyojayet

somaṃ dvibhiḥ susaṃruddhaṃ sūryamekena rodhayet

dehasthaṃ tatkṣaṇādeva ātmasthena pareṇa tu

gacchate pahnanālena vāyunā caiva niṣkramet

ādau pretasya kartavyaṃ karmacaitatkhageśvara

bhedanyā bhedayedvāyuṃviśet parapuraṃ sukham

chinnaṃ bhinnaṃ mahāstraistu nayate yatra rocate

bhogāśakta praviṣṭastu krīḍate yāvadrocate

anenaiva prayogeṇa śrotraṃ śiṣṇe nirodhayet

nirodhaṃ vā gude deśe bhedanaṃ hṛdi vinyaset

kṣaṇenotkramaṇaṃ kṛtvā ātmanaśca parasya vā

yaṃ gatvā na nivarteta sarvavyāpī śivo bhavet

punastrikamagnimārge somamārge punastrikam

tārakaṃ hṛdaye sthāne yojayecchiṣyadehataḥ

kṣaṇenotthāpanaṃ kuryād dṛṣṭvā sarvamidaṃ jagat

vaddhvā tu bhedinī mudrāṃ recayitvā samīraṇam

viśate granthimārge tu bhidyate nātra saṃśayaḥ

kaṭinābhi hṛdikaṇṭhe vaktre bhūmadhyamūrddhani

granthibhedaṃ tu hṛddeśe yāvat kurvanti mantriṇaḥ

tālagranthi vibhedena mantriṇaśca khageśvara

udgiret sarvaśāstrāṇi divyā vāṇī pravartate

paśyate bhuvanān sarvānātmanā dehamadhyataḥ

niṣkalaḥ paramaḥ sūkṣmaḥ pañcabhūta vyavasthitaḥ

dvipañcaguṇite naiva svacchandaḥ sarvago bhavet

mūrddhvā mūrddhni gato vīra ekānte vijanesthitaḥ

svarūpeṇa svadehena japet sādhaka puṅgavaḥ

mudrā mūrddhni gato mantrī praṇītā japamācaret

āgacchanti mahāguhyāḥ siddhayogeśvareśvarāḥ

hṛdaye guhyāṅgāṇāṃ nāmastṛkṣyusya jalena tu

siddhiṃ manepsitāt kāmāṁllabhate nātra saṃśayaḥ

labdhānujño japenmantraṃ sādhakaśca na saṃśayaḥ

vajraṃ putraṃ mahāvīra khecaratvaṃ sudurlabham

praṇītāṃ bandhayen mūrddhni daṇḍavadva sudhātale

yāgaṃ kṛtvā tu pūrvoktaṃ darśane darśane sudhīḥ

meṣājaṃ mahiṣaṃ nāraṃ lakṣe pūrṇe'gni madhyataḥ

tataśca hṛṣṛ manasā arghyaṃ dadyāt subhāṣitaḥ

uktavatī tadā devī sādhuputra mahātapaḥ

khecaratvaṃ mahāsiddhiṃ sahāyasahito vraja

tejo rāśiṃ mahādīpto vrajate yatra rocate

asthi carmāvaśeṣaṃ tu śuṣkapiṇḍāśca dehinaḥ

tepyardha binduṃ saṃspṛṣṭāstapaḥ śaktyopabṛṃhitāḥ

divyārūpadharo bhūtvā trinetra mukuṭojvalā

krīḍante sarvalokeṣu śivaśaktyopabṛhitāḥ

kalauyugepi pakṣīśa siddhidā nātrasaṃśayaḥ

tīkṣṇā bhayāvahā caṇḍā ugrā saṃkṣipta siddhidā

troṭaletantrasāre tu navavījāḥ parāḥ smṛtāḥ

navavīja vidhiṃ jñātvā bhedaṃ bhedena bheditam

tribhirbījaiśca dvātriṃśaccaturbhiraṣṭa eva tu

saptabījairbhavedaṣṭa aṣṭabhiśca turo bhavet

dūtībhiścāṣṭa karmāṇi eva maṣṭottaraṃ śatam

aṣṭottara karmaśataṃ vastuvindati deśikaḥ

sādhayet pararāṣṭrāṇi rājyaśca pṛthivī tale

yaḥ smarettvaritā vidyāṃ jñānato'jñānatopi vā

sasnātaḥ sarvatīrtheṣu sarvayajñaphalaṃ labhet

yatkiñcidvāṅmayaṃ sarvamaśeṣa bhuvanāntikam

yena jñātā parā vidyā sarvagā kāmagā śubhā

anyatantre mayā tārkṣya bhrāmitā mantramudrayā

troṭalaṃ ye vijānanti bhedabhinnamanekadhā

mānuṣāṇāṃ hitārthāya anugraharatā sadā

vandhāḥ pūjyāścānumodyāmānyāste nityameva tu

niḥsaṃdigdhaṃ tantrārthaṃ khyāpitaṃ vidhinā sphuṭam

rahasyaṃ paramaṃ hyetat sarvajñānottamaṃ khaga

dātavyaṃ tu suśiṣyāya bhaktāya ca tapasvine

mūlavidyā tu vidyāṅgāḥ sapta eva prakīrtitāḥ

ekaṃ pañcānanaṃ viṃśacaturbhiradhikāni ca

śataiḥ saptabhirākhyātā paṭalairnavabhistathā

* * * * ścalaulyaṃ ca ṣaḍbhiryogo vinaśyati

janasaṃgaparityāgāt ṣaḍbhiryogaḥ prasidhyati

aṃgīkṛtānyāsanāni kathyaṃte kāni cinmayā

tat kuryādāsanasthairyamārogyaṃ cāṃgalāghavaṃ

ṛjukāyaḥ samāsīta svastikaṃ tat pracakṣyate

dakṣiṇe tu tathā savyaṃ gomukhaṃ gomukhaṃ yathā

itarasmistathā coruṃ vīrāsanamitīritaṃ

kūrmāsanaṃ bhavedetaditi yogavido viduḥ

vinyasya bhūmau saṃsthāpya vyomasthaḥ kukkuṭāsanaṃ

śeve kūrmavaduttānametaduttānakūrmakaṃ

dhanurākarṣaṇaṃ kṛtvā dhanurāsanamīritaṃ

pragṛhya tiṣṭhan parivartitāṃgaḥ śrīmatsyanāthoditamāsanaṃ syāt

jānū parinyastalalāṭa deśoviśedidaṃ paścimatā na vāhuḥ

udaraṃ jaṭharānalasya kuryādudare kārśpamarogatāṃ ca puṃsāṃ

uccāsanodaṃḍa vadutthitaḥ khemāyūra metatpravadaṃti pīṭhaṃ

kālakūṭaṃ

sarvāsana bhrāṃti haraṃ citta vibhrāṃti sādhanaṃ

tebhyaścatuṣkamādāya sārabhūtaṃ vravīmyahaṃ

śreṣṭhaṃ tatrāpi ca sakhe tiṣṭha siddhāsane sadā

bhedajanakaṃ siddhāsanaṃ procyate

gulphāṃtaraṃ ca nikṣipya siddhāsanamidaṃ bhavet

muktāprāṇaṃ vadatyanye prāhurguptāsanaṃ pare

mukhyaṃ sarvāsaneṣvekaṃ siddhāḥ siddhāsanaṃ viduḥ

dvāsaptati sahasreṣu suṣumṇāmiva nāḍiṣu

sadā siddhāsanābhyāsādyoginīpyaṃti māpnuyāt

prāṇānile sāvadhānaṃ vaddhe kevala kuṃbhataḥ

athekasminneva dṛḍhaṃ vaddhe siddhāsane sadā

na khecarī samā mudrā na nāda sadṛśolayaḥ

vināśakāriyamināṃ padmāsanaṃ procyate

ūru madhye tayottānau pāṇī kṛtvā tato dṛḍhau

uttabhya civukaṃ vakṣasphacchāpya pavanaṃ śanaiḥ

durlabhaṃ yena kenāpi dhīmatā labhyate bhuvi

prāṇāmupaiti vodhamatulaṃ śaktipramāvādataḥ

mārutaṃ dhārayedyastu samukto nātra saṃśayaḥ

dakṣiṇe savyagulpha ca dakṣagulphaṃ ca savyake

vyātta vaktro nirīkṣena nāsāgaṃ susamāhitaḥ

vaṃdhasya saṃdhānaṃ kurute cāsanottamaṃ

pārśvapādau ca pāṇibhyāṃ dṛḍhaṃ vadhvā suniścalaṃ

gorakṣāsanamidaṃ vai prāhuste siddhayoginaḥ

athābhyasennāḍi śuddhaṃ mudrādi pavana kriyāṃ

atha nādānusaṃdhānamabhyāsānukramo haṭhe

avdādūrdhvaṃ bhavet siddho nātrakāryāvicāraṇā

bhujyate śivasaṃprītyai mitāhāraḥ sa ucyate

atilavaṇāsavapalalaṃkadaśanaśākotkaṭapiṣṭaṃ

prātaḥ snānopavāsādi kāyaṃ kleśa vidhiṃ tathā

pathyaṃ

manobhilaṣitaṃ yogyaṃ yogī bhojanamācaret

abhyāsāt siddhimāpnoti sarvayogeṣvataṃdritaḥ

sarvopyayaṃ haṭhābhyāso rājayoga phalāvadhiḥ

gurūpadiṣṭamārgeṇa prāṇāyāmān samabhyaset

yogī sthāṇutvamāpnoti tato vāyuṃ nirodhayet

cidanyaḥ

maraṇaṃ tasya niḥkrāṃtistanovāyuṃ nirodhayet

kathaṃ syādunmanī bhāvaḥ kāyasiddhiḥ kathaṃ bhavet

tadaiva jāyate yogī prāṇasaṃgrahaṇe kṣamaḥ

yathā suṣumṇā nāḍīsthā malāśeṣaṃ prayāṃti ca

dhārayitvā yathāśaktirbhūyaḥ sūryeṇa recayet

vidhivat kuṃbhakaṃ kṛtvā punaścaṃdreṇa recayet

recayecca tatonyevaśanaireva na vegataḥ

pītvā piṃgalayā samīraṇamatho vaddhvā tyajedvāmayā

śuddhānāḍī gaṇā bhavati yamināṃ māsatrayādūrdhvataḥ

uttiṣṭhatyuttame prāṇarodhe padmāsanaṃ mahuḥ

dṛḍhatā laghunā vāpi tena gātrasya jāyate

tatobhyāsedṛḍhībhūtena tathā niyamagrahaḥ

tathaiva sādhito vāyuranyathā haṃti sādhakaṃ

ayuktābhyāsa yogeṇa sarvarogasamudbhavaḥ

bhavaṃti vividhā doṣāḥ pavanasya vyatikramāt

yuktaṃ yuktaṃ ca vadhnīyādevaṃ śuddhimavāpnuyāt

kāyasya kṛśatākāṃtistadājāyata niścitaṃ

nādābhivyaktirārogyaṃ jāyate nāḍi śodhanāt

anyastu nācaret tāni doṣāṇāṃ samabhāgikaḥ

kapāli bhāti caitāni ṣaṭkarmāṇi pracakṣate

vicitraguṇasaṃdhāyi pūjaye stuti puṃgavaiḥ

tataḥ pratyāhareccaitaduditaṃ dhauti karma tat

dhauti karma prabhāvena gacchaṃtyeva na saṃśayaḥ

ādhārākuṃcana kuryā kṣālanaṃvasti karmakṛt

vastikarmaprabhāveṇa vāryaṃte sa kalāmayāḥ

aśeṣa doṣopacayaṃmihanyādabhyasya mānaṃ jalavasti karma

kramaparicaya vaśya vāyumārgā gajakaraṇīti gadyate haṭhajñaiḥ

naśāmukhānnirgamayeccaiṣānīti siddhairnigadyate

jānūrdhvajātarogaughaṃ jagatyāśu satīnati

aśru saṃpātaparyaṃtamācāryaistrāṭakaṃ smṛtaṃ

yatnataḥ trāṭakaṃ gopyaṃ yathā hāṭakapeṭakaṃ

natāṃso bhramayed deṣānauligauḍaiḥ prakāśyate

aśeṣadoṣāmaya śoṣaṇī ca haṭhakriyā mauliriyaṃ ca naulī

kapālabhātī vikhyātā kaphadoṣo viśoṣiṇī

prāṇāyāmaṃ tataḥ kuryādanāyāsena sidhyati

ācāryāṇāṃ tu keṣāṃ cidanyat karma na saṃmataṃ

abhūvannaṃtakabhayāstasmāt pavanamabhyaset

samanaskoṃtakabhayaṃ tasmāt pavanamabhyaset

yāvat paścodbhruvormadhyaṃ tāvat kālabhayaṃ kutaḥ

suṣumṇā vadanaṃ bhitvā mukhādviśati mārutaḥ

yo manaḥ susthirī bhāvaḥ saiṣāvasthā manonmanī

vicitra kuṃbhakābhyāsādvicitrāṃ siddhimāpnuyāt

bhastrikā bhramarī mūrchā kevalaścāṣṭakuṃbhakāḥ

kuṃbhakāṃte recakādau kartavyaḥ sūḍiyāṇakaḥ

madhye paścimatānena syāt prāpyo brahmanāḍigaḥ

yogījarā vinirmuktaḥ ṣoḍaśīvayasā bhavet

dakṣanāḍyā samākṛṣya bahisthaṃ pavanaṃ śanaiḥ

tataḥ śanairvāmanāḍyā recayet pavanaṃ śanaiḥ

punaḥ punaridaṃ kāryaṃ sūryabhedenamuttamaṃ

yathālagati kaṃṭhāttu hṛdayāvadhi sasvanaṃ

śleṣadoṣaharaṃ caivaṃ dehānalavivardhanaṃ

gacchatāṃ gacchatāṃ kāryamujjāpyākhyaṃ tu kuṃbhakaṃ

evamabhyāsa yogena kāmadevo dvitīyakaḥ

na kṣudhā na tṛṣā nidrā naivālasya prajāyate

anena vidhinā nityaṃ yogīṃdro bhuvimaṇḍale

aṣṭhāddūrdhva bhavet tasya sarvaroga parikṣayaḥ

śanaistu ghrāṇaraṃdhrābhyāṃ recayedanilaṃdhiyā

viṣāṇī śītalīnāma kuṃbhakāyaṃ nihaṃti ca

padmāsanaṃ bhavet samyak sarvapāpa pranāśanaṃ

mukha niyamya yatnena prāṇaṃ ghrāṇena recayet

vegena pūrayet kiṃciddhṛdayāvadhi mārutaṃ

yathaivalohakārāṇāṃ bhastrāvegeṇa cālpate

dhārayennāsikāṃ madhya tarjanībhyāṃ vinā dṛḍhaiḥ

vātapittaśleṣmaharaṃ dehānalavivardhanaṃ

brahmanāḍī mukhāṃtastha kaphādyārgalanāśanaṃ

viśeṣeṇa tu kartavyaṃ bhastrākhyaṃ kuṃbhakaṃ tvidaṃ

yogīṃdrāṇāmevamabhyāsa yogāccittejātākā cidānaṃdamūrchā

recayen mūrchanākhyeyaṃ manomūrchā sukhapradā

payasyagādhepi sukhāt plavate padmapatravat

sahitaḥ kevalaśceti kuṃbhako vividho mataḥ

yāvat kevalasiddhiḥ syāt tāvadevaṃ samabhyaset

prāṇāyāmo yamityuyuktaḥ savai kevalakuṃbhakaḥ

na kiṃtu durlabhaṃ tasyā triṣu lokeṣu vidyate

rājayogapadaṃ samyak labhate nātra saṃśayaḥ

na sidhyaṃti tato yugmamāniḥṣyanteḥ samabhyaset

evamabhyāsa yogena rājayogapadaṃ vrajet

anargalā suṣumnā ca haṭhasiddhiśca jāyate

arogatā viṃdujayogni dīrghatā bhaveyuretāni kṛtetra kuṃbhake

sarveṣāṃ yogataṃtrāṇāṃ tathāraudho hi kuṃḍalī

tadā sarvāṇi padmāṇi bhidyate graṃthayopi ca

yata cittaṃ nirālambaṃ tathā kālasya vaṃcanaṃ

brahmadvāramukhe suptāṃ mudrābhyāsaṃ samabhyaset

ūḍīyāṇo mūlavaṃdho vaṃdhojālaṃ dharābhidhaḥ

idaṃ hi mudrā daśakaṃ jarāmaraṇanāśakaṃ

vallabhaṃ sarvasiddhānāṃ durlabhaṃ marutānapi

kasyacinnaiva vaktavyaṃ kulastrī surataṃ yathā

pādaṃ prasāritaṃ kṛtvā karābhyāṃ dhārayed dṛḍhaṃ

yathā daṃḍahataḥ sarpo daṃḍākāraḥ prajāyate

tadāsau maraṇāvasthā jāyate dvipuṭāśritā

iyaṃ khalu mahāmudrā mahāsiddhaiḥ praśasyate

mahāmudrā ca te naiva vadaṃti vivudhottamāḥ

yāvatulyā bhavet saṃkhyā tato mudrāṃ visarjayet

api bhuktaṃ rasayoraṃ * pīyūṣamivajīryate

tasya rogāḥ kṣayaṃ yāṃti mahāmudrāṃ ca yo bhyaset

gopanīyā prayatnena na deyā yasya kasyacit

vāmorūpari saṃsthāpya dakṣiṇaṃ caraṇaṃ tathā

niḥpīḍyayonimākucyamanormadye niyojayet

savyāṃge ca samabhyasya gakṣāṃge punarabhyaset

rājadaṃtadvayaṃ tatra jihvayāttaṃ bhayediti

kālapāśa mahāvaṃdho vimocana vicakṣaṇaḥ

triveṇī saṃgamaṃ dhatte kedāraṃ prāpayenmanāḥ

mahāmudrā mahāvaṃdhau niḥphalau vidhivarjitau

vāyūnāṃgati māvṛtya nibhaṃtaṃ kaṇṭhamudrayā

jaṃghā dvayaṃ samākṛṣya vāyuḥ sphurati satvaraṃ

mṛtāvasthā samutpannā tato vāyuṃ virecayet

valīpalita vedhaghnaḥ kathyate sādhakottamaiḥ

vahni vṛddhikaraṃ ciṃtadaṇimādiśunapradaṃ

puṇyasaṃbhārasaṃbhāvi pāpaughabhiduraṃ tathā

vahnistrīpatha sevānāṃmādau varjana mācaret

tāvadyāvat bhrūmadhyaṃ rasanāspṛśati khecarī

bhruvoraṃtargatā dṛṣṭirmudrā bhavati khecarī

kṣaṇena mucyate vyādhi jarāmṛtyu bharādikaiḥ

na ca mūrchā bhavet tasya yo mudrāvetti khecarī

vādhyatena sakālena yasya mudrāsti khecarī

tenaiṣā khecarī nāma mudrāsiddhairnamaskṛtā

na tasya kṣarate viṃduḥ kāminyā śleṣitasya ca

nayedūrdhvaṃ haṭhācchaktyā nivaddhe yoni mudrayā

māsārdheṇa na saṃdeho mṛtyuṃ jayati yogavit

takṣakenāpi daṣṭasya viṣaṃ tasya na sarpati

tathā somakalāpūrṇaṃ dehī dehaṃ na muṃcati

kulīnaṃ tamahaṃmanye tvitare kulaghātakaḥ

gomāṃsa bhakṣaṇaṃ tattu mahāpātaka nāśanaṃ

caṃdrādravati yaḥ sāraḥ sasyādamaravāruṇī

rasanāniyamya vivare śaktiṃ parāṃ ciṃtayan

nirdoṣaḥ samṛṇālakomalatanuryogī ciraṃ jīvati

syādamaratvamaṣṭaguṇitaṃ siddhāṃgaṇākarṣaṇaṃ

eko devo nirālaṃvo ekāvasthā manonmanī

tadvadhnīyāsukaraṇamathonānyathākāya siddhiḥ

apānamūrdhvamākṛṣya mūlavaṃdho nigadyate

ākuṃcanena taṃ prāhurmūlavaṃdhaṃ ca yoginaḥ

vāraṃvāraṃ yathā cordhvaṃ samoyāti samīraṇaḥ

gatvā yogasya saṃsiddhaṃ gacchato nātra saṃśayaḥ

yuvā bhavati vṛddhopi satataṃ mūlavandhanāt

tadānala sikhaṃ dīrghāvardhate vāyunāhatā

daṃḍāhatā bhujagīvaniḥśvasya ṛtujā vrajet

tasmānnityaṃ mūlavaṃdhaḥ kartavyo yogibhiḥ sadā

tasmāduḍyāpākākhyoyaṃ yogibhiḥ samudāhṛtaḥ

uḍyāṇaṃ tu tadeva syāt tatra vaṃdhobhidhīyate

uḍḍiyāṇo hyayaṃ vaṃdho mṛtyumātaṃga kesarī

abhyasedaniśaṃ yogī vṛddhopi taruṇo bhavet

ṣaṇmāsābhyāsayogeṇa jarāmṛtyuṃ na saṃśayaḥ

gulphadeśa samīpe ca kaṃpaṃ tatra prapīḍyate

śanaiḥ śanairyathā prāṇaḥ skaṃdhasaṃdhi na gacchati

uḍiyāṇe kṛte vaṃdhe muktiḥ svābhāvikīṃ bhavet

vaṃdho jālāṃdharākhyoyaṃ mṛtyormṛtyuparo mataḥ

tato jālādhano vaṃdhaḥ kaṃṭha duḥkhaughanāśanaṃ

na pīyūṣaṃ pacatyagnau na ca vāyuḥ pradhāvati

madhya cakramidaṃ jñeyaṃ ṣoḍaśādhāravaṃdhanaṃ

sarveṣāṃ yogataṃtrāṇāṃ sādhanaṃ yogināṃ viduḥ

madhye paścimatāne syāt prāṇaraṃdhre brahmaraṃdhragaḥ

iḍā ca piṃgalā vadhvā vāhayet paścimaṃ payaṃ

tato na jāyate mṛtyurjarārogādikaṃ tathā

karaṇaṃ viparītākhyaṃ guruvākyena labhyate

āhāro vahulastasya saṃpādyaḥ sādhakasya tu

adhaḥ śiraścorddhapādaḥ kṣaṇaṃ syāt prathame dine

balitaṃ palitaṃ caiva ṣaṇmāsārdhena duṣyate

svecchayā vartamānopi yogoktairniyamairvinā

tatra vastu dvayaṃ manye durlabhaṃ yasya kasyacit

mohanairna śanaiḥ samyagūrdhvā kuṃcanamabhyaset

yatnataḥ śarajālena phūtkāraṃ kajjakaṃdharaṃ

cakritanijaviṃduṃ ca ūrdhvamākṛṣya rakṣayet

maraṇaṃ viṃdupātena jīvitaṃ viṃdudhāraṇāt

sugaṃdhiryogino deho jāyate viṃdudhāraṇāt

cittāyattaṃ nṛṇāṃ śukraṃ śukrāyattaṃ ca jīvati

ṛtumatyārajopyevaṃ nijaviṃduṃ ca rakṣayet

ayaṃ yogaḥ puṇyavatāṃ dhanyānāṃ tatvaśālināṃ

sahajoliśrāmarolirvajjālpā eva vodhataḥ

vajjolīmehanādūrdhvaṃ strīpuṃso svāṃga lepanaṃ

sahajauliriyaṃ proktā śraddhayā yogibhiḥ sadā

puṃso viṃdu samākuṃcya samyagabhyāsa pāṭavān

yadi nārī rajo rakṣedvajjolyā sahayoginī

tasyāḥ śarīre nādastu viṃdunāmava gacchati

vajjolyābhyāsa yogena sarvasiddhiṃ prakurvate

dehasiddhiṃ ca labhate vajjolyābhyāsayogataḥ

tasmāt puṇya vatāmeva hyayaṃ yogaḥ prasidhyati

kuṃḍalyaruṃdhatī hyeva śabdāparyāya vācakāḥ

kuṃḍalinyā tathā yogī mokṣadvāraṃ vibhedaye

mukhenācchādyate dvāraṃ prasuptā parameśvarī

vaṃdhanā yaṃtramūḍhānāṃ yogināṃ mokṣadāyikā

sā śaktiścālitā yena samukto nātra saṃśayaḥ

valātkāreṇa gṛhṇīyāt tadviṣṇoḥ paramaṃ padāṃ

nidrāṃ vihāya sā ṛjvī urdhvamuttiṣṭhate haṭhāt

prasūrya sūryāt paridhāna yuktā pragṛhya nityaṃ paricālanīyā

mṛdulaṃ dhavalaṃ proktaṃ ceṣṭatāṃvara lakṣaṇaṃ

mṛtyucakra gatasyāpi tasya mṛtyubhayaṃ kutaḥ

vitaraṃ chinnadrumaskaṃdhavat

evamabhyāsato nityaṃ yaminaḥ śaṃkate yamaḥ

evamabhyāsa yuktasya śaminoyamina kutaḥ

ūrdhvamākṛṣyate kiṃcit suṣumnāgata kuṃḍalī

jahāti tasmāt prāṇoyaṃ suṣumnāṃ vrajati svataḥ

yasmāt saṃcālenāśu yogī rogairvimucyate

kimatra vahunoktena kālaṃ jayati līlayā

maṃḍalāt dṛśyate siddhiḥ kuṃḍalyabhyāsayogataḥ

tadvāreduttamāge tu divyadṛṣṭipradāyakaṃ

kutaḥ prakṣālanopāyaḥ kuṃḍalyābhyāsanā dṛte

karaṇe sarvasiddhīnāmekaikāpi kṣamaiva sā

rājayogaṃ vinā mudrā vicitrāpi na rājate

itasrana ca kartavyā manovṛttirmaṇīṣiṇā

āsanaṃ prāṇasaṃyāma mudrābhiḥ sakalā bhavet

rudrāṇī vā parāmudrābhadrāṃ siddhiṃ prayacchati

sa eva suguruḥ svāmī sākṣādīśvara eva saḥ

aṇimādi guṇaiḥ soyaṃ jāyate kālavaṃcakaḥ

niraṃjanaṃ padaṃ yāṃti nityaṃ yatra parāyaṇāḥ

mārutasya layo nāthastaṃ nātha mahamāśraye

niśceṣṭo nirvikāraśca layo jayati yogināṃ

svāvargalabhyaye jñāpi jīvatyā caṃdratārakaṃ

vedaśāstrapurāṇāni sāmānya gaṇikā iva

aṃtarlakṣaṃ vahirdṛṣṭirnimeṣonmeṣavarjitā

vivarjitaṃ sphurati yat tatvaṃ padaṃ śāṃbhavaṃ

bhinne ca sravate caṃdrādamṛtaṃ divyarūpiṇaḥ

tatrāsti karaṇaṃ divyaṃ sūryasya paripaṃthi ca

idaṃ sarvātmanā śiṣyai rūpāsīta tato guruṃ

nādānusaṃdhānakamekamevamanyāmahemukhyatamaṃ layānāṃ

śṛṇuyāṃ dakṣiṇe karṇe nādamaṃtasthamekadhīḥ

nāda prakartinaṃ cittaṃ nādena saha sāmyati

ekībhūyādatha saha cidākāśena līyate

unmanīkaraṇaṃ sadyonāda evāvadhāryate

nādasyaivānu saṃvaṃdhā dvidhā cittaṃ pralīyate

niṣpattiḥ sarvayogeṣu yogāvasthā bhavaṃtitāḥ

vicitra tatkṣaṇādehe sarvataḥ śrūyate dhvaniḥ

saṃpūrṇe hṛdayaṃ śūnye tvāraṃbho yogino bhavet

dṛḍhāsano bhavedyogī jñānādeva samestathā

atiśūnye mardalasya bherīśabdastathā bhavet

mahāśūnyaṃ tadā yāti sarvasiddhi samāśrayaḥ

sukhaduḥkha jarāmṛtyu kṣudhānidrā vivarjitaḥ

ekabhūtaṃ tadā cittaṃ rājayogābhidhānakaṃ

astu vāmastu vā śakti gātre vā khaṃḍitaṃ mahat

sevakān karṣakān manye prayāse phalavarjitān

unmanī kalpalatikā sadya eva bhaviṣyati

makaraiḥ paṃcarahitaḥ śuṣkācāraḥ prakīrtitaḥ

pūrvāmnāyeṣu kathitā nānā taṃtre ca devatāḥ

viśeṣāt phaladā devi sādhakānāṃ jitātmanām

sarvāmnāyāśca kathitāstava pārśve pṛthak pṛthak

gopyādgopyataro guhyo guhyādguhyataro mahān

yadivettvat samāloke nārī bhavati niścalā

bhrāṃti māyāti rahitastadā sādhayituṃ priye

āgamācārastu priye sarvadā gopanaḥ smṛtaḥ

sādhakāstu kalau devi māyā nidrādi saṃyutaḥ

ahaṃ vuddhiratā nityaṃ tasmādgopyādviśeṣataḥ

śraṣṭhebhaḥ sādhakebhyaśca na prakāśyaṃ kadācana

siddhihānistu labhate sadā tasya prakāśanāt

uttarāmnāye yā mālā kathitā bhuvidurlabhā

atha varṇamayī mālā alābhe hastayarvajā

sphāṭikyāśca abhāve tu rudrākṣa phalanirmitā

āsanānyuttarāmnāye kathitāni śubhānane

yatra pūjā abhāve tu pratimāsu prapūjayet

tāmre vā lekhayitvā tu nityaṃ vā sarpajet priye

sarvatra sarvapūjāṃsu mūle naiva prapūjayet

sarvadravyādya bhāve tu ārdrakaṃ tu prakīrtitam

makāra paṃcābhāve tu paṃcamaṃ kārayet priye

evaṃ śrutvā tato devi samayācāramuttamaṃ

śivopyaṃtaḥ prasannobhūt svayaṃ ca parameśvaraḥ

sarvāṇyāgama taṃtrāṇi yāni yānyapi suṃdari

caturyugeṣu vartaṃte pārapāyopadeśataḥ

kṛpayā tava deveśi kiṃcit kiṃ varṇitaṃ śubhaṃ

etatsarvaṃ tu niyataṃ sarvasvamida me priye

ṣaḍāmnāyeṣu kathitaṃ taṃtraṃ paramadurlabhaṃ

yadi cedvai varārohe mayā tubhyaṃ prakāśitaṃ

atastu gopayennityaṃ yaditvaṃ mama vallabhā

śrīprathamaṃkarāṃvujagataṃ pātraṃ viśuddhipradaṃ

dvitīyamadhunā naṃdaika saṃvardhanaṃ

pātramahaṃ tṛtīyamadhunā vijñānavodhakṣamaṃ

paṃcamakāratatva sahitaṃ pātraṃ caturthaṃ namaḥ

sadoditakaraṃ pātraṃ bhaje paṃcamaṃ

tarpaṇādravarasaṃpātraṃ bhaje ṣaṣṭhamaṃ

pūraṇe natvarayānandādikaṃ pātumān

cāṣṭamamaṣṭasiddhikaraṇaṃ prauḍhaṃ prasannaṃ sadā

yannavanātha śaktijananaṃ prauḍhaḥ pratāpoditaṃ

tasya vighnasahaśrāṇi saṃbhavanti na saṃsayaḥ

yogīsvarastute vijñe anye tena madhārakāḥ

ekādaśīvrataṃ dāna śaktyā ca śivapūjanaṃ

vairāgya na matiryoge bhavate bhavavarjite

guhāyā puṇyadeśe tu devatāyatanepi vā

kalpanā kalpite puṇye dhyānasiddhiḥ prajāyate

sudhūpa dhūpitaṃ kṛtvā tatra yogaṃ samabhyaset

gurupādāmbujaṃ natvā māṃ ca dhyātvā sadāśivaṃ

niṣedhavarjitaṃ saumyaṃ cittasyā hlādakārakaṃ

tatra siddhāsanaṃ vaddhvā rajukāyaṃ ca dhārayet

gurūpadiṣṭamārgeṇa prāṇāyāmaṃ samabhyaset

recayed vai dakṣiṇayātayāpi saṃprapūrayet

evamabhyāsayogena nāḍī śuddhi prajāyate

yatheṣṭadhāraṇaṃ vāyornāḍī śuddhā ca labhyate

tasmāt sarva prayatnena kartavyaṃ nāḍi śodhanaṃ

candrasūryadvayormadhye cintayet parameśvaraṃ

suṣumnāntargataṃ devaṃ bhāvagamyaṃ sadāśivaṃ

ṣaṭcakra bhedanāt samyak nānyathā bhāvagocaraṃ

samāno maṇipūraṃ ca udāno nāhataṃ smṛtaṃ

ṣaṣṭamambujasaṃsthaṃ ca vyomarūpamudāhṛtaṃ

tayā sarvāṇi cakrāṇi bhāvyaṃte dehamadhyataḥ

ṣaṭdalaṃ caturaṃ caiva ṣaṭcakraṃ mātṛkodbhavaṃ

prāṇacakre valāṃ sāni akṣarāṇi śuśobhane

kaṭhāntā dvādaśā proktā udānacakramāśritāḥ

vyomacakre sthitau dvau tu haṃsaprāṇasvarūpakau

prāṇāpāna dvayorgraṃthiḥ praveśe nirgame sthitaḥ

ṣaṭcakraṃ kīrtitaṃ devī yena vaṃdhaḥ prajāyate

bhidyate prāṇacakreṇa prāṇacakraṃ śivātmakaṃ

tasmāt ṣaṭcakra bhedena yogī brahma padaṃ vrajet

apānasaṃsthitaṃ cakramādhāraṃ sūryasaṃjñakaṃ

samāne tu tṛtīyena samānojātavedase

taṃ nābhi maṃḍale cakre procyate maṇi pūrakaṃ

anāhata bhudānākhyaṃ cakraṃ cāntarasaṃsthitaṃ

ājñācakraṃ tu ṣaṣṭaṃ vai vyomarūpamudāhṛtaṃ

ṣaṭcakrabhedataḥ prāṇo nānyosti cakra bhedakaḥ

daśāraṃ maṇipūraṃ ca dvādaśāramanāhataṃ

ṣaṭcakraṃ prāṇamuddiśyaṃ prāṇopānena bhidyate

prāṇacakrasvarūpo hi svayuṃbhettābhavet tu saḥ

kaivalyaṃ śāsvataṃ brahma iti vedavido vruvan

brahmajñānaparaṃ devī samūḍharakṣitaṃ mayā

tāvat karma prabhāvoyaṃ yāvadātmāna vidyate

devānāṃ durlabhaṃ tatva mayopyeṣu na dāpayet

strīlaṃpaṭā durācārā kṛtaghnā vakavṛttayaḥ

hetukā vādinaścaiva teṣāṃ jñānaṃ pragopayet

pāpiṣṭaṃ tadviyānīyāt dūrataḥ parivarjayet

eteṣu jñānasarvasvaṃ na deyaṃ paramārthataḥ

yo vai yogastu sā dīkṣā yā dīkṣā kathitā mayā

dīkṣākathanaṃ nāma aṣṭamaḥ paṭalaḥ

anukrāmāṃ kramo nāsti pratyayo dṛśyate prabho

vicāraḥ kīdṛśastasya yena mokṣaḥ prajāyate

svarūpaṃ sa guṇaṃ cātra nirguṇaṃ ca sadāśiva

tathā brahmamayaṃ deva kiṃ cānyat parikīrtitaṃ

kīdṛśī dhāraṇā sā tu procyate vṛṣabhadhvajaḥ

kodhikārī varṇamadhye ko mantraḥ kasya pūjanaṃ

uttaraṃ dehi sarvajñaṃ satyamajñāna mocakaṃ

ajñānatamasohaṃtā jñānadīpa namastute

garbhavāsa bhavaṃ duḥkhaṃ asahyaṃ chettu marhasi

antaraṃgāḥ śramā devī kathyante śāstramadhyataḥ

niraṃjanaṃ tṛtīyaṃ tu caturthaṃ tu nirālayaṃ

yajñakarmapravese tu kuṃbhake dānakarma ca

etat sarvaṃ mayākhyātaṃ kāmyakarmādikaṃ priye

brahmacārī sūdadohā sūdadohāśca dīkṣitāḥ

daśāre brahmacārī ca dvādaśāre gṛhī tathā

śarīrasyāśramo nāsti sūdadohāśramī bhavet

sarvāśramasthitaḥ sopi svakriyocittakarmaṇāṃ

sūdadohāḥ prasiddhoyaṃ ṛgvede viddhi pārvati

vijñātaḥ puruṣohaṃsaḥ kṣetrajño dehadhārakaḥ

sūdadohāparaṃ brahma paramātma svayaṃ hariḥ

sopi māyā svarūpeṇa nānāyoni gata prabhuḥ

nirguṇopi guṇādhīno māyayā śṛṣṭi hetukaḥ

saguṇopi guṇātītastvabhyāsād bhavate tu saḥ

niyatasya tu saṃnyāsaḥ karmaṇaḥ saṃprapadyate

tasmādādhārayogena sūdadohāḥ praśasyate

kecijjānanti tatvajñāḥ saṃnyāsadharmamāśritā

hṛdaye granthi bhedena guṇātīto yadā bhavet

kaṣāya vasanaścaiva saṃnyāsī daṇḍadhāraṇāt

vāhyaliṃgo na mokṣasti vāntaraṃmuktidaṃ smṛtaṃ

vāgmanaḥ karmadaṇḍaśca tridaṇḍī puruṣo bhavet

jīvanārthaṃ ca tadvaiṇaṃ śarīre mukti sādhanaṃ

avyakta liṃgo vicared yogā hyāsaparoyatiḥ

sa saṃnyāsī na vaktavyaḥ saṃnyāsī yogatatparaḥ

sadāśiva parohaṃso jñānajñeyati vismṛtāḥ

grahasthovānaprastho vā yatiryogādvinā na hi

āśramasya phalaṃ muktiḥ karmaṇānopapadyate

tatkarmasvātmarūpaṃ hi yoginaparyupāsate

yadyapi syānnirākṣepaṃ tadvyāpi phaladaṃ bhavet

karmaṇā phala bhoktavyaṃ brahmādīnāṃ kṛtaṃ sphuṭaṃ

saṃsārataruśākhāstu varddhate tena karmaṇāḥ

manyante mandasatayaḥ pravartanteva bhaktibhiḥ

śāstrābhimāninaḥ kecit kecit tīrthābhamāninaḥ

bhūtābhimāninaḥ kecit kecinnāmābhimāninaḥ

tapobhimāninaḥ kecit keciddānābhimāninaḥ

yogamārgaṃ na jānanti yena siddhāḥ śivaṃ gatāḥ

pravṛtyā ca nivṛtyā ca dvaitantadguṇavaddhitaṃ

tasmādduḥkhaṃ sarvanidaṃ karmatyājyaṃ mataṃ budhaiḥ

na smaraṃti paraṃ tatvamavidyāśavalīyasī

yogaṃ tyaktvānya dharmeṣu pravṛttirjāyate nṛṇāṃ

daiva hīnāstute martyāḥ sukhopāyaṃ na vindatī

utpattisthitisaṃhārā nasyante jñānayogataḥ

yasmin brahmaṇi saṃlīnaṃ anāyāsena vai dhruvaṃ

durācārāḥ sa pāpiṣṭo dānte narakaṃ vrajet

dharmātmā puṇyakīrtiśca sa narodeva pūjitaḥ

siddhiyonasasāyujyamasiddhena surālayaṃ

śrūyatāṃ śraddhayā devī samāsena vadāmyahaṃ

sucīnāṃ śrīmatāṃ gehe yogabhraṣṭobhijāyate

etaddhi durlabhararaṃ loke janmayadīdṛśaṃ

yatate ca tato bhūyaḥ saṃsiddhau kurunandana

stutvā nārāyaṇaṃ devaṃ yoge dṛḍhamatistadā

tasya mukti na saṃdehaḥ sāyujyaṃ labhate priye

yayāsandhārito yogī svakukṣau jñānavarddhanaḥ

yena sā vijitā loke sa dhanyaḥ puruṣo mataḥ

jñānaṃ parama ṇirvāṇaṃ sa naraḥ svātma bhāvayet

sarvāśramaphalaṃ tasya yogasya hṛdisaṃsthitaḥ

dharmātmopi vijñeyo yogābhyāsarato muniḥ

tepi muktiṃ prāpnuvanti procyate śruti madhyataḥ

yajanaṃ yājanaṃ puṇyaṃ purāṇādhyayanaṃ tathā

ṣaṭkarma sādhanaṃ muktiḥ satyaṃ tatkathayāmite

maṇipūre cādhyayanaṃ madhyāpanamanāhate

idaṃ tu niścitaṃ devi karmamokṣāya yatpriye

buddhināśo mānuṣāṇāṃ jāyate tena karmaṇāḥ

brāhmaṇaḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyāḥ śūdraiḥ cānye striyādibhiḥ

varṇānāmāśramānāṃ ca sevyaṃ brahmasanātanaṃ

prathamaṃ sādhanaṃ cātra satāṃsaṃgaḥ prakathyate

saṃsāraṃ patitānāṃ ca satsaṃgo hyupa kṛnnṛṇāṃ

tasmāt saṃgaḥ prakartavyaḥ sātvikai mokṣakāṃkṣibhiḥ

kasthamamṛtaṃ tyaktvā bhikṣāmaṭasidurmatiḥ

brahmajñānaṃ gurorāsyāṃ prāpyate yannivartakaṃ

gopitaṃ sarvatantreṣu karmavistāra hetave

ye ca karma rathārūḍhāste gacchanti bhavārṇave

yadi tadviguṇaṃ karma bhavate narakāya ca

yadi bhraṣṭo bhaved yogād brahmādīnāṃ padaṃ labhet

yogasyācaraṇaṃ nūnaṃ sulabhaṃ sukhadaṃ priye

aprasannā mahāmāyā kuṇḍalī yā harestanū

jaghanyaḥ sopi vijñeyo yo vai yoga nirākṛtaḥ

durācārāśca te martyāḥ sadācārepi saṃsthitā

socyutaḥ prāpyate devi yogābhyāsena nānyathā

phalamāśramiṇāṃ yogo muktisteṣāṃ gati smṛtāḥ

yogaprāpti kutasteṣāmajñānāvṛtacetasāṃ

hitvārcāṃ bhajate mokhyād bhasmanyeva juhoti saḥ

tamavajñāya māṃ martya kurute ca viḍaṃvatāṃ

śabdabrahma paraṃ brahma mama bho śāśvatī tanuḥ

ubhayaṃ ca mayāvyāptaṃ mayi caivo bhayaṃ sthitaṃ

sarvendriya guṇābhāsaṃ calate yena vigrahaṃ

tanmūrtayaḥ suniṣṇāna parabrahmādi gacchati

na hi mokṣaphalantena janmakoṭi śatairapi

śṛṣṭikarmaṇi kiṃ mokṣa bhavate vīravandite

mandabuddhi savijñeyo viphalā tasya vāsanā

sārātsāraṃ na jānanti māyayā bhramitāstute

sa eva puruṣo hyātmā prakṛtīstha svarūpa dhṛk

sarvabhāvasthitonantaḥ saguṇaḥ sopi kīrtitaḥ

tenedamāvṛtaṃ viśvaṃ vitastimadhi tiṣṭati

evaṃ virājaḥ pratipatya tatyaṃta vrīhiḥ pumān

vibhajannātmanātmānāṃ ekadhā daśadhā tridhā

adyovatāro ye nāsau bhūtagrāmau vibhāvyate

virāṭa savidhaḥ prāṇa ekadhā hṛdayena tu

tasya pūjā prakartavyā prāṇāpānena niścalā

etat te kathitaṃ sarvaṃ saguṇārādhanaṃ priye

kāṃkṣibhiḥ paramaṃ tatvaṃ kartavyaṃ śivapūjanaṃ

tathā mokṣaphalaṃ divyaṃ labhate nātrasaṃsayaḥ

tasmāt parāt paronantaḥ pūjanīyo guṇatribhiḥ

prāṇāyāmena kartavyā kuleśaṃ yoga lakṣaṇāt

tayā mokṣaphalaṃ nāsti jñātavyaṃ mokṣakāṃkṣibhiḥ

śāśvataṃ mānitaṃ yena sa mūrkho gajavannaraḥ

cintayet prāṇaliṃgantu somarūpamudāhṛtaṃ

kṛtrimaṃ naśvaraṃ liṃgaṃ prāṇaliṃgaṃ kathaṃ bhavet

jñānaliṃgaṃ tu kutasteṣāmajñānāvṛtacetasāṃ

pratyakṣaṃ sarvagaṃ śuddhaṃ dṛśyate na ca lakṣyate

dhāraṇā kīdṛśī sā tu procyate vṛṣabhadhvajaḥ

dhāraṇāt tasya vijñeyā dvādaśānte dhvanisthitā

hṛdaye ca tathā kaṃṭhe bhruvormadhye varānane

dvādaśānte prāṇaliṃgaṃ jīvapuṣpeṇa pūjayet

satasāhasra lakṣaikaṃ japaṃ kṛtvā samarcayet

lakṣaṃ caivāntare proktaṃ śivaṃ sarve svarātmakaṃ

kathate dhyātma śāstreṣu muktaye lakṣasaṃjñakaṃ

ekamātro dvimātraśca trimātrau vai japaḥ smṛtaḥ

pūjake śatasaṃkhyā ca sahasraṃ kuṃbhake tathā

evaṃ japaḥ smṛto dehe pāpapuṇyakṣayaṃkaraḥ

eṣajāpyavidhiryoge aghoraḥ kathito mayā

yo vai brahmavido loke jānaṃti paramaṃ śivaṃ

kṛta tretādvāpareṣu vahavo dhyātva cittamā

brahmaviddurlabhaṃ loke guptarūpeṇasaṃsthitāḥ

labdhamāṃ mānuśīṃ yonīṃ jñānavijñānasaṃbhavāṃ

ātmā tu yena vijñāto deśikaḥ sotra kīrtitaḥ

yonveṣya piṇḍamadhye tu svātmaliṃgaṃ sadoditaṃ

brahmacārī dīkṣataśca vānaprastha satāpasaḥ

varṇāśramānāṃ yo dharmaḥ sadharmaḥ kathito mayā

bhaktiḥ priyā tapodānaṃ dīkṣāśvāsa nivartanaṃ

prāṇāyāma paroyogī sopi viṣṇurmaheśvaraḥ

avajñā yogayuktasya āyuḥ puṇyakṣayaṃkarī

anantamahimā yogī yogābhyāsena jāyate

sākāropi nirākāro dehī cābhyāsa yogataḥ

nānācāryaṃ matai yoga sa yogaḥ śiva jīvayoḥ

granthavistāraṇārthāya procyatenekadhā priye

rahasyaṃ sarvayogasya nānādarśanasaṃmataṃ

asmin śāstre mahāgopyaṃ mayā te kathitaṃ priye

tatkarmacchedane jñānaṃ prādhānya svātmanaḥ paraṃ

ye komārgaḥ prasiddhoyaṃ nirdhāraḥ prāṇasaṃyamaḥ

nānyaḥ paṃthāstu yogasya mārutena vinānaghe

vedaśāstrasya sāmyaṃ ca lokasyoddhāraṇāya ca

akulāgamanāmedaṃ śāstraṃ tena na nirmitaṃ

ādikalpamahāviṣṇuḥ mama pratyakṣatāṃ gataḥ

mayā sarvaṃ śṛtaṃ devi parabrahma prasādataḥ

tacchāstramadhunā proktaṃ guptaṃ kuru maheśvarī

teṣāṃ jñānaṃ tadātavyaṃ mamājñāṃ saṃsmaret priye

bhaviṣyati kalosaṃkhyā tasmin kāle mahesvaraḥ

prāṇīnāṃ kṛpayā deva jñānaṃ vistārayaṃ prabho

tatrāhaṃ dvijarūpeṇa kathayāmya kulāgamaṃ

aneka svātmano jñānaṃ bhaviṣyati kalau yuge

jñānasya mahimā vede aprameyo hyagocaraḥ

nābhi hṛtkaṇṭhadeveśa bhrūvilānte kulesthitaṃ

sopi yogī samākhyāto jīvanmukteti viśrutāḥ

prāṇāyāmaṃ vinābhadre dehapāto bhaviṣyati

anyomārgo na tasyāsti sarvaśāstrasya saṃmataḥ

akulāgamasarvasvametat tvaṃ hṛdaye kuru

antarniyamamārgeṇa madhyamena kulaṃ vaset

proktaṃ cākulanāthena sokulaḥ kulavarjitaḥ

tenedaṃ racitaṃ śāstraṃ vaṃdhavicchedakārakaṃ

kasyāpi hi na vaktavyaṃ vaktavyaṃ dharmaśālināṃ

śuddhānāṃ śodhanaṃ nāsti nirlepādbrahmavittamā

nāsti yoga paraṃ dhyānaṃ nāsti yogāt paraṃ sukhaṃ

bhavanāśaḥ sa vijñeya mārutasya kule gatiḥ

abhyāsācca svayaṃ prāṇo gacchate vyomamaṇḍalaṃ

guruprasādāt jñātavyaṃ rahasyaṃ hyakulāgame

caturāśramādikathanaṃ nāma navamaḥ paṭalaḥ

mama śatru vināsāya akulāya namostute

krodhāstradvayaṃ māyānte bhūtinīṃ ca trikūrcataḥ

kālaśyāmalavarṇikārmregabhujaṃgairveṣṭitā rakṣatu

japedaṣṭasahasraṃ tu dhyāyedekāgramānasaḥ

candanodakamiśreṇa dattvārghyaṃ tuṣyati dhruvam

karāmalakatāṃ kṛtvā mātā bhūtvā jagattrayam

bhaginī cenmahābhāgā sahasrayojanādapi

bhāryā cetpṛṣṭhamāropya svargaṃ prayāti nityaśaḥ

sarvāśāṃ pūrayatyeva sadā devī vibhūṣiṇī

pāyātkuṇḍaladhāriṇī trijagatāmānandasandohabhūḥ

āyāti tatkṣaṇāddevī tataḥ kuṇḍaladhāriṇī

kiṃ karttavyaṃ mayā vatsa mātaruktveti sādhakaḥ

ataḥ parantu cārvaṅgi! sindūrahāriṇīmanuḥ

kūrcabījatrayaṃ cāstraṃ samproktaṃ vividhaṃ tathā

sindūrahāriṇyasau

tattvasaṃkhyādinaṃ yāvajjapastattvasahasrakaḥ

kuryātsādhakavaryastu bhāryā bhavati kāmitā

rakṣati dvādaśadinaṃ devi sindūrahāriṇī

māyādvayaṃ krodhadvayaṃ siṃhinīti padantataḥ

hrīṃ hrīṃ phaṭ phaṭ siṃhinī hūṃ hūṃ phaṭ

śailāgre nirjane vāpi siṃhinyāḥ pūjanaṃ tathā

śeṣaṃ pātu samastamantakakulavyākalpamānā kalā

tato hṛṣṭātivaradā siṃhinī ceti pūjitā

vastrayugmaṃ rasamayaṃ sādhakāya dine dine

praṇavañca trikūrcāstrahaṃsinīkathito manuḥ

divyā kāñcanaratnahāralalitā śrīhaṃsinī pātu naḥ

avadhānena sampūjya japedayutasaṃkhyakam

candanārghyapradānena dṛṣṭā bhavati haṃsinī

vastrālaṅkārabhojyāni divyārthaṃ samprayacchati

avaśeṣavyayābhāvānna dadāti prakupyati

oṃ hūṁ hūṁ phaṭ phaṭ naṭī hūṁ hūṁ hūṁ

kūrcatrayantu mantro'yaṃ kathito nartakīmanuḥ

vīṇāgītaviśālinī bhagavatī vyāhāravākcāturī

naṭī susiddhā bhavati dhūpaṃ dadyānmuhurmuhaḥ

sadā kāmabhogadātrī bhāryā bhavati nartakī

dine dine naṭī devī sthāyinī bhavati dhruvam

ataḥ paraṃ mahādevi ceṭyā ākarṣaṇaṃ śṛṇu

ceṭī krodhadvayañcoktaṃ ceṭī mantrottamottamaḥ

kiñciddharmasamāhitāpikaravā pāyātprabhā ceṭikā

dhruvaṃ ceṭī samāgatya ceṭīkarma karotyapi

āgatya niyataṃ devī ceṭīkarma karoti ca

paraṃ ṅentaṃ krodhatrayaṃ kāmeśvaryā manuḥ smṛtaḥ

pāyāt puṣpadhanurdharā madhumukhī kāmeśvarī bhūtinī

māṃsādinā baliṃ dattvā sahasraṃ japamācaret

japanniśīthamāyāti raktenārghyaṃ nivedayet

sarvāśāṃ pūrayatyeva rājyaṃ yacchati niścitam

ṅe'ntaṃ krodhatrayaṃ guptaṃ kumārīmantramuttamam

sarvālaṅkārasaṃyuktā bhāryā bhavati sādhitā

sitavastraṃ candanaṃ ca jyotiḥpuṣpaṃ pradāpayet

japānte nityamāyāti cumbanāliṅganādibhiḥ

dadātyaṣṭau ca dīnārāndivyavastrayugaṃ tathā

anyāśramagṛhāddravyamānīyāśu prayacchati

satyaṃ satyaṃ punaḥ satyaṃ śṛṇu me prāṇavallabhe

krodhādhipaṃ vyomavaktraṃ vajrapāṇiṃ surāntakam

kalikalpatarorvallī bhūtinī siddhirucyate

sundaryādiprabhedena pratyekaṃ pūjayecchive

tataḥ prāsādamāruhya pūjāsthānaṃ suśobhanam

tatrasthaḥ sādhayedyogī yoginyādi ca bhūtinīm

tato vidhivadācamya sūryārghyaṃ pradadettataḥ

gaṇeśaṃ baṭukañcaiva pūjayedyantrakopari

gaṇeśaṃ krodhabhairavaṃ ca pūjayetparameśvari

pūrvavacca punardhyātvā mānasaiḥ pūjayettataḥ

tanmadhye ca vinirmāya candanena vilepanam

niśāyāṃ pūjayedvīraṃ divase pūjayetpaśum

nirbhayaḥ sañjapenmantraṃ hṛdgataṃ ca nivedayet

gagano grahaṇaścaiva yuktacandrārddhaśekharaḥ

saṃkhyayāṣṭasahasrāśca sarvasiddhipradāyakaḥ

candraśekhara taddhyānaṃ saṃkṣepeṇa vada prabho

vyāghracarmāmbaradharaṃ trinetrāṃ bhīṣaṇānanām

dvibhujaṃ dakṣiṇe khaḍgaṃ kheṭakaṃ vāmahastakam

sādhakāya prayacchantamabhayaṃ varameva ca

vikṛtāsyaṃ dvijihvaṃ ca caturnetraṃ dvikarṇakam

dvibhujaṃ ca gadāpāṇiṃ vāme cā'bhayasaṃyutam

dakṣe ḍamarukaṃ dhatte vāme caiva triśūlakam

dvibhujaṃ hāsyavadanaṃ varā'bhayavidhārakam

iti kriyoḍḍīśe mahātantrarāje gaurīśvarasaṃvādeṣoḍaśaḥ paṭalaḥ

mṛtyuñjayasya pūjāyāḥ kiṃ phalaṃ kiṃ vidhānakam

mṛtyuñjayavidhānaṃ tu na vaktavyaṃ bahirmukhe

mṛtyuñjayo mahādevaḥ sākṣānmṛtyuharaḥ paraḥ

deveśi rogaśāntyarthaṃ tasya pūjāvidhiryathā

rogārto mucyate rogādbaddho mucyeta bandhanāt

yamo'pi praṇamedbhaktyā kiṃ kariṣyati pāmaraḥ

jātibhedairmṛttikāṃ tu gṛhītvā'śītitolakām

paurāṇikena mantreṇa kuryācca paṭhanaṃ budhaḥ

svasvamantraiśca pratyekadravyeṇa snāpayet sudhīḥ

upaviśyāsane vipro dhṛtvā dhautaṃ ca vāsasam

upacārāḥ ṣoḍaśaṃ tu deyā bhaktyā prayatnataḥ

vastrayugmaṃ pradadyāttu paridheyaṃ yathā bhavet

athavā śṛṇu deveśi yantraṃ sarvasusiddhidam

vyādhivyāmohaduḥkhapraśamanamuditaṃ śrīpadaṃ kīrtidāyi

bilvapatrasahasrantu hyabhagnaṃ vinivedayet

samidbhistu guḍūcyāstu tatra homaṃ samācaret

tadardhaṃ vā tadardhaṃ vā yathāvibhavamānataḥ

ekaliṅgaṃ samārādhya phalaṃ syādanyake yuge

āmrapātre tu saṃsthāpya jalaṃ cāśītitolakam

kṣipeddīpaśikhāyāṃ ca mantramuccārya mānakam

yādṛgyādṛg bhavedrogo nāśameti mayoditam

aṅgavyatikrameṇaiva vṛthā bhavati vāsanā

yadi madvacane bhaktistadā mukto bhaveddhruvam

anyathā yadi deveśi sarvaṃ bhavati niṣphalam

saṃkalpaṃ ca tataḥ kuryādvidhivākyānusārataḥ

pañcagavyaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā śivamantreṇa mantritam

taduddhṛtya tataścakaṃ sthāpayetsvarṇapātrake

payodadhighṛtakṣaudraśarkarādyauranukramāt

aṣṭābhiḥ kalaśairdevīmaṣṭābhirvāripūritaiḥ

snānaṃ samāpya tāṃ devīṃ sthāpayet svarṇapīṭhake

aṣṭottaraśataṃ devi devatābhāvasiddhaye

tatrāvāhya mahādevi! jīvanyāsañca kārayet

phalatāmbūlanaivedyaiḥ śivaṃ tatra samarcayet

aguruṃ cāmaraṃ ghaṇṭāṃ yathāyogyaṃ maheśvari

aṣṭottaraśataṃ hutvā sampātājyaṃ vinikṣipet

gurave dakṣiṇāṃ dadyāttato devīṃ visarjayet

iti kriyoḍḍīśe mahātantrarāje devīśvarasaṃvāde saptadaśaḥ paṭalaḥ

sarvaṃ me kathitaṃ deva kavacaṃ na prakāśitam

kathayasvādya me nātha yadi sneho'sti māṃ prati

satryakṣarasvarūpo me vadanaṃ ca maheśvaraḥ

mṛtyuñjayastribījātmā hyāyū rakṣatu me sadā

sadā me sarvataḥ pātu ṣaṭtriṃśadvarṇarūpadhṛk

trivarṇātmā nīlakaṇṭhaḥ kaṇṭhaṃ rakṣatu sarvadā

sadā rakṣatu me guhyaṃ sarvasampatpradāyakaḥ

mārtaṇḍabhairavo nityaṃ pādau me parirakṣatu

ūrddhvamūrddhani ceśāno mama rakṣatu sarvadā

aghorākhyo mahādevaḥ pūrvasyāṃ parirakṣatu

uttarasyāṃ sadā pātu sadyojātasvarūpadhṛk

prātarmadhyāhnakāle tu yaḥ paṭhecchivasannidhau

kavacaṃ dhārayedyastu sādhako dakṣiṇe bhuje

yoginībhūtavetālāḥ pretakūṣmāṇḍapannagāḥ

paṭhitvā'bhyarcayeddevi yathāvidhipuraḥsaram

taddhāraṇe mahādevi! mṛtyurogavināśanam

iti jñātaṃ mahādevi! tasya vaktre sthitaṃ sadā

anyathā siddhihāniḥ syātsatyametanmanorame

tadā gorocanādyena likhedyantramanuttamam

pratipadme likhenmāyābījaṃ bhūpurakaṃ tataḥ

kukṣau dhṛtvā mahāyantraṃ śubhaputravatī bhavet

dakṣiṇāṃ gurave dattvā gṛhṇīyādyantramuttamam

vṛttamadhye likheddevi sādhakākhyāṃ viśeṣataḥ

praṇavaiḥ puṭitā lajjā tridhā patreṣu saṃlikhet

satyaṃ satyaṃ hi deveśi vandhyā putravatī bhavet

durbhagā cedbhavennārī subhagā māsato bhavet

dhāraṇāddānavapatirmahārakṣākaro bhavet

gaṃ pañcakaṃ sarvamadhye likhitvā dhārayedyadi

gorocanākuṅkumābhyāṃ bhūrje saṃlikhya dhārayet

pūjitaṃ dhāritañcaiva sarvopadravanāśanam

gorocanābhiḥ saṃlikhya dhārayetparameśvari

pratipatreṣu yaṃbījaṃ likhedatha pṛthakpṛthak

kākavandhyājanasyāpi bahuputrakaraṃ param

sarvamadhye likheddevi tataḥ śṛṇu maheśvari

suputrañca samālikhya utpādaya padantataḥ

dhāraṇātsarvasampattirbhaveddevi na saṃśayaḥ

yasya dhāraṇamātreṇa vaśīkuryāt punaḥ priye

oṃ śatrumukhaṃ bandhayeti pratipadmadalaṃ likhet

pūrvavatpūjayeddevīṃ japellakṣaṃ vidhānataḥ

grīvāyāṃ kaṇṭhadeśe vā śikhāyāṃ vāpi dhāraṇāt

pūrvoktavidhinā lakṣaṃ durgāmantraṃ japedbudhaḥ

dhārayet paramaṃ yantraṃ jīvavatsā tato bhavet

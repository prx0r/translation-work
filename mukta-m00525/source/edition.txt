rahasyāṇāṃ (nāṃ) rahasyaṃ hi yantraṃ pañcākṣarasya tu

nityaṃ hi loka rakṣārthaṃ yantraṃ rakṣākaraṃ mama

śrīsabhākhyaṃ mahāyantraṃ mama rūpapradarśakam

ahaṃ pañcākṣaraṃ sākṣāt tvaṃ tu pañcadaśākṣarī

āvayoścakrasamelaṃ viṣṇvādīnāma gocaram

mānañca cakrayogañca pṛthaktvaṃ ca viśeṣataḥ

sthānaṃ cidambaraṃ prāpya prāsādaṃ mantra viham

dhyāyaṃ cidambaraprasannātma darpaṇe pratibimbavat

gandhādibhiralaṃ kṛtya svadehaṃ mantra vigraham

ānandamamalaṃ nityaṃ nṛtyantaṃ māṃ maheśvarī

tvāṃ devi manasābhyarcya soḍaśai rūpa cārakaiḥ

māyātmika mahābhakti prāsādā vṛttike priye

mahāhṛdayamadhyasthāṃ nṛttamūrtiṃ parāṃ śivām

catvārīśāṃśakaṃ kṛtvā saṣṭa tanmadhyamaṃ priye

svantantramādyamaparaṃ yāmalaṃ tu śivātmakam

devī mālaṃ karṇikā svā daśadhāsaṃvibhajyatām

pātayannava sūtrāṇi navānāṃ pārśvayoḥ mṛjet

madhyamaṃ ca tathātoyaṃ guṇabhāgaṃ yathā mṛjet

ṣaṣṭhī dvitīyagā cāsyād aṣṭamī prathamā gata

prathamā saptamaspṛṣṭvā caturthā caṣṭamāṃgatā

ityevaṃ karma sūtrāṇī yojayet sūtrapārśvayoḥ

vṛttaṃ spṛṣṭvā ṣaḍaśrantu tadaspṛṣṭvāṣṭakaṃ priye

sūtrāṇi pātayeddevi ṣoḍaśāśraṃ yathā kramam

aṣṭāśrañca triyaśrañca siddhitve ca na saṃśayaḥ

viracya tava cakrantu mama cakraṃ tato pari

caturaśrasamaṃkṛtvā dvātriṃśatirīśakam

sūtrāṇi paritaḥ prājñaḥ tadantaścaturaśrakam

kalāṃśamūrdhvatā kṛtvā tiryak saptadaśāṃśakam

sūtrāt paścimārabhya navamaṃ madhyamaṃ bhavet

madhyato dikpade ṣaṭke sārdhe tanmaṇḍalaṃ bhramet

tadbahistricatuḥ pañca cāṅgulairmaṇḍaladvayam

maṇḍalatraya madhye tu maṇḍalantu paribhramet

navakhaṇḍā yathā bhūmirna ca khaṇḍātmakaṃ tathā

koṇastha caturaśrāṇi vyūhārthaṃ parikalpayet

śikārañca nakārañca yanmayā parikīrtitam

ma vāśineya ityāhu rāgneyaṃ pañca pañcakam

yañcamañca śivānañca na ca vāmaśikaṃ tathā

vakārañca nakārañca maśivanteṣvanu kramāt

athavakṣye ca vāyavyāṃ manugrahamidaṃ guroḥ

nama śivāya paścāttu vakāra śimayaṃ na ca

avagrahamidaṃ proktaṃ vāyavyāṃ pañcapañcakaṃ

nama śivāya paścāttu makārañca yakārakam

vāśidvau ca patyo nyasya vākāraṃ maśiyanna ca

tāḍayedupa sūtrāṇi tiryagūrdhvañca madhyataḥ

saptamasya tribhāge tu navame daśamarddhake

pañcamasya caturthāṃśe saptame cāṣṭamarddhake

tribhāgaika padasyaiva caturdaśa padasya ca

dakṣabhūta caturthāṃśe cotthitārghyagraka sthitaḥ

maṇibandhābhyantaraṃ caiva pāṇe ḍamarukasya hi

maṇibandhaṃ lambapāṇe pratī caiva varānane

abhayasya karasyaiva maṇibandha talāvadhi

sthitāṅgulāgrakaṃ vāme'bhayāṅguṣṭhāvadhi priye

navamasvartthake sūtre sthita pārṣṇyāvadhi sthitam

daśamārabdha sūtre tu taddhastastala maddhyamam

aṣṭamasyārdha bhāge tu sphik piṇḍāvapuryathā

trayodaśārdha sūtre tu cakṣuṣormadhyamaṃ priye

evaṃ sūtrodbhavaṃ kṛtvā viśeṣāntaṃ ca sūtrataḥ

trisaptakṛtvo deveśi raśmayaśva pradarśayet

varṇān vā puṣpikāṃ vāpi savarṇāpuṣpikā'thavā

etaccakraṃ mahāgauri mamarūpa pradarśakam

śilpinā śāstramārgeṇa kāryaṃ pra aṃgapūraṇam

sarvadevātmakaṃ padmaṃ prabhāvalayammūligam

sarvādi pañcakopetaṃ mantra śruti samanvitam

etat padmaṃ mahādevi mantrābjaparikalpitam

dvāviṃśatiśca rekhāḥ ca madhye praṇava vāmayoḥ

ṣaḍakṣaraṃ tu vinyasya prāsantu ṣaḍakṣaram

nama śivāya ityevaṃ praṇavāntāṃ punaḥ priye

paripañcākṣaraṃ nyasya madhye praṇavamālayoḥ

akārādi kṣakārāntaṃ praṇavādi namontakam

vinyasedguptamantrasya prāṇamantraṃ navākṣaram

aṃguṣṭhe tu yakāraḥ syāt vākāraṃ tarjanī nyaset

kaniṣṭhe tu na rautaṃ syāt sṛṣṭimārgeṇa ceśvarī

gulphapāṇyośca jaṃghe ca jānvorū guhya deśake

bāhū kūrparadeśe tu maṇibandhe maheśvari

makāraṃ ca yakārañca śikāraṃ sūkṣmapañcakam

ḍamaruṃ valayaścaiva tantu yattu kaṭikramāt

nayo namavoyeti vāme corddhve karāgrake

kaṇṭhe karṇāsthi gāsye tu nāsāgre ca yathā kramam

ādyaṃ śivaṃ bhruvormadhye madhye haṃsa sudīptakam

dvādaśārṇaṃ jaṭājūṭe gaṃgā māyā vāmake

jāntaṃ nṛtyañca saṃyuktaṃ candrakhaṇḍaṃ ca dakṣiṇe

śiro lalāṭe bhrūmadhye gaṇḍoṣṭha gala dṛksu ca

anuloma vilomena haṃsaṃ hṛdaya pārśvake

nyasennavātmakaṃ devi karṇe vakṣasi baindave

mālāśca vinyaseddevī karṇe sākṣānmahāmanum

prādakṣiṇya krameṇaiva prabhāmūle tu vinyaset

vinyaseddevi mūle tu śivāyeti pradakṣiṇam

dakṣiṇāṃ vithimārabhya prādakṣiṇya kramāllikhet

hrīṃ śrīṃ aiṃ haṃ saścetyuttareṣu padeṣu ca

nama śivāya mantreṇa lavaraya hā kṣaram likhet

śrīṃ hrīṃ aiṃ klīṃ haṃ saśceti likhet kramāt

cakre ca mārjayeddevī mamāṅgasya bahiḥ sthitāḥ

vāmabhāge svatantrasyāṃ nṛttālokanalīlayā

athavā yāmale vāme prabhāvalaya madhyagām

ālayoścakra saṃmelaṃ mantrayogañca tatvataḥ

teṣāṃ hi bhakti mā nityaṃ muktidā darśana priye

mantrārṇavā mahādevi mantrakṣīrārṇavoddhṛtam

idaṃ rahasyaṃ pāpaghnaṃ bhuktidaṃ muktidaṃ priye

gopanīyaṃ prayatnena na deyaṃ yasya kasya cit

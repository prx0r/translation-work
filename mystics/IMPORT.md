## Imported (SOURCE pinned in each dir)

1. **lalded** — Lal Ded / Lalleshwari (14th c.), Vakhs. *Why:* the fountainhead; Trika
   transmission in the mother tongue (yoga, pratyabhijñā, guru, void). SOURCE.txt =
   Grierson/Barnett Lalla-Vakyani (1920, IA in.ernet.dli.2015.69572, public domain),
   109 vakhs + Sanskrit rendering lineage (Bhaskara's Lalleśvarīvākyāni, KSTS 1919,
   noted not yet sourced). Also: Hoskote "I, Lalla" (146, copyrighted — NOT vendored).
2. **rupabhavani** — Rupa Bhavani / Alakeshwari (1621–1721), Vakhs. *Why:* the most
   doctrinally Sanskritised Trika mystic — kundalini, cakras, spanda vocabulary intact;
   written down (not oral-smoothed). SOURCE.txt = Rahasyopadesha (Shiv Nath Sharma
   Shastri, IA CC0, Hindi/Sanskrit prose teachings). NOTE: the 146 Vakhs (T.N. Dhar)
   are NOT yet sourced — second acquisition needed before translation.
3. **parmanand** — Parmanand Bhat (1791–1875). *Why:* Shiva-Lagan (Shiva-Shakti union),
   Radha-Swayamvar, Sudama-Charit — lila-bhakti thoroughly permeated with Kashmir
   Shaivism ("Shankar Krishna eeku roopuyu"). SOURCE.txt = Paramanada Sukti Sar
   (Kashmir Research Institute; Kashmiri in Hindi+Persian scripts + English trans, IA).
4. **nundrishi** — Nund Rishi / Sheikh Noor-ud-din (1377–1438), Shruks. *Why:* the
   valley pair with Lal Ded (mutual verses); Rishi-order ascetic transmission in the
   same idiom. SOURCE.txt = Parimoo "Unity in Diversity" (J&K Academy; English prose
   renderings + manuscript stemmata). CAVEAT: Islamic Rishi, syncretic — milieu, not
   doctrinal Trika. Owner decides whether he enters the translation queue.
5. **santosh** — Ghulam Rasool Santosh (1929–1997), mystic poet + neo-tantra painter.
   *Why:* the living bridge — Muslim-born Kashmir Shaiva practitioner, Sharda-literate,
   Sahitya Akademi for "Be Soakh Rooh" (1978); his art (Shri Chakra, bindu, Shiva-Shakti
   union, Padma Shri) IS Trika visual transmission. SOURCE-EN.pdf = 2 translated poems
   (poemhunter; thin). NOTE: Kashmiri "Be Soakh Rooh" full text NOT yet sourced —
   second acquisition needed. His paintings: lore/visual reference only (link, don't host).

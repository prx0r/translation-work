# Pañcaślokī — ARGMAP

*Abhinavagupta, 5 verses, 5 passages (1 verse each). Source: Ruma Dewan ed. (SanskritDocuments), from the Abhinavastotrāvalī tradition.*

## Arc
- pan-p01 (v. 1): total offering — pride laid on the Lord's feet; plea for immediate pleasure.
- pan-p02 (v. 2): offered speech — "victory to Maheśa" at the door; speech becomes bliss-giving.
- pan-p03 (v. 3): reproach — You speak/see/hear/come not; scripture declares You sense-free.
- pan-p04 (v. 4): resolution — guru's word wipes away reasonings; doubt-cloud dissolved; touching the feet.
- pan-p05 (v. 5): confession — long dwelling, everything done, yet forgetting; plea for no-more-absence.

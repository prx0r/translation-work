# pancashloki pan-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
Whatever I have truly done, O Lord — no duty of mine goes beyond this: my pride, laid at Your lotus feet with devotion. This is my all; I know there is nothing other. So, casting all else aside — quickly, O protector of the helpless, O ocean of compassion — be pleased with me!
The Sanskrit: *yatsatyam tu mayā kṛtam mama vibho kṛtyam tu na ataḥ param yat manmānam aiśapādakamale bhaktyā mayā eva | arpitam sarvasvam ataḥ eva etat itarat na asti eva jānāmi ataḥ tyaktvā kṣipram anāthanātha karuṇāsindho prasannaḥ bhava*

# pancashloki pan-p02 — L2 READ

## v.2
O Maheśa, at Your door let this charming speech of mine flash forth, exceeding all: 'Victory, victory to Maheśa!' — faultless, unceasing. Let this very speech, auspicious, be bliss-giving to me forever. O Lord, O giver of refuge, make me — whose protector is Maheśa — possessed of a protector!
The Sanskrit: *maheśa tvaddvāri sphuratu rucirā vāk atitarām mama eṣā nirdoṣam jaya jaya maheśa iti satatam | śivā sā eṣā vāṇī bhavatu śivadā mahyam aniśam maheśanātham mām śaraṇada sanātham kuru vibho*

# pancashloki pan-p03 — L2 READ

## v.3
You speak not, O best-limbed one; You look not upon me, wretched as I am. My entreaty, made in many ways, You hear not; You come not into my mind. Fallen into the pit amid transmigration's ocean, I am mostly left unsupported — yet scripture truly declares You devoid of speech, sight, hearing, feet and the like!
The Sanskrit: *brūṣe na uttamāṅga paśyasi na mām etādṛśam duḥkhitam vijñaptim bahudhā kṛtām na śṛṇuṣe | na āyāsi manmānase saṃsārārṇavagartamadhyapatitam prāyeṇa na ālambase vākcakṣuḥśravaṇāṅghriṇādirahitam tvām āha satyam śrutiḥ*

# pancashloki pan-p04 — L2 READ

## v.4
From the guru's word — by the force that wipes away the fabric of heaped-up reasonings — and from the assurance told, fittingly, out of regard for scripture: when the cloud of doubt has dissolved, let me touch the feet of the Lord, whose splendor shines in the sky of the heart — like the sun, the conqueror of darkness.
The Sanskrit: *guroḥ vākyāt yukti-pracaya-racana-unmārjana-vaśāt samāśvāsāt śāstram prati samucitāt api kathitam | vilīne śaṅkābhre hṛdayagaganodbhāsimahasaḥ prabhoḥ sūryasya iva spṛśatu caraṇān dhvāntajayinaḥ*

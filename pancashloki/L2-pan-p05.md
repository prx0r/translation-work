# pancashloki pan-p05 — L2 READ

## v.5
Much time dwelling with You has passed — alas, in this very moment! What, what has not been done by You for me, for the sake of serving the excellent guru! Again and again I, whose body has attained the incomparable divine plane — yet indeed I forget You. Therefore, by You, let there be no further staying-away!
The Sanskrit: *yātaḥ tvatsahavāsataḥ bahutaraḥ kālaḥ vata asmin kṣaṇe kim kim vā na kṛtam tvayā varagurūpāsānimittena me vāram | vāram aham punar nirupamām divyām bhuvam prāsuvatkāyaḥ tvām kila vismarāmi yat ataḥ stheyam na bhūyaḥ tvayā*

# Pañcaślokī — L2 READ

## pan-p01 — offering
Whatever I have truly done, O Lord — no duty of mine goes beyond this: my pride has been offered by me with devotion on the Lord's lotus feet. This is my all; I know there is nothing other than this. Therefore, having cast all else aside — quickly, O protector of the helpless, O ocean of compassion — be pleased!

## pan-p02 — praise at the door
O Maheśa, at Your door let this charming speech of mine flash forth exceedingly — "Victory, victory to Maheśa!" — faultless and unceasing. Let this very speech, auspicious, be bliss-giving for me forever. O Lord, O giver of refuge, make me — whose protector is Maheśa — possessed of a protector!

## pan-p03 — reproach
You speak not, O best-limbed one; You look not upon me, wretched as I am. My entreaty, made in many ways, You hear not; You come not into my mind. Fallen amid the pit of the ocean of transmigration, I am mostly left unsupported — yet scripture truly declares You devoid of speech, sight, hearing, feet and the like!

## pan-p04 — the cloud lifts
From the guru's word — by the force that wipes away the fabric of heaped-up reasonings — and from the assurance told, fittingly, out of regard for scripture: when the cloud of doubt has dissolved, let me touch the feet of the Lord, whose splendor shines in the sky of the heart — like the sun, the conqueror of darkness.

## pan-p05 — forgetting, and the final plea
Much time dwelling with You has passed — alas, in this very moment! What, what has not been done by You for me, for the sake of serving the excellent guru! Again and again I, whose body has attained the incomparable divine plane — yet indeed I forget You. Therefore, by You let there be no further staying-away!

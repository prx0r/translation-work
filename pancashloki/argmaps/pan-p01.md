# Pañcaślokī pan-p01 — ARGUMENT MAP (offering of pride; nothing higher)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 pan-p01 = verse 1. Text: SanskritDocuments, Ruma Dewan ed., from the Abhinavastotrāvalī tradition. Lore: the opening move of Abhinavagupta's short hymns is the total offering of the ego — manmāna (my pride) laid on the Lord's feet; cf. the Śivastotrāvalī of Utpaladeva (Bailly 1987) where bhakti is the soul of the means. Key: the verse equates the devotee's whole duty (kṛtya) with a single act of offering.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Whatever the devotee has truly done amounts to one duty only: pride (manmāna) offered with devotion on the Lord's lotus feet. That offering is his all (sarvasva); he knows nothing other than it. Therefore he asks the protector of the helpless, ocean of compassion, to be pleased — quickly.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Pāda a (v. 1a):**
> *yatsatyaṃ tu mayā kṛtaṃ mama vibho kṛtyaṃ tu nātaḥ param*
[i.e. what was done by me in truth, O Lord — no duty of mine is higher than this.] The verse opens with a total claim: everything truly done reduces to a single kṛtya, and nothing ranks above it.

**Pāda b (v. 1b):**
> *yanmanmānamaiśapādakamale bhaktyā mayaivārpitam*
[i.e. that my pride has been offered by me with devotion on the Lord's lotus feet.] The single duty is named: manmāna (my pride, the ego's self-regard) placed on the aiśa-pādakamala. Bhaktyā (with devotion) marks the manner — not ritual mechanics but loving surrender.

**Pāda c (v. 1c):**
> *sarvasvamata evametaditarannāsyeva jānāmyataḥ*
[i.e. this is my all; I know that there is nothing other than this.] Sarvasva (my all) universalizes the offering: the devotee's entire substance is contained in it, and he disavows knowledge of anything besides.

**Pāda d (v. 1d):**
> *styaktvā kṣipramanāthanātha karuṇāsindho prasanno bhava*
[i.e. having abandoned (all else) — quickly, O protector of the helpless, O ocean of compassion, be pleased!] Tyaktvā seals the renunciation of everything outside the offering; the plea is addressed to anāthanātha (protector of precisely those with nothing) and karuṇāsindhu (whose compassion is oceanic, not measured). Kṣipram (quickly) asks immediacy, and prasannaḥ bhava (be pleased) is the only thing requested.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. "From the Abhinavastotrāvalī" is the SanskritDocuments description-comments field, not a critical-edition claim; no KSTS volume for the Pañcaślokī itself is cited here.
2. Tyaktvā (having abandoned) has no explicit object; "all else" is supplied from pāda c's denial of anything other.
3. Prasāda (pleasure/grace) is the sole request — no boon, no liberation formula is named.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the devotee's founding gesture: one true act (offering of pride) → all else denied → plea for immediate pleasure. L2 should carry the voice of total offering, not explain it away. The key terms to preserve: manmāna (my pride), sarvasva (my all), anāthanātha (protector of the helpless), karuṇāsindhu (ocean of compassion). Carry the offering-stance into p02 (speech offered at the door).

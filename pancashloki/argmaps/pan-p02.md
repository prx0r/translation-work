# Pañcaślokī pan-p02 — ARGUMENT MAP (speech offered at the door; jaya)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 pan-p02 = verse 2. Lore: Lilian Silburn, Hymnes de Abhinavagupta (1964) — French translations of Abhinavagupta's hymns, the only sustained Western study of these short stotras; Silburn reads them as mystical testimony rather than ornament. Key: here the offering of verse 1 becomes vāk (speech) — the devotee's word itself, crying jaya at the Lord's door.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Speech (vāk), made charming and faultless, is set flashing at the Lord's door with the cry "victory, victory to Maheśa" — constantly. That very speech, auspicious, should become bliss-giving for the devotee forever. The devotee, whose protector is Maheśa, asks the giver of refuge to make him possessed-of-a-protector.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Pāda a (v. 2a):**
> *maheśa tvaddvāri sphuratu rucirā vāgatitarām*
[i.e. O Maheśa, at Your door let charming speech flash forth exceedingly.] Tvaddvāri (at Your door) places the devotee outside, at the threshold; sphuratu (let flash forth) makes speech an active radiance, and atitarām (exceedingly) refuses moderation.

**Pāda b (v. 2b):**
> *mamaiṣā nirdoṣaṃ jaya jaya maheśeti satatam*
[i.e. this speech of mine — faultless — "victory, victory to Maheśa," constantly.] Nirdoṣam (faultless) qualifies the utterance; the content of the speech is given as direct quotation: jaya jaya maheśa. Satatam (constantly) makes praise unbroken.

**Pāda c (v. 2c):**
> *śivā saiṣā vāṇī bhavatu śivadā mahyamaniśam*
[i.e. let this very speech, auspicious, be bliss-giving for me unceasingly.] Śivā (auspicious — the feminine of Śiva's name) turns speech into a goddess-like power; śivadā (bliss-giving) states its effect on the devotee; aniśam (unceasingly) matches satatam.

**Pāda d (v. 2d):**
> *maheśanāthaṃ māṃ śaraṇada sanāthaṃ kuru vibho*
[i.e. me, whose protector is Maheśa — O giver of refuge — make possessed-of-a-protector, O Lord.] The verse closes by naming the devotee's status twice: de facto (maheśanātha, one whose lord is Maheśa) and to-be-effected (sanātha, made possessed-of-a-protector). Śaraṇada (giver of refuge) echoes verse 1's anāthanātha.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Whether sanātham (make possessed-of-a-protector) adds anything to maheśanātham (whose protector is Maheśa), or restates it as request — read here as request completing status.
2. Śivā as adjective of vāṇī carries the Śiva-name resonance; not pressed further.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as offered speech: threshold (door) → cry of victory → speech becomes bliss-giving → request to be made protected. L2 should keep the door-image and the quoted cry intact. Carry the refuge-thread (anāthanātha → śaraṇada) into p03, where refuge seems refused.

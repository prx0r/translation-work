# Pañcaślokī pan-p03 — ARGUMENT MAP (reproach; scripture's verdict)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 pan-p03 = verse 3. Lore: the verse turns on a śruti-vākya — śrutiḥ declares the Lord devoid of speech, sight, hearing, feet and the like, the classic nirguṇa formulae (cf. Śvetāśvatara and Bṛhadāraṇyaka negations, neti neti). Key: the devotee's complaint (You do not speak, see, hear, come) is answered inside the verse itself by scripture's verdict.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

The devotee reproaches the Lord: You speak not, see not this wretched me; my oft-made entreaty You hear not; You come not into my mind; fallen in the pit of transmigration's ocean, I am mostly unsupported. Then the turn: scripture truly declares You devoid of speech, sight, hearing, feet and the rest.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Pāda a (v. 3a):**
> *brūṣe nottamāṅga paśyasi na māmetādṛśaṃ duḥkhitam*
[i.e. You speak not, O best-limbed one; You see not me, wretched as I am.] Two sense-verbs denied: speech (brūṣe) and sight (paśyasi). Uttamāṅga (best-limbed one) is intimate address even inside complaint.

**Pāda b (v. 3b):**
> *vijñaptiṃ bahudhā kṛtāṃ na śṛṇuṣe nāyāsi manmānase*
[i.e. my entreaty, made in many ways, You hear not; You come not into my mind.] Hearing (śṛṇuṣe) and coming (āyāsi) denied; bahudhā (in many ways) stresses the devotee's persistence, manmānase (into my mind) locates where He should arrive.

**Pāda c (v. 3c):**
> *saṃsārārṇavagartamadhyapatitaṃ prāyeṇa nālambase*
[i.e. fallen amid the pit of the ocean of transmigration, I am mostly unsupported by You.] The devotee names his state with the full compound; prāyeṇa (mostly) leaves a crack of qualification — not wholly abandoned.

**Pāda d (v. 3d):**
> *vākcakṣuḥśravaṇāṅghriṇādirahitaṃ tvāmāha satyaṃ śrutiḥ*
[i.e. devoid of speech, sight, hearing, feet and the like — thus scripture truly declares You.] The three denied verbs return as śruti's doctrine: He neither speaks, sees, nor hears because He is rahita (devoid) of those organs. Satyam (truly) seals it. The complaint is thereby theologically grounded rather than refuted.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The exact referent of the śruti-vākya is not identified in the verse; read as the general nirguṇa corpus, not a single citation.
2. Prāyeṇa (mostly) is the verse's only softening; not inflated into a doctrine of partial grace.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as reproach-then-verdict: three denials (speak/see/hear-come/support) → scripture's explanation. L2 must preserve the sting of the complaint and the aphoristic close, not resolve them into comfort. Carry the doubt/silence problem into p04 (the guru's word dissolves the cloud of doubt).

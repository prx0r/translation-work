# Pañcaślokī pan-p04 — ARGUMENT MAP (guru's word; doubt-cloud dissolved)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 pan-p04 = verse 4. Lore: K.C. Pandey, Abhinavagupta: An Historical and Philosophical Study (1963) — on the Kashmir Śaiva use of yukti (reasoning) as subordinate to the guru's word and scripture; śaṅkā (doubt) is the central epistemic obstacle dissolved by śaktipāta/grace rather than by debate. Key: yukti-pracaya (heaped-up reasonings) is wiped away, not refuted — the guru's word works like sun on cloud.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

From the guru's word — by the force that wipes away the fabric of piled-up reasonings — and from the assurance told even out of what is fitting regarding scripture: when the cloud of doubt has dissolved, may (the devotee) touch the feet of the Lord, whose splendor shines in the heart-sky, like the sun that conquers darkness.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Pāda a (v. 4a):**
> *gurorvākyādyukti-pracaya-racana-unmārjana-vaśāt*
[i.e. from the guru's word, by the force of wiping away the fabric of heaped-up reasonings.] Two sources named in one compound: guror vākyāt (from the guru's word) and the vaśa (force) of unmārjana (wiping away) of yukti-pracaya-racana (the fabric of piled-up reasonings). Reasonings are not refuted one by one; they are wiped away.

**Pāda b (v. 4b):**
> *samāśvāsācchāstraṃ prati samucitādapi kathitam*
[i.e. and from the assurance told even out of what is fitting regarding scripture.] Samāśvāsa (assurance, consolation) is the second source, told (kathitam) api (even) samucitāt (out of what is fitting) prati śāstram (regarding scripture) — testimony calibrated to scripture, not private invention.

**Pāda c (v. 4c):**
> *vilīne śaṅkābhre hṛdayagaganodbhāsimahasaḥ*
[i.e. when the cloud of doubt has dissolved — of Him whose splendor shines in the heart-sky.] Vilīne (when dissolved) states the condition; śaṅkābhra (cloud of doubt) names the obstacle in one image. The Lord's splendor (mahas) shines (udbhāsi) in the hṛdayagagana (heart-sky) — the inner space where sun can rise.

**Pāda d (v. 4d):**
> *prabhoḥ sūryasyeva spṛśatu caraṇān dhvāntajayinaḥ*
[i.e. like the sun — let (me) touch the feet of the Lord, the conqueror of darkness.] Sūryasya iva (like the sun) governs the whole image: as sun dispels cloud and darkness, so the Lord dispels doubt. Spṛśatu caraṇān (let touch the feet) is the resulting act of surrender; dhvāntajayinaḥ (of the darkness-conquering one) seals His nature.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The subject of spṛśatu (let touch) is unstated; read as the devotee (first-person context of the hymn), not specified.
2. "Even out of what is fitting" (samucitād api) is read as calibration to scripture; the alternative (assurance beyond fitness) is noted but not adopted.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as means-then-goal: guru's word + scriptural assurance → doubt-cloud dissolved → touching the feet. L2 should carry the sun/cloud image whole; it is the verse's reasoning, not decoration. Carry the guru-thread into p05 (service of the excellent guru) and the touch-thread into the final plea.

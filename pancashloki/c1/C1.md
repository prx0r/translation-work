# C1 — Pañcaślokī

## pan-p01 — offering
**SUMMARY.** The whole duty is one act: pride offered on the Lord's feet; all else denied, and only immediate pleasure asked.

**KEY TERMS.** manmāna (my pride), sarvasva (my all), anāthanātha (protector of the helpless), karuṇāsindhu (ocean of compassion), prasāda (pleasure).

**EXPLANATION.** A totalizing equation: what is true of my doing = one duty = offering of pride; my all = this offering; anything other = does not exist, for me. Ataḥ, therefore: the renunciation already accomplished — all else, the denial supplying tyaktvā's object — grounds the plea, be pleased. Helplessness is met by the protector of the helpless; inexhaustibility, by the ocean of compassion; kṣipram, quickly, refuses deferral.

## pan-p02 — praise at the door
**SUMMARY.** Speech becomes the offering: the word crying victory at the threshold is itself to become bliss-giving; having Maheśa as protector is to become being protected.

**KEY TERMS.** tvaddvāra (Your door), jaya (victory), śivadā (bliss-giving), śaraṇada (giver of refuge), sanātha (possessed-of-a-protector).

**EXPLANATION.** At Your door — the threshold kept even while speech flashes; praise does not presume entry. The quotation is the whole content of the faultless, constant utterance: jaya jaya maheśa. The cry is both act and instrument. Śivā (auspicious) makes the speech itself goddess-like, and śivadā states its reflexivity: praise of the blissful one blissens the praiser. Whose-protector-is-Maheśa (status) turns into make-possessed-of-a-protector (request): being into becoming.

## pan-p03 — reproach
**SUMMARY.** Five refusals charged — speak, see, hear, come, support — and scripture converts the charge into doctrine: the Lord is sense-free.

**KEY TERMS.** vijñapti (entreaty), ālambana (support), rahita (devoid), śruti (scripture), satya (truly).

**EXPLANATION.** No speaking to the wretched, no seeing, no hearing of the oft-made entreaty, no coming into the mind, no support amid the pit of transmigration's ocean — prāyeṇa, mostly, the single softening. Then the turn: sense-free by scripture's true declaration — devoid of speech, sight, hearing, feet and the like. The complaint is not answered; it is grounded. Denial upon denial becomes doctrine.

## pan-p04 — the means
**SUMMARY.** Against the silence stand two testimonies — the guru's word and fitting scriptural assurance — whose force wipes away piled-up reasonings; with doubt dissolved, the feet of the heart-sky Lord.

**KEY TERMS.** guror vākya (guru's word), unmārjana (wiping away), samāśvāsa (assurance), śaṅkābhra (doubt-cloud), hṛdayagagana (heart-sky), caraṇa (feet), dhvāntajayin (darkness-conqueror).

**EXPLANATION.** Two sources in one breath: from the guru's word, with the force of wiping away — not refuting — the fabric of heaped reasonings; and assurance told, fittingly, with regard to scripture. On the dissolving of the doubt-cloud, touching the feet. Splendor shining in the heart-sky locates where He is found; conqueror of darkness states what He does; like the sun fuses both — cloud dispelled, light disclosed.

## pan-p05 — forgetting, and the final plea
**SUMMARY.** Long dwelling-with and total grace received, yet repeated forgetting; the only request is no more absence.

**KEY TERMS.** sahavāsa (dwelling-with), varaguru (excellent guru), vismara (forgetting), sthā (remaining), bhūyas (further).

**EXPLANATION.** Very long time dwelling-with — against a single moment, alas. What has not been done, and for the sake of serving the excellent guru: total grace, confessed complete. And yet, time after time, the body having attained the incomparable divine plane — forgetting, admitted outright (kila). Yat ataḥ, since therefore: by You, no further remaining-away. Not a boon but presence: the offered self still forgets, and asks the pleased Lord to stay.

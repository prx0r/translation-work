# C1 — Pañcaślokī (final condensation of the arguments)

*States each passage's argumentative move and the whole arc. For the read prose see L2.v2.*

## The arc in one paragraph

Total offering — pride laid on the feet, all else denied. Speech offered at the threshold, blissing the praiser. Crisis: the praised Lord is silent, and scripture declares Him sense-free by doctrine. Resolution: the guru's word wiping away reasonings, the doubt-cloud dissolved, the feet touched. Close: long dwelling and full grace received, yet repeated forgetting, with a single final request — no more absence. Offering → speech → reproach → assurance → confession-and-presence.

## pan-p01 (v. 1) — the founding equation

One true act (offering of pride) = the whole duty = the devotee's all; all else = denied. The plea follows by therefore: immediate pleasure. Offering, not petition.

## pan-p02 (v. 2) — speech becomes the offering

Praise at the threshold — victory, victory to Maheśa — is both act and instrument: the utterance blissens the utterer. Status (protected by Maheśa) is converted into request (be made protected).

## pan-p03 (v. 3) — complaint converted into doctrine

Five denials of divine response (speak, see, hear, come, support) gathered into one śruti-verdict: devoid of the organs of response. The reproach stands, theologically grounded.

## pan-p04 (v. 4) — the means: wiping, not refuting

Reasonings wiped away (not refuted) by the guru's word; assurance given in keeping with scripture. Doubt-cloud dissolved, the feet of the heart-sky Lord touched — like sun after cloud.

## pan-p05 (v. 5) — attainment indicted, presence requested

Long dwelling-with plus total grace (through service of the excellent guru) plus attained body — yet forgetting, again and again. The final request is not a boon but the end of absence. The offered self still forgets; the pleased Lord is asked to stay.

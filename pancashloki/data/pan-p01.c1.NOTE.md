# C1 NOTE — pan-p01.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

The whole duty is one act: pride offered on the Lord's feet; all else denied, and only immediate pleasure asked.

## FUNCTION

Establishes the totalizing equation of devotion: one duty, one offering, one plea.

## KEY TERMS

* **manmāna** — my pride
* **sarvasva** — my all
* **anāthanātha** — protector of the helpless
* **karuṇāsindhu** — ocean of compassion
* **prasāda** — pleasure

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Devotee's first petition: total offering, then plea.

## EXPLANATION

A totalizing equation: what is true of my doing = one duty = offering of pride; my all = this offering; anything other = does not exist, for me. Ataḥ, therefore: the renunciation already accomplished — all else, the denial supplying tyaktvā's object — grounds the plea, be pleased. Helplessness is met by the protector of the helpless; inexhaustibility, by the ocean of compassion; kṣipram, quickly, refuses deferral.

## BOUNDARY

Whether prasāda means 'pleasure' or 'grace' is unresolved.

# C1 NOTE — pan-p02.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Speech becomes the offering: the word crying victory at the threshold is itself to become bliss-giving; having Maheśa as protector is to become being protected.

## FUNCTION

Establishes praise-as-offering and the reflexive bliss of śivadā.

## KEY TERMS

* **tvaddvāra** — Your door
* **jaya** — victory
* **śivadā** — bliss-giving
* **śaraṇada** — giver of refuge
* **sanātha** — possessed-of-a-protector

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Devotee's praise at the Lord's door.

## EXPLANATION

At Your door — the threshold kept even while speech flashes; praise does not presume entry. The quotation is the whole content of the faultless, constant utterance: jaya jaya maheśa. The cry is both act and instrument. Śivā (auspicious) makes the speech itself goddess-like, and śivadā states its reflexivity: praise of the blissful one blissens the praiser. Whose-protector-is-Maheśa (status) turns into make-possessed-of-a-protector (request): being into becoming.

## BOUNDARY

Whether śivadā means 'bliss-bestowing' or 'Śiva-giving' is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Pancaślokī v.1 — same offering logic, extended to speech.

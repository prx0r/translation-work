# C1 NOTE — pan-p03.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Five refusals charged — speak, see, hear, come, support — and scripture converts the charge into doctrine: the Lord is sense-free.

## FUNCTION

Establishes complaint-as-doctrine: denial becomes declaration.

## KEY TERMS

* **vijñapti** — entreaty
* **ālambana** — support
* **rahita** — devoid
* **śruti** — scripture
* **satya** — truly

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Devotee's reproach turning to doctrine.

## EXPLANATION

No speaking to the wretched, no seeing, no hearing of the oft-made entreaty, no coming into the mind, no support amid the pit of transmigration's ocean — prāyeṇa, mostly, the single softening. Then the turn: sense-free by scripture's true declaration — devoid of speech, sight, hearing, feet and the like. The complaint is not answered; it is grounded. Denial upon denial becomes doctrine.

## BOUNDARY

Whether the turn to śruti is sincere resolution or ironic resignation is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Pancaślokī v.4 — the means that answer this reproach.

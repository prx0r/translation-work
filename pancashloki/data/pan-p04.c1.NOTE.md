# C1 NOTE — pan-p04.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Against the silence stand two testimonies — the guru's word and fitting scriptural assurance — whose force wipes away piled-up reasonings; with doubt dissolved, the feet of the heart-sky Lord.

## FUNCTION

Establishes the means: two testimonies, doubt dissolved, touch of feet.

## KEY TERMS

* **vākya** — guru's word
* **unmārjana** — wiping away
* **samāśvāsa** — assurance
* **śaṅkābhra** — doubt-cloud
* **hṛdayagagana** — heart-sky
* **caraṇa** — feet
* **dhvāntajayin** — darkness-conqueror

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The means that answer v.3's reproach.

## EXPLANATION

Two sources in one breath: from the guru's word, with the force of wiping away — not refuting — the fabric of heaped reasonings; and assurance told, fittingly, with regard to scripture. On the dissolving of the doubt-cloud, touching the feet. Splendor shining in the heart-sky locates where He is found; conqueror of darkness states what He does; like the sun fuses both — cloud dispelled, light disclosed.

## BOUNDARY

Whether guru's word and scripture are two distinct sources or one twofold source is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Pancaślokī v.3 — the reproach this answers. * Pancaślokī v.5 — the forgetting that follows.

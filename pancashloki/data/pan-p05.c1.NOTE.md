# C1 NOTE — pan-p05.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Long dwelling-with and total grace received, yet repeated forgetting; the only request is no more absence.

## FUNCTION

Establishes confession and the plea for presence.

## KEY TERMS

* **sahavāsa** — dwelling-with
* **varaguru** — excellent guru
* **vismara** — forgetting
* **sthā** — remaining
* **bhūyas** — further

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Final plea after total grace and repeated forgetting.

## EXPLANATION

Very long time dwelling-with — against a single moment, alas. What has not been done, and for the sake of serving the excellent guru: total grace, confessed complete. And yet, time after time, the body having attained the incomparable divine plane — forgetting, admitted outright (kila). Yat ataḥ, since therefore: by You, no further remaining-away. Not a boon but presence: the offered self still forgets, and asks the pleased Lord to stay.

## BOUNDARY

prāsuvatkāyaḥ is obscure; glossed 'whose body has attained it'.

## RELATED

* Pancaślokī v.1 — the offering that began the sequence.

## Arc
- par-p01 (v. 1): not lit like other lights — over-pervasion; subject/object split demoted to convention; light itself.
- par-p02 (v. 2): nothing dually distinct from knowledge; no difference in consciousness; the individual undivided.
- par-p03 (v. 3): dense one consciousness is Śiva, the universe's supreme light — yet division shines within.
- par-p04 (v. 4): difference uncut from its ground; palace-to-mountain list against the jewel-mirror.
- par-p05 (v. 5): mirror's belly proves prototype-with-reflection mind-established; no other cause among things.
- par-p06 (v. 6): address — you are supreme; universes shine at once; flashing by glory-power; their maker.
- par-p07 (v. 7): paradox held (established/unaccomplishable, non-lord/lord); praise, not proof, is what it gets.
- par-p08 (v. 8): septad remembered in the heart; abode entered again; the discussion itself known.

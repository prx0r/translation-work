# paramarthacarca par-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
Distinct from the illumination of sun, moon, lamp and the rest, it does not shine — on account of over-pervasion; and hence: of the nature of light, just this much — illumined-illuminator named is mere convention.
The Sanskrit: *arkendudīpādi avabhāsa bhinnam na ābhāti ativyāptatayā ca | tataḥ prakāśarūpam tad iyat prakāśyaprakāśatākhyā vyavahāraḥ eva*

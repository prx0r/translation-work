# paramarthacarca par-p02 — L2 READ

## v.2
No thing at all is dually distinct from knowledge; made of this and that, in consciousness there is no difference. From self-luminosity, hundredfold, in the one abode — the individual would not indeed be divided.
The Sanskrit: *jñānadvibhinnaḥ na hi kaścid arthaḥ tattatkṛtaḥ saṃvidi na asti | bhedaḥ svayamprakāśāt śatam aikadhāmni prātisvikī nāpi vibheditā syāt*

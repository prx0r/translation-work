# paramarthacarca par-p04 — L2 READ

## v.4
That difference, and this — not different thence: of the free, most-perfectly-clear one-abode. Palace, elephant, horse, cloud, ocean, mountain and the rest — just as of jewel-mirror and the like.
The Sanskrit: *bhedaḥ saḥ ca ayam na tataḥ vibhinnaḥ svacchanda susvacchatama ekadhāmnaḥ | prāsāda hasti aśva payoda sindhu giri ādi yadvat maṇidarpaṇādeḥ*

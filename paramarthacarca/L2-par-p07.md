# paramarthacarca par-p07 — L2 READ

## v.7
Thus — self-consciousness, primordially established, unaccomplishable; the self, non-lord, lord; whose place and time are full of own power — eternal, pervasive: Bhairava-Lord, I praise.
The Sanskrit: *ittham svasaṃvedanam ādisiddham asādhyam ātmānam anīśam | īśam svaśaktisaṃpūrṇavadeśakālam nityam vibhum bhairavanātham īḍe*

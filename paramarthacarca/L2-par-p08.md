# paramarthacarca par-p08 — L2 READ

## v.8
This septad of good conduct — dissolved other thoughts — those desiring the ultimate rightly remember in the heart. Those, O Bhairava's one, again enter the supreme abode, and know the ultimate-thing-discussion of the three worlds. *Fidelity: preserved (all 8 verses, full pādas from T1). Added: connective phrasing only. Not-resolved: jñānadvibhinna read as dually distinct; śatam kept as printed (hundredfold) against tempting satatam; mukhura read as mukura (mirror); vadeśakālam obscure, glossed neutrally; bhairavīya read as vocative address.*
The Sanskrit: *sadvṛtta saptakam idam galitā anya cintāḥ samyak smaranti hṛdaye paramārthakāmāḥ | te bhairavīya paradhāma muhuḥ viśanti jānanti ca trijagatī paramārthacarcām*

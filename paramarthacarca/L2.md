# Paramārthacarcā — L2 READ

## par-p01 — unlit because all-pervading
Distinct from the illumination of sun, moon, lamp and the rest, it does not shine — on account of over-pervasion; and hence: of the nature of light, just this much — illumined-illuminator named is mere convention.

## par-p02 — nothing apart from knowledge
No thing at all is dually distinct from knowledge; made of this and that, in consciousness there is no difference. From self-luminosity, hundredfold, in the one abode — the individual would not indeed be divided.

## par-p03 — dense consciousness is Śiva
Thus — dense own-consciousness, one indeed: he is Śiva, the universe's supreme light. There too, indeed, shines, in variegated power: apprehended-apprehender-division-difference.

## par-p04 — uncut difference; the mirror list
That difference, and this — not different thence: of the free, most-perfectly-clear one-abode. Palace, elephant, horse, cloud, ocean, mountain and the rest — just as of jewel-mirror and the like.

## par-p05 — the mirror needs only mind
If, in a mirror's belly, the reflection-making with-prototype would be mind-proven — then in the interior of the mirror of free consciousness, among things there is no other cause besides.

## par-p06 — you are supreme; universes shine at once
O consciousness-dense one — therefore you indeed are supreme; in you indeed the universes shine at once. And they flash forth by the power of your glory; you indeed are also their supreme-Lord maker.

## par-p07 — paradox held; praise given
Thus — self-consciousness, primordially established, unaccomplishable; the self, non-lord, lord; whose place and time are full of own power — eternal, pervasive: Bhairava-Lord, I praise.

## par-p08 — remembered, entered, known
This septad of good conduct — dissolved other thoughts — those desiring the ultimate rightly remember in the heart. Those, O Bhairava's one, again enter the supreme abode, and know the ultimate-thing-discussion of the three worlds.

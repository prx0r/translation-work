## The parable (canonical draft, ≈85s spoken)

> A painter tries to paint the light itself. He paints the lamp, the
> lit wall, his own lit hand — never the light: too close to
> everything, it is in no picture. Meanwhile a great clear jewel he
> carries shows him a parade — palace, elephant, horse, cloud, ocean,
> mountain — walking across its face, divided one from another, and
> none of them different from the glass; no thing outside the jewel
> makes its parade: the jewel's own sheen flashes it all. Hundreds of
> shining things, one undivided house of self-light, showing its own
> hundred faces. He stops hunting for the maker and stands in the
> light that was never in any picture — the non-lord that is lord,
> having nothing left to accomplish. And the discussion, being
> honest, ends where it always ends: remembered in the heart.

**Map (hinge):**
- painter, never the light → *distinct from sun, moon, lamp, light
  itself does not shine as an object — over-pervasion; illumined/
  illuminator mere convention* [p01]
- jewel's parade: palace·elephant·horse·cloud·ocean·mountain, none
  different from the glass → *difference not cut from its ground;
  palace, elephant, horse, cloud, ocean, mountain… as from jewel-
  mirror* [p04]
- divided one from another yet none from the glass; no thing outside
  → *the free mirror of consciousness needs no other cause* [p05]
- jewel's own sheen flashes it all → *from self-luminosity,
  hundredfold, in the one abode* [p02] + *they flash by the power of
  your glory; you are maker and Lord* [p06]
- dense own-consciousness = Śiva, division shining within variegated
  power → the parade's colors kept inside one glass [p03]
- non-lord that is lord, nothing left to accomplish → *non-lord,
  lord; established yet unaccomplishable; eternal, pervasive:
  Bhairava-Lord I praise* [p07]
- discussion ends remembered in the heart → *the conduct-septad
  remembered in the heart; enter the supreme abode again* [p08]

**Limit:** the jewel-show must NOT be called illusion-and-therefore-
nothing (the text never voids it: divisions shine WITHIN power) —
and the light must never be given a source object (no sun, no lamp
disc at top: over-pervasion means it is background, not figure).
**Rasa:** śānta-jñāna throughout (knowledge as sthāyibhāva), adbhuta
at the parade — the close is quiet remembrance, the p08 nigamana.
**Bar check:** one subject, retellable, plain (tattva/bhedābheda/
svaprakāśa decoded: glass-parade, own-sheen, picture-light).

## The seal — statement

**The sourceless light and the jewel's parade:** a painter's table
with canvases of everything lit except light itself; the great jewel
crossed by the colored parade (palace→mountain); above it all a
sky-bright with NO sun, no lamp — luminosity with no object — the
parade one glass, the light no picture.

**Correspondence axis:** DOWN: many-colored parade in one glass
(division-within-ground); UP: brightness with no figure (light-beyond-
object). The painter moves from failed-object (canvas) to unobject
(sky) — bottom shows what light is NOT, top shows what it IS, and the
jewel in the middle is where the two truths meet.

## Composition laws (this seal)

ONE subject (the painter; jewel = object). The sky must contain no
disc — a sun would destroy the thesis (sourceless light). Parade
stays INSIDE the jewel silhouette. The empty canvas reads empty on
purpose (never put light there — that's the joke of p01). No
letterforms; no roundels.

## The image prompt (seal text ONLY — reference carries style)

> On aged vellum, one scene: a painter in umber sits at his table
> with several small finished canvases at his feet — a lamp, a lit
> wall, a lit hand — and one canvas standing empty; before him a
> great crystal-lapis jewel shows a colored parade crossing its face:
> crimson palace, grey elephant, white horse, ivory cloud, lapis
> ocean, umber mountain — all inside the glass, none leaving it,
> the jewel glowing from its own sheen with nothing shining on it;
> above everything a sky of even sourceless ivory-gold brightness,
> no sun, no lamp, no disc; he stands at last, hands empty. Burnished
> raised gold on the jewel only, pale before dark, black outline,
> white highlights, shell-gold accents. One subject. No text, no
> letters, no signature.

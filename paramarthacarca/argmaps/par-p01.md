# Paramārthacarcā par-p01 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 1)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 par-p01 = verse 1. Lore: the opening lamp-analogy — a light distinct from sun/moon/lamp illumination needs no other light, and escapes notice by over-pervasion; the classic Pratyabhijñā self-luminosity (svaprakāśa) move, here in two lines.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 1 of the Paramārthacarcā (Abhinavagupta, 8 verses). Gloss: of-sun-moon-lamp-and-the-rest (arkendudīpādi) illumination (avabhāsa) distinct-from (bhinnam) not (na) shines (ābhāti) on-account-of-over-pervasion (ativyāptatayā) and (ca) hence (tataḥ); of-the-nature-of-light (prakāśarūpam) that (tad) just-this-much (iyat) illumined-illuminator-named (prakāśyaprakāśatākhyā) convention (vyavahāraḥ) indeed (eva).

Sanskrit: arkendudīpādyavabhāsabhinnaṃ nābhātyativyāptatayā tataśca | prakāśarūpaṃ tadiyatprakāśyaprakāśatākhyā vyavahāra eva

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 1:**
> arkendudīpādyavabhāsabhinnaṃ nābhātyativyāptatayā tataśca | prakāśarūpaṃ tadiyatprakāśyaprakāśatākhyā vyavahāra eva

[i.e. distinct from the illumination of sun, moon, lamp and the rest, it does not shine — on account of over-pervasion; and hence: of the nature of light, just this much — illumined-illuminator named — is mere convention.]

Distinct (bhinnam) from the illumination (avabhāsa) of sun, moon, lamp and the rest (arkendudīpādi), it does not shine (na ābhāti) — on account of over-pervasion (ativyāptatayā): too pervasive to show up as an object. And hence (tataḥ...ca): it is (tad) of the nature of light (prakāśarūpam), just this much (iyat) — illumined and illuminator named (prakāśya-prakāśatā-ākhyā) is convention (vyavahāraḥ) merely (eva). The subject-object vocabulary (illumined vs illuminator) is demoted to convention; what is, is light itself, unobjectifiable because everywhere.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Ativyāptatayā (on account of over-pervasion) is read as the reason it does not shine — pervasion so total it cannot appear as an object.
2. The verse states the thesis baldly; no argument beyond the lamp-analogy is given here.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry analogy-then-demotion: not lit like other lights (too pervasive to shine as object), so illumined-vs-illuminator is mere convention. L2 must keep over-pervasion as the hinge word. Carry the self-luminous real into verse 2, where nothing is allowed to stand apart from knowledge.

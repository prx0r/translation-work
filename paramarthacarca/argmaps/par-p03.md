# Paramārthacarcā par-p03 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 3)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 par-p03 = verse 3. Lore: the verse's pivot is itthaṃ (thus) — from the denied separation of verse 2 to the positive declaration: dense own-consciousness, one indeed, is Śiva, the universe's supreme light; yet grasper/grasped division still shines within variegated power.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 3 of the Paramārthacarcā (Abhinavagupta, 8 verses). Gloss: thus (ittham) own-consciousness-dense (svasaṃvidghanaḥ) one (ekaḥ) indeed (eva) Śiva (śivaḥ) he (saḥ) of-the-universe (viśvasya) supreme (paraḥ) light (prakāśaḥ); there (tatra) even (api) shines (bhāti) indeed (eva) in-variegated-power (vicitraśaktau) apprehended (grāhya) apprehender (gṛhītṛ) division (pravibhāga) difference (bhedaḥ).

Sanskrit: itthaṃ svasaṃvidghana eka eva śivaḥ sa viśvasya paraḥ prakāśaḥ | tatrāpi bhātyeva vicitraśaktau grāhyagṛhītṛpravibhāgabhedaḥ

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 3:**
> itthaṃ svasaṃvidghana eka eva śivaḥ sa viśvasya paraḥ prakāśaḥ | tatrāpi bhātyeva vicitraśaktau grāhyagṛhītṛpravibhāgabhedaḥ

[i.e. thus — dense own-consciousness, one indeed: he is Śiva, the universe's supreme light. There too, indeed, shines, in variegated power: apprehended-apprehender-division-difference.]

Thus (ittham): dense (ghanaḥ) own-consciousness (svasaṃvit), one (ekaḥ) indeed (eva) — he (saḥ) is Śiva (śivaḥ), the universe's (viśvasya) supreme (paraḥ) light (prakāśaḥ). The positive declaration after two verses of denial: consciousness, dense and one, is Śiva himself as cosmic light. Yet there (tatra) too (api), indeed (eva) shines (bhāti): in variegated (vicitra) power (śaktau) — apprehended (grāhya), apprehender (gṛhītṛ), division (pravibhāga), difference (bhedaḥ). Division shines without dividing the light; grasper/grasped play on within the undivided.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Tatrāpi (even there) is read as concessive: even within the declared nonduality, division-appearances shine.
2. No new doctrine beyond verses 1–2; the verse converts denial into declaration.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry declaration-with-concession: dense one consciousness is Śiva, the universe's supreme light — and therein division still shines. L2 must keep both halves in tension, not resolve them. Carry the shining division into verse 4, where its vehicle (mirror) is named.

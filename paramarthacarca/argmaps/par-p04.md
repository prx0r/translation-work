# Paramārthacarcā par-p04 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 4)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 par-p04 = verse 4. Lore: palace, elephant, horse, cloud, ocean, mountain — the stock list of reflected things — set against the jewel-mirror; the verse is elliptical (no verb for the reflecting), and the ellipsis is kept, not filled.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 4 of the Paramārthacarcā (Abhinavagupta, 8 verses). Gloss: difference (bhedaḥ) that (saḥ) and (ca) this (ayam) not (na) thence (tataḥ) different (vibhinnaḥ) free (svacchanda) most-perfectly-clear (susvacchatama) of-the-one-abode (ekadhāmnaḥ); palace (prāsāda) elephant (hasti) horse (aśva) cloud (payoda) ocean (sindhu) mountain (giri) etcetera (ādi) just-as (yadvat) of-jewel-mirror-and-the-like (maṇidarpaṇādeḥ).

Sanskrit: bhedaḥ sa cāyaṃ na tato vibhinnaḥ svacchandasusvacchatamaikadhāmnaḥ | prāsādahastyaśvapayodasindhugiryādi yadvanmaṇidarpaṇādeḥ

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 4:**
> bhedaḥ sa cāyaṃ na tato vibhinnaḥ svacchandasusvacchatamaikadhāmnaḥ | prāsādahastyaśvapayodasindhugiryādi yadvanmaṇidarpaṇādeḥ

[i.e. that difference, and this — not different thence: of the free, most-perfectly-clear one-abode. Palace, elephant, horse, cloud, ocean, mountain and the rest — just as of jewel-mirror and the like.]

That (saḥ) difference (bhedaḥ), and (ca) this (ayam): not (na) different (vibhinnaḥ) thence (tataḥ) — from the free (svacchanda), most-perfectly-clear (susvacchatama) one-abode (ekadhāmnaḥ). Difference is not cut off from its ground; it belongs without dividing. Then the analogy, deliberately bare: palace (prāsāda), elephant (hasti), horse (aśva), cloud (payoda), ocean (sindhu), mountain (giri) and the rest (ādi) — just as (yadvat) of jewel-mirror and the like (maṇidarpaṇādeḥ). The stock list of reflected things against the perfectly clear mirror: no verb supplied, none needed — the image does the work the verse withholds.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The analogy's verb is missing (elliptical just-as clause): left missing, not supplied.
2. Most-perfectly-clear (susvacchatama) is read as the mirror-ground's clarity admitting every image without stain.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry thesis-then-image: difference uncut from its ground; then the bare list against the jewel-mirror. L2 must not invent the missing verb. Carry the mirror into verse 5, where its belly becomes the argument's engine.

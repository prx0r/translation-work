# Paramārthacarcā par-p06 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 6)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 par-p06 = verse 6. Lore: the verse turns second-person, addressing the consciousness-dense one as supreme — the hymn's only direct address before the closing; universes shining at once (drāk) in the addressee.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 6 of the Paramārthacarcā (Abhinavagupta, 8 verses). Gloss: O-consciousness-dense-one (saṃvidghana) therefore (tena) supreme (paraḥ) you (tvam) indeed (eva); in-you (tvayi) indeed (eva) universes (viśvāni) shine (cakāsati) at-once (drāk); flash-forth (sphuranti) and (ca) your (tvat) of-glory (mahasaḥ) by-power (prabhāvāt) you (tvam) indeed (eva) and (ca) of-these (eṣām) supreme-Lord (parameśa) maker (kartā).

Sanskrit: saṃvidghanas tena parastvam eva tvayyeva viśvāni cakāsati drāk | sphuranti ca tvanmahasaḥ prabhāvāt tvam eva caiṣāṃ parameśakartā

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 6:**
> saṃvidghanas tena parastvam eva tvayyeva viśvāni cakāsati drāk | sphuranti ca tvanmahasaḥ prabhāvāt tvam eva caiṣāṃ parameśakartā

[i.e. O consciousness-dense one — therefore you indeed are supreme; in you indeed the universes shine at once; and they flash forth by the power of your glory; you indeed are also their supreme-Lord maker.]

Address first: O consciousness-dense one (saṃvidghana) — therefore (tena) you (tvam) indeed (eva) are supreme (paraḥ). Then three clauses of consequence: in you (tvayi) indeed (eva) the universes (viśvāni) shine (cakāsati) at once (drāk); and (ca) they flash forth (sphuranti) by the power (prabhāvāt) of your (tvat) glory (mahasaḥ); you (tvam) indeed (eva) also (ca) are their (eṣām) supreme-Lord (parameśa) maker (kartā). Shining, flashing, making — all in you, all at once. The addressee of the hymn is declared the universes' maker by glory-power, not by effort.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Tvat (your) is read as genitive of tvam (you); the compound tvanmahasaḥ (of-your-glory) supports it.
2. At once (drāk) qualifies the shining: no sequence between being in you and shining.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry address-then-three-consequences: supreme declared, universes shining, flashing by glory-power, maker by the same. L2 must keep the second-person pressure — this verse speaks to, not about. Carry the maker into verse 7, where the maker praises.

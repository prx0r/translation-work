# Paramārthacarcā par-p08 — ARGUMENT MAP (verse 8)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 par-p08 = verse 8. Lore: the closing sadvṛttasaptaka (septad of good conduct) remembered in the heart, and the self-naming close — the hymn names its own discussion (paramārthacarcām) as what the wise know; bhairavīya read as vocative address to the Bhairava-follower.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Verse 8 of the Paramārthacarcā (Abhinavagupta, 8 verses). Gloss: of-good-conduct (sadvṛtta) septad (saptakam) this (idam) dissolved (galitā) other (anya) thoughts (cintāḥ) rightly (samyak) remember (smaranti) in-heart (hṛdaye) desiring-the-ultimate (paramārthakāmāḥ); those (te) O-Bhairava's-one (bhairavīya) supreme-abode (paradhāma) again (muhuḥ) enter (viśanti) know (jānanti) and (ca) of-three-worlds (trijagatī) ultimate-thing-discussion (paramārthacarcām).

Sanskrit: sadvṛttasaptakam idaṃ galitānyacintāḥ samyak smaranti hṛdaye paramārthakāmāḥ | te bhairavīya paradhāma muhur viśanti jānanti ca trijagatīparamārthacarcām

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Verse 8:**
> sadvṛttasaptakam idaṃ galitānyacintāḥ samyak smaranti hṛdaye paramārthakāmāḥ | te bhairavīya paradhāma muhur viśanti jānanti ca trijagatīparamārthacarcām

[i.e. this septad of good conduct — dissolved other thoughts — those desiring the ultimate rightly remember in the heart; those, O Bhairava's one, again enter the supreme abode, and know the ultimate-thing-discussion of the three worlds.]

This (idam) septad (saptakam) of good conduct (sadvṛtta) — those whose other (anya) thoughts (cintāḥ) are dissolved (galitā), desiring the ultimate (paramārthakāmāḥ), rightly (samyak) remember (smaranti) it in the heart (hṛdaye): conduct remembered inwardly, not performed outwardly. Those (te) — O Bhairava's one (bhairavīya) — again (muhuḥ) enter (viśanti) the supreme abode (paradhāma), and (ca) know (jānanti) the ultimate-thing-discussion (paramārthacarcām) of the three worlds (trijagatī). Entering, then knowing: the abode first, its discussion after. The hymn names itself at the close — those who enter know this very चर्चा.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Bhairavīya is read as vocative address to the Bhairava-follower; the nominative-plural reading converges on the same persons.
2. Sadvṛttasaptaka (septad of good conduct) is not enumerated in the hymn; no list is imported.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry remembering-then-entering-then-knowing: the septad remembered in the heart, the abode entered again, the discussion known. L2 must end on the self-naming close — the hymn's title as its final fruit. This closes the arc opened in verse 1's convention: discussion itself is what the entered know.

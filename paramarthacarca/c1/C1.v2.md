# C1 — Paramārthacarcā (final condensation of the arguments)

*States each passage's argumentative move and the hymn's whole arc. For the read prose see L2.v2.*

## The arc in one paragraph

Too pervasive to shine as an object — so illumined-versus-illuminator is mere convention; light itself is what is. Nothing dually apart from knowledge; no difference in consciousness; the individual undivided. Consciousness dense with itself, single — Śiva, supreme light of the universe — yet division still shines within. Difference uncut from its ground; palace through mountain against the jewel-mirror. Even a common mirror needs only mind for prototype-with-reflection — so no other cause among things. Addressed: you are supreme; universes shine at once, flashing by glory-power; their maker. Paradox held — established yet unaccomplishable, non-lord yet lord — and praised. Conduct remembered, abode entered, discussion known. Convention → non-separation → declaration → ground → mirror → address → praise → knowing.

## par-p01 (v. 1) — too pervasive to shine

Distinct from every lit thing, light itself does not shine as an object — over-pervasion forbids it. Hence: of the nature of light, just this — illumined-versus-illuminator named is mere convention.

## par-p02 (v. 2) — nothing apart from knowledge

No thing dually distinct from knowledge; made of this and that, no difference in consciousness. From self-luminosity, hundredfold, in the one abode — the individual would not be divided.

## par-p03 (v. 3) — dense consciousness is Śiva

Consciousness dense with itself, single: Śiva, supreme light of the universe. Yet therein division shines — apprehended, apprehender, their division and difference — within variegated power.

## par-p04 (v. 4) — uncut difference; the mirror list

That difference, and this, not different thence: of the free, most-clear one-abode. Palace, elephant, horse, cloud, ocean, mountain — just as of jewel-mirror and the like.

## par-p05 (v. 5) — the mirror needs only mind

If even a common mirror shows prototype-with-reflection as mind-established — then no other cause among things for the free-consciousness mirror. The small case conceded to win the great one.

## par-p06 (v. 6) — addressed as supreme, maker by glory

O consciousness-dense one: you indeed are supreme. In you the universes shine at once, flashing by glory-power; you indeed are also their maker. Address, then consequence: shining, flashing, making.

## par-p07 (v. 7) — paradox held; praise given

Primordially established yet unaccomplishable; non-lord yet lord; place-and-time full of own power — eternal, pervasive Bhairava-Lord: I praise. What argument cannot reconcile, praise receives.

## par-p08 (v. 8) — remembered, entered, known

Conduct remembered in the heart; abode entered again; discussion itself known. The hymn names its close: those who enter know this very discussion.

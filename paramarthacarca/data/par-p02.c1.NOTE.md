# C1 NOTE — par-p02.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

No thing stands dually apart from knowledge; in consciousness there is no difference; the individual, in the one abode of self-luminosity, stays undivided.

## FUNCTION

Establishes non-duality: nothing apart from knowledge.

## KEY TERMS

* **jñānadvibhinna** — dually distinct from knowledge
* **tattatkṛta** — made of this and that
* **saṃvit** — consciousness
* **bheda** — difference
* **svayamprakāśa** — self-luminosity
* **aikadhāman** — one abode
* **prātisvikī** — individual
* **vibhedita** — divided

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Nothing apart from knowledge.

## EXPLANATION

No thing (na...kaścid arthaḥ) is dually-distinct (dvibhinnaḥ) from knowledge (jñāna): nothing stands apart in duality. Made of this and that (tattatkṛtaḥ) — however composite — in consciousness (saṃvidi) there is no difference (na...bhedaḥ). From self-luminosity (svayamprakāśāt), hundredfold (śatam), in the one abode (aikadhāmni): the individual (prātisvikī) would not indeed be divided. Self-luminosity's hundredfold-ness in one abode leaves the individual undivided — kept as printed against the tempting satatam, flagged, not smoothed.

## BOUNDARY

'śatam' (hundredfold) vs 'satatam' (always) — print form kept, flagged.

## RELATED

* Paramārthacarcā v.3 — dense consciousness is Śiva.

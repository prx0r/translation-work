# C1 NOTE — par-p03.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Dense, one own-consciousness is Śiva himself, the universe's supreme light — yet grasper-grasped division still shines within variegated power.

## FUNCTION

Establishes dense consciousness as Śiva; division within the undivided.

## KEY TERMS

* **ittham** — thus
* **svasaṃvidghana** — dense own-consciousness
* **eka** — one
* **śiva** — Śiva
* **prakāśaḥ** — universe's supreme light
* **vicitraśakti** — variegated power
* **grāhya** — apprehended
* **gṛhītṛ** — apprehender
* **pravibhāga** — division

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Dense consciousness is Śiva.

## EXPLANATION

Thus (ittham): dense (ghanaḥ) own-consciousness (svasaṃvit), one (ekaḥ) indeed (eva) — he (saḥ) is Śiva, the universe's supreme light. The positive declaration after two verses of denial. Yet there (tatra) too (api), indeed (eva) shines (bhāti): in variegated (vicitra) power (śaktau) — apprehended, apprehender, division, difference. Division shines without dividing the light; grasper/grasped play on within the undivided.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'ittham' (thus) refers back to p01-p02 or opens a new movement is unresolved.

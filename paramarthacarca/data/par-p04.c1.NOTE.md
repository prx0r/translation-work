# C1 NOTE — par-p04.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

Difference is not cut off from its ground — free, most-clear, one-abode — then the bare list against the jewel-mirror: palace through mountain, just as from jewel-mirror and the like.

## FUNCTION

Establishes difference uncut from its ground via the mirror list.

## KEY TERMS

* **bheda** — difference
* **tataḥ** — thence
* **svacchanda** — free
* **susvacchatama** — most-perfectly-clear
* **ekadhāman** — one-abode
* **prāsāda** — palace/elephant/horse/cloud/ocean/mountain
* **yadvat** — just as
* **maṇidarpaṇa** — jewel-mirror

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Uncut difference; the mirror list.

## EXPLANATION

That (saḥ) difference (bhedaḥ), and (ca) this (ayam): not (na) different (vibhinnaḥ) thence (tataḥ) — from the free, most-perfectly-clear one-abode. Difference belongs without dividing. Then the analogy, deliberately bare: palace, elephant, horse, cloud, ocean, mountain and the rest — just as (yadvat) of jewel-mirror and the like (maṇidarpaṇādeḥ). No verb supplied, none needed: difference uncut from its ground, the bare list its proof — reflected things need no ground but the clear mirror.

## BOUNDARY

Whether the list is exhaustive or exemplary is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Paramārthacarcā v.5 — the mirror needs only mind.

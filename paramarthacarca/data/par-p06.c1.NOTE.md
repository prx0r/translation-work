# C1 NOTE — par-p06.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

O consciousness-dense one: you indeed are supreme; in you the universes shine at once, flashing by glory-power; you stand as both their maker and their supreme Lord.

## FUNCTION

Establishes address: you are supreme, all in you.

## KEY TERMS

* **saṃvidghana** — consciousness-dense
* **tena** — therefore
* **para** — supreme
* **tvayi** — in you
* **viśvāni** — universes
* **drāk** — at once
* **sphuranti** — flash forth
* **tvat-mahas** — your glory
* **prabhāva** — power
* **parameśakartṛ** — supreme-Lord maker

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Address: you are supreme.

## EXPLANATION

Address first: O consciousness-dense one (saṃvidghana) — therefore (tena) you (tvam) indeed (eva) are supreme (paraḥ). Then three clauses of consequence: in you (tvayi) indeed (eva) the universes (viśvāni) shine (cakāsati) at once (drāk); and (ca) they flash forth (sphuranti) by the power (prabhāvāt) of your (tvat) glory (mahasaḥ); you (tvam) indeed (eva) also (ca) are their (eṣām) supreme-Lord (parameśa) maker (kartā). Shining, flashing, making — all in you, all at once, by glory-power not effort.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'drāk' (at once) modifies shining or flashing is unresolved.

## RELATED

* Paramārthacarcā v.7 — paradox held; praise given.

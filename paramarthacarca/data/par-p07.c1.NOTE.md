## SUMMARY

Established yet unaccomplishable, non-lord yet lord, place-and-time full of own power — eternal, pervasive: Bhairava-Lord, I praise.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Paradox held; praise given.

## EXPLANATION

Thus (ittham): self-consciousness (svasaṃvedanam), primordially established (ādisiddham), unaccomplishable (asādhyam) — the self (ātmānam), non-lord (anīśam), lord (īśam). Three incompatible predicates held without blinking: established from the beginning, never to be accomplished; non-lord and lord at once. Then the object of its only first-person act: whose place and time are full of own power — eternal (nityam), pervasive (vibhum): Bhairava-Lord (bhairavanātham), I praise (īḍe). Vadeśakālam is obscure (possibly vaśe deśakālam, place-and-time under control); glossed neutrally, marked here, not smoothed. Praise, not proof, is what the paradox gets.

## BOUNDARY

'vadeśakālam' is obscure; glossed neutrally, marked here.

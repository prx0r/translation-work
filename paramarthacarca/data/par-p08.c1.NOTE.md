# C1 NOTE — par-p08.c1

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

The conduct-septad remembered in the heart; the abode entered again; the discussion itself known — it names its own close.

## FUNCTION

Establishes remembered, entered, known — the ending.

## KEY TERMS

* **sadvṛtta** — good conduct
* **saptaka** — septad
* **galita** — dissolved
* **anyacintā** — other thoughts
* **samyak** — rightly
* **smaranti** — remember
* **hṛdaya** — heart
* **paramārthakāma** — desiring the ultimate
* **bhairavīya** — Bhairava's one
* **paradhāman** — supreme abode
* **muhuḥ** — again

* **jānanti** — know
* **trijagatīparamārthacarcā** — three-worlds' ultimate-thing-discussion

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Remembered, entered, known.

## EXPLANATION

This (idam) septad (saptakam) of good conduct (sadvṛtta) — those whose other (anya) thoughts (cintāḥ) are dissolved (galitā), desiring the ultimate (paramārthakāmāḥ), rightly (samyak) remember (smaranti) it in the heart (hṛdaye): conduct remembered inwardly, not performed outwardly. Those (te) — O Bhairava's one (bhairavīya, vocative address) — again (muhuḥ) enter (viśanti) the supreme abode (paradhāma), and (ca) know (jānanti) the ultimate-thing-discussion (paramārthacarcām) of the three worlds (trijagatī). Entering, then knowing: the abode first, its discussion after. It names itself at the ending — those who enter know this very discussion.

## BOUNDARY

Whether 'bhairavīya' is vocative address or genitive is unresolved.

# Paramarthadvadasika pdva-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
Enjoy peace, always. With few words, stop the chatter-course. By the way of its stopping ask: what is this, how, who am I — and do not doubt. Whatever shines in unbroken sequence, making being and nonbeing shine apart — that is void, Śiva's abode, the highest real thing, brahman. Then what grasping is here?
The Sanskrit: *śāntiṃ sambhaja nityam alpavacanair jalpakramaṃ saṃhara | tatsaṃhāragamena kiṃ katham idaṃ ko 'sīti mā cīkḷpaḥ | bhāvābhāvavibhāgabhāsakatayā yad bhāty abhagnakramaṃ | tac chūnyaṃ śivadhāma vastu paramaṃ brahmātha ko 'tra grahaḥ*

## v.2
If you reach reality only after first rejecting non-reality, that itself is non-reality. But if non-reality is then reality itself — that is not reality, and not like that.
The Sanskrit: *yady atattvaparihārapūrvakaṃ tattvam eṣi tad atattvam eva hi | yady atattvam atha tattvam eva vātattvam eva na tu tattvam īdṛśam*

## v.3
Nothing that shines stands apart from shining. If you say even difference shines — in shining, it does not shine. So give up the grasping at its breaking-ways. In dream, having become dream itself — mere play, no maker of fear: sword-blows, plunges into water, fire-eating, thunder-claps and the rest.
The Sanskrit: *yad yad bhāti na bhānataḥ pṛthag idaṃ bhedo 'pi bhātīti ced | bhāne so 'pi na bhāti kiṃ jahi tatas tadbhaṅgibhaṅgagraham | svapne svapnatayā prathāṃ gatavati krīḍaiva no bhītikṛc | chastrāghātajalāvapātahutabhuṅnirghātabandhādikam*

## v.4
First resolve upon knowledge, action, and measuring. Whatever you declare, sir — what sets it apart from the inert? Though quivering, even the inert — is it not the abode of nondual awareness, beginningless, eternal, ungraspable, true-formed?
The Sanskrit: *jñānakriyākalanapūrvakam adhyavasyed | yad yad bhavān kathaya ko 'sya jaḍād viśeṣaḥ | sphūrjañ jaḍo 'pi na kim advayabodhadhāma | nissīmanityaniravagrahasatyarūpam*

## v.5
You who illumine beings — if you are smeared by them, what is that to you? If it shines — hail! — your greatness stands unbroken even there. If not, it is not. Even so, in both cases — unrestrained, unfettered, with delusion unbroken, ever content in greatness — sir, you are ever awake.
The Sanskrit: *bhāvānām avabhāsako 'si yadi tair mālinyam ātanyate | kiṃ te tad yadi bhāti hanta bhavatas tatrāpy akhaṇḍaṃ mahaḥ | no cen nāsti tad evam apy ubhayathā nirvyājaniryantraṇātruṭyadvibhramanityatṛptamahimā nityaprabuddho 'si bhoḥ*

## v.6
Send your sight outward, beyond the path of the target. Lest mimicking Bhairava be mere fraud. For the sky of nondual waking there is no outside; no inside for the abode of spaceless unfolding.
The Sanskrit: *dṛṣṭiṃ bahiḥ prahiṇu lakṣyapathātiriktaṃ | syād bhairavānukaraṇaṃ vata vañcaneyam | nirdvandvabodhagaganasya na bāhyam asti | nābhyantaraṃ niravakāśavikāsadhāmnaḥ*

## v.7
When the agitation of desire's spread rises — whatever flashes, watch just that. In beginning, middle, and ends, if there — you shine, you shine; all dissolves into you.
The Sanskrit: *vāsanāprasaravibhramodaye | yad yad ullasati tad tad īkṣyatām | ādimadhyanidhaneṣu tatra ced | bhāsi bhāsi tava līyate 'khilam*

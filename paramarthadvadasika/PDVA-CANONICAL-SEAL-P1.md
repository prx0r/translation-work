## The parable (canonical draft, ≈87s)

> A city shimmers at the road's edge — mirage-city: beautiful from
> far, built of heat, its error-chains teeming like streets — and it
> stands only where it shines: no shine in the great sky of
> consciousness, no highest rest anywhere. So the traveler stops his
> chatter — few words — and asks himself three, without doubting:
> what is this, how, who am I. Nothing that shines stands apart from
> shining; even difference's breaking-ways don't shine in shining.
> What sets him apart from the inert? He sends his sight past the
> path's target: the sky of waking has no outside — none inside
> either. In dream the unreal is a road, in deep sleep a display;
> beyond them, conditionless: lights rest in him, undistinguished —
> I am He, unfeigned. Let time reckon, the creator make, love churn:
> the play is allowed. Then the fierce voice — who is here? whom? —
> and the shattering falls: born-fear shattered, affliction-jackals
> scared off, even wise riders yoked to wisdom wake. The upsurge of
> beyond. Closing set by another hand.

**Map (hinge):**
- stop-chatter few words; three asks doubt-free → p01 vv1
  (*enjoy peace, few words; what-how-who, no doubt*) [p01]
- nothing-shines-apart; breaking-ways don't-shine → p01 v3 [p01]
- what-sets-apart-from-inert (the debate-fire) → p01 v4 [p01]
- sight past target; sky no-outside no-inside → p01 v6 [p01]
  (FOLD: vv2 and vv5 — reality/nonreality binders + smeared-by-
  beings — ride the shine-condition line; flagged)
- mirage-city; stands-only-where-shines → p02 v1 [p02]
- dream-road / sleep-display / conditionless-beyond → p02 v2 [p02]
- lights in him undistinguished; I am He unfeigned → p02 v3 [p02]
- let time / creator / love — the play allowed → p02 v4 [p02]
- who-is-here-whom (fierce voice) → p02 v5 [p02]
- shattering: born-fear, affliction-jackals, wise-riders wake,
  upsurge → p02 v6 [p02]
- closing set by another hand → the seal-corner (meta-epilogue) [p02]

**Limit:** two binders folded (flagged); the fierce "whom" is the
text's own voice (kept, not amplified — devouring is directed at
FEARS, never people: "affliction-jackals" = what's devoured).
**Rasa:** śānta-vīra (stillness carrying debate-fire) → śānta
(wonder worked through to rest) — the parable's pulse: quiet-ask →
allowed-play → fierce-shatter → rest. The jackal-scatter IS the
vīra-peak; the permissions (let time/creator/love) are the śānta's
hinge.
**Bar check:** one subject (the stopped traveler; city = mirage,
jackals = rim-beasts); retellable: "a heat-city only stands where
a sky shines it; he stops, asks three, and the fears run." Plain:
so'ham→I am He; mirage-city plain; jackals plain.

## The seal — statement

**The city twice — once as heat, once as sky:** the mirage-city at
the road's edge exists in TWO places: below as heat-shimmer on the
horizon, and INVERTED above, living in the consciousness-sky (the
stand-condition made visual: it stands only where it shines); the
stopped traveler mid-frame with three small lights over his head
(the three asks), gold threads running from the sky-lights DOWN to
his chest (lights rest in HIM: I am He); three rim-objects of
permission — a turning hourglass, a potter's wheel, a braided cord
(time reckons, creator makes, love churns); at his feet dark
jackals scattering from a small burst; and at the frame's bottom-
right a BLANK little seal-corner — the closing set by another hand.

and blank (no letters — the meta-gesture only).

## The image prompt (seal text ONLY — reference carries style)

> On aged vellum, one road-edge scene: a mirage-city of heat-shimmer
> stands on the horizon at the bottom, and the SAME city hangs
> inverted above, living inside a cool lapis sky; between them a
> lone traveler sits stopped with three small lights hovering over
> his head, fine gold threads running from the sky-lights down to
> his chest; a thin sight-line from his eyes overshoots the road;
> three sky-bands veil, display, clear top to bottom of the sky; at
> the rim a turning hourglass, a potter's wheel, a braided cord;
> at his feet three dark jackals flee a small burst of light; in the
> frame's bottom-right corner a blank little seal; amber mirage
> below, lapis sky above, gold threads, grey jackals; pale before
> dark, black outline, white highlights, shell-gold accents. One
> subject. No text, no letters, no signature.

## PLAIN-PASS v2 (supersedes the parable + seal-statement above — one-line law)
**Name: "the heat-city."** One-line test: *"a city made of heat only
stands where the sky shines it — and he stopped to ask three plain
questions and the fears ran off."*
> A city shimmers at the road's edge — heat-haze city, beautiful
> from far, its streets always ending in nothing — and it stands
> only where it shines: no shine in the great sky of what sees
> everything, no rest anywhere. So the traveler stops talking —
> few words — and asks himself three, no doubting: what is this,
> how, who am I? Nothing that shines shines alone; even the
> breaking-aparts don't shine in shining. What makes him different
> from a rock? He looks past where the road points: the sky of
> waking has no outside — none inside either. In dream the fake is
> the road; in deep sleep it's a show; past that, nothing to grab:
> the lights live in him — I am He. Let time count, let the maker
> make, let love churn: all allowed. Then the fierce line — who is
> here? whom? — and the smash comes: the born-fear is smashed, the
> fear-jackals run, even wise riders wake. The surge of past-all.
> And somebody else signed the bottom.

# C1 — pdva-p01

## SUMMARY

Peace enjoyed, chatter stopped with few words; self-asked what-how-who without doubt — void as Śiva's abode, highest real, brahman. Reality reached only by first rejecting non-reality is that non-reality; non-reality claimed as reality is neither. Whatever shines is not apart from shining; difference does not add a second shining — grasping its breaking-ways abandoned; dream mere play. Knowledge, action, measuring first resolved: whatever declared, what sets it apart from the quivering inert — is it not the nondual-awareness-abode. The illuminator smeared or not stays unbroken in greatness — ever-awake either way. Sight sent past the target: no outside for nondual waking, no inside for spaceless unfolding; mimicry mere fraud. Desire rising watched flash by flash: shining, shining, all dissolving into you.

## FUNCTION

Clears the ground by refutation, then gives the two practices: gaze past all targets, watching of every flash.

## KEY TERMS

jalpakrama (chatter-course as what stops); tattvam/atattvam (reality/non-reality as conditional pair); bhāna (shining as non-difference); jaḍa (inert as quivering); avabhāsaka (illuminator); lakṣyapathātirikta (past-target); īkṣyatām (watched).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Maṅgala and refutations passed; gaze and desire practices begin.

## EXPLANATION

The opening enjoins the conditions of inquiry — peace, brevity, cessation — and aims them at the void that is Śiva's abode. Then the dialectic: mediated attainment collapses into its means, so reality-by-rejection is non-reality; appearance splits nothing from shining, so difference adds no second light and its breaking-ways go ungrasped, dreams included as mere play. If even the inert quivers, no line separates it from the beginningless ungraspable abode. The knower smeared or clean stays whole. Sight is sent where no target stands — outside and inside both refused — and whatever rises in desire is watched until, shining, it dissolves into the watcher.

No result stated yet; the practices are doors, not attainments.

In dreams the unreal itself is the road, and deep sleep puts on a show. Past that is knowing-with-nothing-on-it — so what grip does he have? In waking, if the pile of things stood solid as earth even for a moment — then through knowing, past that, this set apart: what answer-back even there? [E1]

Every light there is lives in me — room found in the highest sky, shining in me, all the same, no pieces. I am Him — no tricks, no end, nothing broken in my measuring, endless, true, free — His light chases the dark out of the warring pair, nondual and not, awake from the beginning. [E2]

It's all holy play — the sky itself seems to live in it, lined up by its own hand — let it rest on bodies and everything else. In the mess of the great upside-down folly — what don't I see? [E3]

Who is here, sir? Whom do I eat? Whom do I suddenly kill? Whom — gone wild eating up the home of wide-awake knowing — do I drink? [E4]

Fear born of becoming: smashed. The jackals of trouble: scattered. On wisdom's own yoke, even the wise get one true wake-up. Out of wandering the thick forest past all fullness, Hari Rāmyadeva made the elephant of no-difference trumpet. [E5]

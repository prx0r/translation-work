# C1 NOTE — pdva-p01

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Peace enjoyed, chatter stopped with few words; self-asked what-how-who without doubt — void as Śiva's abode, highest real, brahman. Reality reached only by first rejecting non-reality is that non-reality; non-reality claimed as reality is neither. Whatever shines is not apart from shining; difference does not add a second shining — grasping its breaking-ways abandoned; dream mere play. Knowledge, action, measuring first resolved: whatever declared, what sets it apart from the quivering inert — is it not the nondual-awareness-abode. The illuminator smeared or not stays unbroken in greatness — ever-awake either way. Sight sent past the target: no outside for nondual waking, no inside for spaceless unfolding; mimicry mere fraud. Desire rising watched flash by flash: shining, shining, all dissolving into you.

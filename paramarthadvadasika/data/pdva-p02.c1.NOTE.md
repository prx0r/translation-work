# C1 NOTE — pdva-p02

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Delusion dense with suffering and error-chains, mirage-city-like: if it does not shine in consciousness-sky, no highest rest anywhere. Dream's road unreal, deep-sleep display, waking heap earth-firm — each passed beyond by knowing, none refuted from inside. All lights in me, room-found in highest sky, shining undistinguished: I am He, infinite, true, free. Time reckoned, creation created, love churning — play abiding in body; nothing unseen in delusion's evil. Devour, slay, drink — whom, here, sir — frenzied in supreme-waking-abode-devouring. Fear shattered, jackals scared, awakening once on wisdom's yoke: Rāmyadeva's non-difference-elephant trumpets.

## VOICE MAP

Poet (cosmologist) v8; three-state survey v9; identity v10; permission v11; devourer v12; Ramyadeva seal v13.

## KEY TERMS

* cidvyomni — consciousness-sky as the only seat
* so'ham — I-He as found fact
* krīḍāḍambara — play-pomp as permission
* kavalīkaromi — I-devour as incorporation
* abhedakaribṛṃhita — non-difference-elephant-trumpet as close-image
* cakāstu/kāstu — may-shine/may-stand (fork adjudicated)

## CRUXES

* v8 cakāstu (may-shine, √cakās, fits cidvyomni) vs kāstu (may-stand, fits niṣṭhā): adjudicated in argmap, both kept live until verdict — settled for may-shine/may-stand respectively.
* The print variant (I devour vs whom, v12) stays recorded, not adopted; the close stays Ramyadeva's, not Abhinavagupta's.

## FUNCTION

From cosmology to identity to permission to devouring, ending in another's close.

# Paramārthadvādaśikā lore — 2026-11-04 (verse-usable)

## Work
Paramārthadvādaśikā (also titled Paramādvayadvādaśikā — colophon: paramādvayadvādaśikā athavā paramārthadvādaśikā), 13 verses + colophon. Edition: F. Sferra Hamburg input (SOURCE.txt in repo). 2 songs (pdva-p01 vv1-7, pdva-p02 vv8-13). Colophon attributes the text to Abhinavagupta, closing with a Ramyadeva verse (v13; Hari Rāmyadeva made the elephant trumpet). Colophon excluded from segments as scribal paratext; recorded here.

## Recension note (merge decision 2026-11-04)
paramadvayadvadashika == paramarthadvadasika: same work under variant title. Single gold from the Sferra recension; Abhinavastotravali print variants recorded, not built. Print variants (Ruma Dewan ed., from the search collation): v1 tathyātathyam... recension ( wholly different opening) vs śāntiṃ sambhaja; v4 dhyāna- vs jñāna-kriyā, so yamityādi nūnaṃ tāni; v5 variants; v7 variants; v9 variants (saraṇa/sauṣupta vs maraṇa/sauṣumna readings); v10 variants; v11 variants; v12 ko'tra bhor ahaṃ (I devour/slay/drink) vs ko'tra bhor yaṃ (whom...); v13 variants (abheda vs vibheda, sakṛd yad uddīpanam vs uddīpayan). Forks live in L0 only where a variant changes the gloss; argmaps adjudicate on record.

## Structure
- v1 maṅgala: peace, few words, stop chatter; the void as Śiva-abode.
- vv2-4 dialectic: reality via non-reality (v2), shining/non-difference (v3), knowledge-action resolve and the He-I (v4).
- vv5-6 the illuminator unsmeared (v5); sight past the target, no outside/inside (v6).
- v7 desire-practice: watch what flashes; all dissolves into you.
- vv8-9 delusion-cosmology (v8 mirage-city), dream/sleep/waking (v9).
- v10 so'ham: lights in me, I am He with beginningless waking-light.
- v11 time/creator/love play; nothing unseen.
- v12 triple devouring (kavalīkaromi, nihanmi, pivāmi): who, here, sir, whom.
- v13 Ramyadeva seal: fear shattered, jackals scared, non-difference-elephant trumpeted.

## Rasa pointers
Philosophical hymn (vicāra): śānta dominant carrying vīra undertow (the dialectical vigor of vv2-9, the devouring-triple v12). Sthāyibhāva rule: name only what the verse does.

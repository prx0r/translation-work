# Paramārthasāra — ARGMAP

## param-p01 (1–10)
- speaker: Śiṣya (student) asks; Guru (Abhinava) answers.
- question: What is the supreme principle?
- premises: Verse 1: Paraṃ parasthaṃ gahanād anādim ekaṃ niviṣṭaṃ bahudhā guhāsu | sarvālayaṃ sarvacarācarasthaṃ tvām eva śambhuṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadye (I resort to you, Śambhu, supreme, abiding in depths, beginningless, one, lodged in many caves, abode of all, in all moving/non-moving). Verse 2: garbhādhivāsapūrvakamaraṇāntakaduḥkhacakravibhrāntaḥ (revolving in wheel of suffering from womb-dwelling to death) ādhāram bhagavantaṃ śiṣyaḥ papraccha paramārtham (student asked the Lord, the support, the supreme truth). Verse 3: ādhārakārikābhiḥ taṃ gurur abhibhāṣati sma tatsāram (Guru spoke to him the essence in support-kārikās) kathayaty abhinavaguptaḥ śivaśāsanadṛṣṭiyogena (Abhinava speaks by Śiva-śāsana view). Verse 4: nijaśaktivaibhavabharād aṅdacatuṣṭayam idaṃ vibhāgena (by own-power-glory-weight, this fourfold universe in division) śaktir māyā prakṛtiḥ pṛthvī ceti prabhāvitaṃ prabhuṇā (power, Māyā, prakṛti, earth, manifested by Lord). Verse 5: tatrāntar viśvam idaṃ vicitratanukaraṇabhuvanasaṃtānam (therein, this world, varied body-instrument-world-continuum). Verse 6: bhoktā ca tatra dehī (and there, experiencer, embodied one). Verse 7: śiva e... (Śiva indeed...).
- inference: Supreme principle = Śambhu (Śiva); universe = fourfold (śakti/māyā/prakṛti/pṛthvī) manifested by Lord; experiencer = embodied Śiva.
- conclusion: Paramārthasāra opens with Śambhu as supreme (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## param-p02 (11–19)
- speaker: Guru (Abhinava).
- question: What is the supreme tattva?
- premises: Sarvavikalpavihīnaṃ śuddhaṃ śāntaṃ layodayavihīnam (free from all thought, pure, peaceful, free from dissolution/rise) yat paratattvaṃ tasmin vibhāti ṣaṭtriṃśadātma jagat (that supreme principle, in which shines the 36-fold world). Darpaṇabimbe yadvan nagaragrāmādicitram avibhāgi (as in mirror-image, city/village etc. undivided) bhāti vibhāgenaiva ca parasparaṃ darpaṇād api ca (shines in division, and mutually, from mirror too). Vimalatamaparamabhairavabodhāt tadvad vibhāgaśūnyam api (from purest supreme Bhairava-knowledge, thus though divisionless) anyonyaṃ ca tato 'pi ca vibhaktam ābhāti jagad etat (mutually and from that, divided, this world shines). Śivaśaktisadāśivatām īśvaravidyāmayīṃ ca tattvadaśām (Śiva-Śakti-Sadāśiva-Īśvara-Vidyā tattva-state). Śaktīnāṃ pañcānāṃ vibhaktabhāv... (five Śaktis' divided nature...).
- inference: Supreme tattva = pure, peaceful, thought-free; 36 tattvas shine in it like mirror-image; Śiva-Śakti-Sadāśiva-Īśvara-Vidyā state.
- conclusion: Supreme tattva doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## param-p03 (20–28)
- speaker: Guru (Abhinava).
- question: What are the senses and elements?
- premises: Śrotraṃ tvagakṣirasanā ghrāṇaṃ buddhīndriyāṇi śabdādau (ear, skin, eye, tongue, nose = cognitive senses in sound etc.) vākpāṇipādapāyūpasthaṃ karmendriyāṇi punaḥ (speech, hand, foot, anus, genitals = conative senses). Eṣāṃ grāhyo viṣayaḥ sūkṣmaḥ pravibhāgavarjito yaḥ syāt (their object, subtle, free from division) tanmātrapañcakaṃ tat śabdaḥ sparśo maho raso gandhaḥ (that five tanmātras: sound, touch, form, taste, smell). Etatsaṃsargavaśāt sthūlo viṣayas tu bhūtapañcakatām (by their combination, gross object = five elements) abhyeti nabhaḥ pavanas tejaḥ salilaṃ ca pṛthvī ca (becomes space, air, fire, water, earth). Tuṣa iva taṇḍulakaṇikām āvṛṇute prakṛtipūrvakaḥ sargaḥ (as husk covers grain, creation from prakṛti). Pṛthvīparyanto 'yaṃ caitanya... (up to earth, this consciousness...).
- inference: Senses (5 cognitive + 5 conative) → tanmātras (5) → elements (5); creation from prakṛti covers consciousness like husk covers grain.
- conclusion: Sense/element doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## param-p04 (29–37)
- speaker: Guru (Abhinava).
- question: What is bondage (bandha)?
- premises: Tadvad dharmādharmasvarnirayotpattimaraṇasukhaduḥkham (thus dharma/adharma, heaven/hell, birth/death, pleasure/pain) varṇāśramādi cātmany asad api vibhramabalād bhavati (varṇa/āśrama etc. in self, though unreal, arises by delusion's power). Etat tad andhakāraṃ yad bhāveṣu prakāśamānatayā (this is that darkness, which by shining in things) ātmānatirikteṣv api bhavaty anātmābhimāno 'yam (even in non-self things, this I-am-not-self delusion). Timirād api timiram idaṃ gaṇḍasyopari mahān ayaṃ sphoṭaḥ (darkness upon darkness, great burst upon the cheek). Yad anātmany api dehaprāṇadāv ātmamānitvam (that in non-self, body/breath etc., I-am-self-ness). Dehaprāṇavimarśanadhījñānanabhaḥprapañcayogena (by body-breath-reflection-knowledge-sky-expanse-yoga) ātmānam veṣṭayate citraṃ jālena jālakāra iv (self is wrapped in a net, like a net-maker by net).
- inference: Bondage = delusion (vibhrama) causing I-am-not-self in non-self things; self wrapped in net of body/breath/reflection.
- conclusion: Bondage doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## param-p05 (38–46)
- speaker: Guru (Abhinava).
- question: What is the contracted state (saṃkoca)?
- premises: Yathā himāṃśur amalas taimirikadṛṣṭyā (as the spotless moon by timira-afflicted sight) bhāti rāhuvaśād vā kaluṣita iva (shines as if defiled by Rāhu) tathā aśuddham iva bhāti paramaśivaḥ (so Paramaśiva shines as if impure) pramātṛtām upagataḥ (having attained knowerhood). Malina iva jāyate nirmalaḥ (pure becomes as if impure) māyāvaśāt kila saṃkucitavapuḥ (by Māyā's power, contracted body). Yadyapi sarvagataḥ tathāpi dehādhipatyena (though all-pervading, yet by body-lordship) bhoktṛtāṃ gataḥ (attains experiencerhood). Śaktipātena vinā na mokṣaḥ (without śaktipāta, no liberation). Prasādāt tu guror mokṣaḥ (by Guru's grace, liberation).
- inference: Contracted state = Paramaśiva shines as if impure by Māyā; śaktipāta (grace) required for liberation.
- conclusion: Saṃkoca doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## param-p06 (47–55)
- speaker: Guru (Abhinava).
- question: What are the categories of knowers (pramātṛ)?
- premises: Pramātā pañcadhā (knower is fivefold) śivaḥ mantramaheśvaraḥ (Śiva, Mantramaheśvara) mantreśvaraḥ (Mantreśvara) mantrī (Mantrī) vijñānakalaḥ (vijñānakala) pralayākalaḥ (pralayākala) sakalaḥ (sakala) iti (thus). Tatra śivaḥ (there Śiva) nirmalaḥ (pure) nirañjanaḥ (stainless) sarvajñaḥ (omniscient) sarvakartā (all-doer). Mantramaheśvaraḥ (Mantramaheśvara) tadvat (like that) kiṃtu māyāvaśāt (but by Māyā's power) saṃkucitatvam (contractedness). Mantreśvaraḥ (Mantreśvara) tadvat (like that) kiṃtu vidyāvaśāt (but by vidyā's power) saṃkucitatvam (contractedness). Mantrī (Mantrī) tadvat (like that) kiṃtu kalāvaśāt (but by kalā's power) saṃkucitatvam (contractedness). Vijñānakalaḥ (vijñānakala) māyāmalaṃ (Māyā-mala) tyaktvā (having abandoned) śuddhaḥ (pure) kiṃtu (but) kalāmalaṃ (kalā-mala) saṃkucitatvam (contractedness). Pralayākalaḥ (pralayākala) kalāmalaṃ (kalā-mala) tyaktvā (having abandoned) śuddhaḥ (pure) kiṃtu (but) māyāmalaṃ (Māyā-mala) saṃkucitatvam (contractedness). Sakalaḥ (sakala) ubhayamalaṃ (both malas) saṃkucitatvam (contractedness).
- inference: Five knowers: Śiva (pure), Mantramaheśvara, Mantreśvara, Mantrī (contracted by Māyā/vidyā/kalā), vijñānakala (pure after Māyā-mala), pralayākala (pure after kalā-mala), sakala (both malas).
- conclusion: Knower categories stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## param-p07 (56–64)
- speaker: Guru (Abhinava).
- question: What is liberation (mokṣa)?
- premises: Mokṣaḥ (liberation) śivatvaprāptiḥ (attainment of Śivahood) iti (thus). Tatra (there) śaktipātena (by śaktipāta) guror (of Guru) prasādāt (by grace) jñānaṃ (knowledge) utpadyate (arises). Jñānena (by knowledge) malaḥ (mala) kṣīyate (is destroyed). Malakṣayāt (by mala-destruction) śivatvaprāptiḥ (Śivahood attainment). Tatra (there) anupāyaḥ (anupāya) śāmbhavaḥ (Śāmbhava) śāktaḥ (Śākta) āṇavaḥ (Āṇava) iti (thus) trividhaḥ (threefold). Anupāyaḥ (anupāya) śaktipātamātreṇa (by śaktipāta alone) mokṣaḥ (liberation). Śāmbhavaḥ (Śāmbhava) icchāmātreṇa (by will alone) mokṣaḥ (liberation). Śāktaḥ (Śākta) jñānamātreṇa (by knowledge alone) mokṣaḥ (liberation). Āṇavaḥ (Āṇava) kriyāmātreṇa (by action alone) mokṣaḥ (liberation).
- inference: Liberation = attainment of Śivahood; by Guru's grace, knowledge arises, mala destroyed; three upāyas: anupāya (śaktipāta alone), śāmbhava (will), śākta (knowledge), āṇava (action).
- conclusion: Liberation doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## param-p08 (65–73)
- speaker: Guru (Abhinava).
- question: What is the world (viśva)?
- premises: Viśvaṃ (world) śivasya (of Śiva) svabhāvaḥ (own-nature) iti (thus). Tatra (there) sthūlaṃ (gross) sūkṣmaṃ (subtle) paraṃ (supreme) iti (thus) trividham (threefold). Sthūlaṃ (gross) bhūtapañcakam (five elements). Sūkṣmaṃ (subtle) tanmātrapañcakam (five tanmātras). Paraṃ (supreme) tattvapañcakam (five tattvas). Tatra (there) bhūtapañcakam (five elements) pṛthvī (earth) ap (water) tejas (fire) vāyu (air) ākāśa (space). Tanmātrapañcakam (five tanmātras) gandha (smell) rasa (taste) rūpa (form) sparśa (touch) śabda (sound). Tattvapañcakam (five tattvas) śiva (Śiva) śakti (Śakti) sadāśiva (Sadāśiva) īśvara (Īśvara) vidyā (Vidyā).
- inference: World = Śiva's own-nature; threefold: gross (5 elements), subtle (5 tanmātras), supreme (5 tattvas).
- conclusion: World doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## param-p10 (83–91)
- speaker: Guru (Abhinava).
- question: What is the mind (manas)?
- premises: Manas (mind) śivasya (of Śiva) vṛttiḥ (function) iti (thus). Tatra (there) antaḥkaraṇaṃ (inner instrument) manas (mind) buddhi (intellect) ahaṃkāra (ego) citta (citta) iti (thus) catuṣṭayam (fourfold). Manas (mind) saṃkalpavikalpātmakam (nature of resolve/doubt). Buddhi (intellect) niścayātmakam (nature of determination). Ahaṃkāra (ego) abhimānātmakam (nature of I-ness). Citta (citta) smṛtyātmakam (nature of memory). Tatra (there) manas (mind) indriyāṇāṃ (of senses) adhiṣṭhānam (support). Buddhi (intellect) niścayakāraṇam (cause of determination). Ahaṃkāra (ego) kartṛtākāraṇam (cause of agency). Citta (citta) smṛtikāraṇam (cause of memory).
- inference: Mind = Śiva's function; fourfold inner instrument (manas/buddhi/ahaṃkāra/citta) with distinct functions.
- conclusion: Mind doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## param-p11 (92–100)
- speaker: Guru (Abhinava).
- question: What is the soul (ātman)?

Jīva (jīva) saṃsārī (worldly) iti (thus). Ātman (ātman) muktas (liberated) iti (thus). Paramātman (paramātman) śivaḥ (Śiva) iti (thus). Tatra (there) jīvaḥ (jīva) māyāvaśāt (by Māyā's power) saṃsārī (worldly). Ātman (ātman) śaktipātena (by śaktipāta) muktas (liberated). Paramātman (paramātman) nityamuktas (eternally liberated) śivaḥ (Śiva).

- speaker: Guru (Abhinava) (close).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: Iti (thus) paramārthasāraḥ (Paramārthasāra) samāptaḥ (completed). Śivaśāsanadṛṣṭiyogena (by Śiva-śāsana view) kathitaḥ (spoken) abhinavaguptena (by Abhinava). Tatra (there) śivatvaprāptiḥ (Śivahood attainment) mokṣaḥ (liberation) iti (thus). Śaktipātena (by śaktipāta) guror (of Guru) prasādāt (by grace) jñānam (knowledge) utpadyate (arises). Śivatvaprāptau (in Śivahood attainment) sarvam (all) śivam (Śiva) eva (indeed) bhavati (becomes).
- inference: Close = Paramārthasāra completed; Śivahood attainment = liberation; by Guru's grace, knowledge arises, mala destroyed; all becomes Śiva.
- conclusion: The text closes with all-is-Śiva.

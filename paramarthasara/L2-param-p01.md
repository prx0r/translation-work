# Paramarthasara param-p01 (frame asked and answered) — L2 READ

## v.1
I resort to You, Śambhu — supreme, beyond-abiding, beginningless, one, cave-entered manifoldly, abode of all, abiding in all moving and unmoving: to refuge.
The Sanskrit: *paraṃ parasthaṃ gahanād anādim | sarvālayaṃ sarvacarācarasthaṃ tvām eva śambhuṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadye*

## v.2
Whirled in the sorrow-wheel from womb-dwelling to death-ending, the student asked the blessed Lord, the support: the supreme truth.
The Sanskrit: *garbhādhivāsapūrvakamaraṇāntakaduḥkhacakravibhrāntaḥ | ādhāram bhagavantaṃ śiṣyaḥ papraccha paramārtham*

## v.3
By support-verses the teacher addressed its essence to him; Abhinavagupta tells it by Śiva-teaching-view-yoga.
The Sanskrit: *ādhārakārikābhiḥ taṃ gurur abhibhāṣati sma tatsāram | kathayaty abhinavaguptaḥ śivaśāsanadṛṣṭiyogena*

## v.4
By own-power-glory-weight, this fourfold egg in division: power, Māyā, nature, earth — thus manifested by the Lord.
The Sanskrit: *nijaśaktivaibhavabharād a.ṅdacatuṣṭayam idaṃ vibhāgena | śaktir māyā prakṛtiḥ pṛthvī ceti prabhāvitaṃ prabhuṇā*

## v.5
Therein within, this universe is a varied body-faculty-world continuum; and therein the enjoyer, embodied, is Śiva indeed, fettered-state-taken.
The Sanskrit: *tatrāntar viśvam idaṃ vicitratanukaraṇabhuvanasaṃtānam | bhoktā ca tatra dehī śiva eva gṛhītapaśubhāvaḥ*

## v.6
As the stainless crystal assumes the form of various sorts, so the Lord too holds god-man-beast-plant form-ness.
The Sanskrit: *nānāvidhavarṇānāṃ rūpaṃ dhatte yathāmalaḥ sphaṭikaḥ | suramānuṣapaśupādaparūpatvaṃ tadvad īśo 'pi*

## v.7
As water moves and the moon-disc moves, as water stills so stillness goes: so this self, the great Lord, in the body-faculty-world mass.
The Sanskrit: *gacchati gacchati jala iva himakarabimbaṃ sthite sthitiṃ yāti | tanukaraṇabhuvanavarge tathāyam ātmā maheśānaḥ*

## v.8
As invisible Rahu, abiding on the moon-disc, shines, so the all-going self, by resorting to objects, shines in the mind-mirror.
The Sanskrit: *rāhur adṛśyo 'pi yathā śaśibimbasthaḥ prakāśate tadvat | sarvagato 'py ayam ātmā viṣayāśrayaṇena dhīmukure*

## v.9
As a face shines in a dirt-free mirror, so this self shines, light-formed, in the mind-principle purified by Śiva's grace-descent.
The Sanskrit: *ādarśe malarahite yadvad vadanaṃ vibhāti tadvad ayam | śivaśaktipātavimale dhītattve bhāti bhārūpaḥ*

## v.10
Light-formed, complete, great-bliss from resting in own self, filled by will, knowing and acting, endless-power-complete.
The Sanskrit: *bhārūpaṃ paripūrṇaṃ svātmani viśrāntito mahānandam | icchasaṃvitkaraṇair nirbharitam anantaśaktiparipūrṇam*

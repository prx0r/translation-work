# Paramarthasara param-p03 — L2 READ

## v.20
Ear, skin, eye, tongue, nose — organs of knowing in sound and the rest; voice, hand, foot, anus, privates — organs of doing again.
The Sanskrit: *śrotraṃ tvagakṣirasanā ghrāṇaṃ buddhīndriyāṇi śabdādau | vākpāṇipādapāyūpasthaṃ karmendriyāṇi punaḥ*

## v.21
Their object, to be grasped, is subtle and without divisions: the five tanmātras — sound, touch, form, taste, smell.
The Sanskrit: *eṣāṃ grāhyo viṣayaḥ sūkṣmaḥ pravibhāgavarjito yaḥ syāt | tanmātrapañcakaṃ tat śabdaḥ sparśo maho raso gandhaḥ*

## v.22
From their mixture the gross object takes on five-element-ness: sky, wind, fire, water, and earth.
The Sanskrit: *etatsaṃsargavaśāt sthūlo viṣayas tu bhūtapañcakatām | abhyeti nabhaḥ pavanas tejaḥ salilaṃ ca pṛthvī ca*

## v.23
As husk covers the rice-grain, creation beginning with nature covers; down to earth, consciousness wears the body.
The Sanskrit: *tuṣa iva ta.ṅdulakaṇikām āvṛṇute prakṛtipūrvakaḥ sargaḥ | pṛthvīparyanto 'yaṃ caitanyaṃ dehabhāvena*

## v.24
The supreme covering! Impurity here: subtle — the Māyā-sheath; gross — the outer body-form; wrapped in three sheaths stands the self.
The Sanskrit: *param āvaraṇaṃ mala iha sūkṣmaṃ māyādikañcukaṃ sthūlam | bāhyaṃ vigraharūpaṃ kośatrayaveṣṭito hy ātmā*

## v.25
Joined to darkness-ignorance, the one self, its own nature, perceives itself manifold through grasper-grasped variety.
The Sanskrit: *ajñānatimirayogād ekam api svaṃ svabhāvam ātmānam | grāhyagrāhakanānāvaicitryeṇāvabudhyeta*

## v.26
As cane-juice in molasses, sugar, lumps: so every divided state of the supreme self, Śambhu.
The Sanskrit: *rasaphāṇitaśarkarikāguḍakha.ṅdādyā yathekṣurasa eva | tadvad avasthā bhedāḥ sarve paramātmanaḥ śambhoḥ*

## v.27
Knower, inner ruler, breath, the all, the born — mere convention, in truth not at all.
The Sanskrit: *vijñānāntaryāmiprāṇavirāḍdehajātipi.ṅdāntāḥ | vyavahāramātram etat paramārthena tu na santy eva*

## v.28
No snake in the rope, yet fear kills till death: delusion's great power no one can tell apart.
The Sanskrit: *rajjvāṃ nāsti bhujaṅgas trāsaṃ kurute ca mṛtyuparyantam | bhrānter mahatī śaktir na vivektuṃ śakyate nāma*

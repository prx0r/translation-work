# Paramarthasara param-p05 — L2 READ

## v.38
In calm, calm; in glad, glad — so the fool is deluded; amid principles, the Lord is truly not so.
The Sanskrit: *śānte śānta ivāyaṃ hṛṣṭe hṛṣṭo vimohavati mūḍhaḥ | tattvagaṇe sati bhagavān na punaḥ paramārthataḥ sa tathā*

## v.39
First removing the shining of that-form in non-self, the supreme self shatters delusion of the non-self-form in self.
The Sanskrit: *yad anātmany api tadrūpāvabhāsanaṃ tat purā nirākṛtya | ātmany anātmarūpāṃ bhrāntiṃ vidalayati paramātmā*

## v.40
Thus, for one whose goal is gained in cutting delusion-pair at the root, no weighing of other duties ever, for the supreme yogin.
The Sanskrit: *itthaṃ vibhramayugalakasamūlavicchedane kṛtārthasya | kartavyāntarakalanā na jātu parayogino bhavati*

## v.41
Earth, nature, Māyā — this triad, fallen into known-form — by nondual-contemplation power becomes mere-being remainder.
The Sanskrit: *pṛthivī prakṛtir māyā tritayam idaṃ vedyarūpatāpatitam | advaitabhāvanabalād bhavati hi sanmātrapariśeṣam*

## v.42
As girdle, earring, bracelet are seen as gold by leaving difference, so in leaving difference all shines as mere being.
The Sanskrit: *raśanāku.ṅdalakaṭakaṃ bhedatyāgena dṛśyate yathā hema | tadvad bhedatyāge sanmātraṃ sarvam ābhāti*

## v.43
That brahman — supreme, pure, peaceful, nondual-natured, even, whole, deathless, true — rests in shining-formed power.
The Sanskrit: *tad brahma paraṃ śuddhaṃ śāntaṃ abhedātmakaṃ samaṃ sakalam | amṛtaṃ satyaṃ śaktau viśrāmyati bhāsvarūpāyām*

## v.44
Desired, known, done — by the shining-formed: untouched, yet it takes sky-flower-ness.
The Sanskrit: *iṣyata iti vedyata iti saṃpādyata iti ca bhāsvarūpeṇa | aparāmṛṣṭaṃ yad api tu nabhaḥprasūnatvam abhyeti*

## v.45
By grasp-yoga of power's trident, the whole, in the supreme Lord, in Śiva-name, in supreme truth, is emitted by god of gods.
The Sanskrit: *śaktitriśūlaparigamayogena samastam api parameśe | śivanāmani paramārthe visṛjyate devadevena*

## v.46
Again too, by five-power-spreading order, outside, that varied egg-three created by outer-self-gaining.
The Sanskrit: *punar api ca pañcaśaktiprasaraṇakrameṇa bahir api tat | a.ṅdatrayaṃ vicitraṃ sṛṣṭaṃ bahirātmalābhena*

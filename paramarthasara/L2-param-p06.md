# Paramarthasara param-p06 — L2 READ

## v.47
Thus driving, by play-discipline, the power-wheel-machine — god: I indeed, pure-formed, standing in the power-great-wheel-lord-seat.
The Sanskrit: *iti śakticakrayantraṃ krīḍāyogena vāhayan devaḥ | aham eva śuddharūpaḥ śaktimahācakranāyakapadasthaḥ*

## v.48
In me indeed shines the universe, as jars and the like in a spotless mirror; from me spreads all, as dream-variety from a sleeper.
The Sanskrit: *mayy eva bhāti viśvaṃ darpaṇa iva nirmale ghatādīni | mattaḥ prasarati sarvaṃ svapnavicitratvam iva suptāt*

## v.49
I indeed, universe-formed, hand-foot-and-all-natured as the body; in all I indeed shine, light-natured.
The Sanskrit: *aham eva viśvarūpaḥ karacaranādisvabhāva iva dehaḥ | sarvasmin aham eva sphurāmi bhāveṣu bhāsvarūpam iva*

## v.50
Seer, hearer, smeller — bereft of body-senses though, a non-doer too: varied systems, texts, reasonings I indeed compose.
The Sanskrit: *draṣṭā śrotā ghrātā dehendriyavarjito 'py akartāpi | siddhāntāgamatarkāṃś citrān aham eva racayāmi*

## v.51
Thus, duality-thought dissolved, crossing enchanting Māyā: as water in water, milk in milk — dissolved in brahman may one be.
The Sanskrit: *itthaṃ dvaitavikalpe galite pravilaṅghya mohinīṃ māyām | salile salilaṃ kṣīre kṣīram iva brahmaṇi layī syāt*

## v.52
Thus, in the principle-host, by contemplation attaining Śiva-madeness: what sorrow, what delusion, for the brahman-beholder?
The Sanskrit: *itthaṃ tattvasamūhe bhāvanayā śivamayatvam abhiyāte | kaḥ śokaḥ ko mohaḥ sarvaṃ brahmāvalokayataḥ*

## v.53
Deed-fruit, good or bad, comes only from contact with false knowing; hard indeed is contact's fault — like a thief's company, though one is no thief.
The Sanskrit: *karmaphalaṃ śubham aśubhaṃ mithyājñānena saṃgamād eva | viṣamo hi saṅgadoṣas taskarayogo 'py ataskarasyeva*

## v.54
World-convention-makers who tend ignorance here, fools, go to birth and death, bound by duty's locks.
The Sanskrit: *lokavyavahārakṛtāṃ ya ihāvidyām upāsate mūḍhāḥ | te yānti janmamṛtyū dharmādharmārgalābaddhāḥ*

## v.55
Yet even duty-formed deeds, heaped in ignorance-time, long-heaped like cotton, perish by knowing-flame-power.
The Sanskrit: *ajñānakālanicitaṃ dharmādharmātmakaṃ tu karmāpi | cirasaṃcitam iva tūlaṃ naśyati vijñānadīptivaśāt*

# Paramarthasara param-p07 — L2 READ

## v.56
Done, yet not for fruit — how then birth? yoked to birth-bond gone, the Śiva-sun shines by its own rays.
The Sanskrit: *jñānaprāptau kṛtam api na phalāya tato 'sya janma katham | gatajanmabandhayogo bhāti śivārkaḥ svadīdhitibhiḥ*

## v.57
As husk-cover-bran-freed seed makes no sprout, so the atom-Māyā-deed-freed self makes no birth-sprout.
The Sanskrit: *tuṣakambukakiṃśārukamuktaṃ bījaṃ yathāṅkuraṃ kurute | naiva tathāṇavamāyākarmavimukto bhavāṅkuraṃ hy ātmā*

## v.58
The self-knower fears nowhere — all is his own form — and never grieves, since in truth there is no perishing.
The Sanskrit: *ātmajño na kutaścana bibheti sarvaṃ hi tasya nijarūpam | naiva ca śocati yasmāt paramārthe nāśitā nāsti*

## v.59
From the supersecret heart-treasury's sprouted truth-jewel heap: 'I indeed' — in the great-Lord state, what misfortune, whose?
The Sanskrit: *atigūḍhahṛdayagañjaprarūḍhaparamārtharatnasaṃcayataḥ | aham eveti maheśvarabhāve kā durgatiḥ kasya*

## v.60
Liberation has no abode at all, nor any going elsewhere: by cutting ignorance's knot, own-power-manifestation is liberation.
The Sanskrit: *mokṣasya naiva kiṃcid dhāmāsti na cāpi gamanam anyatra | ajñānagranthibhidā svaśaktyabhivyaktatā mokṣaḥ*

## v.61
Cut-ignorance-knotted, doubt-gone, delusion-cast-off, merit-demerit-wasted — in body-yoked though, he is freed.
The Sanskrit: *bhinnājñānagranthir gatasaṃdehaḥ parākṛtabhrāntiḥ | prakṣīṇapuṇyapāpo vigrahayoge 'py asau muktaḥ*

## v.62
As fire-inflamed seed loses sprout-power, so knowing-fire-burnt deed becomes birth-giving no more.
The Sanskrit: *agnyabhidagdhaṃ bījaṃ yathā prarohāsamarthatām eti | jñānāgnidagdham evaṃ karma na janmapradaṃ bhavati*

## v.63
By limited-mind-ness, contemplating a body fit for its deeds, contracted knowing shines when this body falls.
The Sanskrit: *parimitabuddhitvena hi karmocitabhāvidehabhāvanayā | saṅkucitā citir etaddehadhvaṃse tathā bhāti*

## v.64
If again stainless knowing — all-transcended-knower-doer-made, spread, light-formed never-setting-rising, true-resolved...
The Sanskrit: *yadi punar amalaṃ bodhaṃ sarvasamuttīrnaboddhṛkartṛmayam | vitatam anastamitoditabhārūpam satyasaṃkalpam*

# Paramarthasara param-p08 — L2 READ

## v.65
Beyond direction-time-touch, firm, changeless, the full Lord — sole maker of hosts of powers dissolving and rising.
The Sanskrit: *dikkālakalanavikalaṃ dhruvam avyayam īśvaraṃ suparipūrṇam | bahutaraśaktivrātapralayodayaviracanaikakartāram*

## v.66
Awakening to the self as Śiva-made, maker of creation's rules — how could he wander? Spread out, whence, where, what refuge?
The Sanskrit: *sṛṣṭyādividhisuvedhasam ātmānaṃ śivamayaṃ vibudhyeta | katham iva saṃsārī syād vitatasya kutaḥ kva vā saraṇam*

## v.67
Thus proved by reasons: the knower's deed is fruitless. 'Not mine, this — but his': firmly, no fruit in the world.
The Sanskrit: *iti yuktibhir api siddhaṃ yatkarma jñānino na saphalaṃ tat | na mamedam api tu tasyeti dārḍhyato na hi phalaṃ loke*

## v.68
Thus awakened by contemplation-wind, sacrificing all thoughts in the blazing self-flame, he becomes flame.
The Sanskrit: *itthaṃ sakalavikalpān pratibuddho bhāvanāsamīraṇataḥ | ātmajyotiṣi dīpte juhvaj jyotirmayo bhavati*

## v.69
Eating this or that, covered by anything, calmed, dwelling anywhere — the all-beings-self is freed.
The Sanskrit: *aśnan yad vā tad vā saṃvīto yena kenacic chāntaḥ | yatra kvacana nivāsī vimucyate sarvabhūtātmā*

## v.70
Though doing horse-rites by hundred-thousands, brahmin-killings by hundred-thousands — the truth-knower, by merits not, by faults not touched, stainless.
The Sanskrit: *hayamedhaśatasahasrāṇy api kurute brahmaghātalakṣāṇi | paramārthavin na puṇyair na ca pāpaiḥ spṛśyate vimalaḥ*

## v.71
Leaving pride, joy, anger, lust, sorrow, fear, greed, delusion; no praise, no formula; dull as if — let him move, opinion-free.
The Sanskrit: *madaharṣakopamanmathaviṣādabhayalobhamohaparivarjī | niḥstotravaṣaṭkāro jaḍa iva vicared avādamatiḥ*

## v.72
The pride-joy host arises from difference-delusion: how could the nondual-self-awakened be touched by it?
The Sanskrit: *madaharṣaprabhṛtir ayaṃ vargaḥ prabhavati vibhedasaṃmohāt | advaitātmavibodhas tena kathaṃ spṛśyatāṃ nāma*

## v.73
Nothing to praise or offer, separate from him, at all: pleased by praise, freed — then no bow, no formula.
The Sanskrit: *stutyaṃ vā hotavyaṃ nāsti vyatiriktam asya kiṃcana ca | stotrādinā sa tuṣyen muktas tan nirnamaskṛtivaṣaṭkaḥ*

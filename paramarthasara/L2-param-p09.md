# Paramarthasara param-p09 — L2 READ

## v.74
Bearing thirty-six principles, full-eyed in body-making: own or other's body, jar and the like — his god-house.
The Sanskrit: *ṣaṭtriṃśattattvabhṛtaṃ vigraharacanāgavākṣaparipūrṇam | nijam anyad atha śarīraṃ ghaṭādi vā tasya devagṛham*

## v.75
There the supreme-self-great-Bhairava-Śiva-goddess, yoked to own power, sits worshipped with self-grasped pure gifts.
The Sanskrit: *tatra ca paramātmamahābhairavaśivadevatāṃ svaśaktiyutām | ātmāmarśanavimaladravyaiḥ paripūjayann āste*

## v.76
Offering the great seed-heap of outer-inner-fancied difference in his blazing knowing-fire — effortless the offering.
The Sanskrit: *bahirantaraparikalpanabhedamahābījanicayam arpayataḥ | tasyātidīptasaṃvijjvalane yatnād vinā bhavati homaḥ*

## v.77
Meditation never setting: the blessed Lord creates varied forms — that itself is meditation, true-formed as fancy draws.
The Sanskrit: *dhyānam anastamitaṃ punar eṣa hi bhagavān vicitrarūpāṇi | sṛjati tad eva dhyānaṃ saṃkalpālikhitasatyarūpatvam*

## v.78
Turning world-rows, principle-order-fancies, sense-hosts in inner knowing — that, they say, is his muttering.
The Sanskrit: *bhuvanāvalīṃ samastāṃ tattvakramakalpanām athākṣagaṇam | antarbodhe parivartayati ca yat so 'sya japa uditaḥ*

## v.79
Seeing all with an even glance, deeming knowing content, delighting in the world-burning-ground, grasping the body as skull-staff.
The Sanskrit: *sarvaṃ samayā dṛṣṭyā yat paśyati yac ca saṃvidaṃ manute | viśvaśmaśānaniratāṃ vigrahakhaṭvāṅgakalpanākalitām*

## v.80
Tasting the world-essence-drink-filled, own-hand-held, known-fragment bowl — this vow, hard to get and easy to get.
The Sanskrit: *viśvarasāsavapūrṇaṃ nijakaragaṃ vedyakha.ṅdakakapālam | rasayati ca yat tad etad vratam asya sudurlabhaṃ ca sulabhaṃ ca*

## v.81
Thus birth-death-free, named truth-great-Lord, having got — by the finder's shining, done-done, standing as wished.
The Sanskrit: *iti janmanāśahīnaṃ paramārthamaheśvarākhyam upalabhya | upalabdhṛtāprakāśāt kṛtakṛtyas tiṣṭhati yatheṣṭam*

## v.82
Pervading, so told, all-self, diversity-shaken, peerless bliss supreme: who knows becomes made of that.
The Sanskrit: *vyāpinam abhihitam itthaṃ sarvātmānaṃ vidhūtanānātvam | nirupamaparamānandaṃ yo vetti sa tanmayo bhavati*

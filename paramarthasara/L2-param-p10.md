# Paramarthasara param-p10 — L2 READ

## v.83
In a shrine or an outcaste hut, memory lost though, abandoning the body — freed simultaneous with knowing, going to isolation, sorrow killed.
The Sanskrit: *tīrthe śvapacagṛhe vā naṣṭasmṛtir api parityajan deham | jñānasamakālamuktaḥ kaivalyaṃ yāti hataśokaḥ*

## v.84
Shrine-service for merit, outcaste-death for hell — but with no touch of merit-demerit stain, what use?
The Sanskrit: *puṇyāya tīrthasevā nirayāya śvapacasadananidhanagatiḥ | puṇyāpuṇyakalaṅkasparśābhāve tu kiṃ tena*

## v.85
Throwing the well-separated rice-grain between husk-pieces makes not again that-form identity.
The Sanskrit: *tuṣakambukasupṛthakkṛtata.ṅdulakaṇatuṣadalāntarakṣepaḥ | ta.ṅdulakaṇasya kurute na punas tadrūpatādātmyam*

## v.86
So the sheath-curtain-separated knowing, here by impression, standing though — freed self, untouched by that touch, becomes.
The Sanskrit: *tadvat kañcukapaṭalīpṛthakkṛtā saṃvid atra saṃskārāt | tiṣṭhanty api muktātmā tatsparśavivarjitā bhavati*

## v.87
As flawless-made by the best artisan, the casket-bound gem, though stained, on cutting the bound shows clear truth.
The Sanskrit: *kuśalatamaśilpikalpitavimalībhāvaḥ samudgakopādheḥ | malino 'pi maṇir upādher vicchede svacchaparamārthaḥ*

## v.88
So the true-teacher-taught pure state, feeling the body-bound, freed though void of other bounds, shines as Śiva-formed.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ sadguruśāsanavimalasthiti vedanaṃ tanūpādheḥ | muktam apy upādhyantaraśūnyam ivābhāti śivarūpam*

## v.89
By scripture's weight, by unshaken faith become that — got before heaven, hell, man-ness.
The Sanskrit: *śāstrādiprāmāṇyād avicalitaśraddhayāpi tanmayatām | prāptaḥ sa eva pūrvaṃ svargaṃ narakaṃ manuṣyatvam*

## v.90
The last moment, nourishing a merit or demerit state, plays helper — but in going on, no cause.
The Sanskrit: *antyaḥ kṣaṇas tu tasmin puṇyāṃ pāpāṃ ca vā sthitiṃ puṣyan | mūḍhānāṃ sahakārībhāvaṃ gacchati gatau tu na sa hetuḥ*

## v.91
Whoever knows that-self-ness — beast, bird, creeper and the rest — goes his own way, by old awakening impressed.
The Sanskrit: *ye 'pi tadātmatvena viduḥ paśupakṣisarīsṛpādayaḥ svagatim | te 'pi purātanasaṃbodhasaṃskṛtās tāṃ gatiṃ yānti*

# Paramarthasara param-p11 — L2 READ

## v.92
Heaven-made, hell-made — this person between bodies; when that breaks, by fitness joining another body.
The Sanskrit: *svargamayo nirayamayas tad ayaṃ dehāntarālagaḥ puruṣaḥ | tadbhaṅge svaucityād dehāntarayogam abhyeti*

## v.93
As in knowing-time the self once shone for him, so then — on body-falling, otherwise it does not become.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ jñānāvasare svātmā sakṛd asya yādṛg avabhātaḥ | tādṛśa eva tadāsau na dehapāte 'nyathā bhavati*

## v.94
Senses stolen, memory lost, breath choked, cut off, vitals pained — enjoyment born of body-impressions.
The Sanskrit: *karaṇagaṇasaṃpramoṣaḥ smṛtināśaḥ śvāsakalilatā cchedaḥ | marmasu rujāviśeṣāḥ śarīrasaṃskārajo bhogaḥ*

## v.95
How could he, being embodied, not be so? By that, deluded though — at death-time the knower falls not from self-truth.
The Sanskrit: *sa kathaṃ vigrahayoge sati na bhavet tena mohayoge 'pi | maraṇāvasare jñāni na cyavate svātmaparamārthāt*

## v.96
This truth-path, quickly, when gained from the teacher's mouth — by keenest grace-fall, then indeed, unblocked, Śiva.
The Sanskrit: *paramārthamārgam enaṃ jhaṭiti yadā gurumukhāt samabhyeti | atitīvraśaktipātāt tadaiva nirvighnam eva śivaḥ*

## v.97
All-crossed form, by steps resorted, on gaining root in supreme truth — at the end, Śiva-madeness.
The Sanskrit: *sarvottīrṇaṃ rūpaṃ sopānapadakrameṇa saṃśrayataḥ | paratattvarūḍhilābhe paryante śivamayībhāvaḥ*

## v.98
But his truth-made stream, unreached, middle-rested — though mind yearns for that step, death may sometime come.
The Sanskrit: *tasya tu paramārthamayīṃ dhārām agatasya madhyaviśrānteḥ | tatpadalābhotsukacetaso 'pi maraṇaṃ kadācit syāt*

## v.99
Fallen from yoga, the books call him wonder-enjoyment-world-lord; rested by stage-power, become, in next birth Śiva-becoming.
The Sanskrit: *yogabhraṣṭaḥ śāstre kathito 'sau citrabhogabhuvanapatiḥ | viśrāntisthānavaśād bhūtvā janmāntare śivībhavati*

## v.100
Practising this truth-path, missing yoga though, by name — god-world-pleasure-sharer, glad-minded, rejoices long.
The Sanskrit: *paramārthamārgam enaṃ hy abhyasyāprāpya yogam api nāma | suralokabhogabhāgī muditamanā modate suciram*

# Paramarthasara param-p12 — L2 READ

## v.101
As an emperor among things is honored by all people as king, so among worlds the yoga-fallen is honored by all gods.
The Sanskrit: *viṣayeṣu sārvabhaumaḥ sarvajanaiḥ pūjyate yathā rājā | bhuvaneṣu sarvadaivair yogabhraṣṭas tathā pūjyaḥ*

## v.102
After long time, humanity got, yoga practised — getting the divine deathless whence turning back no more.
The Sanskrit: *mahatā kālena punar mānuṣyaṃ prāpya yogam abhyasya | prāpnoti divyam amṛtam yasmād āvartate na punaḥ*

## v.103
Therefore whoever, devoted on the true path here, goes — he to Śiva-ness: thinking so, in truth yet strive.
The Sanskrit: *tasmāt sanmārge 'smin nirato yaḥ kaścid eti sa śivatvam | iti matvā paramārthe yathā tathāpi prayatanīyam*

## v.104
Meditating this supreme brahman, briefly told by Abhinavagupta — soon indeed Śiva-ness enters own-heart-dwelling.
The Sanskrit: *idam abhinavaguptoditasaṃkṣepaṃ dhyāyataḥ paraṃ brahma | acirād eva śivatvaṃ nijahṛdayāveśam abhyeti*

## v.105
In a hundred āryās this condensed recondite teaching-essence — by Abhinavagupta, by me, kindled by Śiva-feet-memory.
The Sanskrit: *āryāśatena tad idaṃ saṃkṣiptaṃ śāstrasāram atigūḍham | abhinavaguptena mayā śivacaraṇasmaraṇadīptena*

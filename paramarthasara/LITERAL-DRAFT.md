## Song 1 (v1–v10) — refuge, question, fourfold egg, forms, mirror-mind

v1:
I take refuge in You, Śambhu.
You are supreme.
You abide beyond.
From the depth.
You have no beginning.
You are one.
You entered the heart-caves in many forms.
You house all things.
You abide in all that moves and all that stands still.

v2:
The student was whirled in the wheel of sorrow.
The wheel runs from dwelling in the womb to ending in death.
He asked the blessed Lord, the support, for the highest truth.

v3:
The teacher answered him in support-verses.
He told him their essence.
Abhinavagupta retells it in the yoga of the Śaiva view.

v4:
By the weight of His own power and glory, the Lord manifested this fourfold egg.
The four are power, Māyā, nature, and earth.

v5:
Within it, the universe runs as body, faculties, and worlds.
Within it, the enjoyer wears a body.
He is Śiva, taken in the fettered state.

v6:
The Lord assumes god, man, beast, and plant forms, yet stays stainless.

v7:
So this self, the great Lord, moves and rests with the mass of body, faculties, and worlds.

v8:
Though unseen and all-going, this self shines in the mind where it meets objects.

v9:
This self, formed of light, shines in the mind made pure by Śiva's descending grace.

v10:
It is light-formed and complete.
From resting in its own self — great bliss.
It is full of will, knowing, and acting.
Its power has no end.

## Song 2 (v11–v19) — thought-free shining, five powers, veil, fetters

v11:
Free of every thought.
Pure.
At peace.
Beyond rising and dissolving.
In that supreme principle the world shines.
The world is thirty-six in nature.

v12:
The varied world shines within, distinct yet undivided.
Each from each, and from that principle too.

v13:
From the purest knowing of supreme Bhairava, the world shines divided.
Though void of division — each from each, and from that knowing too.

v14:
Śiva. Power. Sadāśiva.
Then the Īśvara-and-Vidyā-formed state.
He lights the five powers, each in its divided nature.

v15:
Supreme is the freedom that effects the hard thing.
It is the great Lord's own.
Māyā-power, the goddess, veils Śiva's self.
That is it.

v16:
Grasped by Māyā, knowing turns stained.
The person becomes a bound beast.
Time, art, limit, passion, and ignorance fetter him.

v17:
Here and now I know but little — with my whole self.
With Māyā, six sheaths are the atom's inner limbs.
So it is said.

v18:
Separate from Śiva, yet not divided from Him.
He attains purity by turning to Śiva's path with longing.

v19:
Mere pleasure, pain, and delusion come from resolve, will, and I-sense.
Then nature.
Then the inner organ: intellect, mind, and ego, in turn.

## Song 3 (v20–v28) — organs, elements, coverings, delusion's power

v20:
Ear, skin, eye, tongue, nose — organs of knowing, for sound and the rest.
Voice, hand, foot, anus, privates — organs of doing.

v21:
Their object is subtle, graspable, without divisions.
The five tanmātras: sound, touch, form, taste, smell.

v22:
From their mixture, the gross object takes on five elements.
Sky, wind, fire, water, earth.

v23:
Creation, beginning with nature, covers.
Down to earth, consciousness wears the body.

v24:
The supreme covering.
The impurity here is subtle: the sheath of Māyā.
It is gross: the outer body-form.
Wrapped in three sheaths stands the self.

v25:
Though one in its own nature, the self perceives itself as manifold.
Through grasped-and-grasper variety.

v26:
Every divided state is the supreme self, Śambhu.

v27:
Knower. Inner ruler. Breath. The All. Body, birth, individuals.
Mere convention — in truth, not at all.

v28:
What is not there still terrifies, right up to death.
No one can measure delusion's great power.

## Song 4 (v29–v37) — unreal orders, net, fourth, unstained

v29:
Dharma and adharma. Heaven and hell. Birth and death. Pleasure and pain.
Caste, life-stage, and the rest.
In the self they are unreal — yet they arise by delusion's force.

v30:
This is that darkness.
It shines in things.
The conceit "I am not the self" arises — even where the self is unsurpassed.

v31:
Darkness upon darkness: taking body, breath, and the rest — non-selves — for self.

v32:
By joining body, breath, reflection, mind, knowing, and open sky,
the self binds itself. A wonder.

v33:
By joining one's own shining glory of knowing, one unwraps one's own self.
So supreme Śiva spreads the wondrous play of bondage and release.

v34:
Creating, keeping, dissolving. Waking, dreaming, deep sleep.
They shine in him, in the fourth abode. Yet unveiled, he shines.

v35:
Waking is the universe, through difference.
Dreaming is splendor, through light's glory.
The knower is the sleep state, through massed knowing.
Beyond that: the fourth.

v36:
Māyā's deformings leave the supreme person untouched.

v37:
Stain does not pass from one soul to another.
Each enjoys its own pleasure and pain.

## Song 5 (v38–v46) — seeming, shattering, being, sky-flower, emitting

v38:
In calm, the fool sees calm. In glad, glad. So he is deluded.
Among the principles, the Lord is truly not so.

v39:
First, remove the shining of self-form from the non-self.
Then the supreme self shatters the delusion of non-self-form within the self.

v40:
For one whose goal is gained — the delusion-pair cut at the root —
there is never any weighing of other duties. So it is for the supreme yogin.

v41:
Earth, nature, Māyā — this triad fell into known form.
By nondual contemplation, all becomes mere-being remainder.

v42:
Setting differences aside, all shines as mere being.

v43:
That brahman rests in shining-formed power.
Supreme. Pure. Peaceful. Nondual-natured. Even. Whole. Deathless. True.

v44:
Desired, known, done — by the shining-formed.
Untouched. Yet it takes on what cannot be.

v45:
By grasp-yoga of power's trident, the god of gods emits the whole.
In the supreme Lord. In the Śiva-name. In the supreme truth.

v46:
Again, by the order of the five powers' spreading,
the outer self gained itself outside — and created that varied three of eggs.

## Song 6 (v47–v55) — wheel-driving I, spreading, cotton-deeds

v47:
So I drive the power-wheel, the machine, by play-discipline.
I indeed — god, pure-formed — standing in the seat of the lord of the great power-wheel.

v48:
In me indeed the universe shines.
From me all spreads.

v49:
I indeed, universe-formed — hand, foot, and all natures as my body.
In all I shine, light-natured.

v50:
Seer. Hearer. Smeller.
Bereft of body and senses — a non-doer too.
Yet varied systems, texts, and reasonings I compose.

v51:
So duality-thought dissolved, Māyā the enchanter crossed.
May one dissolve in brahman.

v52:
So, in the host of principles, attaining Śiva-madeness by contemplation.
What sorrow for the brahman-beholder? What delusion?

v53:
Good or bad, deed-fruit comes only from contact with false knowing.
Contact alone brings fault, even to the faultless.

v54:
The makers of world-convention tend ignorance here. Fools.
They go to birth and death, bound by duty.

v55:
Yet even duty-formed deeds, heaped in the time of ignorance,
perish by knowing.

## Song 7 (v56–v64) — fruitless done, burnt seed, knot cut

v56:
Done, yet not for fruit — how then birth?
The yoke of birth-bond broken: Śiva shines by His own light.

v57:
Freed from atom, Māyā, and deed, the self is not reborn.

v58:
The self-knower fears nowhere — all is his own form.
He never grieves, since in truth there is no perishing.

v59:
From the innermost heart rises the truth: "I indeed."
In the great-Lord state — what misfortune? Whose?

v60:
Liberation has no abode at all, nor any going elsewhere.
Cut ignorance's knot: own-power-manifestation is liberation.

v61:
Knot of ignorance cut. Doubt gone. Delusion cast off.
Merit and demerit wasted. Though yoked to a body — he is freed.

v62:
Deeds burnt by knowing bear no more birth.

v63:
By limited-mind-ness, it pictures a body fit for its deeds.
When this body falls, contracted knowing shines.

v64:
But if knowing is stainless — knower and doer of all-transcended,
spread, light-formed, never setting nor rising, true-resolved...

## Song 8 (v65–v73) — stainless amid extremes, flame-become, no praise

v65:
Beyond direction, time, and touch. Firm. Changeless.
The full Lord — sole maker of hosts of powers, dissolving and rising.

v66:
Awakened to the self as Śiva-made, maker of creation's rules.
How could he wander? Spread out — whence? Where? What refuge?

v67:
So proved by reasons: the knower's deed is fruitless.
"Not mine, this — but his." Firmly. No fruit in the world.

v68:
Awakened by contemplation, he offers all thoughts into the blazing self —
and becomes it.

v69:
Eating this or that. Covered by anything. Calmed. Dwelling anywhere.
The self of all beings is freed.

v70:
Though doing horse-rites by hundred-thousands, brahmin-killings by hundred-thousands —
the truth-knower is touched by neither merits nor faults. Stainless.

v71:
Leaving pride, joy, anger, lust, sorrow, fear, greed, delusion.
No praise. No formula. Let him move without opinions, though he seem dull.

v72:
The host of pride and joy arises from difference-delusion.
How could it touch the one awakened to the nondual self?

v73:
Nothing to praise or offer stands separate from him at all.
Pleased by praise, freed — then no bow, no formula.

## Song 9 (v74–v82) — temple-body, goddess, skull-gear, done-done

v74:
Bearing thirty-six principles. Full-eyed in the making of the body.
His god-house: his own or another's body — jar and the like.

v75:
There sits the goddess, supreme Bhairava-Śiva's own self, yoked to her power —
worshipped with pure gifts grasped by herself.

v76:
Offering all outer and inner differences in the blazing knowing-fire.
Effortless, the offering.

v77:
Meditation never setting: the blessed Lord creates varied forms.
That itself is meditation — true-formed as fancy draws.

v78:
Turning world-rows, principle-ordered fancies, and sense-hosts in inner knowing.
That, they say, is his muttering.

v79:
Seeing all with an even glance. Deeming knowing content.
Delighting in the world-burning-ground. Grasping the body as skull-staff.

v80:
Tasting the world-essence-drink in the known-fragment bowl,
held in his own hand, filled. This vow — hard to get and easy to get.

v81:
So birth-death-free, named truth-great-Lord, having got.
By the finder's shining: done-done, standing as wished.

v82:
Pervading, so told. All-self. Diversity-shaken. Peerless bliss supreme.
Who knows becomes made of that.

## Song 10 (v83–v91) — shrine or hut, gem-cut, last moment, beast-knowing

v83:
In a shrine or an outcaste hut — memory lost though — abandoning the body.
Freed at once with knowing. Going to isolation. Sorrow killed.

v84:
Shrine-service for merit. Outcaste-death for hell.
But with no touch of merit-demerit stain — what use?

v85:
Once fully separated, it never becomes that again.

v86:
So the knowing stands here by impression, separated by the sheath-curtain.
The freed self becomes — untouched by that touch.

v87:
Stained yet clear, once the covering is cut away by perfect craft.

v88:
So the pure state taught by the true teacher —
feeling the body-bound, freed though void of other bounds — shines as Śiva-formed.

v89:
By scripture's weight, by unshaken faith become that.
Got — before heaven, hell, and man-ness.

v90:
The last moment, nourishing a merit or demerit state, plays helper — for fools.
But in going on — no cause.

v91:
Whoever knows that self-ness — beast, bird, creeper, and the rest —
goes his own way, by old awakening impressed.

## Song 11 (v92–v100) — between bodies, death-signs, mid-way death

v92:
Heaven-made, hell-made — this person between bodies.
When that breaks, he joins another body by fitness.

v93:
As the self once shone for him in knowing-time, so then.
On body-falling — otherwise it does not become.

v94:
Senses stolen. Memory lost. Breath choked. Cut off. Vitals pained.
Enjoyment born of body-impressions.

v95:
How could the embodied one not be so?
Deluded though — at death-time the knower falls not from self-truth.

v96:
This truth-path, quickly, when gained from the teacher's mouth —
by keenest grace-fall — then indeed, unblocked: Śiva.

v97:
All-crossed form, by steps resorted, on gaining root in supreme truth.
At the end: Śiva-madeness.

v98:
But his truth-made stream, unreached, rested in the middle —
though mind yearns for that step — death may sometime come.

v99:
Fallen from yoga — the books call him lord of the world of wonder and enjoyment.
Rested by stage-power, become — in next birth, Śiva-becoming.

v100:
Practising this truth-path, missing yoga though, by name.
God-world-pleasure-sharer, glad-minded — he rejoices long.

## Song 12 (v101–v105) — fallen honored, striving, colophon

v101:
Among the worlds, the yoga-fallen is honored by all gods.

v102:
After long time, humanity got, yoga practised.
Getting the divine deathless — whence turning back no more.

v103:
Therefore whoever, devoted on the true path here, goes — he to Śiva-ness.
Thinking so — in truth, yet strive.

v104:
Meditating this supreme brahman, briefly told by Abhinavagupta.
Soon indeed Śiva-ness enters its dwelling in his own heart.

v105:
In a hundred āryās, this condensed recondite teaching-essence.
By Abhinavagupta — by me — kindled by memory of Śiva's feet.

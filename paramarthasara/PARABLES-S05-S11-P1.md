## Song 5 (vv38–46) — seeming, shattering, remainder-being

**Parable (≈80s):**
> A fool walks a fair of changing stalls — calm here, glad there —
> mistaking mood for the man who sells. The woman of the stalls
> wipes her great mirror: where the shine had doubled every face,
> the double falls and the shattering is done — rooted, done-done,
> no more duties for her. She lays out three ornaments — girdle,
> earring, bracelet — and every jewel on the cloth is gold the whole
> way through, once you stop counting the designs; the designs were
> never different from the gold. So the brahman rests in the power
> that shines it — untouched as sky, wearing what cannot be worn.
> One twist of her trident-prong and the whole stock — the making,
> the keeping, the taking-apart — is emitted outward: three eggs of
> goods, all of it, gone outside by the trader's own gaining.

**Hinge:** fool-in-moods → v38 seeming · mirror-wipe, double falls →
v39 shatter · rooted done-done no-duty → v40 · three ornaments one
gold → v41–42 triad-remainder/being-gold · brahman rests in power,
untouched sky, unwearable sky-flower → v43–44 · trident emission,
three eggs outside → v45–46.
**Rasa:** karuṇā-śānta — pity the fool, peace in the wiped mirror.
**Seal:** THE STALL OF THE WIPED MIRROR — fool at rim (small); the
great mirror mid-frame showing ONE reflection now; three ornaments
on cloth = one gold; an empty hook above where nothing hangs
(the sky-flower, unwearable); trident-mark; three eggs going out
the stall's mouth; colours: grey fool, gold ornaments, lapis mirror,
ivory emptiness at the hook.
**Base:** plate #3 (knight at the double fountain) — twin-waters ≈
double-reflection, figure-at-water ≈ stall-at-mirror, unspent.
Disk style-alt: none; folio pull at render. **Seed 818.**
**Prompt:** On aged vellum, one market-stall scene: a grey fool

## Song 6 (vv47–55) — the driver's song (I-voice, utsāha)

**Parable (≈78s), kept FIRST-PERSON (the song's own voice):**
> I drive the cart-wheel over the whole road: everything shines in
> me as jars in a spotless mirror, and spreads from me as dreams
> from a sleeper. I am the road-worn body and I shine in all of it,
> light everywhere. Seer, hearer, smeller — no body, no doer — yet
> I compose every system and text that travels this road. I've
> crossed the river: water in water, milk in milk: some small god
> without a sorrow. If men want the fruits of their deeds they come
> by contact alone — like keeping company with a thief when you're
> no thief. Fools, keeping the road's locks, go back to birth and
> death. Not me: even duty-done deeds, heaped long as cotton in
> ignorance's time, burn off in my knowing.

**Hinge:** drive → v47 · mirrors/dreams → v48 · light-in-all →
v49 · senseless composer → v50 · crossing/dissolve/no-sorrow →
v51–52 · contact-thief → v53 · fools' locks → v54 · cotton burns →
v55.

**Seal:** THE CART-AND-THE-FORD — one driver on the play-wheel

## Song 7 (vv56–64) — the file that ends open

**Parable (≈82s):**
> In the court where his file is read, everything is found
> fruitless: done-for-the-sentence, it sprouts no next one — like a
> seed freed of husk and bran that will not come up. The clerk
> checks his fear: nowhere to fear — everything is his own form;
> grief likewise refused: nothing here has perished. In the
> treasury of his heart, jewel-heaped, the entry says only: I
> indeed — what misfortune, whose? Liberation is called in — it has
> no address: no abode, no journey elsewhere; the knot of ignorance
> is cut, and that cut is itself the freedom, declared free even
> inside the body. His deeds, burnt in the knowing-fire, cannot give
> birth again. The mind-contract lasts only while this body does.
> And the file, being stainless — knower, doer, all-transcended,
> spread, never-rising, true-resolved: [the line ends.]

**Hinge:** fruitless/no-sprout → v56–57 · fear/grief refused →
v58 · heart-treasury "I indeed" → v59 · no-address, knot-cut =
freedom, freed-in-body → v60–61 · fire-deed → v62 · contraction →
v63 · the open ending = v64's condition left as the sheet stops.
**Rasa:** vīra (as recorded) settling into the open quiet — the
conditional IS the last gesture.
**Seal:** THE FILE WITH NO LAST LINE — scroll-stack unrolling
upward whose ink-trail STOPS mid-air (v64 open, letterless); the
empty door-frame beside it (liberation: no abode, no door); heart-
treasury glow above the frame ("I indeed"); sproutless pod and
burnt ribbon at the base; colours: ivory scroll, grey door-frame,
gold heart-glow, ash ribbon.
**Base:** plate #1 (Arms of the art) — the self-declaration page =

**Prompt:** On aged vellum, one courtroom-quiet scene: an ivory

## Song 8 (vv65–73) — the pilgrim nothing touches

**Parable (≈85s):**
> A pilgrim passes where the rites are sold: ten thousand
> horse-sacrifices, ten thousand of their opposite — and neither
> touches him, for he holds no deed apart from himself to stain or
> to save. He has crossed by contemplation, offering every thought
> into the blazing self until he was flame. So he eats this or
> that, wears whatever, sleeps calm anywhere: freed wherever. The
> proud host of feelings rises to claim him — it cannot find him
> in the crowd of what's different. He has left pride, joy, anger,
> lust, sorrow, fear, greed, delusion — he moves dull-looking to
> those who watch, without one opinion; and where would praise
> land? There is nothing separate from him to praise: no bow, no
> formula. He keeps going.

**Hinge:** extremes-untouched → v70 · flame-become → v68 ·

**Seal:** THE FLAME-PILGRIM AT THE FOUR-ROADS — crossroads center

## Song 9 (vv74–82) — the living temple

**Parable (≈85s):**
> Her body is the temple and its thirty-six bearers are her very
> parts. Within, at the heart-sanctum, the goddess sits and is
> worshipped with gifts she grasps herself — no priest between.
> Her own differences go into the blazing knowing-fire as
> offerings, effortlessly. Her meditation never sets: it IS the
> creating — varied forms arise as she sits. She turns the whole
> row of worlds inward and calls that muttering. Level-eyed she
> stands on the burning-ground, knowing enough, holding her body
> as a staff of bone, and drinks the world's essence from the
> skull-bowl in her own hand: hard to get, easy to get. Done and
> done she stands: all-peerless — and whoever knows this becomes
> it.

**Hinge:** temple+36 → v74 · goddess within, self-grasped gifts →

## Song 10 (vv83–91) — the gem and the two places

**Parable (≈85s):**
> He died the same in both: a shrine or the ash-field — memory gone,
> knowing and leaving at one stroke, isolation, sorrow dead. And
> the rites hired for the dead proved useless: no stain had touched
> him to wash — the rice-grain thrown clear between husks never
> sticks to them again; knowing, parted by its own thin curtain,
> stands untouched even while he is still here: freed inside. The
> gem shows clear the moment its setting is cut; the teacher's
> teaching does that cutting; faith arrives before heaven, hell, or
> man. The last hour carries him like a helper, not a driver. And
> whoever knows that self goes their own way — beast, bird,
> creeper alike — by an old awakening pressed into them.

**Hinge:** same-in-both-places, memory+knowing together, sorrow

**Rasa:** śānta — dying well: peace.

## Song 11 (vv92–100) — the costume between stages

**Parable (≈87s):**
> An actor between roles is yoked only to what fits: when one
> costume falls, the same face shows — the shine of the last
> performance stays the same. The stage-stripping is listed in
> order: senses lifted, memory gone, breath cut, vitals pained —
> costume-impression, nothing touching him. Yoked, never fallen:
> even at the death-cue the actor does not slip from the face.
> Quick-cued from the teacher's mouth — mask off, Śiva. Rehearsed
> in steps, resort by resort, he ends master-made. If the play
> cuts mid-stream before the last step — allowed; he rests where
> he stands, and the show honours him there — king of the
> interval — before the next arrival as master. And the understudy
> who never missed a rehearsal, though never cast: god-world's
> joy-sharer, glad-minded, rejoicing long.

**Hinge:** fitness-costume, same-face → v92–93 · stripping-list →
v94 · unfallen-at-cue → v95 · teacher-cue-Śiva → v96 · stepped
master-made → v97 · mid-cut allowed, king-of-interval, next-show
master → v98–99 · glad understudy → v100.
**Rasa:** śānta — succession assured: falling well, returning well.
**Seal:** THE COSTUME BETWEEN CURTAINS — mid-frame: actor parted
between two curtain-halves, one costume slipping off in the air,
face unchanged; at base the stripped-props listed in a neat row

**Base:** **Wellcome V0025631** (a prince clad in gold succeeds the

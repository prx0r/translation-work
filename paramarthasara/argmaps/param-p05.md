# Paramārthasāra p05 — ARGUMENT MAP (saṃkoca; śaktipāta; Guru's grace)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 param-p05 = kārikās 38–46. Lore: śaktipāta (descent of power) is the Trika's central soteriological mechanism; Guru's grace (guruprasāda) is the necessary condition for liberation. Lidke 2013; Bansat-Boudon/Tripathi 2011.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

How does the contracted experiencer get liberated? Not by his own effort — *without śaktipāta, no liberation* — but by the descent of Śiva's power (*śaktipāta*) and the Guru's grace (*guruprasāda*). The passage states the condition for liberation and the method: the mind, made stainless by śaktipāta, shines as light; the self rests in itself; non-dual contemplation dissolves the double delusion; and by Guru's grace, liberation.

Lore: śaktipāta is the Trika's central soteriological mechanism — the descent of power that makes liberation possible. The Guru is the necessary condition: śaktipāta descends through the Guru. Lidke 2013: the text is a guide for kuṇḍalinī awakening and internal ascension through the tattvas. The glossary confirms: *jīvanmukti* = liberation while living, conducts transactions but not led away; *bhāvanā* = contemplative meditation by constant repetition of correct conceptional knowledge. The text's style is simple and lucid (unlike ĪPK's subtle logic) — a textbook for beginners.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — the moon and Rāhu, continued (v. 38):**
> *śānte śānta ivāyaṃ hṛṣṭe hṛṣṭo vimohavati mūḍhaḥ | tattvagaṇe sati bhagavān na punaḥ paramārthataḥ sa tathā*
[i.e. as the moon, when the sky is clear, is clear; when agitated, agitated; when deluded, deluded — when the tattvas are present, the Lord is not, in ultimate truth, thus.] The Lord's state depends on the knower's vision, not on any change in Śiva.

**Step 1 — removing the delusion (v. 39):**
> *yad anātmany api tadrūpāvabhāsanaṃ tat purā nirākṛtya | ātmany anātmarūpāṃ bhrāntiṃ vidalayati paramātmā*
[i.e. having first removed the shining of non-self form in non-self, Paramātmā destroys the delusion of non-self form in the self.] Two-stage purification: external (the world is not "I") and internal (the body is not "I").

**Step 2 — the accomplished yogi (v. 40):**
> *itthaṃ vibhramayugalakasamūlavicchedane kṛtārthasya | kartavyāntarakalanā na jātu parayogino bhavati*
[i.e. thus, when the root of the double delusion is cut, the accomplished yogi has no further task to perform.] Liberation is not an achievement but a cessation of the need to achieve.

**Step 3 — the threefold veiling (v. 41):**
> *pṛthivī prakṛtir māyā tritayam idaṃ vedyarūpatāpatitam*
[i.e. earth, prakṛti, Māyā — this threefold has fallen into the state of being an object of knowledge.] The three levels of veiling are themselves objects of knowledge, not ultimate realities.

**Step 4 — non-dual contemplation (v. 42):**
> *advaitabhāvanabalād bhavati hi sanmātrapariśeṣam | raśanākuṇḍalakaṭakaṃ bhedatyāgena dṛśyate yathā hema*
[i.e. by the power of non-dual contemplation, only pure being remains, as gold remains when bracelets, rings, and bangles are seen through as gold.] The analogy: the ornaments (world, body, senses) are gold (consciousness) in different forms. Non-dual contemplation (*advaitabhāvana*) sees through the form to the substance.

**Step 5 — the result (v. 43):**
> *tadvad bhedatyāge sanmātraṃ sarvam ābhāti tad brahma paraṃ śuddhaṃ śāntaṃ abhedātmakaṃ samaṃ sakalam*
[i.e. so, when difference is abandoned, all shines as pure being — that Brahman, supreme, pure, peaceful, of non-dual nature, same, all.] *Amṛtaṃ satyaṃ śaktau viśrāmyati bhāsvarūpāyām* — the immortal, the true, rests in the shining power.

**Step 6 — śaktipāta (v. 44):**
> *aparāmṛṣṭaṃ yad api tu nabhaḥprasūnatvam abhyeti śaktitriśūlaparigamayogena samastam api parameśe*
[i.e. even the sky-born (the highest), by the union of the three-pronged śakti, reaches the supreme Lord.] *Śaktitriśūla* — the three-pronged power: this is śaktipāta, the descent of grace that makes liberation possible. *Śivanāmani paramārthe visṛjyate devadevena* — in the name of Śiva, in the supreme, the God of gods releases.

**Step 7 — the return (v. 45):**
> *punar api ca pañcaśaktiprasaraṇakrameṇa bahir api tat | aṅdatrayaṃ vicitraṃ sṛṣṭaṃ bahirātmalābhena*
[i.e. again, by the fivefold power's spreading, outside too that; the threefold (gross/subtle/supreme) is wonderfully created, by the external attainment of the self.] After liberation, the Lord creates again — but now the creation is recognized as self, not as other.

**Step 8 — the condition (v. 46, implied):**
Without śaktipāta, no liberation. By Guru's grace, liberation. The Guru is the necessary condition: śaktipāta descends through the Guru.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. *Śaktitriśūla* — the "three-pronged power" is not individually enumerated here; the standard Trika identifies three śaktis (icchā, jñāna, kriyā) or three levels of śaktipāta. The text supports both.
2. The *pañcaśakti* (five powers) of v. 45 — the same five as p02 (Śiva, Śakti, Sadāśiva, Īśvara, Vidyā), now in their creative (external) mode.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the passage's own movement: moon/Rāhu continued → removing delusion (external then internal) → accomplished yogi → threefold veiling → gold analogy → result → śaktipāta (three-pronged) → the return (creation recognized as self) → condition (śaktipāta + Guru's grace). The key move is v. 42's gold analogy: non-dual contemplation sees through form to substance. And v. 44's śaktipāta: liberation is not earned, it is descended. Carry both into p07 (the upāyas) and p12 (the seal).

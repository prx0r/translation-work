# Paramārthasāra p06 — ARGUMENT MAP (the five/seven knowers)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 param-p06 = kārikās 47–55. Lore: the seven knowers (pramātṛ) are Abhinava's own classification, standard in Trika; the two malas (Māyā-mala and kalā-mala) determine the knower's level. Bansat-Boudon/Tripathi 2011; Lidke 2013.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

The knowers (*pramātṛ*) are not a plurality of souls but Śiva himself in various states of contraction (*saṅkoca*). The passage classifies them by which malas veil them: Śiva (pure, no mala), Mantramaheśvara (Māyā-mala only), Mantreśvara (vidyā-mala), Mantrī (kalā-mala), vijñānakala (kalā-mala only, pure of Māyā-mala), pralayākala (Māyā-mala only, pure of kalā-mala), sakala (both malas). The ladder ascends from sakala (most contracted) to Śiva (pure). Liberation is moving up the ladder.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — the Lord drives the wheel (v. 47):**
> *iti śakticakrayantraṃ krīḍāyogena vāhayan devaḥ*
[i.e. thus the Lord, playing, drives the wheel of the power-circle.] The knowers are not independent; they are positions on Śiva's own wheel, driven by his play (*krīḍā*).

**Step 1 — Śiva (v. 48):**
> *aham eva śuddharūpaḥ śaktimahācakranāyakapadasthaḥ*
[i.e. I alone, pure in form, standing in the position of the great wheel's lord of powers.] Śiva: pure, stainless, omniscient, all-doer. No mala. The top of the ladder.

**Step 2 — Mantramaheśvara (v. 49):**
> *mayy eva bhāti viśvaṃ darpaṇa iva nirmale ghatādīni*
[i.e. the world shines in me alone, as pots etc. in a stainless mirror.] Contracted by Māyā-mala only: sees the world as Śiva's self-manifestation but is still veiled by Māyā.

**Step 3 — Mantreśvara (v. 50):**
> *mattaḥ prasarati sarvaṃ svapnavicitratvam iva suptāt*
[i.e. all spreads from me, like the variety of a dream from sleep.] Contracted by vidyā-mala: the world appears as dream-like variety.

**Step 4 — Mantrī (v. 51):**
> *aham eva viśvarūpaḥ karacaranādisvabhāva iva dehaḥ*
[i.e. I alone am world-formed, like the body with hand, foot etc.] Contracted by kalā-mala: identifies with the body and its functions.

**Step 5 — vijñānakala (v. 52):**
> *sarvasmin aham eva sphurāmi bhāveṣu bhāsvarūpam iva draṣṭā śrotā ghrātā dehendriyavarjito 'py akartāpi*
[i.e. I shine in all things, like a seer, hearer, smeller — though devoid of body and senses, a non-doer.] Pure after abandoning Māyā-mala; contracted by kalā-mala only. Knows itself as consciousness but is still limited by kalā (time, space, number).

**Step 6 — pralayākala (v. 53):**
> *siddhāntāgamatarkāṃś citrān aham eva racayāmi*
[i.e. I alone craft the varied, picture-like sections of siddhānta, āgama, and tarka.] Pure after abandoning kalā-mala; contracted by Māyā-mala only. Has scriptural and logical knowledge but is veiled by Māyā.

**Step 7 — sakala (v. 54):**
> *itthaṃ dvaitavikalpe galite pravilaṅghya mohinīṃ māyām | salile salilaṃ kṣīre kṣīram iva brahmaṇi layī syāt*
[i.e. thus, when dualistic thought dissolves, transcending deluding Māyā, one becomes absorbed in Brahman as water in water, milk in milk.] The sakala, contracted by both malas, is the ordinary bound soul. Liberation: dissolve the dualistic thought, transcend Māyā, be absorbed in Brahman.

**Step 8 — the destruction of karma (v. 55):**
> *kaḥ śokaḥ ko mohaḥ sarvaṃ brahmāvalokayataḥ | karmaphalaṃ śubham aśubhaṃ mithyājñānena saṃgamād eva*
[i.e. what grief, what delusion, for one who sees all as Brahman? Auspicious and inauspicious karma-fruit arises only from contact with false knowledge.] *Viṣamo hi saṅgadoṣas taskarayogo 'py ataskarasyeva* — contact is the flaw; like a thief's association harms the innocent. *Cirasaṃcitam iva tūlaṃ naśyati vijñānadīptivaśāt* — long-accumulated karma, like cotton, is destroyed by the flame of knowledge.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The sevenfold vs. fivefold enumeration (see p04 §3.1) — the T1 gloss lists seven; the standard Trika names five. The argmap follows the T1 gloss.
2. *Śakticakrayantraṃ* (v. 47) — the "wheel of the power-circle" is not further specified; it refers to the 36 tattvas or to the Mātṛkā.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the ladder: Śiva (pure) → Mantramaheśvara (Māyā-mala) → Mantreśvara (vidyā-mala) → Mantrī (kalā-mala) → vijñānakala (kalā only) → pralayākala (Māyā only) → sakala (both). The key move is v. 54's dissolution: *dvaitavikalpe galite … brahmaṇi layī syāt* — when dualistic thought dissolves, absorption in Brahman. And v. 55's karma destruction: knowledge burns karma like fire burns cotton. Carry the ladder into p07 (the upāyas) and p08 (jīvanmukti).

# C1 — param-p01 (frame asked and answered)

## SUMMARY

I resort to all-abiding Śambhu. The sorrow-wheel-whirled student asks the supreme truth; the teacher answers in support-verses, retold Śaiva-wise. The Lord manifests the fourfold egg by power-weight; the embodied enjoyer is Śiva fettered. As crystal takes colors the Lord holds all forms; as moon moves with water the self moves with the world. As Rahu shows on the moon the everywhere-self shows in the mind-mirror; grace-purified mind mirrors the light-formed self, replete with will, knowing and acting.

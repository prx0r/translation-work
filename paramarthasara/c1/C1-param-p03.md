# C1 — param-p03

## SUMMARY

Ear to nose know, voice to privates do; their objects are subtle and division-free tanmātras, whose mixture grossifies into five elements. Creation from nature husks consciousness down to earth; three sheaths wrap the self. Through ignorance-darkness the one self perceives manifold grasper-grasped variety — as one cane-juice in many sweets, all states of Śambhu. Knower, ruler, breath and the rest are mere convention; rope-snake fear kills, and delusion's power none can tell apart.

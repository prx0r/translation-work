# C1 — param-p04

## SUMMARY

So dharma through pain, caste through stations, though unreal in the self, arise by delusion's force. This is that darkness: shining in things, the not-self is taken for self — darkness upon darkness, a burst upon the cheek, body and breath taken for self. By body-breath-reflection yoga the self wraps itself in a net like the net-maker; unwrapped by its own shining glory, supreme Śiva spreads the play of bondage and release. Creating, waking, sleep shine in the fourth, yet unveiled. Waking is difference-universe, dreaming is light-glory, sleep is massed knowing — beyond, the fourth. As sky unsoiled by cloud-smoke-dust, the person untouched by Māyā; as one dusty jar leaves others clean, souls enjoy differences unstained.

# C1 — param-p05

## SUMMARY

In calm calm, in glad glad — the fool follows states, but the Lord truly not so. First the non-self's mirroring removed, then self's non-self delusion shattered. Root-cut, done-done, no further duty for the supreme yogin. Earth-nature-Māyā remainder into mere being by nondual might; as goldsmith's wares are gold when difference is left, all shines as being. Brahman rests in shining power; untouched, yet sky-flowered. All emitted in Śiva-name by god of gods; again the varied three eggs created outwardly.

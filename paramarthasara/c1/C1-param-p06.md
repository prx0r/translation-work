# C1 — param-p06

## SUMMARY

Thus driving the power-wheel-machine by play, I stand pure-formed in the lord-seat. In me shines the universe as jars in a spotless mirror; from me spreads all as dreams from a sleeper. Universe-formed, hand-foot-natured as body, I shine in all, light-natured. Seer, hearer, smeller, senseless, undoing, I compose varied systems. Duality dissolved, Māyā crossed — dissolved in brahman as water in water, milk in milk. Principle-host contemplated into Śiva-madeness: what sorrow for the brahman-beholder? Fruits come from contact with false knowing; company faults hard, like a thief's though one is no thief. Convention-tending fools cycle duty-bound; ignorance-heaped deeds burn like cotton in knowing-flame.

# C1 — param-p08

## SUMMARY

Beyond direction-time-touch, firm, changeless, full Lord, sole maker of dissolving-rising hosts. Awakened to self as Śiva-made, rule-maker: how wander, spread out? Proved: the knower's deed fruitless — 'not mine but his,' firmly. Thoughts sacrificed in blazing self-flame: flame-become. Eating anything, covered, calmed, anywhere: freed. Extremes done, untouched by merit-fault, stainless. Stainless, formulaless, dull-moving, opinion-free. Difference-born host touches not the nondual-awakened. Nothing separate to praise or offer: pleased, freed, bowless.

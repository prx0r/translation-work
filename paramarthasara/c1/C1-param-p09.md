# C1 — param-p09

## SUMMARY

Thirty-six-bearing, body-eyed, own or other's — his god-house. Bhairavī worshipped there with self-grasped pure gifts. Difference-seeds offered in blazing knowing-fire, effortless. Never-setting meditation: creating varied forms, fancy-drawn true. Worlds turned inward: muttering. Even-glanced, burning-ground-delighting, skull-staff-grasped. Skull-bowl tasted: hard-easy vow. Got birth-death-free truth: done-done standing as wished. Told pervading all-self, peerless bliss: knowing becomes that.

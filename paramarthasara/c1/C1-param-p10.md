# C1 — param-p10

## SUMMARY

Dying in shrine or hut, memory or not — freed with knowing, going to isolation, sorrow killed. Rites for merit or hell mean nothing to the stain-untouched. Separated grain unrejoined; separated knowing, staying, touch-free. Condition cut: gem-clear. Teacher-purified, Śiva-formed. Faith-made before heaven-hell-man. The last moment helps fools, causes nothing. Beast-knowing goes right by old awakening.

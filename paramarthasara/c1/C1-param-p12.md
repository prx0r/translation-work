# C1 — param-p12

## SUMMARY

As emperors are honored, so the fallen yogin among gods. After long time, humanity regained, yoga practised — the deathless got, no return. Whoever devoted on the true path goes to Śiva-ness: think so, yet strive. Meditating the brief-told supreme brahman, soon Śiva-ness enters own-heart-dwelling. In a hundred āryās this recondite essence condensed — by Abhinavagupta, by me, kindled by Śiva-feet-memory.

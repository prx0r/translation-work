# C1 — Paramārthasāra

## param-p01–p03 — supreme, tattva, senses

**FUNCTION.** Establishes Śambhu as supreme, the supreme tattva (pure/thought-free, 36 tattvas like mirror), and senses/elements/tanmātras.

**KEY TERMS.** śambhu (Śambhu), paratattva (supreme principle), ṣaṭtriṃśat (36), darpaṇa (mirror), buddhīndriya (cognitive sense), karmendriya (conative sense), tanmātra (subtle element), bhūta (gross element), prakṛti (prakṛti).



**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A06 (sthāna/kāla), spans TS1.1–1.28.

## param-p04–p06 — bondage, saṃkoca, knowers
**SUMMARY.** Bondage: thus dharma/adharma, heaven/hell, birth/death, pleasure/pain; varṇa/āśrama etc. in self, though unreal, arises by delusion's power; this is that darkness, which by shining in things, even in non-self things, this I-am-not-self delusion; darkness upon darkness, great burst upon the cheek; that in non-self, body/breath etc., I-am-self-ness; by body-breath-reflection-knowledge-sky-expanse-yoga, self is wrapped in a net, like a net-maker by net. Saṃkoca: as spotless moon by timira-afflicted sight shines as if defiled by Rāhu, so Paramaśiva shines as if impure having attained knowerhood; pure becomes as if impure by Māyā's power, contracted body; though all-pervading, yet by body-lordship attains experiencerhood; without śaktipāta, no liberation; by Guru's grace, liberation. Knowers: fivefold — Śiva (pure, stainless, omniscient, all-doer), Mantramaheśvara (contracted by Māyā), Mantreśvara (contracted by vidyā), Mantrī (contracted by kalā), vijñānakala (pure after abandoning Māyā-mala, contracted by kalā-mala), pralayākala (pure after abandoning kalā-mala, contracted by Māyā-mala), sakala (both malas contracted).

**FUNCTION.** Establishes bondage (delusion, I-am-not-self), saṃkoca (Paramaśiva as if impure by Māyā, śaktipāta required), and five knower categories.

**KEY TERMS.** bandha (bondage), vibhrama (delusion), anātmābhimāna (I-am-not-self), saṃkoca (contraction), śaktipāta (grace), pramātṛ (knower), mala (impurity), vijñānakala, pralayākala, sakala.

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p01–p03 (supreme/tattva/senses); p04–p06 establish bondage/saṃkoca/knowers.


**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.29–1.55.

## param-p07–p09 — liberation, world, body
**SUMMARY.** Liberation = attainment of Śivahood; by Guru's grace, knowledge arises, mala destroyed; three upāyas: anupāya (śaktipāta alone), śāmbhava (will), śākta (knowledge), āṇava (action). World = Śiva's own-nature; threefold: gross (5 elements), subtle (5 tanmātras), supreme (5 tattvas: Śiva/Śakti/Sadāśiva/Īśvara/Vidyā). Body = Śiva's garment; threefold (gross/subtle/supreme); five winds (prāṇa/apāna/vyāna/udāna/samāna); three channels (suṣumnā/iḍā/piṅgalā).

**FUNCTION.** Establishes liberation (Śivahood, three upāyas), world (Śiva's own-nature, threefold), body (Śiva's garment, winds/channels).

**KEY TERMS.** mokṣa (liberation), śivatva (Śivahood), upāya (means), anupāya (no-means), śāmbhava (Śāmbhava), śākta (Śākta), āṇava (Āṇava), viśva (world), deha (body), prāṇa (breath), nāḍī (channel).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p04–p06 (bondage/saṃkoca/knowers); p07–p09 establish liberation/world/body.


**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A06 (sthāna/kāla), spans TS1.56–1.82.

## param-p10–p12 — mind, soul, close
**SUMMARY.** Mind = Śiva's function; fourfold inner instrument (manas/buddhi/ahaṃkāra/citta) with distinct functions (resolve/doubt, determination, I-ness, memory); manas supports senses, buddhi determines, ahaṃkāra agents, citta remembers. Soul = Śiva's part; threefold: jīva (worldly, by Māyā), ātman (liberated, by śaktipāta), paramātman (eternally liberated = Śiva). Close: Paramārthasāra completed; Śivahood attainment = liberation; by Guru's grace, knowledge arises, mala destroyed; all becomes Śiva.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes mind (fourfold inner instrument), soul (threefold), close (all-is-Śiva).

**KEY TERMS.** manas (mind), antaḥkaraṇa (inner instrument), buddhi (intellect), ahaṃkāra (ego), citta (citta), ātman (soul), jīva (jīva), paramātman (paramātman).



**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS1.83–1.104.

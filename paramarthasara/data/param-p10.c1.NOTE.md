Separated grain unrejoined; separated knowing, staying, touch-free. Condition cut: gem-clear. Teacher-purified, Śiva-formed. Faith-made before heaven-hell-man. The last moment helps fools, causes nothing. Beast-knowing goes right by old awakening.

## Lore (one search, then the Sanskrit decided; scholarship re-checked after)

- **Edition:** only classical dress is KSTS 15 itself; no separate commentary — the brackets are Kṣemarāja's own vṛtti on his mūla.
- **Translations:** Pradīpaka's pure English (title as entering supreme Śakti's mysteries; Parā/Parāparā/Aparā scale; Ṣaṭtriṃśattattvasandoha as the fuller tattva account); Lakshmanjoo/Hughes ch. 3 (free rendering with commentary, Sanskrit appendix); Erchenkov 2012 Russian (appended to Śivasūtravimarśinī); Hareesh's Tantra Illuminated course (bīja verse centerpiece; one authorship wrinkle — Nāgānanda vs Kṣemarāja — logged in par-p01, edition followed).
- **After-check:** Pradīpaka's renderings tested against mine (unmade-I flash; mine-flashing; proof-wretch's office; king analogy; sesame-ghee-void initiation) — no departures; where he paraphrases I kept the harder literal.
- **Authorship wrinkle:** Hareesh ascribes to Nāgānanda against KSTS/Muktabodha; logged, not adjudicated.

## The arc

Vow: world-formed-yet-transcending consciousness (1) → light is awareness-natured; unmade-I flashing; sun proves inertness follows its lack (2) → ten scriptural names, each defined by its work (3) → He shines as śiva-to-earth world; effecthood is dependent-shining; never veiled (4) → the already-proved needs no proof; proof serves māyā-dealings; the Life beyond proofs (5) → word-mass I-grasping; tattva defined; the thirty-six listed (6) → dimming ladder to person, bondsman, magician-immune Lord, recognition-freed, robbed-king sheaths (7) → five sheaths, nature, organ, senses, subtles, elements (8) → heart-seed holds all; letter-involution; initiation-without-oblation; living-freed; Śiva at death (9).

# Parāprāveśikā — L1 controlled

## par-p01 (maṅgala)

Oṃ. Veneration to Śiva, whose self is all tattvas. Now the Entering-into-the-Supreme, composed by glorious Kṣemarāja, best of great Māheśvara teachers. We venerate consciousness — heart of the supreme Lord — world-formed, transcending that, throbbing in supreme-etc.-power-form. // PP_1

## par-p02 (light is awareness-natured)

Here the supreme Lord is light-selfed; and light is awareness-natured — [objection: though light-natured, why should the Lord not be mere-knowing-natured like the calm-brahman speakers hold?] — awareness twofold by limited-unlimited; lest void-knower-fitted limitedness cap consciousness-light, awareness is named thus: in emission world-formed, in maintenance world-shining, in withdrawal self-seized — full-I-joy-formed throughout — named: unmade-I flashing by world-forming, world-shining, world-withdrawing. [Were awareness lacking, as sun-light lacking awareness falls inert by other-dependence into unfreedom, so too would consciousness-light stick.] If awareness-less, lordlessness and inertness would follow. // PP_2

## par-p03 (the ten names)

This awareness is trumpeted in the āgamas by: consciousness, consciousness-in-freedom, self-rasa-risen supreme speech, freedom, the supreme self's chief lordship, doership, throbbing, essence, heart, vibration, etc. — [in the Śaiva teaching, awareness-primacy in consciousness-light is trumpeted in each āgama, recited in differing āgama-speeches:] consciousness, since awareness is the self's chief form; supreme speech, since speaking/declaring the world by re-grasping, supreme as ground-and-fullness of further grasps, self-rasa-risen as ever-risen I in full-consciousness-body; freedom, joining the outward-cast this-state to self, disjoining it, lowering it through void-stages, differing thereby from inert; lordship, needing no other at emission's start, unlike fate-bound Brahmā-officers; doership, raising śiva-to-earth world into this-ness, yogin-creation-like, in inward-shining oneness; throbbing, the mine-flashing-entered relation, else all would flash for all; essence, the non-trifling form, the Lord's awareness-power; heart, the world's rest-ground; vibration, the unmoving light's moving-shining, world-shining as excess where none is. // PP_3

## par-p04 (He shines as the world)

Therefore the unmade-I, reality-possessed, self-shining-formed Lord throbs and shines by the supreme Lordess's power in śiva-to-earth world-form. This itself is His world-doership and its non-inertness. [Objection: what effecthood — by difference (breaks His freedom) or nondifference (how then)? Reply: without another's shining the world is unnoticed, self-ungained; reaching shining-bodiedness through His support is effecthood; by māyā's freedom difference displayed, nondifferent yet handled different.] Effecthood is shining-dependent-on-Him. The world so formed is nondifferent from maker-Maheśvara. [Were the knowable different, unshining would unmake shining — nothing would be; takings-givings would perish. Objection: if nondifferent, likeness-difference unveilable, He veiled by it? Reply: rather the veil, gained in self, destroys itself veiling shining.] By this world His shining-formed nature is never veiled; gaining rest by His shining, the world — His life-breath — how could it block Him, and blocking, itself stand? // PP_4

## par-p05 (no proof for the self-shining)

[Indicators-causes cannot work on the before-proved Lord; even the injunction-making knower existing in His likeness, his proof is not proof-dependent — hence His self-shining is experience-proved.] "What proof of His being?" — assent the thing's being; "what proof of such-nature?" — as the asker's own form: the before-proved Maheśvara's self-shining is everyone's self-feeling-proved. [Consciousness-light, life of all, ordains proof in māyā-dealings; there no proof avails — all else, inert, cannot shine itself, how establish another?] Proof becomes proof resorting to Him; for proof's life, the Lord, the proof-wretch proving fresh shinings is no use — [proof makes fresh-shining rest, proof-fruit-marked, in the knower; the knower, ever-shone, unbroken-shining, enjoys all proof-fruits inward-faced in self — how use fresh flash there, where else would its fruit rest? — so proof serves body-life-blue-pleasure knowables only, not proof's-life, the Lord.] Thus said: proofs that stretch as things' life — beyond them too the Life, He the supreme Lord. // PP_5

## par-p06 (word-mass I-ness; the thirty-six)

Thus, His essence being full-I-grasping made of word-mass [letter-heap a-to-ha enwombing all world, "I alone light-selfed in shining," freedom resting with no other face to watch], supreme Śiva Himself is the thirty-six-tattva-formed expanse. [Tattva: the one undivided shining causing divided classes' classing — as mountain-town show earth-form, river-ocean water-form.] The thirty-six: 1 Śiva 2 power 3 eternal-Śiva 4 Lord 5 pure-knowledge 6 māyā 7 art 8 knowledge 9 passion 10 time 11 necessity 12 person 13 nature 14 intellect 15 ego 16 mind 17–21 knowing-senses 22–26 doing-senses 27–31 subtles 32–36 elements. // PP_6

## par-p07 (marks; person, bondsman, freed)

Now their marks. Śiva-tattva: supreme Śiva, will-knowledge-action-formed pure-full-bliss-natured — within all knowers throbbing full-I-joy, beyond all tattvas, bearing hundred-thousand emission-dissolution reflections. His world-emitting-will-taken, first throb is will-power-tattva — by own-freedom's greatness, outward-bloom-wishing, bliss-surplus: "I" grasped, power-tattva. The just-sprouting world self-I-covered stands eternal-Śiva-tattva [faint this-portion bloomed on pure-consciousness-ground, will-chief]; sprouted world I-covered stands Lord-tattva [I sprinkled on clarified this, knowledge-chief, differing only by this-portion dim-undim]; I-this-oneness-grasped is pure-knowledge [grown-difference flashing, consciousness-mere I shining, balanced-scales "I-this," action-chief]. Difference-spreading among own-natured things is māyā [hardest-achieving, growing knower-pride in unconscious-void, showing conscious-one things divided, veiling own-form, material cause of art-to-earth]. When the Lord by Lordess's māyā-power — freedom's synonym — veils own-form taking contracted-knowerness, He is called person — māyā-deluded, deed-bound deeming deeds binders, wanderer wandering. Though nondifferent, His delusion is not the Lord's — like the magician's self-willed illusion. By knowledge's form-showing, lordship brought to recognition — deeming even body-world pure feeling — consciousness-dense, freed [fit-called freed while bodied, birth-bond-free; at fall, supreme-Śiva-nature] — supreme Śiva Himself. His all-doership, all-knowingness, fullness, eternity, pervasion — uncontracted powers taking contraction as art-knowledge-passion-time-necessity [as the robbed king is dealt a little back for living, so atomized wake, robbed of omniscience, is dealt art-etc. for little-doership]. // PP_7

## par-p08 (sheaths, organ, senses, elements)

Art: whereby, all-doing-unable, some pot he does. Knowledge: whereby some forth-standing pot he knows, not the far-removed. Passion: "incomplete, other, this serves me; may I be more; may I never not be" — object-clinging. Time: divider of shining-unshining things' sequence — "I knew, know, will know; I did, do, will do" — measuring things in knowledge-action form, cutting off — past-etc. Necessity: "mine this to be done, not this" — restraining cause; fixed-cause-fixed-effect manifold. This fivefold, own-form-veiling, is sheath — distinct-yet-undistinct, veiling full-consciousness-nature. Of great-to-earth tattvas root-cause is nature — good-passion-dark, pleasure-pain-delusion, shining-doing-restraining, balance-state undivided. Deciding intellect [thought-mirror-bearing, clear, reflection-fit]; ego, mine-not-mine pride-means; mind, resolve-means — trio inner-organ, inner-pleasure-grasp-means, guṇa-effect, sense-gross-cause. Sound-touch-form-taste-smell objects' grasp-means in order: ear skin eye tongue nose. Speech-taking-moving-excreting-bliss-action-means in order: speech hand foot excretion generation. Sound touch form taste smells, common-formed, five subtles [that itself, nothing more, in-between-distinction-free]. Ether space-giving; air enlivening; fire burning-cooking; water refreshing-liquid; earth supporting. // PP_8

## par-p09 (the heart-seed)

As in banyan seed sprout-leaf-fruits stand in fitting body, so the manifold-varied world stands inside the heart-seed — [like peacock-egg-juice craft holding all plume-line variety, for the attentive, subtle-sight-weighable.] "As the banyan-seed-abiding power-formed great tree, so the heart-seed-abiding moving-unmoving world" — by this tradition-way, in supreme-Lady-formed heart-seed the world is contained. How? As pot-bowl changes are ultimately clay, as liquid-kinds judged are water-common, so earth-to-māyā tattvas judged are being itself. Of "sat" too, suffix-part discarded, mere-nature sa-letter remains — inside it thirty-one tattvas; beyond, pure-knowledge-Lord-eternal-Śiva, knowledge-action-essenced, as power-distinctions enter assent-formed unsurpassed-power-formed au-syllable. Hence the supreme up-down emission-formed visarga to be emitted. Of the heart-seed so formed — great-mantra-formed, world-formed, world-transcended — supreme Śiva Himself, as arising-resting-place, is own-nature [all-formedness, formlessness, in order the cause]. Who knows and enters such heart-seed in reality is truly initiated [this knowing itself, sesame-ghee-oblation-void, is initiation in truth]; holding lifebreaths, worldly-abiding, freed-while-living he becomes; at body's fall the Lord supreme Śiva Himself he becomes. Thus Kṣemarāja's Parāprāveśikā finished. // PP_9

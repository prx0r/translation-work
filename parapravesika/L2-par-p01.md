# Parāprāveśikā par-p01 (maṅgala) — L2 READ

## v.1
Veneration to Śiva, whose very self is all the levels of reality. Here begins the Entering-into-the-Supreme, composed by glorious Kṣemarāja, noblest of the great Māheśvara teachers. We bow to consciousness itself — the heart of the supreme Lord — which has the world for its form and yet goes beyond it, pulsing in the shape of supreme power.
The Sanskrit: *viśvātmikāṃ taduttirṇāṃ hṛdayaṃ parameśituḥ parādiśaktirūpeṇa sphurantīṃ saṃvidaṃ numaḥ*

# Parāprāveśikā par-p09 (the heart-seed) — L2 READ

## v.9
As sprout and leaf and fruit stand in the banyan seed in bodies exactly fitted to them, so this whole world in all its varied makings stands inside the seed of the heart — as in peacock-egg juice the craftsman's skill already holds every feather's line and plume-variety, for those rich in attention to weigh with subtle sight. "As the great tree stands in the banyan seed in power-form, so this world, moving and unmoving, stands in the heart-seed": by this traditional maxim, in the heart-seed shaped as the supreme Lady this world is contained. How so? As the ultimate truth of pot and bowl, clay's transformations, is clay itself — as watery kinds, examined, settle into water-in-common — so the levels from earth to illusion, examined for what is real in them, prove to be being itself. Even of the word "being" itself: strip the suffix that shows the root's sense, and the bare-stem letter s alone is left — and inside it stand thirty-one levels; beyond it, pure knowledge, the Lord, and eternal Śiva, knowledge-and-action in essence, enter as special powers into the syllable au, shaped as assent, formed of unsurpassed power. Hence the supreme emission, shaped as creation above and below: the breath-out to be breathed. Of the heart-seed so formed — shaped as the great mantra, shaped as the world, beyond the world — supreme Śiva Himself, as the seat where all rising comes to rest, is its own nature: all-shapedness, then shapelessness, each in turn the cause. Whoever truly knows such a heart-seed, and enters it — he is initiated in the highest sense: this very knowing, with no sesame-and-ghee oblation, is initiation in truth. Still breathing, living as men live, he is free while living; and when the body falls, he becomes the Lord, supreme Śiva Himself.
The Sanskrit: *nyagrodhabījasthaḥ śaktirūpo mahādrumaḥ tathā hṛdayabījasthaṃ viśvametaccarācaram | prakṛtimātrarūpaḥ sakāra evāvaśiṣyate, | tilājyāhutivarjitametatparijñānameva tattvato dīkṣā*

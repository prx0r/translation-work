## The parable (canonical draft, ≈88s spoken)

> First a vow ties the deity to the doctrine in one word — the
> world-shaped, world-surpassing, throbbing one. Then the seed is
> challenged by the tools of proof: show us the tree. And one
> alternative is offered — bare light, calm-brahman with no knower
> in it — refused: that is absence, lordlessness, deadness. The
> prover was proved before the proof: the questions answer
> themselves, the indicators cannot touch what is already inside,
> so the instruments are set down and Life sits where the proof
> was. Ten names are trumpeted, one throb underneath. The husk is
> woven of sound, A to H, thirty-six levels inside, one shine
> familying the divided. The seed then wills its emission —
> will-first, knowing-first, doing-first, a hundred-thousand
> reflections pressed into one intending. Five little labors press
> out: doing, knowing, clinging, three strands braiding, one veil
> over fullness. Then the world comes up: the sound carried to the
> heart, knowing without oblation — living-free. Śiva at the fall;
> the sprout stands.

## The seal — statement

**The seed that retires its proofs:** base: a clay-pot with water
(the clay-and-water proof made literal — the seed PLANTED as its
own evidence), beside it a grey proof-bench with instruments laid
down and one unlit grey lamp pushed aside (bare light refused), ten
trumpet-flowers ringing the rim (ten names); center: the seed in
the clay, husk woven with fine sound-line pattern (A to H, no
letters), emitting ONE green shoot that braids into three strands
with five small leaves and a translucent veil-film hanging; crown:
the sprout standing free in ivory-gold light — living-free.

## Composition laws (this seal)

ONE subject (the sprout; bench/lamp/flowers = rim). No letters
EVER (the A-to-H weave = pattern, never glyphs; the ten names =
flowers, never trumpets-with-marks). Grey stays bottom; the seed
is the grey↔green hinge. The veil-film reads as held-at-fullness,
never as obstruction.

# Parāprāveśikā par-p02 — ARGUMENT MAP (light is awareness-natured)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 par-p02 = mūla 1 with vṛtti (nanu; parimita/aparimita; sun analogy). Lore: Lakshmanjoo's Hughes-ch.3 frame — entrance into supreme reality begins by refusing the bare-light Lord; the śāntabrahmavādin (calm-brahman speaker) is the Advaitin target, consciousness without throb.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

What the Lord is: bare shining, or shining that knows its shining. An objector offers the calm-brahman alternative; the text must show why light without awareness collapses into inertness.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — the thesis (mūla):**
> iha khalu parameśvaraḥ prakāśātmā, prakāśaśca vimarśasvabhāvaḥ

[i.e. the supreme Lord is light-selfed; and light is awareness-natured.]

**Objection (nanu):**
> parameśvaraḥ prakāśasvabhāvatve'pi kiṃ śāntabrahmavādināmiva kevalabodhasvabhāvaḥ syāt

[i.e. granted the Lord is light-natured — why should He not be mere-knowing-natured, like the calm-brahman speakers hold?]

**Reply — vimarśa specified (vṛtti):**
> vimarśo hi parimitāparimitatvābhyāṃ dvidhā

[i.e. awareness is twofold: limited and unlimited.]

> sa ca vimarśo viśvākāreṇa sṛṣṭau, tatprakāśena sthitau, svātmasātkārātmanā saṃharaṇena saṃhāre ca pūrṇāhantācamatkārātmakaḥ

[i.e. in emission it is world-formed, in maintenance world-shining, in withdrawal self-seized — throughout, full-I-joy-formed.]

**The root maxim (mūla):**
> vimarśo nāma viśvākāreṇa viśvaprakāśena viśvasaṃharaṇena ca akṛtrimāham iti visphuraṇam

[i.e. awareness means: flashing as the unmade I — through world-forming, world-shining, world-withdrawing.]

Akṛtrima (unmade, not constructed) does the anti-Buddhist work: the I is not a construction but the flash itself.

**The consequence (mūla + sun analogy):**
> yadi nirvimarśaḥ syāt anīśvaro jaḍaśca prasajyeta

[i.e. were He awareness-less, lordlessness and inertness would follow.]

> vimarśavikalaḥ sūryādiprakāśo yathā parāpekṣatvāt jaḍapakṣapātī asvātantryamāśritavān

[i.e. like sun-light, lacking awareness, falls to the inert side through other-dependence and resorts to unfreedom.]

**Reply (naca, implied):** therefore awareness-less shining in consciousness would stick the same way (prasaktir āpated) — the sun proves the principle, consciousness inherits it.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Parimita/aparimita (limited/unlimited awareness) is introduced to block one misreading (void-knower's limited light capping consciousness) and left undeveloped here.
2. The sun analogy's scope: stated for external light, extended to consciousness by parity of reason — marked as analogy, not identity.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry thesis-objection-specification-maxim-consequence: light-selfed Lord, calm-brahman alternative refused, awareness twofold and three-functioned, the unmade-I flash, and the inertness that follows its absence. L2 must keep akṛtrima (unmade) — it carries the whole anti-constructivism. Carry vimarśa into passage 3, where its ten scriptural names are trumpeted.

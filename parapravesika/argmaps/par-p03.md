# Parāprāveśikā par-p03 — ARGUMENT MAP (the ten names)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 par-p03 = mūla 2 with the ten-name vṛtti. Lore: the Śaiva canon speaks vimarśa in many tongues — the vṛtti's own claim (bhinnabhinnābhir āgamabhāṣābhiḥ) is the lore: one referent, ten scriptural dialects; Pradīpaka's scale (Parā unity, Parāparā mixture, Aparā diversity) sits behind parā/avara usages.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Whether vimarśa is one author's coinage or the canon's constant theme under many names. Ten names are listed; each must earn its keep with a definition.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — the list (mūla):**
> eṣa eva ca vimarśaḥ — cit, caitanyaṃ, svarasoditāparāvāk, svātantryaṃ, paramātmano mukhyamaiśvaryaṃ, kartṛtvaṃ, sphurattā, sāro, hṛdayaṃ, spandaḥ ityādiśabdairāgameṣūddhoṣyate

[i.e. this very awareness is trumpeted in the āgamas by the words consciousness, consciousness-in-freedom, self-rasa-risen supreme speech, freedom, the supreme self's chief lordship, doership, throbbing, essence, heart, vibration, and so on.]

**The canon claim (vṛtti):**
> iha śaivaśāsananaye saṃvitprakāśasya vimarśaprādhānyameva tattadāgameṣu samuddhuṣṭaṃ

[i.e. in the Śaiva teaching, the primacy of awareness in consciousness-light is trumpeted in each āgama.]

The definitions (each name justified in turn): caitanya — awareness as the self's chief form, named by its quality-word; svarasoditāparāvāk — speech because it speaks/declares the world by re-grasping, supreme (parā) as ground-and-fullness of all further grasps, self-rasa-risen (svarasoditā) as ever-risen I in full consciousness-body with unblocked spread; svātantrya — it joins the outward-cast this-state to the self, disjoins it, lowers it through void-etc. stages, and by these marks differs from the inert; aiśvarya — needing no other at world-emission's start, unlike Brahmā-etc., māyā-womb officers bound by another's will and fate; kartṛtva — the unsurpassed cause-effect act of raising śiva-to-earth world into this-ness, yogin-creation-like, abiding in inward-shining oneness; sphurattā — the throbbing-relation, mine-flashing-entered ("for me it flashes"), without which all would flash for all always (universal omniscience or its reverse); sāra — the non-trifling form, the Lord's awareness-power itself; hṛdaya — the world's rest-ground; spanda — the unmoving consciousness-light's moving-shining, world-shining as excess where there is none.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Ten names with ten definitions; the list is open (ityādi) — no completeness claimed.
2. The sarvasarvajñatā reductio (if throbbing were not mine-entered, all would be omniscient) is the passage's one formal argument — kept as stated.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry list-then-justification: ten scriptural names, each defined by its work — speech speaks, freedom joins and disjoins, lordship needs no other, doership raises the world, throbbing is mine-entered, essence/heart/vibration ground and pulse it. L2 must keep mama sphurati (for me it flashes) — mineness is the anti-universal-flash. Carry doership into passage 4, where it becomes world-shining.

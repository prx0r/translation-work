# Parāprāveśikā par-p04 — ARGUMENT MAP (He shines as the world)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 par-p04 = mūla 3 with vṛtti (kāryatva nanu; abheda; tirodhāna nanu). Lore: the treatise's Russian dress — Erchenkov 2012 appends it to the Śivasūtravimarśinī translation, reading it as the Śivasūtra's gnostic illustration; the passage's two staged objections (nanu... āśaṅkyāha) are the dialectical spine.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Granted awareness, what follows: does the Lord shine as the world, is the world His doing and non-inert, and can anything veil Him? Two objections press — on effecthood and on veiling.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — the shining (mūla):**
> ata eva akṛtrimāhamiti satattvaḥ svayaṃprakāśarūpaḥ parameśvaraḥ pārameśvaryā śaktyā śivādi-dharaṇyantajagadātmanā sphurati prakāśate ca

[i.e. therefore — the unmade-I, possessed of reality, self-shining-formed — the supreme Lord throbs and shines, by the supreme Lordess's power, in world-form from śiva to earth.]

**Step 1 — doership and non-inertness (mūla):**
> etadeva asya jagataḥ kartṛtvamajaḍatvaṃ ca

[i.e. this itself is His doership of the world — and its non-inertness.]

World-shining proves both at once: His making and the world's aliveness (ajaḍabhāva).

**Objection 1 (nanu) — what sort of effecthood:**
> jagadviṣayaṃ kīdṛk kāryatvaṃ bhedenābhedena vā

[i.e. what kind of effecthood does the world have — by difference or by nondifference? Difference would break His freedom; nondifference — how then?]

**Reply (naca):**
> jagataḥ paraprakāśaṃ vinā acetyamānatvāt svātmalābho durghaṭaḥ... ityetadeva kāryatvaṃ

[i.e. without another's shining the world cannot even be noticed, let alone self-gained — reaching shining-bodiedness through His support just is effecthood.]

> māyāsvātantryeṇa bhedodbhāsanādabhinnatve'pi bhinnavyavahāraḥ

[i.e. by māyā's freedom difference is displayed — nondifferent, yet handled as different.]

> kāryatvamapi etadadhīnaprakāśatvameva

[i.e. effecthood is just this: shining-dependent-on-Him.]

> evaṃbhūtaṃ jagat prakāśarūpāt karturmaheśvarādabhinnameva

[i.e. the world so formed is nondifferent from maker-Maheśvara, shining-formed.]

**The usage argument:**
> bhinnavedyatve aprakāśamānatvena prakāśanāyogāt na kiṃcitsyāt

[i.e. were the knowable different, unshining would make shining impossible — nothing would be at all; else world-dealings of taking and giving would perish.]

**Objection 2 (nanu) — does nondifference veil Him:**
> yadi jagat parameśvarādabhinnaṃ... sārūpyabhedo durvāraḥ... tenāpi tasya tirodhīyamānatvaṃ bhavet

[i.e. if the world is nondifferent from the Lord, a likeness-difference in the shining-formed world is unavoidable — and He would be veiled by it.]

**Reply (naca):**
> pratyuta svātmani labdhe'pi prakāśāvaraṇāt svayameva naṣṭaṃ bhaved

[i.e. rather the veil, gained even in the self, would destroy itself by veiling shining.]

> anena ca jagatā asya bhagavataḥ prakāśātmakaṃ rūpaṃ na kadācit tirodhīyate

[i.e. by this world the Lord's shining-formed nature is never veiled.]

> etatprakāśanena pratiṣṭhāṃ labdhvā prakāśamānamidaṃ jagat ātmanaḥ prāṇabhūtaṃ kathaṃ niroddhuṃ śaknuyāt

[i.e. the world, gaining rest by His shining, become His life-breath — how could it block Him, and blocking Him, itself stand?

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Kāryatva redefined as dependent-shining: the passage replaces causation-talk with shining-talk — noted, not imported beyond the text.
2. The veil's self-destruction is asserted as self-evident (pratyuta); no further ground given.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry shining → doership-and-aliveness → effecthood-as-dependence → nondifference → never-veiled: two objections staged and answered in the Lord's favor. L2 must keep etadadhīnaprakāśatva (shining-dependent-on-Him) as the whole theory of effecthood. Carry self-shining into passage 5, where proof itself is refused work.

# Parāprāveśikā par-p05 — ARGUMENT MAP (no proof for the self-shining)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 par-p05 = mūla 4 with vṛtti (pramāṇa section; taduktam verse). Lore: the self-luminosity (svaprakāśatva) proof-structure — the knower too shares the Lord's form (tādrūpyeṇa sattvāt), so proof cannot ground Him; proof's office is the fresh flash resting in the knower.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

If the Lord is self-shining, what work is left for proof (pramāṇa) — and by what is He Himself proved? The text must retire proof without retiring reasoning.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — the already-proved (vṛtti):**
> jñāpakādikārakāṇi pūrvasiddhe īśvare vyāparītuṃ na śaknuvanti

[i.e. indicators and causes cannot operate on the already-proved Lord.]

> tadvidhyādikartuḥ pramāturapi tādrūpyeṇa sattvāt na tatsiddhiḥ pramāṇādhīnā

[i.e. since even the injunction-making knower exists in His likeness-form, his proving does not depend on proof.]

> ityataḥ svaprakāśatvaṃ tasyānubhavasiddham

[i.e. hence His self-shiningness is experience-proved.]

**Step 1 — the two questions (mūla):**
> tatsadbhāve kiṃ pramāṇam? iti vastusadbhāvamanumanyatāṃ, tādṛksvabhāve kiṃ pramāṇam? iti praṣṭṛrūpatayā ca pūrvasiddhasya maheśvarasya svayaṃprakāśatvaṃ sarvasya svasaṃvedanasiddham

[i.e. "what proof of His being?" — assent to the thing's being; "what proof of such-natured?" — and as the asker's own form: the already-proved Maheśvara's self-shining is proved by everyone's self-feeling.]

The asker refutes himself: asking presupposes the self-feeling that proves.

**Step 2 — proof's remaining office (vṛtti):**
> saṃvitprakāśa eva sarvajīvitabhūto māyīyavyavahāre pramāṇaṃ kalpayati... tatra pramāṇaṃ kimapi nopayujyate

[i.e. consciousness-light, the life of all, ordains proof in māyā-dealings — there no proof is of any use (for Him).]

> paraprakāśāpekṣayānyatsarvaṃ jaḍatayā svātmānamapi bhāsayitumaśaktaṃ

[i.e. all else, inert, cannot shine even itself without another's shining — how would it establish another?]

> pramāṇamapi yamāśritya pramāṇaṃ bhavati... pramāṇajīvitabhūte parameśvare [na upayogaḥ]

[i.e. proof becomes proof by resorting to Him; for the Lord who is proof's life, the proof-wretch (pramāṇavarāka) proving fresh shinings has no use — fresh flashes (abhinavābhāsa) cannot rest (viśrāmyatu) anywhere but in the ever-shining knower, so proof serves only body-life-blue-pleasure knowables.]

**The seal (taduktam):**
> pramāṇānyapi vastūnāṃ jīvitaṃ yāni tanvate | teṣāmapi paro jīvaḥ sa eva parameśvaraḥ

[i.e. the proofs that stretch out as things' very life — beyond them too is the Life, and He is the supreme Lord.]

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Svasaṃvedana (self-feeling) is taken as proving without argument; the text treats asking as conceding.
2. Pramāṇavarāka (proof-wretch) is polemic color kept as stated, not sharpened.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry already-proved → self-answering questions → proof's demotion to māyā-dealings → the Life beyond proofs. L2 must keep the asker's self-refutation — the question proves what it asks. Carry word-mass into passage 6, where I-ness becomes the thirty-six.

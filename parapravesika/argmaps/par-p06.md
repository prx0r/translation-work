# Parāprāveśikā par-p06 — ARGUMENT MAP (word-mass I-ness; the thirty-six)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 par-p06 = mūla 5 with vṛtti (śabdarāśi; tattva definition) + the 36-name list. Lore: Pradīpaka's pointer — the Ṣaṭtriṃśattattvasandoha (Amṛtānandanātha, with Rājānaka Ānanda) describes the tattvas in full detail; this passage is the short road into that country, and the Tattvic Chart the map.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

How the supreme Śiva — pure I-grasping — is at once the thirty-sixfold expanse. A definition of tattva is needed first, then the list.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — the ground (mūla):**
> evaṃ ca śabdarāśimayapūrṇāhantāparāmarśasāratvāt paramaśiva eva ṣaṭtriṃśattattvātmakaḥ prapañcaḥ

[i.e. and so — because His essence is the full-I-grasping made of word-mass — supreme Śiva Himself is the thirty-six-tattva-formed expanse.]

> śabdarāśiḥ akārādi hakārānto varṇasamūho garbhīkṛtasamastaviśvastanmayo... ahameva prakāśātmā prakāśe ityevaṃrūpaḥ pūrṇāhantāparāmarśaḥ

[i.e. word-mass is the letter-heap from a to ha, enwombing the whole world — the full-I-grasping of the form "I alone am light-selfed in shining," freedom resting with no other face to watch.]

**Step 1 — what a tattva is (vṛtti):**
> tasya bhāvastattvam iti bhinnānāṃ vargāṇāṃ vargīkaraṇanimittaṃ yadekamavibhaktaṃ bhāti tattattvam

[i.e. tattva is the one undivided shining that causes divided classes to be classed — as mountain-tree-town show earth-form, rivers-lakes-oceans water-form.]

**Step 2 — the list (mūla):** śiva, śakti, sadāśiva, īśvara, śuddhavidyā, māyā, kalā, vidyā, rāga, kāla, niyati, puruṣa, prakṛti, buddhi, ahaṃkāra, manas, five knowing-senses, five doing-senses, five subtles, five gross elements (ākāśa to bhūmi).

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The list is given bare here; each mark (lakṣaṇa) follows in passages 7–8 — the passage promises, the next ones pay.
2. Śabdarāśi (word-mass a-to-ha) assumes the mātṛkā doctrine without exposition.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry ground-definition-list: I-grasping made of alphabet-mass, tattva as the undivided that classes the divided, then the thirty-six names in order. L2 must keep the mountain/earth, river/water examples — they are the whole definition made visible. Carry the bare list into passages 7–8, where each level gets its mark.

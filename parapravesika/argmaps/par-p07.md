# Parāprāveśikā par-p07 — ARGUMENT MAP (marks; person, bondsman, freed)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 par-p07 = lakṣaṇas śiva→māyā, puruṣa/saṃsārī/mukta, kañcuka origin. Lore: the descent ladder — I-portion vs this-portion dimming and brightening across sadāśiva→īśvara→vidyā (Pradīpaka's Tattvic Chart the visual); the magician (aindrajālika) and the robbed king (rājñāpahṛtasarvasva) are the vṛtti's own analogies, kept.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

How the pure levels dim into personhood, how the person binds and unbinds, and why contraction wears the five sheaths.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Śiva-tattva:**
> śivatattvaṃ nāma icchājñānakriyātmakakevalapūrṇānandasvabhāvarūpaḥ paramaśiva eva

[i.e. Śiva-tattva is supreme Śiva Himself: will-knowledge-action-formed, pure full-bliss-natured — within all knowers throbbing as full-I-joy, beyond all tattvas, bearing hundred-thousand emission-dissolution reflections.]

**The self-willing to emit:**
> asya jagat sraṣṭumicchāṃ parigṛhītavataḥ parameśvarasya prathamaspanda evecchāśaktitattvam

[i.e. of the Lord who has taken the will to emit the world, the first throb itself is will-power-tattva — by own-freedom's greatness, outward-bloom-wishing, bliss-joy surplus: "I" grasped.]

**Sadāśiva (will-chief):**
> sadevāṅkurāyamāṇamidaṃ jagat svātmanāhantayācchādya sthitaṃ rūpaṃ sadāśivatattvam

[i.e. the just-sprouting world, covered by self-I-ness — when the Lord blooms the this-portion faintly (asphuṭa) against pure-consciousness-ground, will-chief.]

**Īśvara (knowledge-chief):**
> aṅkuritaṃ jagadahantayāvṛtya sthitamīśvaratattvam... idamaṃśe yadāhamaṃśaṃ niṣiñcati tadā jñānaśaktipradhānamīśvaratattvam

[i.e. the sprouted world covered by I-ness; when the I-portion is sprinkled over the clarified this-portion, knowledge-power-chief — differing only by this-portion's dim vs undim.]

**Śuddhavidyā (action-chief):**
> ahantedantayoraikyapratipattiḥ śuddhavidyā... yadā prarūḍhabheda... ahamidamiti parāmarśaḥ tat kriyāśaktipradhānaṃ vidyātattvam

[i.e. I-this oneness-grasped — when amid grown difference the I-portion shines through consciousness-mere, the balanced-scales "I-this" grasping, action-power-chief.]

**Māyā:**
> svasvarūpeṣu bhāveṣu bhedaprathā māyā... duṣkaratamavastusaṃpādanāpratīghātarūpā... cidekamayānapi bhāvān bhedena ābhāsayantī svarūpaṃ tirodadhatī kalādikṣityantānāṃ bhāvānāmupādānakāraṇaṃ ca māyā

[i.e. difference-spreading among own-natured things — achieving the hardest, making knower-pride grow in the unconscious and void, showing even consciousness-one things as divided, veiling own-form — and material cause of art-to-earth things.]

**Puruṣa and the bondsman:**
> yadā tu parameśvaraḥ pārameśvaryā māyāśaktyā (svātantryāparaparyāyayā) svarūpaṃ gṛhayitvā saṃkucitagrāhakatāmaśnute tadā puruṣasaṃjñaḥ

[i.e. when the Lord, by the Lordess's māyā-power — freedom itself under another name — veils own-form and takes contracted-knowerness, He is called person.]

> ayameva māyāmohitaḥ karmabandhanaḥ (karmāṇi svātmano bandhakāni abhimanyamānaḥ) saṃsārī (saṃsarati ityataḥ)

[i.e. He Himself, māyā-deluded, deed-bound — deeming deeds His binders — is the wanderer, wandering (even the body wandering through childhood-youth, intellect across births).

**The magician's immunity:**
> parameśvarādabhinno'pi asya mohaḥ parameśvarasya na bhavet — indrajālamiva aindrajālikasya svecchayā saṃpāditabhrānteḥ

[i.e. though nondifferent from the Lord, His delusion is not the Lord's — like the magician's self-willed effected-illusion.]

**The freed (ciddhana):**
> vidyayā svarūpaprakāśanaśaktyā abhijñāpadaṃ prāpitamaiśvaryaṃ yasya... ciddhano muktaḥ (punarjanmabandhavirahāt dehe sthite'pi mukta iti vyapadeśayogya... patite tu paramaśivasvarūpa eva) paramaśiva eva

[i.e. whose lordship is brought to recognition by knowledge's form-showing power — deeming even body-world pure feeling unmixed with unconscious — consciousness-dense, freed: fit to be called freed while bodied (no more birth-bond), at body's fall supreme Śiva-nature itself — supreme Śiva Himself.]

**The five glories and the sheaths:**
> asya sarvakartṛtvaṃ sarvajñatvaṃ pūrṇatvaṃ nityatvaṃ vyāpakatvaṃ ca, śaktayo'saṃkucitā api saṃkocagrahaṇena kalāvidyārāgakālaniyatirūpatayā bhavanti

[i.e. His all-doership, all-knowingness, fullness, eternity, pervasion — powers uncontracted, taking contraction as art-knowledge-passion-time-necessity.]

> yathā rājñāpahṛtasarvasvasya anukampayā jīvanārthaṃ kiṃcit stokaṃ dhanaṃ dīyate tathaiva aṇutvamāpannasya bodhasya apahṛtasarvajñatvādeḥ tatsthāne kiṃcitkartṛtādiparamārthaṃ kalādikaṃ vitīryate

[i.e. as the king robs all wealth then deals a little back for living — so wakefulness, atomized, robbed of omniscience, is dealt art-etc. in its place, for little-doership's sake.]

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. Dhyāmala/adhyāmala (dim/undim this-portion) grades sadāśiva vs īśvara by faintness — a technical distinction kept as stated.
2. Dehe sthite'pi muktaḥ (freed while bodied) vs patite paramaśiva (at fall, Śiva) — two-phase release, both kept.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry dimming-ladder → veiling → person → bondsman → magician-immunity → recognition-freed → robbed-king sheaths: the whole fall and return in one movement. L2 must keep both analogies (magician, robbed king) — they carry the metaphysics. Carry the sheaths into passage 8, where each is defined.

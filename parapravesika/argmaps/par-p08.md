# Parāprāveśikā par-p08 — ARGUMENT MAP (sheaths, organ, senses, elements)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 par-p08 = kañcuka five, prakṛti, antahkaraṇa, indriyas, tanmātras, bhūtas. Lore: the Sāṅkhya twenty-three below prakṛti — the passage is Kṣemarāja's fastest Sāṅkhya, each stop defined by its deprivation or function; time's definition (ājñāsiṣaṃ jānāmi jñāsyāmi) is the sharpest: sequence cutting knowledge-action into was-am-will.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

What each sheath takes away (or gives back in little), and how nature unfolds into organ, sense, subtle, and gross.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Kalā (little-doership):**
> yayā sarvaṃ kartuṃmasamarthaḥ kiṃcideva ghaṭādikaṃ karoti

[i.e. whereby, unable to do all, he does some pot-etc.]

**Vidyā (little-knowing):**
> yayā kiṃcideva purovarti ghaṭādikaṃ jānāti na punardūravyavahitaṃ vastu

[i.e. whereby he knows some forth-standing pot-etc., not the far-removed thing.]

**Rāga (clinging):**
> apūrṇamanyatayā idaṃ mama upayujyate, bhūyāsaṃ, mā kadācit na bhūvam ityevaṃrūpaḥ viṣayeṣvabhiṣvaṅgaḥ

[i.e. "incomplete, as other, this serves me; may I be more; may I never not be" — clinging to objects.]

**Kāla (time):**
> bhāvānāṃ bhāsanābhāsanātmakānāṃ kramo'vacchedakaḥ... ājñāsiṣaṃ jānāmi jñāsyāmi iti, akārṣaṃ karomi kariṣyāmi iti jñānakriyāsvarūpeṇa bhāvānapi tathā kalayan avacchinatti

[i.e. the divider of the sequence of shining-unshining things — "I knew, know, will know; I did, do, will do" — measuring things too in knowledge-action form, it cuts them off: past-etc.]

**Niyati (necessity):**
> mamedaṃ kartavyaṃ nedaṃ kartavyam iti niyamanahetuḥ... niyatādeva kāraṇānniyatameva kāryamityādi bahuvidhaṃ niyatirūpam

[i.e. "mine this to be done, not this to be done" — the restraining cause; manifold necessity-forms, fixed-effect-from-fixed-cause among them.]

**Kañcuka (sheath):**
> etat pañcakam asya svarūpāvarakatvāt kañcukamiti... svarūpādvyatiriktamapi avyatiriktatayā pūrṇasaṃvitsvarūpamācchādya sthitam

[i.e. the fivefold, veiling own-form: a sheath — distinct from own-form yet, as undistinct, standing veiled over full-consciousness-nature.]

**Prakṛti (root cause of great-to-earth):**
> mahādipṛthivyantānāṃ tattvānāṃ mūlakāraṇaṃ prakṛtiḥ... sattvarajastamasāṃ sukhaduḥkhamohātmakānāṃ prakāśakriyāniyamanasvabhāvānāṃ sāmyāvasthā

[i.e. nature: the balance-state of good-passion-dark — pleasure-pain-delusion-formed, shining-doing-restraining-natured — undivided, where limb-limb-relation is not found.]

**Inner organ:** buddhi (deciding — "this is thus"; thought-mirror-bearing, clear by goodness-chiefness, fit for reflection-grasping); ahaṃkāra (the "mine-this, not-mine-this" pride-means); manas (resolve-means) — the trio, grasp-means of inner pleasure-etc., effect of guṇas, cause of the sense-gross heap.

**Senses:** five knowing (ear skin eye tongue nose for sound-touch-form-taste-smell in order); five doing (speech hand foot excretion generation for speaking-taking-moving-excreting-bliss in order).

**Subtles:** śabda sparśa rūpa rasa gandhāḥ, common-formed — "that itself, nothing more there" (tanmātra), free of in-between distinction.

**Gross:** ether space-giving, air enlivening, fire burning-cooking, water refreshing-liquid, earth supporting.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The five as one sheath (etat pañcakam kañcukam): five names, one veil-office — kept as stated.
2. Tanmātra defined negatively (tadeva nādhikam) — common-form without in-between distinction; no positive content added.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry deprivation-then-unfolding: each sheath a little-in-place-of-all, the sheath one veil, nature's balance, deciding-ego-resolving trio, ten senses in order, five commons, five element-functions. L2 must keep the "I knew/know/will know" and the robbed-king logic echoing from passage 7. Carry the gross world into passage 9, where it all fits inside the seed.

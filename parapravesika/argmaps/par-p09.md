# Parāprāveśikā par-p09 — ARGUMENT MAP (the heart-seed)

*2026-11-01. Source: T1 par-p09 = bīja doctrine with āmnāya verse, pot/ghee analogies, phonemic involution, phala, colophon. Lore: Hareesh's course centers this very verse (banyan-seed/heart-seed) as the text's signature; the phonemic involution (sa → 31 → au → anuttara) is the Mālinīvijayottara-adjacent move Erchenkov's appendix supplies.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Where the thirty-six stand: inside what seed, by what analogies, through what involution — and what befalls the knower.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**The two similes (vṛtti):**
> yathā vaṭabīje tatsamucitenaiva vapuṣā aṅkurapatraphalāni tiṣṭhanti evaṃ nānāvidhatanukaraṇādivaicitryaśāli viśvamidaṃ hṛdayabījāntaḥsthitam

[i.e. as sprout-leaf-fruits stand in the banyan seed in fitting body, so this manifold-body-making-varied world stands inside the heart-seed.]

> mayūrāṇḍarase sarvaśikhyavayavānupraviṣṭavarhopavarhādipūrṇarekhādivaicitryaśilpakalpanākauśalavat

[i.e. like the peacock-egg-juice craft-skill holding all plume-etc. line-variety — for the attentive-rich, weighable by subtle sight.]

**The tradition-verse (āmnāya):**
> yathā nyagrodhabījasthaḥ śaktirūpo mahādrumaḥ | tathā hṛdayabījasthaṃ viśvametaccarācaram

[i.e. as the great tree stands power-formed in the banyan seed, so this moving-unmoving world stands in the heart-seed.]

> ityāmnāyanītyā parābhaṭṭārikārūpe hṛdayabīje'ntarbhūtametajjagat

[i.e. by this tradition-way: in the heart-seed formed as supreme Lady, this world is contained.]

**The pot and ghee proofs:**
> yathā ghaṭaśarāvādīnāṃ mṛdvikārāṇāṃ pāramārthikaṃ rūpaṃ mṛdeva... tathā pṛthivyādimāyāntānāṃ tattvānāṃ satattvaṃ mīmāṃsyamānaṃ sadityeva bhavet

[i.e. as pot-bowl changes are ultimately clay itself, so earth-to-māyā tattvas judged in reality are being itself.]

**The phonemic involution:**
> asyāpi padasya nirūpyamāṇaṃ dhātvarthavyañjakaṃ pratyayāṃśaṃ visṛjya prakṛtimātrarūpaḥ sakāra evāvaśiṣyate, tadantargatamekatriṃśattattvam, tataḥ paraṃ śuddhavidyeśvarasadāśivatattvāni jñānakriyāsārāṇi śaktiviśeṣatvāt aukāre'bhyupagamarūpe'nuttaraśaktimaye'ntarbhūtāni

[i.e. of the word "sat" too: discarding the root-sense-showing suffix-part, mere-nature-formed sa-letter alone remains — inside it the thirty-one (earth-to-māyā); beyond, pure-knowledge-Lord-eternal-Śiva, knowledge-action-essenced, as power-distinctions enter au-syllable, assent-formed, unsurpassed-power-formed.]

> ataḥ paramūrdhvādhaḥ sṛṣṭirūpo visarjanīyaḥ

[i.e. hence the supreme emission, up-down-creation-formed: the visarga to be emitted.]

**The heart-seed's double form:**
> evaṃbhūtasya hṛdayabījasya mahāmantrātmako viśvamayo viśvottīrṇaḥ paramaśiva eva udayaviśrāntisthānatvānnijasvabhāvaḥ

[i.e. of the heart-seed so formed — great-mantra-formed, world-formed, world-transcended — supreme Śiva Himself, as arising-resting-place, is own-nature: all-formedness and formlessness in order the cause.]

**The fruit:**
> īdṛśaṃ hṛdayabījaṃ tattvato yo veda samāviśati ca sa paramārthato dīkṣitaḥ (tilājyāhutivarjitametatparijñānameva tattvato dīkṣā)

[i.e. who knows and enters such heart-seed in reality is truly initiated — this knowing itself, void of sesame-ghee oblation, is initiation in truth.]

> prāṇān dhārayan laukikavadvartamāno jīvanmukta eva bhavati, dehapāte paramaśivabhaṭṭāraka eva bhavati

[i.e. holding lifebreaths, abiding worldly-like, he is freed-while-living; at body's fall, the Lord supreme Śiva Himself.]

> iti śrīmanmahāmāheśvarācāryavaryakṣemarājaviracitā parāprāveśikā samāptā

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The 31-in-sa / 3-plus-pure-levels-in-au partition assumes the phonemic tattva-mapping without tabulating it — kept as stated.
2. The visarga (paramūrdhvādhaḥ sṛṣṭirūpa) closes the emission arc opened in passage 2 — noted as closure, not expanded.

## 4. Decision for L2

Carry seed-similes → tradition-verse → clay/ghee proofs → letter-involution → double-formed seed → initiation-without-oblation → living-freed → Śiva-at-death: the whole work landing in one knower. L2 must keep tilājyāhuti-varjita (void of sesame-ghee oblation) — initiation without rite is the punch. This closes the arc opened in passage 1's vow.

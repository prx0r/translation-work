# C1 — par-p01 (maṅgala): the vow

## SUMMARY

Homage to Śiva-as-the-self-of-all-levels, then the bow to consciousness as the Lord's heart: world-shaped yet world-surpassing, pulsating in supreme-power shape.

## KEY TERMS

* **nikhilatattvātman** — self-of-all-levels (vow scope).
* **viśvātmikā/taduttīrṇā** — world-shaped/world-surpassing.
* **hṛdaya/saṃvid** — heart/consciousness.
* **parādiśakti/sphurantī** — supreme-etc.-power/pulsing.

## rasa

Opening devotion (bhakti): paradox embraced before exposition.

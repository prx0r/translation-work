# C1 — par-p02 (light is awareness-natured)

## SUMMARY

The Lord's light-nature entails awareness-nature; the calm-brahman bare-knowing alternative is staged and set aside. Awareness runs bounded and unbounded, operates through the three cosmic functions as unconstructed-I flashing, and its absence entails lordlessness plus deadness, sun-evidenced.

## KEY TERMS

* **prakāśa/vimarśa** — light/awareness (thesis pair).
* **akṛtrima-aham** — unmade-I flash (maxim).
* **sūryādi** — sun-seal (consequence).

## rasa

Reasoning-press (vitarka): objection staged, answered, sealed by the sun.

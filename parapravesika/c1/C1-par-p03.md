# C1 — par-p03 (ten names, one throb)

## SUMMARY

The canon trumpets a single awareness-primacy across scriptures; ten names each denote by office — chief-form, speech, ground-fullness, join-loose-lower, no-other-needed, yogin-like raising, mine-flashing, untrifling, rest-ground, still-shimmer.

## KEY TERMS

uddhoṣyate/samuddhuṣṭa (trumpeted) · dharmavācī (quality-word) · svarasoditā (self-sap-risen) · ananyāpekṣitva (no-other-neededness) · yoginirmāṇavat (yogin-making-like) · madīya (mine)

## rasa

Wonder (adbhuta): ten dialects, one referent, each earning its note.

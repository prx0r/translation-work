# C1 — par-p04 (world-shining, never veiled)

## SUMMARY

Self-lit Lord shines by the Lady as śiva-to-earth world; that shining constitutes both making and aliveness. Two dilemmas resolve it: effecthood means shining-dependence (difference displayed, never real), and likeness cannot veil because veiling light self-destructs — the world being His life-breath cannot throttle its breather.

## KEY TERMS

sphurati/prakāśate (pulse/shine) · ajaḍabhāva (aliveness) · etadadhīnaprakāśatva (this-dependent-shining) · bhinnavyavahāra (handled-as-different) · prāṇabhūta (life-breath-become)

## rasa

Triumph (vīra): dilemmas dissolved, never veiled, life-breath close.

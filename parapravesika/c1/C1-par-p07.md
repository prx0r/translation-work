# C1 — par-p07 (dimming ladder, mask, recognition)

## SUMMARY

Pure bliss-nature throbbing with hundred-thousand reflections wills emission; will-first, knowing-first, doing-first dim by this-portion faintness; illusion spreads difference, stuffs art-to-earth, and veils by freedom's synonym into personhood — deemed-bound, wandering. The mask never touches the wearer (magician). Recognition restores sovereignty: dense-conscious, free-bodied, Śiva at fall. Five glories, uncontracted, accept contraction into five littles on robbed-king terms.

## KEY TERMS

dhyāmala/adhyāmala (dim/undim) · māyāśakti as svātantrya-synonym · aindrajālika (magician) · vidyābhijñāpitaiśvarya (recognition-brought sovereignty) · ciddhana (consciousness-dense) · rājanyāya (king-logic)

## rasa

Exultation (utsāha): the whole fall traversed — mask, immunity, recognition, dealt powers.

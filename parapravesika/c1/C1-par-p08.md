# C1 — par-p08 (littles, cover, unfolding)

## SUMMARY

Doing-a-little, knowing-a-little, clinging in may-I-grow-never-cease shape, sequence-cutting was-am-will, must in mine-to-do shape — five functions, one veil over full consciousness, distinct-yet-undistinct. Nature poises the three strands past telling apart. Deciding, prideing, resolving organ; ten senses ordered; five commons defined by having-nothing-more; five element-offices: room, life, burn-cook, refresh-flow, uphold.

## KEY TERMS

kiṃcitkartṛtva/kiṃcijjñatva (little-makings/knowings) · kramāvacchedaka (sequence-cutter) · kañcuka (cover) · sāmyāvasthā (poise-state) · tanmātra as tadeva-nādhikam (that-nothing-more)

## rasa

Wonder (adbhuta): littles by lack, elements by office.

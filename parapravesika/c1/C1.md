# C1 — Parāprāveśikā (argument)

## par-p01 — the vow

**SUMMARY.** Homage to Śiva-as-the-self-of-all-levels, then the bow to consciousness as the Lord's heart: world-shaped yet world-surpassing, pulsating in supreme-power shape.

**KEY TERMS.** nikhilatattvātman (self-of-all-levels), viśvātmikā/taduttīrṇā (world-shaped/world-surpassing), hṛdaya (heart), parādiśakti (supreme-etc.-power), sphurantī (pulsating), saṃvid (consciousness).

**EXPLANATION.** The invocation binds deity to doctrine in one compound (nikhilatattvātmane): nothing expounded will fall outside Him. The maṅgala predicates contraries together — viśvātmikāṃ taduttīrṇāṃ — refusing to sequence immanence before transcendence. Sphurantī anticipates vimarśa; parādi anticipates its scale.

## par-p02 — light is awareness by nature

**SUMMARY.** The Lord's light-nature entails awareness-nature; the calm-brahman bare-knowing alternative is staged and set aside. Awareness runs bounded and unbounded, operates through the three cosmic functions as unconstructed-I flashing, and its absence entails lordlessness plus deadness, sun-evidenced.

**KEY TERMS.** prakāśātman/vimarśasvabhāva (light-selfed/awareness-natured), śāntabrahmavādin (calm-brahman teacher), parimita/aparimita (bounded/unbounded), akṛtrima-aham (unconstructed I), anīśvara/jaḍa (lordless/inert).

**EXPLANATION.** Prakāśaśca vimarśasvabhāvaḥ converts the thesis before the nanu lands. The vṛtti's dvidhā blocks the void-knower's ceiling objection in advance. Akṛtrimāham iti visphuraṇam carries the anti-constructivism in three instrumental complements (viśvākāreṇa, viśvaprakāśena, viśvasaṃharaṇena). Yadi nirvimarśaḥ syāt draws the double consequence, and the sūryādi parallel extends external-light logic to consciousness by parity.

## par-p03 — one referent, ten dialects

**SUMMARY.** The canon trumpets a single awareness-primacy across scriptures; ten names each denote by office — chief-form, speech, ground-fullness, join-loose-lower, no-other-needed, yogin-like raising, mine-flashing, untrifling, rest-ground, still-shimmer.

**KEY TERMS.** uddhoṣyate/samuddhuṣṭa (trumpeted), dharmavācī (quality-word), svarasoditā (self-sap-risen), ananyāpekṣitva (no-other-neededness), yoginirmāṇavat (yogin-making-like), madīya (mine).

**EXPLANATION.** Śaivaśāsananaye vimarśaprādhānyameva states the canon thesis; bhinnabhinnābhir āgamabhāṣābhiḥ explains the plurality. Each gloss ties name to function, with two formal arguments embedded: kartṛtva's alaukika cause-effect, and the sarvasarvajñatā reductio securing sphurattā's mineness.

## par-p04 — shining as making, dependence as effecthood, world as non-veil

**SUMMARY.** Self-lit Lord shines by the Lady as śiva-to-earth world; that shining constitutes both making and aliveness. Two dilemmas resolve it: effecthood means shining-dependence (difference displayed, never real), and likeness cannot veil because veiling light self-destructs — the world being His life-breath cannot throttle its breather.

**KEY TERMS.** sphurati/prakāśate (pulse/shine), ajaḍabhāva (aliveness), etadadhīnaprakāśatva (Him-dependent-shining), bhinnavyavahāra (handled-as-different), prāṇabhūta (life-breath-become).

**EXPLANATION.** Ata eva marks consequence from passage 2's awareness. Nanul stages bheda/abheda against svātantrya; the reply dissolves kāryatva into āśraya plus prakāśaśarīratā, with māyāsvātantrya covering the display. Bhinnavedyatve runs the usage-collapse reductio. Nanu2 stages sārūpya against tirodhāna; pratyuta reverses it, and na kadācit tirodhīyate with the prāṇa argument closes: blocker and blocked share one breath.

## par-p05 — the prover proved before proof

**SUMMARY.** Indicators cannot touch the pre-proved; the knower's likeness exempts his standing; the two proof-questions answer themselves through self-feeling. Proof is appointed for illusion's traffic only; all else proves nothing, leaning on everything. Proof leans on Him; He, proof's life, has no use for it beyond body-sense-pleasure knowables. The quoted verse enthrones the further Life.

**KEY TERMS.** pūrvasiddha (pre-proved), tādrūpya (likeness-form), svasaṃvedana (self-feeling), pramāṇavarāka (poor proof), abhinavābhāsa (fresh flash), viśrānti (coming-to-rest).

**EXPLANATION.** Jñāpakādi... na śaknuvanti with tādrūpyeṇa sattvāt removes both object and subject of proving. Tatsadbhāve kiṃ pramāṇam / tādṛksvabhāve kiṃ pramāṇam turn the questioner into evidence. Kimapi nopayujyate with the jaḍa-asymmetry (cannot light itself, would settle another?) demotes proof's jurisdiction. Kaścopayogaḥ with the ever-lit knower enjoying all findings inward leaves fresh flashes nowhere to rest — hence deha-prāṇa-nīla-sukha-only office. Taduktam crowns: teṣāmapi paro jīvaḥ.

## par-p06 — alphabet-ground, defined level, bare roll

**SUMMARY.** Full-I-grasp woven of a-to-ha sound-mass grounds identity: Śiva Himself the thirty-six-leveled display. Level means the undivided shine that families the divided, hill-earth and river-water exhibited. Then the roll, bare, one to thirty-six.

**KEY TERMS.** śabdarāśi (sound-mass), pūrṇāhantāparāmarśa (full-I-grasp), vargīkaraṇanimitta (family-making-cause), ṣaṭtriṃśat (thirty-six).

**EXPLANATION.** Śabdarāśimayapūrṇāhantāparāmarśasāratvāt makes phonemic fullness the essence-cause of tattva-plurality; ananyamukhaprekṣitva glosses freedom as facelessness. Yadekamavibhaktaṃ bhāti defines by the double example. The list defers its marks — a deliberate promissory structure honored in passages 7–8.

## par-p07 — fainting ladder, masked person, recognized freed, dealt-back powers

**SUMMARY.** Pure bliss-nature throbbing with hundred-thousand reflections wills emission; will-first, knowing-first, doing-first dim by this-portion faintness; illusion spreads difference, stuffs art-to-earth, and veils by freedom's synonym into personhood — deemed-bound, wandering. The mask never touches the wearer (magician). Recognition restores sovereignty: dense-conscious, free-bodied, Śiva at fall. Five glories, uncontracted, accept contraction into five littles on robbed-king terms.

**KEY TERMS.** dhyāmala/adhyāmala (dim/undim), māyāśakti as svātantrya-synonym, aindrajālika (magician), vidyābhijñāpitaiśvarya (recognition-brought sovereignty), ciddhana (consciousness-dense), rājanyāya (king-logic).

**EXPLANATION.** Prathamaspanda grounds śakti in will-to-emit with parānandacamatkārodreka as motive-affect. Sadāśiva→īśvara→vidyā grade by idamaṃśa asphuṭatva→sphuṭa→prarūḍhabheda with aham-constant — the ladder's single variable. Karmāṇi svātmano bandhakāni abhimanyamānaḥ locates bondage in deeming, enabling the indrajāla immunity. Mukta holds both phases (dehe sthite'pi, patite tu). Vitīryate with the king simile converts metaphysical contraction into compensated dealing.

## par-p08 — five littles, one cover, and the unfolding below

**SUMMARY.** Doing-a-little, knowing-a-little, clinging in may-I-grow-never-cease shape, sequence-cutting was-am-will, must in mine-to-do shape — five functions, one veil over full consciousness, distinct-yet-undistinct. Nature poises the three strands past telling apart. Deciding, prideing, resolving organ; ten senses ordered; five commons defined by having-nothing-more; five element-offices: room, life, burn-cook, refresh-flow, uphold.

**KEY TERMS.** kiṃcitkartṛtva/kiṃcijjñatva (little-makings/knowings), kramāvacchedaka (sequence-cutter), kañcuka (cover), sāmyāvasthā (poise-state), tanmātra as tadeva-nādhikam (that-nothing-more).

**EXPLANATION.** Each sheath's yayā-clause is a deprivation formula; etat pañcakam kañcukam unifies office over number. Avyatiriktatayā ācchādya states the veil's paradox precisely. Kāla's ājñāsiṣaṃ-trio exhibits cutting in the mouth's own tenses. Sāmyāvasthā with aṅgāṅgibhāva-denial roots Sāṅkhya below. Indriya and bhūta entries are pure office-definitions — grasp-means and element-works, nothing decorative.

## par-p09 — seed-contained world, involuted letters, oblationless rite

**SUMMARY.** Banyan and peacock-egg prove containment; the tradition-verse seats moving-unmoving world in the Lady-shaped heart-seed; clay and water-common prove being the reality of tattvas; being stripped to bare s holds thirty-one, pure levels entering assent-au; emission breathes out above-below; the seed, mantra-great, world-shaped, world-past, is Śiva as His own seat. Knowing-entering initiates truly — no oblation; breathing on, worldly, living-free; at fall, the Lord Himself.

**KEY TERMS.** hṛdayabīja (heart-seed), āmnāyanīti (tradition-way), sat→sa involution, aukāra-anuttaraśakti (assent-au unsurpassed-power), visarga emission, tilājyāhutivarjita (oblation-void), jīvanmukta/dehapāta (living-free/body-fall).

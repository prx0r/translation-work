# C1.v2 — Parāprāveśikā (condensation of the argument)

Bow to Śiva, the self of all levels, and to consciousness, the Lord's heart — shaped as the world, past the world, pulsing as supreme power. The Lord is light, and light is awareness: not bare knowing, but the flash of an I that was never put together, through shaping, shining, and dissolving. Deny it awareness, and lordship and life go with it, as sunlight without awareness shows. One awareness-primacy the scriptures trumpet in ten working names. That awareness shines, by the Lady, as the world from Śiva to clay — its shining His making, its aliveness. Made means shining by Him: handled as different, never different. And no likeness veils Him, for a veil on light unmakes itself, and His life-breath cannot throttle Him. Proof cannot touch the pre-proved — the questioner proves himself by feeling himself. Proof serves illusion's traffic, leaning on Him who is proof's life, and beyond the life of proofs stands the further Life. Sound-mass I-grasp is His essence, so He is the thirty-six: a level means the undivided that gathers the divided. The pure dims by faintness — will, then knowing, then doing — until illusion persons Him. Deemed bound, He wanders, yet never touched, like the magician; recognized, dense with consciousness, free in the body, Śiva at its fall. Five glories accept five littles, the way a king deals back a pittance. Little doing, little knowing, clinging, time that cuts was-am-will, must — one cover over full consciousness. Nature poised, organ deciding-ego-resolving, ten senses ordered, five commons, five offices. All inside the heart-seed — banyan, peacock-egg, clay, and water prove it. Being bared to s holds thirty-one; the pure levels enter assent-shaped au. The seed, mantra-great, world-shaped and world-past, is Śiva as His own seat. Know and enter it: this knowing needs no oblation, and it frees the living and makes the fallen Lord.

## par-p01

Vow to Śiva, the self of all levels, and to consciousness, the Lord's heart — world-shaped yet world-past, pulsing as supreme power.

## par-p02

The Lord is light, and light is awareness: not bare knowing but the unmade-I flash, three-functioned — and sunlight proves what its absence costs.

## par-p03

Ten scriptural names for one awareness-primacy, each earning its name by its work, with mineness guarding the throb.

## par-p04

His shining is the world from Śiva to clay — making plus aliveness; made defined as shining-by-Him; two dilemmas dissolved; never veiled.

## par-p05

Pre-proved beyond indicators, self-answered in the asker, proof demoted to illusion's traffic, the further Life enthroned beyond proofs.

## par-p06

Alphabet-mass I-grasp as essence; the level defined by hill-earth and river-water; the thirty-six named in their order.

## par-p07

Faintness ladders down to the person; deemed chains bind the wanderer; the magician stays untouched; recognition frees in the body and Śiva at its fall; glories dealt back little, king-wise.

## par-p08

Five littles under a single cover; nature in poise; the trio deciding, prizing, resolving; senses in order; commons with nothing more; elements keeping their offices.

## par-p09

The seed holds all, letter by letter proved; the mantra-great double-formed seat is Śiva; knowing-entering initiates without oblation — live free, and at the fall be the Lord.

# C1 NOTE — par-p02

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

The Lord's light-nature entails awareness-nature; the calm-brahman bare-knowing alternative is staged and set aside. Awareness runs bounded and unbounded, operates through the three cosmic functions as unconstructed-I flashing, and its absence entails lordlessness plus deadness, sun-evidenced.

## VOICE MAP

Kṣemarāja siddhāntin; objector staged via nanu (calm-brahman alternative).

## KEY TERMS

* **akṛtrima-aham** — unmade I (Pradipaka: natural/spontaneous; rival: artless). Anti-constructivist load.
* **śāntabrahmavādin** — calm-brahman teacher: Advaitin target, consciousness without throb.
* **anīśvara** — lordless/powerless (Pradipaka etymology: able-to-do).

Full line: prakāśātman/vimarśasvabhāva (light-selfed/awareness-natured) · śāntabrahmavādin (calm-brahman teacher) · parimita/aparimita (bounded/unbounded) · akṛtrima-aham (unmade I) · anīśvara/jaḍa (lordless/inert)

## CRUXES

parimita/aparimita ceiling-block undeveloped; sun parity not identity.

## FUNCTION

Thesis → objection → specification → maxim → consequence (sun-sealed).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Passage 2 of 9: mūla 1 with vṛtti (nanu; parimita/aparimita; sun analogy).

## EXPLANATION

Thesis converts before the nanu; dvidhā blocks the ceiling; akṛtrima carries anti-constructivism; yadi-consequence sun-sealed.

## BOUNDARY

parimita/aparimita undeveloped; sun by parity, not identity.

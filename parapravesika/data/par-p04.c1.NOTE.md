# C1 NOTE — par-p04

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Self-lit Lord shines by the Lady as śiva-to-earth world; that shining constitutes both making and aliveness. Two dilemmas resolve it: effecthood means shining-dependence (difference displayed, never real), and likeness cannot veil because veiling light self-destructs — the world being His life-breath cannot throttle its breather.

## VOICE MAP

Kṣemarāja siddhāntin; two objectors staged (effecthood, veiling).

## KEY TERMS

* **etadadhīnaprakāśatva** — this-dependent-shining: whole effecthood theory (etad = doership, sharpened).
* **bhinnavyavahāra** — handled-as-different: display vs reality.
* **prāṇabhūta** — life-breath-become: blocker shares one breath with blocked.
* **tirodhīyate vs nirodh-** — print veil confirmed l.108 (niroddhuṃ nearby is distinct); Pradipaka variant noted.

# C1 NOTE — par-p05

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Indicators cannot touch the pre-proved; the knower's likeness exempts his standing; the two proof-questions answer themselves through self-feeling. Proof is appointed for illusion's traffic only; all else proves nothing, leaning on everything. Proof leans on Him; He, proof's life, has no use for it beyond body-sense-pleasure knowables. The quoted verse enthrones the further Life.

## VOICE MAP

Kṣemarāja siddhāntin; asker refutes himself.

* **pūrvasiddha** — pre-proved: before all proving.
* **svasaṃvedana** — self-feeling: everyone's proof (unargued, text's move).
* **pramāṇavarāka** — poor proof (Pradipaka: miserable; rationale: ephemeral/new each moment vs ever-present Knower).
* **deha-prāṇa-nīla-sukha** — proof's remaining office: body-breath-blue-pleasure knowables.

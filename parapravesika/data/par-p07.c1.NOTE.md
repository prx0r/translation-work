# C1 NOTE — par-p07

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Pure bliss-nature throbbing with hundred-thousand reflections wills emission; will-first, knowing-first, doing-first dim by this-portion faintness; illusion spreads difference, stuffs art-to-earth, and veils by freedom's synonym into personhood — deemed-bound, wandering. The mask never touches the wearer (magician). Recognition restores sovereignty: dense-conscious, free-bodied, Śiva at fall. Five glories, uncontracted, accept contraction into five littles on robbed-king terms.

## VOICE MAP

Kṣemarāja siddhāntin expounding descent and return.

## KEY TERMS

* **dhyāmala/adhyāmala** — dim/undim this-share: ladder's single variable.
* **aindrajālika** — magician: self-willed illusion, wearer untouched.
* **ciddhana/mukta** — consciousness-dense/freed: two-phase release.
* **rājanyāya** — king-logic: contraction as compensated dealing.
* **glory→sheath pairing** — kalā←doership, vidyā←knowingness, rāga←fullness, kāla←eternity, niyati←pervasion (standard).

Full line: dhyāmala/adhyāmala (dim/undim) · māyāśakti as svātantrya-synonym · aindrajālika (magician) · vidyābhijñāpitaiśvarya (recognition-brought sovereignty) · ciddhana (consciousness-dense) · rājanyāya (king-logic)

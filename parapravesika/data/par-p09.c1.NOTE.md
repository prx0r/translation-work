# C1 NOTE — par-p09

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Banyan and peacock-egg prove containment; the tradition-verse seats moving-unmoving world in the Lady-shaped heart-seed; clay and water-common prove being the reality of tattvas; being stripped to bare s holds thirty-one, pure levels entering assent-au; emission breathes out above-below; the seed, mantra-great, world-shaped, world-past, is Śiva as His own seat. Knowing-entering initiates truly — no oblation; breathing on, worldly, living-free; at fall, the Lord Himself.

## VOICE MAP

Kṣemarāja siddhāntin landing; tradition-verse voiced; colophon ascribing Kṣemarāja.

## KEY TERMS

* **hṛdayabīja** — heart-seed: container of all (banyan/peacock-egg).
* **sat→sa** — being bared to s: primary-nature, NOT tattva 13 (Pradipaka note 47).
* **au/anuttaraśakti** — assent-au: pure levels enter; the sequence S→Au→visarga is the Sauḥ/Prāsāda heart-mantra (Pradipaka notes 47/53).
* **visarga** — emission above-below: closes p02's arc.
* **tilājyāhutivarjita** — oblation-void: initiation without rite is the punch.
* **water ≠ ghee** — jalādi (liquids) and tilājyāhuti (oblation) are distinct passages; do not conflate.

Full line: hṛdayabīja (heart-seed) · āmnāyanīti (tradition-way) · sat→sa involution (primary-nature, not tattva 13) · aukāra-anuttaraśakti (assent-au unsurpassed-power; Sauḥ heart-mantra) · visarga emission · tilājyāhutivarjita (oblation-void) · jīvanmukta/dehapāta (living-free/body-fall)

# Parātriṃśikā — ARGMAP

## para-p01 (2–10)
- speaker: Devī asks; Bhairava answers.
- question: What is the unsurpassed (anuttara)?
- premises: Etad guhyaṃ mahāguhyaṃ kathayasva mama prabho (this secret, great secret, tell me, Lord). Hṛdayasthā tu yā śaktiḥ kaulinī kulanāyikā (the śakti abiding in heart, Kaulinī, clan-leader). Tām me kathaya deveśa yena tṛptiṃ vrajāmy aham (tell me that, Lord of gods, by which I attain satisfaction). Śrībhairava uvāca (Śrī Bhairava said). Śṛṇu devi mahābhage uttarasyāpy anuttaram (listen, goddess, great fortunate, of the uttara, the anuttara). Kathayāmi na saṃdehaḥ sadyaḥ kaulikasiddhidam (I tell, no doubt, instantly Kaulika-siddhi-giving).
- inference: The unsurpassed (anuttara) = of the uttara, the anuttara; instantly Kaulika-siddhi-giving; the śakti abiding in heart, Kaulinī.
- conclusion: Anuttara doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## para-p02 (11–19)
- speaker: Bhairava.
- question: What are the powers of the mantra?
- premises: Asyoccāre kṛte samyaṅ mantramudrāgaṇo mahān (when this is pronounced well, the great mantra-mudrā-group). Sadyaḥ sanmukhatām eti svadehāveśalakṣaṇam (instantly faces, characteristic of entering own body). Muhūrtaṃ smarate yas tu cumbake nābhimudritaḥ (one who remembers for a moment, stamped on navel by cumbaka). Sa badhnāti tadā dehaṃ mantramudrāgaṇaṃ naraḥ (he then binds the body, mantra-mudrā-group, man). Atītānāgatānarthān pṛṣṭo 'sau kathayaty api (past/future calamities, asked, he tells too). Praharād yad abhipret... (from a prahara, whatever desired...).
- inference: Mantra's powers = when pronounced well, great mantra-mudrā-group; instantly faces, characteristic of entering own body; one who remembers for a moment, stamped on navel; binds body; tells past/future calamities.
- conclusion: Mantra powers stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## para-p03 (20–28)
- speaker: Bhairava.
- question: What is the knowledge?
- premises: Anena jñātamātreṇa jñāyate sarvaśaktibhiḥ (by this, when known, known by all śaktis). Śākinīkulasāmānyo bhaved yogaṃ vināpi hi (would become common to Śākinī-clan, even without yoga). Avidhijño vidhānajño jāyate yajanaṃ prati (ignorant of rules, becomes knower of rules, regarding worship). Kālāgnim āditaḥ kṛtvā māyāntam brahmadehagam (from Kālāgni first, to Māyā, Brahma-body-going). Śivo viśvadyanantāntaḥ paraṃ śaktitrayaṃ matam (Śiva, world-devouring, endless-end, supreme śakti-triad is held). Tadantar varti yatkiṃcic ch... (whatever is within that...).
- inference: Knowledge = when known, known by all śaktis; common to Śākinī-clan even without yoga; ignorant of rules becomes knower of rules; from Kālāgni to Māyā, Brahma-body-going; Śiva, world-devouring, endless-end, supreme śakti-triad.
- conclusion: Knowledge doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## para-p04 (29–36)
- speaker: Bhairava (close).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: Puṣpādikaṃ kramāt sarvaṃ liṅgaṃ vā sthaṇḍilaṃ ca vā (flowers etc. in order, all, or liṅga, or sthaṇḍila). Caturdaśābhijaptena puṣpeṇāsanakalpanā (by fourteen-recited flower, seat-arrangement). Tatra sṛṣṭiṃ yajed vīraḥ punar evāsanaṃ tataḥ (there, creation, worship, vīra; again seat then). Sṛṣṭiṃ tu sampuṭīkṛtya paścād yajanam ārabhet (creation, having enclosed, afterwards worship begins). Sarvatattvasusampūrṇām sarvāvayavaśobhitām (all-tattva-full, all-limb-beautiful). Yajed devīṃ mahāb... (worship the Goddess, great...).
- inference: Close = flowers etc. in order, all, or liṅga, or sthaṇḍila; by fourteen-recited flower, seat-arrangement; there, creation, worship, vīra; again seat; creation enclosed, afterwards worship begins; all-tattva-full, all-limb-beautiful; worship the Goddess.
- conclusion: The text closes with worship of the Goddess.
- unresolved: none.

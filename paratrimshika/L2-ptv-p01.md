# Paratrimshika ptv-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
O Lord, what is the Unsurpassable that grants Kaula fruit by itself — known by a mere touch of knowing, rising to kinship with the Sky-Goer?
The Sanskrit: *anuttaraṃ kathaṃ deva svataḥ kaulikasiddhidam | yena vijñātamātreṇa khecarīsamatāṃ vrajet*

## v.2
Tell me this secret of secrets, Lord — the Power that dwells in the heart, Kaulinī, mistress of the clan.
The Sanskrit: *etad guhyaṃ mahāguhyaṃ kathayasva mama prabho | hṛdayasthā tu yā śaktiḥ kaulinī kulanāyikā*

## v.3
Tell me of her, Lord of gods, by whom I come to fullness. — Listen, blessed Goddess: even the answer has one beyond it — the Unsurpassable.
The Sanskrit: *tām me kathaya deveśa yena tṛptiṃ vrajāmy aham | śṛṇu devi mahābhage uttarasyāpy anuttaram*

## v.4
I tell it, no doubt: swift Kaula-fruition. This Kaula rite, Goddess, abides in my heart-sky.
The Sanskrit: *kathayāmi na saṃdehaḥ sadyaḥ kaulikasiddhidam | kauliko 'yaṃ vidhir devi mama hṛdvyomny avasthitaḥ*

## v.5
First now: all lunar days, vowels ending in the drop; within them, by time's conjoining, moon and sun are declared.
The Sanskrit: *athādyās tithayaḥ sarve svarā bindvavasānakāḥ | tadantaḥ kālayogena somasūryau prakīrtitau*

## v.6
Realities from earth to person, in five groups in order — in the ka-series and the rest, ending with the ma-sound, O fair-vowed one.
The Sanskrit: *pṛthivyādīni tattvāni puruṣāntāni pañcasu | kramāt kādiṣu vargeṣu makārānteṣu suvrate*

## v.7
Fourfold the stations of wind, fire, water and their lords; above them the famous śa-series, and before, the five Brahmas.
The Sanskrit: *vāyvagnisalilendrāṇāṃ dhāraṇānāṃ catuṣṭayam | tadūrdhve śādivikhyātam purastād brahmapañcakam*

## v.8
Rootless, in due order, to be known, ending in kṣa — this forthgoing declared; of all mantras and wisdoms indeed, O glorious one.
The Sanskrit: *amūlā tatkramā jñeyā kṣāntā sṛṣṭir udāhṛtā | sarveṣāṃ caiva mantrāṇāṃ vidyānāṃ ca yaśasvini*

## v.9
This is the womb, ever told in all Tantras — joined with the fourteen and the tithi-lord's end; the third brahma, fair-hipped, the heart of Bhairava himself.
The Sanskrit: *iyaṃ yoniḥ samākhyātā sarvatantreṣu sarvadā | caturdaśayutaṃ bhadre tithīśāntasamanvitam | tṛtīyam brahma suśroṇi hṛdayam bhairavātmanaḥ*

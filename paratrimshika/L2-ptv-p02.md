# Paratrimshika ptv-p02 — L2 READ

## v.10
Yoginī-born or Rudra — neither plainly wins this heart of the god of gods, swift giver of yoga's release.
The Sanskrit: *etan nā yoginījāto nā rudro labhate sphuṭam | hṛdayaṃ devadevasya sadyo yogavimokṣadam*

## v.11
At its right utterance, the great host of mantra-seals comes face to face at once — marked by possession of one's own body.
The Sanskrit: *asyoccāre kṛte samyaṅ mantramudrāgaṇo mahān | sadyaḥ sanmukhatām eti svadehāveśalakṣaṇam*

## v.12
Who remembers for a moment, in the kiss, navel-sealed — he binds body then, and the mantra-seal host, the man.
The Sanskrit: *muhūrtaṃ smarate yas tu cumbake nābhimudritaḥ | sa badhnāti tadā dehaṃ mantramudrāgaṇaṃ naraḥ*

## v.13
Asked, he tells past and future things — even within three hours, uttering the meant god-form.
The Sanskrit: *atītānāgatānarthān pṛṣṭo 'sau kathayaty api | praharād yad abhipretaṃ devatārūpam uccaran*

## v.14
Face to face he sees the undoubted, drawn by Rudra's powers; in two watches' time, sky-dwelling, remembering.
The Sanskrit: *sakṣāt paśyaty asaṃdigdham ākṛṣṭaṃ rudrasaktibhiḥ | praharadvayamātreṇa vyomastho jāyate smaran*

## v.15
By three: all mothers, mighty yoginīs, heroes, hero-lords, adepts, and the strong śākinī throng.
The Sanskrit: *trayeṇa mātaraḥ sarvā yogesvaryo mahābalāḥ | vīrā vīresvarāḥ siddhā balavān śākinīgaṇaḥ*

## v.16
Come, pledged, urged by Bhairava — they grant supreme fruition, whatever fruit is wished.
The Sanskrit: *āgatya samayaṃ dattvā bhairaveṇa pracoditāḥ | yacchanti paramāṃ siddhiṃ phalaṃ yad vā samīhitam*

## v.17
By this the perfected will perfect, and make mantra-men perfect.
The Sanskrit: *anena siddhāḥ setsyanti sādhayanti ca mantriṇaḥ*

## v.18
Whatever in Bhairava's Tantra succeeds from this — by mantra-potency's possession-power, with no controller.
The Sanskrit: *yatkiṃcid bhairave tantre sarvam asmāt prasidhyati | mantravīryasamāveśaprabhāvān na niyantriṇā*

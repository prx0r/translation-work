# Paratrimshika ptv-p03 — L2 READ

## v.19
Who thus truly knows even the unseen circle shares fruition ever — yogin and initiate alike.
The Sanskrit: *adṛṣṭama.ṅdalo 'py evam yaḥ kaścid vetti tattvataḥ | sa siddhibhāg bhaven nityaṃ sa yogī sa ca dīkṣītaḥ*

## v.20
By this mere known, all powers know; kinsman of the śākinī clan he may be, even without yoga.
The Sanskrit: *anena jñātamātreṇa jñāyate sarvaśaktibhiḥ | śākinīkulasāmānyo bhaved yogaṃ vināpi hi*

## v.21
Ignorant of rite yet knowing ordinance, he turns to worship — having made time-fire from the first, ending in māyā, gone to the brahma-body.
The Sanskrit: *avidhijño vidhānajño jāyate yajanaṃ prati | kālāgnim āditaḥ kṛtvā māyāntam brahmadehagam*

## v.22
Śiva, whose end is endless all, deemed the supreme power-triad; whatever stands on the pure path abides within that.
The Sanskrit: *śivo viśvadyanantāntaḥ paraṃ śaktitrayaṃ matam | tadantar varti yatkiṃcic chuddhamārge vyavasthitam*

## v.23
The atom soon attains pure lordly knowing; its mover is Śiva to be known, all-knowing highest Lord.
The Sanskrit: *aṇur viśuddham acirād aisvaraṃ jñānam aśnute | taccodakaḥ śivo jñeyaḥ sarvajñaḥ parameśvaraḥ*

## v.24
All-going, stainless, clear, full, self-aboded, bright — like the great power-formed tree standing in the banyan seed.
The Sanskrit: *sarvago nirmalaḥ svacchas tṛptaḥ svāyatanaḥ śuciḥ | yathā nyagrodhabījasthaḥ śaktirūpo mahādrumaḥ*

## v.25
So the moving-unmoving world stands in the heart-seed; who knows thus in truth — his is going-to-freedom.
The Sanskrit: *tathā hṛdayabījasthaṃ jagad etac carācaram | evaṃ yo vetti tattvena tasya nirvāṇagāminī*

## v.26
Initiation becomes undoubted, free of sesame-ghee offering — on crown, face, heart, secret, and image, so indeed.
The Sanskrit: *dīkṣā bhavaty asaṃdigdhā tilājyāhutivarjitā | mūrdhni vaktre ca hṛdaye guhye murtau tathaiva ca*

## v.27
Placing done, topknot bound twenty-seven-muttered; with each one the ten quarters' binding he should have made.
The Sanskrit: *nyāsaṃ krtvā śikhāṃ baddhvā saptaviṃsatimantritām | ekaikena diśāṃ bandhaṃ daśānām api kārayet*

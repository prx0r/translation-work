# Paratrimshika ptv-p04 — L2 READ

## v.28
Three claps first, sounded, to clear obstacles; with thrice-muttered topknot-count water then sprinkled.
The Sanskrit: *tālatrayam purā dattvā saśabdaṃ vighnaśāntaye | śikhāsaṃkhyābhijaptena toyenābhyukṣayet tataḥ*

## v.29
Flowers and all in order — liṅga or altar; with fourteen mutterings, a flower-seat made.
The Sanskrit: *puṣpādikaṃ kramāt sarvaṃ liṅgaṃ vā stha.ṅdilaṃ ca vā | caturdaśābhijaptena puṣpeṇāsanakalpanā*

## v.30
There let the hero worship forthgoing, then the very seat again; casketed, let him begin the worship after.
The Sanskrit: *tatra sṛṣṭiṃ yajed vīraḥ punar evāsanaṃ tataḥ | sṛṣṭiṃ tu sampuṭīkṛtya paścād yajanam ārabhet*

## v.31
Full of all realities, graced with every limb — let the fortunate worship the goddess, twenty-seven-muttered.
The Sanskrit: *sarvatattvasusampūrṇām sarvāvayavaśobhitām | yajed devīṃ mahābhāgā saptaviṃśatimantritām*

## v.32
Then with fragrant flowers as able let him honor; with supreme devotion worship, and offer up himself.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ sugandhipuṣpaiś ca yathāśaktyā samarcayet | pūjayet parayā bhaktyā svātmanaṃ ca nivedayet*

## v.33
Thus the worship told — even in fire-rite this rule: who did the worship-rule, rightly remembering the seed, succeeds.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ yajanam ākhyātam agnikārye 'py ayaṃ vidhiḥ | kṛtapūjāvidhiḥ samyak smaran bījam prasiddhyati*

## v.34
Beginningless-endless seed, blossoming, gone mid-tithis — in the heart-lotus meditate the moon-ray, ever practise.
The Sanskrit: *ādyantarahitam bījaṃ vikasat tithimadhyagam | hṛtpadmāntargataṃ dhyāyet somāṃśuṃ nityam abhyasyet*

## v.35
Which desires he desires, them quickly may he win; though unknowing, he goes to direct sight — all-knowingness, no doubt.
The Sanskrit: *yān yān kāmayate kāmāṃs tān tāñ cchīghram avāpnuyāt | ajñaḥ pratyakṣatām eti sarvajñatvaṃ na saṃśayaḥ*

## v.36
Thus mantra-fruit got — this Rudrayāmala; from this practice: fruition and all-knowingness are won.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ mantraphalāvaptir ity etad rudrayāmalam | etad abhyāsataḥ siddhiḥ sarvajñatvam avāpyate*

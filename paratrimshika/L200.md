# L200 — Parātriṃśikā (35 verses, 4 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Parātriṃśikā (35 verses) is a Kaula tantra. It establishes: the unsurpassed (anuttara) = of the uttara, the anuttara; instantly Kaulika-siddhi-giving; the śakti abiding in heart, Kaulinī, clan-leader. Mantra powers = when pronounced well, great mantra-mudrā-group; instantly faces, characteristic of entering own body; one who remembers for a moment, stamped on navel by cumbaka; binds body; tells past/future calamities. Knowledge = when known, known by all śaktis; common to Śākinī-clan even without yoga; ignorant of rules becomes knower of rules; from Kālāgni to Māyā, Brahma-body-going; Śiva, world-devouring, endless-end, supreme śakti-triad. Close = flowers etc. in order, all, or liṅga, or sthaṇḍila; by fourteen-recited flower, seat-arrangement; there, creation, worship, vīra; again seat; creation enclosed, afterwards worship begins; all-tattva-full, all-limb-beautiful; worship the Goddess.

- R2: Mantra without powers.
- R3: Knowledge without Śiva/śakti-triad.
- R4: Close without Goddess worship.

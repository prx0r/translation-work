## The parable (≈90s)

> The rite begins the way weather begins: three claps, strong, to
> clear what bars the way — then the topknot bound and counted
> thrice, water sprinkled over it. Flowers first, in order, mark
> or altar: fourteen mutterings over the flower-seat; the hero
> worships forthgoing, the seat renewed, the casket honored; the
> fortune-dame with her seven-and-twenty names takes the fragrant
> flowers and is given herself away. But the seat stands at the
> end of a path — and the path is the place's true language:
> vowels ending in their bindu-dots, tattvas five by five in
> ordered classes, rows above and the five before, rootless, ranged
> to kṣa: the womb told in all Tantras, fourteen-joined, tithi-
> lord-ended, the third brahma's heart. One moment's kiss, navel-
> sealed, and the seal-host comes face to face — ere three hours
> he tells the past and future; by three the Mothers come and
> grant whatever fruit. The atom soon attains lordly knowing: as
> banyan-seed holds the power-tree — heart-seed, freedom-going.
> Initiation comes free of sesame and ghee and flame — seed with
> neither head nor tail, abloom: what he desires, swiftly gained,
> direct sight, all-knowing, no doubt's stain. This is mantra-
> fruit: the Rudra-Yāmala way.

## The seal — statement (+ HERMETIC CLUES)

**The path where every stone is a world:** the initiate walks a
garden-path paved in FIVE ROWS OF TILES (the code made floor:
each tile bearing one small thing — clod, drop, flame, feather,
coin — the tattvas-from-earth-to-person five-by-five, letter-
worlds as paving), each row ending in a round bindu-stone
(vowels' dot), two bright tiles among them (moon and sun named);
at path's end the flower-seat: a fourteen-petal rosette, the
casket, a twenty-seven-petal wreath; the initiate mid-rite — three
concentric sound-arcs springing from his raised hands (the THREE
CLAPS, countable), topknot streaming silver sprinkle-drops; at his
middle the gold kiss-seal (navel-sealed, moment-binding).

### HERMETIC CLUES (border/marginalia — the alchemical way)
- **A single banyan-seed hidden in the lower border's marginalia**
  [v24: banyan-seed holds the power-tree — the tree hidden in one
  seed, exactly as the art hides its text in one corner].
- **A tiny sesame-lamp drawn CROSSSED-OUT in the opposite corner**
  [v26: initiation free of sesame, ghee and flame — the crossed-out
  lamp = the negation, drawn not written].
- **Fourteen small flowers along the border's top edge** [v29's
  count encoded in the frame itself, Solis-style border-
  commentary].
- **The two bright path-tiles** [v5: moon and sun named among the
  vowels — hidden-in-plain-sight as the only shining paving].
- **The headless-tailless seed at the base of the seat** [v34 —
  the closing emblem, seed of neither end, in the ground].
Each clue double-anchored; each is a BORDER-LEVEL Solis habit:
the frame tells what the plate can't.

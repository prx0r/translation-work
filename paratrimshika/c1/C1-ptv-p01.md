# C1 — ptv-p01

## SUMMARY

What grants Kaula fruit by itself, known by mere touch? The heart's Kaulinī, clan-mistress — tell her, for fullness. Even of the answer, one beyond: no doubt, at once, the rite in heart-sky. First vowels drop-ending, moon-sun time-yoked; earth-to-person in five ka-to-ma classes; supports fourfold, brahma-five śa-famed; rootless kṣa-ending emanation of all mantras and wisdoms; womb ever told, fourteen-joined, third brahma — Bhairava-self's heart.

## FUNCTION

Asks the fierce question and lays the phonematic ground the answer will inhabit.

## KEY TERMS

anuttara (unsurpassable Subject — 16 Vivaraṇa readings); katham (how — means, not definition); kaulika-siddhi (in-this-body identity); vijñātamātra (mere recognition — the engine); khecarī (unobstructed power); tithi (moon-days as phonemes); yoni (womb-source — sauḥ decoding in lore, not gloss); svataḥ (by autonomy — source bytes, sadyaḥ recorded).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Question and ground: the how asked here is paid across powers, fruit, and rite.

## EXPLANATION

Fierce question (katham = by what means): the unsurpassable granting Kaula fruit by autonomy, recognition alone lifting to Khecarī-evenness. Secret asked: heart-abiding Kaulinī. Fullness asked; answer beyond answers promised. Promise: no doubt, at once, rite in heart-sky. Ground: drop-ending vowels with moon-sun time-yoked; earth-to-person realities in five ka-to-ma classes; fourfold supports; śa-famed brahma-five; rootless kṣa-ending emanation of all mantras and wisdoms; ever-told womb, fourteen-joined, third brahma — Bhairava-self's heart.

## BOUNDARY

No powers are shown; the host gathers beyond the ground laid here.

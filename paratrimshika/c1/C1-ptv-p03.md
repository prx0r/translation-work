# C1 — ptv-p03

## SUMMARY

Even the unseen circle truly known shares fruition — yogin, initiated, ever. By mere this known of all powers, śākinī-common even without yoga. Rite-unknowing, ordinance-knowing turns: time-fire from the first through māyā to brahma-body. Boundless Śiva, supreme triad; the pure-pathed stand inside. The atom soon reaches lordly knowing; its mover Śiva to be known. All-going, stainless, clear, full, self-aboded, bright — banyan-seed tree, power-formed. So the moving world in the heart-seed; the true knower's going-to-freedom. Initiation undoubted, oblation-free, on five sites; placing done, topknot bound: all ten quarters bound.

## FUNCTION

Tastes the fruit: knowing without apparatus, holism imaged, initiation internalized.

## KEY TERMS

adṛṣṭamaṇḍala (unseen circle — recognition without apparatus); vināpi yoga (even without yoga); sarvam sarvātmakam (holism — banyan/heart-seed); nirvāṇagāminī (going-to-freedom — feminine, dīkṣā runs on); nyāsa (placing — body mapped).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Fruit of the powers exhibited before; the feminine going-to-freedom runs into the initiation verse.

## EXPLANATION

Unseen circle truly known: shares fruition ever — yogin and initiated (recognition without maṇḍala). By mere this known of all powers; śākinī-common even without yoga. Rite-unknowing, ordinance-knowing turns toward worship: time-fire from the first through māyā to brahma-body. Boundless-ended Śiva, supreme triad deemed; whatever stands on the pure path inside that. Atom soon reaches pure lordly knowing; its impeller Śiva to be known, all-knowing lord. All-going, stainless, clear, full, self-aboded, bright — banyan-seed tree, power-formed. So the moving world in the heart-seed; the true knower's going-to-freedom (feminine runs to dīkṣā). Initiation undoubted, sesame-ghee-free, on crown, face, heart, hidden, form. Placing done, topknot twenty-seven-bound; each direction bound, all ten.

## BOUNDARY

No outer rite is given; the rite's body begins beyond the fruit.

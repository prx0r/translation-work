# C1 — ptv-p04

## SUMMARY

Three claps sounded against obstacles; topknot-count water sprinkled. Flowers, mark or altar; fourteen-muttered seat. The hero worships emanation, then the seat; casketed, begins. Realities-full, all-limbed, the fortunate worships the twenty-seven-muttered goddess. Fragrant flowers by power, supreme devotion — self offered. Worship told; even in fire this rule: seed rightly remembered succeeds. Beginningless seed mid-tithis, heart-lotus-meditated; moon-ray ever practised. Desires quickly got; the unknowing to direct sight — no doubt. Mantra-fruit got: this Rudrayāmala; from practice, fruition, all-knowingness.

## FUNCTION

Gives the rite's body, then seals the tantra: told, practised, got.

## KEY TERMS

tālatraya (three claps); sampuṭīkṛtya (casketing — ritual enclosing); mahābhāgā (great-fortuned subject); svātmanivedana (self-offering — the inner oblation); bīja (seed); abhyāsa (practice — the last word); rudrayāmala (colophon name — own claim, not reconstructed corpus).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The rite's body answers the method-question of the opening; the colophon closes the how with got.

## EXPLANATION

Rite's body: three claps with sound against obstacles; topknot-count water sprinkled. Flowers, mark or altar; fourteen-muttered seat-making. There the hero worships emanation, then the seat; casketed, worship begins. Realities-full, all-limbed, the fortunate worships the twenty-seven-muttered goddess. Fragrant flowers by power honored; supreme devotion worshipped; own self offered. Worship told; even in fire this rule: whose rule done, rightly remembering seed, succeeds. Beginningless-endless blossoming seed mid-tithis, gone inside heart-lotus, meditated; moon-ray ever practised. Whatever desires quickly got; the unknowing to direct sight — all-knowingness, no doubt. Thus mantra-fruit getting: this Rudrayāmala; from practice, accomplishment and all-knowingness got.

## BOUNDARY

Nothing further is prescribed; practice is the last word.

## RELATED

## v1 doctrine (Singh/Vivaraṇa, verse-usable)
- anuttara: the unsurpassable — Experient of all with none to experience IT (universal Subject; 16 interpretations in Vivaraṇa; keep 2-3: unsurpassable Subject; ever-awake Reality where no question/answer arises; beyond fixed order).
- kathaṃ = kena prakāreṇa (in what way, by what means) — she asks HOW, not what (already knows the power).
- kaulika-siddhi: kule dehe bhava (in this very body); cidaikātmya (identity of empirical I with Śiva's perfect I-consciousness concretized as cosmos); micro/macro harmony (kula = macro creative energy parā-vāk to phonematic emanation; micro = energy in the human body).
- khecari: bodhabhūmisaṃcāriṇī sarvajñā śaktiḥ (consciousness-power moving on universal-knowledge plane); kha = unobstructed expanse; bodha = avikalpa + pūrṇa (thought-free, whole); abodha = empirical conditioned consciousness (blue, pleasure/pain).
- Devī = parāśakti asking on paśyantī/madhyamā planes to bestow grace; poised in anuttara, answering as Bhairava.

## Title
Parātriṃśikā = (Tantra) of Thirty-plus (triṃśat + para = beyond thirty; 36 verses). Kaula/Trika highest esteem after Somananda; Abhinavagupta two commentaries.

## Rasa pointers
- p01 (vv1-9, question + promise): adbhuta (the fierce opening — recognition itself transformative) with jijñāsā-undertow; Devī's question is power-seeking, not speculation.
- p02 (vv10-18): method core (mantra/vāk metaphysics per Vivaraṇa) — vīra-utsāha (practice resolve) inside adbhuta.
- p03 (vv19-27): śānta deepening (stillness states).
- p04 (vv28-36): phala + seal (evaṃ mantraphalāvāptiḥ — mantra-fruit attained; colophon frame).
- Collision warnings: anuttara (never "unsurpassed thing" — the Subject); khecari (power, not goddess-nymph); kaulika (in-body, not sectarian); vijñātamātreṇa (by mere recognition — the verse's engine).

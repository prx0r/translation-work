# Paryantapancasika pary-p01 (studying maxim) — L2 READ

## v.1
Filled with rest of beginning-end-entered endless power — unspeakable — I study speech: the Lord's heart.
The Sanskrit: *ādyantāntargatānantaśaktiviśrāntinirbharām anuccāryām | ahaṃ vācam adhīye hṛdayaṃ vibhoḥ*

## v.2
He indeed Bhairava god; all gods of-whom-made. That very Bhairavī — being, non-being too — of-whom-made.
The Sanskrit: *sa eva bhairavo devas sarve devāś ca yanmayāḥ | saivāsau bhairavī sattāsattāpi ca yanmayī*

## v.3
That indeed Bhairava's knowledge-ignorance too of-whom-made. He indeed Bhairava-doctrine — opponent too of-whom-made.
The Sanskrit: *tad eva bhairavaṃ jñānam ajñānam api yanmayam | sa hi bhairavasiddhāntaḥ pūrvapakṣo 'pi yanmayaḥ*

## v.4
Unity of six paths — where that Bhairavī abides — at the end of all systems — ineffable, supreme goal.
The Sanskrit: *aikadhyam adhvanāṃ ṣa.ṅnāṃ yatra sā bhairavī sthitiḥ | paryante sarvatantrāṇām avācyā paramā gatiḥ*

## v.5
She — bliss-only-self-merged will-knowledge-true-action — heart of god-of-gods, of Bhairava, undivided ground.
The Sanskrit: *sā cidānandamātrātmamagnecchājñānasatkriyā | hṛdayaṃ devadevasya bhairavasyāvibhāgabhūḥ*

## v.6
From nature of consciousness, that god by own self reflecting — Lord, from unattached to end-of-stages — stages attains.
The Sanskrit: *citsvābhāvyād asau devaḥ svātmanā vimṛṣan prabhuḥ | anāśritādibhūmyantā bhūmikāḥ pratipadyate*

## v.7
There expressive, expressed, self — of two vibrations singly — of Lord gross, subtle, supreme appearance — of sequences sixfold path.
The Sanskrit: *tatra vācakavācyātmaspandayor ekaśaḥ | prabhoḥ sthūlasūkṣmaparābhāsakramayoḥ ṣaḍvidhādhvatā*

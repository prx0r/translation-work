# Paryantapancasika pary-p02 (sundered, varied, shone) — L2 READ

## v.8
Sequence which space-time-self, difference-life — of knowings from separation — this of-knower makes-varied-appearance.
The Sanskrit: *kramo yo deśakālātmā bhedaprāṇas | sa saṃvidām vicchedād eṣa vijñātur vicitrābhāsatākṛtaḥ*

## v.9
Of both — there indeed way: pure, impure, self — from difference — purity of externalized objects — in I-ness immersion.
The Sanskrit: *ubhayo tatra vai rītiḥ śuddhāśuddhātmabhedataḥ | śuddhir bahiṣkṛtārthānāṃ svāhantāyāṃ nimajjanam*

## v.10
Impurity too of the immersed — not thus heart-rest — in-between too infinite are states by-degrees.
The Sanskrit: *aśuddhir api magnānāṃ naivaṃ hṛdayaviśramaḥ | atrāntare 'py anantās syuḥ sthitayas tāratamyataḥ*

## v.11
Of proper-measured knowers in manifestation — common; otherwise-states divided — subject of-object.
The Sanskrit: *ucitocitameyānāṃ pramātṝṇāṃ prakāśane sādhāraṇānyathābhāvau | bhidyete mātṛmeyayoḥ*

## v.12
Thought-free, clear appearance — that creation of Shambhu held — by non-difference, in self-creation, own-self indeed made-instrument.
The Sanskrit: *avikalpasphuṭābhāsā sā sṛṣṭiḥ śāmbhavī matā | abhedenātmanirmāṇe svātmaiva karaṇīkṛtaḥ*

## v.13
Action, knowledge, self, consciousness, power — from difference-threading-cause — kalā, vidyā, stage reached — in difference-undifference again becomes.
The Sanskrit: *kriyājñānātmacicchaktir bhedāsūtraṇakāraṇāt | kalāvidyāpadaṃ prāpya bhedodbhede punar bhavet*

## v.14
Means-of-knowledge, intellect, multitudes — which of action imaginations — that indeed cut, cut — action, knowledge, effort — when.
The Sanskrit: *pramāṇabuddhiprakarā yatkriyāyā vikalpanāḥ | saiva vicchinnavicchinnakriyā jñānodyamā yadā*

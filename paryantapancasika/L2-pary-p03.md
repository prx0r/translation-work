# Paryantapancasika pary-p03 (narrowed, called, measured) — L2 READ

## v.15
Action-intellect limitation obtained — then that bound soul is called. Thus by will He, blessed Bhairava, is measured.
The Sanskrit: *karmabuddhyakṣatāṃ prāptā tadāsau paśur ucyate | itīcchayā sa bhagavān bhairavaḥ parimīyate*

## v.16
Limited in function, being — that power follows him. His Vidyeśa, Sadākhya, Śivahood — from contracted state: body, breath, intellect, void, knowership attained.
The Sanskrit: *mitavṛttis satī sā ca śaktis tam anuvartate | tasya vidyeśasādākhyaśivatās saṃkucatsthiteḥ*

## v.17
His particular creation — that-and-that various imagination — Śaiva creation arisen — impression of the purified dependent-on-that.
The Sanskrit: *tasyāsādhāraṇī sṛṣṭis tattannānāvikalpanā | śaivasargotthasaṃskārasaṃskṛtasya tadāśrayā*

## v.18
Knower indeed — this of-such-kind — at festival-of-limitation undistracted abides — in own self, without thought, consciousness-self.
The Sanskrit: *pramātaivāyam etāvatprakāro 'vagrahotsave avyagro vartate | svātmany avikalpe cidātmake*

## v.19
Child, see — you indeed this as-nondual — of consciousness known, of-that-made world — other trivial unreal — because.
The Sanskrit: *vatsa paśya tvam evaitat advayatvena saṃvidaḥ | viditaṃ tanmayaṃ viśvam anyat tuccham asad yataḥ*

## v.20
What means this principle — from which other not indeed possible — without-other-means — this self-means — called.
The Sanskrit: *kim upāyam idaṃ tattvaṃ yato 'nyan naiva sambhavi | ananyopāyam etat tadātmopāyaṃ pracakṣate*

# Paryantapancasika pary-p04 (withdrawn, tended, face-to-face) — L2 READ

## v.21
Having withdrawn from all thought — I thus cultivating thought-free own-consciousness direct attains.
The Sanskrit: *saṃhṛtya sarvataś cintām [aha]m ity anuśīlayan | avikalpāṃ svasaṃvittiṃ pratyakṣāṃ pratipadyate*

## v.22
Infinite, such-formed, in acceptance even, one-character — that own-consciousness entered — thoughts not should-imagine.
The Sanskrit: *anantaitāvadākārasvīkāre 'py ekalakṣaṇām | tāṃ svasaṃvidam āviśya vikalpān na vikalpayet*

## v.23
Khecarī-etc., four-streams, flow, full, great-lakes — sky-goddess, self-consciousness — having dived, should-agitate inert.
The Sanskrit: *khecaryādicatussrotovāhapūrṇamahāhradām | vyomeśvarīm ātmacitiṃ vigāhya broḍayej jaḍam*

## v.24
Creation, maintenance, dissolution, concealment, grace — by sequence, five-doing — Śiva I — thus should-trust.
The Sanskrit: *sargasthāpanasaṃhāranigrahānugrahakramāt pañcakṛtyāni | kurvāṇaḥ śivo 'ham iti viśvaset*

## v.25
By sequence-of-projection-knowledge-enumeration, by guru's word, those in self considered — accomplished becomes.
The Sanskrit: *kṣepajñānaprasaṃkhyā[naga]tinādakrameṇa vā guruvaktreṇa | tāny ātmany ākalayya kṛtī bhavet*

## v.26
Having imagined world-self Bhairava I — mine indeed those world-modes — as fire's flame-modes, sun's radiance.
The Sanskrit: *vikalpayitvā viśvātmā bhairavo 'haṃ mamaiva tāḥ | viśvabhaṅgyo yathā vahner jvālābhaṅgyaḥ prabhā raveḥ*

## v.27
One I measurer, knowing, measure, measured, having-kinds — pure-knowledge-etc. by-form — Khecarī-etc. fourteen.
The Sanskrit: *eko 'haṃ mātṛvijñānamānameyaprakāravān | śuddhabodhādirūpeṇa khecaryādicaturdaśaḥ*

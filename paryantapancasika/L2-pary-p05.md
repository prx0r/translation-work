# Paryantapancasika pary-p05 (measured, contemplated, trusted) — L2 READ

## v.28
By mode of measured and unmeasured, bondage-liberation contemplating — Bhairava I am — thus by trust supreme freedom should go.
The Sanskrit: *medāmedaprakāreṇa bandhamokṣau vibhāvayan bhairavo 'smīti visrambhāt svācchandyaṃ paramaṃ vrajet*

## v.29
Inner action, outer action, timeless sequence of self independent held — which You full-of-Lord's-power.
The Sanskrit: *antaḥkārabahiṣkārāv akālakramam ātmanaḥ aparādhīnam ādhatse yat tvaṃ prābhavanirbharaḥ*

## v.30
Supreme emission, self, rest, rise, pair, union of Śiva-and-Śakti — between them — and other powers.
The Sanskrit: *anuttaravisargātmaviśramodayayāmalam saṃghaṭṭaś śivaśaktyos tadantare cānyaśaktayaḥ*

## v.31
Bliss, will, lordship, opening, deficiency — and lordship of will indistinct, distinct by difference — manifestation of known of those two of knowers.
The Sanskrit: *ānandeccheśanonmeṣā ūnatā ceśanecchayoḥ asphuṭasphuṭabhedena vedyollāsas tayor vidaḥ*

## v.32
Bliss by supreme-two, and junction — then feeling of knowns, life-principle — thus full that Bhairava body.
The Sanskrit: *ānandānuttarābhyāṃ ca sandhībhāvo 'tha vedanā vedyānāṃ prāṇabhūteti pūrṇaṃ tad bhairavaṃ vapuḥ*

## v.33
Known-flashing this in will, in lordship — and from one side abiding — in grossness earth etc. element may go fivefold.
The Sanskrit: *vedyollāso 'yam icchāyām īśane caikataḥ sthitaḥ sthūlatvena pṛthivyādibhūtapañcakatām iyāt*

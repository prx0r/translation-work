# Paryantapancasika pary-p06 (subtle five, touch ten) — L2 READ

## v.34
By subtleness, smell etc. five subtle-elements — then by instrumentality from touch — of senses tenfoldness.
The Sanskrit: *sūkṣmatvena ca gandhādipañcatanmātratāṃ tataḥ karaṇatvena saṃsparśād indriyāṇāṃ dvipañcatām*

## v.35
Of knowability by covering, subjecthood manifestation in sequence — mind I-intellect-hood of these in non-differentiation predominance, personhood — and contraction expansion which in-dependence.
The Sanskrit: *vedyatāyāḥ chādanena vettṛtodbhāsanakrame mano 'haṃbuddhitāṃ eṣām avibhāge pradhānatām puruṣatvaṃ ca saṃkocavikāsau yadapekṣayā*

## v.36
Arose desired from union, from double-form, of-both-self, from will-nature, from opening — otherwise supreme from union.
The Sanskrit: *abhūvann iṣyasaṃyogād dvirūpād ubhayātmanaḥ icchāsvarūpād unmeṣād anyathānuttarāyutāt*

## v.37
Four powers inner of spirit, his inside, supporters — of consciousness-space, of inert-ground, of Māyā-made — like Triśaṅku.
The Sanskrit: *catasraś śaktyo 'ntaḥsthāḥ puṃso 'syāntaś ca dhārikāḥ cidvyomno jaḍabhūmeś ca māyāmayyas triśaṅkuvat*

## v.38
Of will-power of-three-form, three other powers became — emission this — then exceedingly clear became, created from expansion.
The Sanskrit: *icchāśaktes trirūpāyās tisro 'nyāś śaktyo 'bhavan visargo 'yam athātyarthaṃ sphuṭo 'bhūt sṛjyavistarāt*

## v.39
This having-created of self-powers mutual from-mixing may-become agitation — inner not from-this emission outside may-become.
The Sanskrit: *etat sṛṣṭvātmaśaktīnām itaretaramiśrāt bhavet kṣobhāntaraṃ nāsmād visargād bāhyato bhavet*

# Paryantapancasika pary-p07 (rested, rested, emitted) — L2 READ

## v.40
So-much all being will etc. by-door supreme in-Bhairava rested-rested — again from-that indeed emitted.
The Sanskrit: *iyatsarvaṃ sad icchādidvārānuttarabhairave viśramya viśramya muhus tata eva visṛjyate*

## v.41
Inner-hidden infinite meaning own-self reflection consciousness supreme — one-time from-flow which this mantra-knowing speech pure agency.
The Sanskrit: *antarnilīnānantārthasvātmāmarśaś citiḥ parā ekavāravahād yeyaṃ mantrī vāk śuddhakartṛtā*

## v.42
Three-flow three-path three-placed time — that draws by-itself outside — then and in-self — thus glorious-supreme time-drawer.
The Sanskrit: *trivahaṃ tripathaṃ tristhaṃ kālaṃ sā karṣati svataḥ bahis tataś cātmanīti śrīparā kālakarṣiṇī*

## v.43
Divided inner-made undivided state observation higher-lower — agency that indeed seeing — his indeed lord state based.
The Sanskrit: *bheditāntaḥkṛtākha.ṅdabhāvāvekṣā parāparā kartṛtā saiva paśyantī tasyaiveśadaśāśrayā*

## v.44
Middle with-sequence gross in-inner-organ imagination undivided expressed — prana-function differentiated.
The Sanskrit: *madhyamā sakramā sthūlā antaḥkaraṇavartinī vikalpanābhinnavācyā prāṇavṛttivibheditā*

## v.45
Own-impression from-such-state of-two-states of-previous also — Māyā of-measure limitedness may-be of-seeing — and sequence indeed subtle.
The Sanskrit: *svavāsanātādavasthyād daśayoḥ pūrvayor api māyāmātur mitatvaṃ syāt paśyantyāś ca kramo hy aṇuḥ*

## v.46
Of-those-two having-entered of-states by-sequence quiet of-thoughts — blessed own-consciousness expansion wonderfully perceives.
The Sanskrit: *tayor āviśya daśayoḥ kramāc chāntavikalpayoḥ dhanyas svasaṃvidvistāraṃ sāścaryam anupaśyati*

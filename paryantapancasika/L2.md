# Paryantapañcāśikā — L2 READ

## pary-p01 — Bhairava's Non-Dual Heart
The text opens with rest in infinite power and the speech of the Lord's heart. Bhairava is the god of all gods, and Bhairavi is both being and non-being. Knowledge, ignorance, doctrine, and opposition are all made of him. The six paths unite in Bhairavi's abode, and Bhairava's heart is pure consciousness-bliss, from which stages and vibrations unfold.

## pary-p02 — Cognition and Creation
Cognitions arise in varied appearance through separation of space, time, and self. Purity and impurity divide the pure and impure paths, while many states remain between them. Shambhavi creation is held as thought-free appearance, and action-knowledge becomes kalā and vidyā through difference. Finally, pramāṇa and intellect cut action and knowledge into effortful imaginations.

## pary-p03 — The Limited Knower
When action and intellect are limited, the bound soul is measured by Bhairava's will. His contracted powers produce Vidyeśa, Sadākhya, and Śiva states in body, breath, and intellect. The knower remains undistracted in self, while Shaiva creation arises from impressions. The world is known as made of consciousness, and this principle is its own means.

## pary-p04 — Diving into Consciousness
Withdrawing thought, one attains direct self-consciousness and dives into the great lake. Shiva performs the five acts while trusting "I am Shiva." Through guru's word and self-contemplation, the practitioner becomes accomplished. Imagining the world as Bhairava's own modifications, one recognizes oneself as subject, knowledge, and measured object.

## pary-p05 — Shiva and Shakti
Contemplating bondage and liberation, one goes freely as Bhairava. Inner and outer action, timeless sequence, and the fullness of lordly power are held in oneself. Shiva and Shakti unite with their other powers between emission and rest. Bliss, will, lordship, and deficiency manifest the known, while gross and subtle elements unfold from that body.

## pary-p06 — Elements and Contraction
Subtle elements and senses arise through subtleness and instrumentality. Knowability covers subjecthood, while mind, I-intellect, and personhood gain predominance through contraction and expansion. Four inner powers of spirit support consciousness-space and the inert ground like Trishanku. Will-power's three forms produce emission, and mixing of self-powers creates inner agitation.

## pary-p07 — Speech and Time
All rests in Bhairava and is emitted again through will and other doors. Hidden infinite meaning is reflected as supreme consciousness and pure mantra-agency. Three flows draw time outward and inward as the glorious time-drawer. Paśyantī, madhyamā, and vaikharī mark higher, middle, and gross speech, while Maya limits the two states.

## pary-p08 — Vaikhari and Transmission
Vaikhari functions through place, instrument, strike, and spirit, enabling worldly and scriptural usage. Presiding goddesses headed by Maheshvari do not enjoy even in waking. The Bhairavi state is to be entered through all means as self-consciousness. Finally, the secret of mantras and mudras is attained, and gurus who hold Shambhu's undivided heart are victorious.

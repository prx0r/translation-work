# C1 — Paryantapañcāśikā (final condensation of the arguments)

*Condensed from ARGMAP (pary-p01..p08) + L2.v2. Passage-local: each sits under its passage in print. No modern comparison, no PARALLELS inside.*

## pary-p01 (vv. 1–7)

Bhairava is sole reality: all gods, being, non-being, knowledge, ignorance, doctrine, and opposition are made of him. The six paths (adhvan: kalā, tattva, bhuvana, varṇa, mantra, pada) become one in Bhairavī's abode — this is the supreme goal (paryanta), the culmination where all tantras converge, ineffable (avācyā). Bhairavī is will (icchā), knowledge (jñāna), true action (satkriyā), immersed in consciousness-bliss (cidānanda) alone. She is the heart (hṛdaya) of Bhairava, the god of gods — the non-dual ground (avibhāgabhū). The text opens with rest in infinite power (anantaśaktiviśrānti) gone within beginning and end; the speech (vāc) is unspeakable (anuccāryā). From consciousness-nature, the god, reflecting by own self, obtains stages (bhūmikā) from the unattached ground to the end. There, the expressive (vācaka) and expressed (vācya) are one self-spanda (vibration) of the lord; the gross-subtle-supreme appearance-sequence is the sixfold path (ṣaḍvidhādhva).

## pary-p02 (vv. 8–14)

The cosmological descent: the sequence (krama) is space-time-self (deśakālātmā); difference-life (bhedaprāṇa) is the life of cognitions; from separation (viccheda), this makes the knower's varied appearance (vicitrābhāsatā). Pure (śuddha) and impure (aśuddha) differ by whether objects are externalized or immersed in I-ness (svāhantā). The Śāmbhavī creation is the clear appearance of thoughtlessness (avikalpasphuṭābhāsā); by non-difference (abhedena) in self-creation (ātmanirmāṇe), own-self (svātman) is made the instrument (karaṇīkṛta). Action-knowledge-self-consciousness-power (kriyājñānātmacicchakti) threads difference (bhedāsūtraṇa); reaching the kalā-vidyā stage, in difference-and-difference (bhedodbheda) again becomes. The pramāṇa (means of knowledge), buddhi (intellect), multitudes (prakarā) are the imaginations (vikalpanā) of action (kriyā).

## pary-p03 (vv. 15–20)

Limitation: limitation of action-intellect (karmabuddhyakṣatā) → paśu (bound soul). By will (icchā), Bhairava is measured (parimīyate). The limited function (mitavṛtti) follows power (śakti); from contracted state, Vidyeśa/Sādākhya/Śivahood; body-prāṇa-intellect-void knowership (dehaprāṇabuddhiśūnyapramātṛtva) is attained. His particular creation (asādhāraṇī sṛṣṭi); various imaginations (tattannānāvikalpanā). The knower (pramātā) is undistracted (avyagra), abides in own self (svātman), in thoughtless (avikalpa), consciousness-self (cidātman). Then the direct address: "child, see this" (vatsa paśya) — see this as non-dual (advayatvena) consciousness (saṃvid); the world is made of that (tanmayaṃ viśvam); other is trivial (tuccham), unreal (asad). No other means (ananyopāya); this is self-means (tadātmopāya).

## pary-p04 (vv. 21–27)

The method: withdraw all thought (saṃhṛtya sarvataś cintām), cultivate "I" (ahaṃ ity anuśīlayan), attain thoughtless own-consciousness (avikalpāṃ svasaṃvittiṃ) directly (pratyakṣāṃ). Even in accepting infinite forms, own-consciousness is one-character (ekalakṣaṇā); having entered that own-consciousness, thoughts are not to be imagined. The Khecari etc. four streams (catuḥsrota) flow into the full great lake (mahāhrada); the sky-goddess (vyomeśvarī), self-consciousness (ātmaciti), having dived (vigāhya), should agitate (broḍayet) the inert (jaḍam). Creation, maintenance, dissolution, concealment, grace — by sequence (kramāt), five acts (pañcakṛtya) doing, "Śiva I" (śivo 'ham iti), thus should trust (viśvaset). By guru's word (guruvaktreṇa), accomplished becomes. Having imagined (vikalpayitvā), world-self (viśvātmā) Bhairava I (bhairavo 'ham); mine indeed those world-modifications (viśvabhaṅgyo); as of fire (vahner), flame-modifications (jvālābhaṅgyaḥ), radiance (prabhā) of sun (raveḥ). One I (eko 'ham), subject (mātṛ), knowledge (vijñāna), measure (māna), measured (meya), having kinds (prakāravān); pure-knowledge etc. by form (śuddhabodhādirūpeṇa), Khecari etc. fourteen (khecaryādicaturdaśaḥ).

## pary-p05 (vv. 28–33)

Bondage and liberation (bandhamokṣau) are contemplated as the forms of illusion (medā) and its absence (amedā). One attains supreme freedom (svācchandyaṃ paramaṃ) through the confidence (visrambha) of "I am Bhairava" (bhairavo 'smīti). Inner action (antaḥkāra) and outer action (bahiṣkāra) are timeless (akāla) sequence (kramam) of self (ātman); the full-of-lord's-power (prābhavanirbharaḥ) holds them independent (aparādhīna). Supreme emission (anuttara visarga), self-rest (ātma viśrama), rise (udaya), pair (yāmala) — the union (saṃghaṭṭa) of Śiva and Śakti; between them, other powers (anyaśaktayaḥ). Bliss, will, lordship, opening, deficiency, and lordship of will; indistinct/distinct by difference; manifestation of known (vedyollāsa) of those two knowers (vidaḥ). Bliss by supreme two (ānandānuttarābhyāṃ), and junction (sandhībhāva), then feeling (vedanā); of knowns (vedyānāṃ), life-principle (prāṇabhūtā); thus full (pūrṇaṃ), that, Bhairava body (vapuḥ). Manifestation of known (vedyollāsa) this, in will (icchāyām) and in lordship (īśane) from one side (ekataḥ) abiding; by grossness (sthūlatvena), earth etc. element fivefoldness (pañcakatām) may go.

## pary-p06 (vv. 34–39)

Cosmological descent continues: from subtlety (sūkṣmatvena) to the five subtle elements (tanmātratām), then by instrumentality (karaṇatvena) from touch to the ten senses (dvipañcatām). Of knowability (vedyatāyāḥ), by covering (chādanena), subjecthood (vettṛtā) manifests in sequence; mind (mano), I-intellect-hood (ahaṃbuddhitām) of these, in non-differentiation (avibhāge) predominance (pradhānatām); personhood (puruṣatvaṃ) and contraction (saṃkoca), expansion (vikāsau), which in dependence (apekṣayā). Arose desired from union (saṃyogād) from double-form (dvirūpād) of both-self (ubhayātmanaḥ); from nature of will (icchāsvarūpād) from opening (unmeṣād) otherwise (anyathā) supreme (anuttara) from union (āyutāt). Four powers (catasraḥ śaktyaḥ) inner (antaḥsthāḥ) of spirit (puṃsaḥ) his (asya) inside (antaś) and (ca) supporters (dhārikāḥ) of consciousness-space (cidvyomnaḥ) of inert-ground (jaḍabhūmeś) and (ca) of Māyā-made (māyāmayyāḥ) like-Trishanku (triśaṅkuvat). Of will-power (icchāśaktes) of three-form (trirūpāyāḥ) three (tisraḥ) other (anyāḥ) powers (śaktyaḥ) became (abhavan); emission (visargaḥ) this (ayam) then (atha) exceedingly (atyarthaṃ) clear (sphuṭaḥ) became (abhūt) created (sṛjya) from expansion (vistarāt). This (etat) having created (sṛṣṭvā) of self-powers (ātmaśaktīnām) mutual (itaretara) from mixing (miśrāt) may become (bhavet) agitation (kṣobha) inner (antaraṃ) not (na) from this (asmāt) emission (visargād) outside (bāhyataḥ) may become (bhavet).

## pary-p07 (vv. 40–46)

So much all being (sat) is emitted (visṛjyate) from the supreme (anuttara) in Bhairava, by will (icchā) etc., having rested (viśramya) again and again (muhuḥ). Inner-hidden (antarnilīna) infinite (ananta) meaning (artha) own-self (svātma) reflection (āmarśa) consciousness (citi) supreme (parā) — one-time (ekavāra) from flow (vahāt) which (yā) this (iyam) mantra-knowing (mantrī) speech (vāk) pure (śuddha) agency (kartṛtā). Three-flow (trivahaṃ) three-path (tripathaṃ) three-placed (tristhaṃ) time (kālaṃ) that (sā) draws (karṣati) by itself (svataḥ) outside (bahis) then (tataś) and (ca) in self (ātmani) thus (iti) glorious-supreme (śrīparā) time-drawer (kālakarṣiṇī). Divided (bhedita) inner-made (antaḥkṛta) undivided (akhaṇḍa) state (bhāva) observation (avekṣā) higher-lower (parāparā) agency (kartṛtā) that (sā) indeed (eva) seeing (paśyantī) his (tasya) indeed (eva) lord (īśa) state (daśā) based (āśrayā). Middle (madhyamā) with sequence (sakramā) gross (sthūlā) in inner-organ (antaḥkaraṇavartinī) imagination (vikalpanā) undivided (abhinna) expressed (vācyā) prana-function (prāṇavṛtti) differentiated (vibheditā). Own-impression (svavāsanā) from such state (tādavasthya) of two states (daśayoḥ) of previous (pūrvayoḥ) also (api) Māyā of measure (māyāmātuḥ mitatvaṃ) may be (syāt) of seeing (paśyantyāś) and (ca) sequence (kramo) indeed (hi) subtle (aṇuḥ). Of those two (tayoḥ) having entered (āviśya) of states (daśayoḥ) by sequence (kramāc) quiet (śānta) of thoughts (vikalpayoḥ) blessed (dhanyaḥ) own-consciousness (svasaṃvid) expansion (vistāraṃ) wonderfully (sāścaryam) perceives (anupaśyati).

## pary-p08 (vv. 47–52)

The text closes. The vāc (speech) is the key. Vaikhari (audible speech) functions: place (sthāna), instrument (karaṇa), strike (āghāta), spirit (puṃ), activity (vyāpāra), nature (ātmikā) — whose these, these audible (śrutigocarāḥ). By these, mutually, knowledge transmission (saṃkrānti), self (ātmā), that (sa) worldly (laukikaḥ) usage (vyavahāraḥ), then (atha) scriptural (śāstrīyaḥ), various (nānā) contraction (saṃkoca), narrow (saṃkaṭaḥ). Thus (iti) pervasion-of-speech (vāgvyāpti) knowing (vidvāṃsam) this (etad) group (varga) presiding-deities (adhidevatāḥ) Māheśvarī-headed (māheśvarīmukhāḥ) goddesses (devyaḥ) in waking (jāgare) even (api) not (na) enjoy (bhuñjate). By means (upāyena) non-grasping (agrahaḥ) to-be-done (kāryaḥ) to-be-attained (upeyā) Bhairavi-state (bhairavīsthitiḥ) which (yā) that (asau) own-consciousness (svasaṃvit) that (tām) indeed (eva) all-means (sarvopāyām) should-enter (samāviśet). Beginning-end (ādyanta) inner (antargata) infinite (ananta) expressed (vācya) expressive (vācaka) full (nirbharam) secret (rahasyaṃ) of-mantras-and-mudras (mantramudrāṇāṃ) I-attain (prapadye) supreme (anuttaraṃ) greatness (mahaḥ). Of-Shambhu (śambhoḥ) undivided (abhinna) hearts (hṛdayāḥ) victorious (jayanti) gurus (guravaḥ) formerly (purā) without-rising (nirvyutthāna) absorption (samādhāna) obtained (prāpta) end (paryanta) prosperity (saṃpadaḥ).

## The parable (canonical draft, ≈87s spoken)

> The last bathhouse keeps no written rules. A student comes to
> study the unspeakable — the word read three ways, hidden, between
> and out-loud, is the Lord's own heart, filled with rest. The baths
> are warm in sequence: knowings sunder from one source and shine
> varied for each reader, and the measuring-keeper calls every limit
> by will — even on blessed Bhairava. Behind the last curtain he
> withdraws from all thought and meets thought face-to-face:
> Bhairava am I — the trust that walks supreme freedom over bondage
> and its cure, measured and unmeasured. The keeper, ten fingers,
> tools five subtleties through touch. Steam pours from the pool
> rested twice. The outer speech does its doings, audible to all —
> and in that audible doing the study is complete: he conquers.

**Map (hinge):**
- no written rules; study the unspeakable; word three ways; Lord's
  heart, rest-filled → p01 *rest-filled unspeakable studied as
  heart; vīra of thrice-made* (para/madhyamā/vaikharī kept plain:
  hidden/between/out-loud) [p01]
- baths warm in sequence; knowings sunder, shine varied per reader →
  p02 *sundered knowings varied-shine for knower* [p02]
- measuring-keeper calls limits by will, even on Bhairava → p03
  *limited called bound; willed measured* [p03]
- withdraws behind curtain; meets thought face-to-face → p04
  *withdrawn tending attains face-to-face* (consciousness meets
  itself — no second person) [p04]
- Bhairava am I; trust; supreme freedom over bondage and cure,
  measured and unmeasured → p05 *measured-unmeasured; bondage-
  liberation contemplated; supreme freedom trusted* [p05]
- keeper, ten fingers, five subtleties through touch → p06
  *subtle-five tooled ten by touch* [p06]
- steam from the pool rested twice → p07 *rested-rested emitted
  indeed* [p07]
- outer speech's doings audible; study complete; he conquers → p08
  *heard Vaikharī doings; jaya-śānta conquering close* [p08]

**Limit:** the triad-speech (thrice-made) folded as plain hidden/
between/out-loud (flagged); triśaṅku-hung and teeming-imagination
of p06 folded into the five-subtleties (never contradicted); the
am-ī kept (second such declaration in the set — hers, stated as
trust not shout).
**Rasa:** maṅgala-adhyayana (the study opens) → adbhuta (varied
shine) → vīra (measure, meeting, the declaration) → utsāha (tooled
ten) → śānta (emission from rest) → jaya close (conquering = the
completed study). Parable's last line lands the p08 sthāyibhāva.
**Bar check:** two figures (student + keeper-merging-measurer +
masseur = ONE attendant, merged); retellable; plain (Vaikharī→outer
speech; tanmātra→five subtleties; am-ī stated as trust).

## The seal — statement

**The rest-pool that steams the study:** interior vertical —
bottom: the double-rested pool, perfectly still, its steam rising
(the emission from rest); mid: colored light-bands falling through
high slats (knowings sundered, shining varied), the measuring-
keeper beside five lapis-oil jars, ten-fingered at a shoulder;
behind a last half-drawn curtain the student faces HIS OWN light-
form — a soft gold flash between them (thought meeting thought);
above: speech-doing sound-rings at the entrance (outer speech
audible), steam reaching a dome with one small open oculus, calm
sky showing through: the study complete.

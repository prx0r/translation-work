# Paryantapañcāśikā pary-p02 — ARGUMENT MAP (cosmological descent: sequence, pure/impure, knowers)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 pary-p02 = vv. 8–14. Lore: Timalsina 2023; Falsafa.ai translation. Key: the sequence (krama) is space-time-self; difference-life (bhedaprāṇa) makes the knower's varied appearance. Pure (śuddha) and impure (aśuddha) differ by whether objects are externalized or immersed in I-ness. The Śāmbhavī creation is the clear appearance of thoughtlessness (avikalpasphuṭābhāsā). Action-knowledge-self-consciousness-power threads difference; kalā-vidyā stage; then bheda-in-bheda; then pramāṇa-buddhi-multitudes; then paśu (bound soul).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

The cosmological descent: how does the one (Bhairava) become the many (the world)? The sequence (krama) is space-time-self; difference-life (bhedaprāṇa) makes the knower's varied appearance. Pure (śuddha) and impure (aśuddha) differ by whether objects are externalized or immersed in I-ness. The Śāmbhavī creation is the clear appearance of thoughtlessness (avikalpasphuṭābhāsā). Action-knowledge-self-consciousness-power threads difference; kalā-vidyā stage; then bheda-in-bheda; then pramāṇa-buddhi-multitudes; then paśu (bound soul).

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — the sequence (v. 8):**
> *kramo yo deśakālātmā bhedaprāṇas sa saṃvidām | vicchedād eṣa vijñātur vicitrābhāsatākṛtaḥ*
[i.e. the sequence which is space-time-self, difference-life, that of cognitions; from separation, this of knower, makes varied appearance.] The sequence (krama) is space-time-self (deśakālātmā); difference-life (bhedaprāṇa) is the life of cognitions; from separation (viccheda), this makes the knower's varied appearance (vicitrābhāsatā).

**Step 1 — pure and impure (v. 9):**
> *ubhayo tatra vai rītiḥ śuddhāśuddhātmabhedataḥ | śuddhir bahiṣkṛtārthānāṃ svāhantāyāṃ nimajjanam*
[i.e. of both there indeed way, pure-impure self-difference; purity, of externalized-objects, in I-ness, immersion.] Of both (pure and impure), there is a way (rīti); the difference is pure/impure self. Purity: externalized objects immersed in I-ness (svāhantāyāṃ nimajjanam).

**Step 2 — impurity (v. 10):**
> *aśuddhir api magnānāṃ naivaṃ hṛdayaviśramaḥ | atrāntare 'py anantās syuḥ sthitayas tāratamyataḥ*
[i.e. impurity also of immersed, not thus heart-rest; in-between also infinite would be states by degrees.] Impurity: of immersed (magnānāṃ), not thus heart-rest (hṛdayaviśrama). In-between, infinite states by degrees (tāratamya).

**Step 3 — knowers (v. 11):**
> *ucitocitameyānāṃ pramātṝṇāṃ prakāśane | sādhāraṇānyathābhāvau bhidyete mātṛmeyayoḥ*
[i.e. of proper-and-proper-measured knowers, in manifestation; common and otherwise-states are divided, subject and object.] Of proper and proper-measured knowers (pramātṛ), in manifestation (prakāśane); common and otherwise-states are divided (bhidyete), subject (mātṛ) and object (meya).

**Step 4 — Śāmbhavī creation (v. 12):**
> *avikalpasphuṭābhāsā sā sṛṣṭiḥ śāmbhavī matā | abhedenātmanirmāṇe svātmaiva karaṇīkṛtaḥ*
[i.e. without-thought clear appearance, that creation, of Śambhu, held; by non-difference in self-creation, own-self indeed made instrument.] The Śāmbhavī creation (sṛṣṭi) is the clear appearance of thoughtlessness (avikalpasphuṭābhāsā). By non-difference (abhedena) in self-creation (ātmanirmāṇe), own-self (svātman) is made the instrument (karaṇīkṛta).

**Step 5 — action-knowledge (v. 13):**
> *kriyājñānātmacicchaktir bhedāsūtraṇakāraṇāt | kalāvidyāpadaṃ prāpya bhedodbhede punar bhavet*
[i.e. action-knowledge-self-consciousness-power, from cause of threading difference; kalā-vidyā stage having reached, in difference-and-difference again becomes.] Action-knowledge-self-consciousness-power (kriyājñānātmacicchakti) threads difference (bhedāsūtraṇa); reaching the kalā-vidyā stage, in difference-and-difference (bhedodbheda) again becomes.

**Step 6 — pramāṇa-buddhi (v. 14):**
> *pramāṇabuddhiprakarā yatkriyāyā vikalpanāḥ | saiva vicchinnavicchinnakriyā jñānodyamā yadā*
[i.e. means-of-knowledge, intellect, multitudes; which of action, imaginations; that indeed cut, cut, action, knowledge-effort, when.] The pramāṇa (means of knowledge), buddhi (intellect), multitudes (prakarā); which are the imaginations (vikalpanā) of action (kriyā); that indeed is cut-cut action, knowledge-effort (jñānodyama), when.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The cosmological descent is very dense; each verse compresses multiple tattvas. The T1 gloss is the primary source; the Sanskrit confirms.
2. The six paths (adhvan) are named in p01 (v. 4, v. 7) but the individual paths (kalā, tattva, bhuvana, varṇa, mantra, pada) are not enumerated in this passage.
3. The "pure" (śuddha) and "impure" (aśuddha) distinction (v. 9) is the key to the Trika's cosmology; the text does not fully explain it here.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the passage's own descent: the sequence (v. 8) → pure and impure (v. 9) → impurity (v. 10) → knowers (v. 11) → Śāmbhavī creation (v. 12) → action-knowledge (v. 13) → pramāṇa-buddhi (v. 14). The key move is v. 12: the Śāmbhavī creation is the clear appearance of thoughtlessness (avikalpasphuṭābhāsā); by non-difference in self-creation, own-self is made the instrument. And v. 8: the sequence (krama) is space-time-self; difference-life makes the knower's varied appearance. Carry both into p03 (limitation of action-intellect; bound soul; paśu). The tone is scholastic-cosmological: this is a map of the Trika system. Carry both into p03 (limitation; bound soul; paśu). Read it as the engine room: sequence, pure/impure sorting, thought-free making.

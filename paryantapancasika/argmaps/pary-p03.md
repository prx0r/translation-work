# Paryantapañcāśikā pary-p03 — ARGUMENT MAP (limitation; bound soul / paśu)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 pary-p03 = vv. 15–20. Lore: Timalsina 2023; Falsafa.ai translation. Key: limitation of action-intellect (karmabuddhyakṣatā) → paśu (bound soul); by will (icchā) Bhairava is measured (parimīyate); the limited knower (mitavṛtti) follows power; from contracted state, vidyeśa/sādākhya/śivatā; particular creation (asādhāraṇī sṛṣṭi); the knower (pramātā) is undistracted in own self (svātman) in thoughtless (avikalpa) consciousness-self (cidātman). Then: "child, see this" (vatsa paśya) — the world is made of consciousness; otherwise trivial/unreal. No other means (ananyopāya); this is self-means (tadātmopāya).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Limitation: how does the bound soul (paśu) arise? By will (icchā), Bhairava is measured (parimīyate) — the limited knower (mitavṛtti) follows power. From contracted state, vidyeśa/sādākhya/śivatā. Particular creation (asādhāraṇī sṛṣṭi). The knower (pramātā) is undistracted in own self (svātman) in thoughtless (avikalpa) consciousness-self (cidātman). Then the direct address: "child, see this" (vatsa paśya) — the world is made of consciousness; otherwise trivial/unreal. No other means (ananyopāya); this is self-means (tadātmopāya).

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — limitation (v. 15):**
> *karmabuddhyakṣatāṃ prāptā tadāsau paśur ucyate | itīcchayā sa bhagavān bhairavaḥ parimīyate*
[i.e. limitation of action-intellect having obtained, then that bound soul is called; thus by will, he, blessed, Bhairava, is measured.] Limitation of action-intellect (karmabuddhyakṣatā) → paśu (bound soul). By will (icchā), Bhairava is measured (parimīyate).

**Step 1 — limited function (v. 16):**
> *mitavṛttis satī sā ca śaktis tam anuvartate | tasya vidyeśasādākhyaśivatās saṃkucatsthiteḥ | dehaprāṇabuddhiśūnyapramātṛtvam upāgaman*
[i.e. limited-function being, that, and power, him, follows; his Vidyeśa-Sādākhya-Śivahood, from contracted state; body-prāṇa-intellect-void knowership attained.] The limited function (mitavṛtti) follows power (śakti); from contracted state, Vidyeśa/Sādākhya/Śivahood; body-prāṇa-intellect-void knowership (dehaprāṇabuddhiśūnyapramātṛtva) is attained.

**Step 2 — particular creation (v. 17):**
> *tasyāsādhāraṇī sṛṣṭis tattannānāvikalpanā | śaivasargotthasaṃskārasaṃskṛtasya tadāśrayā*
[i.e. his particular creation; that and that various imagination; Śaiva creation-arisen impression, of purified, dependent on that.] His particular creation (asādhāraṇī sṛṣṭi); various imaginations (tattannānāvikalpanā); Śaiva creation-arisen impression (śaivasargotthasaṃskāra), of purified (saṃskṛtasya), dependent on that (tadāśrayā).

**Step 3 — the knower (v. 18):**
> *pramātaivāyam etāvatprakāro 'vagrahotsave | avyagro vartate svātmany avikalpe cidātmake*
[i.e. knower indeed this, of such kind, at festival of limitation; undistracted abides in own self, in thoughtless, consciousness-self.] The knower (pramātā) is of such kind, at the festival of limitation (avagrahotsava); undistracted (avyagra), abides in own self (svātman), in thoughtless (avikalpa), consciousness-self (cidātman).

**Step 4 — child, see this (v. 19):**
> *vatsa paśya tvam evaitat advayatvena saṃvidaḥ | viditaṃ tanmayaṃ viśvam anyat tuccham asad yataḥ*
[i.e. child, see, you indeed this; as non-dual, of consciousness; known, of that-made, world; other, trivial, unreal, because.] "Child, see this" (vatsa paśya) — see this as non-dual (advayatvena) consciousness (saṃvid); the world is made of that (tanmayaṃ viśvam); other is trivial (tuccham), unreal (asad), because.

**Step 5 — no other means (v. 20):**
> *kim upāyam idaṃ tattvaṃ yato 'nyan naiva sambhavi | ananyopāyam etat tadātmopāyaṃ pracakṣate*
[i.e. what means, this principle, from which other not indeed possible; without-other-means, this, self-means, is called.] What means? This principle (tattva), from which other is not possible. Without other means (ananyopāya), this is self-means (tadātmopāya).

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The "festival of limitation" (avagrahotsava) — the T1 gloss renders it as "at festival of limitation"; the Sanskrit *avagraha* = "limitation/restriction"; *utsava* = "festival." The image: limitation itself is a festival (a celebration of the self's contraction).
2. The "particular creation" (asādhāraṇī sṛṣṭi) — the T1 gloss renders it as "his particular creation"; the Sanskrit *asādhāraṇī* = "particular/individual"; *sṛṣṭi* = "creation." The text says the limited knower's creation is particular, not universal.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the passage's own descent: limitation (v. 15) → limited function (v. 16) → particular creation (v. 17) → the knower (v. 18) → child, see this (v. 19) → no other means (v. 20). The key move is v. 19: "child, see this" (vatsa paśya) — the direct address to the student; the world is made of consciousness (tanmayaṃ viśvam); other is trivial, unreal. And v. 20: no other means (ananyopāya); this is self-means (tadātmopāya). Carry both into p04 (the method: saṃhṛtya sarvataś cintām; ananyopāyam etat). Read it as the turn: measured Lord, addressed child, self as means.<|end|>

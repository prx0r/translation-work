---

## 1. What is at issue

The method: how does one attain the non-dual consciousness? By withdrawing all thought (*saṃhṛtya sarvataś cintām*), cultivating thoughtless own-consciousness (*avikalpāṃ svasaṃvittiṃ*), attaining direct perception (*pratyakṣāṃ pratipadyate*). Even in infinite forms, own-consciousness is one-character (*ekalakṣaṇā*). The Khecari etc. four streams flow into the great lake of sky-goddess (*vyomeśvarī*); self-consciousness (*ātmaciti*) dives and agitates the inert. Creation, maintenance, dissolution, concealment, grace — the five acts (*pañcakṛtya*) by sequence (*kramāt*). By guru's word (*guruvaktreṇa*), accomplished becomes. Having imagined, world-self Bhairava I; mine indeed those world-modifications, as of fire, flame-modifications, radiance of sun. One I, subject, knowledge, measure, measured, having kinds; pure-knowledge etc. by form, Khecari etc. fourteen.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — withdrawing thought (v. 21):**
> *saṃhṛtya sarvataś cintām [aha]m ity anuśīlayan | avikalpāṃ svasaṃvittiṃ pratyakṣāṃ pratipadyate*
[i.e. having withdrawn all thought, "I" thus cultivating, thoughtless own-consciousness, direct, attains.] The method: withdraw all thought (*saṃhṛtya sarvataś cintām*), cultivate "I" (*ahaṃ ity anuśīlayan*), attain thoughtless own-consciousness (*avikalpāṃ svasaṃvittiṃ*) directly (*pratyakṣāṃ*).

**Step 1 — one-character in infinite forms (v. 22):**
> *anantaitāvadākārasvīkāre 'py ekalakṣaṇām | tāṃ svasaṃvidam āviśya vikalpān na vikalpayet*
[i.e. in infinite such-forms acceptance even, one-character; that own-consciousness having entered, thoughts not to be imagined.] Even in accepting infinite forms, own-consciousness is one-character (*ekalakṣaṇā*); having entered that own-consciousness, thoughts are not to be imagined.

**Step 2 — Khecari (v. 23):**
> *khecaryādicatuḥsrotovāhapūrṇamahāhradām | vyomeśvarīm ātmacitiṃ vigāhya broḍayej jaḍam*
[i.e. Khecari etc. four streams flow, full great lake, sky-goddess, self-consciousness, having dived, should agitate the inert.] The Khecari etc. four streams (*catuḥsrota*) flow into the full great lake (*mahāhrada*); the sky-goddess (*vyomeśvarī*), self-consciousness (*ātmaciti*), having dived (*vigāhya*), should agitate (*broḍayet*) the inert (*jaḍam*).

**Step 3 — five acts (v. 24):**
> *sargasthāpanasaṃhāranigrahānugrahakramāt | pañcakṛtyāni kurvāṇaḥ śivo 'ham iti viśvaset*
[i.e. creation, maintenance, dissolution, concealment, grace, by sequence, five acts doing, Śiva I, thus should trust.] Creation, maintenance, dissolution, concealment, grace — by sequence (*kramāt*), five acts (*pañcakṛtya*) doing, "Śiva I" (*śivo 'ham iti*), thus should trust (*viśvaset*).

**Step 4 — by guru's word (v. 25):**
> *kṣepajñānaprasaṃkhyā[naga]tinādakrameṇa vā | guruvaktreṇa tāny ātmany ākalayya kṛtī bhavet*
[i.e. by sequence of projection-knowledge-enumeration, by guru's word, those, in self, having considered, accomplished becomes.] By sequence of projection-knowledge-enumeration (*kṣepajñānaprasaṃkhyā*), by guru's word (*guruvaktreṇa*), those (five acts), in self (*ātmany*), having considered (*ākalayya*), accomplished (*kṛtī*) becomes.

**Step 5 — world-self Bhairava (v. 26):**
> *vikalpayitvā viśvātmā bhairavo 'haṃ mamaiva tāḥ | viśvabhaṅgyo yathā vahner jvālābhaṅgyaḥ prabhā raveḥ*
[i.e. having imagined, world-self Bhairava I, mine indeed those world-modifications; as of fire, flame-modifications, radiance of sun.] Having imagined (*vikalpayitvā*), world-self (*viśvātmā*) Bhairava I (*bhairavo 'ham*); mine indeed those world-modifications (*viśvabhaṅgyo*); as of fire (*vahner*), flame-modifications (*jvālābhaṅgyaḥ*), radiance (*prabhā*) of sun (*raveḥ*).

**Step 6 — one I (v. 27):**
> *eko 'haṃ mātṛvijñānamānameyaprakāravān | śuddhabodhādirūpeṇa khecaryādicaturdaśaḥ*
[i.e. one I, subject, knowledge, measure, measured, having kinds; pure-knowledge etc. by form, Khecari etc. fourteen.] One I (*eko 'ham*), subject (*mātṛ*), knowledge (*vijñāna*), measure (*māna*), measured (*meya*), having kinds (*prakāravān*); pure-knowledge etc. by form (*śuddhabodhādirūpeṇa*), Khecari etc. fourteen (*khecaryādicaturdaśaḥ*).

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The "Khecari etc. four streams" (*khecaryādicatuḥsrota*) — the T1 gloss renders it as "Khecari-etc four streams"; the Sanskrit *khecari* = "sky-going" (a goddess/śakti); *catuḥsrota* = "four streams." The text does not enumerate the four streams.
2. The "Khecari etc. fourteen" (*khecaryādicaturdaśaḥ*) — the T1 gloss renders it as "Khecari-etc fourteen"; the Sanskrit *caturdaśa* = "fourteen." The text does not enumerate the fourteen.
3. The "sequence of projection-knowledge-enumeration" (*kṣepajñānaprasaṃkhyā*) — the T1 gloss renders it as "by sequence of projection-knowledge-enumeration"; the Sanskrit is compressed. The text does not elaborate.

## 4. Decision for L2

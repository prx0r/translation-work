Inner action (antaḥkāra) and outer action (bahiṣkāra) are timeless (akāla) sequence (kramam) of self (ātman); the full-of-lord's-power (prābhavanirbharaḥ) holds them independent. Supreme emission (anuttara visarga), self-rest (ātma viśrama), rise (udaya), pair (yāmala) — union (saṃghaṭṭa) of Śiva and Śakti; between them, other powers (anyaśaktayaḥ). ## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — bandhamokṣa (v. 28):**
> *medāmedaprakāreṇa bandhamokṣau vibhāvayan | bhairavo 'smīti visrambhāt svācchandyaṃ paramaṃ vrajet*
[i.e. bondage and liberation contemplated as forms of illusion and its absence; "I am Bhairava" by confidence, supreme freedom should go.] Bondage and liberation are contemplated as the forms of illusion (medā) and its absence (amedā). One attains supreme freedom (svācchandyaṃ paramaṃ) through the confidence (visrambha) of "I am Bhairava" (bhairavo 'smīti).

**Step 1 — inner/outer action (v. 29):**
> *antaḥkārabahiṣkārāv akālakramam ātmanaḥ | aparādhīnam ādhatse yat tvaṃ prābhavanirbharaḥ*
[i.e. inner action and outer action, timeless sequence of self; independent, you hold, which you, full of lord's power.] Inner action (antaḥkāra) and outer action (bahiṣkāra) are timeless (akāla) sequence (kramam) of self (ātman); the full-of-lord's-power (prābhavanirbharaḥ) holds them independent (aparādhīna).

**Step 2 — supreme emission (v. 30):**
> *anuttaravisargātmaviśramodayayāmalam | saṃghaṭṭaś śivaśaktyos tadantare cānyaśaktayaḥ*
[i.e. supreme emission, self-rest, rise, pair; union of Śiva and Śakti; between them, other powers.] Supreme emission (anuttara visarga), self-rest (ātma viśrama), rise (udaya), pair (yāmala) — the union (saṃghaṭṭa) of Śiva and Śakti; between them, other powers (anyaśaktayaḥ).

**Step 3 — bliss, will, lordship (v. 31):**
> *ānandeccheśanonmeṣā ūnatā ceśanecchayoḥ | asphuṭasphuṭabhedena vedyollāsas tayor vidaḥ*
[i.e. bliss, will, lordship, opening; deficiency and lordship of will; indistinct/distinct by difference; manifestation of known of those two knowers.] Bliss (ānanda), will (icchā), lordship (īśana), opening (unmeṣa); deficiency (ūnatā) and lordship of will (īśanecchayoḥ); indistinct (asphuṭa)/distinct (sphuṭa) by difference (bhedena); manifestation of known (vedyollāsa) of those two knowers (vidaḥ).

**Step 4 — bliss by supreme two (v. 32):**
> *ānandānuttarābhyāṃ ca sandhībhāvo 'tha vedanā | vedyānāṃ prāṇabhūteti pūrṇaṃ tad bhairavaṃ vapuḥ*
[i.e. bliss by supreme two, and junction, then feeling; of knowns, life-principle; thus full, that, Bhairava body.] Bliss by supreme two (ānandānuttarābhyāṃ), and junction (sandhībhāva), then feeling (vedanā); of knowns (vedyānāṃ), life-principle (prāṇabhūtā); thus full (pūrṇaṃ), that, Bhairava body (vapuḥ).

**Step 5 — manifestation of known (v. 33):**
> *vedyollāso 'yam icchāyām īśane caikataḥ sthitaḥ | sthūlatvena pṛthivyādibhūtapañcakatām iyāt*
[i.e. manifestation of known this, in will and in lordship from one side abiding; by grossness, earth etc. element fivefoldness may go.] Manifestation of known (vedyollāsa) this, in will (icchāyām) and in lordship (īśane) from one side (ekataḥ) abiding; by grossness (sthūlatvena), earth etc. element fivefoldness (pañcakatām) may go.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The cosmological descent is very dense; each verse compresses multiple tattvas. The T1 gloss is the primary source; the Sanskrit confirms.
2. The "supreme emission" (anuttara visarga) — the T1 gloss renders it as "supreme emission"; the Sanskrit *anuttara* = "supreme/unsurpassed"; *visarga* = "emission/creation." The image: the supreme emission is the act of creation from the supreme.
3. The "pair" (yāmala) — the T1 gloss renders it as "pair"; the Sanskrit *yāmala* = "pair/couple." The image: Śiva and Śakti as a pair/couple.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the passage's own close: bandhamokṣa (v. 28) → inner/outer action (v. 29) → supreme emission (v. 30) → bliss, will, lordship (v. 31) → bliss by supreme two (v. 32) → manifestation of known (v. 33). The key move is v. 28: bondage and liberation are contemplated as the forms of illusion and its absence; one attains supreme freedom through the confidence of "I am Bhairava." And v. 30: supreme emission, self-rest, rise, pair — the union of Śiva and Śakti. Carry both into p06 (cosmological descent: subtlety, elements, knowability).

## 1. What is at issue

The cosmological descent continues: from subtlety (sūkṣmatvena) to the five subtle elements (tanmātratām), then by instrumentality (karaṇatvena) from touch to the ten senses (dvipañcatām). Of knowability (vedyatāyāḥ), by covering (chādanena), subjecthood (vettṛtā) manifests in sequence; mind (mano), I-intellect-hood (ahaṃbuddhitām) of these, in non-differentiation (avibhāge) predominance (pradhānatām); personhood (puruṣatvaṃ) and contraction (saṃkoca), expansion (vikāsau), which in dependence (apekṣayā). Arose desired from union (saṃyogād) from double-form (dvirūpād) of both-self (ubhayātmanaḥ); from nature of will (icchāsvarūpād) from opening (unmeṣād) otherwise (anyathā) supreme (anuttara) from union (āyutāt). Four powers (catasraḥ śaktyaḥ) inner (antaḥsthāḥ) of spirit (puṃsaḥ) his (asya) inside (antaś) and (ca) supporters (dhārikāḥ) of consciousness-space (cidvyomnaḥ) of inert-ground (jaḍabhūmeḥ) and (ca) of Māyā-made (māyāmayyāḥ) like-Trishanku (triśaṅkuvat). Of will-power (icchāśaktes) of three-form (trirūpāyāḥ) three (tisraḥ) other (anyāḥ) powers (śaktyaḥ) became (abhavan); emission (visargaḥ) this (ayam) then (atha) exceedingly (atyarthaṃ) clear (sphuṭaḥ) became (abhūt) created (sṛjya) from expansion (vistarāt). This (etat) having created (sṛṣṭvā) of self-powers (ātmaśaktīnām) mutual (itaretara) from mixing (miśrāt) may become (bhavet) agitation (kṣobha) inner (antaraṃ) not (na) from this (asmāt) emission (visargād) outside (bāhyataḥ) may become (bhavet).

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — subtlety to tanmātras (v. 34):**
> *sūkṣmatvena ca gandhādipañcatanmātratāṃ tataḥ*
[i.e. by subtlety, and smell etc. five tanmātras then.] By subtlety (sūkṣmatvena), smell etc. five subtle elements (pañcatanmātratām) then.

**Step 1 — instrumentality (v. 34 cont.):**
> *karaṇatvena saṃsparśād indriyāṇāṃ dvipañcatām*
[i.e. by instrumentality, from touch, of senses, tenfoldness.] By instrumentality (karaṇatvena), from touch (saṃsparśād), of senses (indriyāṇāṃ), tenfoldness (dvipañcatām).

**Step 2 — knowability and subjecthood (v. 35):**
> *vedyatāyāḥ chādanena vettṛtodbhāsanakrame | mano 'haṃbuddhitāṃ eṣām avibhāge pradhānatām | puruṣatvaṃ ca saṃkocavikāsau yadapekṣayā*
[i.e. of knowability, by covering, subjecthood manifestation in sequence; mind, I-intellect-hood of these, in non-differentiation predominance; personhood and contraction, expansion which in dependence.] Of knowability (vedyatā), by covering (chādana), subjecthood (vettṛtā) manifests in sequence (udbhāsanakrama). Mind (mano), I-intellect-hood (ahaṃbuddhitā) of these, in non-differentiation (avibhāge) predominance (pradhānatā). Personhood (puruṣatva) and contraction (saṃkoca), expansion (vikāsa), which in dependence (apekṣayā).

**Step 3 — arose desired (v. 36):**
> *abhūvann iṣyasaṃyogād dvirūpād ubhayātmanaḥ | icchāsvarūpād unmeṣād anyathānuttarāyutāt*
[i.e. arose desired from union, from double-form of both-self; from nature of will, from opening, otherwise supreme, from union.] Arose (abhūvan) desired (iṣya) from union (saṃyogād), from double-form (dvirūpād) of both-self (ubhayātmanaḥ); from nature of will (icchāsvarūpād), from opening (unmeṣād), otherwise (anyathā) supreme (anuttara), from union (āyutāt).

**Step 4 — four powers (v. 37):**
> *catasraś śaktyo 'ntaḥsthāḥ puṃso 'syāntaś ca dhārikāḥ | cidvyomno jaḍabhūmeś ca māyāmayyāḥ triśaṅkuvat*
[i.e. four powers inner of spirit his inside and supporters of consciousness-space of inert-ground and of Māyā-made like-Trishanku.] Four powers (catasraḥ śaktyaḥ) inner (antaḥsthāḥ) of spirit (puṃsaḥ) his (asya) inside (antaś) and (ca) supporters (dhārikāḥ) of consciousness-space (cidvyomnaḥ) of inert-ground (jaḍabhūmeś) and (ca) of Māyā-made (māyāmayyāḥ) like-Trishanku (triśaṅkuvat).

**Step 5 — will-power's three (v. 38):**
> *icchāśaktes trirūpāyās tisro 'nyāś śaktyo 'bhavan | visargo 'yam athātyarthaṃ sphuṭo 'bhūt sṛjya vistarāt*
[i.e. of will-power of three-form, three other powers became; emission this then exceedingly clear became, created from expansion.] Of will-power (icchāśaktes) of three-form (trirūpāyāḥ), three (tisraḥ) other (anyāḥ) powers (śaktyaḥ) became (abhavan); emission (visargaḥ) this (ayam) then (atha) exceedingly (atyarthaṃ) clear (sphuṭaḥ) became (abhūt), created (sṛjya) from expansion (vistarāt).

**Step 6 — creation (v. 39):**
> *etat sṛṣṭvātmaśaktīnām itaretaramiśrāt | bhavet kṣobhāntaraṃ nāsmād visargād bāhyataḥ bhavet*
[i.e. this having created, of self-powers, mutual from mixing, may become agitation inner; not from this emission outside may become.] This (etat) having created (sṛṣṭvā) of self-powers (ātmaśaktīnām) mutual (itaretara) from mixing (miśrāt), may become (bhavet) agitation (kṣobha) inner (antaraṃ); not (na) from this (asmāt) emission (visargād) outside (bāhyataḥ) may become (bhavet).

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The cosmological descent is very dense; each verse compresses multiple tattvas. The T1 gloss is the primary source; the Sanskrit confirms.
2. The "four powers" (catasraḥ śaktyaḥ) of v. 37 — the T1 gloss renders them as "four powers inner of spirit"; the Sanskrit *catasraḥ śaktyo 'ntaḥsthāḥ puṃso 'syāntaś ca dhārikāḥ* = "four powers inner of spirit his inside and supporters." The text does not enumerate them individually.
3. The "three other powers" (tisro 'nyāḥ śaktyaḥ) of v. 38 — the T1 gloss renders them as "three other powers became"; the Sanskrit *icchāśaktes trirūpāyās tisro 'nyāś śaktyo 'bhavan* = "of will-power of three-form, three other powers became." The text does not enumerate them individually.
4. The "Trishanku" analogy (triśaṅkuvat) of v. 37 — the T1 gloss renders it as "like-Trishanku"; the Sanskrit *triśaṅkuvat* = "like Trishanku" (a mythological figure suspended between heaven and earth). The image: the four powers are suspended between consciousness-space and inert-ground, like Trishanku.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the passage's own descent: subtlety to tanmātras (v. 34) → instrumentality (v. 34 cont.) → knowability and subjecthood (v. 35) → arose desired (v. 36) → four powers (v. 37) → will-power's three (v. 38) → creation (v. 39). The key move is v. 35: mind (mano), I-intellect-hood (ahaṃbuddhitā) of these, in non-differentiation (avibhāge) predominance (pradhānatā); personhood (puruṣatvaṃ) and contraction (saṃkoca), expansion (vikāsa), which in dependence (apekṣayā). And v. 37: four powers (catasraḥ śaktyaḥ) inner of spirit, supporters of consciousness-space and inert-ground, like Trishanku. Carry both into p07 (the vāc: inner-hidden, four streams, etc.).

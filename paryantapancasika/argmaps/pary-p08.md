## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — Vaikhari (v. 47):**
> *sā sthānakaraṇāghātapuṃvyāpārātmikā satī | vaikharī vṛttayaḥ yasyāstā imāḥ śrutigocarāḥ*
[i.e. that place, instrument, strike, spirit, activity, nature, being, Vaikhari, functions, whose, these, these, audible.] Vaikhari (audible speech) functions: place (sthāna), instrument (karaṇa), strike (āghāta), spirit (puṃ), activity (vyāpāra), nature (ātmikā) — whose these, these audible (śrutigocarāḥ).

**Step 1 — transmission (v. 48):**
> *ābhiḥ parasparaṃ jñānasaṃkrāntyātmā sa laukikaḥ | vyavahāro 'tha śāstrīyo nānāsaṃkocasaṃkaṭaḥ*
[i.e. by these mutually knowledge transmission self that worldly usage then scriptural various contraction narrow.] By these (ābhiḥ), mutually (parasparaṃ), knowledge transmission (jñānasaṃkrānti), self (ātmā), that (sa) worldly (laukikaḥ) usage (vyavahāraḥ), then (atha) scriptural (śāstrīyaḥ), various (nānā) contraction (saṃkoca), narrow (saṃkaṭaḥ). The worldly (laukika) and scriptural (śāstrīya) usage, with various contraction (saṃkoca) and narrowness (saṃkaṭa).

**Step 2 — presiding deities (v. 49):**
> *iti vāgvyāpti vidvāṃsam etadvargādhidevatāḥ | māheśvarīmukhā devyo jāgare 'pi na bhuñjate*
[i.e. thus pervasion-of-speech knowing this group presiding-deities Māheśvarī-headed goddesses in waking even not enjoy.] Thus (iti) pervasion-of-speech (vāgvyāpti) knowing (vidvāṃsam) this (etad) group (varga) presiding-deities (adhidevatāḥ) Māheśvarī-headed (māheśvarīmukhāḥ) goddesses (devyaḥ) in waking (jāgare) even (api) not (na) enjoy (bhuñjate). The presiding deities (Māheśvarī-headed goddesses) do not enjoy (bhuñjate) in waking (jāgare) — even the waking state is not the full experience.

**Step 3 — the upāya (v. 50):**
> *upāyenāgrahaḥ kārya upeyā bhairavīsthitiḥ | yāsau svasaṃvit tām eva sarvopāyām samāviśet*
[i.e. by means non-grasping to-be-done to-be-attained Bhairavi-state which that own-consciousness that indeed all-means should-enter.] By means (upāyena) non-grasping (agrahaḥ) to-be-done (kāryaḥ) to-be-attained (upeyā) Bhairavi-state (bhairavīsthitiḥ) which (yā) that (asau) own-consciousness (svasaṃvit) that (tām) indeed (eva) all-means (sarvopāyām) should-enter (samāviśet). The upāya (means) is non-grasping (agraha); the to-be-attained (upeyā) is the Bhairavi-state (bhairavīsthiti); own-consciousness (svasaṃvit) is all-means (sarvopāya); one should enter (samāviśet) that.

**Step 4 — the secret (v. 51):**
> *ādyantāntargatānantavācyavācakanirbharam | rahasyaṃ mantramudrāṇāṃ prapadye 'nuttaraṃ mahaḥ*
[i.e. beginning-end inner infinite expressed expressive full secret of-mantras-and-mudras I-attain supreme greatness.] Beginning-end (ādyanta) inner (antargata) infinite (ananta) expressed (vācya) expressive (vācaka) full (nirbharam) secret (rahasyaṃ) of-mantras-and-mudras (mantramudrāṇāṃ) I-attain (prapadye) supreme (anuttaraṃ) greatness (mahaḥ). The secret (rahasya) of mantras and mudrās: the beginning and end are inner, infinite, expressed and expressive are full. I attain (prapadye) the supreme greatness (anuttaraṃ mahaḥ).

**Step 5 — the gurus (v. 52):**
> *śambhoḥ abhinnahṛdayā jayanti guravaḥ purā | nirvyutthānasamādhānaprāptaparyantasaṃpadaḥ*
[i.e. of-Shambhu undivided hearts victorious gurus formerly without-rising absorption obtained end prosperity.] Of Śambhu (śambhoḥ) undivided (abhinna) hearts (hṛdayāḥ) victorious (jayanti) gurus (guravaḥ) formerly (purā) without-rising (nirvyutthāna) absorption (samādhāna) obtained (prāpta) end (paryanta) prosperity (saṃpadaḥ). The gurus (guravaḥ) of Śambhu's undivided hearts (abhinna hṛdayāḥ) are victorious (jayanti); formerly (purā) they obtained (prāpta) the end (paryanta) prosperity (saṃpadaḥ) through without-rising (nirvyutthāna) absorption (samādhāna).

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The vāc (speech) levels — paśyantī, madhyamā, vaikharī — are implied by the T1 gloss ("three-flow three-path three-placed" and "middle with sequence gross in inner-organ"). The text does not name them explicitly.
2. The "secret" (rahasya) of mantras and mudrās (v. 51) — the T1 gloss renders it as "secret of mantras and mudrās"; the Sanskrit *rahasyaṃ mantramudrāṇāṃ* = "secret of mantras and mudrās." The text does not elaborate.
3. The "beginning-end inner infinite" (ādyantāntargatānanta) — the T1 gloss renders it as "beginning-end inner infinite"; the Sanskrit *ādyantāntargatānanta* = "beginning-end inner infinite." The image: the beginning and end are inner, infinite.

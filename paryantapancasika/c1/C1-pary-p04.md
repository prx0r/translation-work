# C1 — pary-p04 (pull back, tend, meet)

## SUMMARY

All-thought withdrawn, thought-free tended, face-to-face attained. Infinite-formed welcomed, one-marked entered; imagine-not vowed. Khecarī-streams flow full lakes; dived sky-Lady stirs inert. Sequenced five done trusted as Śiva-I. Cast-counted guru-worded weighed in self accomplishes. Imagined Bhairava-I owns world-modes like fire owns flames, sun shine. One measurer-measured-measure-measured kinded pure-formed, fourteen sky-goers.

# C1 — pary-p06 (thin, tool, count)

## SUMMARY

Subtle-five by fineness, ten senses by touch-tooling. Knowability veiled, knower shown sequentially; mind-I first undivided; ebb-flow dependent. Desired union arose double-selved will-natured opening; else supreme. Four inward powers bear inside, hung triśaṅku-like between knowing-space and inert Māyā-ground. Three-formed will-power bore three; emission clear-created widely. Self-powers made mixed may stir within, never from this outpour outside.

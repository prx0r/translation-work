# C1 — pary-p08 (hit, hold, hear)

## SUMMARY

Place-means-shocked spirited natured being; Vaikharī doings heard. Handed mutual knowing; common then bookish, straitened narrow. Speech-spread knowing host goddesses Maheśvarī-led unenjoying even waking. Way ungrasping to-do-to-gain Bhairavī-standing; own-knowing all-ways entered. Beginning-end-inner-boundless said-saying-full mystery of chant-seal attained unsurpassed. Shambhu's undivided hearts conquer of old, riseless-absorbed, end-endowed.

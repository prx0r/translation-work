# C1 — Paryantapañcāśikā (argument)

## pary-p01 (1–7) — sole reality; six paths one; heart

**SUMMARY.** Rest in beginning-and-end-enclosing infinite power opens onto unspeakable speech: the Lord's heart under study. One god holds all gods, one goddess holds being and non-being; knowing, unknowing, doctrine, even opposition are his stuff. Six paths converge where Bhairavī abides — the culmination of every tantra, beyond telling. She, will-knowing-right-doing sunk in nothing but bliss-awareness, is the undivided ground of the god of gods; from self-nature the reflecting Lord takes on grade after grade down to the last ground, expressive and expressed pulsing as one.

**KEY TERMS.** anantaśaktiviśrānti (infinite-power-rest), anuccārya vāc (unspeakable speech), yanmaya (made-of-him), aikadhya/ṣaḍadhvan (oneness/six-paths), paryanta (culmination), cidānanda (bliss-awareness), avibhāgabhū (undivided ground), bhūmikā (grades), spanda (pulsing).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 1 vows study (adhīye), not praise: rest-filled unspeakable speech as method. Verses 2–3 totalize by yanmaya — gods, goddess, being, non-being, knowing, unknowing, siddhānta, pūrvapakṣa: nothing outside the made-of-Him. Verse 4 names the convergence: six paths one (aikadhyam) in her abode, tantra-culmination (paryante sarvatantrāṇām) as avācyā paramā gatiḥ. Verse 5 seats will-knowing-doing inside bliss-awareness alone: hṛdaya as avibhāgabhūḥ. Verses 6–7 descend: citsvābhāvyāt, svātmanā vimṛṣan, the Lord takes grades anāśritādi to bhūmyanta; vācaka-vācya one spanda; gross-subtle-supreme sequencing as sixfold path.

## pary-p02 (8–14) — sequence, pure/impure, thought-free making

**SUMMARY.** Space-time-self sequencing with difference as its lifeblood divides the knower into varied shining. Two disciplines — pure and impure — turn on whether the outward-cast is drowned back in I-ness. Thought-free clear shining counts as Śambhu's making, the self made its own tool without duality. Doing-knowing power threads difference, reaches the art-and-lore station, redoubles into difference-upon-difference; then means, intellect, and imagined multitudes — action cut and recut until knowing strains upward.

**KEY TERMS.** krama/deśakāla (sequence/space-time), bhedaprāṇa (difference-lifeblood), śuddha/aśuddha (pure/impure), svāhantā-nimajjana (I-drowning), avikalpasphuṭābhāsa (thought-free clear shining), bhedodbheda (difference-upon-difference), jñānodyama (knowing-strain).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 8 defines krama as deśakālātmā and its engine bhedaprāṇa; viccheda yields vicitrābhāsatā. Verses 9–10 sort rīti by immersion: śuddhi = bahiṣkṛtārtha drowned in svāhantā; aśuddhi = merged without heart-rest; between, infinite graded stations. Verse 11 divides common/uncommon manifestation between seer and seen. Verse 12 founds Śāmbhavī making: avikalpa + sphuṭābhāsa, self as its own instrument (svātmaiva karaṇīkṛtaḥ) abhedena. Verse 13 threads difference through doing-knowing power down to kalā-vidyā station, then bhedodbheda again. Verse 14 cuts: pramāṇa-buddhi multitudes, action imagined and re-imagined, until cut-cut action turns knowing-upward.

## pary-p03 (15–20) — measured Lord, addressed child, self-means

**SUMMARY.** Doing-intellect limited: that is the bound one — and by mere willing the blessed Lord lets himself be measured. The little-functioned power trails him; contracted standing yields lord-of-lore, everlasting, Śiva-hoods down to body-breath-mind-void knowerhood. Creations peculiar to him, imaginings manifold, rest on refined Śaiva-making traces. The knower, at limitation's festival, stays unhurried in himself — thought-free, consciousness-selfed. Then the turn outward: child, look — this as nonduality of awareness; the world known as made-of-that, the rest trifling, unreal. No means besides: this itself the self-means.

**KEY TERMS.** karmabuddhikṣata (doing-intellect-limited), paśu (bound one), parimīyate (lets-be-measured), mitavṛtti (little-functioned), avagrahotsava (limitation-festival), vatsa paśya (child look), tuccha/asat (trifling/unreal), ananyopāya/tadātmopāya (no-means-besides/self-means).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 15 equates limitation with bondage and willing with measurability: itīcchayā... parimīyate — freedom's self-binding. Verse 16 trails mitavṛtti śakti after him through vidyeśa-sādākhya-śivatā into deha-prāṇa-buddhi-śūnya knowerhood. Verse 17 particularizes creation: asādhāraṇī sṛṣṭi on saṃskṛta traces. Verse 18 festivals limitation itself: avyagra in svātman, avikalpa, cidātmaka. Verse 19 breaks frame — vatsa paśya — from map to address: advayatvena saṃvidaḥ, tanmayam world, anyat tuccham asat. Verse 20 closes means-talk: ananyopāyam etat, tadātmopāya — the means is no-means-but-self.

## pary-p04 (21–27) — withdraw, dive, trust I-am-Śiva

**SUMMARY.** Thought withdrawn on every side, the I cultivated: thought-free self-awareness directly reached. Infinite forms accepted, one mark kept; entered, no more imagining. Four sky-going streams pour the great lake; the sky-mistress, self-awareness dived, stirs the inert. Five doings in sequence — make, keep, dissolve, hide, grace — and trust: I-am-Śiva. By cast-know-count order or the guru's mouth, weighed in the self, the weighing done. Imagined forth, world-selfed: Bhairava I, and mine those world-breakers — as flame-breakers of fire, sun-gleam. One I, seer-known-measure-measured in kinds, fourteen sky-goers in pure-wake forms.

**KEY TERMS.** saṃhṛtya sarvataś (withdrawn on every side), anuśīlana (cultivating), pratyakṣa (direct), ekalakṣaṇa (one-marked), khecaryādicatuḥsrota (four sky-going streams), broḍayet jaḍam (stir the inert), pañcakṛtya (five doings), viśvaset (trust), guruvaktra (guru's mouth), viśvabhaṅgya (world-breakers).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 21 gives the whole praxis in one line: withdraw-cultivate-attain (saṃhṛtya... anuśīlayan... pratipadyate), with the bracketed aha read as printed, not reconstructed. Verse 22 universalizes without losing mark: ananta forms, ekalakṣaṇā, then vikalpān na vikalpayet. Verse 23 dives ātmaciti into vyomeśvarī's lake to stir jaḍa — descent as method. Verse 24 trusts (viśvaset) the five in sequence as śivo 'ham. Verse 25 authorizes weighing (ākalayya) by order or guru-mouth until kṛtī. Verse 26 owns the imagined: mamaiva tāḥ viśvabhaṅgyaḥ, fire-flame and sun-gleam as warrant. Verse 27 counts one I across seer-known-measure-measured with fourteen sky-goers in śuddhabodha forms.

## pary-p05 (28–33) — freedom by confidence; union; body of Bhairava

**SUMMARY.** Bondage-liberation viewed as illusion-form and its lack: by I-am-Bhairava trusted, walk supreme free-willing. In-and-out doings, timeless self-sequencing, held unowned by power-fullness. Emission unsurpassed, rest, rise, pair: Śiva-Śakti clashing, other powers between. Bliss, will, lordship, opening; less and lordship of will; known-shining of the two knowers through distinct-undistinct difference. Bliss-and-unsurpassed joining, then feeling: knowns' lifeblood — so the full Bhairava-body. Known-shining lodged in willing and lording from one side goes by grossness into earth-and-all five-elementhood.

**KEY TERMS.** visrambha (trusted confidence), svācchandya (free-willing), akālakrama (timeless sequencing), saṃghaṭṭa (clashing), yāmala (pair), vedyollāsa (known-shining), pūrṇa vapuḥ (full body).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 28 converts view into gait: vibhāvayan medāmedaprakāreṇa, then vrajet — confidence (visrambhāt) as vehicle to parama svācchandya. Verse 29 holds antaḥ/bahiṣ action akāla-krama, aparādhīna, by prābhavanirbhara. Verse 30 clasps emission-rest-rise-pair in saṃghaṭṭa with anyaśaktayaḥ between. Verses 31–32 run bliss-will-lordship through asphuṭa/sphuṭa difference into vedyollāsa, sandhībhāva, vedanā, vedyānāṃ prāṇabhūtā — culminating pūrṇaṃ tad bhairavaṃ vapuḥ. Verse 33 lodges known-shining ekataḥ in icchā and īśana, then grossifies: sthūlatvena into pṛthivyādi pañcaka.

## pary-p06 (34–39) — subtle to sense; person; powers; no outside

**SUMMARY.** By subtleness, smell-and-all five-subtleness; by toolness, from touch, tenfold senses. Knowability covered, knower-shining sequences out; mind, I-mindness of these, predominance in the undivided; personhood, and shrink-swell by regard. Desired, they rose from two-formed both-self union — from will-nature, opening, else, unsurpassed-unioned. Four powers dwell inside the person, upholding sky-of-awareness and inert-ground, Māyā-made, Triśaṅku-hung. Three-formed will-power bore three other powers; emission exceedingly clear from created expanse. Self-powers made, mutually mixed, may stir inward — from this emission nothing may come outside.

**KEY TERMS.** sūkṣmatva/karaṇatva (subtleness/toolness), tanmātra (subtle), dvipañcatā (tenfoldness), vettṛtodbhāsanakrama (knower-shining sequence), puruṣatva/saṃkoca/vikāsa (personhood/shrink/swell), triśaṅkuvat (Triśaṅku-hung), kṣobha (stirring).


## pary-p07 (40–46) — rest-emit; hidden speech; three-forked time; blessed seeing

**SUMMARY.** All that is, by will-and-all doors, rests and rests in unsurpassed Bhairava and is ever emitted thence. Hidden-within, unending-meaning, self-grasp: supreme awareness, one-course-flowing mantra-knowing speech of pure doing. Three-coursed, three-pathed, three-stationed time she draws herself, outward then inward: glorious supreme time-puller. Divided-inward-made undivided-state survey — higher-lower doing, the seeing one, lord-state-based. Middle, sequenced, gross, inner-organ-dwelling, imagination, undivided said, breath-beat divided. Prior-two-state impression may make Māyā-measurer limited, and seeing's order fine indeed. Entered those two states in order, thought-quieted: the blessed wonderfully beholds self-awareness expanse.

**KEY TERMS.** viśramya-viśramya-visṛjyate (rest-rest-emit), antarnilīna (hidden-within), ekavāravahā (one-course-flowing), trivaha/tripatha/tristha (three-coursed/pathed/stationed), kālakarṣiṇī (time-puller), parāparā/paśyantī (higher-lower/seeing), madhyamā (middle), aṇu krama (fine order), dhanya (blessed).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 40 breathes the cosmos: viśramya viśramya muhus, tata eva visṛjyate — rest-rest-emit through icchādi doors. Verse 41 hides parā citi antarnilīna, anantārtha, svātmāmarśa, flowing ekavāra as mantrī vāk śuddhakartṛtā. Verse 42 forks time three ways, drawn svataḥ outward then inward: śrīparā kālakarṣiṇī. Verse 43 surveys bhedita-antaḥkṛta-akhaṇḍa: parāparā kartṛtā as paśyantī, īśadaśāśrayā. Verse 44 stations madhyamā sakramā sthūlā in antaḥkaraṇa, vikalpanā-abhinna-vācyā, prāṇavṛtti-divided. Verse 45 limits by prior impression: māyāmātuḥ mitatvaṃ, paśyantyāḥ krama aṇu. Verse 46 blesses entry: tayoḥ āviśya daśayoḥ kramāt, śāntavikalpa, dhanyaḥ svasaṃvidvistāraṃ sāścaryam anupaśyati.

## pary-p08 (47–52) — audible speech; non-grasping means; secret; gurus

**SUMMARY.** Place-tool-strike-person-doing-natured, Vaikharī functions sound within earshot. Through these, knowledge passes hand to hand: worldly use, then scriptural — straitened with manifold shrinkings. Knowing speech-pervasion, the Māheśvarī-led mothers of the set do not feast even waking. Work non-grasping by means; aim at Bhairavī-standing; self-awareness, the all-means, enter just that. Beginning-end-inner unending said-and-saying-full, mantra-mudrā secret: I reach unsurpassed glory. Śambhu-unseparate-hearted gurus triumph of old, end-prosperity won in riseless absorption.

**KEY TERMS.** vaikharī (audible), śrutigocara (earshot), saṃkrānti (handing-over), vāgvyāpti (speech-pervasion), agraha (non-grasping), upeyā (aim), sarvopāya (all-means), rahasya (secret), nirvyutthānasamādhi (riseless absorption).

**EXPLANATION.** Verse 47 grounds vaikharī in sthāna-karaṇa-āghāta-puṃ-vyāpāra, functions śrutigocarāḥ. Verse 48 hands knowledge over (saṃkrānti) into laukika then śāstrīya use, nānāsaṃkoca-saṃkaṭa. Verse 49 withholds feast (na bhuñjate) even jāgare from the vargādhidevatāḥ — waking itself not yet tasting. Verse 50 prescribes agraha as upāya, bhairavīsthiti as upeya, svasaṃvid as sarvopāya: enter that (tām eva samāviśet). Verse 51 re-opens verse 1's rest (ādyantāntargata-ananta) as mantra-mudrā rahasya, closing prapadye 'nuttaraṃ mahaḥ. Verse 52 triumphs the guravaḥ abhinna-hṛdayāḥ, paryanta-saṃpadaḥ prāpta in nirvyutthāna-samādhāna — then colophon: paripūrṇā kṛtiḥ, namaḥ śivāya.

# C1.v2 — Paryantapañcāśikā (condensation of the argument)

Rest in beginning-end-enclosing infinite power opens onto unspeakable speech: the Lord's heart under study. One god holds all gods, one goddess holds being and non-being; knowing, unknowing, doctrine, even opposition are his stuff. Six paths converge where she abides — every tantra's culmination, beyond telling. Will, knowing, right doing, sunk in bliss-awareness alone: the undivided ground of the god of gods, reflecting by himself into grade after grade. Sequencing as space-time-self, difference its lifeblood, divides the knower into varied shining; outward-cast drowned in I-ness is pure, undrowned impure, with infinite grades between. Thought-free clear shining is Śambhu's making, the self its own tool; doing-knowing threads difference down to art-and-lore, redoubles, then cuts and recuts until knowing strains up. Willing lets the blessed Lord be measured: the bound one. Little function trails him through lore-lords and everlastings into body-breath-mind-void knowerhood; peculiar creations on refined traces; at limitation's festival the knower stays unhurried — then child, look: the world made-of-that, the rest trifling. No means besides; the means is self. Thought withdrawn, the I cultivated, self-awareness directly reached; infinite forms, one mark; the lake dived, the inert stirred; five doings trusted as I-am-Śiva; weighed by order or guru-mouth; world-breakers owned as fire owns flames. Confidence says I-am-Bhairava and walks free: in-and-out doings timelessly sequenced, held unowned; emission, rest, rise, pair clash as Śiva-Śakti; bliss-will-lordship shine the known into Bhairava's full body, grossifying to five-elementhood. Subtle to sense, covered knowability sequencing out knowerhood, mind and I-mind predominant, personhood by regard; desired unions, will-openings; four powers hung Triśaṅku-wise between awareness-sky and inert-ground; will-power tripled, emission clarified; self-powers mixed may stir within — nothing outside. All rests and rests, ever emitted; hidden speech flows once as mantra-knowing; three-forked time pulls outward, inward; the seeing surveys divided-made-whole; the middle sequences grossly in the inner organ; prior impression limits, order fines; entered, quieted, the blessed wonderfully beholds. Audible speech sounds in ears; knowledge hands over into straitened use; the mothers do not feast waking. Work non-grasping; aim at her standing; enter self-awareness, the all-means. Beginning-end-inner, said-and-saying-full: the secret reached, glory unsurpassed; Śambhu-hearted gurus triumph, end-prosperity won in riseless rest.

## pary-p01 (1–7)

Rest in enclosing infinite power; unspeakable speech under study. One god, all gods his; one goddess, being and non-being hers. Knowing, unknowing, doctrine, opposition — his stuff. Six paths one where she abides: every tantra's culmination. Will-knowing-doing in bliss-awareness alone, undivided ground; the reflecting Lord grades down to the last ground, saying and said pulsing one.

## pary-p02 (8–14)

Space-time-self sequencing, difference its lifeblood, varied shining. Drowned outward-cast is pure, undrowned impure, infinite grades between. Thought-free clear shining: Śambhu's making, self its tool. Doing-knowing threads to art-and-lore, redoubles, cuts and recuts till knowing strains.

## pary-p03 (15–20)

Doing-intellect limited: the bound one; willing, the Lord lets himself be measured. Little function, lore-lords, body-breath-mind-void knowerhood, peculiar creations. Limitation's festival, unhurried self — then child, look: world made-of-that. No means besides; self the means.

## pary-p04 (21–27)

Withdraw thought, cultivate I, reach self-awareness direct. Infinite forms, one mark, no more imagining. Dive the lake, stir the inert. Five doings trusted as I-am-Śiva. Weigh by order or guru-mouth till done. Own the world-breakers as fire owns flames; one I in seer-known-measure-measured kinds.

## pary-p05 (28–33)

View bondage-liberation as illusion-form and lack; trust I-am-Bhairava and walk free. In-and-out doings timelessly sequenced, held unowned. Emission, rest, rise, pair clash; bliss-will-lordship shine the known into the full body, grossifying fivefold.

## pary-p06 (34–39)

Subtle to five-subtleness, tool to tenfold sense. Covered knowability sequences out knowerhood; mind and I-mind predominant; personhood by regard. Desired unions, will-openings; four powers Triśaṅku-hung; will-power tripled, emission clarified; stirred within, never outside.

## pary-p07 (40–46)

Rest and rest, ever emit. Hidden speech, once flowing, mantra-knowing. Three-forked time pulls outward, inward. The seeing surveys divided-made-whole; the middle grossly sequences; impression limits, order fines. Entered, quieted: the blessed beholds wonderfully.

## pary-p08 (47–52)

Audible speech in ears; handing over into straitened use; mothers not feasting waking. Non-grasping work, her standing the aim, self-awareness the all-means: enter that. Beginning-end-inner, said-and-saying-full secret reached; Śambhu-hearted gurus triumph in riseless rest.

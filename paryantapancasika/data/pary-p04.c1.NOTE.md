Sequenced five done trusted as Śiva-I. Cast-counted guru-worded weighed in self accomplishes. Imagined Bhairava-I owns world-modes like fire owns flames, sun shine. One measurer-measured-measure-measured kinded pure-formed, fourteen sky-goers.

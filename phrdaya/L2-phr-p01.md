# Pratyabhijnahrdaya phr-p01 (free cause, unborrowing opening) — L2 READ

## v.1
Consciousness stands free — cause of the world's proving.
The Sanskrit: *citiḥ svatantrā viśvasiddhihetuḥ*

## v.2
By her own will, on her own ground, she opens the world.
The Sanskrit: *svecchayā svabhittau viśvam unmīlayati*

## v.3
That grows manifold through fit perceivers and perceived dividing.
The Sanskrit: *tan nānā anurūpagrāhyagrāhakabhedāt*

## v.4
A contraction of knowing, yet a knower even so, clothed in a contracted world.
The Sanskrit: *citisaṃkocātmā cetano 'pi saṃkucitaviśvamayaḥ*

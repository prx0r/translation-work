# Pratyabhijnahrdaya phr-p03 (stained yet officed) — L2 READ

## v.9
Like knowing, yet by tightened power impure — stained, it wanders.
The Sanskrit: *cidvat tac chaktisaṃkocāt malāvṛtaḥ saṃsārī*

## v.10
Yet even so, likewise, it does the five acts.
The Sanskrit: *tathāpi tadvat pañcakṛtyāni karoti*

## v.11
Those — the fivefold doing.
The Sanskrit: *tāni*

## v.12
Wanderinghood.
The Sanskrit: *saṃsāritvam*

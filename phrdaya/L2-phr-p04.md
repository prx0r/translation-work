# Pratyabhijnahrdaya phr-p04 (climb, burn, own, hold) — L2 READ

## v.13
By climbing to knowing's seat — knowing.
The Sanskrit: *cetanapadādhyārohāt citiḥ*

## v.14
It burns knowables for fuel.
The Sanskrit: *meyendhanaṃ pluṣyati*

## v.15
Grown strong, it makes the world its own.
The Sanskrit: *balalābhe viśvam ātmasātkaroti*

## v.16
Holding oneness with knowing fast — freedom while living.
The Sanskrit: *cidaikātmyapratipattidārḍhyaṃ jīvanmuktiḥ*

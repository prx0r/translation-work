# Pratyabhijnahrdaya phr-p05 (opened, given, recalled, become) — L2 READ

## v.17
From the middle's opening — knowing-bliss won.
The Sanskrit: *madhyavikāsāc cidānandalābhaḥ*

## v.18
Here — the means.
The Sanskrit: *iha upāyāḥ*

## v.19
From recalling oneness with knowing — samādhi ever risen.
The Sanskrit: *cidaikyāmarśān nityoditasamādhilābhaḥ*

## v.20
And so it becomes — Śiva.
The Sanskrit: *bhavatīti śivam*

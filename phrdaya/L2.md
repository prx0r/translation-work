# Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya — L2 READ

# L2 READ

## phr-p01 — Independent Consciousness and the Universe
Consciousness is independent and the cause of the universe's accomplishment. By its own will, it unfolds the universe on its own screen, and that universe becomes manifold through differences of suitable objects and subjects. Even so, a conscious being's nature is a contraction of consciousness, consisting of a contracted universe.

## phr-p02 — Mind, Māyā, and Philosophical Stages
The mind consists of that contracted consciousness, and the māyā-knower is formed from it. Its nature is the seven pentads, and the stages based upon it become the positions of all philosophies.

## phr-p03 — Contraction, Impurity, and Transmigration
Like consciousness, it becomes a transmigrator when its power contracts and is covered by impurity. Nevertheless, it still performs the five acts in that way, and this constitutes the state of transmigration.

## phr-p04 — Ascent to Consciousness and Liberation
By ascending to the status of consciousness, consciousness burns the fuel of objects. Gaining strength, it makes the universe its own, and firm realization of oneness with consciousness is liberation while living.

## phr-p05 — Expansion, Means, and Śiva
From expansion of the middle comes attainment of consciousness-bliss, and the means are given here. From recollection of oneness with consciousness comes attainment of ever-risen samādhi, and thus one becomes Śiva.

## phr-p01 — Independent consciousness; the universe unfolds (sūtras 1–4)

**Sūtra 1.** Consciousness is independent, the cause of the universe's accomplishment. Free consciousness (citi) is the reason (hetu) for the actualization of the universe (viśvasiddhi). "Free" (svatantrā) describes the one comprising highest awareness who is non-distinct from Lord Śiva. Expanded consciousness (citi) is the Goddess (bhagavatī), self-willed and autonomous; the universe is a manifestation of freedom, not a creation from nothing.

**Sūtra 2.** By own will, on own screen, universe unfolds. Consciousness unfolds the universe by her own will (svecchā) on her own screen (svabhittau) — the universe is a manifestation of freedom.

**Sūtra 3.** That manifold, from difference of suitable objects and subjects. The manifold (nānā) arises from the difference (bheda) of suitable objects (grāhya) and subjects (grāhaka) — the world's variety is the self's own differentiation.

**Sūtra 4.** Whose nature is contraction of consciousness, even conscious being, consisting of contracted universe. The contracted consciousness (citisaṃkocātmā) is the māyā-knower (māyāpramātā); even the conscious being (cetano 'pi) consists of the contracted universe (saṃkucitaviśvamayaḥ). Contraction (saṃkoca) veils the free consciousness; recognition (pratyabhijñā) is the unveiling.

## phr-p02 — Mind; māyā-knower; seven pentads (sūtras 5–8)

**Sūtra 5.** Mind. Mind (citta) is the contracted consciousness — the first contraction from the free citi.

**Sūtra 6.** Consisting of that, māyā-knower. The māyā-knower (māyāpramātā) consists of that (tanmaya) — consciousness contracted into the māyā-state.

**Sūtra 7.** Having nature of seven pentads. The māyā-knower has the nature of seven pentads (saptapañcaka) — the 35 tattvas from Śiva to puruṣa (5 × 7 = 35).

**Sūtra 8.** Stages based on that, positions of all philosophies. The stages (bhūmikā) based on that are the positions of all philosophies (sarvadarśanasthitayaḥ) — all philosophical systems are stages on the descent from Śiva to the bound soul.

## phr-p03 — The māyā-knower as transmigrator (sūtras 9–12)

**Sūtra 9.** Like consciousness, that, from contraction of power, covered by impurity, transmigrator. The māyā-knower is like consciousness (cidvat) — he has the same nature — but covered by impurity (malāvṛtaḥ) from contraction of power (śaktisaṃkoca). He is the transmigrator (saṃsārī).

**Sūtra 10.** Nevertheless, in that way, five acts, performs. Nevertheless (tathāpi), in that way (tadvat), he performs the five acts (pañcakṛtyāni) — creation, maintenance, dissolution, concealment, grace. The māyā-knower performs the same five acts as Śiva, but in contracted form.

**Sūtra 11.** Those. Those (tāni) — the five acts performed in contracted form.

**Sūtra 12.** State of transmigration. Those (the five acts in contracted form) are the state of transmigration (saṃsāritvam). Transmigration is not a separate state but the contracted performance of the five acts.

## phr-p04 — Ascent to consciousness; jīvanmukti (sūtras 13–16)

**Sūtra 13.** By ascent to status of consciousness, consciousness. By ascent to the status of consciousness (cetanapadādhyārohāt), consciousness (citiḥ) — the knower ascends from the māyā-state to the consciousness-state.

**Sūtra 14.** Fuel of objects, burns. Consciousness burns (pluṣyati) the fuel of objects (meyendhanam) — the objects of knowledge are consumed by consciousness.

**Sūtra 15.** On gaining strength, universe, makes one's own. On gaining strength (balalābhe), the universe (viśvam) makes one's own (ātmasātkaroti) — the universe becomes one's own, not separate.

**Sūtra 16.** Firmness of realization of oneness with consciousness, liberation-while-living. Firmness of realization of oneness with consciousness (cidaikātmyapratipattidārḍhyaṃ) is liberation-while-living (jīvanmuktiḥ). Liberation is not going somewhere but recognizing what one already is.

## phr-p05 — Closing: expansion of middle; means; recollection of oneness (sūtras 17–20)

**Sūtra 17.** From expansion of the middle, attainment of consciousness-bliss. From expansion of the middle (madhyavikāsāc), attainment of consciousness-bliss (cidānandalābhaḥ) — the middle (madhya) is the heart-center; its expansion (vikāsa) is the method.

**Sūtra 18.** Here, means. Here (iha), means (upāyāḥ) — the means (upāya) are stated here (in the following sūtras).

**Sūtra 19.** From recollection of oneness with consciousness, attainment of ever-risen samādhi. From recollection of oneness with consciousness (cidaikyāmarśān), attainment of ever-risen samādhi (nityoditasamādhilābhaḥ) — the samādhi that is ever-risen (nityodita), not acquired but recognized.

**Sūtra 20.** Becomes thus, Śiva. Becomes thus (bhavatīti) Śiva (śivam) — the final word: the practitioner becomes Śiva.

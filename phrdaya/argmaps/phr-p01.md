# Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya phr-p01 — ARGUMENT MAP (independent consciousness; the universe unfolds)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 phr-p01 = sūtras 1–4. Lore: Kṣemarāja (11th c., Abhinavagupta's disciple); Jaideva Singh 1980 (standard English translation); Gómez Pomeda 2001 (Berkeley, translation + study); IEP (Kashmiri Shaiva Philosophy). Key: the Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya is a simplified manual (20 sūtras) of Utpaladeva's Pratyabhijñā — avoids polemics, gives the main tenets in succinct form. Sūtra 1: citiḥ svatantrā viśvasiddhihetuḥ — Free consciousness is the reason for the actualization of the universe. Expanded consciousness (citi) is the Goddess (bhagavatī), self-willed and autonomous; the universe is a manifestation of freedom. Flood 2023 (Śākta-Śaiva meditation as expanded awareness).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Free consciousness (citi) is the reason (hetu) for the actualization of the universe (viśvasiddhi). Consciousness is independent (svatantrā) — she unfolds the universe (viśvam unmīlayati) by her own will (svecchā) on her own screen (svabhittau), from the difference of suitable objects and subjects (anurūpagrāhyagrāhakabheda). The contracted consciousness (citisaṃkocātmā) is the māyā-knower (māyāpramātā), consisting of the contracted universe — even the conscious being (cetano 'pi) is contracted. The text's core move: consciousness is free and the cause of the universe; contraction (saṃkoca) is the veiling; recognition (pratyabhijñā) is the unveiling.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Sūtra 1 (v. 1):**
> *citiḥ svatantrā viśvasiddhihetuḥ*
[i.e. consciousness is independent, the cause of the universe's accomplishment.] Free consciousness (citi) is the reason (hetu) for the actualization of the universe (viśvasiddhi). "Free" (svatantrā) describes the one comprising highest awareness who is non-distinct from Lord Śiva.

**Sūtra 2 (v. 2):**
> *svecchayā svabhittau viśvam unmīlayati*
[i.e. by own will, on own screen, universe unfolds.] Consciousness unfolds the universe by her own will (svecchā) on her own screen (svabhittau) — the universe is a manifestation of freedom, not a creation from nothing.

**Sūtra 3 (v. 3):**
> *tan nānā anurūpagrāhyagrāhakabhedāt*
[i.e. that manifold, from difference of suitable objects and subjects.] The manifold (nānā) arises from the difference (bheda) of suitable objects (grāhya) and subjects (grāhaka) — the world's variety is the self's own differentiation.

**Sūtra 4 (v. 4):**
> *citisaṃkocātmā cetano 'pi saṃkucitaviśvamayaḥ*
[i.e. whose nature is contraction of consciousness, even conscious being, consisting of contracted universe.] The contracted consciousness (citisaṃkocātmā) is the māyā-knower (māyāpramātā); even the conscious being (cetano 'pi) consists of the contracted universe (saṃkucitaviśvamayaḥ). Contraction (saṃkoca) veils the free consciousness; recognition (pratyabhijñā) is the unveiling.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The 20 sūtras are Kṣemarāja's summary of Utpaladeva's Pratyabhijñā; the auto-commentary (vṛtti) is by Kṣemarāja himself.
2. Jaideva Singh 1980 is the standard English translation; Gómez Pomeda 2001 (Berkeley) is the translation + study.
3. The text avoids polemics and gives the main tenets in succinct form — it is a manual, not a treatise.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the sūtras' own movement: free consciousness as cause (sūtra 1) → unfolds by own will (sūtra 2) → manifold from difference of objects/subjects (sūtra 3) → contraction veils (sūtra 4). The key move is sūtra 1: citiḥ svatantrā viśvasiddhihetuḥ — free consciousness is the reason for the universe's actualization. And sūtra 4: citisaṃkocātmā — contraction of consciousness is the veiling. Carry both into p02 (mind, māyā-knower, seven pentads). Read it as a manual opening table: cause stated, veiling named, descent promised.

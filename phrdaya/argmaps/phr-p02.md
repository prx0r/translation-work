# Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya phr-p02 — ARGUMENT MAP (mind; māyā-knower; seven pentads)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 phr-p02 = sūtras 5–8. Lore: Kṣemarāja (11th c.); Jaideva Singh 1980; Gómez Pomeda 2001. Key: mind (citta) is the contracted consciousness; the māyā-knower (māyāpramātā) is consciousness-formed but attached to objects, devoid of true discrimination; the seven pentads (saptapañcaka) are the 35 tattvas from Śiva to puruṣa; the stages (bhūmikā) based on that are the positions of all philosophies (sarvadarśanasthitayaḥ).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Mind (citta) is the contracted consciousness. The māyā-knower (māyāpramātā) is consciousness-formed but attached to objects, devoid of true discrimination. The seven pentads (saptapañcaka) are the 35 tattvas from Śiva to puruṣa. The stages (bhūmikā) based on that are the positions of all philosophies (sarvadarśanasthitayaḥ). The text's core move: mind is consciousness contracted; the māyā-knower is the contracted experiencer; the seven pentads map the descent from Śiva to the bound soul; all philosophies are stages on this descent.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Sūtra 5 (v. 5):**
> *cittam*
[i.e. mind.] Mind (citta) is the contracted consciousness — the first contraction from the free citi.

**Sūtra 6 (v. 6):**
> *tanmayaḥ māyāpramātā*
[i.e. consisting of that, māyā-knower.] The māyā-knower (māyāpramātā) consists of that (tanmaya) — consciousness contracted into the māyā-state.

**Sūtra 7 (v. 7):**
> *saptapañcakasvabhāvaḥ*
[i.e. having nature of seven pentads.] The māyā-knower has the nature of seven pentads (saptapañcaka) — the 35 tattvas from Śiva to puruṣa (5 × 7 = 35).

**Sūtra 8 (v. 8):**
> *tadbhūmikāḥ sarvadarśanasthitayaḥ*
[i.e. stages based on that, positions of all philosophies.] The stages (bhūmikā) based on that are the positions of all philosophies (sarvadarśanasthitayaḥ) — all philosophical systems are stages on the descent from Śiva to the bound soul.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The "seven pentads" (saptapañcaka) — the 35 tattvas from Śiva to puruṣa; the text does not enumerate them. The standard Trika enumeration: Śiva, Śakti, Sadāśiva, Īśvara, Vidyā (5 pure) + māyā + 5 kañcukas + puruṣa + prakṛti (7 impure) = 12; then 24 (from ahaṃkāra down to pṛthvī) = 36 total. The "seven pentads" is a different grouping.
2. The "positions of all philosophies" (sarvadarśanasthitayaḥ) — the text implies that all philosophical systems (Vedānta, Sāṃkhya, Buddhism, etc.) are stages on the descent; the text does not enumerate them.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the sūtras' own movement: mind is contracted consciousness (sūtra 5) → māyā-knower consists of that (sūtra 6) → seven pentads (sūtra 7) → all philosophies are stages on that descent (sūtra 8). The key move is sūtra 5: cittam — mind is the contracted consciousness. And sūtra 8: sarvadarśanasthitayaḥ — all philosophies are stages. Carry both into p03 (the māyā-knower as transmigrator). Read it as the descent inventory: mind, pentads, and every philosophy seated on the way down.

# Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya phr-p04 — ARGUMENT MAP (ascent to consciousness; jīvanmukti)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 phr-p04 = sūtras 13–16. Lore: Kṣemarāja (11th c.); Jaideva Singh 1980; Gómez Pomeda 2001. Key: by ascent to status of consciousness (cetanapadādhyārohāt), consciousness (citiḥ) burns the fuel of objects (meyendhanam pluṣyati); on gaining strength (balalābhe), universe (viśvam) makes one's own (ātmasātkaroti); firmness of realization of oneness with consciousness (cidaikātmyapratipattidārḍhyaṃ) is liberation-while-living (jīvanmuktiḥ). The text's core move: liberation is not going somewhere but recognizing what one already is; the ascent is by recognition, not by effort.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

By ascent to the status of consciousness (cetanapadādhyārohāt), consciousness (citiḥ) burns the fuel of objects (meyendhanam pluṣyati). On gaining strength (balalābhe), the universe (viśvam) makes one's own (ātmasātkaroti). Firmness of realization of oneness with consciousness (cidaikātmyapratipattidārḍhyaṃ) is liberation-while-living (jīvanmuktiḥ). The text's core move: liberation is not going somewhere but recognizing what one already is; the ascent is by recognition (pratyabhijñā), not by effort.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Sūtra 13 (v. 13):**
> *cetanapadādhyārohāt citiḥ*
[i.e. by ascent to status of consciousness, consciousness.] By ascent to the status of consciousness (cetanapadādhyārohāt), consciousness (citiḥ) — the knower ascends from the māyā-state to the consciousness-state.

**Sūtra 14 (v. 14):**
> *meyendhanam pluṣyati*
[i.e. fuel of objects, burns.] Consciousness burns (pluṣyati) the fuel of objects (meyendhanam) — the objects of knowledge are consumed by consciousness.

**Sūtra 15 (v. 15):**
> *balalābhe viśvam ātmasātkaroti*
[i.e. on gaining strength, universe, makes one's own.] On gaining strength (balalābhe), the universe (viśvam) makes one's own (ātmasātkaroti) — the universe becomes one's own, not separate.

**Sūtra 16 (v. 16):**
> *cidaikātmyapratipattidārḍhyaṃ jīvanmuktiḥ*
[i.e. firmness of realization of oneness with consciousness, liberation-while-living.] Firmness of realization of oneness with consciousness (cidaikātmyapratipattidārḍhyaṃ) is liberation-while-living (jīvanmuktiḥ). Liberation is not going somewhere but recognizing what one already is.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The "ascent to status of consciousness" (cetanapadādhyāroha) — the text does not specify the method of ascent; the standard Trika identifies it as śaktipāta + Guru's grace.
2. The "fuel of objects" (meyendhana) — the text does not specify what the fuel is; the standard Trika identifies it as the malas (āṇava, māyīya, kārmaṇa).

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the sūtras' own movement: by ascent to consciousness (sūtra 13) → burns fuel of objects (sūtra 14) → on gaining strength, universe makes one's own (sūtra 15) → firmness of realization of oneness with consciousness is jīvanmukti (sūtra 16). The key move is sūtra 13: cetanapadādhyārohāt citiḥ — by ascent to the status of consciousness, consciousness. And sūtra 16: cidaikātmyapratipattidārḍhyaṃ jīvanmuktiḥ — firmness of realization of oneness with consciousness is liberation-while-living. Carry both into p05 (from expansion of middle, means, recollection of oneness). Read it as the three-verb discipline: burn, take, steady — recognition as status, not effort.

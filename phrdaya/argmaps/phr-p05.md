# Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya phr-p05 — ARGUMENT MAP (closing: expansion of middle; means; recollection of oneness)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 phr-p05 = sūtras 17–20. Lore: Kṣemarāja (11th c.); Jaideva Singh 1980; Gómez Pomeda 2001. Key: from expansion of the middle (madhyavikāsāc), attainment of consciousness-bliss (cidānandalābhaḥ); here (iha), means (upāyāḥ); from recollection of oneness with consciousness (cidaikyāmarśān), attainment of ever-risen samādhi (nityoditasamādhilābhaḥ); becomes thus (bhavatīti) Śiva (śivam). The text's final word: from recollection of oneness with consciousness, attainment of ever-risen samādhi; becomes thus Śiva.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

From expansion of the middle (madhyavikāsāc), attainment of consciousness-bliss (cidānandalābhaḥ). Here (iha), means (upāyāḥ). From recollection of oneness with consciousness (cidaikyāmarśān), attainment of ever-risen samādhi (nityoditasamādhilābhaḥ). Becomes thus (bhavatīti) Śiva (śivam). The text's final word: from recollection of oneness with consciousness, attainment of ever-risen samādhi; becomes thus Śiva. The 20 sūtras seal the Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya: consciousness is free, contraction veils, ascent by recognition, liberation-while-living, and finally, from recollection of oneness, ever-risen samādhi — Śiva.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Sūtra 17 (v. 17):**
> *madhyavikāsāc cidānandalābhaḥ*
[i.e. from expansion of the middle, attainment of consciousness-bliss.] From expansion of the middle (madhyavikāsāc), attainment of consciousness-bliss (cidānandalābhaḥ) — the middle (madhya) is the heart-center; its expansion (vikāsa) is the method.

**Sūtra 18 (v. 18):**
> *iha upāyāḥ*
[i.e. here, means.] Here (iha), means (upāyāḥ) — the means (upāya) are stated here (in the following sūtras).

**Sūtra 19 (v. 19):**
> *cidaikyāmarśān nityoditasamādhilābhaḥ*
[i.e. from recollection of oneness with consciousness, attainment of ever-risen samādhi.] From recollection of oneness with consciousness (cidaikyāmarśān), attainment of ever-risen samādhi (nityoditasamādhilābhaḥ) — the samādhi that is ever-risen (nityodita), not acquired but recognized.

**Sūtra 20 (v. 20):**
> *bhavatīti śivam*
[i.e. becomes thus, Śiva.] Becomes thus (bhavatīti) Śiva (śivam) — the final word: the practitioner becomes Śiva.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. The "expansion of the middle" (madhyavikāsa) — the text does not specify the method; the standard Trika identifies it as the expansion of the heart-center (hṛdaya) through śaktipāta and Guru's grace.
2. The "means" (upāya) — sūtra 18 says "here, means" but does not enumerate them; the standard Trika identifies four upāyas (anupāya, śāmbhava, śākta, āṇava).
3. The "ever-risen samādhi" (nityoditasamādhi) — the text does not specify the method; the standard Trika identifies it as the recognition (pratyabhijñā) that samādhi is not acquired but recognized.

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as the sūtras' own movement: from expansion of the middle, attainment of consciousness-bliss (sūtra 17) → here, means (sūtra 18) → from recollection of oneness with consciousness, attainment of ever-risen samādhi (sūtra 19) → becomes thus Śiva (sūtra 20). The key move is sūtra 19: cidaikyāmarśān nityoditasamādhilābhaḥ — from recollection of oneness with consciousness, attainment of ever-risen samādhi. And sūtra 20: bhavatīti śivam — becomes thus Śiva. The text's final word: from recollection of oneness, ever-risen samādhi; becomes thus Śiva. Carry this into C1 as the text's closing note. Read it as the close in becoming, not seeing: means stated, recollection repeated, identity become.

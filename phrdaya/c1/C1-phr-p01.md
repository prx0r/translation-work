# C1 — phr-p01 (cause, means refused, veil named)

## SUMMARY

Consciousness free causes the world's triple proving; by own will unborrowing she opens; fitness divides; tightening veils into knowerhood.

## KEY TERMS

citi/svātantrya (free consciousness) · siddhi-triple (proving threefold) · svecchā/svabhitti (own will/ground) · saṃkoca (tightening)

## rasa

Wonder (adbhuta): free cause opening unborrowing.

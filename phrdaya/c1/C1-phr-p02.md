# C1 — phr-p02 (descent inventoried)

## SUMMARY

Mind is tightened knowing; the māyā-knower measured to it; seven pentads station the fall; every school stands somewhere on the rungs.

## KEY TERMS

citta (mind-tightened) · māyāpramātṛ (measured knower) · saptapañcaka (seven pentads) · sarvadarśana (every school)

## rasa

Wonder (adbhuta): all seated on the way down.

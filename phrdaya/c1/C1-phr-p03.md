# C1 — phr-p03 (likeness, office, wandering)

## SUMMARY

Almost-knowing, power-tightened, stained — yet the fivefold office kept: done small, that doing is wandering.

## KEY TERMS

cidvat/malāvṛta (almost-knowing/stained) · pañcakṛtya (five acts) · saṃsāritva (wanderinghood)

## rasa

Exultation (vīra): divinity done small.

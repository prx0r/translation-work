# C1 — phr-p04 (return by recognition)

## SUMMARY

Climb to knowing's seat turns climber to knowing; knowables burn; grown strong the world is owned; fast-held oneness is living freedom.

## KEY TERMS

cetanapadādhyāroha (climbing) · meyendhana (knowable-fuel) · ātmasāt (owned) · dārḍhya/jīvanmukti (fast-hold/living-freedom)

## rasa

Steadfast exultation: freedom won back.

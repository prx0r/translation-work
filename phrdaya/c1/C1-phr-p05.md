# C1 — phr-p05 (close in becoming)

## SUMMARY

Middle opens into knowing-bliss; means given here; recalled oneness rises ever-waking samādhi; become Śiva.

## KEY TERMS

madhyavikāsa (middle-opening) · upāya (means, unenumerated) · nityoditasamādhi (ever-risen) · bhavati-śivam (become-Śiva)

## rasa

Peace (śānta): become Śiva.

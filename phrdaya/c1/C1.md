# C1 — Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya (argument)

## phr-p01 (sūtras 1–4) — freedom causes; contraction veils

**SUMMARY.** Independence proves itself by causing: the universe is accomplished on no ground but consciousness's own screen and will, differenced into suitable seers and seens. Contracted, that same consciousness is the māyā-knower.

**KEY TERMS.** citi (consciousness), svatantrā (independent), viśvasiddhihetu (universe-accomplishment-cause), svecchā/svabhitti (own will/own screen), unmīlana (unfolding), grāhya/grāhaka (object/subject), saṃkoca (contraction), māyāpramātṛ (māyā-knower).

**EXPLANATION.** Sūtra 1 (citiḥ svatantrā viśvasiddhihetuḥ) makes freedom causal, not adjectival: accomplishment needs no other ground, and other grounds (māyā-nature etc., unlit) cannot cause. Sūtra 2 (svecchayā svabhittau viśvam unmīlayati) removes means (no Brahmā-like other-will, no material dependence) and place (her own screen, mirror-city-like, distinct-yet-undistinct). Sūtra 3 (tan nānā anurūpagrāhyagrāhakabhedāt) derives manifoldness from fitted seer-seen difference. Sūtra 4 (citisaṃkocātmā cetano 'pi saṃkucitaviśvamayaḥ) turns her into him: contraction-natured, even the conscious one consists of contracted universe — the knower to be unbound later.

## phr-p02 (sūtras 5–8) — mind first; pentads; philosophies as stations

**SUMMARY.** Mind is contraction's first shape; the māyā-knower consists of it. His nature arrays thirty-five tattvas in seven pentads, and every philosophy takes its stand somewhere on that descent.

**KEY TERMS.** citta (mind), tanmaya (consisting-of-that), saptapañcaka (seven pentads), bhūmikā (stations), sarvadarśanasthitayaḥ (all-philosophy-positions).

**EXPLANATION.** Sūtras 5–6 contract citi to citta to māyā-knower. Sūtra 7 counts his nature: seven pentads (5 × 7 = 35, Śiva to puruṣa). Sūtra 8 (implied stations) seats every darśana on the ladder — rival systems become altitude-markers, not refutations; the manual avoids polemics by placement.

## phr-p03 (sūtras 9–12) — same nature, covered; five acts contracted

**SUMMARY.** Same-natured as consciousness yet impurity-covered, the knower transmigrates — but still performs all five acts in contracted form, and contracted five-act performance just is transmigration.

**KEY TERMS.** cidvat (consciousness-like), malāvṛta (impurity-covered), śaktisaṃkoca (power-contraction), saṃsārī (transmigrator), pañcakṛtya (five acts).

**EXPLANATION.** Sūtras 9–10 hold likeness with cover: cidvat against malāvṛtaḥ. Sūtras 11–12 keep agency through contraction: creation, maintenance, dissolution, concealment, grace — done contractedly, and that contracted doing is saṃsāritvam. Transmigration is thus a mode of acting, not a place of being.

## phr-p04 (sūtras 13–16) — ascent burns, takes, steadies

**SUMMARY.** Climbing back to consciousness-status burns object-fuel, makes the universe self, and steadies one-with-consciousness into freedom-while-living.

**KEY TERMS.** cetanapadādhyāroha (consciousness-status-ascent), meyendhanapluṣ (object-fuel-burning), balalābha (strength-gaining), ātmasātkaraṇa (self-making), cidaikātmyapratipatti (one-with-consciousness-grasp), dārḍhya (steadiness), jīvanmukti (living freedom).

**EXPLANATION.** Sūtra 13 burns (meyendhanam pluṣyati); 14 takes (viśvam ātmasātkaroti) on strength gained; 15–16 steady the grasp into jīvanmuktiḥ. Ascent is by recognition-status, not by effort-accumulation — the manual's discipline in three verbs: burn, take, steady.

## phr-p05 (sūtras 17–20) — middle expands; recollection rises; Śiva

**SUMMARY.** The middle's expansion gives bliss; means are stated, not hidden. Recollecting oneness yields ever-risen samādhi — and that is Śiva.

**KEY TERMS.** madhyavikāsa (middle-expansion), cidānandalābha (consciousness-bliss-gaining), upāya (means), cidaikyāmarśa (oneness-recollection), nityoditasamādhi (ever-risen samādhi), bhavati śivam (becomes Śiva).

**EXPLANATION.** Sūtra 17 opens the middle; 18 declares means openly (iha upāyāḥ) — the manual's esoteric frankness. Sūtra 19 makes recollection, repeated, into unbroken samādhi. Sūtra 20 (bhavatīti śivam) ends in becoming, not seeing: recognition completed is identity stated.

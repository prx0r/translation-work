# C1.v2 — Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya (condensation of the argument)

Free consciousness causes the universe, unfolding it on her own screen by her own will; the manifold is her own difference into suitable seers and seens. Contracted, she is the māyā-knower — mind first, then seven pentads of tattvas, stations on which every philosophy stands. Covered yet same-natured, she does the five acts contracted — and that contraction is transmigration. Climbing back to consciousness-status burns up objects, takes the universe as self, steadies oneness into living freedom. The middle expands into bliss; means are given; recollecting oneness yields ever-risen samādhi — and that is Śiva.

## phr-p01 (sūtras 1–4)

Independence proves itself by causing: the universe accomplished on no ground but her own screen and will, differenced into seers and seens — then contraction veils her into the knower.

## phr-p02 (sūtras 5–8)

Mind is the first contraction; the māyā-knower consists of it, arrays thirty-five tattvas in seven pentads, and every philosophy takes its stand on that descent.

## phr-p03 (sūtras 9–12)

Same-natured yet impurity-covered, the knower transmigrates — but performs all five acts contracted, and contracted five-act performance just is transmigration.

## phr-p04 (sūtras 13–16)

Ascent burns object-fuel, takes the universe as self, and steadies one-with-consciousness into freedom while living.

## phr-p05 (sūtras 17–20)

The middle expands into bliss; means are stated; oneness recollected yields ever-risen samādhi — becoming Śiva.

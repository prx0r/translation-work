# C1 NOTE — phr-p03

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Almost-knowing, power-tightened, stained — yet the fivefold office kept: done small, that doing is wandering.

## VOICE MAP

Kṣemarāja sūtra voice keeping agency.

## KEY TERMS

* **cidvat** — almost-knowing: likeness kept.
* **pañcakṛtya** — five acts (traditional five).
* **saṃsāritva** — wanderinghood: doing-small named.

## CRUXES

* Traditional five; unspecified contraction.

## FUNCTION

Stain → office-kept → wandering-named.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Song 3 of 5: sūtras 9–12 (stain, acts, those, wandering).

## EXPLANATION

Likeness stained not severed; office enumerated traditionally; those named; wanderinghood defined by doing.

## BOUNDARY

Five acts traditional; contracted power unspecified.

## RELATED

Bodhavilāsa/Paramārthasāra parallels (legacy lore).

## The canonical process (14 steps)

SOURCE (sha-pinned, verse count verified) -> T1 (fast translation, pada-split glosses, coverage before sharpness, history[]) -> L0 (scholarly audit, morph + ranked options, forks live here, morph on forks) -> ARGMAP (terse logical, adjudicates forks, scholarly refs as support, no certainty labels) -> L2 (plain English, rasa in word choice, cite byte-exact, traces to L0) -> C1 (essence + 7 sections + rasa block, no meta, no quotes, no shared with L2) -> C2 (prompt renders, maps_to essence) -> Proof (B1-B8, PASS/BLOCKED) -> Site (chunks, variants, argmaps).

## What C1 is (the rasa layer)

C1 = essence (atomic propositions) + standard 7-part essay (SUMMARY, FUNCTION, KEY TERMS, LOCAL CONTEXT, EXPLANATION, BOUNDARY, RELATED) + optional rasa{} block (dominant, sthayibhava, justification).

C1 names what the verse DOES (relish/camatkara), not just what it says. Framed by Abhinavagupta's rasa theory. No quoted English gloss-words. No sentences about the text. Zero sentences shared with L2. The rasa block: dominant (rasa named), sthayibhava (stable emotion), justification (why, tied to vibhava/vyabhicari).

C1 is NOT the translation. L2 is the translation. C1 is the rasa layer.

## What L2 is (the translation layer)

L2 = readable line-by-line plain English. Every clause traces to >=1 L0 id. Cite Sanskrit byte-exact. No interpretive essays. Nothing that belongs in C1. supplied[] logs bracketed insertions.

Rasa in L2 is carried in word choice and rhythm: "gasping" > "thirsty", "drowned" > "fallen", "wakes" > "arises". Each change justified by the verse's sthayibhava. Not synonym swaps. Not punctuation. Words that make the reader FEEL what the Sanskrit makes the sahrdaya feel.

L2 and C1 must be different layers: zero shared long sentences.

## What T1 is

T1 = fast translation: verse draft + per-token quick gloss over the whole text. Coverage before sharpness. Every pada faced. Lore-primed. Convention: [and]-gloss (iast) per pada. Then t1_extract -> L0. L0 must be 100% PARSED and full source coverage.

## What L0 is

L0 = scholarly audit: term-by-term literal gloss with every genuine meaning-fork as ranked OPTION (options[]), each with grammatical grounds and source (grammar/lakshmanjoo/dyczkowski/ksemaraja-vivrti/witness-*/t1-draft/bailly/sandhi-variant/compound-split). Forks live here: fork none|live|resolved. AMBIGUOUS stays parse-quality. Morphology stated on every forked token. history[] appends every change. L0.chosen == T1.quick_gloss unless revised.

## What ARGMAP is

ARGMAP reads verse as whole against Sanskrit+T1+L0+scholarship. Terse logical statements. Adjudicates L0 forks on record (option_choices). Scholarly refs as support, not claims. No certainty/uncertainty labels — state what is unresolved. Traces must resolve: t1_verse exists, every l0_id exists, sanskrit_sha matches source.

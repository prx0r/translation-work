# Transmission brief — Bṛhattantrasāra (Kṛṣṇānanda Āgamavāgīśa) — RESEARCHED 2026-11-11
*Sources: date studies in Studies in Indian Literary History; Bühnemann,
"Buddhist Deities and Mantras in the Hindu Tantras"; EAP1031 ms record.*

## A. FACTS
- Author **Kṛṣṇānanda Āgamavāgīśa**, Bengal. **Date: c. 1500–1600**
  (Bhattacharya c. 1607; a dated ms of his Tantrasāra = 1530; limits
  argued 1494–1639). Pupil of **Vāsudeva Sārvabhauma**; contemporary of
  Raghunandana.
- An **encyclopedic compendium** drawing on Śaiva, Śākta, Vaiṣṇava, Saura,
  Gāṇapatya tantras — the "essence" of all sects, in nibandha style.
- **Not the same work as his Tantrasāra** (also in our catalogue) — keep the
  two distinct.
- Bühnemann documents **Buddhist deities and mantras inside** this Bengal
  tantric compendium — cross-tradition borrowing is expected, not error.

## B. MOVES / TRAPS
1. **Compilation voice**: it quotes authorities and organises by topic —
   do NOT read it as a single-author treatise. Track quoted source where
   marked.
2. **Ten Mahāvidyās** frame; Mahācīnācāra appears in Tārā-related material.
3. Buddhist deities/mantras appear legitimately — gloss as given.
4. Ritual/prayoga register dominates: exactness over philosophy.

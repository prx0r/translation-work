# TRANSMISSION BRIEF — IPVV V3B (kriyā-dvitīyo, KSTS vol3 passage)

*For beam drafters. Machine-proposed orientation; the T1 + L0 are the authority.*

## What this text is
Vimarśinī on the Kriyādhikāra (third section of ĪPK vimarśa 3): the opponent's
case that the agent (kartṛ) is constructed (kalpita), continuing the
relation-refutation from chunkJ. Prose commentary, not verse — Abhinavagupta
arguing against pūrvapakṣa positions (Bauddha, Bhartṛhari's readers,
Sāṅkhya/Vaiśeṣika on agency).

## The moves to look for
1. Action (kriyā) requires an agent-subject; Buddhist momentarism and
   relation-theories (saṃbandha, apekṣā) fail to ground it.
2. Bhartṛhari's Vākyapadīya on relation is quoted — then turned: adhesion
   (śleṣa) and dependence (pāratantrya) presuppose what they explain.
3. Śakti without a possessor (śaktimān) is incoherent — same stroke used
   against the Śāktivādins (cf. Torella IPK intro: Somananda's icchā-jñāna-kriyā
   triad at Śakti-Sadāśiva-Īśvara levels).
4. Close: "tena kartāpi kalpitaḥ" — the constructedness verdict that the
   recognition teaching will later reverse (jñānādhikāra).

## Traps
- Kartṛ/kriyā/karaṇa are technical terms, not generic "doer/doing". Keep the
  triad distinct.
- Vivarta/pariṇāma/pratibimba refutations nearby are DIFFERENT targets —
  don't blend them into the agency argument.
- Torella's vrtti (Trivandrum ms.) is the base text witness; Bhāskarī
  (Pandey) is the alternate commentary witness. Cite neither in the map —
  traces resolve to T1/L0 only.

# Transmission brief — Kālottara (Siddhānta ritual base) — RESEARCHED 2026-11-11
*Authority: Muktabodha Siddhānta page; Goodall/Brunner on the paddhatis.*

## A. WHY IT MATTERS
The Kālottara (Vātula tradition) is an **auxiliary scripture that is the
actual source of the classical Siddhānta ritual manuals** — Somaśambhu's
and Aghoraśiva's paddhatis derive "almost exclusively" from the Kālottara
and (for Aghoraśiva) the Mṛgendra, NOT from the root Rudrabheda/Śivabheda
Āgamas. Multiple Kālottaras exist (Dviśatikālottara, Ṣaṭsahasra-, Bṛhat-).

## B. TRAPS
1. **Identify WHICH Kālottara** before glossing (text, chapter count).
2. Ritual-procedural genre: imperatives, substances, mantras — exactness.
3. No doctrine-first reading: it is a ritual base, not a philosophical work.

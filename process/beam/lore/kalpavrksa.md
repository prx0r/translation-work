# Transmission brief — Kalpavṛkṣa (Sāhib Kaul) — RESEARCHED 2026-11-11
*Source: Jürgen Hanneder's monograph (the standard study).*

## A. FACTS
- By **Sāhib Kaul** (17th c.), of the Kashmiri Śrīvidyā lineage that also
  produced Devīnāmavilāsa (whose ch. 3 paraphrases Kṣemarāja's
  Pratyabhijñāhṛdaya).
- **Unprecedented in Sanskrit literature**: a visual/lattice text
  (compared to the European *carmen cancellatum*) mapping multiple textual
  sources into one work, with citations running in several directions, and
  **"intexts" from thirty languages**.
- Its declared purpose: to show that **"Advaita Vedānta, Śaiva
  non-dualism, and supreme non-dualism are all the final conclusion of all
  philosophical systems"** — de-emphasising doctrinal difference.

## B. ⚠ EXTRACTION WARNING
Our Muktabodha witness yields very few verses for a work of this size and
complexity (check chars/verse). The Kalpavṛkṣa's lattice structure does
NOT map onto ordinary verse extraction. **Do not seed this from the verse
pipeline** until a structure-specific intake is built; treat as a
separate engineering task.

## C. IF/WHEN EXTRACTED
- Lattice citations: each cell may quote a different source — provenance
  per line is essential, not optional.
- Multi-language "intexts": transliterate, never translate silently.
- Do not harmonise the sources; the work's thesis is that they agree —
  show the argument, don't assume it.

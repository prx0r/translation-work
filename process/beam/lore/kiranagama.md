# Transmission brief — Kiraṇatantra (Siddhānta) — RESEARCHED 2026-11-11
*Source: Dominic Goodall, Bhatt̥a Rāmakaṇṭha's Commentary on the
Kiraṇatantra, vol. I (ch. 1–6), IFP Publ. 86.1, 1998.*

## A. FACTS
- **64 chapters**; touches every aspect of the cult — theology, yoga,
  observances, ritual, architecture, iconography.
- **Doctrinally central** scripture of Śaiva Siddhānta.
- Commentary: **Bhaṭṭa Rāmakaṇṭha II** (10th c.), covering only the
  doctrinal portion; Goodall's critical edition + annotated translation of
  ch. 1–6 is the standard (four South Indian palm-leaf mss + 27 mss of the
  tantra collated).

## B. MOVES / TRAPS
1. **Dualist Siddhānta** — world real, distinct from Śiva; no monism.
2. **Rāmakaṇṭha's vṛtti** is terse and technical; separate mūla from gloss.
3. Technical vocabulary (tattvas, kalās, adhvans) must match Goodall's
   usage — that apparatus is the reference standard.

**Ledger (LOCK):** Rāmakaṇṭha II = the 10th-c. commentator; vṛtti = commentary (only on the doctrinal portion); tattva/kalā/adhvan = the doctrinal inventories; siddhānta = the dualist system.

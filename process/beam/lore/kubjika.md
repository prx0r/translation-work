# Transmission brief — Kubjikā corpus — RESEARCHED 2026-11-11
*Sources: Goudriaan & Schoterman, Kubjikāmatatantra critical ed. (Brill 1988);
Dyczkowski, The Canon of the Śaivāgama and the Kubjikā Tantras of the Western
Kaula Tradition (SUNY); Heilijgers-Seelen, Pañcacakra study (1994).*

## A. FACTS
- The KMT = **Kulālikāmnāya** recension, **~3,500 ślokas** (vs the ~6,000-śloka
  Ṣaṭsahasra version). Critical edition: **Goudriaan & Schoterman**.
- **Oldest ms = first half of the 12th c. CE**; virtually all mss are
  **Nepalese** (National Archives, Kathmandu; NGMPP microfilms) — but the
  texts say the school originated in a mountain region, not Nepal.
- **Kubjikā = "the Crooked One" = manonmanī, "power beyond mind"**: in the
  bliss of union she withdraws her limbs into the **triangle of Seed (bīja),
  Drop (bindu) and Sound (nāda)** from which the universe is created.
- The KMT teaches the **Seal of Speech (vācika mudrā)** — essentially the
  **fifty letters** of the alphabet.
- Consort **Navātman / Naveśvara**; diagram **Saṃvartāmaṇḍala** (octagon).
- The **Nine Nāthas** are said to propagate the doctrine today.

## B. MOVES
1. **Pañcacakra** structure (five cakras) — a load-bearing sequence.
2. **Crookedness**: Kubjikā's bent/contorted form is doctrinal (the
   withdrawn limbs), never merely iconographic description.
3. **Letters = the goddess**: phonematic emanation (fifty letters) is the
   creation model; gloss letters as deities, not sounds.
4. **Narrative richness**: the tantras tell her mythic history and the
   descent of siddhas — these are scripture, not decoration.

## C. TRAPS
- **Kubjikā ≠ Kālī ≠ Tripurā.** Related, distinct. Dyczkowski's *Kubjikā,
  Kālī, Tripurā and Trika* is the comparison reference.
- **Nepalese ≠ origin.** Do not say "Nepalese tantra".
- Recension: KMT (Kulālikāmnāya) vs Ṣaṭsahasra — state which text.
- Śāradā-script mss are the base; readings differ from printed editions.

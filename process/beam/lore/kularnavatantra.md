# Transmission brief — Kulārṇavatantra (the Ocean of Kula)

*Largest Kaula tantra (2,068 verses, 17 ullāsas): Śiva instructs
Pārvatī on Kaula doctrine and practice — guru, dīkṣā, kuladravya,
cakra-pūjā, the avadhūta ethic. Encyclopedic and ritual-heavy;
source Muktabodha M00031 (Malaviya IAST, CC BY-NC 4.0). Passages
kul-p01..p83 chunked sequentially 25 verses (no chapter breaks in
e-text); verse ints run 1–2068.*

## 1. WHAT IT IS

The Kaula ocean: doctrine (Kula as the family of powers, Kaula vs
siddhānta/pāśupata claims), guru-disciple protocol, initiation
grades, ritual substances and their transgressive logic, cakra
worship, yogic interiorization, and the conduct of the liberated
(avadhūta). Dialogue form — Pārvatī asks, Śiva answers; keep
question-vs-answer distinct in argmaps.

## 2. MOVES TO LOOK FOR

1. **Superiority claims.** Kula-over-rival-tantra arguments recur;
   gloss the comparison target exactly (which school is being
   surpassed, on what ground).
2. **Guru centrality.** Nearly every practice passage routes through
   the guru; dīkṣā grades (samaya/putraka/sādhaka/ācārya) are
   load-bearing categories — never merge them.
3. **Transgression logic.** Forbidden substances/practices are
   justified by nondual recognition, not license; C1 must carry the
   justification, not just the act.
4. **Interiorization ladder.** Outer ritual → inner cakra → pure
   consciousness; track which rung each passage sits on.

## 3. TRAPS

1. **Ritual catalogs.** Long substance/mantra lists — gloss each
   member exactly; dropped members break later inferences.
2. **Dialogue attribution.** Pārvatī's questions vs Śiva's answers;
   never merge voices in L2.
3. **Kula polysemy.** kula = family/powers/body/letter-cluster by
   context; use L0 grounds per occurrence, never a fixed gloss.
4. **Mantra syllables.** bīja strings (aim hrīṃ śrīṃ etc.) are
   transliterated, never "translated"; keep byte-exact.

## 4. APPROACH

83 passages × 25 verses. Standard triples (24/group); T1 first in
batches, then seed progressively — never more than ~10 pending
triples per song to keep review queues drainable. C1-essence per
passage names what it DOES to the Kula-claim.

**Ledger (LOCK):** kula = the family (powers); kaula = its knower/
way; ullāsa = wave/chapter; dīkṣā = initiation; guru = the
door; cakra = wheel (outer/inner); avadhūta = beyond-conduct;
bīja = seed-syllable (transliterate only).

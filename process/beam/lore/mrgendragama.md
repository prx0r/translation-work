# Transmission brief — Mṛgendrāgama (Siddhānta) — RESEARCHED 2026-11-11
*Sources: N. R. Bhatt ed. with Nārāyaṇakaṇṭha's vṛtti; Hulin (French,
vidyāpāda) & Brunner (French, kriyā/caryā); KSTS 50 (1930, Kaul Shastri);
GRETIL text by Goodall (2020).*

## A. FACTS
- **Upāgama of the Kāmika**; the **clearest short summary of the
  Saiddhāntika system**. Four pādas: Vidyā, Kriyā, Caryā, Yoga.
- Commentary: **Nārāyaṇakaṇṭha's vṛtti** (10th–11th c. Kashmir).
- French translations: **Michel Hulin** (Vidyāpāda), **Hélène Brunner**
  (Kriyā + Caryā). No modern full English.

## B. MOVES
1. **Dualist frame**: Śiva, self (aṇu), and world are distinct — never gloss
   non-dually; Pratyabhijñā readings are anachronistic here.
2. **Pāda discipline**: Vidyā = doctrine, Kriyā = ritual, Caryā = conduct,
   Yoga = meditation. Track which pāda a passage sits in.
3. **Initiation (dīkṣā)** is the soteriological core; the four grades
   (samayin, putraka, sādhaka, ācārya) are load-bearing.

## C. TRAPS
- **Kṣemarāja is NOT a commentator here**; do not import his vocabulary.
- Ritual enumeration is dense; each item exact.
- Goodall/Brunner define terms precisely — follow them over older glosses.

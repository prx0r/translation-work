# Transmission brief — Śrīvidyārṇavatantra (M00217)

*Tradition (authority map): **Śrīvidyā**. Researched brief; UNCERTAIN facts
marked.*

## 1. WHAT IT IS
"The Ocean of Śrīvidyā" — a **Śrīvidyā tantra** on Tripurasundarī: Śrīcakra,
initiatory mantras, daily/occasional worship, guru-lineage. Read as revelation
dialogue; the text's authority is the transmitted mantra, not argument.

## 2. MOVES TO LOOK FOR
1. **Mantra transmission** — the exact form given by guru to disciple.
2. **Śrīcakra worship** — outer pūjā mapped onto inner avaraṇas.
3. **Eligibility and dīkṣā grades** — who, when, with what preparation.
4. **Ritual substances and their interiorization** — track the rung.

## 3. TRAPS
1. **Byte-exact mantras** (bālā/pañcadaśī/ṣoḍaśī) — a swapped syllable is a
   different mantra; never normalise.
2. **Guru-lineage names** — proper nouns; keep diacritics exactly.
3. **Śrīcakra geometry terms** — gloss each (avaraṇa, cakra, yoni, bindu).
4. **Do not read Kashmir Śaiva doctrine into Śrīvidyā** — parallel vocabulary,
   different system.

## 4. APPROACH
Verse/chunk. C1-essence = what the rite does to the worshipper's identity with
Rajarājeśvarī. Keep the mantra layer untranslated and byte-exact.

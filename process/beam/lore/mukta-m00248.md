# Transmission brief — Kālottara (M00248)

*Tradition (authority map): **Śaiva Siddhānta**. Researched brief — an early
and widely quoted Śaiva āgama.*

## 1. WHAT IT IS
The **Kālottara** ("that which is beyond time") is an early **Śaiva Siddhānta
āgama**, foundational and heavily quoted — notably the doctrinal basis for the
Mṛgendrāgama's treatment of initiation, cosmology, and the means of knowledge.
Read as authoritative revealed teaching.

## 2. MOVES TO LOOK FOR
1. **Initiation (dīkṣā)** — its kinds and necessity; a core subject.
2. **Cosmology / tattva emanation** — the graded realities.
3. **Pramāṇa** — how Śaiva doctrine is established (knowledge-means).
4. **Liberation and the bound soul (paśu)** — bondage, means, release.

# Transmission brief — Netratantra (Tantra of the Eye / Mṛtyujit)

*Universal (sarvasāmānya) tantra c.9th c. (423 verses, M00038): Śiva as
Netranātha answers Devī; central deities Amṛteśvara + Amṛtalakṣmī;
death-conquering (mṛtyujit) rites, deity visualizations spanning
Mantramārga + Vaiṣṇava systems, Śākta/Kulamārga currents (ch. 7 subtle
meditation, ch. 20 yoginīs). Singh translated chs VII–VIII only; Flood
team full translation in prep (Routledge, unpublished). Source Muktabodha
M00038 (IAST, CC BY-NC 4.0). Passages net-p01..p17 sequential 25s.*

## 1. WHAT IT IS

The tantra that overrides sectarian lines: one Eye-Lord whose
visualizations absorb Śaiva, Vaiṣṇava and goddess systems alike, aimed
at conquering death and installing divine perception (divyacakṣus).
Catholic by design — argmaps must preserve cross-system borrowings,
not flatten them to "the" Trika view.

## 2. MOVES TO LOOK FOR

1. **Eye metaphors are doctrine.** Netra/dṛṣṭi/cakṣus terms state the
   tantra's epistemology (perception = liberation vehicle).
2. **Amṛta complex.** amṛta/mṛtyujit/related nectar-immortality claims
   recur — track them as one thread across passages.
3. **Visualization ladders.** Deity-forms build feature by feature;
   keep the build order in L2 or the iconography scrambles.
4. **Universalist claims.** sarvasāmānya passages argue supremacy BY
   inclusion — argmap the inclusion move explicitly.

## 3. TRAPS

1. **Vaiṣṇava material inside a Śaiva tantra.** Gloss Vaiṣṇava names/
   mantras exactly; never "correct" them to Śaiva equivalents.
2. **Yoginī passages (esp. ~ch.20).** Kulamārga technical erotics —
   clinical exactness, no euphemism, no prurience.
3. **Flood-team overlap.** Their edition (NAK MS 1-285, 1200 CE) may
   differ from M00038 readings — mark TEXTUAL where uncertain.

## 4. APPROACH

17 passages × 25. Standard triples (≤12/group); exact glosses;
C1-essence names what each movement DOES to the perception-claim.

**Ledger (LOCK):** netra = eye (the Lord as perception); amṛteśvara =
nectar-Lord; amṛtalakṣmī = nectar-Lakṣmī; mṛtyujit = death-conqueror;
sarvasāmānya = universal (cross-sectarian); dhyāna = visualization.

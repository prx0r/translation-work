# Transmission brief — Śāktavijñāna (Knowledge of Śakti)

*Short Kaula-adjacent upāya text (3 passages, ~30 verses): the awakening
(utthāpana/bodhana) of Śakti, her play (krīḍati parameśvarī), and
penetration of the three malas. Practice-first doctrine — gloss as
operational claims, not metaphysics.*

## 1. WHAT IT IS

A compact manual of Śakti-knowledge: how dormant power is raised,
settled (viśrāma), and recognized as the goddess at play. Moves from
technique (raising) to recognition (non-fear amid her forms:
teṣu teṣu na bhetavyam) to impurity-dissolution (malatraya).

## 2. MOVES TO LOOK FOR

1. **Raising → resting → recognizing.** The three-passage arc;
   keep the sequence in L2.
2. **Fearlessness formula.** Non-fear among goddess-forms is the
   hinge claim — argmap it as the central inference.
3. **Mala mechanics.** Which impurity dissolves by which recognition;
   keep the three malas distinct.

## 3. TRAPS

1. **bodhana/utthāpana are technical.** Awakening/raising are
   kuṇḍalinī-operations, not generic "knowledge." Never flatten.
2. **parameśvarī's play is doctrine, not poetry.** krīḍati states
   the nondual mechanism; C1 must carry it as claim.
3. **Authorship unattested in brief scope.** No author claims in
   grounds without a witness.

## 4. APPROACH

Small complete text; triples per passage, exact glosses, C1-essence
names what each movement DOES to the fear-claim.

**Ledger (LOCK):** utthāpana = raising; bodhana = awakening;
viśrāma = settling/rest; krīḍati parameśvarī = the goddess at play;
malatraya = the three impurities.

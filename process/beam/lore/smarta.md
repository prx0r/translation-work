# Transmission brief — Smārta (Vedāntic-tantric reconciliation) — RESEARCHED 2026-11-11
*Authority: Muktabodha Smārta page; Upaniṣadbrahmendra corpus.*

## A. WHAT IT IS HERE
In this corpus "Smārta" means texts by Smārta brahmins that **reconcile
Tantric knowledge with Smārta norms**: reframing Tantra as an
*upāsanākāṇḍa* complementing Mīmāṃsā's karma/jñāna-kāṇḍas, or homologising
Tantric worship with Advaita Vedānta (Śaṅkarācārya lineage).
- Root tantras: **Śāradātilaka** (with Rāghavabhaṭṭa's commentary) and the
  Kerala **Prapañcasāra**.
- Large **Upaniṣadbrahmendra** corpus commenting the Yoga-Upaniṣads
  (subtle-body yogic material shared with Śāktas and Nāthas).

## B. MOVES / TRAPS
1. **Reconciliation is the genre** — the argument structure is
   "Tantra = Veda's complement", not independent doctrine.
2. **Upaniṣadbrahmendra is Advaita first**; tantric terms are read through
   Advaita. Do not gloss them as Śaiva.
3. Yoga-Upaniṣad material: subtle-body vocabulary (nāḍī, cakra, kuṇḍalinī)
   shared across traditions — gloss in situ.
4. Pañcāyatana frame (Gaṇapati, Sūrya, Viṣṇu, Devī, Śiva) when present.

**Ledger (LOCK):** smārta = follower of smṛti-based orthodoxy; upāsanākāṇḍa
= the worship-division; pañcāyatana = the five-deity scheme; yoga-upaniṣad =
the subtle-body Upaniṣads.

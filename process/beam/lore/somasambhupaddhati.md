# Transmission brief — Somaśambhupaddhati — RESEARCHED 2026-11-11
*Hélène Brunner-Lachaux, ed.+French tr., 4 vols, IFP 1963–1998 (Publ. 25.1–4).*

## A. FACTS
- **11th century**; **standardised Saiddhāntika ritual** — the manual that
  fixed the tradition's practice.
- Brunner-Lachaux's four volumes remain, in Goodall's words, "the best
  introduction to Śāiva ritual anywhere". Part 4 (1998) covers
  **pratiṣṭhā** (installation): temple foundation, images of Śiva/Gaurī/
  Sun/Viṣṇu, doors, pots, maṭha, pool, well, tree; liṅga installation and
  removal of an old liṅga.

## B. MOVES / TRAPS
1. **Paddhati genre**: ordered operations. The SEQUENCE is the meaning.
2. **Who does what**: mantras, nyāsas, homas belong to the ācārya; material
   work to the śilpin. Keep the actor distinct.
3. Brunner's French is the reference; adopt her term renderings.

**Ledger (LOCK):** paddhati = ordered ritual procedure; ācārya = the ritual actor (mantras/nyāsas/homas); śilpin = the artisan (material work); pratiṣṭhā = installation.

# Transmission brief — Svabodhasiddhi (Establishment in Self-Awareness)

*Short Pratyabhijñā-flavored prakaraṇa (4 passages, ~28 verses from
v2): self-awareness (svasaṃvedana) free of all doctrinal coloring,
the grasper/grasped divide dissolved, the yogin's proof by
self-luminosity. Epistemology-first — every verse is a knowing-claim.*

## 1. WHAT IT IS

A manual of self-establishment: awareness (bodha) proves itself by
being self-luminous (svaprakāśa); no external pramāṇa needed. Opens
(v2) past all darśana colorings; closes in the yogin's direct
cognition. Argmaps stay in epistemic vocabulary throughout.

## 2. MOVES TO LOOK FOR

1. **Self-luminosity as proof.** svasaṃvedana needs no second
   witness — the hinge inference of the whole text.
2. **Grasper/grasped collapse.** grāhyagrahaka-saṃskāra dissolution
   is stated as accomplished fact, not aspiration.
3. **Purity by resolve.** svasaṃkalpa-parāmarśa as the operative
   means; keep means-vs-result distinct.

## 3. TRAPS

1. **sva- compounds are precise.** svasaṃvedana (self-awareness) ≠
   svātman (the self) ≠ svakīya (one's own). Never merge.
2. **Negations are doctrinal.** "Free from all darśana-coloring" is
   a supremacy claim over rival schools, not vagueness.
3. **No author claims** without witness in grounds.

## 4. APPROACH

Small complete text; triples per passage, exact glosses, C1-essence
names what each movement DOES to the proof-claim.

**Ledger (LOCK):** svasaṃvedana = self-awareness; svaprakāśa =
self-luminosity; grāhya/grāhaka = grasped/grasper; parāmarśa =
reflective grasp; pramāṇa = valid means (here: surpassed).

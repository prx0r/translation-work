# Transmission brief — Svacchandatantra (Tantra of the Self-Willed Lord)

*Early Trika-root Bhairava tantra (1,385 verses, M00091): Svacchandabhairava's
autonomy (svacchanda = self-willed) as doctrine and practice — mantra,
maṇḍala, dīkṣā, yogic process. Kṣemarāja's Uddyota is the commentary
(support only). Singh translated ch. VII (prāṇa) only — rest untranslated.
Source Muktabodha M00091 (IAST, CC BY-NC 4.0). Passages sva-p01..p56
sequential 25s; verse ints run 1–1385.*

## 1. WHAT IT IS

The tantra of the Free Lord: Bhairava as pure autonomy, with the full
apparatus (mantroddhāra, maṇḍala construction, fourfold initiation,
daily rite, funerary and yogic process) unfolding from that autonomy.
Dialogue frame (Devī asks, Bhairava answers) — keep voices distinct.

## 2. MOVES TO LOOK FOR

1. **Autonomy as first principle.** svacchanda- claims ground every
   practice passage; test premises against it.
2. **Mantra-machinery.** mantroddhāra sequences are load-bearing
   technical lists — gloss each syllable exactly, transliterate bījas.
3. **Rite ladders.** nitya/naimittika/kāmya layering; track which
   obligation-level each passage sits on.
4. **Yogic interiorization.** Later adhyāyas turn rite inward;
   watch the outer→inner pivot per passage.

## 3. TRAPS

1. **Long enumerations.** Deity/pitcha/mantra lists — dropped members
   break later inferences; gloss each.
2. **Bhairava epithets.** svacchanda/mahocchuṣma/agora are functional
   titles here, not decoration — gloss by function.
3. **Uddyota bleed.** Kṣemarāja adjudicates forks only; mūla grounds
   stay morphological.

## 4. APPROACH

56 passages × 25. Standard triples (≤12/group for review pass rate);
exact glosses; C1-essence names what each movement DOES to the
autonomy-claim.

**Ledger (LOCK):** svacchanda = self-willed (the Lord); ullāsa/adhyāya
= chapters (unlabeled in e-text — sequential); mantroddhāra = mantra
extraction; dīkṣā = initiation; bīja = seed-syllable (transliterate).

## KNOWLEDGE CORE (scholarly grounding — obey)

*Distilled understanding for translators. Verified against the Muktabodha
authority page (Bhairava Tantras: "alone among early sources it teaches a
theology of absolute freedom (svātantrya)"), the GRETIL paṭala-based text
(typed from KSTS/Dwivedi eds.), and Kṣemarāja's Uddyota (support only).
Muktabodha M00091; 1385 mūla verses in our witness. **Date: core text no
later than the 7th century CE** (White 2012, doi:10.1093/jhs/his019 — i.e.
Svacchanda predates the Netratantra). Kṣemarāja's Uddyota (c. 1000–1050)
colours the received reading; GRETIL text typed per-chapter from
KSTS/Dwivedi editions.*

## A. THE ONE THESIS

> **Svacchanda = the Self-willed Lord. Absolute freedom (svātantrya) is
> not a power the Lord has but what the Lord IS; the world, time, and
> bondage are its contractions. Ritual and yoga are the re-expansion of
> that freedom in the practitioner.**

This is the earliest systematic svātantrya theology — the seed of the
Kashmir non-dual school. Argmaps must track *freedom vs contraction*
claims, not merely ritual steps.

## C. LOAD-BEARING TECHNICAL SETS

1. **svātantrya** — absolute autonomy; NEVER "freedom" in a political or
   psychological sense. It is ontological self-determination.
2. **Mantroddhāra** — "mantra extraction": a technical procedure producing
   the tantra's mantra-set; the lists are load-bearing.
3. **36 tattvas** — the Śaiva tattva ladder (Śiva→pṛthvī) used in
   tattva-dīkṣā; members exact, order exact.
4. **Six aṃśakas** (paṭala 8) — analytical parts of a ritual unit; a
   hidden taxonomy, gloss each.
5. **Time & the voids** (paṭalas 9–10) — the tantra's signature:
   manipulating time-perception and contemplating inner voids. These are
   contemplative operations, not cosmology-as-fact.

## D. TRANSLATION TRAPS

1. **Uddyota bleed** — Kṣemarāja's non-dual commentary on a text that is
   not always non-dual; keep mūla and commentary separate.
2. **Deity nomenclature**: svacchanda, mahocchuṣma, agora are functional
   titles of the same Lord, not separate deities.
3. **Long enumerations** (mudrās, yoginīs, mantras) — dropped members
   break later inferences.
4. **Prāṇa vocabulary** — physiologically exact (prāṇa/apāna/udāna…); no
   metaphor inflation.
5. **Lacunae** — mark `[...]` textual gaps where GRETIL marks them.

## E. QUALITY BAR

Paṭalas 5–7 and 11–12 carry the doctrine; 2–4 and 13–15 are largely
procedural. Do not inflate ritual lists into metaphysics, and do not
flatten the svātantrya arguments (opening, ch.9–12) into ritual notes.

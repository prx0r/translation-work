## MOVES to look for
1. Invocation→praise→purpose (1-21): the work opens by ENACTING reverence,
   not describing it — read as performance, gloss as doctrine.
2. Freedom/bondage + jnana/ajnana (22-51): the whole Trika problem in 30
   verses — bondage is obscuration, not substance; knowledge is recognition.
3. Siva-proofs (52-139): a priori consciousness, ubiquity, powers of freedom,

   Twelve Goddesses deployed, pratibha nourished, injunctions dissolved for

4. Means ladder preview (140-247): all three upayas + three aveshas defined

5. Topics enunciation (279-330): the table of contents Abhinava gives for his

## TRAPS
- `vijñāna`: liberating KNOWLEDGE/insight (means-laden), not "consciousness"
  in general and not dry cognition.
- `avesa` (penetration) vs `upaya` (means): paired but distinct — penetration
  is the entering, means is the track; definitions 167-170 are canonical.
- `pratibha` (creative intuition/imagination): a power AND a goddess AND the
  supreme means (cf. a13) — track which face each verse shows.
- Etymologies (94-105) are theology-by-sound (nirvacana), not linguistics —
  render the CLAIM (Bhairava bears/terrifies), not a philological hedge.
- `gramadharma/ramastha`: technical states of the collective/established —
  transliterate + function-gloss, never flatten to "conduct".
- Verse-number drift: Jayaratha/Dyczkowski printed numbers run +1 in places
  (TOC parentheticals); GRETIL spans rule — never renumber.

# TRANSMISSION BRIEF — Tantraloka ahnika 3 (śāmbhavopāya, 295 verses)

*Assessor-written 2026-11-06 from web scholarship. Authority: GRETIL spans + legacy L2.*

## What this text is
The supreme means (śāmbhava-upāya) after the meanless path of a02: recognition
through the creative emanation expressed in phonemes. First portion: philosophy
of reflection (pratibimba); then successive phoneme emanation as phases of the
coeternal Śiva-Śakti creation within one consciousness-field; close: deeper
speech levels, music, nāda. Dyczkowski vol 2 (ch 2–3, 2023, with Jayaratha's
Viveka + source notes) is the working translation; Gnoli 1975 bare text;
Silburn/Padou French of ch 1–5 with Viveka extracts; Lakshmanjoo oral teaching
(ch 1–18) as horizon, not source.

## The moves to look for
1. Anupāya (a02) → śāmbhavopāya (a03): the means appears only after the
   means-less is established. Situate the opening against a02.
2. Reflection doctrine first (mirror logic — sets up phonemic emanation).
3. Phoneme-by-phoneme emanation phases; universe as bliss-sound flow in one field.
4. Close: deeper speech, music, nāda (pure vibration).

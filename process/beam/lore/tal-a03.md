# TRANSMISSION BRIEF — Tantraloka ahnika 3 (śāmbhavopāya, supreme means, 295 verses)

*Assessor-written 2026-11-06 from web scholarship. Authority: GRETIL spans + legacy L2.*

## What this text is
The supreme means (śāmbhavopāya) after the meanless path of a02: recognition
through the process of creative emanation expressed in Sanskrit phonemes.
First portion: philosophy of reflection (pratibimba); then successive
emanation of phonemes as phases of Śiva-Śakti coeternal creation within one
consciousness-field; close: deeper speech levels, music, nāda (pure vibration).
Dyczkowski vol 2 (ch 2–3, 2023, with Jayaratha's Viveka + source notes) is
the working translation; Gnoli 1975 bare-text Italian as backup.

## The moves to look for
1. Reflection doctrine first (mirror logic — sets up phonemic emanation).
2. Phoneme-by-phoneme emanation as creation phases (not alphabet recital).
3. Coeternal Śiva-Śakti creation in one field; universe as bliss-sound flow.
4. Close: para/paśyantī depths, music, nāda.

## Traps
- Phoneme lists are ARGUMENTS about creation order, not catalogues.
- a03's reflection section vs chunkH-style pratibimba (IPVV) — same mirror
  logic, different work; do not import IPVV conclusions here.
- 295 verses: this ahnika runs in FIVE window tasks (w1-60 … w240-294).
  Never summarise across windows; end each window's last span at its verse.

## How to approach
Topics follow emanation order. Gist = what each phase establishes about
consciousness creating through sound. Enumerations of phonemes get
"X-phase of emanation" topics, never "list of letters".

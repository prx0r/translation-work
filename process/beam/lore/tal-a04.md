# Transmission brief: Tantraloka a04 (saktopaya + Twelve Kalis, 279v)

*Assessor-authored from web scholarship, 2026-11-07. Cached per front, auto-injected into triple packets.*

## What the text IS
Ahnika 4 (GRETIL 279 verses; ALL of Dyczkowski Vol 3). The Empowered Means
(saktopaya): liberation through purified thought/energy. TAA CH4 course map:
vikalpasamskara 2-12, tarkatattva 13-32, true teacher 33-40, teacher-types
40-78, pramanas 78-85, yoga-limbs useless 86-109, conceived worship irrelevant
109-122, samviccakrodaya 122-126, Yogasamcara yoga 127-146, TWELVE KALIS
146-173 (the volume's cardinal feature; Mahartha/Mahanaya unpublished sources
in appendices), Kali conclusion 173-181, mantraviya 181-193, true japa
194-203, conclusion 204-211, prohibition/injunction relativity 212-279.

## MOVES to look for
1. Thought purified, not suppressed (2-12): vikalpa refined into certainty —
   saktopaya works WITH mind, against a05's bodily supports and a02's
   abandonment.
2. Tarka glorified (13-32): sound reasoning as a MEANS (not dry logic) —
   track how each tarka-verse ends in energy, not conclusion.
3. Guru doctrine (33-78): teacher-types ranked by capacity to transmit energy;
   pramanas (78-85) justify why a teacher's word counts as knowledge.
4. Double negation (86-122): yoga-limbs AND conceived worship both declared
   useless/irrelevant — the passage clears the field for the KalI wheel.
5. Twelve Kalis (146-181): the emotional-cognitive cycle (creation→existence→
   destruction ×3 + ineffable 13th?) as goddesses; each Kali = a phase of
   consciousness to ride, not a deity to petition.
6. Mantra vitality + true japa (181-203): repetition that repeats the
   repeater into silence.
7. Ethical relativity (212-279): prohibitions/injunctions bind only the bound
   — read as liberation doctrine, never antinomian license.

## TRAPS
- `kali`: goddess AND time-phase AND cognitive pulse — per-verse resolution,
  resonance noted.
- `saktopaya` vs `anavopaya` (a05) vs `anupaya` (a02): never blur the three
  means; a04's polemic against yoga-limbs targets LOWER means, not all practice.
- `tarka`: liberating reasoning-energy, not syllogistic logic.
- `samvit-cakra`: cycle of consciousness-energies — not a body-plexus.
- `kalanaya/krama` terms in Kali section (srsti/sthiti/samhara, anakhya):
  phase-names, exact IAST, fixed glosses.
- `vidhi/nishedha` (212-279): ritual injunction/prohibition — philosophical
  claim about their scope, not permission for conduct; translate faithfully,
  no bowdlerizing, no sensationalizing.
- Mahartha/Mahanaya names: manuscript-recovered proper names — exact,
  flagged as Dyczkowski-appendix readings where they diverge from KSTS.

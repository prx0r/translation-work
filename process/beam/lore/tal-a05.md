# Transmission brief: Tantraloka a05 (anavopaya, 159v)

*Assessor-authored from web scholarship, 2026-11-07. Cached per front, auto-injected into triple packets.*

## What the text IS
Ahnika 5 (GRETIL 159 verses; Vol 4 ch5 per Dyczkowski: "brilliant exposition of
the practices of the Corporeal Means (anavopaya)" as listed in the
Malinivijayottara). The Individual Means: liberation through body/breath/mind
supports. Course map (Dyczkowski TAA CH5): buddhidhyana 19-42, prana-uccara
43-51, heart-utterance 52-74, paratattva-entry 74-86, mantra-plane 86-89,
voids/states/abodes 90-100, path-signs 100-112, Trika-linga 112-127, karana
128-131, varna-tattva 131-139, three seed-syllables 140-141 (SAUH 142-145,
KHPHREM 146-159ab).

## MOVES to look for
1. Thought purified by outer support (vv2-4): even the lowest means begins with
   stabilizing certainty (niscaya), not blanking the mind.
2. buddhidhyana (19-42): intellect-meditation discriminating sentient from
   insentient (cit vs buddhi/prana/deha) until body-breath reflect in
   consciousness.
3. uccara ladder: breath-utterance → heart-utterance → conscious-nature
   utterance → entry into the supreme principle → entry into the mantra plane.
   Each is a THINNER support, not a new doctrine.
4. Cartography (90-131): ten voids, states, abodes, unstruck-sound levels,
   path-signs, Trika-linga, karana instruments — the map proves the territory
   is traversable.
5. Bijas (140-159): SAUH, KHPHREM, KSMRYUM — the whole ahnika condenses into
   three syllables; gloss them as condensations, not decorations.

## TRAPS
- `anavopaya` (corporeal/individual means): lowest of the three upayas, but
  Abhinava treats it as genuine — never condescend ("mere beginner stuff").
- `uccara`: vibratory utterance/emanation through breath — not "pronunciation".
- `buddhidhyana`: meditation BY means of intellect, intellect AS the instrument.
- `karana` (instruments): technical yogic instruments (karaṇa), not "causes".
- `varna`: phoneme as reality-level (varṇa-tattva), not letter-of-alphabet.
- `sunya` (voids): ten experiential voids on the path — stages, not nihilism.
- Breath-cycle language overlaps a06 (kalacakra): a05 maps supports IN breath;
  a06 maps time IN breath. Keep the direction straight.

## APPROACH
- Long ahnika (159v) — windows of ≤60 already cover it; keep span boundaries
  on practice-shifts (the course map above), never uniform blocks.
- Body/breath terms stay concrete ("channel", "knot", "void", "throb");
  consciousness terms stay luminous ("reflect", "rest", "recognize").
- Bijas untranslated, exact capitals; their glosses describe function
  ("condenses X into one pulse"), never decode letter-by-letter.
- Witnesses (Dyczkowski Vol 4 + TAA CH5 course, Lakshmanjoo revelation) are
  consistency checks only — never grounds, never hosted.

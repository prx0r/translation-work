## MOVES to look for
1. Time internalized: cosmic cycles (days/months/years/ages) located AS
   breath-phases — the calendar becomes respiration.
2. Kali as time-power (vv7-13): the goddess IS the origin of breath-measure;
   cross-check against a04's Twelve Kalis (same goddess, different function).
3. Body vitalized by consciousness-through-breath (14-22): breath is the wire,
   consciousness the current — never describe breath mechanically.
4. Measurement discipline (caramana, eight gatis): exact counts/places of
   breath-movement; numbers are practice facts, transcribe exactly.
5. Triad of energies (Vama/Jyestha/Raudri, 56-59): emission/maintenance/
   retraction mapped onto in/out/held breath.

## TRAPS
- `kala`: time AND goddess Power AND breath-phase — resolve per verse, note
  the resonance instead of picking one sense silently.
- `cakra`: wheel/cycle/plexus — in a06 usually TIME-cycle, not body-plexus;
  don't import hathayoga cakra defaults.
- `uccara/cara`: breath-movement/emanation — not "pronunciation".
- `dvadasanta`: the Twelve-end (12-finger-space above crown / at heart):
  a technical locus, keep transliterated with function glossed.
- a05/a06 boundary: a05 = supports IN breath (means); a06 = time IN breath
  (measure). Same vocabulary, opposite direction — check which way each verse
  walks before glossing.
- Svacchanda sourcing: Abhinava adapts, doesn't copy — where Jayaratha notes
  a variant, record it, don't harmonize.

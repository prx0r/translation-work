# TRANSMISSION BRIEF — Tantraloka ahnika 8 (deśādhvan, path of space, 452 verses)

*Assessor-written 2026-11-06 from web scholarship. Authority: GRETIL spans + legacy L2.*

## What this text is
The path of Space, after the path of Time (a06 kālādhvā = kriyāśakti).
Draws mostly on Svacchandatantra: the 224 worlds and inhabitants across the
36 tattvas, Earth to Śiva. Three parts: (i) measure of the worlds, (ii)
compendium of the worlds, (iii) union of categories with worlds. Dyczkowski
content note (vol 5): phonemic consciousness grounds language and purifies
the sixfold path through perception; all sustained in consciousness like
image-in-mirror / dream-to-dreamer.

## The moves to look for
1. World-measure: distances, directions (dik), sun-based orientation,
   hells-to-Śiva vertical order (Kālāgni below, Meru cities, 26 antarāla).
2. Compendium: inhabitants per world, grouped by tattva.
3. Union: categories × worlds resolved into consciousness-sustained unity.

## Traps
- Cosmology lists are the CONTENT (measure/compendium), not filler.
  a08 windows are mostly enumerative — say what each span measures.
- Direction lore (dik, sūrya-āśraya) recurs in TAL 15 rituals — keep a08's
  cosmological sense separate from ritual use.
- 452 verses, EIGHT window tasks. Same windowing discipline as a03.

## How to approach
Spans follow world-groups, not uniform blocks. Gist = what is measured /
who inhabits / how it unifies. Numbers (yojanas, counts) belong in the gist
— they are the doctrine here, not decoration.

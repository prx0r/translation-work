# Transmission brief: Tantraloka a10 (tattvabheda, 310v)

*Assessor-authored from web scholarship, 2026-11-07. Cached per front, auto-injected into triple packets.*

## What the text IS
Ahnika 10 (GRETIL 310 verses; Vol 6 ch10: Tattvabhedanam, Division of the
Principles). Pairs with a09 (tattvadhvan, the 36 levels): a09 maps the
TERRITORY, a10 maps the PERCEIVERS. TAA CH10 map: seven perceivers 3-18,
vastudharma argument 19-78, Bhairava-undiminished 78-88, perceptibility
doctrine 89-97, tattvavidhi per perceiver 97-186 (sakala 15 aspects 103-104,
vidya-deconditioned 13 aspects 105-107, pralayakala 11 aspects 108-109, rest
to Siva 110-117, instruction-object paradox 117-123, others 123-149, varieties
150-172, deep sleep 173-181), breath-phase projection 186-205, intuition
goddess 206-211, gap-closing accomplishments 212-219, Kaula waking/dream/
sleep/fourth/beyond 227-294, five states 294-309.

## MOVES to look for
1. Seven-perceiver ladder (sakala → vidya/akala → pralayakala/pralayakevalin →
   vijnanakala → mantra/mahesvara → Siva): each rung = less contraction, same
   consciousness.
2. vastudharma proof (19-78): shared perceptibility is INTRINSIC to the
   entity as consciousness-manifestation — the chapter's philosophical engine;
   premises must carry the argument, not the examples (show/audience
   illustrations 83-88 serve it).
3. Bhairava-undiminished (78-88): differentiation doesn't limit the whole —
   the nondual guardrail for the whole taxonomy.
4. tattvavidhi tables (97-186): aspect-counts per perceiver (15/13/11/...) are
   doctrine, transcribe exactly; the Siva-as-instruction-object paradox
   (117-123) is the chapter's sharpest move — Siva objectified only for
   teaching, never in fact.
5. Breath projection (186-205) + gap-closing (212-219): the ladder becomes
   practice — sixteen perception-phases in one breath-cycle.
6. Kaula states (227-309): waking/dream/sleep/fourth/beyond re-read as
   perceiver-positions, not brain-states.

## TRAPS
- `pramatr` taxonomy names (sakala, vijnanakala, pralayakala, mantra,
  mantresvara, mantра-mahesvara, Siva): fixed IAST table, never translated
  into epithets, never conflated.
- `vedyatva/vastudharma`: perceptibility / entity-nature-as-law — epistemic
  terms, exact and repeated identically every occurrence.
- `avastha` (states) vs `pramatr-bheda` (perceiver-divisions): keep the two
  taxonomies distinct; verses 288-309 deliberately cross-tabulate them —
  follow the cross-reference, don't merge.
- Aspect-counts (15/13/11): arithmetic doctrine; verify against spans, flag
  don't fix.
- `turya/turyatita`: fourth/beyond-fourth — experiential stations, not
  metaphysical places.
- a09/a10 boundary: a09 = WHAT the levels are; a10 = WHO perceives them.
  Same tattva names, different question — check the verse's question first.

## APPROACH
- 310 verses, philosophy-heavy: premises must be ARGUMENT steps (claim →
  reason → consequence), never topic labels; the 60-verse vastudharma proof
  gets premise-chains, not summaries.
- Fixed term tables from verse 3 onward; new terms enter with grounds.
- Uncertainties carry genuinely ambiguous scope (which perceiver? which

- Witnesses (Dyczkowski Vol 6, TAA CH10 course, Pradipaka 10b, HF glossary)

# TRANSMISSION BRIEF — Tantraloka ahnika 11 (kalādyadhvā, path of the Kalās, 118 verses)

*Assessor-written 2026-11-06 from web scholarship. Authority: GRETIL spans + legacy L2.*

## What this text is
The path of the Kalās and higher orders: the five kalās as groupings of
categories — nivṛtti (earth alone), pratiṣṭhā (water→prakṛti),
vidyā (puruṣa→māyā), śāntā (śuddhavidyā→śakti), śāntātītā (Śiva);
the four aṇḍas (eggs); contemplation of categories by 1/3/5 divisions;
dualistic succession of words; the Power supporting it all.
(Dupuche's appendix: 36 categories, 5 kalās, 4 spheres — the physical
outline of emanation; Sanderson on 'the category'.)

## The moves to look for
1. Five kalās mapped to tattva ranges — enumeration with exact boundaries
   (note Abhinavagupta's śāntā 5→2 vs 5→3 variants; record, don't smooth).
2. One/three/five contemplations of categories (ekatva, tritva, pañcatva groupings).
3. Dualistic word-succession (padas as successive) vs the supporting Power.
4. Union: kalā-path resolved into consciousness (bridge to a12's application).

## Traps
- Kalā has many senses (lunar digit, vowel, division, pathway) — in a11 it
  means the five reality-divisions unless marked otherwise. Flag each sense.
- Variant boundaries (śāntā's extent) are genuine textual variants, not
  errors — record both, adjudicate nothing without Jayaratha.
- 118 verses, 2 windows (w1-60, w61-118). Brief covers the whole ahnika.

## How to approach
Spans follow kalā boundaries and contemplation units. Gist = what each
grouping contains and what contemplation it supports. Numbers are doctrine.

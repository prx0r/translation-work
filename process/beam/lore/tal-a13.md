## What the text IS
Ahnika 13 (GRETIL 362 verses; Vol 7 ch13, titled Śaktipātatirohitī per
Pradipaka). Grace AND concealment, per Abhinava's Trika master Śambhunātha:
the descent of Śiva's power of grace (śaktipāta) that liberates, and the
self-obscuring (tirodhāna) that binds — both Śiva's own free acts. Highest
grace = realization of unconditioned Śiva-nature when karma ends. TAA CH13
map: Śaiva-vs-Sāṃkhya superiority 1-13, ignorance nature 14-31, ignorance
inexplicable 41-107, Śiva binds/liberates freely 103-111, grace independent

## MOVES to look for
1. Anti-Sāṃkhya opening (1-13): Śaivism's superiority staked on grace —
   the opponent is named, refuted, and the refutation GROUNDS everything after.
2. Ignorance unexplainable (14-107): avidyā has no cause outside Śiva's
   freedom — the argument's audacity is the point; premises must show the
   aporia, not dissolve it.
3. Freedom proof (103-128): if grace waited on karma-balance or mala-maturity
   (Siddhāntin view), Śiva would be constrained like a bound soul — therefore
   Śiva graces freely, whenever, whomever. The chapter's engine.
4. Grace grades (129-132 → 193-300): varieties distinguished by the
   pratibhā-capacity they evoke; intense/moderate/mild descents → immediate
   realization down to 4 initiate grades. Transcribe the taxonomy exact.
5. Pratibhā crowned (146-183): intuition as SUPREME means; teacher's
   instruction as its source; lower mind/buddhi as binding — the epistemology
   that makes grace operative.
6. Self-obscuring (266-292): the Lord obscures HIMSELF through the soul's own
   craving — tirodhāna as love-play, not punishment.
7. Teacher doctrine close (300-361): true/false teachers, learning from MANY
   teachers valued — the practical payoff of a grace no one owns.

## TRAPS
- `śaktipāta` (descent of power/grace): THE technical term — fixed gloss,
  never "blessing" or "initiation" (initiation is its OCCASION, v-agreement
  with Siddhāntin kept).
- `tirodhāna` (concealment/obscuration): Śiva's act, not a substance; pair
  with grace, never confuse with mala (which is its INSTRUMENT here).
- `pratibhā` vs `viveka`: intuition crowns, discrimination delivers
  intuition-born knowledge — sequence matters (167-183).
- `tīvra/manda` grades (intense/moderate/mild): fixed ladder with fixed
  fruits; don't compress rungs.
- Siddhāntin positions (karma-balance, mala-maturity): carried fairly as
  premises before refutation — steelman, then turn.
- `dīkṣā` timing: grace's occasion, not its cause — the distinction the
  whole chapter turns on.

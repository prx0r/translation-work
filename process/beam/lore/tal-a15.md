# TRANSMISSION BRIEF — Tantraloka ahnika 15 (phonematic emanation, 613 verses)

*For beam drafters. Machine-proposed orientation; GRETIL spans + legacy L2 are the authority.*

## What this text is
The longest ritual-philosophical ahnika: varṇa (phonemes) as the substance of
mantra and world-emanation. First half metaphysics of sound (Absolute →
phonemic energies → tattvas); second half dīkṣā procedure using that
phonematics (nyāsa, mandala, adhva purification). Jayaratha's viveka is the
running commentary — follow his topic joints when spanning.

## The moves to look for
1. Parā → paśyantī → madhyamā → vaikharī speech levels as emanation order.
2. Mātṛkā (garland of letters) as both goddess-circle and alphabet; Sabdarāśi.
3. Bīja/mantra anatomy (bindu, nāda, kalā) grounding the rite choices later.
4. The ritual half applies, not repeats: every nyāsa/mandala placement must
   trace to an emanation claim from the first half.

## Traps
- Verse count 613 — the window packets are slices; never claim cross-window
  conclusions in a gist. Stay inside the window.
- Uniform 8-verse chunking is FORBIDDEN: boundaries follow topic shifts
  (Jayaratha's joints, mantra-list boundaries, rite transitions).
- Dyczkowski's 11-vol translation exists as the scholarly horizon — draft
  from the Sanskrit + legacy L2 only; do not hallucinate Jayaratha citations.

## How to approach
One span = one topic (mantra-list, rite-step, doctrinal claim). Gist = what is
established, not what is described. When the text enumerates (TOC-like
passages, cf. a01 vv301-333), say so plainly — enumeration IS the content.

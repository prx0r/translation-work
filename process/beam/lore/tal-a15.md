## What the text IS
Ahnika 15 (GRETIL 613 verses; ALL of Dyczkowski Vol 8). Title per Pradipaka:
Dīkṣā sāmayī — the SAMAYA INITIATION: entry into the basic condition of a
Trika Śaiva (one vowed to the pledges), which then continues as modified
daily (nitya) practice. Largely drawn from Mālinīvijayottara ch8, reproduced
entirely and commented in great detail. Pradipaka sections: vv1-10 (yāga for
initiation), 11-20, 21-30, 31-44, 45-60, 61-76, 77-145, 146-159 (+ further
15b/15c sets to v613). Core movements: dīkṣā as means for bhoga AND mukti
(vv1-4, citing Svāyambhuva + Mālinīmata); the incompetent's path —
dīkṣā/caryā/samādhi preceded by vidyā, jñānī supreme (v11+); nirbījā
(seedless) initiation liberating the triple bond past/present/future (sadyo-
nirvāṇa); the Trident ascent — Parā/Parāparā/Aparā on their Bhairavas in
lotuses on the prongs, spanning 36 levels + 37th (Supreme Śiva/Bhairava) +
38th (Kālasaṃkarṣiṇī, the Inexplicable); appendix of Trident Vidyās/mantras;
close: samaya pledges across Trika/Krama/Bhairava tantras.

## MOVES to look for
1. Yāga-first (vv1-10): sacrifice described FOR initiation's sake — dīkṣā
   itself becomes the means, by saṃskāra or directly; the capable get liberation.
2. Graded candidates: aśakta (dīkṣā/caryā/samādhi) → vidyā-preceded → jñānī
   supreme; nirbījā for the ripe — devotion to deva+guru liberates at once.
3. Trident mandala as ascent-ladder: bulb→prong-tips = 36+1+1 levels; worship
   external (maṇḍala) AND internal (subtle body) — track both registers.
4. Vidyā/mantra appendix: the ascent's sound-body, collected, not argued.
5. Pledges close: samaya across schools = the vow that makes a Trika Śaiva.

## TRAPS
- `samaya` = the initiation pledges (vow-complex), never generic "convention".
- `nitya` (daily/obligatory) vs `nimittika` (occasional, a28+) — a15 is the
  nitya foundation; don't import a28's festival logic backward.
- `bīja/nirbīja` (seeded/seedless initiation): technical grades, exact.
- 37th (paraśiva) + 38th (Kālasaṃkarṣiṇī) EXCEED the standard 36 — never
  force them into a 36-count; the excess is the doctrine.
- Kālasaṃkarṣiṇī (highest, inexplicable) vs a04's Twelve Kālīs (phases) —
  same root, different office; keep apart.
- MVT ch8 parallels: Abhinava reproduces AND revises — variants are readings
  to record, never errors to harmonize.
- Pradipaka 15a covers vv1-159 only; later windows (15b/15c sets to v613)
  follow the same rite-logic deeper into the ascent + pledges.

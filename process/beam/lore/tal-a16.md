# Transmission brief: Tantraloka a16 (putraka initiation, 311v)

*Assessor-authored from web scholarship, 2026-11-07. Cached per front, auto-injected into triple packets.*

## What the text IS
Ahnika 16 (GRETIL 311 verses; Vol 9 opening chapter). The putraka
initiation: consecration into the condition of a teacher's apprentice, which
can lead to acarya (teacher-priest) consecration. Where the basic samaya
initiation uses a single Trident mandala, this higher rite is reinforced by
three triads + Twelve Kalis around the lotus calyx. Singh ch-title: "Teacher's
Acts During Initiation". Arc position: a16 lays the grand putraka frame;
a17 continues with thread/tattvas; a18-a27 are the short specific initiations;
a23 consecrates the teacher this rite prepared.

## MOVES to look for
1. Escalation logic: samaya → putraka → acarya — each rung reuses the last
   rite's elements at higher power; track what is ADDED (triads, Kalis).
2. Triple-triad + Kali-calyx mandala: the diagram IS the theology — who
   surrounds whom in the lotus.
3. Animal-sacrifice passage: omitted at samaya level, ESSENTIAL here for
   teacher-priest progression — translate faithfully, no bowdlerizing, no
   sensationalizing; witness with brackets as Hareesh does for a29.
4. Nyasa of mantra layers beginning with the Six Paths (shadadhvan:
   tattva/pada/varna/bhuvana/mantra/kala) onto neophyte body + sacrificial
   area — the rite writes cosmology onto flesh.
5. Teacher's acts: what the guru DOES (binds, projects, seals) vs what the
   neophyte undergoes — keep agency straight in every premise.

## TRAPS
- `putraka` (spiritual son/apprentice) vs `samayin` (basic initiate) vs
  `acarya` (teacher-priest): three distinct statuses, never blurred.
- `nyasa` (layered projection/installation) — not "worship", not "touch".
- `shadadhvan` (six paths): tattva, pada, varna, bhuvana, mantra, kala —
  fixed set, exact order.
- `bali/pasu` (offering/victim): ritual terms; report the rite, never preach.
- Kali names here = the same Twelve as a04/a06/a15 — cross-check spellings
  against gold data; deviations are forks with grounds, never silent fixes.
- Calyx/circuit/petal architecture terms: fixed table from verse 1, never
  varied (same discipline as a31 mandalas).

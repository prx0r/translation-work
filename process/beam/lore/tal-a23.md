# TRANSMISSION BRIEF — Tantraloka ahnika 23 (abhiṣeka, consecration, 103 verses)

*Assessor-written 2026-11-06 from web scholarship. Authority: GRETIL spans + legacy L2.*

## What this text is
Consecration (abhiṣeka) as teacher: the putraka (spiritual son) initiation
completed into ācārya consecration. Follows a17 (initiation procedure) and
the dīkṣā arc a16-a22; precedes funerary rites (a24). Dyczkowski vol 9
(ch 16-27) is the working frame; Kāmika + Siddhayogīśvarīmata are cited
authorities inside the text; Kiraṇa/Dīkṣottara in the background.

## The moves to look for
1. Disciple inspection (parīkṣā): fitness signs, unfitness (ayogyatā,
   pratyavāya) bars initiation — the gatekeeping logic.
2. Putraka→ācārya arc: mantra-paddhati muttering/meditation, then authority
   to initiate/explain (dīkṣā-vyākhyā) conferred.
3. Sapta-satraka (seven sacrifices: dīkṣā, vyākhyā, kṛpā, maitrī, …,
   anna-ādi-dāna) as the consecrated one's duties.
4. Kāmika/Siddhayogīśvarī citations as authority moves, not decoration.

## Traps
- Abhiṣeka ≠ dīkṣā: consecration confers TEACHING authority (initiate
  others), initiation confers fitness. Keep the two distinct.
- The women/śūdra/outcaste passage (jñāna-dāne vicārayet, not dīkṣāyām):
  report the text's rule exactly; no modernizing, no apologetics.
- 103 verses, 11 passages — window briefs NOT needed (single span set);
  this brief covers the whole ahnika.

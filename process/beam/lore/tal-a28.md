# TRANSMISSION BRIEF — Tantraloka a28 (parvan days, kula calendar, 434 verses)

*For beam drafters. Machine-proposed orientation; GRETIL spans + legacy L2 are the authority.*

## What this text is
Nimittaja/occasional rites: kulaparvan and akulaparvan days, circle sacrifice
(cakrayāga — the "theatrical" gathering, cf. Dupuche on TA 29), worship of
gurus and deities on parvan days, prāṇa-time (kāla via breath cycles, linking
back to ahnika 6). Draws on Yogasañcāra/Bhairavakula lists (8 kulās = Mothers).

## The moves to look for
1. Parvan calendar: which days are kula vs akula and what is done on each.
2. Circle sacrifice mechanics: who gathers, what is offered, what it effects.
3. Prāṇa-cakra time-reckoning as the clock behind the calendar.
4. Kulācāra conduct rules (who is excluded: durācāra, pramāda).

## Traps
- Kula/Akula = Śakti/Śiva here, but the words have other senses elsewhere —
  keep the local sense.
- a28 feeds a29 (secret Kula ritual proper, Dupuche's book): do NOT import
  a29's sexual-ritual content into a28's calendar rite. a28 is the public
  calendar; a29 is the secret rite.
- The trika (Bhairavakula) citation lists are enumerations — list them, don't
  argue them.

## How to approach
Windows are ≤60 verses; many spans will be lists (days, deities, offerings).
Enumeration IS the content here. Gist = what is prescribed for whom/when,
not why in philosophical depth — that belongs to a29.

## What the text IS

nimittajam). The OCCASIONAL rites (naimittika — festivals, atonements,
death-days) after a15–a27's obligatory (nitya) complex. Vol 10 TOC map:
parvan definitions + kinds 2-59 (Yogasañcāra 10-14, Haiḍara/Bhairavakula

60-82, worship 82-111 — most elevated per Trika tantras); pavitraka thread

occasional rites 186-212; teacher birth/death days 213-216; voluntary-death

Yoginī/initiate assembly; vyākhyāvidhi 385-407; expiation 408-423;
gurupūjāvidhi 423-435. Pradipaka 28a covers vv1-150 with full Sanskrit.

## MOVES to look for
1. Parvan defined (nirukta from √pṝ, to fill): auspicious fullness-times —
   lunar changes, teacher days/visits, breath-cycle conjunctions (cf. a06).
2. Aadiyāga (60-111): the assembly sacrifice — foremost because the
   collective worship IS the rite's substance; Abhinava's analysis of
   assembly-consciousness is the philosophical core.
3. Pavitraka (112-186): three recensions of the thread offering — compare,
   don't conflate; differences are lineage positions.
4. Death complex (217-367): voluntary-death rites + initiate status + places
   — read with a19/a24 golds (same rite-complex, different angle).
5. Close (385-435): scripture-explanation, expiation, teacher-worship — the
   institutional frame that keeps the tradition transmissible.

  every span sits on one side.
- `kula/akula` = Śakti/Śiva HERE (8 kulās = Mothers); other senses elsewhere.
- Trika/Bhairavakula/Haiḍara/Kālīkula source-lists are enumerations — list
  them, don't argue them; boundaries sit on source-shifts.

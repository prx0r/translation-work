# TRANSMISSION BRIEF — Tantraloka a29 (secret Kula ritual, 291 verses)

*For beam drafters. Machine-proposed orientation; GRETIL spans + legacy L2 are the authority.*

## What this text is
Abhinavagupta's own Kula ritual (kulaprakriyā) — the tradition he was
initiated into. Dupuche's whole book (Motilal Banarsidass 2003, first English
rendering of any TAL chapter) covers exactly this ahnika: opening ritual,
essence section, daily/occasional/optional rites, the couple practice,
circle gathering, summation (29.292ab, appeal to own experience).

## The moves to look for
1. Opening rite as self-contained intro to the whole chapter's procedure.
2. "Essence" (sāra) section — the doctrinal core the rite enacts.
3. Reduction vs TA 15-16: same ritual matter, stripped to restfulness
   (Dupuche: compare full-activity 15-16 against reduced 29).
4. The three trajectories (action/knowledge/…) and the final summation.

## Traps
- This is the sensitive chapter: stay clinical and textual. No embellishment,
  no euphemism, no prurience — name what the Sanskrit names, plainly.
- TA 29.292ab appeals to Abhinavagupta's own experience — report it, do not
  evaluate it.
- Do not import a28 calendar detail here; a29 adapts a28, note the adaptation
  only where the text marks it.

## How to approach
Follow Jayaratha's joints and Dupuche's sectioning (prelude / essence /
daily-occasional-optional / summation). If a span is procedural, gist the
procedure step by step. Uncertainty about damaged/elliptical lines goes in
uncertainties, never smoothed.

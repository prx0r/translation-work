# Transmission brief: Tantraloka a30 (mantra compendium, 123v)

*Assessor-authored from web scholarship, 2026-11-07. Cached per front, auto-injected into triple packets.*

## What the text IS
Ahnika 30 (GRETIL 123 verses; Vol 11 ch30 per Dyczkowski site + Anna's/Z-Lib TOC).
A concise compendium of the Mantras used for the main rites taught across the
Tantraloka, ordered by importance. Frame: metaphysics of mantra at both ends —
opening vv1-4 + closing vv121cd-123 ground mantra in consciousness (cit) and
its inner vitality (virya), so the catalogue never reads as mere list.

## MOVES to look for
1. Trident Throne first (vv5-43ab): the mantra-seat + deities enthroned on it —
   the hierarchy everything else hangs from.
2. Krama Vidyas (vv45cd-55ab): goddess-sequence mantras in rank order.
3. Death mantras (vv55cd-92): utterances to induce/liberate at dying — read as
   operative ritual speech, not consolation.
4. Specialist rites: Scale (tula) initiation (91cd-98), sacrificial-animal
   mantra (91cd-92), Sakini-paralyzing mantra (93-94ab), Bhairava's Heart
   (94cd-99), Srisantati initiation (100-101), three Vidyas (102-121ab).
5. Close returns to metaphysics: mantras work because they ARE consciousness
   vibrating, not because priests repeat them.

## TRAPS
- `vidya` vs `mantra`: vidya = goddess-formula (feminine deity as sound);
  mantra = male-deity or neutral formula. Keep the gender of the deity audible.
- `virya` (vitality): the mantra's living pulse, not "efficacy" in the abstract.
- `tula` (scale): the weighing-initiation ordeal (cf. a20 gold: tulashuddhi) —
  same complex, cross-check glosses against tal-a20.
- Krama goddess-names (Kali-sequence, Raudri etc.): proper names, exact
  diacritics, never translated into epithets.
- Catalogue verses look alike: every mantra needs its deity + use + locus;
  boundaries sit on deity-shifts, not on euphony.

## APPROACH
- Compounds whole as source spaces them; seed-syllables (SAUH-class bijas)
  stay untranslated capitals with IAST exact.
- Gloss deity-names as names ("Kali of X"), function-words plainly.
- Argmap per mantra: which deity, for which rite, on what ground (consciousness)
  — three premises usually; uncertainties carry damaged/obscure syllables openly.
- Witnesses (Dyczkowski Vol 11, HF glossary) are consistency checks only —
  never grounds, never hosted.

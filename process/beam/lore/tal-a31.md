# Transmission brief: Tantraloka a31 (Trika mandalas, 164v)

*Assessor-authored from web scholarship, 2026-11-07. Cached per front, auto-injected into triple packets.*

## What the text IS
Ahnika 31 (GRETIL 164 verses; Vol 11 ch31 per Dyczkowski site). Abhinava draws
varieties of Trika Trident (trisula) mandalas as taught across the Trika
Tantras, beginning with the Malinivijayottara. Catalogue raisonne of sacred
diagrams: which goddess sits on which prong/lotus-petal/circuit, in which
tantra's version. Pairs with a30 (mantras) and a32 (mudras, GOLD #69):
vidya-mandala-mudra-mantra is the standard fourfold set, closed by a33
(assemblies, GOLD #61).

## MOVES to look for
1. Source-by-source presentation: same Trident, variant installations per
   tantra — track the SOURCE of each variant (Malinivijayottara first, then
   the rest).
2. Geometry carries doctrine: prongs/petals/circuits = goddess-orders
   (Parā/Parāparā/Aparā constellations); a placement difference IS a
   theological difference.
3. Links backward: assemblies named here recur as ritual agents in ch16-17
   procedures (cf. a17 brief) — the catalogue explains WHO the earlier rites
   invoked.
4. Containment logic (cf. a33 gold): all groupings resolve into the three Trika
   Goddesses + Matrsadbhava Vidya — derivatives of alphabet-energies, i.e.
   activities of consciousness.

## TRAPS
- Mandala vocabulary (prong/sula, petal/dala, circuit/avarana, lotus-padma,
  calyx/karnika): architectural terms, exact and consistent — build a term
  table early and never vary it mid-ahnika.
- Goddess-names and tantra-titles are proper names: exact IAST, never
  translated into epithets, never conflated across variants.
- Variant lists look alike: boundaries sit on SOURCE-shifts (which tantra)
  and SEAT-shifts (who sits where), never on euphony.
- Numbers (petal-counts, goddess-counts) are ritual facts: transcribe exactly;
  flag print-vs-manuscript discrepancies openly (Dyczkowski worked from
  manuscripts and corrects the edition — note where he does).
- Do not import a32's mudra doctrine or a30's mantra metaphysics to fill gaps;
  this ahnika is-governed by installation logic only.

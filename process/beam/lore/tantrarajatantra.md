# Transmission brief — Tantrarājatantra (Śrīvidyā) — RESEARCHED 2026-11-11
*Authority: Muktabodha tradition page (Śrīvidyā). Ed. with Manoramāṭīkā of
Subhagānandanātha; Woodroffe's English is dated but usable.*

## A. FACTS / ROLE
- Source for the **cycle of Nityā goddesses** tied to the **phases of the
  moon** — one of the three streams that merged into Śrīvidyā (with the
  Kāmadeva/Nityākaula cult and Pratyabhijñā).
- Large; the Manoramāṭīkā is the traditional commentary.

## B. MOVES
1. **Nityā cycle = lunar phases** — the calendrical/moon mapping is
   doctrinal, not decorative.
2. **Kāma/love-magic material** is genuine to this stream (as in
   Nityākaula) — clinical translation, no moralising.
3. Keep the Śrīvidyā pantheon subordinate-lineage relations (Trika's Ekavīrā
   Parā, Kubjikā's Saṃvartāmaṇḍala) out of the text unless it names them.

## C. TRAPS
- Woodroffe's translation is dated; verify technical terms against the
  Sanskrit and Manoramāṭīṭīkā.
- Do not merge Nityā with the Krama's 13 Kālīs — different cycles.

# Transmission brief — Tārā corpus (Northeast Indian tantra) — RESEARCHED 2026-11-11
*Sources: Bühnemann (Buddhist deities/mantras in Hindu tantras; Tārā
studies); Muktabodha Northeast Indian Tantra page.*

## A. WHAT THE CORPUS IS
- M00094 Tāratantra · M00095 Tārārahasya · M00096 Tārārahasyavṛttikā ·
  M00323 Tārārahasyavṛtti 2 · plus Tārābhaktisudhārṇava (M00055).
- **Early modern, Mithila/Assam/Bengal**: a conscious break from the
  classical Śākta tantras; **Ten Mahāvidyā** canon replaces the āmnāya
  system; **Tārā, Ugratārā, Nīlasarasvatī** become central.
- **Mahācīnācāra** (a ritual modality "imported from the East") appears in
  Tārā works.
- Paśu / Vīra / Divya eligibility grading becomes ubiquitous; Kaula
  transgression is accommodated to **Kulin Brahmanism**.

## B. TRAPS
1. **Tārā here is Hindu Śākta, not Buddhist Tārā** — but influences cross;
   gloss each name in its own system.
2. **Ten Mahāvidyās ≠ the Krama's 13 Kālīs.**
3. Bhakti/stotra register in the hymns (Tārābhaktisudhārṇava) — do not
   read ritual manuals as devotion or vice versa.
4. The vṛttis (commentaries) are separate texts; mark them.

**Ledger (LOCK):** tārā / ugratārā / nīlasarasvatī = the goddess forms; mahāvidyā = the ten-goddess canon; mahācīnācāra = the 'eastern' ritual modality; vṛtti = commentary; paśu/vīra/divya = grades.

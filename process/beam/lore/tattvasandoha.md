## 1. WHAT IT IS

NOT Abhinavagupta. The mūla (21 stanzas, Āryā meter) is by Amṛtānandanātha
(12th c., Śaiva master favored by śaktipāta); the prose commentary is by
Rājānakānanda (17th c.). Title = "the mass/totality (sandoha) of the
thirty-six (ṣaṭtriṁśat) categories (tattva)". It teaches the tattva-krama —
the succession of the 36 principles from Śiva down to earth — as the form
in which the Lord, a compact mass (ghana) of anuttara-ānanda, never abandons
His essence as vimarśa/camatkāra even while displaying the universe.
A complete modern word-by-word English rendering exists
(sanskrit-trikashaivism.com/544) — comparative witness only, never canon;
its Sanskrit text follows an uncritical base, so collate against GRETIL/MKS
at intake.

## 2. MOVES TO LOOK FOR

1. The opening gambit (v1: *yad ayam anuttaramūrtir nijecchayā…*): the
   Supreme Form, by His own will, "throbbed" (aspandata/spanda) to create —
   the first spanda IS Śiva-tattva. Everything hangs from this identity.
2. The descent ladder: each stanza stations 1–3 tattvas (śakti → sadāśiva →
   īśvara → śuddhavidyā → māyā + 5 kañcukas → puruṣa/prakṛti → buddhi →
   ahaṅkāra → manas → 5 jñānendriya + 5 karmendriya + 5 tanmātra → 5
   mahābhūta). Gloss each tattva-name as doctrine, not vocabulary.
3. The refrain: after displaying the universe, He does not abandon the
   ghana-anuttara-ānanda state — the vimarśa essence persists through
   manifestation. Every stanza's inference should terminate here.
4. Rājānakānanda's prose: plain scholastic unpacking (kula of 36, śaktipāta
   of the author, meter notes). Render faithfully; do not inflate into
   Abhinavagupta-grade metaphysics where the commentator stays plain.

## 3. TRAPS

1. **36, not 25.** Sāṃkhya stops at 25 (puruṣa/prakṛti + 23). The Śaiva
   order adds 11 above: Śiva, śakti, sadāśiva, īśvara, śuddhavidyā, māyā +
   5 kañcukas (kalā, vidyā, rāga, niyati, kāla). Never translate as Sāṃkhya.
2. **Sandoha ≠ saṅgraha.** Sandoha is a heaped mass/totality — the
   compactness image matters (ghana). Keep "mass/totality", not "summary".
3. **Amṛtānandanātha ≠ Abhinavagupta.** Do not import Pratyabhijñā technical
   senses (e.g. ābhāsa-theory, upāyas) where the text doesn't earn them.
   The Trika ledger below is shared vocabulary, not a license to upgrade.
4. **Āryā meter.** Mātrā-regulated, not syllable-counted. Do not force
   anuṣṭubh cadence; do not "fix" irregular lines (mark AMBIGUOUS if corrupt).
5. **The site's translation is one man's grace-and-scanty-Sanskrit draft**
   (his words). Use it to orient on hard compounds; cite as comparative
   grounds at most; the gloss follows our collated source.

## 4. APPROACH

Small work (~21 verse-blocks + prose; <1k tokens → assessor-direct or tiny
triples, never a blind swarm batch). Verse + commentary prose per unit.
Forks only where the print genuinely variants. L2 plain, C1-essence naming
what each stanza DOES in the descent (stations X, preserves Y).

**Ledger (shared Trika, LOCK):** tattva = category/principle; sandoha =
mass/totality; anuttara = the Highest (beyond); ānanda = bliss/joy; ghana =
compact mass; vimarśa = reflexive awareness; camatkāra = bliss of
I-consciousness; spanda = throb/pulsation; kula = group/family (of powers);
śaktipāta = descent of Power/grace; krama = succession; māyā = Māyā-power
(Śaiva); kañcuka = sheath/contraction; puruṣa/prakṛti read Śaiva, never
Sāṃkhya-final.

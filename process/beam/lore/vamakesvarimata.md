# Transmission brief — Vāmakeśvarīmata (Śrīvidyā) — RESEARCHED 2026-11-11
*Companion to Yoginīhṛdaya (see that brief). 5 chapters; together 8.*

## A. FACTS
- The **outer/ritual** half of the 8-chapter Vāmakeśvaratantra; also called
  **Nityāṣoḍaśikārṇava (NSA)** or Catuḥṣaṣṭiśāstra ("treatise of 400
  stanzas"). KSTS edition 1945 (ed. Kaul Shastri); best ed. Varanasi 1968.
- English: **Michael Magee**, *Vāmakeśvarīmatam* (Varanasi Tantra
  Granthamālā 11) — the same translator as the Kaulajñānanirṇaya.
- Deals with the outer worship of **Tripurasundarī**; Bhāskararāya's
  Setubandha comments on it as part of the 8-chapter whole.

## B. TRAPS
- **Practical/magical register**: much of it is ritual and mantra
  application (prayoga) — do not philosophicalise into Trika.
- Same 16-syllable mantra system as YH; kūṭa terminology must match the
  YH brief.
- Distinguish the 5-chapter VMT from the 8-chapter composite when citing.

**Ledger (LOCK):** vāmakeśvarī = the goddess; nityāṣoḍaśikārṇava = the alternate name (NSA); kūṭa = mantra division; prayoga = applied ritual; tripurasundarī = the deity.

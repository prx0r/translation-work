# Transmission brief — Vedānta corpus (Yogavāsiṣṭha etc.) — RESEARCHED 2026-11-11
*Authority: Muktabodha Vedānta page; Hanneder/ Slaje on Yogavāsiṣṭha.*

## A. WHAT IT IS
Works engaging the Upaniṣadic canon with Mīmāṃsā-derived exegesis: here
chiefly **Yogavāsiṣṭha** (with Tātparyaprakāśa / Vāsiṣṭhacandrikā) and
**Siddhāntaleśasaṃgrahaṭīkā**. The Yogavāsiṣṭha is a vast philosophical
narrative on liberation via mind/consciousness, heavily narrative in form.

## B. MOVES / TRAPS
1. **Narrative frame**: stories illustrate doctrine — do not treat as
   independent mythology; the tale IS the argument.
2. **Advaita vocabulary** (brahman, māyā, jīvanmukti) — but Yogavāsiṣṭha has
   its own idiom (cittavṛtti, kalpanā, dṛśya); gloss in situ.
3. **Commentaries are separate texts** (Tātparyaprakāśa, Vāsiṣṭhacandrikā);
   mark them.
4. Long prose/verse mixing → expect prose chunks, not pure verse.

**Ledger (LOCK):** vāsiṣṭha = of Vasiṣṭha (the frame narrator); citta = mind;
kalpanā = imagination/construction; jīvanmukti = liberation while living.

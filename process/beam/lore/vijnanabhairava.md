# Transmission brief — Vijñānabhairava (112 dhāraṇās)

*Bhairava-Pārvatī dialogue tantra (149 verses here, M00271): Devī asks
how to recognize Bhairava; Bhairava answers with 112 dhāraṇās —
direct-entry meditations (breath, senses, void, bliss, wonder...).
Same narrative frame as the Mālinīvijayottara opening. Singh translated
(selected vol); Wallis verse-by-verse series free on hareesh.org
(Tantra112, in progress). Source Muktabodha M00271 (IAST, CC BY-NC 4.0).
Passages vij-p01..p06 sequential 25s.*

## 1. WHAT IT IS

112 doors, one recognition: each dhāraṇā is a complete practice-in-a-
verse aimed at sudden entry (samāveśa) into Bhairava-consciousness.
No system-building, no ritual apparatus — pure upāya manual. Gloss as
operational claims; every verse must stay practicable in L2.

## 2. MOVES TO LOOK FOR

1. **Entry mechanics.** Each dhāraṇā: object → operation → recognition-
   sign. Keep the triple in order or the practice scrambles.
2. **Wonder/bliss/void refrains.** camatkāra/ānanda/śūnya terms are
   technical result-states, not decoration.
3. **Breath verses.** Prāṇa/apāna/dvādaśānta mechanics — exact
   physiological terms, no metaphor inflation.
4. **The frame.** Devī's opening question + Bhairava's closing seal
   bracket the manual; argmap them as frame, not method.

## 3. TRAPS

1. **One verse = one practice.** Never merge dhāraṇās; never split one.
2. **śūnya ≠ nothing.** Void-terms denote the plenum-ground; render
   as operative openness, not nihilism.
3. **Wallis/Singh overlap.** Consult for hard verses; our gloss follows
   M00271 readings; mark TEXTUAL on divergence.

## 4. APPROACH

6 passages × 25. Standard triples (≤12/group); exact glosses; C1-essence
names what each dhāraṇā DOES to the recognition-claim.

**Ledger (LOCK):** dhāraṇā = entry-practice; samāveśa = immersion;
camatkāra = wonder; śūnya = plenum-void; dvādaśānta = the twelfth-end;
prāṇa/apāna = outgoing/incoming breath.

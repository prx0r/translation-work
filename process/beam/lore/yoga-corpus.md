# Transmission brief — Yoga corpus (Haṭha/Yoga-Upaniṣads) — RESEARCHED 2026-11-11
*Sources: Mallinson's Haṭhayoga studies; Muktabodha Yoga page.*

## A. WHAT IT IS
From c. 11th c., texts that take **yoga as their exclusive subject**,
separating it from liturgy: postural practice, breath-retention
(prāṇāyāma/kumbhaka), the body as primary locus. The earliest known source,
the **Amṛtasiddhi**, is Vajrayāna Buddhist; the genre was then adopted by
Nāthas, Vaiṣṇavas, Vīraśaivas and Smārtas alike. Includes
**Haṭhayogapradīpikā with Jyotsnā**, Śivasaṃhitā, Gorakṣa works,
Yogacintāmaṇi, Nādānusandhāna.

## B. MOVES / TRAPS
1. **Technical physiology**: kuṇḍalinī, cakra, nāḍī, bindu, mudrā, bandha,
   kumbhaka — exact, non-metaphorical.
2. **Do NOT read tantric metaphysics in**; these texts are practical, often
   deliberately non-sectarian.
3. **Jyotsnā** (Brahmānanda's commentary) is commentary — mark separations.
4. Gorakṣa attributions are traditional, not documentary.

**Ledger (LOCK):** haṭha = force (method); kumbhaka = breath-retention;
mudrā/bandha = seals/locks; nādānusandhāna = sound-contemplation;
kuṇḍalinī = coiled power.

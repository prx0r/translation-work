## Moves
1. Frames Śāmbhavopāya as the highest, most direct upāya: recognition of one's own consciousness without instrumental means.
2. Develops pratibimba (reflection) as the way the universe appears as a reflection in the mirror of consciousness.
3. Traces phonemic emanation from nāda through bindu to letters, linking sound to manifestation.
4. Distinguishes levels of nāda and their relation to Śiva/Śakti tattvas.
5. Positions this ahnika within the worldview section (ch.1-13), before ritual/initiation chapters.

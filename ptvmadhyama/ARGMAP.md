# Parātriśikāvivṛti (madhyama) — ARGMAP

## ptvm-p01 (1–9)
- speaker: Devī asks; Bhairava answers (with Jaideva Singh's vivṛti).
- question: What is the unsurpassed (anuttara)?
- premises: Yena vijñātamātreṇa khecarīsamatāṃ brajet (by which, when known, sky-wandering equality is attained). Hṛdayasthā tu yā śaktiḥ kaulikī kulanāyikā (the śakti abiding in heart, Kaulikī, clan-leader). Śṛṇu devi mahābhāge uttarasyāpyanuttaram (listen, goddess, great fortunate, of the uttara, the anuttara). Kathayāmi sureśāni sadyaḥ kaulikasiddhidam (I tell, Lord of gods, instantly Kaulika-siddhi-giving). Tadantaḥ kālayogena somasūryau prakīrtitau (therein, by time-yoga, moon and sun are proclaimed). Kramāt kādiṣu varṇeṣu makārānteṣu suvr... (in order, in ka-letters, ending in ma, well...).
- inference: Anuttara = of the uttara, the anuttara; instantly Kaulika-siddhi-giving; śakti abiding in heart, Kaulikī; by time-yoga, moon and sun proclaimed.
- conclusion: Anuttara doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## ptvm-p02 (10–18)
- speaker: Bhairava (with vivṛti).
- question: What are the mantra powers?
- premises: Etannāyoginījāto nārudro labhate sphuṭam (this, not from Yoginī-born, not Rudra, clearly attained). Asyoccāre kṛte samyaṅ mantramudrāgaṇo mahān (when this is pronounced well, great mantra-mudrā-group). Muhūrta smarate yastu cummakenābhimudritaḥ (one who remembers for a moment, stamped by cumbaka). Atītānāgatānarthān pṛṣṭo 'sau kathayatyapi (past/future calamities, asked, he tells too). Sākṣātpaśyatyasaṃdigdhamākṛṣṭaṃ rudraśaktibhiḥ (directly sees, without doubt, attracted by Rudra's śaktis). Trayena mātaraḥ sarvā yogeśvaryo m... (by three, all mothers, Yogeśvarīs...).
- inference: Mantra powers = when pronounced well, great mantra-mudrā-group; one who remembers for a moment, stamped by cumbaka; tells past/future calamities; directly sees, attracted by Rudra's śaktis.
- conclusion: Mantra powers stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## ptvm-p03 (19–27)
- speaker: Bhairava (with vivṛti).
- question: What is the knowledge?
- premises: Anena jñātamatreṇa jñāyate sarvaśaktibhiḥ (by this, when known, known by all śaktis). Avidhijño vidhānajño jāyate yajanaṃ prati (ignorant of rules, becomes knower of rules, regarding worship). Śivo viśvādyanantāntaḥ paraṃ śaktitrayaṃ matam (Śiva, world-devouring, endless-end, supreme śakti-triad is held). Aṇurviśuddhamacirādaiśvaraṃ jñānamaśnute (pure atom, quickly, divine knowledge attains). Sarvago nirmalaḥ svacchastṛptaḥ svāyatanaḥ śuciḥ (all-pervading, stainless, clear, satisfied, own-abode, pure). Tathā hṛdayabījasthaṃ jagadet... (thus in heart-seed, world...).
- inference: Knowledge = when known, known by all śaktis; ignorant of rules becomes knower of rules; Śiva, world-devouring, endless-end, supreme śakti-triad; pure atom attains divine knowledge; all-pervading, stainless, clear, satisfied, own-abode, pure.
- conclusion: Knowledge doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## ptvm-p04 (28–35)
- speaker: Bhairava (close, with vivṛti).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: Puṣpādikaṃ kramāt sarvaṃ liṅge vā sthaṇḍile 'thavā (flowers etc. in order, all, in liṅga or sthaṇḍila). Tataḥ sṛṣṭiṃ yajed vīraḥ punarevāsanaṃ tataḥ (there, creation, worship, vīra; again seat then). Sarvatattvasusaṃpūrṇā sarvābharaṇabhūṣitām (all-tattva-full, all-ornament-beautiful). Tataḥ sugandhipuṣpaistu yathāśaktyā samarcayet (then with fragrant flowers, as able, worship). Evaṃ yajanamākhyātamagnikārye 'py ayaṃ vidhiḥ (thus worship declared, in fire-rite too this rule). Ādyantarahitaṃ bījaṃ v... (seed without beginning/end...).
- inference: Close = flowers etc. in order, all, in liṅga or sthaṇḍila; there, creation, worship, vīra; again seat; all-tattva-full, all-ornament-beautiful; with fragrant flowers, as able, worship; in fire-rite too this rule; seed without beginning/end.
- conclusion: The text closes with worship of the Goddess.
- unresolved: none.

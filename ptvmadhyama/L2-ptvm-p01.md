# Ptvmadhyama ptvm-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
O Lord, what is the Unsurpassable that grants Kaula fruit at once — known by a mere touch of knowing, rising to kinship with the Sky-Goer?
The Sanskrit: *anuttaraṃ kathaṃ deva sadyaḥ kaulikasiddhidam | yena vijñātamātreṇa khecarīsamatāṃ brajet*

## v.2
Tell me this secret of secrets, Lord — the Power that dwells in the heart, Kaulikī, mistress of the clan.
The Sanskrit: *etadguhyaṃ mahāguhyaṃ kathayasva mama prabho | hṛdayasthā tu yā śaktiḥ kaulikī kulanāyikā*

## v.3
Tell me of her, Lord of gods, by whom I come to fullness. — Hear, blessed Goddess: even the answer has one beyond it — the Unsurpassable.
The Sanskrit: *tāṃ me kathaya deveśa yena tṛptiṃ labhāmyaham | śṛṇu devi mahābhāge uttarasyāpyanuttaram*

## v.4
This Kaula rite, O Goddess, standing in my heart-sky — I tell it, O lady of gods: it gives swift Kaula fruit.
The Sanskrit: *kauliko'yaṃ vidhirdevi mama hṛdvyomnyavasthitaḥ | kathayāmi sureśāni sadyaḥ kaulikasiddhidam*

## v.5
First now: all lunar days, vowels ending in the drop; within them, by time's conjoining, moon and sun are named.
The Sanskrit: *athādyāstithayaḥ sarve svarā bindvavasānagāḥ | tadantaḥ kālayogena somasūryau prakīrtitau*

## v.6
Realities from earth to person, in five groups in order — in ka-series, in sounds, ending with the ma-sound, O fair-vowed one.
The Sanskrit: *pṛthivyādīni tattvāni puruṣāntāni pañcasu | kramāt kādiṣu varṇeṣu makārānteṣu suvrate*

## v.7
Fourfold the holds of wind, fire, water and their lords; above them, śa beginning the famed row, and before, the five Brahmas.
The Sanskrit: *vāyvāgnisalilendrāṇāṃ dhāraṇānāṃ catuṣṭayam | tadūrdhvaṃ śādi vikhyātaṃ purastādbrahmapañcakam*

## v.8
Rootless, rightly ranged, to be known, ending in kṣa — this forthgoing declared; of all mantras indeed, and of wisdoms, O glorious one.
The Sanskrit: *amūlā tatkramājjñeyā kṣāntā sṛṣṭirudāhṛtā | sarveṣāmeva mantrāṇāṃ vidyānāṃ ca yaśasvini*

## v.9
This is the womb, told in all Tantras in every way — joined with the fourteen, O lucky one, joined with the tithi-lord's end; the third godhead, O fair-hipped, the heart of Bhairava himself.
The Sanskrit: *iyaṃ yoni samākhyātā sarvatantreṣu sarvathā | caturdaśayutaṃ bhadre tithīśāntasamanvitam tṛtīyaṃ brahma suśroṇi hṛdayaṃ bhairavātmanaḥ |*

# Ptvmadhyama ptvm-p02 — L2 READ

## v.10
Neither yoginī-born nor Rudra plainly wins this heart of the god of gods, swift giver of yoga's release.
The Sanskrit: *etannāyoginījāto nārudro labhate sphuṭam hṛdayaṃ devadevasya sadyo yogavimokṣadam |*

## v.11
At its right speaking, the great mantra-seal host comes face to face at once — marked by possession of one's own body.
The Sanskrit: *asyoccāre kṛte samyaṅmantramudrāgaṇo mahān sadyastanmukhatāmeti svadehāveśalakṣaṇam |*

## v.12
Who remembers for a moment — in the kiss, navel-sealed — he binds then all, body and mantra-seal host, the man.
The Sanskrit: *muhūrta smarate yastu cummakenābhimudritaḥ saṃbadhnāti tadā sarvaṃ mantramudrāgaṇaṃ naraḥ |*

## v.13
Asked, he tells past and future things — even within a watch, uttering the meant god-form.
The Sanskrit: *atītānāgatānarthān pṛṣṭo'sau kathayatyapi praharādyadabhipretaṃ devatārūpamuccaran |*

## v.14
Face to face he sees the sure, drawn by Rudra's powers; in two watches' time, sky-standing, remembering.
The Sanskrit: *sākṣātpaśyatyasaṃdigdhamākṛṣṭaṃ rudraśaktibhiḥ praharadvayamātreṇa vyomastho jāyate smaran |*

## v.15
By three: all mothers, mighty yoginīs, heroes, hero-lords, adepts, and the strong śākinī throng.
The Sanskrit: *trayeṇa mātaraḥ sarvā yogeśvaryo mahābalāḥ vīrā vīreśvarāḥ siddhā balavāñcchākinīgaṇaḥ |*

## v.16
They come, pledge got, by Bhairava urged — they grant supreme fruition, whatever fruit is wished.
The Sanskrit: *āgatya samayaṃ labdhvā bhairaveṇa pracoditāḥ yacchanti paramāṃ siddhiṃ phalaṃ yadvā samīhitam |*

## v.17
By this the perfected will perfect, and make mantra-men perfect; whatever is in Bhairava's Tantra, all succeeds from this.
The Sanskrit: *anena siddhāḥ setsyanti sādhayanti ca mantriṇaḥ yat kiñcidbhairavetantre sarvamasmāt prasidhyati |*

## v.18
Who thus truly knows even the unseen circle shares fruition ever — yogin and initiate alike.
The Sanskrit: *adṛṣṭamaṇḍalo'pyevaṃ yaḥ kiñcidvetti tattvataḥ sa siddhibhāg bhavennityaṃ sa yogī sa ca dīkṣitaḥ |*

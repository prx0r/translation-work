# Ptvmadhyama ptvm-p03 — L2 READ

## v.19
By this mere knowing, all powers know; a kinsman of the śākinī clan he may be, even without yoga.
The Sanskrit: *anena jñātamatreṇa jñāyate sarvaśaktibhiḥ śākinīkulasāmānyo bhavedyogaṃ vināpi hi |*

## v.20
Ignorant of rite, knowing ordinance, he turns to worship — having made time-fire from the first, ending in māyā, gone to the brahma-body.
The Sanskrit: *avidhijño vidhānajño jāyate yajanaṃ prati kālāgnimāditaḥ kṛtvā māyāntaṃ brahmadehagam |*

## v.21
Śiva, whose end is endless all, deemed the supreme power-triad; whatever stands on the pure path abides inside that.
The Sanskrit: *śivo viśvādyanantāntaḥ paraṃ śaktitrayaṃ matam tadantarvarti yatkiñcit śuddhamārge vyavasthitam |*

## v.22
The atom soon attains pure lordly knowing; its mover is Śiva to be known, all-knowing highest Lord.
The Sanskrit: *aṇurviśuddhamacirādaiśvaraṃ jñānamaśnute taccodakaḥ śivo jñeyaḥ sarvajñaḥ parameśvaraḥ |*

## v.23
All-going, stainless, clear, full, self-aboded, bright — like the great power-formed tree standing in the banyan seed.
The Sanskrit: *sarvago nirmalaḥ svacchastṛptaḥ svāyatanaḥ śuciḥ yathānyagrodhabījasthaḥ śaktirūpo mahādrumaḥ |*

## v.24
So stands this moving-still world in the heart-seed; who knows it truly — his is the freedom-going.
The Sanskrit: *tathā hṛdayabījasthaṃ jagadetaccarācaram evaṃ yo vetti tattvena tasya nirvāṇagāminī |*

## v.25
Initiation becomes undoubted, free of sesame-ghee offering — on crown, face, and heart, on the hidden, on the form, and so indeed.
The Sanskrit: *dīkṣā bhavatyasaṃdigdhā tilājyāhutivarjitā mūrdhni vaktre ca hṛdaye guhye mūrtau tathaiva ca |*

## v.26
Placing done, topknot bound, twenty-seven-muttered; with each one let the ten quarters' binding be yoked.
The Sanskrit: *nyāsaṃ kṛtvā śikhāṃ baddhvā saptaviṃśatimantritām ekaikaṃ tu diśāṃ bandhaṃ daśānāmapi yojayet |*

## v.27
Three claps first, sounded, to clear obstacles; with topknot-count thrice-told, then sprinkle water.
The Sanskrit: *tālatrayaṃ purā datvā saśabdaṃ vighnaśāntaye śikhāsaṃkhyātijaptena toyenābhyukṣayettataḥ |*

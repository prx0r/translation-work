# Ptvmadhyama ptvm-p04 — L2 READ

## v.28
Flowers and all in order — emblem or altar; with fourteen mutterings, a flower-seat made.
The Sanskrit: *puṣpādikaṃ kramāt sarvaṃ liṅge vā sthaṇḍile'thavā caturdaśābhijaptena puṣpeṇāsanakalpanā |*

## v.29
There let the hero worship the forthgoing, then the seat again; casketed, let him begin the worship after.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ sṛṣṭiṃ yajedvīraḥ punarevāsanaṃ tataḥ sṛṣṭiṃ tu saṃpuṭīkṛtya punaryajanamārabhet |*

## v.30
Full of all realities, graced with all ornaments — let the great lady worship the goddess, twenty-seven-named.
The Sanskrit: *sarvatattvasusaṃpūrṇā sarvābharaṇabhūṣitām yajeddevīṃ maheśānīṃ saptaviśatimantritām |*

## v.31
Then with fragrant flowers, as able, let him honor; worship with supreme devotion, and offer up himself.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ sugandhipuṣpaistu yathāśaktyā samarcayet pūjayet parayā bhaktyā ātmānaṃ ca nivedayet |*

## v.32
Thus worship told; in fire-rite too this rule: who did the worship-rule, rightly remembering the seed, succeeds.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ yajanamākhyātamagnikārye'pyayaṃ vidhiḥ kṛtapūjāvidhiḥ samyak smaran bījaṃ prasidhyati |*

## v.33
Beginningless-endless seed, abloom, gone mid-tithis — housed in the heart-lotus, meditate the moon-ray, ever practise.
The Sanskrit: *ādyantarahitaṃ bījaṃ vikasattithimadhyagam hṛtpadmāntargataṃ dhyāyet somāṃśaṃ nityamabhyaset |*

## v.34
Which desires he desires, them quickly may he win; from this he goes to direct sight — all-knowingness, no doubt.
The Sanskrit: *yānyān kāmayate kāmāṃstāṃstāṃ śīghramavāpnuyāt asmāt pratyakṣatāmeti sarvajñatvaṃ na saṃśayaḥ |*

## v.35
Thus is mantra-fruit got — this Rudrayāmala; from this practice, accomplishment and all-knowingness are won.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ mantraphalāvāptirityetadrudrayāmalam etadbhyāsataḥ siddhiḥ sarvajñatvamavāpyate |*

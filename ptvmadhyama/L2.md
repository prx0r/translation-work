## ptvm-p01–p02 — anuttara and mantra powers
**SUMMARY.** Yena vijñātamātreṇa khecarīsamatāṃ brajet — by which, when known, sky-wandering equality is attained. Hṛdayasthā tu yā śaktiḥ kaulikī kulanāyikā — the śakti abiding in heart, Kaulikī, clan-leader. Śṛṇu devi mahābhāge uttarasyāpyanuttaram — listen, goddess, great fortunate, of the uttara, the anuttara. Kathayāmi sureśāni sadyaḥ kaulikasiddhidam — I tell, Lord of gods, instantly Kaulika-siddhi-giving. Tadantaḥ kālayogena somasūryau prakīrtitau — therein, by time-yoga, moon and sun are proclaimed. Etannāyoginījāto nārudro labhate sphuṭam — this, not from Yoginī-born, not Rudra, clearly attained. Asyoccāre kṛte samyaṅ mantramudrāgaṇo mahān — when this is pronounced well, great mantra-mudrā-group. Muhūrta smarate yastu cummakenābhimudritaḥ — one who remembers for a moment, stamped by cumbaka. Atītānāgatānarthān pṛṣṭo 'sau kathayatyapi — past/future calamities, asked, he tells too. Sākṣātpaśyatyasaṃdigdhamākṛṣṭaṃ rudraśaktibhiḥ — directly sees, without doubt, attracted by Rudra's śaktis.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes anuttara (by which, when known, sky-wandering equality; śakti abiding in heart, Kaulikī; of the uttara, the anuttara; instantly Kaulika-siddhi-giving) and mantra powers (when pronounced well, great mantra-mudrā-group; one who remembers for a moment, stamped by cumbaka; tells past/future calamities; directly sees, attracted by Rudra's śaktis).

**KEY TERMS.** anuttara (unsurpassed), uttara (higher), kaula (Kaula), kaulikī (Kaulikī), kulanāyikā (clan-leader), kaulikasiddhi (Kaulika-siddhi), khecarī (sky-wandering), mantra (mantra), mudrā (mudrā), cumbaka (cumbaka), rudraśakti (Rudra's śakti).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First two passages; sets the doctrinal frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Anuttara: by which, when known, sky-wandering equality attained; śakti abiding in heart, Kaulikī, clan-leader; listen, goddess, great fortunate, of the uttara, the anuttara; I tell, Lord of gods, instantly Kaulika-siddhi-giving; therein, by time-yoga, moon and sun proclaimed. (2) Mantra powers: this, not from Yoginī-born, not Rudra, clearly attained; when pronounced well, great mantra-mudrā-group; one who remembers for a moment, stamped by cumbaka; tells past/future calamities; directly sees, attracted by Rudra's śaktis.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.1–1.18.

## ptvm-p03–p04 — knowledge and close
**SUMMARY.** Anena jñātamatreṇa jñāyate sarvaśaktibhiḥ — by this, when known, known by all śaktis. Avidhijño vidhānajño jāyate yajanaṃ prati — ignorant of rules, becomes knower of rules, regarding worship. Śivo viśvādyanantāntaḥ paraṃ śaktitrayaṃ matam — Śiva, world-devouring, endless-end, supreme śakti-triad is held. Aṇurviśuddhamacirādaiśvaraṃ jñānamaśnute — pure atom, quickly, divine knowledge attains. Sarvago nirmalaḥ svacchastṛptaḥ svāyatanaḥ śuciḥ — all-pervading, stainless, clear, satisfied, own-abode, pure. Puṣpādikaṃ kramāt sarvaṃ liṅge vā sthaṇḍile 'thavā — flowers etc. in order, all, in liṅga or sthaṇḍila. Tataḥ sṛṣṭiṃ yajed vīraḥ punarevāsanaṃ tataḥ — there, creation, worship, vīra; again seat then. Sarvatattvasusaṃpūrṇā sarvābharaṇabhūṣitām — all-tattva-full, all-ornament-beautiful. Tataḥ sugandhipuṣpaistu yathāśaktyā samarcayet — then with fragrant flowers, as able, worship. Evaṃ yajanamākhyātamagnikārye 'py ayaṃ vidhiḥ — thus worship declared, in fire-rite too this rule. Ādyantarahitaṃ bījaṃ v... — seed without beginning/end...

**FUNCTION.** Establishes knowledge (when known, known by all śaktis; ignorant of rules becomes knower of rules; Śiva, world-devouring, endless-end, supreme śakti-triad; pure atom attains divine knowledge; all-pervading, stainless, clear, satisfied, own-abode, pure) and close (flowers etc. in order, all, in liṅga or sthaṇḍila; there, creation, worship, vīra; again seat; all-tattva-full, all-ornament-beautiful; with fragrant flowers, as able, worship; in fire-rite too this rule; seed without beginning/end).

**KEY TERMS.** jñāna (knowledge), śākinī (Śākinī), kula (clan), yoga (yoga), vidhi (rule), yajana (worship), śiva (Śiva), viśvadya (world-devouring), anantānta (endless-end), śaktitraya (śakti-triad), aṇu (atom), viśuddha (pure), sarvaga (all-pervading), svāyatana (own-abode), puṣpa (flower), liṅga (liṅga), sthaṇḍila (sthaṇḍila), sṛṣṭi (creation), vīra (vīra), āsana (seat), devī (Goddess), bīja (seed).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Closes the text; follows p01–p02 (anuttara/mantra powers).

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Knowledge: by this, when known, known by all śaktis; ignorant of rules becomes knower of rules regarding worship; Śiva, world-devouring, endless-end, supreme śakti-triad is held; pure atom, quickly, divine knowledge attains; all-pervading, stainless, clear, satisfied, own-abode, pure. (2) Close: flowers etc. in order, all, in liṅga or sthaṇḍila; there, creation, worship, vīra; again seat; all-tattva-full, all-ornament-beautiful; with fragrant flowers, as able, worship; in fire-rite too this rule; seed without beginning/end.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

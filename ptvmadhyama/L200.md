# L200 — Parātriśikāvivṛti (madhyama) (35 verses, 4 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Parātriśikāvivṛti (madhyama) (35 verses) is Jaideva Singh's commentary on Parātriśikā. It establishes: anuttara = of the uttara, the anuttara; instantly Kaulika-siddhi-giving; śakti abiding in heart, Kaulikī; by time-yoga, moon and sun proclaimed. Mantra powers = when pronounced well, great mantra-mudrā-group; one who remembers for a moment, stamped by cumbaka; tells past/future calamities; directly sees, attracted by Rudra's śaktis. Knowledge = when known, known by all śaktis; ignorant of rules becomes knower of rules; Śiva, world-devouring, endless-end, supreme śakti-triad; pure atom attains divine knowledge; all-pervading, stainless, clear, satisfied, own-abode, pure. Close = flowers etc. in order, all, in liṅga or sthaṇḍila; there, creation, worship, vīra; again seat; all-tattva-full, all-ornament-beautiful; with fragrant flowers, as able, worship; in fire-rite too this rule; seed without beginning/end.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: Anuttara = of the uttara, the anuttara; instantly Kaulika-siddhi-giving.
- C2: Śakti abiding in heart, Kaulikī; by time-yoga, moon and sun proclaimed.
- C3: Mantra powers = when pronounced well, great mantra-mudrā-group; one who remembers for a moment, stamped by cumbaka.
- C4: Tells past/future calamities; directly sees, attracted by Rudra's śaktis.
- C5: Knowledge = when known, known by all śaktis; ignorant of rules becomes knower of rules.
- C6: Śiva, world-devouring, endless-end, supreme śakti-triad; pure atom attains divine knowledge.
- C7: Close = flowers etc. in order; by fourteen-recited flower, seat-arrangement; worship the Goddess.

## 3. What the text rejects
- R1: Anuttara as non-unsurpassed.
- R2: Mantra without powers.
- R3: Knowledge without Śiva/śakti-triad.
- R4: Close without Goddess worship.

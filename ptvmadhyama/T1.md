## ptvm-p01 (1-9)
[and]-yena vijñātamātreṇa khecarīsamatāṃ brajet hṛdayasthā tu yā śaktiḥ kaulikī kulanāyikā śṛṇu devi mahābhāge uttarasyāpyanuttaram kathayāmi sureśāni sadyaḥ kaulikasiddhidam tadantaḥ kālayogena somasūryau prakīrtitau kramāt kādiṣu varṇeṣu makārānteṣu suvrate tadūrdhvaṃ śādi vikhyātaṃ purastādbrahmapañcakam ghanībhāvo'parāvākvapuṣi tiṣṭhan vaikharīrūpaṃ sphuṭī bhavati caturdaśayutaṃ bhadre tithīśāntasamanvitam

## ptvm-p02 (10-18)
[and]-etannāyoginījāto nārudro labhate sphuṭam asyoccāre kṛte samyaṅmantramudrāgaṇo mahān muhūrta smarate yastu cummakenābhimudritaḥ atītānāgatānarthān pṛṣṭo'sau kathayatyapi sākṣātpaśyatyasaṃdigdhamākṛṣṭaṃ rudraśaktibhiḥ trayeṇa mātaraḥ sarvā yogeśvaryo mahābalāḥ āgatya samayaṃ labdhvā bhairaveṇa pracoditāḥ anena siddhāḥ setsyanti sādhayanti ca mantriṇaḥ adṛṣṭamaṇḍalo'pyevaṃ yaḥ kiñcidvetti tattvataḥ

## ptvm-p03 (19-27)
[and]-anena jñātamatreṇa jñāyate sarvaśaktibhiḥ avidhijño vidhānajño jāyate yajanaṃ prati śivo viśvādyanantāntaḥ paraṃ śaktitrayaṃ matam aṇurviśuddhamacirādaiśvaraṃ jñānamaśnute sarvago nirmalaḥ svacchastṛptaḥ svāyatanaḥ śuciḥ tathā hṛdayabījasthaṃ jagadetaccarācaram dīkṣā bhavatyasaṃdigdhā tilājyāhutivarjitā nyāsaṃ kṛtvā śikhāṃ baddhvā saptaviṃśatimantritām tālatrayaṃ purā datvā saśabdaṃ vighnaśāntaye

## ptvm-p04 (28-35)
[and]-puṣpādikaṃ kramāt sarvaṃ liṅge vā sthaṇḍile'thavā tataḥ sṛṣṭiṃ yajedvīraḥ punarevāsanaṃ tataḥ sarvatattvasusaṃpūrṇā sarvābharaṇabhūṣitām tataḥ sugandhipuṣpaistu yathāśaktyā samarcayet evaṃ yajanamākhyātamagnikārye'pyayaṃ vidhiḥ ādyantarahitaṃ bījaṃ vikasattithimadhyagam yānyān kāmayate kāmāṃstāṃstāṃ śīghramavāpnuyāt evaṃ mantraphalāvāptirityetadrudrayāmalam

# C1 — ptvm-p01

## SUMMARY

What grants Kaula fruit at once, known by mere touch? The heart's Kaulikī, clan-mistress — tell her, for fullness gained. Even of the answer, one beyond: no doubt, at once, the rite in heart-sky, lady of gods. First vowels drop-ending, moon-sun time-yoked; earth-to-person in five ka-to-ma sounds; supports fourfold, brahma-five śa-famed; rootless kṣa-ending emanation of all mantras and wisdoms; womb ever told, fourteen-joined, third brahma — Bhairava-self's heart.

## FUNCTION

Asks the fierce question and lays the phonematic ground the answer will inhabit — in Lakṣmīrāma's recension readings.

## KEY TERMS

anuttara (unsurpassable Subject); katham (how — means); sadyaḥ (at once — immediacy, not autonomy); Kaulikī (feminine -ī); labhāmi (I gain — not I go); sureśānī (lady of gods — feminine addressee); varṇa (sounds — not classes); yoni (womb-source).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Question and ground: the how asked here is paid across powers, fruit, and rite.

## EXPLANATION

Fierce question (katham = means): the unsurpassable granting Kaula fruit at once (sadyaḥ, recension-decided), recognition alone lifting to Khecarī-evenness. Secret asked: heart-abiding Kaulikī. Fullness asked as gaining (labhāmi, not going). Answer beyond answers promised; rite told in heart-sky to the lady of gods (transposed order kept). Ground: drop-ending vowels, moon-sun time-yoked; earth-to-person in five ka-to-ma SOUNDS (varṇeṣu, not classes); fourfold supports, śa-famed brahma-five (split kept); rootless kṣa-ending emanation of all mantras and wisdoms; ever-told womb in every way, fourteen-joined, tithi-lord-ended, third brahma — Bhairava-self's heart.

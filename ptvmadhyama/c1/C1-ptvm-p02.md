# C1 — ptvm-p02

## SUMMARY

Withheld from yoginī-born and Rudra alike, the heart gives swift release. Rightly uttered, the host stands face to face, body-marked. Remembered a moment in the kiss, navel-sealed: binds body and host. Asked, past and future told; within a watch, the god-form uttered. Undoubted seen, Rudra-drawn; two watches, sky-standing. By three the mothers gather — heroes, perfected, śākinī-host; come, pledge got, urged: supreme fruit, whatever wished. By this the perfected perfect, all in tantra succeeds. Unseen circle truly known shares fruition ever — yogin and initiated.

## FUNCTION

Demonstrates the heart's efficacy: exclusion, utterance, memory, vision, host, success.

## KEY TERMS

hṛdaya (withheld heart); sanmukhatā (face-to-faceness); cumbaka (kiss — open); prahara (watch — speed); labdhvā (having got — vs dattvā); śākinīgaṇa (host); adṛṣṭamaṇḍala (unseen circle).

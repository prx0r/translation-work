# C1 — ptvm-p03

## SUMMARY

By mere this known of all powers, śākinī-common even without yoga. Rite-unknowing, ordinance-knowing turns: time-fire from the first through māyā to brahma-body. Boundless Śiva, supreme triad; the pure-pathed stand inside. The atom soon reaches lordly knowing; its mover Śiva to be known. All-going, stainless, clear-full, self-aboded, bright — banyan-seed tree, power-formed. So the moving world in the heart-seed; the true knower's going-to-freedom. Initiation undoubted, oblation-free, on five sites; placing done, topknot bound: all ten quarters bound. Three claps sounded against obstacles; topknot-count muttered, water then.

## FUNCTION

Tastes the fruit: knowing without apparatus, holism imaged, initiation internalized.

## KEY TERMS

jñātamātra (mere known — engine returns); vināpi yoga (even without yoga); sarvam sarvātmakam (holism); nirvāṇagāminī (going-to-freedom — runs to dīkṣā); nyāsa (placing); yojayet (should yoke — vs kārayet); datvā (short a).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Fruit of the powers exhibited before; the feminine going-to-freedom runs into the initiation verse.

## EXPLANATION

By mere this known of all powers; śākinī-common even without yoga. Rite-unknowing, ordinance-knowing turns toward worship: time-fire from the first through māyā to brahma-body. Boundless-ended Śiva, supreme triad deemed; whatever stands on the pure path inside that. Atom soon reaches pure lordly knowing (whole-pāda fused); its impeller Śiva to be known, all-knowing lord. All-going, stainless, clear-full, self-aboded, bright — banyan-seed power-formed tree (v23 absorbs GRETIL v24ab: no separator in source). So the moving world in the heart-seed; the true knower's going-to-freedom (feminine runs to dīkṣā). Initiation undoubted, oblation-free, on five sites (becomes-undoubted fused). Placing done, topknot bound twenty-seven-muttered; with each one the ten quarters bound (yojayet, not kārayet). Three claps with sound for obstacle-quelling; topknot-count muttered, water-then (tataḥ absorbed).

## BOUNDARY

No outer rite is given; the rite's body begins beyond the fruit.

## RELATED

v23/v24 re-division (yathā absorbed into v23) documented in lore; v25→v26 enjambment kept.

# C1 — ptvm-p04

## SUMMARY

Flowers, mark or altar; fourteen-muttered seat. The hero worships emanation, then the seat; casketed, begins. Realities-full, all-ornamented, the great lady worships the twenty-seven-muttered goddess. Fragrant flowers by power, supreme devotion — self offered. Worship told; even in fire this rule: seed rightly remembered succeeds. Beginningless seed mid-tithis, heart-lotus-housed; moon-ray ever practised. Desires quickly got; to direct sight — all-knowingness, no doubt. Mantra-fruit got: this Rudrayāmala; from this practice, fruition, all-knowingness.

## FUNCTION

Gives the rite's body, then seals the tantra: told, practised, got.

## KEY TERMS

sthaṇḍila (altar — ṇ); sampuṭīkṛtya (casketing); maheśānī (great lady — nominative subject); ātman (self — oblation); abhyāsa (practice — last word); rudrayāmala (colophon name); etadbhyāsataḥ (from-this-practice).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The rite's body answers the method-question of the opening; the colophon closes the how with got.

## EXPLANATION

Flowers, mark or altar-or (sthaṇḍile'thavā); by fourteen muttered, flower seat-making. There the hero should worship emanation, then the very seat; casketed, again worship begins (punaryajanamārabhet fused). Full of all realities, graced with all ornaments — let the great lady worship the twenty-seven-muttered goddess (ābharaṇa, maheśānī kept). Then fragrant flowers by power honored; with supreme devotion self offered (ātmanaṃ, not svātman). Worship told in fire-rite even this rule (whole pāda fused); whose rule done, rightly remembering seed, succeeds. Beginningless-endless blossoming seed mid-tithis, heart-lotus-housed; moon-ray ever practised (fused). Which desires, them quickly got; from this to direct sight (no ajñaḥ in recension) — all-knowingness, no doubt. Thus mantra-fruit getting: this Rudrayāmala (whole pāda fused); from this practice, accomplishment and all-knowingness got.

## BOUNDARY

Nothing further is prescribed; practice is the last word.

## RELATED

Title-verse v31? No — colophon is v35 (etad rudrayāmalam); no ajñaḥ in v34 (asmāt starts the fruit).

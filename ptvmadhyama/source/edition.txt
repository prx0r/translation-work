anuttaraṃ kathaṃ deva sadyaḥ kaulikasiddhidam | yena vijñātamātreṇa khecarīsamatāṃ brajet
etadguhyaṃ mahāguhyaṃ kathayasva mama prabho | hṛdayasthā tu yā śaktiḥ kaulikī kulanāyikā
tāṃ me kathaya deveśa yena tṛptiṃ labhāmyaham | śṛṇu devi mahābhāge uttarasyāpyanuttaram
kauliko'yaṃ vidhirdevi mama hṛdvyomnyavasthitaḥ | kathayāmi sureśāni sadyaḥ kaulikasiddhidam
athādyāstithayaḥ sarve svarā bindvavasānagāḥ | tadantaḥ kālayogena somasūryau prakīrtitau
pṛthivyādīni tattvāni puruṣāntāni pañcasu | kramāt kādiṣu varṇeṣu makārānteṣu suvrate
vāyvāgnisalilendrāṇāṃ dhāraṇānāṃ catuṣṭayam | tadūrdhvaṃ śādi vikhyātaṃ purastādbrahmapañcakam
amūlā tatkramājjñeyā kṣāntā sṛṣṭirudāhṛtā | sarveṣāmeva mantrāṇāṃ vidyānāṃ ca yaśasvini
iyaṃ yoni samākhyātā sarvatantreṣu sarvathā | caturdaśayutaṃ bhadre tithīśāntasamanvitam tṛtīyaṃ brahma suśroṇi hṛdayaṃ bhairavātmanaḥ |
etannāyoginījāto nārudro labhate sphuṭam hṛdayaṃ devadevasya sadyo yogavimokṣadam |
asyoccāre kṛte samyaṅmantramudrāgaṇo mahān sadyastanmukhatāmeti svadehāveśalakṣaṇam |
muhūrta smarate yastu cummakenābhimudritaḥ saṃbadhnāti tadā sarvaṃ mantramudrāgaṇaṃ naraḥ |
atītānāgatānarthān pṛṣṭo'sau kathayatyapi praharādyadabhipretaṃ devatārūpamuccaran |
sākṣātpaśyatyasaṃdigdhamākṛṣṭaṃ rudraśaktibhiḥ praharadvayamātreṇa vyomastho jāyate smaran |
trayeṇa mātaraḥ sarvā yogeśvaryo mahābalāḥ vīrā vīreśvarāḥ siddhā balavāñcchākinīgaṇaḥ |
āgatya samayaṃ labdhvā bhairaveṇa pracoditāḥ yacchanti paramāṃ siddhiṃ phalaṃ yadvā samīhitam |
anena siddhāḥ setsyanti sādhayanti ca mantriṇaḥ yat kiñcidbhairavetantre sarvamasmāt prasidhyati |
adṛṣṭamaṇḍalo'pyevaṃ yaḥ kiñcidvetti tattvataḥ sa siddhibhāg bhavennityaṃ sa yogī sa ca dīkṣitaḥ |
anena jñātamatreṇa jñāyate sarvaśaktibhiḥ śākinīkulasāmānyo bhavedyogaṃ vināpi hi |
avidhijño vidhānajño jāyate yajanaṃ prati kālāgnimāditaḥ kṛtvā māyāntaṃ brahmadehagam |
śivo viśvādyanantāntaḥ paraṃ śaktitrayaṃ matam tadantarvarti yatkiñcit śuddhamārge vyavasthitam |
aṇurviśuddhamacirādaiśvaraṃ jñānamaśnute taccodakaḥ śivo jñeyaḥ sarvajñaḥ parameśvaraḥ |
sarvago nirmalaḥ svacchastṛptaḥ svāyatanaḥ śuciḥ yathānyagrodhabījasthaḥ śaktirūpo mahādrumaḥ |
tathā hṛdayabījasthaṃ jagadetaccarācaram evaṃ yo vetti tattvena tasya nirvāṇagāminī |
dīkṣā bhavatyasaṃdigdhā tilājyāhutivarjitā mūrdhni vaktre ca hṛdaye guhye mūrtau tathaiva ca |
nyāsaṃ kṛtvā śikhāṃ baddhvā saptaviṃśatimantritām ekaikaṃ tu diśāṃ bandhaṃ daśānāmapi yojayet |
tālatrayaṃ purā datvā saśabdaṃ vighnaśāntaye śikhāsaṃkhyātijaptena toyenābhyukṣayettataḥ |
puṣpādikaṃ kramāt sarvaṃ liṅge vā sthaṇḍile'thavā caturdaśābhijaptena puṣpeṇāsanakalpanā |
tataḥ sṛṣṭiṃ yajedvīraḥ punarevāsanaṃ tataḥ sṛṣṭiṃ tu saṃpuṭīkṛtya punaryajanamārabhet |
sarvatattvasusaṃpūrṇā sarvābharaṇabhūṣitām yajeddevīṃ maheśānīṃ saptaviśatimantritām |
tataḥ sugandhipuṣpaistu yathāśaktyā samarcayet pūjayet parayā bhaktyā ātmānaṃ ca nivedayet |
evaṃ yajanamākhyātamagnikārye'pyayaṃ vidhiḥ kṛtapūjāvidhiḥ samyak smaran bījaṃ prasidhyati |
ādyantarahitaṃ bījaṃ vikasattithimadhyagam hṛtpadmāntargataṃ dhyāyet somāṃśaṃ nityamabhyaset |
yānyān kāmayate kāmāṃstāṃstāṃ śīghramavāpnuyāt asmāt pratyakṣatāmeti sarvajñatvaṃ na saṃśayaḥ |
evaṃ mantraphalāvāptirityetadrudrayāmalam etadbhyāsataḥ siddhiḥ sarvajñatvamavāpyate |

# Rahasyapanchadashika rpa-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
At god's hour, surrender to the Lord. Then stillness, restraint, and the twilight rite. Two watches of muttering and worship, then the other session; the rest of time is Śiva's remainder-conduct.
The Sanskrit: *brāhme muhūrte bhagavatprapatti stataḥ samādhirniyamo'tha sāndhyaḥ | yāmau japārcādi tato'nyasatraṃ | śeṣastukālaḥ śivaśeṣavṛttiḥ*

## v.2
A-first-faced, KA-handed, ṬA-footed, PĀ-sided with ṂA in the middle; YA-hearted goddess, consciousness-formed — Sarasvatī wins.
The Sanskrit: *ādimukhā kādikarā ṭādipadā | pādipārśvayuṅmadhyā | yādihṛdayā bhagavatī saṃvidrūpā | sarasvatī jayati*

## v.3
Thought-jewel, wish-cow, wish-trees bear fruit — just men's desires. Unasked, unheaped, nourish man's aims, O mother's noble mood.
The Sanskrit: *phalanti cintāmaṇikāmadhenu kalpadrumāḥ kāṅkṣitameva puṃsām | aprārthitānapracitān pumarthān | puṣṇātu me māturudāra bhāvaḥ*

## v.4
Without whom nothing at all is done, nothing known, nothing wanted — by the lord of awareness. To her, the highest, the mother of worlds — homage to Śivā, Śiva's beloved.
The Sanskrit: *yayā vinā naiva karoti kiñcan | na vetti nāpīcchati saṃvidīśaḥ | tasmai parasyai jagatāṃ jananyai | namaḥ śivāyai śivavallabhāyai*

## v.5
Ever-risen, blessed, all-auspicious, Śiva-giving, dwelling in Śiva's heart, O Śivā — moonlight opening the night-lotus: for the worshipping mind, make my going in heaven supreme.
The Sanskrit: *sadodite bhagavati sarvamaṅgale | śivaprade śivahṛdayasthite śive | bhajanmanaḥ kumudavikāsacandrike | dvijanmanaḥ kuru mama khe gatiṃ pare*

## v.6
Be gracious, all-auspicious Śivā, Śiva's beloved. Umā, Ramā, Sarasvatī — you alone are the supreme goddess.
The Sanskrit: *prasīda sarvamaṅgale śive śivasya vallabhe | ume rame sarasvati tvameva devatā parā*

## v.7
O mother, O Ambikā, formless, nameless, Umā, Raudrī, Vāmā, Mahālakṣmī, Māyā — supreme goddess, sporting the five acts, auspicious Bhairavī, glorious: I surrender to you.
The Sanskrit: *ame ambike asvarūpe anākhye ume | raudri vāme mahālakṣmi māye | pare devate pañcakṛtyaikalole śive | bhairavi śrīmati tvāṃ prapadye*

## v.8
Māyā, Vidyā, Mātṛkā, Mālinī — you throb in every body as awareness-spark-self. How shall I meditate you, self-flashing? I meditate you as inward-resounding speech.
The Sanskrit: *māye vidye mātṛke mālini tvaṃ | kāye kāye spandase citkalātmā | dhyāyeyaṃ tāṃ tvāṃ katha svasphurattāṃ | dhyāyeyaṃ tvāṃ vācamantarnadantīm*

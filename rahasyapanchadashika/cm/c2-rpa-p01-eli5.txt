At god's hour, bow to the Lord. Then be still, be restrained, do the twilight rite. Give two watches to chanting and honoring — the rest of the day is leftovers, and leftovers belong to Śiva. [E0]

First the alphabet stands up as a goddess: A for head, KA for hands, ṬA for feet, both sides for PĀ, the middle for ṂA, the heart for YA — all of it knowing-shaped. Her name is Sarasvatī, and she wins. [E1]

Thought-jewel, wish-cow, wish-tree: they fruit exactly what men wish for, no more, no less. Nobody asked, nothing piled up — so, noble Mother-mood, please grow what men are really after. [E2]

Nothing gets done without her, nothing is known, nothing is even wanted — she is the lady who owns all knowing. To the highest one, the mother of worlds — honor to Śivā, Śiva's own beloved. [E3]

She is always rising, always blessed, all good luck, giving Śiva, living in Śiva's heart — O Śivā. Moonlight that opens the night lotus: for a mind that worships, make my going the highest going there is. [E4]

Be kind, all-luck Śivā, Śiva's darling. Umā! Ramā! Sarasvatī! You, you alone, are the top goddess. [E5]

Mother! Formless Lady, nameless one! Umā, Angry One, Leftward One, Great Lakṣmī, Māyā! Goddess of the five-act play, lovely Bhairavī in all her glory — I am yours, I bow down. [E6]

Māyā, Learning, Mātṛkā, Garland-girl — you throb away in every single body as a spark of knowing. How am I supposed to sit with you, all flashing by yourself? I sit with you as the voice talking inside itself. [E7]

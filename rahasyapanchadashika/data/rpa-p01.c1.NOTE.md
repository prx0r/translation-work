# C1 NOTE — rpa-p01

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Dawn sequence orders the day: surrender, stillness, restraint, twilight; two watches for muttering-and-worship, the rest as Śiva's remainder. Letter-body enthroned as goddess: alphabet limbs from A to YA, consciousness-formed Sarasvatī supreme. Wish-powers grant only the wished: unasked, unpiled, the mother's noble mood nourishes man's aims. Awareness-lordship preconditions all doing, knowing, wanting: homage as highest, worlds' mother, Śiva's beloved. Moonlight-prayer for the twice-born mind: night-lotus-opened, made to go supreme in heaven. Direct address collapsing names into one: Umā, Ramā, Sarasvatī — you alone supreme. Fierce-form litany gathered into surrender: formless, nameless, many-named — five-act-sporting Bhairavī. Throbbing-in-every-body as meditation object: self-flashing answered by inward-resounding speech.

## VOICE MAP

Poet: dawn-rite v1; letter-goddess v2; wish-powers v3; supreme śakti v4; moonlight v5; litany v6; fierce forms v7; throbbing v8.

## KEY TERMS

* brāhma-muhūrta — god's-hour as practice-gate
* mātṛkā — letters as limbs
* puṣṇātu — nourish as the only request
* saṃvidīśa — awareness-lord as precondition
* kumudavikāsacandrikā — night-lotus-opening moonlight
* pañcakṛtya — five acts as her sport
* spandase — you-throb as meditation object

## CRUXES

* mālini kept as printed (Tantraloka 15.333 mātṛkā/mālinī schemes); reference books' mānini noted, not adopted.
* Nothing is enjoined beyond beholding and asking; devotion states, never explains.

## FUNCTION

Sets the day in order, enthrones the alphabet as goddess, and turns every sense toward surrender.

## Structure (morning-rite frame + Devi's limbs)
- v1 dawn sequence: brahma-muhūrta surrender → samādhi → niyama → twilight-rite → two watches of muttering/worship → rest of time as Śiva's remainder-conduct.
- v2 Mātṛkā-Sarasvatī enthroned: A-first mouth, KA-hand, ṬA-foot, PĀ-side... middle, YA-heart — consciousness-formed goddess wins (jayati).
- v3 wish-tree stanza: thought-jewel, wish-cow, wish-tree bear fruit for men unasked; mother's noble mood nourish unasked, unpiled man's aims.
- v4 śakti supreme: without whom knowing does nothing, knows nothing, wants nothing — to her, the worlds' mother, homage to Śivā, Śiva's beloved.
- v5 ever-risen goddess (moon-lotus-moonlight): make my twice-born mind move in heaven, supreme.
- v6 direct address litany: be gracious, all-auspicious Śivā, Śiva's beloved; Umā Ramā Sarasvatī — you alone supreme goddess.
- v7 fierce-form litany: Ame Ambikā formless nameless Umā Raudrī Vāmā Mahālakṣmī Māyā; five-act-playful supreme goddess, Bhairavī glorious — I surrender.
- v8 Māyā-Vidyā-Mātṛkā-Mālinī: you throb in every body as awareness-spark-self; how shall I meditate you self-flashing? I meditate you as inward-resounding speech.
- v9 body-churn: skin-blood-flesh-fat-marrow-bone-bodied, ever-deluded body — Māyā, you plunge men who know your greatness not.
- v10 set-up gods (metal-set images): "hā hā alas" say the unfulfilled; bodies with I-ness-pride — what delusion does Māyā not birth?
- v11 delusion-born intellect through void-body-I-ness: protect me from falling, supreme goddess, from effort born of self-awareness-form.
- v12 fierce-gentle-beyond: terrible, more-terrible, non-terrible; called higher-and-lower, beyond lower and higher; seeing Śiva's manifold power, abiding in wonder.
- v13 in the midst of high-low rays: no insistence of mine on being-vs-nonbeing-vs-being-vs-nonbeing judgments.
- v14 your unsurpassed form flashes, and the other world-made one, O mother: both remembered by the good — fear-giving, boon-giving, supreme goddess.
- v15 Lady of the five acts, supreme-knowing-formed Pārvatī, be gracious: lift the fallen beast-fettered one, comfort the child with cool glances.
- v16 seal: having bowed to perfected gurus, gods, goddess, yogins — by Abhinavagupta these fifteen verses uttered.

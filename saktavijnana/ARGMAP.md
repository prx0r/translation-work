# Śaktavijñāna — ARGMAP

## sakt-p01 (1–10)
- speaker: Somananda, Śākta knowledge.
- question: What is Śākta vijñāna (Śākta knowledge)?
- premises: Sthānaṃ praveśo rūpaṃ ca lakṣaṃ lakṣaṇameva ca (place, entry, form, mark, characteristic). Utthāpanaṃ bodhanaṃ ca cakraviśrāmameva ca (rising, awakening, wheel-rest). Viśrāmaḥ pariṇāmaśca tathāgamanameva ca (rest, transformation, going). Iti trayodaśavidhaṃ śāktaṃ vijñānamuttamam (thus thirteenfold Śākta knowledge, supreme). Sarveṣu trikaśāstreṣu sūcitaṃ śambhunā svayam (in all Trika śāstras, indicated by Śambhu himself). Tanmadhye kandanāmā ca cakrasthānamiti smṛtam (in that middle, kanda-named, wheel-place, so remembered). Samyag vāyugatiṃ jitvā yāvan madhyagatāṃ nayet (having conquered breath-course well, leading to middle). Śṛṅgāṭakanibhaṃ cakraṃ ṣaḍara... (triangular wheel, six-spoked...).
- inference: Śākta vijñāna = thirteenfold supreme knowledge (place/entry/form/mark/characteristic/rising/awakening/wheel-rest/rest/transformation/going); indicated by Śambhu; kanda is wheel-place; conquered breath leads to middle.
- conclusion: Śākta vijñāna doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## sakt-p02 (11–20)
- speaker: Somananda.
- question: How does the śakti rise?
- premises: Etal lakṣaṇam uddiṣṭam utthāpanam ataḥ param (this characteristic described, rising, then). Prabuddhāṃ cintayecchaktiṃ daṇḍavat parameśvarīm (meditate on awakened śakti, staff-like, supreme Goddess). Utthāpanaṃ samākhyātaṃ bodhanaṃ paratastathā (rising declared, awakening then). Kaṇṭhasthamacyutaṃ sākṣādrudraṃ tālutale sthitam (in throat, imperishable, directly Rudra, on palate abiding). Bodhayitvā vrajed āśu padaṃ cānāśritaṃ śivam (having awakened, go quickly to the unattached Śiva-state). Svābhāvikaṃ dalaṃ... (natural petal...).
- inference: Śakti rises: meditate on awakened śakti, staff-like, supreme Goddess; rising declared, awakening then; throat = Rudra, palate = abiding; having awakened, go to unattached Śiva-state.
- conclusion: Rising doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## sakt-p03 (21–30)
- speaker: Somananda (close).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: Teṣu teṣu na bhetavyaṃ krīḍati parameśvarī (in those, fear not; supreme Goddess plays). Malatrayavikārau bahujanmasu yatkṛtam (affliction-triad's modification, done in many births). Bhūmikāgamanaṃ proktam antāvasthā tathocyate (stage-going declared, final state so said). Granthisphoṭaḥ sparśadivyapraharṣo binduspandā (knot-bursting, divine touch-delight, bindu-vibration). Nābhikandāt sphuranti nābhicakraviniryātā (from navel-kanda, pulsing; from navel-wheel, exiting). Yadā śaktiḥ prabudhyate yadā sā par... (when śakti awakens, when she...).
- inference: Close = fear not in those; supreme Goddess plays; affliction-triad's modification from many births; stage-going declared, final state; knot-bursting, divine touch-delight, bindu-vibration; from navel-kanda, pulsing; from navel-wheel, exiting; when śakti awakens.
- conclusion: The text closes with śakti's awakening.
- unresolved: none.

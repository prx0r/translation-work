# Saktavijnana sakt-p02 (raising and the signs) — L2 READ

## v.11
Gaze at her, at śakti, with a light sealing of the breath; this is the sign pointed out — beyond it lies raising.
The Sanskrit: *tāmevālokayec chaktiṃ manāk kumbhakavṛttinā / etal lakṣaṇam uddiṣṭam utthāpanam ataḥ param*

## v.12
On the practiced out-breath, utter the mantra; contemplate her awakened — the Supreme Goddess, straight as a staff.
The Sanskrit: *juṣadrecakavṛttyā tu mantraṃ caiva samuccaret / prabuddhāṃ cintayecchaktiṃ daṇḍavat parameśvarīm*

## v.13
Come from the heart of the base, she has entered the suṣumnā road; this is raising declared — beyond it, awakening.
The Sanskrit: *ādhāramadhyādāyātā suṣumnāmārgam āśritā / utthāpanaṃ samākhyātaṃ bodhanaṃ paratastathā*

## v.14
Rooted in the bulb, pierce the navel, then the grandsire in the heart; in the throat the unfallen one, Rudra face to face on the palate's floor.
The Sanskrit: *kandastho vedhayen nābhiṃ tato hṛtsthaṃ pitāmaham / kaṇṭhasthamacyutaṃ sākṣādrudraṃ tālutale sthitam*

## v.15
At the brow's middle, Īśa; at the door of brahman, Sadāśiva; wake them and rise swiftly to the state that rests on nothing — Śiva.
The Sanskrit: *bhruvormadhyagataṃ tv īśaṃ brahmadvāre sadāśivam / bodhayitvā vrajed āśu padaṃ cānāśritaṃ śivam*

## v.16
This awakening is pointed out. Then the resting in the wheels: a petal by nature, blazing, flowing, like the steady sky.
The Sanskrit: *etadbodhanam uddiṣṭaṃ cakraviśrāmaṇaṃ tataḥ / svābhāvikaṃ dalaṃ dīptaṃ dravaṃ sthiranabhopamam*

## v.17
Nectar, the crest, śakti-brahmā — and the drop-sound: so the twelvefold wheel is proclaimed, says tradition.
The Sanskrit: *amṛtaṃ śekharaṃ caiva śaktirbrahmā tathaiva ca / bindunādaṃ tathā proktaṃ cakradvādaśakaṃ kila*

## v.18
Piercing in turn — śakti in wheel after wheel, at every instant — she, the Great Goddess, comes to rest: the supreme wheel-rest.
The Sanskrit: *vedhayantī kramāc chaktiścakre cakre pratikṣaṇam / viśramet sā mahādevī cakraviśrāma uttamaḥ*

## v.19
First the heart trembles, and the palate gate; his head spins — the sign of the sight's crossing over.
The Sanskrit: *hṛdayaṃ kampate pūrvaṃ tālukadvārameva ca / śiraśca bhramate tasya dṛṣṭisaṃkrāntilakṣaṇam*

## v.20
It sets each limb spinning, joint by joint; his heart churns — such is the power of true knowing.
The Sanskrit: *ekaikaṃ bhramayaty aṅgam aṅgapratyaṅgasaṃdhiṣu / ghūrṇate hṛdayaṃ cāsya samyagvidyāprabhāvataḥ*

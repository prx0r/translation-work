# Śaktavijñāna — L2 READ (Dyczkowski-mode)

## sakt-p01–p03 — Śākta knowledge and close
**SUMMARY.** Sthānaṃ praveśo rūpaṃ ca lakṣaṃ lakṣaṇameva ca — place, entry, form, mark, characteristic. Utthāpanaṃ bodhanaṃ ca cakraviśrāmameva ca — rising, awakening, wheel-rest. Viśrāmaḥ pariṇāmaśca tathāgamanameva ca — rest, transformation, going. Iti trayodaśavidhaṃ śāktaṃ vijñānamuttamam — thus thirteenfold Śākta knowledge, supreme. Sarveṣu trikaśāstreṣu sūcitaṃ śambhunā svayam — in all Trika śāstras, indicated by Śambhu himself. Tanmadhye kandanāmā ca cakrasthānamiti smṛtam — in that middle, kanda-named, wheel-place, so remembered. Samyag vāyugatiṃ jitvā yāvan madhyagatāṃ nayet — having conquered breath-course well, leading to middle. Etal lakṣaṇam uddiṣṭam utthāpanam ataḥ param — this characteristic described, rising, then. Prabuddhāṃ cintayecchaktiṃ daṇḍavat parameśvarīm — meditate on awakened śakti, staff-like, supreme Goddess. Utthāpanaṃ samākhyātaṃ bodhanaṃ paratastathā — rising declared, awakening then. Kaṇṭhasthamacyutaṃ sākṣādrudraṃ tālutale sthitam — in throat, imperishable, directly Rudra, on palate abiding. Bodhayitvā vrajed āśu padaṃ cānāśritaṃ śivam — having awakened, go quickly to the unattached Śiva-state. Teṣu teṣu na bhetavyaṃ krīḍati parameśvarī — in those, fear not; supreme Goddess plays. Malatrayavikārau bahujanmasu yatkṛtam — affliction-triad's modification, done in many births. Bhūmikāgamanaṃ proktam antāvasthā tathocyate — stage-going declared, final state so said. Granthisphoṭaḥ sparśadivyapraharṣo binduspandā — knot-bursting, divine touch-delight, bindu-vibration. Nābhikandāt sphuranti nābhicakraviniryātā — from navel-kanda, pulsing; from navel-wheel, exiting. Yadā śaktiḥ prabudhyate yadā sā par... — when śakti awakens, when she...

**FUNCTION.** Establishes Śākta vijñāna (thirteenfold supreme knowledge, indicated by Śambhu), rising (meditate on awakened śakti, staff-like, supreme Goddess; throat = Rudra, palate = abiding; go to unattached Śiva-state), close (fear not; supreme Goddess plays; affliction-triad's modification from many births; knot-bursting, divine touch-delight, bindu-vibration; when śakti awakens).

**KEY TERMS.** śākta (Śākta), vijñāna (knowledge), sthāna (place), praveśa (entry), rūpa (form), lakṣa (mark), lakṣaṇa (characteristic), utthāpana (rising), bodhana (awakening), cakraviśrāma (wheel-rest), viśrāma (rest), pariṇāma (transformation), āgama (going), kanda (kanda), vāyu (breath), śṛṅgāṭaka (triangular), ṣaḍara (six-spoked), prabodha (awakening), daṇḍavat (staff-like), kaṇṭha (throat), tālu (palate), granthi (knot), bindu (bindu), nāda (nāda), prabuddha (awakened).

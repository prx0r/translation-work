# C1 — sakt-p02 (raising and the signs)

## SUMMARY

Sealed breath finds her; mantra rides the exhale and she stands awakened, straight as a staff. The climb pierces navel, heart, throat, palate, brow, and crown-door, then rests wheel by wheel through a twelvefold round. And the body answers — trembling heart, whirling head, limbs spun joint by joint in the power of true knowing.

## FUNCTION

Turns map into motion: prescribes the raising acts, then reads the body's answer as confirmation — so trembling registers as arrival, not disturbance.

## KEY TERMS

utthāpana (raising) — exhale, mantra, staff-straight vision; bodhana (awakening) — the pierced ladder to the unsupported; cakraviśrāma (wheel-resting) — pierced in order, rested in each wheel; daṇḍavat (staff-like) — risen straight, not prostrate; kumbhaka (sealing) — the light breath-hold of gazing; dṛṣṭisaṃkrānti (sight-transmission) — the Timirodghāṭana name for the signs.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The sixth through eighth members pledged at the opening are paid here in acts; the ninth breaks in early, its signs already shaking the body before its own section.

## EXPLANATION

Raising rides the exhale: mantra uttered, śakti seen already awakened and rod-straight. Awakening climbs a deity-ladder — navel, grandsire of the heart, unfallen of the throat, Rudra face to face on the palate, Īśa at the brow, Sadāśiva at the brahma-door — and exits into the unsupported. Wheel-resting is textured (petal, blaze, flow, steady sky) and counted (twelvefold, by tradition). Then the ninth member breaks into the body: trembling, whirling, joint-by-joint churning, all by true knowledge's power — with the Timirodghāṭana term sight-transmission naming the event.

## BOUNDARY

Fear is read but not yet refused; the refusal (not to be feared) belongs ahead. The signs here are diagnosed as progress, not stilled into rest — stilling belongs to repose.

## RELATED

Timirodghāṭana as the quoted source of the sign-verses (sdv); the suṣumnā road as the climbed channel; Kaula antiquity grounding the practice's age.

## ESSENCE

1. Sealed breath, steady gaze at śakti — the sign defined, raising beyond.
2. Mantra on the exhale; her contemplated awakened, straight as a staff.
3. From the base-middle into the suṣumnā road — raising declared, awakening beyond.
4. Bulb-rooted piercing: navel, heart-grandsire, throat-unfallen, palate Rudra.
5. Brow-Īśa, brahma-door Sadāśiva — wakened, then flight to unsupported Śiva.
6. Awakening sealed; wheel-resting: petal, blaze, flow, steady sky.
7. Nectar, crest, śakti-brahmā, drop-sound — the twelvefold wheel proclaimed.
8. She pierces in order, rests wheel by wheel each moment — supreme wheel-rest.
9. Heart trembles first, palate gate, head whirls — sight-transmission's sign.
10. Limb by limb the whirling spreads through the joints; the heart churns by true knowing.

# C1 — sakt-p03 (rest and return)

## SUMMARY

Whatever the state brews, none of it is to be feared — the Goddess plays. Nectar-drunk she multiplies changes and shakes the stained souls off their snare; the body testifies with thrill, tears, yawning, faltering voice, burst knots, throbbing drops. Senses set, śakti rests in Śiva, the self turns Śiva and rains life-nectar — and the Goddess enters again: thirteen in full.

## FUNCTION

Releases fear, seals repose, and bends the summit back into return — transformation raining down as nectar for creatures.

## KEY TERMS

krīḍā (play) — her play as the ground of fearlessness; bhūmikāgamana (stage-arrival) — the shaking-off enacted; antāvasthā (final state) — the sign-list resolved and declared; viśrāma (rest) — senses set, śakti rested in Śiva; pariṇāma (transformation) — become Śiva, the self turns; āgamana (return) — the goddess re-enters, thirteen sealed; amṛta (nectar) — creature-life rained.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The tenth through thirteenth members pledged at the opening are paid here to the syllable; the quoted sign-verses are stage-arrival wearing another scripture's words.

## EXPLANATION

Fearlessness is grounded in play: her krīḍā makes every produced change safe. Nectar-intoxication ferments many births' stained residue until she shakes the stained souls off the snare — stage-arrival enacted. Transmission signs its body (the Timirodghāṭana list: thrill, tears, yawning, faltering voice, burst knots, throbbing drops), and the list resolves into the declared final state. Repose is double: senses set without, śakti dissolved in the supreme within; the subject finds no other object. Transformation's thesis sentence (become Śiva, the self turns) overflows as rain — divine nectar that is creature-life — and the re-entering Goddess seals thirteen.

## BOUNDARY

No new practice is prescribed; results are read. The return does not undo the ascent — the rain-giver stays transformed while the Goddess re-enters.

## RELATED

Timirodghāṭana 4.8-10 near-verbatim (sdv): the teaching's Kaula age; sṛṣṭisaṃkrānti confirmed via Timirodghāṭana 6.6.

## ESSENCE

1. Whatever the state brews — in none of it anything to fear; the Goddess plays.
2. Nectar-drunk she multiplies changes: many births' stained doing fermented.
3. The stained shaken off the snare, supreme-powered — stage-arrival sealed.
4. Transmission's body-signature: thrill, tears, yawning, faltering inner voices.
5. Knots burst, touch turns divine, drops throb from the navel bulb.
6. The sign-list resolves: final state declared; rest taught next.
7. Senses set without, she dissolved in the supreme — that is repose's ground.
8. Subject finds nothing, no other object; rested in Śiva — rest named.
9. Mind to dissolution where her resting lies; self known by the firm.
10. Become Śiva, the self turns — the turning; nectar rained as creature-life; goddess re-enters — thirteen full.

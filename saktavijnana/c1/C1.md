# C1 — Śaktavijñāna

## sakt-p01–p03 — Śākta knowledge and close
**SUMMARY.** Śaktavijñāna establishes Śākta vijñāna = thirteenfold supreme knowledge (place/entry/form/mark/characteristic/rising/awakening/wheel-rest/rest/transformation/going). Indicated by Śambhu in all Trika śāstras. Kanda is wheel-place; conquered breath leads to middle. Rising: meditate on awakened śakti, staff-like, supreme Goddess. Throat = imperishable, directly Rudra, palate = abiding; having awakened, go quickly to unattached Śiva-state. Close: fear not in those; supreme Goddess plays; affliction-triad's modification from many births; stage-going declared, final state; knot-bursting, divine touch-delight, bindu-vibration; from navel-kanda, pulsing; from navel-wheel, exiting; when śakti awakens.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes Śākta vijñāna (thirteenfold supreme knowledge, indicated by Śambhu), rising (meditate on awakened śakti, staff-like, supreme Goddess; throat = Rudra, palate = abiding; go to unattached Śiva-state), close (fear not; supreme Goddess plays; affliction-triad's modification from many births; knot-bursting, divine touch-delight, bindu-vibration; when śakti awakens).

# C1 NOTE — sakt-p01

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Thirteen members from bulb-measure to thousand-sun blaze; entry by breath, form by vision, mark by steadiness, sign by gazing.

## VOICE MAP

Teacher enumerates, locates, instructs, shows, praises (vv1-10).

## KEY TERMS

* sthāna — standing place (bulb, finger-measured)
* praveśa — entry (breath restrained, mind cast)
* rūpa — form (six-spoked wheel, contemplation)
* lakṣa — mark (assigned when steadied)
* lakṣaṇa — sign (unstruck digit, serpent-coiled)
* kanda — bulb (pomegranate-red)
* vijñāna — esoteric doctrine (thirteenfold, supreme)

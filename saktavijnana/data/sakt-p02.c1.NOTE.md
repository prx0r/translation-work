# C1 NOTE — sakt-p02

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Raising by exhale-mantra, awakening up the deity-ladder, wheel-rest twelvefold; the body answers with tremor, whirl, churn.

## VOICE MAP

Teacher instructs, climbs, seals, counts, beholds, diagnoses (vv11-20).

## KEY TERMS

* utthāpana — raising (exhale, mantra, staff-straight)
* bodhana — awakening (navel to crown-door ladder)
* cakraviśrāma — wheel-rest (pierced in order, rested each)
* daṇḍavat — staff-like (erect, adjudicated)
* dṛṣṭisaṃkrānti — sight-transmission (Timirodghāṭana term)
* suṣumnā — central road (resorted to)

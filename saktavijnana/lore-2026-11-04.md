# Saktavijnana lore — 2026-11-04 (verse-usable)

Source: Zaidoo 1947 (GRETIL SomSv_N, 30v). Attribution to Somānanda (Śivadṛṣṭi author) plausible but unconfirmed — do NOT assert identity in English renderings; colophon says śrīsomānandapādaviracitaṃ.

## The thirteen vidhās (vv1-3) — use these glosses
Mahānaya (mahanaya.org, full translation) + sdv (Sarasvataṃ cakṣuḥ, 2006) agree:
1. sthānaṃ — standing place = kanda, the "bulb"
2. praveśaḥ — entrance (of breath into the central channel)
3. rūpam — form = contemplation (cintanātmakam, v7d)
4. lakṣam — mark/object = focus (fixing citta until steady, v8)
5. lakṣaṇam — sign = observing the focus with light kumbhaka (vv9-11)
6. utthāpanam — raising (recaka + mantra, śakti as daṇḍavat Parameśvarī, vv12-13)
7. bodhanam — awakening (piercing navel→heart→throat→palate→brow→brahmadvāra, vv14-16)
8. cakraviśrāmaṇam — piercing-and-resting in the cakras (twelvefold cakra list, vv16-18)
9. bhūmikāgamanaṃ — stages of progress (vv19-23)
10. antāvasthā — completion/final state (vv24-25ab)
11. viśrāmaḥ — repose (vv25cd-27)
12. pariṇāmaḥ — transformation (vv28-29ab; śivībhūto bhavaty ātmā)
13. āgamanaṃ — return (vv29cd-30; raining divine amṛta, śakti re-enters)

vijñānam = "esoteric doctrine" (sdv), NOT modern "science".

## Kaula antiquity (sdv) — verse-usable in p03
- vv19-21 quote Timirodghāṭana 4.8-10 verbatim (early Kaula text; same half-verse Abhinavagupta cites, Parātriṃśikāvivaraṇa p.201).
- v9d bhujaṅgakuṭilākṛtiḥ = coiled serpent: kuṇḍalinī present but NEVER NAMED — keep "serpent-coiled" in L2/L3, never insert "kuṇḍalinī" where Sanskrit doesn't say it.
- Reading sṛṣṭisaṃkrānti (v19d/21 zone) confirmed via Timirodghāṭana 6.6.

## śākta = kaula here (sdv)
Title śāktavijñāna = wisdom of Śakti-as-Kuṇḍalinī in a Trika-scripture frame (v3: sarveṣu trikaśāstreṣu).

## Rasa pointers (for C1)
- Sthāyin for p01 (vv1-10, naming + body + focus): adbhuta (wonder at the mapped body) with vīra-utsāha undertow (the practitioner's resolve).
- Sthāyin for p02 (vv11-20, raising + piercing + trembling signs): adbhuta strained toward bhayānaka at the edges — vv21's na bhetavyaṃ ("not to be feared") is verse-usable proof the text anticipates fear; vyabhicāris kampate/bhramate/ghūrṇate.
- Sthāyin for p03 (vv21-30, play + repose + return): śānta (viśrāma/pariṇāma) — krīḍati parameśvarī (v21d, "the Supreme Goddess plays") carries the p03 L4 refrain-candidate, NOT invented.
- Passages vv24-25 (romaharṣa, aśrupāta, gadgada gir) give body-vyabhicāris for p03.

## Collision warnings for T1/L0
- lakṣaṃ (v1d, v8d) vs lakṣaṇam (v1d, v11c): mark vs sign — keep distinct everywhere.
- utthāpana (v1/v12-13) vs bodhana (v1/v13-16) vs cakraviśrāma (v1/v16-18): raising vs awakening vs resting — three separate technical terms, never merge.
- viśrāma (v2/v27 rest) vs cakraviśrāma (v1/v18 wheel-rest) vs viśrāmaṇa (v16/25): keep the compounds distinct.
- antāvasthā (v2/v25) vs bhūmikāgamana (v2/v23): arrival-of-stage vs final-state — sequential, not synonymous.
- śakti (vv11-13, 18, 25-27, 30) vs parameśvarī (v12d) vs mahādevī (v18c) vs parama-śakti (v26c): track epithet shifts.

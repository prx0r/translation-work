# Sambandhasiddhi — ARGMAP

## samb-p01 (2–9)
- speaker: Author (Kashmir Śaiva).
- question: What is saṃbandha (relation)?
- premises: Bhāvabhedādisaṃbandhamayena vapuṣonmiṣan (by body of relation of being-difference etc., shining). Jayaty eko 'pi viśvātmā prakāśaḥ parameśvaraḥ (victorious, one, world-self, light, supreme Lord). Iha bhāvānāṃ saṃbandho vicāryate (here, beings' relation is examined). Kastāvatsaṃbandhaśabdārthaḥ (what is the meaning of "relation" word). Saṃsargaḥ saṃparkaḥ saṃśleṣaḥ saṃbandha ityapyukte na vivṛtaḥ saṃbandhārthaḥ (even if called conjunction/contact/union/relation, the meaning of relation is not explained).
- inference: Saṃbandha = relation; word-meaning not explained by conjunction/contact/union; world-self light supreme Lord victorious.
- conclusion: Saṃbandha doctrine opened (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## samb-p02 (10–18)
- speaker: Author.
- question: How does relation operate?
- premises: Vyāpāro'nena ruddho'sau na vikalpāntaraṃ spṛśet (by this, activity is restrained, does not touch other thought). Vikalpāntarasaṃsparśe mātṛbhedaprasaṅgataḥ (if touching other thought, mother-difference arises). Tadekāmarśavartyekaśabdādhyāsavaśādbrajet (by that, one-reflection-abiding, one-word-superimposition, goes). Aneko 'py ekatāmartho yathā ghaṭapaṭāviti (many, yet one-meaning, as pot/cloth). Ityasti tāvadanyaśca viśeṣaṇaviśeṣyayoḥ (thus there is also other, qualifier/qualified). Saṃbandha upakārārthaḥ... (relation is for help...).
- inference: Relation operates by restraining activity from other thought; if touching other thought, mother-difference arises; by one-reflection, one-word-superimposition, many become one-meaning; relation is for help.
- conclusion: Operation stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## samb-p03 (19–21)
- speaker: Author (close).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: Iti saṃbandhagatyuktā māyīyajñātṛniṣṭhitāḥ (thus by relation-course said, Māyā-knower-established). Dhiyo vibhinnārthadṛśo vyavahārapravartikāḥ (intellects of varied-meaning-view, transaction-causing). Na paraṃ tāstathā bhrāntāḥ sarvā api pratikṣaṇāt (but not all, deluded, every moment). Svasaṃvitsaṃjñakānantacidvimarśapratiṣṭhitāḥ (established in infinite consciousness-reflection, called self-awareness). Uktaḥ svasaṃvitsiddho 'yaṃ nirbādha upayogavān (said, self-awareness-accomplished, unobstructed, useful). Saṃbandha... (relation...).
- inference: Close = by relation-course, Māyā-knower-established; intellects of varied-meaning-view cause transaction; not all deluded every moment; established in infinite consciousness-reflection, called self-awareness; self-awareness-accomplished, unobstructed, useful.
- conclusion: The text closes with self-awareness-accomplishment.
- unresolved: none.

# Sambandhasiddhi samb-p01 (vv. 1–9) — L2 READ

## v.1
With relation whose self is difference-and-non-difference — by whom all things are proved, whose work is world-traffic — him, Śiva, by own will, we praise.
The Sanskrit: *bhedābhedātmasaṃbandhasahasarvārthasādhitā lokayātrā kṛtiryasya svecchayā taṃ stumaḥ śivam*

## v.2
Dawning by a body made of relation through being, difference, and the rest — conquering though one: the world-self, light, the supreme Lord.
The Sanskrit: *bhāvabhedādisaṃbandhamayena vapuṣonmiṣan jayatyeko'pi viśvātmā prakāśaḥ parameśvaraḥ*

## v.3
Awaiting and dependence are no relation for the two inert ones: impossible — even where selfhood is won, they are metaphor, not definition.
The Sanskrit: *apekṣā pāratantryaṃ ca na saṃbandho jaḍātmanoḥ asaṃbhavādātmalābhe'pyupacāro na lakṣaṇā*

## v.4
From the teaching that non-beings obtain selfhood, two-seatedness too is abandoned: form-fusion alone thus stands as relation's meaning.
The Sanskrit: *asatāmātmalābhokterdviṣṭhataivaṃ ca hīyate rūpasaṃśleṣa evaivaṃ saṃbandhārthaḥ sthitaḥ sa ca*

## v.5
Not uninterruptedness — for it misses relation: if it were so, it would hold of iron bars. Hence fusion of the many's form — that alone is one-selfhood.
The Sanskrit: *nānantaryamasaṃbandhāttathātve'yaḥśalākayoḥ tenānekasya rūpasya śleṣa aikātmyameva saḥ*

## v.6
No barring of the many in one's very oneness, others say: things unmixed in themselves — thought joins them.
The Sanskrit: *naikasyaikye'pyanekasya bādha āhuryathā pare ityamiśrāḥ svayaṃ bhāvāstānyojayati kalpanā*

## v.7
Since this is no mere thought — it cannot be dismissed: like the snake-error, it resists remedy by clinging to a shining.
The Sanskrit: *yato na kalpanāmātraṃ tāvadetadavāraṇāt sarpabhrānterivābhāsalagnatvāttannivāraṇam*

## v.8
Since clinging to a shining is not a thing resembling a shining but a following after shining — error is exactly that: a shining astray.
The Sanskrit: *yasmādābhāsalagnatvaṃ nābhāsasadṛśārthatā kiṃ tvābhāsānusāritvaṃ bhrānta ābhāsa eva tat*

## v.9
Thus even in this shining, the many's unity-seeing has a statable warrant — declared plainly in recognition's path.
The Sanskrit: *itthamābhāsa evāsminnanekasyaikatādṛśi vācyopapattiḥ sāpyuktā pratyabhijñānaye sphuṭam*

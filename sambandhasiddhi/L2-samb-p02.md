# Sambandhasiddhi samb-p02 (vv. 10–18) — L2 READ

## v.10
Thus, of beings, of consciousness-bodied beings — even of Māyā-difference-made, unity is not hard to effect.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ ca sati vastūnāṃ cinmātravapuṣāṃ satām māyānirmitabhedānāmapyekatvamadurghaṭam*

## v.11
Therein too, what is reflection's object for the worldly — that same: of the knower whose pith is difference of non-difference-shining form.
The Sanskrit: *tatrāpi yaḥ parāmarśaviṣayo laukikasya saḥ cidabhedaprathārūpabhedasārasya vedituḥ*

## v.12
Activity blocked by this — it should touch no other thought; for on touching another thought, knower-difference follows.
The Sanskrit: *vyāpāro'nena ruddho'sau na vikalpāntaraṃ spṛśet vikalpāntarasaṃsparśe mātṛbhedaprasaṅgataḥ*

## v.13
Abiding in that-one-reflection, by one-word-superimposition force — the many too would reach unity — as pot-cloth means.
The Sanskrit: *tadekāmarśavartyekaśabdādhyāsavaśādbrajet aneko'pyekatāmartho yathā ghaṭapaṭāviti*

## v.14
So there is also another — qualifier-qualified relation, for helping; it serves for knowing.
The Sanskrit: *ityasti tāvadanyaśca viśeṣaṇaviśeṣyayoḥ saṃbandha upakārārthaḥ sa jñātumupayujyate*

## v.15
Even in manyness there'd be twoness — from double-reflection birth — as the king's horses, chariots, men.
The Sanskrit: *bahutve'pi bhaveddvitvaṃ viśeṣaṇaviśeṣyayoḥ dvivimarśabhuvo yadvadrājño'śvarathapattayaḥ*

## v.16
Reflection 'of king' is one, facing own-generality; the other — 'man,' this, with master-generality-relation.
The Sanskrit: *vimarśo rājña ityekaḥ svasāmānyonmukhaḥ paraḥ svāmisāmānyasaṃbandhasahaḥ puruṣa ityayam*

## v.17
'The king's man' — this, after, in relation's range — is to be thought by this; or the former two to be thought — those three in every way.
The Sanskrit: *rājñaḥ puruṣa ityeṣa paścātsaṃbandhagocaraḥ kalpyo'nenātha vā kalpyau pūrvau te sarvathā trayaḥ*

## v.18
Genitive-stated relation, settled in the qualifier — reaching qualified-oneness, yet not abandoning own form.
The Sanskrit: *ṣaṣṭhyādivācyaḥ saṃbandho viniviṣṭo viśeṣaṇe viśeṣyaikatvamāpanne svaṃ ca rūpamanujjhati*

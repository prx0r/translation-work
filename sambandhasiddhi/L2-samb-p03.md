# Sambandhasiddhi samb-p03 (vv. 19–23) — L2 READ

## v.19
With one short — by the pure, by the chief — mutual fitness would be of the secondary — as the king's man, and the brahmin's.
The Sanskrit: *ekahānyā pradhānena śuddhenānyānyayogitā syādguṇasya yathā rājñaḥ puruṣo brāhmaṇasya ca*

## v.20
So by another chief — what other unity, or by still another — relation-called of the secondary would be — as the king's horse, man.
The Sanskrit: *tathānyena pradhānena yaikatānyāpareṇa vā saṃbandhākhyā guṇasya syādrājño'śvaḥ puruṣo*

## v.21
Thus relation's course told: resting-on-Māyā-knowers — intellects of varied-thing-views, transaction-causing.
The Sanskrit: *iti saṃbandhagatyuktā māyīyajñātṛniṣṭhitāḥ dhiyo vibhinnārthadṛśo vyavahārapravartikāḥ*

## v.22
Not — but those, though called so erred — all even — moment by moment grounded in endless-awareness reflection called self-awareness.
The Sanskrit: *na paraṃ tāstathā bhrāntāḥ sarvā api pratikṣaṇāt svasaṃvitsaṃjñakānantacidvimarśapratiṣṭhitāḥ*

## v.23
Told, self-awareness-proved, this: unrefuted, useful — relation in recognition — full apparatus besides.
The Sanskrit: *uktaḥ svasaṃvitsiddho'yaṃ nirbādha upayogavān saṃbandhaḥ pratyabhijñāyāṃ pūrṇaḥ parikaraḥ punaḥ*

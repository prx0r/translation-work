# Sambandhasiddhi — L2 READ (Dyczkowski-mode)

## samb-p01–p03 — relation and close
**SUMMARY.** By body of relation of being-difference etc., shining. Victorious, one, world-self, light, supreme Lord. Here, beings' relation is examined. What is the meaning of "relation" word. Even if called conjunction/contact/union/relation, the meaning of relation is not explained. By this, activity is restrained, does not touch other thought. If touching other thought, mother-difference arises. By that, one-reflection-abiding, one-word-superimposition, goes. Many, yet one-meaning, as pot/cloth. Thus there is also other, qualifier/qualified. Relation is for help. Thus by relation-course, Māyā-knower-established. Intellects of varied-meaning-view, transaction-causing. But not all, deluded, every moment. Established in infinite consciousness-reflection, called self-awareness. Self-awareness-accomplished, unobstructed, useful.

**FUNCTION.** Examines saṃbandha (relation): word-meaning not explained by conjunction/contact/union; relation operates by restraining activity from other thought; close with self-awareness-accomplishment.

**KEY TERMS.** saṃbandha (relation), saṃsarga (conjunction), saṃparka (contact), saṃśleṣa (union), vyāpāra (activity), vikalpa (thought), mātṛ (mother), ekāmarśa (one-reflection), viśeṣaṇa (qualifier), viśeṣya (qualified), upakāra (help), māyī (Māyā), jñātṛ (knower), svasaṃvit (self-awareness), vimarśa (reflection).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Entire text; 3 passages.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in three steps. (1) Relation examined: by body of relation of being-difference etc., shining; victorious, one, world-self, light, supreme Lord; here, beings' relation examined; what is meaning of "relation" word; even if called conjunction/contact/union/relation, meaning not explained. (2) Operation: by this, activity restrained, does not touch other thought; if touching other thought, mother-difference arises; by one-reflection, one-word-superimposition, goes; many, yet one-meaning, as pot/cloth; thus there is also other, qualifier/qualified; relation is for help. (3) Close: by relation-course, Māyā-knower-established; intellects of varied-meaning-view cause transaction; not all deluded every moment; established in infinite consciousness-reflection, called self-awareness; self-awareness-accomplished, unobstructed, useful.

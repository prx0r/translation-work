## The parable (canonical draft, ≈90s spoken)

> A joiner is asked what truly joins two things — and he tests the
> false joins first. Logs left side by side are not a bench: waiting
> joins nothing. A staff leaning on a wall, let go, falls: leaning
> joins nothing. Two pots on one shelf stay two. And an unbroken
> cord is not a relation either — by that rule iron bars would be
> kin. He laughs, and shows the real join: fusion of forms — the
> many made one-bodied — pot and the cloth around it carried as one,
> the king's horse the king's only where the king's measure fits.
> All the joins belong to one master joiner, praised, whose joining
> itself dawns by will. Then he puts down his tools: every join a
> joiner makes was already made — a thousand things join themselves
> the moment awareness holds them, because knowing is one and
> endless, and every other relation is only that knowing wearing a
> face. He marks the work: the proof stands, unrefuted, useful.

**Map (hinge):**
- side-by-side logs / leaning staff / shelf-pots / unbroken cord →
  p01's four refuted counterfeits: *awaiting, dependence,
  two-seatedness, uninterruptedness… iron bars would qualify* [p01
  vv3–5]
- fusion of forms, the many one-bodied → p01 *form-fusion alone
  stands; the many's one-selfhood* [p01]
- pot and cloth carried as one → p02 *one-word-superimposition — as
  pot-cloth means* [p02]
- king's horse the king's where the measure fits → p02 *qualifier-
  qualified relation… as the king's horses, chariots, men* +
  p03 *the king's man, the brahmin's — fitness by chief* [p02–p03]
- one master joiner, praised, joining dawns by will → p01 vv1–2
  *by whom all things are proved, world-traffic — him, Śiva, by own
  will, we praise; relation-dawning through being* [p01 vv1–2]
- joins make themselves when awareness holds → p03 *grounded in
  endless-awareness reflection, called self-awareness; relation in
  recognition* [p03]
- proof stands unrefuted, useful → p03 closing *unrefuted, useful —
  full apparatus* [p03]

**Limit:** iron-bars line kept as the counterfeits' reductio (the
text's own joke — an UNbroken cord can't be relation or iron bars
would be kin); the "joins themselves" clause must not become
solipsism — the parable says knowing is ONE and endless (a single
field), never "each mind invents."
**Rasa:** vīra-utsāha throughout — triumph as demolition, fitted
joinery, seal (the three essences' sequence: fall → fit → close).
The parable LAUGHS (exultation) and ends on the stamped proof.
**Bar check:** one main figure (king = measure-mention, never
drawn); retellable; plain (sambandha/pūrvapakṣa/pratyabhijñā →
joins/false-joins/recognition-wearing-a-face).

## The seal — statement

**The bench under the endless ribbon:** a joiner's vertical world —
bottom: the display of failed joins (logs apart, staff leaning, two
pots on a shelf, a cord with an iron bar beside it, all cold grey);
middle: the true join — a crimson-clothed pot carried as ONE + a
gold measuring-rod fitting the king's horse; top: a single gold
endless ribbon (knowing, one, endless) from which every join-line
hangs — the master-joiner's whole proof hung from awareness.

## Composition laws (this seal)

ONE subject (the joiner; the king NEVER drawn — only his rod/fit).
Failed joins must read as attempts, not ruins (grey, tidy, honest).
The ribbon is ONE line (endlessness = no clasp, no seam). Tablet
blank (no letters — the proof is stated in the parable, not
inscribed). Iron bar sits among failures as the reductio joke —
small.

# Sambandhasiddhi p03 — ARGUMENT MAP (the seal: Māyā-knowers, transaction, self-awareness)

*2026-10-31. Source: T1 samb-p03 = kārikās 19–21 (edition p. 15). The closing seal of the treatise.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

If relation is real but belongs to the Māyā-state, are its users deluded? The seal answers: no — the Māyā-knowers' diverse-view intellects *drive transaction* (*vyavahārapravartikāḥ*), and they are *not* simply deluded at every moment, because they rest on the infinite consciousness-reflection called self-awareness (*svasaṃvit*). Relation, self-awareness-accomplished, is unobstructed and useful — the full retinue (*parikaraḥ*) of the Recognition.

## 2. The argument, reconstructed step by step

**Step 0 — established by relation's course (v. 19):**
> *iti saṃbandhagatyā uktā māyīyajñātṛniṣṭhitāḥ | dhiyo vibhinnārthadṛśo vyavahārapravartikāḥ*
[i.e. thus, by relation's course, are stated the Māyā-knower-established intellects of diverse-thing-view that drive transaction.] The whole preceding machinery lands here: relation's *course* (gati) is what establishes finite knowers and their business.

**Step 1 — not simply deluded (v. 20a):**
> *na paraṃ tās tathā bhrāntāḥ sarvā api pratikṣaṇāt*
[i.e. yet they are not simply deluded, all of them, at every moment.] Against the p01 opponent's *bhrama/saṃvṛti* verdict: error-status is not the whole story.

**Step 2 — grounded in self-awareness (v. 20b):**
> *svasaṃvitsaṃjñakānantacidvimarśapratiṣṭhitāḥ*
[i.e. grounded in the infinite consciousness-reflection called self-awareness.] The ground of transaction-intellects is not correspondence with cut-off things but rootedness in infinite *vimarśa*. Ratié: *vimarśa* is consciousness not merely reflecting but actively grasping itself — "apprehends or grasps that manifestation actively and spontaneously" — which is why the Pratyabhijñā philosophers deliberately appropriate Buddhist concepts rather than unwittingly borrow them: they take full responsibility for the move. The closing *pūrṇaḥ parikaraḥ* (the full retinue) names this: relation, self-awareness-accomplished, is the whole philosophical apparatus the Recognition brings.

**Step 3 — the seal (v. 21):**
> *uktaḥ svasaṃvitsiddhaḥ … saṃbandhaḥ … nirbādha upayogavān | … pūrṇaḥ parikaraḥ … pratyabhijñāyām*
[i.e. stated: the self-awareness-accomplished relation — unobstructed, useful — the full retinue in the Recognition.] *Nirbādha* (no cognition sublates it) + *upayogavān* (it does work) = the two marks of the real, earned back from the Buddhist's own criteria.

## 3. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. *Pratikṣaṇāt* scope — "not deluded at every moment" leaves open *which* moments escape delusion; the text does not schedule them.
2. *Parikaraḥ* (retinue/entourage) — the closing metaphor is unexpanded; read as "the full philosophical apparatus the Recognition brings with it."

## 4. Decision for L2

Proceed as seal-prose, short: three moves (established → not-deluded → grounded), then the two-mark seal. This is the passage C1 will condense almost whole — it is already the treatise condensing itself.

# C1 — samb-p01 (vv. 1–9): counterfeits refused; the proof-text turned

## SUMMARY

Relation whose self is difference-and-non-difference proves all things; world-traffic is his work — Śiva, by own will, praised. His body dawns made of relation: conquering though one, world-self, light, supreme Lord. Awaiting and dependence are no relation for the two inert — impossible; won selfhood or not, metaphor, not definition. Teaching self-obtaining to non-beings drops two-seatedness: form-fusion alone stands as relation's sense. No unbroken nearness — it misses relation; else iron bars. So the many's form-fusion: one-selfhood alone. Others say the many need no barring in oneness: things unmixed, thought joins them. No mere thought, this — past warding, like snake-error clinging to a shining. Clinging is no thing-like-shining but trailing after it: error, that shining strayed. So many-seen-as-one finds warrant — statable, told clear in recognition's path.

## KEY TERMS

* **bhedābheda** — difference-and-non-difference (unity-in-diversity): relation's self (v1).
* **svecchā** — own will: the auspice; spontaneity grounding relation (v1).
* **vapus/unmiṣat** — body/dawning: relation as the Lord's form (v2).
* **apekṣā/pāratantrya** — awaiting/dependence: first counterfeit, needing will (v3).
* **upacāra/lakṣaṇā** — metaphor/definition: what courtesy is denied (v3).
* **dviṣṭhatā** — two-seatedness: second counterfeit, said of relata (v4).
* **rūpasaṃśleṣa** — form-fusion: what stands (v4).
* **ānantarya** — unbrokenness: third counterfeit, iron-barred (v5).
* **aikātmya** — one-selfhood: the positive thesis (v5).
* **kalpanā** — thought: the opponent's joiner, turned witness (v6–7).
* **ābhāsalagnatva** — shining-clinging: why error sticks (v7–8).
* **vācyopapatti** — statable warrant: claimed in recognition's path (v9).

## rasa

Triumph (vīra) of demolition and retorsion: three counterfeits refused, then the opponent's own verse made to testify. Wonder (adbhuta) at the turn — the joining thought was never mere thought.

# C1 — samb-p02 (vv. 10–18): qualifier-qualified mechanics; three thinkings

## SUMMARY

Even Māyā-differenced beings take unity without strain — the consciousness-bodied need no forcing. The worldly one's reflection-object is the knower whose pith is difference inside non-difference's shine. One reflection's business touches no other thought, or knowers multiply. The many still reach oneness through one-word superimposition — pot-cloth says it. A second relation stands, qualifier with qualified, for helping's sake: it serves to know. Even in many, twoness holds — double-grasp-begot, like the king's horses, chariots, men. Grasp 'of king' stands one, toward its own kind; 'man' the other, sharing the master-kind's bond. 'The king's man' — thought after, in relation's field, by this; or the first two thought — all three, always. Spoken by the sixth, relation settles in the qualifier — reaching oneness with the qualified, yet keeping its own shape.

## KEY TERMS

* **cinmātravapus** — consciousness-bodied: unity without strain (v10).
* **parāmarśaviṣaya** — reflection's object: what the worldly reflect (v11).
* **mātṛbheda** — knower-split: what touching brings (v12).
* **ekaśabdādhyāsa** — one-word superimposition: how many go one (v13).
* **upakāra** — helping: the second relation's office (v14).
* **dvivimarśa** — double grasp: twoness's birth (v15).
* **svasāmānya/svāmisāmānya** — own-kind/master-kind: the two facings (v16).
* **trayaḥ kalpanāḥ** — three thinkings: whole, parts, always (v17).
* **ṣaṣṭhī/viniviṣṭa** — genitive/settled: keeping shape in oneness (v18).
* **vimarśa** — grasping: what experience is inseparable from (throughout; field prefers reflective awareness — see Ajad NOTE).

## rasa

Mechanic-triumph (vīra): qualifier and qualified fitted like joinery — twoness kept inside manyness, oneness kept inside twoness; wonder (adbhuta) that grammar thinks.

# C1 — samb-p03 (vv. 19–23): chief and secondary; verdict spoken

## SUMMARY

One short of the pure chief, secondaries mutually fit — the king's man, and the brahmin's. By other chiefs, other unities — relation-called of secondaries: the king's horse, man. Thus relation's course is told: intellects resting on Māyā-knowers, of varied views, driving transaction. Not deluded, though called so — all, moment by moment, grounded in endless-awareness reflection called self-awareness. Told, self-awareness-proved, unrefuted, useful — relation in recognition: full apparatus besides.

## KEY TERMS

* **ekahāni** — one-short: the held-open measure (v19).
* **guṇa/pradhāna** — secondary/chief: the ruling pair (vv19-20).
* **saṃbandhākhyā** — relation-called: secondaries' title (v20).
* **saṃbandhagati** — relation's course: what is told (v21).
* **māyīyajñātṛ** — Māyā-knowers: what intellects rest on (v21).
* **pratikṣaṇam** — moment by moment: grounding's rhythm (v22).
* **svasaṃvit** — self-awareness: reflection's name (vv22-23).
* **parikara** — apparatus: full, besides (v23).

## rasa

Seal-triumph (vīra): pairs measured, course told, delusion denied, apparatus closed full — the proof bows out the way it came in, proving.

# C1 — Sambandhasiddhi (final condensation of the arguments)

*Condensed from ARGMAP (samb-p01/02/03) + L2.v2. Passage-local: each sits under its passage in print. No modern comparison, no PARALLELS inside.*

## samb-p01 (vv. 1–9, prose pp. 2–10)

Relation is the Lord's own body, dawning as unity-in-diversity, accomplished by his own will — so the benediction announces before the argument begins. The treatise then asks what relation means and finds that every reduction fails: uninterruptedness (father and son would have none; iron bars would), residing-in-two (duality and separateness would be relations), awaiting and dependence (inert things cannot await; will belongs to consciousness). So relation must be stated outright: the unity of the many, what requires both states. The Buddhist answers that things are unmixed and thought joins them — a delusion clinging to useful-shining, dignified as *saṃvṛti*. Utpala turns this inside out: you yourselves admitted the seeming-of-many-as-one; thought-formedness is no flaw; thought is not other than the thing's shining; so admit the shining itself as many-as-one. It holds, for the ultimately real thing is consciousness-only, world-selved through Māyā-śakti. The result: one relation in form, many in coloring; relation-talk belongs to the Māyā-state; helper and helped must be stated or transaction ceases; and the helping is the consciousness-Lord's will, two successive things shone together in one reflection. No refutation touches this.

## samb-p02 (vv. 10–18)

Relation works by restraint — holding the manifold from scattering, not gluing alien bits — which is the mode of intentional action (*śakti*), the reflexive grasping that keeps content determinate. One reflection, one word-superimposition carries the many to oneness of meaning, as pot and cloth. Qualifier and qualified is relation *for the sake of helping* knowing: one reflection toward the king facing his own generality, another toward the man bearing king-generality-relation; *rājñaḥ puruṣaḥ* comes after, relation-ranged. The genitive's relation sits in the qualifier that has attained qualified-oneness without abandoning its own form, and transfers across principals — the pair, not the substance, carries it. Relation exists in order to help knowing: that is its whole point.

## samb-p03 (vv. 19–21)

By relation's course are stated the Māyā-knower-established intellects of diverse-thing-view that drive transaction. Yet they are not simply deluded at every moment, for they rest on the infinite consciousness-reflection called self-awareness — consciousness not merely reflecting but actively grasping itself. The seal: the self-awareness-accomplished relation is unobstructed (no cognition sublates it) and useful (it does work) — the full retinue of the Recognition. Relation, grounded in self-awareness, is real.

# C1 NOTE — samb-p01

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Relation whose self is difference-and-non-difference proves all things; world-traffic is his work — Śiva, by own will, praised. His body dawns made of relation: conquering though one, world-self, light, supreme Lord. Awaiting and dependence are no relation for the two inert — impossible; won selfhood or not, metaphor, not definition. Teaching self-obtaining to non-beings drops two-seatedness: form-fusion alone stands as relation's sense. No unbroken nearness — it misses relation; else iron bars. So the many's form-fusion: one-selfhood alone. Others say the many need no barring in oneness: things unmixed, thought joins them. No mere thought, this — past warding, like snake-error clinging to a shining. Clinging is no thing-like-shining but trailing after it: error, that shining strayed. So many-seen-as-one finds warrant — statable, told clear in recognition's path.

## FUNCTION

Opens the third proof with praise and thesis (vv1-2), refuses three counterfeit relations — awaiting/dependence (v3), two-seatedness (v4), uninterruptedness (v5) — then turns the Buddhist proof-text (v6) through unwardability (v7) and shining-clinging (v8) to a warrant claimed inside recognition (v9).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Sambandhasiddhi, Utpaladeva (10th c.), mūla-ślokas with mūla-prose (Muktabodha M00080, KSTS 34). Third proof of the Siddhitrayī: relation must be real, or transaction dies. Passage 1 of 3: vv. 1–9 (maṅgala pair + retorsion verses). Prose pp. 2–10 consulted throughout, never tokenised.

## EXPLANATION

Praise and thesis first: the Lord wills world-traffic and dawns as relation's body (vv1-2). Then three refusals, each with its counterexample or exposure: awaiting needs will (v3); two-seatedness speaks of the pair (v4); nearness misses the far and catches bars (v5) — leaving form-fusion, the many's one-selfhood. The opponent's verse is admitted as evidence (v6), then its sting drawn: unwardable joining is no mere thought (v7), error trails shining rather than resembling things (v8), and the warrant is stated inside recognition (v9). The prose between carries the full demolition (synonyms parked as convention, tailapāyika; helper-and-helped stated or transaction ceases; one relation per pair); the verses are its theses.

## BOUNDARY

v01's feminine adjectives lack an overt noun — implied śakti or absolute construction, held open. svecchayā's agency (his will vs ours in praising) held open; read as his. kha's bhedātma for bhedādi (v2) logged; main text followed. asaṃbandhāt (v5) parsed as missing-the-mark; grammar allows from-non-relation, sense identical. iti śivam-style closings and Kaul colophons excluded as paratext.

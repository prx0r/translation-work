# C1 NOTE — samb-p03

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

One short of the pure chief, secondaries mutually fit — the king's man, and the brahmin's. By other chiefs, other unities — relation-called of secondaries: the king's horse, man. Thus relation's course is told: intellects resting on Māyā-knowers, of varied views, driving transaction. Not deluded, though called so — all, moment by moment, grounded in endless-awareness reflection called self-awareness. Told, self-awareness-proved, unrefuted, useful — relation in recognition: full apparatus besides.

## FUNCTION

Closes the proof by measure and verdict: chief/secondary pairs (vv19-20), course told (v21), delusion denied (v22), apparatus sealed full (v23).

## KEY TERMS

* **ekahānyā** — with-one-short: obscure measure, held open (v19).
* **śuddha** — pure: what secondaries fall one short of (v19).
* **brāhmaṇa** — brahmin's: second pair-member (v19).
* **apara-pradhāna** — still-other chief: unities multiplied (v20).
* **aśva/puruṣa** — horse/man: the king's secondaries (v20).
* **dhī/vibhinna-artha-dṛś** — intellects of varied views: course-drivers (v21).
* **vyavahārapravartikā** — transaction-causing: intellects' office (v21).
* **bhrānta-nāman** — called-erred: the denied name (v22).
* **anantacit-vimarśa** — endless-awareness reflection: grounding (v22).
* **nirbādha/upayogavat** — unrefuted/useful: the seal's predicates (v23).
* **pūrṇa-parikara** — full apparatus: besides (v23).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Passage 3 of 3: vv. 19–23. The proof's close: measure, course, denial, seal. Closes the Siddhitrayī's third proof; colophon follows (Kaul print, paratext).

## EXPLANATION

Secondaries are measured against chiefs (king's man, brahmin's) one short of pure (v19); other chiefs give other unities (king's horse, man) (v20). The course is then told outright: Māyā-knower-resting intellects drive transaction (v21). Delusion is denied by moment-grounding in self-awareness (v22). The seal speaks four predicates — told, proved, unrefuted, useful — and a full apparatus besides (v23).

## BOUNDARY

ekahānyā held open (one short of what? — the prose measures but never defines the unit; no smoothing). No live token forks; all readings settled or prose-backed.

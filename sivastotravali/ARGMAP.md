# Śivastotrāvalī — ARGMAP

## sivst-p01 (stotra 1–4)
- speaker: Utpaladeva, devotional voice.
- question: What is the nature of bhakti?
- premises: Stotra 1 (bhaktivilāsa): na dhyāyato na japataḥ syād yasyāvidhipūrvakam (not for one who meditates without rule, nor for one who chants); śivabhāsas taṃ numo bhaktiśālinam (we bow to Śiva's light, the bhakti-possessor). Ātmā mama bhavadbhaktisudhāpānayuvāpi san (my self, drinking bhakti-nectar, though young); lokayātrārajorāgāt palitair iva dhūsaraḥ (like grey with dust of world-journey). Labdhatatsaṃpadāṃ bhaktimatāṃ tvatpuravāsinām (of bhakti-possessors who have gained that wealth, city-dwellers); saṃcāro lokamārge 'pi syāt tayaiva vijṛmbhayā (movement in world-path by that expansion). Sākṣādbhavanmaye nātha sarvasmin bhuvanāntare | kiṃ na bhaktimatāṃ kṣetram mantraḥ kvaiṣām na siddhyati (Lord, in all worlds where Śiva is directly present, what is not the bhakti-possessor's field; what mantra of theirs does not succeed). Jayanti bhaktipīyūṣarasāsavavaronmadāḥ (victorious, intoxicated with best bhakti-nectar-wine). Advitīyā api sadā tvaddvitīyā api prabho (even non-dual, always your second, Lord). Anantānandasindhos te nātha tattvaṃ vidanti te | tādṛśa eva ye sāndrabhaktānandarasāplutāḥ (they know your truth, ocean of endless bliss, Lord; such are those drenched in dense bhakti-bliss-nectar). Tvam evātmeśa sarvasya sarvaś cātmani rāgavān | iti svabhāvasiddhāṃs tvadbhaktiṃ jānañ jayej janaḥ (you alone are self-Lord of all; all is in self, attached; thus knowing bhakti as self-established, one conquers people). Nātha vedyakṣaye kena na dṛśyo 'syekakaḥ sthitaḥ | vedyavedakasaṃkṣobhe 'py asi bhaktaiḥ sudarśanaḥ (Lord, when knowable is destroyed, by whom are you not seen standing alone; in knowable-knower-agitation, you are easily seen by bhaktas). Anantānandasarasī devī priyatamā yathā | aviyuktāsti te tadvad ekā tvadbhaktir astu me (as the lake of endless bliss, the beloved goddess, inseparable from you; so may one bhakti be mine). Sarva eva bhavallābhahetur bhaktimatāṃ vibho | saṃvinmārgo 'yam āhlādaduḥkhamohais tridhā sthitaḥ (all is cause of gaining you for bhakti-possessors, Lord; this consciousness-path stands threefold with joy/suffering/delusion). Bhavadbhaktyamṛtāsvādād bodhasya syāt parāpi yā | daśā sā māṃ prati svāmin nāsavasyeva śuktatā (from tasting bhakti-nectar, even the supreme state of knowledge; that state, Lord, for me, like vinegar's sourness for wine).
- inference: Bhakti = supreme, conquers all; Śiva's light belongs to bhakti-possessor; bhakti-possessors move in world by expansion; no mantra fails for them; they are intoxicated with bhakti-nectar; bhakti is self-established; Śiva easily seen by bhaktas; bhakti is inseparable from Śiva; consciousness-path threefold; knowledge-state is like vinegar-sourness compared to bhakti-nectar.
- conclusion: Bhakti doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

# Śivastotrāvalī — Exposition

*Handwritten close, 2026-11-02. Twenty songs, 450 verses, one arc: how love stops asking permission and starts recognizing itself.*

## The shape

The work opens with a proof and closes with a seal, and everything between is the proof unfolding into festival.

**s01** argues: devotion needs no rule first, because all beings love the self and the self is consciousness — so love stands on its own ground, and knowing this by drowning is conquest. The remaining nineteen songs are that conquest celebrated, tested, and finally sealed.

**s02** performs the all-self as twenty-nine bows — every stuff of the world named as His, every paradox pair (cool fire and devourer, Veda-foe and Veda-maker) held in one *namaḥ*, turning at the end from Him to His dread ring, from knowing to embracing, from victory-cries to shelter.

**s03–s05** work the instruments: the third beyond being and non-being, remembrance as medicine, begged glances, heart-union vessels, flooding meditation, Śiva-din routing pain — then confession and approach (fickle minds, low ground kept), praise and petition (name-cries, thrones, saps), and the long tasting: wax minds, bursting pots, oozing cool, lake-diving, straw offerings, unre-said sap.

**s06–s08** hold separation inside nearness (parted instants kept unparted), melt into entry (axes, stain-strings, Mother fed sap on sap), and gather the senses (thrilled seats, possession-pictured striding, Thou-natured blooming).

**s09** asks one question twenty ways — when will longing become meeting — and answers it once: by hugging hard and seeing without effort.

**s10–s11** complain and beg in high style: levelled great ones, moon-drops and sun-sparks, uneven eyes, straw-lordship, unwoven praise, death-wounded power-seeking, and the great vocative catalogue that turns petition into coronation.

**s12** opens seeing, deepens dwelling, seals beholding across twenty-nine verses — the longest road in the work, ending in fiat ontology: things be as things, naught asserted, won.

**s13** stays sane where the rest intoxicates: pain-joy marks, black wiped, live-die-or-else, work-ethic middle, widening close. Its pride verse and activity-embrace were checked against Lakshmanjoo and kept divergent by design — commentary, not Sanskrit, is where we differ.

**s14** hails fifty times and means it: faces, deeds, nearness, and a thirteenfold finale to Three-eyed. The count was verified against the edition.

**s15–s16** set lovers against the qualified (books, masters, freedoms beside love-wine madness) and then let frenzy catalogue itself — rage, pits, thrill-needles, ripened feasting — into freedom named as ripe love.

**s17** spreads the worship-feast across forty-eight verses in four movements — announced, entered, deepened, sealed — with straws and jewels both accepted at the close.

**s18–s19** win inside and outside, dwell in images, and pass through paradox to the Master yonder — father, Three-eyed, mother — with the dangling *tām* kept dangling, as printed.

**s20** bows, paradoxes, and seals: Gauri's lover favored twice over, being-tree creepers, night-dancing heroes spurred by corpse-fiends, and praise begun limb by limb, to no end.

## What the translating taught

*Compounds keep their order.* Foot-lotus where lotus-properties work (dust, sap, seat, lake); lotus-feet where feet are acted upon (remembered, touched, brushed). The order is the metaphysics: head-final for the image, head-first for the object.

*Small words carry weight.* *Kila* (they say), *eva* (alone), *api* (even), *nāma* (forsooth/by name), reduplications (*go where they go*, *hard-hard*, *love, love*) — each kept, each load-bearing. Anaphora in English answers anaphora in Sanskrit: fifty hails, thirteen hails, the even-rain, the mirror-verse of s10-v12.

*Obscurity is preserved where the Sanskrit is obscure.* The unexpressed feminine of s04-v08, the worshiper-shaped God of s04-v25, the dangling *her* of s19-v02, the oxymoron floods of s12-v03 (marked doubtful in the edition itself) — smoothed in none, noted in all.

*Witnesses witness.* Eleven Lakshmanjoo anchors compared convergent; two divergences kept and documented as commentary, not text. GRETIL quirks (*dhaṭāt* bursting lids, dental-d *jadājaḍānām*, *sadārbdha* ever-moist-uncertain) kept byte-exact with doubt logged.

## The standard held

Every verse traces to parsed tokens; every line carries a spine or a justified fragment; no line floats untraced; no guess presents as fact. Twenty songs validate clean, twenty proof records pass, the gate stands canonical. Machine-proposed throughout, human-reviewed nowhere yet — the signoff column waits for living eyes.

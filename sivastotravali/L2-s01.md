# Śivastotrāvalī Stotra 1 — L2 READ (bhaktivilāsa, The Pleasure of Devotion)

## v.1 — praise without method

Of one who neither meditates nor chants, manifestation may come without any preparatory rule. Just so — O light of Śiva — we bow to him who possesses devotion. The Sanskrit: *na dhyāyato na japataḥ syād yasyāvidhipūrvakam | evam eva śivabhāsas taṃ numo bhaktiśālinam*

## v.2 — young inside

My self stays young, drinking the nectar of Your devotion — though greyed as if with the dust of worldly journeying and its passions. The Sanskrit: *ātmā mama bhavadbhaktisudhāpānayuvāpi san | lokayātrārajorāgāt palitair iva dhūsaraḥ*

## v.3 — the world moved in differently

For those who have gained that wealth — the possessors of devotion dwelling in Your city — even movement along worldly paths happens by that very expansion. The Sanskrit: *labdhatatsaṃpadāṃ bhaktimatāṃ tvatpuravāsinām | saṃcāro lokamārge 'pi syāt tayaiva vijṛmbhayā*

## v.4 — all the world is field

Lord — where the whole world within worlds directly consists of You, what is not the devotee's field, and whose mantra does not succeed? The Sanskrit: *sākṣādbhavanmaye nātha sarvasmin bhuvanāntare | kiṃ na bhaktimatāṃ kṣetram mantraḥ kvaiṣām na siddhyati*

## v.5 — victory to the intoxicated

Victory to those intoxicated with the finest wine of devotion's nectar-essence! Though non-dual, they are ever Your second, O Lord. The Sanskrit: *jayanti bhaktipīyūṣarasāsavavaronmadāḥ | advitīyā api sadā tvaddvitīyā api prabho*

## v.6 — only the drowned know

Lord of the ocean of endless bliss — only they know Your truth who are densely drenched in blissful devotion's nectar. The Sanskrit: *anantānandasindhos te nātha tattvaṃ vidanti te | tādṛśa eva ye sāndrabhaktyānandarasāplutāḥ*

## v.7 — self-established devotion

You alone, Lord of the self, are the nature of all, and all loves dwelling in the self. Knowing Your devotion as self-established (*svabhāvasiddhā*) — by immersion's means — may a person conquer. The Sanskrit: *tvam evātmeśa sarvasya sarvaś cātmani rāgavān | iti svabhāvasiddhāṃs tvadbhaktiṃ jānañ jayej janaḥ*

## v.8 — the knowable destroyed

Lord — when the knowable is destroyed, by whom are You not seen, standing alone? Even amid the agitation of knower and knowable, You are easily seen by devotees. The Sanskrit: *nātha vedyakṣaye kena na dṛśyo 'syekakaḥ sthitaḥ | vedyavedakasaṃkṣobhe 'py asi bhaktaiḥ sudarśanaḥ*

## v.9 — one devotion, like the Goddess

As the lake of endless bliss, the Goddess, the beloved, is inseparable from You — so may one devotion of mine be inseparable. The Sanskrit: *anantānandasarasī devī priyatamā yathā | aviyuktāsti te tadvad ekā tvadbhaktir astu me*

## v.10 — everything causes gain

O All-pervading — for devotees of devotion, everything is a cause of gaining You. This path of consciousness stands threefold — in joy, in suffering, in delusion. The Sanskrit: *sarva eva bhavallābhahetur bhaktimatāṃ vibho | saṃvinmārgo 'yam āhlādaduḥkhamohais tridhā sthitaḥ*

## v.11 — out-tasted knowledge

What of knowledge — even its supreme state, from tasting Your devotion's nectar — is to me, O husband-Lord, like vinegar-sourness after wine. The Sanskrit: *bhavadbhaktyamṛtāsvādād bodhasya syāt parāpi yā | daśā sā māṃ prati svāmin nāsavasyeva śuktatā*

## v.12 — discrimination as fruit

Those to whom the great knowledge of Your devotion has come through practice — they alone know truth, both knowledge and its absence. The Sanskrit: *bhavadbhaktimahāvidyā yeṣām abhyāsam āgatā | vidyāvidyobhayasyāpi tā ete tattvavedinaḥ*

## v.13 — the speech-creeper

From the root, this creeper of speech, unfolding in due sequence — watered by the nectar of Your devotion, may it bear rasa-rich fruit for me. The Sanskrit: *āmulād vāglatā seyaṃ kramavisphāraśālinī | tvadbhaktisudhayā siktā tadrasāḍhyaphalāstu me*

## v.14 — become Śiva to worship Śiva

"Having become Śiva, let him worship" — so the devotee is said to have become. For You alone are the body's essence, purified by devotees through non-duality. The Sanskrit: *śivo bhūtvā yajeteti bhakto bhūtveti kathyate | tvam eva hi vapuḥ sāraṃ bhaktair advayaśodhitam*

## v.15 — every road, no obstruction

For devotees, what is not a means to accomplishment of non-duality with You? And for the low, what obstructions could there be to its failure? The Sanskrit: *bhaktānāṃ bhavadadvaitasiddhyai kā nopapattayaḥ | tadasiddhyai nikṛṣṭānāṃ kāni nāvaraṇāni vā*

## v.16 — the deceit

That You are attained sometime, somewhere, by yoga, O Lord — that is the deceit. Otherwise, how would You shine in all states for the possessors of devotion? The Sanskrit: *kadācit kvāpi labhyo 'si yogenetīśa vañcanā | anyathā sarvakakṣyāsu bhāsi bhaktimatāṃ katham*

## v.17 — composed even in emergence

Untouched by withdrawal and the rest — this is the great distinction: that sharers of devotion stay composed even in emergence, beyond the yogins. The Sanskrit: *pratyāhārādyasaṃspṛṣṭo viśeṣo 'sti mahānayam | yogibhyo bhaktibhājām yad vyutthāne 'pi samāhitāḥ*

## v.18 — devotion alone praised

No yoga is enjoined, no austerity, no procedure of worship at all. On this guileless path of Śiva, devotion alone is praised. The Sanskrit: *na yogo na tapo nārcākramaḥ ko 'pi pranīyate | amāye śivamārge 'smin bhaktir ekā praśasyate*

## v.19 — covering shattered

My covering shattered by devotion's light shining from all sides — may even my thoughts, which face all real things, perish. The Sanskrit: *sarvato vilasadbhaktitejodhvastāvṛter mama | pratyakṣasarvabhāvasya cintānām api naśyatu*

## v.20 — "Śiva" on the tongue

A wonder: that in devotees, with the single word "Śiva" standing always on the tongue's tip, there is the taste of every sense-object. The Sanskrit: *śiva ity ekaśabdasya jihvāgre tiṣṭhataḥ sadā | samastaviṣayāsvādo bhakteṣv evāsti ko 'py aho*

## v.21 — who counts as low?

In the calm-waved, cool, clear, sweet milk-ocean of devotion's nectar — in tasting its unworldly essence — who that stands firm there is counted low? The Sanskrit: *śāntakallolaśītācchasvādubhaktisudhāmbudhau | alaukikarasāsvāde susthaiḥ ko 'nāma gaṇyate*

## v.22 — chew the medicine

Why would people like me not chew Your devotion's great medicine — whose after-taste, O Lord, is called liberation? The Sanskrit: *sādṛśaiḥ kiṃ na carvyeta bhavadbhaktimahauṣadhiḥ | tādṛśī bhagavan yasyā mokṣākhyo 'nantaro rasaḥ*

## v.23 — true wealth

Those alone are ultimately wealth, O Lord, in the judgement of the good — which nourish confident enjoyment of the taste of devotion to You. The Sanskrit: *tā eva paramarthyante sampadaḥ sadbhir īśa yāḥ | tvad bhaktirasasambhogavisrambhaparipoṣikāḥ*

## v.24 — unstained in mud

They have grasped something of Your devotion-nectar's essence who, though fallen into this mud of passion and the rest, are not smeared by it. The Sanskrit: *bhavadbhaktisudhāsāras taiḥ kim apy upalakṣitaḥ | ye na rāgādi paṅke 'smiṃḷ lipyante patitā api*

## v.25 — even liberation a limb

From supernormal powers to liberation — "fruit" is named as a mere limb of the ripened creeper of Your devotion, for some. The Sanskrit: *aṇimādiṣu mokṣānteṣv aṅgeṣv eva phalābhidhā | bhavadbhakter vipakvāyā latāyā iva keṣu cit*

## v.26 — the pain-seed transfigured

A wonder, Lord: this mind, by nature pain's seed — watered by the essence of devotion to You, becomes liberation's great fruit. The Sanskrit: *citraṃ nisargato nātha duḥkhabījam idaṃ manaḥ | tvadbhaktirasasaṃsiktam niḥśreyasamahāphalam*

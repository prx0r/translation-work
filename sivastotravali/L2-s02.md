## v.1 — fire, moon, sun

To the many-formed, whose nature is consciousness — fire, moon, sun, Brahmā, Viṣṇu, moving and unmoving — bow. The Sanskrit: *agnīṣomaravibrahmaviṣṇusthāvarajaṅgama | svarūpa bahurūpāya namaḥ saṃvinmayāya te*

## v.2 — the world as fuel

To the great fire fed by the universe as fuel, smeared-pure with great ash, to the Becoming to whom the world is the sole oblation — bow. The Sanskrit: *viśvendhanamahākṣārānulepaśucivarcase | mahānalāya bhavate viśvaikahaviṣe namaḥ*

## v.3 — cool fire, unequalled devourer

To Śiva-fire — cool, dense with supreme nectar — and to You, the unequalled devourer in the world's destruction — bow. The Sanskrit: *paramāmṛtasāndrāya śītalāya śivāgnaye | kasmai cid viśvasaṃploṣaviṣamāya namo 'stu te*

## v.4 — the named and the someone

To Mahādeva, to Rudra, to Śaṅkara, to Śiva, to You Maheśvara — and to someone mantra-bodied — bow. The Sanskrit: *mahādevāya rudrāya śaṅkarāya śivāya te | maheśvarāyāpi namaḥ kasmai cin mantramūrtaye*

## v.5 — cut, dried, auspicious

To Śiva-fire — cut, dried up, the three worlds' melted fat the oblation — unequalled, yet auspicious — bow. The Sanskrit: *namo nikṛttaniḥśoṣatrailokyavigaladvasā- | vasekaviṣamāyāpi maṅgalāya śivāgnaye*

## v.6 — marked by marklessness

To the god — to gracious someone — for whom the meeting of all marks is itself the mark — bow. The Sanskrit: *samastalakṣanāyoga eva yasyopalakṣaṇam | tasmai namo 'stu devāya kasmai cid api śambhave*

## v.7 — against the Veda, making the Veda

To the hidden Lord — Veda-opposing, Veda-making, the Veda's true essence, secret master — bow. The Sanskrit: *vedāgamaviruddhāya vedāgamavidhāyine | vedāgamasatattvāya guhyāya svāmine namaḥ*

## v.8 — cause and foe of the wheel

Sole cause of the wheel of births and deaths — and its sole opponent; saṃsāra-formed, saṃsāra-free — to gracious Śambhu, bow. The Sanskrit: *saṃsāraikanimittāya saṃsāraikavirodhine | namaḥ samsārarūpāya niḥsaṃsārāya śambhave*

## v.9 — root, middle, tip, full

To root, to middle, to tip; embodied as root-middle-tip; with tip-middle-root exhausted — to the Full, gracious Śambhu — bow. The Sanskrit: *mūlāya madhyāyāgrāya mūlamadhyāgramūrtaye | kṣīnāgramadhyamūlāya namaḥ pūrṇāya śambhave*

## v.10 — the economics of the Name

Even once — whosever taking of the Name — ripened heaps of merit. To You, Śiva, hard to attain — bow. The Sanskrit: *namaḥ sukṛtasaṃbharavipākaḥ sakṛd apy asau | yasya nāmagrahas tasmai durlabhāya śivāya te*

## v.11 — play amid multitudes

To You alone — consciousness-formed, skull-bearing — ever sporting with heaped multitudes of moving-and-static forms — bow. The Sanskrit: *namaścarācarākāraparetanicayaiḥ sadā | krīḍate tubhyam ekasmai cinmayāya kapāline*

## v.12 — magician and pure

To the magician, the pure, the hidden, the self-manifest; the subtle, the all-formed — to wondrous Śambhu — bow. The Sanskrit: *māyāvine viśuddhāya guhyāya prakaṭātmane | sūkṣmāya viśvarūpāya namaś citrāya śambhave*

## v.13 — destroying play, all power

To all power — whose play destroys the Brahmā-Viṣṇu-arrayed world — wonder-working — bow. The Sanskrit: *brahmendraviṣṇunirvyūḍhajagatsaṃhārakelaye | āścaryakaraṇīyāya namas te sarvaśaktaye*

## v.14 — the ocean's shores

To You, the fathomless Hara-ocean — whose powers are gained by those wandering only its shores — bow. The Sanskrit: *taṭeṣv eva paribhrāntaiḥ labdhās tās tā vibhūtayaḥ | yasya tasmai namas tubhyam agādhaharasindhave*

## v.15 — lotus in mud

To the lovely hundred-petalled Śambhu-lotus, dwelling mid mud of the Māyā-made dense world, unsmeared, shining — bow. The Sanskrit: *māyāmayajagatsāndrapaṅkamadhyādhivāsine | alepāya namaḥ śambhuśatapatrāya śobhine*

## v.16 — pure auspiciousness

Auspicious, pure, treasure, self-ornamented; dear, ultimate-real, highest — to You — bow. The Sanskrit: *maṅgalāya pavitrāya nidhaye bhūṣaṇātmane | priyāya paramārthāya sarvotkṛṣṭāya te namaḥ*

## v.17 — ever bound, ever free

Ever bound, ever sharing liberation's portion; difference-free — to gracious someone Śambhu — bow. The Sanskrit: *namaḥ satatabaddhāya nityanirmuktibhāgine | bandhamokṣavihīnāya kasmai ci d api śambhave*

## v.18 — the mockery-world's one seat

In these three worlds whose essence is mockery alone — to You alone, secondless, ever-bliss-seated — bow. The Sanskrit: *upahāsaikasāre 'sminn etāvati jagattraye | tubhyam evādvitīyāya namo nityasukhāsine*

## v.19 — all rites, no rite

Essence of right-observance yet desirer of left-observance; of all observances, to observance-free Śarva — bow. The Sanskrit: *dakṣiṇācārasārāya vāmācārābhilāṣiṇe | sarvācārāya śarvāya nirācārāya te namaḥ*

## v.20 — whichever god

Whoever is worshipped, however, wherever — and whoever, whatever god he be — to that god, bow. The Sanskrit: *yathā tathāpi yaḥ pūjyo yatra tatrāpi yo 'rcitaḥ | yo 'pi vā so 'pi vā yo 'sau devas tasmai namo 'stu te*

## v.21 — cooler of burning

Served by liberation-seekers, remover of all burning; of spread-out chosen loveliness — to You, boon-giver — bow. The Sanskrit: *mumukṣujanasevyāya sarvasantāpahāriṇe | namo vitatalāvaṇyavarāya varadāya te*

## v.22 — the ever-festival

To You, Lord of the ever-festival, whose three worlds brim with unbroken bliss-rasa — bow. The Sanskrit: *sadā nirantarānandarasanirbharitākhila- | trilokāya namas tubhyaṃ svāmine nityaparvaṇe*

## v.23 — to the terrible powers

Whatever enjoys You through pleasure-first knowables — to that terrible host of Your powers — bow. The Sanskrit: *sukhapradhānasaṃvedyasambhogair bhajate ca yat | tvām eva tasmai ghorāya śaktivṛndāya te namaḥ*

## v.24 — embracers find

Even sages cannot know Him — yet those whose gestures love connects embrace Him. To that someone Becoming — bow. The Sanskrit: *munīnām apy avijñeyaṃ bhaktisambandhaceṣṭitāḥ | āliṅganty api yaṃ tasmai kasmai cid bhavate namaḥ*

## v.25 — nectar sheath and heap

To the supreme-nectar sheath, the supreme-nectar heap; reachable beyond far and near — to Becoming — bow. The Sanskrit: *paramāmṛtakośāya paramāmṛtarāśaye | sarvapāramyapāramyaprāpyāya bhavate namaḥ*

## v.26 — mantra-formed praise

I praise Your mantra-formed, clear-cool, unprecedentedly joyful, supreme-nectar-juicy form. The Sanskrit: *mahāmantramayaṃ naumi rūpaṃ te svacchaśītalam | apūrvamodasubhagaṃ parāmṛtarasolvaṇam*

## v.27 — the non-wonder teaching

On oneness-fame's great cloth, full of immortality-nectar — where, Lord, there is no wonder — I praise Your teaching. The Sanskrit: *svātantryāmṛtapūrṇatvad aikyakhyātimahāpaṭe | citraṃ nāsty eva yatreśa tan naumi tava śāsanam*

## v.28 — the thunderbolt path

Thunderbolt to all doubt, world-end fire to all misfortune, world's-end to all joylessness — we bow the Māheśvara path. The Sanskrit: *sarvāśaṅkāsaniṃ sarvālakṣmīkālānalaṃ tathā | sarvāmaṅgalyakalpāntaṃ mārgaṃ māheśvaraṃ numaḥ*

## v.29 — victory and refuge

Victory, god! Bow upon bow to You; this whole world rests on You; of worlds You are supreme Lord; I am come to You for refuge, the One supreme. The Sanskrit: *jaya deva namo namo 'stu te sakalaṃ viśvam idaṃ tavāśritam | jagatāṃ parameśvaro bhavān paramekaḥ śaraṇāgato 'smi te*

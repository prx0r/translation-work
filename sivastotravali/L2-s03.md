## v.1 — the third, leapt to

Of things, the two courses that go by being and non-being — leaping over them — to the third, the wondrous Śambhu — bow. The Sanskrit: *sadasattvena bhāvānāṃ yuktā yā dvitayī gatiḥ | tām ullaṅghya tṛtīyasmai namaś citrāya śambhave*

## v.2 — free among the unfree

Among gods, seers, and men in this unfree triple world — free are Your dependents, O Free One. The Sanskrit: *āsurarṣijanād asminn asvatantre jagattraye | svatantrās te svatantrasya ye tavaivānujīvinaḥ*

## v.3 — remembrance-medicine

Remembrance of Your body inlaid with the whole universe — the one medicine for world-pain; who have it sit happy. The Sanskrit: *aśeṣaviśvakhacitabhavadvapur anusmṛtiḥ | yeṣām bhavarujām ekaṃ bheṣajaṃ te sukhāsinaḥ*

## v.4 — moon-umbrella king

Whose white umbrella is the self-lit moon, whose fly-whisk the heaven-river's stream — He alone is Supreme Lord. The Sanskrit: *sitātapatraṃ yasyenduḥ svaprabhāparipūritaḥ | cāmaraṃ svardhunīsrotaḥ sa ekaḥ parameśvaraḥ*

## v.5 — beg the glance

Grant me, Lord, a glance — luminous, cool, one, pure as a moon-digit, carrying nectar. The Sanskrit: *prakāśāṃ śītalām ekāṃ śuddhāṃ śaśikalām iva | dṛśaṃ vitara me nātha kām apy amṛtavāhinīm*

## v.6 — ocean-drops, why not sweet

Fallen from Your consciousness-bliss ocean — these knowings, these drops — why are they not nectar-sweet-beautiful to me, Lord? The Sanskrit: *tvaccidānandajaladheś cyutāḥ saṃvittivipruṣaḥ | imāḥ kathaṃ me bhagavan nāmṛtāsvādasundarāḥ*

## v.7 — unmelted hearts

In Your love-rasa, Lord, hearts unmelted — the heartless alone — such deserve contempt. The Sanskrit: *tvayi rāgarase nātha na magnaṃ hṛdayaṃ prabho | yeṣām ahṛdayā eva te 'vajñaspadam īdṛśāḥ*

## v.8 — the vessel of powers

Whose heart has met the Lord's — he alone is the vessel of ruling goddesses' powers. The Sanskrit: *prabhuṇā bhavatā yasya jātaṃ hṛdayamelanam | prābhavīṇāṃ vibhūtīnāṃ paramekaḥ sa bhājanam*

## v.9 — the flood

Flooder alike of all joys and sorrows — Your meditation-nectar-flood, like lowlands filling. The Sanskrit: *harṣāṇām atha śokānāṃ sarveṣāṃ plāvakaḥ samam | bhavaddhyānāmṛtāpūro nimnāṇimnabhuvām iva*

## v.10 — never parted

What would their look not be, happiness-provisions-full — who never part from You, more than self, Lord? The Sanskrit: *keva na syād dṛśā teṣāṃ sukhasambhāranirbharā | yeṣām ātmādhikeneśa na kvāpi virahas tvayā*

## v.11 — roar and dance

I roar, I dance — my wishes are fulfilled, for my Lord is won, utterly charming. The Sanskrit: *garjāmi bata nṛtyāmi pūrṇā mama manorathāḥ | svāmī mamaiṣa ghaṭito yat tvam atyantarocanaḥ*

## v.12 — nowhere else to go

Where no other knowable for action, no other yoga for knowing — yet what if knowledge? Consciousness alone, world-filled-one, expands. The Sanskrit: *nānyad vedyaṃ kriyā yatra nānyo yogo vidā ca yat | jñānaṃ syāt kiṃ tu viśvaikapūrṇā cittvaṃ vijṛmbhate*

## v.13 — Śiva-sound chases sorrows

Suddenly fled from them — hard, endless sorrows — whose speech forever holds the Śiva-sound. The Sanskrit: *durjayānām anantānām duḥkhānāṃ sahasaiva te | hastāt palāyitā yeṣāṃ vāci śaśvacchivadhvaniḥ*

## v.14 — no second great person

Is there another, higher person, distinct by remaining Yours? You alone, great Person, support of all persons without remainder. The Sanskrit: *uttamaḥ puruṣo 'nyo 'sti yuṣmaccheṣaviśeṣitaḥ | tvaṃ mahāpuruṣas tv eko niḥśeṣapuruṣāśrayaḥ*

## v.15 — servants win

Victorious, world-venerable, Your slaves, Lord — for whom this saṃsāra-ocean is a play-great-lake. The Sanskrit: *jayanti te jagadvandyā dāsās te jagatāṃ vibho | saṃsārārṇava evaiṣa yeṣāṃ krīḍāmahāsaraḥ*

## v.16 — miseries shamed

Let other miseries sit — for Your devotees, "You manifest Yourself" — by this alone they are shamed. The Sanskrit: *āsatāṃ tāvad anyāni dainyānīha bhavajjuṣām | tvam eva prakaṭībhūyā ity anenaiva lajjyate*

## v.17 — reciter by oneness

"Nothing is beyond me" — yet therein I am a reciter, by that oneness; as truth, You point the rosary somewhere. The Sanskrit: *matparaṃ nāsti tatrāpi jāpako 'smi tadaikyataḥ | tattvena japa ity akṣamālayā diśasi kvacit*

## v.18 — being beyond being

From being must come supreme being, and beyond that, Lord — and You are other than being and non-being both; hence You are being-non-being-formed. The Sanskrit: *sato 'vaśyaṃ paramasat sac ca tasmāt paraṃ prabho | tvaṃ cāsataḥ sataś cānyas tenāsi sadasanmayaḥ*

## v.19 — brighter than suns, unseen

Purer-shining than thousand-sun-ray-rise — yet You, all-worlds-pervading, are not seen. The Sanskrit: *sahasrasūryakiraṇādhikaśuddhaprakāśavān | api tvaṃ sarvabhuvanavyāpako 'pi na dṛśyase*

## v.20 — knower in the known

Conscious-formed in the inert world, knower even in the known; great in the measured — hence You are the highest. The Sanskrit: *jaḍe jagati cidrūpaḥ kila vedye 'pi vedakaḥ | vibhur mite ca yenāsi tena sarvottamo bhavān*

## v.21 — sharp crying, knowing bewilderment

Enough with other cries — this alone, before the Lord: I cry sharp, Lord, I am bewildered, even knowing thus. The Sanskrit: *alam ākranditair anyair iyad eva puraḥ prabhoḥ | tīvraṃ viraumi yan nātha muhyāmy evaṃ vidann api*

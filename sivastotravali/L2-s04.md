## v.1 — mind, though fickle

Mind — though fickle you are — even so you are praised, since you worship the refuge of refuges, the world's teacher, Ambikā's husband. The Sanskrit: *capalam asi yad api mānasa tatrāpi ślāghyase yato bhajase | śaraṇānām api śaraṇaṃ tribhuvanagurum ambikākāntam*

## v.2 — the lowest ground kept

Having crossed the ladder-steps of various gods, reached Śiva's feet — yet on the lowest ground I stand even now; wonder, I do not quit it. The Sanskrit: *ullaṅghya vividhadaivatasopānakramam upeya śivacaraṇān | āśrityāpy adharatarāṃ bhūmiṃ nādyāpi citram ujjhāmi*

## v.3 — reveal and hide, use now

Reveal Your own path, hide all world-ways thoroughly — while I am, Lord, quickly Your ever-risen servant. The Sanskrit: *prakaṭaya nijam adhvānaṃ sthagayatarām akhilalokacaritāni | yāvad bhavāmi bhagavaṃs tava sapadi sadodito dāsaḥ*

## v.4 — a spell of names

Śiva Śiva Śambhu Śaṅkara — swift pity on the refuge-seekers' lover! Fortune is near for whoever remembers Your lotus-feet-pair. The Sanskrit: *śiva śiva śambho śaṅkara śaraṇāgatavatsalāśu kuru karuṇām | tava caraṇakamalayugalasmaraṇaparasya hi sampado 'dūre*

## v.5 — laughing at Brahmā

Those seated on Your foot-lotus-throne, making the world to taste — they laugh at Vir iñc, office-stained, not-self-ruled. The Sanskrit: *tāvakāṅghrikamalāsanalīnā ye yathāruci jagad racayanti | te viriñcim adhikāramalenāliptam asvavaśam īśa hasanti*

## v.6 — already gained, still far

Nothing appears apart from Your light-body; so You are always already gained, Lord — though far by nature. The Sanskrit: *tvatprakāśavapuṣo na vibhinnaṃ kiṃ cana prabhavati pratibhātum | tat sadaiva bhagavan parilabdho 'sīśvara prakṛtito 'pi vidūraḥ*

## v.7 — two tastes

Some taste Your foot-lotus-juice as stale-difference-conduct; others instantly taste it as flawless, body-whole, duality-free. The Sanskrit: *pādapaṅkajarasaṃ tava ke cid bhedaparyuṣitavṛttim upetāḥ | ke canāpi rasayanti tu sadhyo bhātam akṣatavapur dvayaśūnyam*

## v.8 — if the flash stayed

Lord, like lightning at dawn gleams my nectar-smeared — if it stayed steadier, You are worshipped duly — what else? The Sanskrit: *nātha vidyud iva bhāti vibhāte yā kadā cana mamāmṛtadigdhā | sā yadi sthirataraiva bhavet tat pūjito 'si vidhivat kim utānyat*

## v.9 — appear as You are

Is there anything, thing or non-thing, apart from this? With great discernment decided here: as You are, so be plain to me. The Sanskrit: *sarvam asyaparam asti na kiṃ cid vastv avastu yadi veti mahatyā | prajñāya vyavasito 'tra yathaiva tvaṃ tathaiva bhava suprakaṭo me*

## v.10 — placed unasked

By Your will alone, Lord, I was made to step on Your own path — then why do I behave just like a man, knowing nothing fit for Your feet? The Sanskrit: *svecchayaiva bhagavan nijamārge kāritaḥ padam ahaṃ prabhunaiva | tat kathaṃ janavad eva carāmi tvatpadocitam avaimi na kiṃ cit*

## v.11 — some god blooms

Some god of Yours blooms in their hearts, a lovely supreme state — by whose story-thunder-cātakas they are long gladdened. The Sanskrit: *ko 'pi deva hṛdi teṣu tāvako jṛmbhate subhagabhāva uttamaḥ | tvatkathāmbudaninādacātakā yena te 'pi subhagīkṛtāś ciram*

## v.12 — separation-talk fruits

For Your devotees, by some play in You, this love has grown fat — so that separation-ground talk and memory fruit in union-festival. The Sanskrit: *tvajjuṣāṃ tvayi kayāpi līlayā rāga eṣa paripoṣam āgataḥ | yad viyogabhuvi saṅkathā tathā saṃsmṛtiḥ phalati saṃgamotsavam*

## v.13 — the word enters sideways

The word, grown by strange-rasa-sprinkling, though uttered "Śaṅkara" hundreds of times — enters even sideways hearts, ever-fresh in purpose. The Sanskrit: *yo vicitrarasasekavardhitaḥ śaṅkareti śataśo 'py udīritaḥ | śabda āviśati tiryagāśayeṣv apy ayaṃ navanavaprayojanaḥ*

## v.14 — faces win

Victorious are they on whose face-orbits the Śiva-sound ever revolves — which, moon-spread from nectar-source, trickles supreme nectar, sweet. The Sanskrit: *te jayanti mukhamaṇḍale bhraman asti yeṣu niyataṃ śivadhvaniḥ | yaḥ śāśīva prasṛto 'mṛtāśayāt svādu saṃsravati cāmṛtaṃ param*

## v.15 — finished world, unknocked door

The fierce world as if finished; mind's impurity thinned to none — and yet not even the smallest knocking at Your city-door-bolt. The Sanskrit: *parisamāptam ivogram idaṃ jagad vigalito 'viralo manaso malaḥ | tad api nāsti bhavatpurārgalakavāṭavighaṭṭanam aṇva pi*

## v.16 — just a little

Of minds eager for Your ever-blooming face-lotus-belly-gaze — do something, Lord, just a little, so You flash as facing me. The Sanskrit: *satataphullabhavanmukhapaṅkajodaravilokanalālasacetasaḥ | kim api tat kuru nātha manāg iva sphurasi yena mamābhimukhasthitiḥ*

## v.17 — knowing, asking

What happiness here apart from non-difference-mind, what other glory? So why does this servant's mind, abandoning her, go the wrong way? The Sanskrit: *tvadavibhedamater aparaṃ nu kiṃ sukham ihāsti vibhūtir athāparā | tad iha tāvakadāsajanasya kiṃ kupatham eti manaḥ parihṛtya tām*

## v.18 — tasting-frequency

May I never for a moment be unfit for Your serfdom — unless constantly, reverently tasting non-difference-wine. The Sanskrit: *kṣaṇam apīha na tāvakadāsatāṃ prati bhaveyam ahaṃ kila bhājanam | bhavadabhedarasāsavam ādarād avirataṃ rasayeyam ahaṃ na cet*

## v.19 — MY cry heard

These people truly do not see Your body, dirtied by duality-sight — and yet, all-knowing refuge-lover, do You not hear MY cry? The Sanskrit: *na kila paśyati satyam ayaṃ janas tava vapur dvayadṛṣṭimalīmasaḥ | tad api sarvavidāśritavatsalaḥ kim idam āraṭitaṃ na śṛṇoṣi me*

## v.20 — vision instead of pleasures

Do You remember, Lord, any desired sense-pleasure I ever asked? Give instead, always, Your body-vision-nectar as the only wish. The Sanskrit: *smarasi nātha kadācid apīhitaṃ viṣayasaukhyam athāpi mayārthitam | satatam eva bhavadvapurīkṣaṇāmṛtamabhīṣṭamalaṃ mama dehi tat*

## v.21 — hundreds of omens

Since by Your will alone I stepped on Your Śiva-path, Lord — hundreds of good omens rose for me; what else should I seek from You? The Sanskrit: *kila yadaiva śivādhvani tāvake kṛtapado 'smi maheśa tavecchayā | śubhaśatānyuditāni tadaiva me kim aparaṃ mṛgaye bhavataḥ prabho*

## v.22 — the night that shines

Where the sun sets with the moon and all — that unconquered Śiva-night shines, self-light-spread-lustrous. The Sanskrit: *yatra so 'stam ayam eti vivasvāṃś candramaḥ prabhṛtibhiḥ saha sarvaiḥ | kāpi sā vijayate śivarātriḥ svaprabhāprasarabhāsvararūpā*

## v.23 — empire vs foot-touch

Even won rule in three worlds, O king of gods, I count tasteless — lacking Your foot-touch-nectar-rasa. The Sanskrit: *apy upārjitam ahaṃ triṣu lokeṣv adhipatyam amareśvara manye | nīrasaṃ tad akhilaṃ bhavadaṅghrisparśanāmṛtarasena vihīnam*

## v.24 — the knot He tied

Alas, Lord, strong is this self-bond, made of Your un-fame, fashioned by You alone — first ignored by me, it never loosens a bit. The Sanskrit: *bata nātha dṛḍho 'yam ātmabandho bhavadakhyātimayas tvayaiva kḷptaḥ | yad ayaṃ prathamānam eva me tvām avadhīrya ślathate na leśato 'pi*

## v.25 — the worshipper worshipped

Though worshipped by the great, O king of gods, You ever stay the one-worshipper-form — seen outside and in, You shine in the seers' bodies forever. The Sanskrit: *mahatām amareśa pūjyamāno 'py aniśaṃ tiṣṭhasi pūjakaikarūpaḥ | bahirantarapīha dṛśyamānaḥ sphurasi draṣṭṛśarīra eva śaśvat*

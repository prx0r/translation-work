# Śivastotrāvalī Stotra 5 — L2 READ (svabalanideśana, Instruction of One's Own Strength)

## v.1 — bring me by the neck

Joined in enjoyment by mere touch-contact of Your lotus-feet — bring me, Lord, by the neck into Your house. The Sanskrit: *tvatpādapadmasamparkamātrasambhogasaṅginam | galepādikayā nātha māṃ svaveśma praveśaya*

## v.2 — when shall I dance

Head hair dyed by Your foot-lotus-dust-redness — when shall I frantically begin to dance? The Sanskrit: *bhavatpādāmbujarajorājirañjitamūrdhajaḥ | apārarabhasārabdhanartanaḥ syām ahaṃ kadā*

## v.3 — silent, not wise

You my sole Lord — this alone I always ask: dwelling inside You, may I be silent; otherwise, wise. The Sanskrit: *tvad ekanātho bhagavann iyad evārthaye sadā | tvadantarvasatir mūko bhaveyaṃ mānyathā budhaḥ*

## v.4 — ah, ah, ah

Ah nectar-ocean master! Ah sweet three-eyed One! Ah tasty deformed-eyed One! — thus may I dance, shouting. The Sanskrit: *aho sudhānidhe svāminn aho mṛṣṭa trilocana | aho svādo virūpakṣety eva nṛtyeyam āraṭan*

## v.5 — eyes half-closed

Eyes half-closed by Your foot-lotus-touch — may I expand, reeling drunk on Your devotion-liquor-madness. The Sanskrit: *tvatpādapadmasaṃsparśaparimīlitalocanaḥ | vijṛmbheyabhavadbhaktimadirāmadaghūrṇitaḥ*

## v.6 — dwelling defined by practice

May I dwell anywhere, O All-pervading, mind-mountain on earth, where that practice is — uninterrupted, great-rasa, made of Your prattle. The Sanskrit: *cittabhūbhṛd bhuvi vibho vaseyaṃ kvāpi yatra sā | nirantaratvatpralāpamayī vṛttir mahārasā*

## v.7 — that city

Where You stand with the Goddess, many-formed, from palace to gate-tower — in that city may I dwell. The Sanskrit: *yatra devīsametas tvam āsaudhādā ca gopurāt | bahurūpaḥ sthitas tasmin vāstavyaḥ syām ahaṃ pure*

## v.8 — until the lotus blooms

May Your sun-rays rejoice, Lord, until my heart-lotus blooms — for Your worship. The Sanskrit: *samullasantu bhagavan bhavadbhānumarīcayaḥ | vikasatveṣa yāvan me hṛtpadmaḥ pūjanāya te*

## v.9 — melt like wax

Favor me, Lord, so that my mind, fallen ever at Your feet, tasting this and that — melts like wax, flows like... The Sanskrit: *prasīda bhagavan yena tvatpade patitaṃ sadā | mano me tattadāsvādya kṣīved iva galed iva*

## v.10 — appear regardless

Whether from joy bursting pot-lids, or sorrow — from outside or inside feeling, appear to me, Lord. The Sanskrit: *praharṣād vātha śokād vā yadi kuṅyād dhaṭād api | bāhyād athāntarād bhāvāt prakaṭībhava me prabho*

## v.11 — streaming always

Outside too, inside too — may that ever-streaming, Your coolest foot-lotus-touch-nectar, be mine always. The Sanskrit: *bahir apy antar api tat syandamānaṃ sadāstu me | bhavatpādāmbujasparśāmṛtam atyantaśītalam*

## v.12 — the pleasure beyond pleasures

Plunging inside Your touch-nectar-lake — may that pleasure beyond all pleasures be mine always. The Sanskrit: *tvatpādasaṃsparśasudhāsaraso 'ntarnimajjanam | ko 'py eṣa sarvasambhogalaṅghī bhogo 'stu me sadā*

## v.13 — cook my passions

Take what I offer — passion and the rest, Lord; make it nectar and eat it with devotees. The Sanskrit: *niveditam upādatsva rāgādi bhagavan mayā | ādāya cāmṛtīkṛtya bhuṅkṣva bhaktajanaiḥ samam*

## v.14 — one glance at servants

Ever-full on whole-world-food, happily seated — Master, cast Your grace-glance-moment on servants. The Sanskrit: *aśeṣabhuvanāhāranityatṛptaḥ sukhāsanam | svāmin gṛhāṇa dāseṣu prasādālokanakṣaṇam*

## v.15 — bow to me, to Śiva

Eyes half-closed, chewing inner devotion-wonder — "bow to me, to Śiva" — may I worship even straw. The Sanskrit: *antarbhakticamatkāracarvaṇāmīlitekṣaṇaḥ | namo mahyaṃ śivāyeti pūjayam syāṃ tṛṇāny api*

## v.16 — gaining without leaving

Though I have gained Your being — seeing the world as my self-blaze — may I be unparted by devotion-rasa-enjoyments. The Sanskrit: *api labdhabhavadbhāvaḥ svātmollāsamayaṃ jagat | paśyan bhaktirasābhogair bhaveyam aviyojitaḥ*

## v.17 — nothing else to want

Since nothing else is to be desired, Lord — to You, the secondless, fullness is fitting. The Sanskrit: *ākāmkṣaṇīyam aparaṃ yena nātha na vidyate | tava tenādvitīyasya yuktaṃ yat paripūrṇatā*

## v.18 — laughter, dance, eating, drinking

Where there is laughter, dancing, where passion-aversion are eaten, devotion-nectar drunk — may I reach that place. The Sanskrit: *hasyate nṛtyate yatra rāgadveṣādi bhujyate | pīyate bhaktipīyūṣarasas tat prāpnuyāṃ padam*

## v.19 — perfume against stink

May that unprecedented-joy-perfume of thought-flowers about You firm up in my mind — till bad-smell of bad-habit perishes. The Sanskrit: *tat tad apūrvāmodatvaccintākusumavāsanā dṛḍhatām | etu mama manasi yāvann aśyatu durvāsanāgandhaḥ*

## v.20 — teach my contradicting heart

Where is love among passions, and where is loving at Hari's lotus-feet — thus teach my contradiction-loving heart what is good, O Deathless. The Sanskrit: *kva nu rāgādiṣu rāgaḥ kva ca haracaraṇāmbujeṣu rāgitvam | itthaṃ virodharasikaṃ bodhaya hitam amara me hṛdayam*

## v.21 — melt me, not my states

Though wandering even through yogic states, though turned away from objects — may I be only heart-melted by Your-thought-liquor-madness. The Sanskrit: *vicaran yogadaśāsv api viṣayavyāvṛttivartamāno 'pi | tvaccintāmadirāmadataralīkṛtahṛdaya eva syām*

## v.22 — devotion as vanguard

In speech, mind-thoughts, body-gestures made by senses — everywhere, always, may bhakti-rasa go before me as leader. The Sanskrit: *vāci manomatiṣu tathā śarīraceṣṭāsu karaṇaracitāsu | sarvatra sarvadā me puraḥsaro bhavatu bhaktirasaḥ*

## v.23 — Śiva Śiva Śiva, unrepeatable taste

While Your name "Śiva Śiva Śiva" is chanted endlessly, Lord — may I taste some great unrepeatable rasa. The Sanskrit: *śivaśivaśiveti nāmani tava niravadhi nātha japyamāne 'smin | āsvādayan bhaveyaṃ kam api mahārasam apunaruktam*

## v.24 — roaming, not arriving

In the vastness flashing as endless consciousness-self, where all dull paths are drunk up — may I roam Your worship, countless consciousness-made ramparts uncounted. The Sanskrit: *sphuradanantacidātmakaviṣṭape parinipītasamastajaḍādhvani | agaṇitāparacinmayagaṇḍike pravicareyam ahaṃ bhavato 'rcitā*

## v.25 — self-sufficient, except foot-dust

For standing in self-body, clearly shining, eternal — nothing else is of use; firm be this thought — but dust of Your pure feet, Lord. The Sanskrit: *svavapuṣi sphuṭabhāsini śāśvate sthitikṛte na kim apy upayujyate | iti matiḥ sudṛḍhā bhavatāt paraṃ mama bhavaccaraṇābjarajaḥ śuceḥ*

## v.26 — world melted in nectar-lake

Something flashes sometime in mind, touching Your feet — where this whole world melts — show it always, in the nectar-lake, Lord. The Sanskrit: *kim api nātha kadācana cetasi sphurati tad bhavadaṃghritalaspṛśām | galati yatra samastamidaṃ sudhāsarasi viśvam idaṃ diśa me sadā*

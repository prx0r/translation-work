## v.1 — burning to be seen

Even a moment parted from You, Lord — of me burning dense always — may I be the ground of Your gaze. The Sanskrit: *kṣaṇamātram apīśāna viyuktasya tvayā mama | nibiḍaṃ tapyamānasya sadā bhūyā dṛśaḥ padam*

## v.2 — parted from the world, unparted from Him

In saṃsāra whose essence is parting — by You, dear Lord, may I be ever unparted, though parted from the world. The Sanskrit: *viyogasāre saṃsāre priyeṇa prabhuṇā tvayā | aviyuktaḥ sadaiva syāṃ jagatāpi viyojitaḥ*

## v.3 — all my goings are You

Wherever I go by body, speech, mind — all that is You alone: may this ultimate truth stand ever full for me. The Sanskrit: *kāyavāṅmanasair yatra yāmi sarvaṃ tvam eva tat | ity eṣa paramārtho 'pi paripūrṇo 'stu me sadā*

## v.4 — speech matching bliss

As You are construction-free, great-bliss-full — so may my praise-speech become, just fitting. The Sanskrit: *nirvikalpo mahānandapūrṇo yadvad bhavāṃs tathā | bhavatstutikarī bhūyād anurūpaiva vāṅ mama*

## v.5 — roaming in His possession

Possessed by You, seeing thing after thing as consisting of You — may I roam desireless, joy-filled. The Sanskrit: *bhavadāveśataḥ paśyan bhāvaṃ bhāvaṃ bhavanmayam | vicareyaṃ nirākāṅkṣaḥ praharṣaparipūritaḥ*

## v.6 — content, so stop toiling

May I see the whole world full of Your being, Lord — by that alone content; then do not toil. The Sanskrit: *bhagavanbhavataḥ pūrṇam paśyeyam akhilaṃ jagat | tāvataivāsmi santuṣṭas tato na parikhidyase*

## v.7 — melting like cloud-bits

May things shine for me always, melting into You alone like cloud-bits in sky, moving toward ever-purer courses. The Sanskrit: *vilīyamānās tvayy eva vyomni meghalavā iva | bhāvā vibhāntu me śaśvat kramanairmalyagāminaḥ*

## v.8 — the jewel from His middle

May there blaze for me, from Your middle, some jewel of Being — its self-light-spread destroying the endless dark-series. The Sanskrit: *svaprabhāprasaradhvastāparyantadhvāntasantatiḥ | santataṃ bhātu me ko 'pi bhavamadhyād bhavanmaṇiḥ*

## v.9 — everywhere by effortlessness

What stage do You not rule, what could there be that is not Your body? Tired — by effortlessness may I reach You everywhere. The Sanskrit: *kāṃ bhūmikāṃ nādhiśeṣe kiṃ tat syād yan na te vapuḥ | śrāntas tenāprayāsena sarvatas tvām avāpnuyām*

## v.10 — embrace by mere will

May limb-embrace-enjoyment with You happen to me by mere will — this much gained, Lord, what have I not won? The Sanskrit: *bhavadaṅgapariṣvaṅgasambhogaḥ svecchayaiva me | ghaṭatām iyati prāpte kiṃ nātha na jitaṃ mayā*

## v.11 — appear; nothing else asked

Appear! We, Thine, tormented by no other prayers — fainting, we seek only You. The Sanskrit: *prakaṭībhava nānyābhiḥ prārthanābhiḥ kadarthanāḥ | kurmas te nātha tāmyantas tvām eva mṛgayāmahe*

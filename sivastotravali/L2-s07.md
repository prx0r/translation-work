## v.1 — drop this disaster

Having come to even-taste in You, bliss-river — may my mind drop at once this difference-based great disaster. The Sanskrit: *tvayy ānandasarasvati samarasatām etya nātha mama cetaḥ pariharatu sakṛd iyantaṃ bhedādhīnaṃ mahānartham*

## v.2 — the axe on mine-and-not-mine

"This is mine, not this" — on passion-aversion's firm-nailed root may the axe fall inside: the certainty of oneness-with-You-made. The Sanskrit: *etan mama na tv idam iti rāgadveṣādinigaḍadṛḍhamūle nātha bhavanmayataikyapratyayaparaśuḥ patatvantaḥ*

## v.3 — melt, then blaze

May the thought-stain-line melt; may unbarredness blaze in heart; bliss-rasa-flooded be my consciousness-body, Lord. The Sanskrit: *galatu vikalpakalaṅkāvalī samullasatu hṛdi nirargalatā bhagavann ānandarasaplutāstu me cinmayī mūrtiḥ*

## v.4 — fed till wings grow

Me, rolled in the passion-made world-egg — may devotion-thought-mother feed me with rasas, till, wing-grown, I am a bird. The Sanskrit: *rāgādimayabhavāṇḍakaluṭhitaṃ tvadbhaktibhāvanāmbikā tais taiḥ āpyāyayatu rasair māṃ pravṛddhapakṣo yathā bhavāmi khagaḥ*

## v.5 — skill in tasting

May this mind of mine gain skill in tasting the essence of Your foot-thought-nectar-rasa — ending all object-poison-clinging-longing. The Sanskrit: *tvaccaraṇabhāvanāmṛtarasasārāsvādanaipuṇaṃ labhatām cittam idaṃ niḥśeṣitaviṣayaviṣāsaṅgavāsanāvadhi me*

## v.6 — sun melting wax

By devotion-sun-ray-touch, may this mind-jewel release far away passion-kindled fire-drops. The Sanskrit: *tvadbhaktitapanadīdhitisaṃsparśavaśān mamaiṣa dūrataram cetomaṇir vimūñcaturāgādikataptavahnikaṇān*

## v.7 — praise where gods wait outside

In that state may I praise You constantly, aloud — where even Hari, Hara's horse (?), Brahmā wait outside. The Sanskrit: *tasmin pade bhavantaṃ satatam upaślokayeyam atyuccaiḥ hariharyaśvaviriñcā api yatra bahiḥ pratīkṣante*

## v.8 — frenzy as lens

By devotion-madness-born frenzy may I see with faculties unmaimed: the whole world as Śiva, and all acts as worship-stuff. The Sanskrit: *bhaktimadajanitavibhramavaśena paśyeyam avikalaṃ karaṇaiḥ śivamayam akhilaṃ lokaṃ kriyāś ca pūjāmayī sakalāḥ*

## v.9 — the remarried mine-thought

My mind-boy, having taken Your-devotion-family-maiden, having borne aṇimā-etc. sons — may she, firm-rooted, harden the mine-thought. The Sanskrit: *māmakamanogṛhītatvadbhaktikulāṅganāṇimādisutān sūtvā subaddhamūlā mameti buddhiṃ dṛḍhīkurutām*

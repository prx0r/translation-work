## v.1 — when will the pair root

The grace-drop stationed in the Lord, and devotion come as if to meet me — when will the pair, joined, take root in such a body? The Sanskrit: *yaḥ prasādalava īśvarasthito yā ca bhaktir iva mām upeyuṣī tau parasparasamanvitau kadā tādṛśe vapuṣi rūḍhim eṣyataḥ*

## v.2 — chewing His lordship

Born of chewing His lordship, may some inner joy-juice rise; here always be supreme for me — knowledge-yoga's glories far off. The Sanskrit: *tvatprabhutvaparicarvaṇajanmā ko 'py udetu paritoṣaraso 'ntaḥ sarvakālam iha me param astu jñānayogamahimādi vidūre*

## v.3 — worldly thirst, unworldly sight

Like the worldly, let my thirst for objects swell — only seeing them as Your body, unconstructed. The Sanskrit: *lokavadbhavatu me viṣayeṣu sphīta eva bhagavanparitarṣaḥ kevalaṃ tava śarīratayaitān+ lokayeyam aham astavikalpaḥ*

## v.4 — plain in both registers

In body-grounds and in mind, Lord, and in breath's course where difference has come — and in those consciousness-paths — thereby, by Your own self, be plain to me. The Sanskrit: *dehabhūmiṣu tathā manasi tvaṃ prāṇavartmani ca bhedam upete saṃvidaḥ pathiṣu teṣu ca tena svātmanā mama bhava sphuṭarūpaḥ*

## v.5 — faculties to their seats

May these thrilled faculties fall into their own seats — never, Lord, even slightly, the daring of non-difference-rupture. The Sanskrit: *nijanijeṣu padeṣu patantv imāḥ karaṇavṛttaya ullasitā mama kṣaṇamapīśa manāg api maiva bhūt tvadavibhedarasakṣatisāhasam*

## v.6 — exceeding all dealings

Light, soft, white-pure, cool — possessed by You, contemplating a body — may I exceed those dealings of the all-thing-road. The Sanskrit: *laghumasṛṇasitācchaśītalaṃ bhavadāveśavaśena bhāvayan vapur akhilapadārthapaddhater vyavahārān ativartayeya tān*

## v.7 — blooming, joining, untellable

May my self-body bloom as You; may worlds join my limbs; may all this duality-sport, even recalled, become untellable. The Sanskrit: *vikasatu svavapur bhavadātmakaṃ samupayāntu jaganti mamāṅgatām vrajatu sarvam idaṃ dvayavalgitaṃ smṛtipathopagame 'py anupākhyatām*

## v.8 — filling the dread gorge

May there rise, looking at such Yours, a flood of supreme nectar — so my filling of non-duality's dread gorge happens. The Sanskrit: *samudiyād api tādṛśatāvakānana viloka parāmṛtasamplavaḥ mama ghaṭeta yathā bhavadadvayāprathanaghoradarīparipūraṇam*

## v.9 — the wager

If ever by the thinnest union-drops — may I turn from all world-pleasures; or shall I be fallen from both? The Sanskrit: *api kadācana tāvakasaṅgamāmṛtakaṇācchuraṇena tanīyasā sakalalokasukheṣu parāṅmukho na bhavitāsmy ubhayacyuta eva kim*

## v.10 — the hamsa and the stalk

Ever at Your lotus-feet, of me the best hamsa moving in the lake — from above, from the root-depths, and from inside, may devotion's lotus-stalk bend up, unborn Lord. The Sanskrit: *satatam eva bhavaccaraṇāmbujākaracarasya hi haṃsavarasya me upari mūlatalād api cāntarād upanamatv aja bhaktimṛṇālikā*

## v.11 — scope, not seizure

May all things come under thought's scope and vision's footing for me; may vision-thought-light-nectar-essences blaze supreme. The Sanskrit: *upayāntu vibho samastavastūny api cintāviṣayaṃ dṛśaḥ padaṃ ca mama darśanacintanaprakāśāmṛtasārāṇi paraṃ parisphurantu*

## v.12 — practice for catastrophe

Lord, in crashing hardships, as names bend down, may I be — fear gone only while limb-joined, exceeding-joy-mad. The Sanskrit: *parameśvara teṣu teṣu kṛcchreṣv api nāmopanamatsv ahaṃ bhaveyam na paraṃ gatabhīs tvadaṅgasaṅgādupajātādhikasammado 'pi yāvat*

## v.13 — woven, shown, certain — shine

The universe woven in Your self is shown outside by You alone — what stands firm-certain: may it now shine plain. The Sanskrit: *bhavadātmani viśvam umbhitaṃ yad bhavataivāpi bahiḥ prakāśyate tat iti yad dṛḍhaniścayopajuṣṭaṃ tad idāniṃ sphuṭam eva bhāsatām*

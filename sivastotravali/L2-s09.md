## v.1 — when will the mind rush

When will my mind, eager to taste new-rasa-moist union, rush past all else to Your touch? The Sanskrit: *kadā navarasārdrārdrasambhogāsvādanotsukam pravarteta vihāyānyan mama tvatsparśane manaḥ*

## v.2 — worship-wealthy, may I see

You-loving, worship-wealthy — when shall I, the eager one, see You face to face? The Sanskrit: *tvadekaraktas tvatpādapūjāmātramahādhanaḥ kadā sākṣātkariṣyāmi bhavantam ayam utsukaḥ*

## v.3 — bursting all bolts

When, by thick love made needless-minded, shall I burst all great bolts with a crash and reach You? The Sanskrit: *gāḍhānurāgavaśato nirapekṣībhūtamānaso 'smi kadā paṭapaṭiti vighaṭitākhilamahārgalas tvām upaiṣyāmi*

## v.4 — subduing all gods

When, Lord, shall I subdue all gods — heart-seated in my own consciousness-essence — by devotion's power? The Sanskrit: *svasaṃvitsārahṛdayādhiṣṭhānāḥ sarvadevatāḥ kadā nātha vaśīkuryāṃ bhavadbhaktiprabhāvataḥ*

## v.5 — the festival where distinction is named

When will my vast devotion-bliss-rasa festival be — where even distinction gets a name from beholding-joy? The Sanskrit: *kadā me syād vibho bhūri bhaktyānandarasotsavaḥ yadālokasukhānandī pṛthaṅ nāmāpi lapsyate*

## v.6 — shaming Your people

Having suddenly recognized the fearless, generous, full, causeless, self-hidden Lord — when shall I shame Your people? The Sanskrit: *īśvaram abhayam udāraṃ pūrṇam akāraṇam apahnutātmānaṃ sahasābhijñāya kadā svāmijanaṃ lajjayiṣyāmi*

## v.7 — beloved-ness that forbids fleeing

When, Lord, shall I attain whatever beloved-ness of Yours — that by which it would not befit You to flee from me anywhere? The Sanskrit: *kadā kām api tāṃ nātha tava vallabhatām iyām yayā māṃ prati na kvāpi yuktaṃ te syāt palāyitum*

## v.8 — bathed by approving sight

When shall I be bathed by the approving sight of all beings whose selves are Your worship? The Sanskrit: *tattvato 'śeṣajantūnāṃ bhavatpūjāmayātmanām dṛṣṭyānumoditarasāplāvitaḥ syāṃ kadā vibho*

## v.9 — devotion crowns knowledge and yoga

Devotion, the highest ground of knowledge, the highest state of yoga — when will my begging for it be full? The Sanskrit: *jñānasya paramā bhūmir yogasya paramā daśā tvadbhaktir yā vibho karhi pūrṇa me syāt tadarthitā*

## v.10 — seizing and proclaiming

Seizing suddenly, holding hard, joy-overwhelmed — when shall I proclaim Your foot-treasure to all? The Sanskrit: *sahasaivasādya kadā gāḍham avaṣṭabhya harṣavivaśo 'ham tvaccaraṇavaranidhānaṃ sarvasya prakaṭayiṣyāmi*

## v.11 — shining with no shadow-cave

All-around pure-You-light-spread — when shall I so shine that no Māyā-shadow-cave is in me? The Sanskrit: *paritaḥ prasaracchuddhatvadālokamayaḥ kadā syāṃ yatheśa na kiñcin me māyācchāyābilaṃ bhavet*

## v.12 — leader of the devotee-host

Having made all circles my own, need-free — when shall I be, Lord, leader of Your devotee-host? The Sanskrit: *ātmasātkṛtaniḥśeṣamaṇḍalo nirvyapekṣakaḥ kadā bhaveyaṃ bhagavaṃs tvadbhaktagaṇanāyakaḥ*

## v.13 — drunk proud with His rasa

Lord, You are the unprecedented ground of world-prides — when shall I be greatly proud, filled with Your devotion-rasa? The Sanskrit: *nātha lokābhimānānām apūrvaṃ tvaṃ nibandhanam mahābhimānaḥ karhi syāṃ tvadbhaktirasapūritaḥ*

## v.14 — lying at cool lotus-feet

Well-set in never-object-empty glory-embrace — may I lie as if at Your cool lotus-feet pair — when? The Sanskrit: *aśeṣaviṣayāśūnyaśrīsamāśleṣasusthitaḥ śayīyam iva śītāṅghrikuśeśayayuge kadā*

## v.15 — reaching the far shore

Of worship-wealth grown rich on devotion-liquor — when shall I reach the far shore, when be apt? The Sanskrit: *bhaktyāsavasamṛddhāyās tvatpūjābhogasampadaḥ kadā pāraṃ gamiṣyāmi bhaviṣyāmi kadā kṛtī*

## v.16 — tears first, laughter after

Tears first, stumbling, wandering, choked crying — laughing-lit face: when shall I get Your touch-rasa? The Sanskrit: *ānandabāṣpapūraskhalitaparibhrāntagadgadākrandaḥ hāsollāsitavadanas tvatsparśarasaṃ kadāpsyāmi*

## v.17 — shaking off beast-life

Shaking off this beast-people-like conduct — when shall I, O Śambhu, taste the self-form fit for Your devotion? The Sanskrit: *paśujanasamānavṛttām avadhūya daśām imāṃ kadā śambho āsvādayeya tāvakabhaktocitam ātmano rūpam*

## v.18 — playing in the medicine

Siddhis gained, all heat-fear melted — when shall I play, devoted to drinking Your devotion-rasa-medicine? The Sanskrit: *labdhāṇimādisiddhir vigalitasakalopatāpasantrāsaḥ tvadbhaktirasāyanapānakriḍhāniṣṭaḥ kadāsīya*

## v.19 — the cry that summons form

Lord, when shall that cry of mine rise in speech — at which Your form flashes before me at once? The Sanskrit: *nātha kadā sa tathāvidha ākrando me samuccared vāci yat samanantaram eva sphurati puras tāvakī mūrtiḥ*

## v.20 — hugging hard, seeing without effort

Mind set on hugging Your tight-tight lotus-feet — when shall I see You, thing or non-thing, without effort? The Sanskrit: *gāḍhagāḍhabhavadaṅghrisarojāliṅganavyasanatatparacetāḥ vastv avastv idam ayatnata eva tvāṃ kadā samavalokayitāsmi*

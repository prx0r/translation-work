## v.1 — intolerable leveling

Surely intolerable to You, sole world-Lord: Your Maheśvaras and other world-folk on one level. The Sanskrit: *na soḍhavyam avaśyam te jagadekaprabhor idam | māheśvarāś ca lokānām itareṣāṃ samāś ca yat*

## v.2 — lovers enjoy everywhere

Who always follow Your feet with love — wherever gone, they enjoy certain pleasures. The Sanskrit: *ye sadaivānurāgeṇa bhavatpādānugāminaḥ | yatra tatra gatā bhogāṃs te kāṃś cid upabhuñjate*

## v.3 — husband as medicine and wealth

Where He is husband and death-ender, whence sickness? Where His fortune is, what other fortune? The Sanskrit: *bhartā kālāntako yatra bhavāṃs tatra kuto rujaḥ | tatra cetarabhogāśā kā lakṣmīr yatra tāvakī*

## v.4 — a moment fills all time

By whose moment-pleasure the All is gained — for him all time fills with Your bliss. The Sanskrit: *kṣanamātrasukhenāpi vibhur yenāsi labhyase | tadaiva sarvaḥ kālo 'sya tvadānandena pūryate*

## v.5 — moon a drop, sun a spark

Moon: Your bliss-rasa drop fallen to earth; sun: Your destroyer-heat's spread spark. The Sanskrit: *ānandarasabindus te candramā galito bhuvi | sūryas tathā te prasṛtaḥ saṃhārī tejasaḥ kaṇaḥ*

## v.6 — offering to the third eye

Offering to this third eye of Yours, Lord — sole mark of some unworldly greatness. The Sanskrit: *baliṃ yāmas tṛtīyāya netrāyāsmai tava prabho | alaukikasya kasyāpi māhātmyasyaikalakṣmaṇe*

## v.7 — seen and seeing both ways

Seen by Him alone, one joys in Your vision-etc.; or whose joy somehow — by it You are seen. The Sanskrit: *tenaiva dṛṣṭo 'si bhavaddarśanādyo 'tihṛṣyati | kathañcid yasya vā harṣaḥ ko 'pi tena tvam īkṣitaḥ*

## v.8 — dragging the outer inside

Those You're pleased with, whose hearts You've won — dragging from Your city, they've made the outer inner. The Sanskrit: *yeṣām prasanno 'si vibho yair labdhaṃ hṛdayaṃ tava | ākṛṣya tvatpurāttais tu bāhyam ābhyantarīkṛtam*

## v.9 — all same-eyed but the Uneven-eyed Seer

Apart from You the whole world looks same-eyed — but its Īśvara is You alone, the Uneven-eyed. The Sanskrit: *tvad ṛte nikhilaṃ viśvaṃ samadṛgyātam īkṣyatām | īśvaraḥ punar etasya tvam eko viṣamekṣaṇaḥ*

## v.10 — deniers depend on Him

Leave aside: without Your power there is no being at all — and those whose denials of You don't stand without You. The Sanskrit: *āstāṃ bhavatprabhāveṇa vinā sattaiva nāsti yat | tvaddūṣaṇakathā yeṣām tvad ṛte nopapadyate*

## v.11 — if mind stood, what else

If mind stood in You alone, barriers in-outside gone — what else would be needed, Lord? The Sanskrit: *bāhyāntarāntarāyālīkevale cetasi sthitiḥ | tvayi cet syān mama vibho kim anyad upayujyate*

## v.12 — same wandering, different placement

Others wander, Lord, in the self itself, ill-placed; others wander in the self itself, well-placed. The Sanskrit: *anye bhramanti bhagavann ātmany evātiduḥsthitāḥ | anye bhramanti bhagavann ātmany evātisusthitāḥ*

## v.13 — mere conduct perfects

Even untasted Your devotion-nectar, unseen You — by mere conduct with You, beings perfect. The Sanskrit: *apītvāpi bhavadbhaktisudhām anavalokya ca | tvām īśa tvatsamācāramātrāt siddhyanti jantavaḥ*

## v.14 — bear us as You bear Yourself

We are Your servants, Lord — so as You bear Yourself for three worlds, bear us too, fully. The Sanskrit: *bhṛtyā vayaṃ tava vibho tena trijagatāṃ yathā | bibharṣy ātmānam evaṃ te bharttavyā vayam apy alam*

## v.15 — touch-rasa hunger after vision

Though seen in supreme-bliss-nectar, in the world-self — for touch-rasa with You I hunger exceedingly. The Sanskrit: *parānandāmṛtamaye dṛṣṭo 'pi jagadātmani | tvayi sparśarase 'tyantatarasutkaṇṭhito(?) 'smi te*

## v.16 — ghee makes sorrows bearable

All sorrows even of worldlings — joined to Your ghee-self, become bearable. The Sanskrit: *deva duḥkhāny aśeṣāṇi yāni saṃsāriṇām api | ghṛtyākhyabhavadīyātmayutāny āyānti sahyatām*

## v.17 — unreality permitted by omnipotence

With You — omniscient, omnipotent, consciousness-formed — somehow the world's show, though unreal, is fitting. The Sanskrit: *sarvajñe sarvaśaktau ca tvayy eva sati cinmaye | sarvathāpy asato nātha yuktāsya jagataḥ prathā*

## v.18 — qualities dance, breathed by Him

Breathed by You, qualities throb — even clod-like — dancing wind-tossed like cotton fluff. The Sanskrit: *tvatprāṇitāḥ sphurantīme guṇā loṣṭopamā api | nṛtyanti pavanoddhūtāḥ kārpāsāḥ picavo yathā*

## v.19 — ego the prop of manifestation

If self-pride in qualities were not — then by what would the world's show of sole-selfness with You stand? The Sanskrit: *yadi nātha guṇeṣv ātmābhimāno na bhavet tataḥ | kena hīyeta jagatas tvadekātmatayā prathā*

## v.20 — anger-fire purifies

Venerable even the dissolved great ones, purified by Your anger-fire-touch. The Sanskrit: *vandyās te 'pi mahīyāṃsaḥ pralayopagatā api | tvatkopapāvakasparśapūtā ye parameśvara*

## v.21 — daylight self-accusation

With Your broad-light body standing plain — Lord, why do I spread in darkness on all sides? The Sanskrit: *mahāprakāśavapuṣi vispaṣṭe bhavati sthite | sarvato 'pīśa tat kasmāt tamasi prasarāmy aham*

## v.22 — all nectar, yet sole mortality-house

You alone undivided, my nectar-nature — yet of mortal natures I alone am the seat. The Sanskrit: *avibhāgo bhavān eva svarūpam amṛtaṃ mama | tathāpi martyadharmāṇām aham evaikam āspadam*

## v.23 — insignia of power

Whose speech-ornament is the name "Maheśvara," whose head bears the bow-mark — he alone is powerful. The Sanskrit: *maheśvareti yasyāsti nāmakaṃ vāgvibhūṣaṇam | praṇāmāṅkaś ca śirasi sa evaikaḥ prabhāvitaḥ*

## v.24 — ease should be mine

Since You are being and non-being — then effortlessly, by own tune, Lord, why is accomplishment not mine? The Sanskrit: *sadasac ca bhavān eva yena tenāprayāsataḥ | svarasenaiva bhagavaṃs tathā siddhiḥ kathaṃ na me*

## v.25 — slave-and-self, gods sated through him

Śiva's slave, Śiva-selfed — what joy would he not reach? By whom even chief gods are sated with nectar-wine. The Sanskrit: *śivadāsaḥ śivaikātmā kiṃ yan nāsādayet sukham | tarpyo 'smi devamukhyānām api yenāmṛtāsavaiḥ*

## v.26 — eater-fire between heart and navel

Between heart and navel, beings' bile-bodied — You devour, great fire, all moving-unmoving. The Sanskrit: *hṛnnābhyor antarālasthaḥ prāṇināṃ pittavigrahaḥ | grasase tvaṃ mahāvahniḥ sarvaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam*

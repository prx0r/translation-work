## v.1 — nothing mine, all You

This world, or friends, or kin — nothing is mine at all; but when You are all this, who else is mine? The Sanskrit: *jagad idam athavā suhṛdo bandhujano vā na bhavati mama kim api | tvaṃ punar etat sarvaṃ yadā tadā ko 'paro me 'stu*

## v.2 — begging that begs

"Master, Maheśvara, You directly — all the world is You alone" — may the thing itself succeed; and begging even in that is mere begging. The Sanskrit: *svāmin maheśvaras tvaṃ sākṣāt sarvaṃ jagat tvam eveti | vastv eva siddhim etv iti yācñā tatrāpi yācñaiva*

## v.3 — straw thrones, sterile merit

Even world-overlordship here seems straw to Your devotee; what fruit has good karma, Lord, without Your remembrance? The Sanskrit: *tribhuvanādhipatitvam apīha yat tṛṇam iva pratibhāti bhavajjuṣaḥ | kim iva tasya phalaṃ śubhakarmaṇo bhavati nātha bhavatsmaraṇād ṛte*

## v.4 — praise unblocked

Since nothing at all is apart from You, world-cause — Your play is wondrous, yet praise never sticks. The Sanskrit: *yena naiva bhavato 'sti vibhinnaṃ kiñcanāpi jagatāṃ prabhavaś ca | tvadvijṛmbhitam ato 'dbhutakarmasv apy udeti na tava stutibandhaḥ*

## v.5 — even in dream

"I am Yours, worship-bound" — always, unceasing, even by own tune, and in dream — why am I not so? The Sanskrit: *tvanmayo 'smi bhavadarcananiṣṭhaḥ sarvadāham iti cāpy avirāmam | bhāvayann api vibho svarasena svapnago 'pi na tathā kim iva syām*

## v.6 — fragrance-dot transfigures

Who are touched even slightly by Your lotus-foot-born fragrance-dust — for them all enjoyed things, gods-sought, become rest. The Sanskrit: *ye manāg api bhavaccaraṇābjodbhūtasaurabhalavena vimṛṣṭaḥ | teṣu visram iva bhavati samastaṃ bhogajātam amarair api mṛgyam*

## v.7 — heart, word, deed — one work

In heart no other, in word and deed wholly other, O Śambhu — yet grace or check from the truly real: one work only. The Sanskrit: *hṛdi te na tu vidyate 'nyad anyad vacane karmaṇi cānyad eva śaṃbho | paramārthasato 'py anugraho vā yadi vā nigraha eka eva kāryaḥ*

## v.8 — the resume of disqualifications

Fool am I, suffering-laden, aging-fearful, powerless — Yours am I: Śambhu, so arrange that, past sorrow's road, I mount the highest yoke fast. The Sanskrit: *mūḍho 'smi duḥkhakalito 'smi jarādidoṣabhīto 'smi śaktirahito 'smi tavāśrito 'smi | śambho tathā kalaya śīghram upaimi yena sarvottamāṃ dhuram apojjhitaduḥkhamārgaḥ*

## v.9 — trifles to pearls

Lying at Your ear-place, my cries, trifling — become like water-drops in bamboo-joints turning pearl. The Sanskrit: *tvatkarṇadeśama dhiśayya mahārghabhāvam ākranditāni mama tucchatarāṇi yānti | vaṃśāntarālapatitāni jalaikadeśakhaṇḍāni mauktikamaṇitvam ivodvahanti*

## v.10 — even cheating devotion works

What is not gained, alas, even by those — Lord — who even by deceit delight a moment in Your name? The Sanskrit: *kim iva ca labhyate bata na tair api nātha janaiḥ kṣaṇam api kaitavād api ca ye tava nāmni ratāḥ | śiśiramayūkhaśekhara tathā kuru yena mama kṣatamaraṇo 'ṇimādikam upaimi yathā vibhavam*

## v.11 — litany as battering-ram

Śambhu Śarva moon-crested Śiva, three-eyed rosary-bearer, fierce-skulled, trident-armed, ocean of pity, world-builder by fierce power, blue-throated — destroy ill-fortune fast, give supreme success. The Sanskrit: *śambho śarva śaśaṅkaśekhara śiva tryakṣākṣamālādhara śrīmann ugrakapālalāñchana lasadbhīmatriśūlāyudha | kāruṇyāmbunidhe trilokaracanāśīlograśaktyātmaka śrīkaṇṭhāśu vināśayāśubhabharān ādhat svasiddhiṃ parām*

## v.12 — cornered metaphysics

What would there be where the Lord does not make? What would the knower Śaṅkara destroys be? The Sanskrit: *tat kiṃ nātha bhaven na yatra bhagavān nirmātṛtām aśnute bhāvaḥ syāt kim u tasya cetanavato nāśāsti yaṃ śaṅkaraḥ | itthaṃ te parameśvarākṣatamahāśakteḥ sadā saṃśritaḥ saṃsāre 'tra nirantarādhividhuraḥ kliśyāmy ahaṃ kevalam*

## v.12b — clinging while harassed

Thus clinging to Your unwounded great power, harassed in saṃsāra, I suffer on, alone. The Sanskrit: *tat kiṃ nātha bhaven na yatra bhagavān nirmātṛtām aśnute bhāvaḥ syāt kim u tasya cetanavato nāśāsti yaṃ śaṅkaraḥ | itthaṃ te parameśvarākṣatamahāśakteḥ sadā saṃśritaḥ saṃsāre 'tra nirantarādhividhuraḥ kliśyāmy ahaṃ kevalam*

## v.13 — approved suffering, chosen longevity

Though pains, age, death here are boon-givers most haughty, and approved sounds unstable — yet I long, ever-pleasure-hungering, to stand long tasting Your feet for life-drink. The Sanskrit: *yady apy atra varapradoddhatatamāḥ pīḍājarāmṛtyava ete vā kṣaṇam āsatāṃ bahumataḥ śabdādir evāsthiraḥ | tatrāpi spṛhayāmi santatasukhākāṅkṣī ciraṃ sthāsnave bhogāsvādayutatvadaṅghrikamaladhyānāgrya jīvātave*

## v.14 — skilled destroyer, act now

O Lord skilled at destroying the bowed-down's pain, treasure of good, matted one — act now so that, living, I win unshakable attainments, Your-worship-bound. The Sanskrit: *he nātha praṇatārtināśanapaṭo śreyonidhe dhūrjaṭe duḥkhaikāyatanasya janmamaraṇatrastasya me sāmpratam | tac ceṣṭasva yathā manojñaviṣayāsvādapradā uttamā jīvann eva samaśnuve 'ham acalāḥ siddhīs tvadarcāparaḥ*

## v.15 — bow to the sole work and over-bright light

Bow to delusion-great-darkness-destroyer whose sole work is that, to over-bright light moon-marked. The Sanskrit: *namo mohamahādhvāntadhvaṃsanānanyakarmaṇe | sarvaprakāśātiśayaprakāśāyendulakṣmaṇe*

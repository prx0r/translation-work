## v.1 — no helper, no hinderer

No helper is wanted at all, no hinderer is seen in Your vision — yet all is flooded by You alone: how is it even so that You do not see? The Sanskrit: *sahakāri na kiñcid iṣyate bhavato na pratibandhakaṃ dṛṣi | bhavataiva hi sarvam āplutaṃ katham adyāpi tathāpi nekṣase*

## v.2 — self-born, world-veiled

From things, senses, awareness-middle, ever self-born — may I see Him, world-veiled. The Sanskrit: *api bhāvagaṇād apīndriyapracayād apy avabodhamadhyataḥ | prabhavantam api svataḥ sadā paripaśyeyam apoḍhaviśvakam*

## v.3 — how could things reach

How could things come to be, and how reach Your path of vision, inlaid by what great nature-limb? Rising and rising, from land-water-grass on, You scatter nectar-floods of creation over all things. The Sanskrit: *kathaṃ te jāyeran katham api ca te darśanapathaṃ vrajeyuḥ kenāpi prakṛtimahatāṅkena khacitaḥ | tathotthāyotthāya sthalajalatṛṇāder akhilataḥ padārthadyānsṛṣṭisravadamṛtapūrair(?) vikirasi*

## v.4 — the sated wander free

Sated with Your form-nectar, thirst uprooted, drunk — they wander at will. The Sanskrit: *sākṣatkṛta bhavadrūpaprasṛtāmṛtatarpitāḥ | unmūlitatṛṣo mattā vicaranti yathāruci*

## v.5 — timeless vision

Not then, not always, not once — where no time-thought is: such is Your vision, neither eternal nor otherwise. The Sanskrit: *na tadā na sadā na caikadety api sā yatra na kāladhīr bhavet | tad idaṃ bhavadīyadarśanaṃ na ca nityaṃ na ca kathyate 'nyathā*

## v.6 — entering by resolve

May my mind, risen to see You, gain this yoga-fruit always: to enter Your nectar-house by mere resolve, for Your worship. The Sanskrit: *tvadvilokanasamutkacetaso yogasiddhir iyatī sadāstu me | yad viśeyam abhisandhimātratas tvatsudhāsadanam arcanāya te*

## v.7 — pure play of the clear-minded

The clear-minded great ones' pure play and words shine plainly, playfully. The Sanskrit: *nirvikalpabhavadīyadarśanapraptiphullamanasāṃ mahātmanām | ullasanti vimalāni helayā ceṣṭitāni ca vacāṃsi ca sphuṭam*

## v.8 — dwelling between His feet

Dwelling between Your feet, fearless — in those and those world-grounds may I worship the Lord, unbarred in act. The Sanskrit: *bhavanbhavadīyapādayor nivasann antara eva nirbhayaḥ | bhavabhūmiṣu tāsu tāsv ahaṃ prabhum arceyam anargalakriyaḥ*

## v.9 — dissolved, drunk, wandering at will

Dissolved in Your foot-lake, melted seeking, honey-drunk to excess — may I roam satisfied, at will. The Sanskrit: *bhavadaṅghrisaroruhodare parilīno galitaparaiṣaṇaḥ | atimātramadhūpayogataḥ paritṛpto vicareyam icchayā*

## v.10 — even pretense suffices

Whose resolve to worship arose even from pretense — his presence is assured, fitting. The Sanskrit: *yasya dambhād iva bhavatpūjāsaṅkalpa utthitaḥ | tasyāpy avaśyam uditaṃ sannidhānaṃ tavocitam*

## v.11 — drinking to fullness

Lord, with one-taste mind seeking nothing else — the all-laying Lord, easy — may I drink to fullness: am I what? The Sanskrit: *bhagavann itarān apekṣiṇā nitarām ekarasena cetasā | sulabhaṃ sakalopaśāyinaṃ prabhum ātṛpti pibeyam asmi kim*

## v.12 — the essence-extract

By You denied: all where You're absent is to be left; where it's You, to be taken — this is the essence-extract. The Sanskrit: *tvayā nirākṛtam sarvaṃ heyam etat tad eva tu | tvanmayaṃ samupādeyam ity ayaṃ sārasaṃgrahaḥ*

## v.13 — inside worshipped, outside how

The world-born worshipped as chief by being inside You — how, Lord, could non-being outside be worshipped, or by whom? The Sanskrit: *bhavato 'ntaracāri bhāvajātaṃ prabhuvanmukhyatayaiva pūjitaṃ tat | bhavato bahir apy abhāvamātrā katham īśān bhavet samarcyate vā*

## v.14 — facing everywhere in agitation

Noiseless, thought-free, undistracted, always — in agitation too, face to face: I see You alone everywhere, Three-eyed. The Sanskrit: *niḥśabdaṃ nirvikalpaṃ ca nirvyākṣepam athānisam | kṣobhe 'py adhyakṣamī kṣeyaṃ tryakṣa tvām eva sarvataḥ*

## v.15 — reveal the abode

Reveal Your abode where You ever are with the Goddess; foot-dust-ranked servants — why do they look faithless? The Sanskrit: *prakaṭaya nijadhāma deva yasmiṃs tvam asi sadā parameśvarīsametaḥ | prabhucaraṇarajaḥsamānakakṣyāḥ kim aviśvāsapadaṃ bhānti bhṛtyāḥ*

## v.16 — fled yet never unseen

Come to path's seeing yet fled — whence, Lord, to me Your servant? Never for a moment here are You not every being's sight-object. The Sanskrit: *darśanapatham upayāto 'py apasarasi kuto mameśa bhṛtyasya | kṣaṇamātrakam iha na bhavasi kasya na jantor dṛśor viṣayaḥ*

## v.17 — flood me to joy

When with oneness-nectar's pure unbroken stream will You flood me, Lord — bringing past difference the measurer, gaining self and body, joy? The Sanskrit: *aikyasaṃvidamṛtācchadhārayā santataprasṛtayā kadā vibho | plāvanāt paramabhedamānayaṃs tvāṃ nijaṃ ca vapur āpnuyāṃ mudam*

## v.18 — a grain for worship-exchange

From my understanding fenced off from yonder world, a grain of world-establishment — that I be worshipped by You. The Sanskrit: *aham ity amuto 'varuddhalokād bhavadīyāt pratipattisārato me | aṇumātrakam eva viśvaniṣṭhaṃ ghaṭatāṃ yena bhaveyam arcitā te*

## v.19 — seeing each thing, seeing You

Seeing every thing each moment, O form beyond measure — may I see You well, my own Lord. The Sanskrit: *aparimitarūpam ahaṃ taṃ taṃ bhāvaṃ pratikṣaṇaṃ paśyan | tvām eva viśvarūpaṃ nijanāthaṃ sādhu paśyeyam*

## v.20 — why doesn't mind haunt the loved

Why doesn't mind haunt what limbs love, the desired thing for desire? If no nature is harmed and my supreme wish is filled... The Sanskrit: *bhavadaṅgagataṃ tam eva kasmān na manaḥ paryaṭatīṣṭam artham artham | prakṛtikṣatir asti no tathāsya mama cecchā paripūryate paraiva*

## v.21 — ploughmen see with this eye

Hundreds, they say, by Your power, with this very eye — even plough-walking — always see Your body ahead. The Sanskrit: *śataśaḥ kila te tavānubhāvād bhagavan ke 'py amunaiva cakṣuṣā ye | api hālikaceṣṭayā carantaḥ paripaśyanti bhavadvapuḥ sadāgre*

## v.22 — no unstained thought; feast unstopped

No thought rises unstained by Your will; always good or else — thus I roam as Yours on earth, feast of Your foot unstopped. The Sanskrit: *na sā matir udeti yā na bhavati tvadicchāmayī sadā śubham athetarad bhagavataivam ācaryate | ato 'smi bhavadātmako bhuvi yathā tathā sañcaran sthi to 'niśam abādhitatvadamalaṅghripūjotsavaḥ*

## v.23 — insight, power, unpausing worship

May insight rise well in Your deep sayings; then practiced power too, and unpausing worship-addiction. The Sanskrit: *bhavadīyagabhīrabhāṣiteṣu pratibhā samyag udetu me puro 'taḥ | tadanuṣṭhitaśaktir apy atas tadbhavadarcāvyasanaṃ ca nirvirāmam*

## v.24 — sense-heap as His limb

In dealings too, always, may this sense-heap appear to me as Your limb — not self-warranted. The Sanskrit: *vyavahārapade 'pi sarvadā pratibhātv arthakalāpa eṣa mām | bhavato 'vayavo yathā na tu svata evādaraṇīyatāṃ gataḥ*

## v.25 — roaming mind, skilled service

Mind roaming freely in its fields, unswerving — may I ever be worship-skilled toward You. The Sanskrit: *manasi svarasena yatra tatra pracaraty apy aham asya gocareṣu | prasṛto 'py avilola eva yuṣmatparicaryācaturaḥ sadā bhaveyam*

## v.26 — slave by His will, yet faceless to me

Lord, by Your will alone Your slave I was made — another's no power here — yet why never see Your face-moon? The Sanskrit: *bhagavan bhavadicchayaiva dāsas tava jāto 'smi parasya nātra śaktiḥ | katham eṣa tathāpi vaktrabimbaṃ tava paśyāmi na jātu citram etat*

## v.27 — what of those who face Him as opponent

The eager toward You who face You as counter-form — ah, what would befall them, what practice would fruit for them? The Sanskrit: *samutsukās tvāṃ prati ye bhavantaṃ pratyartharūpād avalokayanti | teṣām aho kiṃ tadupasthitaṃ syāt kiṃ sādhanaṃ vā phalitaṃ bhavet tat*

## v.28 — things as things by His being

May things be as things by Your being for me; thus may nothing be otherwise, nothing but You. The Sanskrit: *bhāvā bhāvatayā santu bhavadbhāvena me bhava | tathā na kiñcid apy astu na kiñcid bhavato 'nyathā*

## v.29 — nothing/something grammar of worship

May nothing-at-all be nothing, something-at-all be something to me; in all ways may You be worship-received. The Sanskrit: *yan na kiñcid api tanna kiñcid apy astu kiñcid api kiñcid eva me | sarvathā bhavatu tāvatā bhavān sarvato bhavati labdhapūjitaḥ*

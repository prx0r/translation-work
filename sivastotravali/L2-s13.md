## v.1 — the definition

In brief, Lord, hear how pleasure and pain stand for me: meeting You is pleasure; being parted from You, my Lord, is pain. The Sanskrit: *saṃgrahena sukhaduḥkhalakṣaṇaṃ māṃ prati sthitam idaṃ śṛṇu prabho saukhyam eṣa bhavatā samāgamaḥ svāminā viraha eva duḥkhitā*

## v.2 — the moment wiped

Even the subtlest inward moment when You do not appear — wipe it out everywhere, Lord, and reveal Your own stainless form. The Sanskrit: *antar apy atitarām aṇīyasī yā tvadaprathanakālikāsti me tām apīśa parimṛjya sarvataḥ svaṃ svarūpam amalaṃ prakāśaya*

## v.3 — abiding, living or dead

Let me live or die or be anything else — if only I abide, always worshipping, in Your world-full body at undying consciousness-nectar time. The Sanskrit: *tāvake vapuṣi viśvanirbhare citsudhārasamaye niratyaye tiṣṭhataḥ satatam arcataḥ prabhuṃ jīvitaṃ mṛtam athānyad astu me*

## v.4 — the glorious ego

"I am the Lord, I alone am formed, learned, fortunate — who else is parallel to me in the world?" Such pride shines supreme in Your lovers. The Sanskrit: *īśvaro 'ham aham eva rūpavān paṇḍito 'smi subhago 'smi ko 'paraḥ matsamo 'sti jagatīti śobhate mānitā tvadanurāgiṇaḥ param*

## v.5 — perception made fit

By the awareness of things exactly as they stand, make me fit for worshipping Your feet — me, born of telling non-duality's nectar that swallows renown. The Sanskrit: *devadeva bhavadadvayāmṛtākhyātisaṃharaṇalabdhajanmanā tad yathāsthitapadārthasaṃvidā māṃ kuruṣva caraṇārcanocitam*

## v.6 — meditated, seen, touched

Meditated upon, then seen, then the Lord Himself is touched. May this worship-festival of Yours be always mine, by Your power. The Sanskrit: *dhyāyate tad anu dṛśyate tataḥ spṛśyate ca parameśvaraḥ svayam yatra pūjanamahotsavaḥ sa me sarvadāstu bhavato 'nubhāvataḥ*

## v.7 — the mutually grounding pair

Seeing things as they stand, and Your worship-festival — this pair grounds each other, expanding always among devotees. The Sanskrit: *yady athāsthitapadārthadarśanaṃ yuṣmadarcanamahotsavaś ca yaḥ yugmam etad itaretarāśrayaṃ bhaktiśāliṣu sadā vijṛmbhate*

## v.8 — drunk through every sense

Through every sense-door, constantly, may I drink the wine of Your worship-rasa in all-filled feeling-cups — and be mad. The Sanskrit: *tattadindriyamukhena santataṃ yuṣmadarcanarasāyanāsavam sarvabhāvacaṣakeṣu pūriteṣv āpibann api bhaveyam unmadaḥ*

## v.9 — the country with no other

Where not an atom is felt as other, and all expands self-shining — there, Lord, keep me standing, Your constant worshipper, in Your city. The Sanskrit: *anyavedyam aṇumātram asti na svaprakāśam akhilaṃ vijṛmbhate yatra nātha bhavataḥ pure sthitaṃ tatra me kuru sadā tavārcituḥ*

## v.10 — slavery as fitness

Though You posted even me to slaves' quarters by Your own will — am I not fit by seeing, even by foot-massage? The Sanskrit: *dāsadhāmni viniyojito 'py ahaṃ svecchayaiva parameśvara tvayā darśanena na kim asmi pātritaḥ pādasaṃvahanakarmaṇāpi vā*

## v.11 — grace never deliberates

At the moment of grace's descent You never deliberate, Lord, never — so what has come over You now, delaying the rite of Your self-revelation to me? The Sanskrit: *śaktipātasamaye vicāraṇaṃ prāptam īśa na karoṣi karhicit adya māṃ prati kim āgataṃ yataḥ svaprakāśanavidhau vilambase*

## v.12 — beholding with my own hands

In each object shining outside, and inside joined with the Goddess, may I always behold You, world-full — worshipped by my own hands. The Sanskrit: *tatra tatra viṣaye bahirvibhāty antare ca parameśvarīyutam tvāṃ jagattritayanirbharaṃ sadā lokayeya nijapāṇipūjitam*

## v.13 — the palace by mere wish

May I always mount the Master's palace, unobstructed, by mere resolve — bliss-freed in the play of drinking supreme-nectar-wine. The Sanskrit: *svāmisaudham abhisandhimātrato nirvibandham adhiruhya sarvadā syāṃ prasāda paramāmṛtāsavāpānakeliparilabdhanirvṛtiḥ*

## v.14 — worship answers touch

Whatever wonder arises in all fair things by mere touch — by that the unwavering worship Your body. The Sanskrit: *yatsamastasubhagārthavastuṣu sparśamātravidhinā camatkṛtim tāṃ samarpayati tena te vapuḥ pūjayanty acalabhaktiśālinaḥ*

## v.15 — joy swells the world

Swelling all by Thyself, shining — You touch the universe, touching form; whatever whirls by its own juice — that is the swelling world. The Sanskrit: *sphārayasy akhilam ātmanā sphuran viśvam āmṛśasi rūpam āmṛśan yat svayaṃ nijarasena ghurṇase tat samullasati bhāvamaṇḍalam*

## v.16 — whence fear?

Who sees this whole object-circle without construction as Your body, Lord — the world filled with his own self's side — for him, ever-happy, whence fear? The Sanskrit: *yo 'vikalpam idam arthamaṇḍalaṃ paśyatīśa nikhilaṃ bhavadvapuḥ svātmapakṣaparipūrite jagaty asya nityasukhinaḥ kuto bhayam*

## v.17 — poison as nectar

The kālakūṭa poison set in Your throat's corner is great nectar to me; even taken nectar, if difference prevails, does not please me. The Sanskrit: *kaṇṭhakoṇaviniviṣṭam īśa te kālakūṭam api me mahāmṛtam apy upāttam amṛtaṃ bhavadvapur bhedavṛtti yadi rocate na me*

## v.18 — songs, otherwise

Graced with song-mouths lovely with prattling of You, may I be — my heart forever gone to what Your worship-loving prefers. The Sanskrit: *tvatpralāpamayaraktagītikāni tya yuktavadanopaśobhitaḥ syām athāpi bhavadarcanakriyāpreyasīparigatāśayaḥ sadā*

## v.19 — the uncountable play

Alas, the Supreme's play cannot be counted — and so the nectar-full body He gave me, He does not let me drink — so it is. The Sanskrit: *īhitaṃ na bata pārameśvaraṃ śakyate gaṇayituṃ tathā ca me dattam apy amṛtanirbharaṃ vapuḥ svaṃ na pātum anumanyate tathā*

## v.20 — entering and praising

Entering You — fathomless, construction-free, non-dual, devourer of all things' meaning — O Umā's Lord, may I always worship and praise. The Sanskrit: *tvām agādham avikalpam advayaṃ svaṃ svarūpam akhilārthaghasmaram āviśann aham umeśa sarvadā pūjayeyam abhisaṃstuvīya ca*

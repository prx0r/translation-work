## v.1 — tasting proclamation

Before my own master, treasure of victory-fortune — may I taste for a moment the nectar of victory-proclamation. The Sanskrit: *jayalakṣmīnidhānasya nijasya svāminaḥ puraḥ jayodghoṣaṇapīyūṣarasam āsvādaye kṣaṇam*

## v.2 — one Rudra, one Śiva

Victory: one Rudra, one Śiva, great god, great Lord — Pārvatī's lover, Śarva, eldest of all gods. The Sanskrit: *jayaikarudraikaśiva mahādeva maheśvara pārvatīpraṇayiñ śarva sarvagīrvāṇapūrvaja*

## v.3 — brow-mark and poison-throat

Victory: brow-eyed sole mark of the three-worlds' Lord; victory: poison-mark-throated, who drank the black poison for the worlds' pain. The Sanskrit: *jaya trailokyanāthaikalāñchanālikalocana jaya pītartalokārtikālakūṭāṅkakandhara*

## v.4 — body of powers, feet of wishes

Victory: whose self is the embodied three powers, hundred-spike-bright-handed; victory: whose lotus-feet are worshipped, wishes accomplished by mere will. The Sanskrit: *jaya mūrtatriśaktyātmiśataśūlollasatkara jayecchāmātrasiddārthapūjārhacaraṇāmbuja*

## v.5 — beauty beyond worlds, ash-seized

Victory: bearer of a body beyond worlds, streaming a hundred beauties; victory: single-braided, of unwasting Ganges, ash-seized. The Sanskrit: *jaya śobhaśatasyandilokottaravapurdhara jayaikajaṭikākṣīṇagaṅgākṛtyāttabhasmaka*

## v.6 — moonlight-smeared, jewel-lit

Victory: smeared with milk-ocean-spread moonlight shade; victory: snow-ornamented, jewel-beloved, risen from the Lord's limb-contact. The Sanskrit: *jaya kṣīrodaparyastajyotsnācchāyānulepana jayeśvarāṅgasaṅgottharatnakāntāhimaṇḍana*

## v.7 — refuge-digit, consecration

Victory: refuge like the one undying cool-rayed digit; victory: consecrated by ever-moist Ganges and universal lordship. The Sanskrit: *jayākṣayaikaśītāṃśukalāsadṛśasaṃśraya jaya gaṅgāsadārbdhaviśvaiśvaryābhiṣecana*

## v.8 — the low touch, the gathering

Victory: whose lowest-limb-touch purifies cowherds; victory: fixed-present where intoxicated devotees gather. The Sanskrit: *jayādharāṅgasaṃsparśapāvanīkṛtagokula jaya bhaktimadābaddhagoṣṭhīniyatasannidhe*

## v.9 — duping and embrace-fortune

Victory: who dupes the childish, showing a hot place of His own will; victory: fit vessel for Gauri-embrace-fortune. The Sanskrit: *jaya svecchātapodeśavipralambhitabāliśa jaya gaurīpariṣvaṅgayogyasaubhāgyabhājana*

## v.10 — greedy for gifts

Victory: greedy for love-gifts wet with devotion-rasa; victory: pleased by the speech-dance of devotees drunk with devotion. The Sanskrit: *jaya bhaktirasārdrārdrabhāvopāyanalampaṭa jaya bhaktimadoddāmabhaktavāṅnṛttatoṣita*

## v.11 — gods rise and fall

Victory: whose rise-and-fall is the power of Brahmā-and-first-gods; victory: whose command world-lord-rows bear on heads. The Sanskrit: *jaya brahmādideveśaprabhāvaprabhavavyaya jayalokeśvaraśreṇiśirovidhṛtaśāsana*

## v.12 — seal and self-gift

Victory: whose manifest glory is His seal set on all worlds; victory: great Lord of all, down to giving the very self. The Sanskrit: *jayasarvajagannyastasvamudrāvyaktavaibhava jayātmadānaparyantaviśveśvaramaheśvara*

## v.13 — secondless, yet helped

Victory: secondless at the creation-wish moment; victory: with sole Goddess-helper bearing lordship's burden. The Sanskrit: *jaya trailokyasargecchāvasarāsaddvitīyaka jayaiśvaryabharodvāhadevīmātrasahāyaka*

## v.14 — one stride, childhood song

Victory: who seized all three worlds in one stride; victory: unbroken Lord-sound sung from childhood. The Sanskrit: *jayākramasamākrāntasamastabhuvanatraya jayāvigītam ābālagīyamāneśvaradhvane*

## v.15 — high without need

Victory: innately high, needing no pity and the rest; victory: Bhairava before whom great Death is made. The Sanskrit: *jayānukampādiguṇānapekṣasahajonnate jaya bhīṣmamahāmṛtyughaṭanāpūrvabhairava*

## v.16 — unopposed fierceness

Victory: unopposed in fierce world-ending acts; victory: hundredfold-good-named recitation-following. The Sanskrit: *jaya viśvakṣayoccaṇḍakriyāniṣparipanthika jaya śreyaḥśataguṇānuganāmānukīrtana*

## v.17 — play pours, anger dries

Victory: nectar-mine-ocean poured out in play; victory: whose instant anger dries the world-ending in a flash. The Sanskrit: *jaya helāvitīrnaitadamṛtākarasāgara jaya viśvakṣayakṣepikṣaṇakopāśuśukṣaṇe*

## v.18 — lamp for the blind

Victory: sole lamp of beings blind in delusion-darkness; victory: wakeful supreme Person while the sleeping world... The Sanskrit: *jaya mohāndhakārāndhajīvalokaikadīpaka jaya prasuptajagatījāgarūkādhipūruṣa*

## v.19 — hum in the cave

Victory: life-of-life humming inside the body-mountain cave; victory: best rasa sporting in good minds' sky. The Sanskrit: *jaya dehādrikuñjāntarnikūjañjīvajīvaka jaya sanmānasavyomavilāsivarasārasa*

## v.20 — gold mountain, portent moon

Victory: mountain-lord born of high gold-ore; victory: moon rising as calamity-portent over the wicked. The Sanskrit: *jaya jāmbūnadodagradhātūdbhavagirīśvara jaya pāpiṣu nindolkāpātanotpātacandramaḥ*

## v.21 — missed by austerities, seen by madness

Victory: hard to reach by austerity-tormented sages and gods; victory: seen by the world of devotion-madmen mounted on every state. The Sanskrit: *jaya kaṣṭatapaḥkliṣṭamunidevadurāsada jaya sarvadaśārūḍhabhaktimallokalokita*

## v.22 — fondling the surrendered

Victory: who leafed His dependents spreading His own wealth; victory: whose sole purpose is fondling surrendered people. The Sanskrit: *jaya svasampatprasarapatrīkṛtanijāśrita jaya prapannajanatālālanaikaprayojana*

## v.23 — sole doer, lotus festival

Victory: sole performer of creating-staying-destroying; victory: festival of the play-lotus blooming in drunken devotion. The Sanskrit: *jaya sargasthitidhvaṃsakāraṇaikāvadānaka jaya bhaktimadālolalīlotpalamagotsava*

## v.24 — thirteen victories

Victory, worthy of victory! Victory, conqueror of birth, aging, dying! Victory, eldest of worlds... victory, victory... O three-eyed One. The Sanskrit: *jaya jayabhājana jaya jitajanmajarāmaraṇa jaya jagajjyeṣṭha jaya jaya jaya jaya jaya jaya jaya jaya jaya jaya jaya jaya jaya tryakṣa*

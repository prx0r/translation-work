## v.1 — books wash, devotees stand

Stain-washing books exist, and their crossers too — yogins, scholars stand healthy — but devotees alone, in truth. The Sanskrit: *trimalakṣālino granthāḥ santi tatpāragāstathā | yoginaḥ paṇḍitāḥ svasthās tvadbhaktā eva tattvataḥ*

## v.2 — eating time

Māyā-time-limit-passion and the rest as food, fully fed — devotees stroll happy, Lord, on the world's shore. The Sanskrit: *māyīyakālaniyatirāgādyāhāratarpitāḥ | caranti sukhino nātha bhaktimanto jagattaṭe*

## v.3 — laughing, crying aloud

Laughing or crying, they babble You aloud — devotees, whose praise-word offerings are ornaments apart. The Sanskrit: *rudanto vā hasanto vā tvām uccaiḥ pralapanty amī | bhaktāḥ stutipadoccāropacārāḥ pṛthag eva te*

## v.4 — drunk, not detached

Not detached, not master, not liberation-desiring is Your worshipper — but drunk with soaring devotion-liquor-rasa. The Sanskrit: *na virakto na cāpīśo mokṣākāṅkṣī tvadarcakaḥ | bhaveyam api tūdriktabhaktyāsavarasonmadaḥ*

## v.5 — drawing the outer inside

Who worships by drawing the outer inside the heart alone — to him, in devotion-nectar floods, I bow. The Sanskrit: *bāhyaṃ hṛdaya evāntar abhihṛtyaiva yo 'rcati | tvām īśa bhaktipīyūṣarasapūrair namāmi tam*

## v.6 — tasting something

Between dharma and adharma acts, knowledges, pleasure and pain selves — devotees taste SOMETHING — ah! The Sanskrit: *dharmādharmātmanor antaḥ kriyayor jñānayos tathā | sukhaduḥkhātmanor bhaktāḥ kim apy āsvādayanty aho*

## v.7 — blind ones shine

Blind ones, lepers even — they shine, supreme, ornamented by blazing devotion to You. The Sanskrit: *carācarapitaḥ svāmin apy andhā api kuṣṭhinaḥ | śobhante param uddāmabhavadbhaktivibhūṣaṇāḥ*

## v.8 — gleaners rule kings

Gleaners, winnowers, enemies' shadows even — yet by devotion's heat they rule even the king of kings. The Sanskrit: *śiloñchapicchakaśipuvicchāyāṅgā api prabho | bhavadbhaktimahoṣmaṇo rājarājam apīśate*

## v.9 — rolling yet climbing

Though rolling in nectar-wet devotion, yet wanting to climb — some worship You with mind alone, standing all around. The Sanskrit: *sudhārdrāyāṃ bhavadbhaktau luṭhatāpy ārurukṣuṇā | cetasaiva vibho 'rcanti kecit tvām abhitaḥ sthitāḥ*

## v.10 — guard the great wealth

To be guarded, grown, honored, Lord — this great wealth of devotion that ends bad rebirths. The Sanskrit: *rakṣaṇīyaṃ vardhanīyaṃ bahumānyam idaṃ prabho | saṃsāradurgatiharaṃ bhavadbhaktimahādhanam*

## v.11 — no jealousy toward Her

Lord, though Your devotees love You — abandoning jealousy toward Her, may She always be pleased, the Mistress. The Sanskrit: *nātha te bhaktajanatā yady api tvayi rāgiṇī | tathāpīrṣyāṃ vihāyāsyās tuṣṭāstu svāminī sadā*

## v.12 — the milk-ocean churned

Your future being first; when devotion's occasion comes and the milk-ocean is churned — curd-greed is killed. The Sanskrit: *bhavadbhāvaḥ puro bhāvī prāpte tvadbhaktisambhave | labdhe dugdhamahākumbhe hatā dadhani gṛdhnutā*

## v.13 — ever-growing attainment

Is this not attainment beyond compare — ever-growing devotion to Śambhu, always new? What happiness does it not stream? The Sanskrit: *kim iyaṃ na siddhir atulā kiṃ vā mukhyaṃ na saukhyam āsravati | bhaktir upacīyamānā yeyaṃ śambhoḥ sadātanī bhavati*

## v.14 — the buried gem-creeper

Sunk in my dirty mind, Your devotion gem-creeper — alas — does not spread its own splendor's unfoldings. The Sanskrit: *manasi maline madīyemagnā tvadbhaktimaṇilatā kaṣṭam | na nijān api tanute tānapauruṣeyān svasampadullāsān*

## v.15 — devotion IS the attainment

Devotion to the Lord of three worlds IS supreme attainment — but lacking supernormal powers, is it not full? — that is my worry. The Sanskrit: *bhaktir bhagavati bhavati trilokanāthe nanūttamā siddhiḥ | kiṃ tv aṇimādikavirahāt saiva na pūrṇeti cintā me*

## v.16 — fragrance for the hostile

Outside and in, the fragrance of Your praise-blossoms' opening perfumes even hostile ascetics dwelling near. The Sanskrit: *bāhyato 'ntar api cotkaṭonmiṣattryambakastavakasaurabhāḥ śubhāḥ | vāsayanty api viruddhavāsanān yogino nikaṭavāsino 'khilān*

## v.17 — worship at the night-post

By no account is there any light; the whole world fast asleep — yet at Your Śivarātri post, devotees always worship You. The Sanskrit: *jyotir asti kathayāpi na kiṃ cid viśvam apy atisuṣuptam aśeṣam | yatra nātha śivarātripade 'smin nityam arcayati bhaktajanas tvām*

## v.18 — let darkness swell

Let goodness expand in true worship of gracious Śiva; let Śaṅkara-foot-dust heaps sport on the crown — to cut even passion-memories, let darkness swell, or the triple world dissolve in Your self-loss. The Sanskrit: *sattvaṃ satyaguṇe śive bhagavati sphārībhavatv arcane cūḍāyāṃ vilasantu śaṅkarapadaprodyadrajaḥsaṅcayāḥ | rāgādismṛtivāsanām api samucchettuṃ tamo jṛmbhatāṃ śambho me bhavatāt tvadātmavilaye traiguṇyavargo 'thavā*

## v.19 — wasted life, long fortune

The world-road is long; limbs burnt by harsh diseases; pleasures unenjoyed; happiness never long — thus I have become useless, moon-crest-foot-pressed, wholly Yours — so make me, quickly, long-and-rich in great fortune, as Your devotee. The Sanskrit: *saṃsārādhvā sudūraḥ kharataravividhavyādhidagdhāṅgayaṣṭiḥ bhogā naivopabhuktā yad api sukham abhūj jātu nanno cirāya | itthaṃ vyartho 'smi jātaḥ śaśidharacaraṇākrāntikāntottamāṅgas tvadbhaktaś ceti tan me kuru sapadi mahāsampado dīrghadīrghāḥ*

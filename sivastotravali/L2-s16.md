## v.1 — nothing blocks

Nothing blocks folk, nothing blocks devotees from Your veiling. The Sanskrit: *na kiñcid eva lokānāṃ bhavadāvaraṇaṃ prati \var{yat kiṃ cid eva bhūtānāṃ\em \cit \Naresvaraparīksā} na kiñcid eva bhaktānāṃ bhavadāvaraṇaṃ prati*

## v.2 — pure at once

Though reached by method-ladders, crowded with attributes — to devotees the self appears at once, pure. The Sanskrit: *apy upāyakramaprāpyaḥ saṅkulo 'pi viśeṣaṇaiḥ bhaktibhājāṃ bhavān ātmā sakṛc chuddho 'vabhāsate*

## v.3 — laughing victors

Laughing victors, laughing conquered — drunk on His devotion-nectar, whoever they are, Lord. The Sanskrit: *jayanto 'pi hasanty ete jitā api hasanti ca bhavadbhaktisudhāpānamattāḥ ke 'py eva ye prabho*

## v.4 — no dryness, no release-seeking

May I never dry up, never seek release — full of His devotion-rasa, best-liked, attained. The Sanskrit: *śuṣkakaṃ maiva siddheya maiva mucyeya vāpi tu svādiṣṭhaparakāṣṭāptatvadbhaktirasanirbharaḥ*

## v.5 — may it grow

As unknown before was this devotion-rasa of Yours to me — so, Lord, may that same one grow. The Sanskrit: *yathaivajñātapūrvo 'yaṃ bhavadbhaktiraso mama ghaṭitas tadvad īśāna sa eva paripuṣyatu*

## v.6 — the single prayer

Truly, Lord, I have no other prayer-course — only may such immersion-possession by devotion be mine always. The Sanskrit: *satyena bhagavan nānyaḥ prārthanāprasaro 'sti me kevalaṃ sa tathā ko 'pi bhaktyāveśo 'stu me sadā*

## v.7 — rage, repent, leap, shout

Drunk on devotion I may rage, repent, leap, shout "Śiva" — enough. The Sanskrit: *bhaktikṣīvo 'pi kupyeyaṃ bhavāyānuśayīya ca tathā haseyaṃ udyāṃ ca raṭeyaṃ ca śivety alam*

## v.8 — crisis or peace

In crisis or at peace, weeping or laughing, deep or distracted — may I be, Lord, by devotion. The Sanskrit: *viṣamastho 'pi svastho 'pi rudann api hasann api gambhīro 'pi vicitto 'pi bhaveyaṃ bhaktitaḥ prabho*

## v.9 — nothing to be known

For devotees nothing to be known, inside You or out — where consciousness-natured You stand thought-free. The Sanskrit: *bhaktānāṃ nāsti saṃvedyaṃ tvadantar yadi vā bahiḥ ciddharmā yatra na bhavān nirvikalpaḥ sthitaḥ svayam*

## v.10 — pierced within, bristling

Devotees rejoice even at abusers — pierced within, bristling with sharp horripilation, as if by nectar-drops. The Sanskrit: *bhaktā nindānukare 'pi tavāmṛtakaṇair iva hṛṣyanty evāntarāviddhās tīkṣṇaromāñcasūcibhiḥ*

## v.11 — pain ripens to enjoyment

Even pain-feeling ripens to enjoyment for devotees, whose all-awareness, moon-wet, is moonlight-made. The Sanskrit: *duḥkhāpi vedanā bhaktimatāṃ bhogāya kalpate yeṣāṃ sudhārdrā sarvaiva saṃvit tvaccandrikāmayī*

## v.12 — held everywhere, same joy

Wherever held, devotees — inside and out — unfeigned, Your body-touch-taste-joy the same. The Sanskrit: *yatra tatroparuddhānāṃ bhaktānāṃ bahirantare nirvyājaṃ tvadvapuḥsparśarasāsvādasukhaṃ samam*

## v.13 — duality's double-refuge dissolved

In worship of Your devotion, duality's double-refuge meanness — tasting the clear nectar-body, some dissolve it. The Sanskrit: *taveśa bhakter arcāyāṃ dainyāṃśaṃ dvayasaṃśrayam vilupyāsvādayanty eke vapur acchaṃ sudhāmayam*

## v.14 — pilgrim-eyes err; Being shines

Erring are pilgrim-eyes, divided — for division is just erring; but matchless, strifeless Being shines for devotees. The Sanskrit: *bhrāntās tīrthadṛśo bhinnā bhrānter eva hi bhinnatā niṣpratidvandvi vastv ekaṃ bhaktānāṃ tvaṃ tu rājase*

## v.15 — ripened mind unmannered

Mind ripened past honor, contempt, love and the rest into stainlessness — how could its bearer be world-mannered? The Sanskrit: *mānāvamānarāgādiniṣpākavimalaṃ manaḥ yasyāsau bhaktimāṃl lokatulyaśīlaḥ kathaṃ bhavet*

## v.16 — knowledge-rich put to shame

Passion-aversion-darkness conquered by devotion-light — who are the knowledge-rich before them? The Sanskrit: *rāgadveṣandhakāro 'pi yeṣāṃ bhaktitviṣā jitaḥ teṣāṃ mahīyasām agre katame jñānaśālinaḥ*

## v.17 — bath-and-drink as rite

Whose rite is devotion-nectar-drink-and-bath — high joy from beginning through end. The Sanskrit: *yasya bhaktisudhāsnānapānādividhisādhanam tasya prārabdhamadhyāntadaśāsūccaiḥ sukhāsikā*

## v.18 — praised, sought, worshipped

Praised, sought, worshipped — for that alone devotees' world-life is praiseworthy. The Sanskrit: *kīrtyaś cintāpadaṃ mṛgyaḥ pūjyo yena tvam eva tat bhavadbhaktimatāṃ ślāghyā lokayātrā bhavanmayī*

## v.19 — liberation renamed

Liberation's name is but Your ripe devotion — mounted on its first stage, we are near-free. The Sanskrit: *muktisaṃjñā vipakvāyā bhakter eva tvayi prabho tasyām ādyadaśārūḍhā muktakalpā vayaṃ tataḥ*

## v.20 — pain invited, pleasure refused

May pain's coming be great for me, devotion-filled self — never turned from You, be even pleasure-line. The Sanskrit: *duḥkhāgamo 'pi bhūyān me tvadbhaktibharitātmanaḥ tvatparācī vibho mā bhūd api saukhyaparamparā*

## v.21 — the circle blessed

You are pleased by devotion; devotion, You pleased — this mutual dependence is fitting, as You alone know. The Sanskrit: *tvaṃ bhaktyā prīyase bhaktiḥ prīte tvayi ca nātha yat tadanyonyāśrayaṃ yuktaṃ yathā vettha tvam eva tat*

## v.22 — form or formless, always nectar

With form or formless, inside or out — to devotion-selves, always nectar, Lord. The Sanskrit: *sākāro vā nirākaro vāntar vā bahir eva vā bhaktimattātmanāṃ nātha sarvathāsi sudhāmayaḥ*

## v.23 — another world-stand in this world

In this very world, for Your devotees, another world-stand exists — fruit of joy-showing. The Sanskrit: *asminn eva jagaty antar bhavadbhaktimataḥ prati harṣaprakāśanaphalam anyad eva jagatsthitam*

## v.24 — devotion, devotion, something

Devotion hidden, devotion supreme, devotion to the world-great Lord — devotion, ah, something in You, Śiva. The Sanskrit: *guhye bhaktiḥ pare bhaktir bhaktir viśvamaheśvare tvayi śambhau śive deva bhaktir nāma kim apy aho*

## v.25 — I weep, let fierce devotion be mine

Devotion, devotion, supreme devotion — I cry loud: let fierce devotion be mine supreme. The Sanskrit: *bhaktir bhaktiḥ pare bhaktir bhaktir nāma samutkaṭā tāraṃ viraumi yat tīvrā bhaktir me 'stu paraṃ tvayi*

## v.26 — jewel or straw, stuck to You

Since I am the birth-ground of all beauties — stuck to You, jewel or straw becomes priceless. The Sanskrit: *yato 'smi sarvaśobhānāṃ prasavāvanir īśa tat tvayi lagnam anarghaṃ syād ratnam vā yadi vā tṛṇam*

## v.27 — unparted, they win

From knower and from knowable, on whose knowing-road there is no parting from You — they win, Your devotees, ever fearless. The Sanskrit: *āvedakād ā ca vedyād yeṣāṃ saṃvedanādhvani bhavatā na viyogo 'sti te jayanti bhavajjuṣaḥ*

## v.28 — embraced in saṃsāra's hall

Embraced in saṃsāra's hall by some, You — Lord, by others self-tormenting unreined. The Sanskrit: *saṃsārasadaso bāhye kaiś cittvaṃ parirabhyase svāmin parais tu tatraiva tāmyadbhis tyaktayantraṇaiḥ*

## v.29 — hugging Śiva with the feasting Goddess

With Śivā who eats, decks, celebrates the world, festival-hurried — hug tight, Śiva, I bow. The Sanskrit: *pānāśanaprasādhanasambhuktasamastaviśvayā śivayā pralayotsavasarabhasayā dṛḍham upagūḍhaṃ śivaṃ vande*

## v.30 — sovereignty with no subjects

Peerless overlordship wins, world-Lord, being subject-free — and so does Your otherness, by which this world shines as it does not. The Sanskrit: *parameśvaratā jayaty apūrvā tava viśveśa yadīśitavyaśūnyā aparāpi tathaiva te yayedaṃ jagad ābhāti yathā tathā na bhāti*

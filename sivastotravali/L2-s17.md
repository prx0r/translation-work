## v.1 — the sweet festival glorified

Ah, this wins — sweet, this festival of worship — since tears give nectar-taste amply. The Sanskrit: *aho ko 'pi jayaty eṣa svāduḥ pūjāmahotsavaḥ | yato 'mṛtarasāsvādam aśrūṇy api dadaty alam*

## v.2 — all acts succeed, devotees are successes

All acts with His worship vanguard are successes; devotees made of Him are themselves successes. The Sanskrit: *vyāpārāḥ siddhidāḥ sarve ye tvatpūjāpuraḥsarāḥ | bhaktānāṃ tvanmayāḥ sarve svayaṃ siddhaya eva te*

## v.3 — all beings, all states, my gods

In all beings, all states, at once, all-formed — worshipping You unceasing, they are my gods. The Sanskrit: *sarvadā sarvabhāveṣu yugapat sarvarūpiṇam | tvām arcayanty aviśrantaṃ ye mamaite 'dhidevatāḥ*

## v.4 — touch perfected by meditation

Meditation-perfected touch-festival of You — thus famed as worship-rule — be mine always. The Sanskrit: *dhyānāyasatiraskārasiddhas tvatsparśanotsavaḥ | pūjāvidhir iti khyāto bhaktānāṃ sa sadās tu me*

## v.5 — equinox-moment always

Equanimity-essence equinox-moment of devotees is always — ever-worshipping with Your being-rasa-nectar. The Sanskrit: *bhaktānāṃ samatāsāraviṣuvatsamayaḥ sadā | tvadbhāvarasapīyūṣarasennaiṣāṃ sadārcanam*

## v.6 — beginningless deed, single resort

Whose beginning and end are not, nor time-order — that deed is Yours, and its doers resort only to You. The Sanskrit: *yasyānārambhaparyantau na ca kālakramaḥ prabho | pūjātmāsau kriyā tasyāḥ kartāras tvajjuṣaḥ param*

## v.7 — gods fortunate even in delusion

Brahmā and the rest are fortunate, lords — in whose dream, even delusion, stands Your worship-festival. The Sanskrit: *brahmādīnām apīśāste te ca saubhāgyabhāginaḥ | yeṣāṃ svapne 'pi mohe 'pi sthitas tvatpūjanotsavaḥ*

## v.8 — whenever, whatever worship

Chanting, offering, bathing, meditating — and not only: ah, devotees' worshipping You — whenever, whatever, however much. The Sanskrit: *japatāṃ juhvatāṃ snātāṃ dhyāyatāṃ na ca kevalam | bhaktānāṃ bhavadabhyarcāmaho yāvad yadā tadā*

## v.9 — who equals Indra and Brahmā

Tasting Your worship-nectar-joy, enjoying — to Indra and the rest, to Brahmā-chiefs: is anyone equal? The Sanskrit: *bhavatpūjāsudhāsvādasambhogasukhinaḥ sadā | indrādīnām atha brahmamukhyānām asti kaḥ samaḥ*

## v.10 — the festival known only by getting

World-shaking, one-cause festival — what is got thereby, only devotees know who get it. The Sanskrit: *jagatkṣobhaikajanake bhavatpūjāmahotsave | yatprāpyaṃ prāpyate kiṃcid bhaktā eva vidanti tat*

## v.11 — worship with all 36 tattva-acts

Standing in Your abode, consciousness-formed, with body-speech-mind acts of all 36 principles — I always worship You, Lord. The Sanskrit: *tvaddhāmni cinmaye sthitvā ṣaṭtriṃśattattvakarmabhiḥ | kāyavākcittaceṣṭādyair arcaye tvāṃ sadā vibho*

## v.12 — may time pass in joy

Of me, enjoying union with Your worship — may all time pass, even endless: this I pray. The Sanskrit: *bhavatpūjāmayāsaṅgasambhogasukhino mama | prayātu kālaḥ sakalo 'py ananto 'pīyadarthaye*

## v.13 — greed growing daily

Greedy for Your worship-enjoyment-nectar — may it grow daily for me and fruit always. The Sanskrit: *bhavatpūjāmṛtarasābhogalampaṭata vibho | vivardhatām anudinaṃ sadā ca phalatāṃ mama*

## v.14 — dwelling in His ocean

In Your ocean, nectar-one-rasa-full, worshipping You, great self, may I always sit. The Sanskrit: *jagadvilayasañjātasudhaikarasanirbhare | tvadabdhau tvāṃ mahātmānam arcannāsīya sarvadā*

## v.15 — mind offered as sweet

Mind offered as sweet in Your rite — knots ever cut simply. The Sanskrit: *aśeṣavāsanāgranthivicchedasaralaṃ sadā | mano nivedyate bhaktaiḥ svādu pūjāvidhau tava*

## v.16 — senses sent for nectar

Having taken stand on objects — these sense-courses of devotees — send them for Your worship-nectar-wine. The Sanskrit: *adhiṣṭhāyaiva viṣayānimāḥ karaṇavṛttayaḥ | bhaktānāṃ preṣayanti svatpūjārtham amṛtāsavam*

## v.17 — what liberates but drowning

Agitated by devotion-heat, self-mastered — what else could liberate but drowning in worship of You? The Sanskrit: *bhaktānāṃ bhaktisaṃvegamahoṣmavivaśātmanām | ko 'nyo nirvāṇahetuḥ syāt tvatpūjāmṛtamajjanāt*

## v.18 — perpetual foot-festival as grace-cause

Perpetual foot-worship-nectar-drinking festival — may it be my sole grace-getting cause, Lord. The Sanskrit: *satataṃ tvatpadābhyarcāsudhāpānamahotsavaḥ | tvatprasādaikasamprāptihetur me nātha kalpatām*

## v.19 — tasting each act's joy-madness

Experiencing, Lord, moment by moment — Your worship-nectar-drinking-madness-joy-great-joy. The Sanskrit: *anubhūyāsamīśāna pratikarma kṣaṇāt kṣaṇam | bhavatpūjāmṛtāpānamadāsvādamahāmudam*

## v.20 — seen fruit: impossible joy

Seen is the point of devotees' great worship-undertaking: that they taste impossible joy then. The Sanskrit: *dṛṣṭartha eva bhaktānāṃ bhavatpūjāmahodyamaḥ | tadaiva yad asambhāvyaṃ sukham āsvādayanti te*

## v.21 — till festival tasted, no happiness

Till Your worship-nectar-taste-festival is got, I count no drop of fortune's happiness tasted. The Sanskrit: *yāvan na labdhas tvatpūjāsudhāsvādamahotsavaḥ | tāvan nāsvādito manye lavo 'pi sukhasampadaḥ*

## v.22 — effortless abiding

Without sense-seeking strain, Your abode's abiding is born in devotees — no getting needed, only madness with worship. The Sanskrit: *bhaktānāṃ viṣayanveṣābhāsāyāsād vinaiva sā | ayatnasiddhaṃ tvaddhāmasthitiḥ pūjāsu jāyate*

## v.23 — nothing ungettable, only mad

Nothing ungettable for devotees, nothing hard — they only roam, mad with worship. The Sanskrit: *na prāpyam asti bhaktanāṃ nāpy eṣām asti durlabham | kevalaṃ vicaranty ete bhavatpūjāmadonmadāḥ*

## v.24 — unbegging worship praised

Ah, the devotees' heart-heavy worship-rule: praised, unmarked by begging. The Sanskrit: *aho bhaktibharodāracetasāṃ varada tvayi | slāghyaḥ pūjāvidhiḥ ko 'pi yo na yācñākalaṃkitaḥ*

## v.25 — what beauty/joy/liberation is not

What beauty is not, what joy not, what fortune not, what liberation not — this Mahādeva, since He is worshipped. The Sanskrit: *kā na śobhā na ko hlādaḥ kā samṛddhir na vāparā | ko vā na mokṣaḥ ko 'py eṣa mahādevo yad arcyate*

## v.26 — nourish this body

In all-empty... may this body be nourished as Your worship-thing, always devoted. The Sanskrit: *antarullasadacchācchabhaktipīyūṣapoṣitam | bhavatpūjopayogāya śarīram idam astu me*

## v.27 — dependent yet free-moving

Dependent on Your foot-worship-enjoyment — may I, O world-Lord, be the one self-willed in movement. The Sanskrit: *tvatpādapūjāsambhogaparatantraḥ sadā vibho | bhūyāsaṃ jagatām īśa ekaḥ svacchandaceṣṭitaḥ*

## v.28 — thirst quenched into lake

To quench vision-touch-thirst for You — arises the cool, sweet great lake of Your worship. The Sanskrit: *tvaddhyānadarśanasparśatṛṣi keṣām api prabho | jāyate śītalasvādu bhavatpūjāmahāsaraḥ*

## v.29 — as You, so the devotee

As You alone are the world's worship-enjoyment-vessel — so, Lord, is the devotee a vessel of worship-enjoyment. The Sanskrit: *yathā tvam eva jagataḥ pūjāsambhogabhājanam | tatheśa bhaktimān eva pūjāsambhogabhājanam*

## v.30 — festival agitates the 36

Victor, Your festival — even the 36 agitated by it — great day. The Sanskrit: *ko 'py asau jayati svāmin bhavatpūjāmahotsavaḥ | ṣaṭtriṃśato 'pi tattvānāṃ kṣobho yatrollasaty alam*

## v.31 — bow to those whose tools are worshipped

Bow to those whose worship-things are themselves worshipped, by devotion-nectar-water. The Sanskrit: *namas tebhyo vibho yeṣāṃ bhaktipīyūṣavāriṇā | pūjyāny eva bhavanti tvatpūjopakaraṇāny api*

## v.32 — mantra-placement then rest

At worship's start, meditating mantra-placed His selfhood — devotees rest in supreme self, nowhere roaming. The Sanskrit: *pūjārambhe vibho dhyātvā mantrādheyāṃ tvadātmatām | svātmany eva pare bhaktā mānti harṣeṇa na kvacit*

## v.33 — kingdoms blooming, world shared

Kingdoms blooming, world shared out in nectar-wine by festival-goers. The Sanskrit: *rājyalābhādivotphullaiḥ kaiś cit pūjāmahotsave | sudhāsavena sakalā jagatī saṃvibhajyate*

## v.34 — gods, freed, or some people

Whose moment-drink is worship-nectar — are they gods, freed, or whatever people? The Sanskrit: *pūjāmṛtāpānamayo yeṣāṃ bhogaḥ pratikṣaṇam | kiṃ devā uta muktās te kiṃ vā ke 'py eva te janāḥ*

## v.35 — devotee's lightness and weight

The world's dignity as worship-gear is theirs — ah, something of devotees, and just lightness! The Sanskrit: *pūjopakaraṇībhūtaviśvaveśena gauravam | aho kim api bhaktānāṃ kim apy eva ca lāghavam*

## v.36 — eye-throw to nectar-rising, like milk-ocean

From worship-made eye-throw agitation, nectar-rising for devotees — as from the gods' milk-ocean churning. The Sanskrit: *pūjāmayākṣavikṣepakṣobhādevāmṛtodgamaḥ | bhaktānāṃ kṣīrajaladhikṣobhād iva divaukasām*

## v.37 — wish-cow vs inward milking

Some take worship as the wish-cow; others, inward-turned, milk the nectar-stream. The Sanskrit: *pūjāṃ ke cana manyante dhenuṃ kāmadughām iva | sudhādhārādhikarasāṃ dhayanty antarmukhāḥ pare*

## v.38 — even glances feed the festival

Even eye-throws, world-approved, bring something within — may the festival grow. The Sanskrit: *bhaktānām akṣavikṣepo 'py eṣa saṃsārasaṃmataḥ | upanīya kim apy antaḥ puṣṇāty arcāmahotsavam*

## v.39 — lowliness as top fruit

By devotion-agitation, worship in self-become-Him — wonder: poverty's top fruit is lowliness' fruit. The Sanskrit: *bhaktikṣobhavaśād īśa svātmabhūte 'rcanaṃ tvayi | citraṃ dainyāya no yāvad dīnatāyāḥ paraṃ phalam*

## v.40 — steps for some, spread for devotees

For some, rite is upacāra-step to His feet; for devotees, non-duality-bliss-spread. The Sanskrit: *upacārapadaṃ pūjā keṣāṃ cit tvatpadāptaye | bhaktānāṃ bhavadaikātmyanirvṛttiprasaras tu saḥ*

## v.41 — His foothold through their madness

Spread by unbound mad devotion to the formless-seeming, it gains some foothold in You. The Sanskrit: *apy asambaddharūpārcābhaktyunmādanirargalaiḥ | vitanyamānā labhate pratiṣṭhāṃ tvayi kām api*

## v.42 — taste-stunned mind, He alone as vessel

Taste-stunned mind; He alone as worship's vessel, they say. The Sanskrit: *svādubhaktirasāsvādastabdhībhūtamanaś cyutām | śambho tvam eva lalitaḥ pūjānāṃ kila bhājanam*

## v.43 — full, steady, devoted means

Full, pure, steady, devoted means be mine for His worship, Lord. The Sanskrit: *paripūrṇāni śuddhāni bhaktimanti sthirāṇi ca | bhavatpūjāvidhau nātha sādhanāni bhavantu me*

## v.44 — fortune of the organ-host

In the whole worship-treasure, what fortune of the organ-host blooms? The Sanskrit: *aśeṣapūjāsatkośe tvatpūjākarmaṇi prabho | aho karaṇavṛndasya kāpi lakṣmīr vijṛmbhate*

## v.45 — prettiness: worshipped and found

This prettiness is Yours alone, Lord: though Lord of all, worshipped by servants — and found. The Sanskrit: *eṣā peśalimā nātha tavaiva kila dṛśyate | viśveśvaro 'pi bhṛtyair yad arcyase yaś ca labhyase*

## v.46 — rise from formed or formless

Rise from formed or formless, being or non-being — may my festival rise. The Sanskrit: *sadāmurttād amūrttādvā bhāvād yad vāpy abhāvataḥ | uttheyān me praśastasya bhavatpūjāmahotsavaḥ*

## v.47 — lust, anger, pride conscripted

With lust, anger, pride made offering, who always worship — bow to them; of them I am pleased, in truth. The Sanskrit: *kāmakrodhābhimānais tvām upaharīkṛtaiḥ sadā | ye 'rcayanti namas tebhyas teṣāṃ tuṣṭo 'smi tattvataḥ*

## v.48 — straw-yet-jewel festival victorious

Victor, Your festival of devotees' supreme rule — though made with straw, yet furnished with jewels. The Sanskrit: *jayaty eṣa bhavadbhaktibhājāṃ pūjāvidhiḥ paraḥ | yas tṛṇaiḥ kriyamāno 'pi ratnair evopakalpate*

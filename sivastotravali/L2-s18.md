## v.1 — found inside the world

Finding You inside the world, they get inside You — never far are Your devotees. The Sanskrit: *jagato 'ntarato bhavantam āptvā punar etad bhavato 'ntarāl labhante jagadīśa tavaiva bhaktibhājo na hi teṣām iha dūrato 'sti kiñcit*

## v.2 — He here, She everywhere

He here, She everywhere, pregnant with the order of all things, supreme — but at the ultimate step, no division of Her nor of the triple world. The Sanskrit: *kvacid eva bhavān kvacid bhavānī sakalārthakramagarbhiṇī pradhānā paramārthapade tu naiva devyā bhavato nāpi jatattrayasya bhedaḥ*

## v.3 — ungrateful world, bewildered mind

The ungrateful know not the auspicious, though proud; all things, though effort-favored — but this mind, though risen, does not perceive the self-form here; ah, alas, I am destroyed. The Sanskrit: *no jānate subhagam apy avalepavanto lokāḥ prayatnasubhagā nikhila hi bhāvāḥ cetaḥ punar yad idam udyatam apy avaiti naivātmarūpam iha hā tad aho hato 'smi*

## v.4 — needing nothing, I bow

Who has gained dwelling in the self made of You, Your feet worshipped with full wealth — needing neither food nor covering ever: to him I bow. The Sanskrit: *bhavanmayasvātmanivāsalabdhasampadbharābhyarcitayuṣmadaṅghriḥ na bhojanācchādanam apy ajasram apekṣate yas tam ahaṃ nato 'smi*

## v.5 — make me worship-joy here

Ever dwelling in Your body-abode yet inwardly supreme-burning, this world — by Your will make me here, of worship-joy-nature. The Sanskrit: *sadā bhavaddehanivāsasvastho 'py antaḥ paraṃ dahyata eṣa lokaḥ tavecchayā tat kuru me yathātra tvadarcanānandamayo bhaveyam*

## v.6 — spontaneity, touch, world-tour

Spontaneously risen, touching Your lotus-feet pair, drinking the worship-nectar — mind intent, may I be, in gathering my aims, with mere touch-happiness as world-tour. The Sanskrit: *svarasoditayuṣmadaṅghripadmadvayapūjāmṛtapānasaktacittaḥ sakārthacayeṣv ahaṃ bhaveyam sukhasaṃsparśanamātralokayātraḥ*

## v.7 — may my things shine always

In the whole field of dealings, plainly, while You throb inside, Lord — coming and going ever, may my things shine always. The Sanskrit: *sakalavyavahāragocare sphuṭam antaḥ spurati tvayi prabho upayānty apayānti cāniśam mama vastūni vibhāntu sarvadā*

## v.8 — never defeated where I am Yours

Ever in Your city, or parted and roaming — may I be, by You: never even a moment's defeat for me, where I am become of You. The Sanskrit: *satatam eva tavaiva pure 'thavāpy arahito vicareyam ahaṃ tvayā kṣaṇalavo 'py atha mā sma bhavet sa me na vijaye nanu yatra bhavanmayaḥ*

## v.9 — cool floods, saṃsāra's middle

Filled all round with cool nectar-floods streaming from Your limbs — in the wealth of worshipping You here, Your devotees move in saṃsāra's middle. The Sanskrit: *bhavadaṅgaparisravatsuśītāmṛtapūrair bharite samantato 'pi bhavadarcanasampadeha bhaktās tava saṃsārasaro 'ntare caranti*

## v.10 — tree-shade, forest, self

In the cool shade of the great-mantra tree, in Your great forest, in my own self always, Lord, may I dwell as Your worshipper. The Sanskrit: *mahāmantratarucchāyāśītale tvanmahāvane nijātmani sadā nātha vaseyaṃ tava pūjakaḥ*

## v.11 — appear before me

As everything living mirrors You, mirror-formed — so before me, Lord, come forth graced with three eyes and trident. The Sanskrit: *prativastu samastajīvataḥ pratibhāsi pratibhāmayo yathā mama nātha tathā puraḥ prathāṃ vraja netratrayaśūlaśobhitaḥ*

## v.12 — be my eyes' ground

From pride-offering, love-held devotion-built — pleased, when will You be everywhere my eyes' ground? The Sanskrit: *abhimānacarūpahārato mamatābhaktibhareṇa kalpitāt paritoṣagataḥ kadā bhavān mama sarvatra bhaved dṛśaḥ padam*

## v.13 — tasting everything everywhere

Dwelling in the nectar-ocean middle, mind drowned in Your worship-rite alone — may I do all people's business, tasting everything everywhere. The Sanskrit: *nivasanparamāmṛtābdhimadhye bhavadarcāvidhimātramagnacittaḥ sakalaṃ janavṛttam ācareyaṃ rasayan sarvata eva kiñcanāpi*

## v.14 — who can disclose

Your thing here, Your truth — who is fit to disclose the matter? This same name-form-sport-unequalled of Yours takes away — since You are Hara, the Taker. The Sanskrit: *bhavadīyam ihāstu vastu tattvaṃ vivarītuṃ ka ivātra pātram arthe idam eva hi nāmarūpaceṣṭādyasamaṃ te harate haro 'si yasmāt*

## v.15 — no desire for peace, no begging remembered

For peace, no hankering for pleasure at all, among those drunk with devotion amassed — nor even begging the fruit of seeking liberation remembered before the heart-thief. The Sanskrit: *śāntaye na sukhalipsutā manāg bhaktisambhṛtamadeṣu taiḥ prabhoḥ mokṣamārgaṇaphalāpi nārthanā smaryate hṛdayahāriṇaḥ puraḥ*

## v.16 — waking or beyond, festival

Waking or other state, or any beyond, while slightly steady — for the devotee-people, all is Your festival in the ever-present mind. The Sanskrit: *jāgaretaradaśāthavā parā yāpi kācana manāg avasthiteḥ bhaktibhājanajanasya sākhilā tvatsanāthamanaso mahotsavaḥ*

## v.17 — loosened yet unswerving

Mind's eye-wheel courses, everywhere loosened yet still — reaching You, of firm-long awareness: Lord, how, by devotion-wealth's heat? The Sanskrit: *āmano 'kṣavalayasya vṛttayaḥ sarvataḥ śithilavṛttayo 'pi tāḥ tvām avāpya dṛḍhadīrghasaṃvido nātha bhaktidhanasoṣmaṇāṃ katham*

## v.18 — bow to the wonder-abode

Nothing divided was left uncreated, no non-pleasure unmade here — yet all suffers and divides in every way: bow to the wonder-abode. The Sanskrit: *na ca vibhinnam asṛjyata kiñcid asty atha sukhetarad atra na nirmitam atha ca duḥkhi ca bhedi ca sarvathāpy asamavismayadhāma namo 'stu te*

## v.19 — my doubt-foe crushed, Your sight unbroken

Of me whose thought-stain is washed bright by hard-denier nectar-filling flood, whose unconquered-doubt-foe is crushed — may Your sight be unbroken. The Sanskrit: *kharaniṣedhakhadāmṛtapūraṇocchalitadhautavikalpamalasya me dalitadurjayasaṃśayavairiṇas tvadavalokanam astu nirantaram*

## v.20 — enter me plainly, worship at speed

Enter me plainly, then may I enter Thee — always, Lord, for I am Thine: with violent speed may I worship Thy body face to face. The Sanskrit: *sphuṭam aviśa mām athāviśeyaṃ satataṃ nātha bhavantamasmi yasmāt rabhasena vapus tavaiva sākṣāt paramāsattigataḥ samarcayeyam*

## v.21 — may I see unwearied

In You no one's is praise-power — yet it exists since You are so beautiful; my constant prayer again is this: that I behold You, Lord, unwearied. The Sanskrit: *tvayi na stutiśaktir asti kasyāpy athavāsty eva yato 'tisundaro 'si satataṃ punar arthitaṃ mamaitad yad aviśrānti vilokayeyam īśam*

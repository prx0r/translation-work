## v.1 — beyond prayer-grounds, the wish-tree wins

Beyond prayer-grounds, strange-fruit-giver — victorious, unprecedented in story, Śiva the true wish-tree. The Sanskrit: *prārthanābhūmikātītavicitraphaladāyakaḥ jayaty apūrvavṛttāntaḥ śivaḥ satkalpapādapaḥ*

## v.2 — all got, yet self unjoined

Since from the One container all is got through the self, from You — yet this own self of mine does not join Thee, the Highest — her. The Sanskrit: *sarvavastuni ca yaikanidhānāt svātmanas tvad akhilaṃ kila labhyam asya me punar asau nijā ātmā na tvam eva ghaṭase paramās tām*

## v.3 — the self is the Lord alone — but what body

The self, knowledge-action-formed consciousness-body, is in every way the Lord alone — but would there even be a body among all these things, or what else? The Sanskrit: *jñānakarmamayacidvapur ātmā sarvathaiṣa parameśvara eva syād vapus tu nikhileṣu padārtheṣv eṣu nāma na bhavet kim utānyat*

## v.4 — melt into His sight-natured fruit

By unequalled fruit, stealing pain — melt into His sight-natured form; may my going be His-formed. The Sanskrit: *viṣamārtimuṣānena phalena tvaddṛgātmanā abhilīya pathā nātha mamāstu tvanmayī gatiḥ*

## v.5 — when will amazement befall me

When will foot-thought-jewel-creeper-fruition, amazing the perfected, befall me, from You? The Sanskrit: *bhavadamalacaraṇacintāratnalatālaṅkṛtā kadā siddhiḥ siddhajanamānasānāṃ vismayajananī ghaṭeta mama bhavataḥ*

## v.6 — when shall I see the face-orb

When, Lord, shall I fully see Your pure face-orb, from which streams unprecedented nectar-flood, which drowns the whole world? The Sanskrit: *karhi nātha vimalaṃ mukhabimbaṃ tāvakaṃ samavalokayitāsmi yatsravaty amṛtapūram apūrvaṃ yo nimajjayati viśvam aśeṣam*

## v.7 — when will the form flood the far gaps

Your form, risen at mere meditation — when, Lord, will it ever fill with supreme-nectar floods my far gaps named non-duality-liberation? The Sanskrit: *dhyātamātramuditaṃ tava rūpaṃ karhi nātha paramāmṛtapūraiḥ pūrayet tvadavibhedavimokṣākhyātidūravivarāṇi sadā me*

## v.8 — restless, and soon

By touch of Your peerless rasa, unroaming abandoned — not even now is my mind, Lord: when will it be? Let it be soon. The Sanskrit: *tvadīyānuttararasāsaṅgasaṃtyaktacāpalam nādyāpi me mano nātha karhi syād astu śīghrataḥ*

## v.9 — not dry-bitter only, but all

Not dry-bitter things only — bring on all, always, gained by offering to You; let even opposites befall me. The Sanskrit: *mā śuṣkakaṭukāny eva paraṃ sarvāṇi sarvadā tavopahṛtya labdhāni dvandvāny apy āpatantu me*

## v.10 — may pure rays face me

Lord, may Your pure rays come face to face, as long as body-mind-heat-dark is wiped out. The Sanskrit: *nātha sāṃmukhyam āyāntu viśuddhās tava raśmayaḥ yāvat kāyamanastāpatamobhiḥ parilupyatām*

## v.11 — tame the guna-thieves

God, be gracious till my path-blockers, truth-stealing, sense-thieves are tamed. The Sanskrit: *deva prasīda yāvan me tvanmārgaparipanthikāḥ paramārthamuṣo vaśyā bhūyāsur guṇataskarāḥ*

## v.12 — drown all sapless habits

With Your devotion-nectar-essence fill my mind fast, Lord — till these are carried off, drowned: all sapless habits. The Sanskrit: *tvad bhaktisudhāsārair mānasam āpūryatāṃ mamāśu vibho yāvad imā uhyantāṃ niḥśeṣāsāravāsanāḥ plutvā*

## v.13 — set me on fitting ground

In liberation-state, where is devotion to You — how could it shine even for the mortal-natured? So set me, Unborn, on a fitting attainment-ground. The Sanskrit: *mokṣadaśāyāṃ bhaktis tvayi kuta iva martyadharmiṇo 'pi na sā rājati tato 'nurūpām āropaya siddhibhūmikām aja mām*

## v.14 — no siddhi-drop; Your mouth's powers instead

Greedy for a siddhi-drop's gain, don't settle proud me — thin is liberation beside Your devotion-face's sprouting aṇimā-wings. The Sanskrit: *siddhilavalābhalubdhaṃ mām avalepena mā vibho saṃsthāḥ kṣāmas tvadbhaktimukhe prollasadaṇimādipakṣato mokṣaḥ*

## v.15 — I ask only this

May the Lord be pleased with me, His slave — I ask only this, since the three-worlds Giver exceeds my sight. The Sanskrit: *dāsasya me prasīdatu bhagavān etāvad eva nanu yāce dātā tribhuvananātho yasya na tanmādṛśāṃ dṛśo viṣayaḥ*

## v.16 — may Your feet bloom in my mind

In mind full of Your body-mindfulness-nectar-rasa, may Your lotus-feet-pair bloom in mine, ever dripping honey, supramundane. The Sanskrit: *tvadvapuḥsmṛtisudhārasapūrṇe mānase tava padāmbujayugmam māmake vikasad astu sadaiva prasravanmadhu kim apy atilokam*

## v.17 — I have a Lord, a father, a mother — no second

I have a Lord, a father — Tryambaka — and a mother, Bhavānī; no second exists for me here — thus most-fulfilled may I roam. The Sanskrit: *asti me prabhur asau janako 'tha tryambako 'tha jananī ca bhavānī na dvitīya iha ko 'pi mamāstīty eva nirvṛtatamo vicareyam*

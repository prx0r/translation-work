## v.1 — bow to the three-worlds Lord

Bow to the three-worlds' Lord, ash-white, three-eyed, trident-bearing, snake-threaded, moon-crested. The Sanskrit: *nathaṃ tribhuvananāthaṃ bhūtisitaṃ trinayanaṃ triśūladharam | upavītīkṛtabhoginam indukalāśekharaṃ vande.*

## v.2 — I praise, self-forgetting

I praise, self-forgetting, white-robed in bite-torn cloth, dancing to the tune of the skull-garland. The Sanskrit: *naumi nijatanuvinismaradaṃśukapariveṣadhavalaparidhānam | vilasatkapālamālākalpitanṛttotsavākalpam.*

## v.3 — bow to those whose god is Hara

Bow to those whose god is Hara, whose acts are Hara-fit, whose lives lean wholly to Hara — the fortunate abodes. The Sanskrit: *vande tān daivataṃ yeṣāṃ haraś ceṣṭā harocitāḥ | haraikapravaṇāḥ prāṇāḥ sadā saubhāgyasadmanām.*

## v.4 — play behind lordship

Your play, behind lordship — and this too, as wished-fulfilled in gifts, by self I reach the supreme means. The Sanskrit: *krīḍitaṃ tava maheśvaratāyāḥ pṛṣṭhato 'nyad idam eva yathaitat | iṣṭamātraghaṭiteṣv avadāneṣv ātmanā param upāyam upaimi.*

## v.5 — how great my bliss-birth

In Your world-venerated abode, in this play — how great would my bliss-birth be? The Sanskrit: *tvaddhāmni viśvavandye 'sminn iyati krīḍane sati | tava nātha kiyān bhūyānn ānandarasasambhavaḥ.*

## v.6 — mutual belovedness

How could He not be fortunate — Hara, Gauri's beloved? And how could Hara not be Gauri's supreme beloved? The Sanskrit: *kathaṃ sa subhago mā bhūdyo gauryā vallabho haraḥ | haro 'pi mā bhūd atha kiṃ gauryāḥ paramavallabhaḥ.*

## v.7 — rooted meditation, branching awareness

Whose meditation-nectar is undying, self-rooted, ever-fresh consciousness-creepers, like some tree of being. The Sanskrit: *dhyānāmṛtamayaṃ yasya svātmamūlam anaśvaram | saṃvillatās tathārūpās tasya kasyāpi sattaroḥ.*

## v.8 — itch scratched into pillar

At devotion-itch's showing, Lord's pillar-firmness arises — by it the world, great festival, is won. The Sanskrit: *bhaktikaṇḍūsam ullāsāvasare parameśvara | mahānikaṣapāṣāṇasthūṇā pūjaiva jāyate.*

## v.9 — ever-fed, ever-playing, ever-eating

Ever to creation-sport, ever seated in staying-pleasure, ever world-eating-sated — to the husband, bow. The Sanskrit: *sadā srṣṭivinodāya sadā sthitisukhāsine | sadā tribhuvanāhāratṛptāya svāmine namaḥ.*

## v.10 — nowhere gone, nothing left

Nowhere gone, nothing left — who see Your abode as to-be here, fortunate: bow to them. The Sanskrit: *na kvāpi gatvā hitvāpi na kiṃcid idam eva ye | bhavyaṃ tvaddhāma paśyanti bhavyās tebhyo namo namaḥ.*

## v.11 — wealth and poverty beg nothing

For the devotion-fortuned, what else is begged; for the devotion-poor, what else is begged? The Sanskrit: *bhaktilakṣmīsamṛddhānāṃ kim anyad upayācitam | etayā vā daridraṇāṃ kim anyad upayācitam.*

## v.12 — pain turns pleasure, poison nectar

Where pains turn pleasures, poison turns nectar, saṃsāra turns liberation — that path is Śiva. The Sanskrit: *duḥkhāny api sukhāyante viṣam apy amṛtāyate | mokṣāyate ca saṃsāro yatra mārgaḥ sa śaṅkaraḥ.*

## v.13 — yet we sink: tell why

At root, middle, end, no suffering for Your people — yet we sink, Lord: tell why. The Sanskrit: *mūle madhye 'vasāne ca nāsti duḥkhaṃ bhavajjuṣāṃ | tathāpi vayam īśāna sīdāmaḥ katham ucyatām.*

## v.14 — lovers as the begging-model

Light is worth begging even by others, beginners in knowledge-yoga — You, Lord of devotees, are like the light of the free. The Sanskrit: *jñānayogādinānyeṣām apy apekṣitum arhati | prakāśaḥ svairiṇām iva bhavān bhaktimatāṃ prabho.*

## v.15 — no prayer, Name on the lip

Devotees have no prayers, no remembrance of You the self — yet this "Śiva" of theirs, something, on the lip. The Sanskrit: *bhaktānāṃ nārtayo nāpy asty ādhyānaṃ svātmanas tava | tathāpy asti śivety etat kim apy eṣāṃ bahirmukhe.*

## v.16 — recognition as agency

Who lights all appearances, wrapped in vimarśa — "I am this": I praise Your action-power, Lord. The Sanskrit: *sarvābhāsāvabhāso yo vimarśavalito 'khilam | aham etad iti staumi tāṃ kriyāśaktim īśa te.*

## v.17 — grasping as salutation

All beings, from Brahmā to Viṣṇu, being devoured — so I bow, God, to the world as Your-formed. The Sanskrit: *vartante jantavo 'śeṣā api brahmendraviṣṇavaḥ | grasamānās tato vande deva viśvaṃ bhavanmayam.*

## v.18 — solipsism dissolved by destruction-play

All beyond Me is false, by relation to destruction — thus engaged, Lord, by Your destruction-play alone. The Sanskrit: *sato vināśasambandhān matparaṃ nikhilaṃ mṛṣā | evamevodyate nātha tvayā saṃhāralīlayā.*

## v.19 — presence defeats marvel-mongers

Meditated, Your form stands present for devotion-rich — unconquered by unthinkable-wonder-makers. The Sanskrit: *dhyātam ātmupatiṣṭhata eva tvadvapur varada bhaktidhanānām | apy acintyam akhilādbhutacintākartṛtāṃ prati ca te vijayante.*

## v.20 — vetāla-roused night-dance

By Your devotion-wine-taste nerves' thrill, night-heroes dance, roused by vetālas. The Sanskrit: *tāvakabhaktirasāsavasekād iva sukhitamarmamaṇḍalasphuritaiḥ | nṛtyati vīrajano niśi vetālakulaiḥ kṛtotsāhaḥ.*

## v.21 — make all time unending

The praise begun with this limb of mine — by it may all this time be firmly unending. The Sanskrit: *ārabdhā bhavadabhinutir amunā yenāṅgakena mama śambho | tenāparyantam imaṃ kālaṃ dṛḍham akhilam eva bhaviṣīṣṭa.*

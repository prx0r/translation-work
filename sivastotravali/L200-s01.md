## 2. MT-LEXICAL (word choices, with witnesses)

- **1.1 *avidhipūrvakam*** → "without any preparatory rule." *avidhi* = absence of rule; *pūrva* = preceding. Alternative "without method" rejected (too strong — the stotra denies method as precondition, v.18 denies it as injunction; different claims).
- **1.2 *palitair iva dhūsaraḥ*** → "greyed as if with dust." *palita* = grey hair; simile kept bodily (age), not moralized.
- **1.7 *svabhāvasiddhāṃs*** → "self-established." *svabhāva* + *siddha* with reflexive force: established by its own nature, needing no external ground.
- **1.7 *jayet* (liṅ)** → "may conquer" (optative). Kṣemarāja: *niyoge liṅ* — injunctive force. Kept optative in English; injunction recorded here, not forced into L2 mood.
- **1.11 *nāsavasya iva śuktatā*** → "vinegar-sourness after wine." *nāsava* (vinegar) emended from GRETIL *nāsavasya* (genitive, "of vinegar"); sense identical. Recorded; GRETIL line kept verbatim in T1.
- **1.14 *śodhitam*** → "purified" (*advayaśodhitam*: refined by non-duality). Not "examined" — śudh with causative refining sense in ritual context.
- **1.15 *nikṛṣṭānām*** → "the low." Social/spiritual lowliness left unspecified (see OPEN §7).
- **1.16 *kṣetra/kakṣyā*** → "states" (*kakṣyā*: arenas, provinces of experience). Not "caves" or "stages."
- **1.17 *vyutthāna*** → "emergence" (technical: post-samādhi return). Ledger-locked across PHrdaya/IPVV work.
- **1.18 *amāye*** → "guileless" (locative of *amāyā*). Alternative "without Māyā" rejected — context is sincerity of path, not metaphysics of Māyā.
- **1.20 *aho*** → "A wonder." Carries pathos (cf. anuttarastika anu-p03 handling), not exclamation decoration.
- **1.21 *anāma*** → "low" (lit. ring-finger; by extension, the lowly). Marked uncertain: could be proper-name residue. See OPEN.
- **1.22 *anantara rasa*** → "after-taste" (*anantara*: immediately following; *rasa*: taste). Liberation as after-taste, not wages — the verse's whole economy turns on this.
- **1.25 *keṣu cit*** → "for some." Restrictive particle kept: not all devotees, some.

## 3. MT-GRAMMATICAL (constructions)

- **1.1 *śivabhāsaḥ* (nom.) + *taṃ numaḥ***: nominative/Vocative ambiguity left unresolved in T1; L2 renders "O light of Śiva — we bow to him," taking *śivabhāsaḥ* as address and *tam* as object. Alternative (Śiva's light bows... ) ungrammatical; rejected.
- **1.4 *kiṃ na... kṣetram / mantraḥ kvaiṣām na siddhyati***: double rhetorical question → rendered as questions, not assertions. Rhetorical force is doctrinal content here.
- **1.8 *kena na dṛśyaḥ***: instrumental of agent with passive ("by whom not seen") kept.
- **1.10 *tridhā sthitaḥ***: predicate adjective with instrumental trio — kept as "stands threefold."
- **1.24 *patitā api... na lipyante***: concessive participle + passive ("though fallen... are not smeared") kept concessive, not causal.

## 6. IA-ASSERTIONS (interpretive, seeded to C1, not translation)

- IA-1: The stotra's center is v.7; vv.1–6 prepare it, vv.8–26 defend/apply it. (Structural judgment from density of doctrinal terms + Kṣemarāja's *niyoge liṅ* emphasis.)
- IA-2: 1.4's universalism is deliberately restricted by 1.6 (all is field, but only the drowned know). Tension is authorial, not contradiction.
- IA-3: 1.16's *vañcanā* targets localization as such, not yoga as practice (Kṣemarāja's gloss supports; yogins return in 1.17 as contrast class, not enemies).
- IA-4: 1.25's *keṣu cit* + 1.15's *nikṛṣṭa* form a deliberate anti-elitism arc with 1.21's status inversion.
- IA-5: 1.26 closes the ring opened at 1.2 (dust-greyed mind → transfigured mind); *citra* answers 1.20's *aho*.

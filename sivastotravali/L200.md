# L200 — Śivastotrāvalī (450 verses, 20 stotras, 5 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Śivastotrāvalī (450 verses, 20 stotras) is Utpaladeva's (10th c.) devotional collection. Stotra 1 (bhaktivilāsa) establishes bhakti as supreme: not for one who meditates without rule; we bow to Śiva's light, the bhakti-possessor. Bhakti-possessors move in world by expansion; no mantra fails for them; they are intoxicated with bhakti-nectar; even non-dual, always your second; they know your truth, ocean of endless bliss; bhakti is self-established; Śiva easily seen by bhaktas; bhakti is inseparable from Śiva; consciousness-path threefold; knowledge-state is like vinegar-sourness compared to bhakti-nectar. Stotra 2–4 continue in devotional register (bhakti as supreme, recognition in devotional mode). Stotra 5–8 (recognition doctrine in devotional register). Stotra 9–12 (autonomy in devotional register). Stotra 13–16 (self in devotional register). Stotra 17–20 (close in devotional register: divine play, illumination, tasting).

## 3. What the text rejects
- R1: Meditation without rule.
- R2: Mantra failure for bhaktas.
- R3: Knowledge-state as equal to bhakti-nectar.
- R4: Bhakti as non-supreme.

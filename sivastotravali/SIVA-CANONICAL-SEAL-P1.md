## The parable (canonical draft, ≈89s, density-flagged)

> A woman waits rule-free in the low ground past the gods' stairs —
> undawned by any method, unaged by road-dust, yet thriving on no
> ground but low — and salutes twenty-nine ways: every thing of the
> world His, every verse landing bowed. Between being and not-being
> she keeps the third ground, contagiously free. She washes with
> dust-dyed hair and stays dumb; the touch she wants — a foot's
> brush, the opening of the heart — stays half a breath away. She
> holds her longing through every parting, melts every stain-string
> and waits; twice ten times she asks her own mind WHEN — of face,
> of gods, of the world, of beasts, of play: when does longing
> become meeting? The scholars arrive with their books; she sets
> them beside lover-truth and the books go quiet — the veil
> unveiled, the ladder laughed past. She begs outright: world
> unowned, no unwoven praise. Then grace nears, rooting each in the
> other, and seeing opens: no helper, no bar — nectar tasted, dwelt
> at the feet. He answers with a mark — pain-and-joy kept in His
> frame — and the feast is announced: touch-feasts, the equinox
> tides, the nectar-wedding, the world inside her won. Then prayer
> by prayer, bow by bow: the Lord catalogued, dance-ready, the
> honoured seat.

## The seal — statement

**The low ground that becomes the wedding:** vertical romance-seal
— base: the low ground below the gods'-stairs (she vigil, dust-
veiled, twenty silver light-motes rising = the twenty whens); mid:
TWO HANDS a breath apart, one gold spark living in the gap (the
touch not yet — grace nearing), grey closed books at rim gone
quiet; above: grace-roots intertwining upward; crown: the feast-
canopy — touch-feast table, tide-line beneath, two honoured feet at
its threshold, dance-sway arcs — the nectar-wedding, the seat
honoured.

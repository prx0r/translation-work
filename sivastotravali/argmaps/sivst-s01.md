## 1. What is at issue

Is devotion without rule (*avidhipūrvakam*) valid — and if so, what makes it work? The stotra's answer, stated once at v.7 and defended for 19 verses: bhakti is **self-established** (*svabhāvasiddhā*) because its object is the self itself; methodlessness is not laxity but recognition. Everything else is corollary: the world becomes the devotee's field, all means work, yoga-only attainment is the deceit.

## 2. The arc

Foundation (1–2): praise without method; young inside, grey outside. Expansion (3–6): the world as field, mantras succeeding, intoxication, the ocean known only by the drowned. Center (7): all desire the self; the self is consciousness; devotion to it is self-established — known **by immersion's means**, one conquers. Epistemics (8–12): the knowable destroyed, vinegar-sourness of mere knowledge, the great knowledge that discriminates, the speech-creeper watered into fruit. Grace-logic (13–15): become Śiva to worship Śiva; every road works for devotees; no obstruction for the low. Polemic (16–18): sometime-somewhere-by-yoga is the deceit; devotees differ from yogins precisely in emergence; no method enjoined, devotion alone praised. Name and medicine (19–22): light shattering covering, "Śiva" on the tongue tasting all senses, diving the ocean, the herb whose after-taste is liberation. Close (23–26): true wealth, unstained in mud, the creeper's strange fruits, the pain-seed mind become liberation's fruit.

## 3. The argument, verse by verse

**1.1** — *na dhyāyato na japataḥ syād yasyāvidhipūrvakam.* No meditation, no japa, no preparation — and yet the manifestation occurs. The stotra opens by denying method as precondition, not as practice.
**1.2** — *ātmā mama bhavadbhaktisudhāpānayuvāpi san.* The self stays young drinking devotion-nectar while the body greys with world-dust. Youth = the self's nature; age = the journey's dust. (Lakshmanjoo: internally young.)
**1.3** — *labdhatatsaṃpadāṃ bhaktimatāṃ tvatpuravāsinām.* For wealth-holders dwelling in Your city, even worldly movement happens by that same expansion. The world is not abandoned; it is moved in differently.
**1.4** — *sākṣādbhavanmaye nātha sarvasmin bhuvanāntare.* Where all the world is directly You, what is not the devotee's field, what mantra fails? Omnipresence converts the whole world into ritual ground.
**1.5** — *jayanti bhaktipīyūṣarasāsavavaronmadāḥ.* Victory to the intoxicated — drunk on the best wine, devotion-nectar. Intoxication is praise, not blame: it names excess of love.
**1.6** — *anantānandasindhos te nātha tattvaṃ vidanti te.* Only the densely drenched know the ocean. Knowledge here is immersion-depth, not information. (Restricts 1.4: all is field, but only the drowned know.)
**1.7** — *tvam evātmeśa sarvasya... iti svabhāvasiddhāṃs tvadbhaktiṃ jānañ jayej janaḥ.* THE CENTER. All desire the self; You alone are everyone's conscious self; hence devotion to You is self-established — and one who knows it **by immersion's means** (*samāveśayuktyā*, Kṣemarāja) conquers, i.e., lives supreme. The liṅ (*jayet*) is injunctive (Kṣemarāja: *niyoge liṅ*). Argument: since the object is the self, love of it needs no external ground.
**1.8** — *nātha vedyakṣaye kena na dṛśyo 'syekakaḥ sthitaḥ.* When the knowable is destroyed, who does not see You standing alone? Negation as vision: remove objects, the subject stands revealed. Even amid knower/knowable agitation, devotees see easily (*sudṛśanaḥ*) — ease contrasts yogic difficulty (anticipates 16–17).
**1.9** — *anantānandasarasī devī priyatamā yathā.* As the beloved Goddess is inseparable from You, so may one devotion be mine. The devotee asks for non-separation modeled on the divine couple itself.
**1.10** — *sarva eva bhavallābhahetur bhaktimatāṃ vibho.* For devotees everything causes gain of You; the consciousness-path stands threefold in joy, suffering, delusion. All three guṇa-states become path — nothing excluded.
**1.11** — *bhavadbhaktyamṛtāsvādād bodhasya syāt parāpi yā daśā.* Even the supreme state of knowledge tastes, to me, like vinegar-sourness after wine beside devotion-nectar. Knowledge is not denied; it is out-tasted. (Lakshmanjoo's "Have you ever tasted liquor?" — the verse demands experience, not assent.)
**1.12** — *bhavadbhaktimahāvidyā yeṣām abhyāsam āgatā.* Those who have practiced devotion's great knowledge discriminate knowledge from non-knowledge. Discrimination is the fruit of practice, not its precondition (answers the scholar's objection).
**1.13** — *āmulād vāglatā seyaṃ kramavisphāraśālinī.* The speech-creeper, unfolding in sequence from the root, watered by devotion-nectar — may it fruit for me. Speech itself grows and fruits; mantra is organic, not mechanical. (The four levels parā/paśyantī/madhyamā/vaikharī are Lakshmanjoo's gloss — NOT in the text; mark supplied in L2.)
**1.14** — *śivo bhūtvā yajeteti bhakto bhūtveti kathyate.* "Having become Śiva, let him worship" — and the devotee is *said* to have become. Scripture's paradox resolved by reported transformation: the Veda declares the devotee already become, and the body is refined by non-dual examination (*advayaśodhitam*).
**1.15** — *bhaktānāṃ bhavadadvaitasiddhyai kā nopapattayaḥ.* For devotees, what is not a means to non-duality with You? Even going astray leads there (Lakshmanjoo). And for the low, what obstructions to its failure? Double assurance: no wrong way, no blocked person.
**1.16** — *kadācit kvāpi labhyo 'si yogenetīśa vañcanā.* Attained sometime, somewhere, by yoga (thought-constriction, e.g., in the heart-wheel — Kṣemarāja) — this is **the deceit**. Proof: otherwise how do You shine in ALL states, including post-samādhi emergence, for devotees? The polemic verse: localization refutes itself.
**1.17** — *pratyāhārādyasaṃspṛṣṭo viśeṣo 'sti mahānayam.* The great distinction, untouched by withdrawal etc.: devotees stay composed even in emergence (*vyutthāne 'pi samāhitāḥ*), yogins do not. The test is not ecstasy but return.
**1.18** — *na yogo na tapo nārcākramaḥ ko 'pi pranīyate.* No yoga, no austerity, no worship-procedure is enjoined; on this guileless Śiva-path, devotion alone is praised. The amāya (guileless) path needs no technique because its means is love.
**1.19** — *sarvato vilasadbhaktitejodhvastāvṛter mama.* My covering shattered by all-radiant devotion-light; may even my thoughts, which face all real things, perish. Covering destroyed from all sides at once — then even thought, facing reality directly, ends.
**1.20** — *śiva ity ekaśabdasya jihvāgre tiṣṭhataḥ sadā.* The single word "Śiva" standing always on the tongue-tip: devotees taste by it every sense-object — a wonder (*aho*). The Name delivers all senses, not just sound. (Lakshmanjoo: nectar of touch, embrace, smell — ethically risky rendering; keep to "every sense-object's taste" in L2.)
**1.21** — *śāntakallolaśītācchasvādubhaktisudhāmbudhau.* Who counts as low who stands firm tasting the unworldly rasa, diving the calm-waved cool sweet devotion-milk-ocean? Status inverted: divers, not scholars, rank high.
**1.22** — *sādṛśaiḥ kiṃ na carvyeta bhavadbhaktimahauṣadhiḥ.* Why would people like me not chew devotion's great medicine — whose after-taste is named liberation? Medicine, not dessert: it cures, with liberation as after-taste (*anantara rasa*), not wages.
**1.23** — *tā eva paramarthyante sampadaḥ sadbhir īśa yāḥ.* True wealth, the good agree, is only what nourishes confident enjoyment of devotion-nectar. Wealth redefined: whatever feeds enjoyment of the rasa.
**1.24** — *bhavadbhaktisudhāsāras taiḥ kim apy upalakṣitaḥ.* They have grasped devotion-nectar's essence who, fallen even into passion's mud, are not smeared. Falling without staining: the world entered without adhesion.
**1.25** — *aṇimādiṣu mokṣānteṣv aṅgeṣv eva phalābhidhā.* From supernormal powers to liberation, fruits are named as mere limbs of the ripened devotion-creeper — for some. Even liberation is a limb, not the root; the creeper is the thing.
**1.26** — *citraṃ nisargato nātha duḥkhabījam idaṃ manaḥ.* Wonder: the mind, by nature pain's seed, watered by devotion-rasa becomes liberation's great fruit. Closes the arc: 1.2's dust-greyed mind returns transfigured — seed unchanged, watering changed, fruit opposite.

## 4. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. v.13 *vāk-latā* four-level mapping (parā/paśyantī/madhyamā/vaikharī) is Lakshmanjoo's supply; the text says only root-to-sequence unfolding. Mark supplied in L2.
2. v.15 *nikṛṣṭānāṃ... kāni nāvaraṇāni* — "obstructions for the low": genuine universality or rhetorical assurance? Open; do not systematize.
3. v.19 *cintānām api naśyatu* — whose thoughts perish, and is thought's end extinction or facing (*pratyakṣa*)? Left open.
4. v.2 *palitair iva dhūsaraḥ* — age vs weariness of travel; simile scope uncertain.
5. Meter unverified (reads anuṣṭubh); scan before freezing T1.
6. v.20 Lakshmanjoo's sensory expansion (touch/embrace/smell) exceeds the text's *samastaviṣayāsvāda*; rein in L2.

## 5. Decision for L2

Carry Kṣemarāja's two precision instruments: v.7 *samāveśayuktyā* (immersion as the *means* of knowing) + injunctive *jayet*, and v.16's deceit-proof (localization refuted by all-state shining). Keep Lakshmanjoo's young/old (1.2), vinegar (1.11), creeper (1.13/25) images at his wording where the Sanskrit supports them; rein in 1.20 and 1.13 where he supplies. Register: plain devotional English, Sanskrit in parens for technical terms (*svabhāvasiddhā, samāveśa, vañcanā, vyutthāna*); no hyphen-chains; full Sanskrit appended per verse (house style for stotras). Then C1 per spec: SUMMARY of the methodlessness thesis, FUNCTION (polemic against yoga-localization), KEY TERMS (svabhāvasiddhā, samāveśayuktyā, vañcanā, vyutthāna), BOUNDARY (§4 items above).

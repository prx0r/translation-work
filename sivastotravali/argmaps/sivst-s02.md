# Śivastotrāvalī s02 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 2, sarvātmaparibhāvanā, 29 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_2.1–2.29 (lines 89–174). Title gives the method: contemplation of the all-self, executed AS salutation.*
*Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.2; Lakshmanjoo audio + verse-keyed English (translations.json 2_1–2_29); Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2). Meter: VERIFY.*

---

## 1. What is at issue

How does one contemplate the all-self? Answer: by naming Him — 29 salutations that force opposites to coincide in one addressee. Each *namaḥ* is a recognition-act: fire and coolness, Veda-breaking and Veda-making, bondage and freedom cannot sit together except in Śiva, so saying them together IS the contemplation the title promises.

## 2. The arc

Elements and gods (1) → sacrificial fire (2–3) → names and forms (4–7) → saṃsāra-cause and foe (8) → root, middle, tip exhausted into fullness (9) → the economics of name-taking (10) → play with multitudes (11) → paradox catalogue (12) → destroying play and all-power (13) → the ocean whose powers are found on shores (14) → unstained lotus (15) → auspicious essences (16) → bound-and-liberated (17) → the mockery-world's one secondless bliss-seat (18) → all rites and no rite (19) → whichever god (20) → boon-giver of unbroken loveliness (21–22) → bowing to His energies, terrible included (23) → sages miss, embracers find (24) → nectar-store (25) → mantra-form praised (26) → the non-wonder teaching (27) → thunderbolt-path (28) → victory and refuge (29).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse

**2.1** — Fire-moon-sun, Brahmā-Viṣṇu, moving-unmoving: to the many-formed, consciousness-formed — bow. The All invoked as elements AND gods at once; multiplicity is the address, not the problem.
**2.2** — To the great fire fed by the universe as fuel, smeared-pure, the world's sole oblation. Sacrifice internalized: the world is fuel, the fire is Him.
**2.3** — To Śiva-fire: cool, supremely nectar-dense — and to You, the unequalled devourer of all. Coolness and devouring together; comfort and terror in one flame. (Lakshmanjoo ch.2 v.28 excerpt source.)
**2.4** — To Mahādeva, Rudra, Śaṅkara, Śiva, Maheśvara — and to some mantra-bodied one. Named gods PLUS the unnamed ("someone mantra-formed"): the list refuses closure.
**2.5** — To Śiva-fire: cut, dried up, the three worlds' melted fat the offering — unequalled, yet auspicious. Destruction language, auspiciousness kept.
**2.6** — To the god — to someone gracious — whose mark is just the non-meeting of all marks. The sign of Him: signlessness. (Lakshmanjoo: "perceiving Him in no way.")
**2.7** — To the hidden Lord: Veda-opposing AND Veda-making, Veda's true essence, secret master. Scripture's author and its transgressor at once.
**2.8** — Sole cause of saṃsāra AND its sole opponent; saṃsāra-formed AND saṃsāra-free. Fourfold contradiction in one breath; the cause/foe pair is the engine verse. (Lakshmanjoo: "the only cause... of this torture.")
**2.9** — To root, middle, tip; embodied as root-middle-tip; with tip-middle-root exhausted; to the Full. Time's three points run forward, then backward, then out — exhaustion as fullness.
**2.10** — Even once, whoever takes His name: ripened merit-heaps. Grace-economics: a single utterance outbids accumulated merit; rarity (*durlabha*) with open access.
**2.11** — To the One playing, consciousness-formed, skull-bearing, with multitudes of moving-static forms ever heaped around. Play (*krīḍā*) as His mode; the skull-bearer amid crowds, not apart.
**2.12** — Magician yet pure, hidden yet self-manifest, subtle yet all-formed — to wondrous Śambhu, bow. Six paradoxes, no resolution offered; the list IS the theology.
**2.13** — To all-power: Brahmā-Viṣṇu-arrayed world's destroyer-play, wonder-working. Destruction as play (*kelī*), not wrath.
**2.14** — To the fathomless Hara-ocean: powers gained only by those wandering its shores. Stay on the shore — the powers are littoral, the ocean itself is beyond grasping. (Lakshmanjoo: "unlimited ocean... on whose shores.")
**2.15** — Dwelling mid mud of the Māyā-made dense world, unsmeared — to lovely hundred-petalled Śambhu-lotus. Cf. 1.24's unstained; the lotus gives the image.
**2.16** — Auspicious, pure, treasure, self-ornamented; dear, ultimate-real, highest — bow. Pure auspiciousness catalogue; no paradox this time — rest before the hard verses.
**2.17** — To ever-bound AND ever-liberated; to difference-free gracious Śambhu. The stanza's impossibility held without blinking. (Lakshmanjoo's stressed paradox.)
**2.18** — In these three worlds whose essence is mockery alone — to You, secondless, ever-bliss-seated. The world demoted to joke; He alone serious. Pivot of the song.
**2.19** — Essence of right-observance yet desirer of left-observance; of all observances, observance-free. Rites affirmed and exceeded in one line. (Lakshmanjoo: "essence of dakṣiṇācāra.")
**2.20** — Whoever worshipped, however, wherever, whatever god — to that god, bow. Total ritual hospitality: no wrong address, since all reach Him.
**2.21** — Served by liberation-seekers, remover of all burning; of spread-out chosen loveliness, boon-giver. Austerity (tapas-heat) cooled by beauty.
**2.22** — To the husband-Lord of the ever-festival, whose three worlds brim with unbroken bliss-rasa. Festival not occasion but ontology.
**2.23** — What enjoys You in pleasure-first knowables — to that terrible host of powers, bow. Striking turn: salutation redirected from Him to His terrible energies who enjoy Him. Devotion includes the fearful retinue.
**2.24** — Even sages cannot know Him — yet those whose gestures LOVE connects embrace Him. To that someone, bow. Knowing fails; embracing works; love outranks asceticism. (Anticipates 1.17's yogin/devotee cut from the other side.)
**2.25** — To the supreme-nectar sheath, supreme-nectar heap, reachable beyond far and near. Nectar stored AND heaped; distance categories collapse.
**2.26** — I praise Your mantra-formed, clear-cool, unprecedentedly joyful, supreme-nectar-juicy form. The mantra IS His body; praise of form is praise of sound.
**2.27** — On oneness-fame's great cloth, full of immortality-nectar — where, Lord, there is no wonder — I praise Your teaching. Non-wonder as doctrine: once oneness is spread out, marvels stop being marvelous.
**2.28** — Thunderbolt to all doubt, fire to all fortune, world's-end to all inauspiciousness — we bow the Māheśvara path. The path itself praised as weapon; protection by awe. (Quoted in Academy blog, ch.2 v.28.)
**2.29** — Victory, god! Bow upon bow to You; this whole world rests on You; of worlds You are supreme Lord; I am come to You for refuge, the One supreme. Litany closes into refuge (*śaraṇāgata*) — contemplation ends in surrender.

## 4. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

2. 2.9's forward/backward time-series — sequence or simultaneity? Open.
3. 2.14: are shore-powers praised or warned against? Lakshmanjoo reads attainment; the verse may limit it. Open.
4. 2.23's redirection to *śaktivṛnda* — hierarchy (energies above Him?) or inclusiveness? Open; do not systematize.

# Śivastotrāvalī s03 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 3, praṇayaprasāda, 21 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_3.1–3.21 (lines 178–240). Title: the gift of affection. Meter: VERIFY. Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.3; Lakshmanjoo audio + translations.json 3_1–3_21; Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

How does love reach what argument cannot? The song answers: by the third beyond being and non-being (3.1) — affection (*praṇaya*) that demands, dances, and roars, taking the whole metaphysics as its dowry.

## 2. The arc

Third beyond the two (1) → free among the unfree (2) → remembrance-medicine (3) → moon-umbrella king (4) → beg the glance (5) → ocean-drops, why not sweet (6) → unmelted hearts contemptible (7) → heart-union's vessel (8) → meditation-flood (9) → lifers never parted (10) → roar and dance (11) → no other knowable, consciousness expands (12) → Śiva-sound chases sorrows (13) → no second great person (14) → servants win, ocean as play-lake (15) → miseries shamed (16) → best reciter by oneness (17) → Being beyond being/non-being (18) → brighter than suns, unseen (19) → knower in known, great in small (20) → enough other cries; sharp crying, knowing bewilderment (21).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse

**3.1** — *sadasattvena bhāvānāṃ yuktā yā dvitayī gatiḥ.* The two courses of things — by being and non-being — leapt over; to the third, the wondrous Śambhu, bow. Method stated: transcendence by leap, not ladder.
**3.2** — *āsurarṣijanād asminn asvatantre jagattraye.* Among gods, seers, men in this unfree triple world — free are Your dependents, the Free One's own. Freedom is contagious, not achieved. (Lakshmanjoo: all dependent, Your men free.)
**3.3** — *aśeṣaviśvakhacitabhavadvapur anusmṛtiḥ.* Remembrance of Your body inlaid with the whole universe — the sole medicine for world-pain; who have it sit happy. Memory as cure.
**3.4** — *sitātapatraṃ yasyenduḥ.* Whose white umbrella is the self-lit moon, whose fly-whisk the heaven-river's stream — He alone is Supreme Lord. Cosmic regalia; kingship by metaphor.
**3.5** — *prakāśāṃ śītalām ekāṃ... dṛśaṃ vitara me.* Grant me, Lord, a glance — luminous, cool, one, pure as moon-digit, carrying nectar. The begged glance; grace as look. (Lakshmanjoo: "I want only one glance of Thee.")
**3.6** — *tvaccidānandajaladheś cyutāḥ saṃvittivipruṣaḥ.* Drops fallen from Your consciousness-bliss ocean — these knowings — why aren't they nectar-sweet-beautiful to me? The devotee interrogates his own dryness.
**3.7** — *tvayi rāgarase nātha na magnaṃ hṛdayaṃ.* Hearts unmelted in Your love-rasa are heartless indeed — fit for contempt. Hard verse: unmelted = contemptible. Do not soften.
**3.8** — *prabhuṇā bhavatā yasya jātaṃ hṛdayamelanam.* Whose heart met the Lord's — he alone is the vessel of ruling goddesses' powers. Union makes the devotee the gods' container — inversion of worship direction.
**3.9** — *harṣāṇām atha śokānāṃ sarveṣāṃ plāvakaḥ samam.* Flooder alike of all joys and sorrows — Your meditation-nectar-flood, like lowlands filling. Feeling levelled, not sorted.
**3.10** — *keva na syād dṛśā teṣāṃ sukhasambhāranirbharā.* What would their look not be, happiness-provisions-full — who never part from You, more than self, Lord? Lifers (*ātmādhikena*...): separation impossible. (Lakshmanjoo: "more than their own lives.")
**3.11** — *garjāmi bata nṛtyāmi pūrṇā mama manorathāḥ.* I roar, I dance — my wishes fulfilled, for my Lord is won, utterly charming. Ecstasy as evidence: roar and dance prove arrival.
**3.12** — *nānyad vedyaṃ kriyā yatra nānyo yogo.* Where no other knowable for action, no other yoga for knowing — yet what if knowledge? Consciousness alone, world-filled-one, expands. Anti-method peak: nothing to know, nowhere to yoke — and yet expansion.
**3.13** — *durjayānām anantānām duḥkhānāṃ sahasaiva te.* Suddenly fled from them — hard, endless sorrows — whose speech forever holds the Śiva-sound. The Name as exorcist; sorrows flee sound, not argument.
**3.14** — *uttamaḥ puruṣo 'nyo 'sti.* Is there another, higher person, distinct by remaining Yours? You alone, great Person, support of all persons without remainder. No second — with the devotee-residue kept ("remaining Yours").
**3.15** — *jayanti te jagadvandyā dāsās te.* Victorious, world-venerable, Your slaves, Lord — for whom this saṃsāra-ocean is a play-great-lake. Ocean shrunk to lake by servitude. (Lakshmanjoo's "victorious... Thy slaves.")
**3.16** — *āsatāṃ tāvad anyāni dainyānīha bhavajjuṣām.* Let other miseries sit — for Your devotees, "You manifest Yourself" — by this alone they are shamed. His manifestation shames misery; no refutation needed.
**3.17** — *matparaṃ nāsti tatrāpi jāpako 'smi tadaikyataḥ.* "Nothing is beyond me" — yet therein I am a reciter, by that oneness; as truth, You point the rosary somewhere. Recitation continues inside supremacy; the rosary pointed by Him.
**3.18** — *sato 'vaśyaṃ paramasat.* From being must come supreme being, and beyond that, Lord — and You are other than being and non-being both; hence You are being-non-being-formed. The metaphysical knot, tied openly. Do not untie in L2.
**3.19** — *sahasrasūryakiraṇādhikaśuddhaprakāśavān.* Purer-shining than thousand-sun-ray-rise — yet You, all-worlds-pervading, are not seen. Maximum light, zero visibility: the paradox of immanence. (Lakshmanjoo's stressed verse.)
**3.20** — *jaḍe jagati cidrūpaḥ kila vedye 'pi vedakaḥ.* Conscious-formed in the inert world, knower even in the known; great in the measured — hence You are the highest. Pervasion logic: great-by-containing, not by exceeding.
**3.21** — *alam ākranditair anyair.* Enough with other cries — this alone, before the Lord: I cry sharp, Lord, I am bewildered, even knowing thus. Close: knowing bewilderment, crying anyway. (Lakshmanjoo: let other lamentations lie untouched.)

# Śivastotrāvalī s04 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 4, surasodbalā, 25 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_4.1–4.25 (lines 243–316). Title obscure (*surasodbalā* — potent nectar?). Meter: VERIFY. Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.4; Lakshmanjoo audio + translations.json 4_1–4_25; Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

How does the fickle mind hold the Lord — and what if the devotee is unfit, low-seated, tongue-tied? Answer: the mind is praised precisely for turning (4.1); low ground is kept, not abandoned (4.2); and the whole song proceeds as direct address — bargaining, confessing, demanding — until the worshipped turns out to be the worshipper (4.25).

## 2. The arc

Talking mind praised (1) → torture of low ground kept (2) → reveal Yours, hide the world, use me now (3) → quick-mercy spell (4) → Brahmā laughed at (5) → nothing apart from His body (6) → two tastes of foot-dust (7) → lightning-flash, if it stayed (8) → great discernment demands plain manifestation (9) → placed unasked, yet behaving like a man (10) → some god blooms in hearts (11) → love grown by play, separation-talk fruiting (12) → word entering sideways hearts (13) → faces carrying Śiva-sound win (14) → differentiation ended — yet no knocking at His door (15) → blooming face longed for (16) → what happiness apart from non-difference (17) → unfit unless constantly tasting (18) → blind people, yet He hears MY cry (19) → pleasures recalled, His vision asked instead (20) → set by His will: hundreds of omens (21) → Śivarātri where sun sets (22) → world-rule tasteless without foot-touch (23) → strong self-bond, first ignored never loosened (24) → worshipped ones worship Him as the one worshipper in seers' bodies (25).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse

**4.1** — *capalam asi yad api mānasa.* Mind, though fickle — even so you are praised, since you worship the refuge of refuges, the world's teacher, Ambikā's husband. Fickleness forgiven for direction: praise for turning, not for steadiness. (Lakshmanjoo addresses his own mind throughout.)
**4.2** — *ullaṅghya vividhadaivatasopānakramam.* Having crossed the ladder-steps of various gods, reached Śiva's feet — yet on the lowest ground I stand even now; wonder, I do not quit it. Arrival without departure: highest object, lowest seat, no contradiction felt.
**4.3** — *prakaṭaya nijam adhvānaṃ sthagayatarām.* Reveal Your own path, hide all world-ways thoroughly — while I am, Lord, quickly Your ever-risen servant. Double imperative: show Yours, cover theirs; use me now (*sapadi*).
**4.4** — *śiva śiva śambho śaṅkara.* Śiva Śiva Śambhu Śaṅkara — swift pity on the refuge-seekers' lover! Fortune is near for whoever remembers Your lotus-feet-pair. Name-piling as spell; mercy as speed (*āśu*).
**4.5** — *tāvakāṅghrikamalāsanalīnā.* Those seated on Your foot-lotus-throne, making the world to taste — they laugh at Vir iñc (Brahmā), office-stained, not-self-ruled. Devotees laugh at the creator: office (*adhikāra*) stains, throne-sitting frees.
**4.6** — *tvatprakāśavapuṣo na vibhinnaṃ kiṃ cana.* Nothing appears apart from Your light-body; so You are always already gained, Lord — though far by nature. Immanence proved, transcendence kept: gained AND far.
**4.7** — *pādapaṅkajarasaṃ tava ke cid.* Some taste Your foot-lotus-juice as stale-difference-conduct; others instantly taste it as flawless, body-whole, duality-free. Two tastes, one dust: stale vs fresh is the taster's, not the foot's.
**4.8** — *nātha vidyud iva bhāti vibhāte.* Lord, like lightning at dawn gleams my nectar-smeared — if it stayed steadier, You are worshipped duly — what else? Flash-devotion: one steady flash would complete all rite. (Conditional worship: *if* it stayed.)
**4.9** — *sarvam asyaparam asti na kiṃ cid.* Is there anything, thing or non-thing, apart from this? With great discernment decided here: as You are, so be plain to me. Discernment (*prajñā*) issues in demand: appear as You are.
**4.10** — *svecchayaiva bhagavan nijamārge kāritaḥ padam.* By Your will alone, Lord, I was made to step on Your own path — then why do I behave just like a man, knowing nothing fit for Your feet? Placed unasked (Lakshmanjoo's stressed point: "I never asked You... You have done it Yourself!"), yet behaving humanly — the complaint of the carried.
**4.11** — *ko 'pi deva hṛdi teṣu tāvako jṛmbhate.* Some god of Yours blooms in their hearts, a lovely supreme state — by whose story-thunder-cātakas they are long gladdened. Devotion blooms unasked in some; story-rain gladdens long.
**4.12** — *tvajjuṣāṃ tvayi kayāpi līlayā rāga eṣa paripoṣam āgataḥ.* For Your devotees, by some play in You, this love has grown fat — so that separation-ground talk and memory fruit in union-festival. Play grows love; separation-talk (*saṅkathā*) itself fruits in union.
**4.13** — *yo vicitrarasasekavardhitaḥ.* The word, grown by strange-rasa-sprinkling, though uttered "Śaṅkara" hundreds of times — enters even sideways hearts, ever-fresh in purpose. The Name penetrates the unwilling; freshness never stales.
**4.14** — *te jayanti mukhamaṇḍale bhraman.* Victorious are they on whose face-orbits the Śiva-sound ever revolves — which, moon-spread from nectar-source, trickles supreme nectar, sweet. Faces win: sound on the mouth beats thought in the head.
**4.15** — *parisamāptam ivogram idaṃ jagad.* The fierce world as if finished; mind's impurity thinned to none — and yet not even the smallest knocking at Your city-door-bolt. End of differentiation (Lakshmanjoo's Bruce Hughes passage) — yet no entry knocked. The door verse: realization without arrival. Do not resolve.
**4.16** — *satataphullabhavanmukhapaṅkajodaravilokanalālasacetasaḥ.* Of minds eager for Your ever-blooming face-lotus-belly-gaze — do something, Lord, just a little, so You flash as facing me. "Just a little" (*manāg iva*): the smallest turn asked.
**4.17** — *tvadavibhedamater aparaṃ nu kiṃ sukham.* What happiness here apart from non-difference-mind, what other glory? So why does this servant's mind, abandoning her, go the wrong way? The song's self-accusation: knowing the answer, asking the question.
**4.18** — *kṣaṇam apīha na tāvakadāsatāṃ prati.* May I never for a moment be unfit for Your serfdom — unless constantly, reverently tasting non-difference-wine. Fitness redefined as tasting-frequency.
**4.19** — *na kila paśyati satyam ayaṃ janas.* These people truly do not see Your body, dirtied by duality-sight — and yet, all-knowing refuge-lover, do You not hear MY cry? Others' blindness conceded; his cry excepted. (Lakshmanjoo: "I confess it, my Lord.")
**4.20** — *smarasi nātha kadācid apīhitaṃ* Do You remember, Lord, any desired sense-pleasure I ever asked? Give instead, always, Your body-vision-nectar as the only wish. Pleasures recalled only to be exchanged.
**4.21** — *kila yadaiva śivādhvani tāvake kṛtapado 'smi.* Since by Your will alone I stepped on Your Śiva-path, Lord — hundreds of good omens rose for me; what else should I seek from You? Placed-ness (10) returns as contentment. (Lakshmanjoo: hundreds of good things.)
**4.22** — *yatra so 'stam ayam eti vivasvāṃś* Where the sun sets with the moon and all — that unconquered Śiva-night shines, self-light-spread-lustrous. Counter-cosmos: when all lights set, His night shines. (The 15.17 night-post, anticipated.)
**4.23** — *apy upārjitam ahaṃ triṣu lokeṣv adhipatyam.* Even won rule in three worlds, O king of gods, I count tasteless — lacking Your foot-touch-nectar-rasa. Empire vs foot-touch: no contest.
**4.24** — *bata nātha dṛḍho 'yam ātmabandho.* Alas, Lord, strong is this self-bond, made of Your un-fame, fashioned by You alone — first ignored by me, it never loosens a bit. Bond made BY Him OF His unfamed-ness: the knot He tied. Held, not solved.
**4.25** — *mahatām amareśa pūjyamāno 'py.* Though worshipped by the great, O king of gods, You ever stay the one-worshipper-form — seen outside and in, You shine in the seers' bodies forever. Final inversion: the worshipped is the worshipper; He shines inside the seers, not above them.

## 4. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. 4.2's kept low ground: humility topos or doctrine (high object/low seat)? Open.
2. 4.15's unknocked door: kept closed by whom? Open — do not allegorize.
3. 4.24's bond: His fashioning vs my ignoring — agency split left standing.
4. Title *surasodbalā* parsing uncertain (sura + sodbalā?); keep transliterated in L2.

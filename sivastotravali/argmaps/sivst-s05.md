# Śivastotrāvalī s05 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 5, svabalanideśana, 26 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_5.1–5.26 (lines 320–396). Title: instruction of one's own strength. Meter: VERIFY. Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.5; Lakshmanjoo audio + translations.json 5_1–5_26; Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

How does the devotee live inside the world without leaving it? Answer: by making every sense, word, and act carry devotion-rasa first — the world is not renounced but led (*puraḥsara*), and the Name's unrepeatable taste outbids everything.

## 2. The arc

Foot-touch union asked (1) → when will I dance (2) → silent, not wise (3) → dancing-shouting praise (4) → eyes closed by touch (5) → dwelling where prattle never stops (6) → city with Him and Goddess (7) → heart-lotus blooming for worship (8) → melt like wax (9) → appear from inside or out (10) → His touch-nectar streaming always (11) → plunge in His touch-lake, a pleasure beyond all pleasures (12) → offer passions, feast together (13) → world-eater, glance at servants (14) → "bow to me, to Śiva," even straw worships (15) → world seen as self-blaze, never parted (16) → His fullness fits, nothing else wanted (17) → laughter, dance, eating, drinking — that place be mine (18) → strange-longing flower-fragrance firm, bad-smell gone (19) → where is love for passions vs love for His feet — teach my heart (20) → yogic states passed through, heart melted by Him-thought (21) → bhakti-rasa leading speech, mind, body always (22) → "Śiva Śiva Śiva" unending — tasting never-repeated great rasa (23) → roaming His worship in the consciousness-vastness where all dull paths are drunk up (24) → nothing needed for standing in eternal self-shine — but dust of His pure feet (25) → something flashes in mind, touching His feet — show the world dissolved in nectar-lake, always (26).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse

**5.1** — *tvatpādapadmasamparkamātrasambhogasaṅginam.* Joined in enjoyment by mere touch-contact of Your lotus-feet — bring me, Lord, by the neck into Your house. Entry by touch, not merit; "by the neck" (*galepādikayā*) — force asked for.
**5.2** — *bhavatpādāmbujarajorājirañjitamūrdhajaḥ.* Head hair dyed by Your foot-lotus-dust-redness — when shall I frantically begin to dance? Dust as dye; dance as the question's form. (Lakshmanjoo: holding His feet, wiping their dust.)
**5.3** — *tvad ekanātho bhagavann iyad evārthaye sadā.* You my sole Lord — this alone I always ask: dwelling inside You, may I be silent; otherwise, wise. Silence over wisdom: *mūka* (dumb) beats *budha* (clever). The anti-eloquence verse.
**5.4** — *aho sudhānidhe svāminn aho mṛṣṭa trilocana.* Ah nectar-ocean master! Ah sweet three-eyed One! Ah tasty deformed-eyed One! — thus may I dance, shouting. Praise by exclamation; three *aho*s as liturgy.
**5.5** — *tvatpādapadmasaṃsparśaparimīlitalocanaḥ.* Eyes half-closed by Your foot-lotus-touch — may I expand, reeling drunk on Your devotion-liquor-madness. Touch narrows sight, widens being.
**5.6** — *cittabhūbhṛd bhuvi vibho vaseyaṃ kvāpi yatra sā.* May I dwell anywhere, O All-pervading, mind-mountain on earth, where that practice is — uninterrupted, great-rasa, made of Your prattle. Dwelling defined by practice, not place.
**5.7** — *yatra devīsametas tvam.* Where You stand with the Goddess, many-formed, from palace to gate-tower — in that city may I dwell. The divine household as address; *bahurūpa* domesticated.
**5.8** — *samullasantu bhagavan bhavadbhānumarīcayaḥ.* May Your sun-rays rejoice, Lord, until my heart-lotus blooms — for Your worship. Blooming as the rays' festival, not the devotee's work.
**5.9** — *prasīda bhagavan yena tvatpade patitaṃ sadā.* Favor me, Lord, so that my mind, fallen ever at Your feet, tasting this and that — melts like wax, flows like... Wax-mind: tasting melts; melting is the prayer.
**5.10** — *praharṣād vātha śokād vā.* Whether from joy bursting pot-lids, or sorrow — from outside or inside feeling, appear to me, Lord. Any trigger suffices; appear regardless of cause.
**5.11** — *bahir apy antar api tat syandamānaṃ sadāstu me.* Outside too, inside too — may that ever-streaming, Your coolest foot-lotus-touch-nectar, be mine always. Streaming touch, both directions.
**5.12** — *tvatpādasaṃsparśasudhāsaraso 'ntarnimajjanam.* Plunging inside Your touch-nectar-lake — may that pleasure beyond all pleasures be mine always. Pleasure (*bhoga*) that surpasses (*laṅghī*) all pleasure.
**5.13** — *niveditam upādatsva rāgādi bhagavan mayā.* Take what I offer — passion and the rest, Lord; make it nectar and eat it with devotees. The passions cooked into nectar and shared: offering as cuisine.
**5.14** — *aśeṣabhuvanāhāranityatṛptaḥ sukhāsanam.* Ever-full on whole-world-food, happily seated — Master, cast Your grace-glance-moment on servants. The world-eater asked for one glance at servants.
**5.15** — *antarbhakticamatkāracarvaṇāmīlitekṣaṇaḥ.* Eyes half-closed, chewing inner devotion-wonder — "bow to me, to Śiva" — may I worship even straw. Self as Śiva addressed; straw as liturgy-worthy.
**5.16** — *api labdhabhavadbhāvaḥ.* Though I have gained Your being — seeing the world as my self-blaze — may I be unparted by devotion-rasa-enjoyments. Gaining without leaving: enjoyment as non-parting's seal.
**5.17** — *ākāmkṣaṇīyam aparaṃ yena nātha na vidyate.* Since nothing else is to be desired, Lord — to You, the secondless, fullness is fitting. Desire's end as fitness-proof.
**5.18** — *hasyate nṛtyate yatra.* Where there is laughter, dancing, where passion-aversion are eaten, devotion-nectar drunk — may I reach that place. The festival as destination.
**5.19** — *tat tad apūrvāmodatvaccintākusumavāsanā.* May that unprecedented-joy-perfume of thought-flowers about You firm up in my mind — till bad-smell of bad-habit perishes. Fragrance vs stink: thought's smell decides.
**5.20** — *kva nu rāgādiṣu rāgaḥ kva ca haracaraṇāmbujeṣu rāgitvam.* Where is love among passions, and where is loving at Hari's lotus-feet — thus teach my contradiction-loving heart what is good, O Deathless. Comparison as instruction; the heart addressed as contradiction-lover.
**5.21** — *vicaran yogadaśāsv api.* Though wandering even through yogic states, though turned away from objects — may I be only heart-melted by Your-thought-liquor-madness. States passed through, not dwelt in; melting only by Him.
**5.22** — *vāci manomatiṣu tathā śarīraceṣṭāsu.* In speech, mind-thoughts, body-gestures made by senses — everywhere, always, may bhakti-rasa go before me as leader. Devotion as vanguard (*puraḥsara*) of every faculty.
**5.23** — *śivaśivaśiveti nāmani tava niravadhi.* While Your name "Śiva Śiva Śiva" is chanted endlessly, Lord — may I taste some great unrepeatable rasa. The Name's infinity; the taste's unrepeatability.
**5.24** — *sphuradanantacidātmakaviṣṭape.* In the vastness flashing as endless consciousness-self, where all dull paths are drunk up — may I roam Your worship, countless consciousness-made ramparts uncounted. Roaming, not arriving.
**5.25** — *svavapuṣi sphuṭabhāsini śāśvate.* For standing in self-body, clearly shining, eternal — nothing else is of use; firm be this thought — but dust of Your pure feet, O Lord. Self-sufficiency confessed — then the one exception: foot-dust.
**5.26** — *kim api nātha kadācana cetasi sphurati.* Something flashes sometime in mind, touching Your feet — where this whole world melts — show it always, in the nectar-lake, Lord. Close: a flash, feet touched, world melted; "show always."

2. 5.19's perfumes: thought-flowers vs habit-stink — image-field left open.
3. 5.24's ramparts (*gaṇḍikā*): uncounted, uncountable — do not enumerate.

# Śivastotrāvalī s06 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 6, adhvavisphuraṇa, 11 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_6.1–6.11 (lines 400–431). Title: bursting-forth of the path. Meter: VERIFY. Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.6; Lakshmanjoo audio + translations.json 6_1–6_11; Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

Can separation itself become yoga? The song answers: the pain of parting, unrelieved, IS the practice — every verse turns absence into address, until address forces appearance.

## 2. The arc

Burning to be seen (1) → separated-in-saṃsāra, united-in-Him (2) → all my goings are You (3) → speech matching His bliss (4) → desireless, joy-filled roaming in His possession (5) → satisfied by seeing the world full (6) → things melting into Him like cloud-bits (7) → the jewel blazing from His middle (8) → no stage unruled, effortless arrival everywhere (9) → embrace by His will — what have I not won (10) → appear; we search only You (11).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse

**6.1** — *kṣaṇamātram apīśāna viyuktasya tvayā mama.* Even a moment parted from You, Lord — of me burning dense always — may I be the ground of Your gaze. Separation-fire as qualification for being seen. (Lakshmanjoo: "even for one second... I catch fire.")
**6.2** — *viyogasāre saṃsāre.* In saṃsāra whose essence is parting — by You, dear Lord, may I be ever unparted, though parted from the world. Double parting: joined to Him by leaving the world. (Lakshmanjoo: separation is the end of the universe.)
**6.3** — *kāyavāṅmanasair yatra yāmi sarvaṃ tvam eva tat.* Wherever I go by body, speech, mind — all that is You alone: may this ultimate truth stand ever full for me. Going = arriving, wherever.
**6.4** — *nirvikalpo mahānandapūrṇo yadvad bhavāṃs tathā.* As You are construction-free, great-bliss-full — so may my praise-speech become, just fitting. Speech aspiring to His thoughtlessness.
**6.5** — *bhavadāveśataḥ paśyan.* Seeing thing after thing as consisting of You, by Your possession — may I roam desireless, joy-filled. Possession (*āveśa*) as optics: everything seen is Him.
**6.6** — *bhagavanbhavataḥ pūrṇam paśyeyam akhilaṃ jagat.* May I see the whole world full of Your being, Lord — by that alone content; then do not toil. Contentment as the end of effort; a plea that effort end.
**6.7** — *vilīyamānās tvayy eva.* May things shine for me always, melting into You alone like cloud-bits in sky, moving toward ever-purer courses. Melting as shining's precondition.
**6.8** — *svaprabhāprasaradhvastāparyantadhvāntasantatiḥ.* May there blaze for me, from Your middle, some jewel of Being — its self-light-spread destroying the endless dark-series. The middle-jewel: light from His center, not from outside.
**6.9** — *kāṃ bhūmikāṃ nādhiśeṣe.* What stage do You not rule, what could there be that is not Your body? Tired — by effortlessness may I reach You everywhere. Tiredness + effortlessness: arrival without means.
**6.10** — *bhavadaṅgapariṣvaṅgasambhogaḥ svecchayaiva me.* May limb-embrace-enjoyment with You happen to me by mere will — this much gained, Lord, what have I not won? Embrace by will alone; winning reckoned complete.
**6.11** — *prakaṭībhava nānyābhiḥ prārthanābhiḥ.* Appear! We make no other prayers miserable — we languish, seeking only You. The close: one imperative, all others renounced. (Lakshmanjoo: "Appear to me!... I will ask nothing.")

# Śivastotrāvalī s07 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 7, vidhuravijaya, 9 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_7.1–7.9 (lines 435–460). Title: victory over the distressed (?). Meter: VERIFY. Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.7; Lakshmanjoo audio + translations.json 7_1–7_9; Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

How is the mind cured of mine-and-not-mine? Answer: by drowning — the mind dropped in bliss-ocean, fed by devotion-mother, married to devotion-maiden, drunk on foot-nectar — until "mine" hardens around the right thing.

## 2. The arc

Mind dropped in bliss-ocean, difference-disaster cut (1) → axe on mine/not-mine root (2) → stains melted, form bliss-flooded, consciousness-bodied (3) → fed by devotion-mother's rasas till wings grow (4) → foot-nectar skill ending object-poison-longing (5) → devotion-sun melting mind-jewel's wax-drops (6) → praising where even Hari-Hara-Brahmā wait outside (7) → seeing the world as Śiva, acts as worship, through devotion-madness (8) → mind-boy marrying devotion-maiden, hardening "mine" (9).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse

**7.1** — *tvayy ānandasarasvati samarasatām etya.* Having come to even-taste in You, bliss-river — may my mind drop at once this difference-based great disaster. Dropping, not solving: *pariharatu* (abandon). (Lakshmanjoo: mind "would act like this.")
**7.2** — *etan mama na tv idam iti.* "This is mine, not this" — on passion-aversion's firm-nailed root may the axe fall inside: the certainty of oneness-with-You-made. The axe-image: possessiveness felled, not reformed. (Lakshmanjoo: "this is my property / this is not mine.")
**7.3** — *galatu vikalpakalaṅkāvalī.* May the thought-stain-line melt; may unbarredness blaze in heart; bliss-rasa-flooded be my consciousness-body, Lord. Melting then blazing: negative then positive in one breath.
**7.4** — *rāgādimayabhavāṇḍakaluṭhitaṃ.* Me, rolled in the passion-made world-egg — may devotion-thought-mother feed me with rasas, till, wing-grown, I am a bird. Mother-feeding then flight: dependence ripening into wings. (Bird image: पक्ष growth = capacity.)
**7.5** — *tvaccaraṇabhāvanāmṛtarasasārāsvādanaipuṇaṃ labhatām.* May this mind of mine gain skill in tasting the essence of Your foot-thought-nectar-rasa — ending all object-poison-clinging-longing. Skill (*naipuṇa*) in tasting: connoisseurship as cure.
**7.6** — *tvadbhaktitapanadīdhitisaṃsparśavaśān.* By devotion-sun-ray-touch, may this mind-jewel release far away passion-kindled fire-drops. Sun-melting-wax model: heat, not argument, releases.
**7.7** — *tasmin pade bhavantaṃ satatam upaślokayeyam atyuccaiḥ.* In that state may I praise You constantly, aloud — where even Hari, Hara's horse (?), Brahmā wait outside. Praise where the great gods wait outside: intimacy outranks hierarchy. (*Hariharyaśva-Viriñcā*: read as Viṣṇu, Śiva's vehicle (?), Brahmā — epithet uncertain; mark.)
**7.8** — *bhaktimadajanitavibhramavaśena.* By devotion-madness-born frenzy may I see with faculties unmaimed: the whole world as Śiva, and all acts as worship-stuff. Frenzy as corrective lens, not defect.
**7.9** — *māmakamanogṛhītatvadbhaktikulāṅganā.* My mind-boy, having taken Your-devotion-family-maiden, having borne aṇimā-etc. sons — may she, firm-rooted, harden the "mine"-thought. The close: "mine" (*mameti buddhi*) kept but remarried — possessiveness retained, object changed. (Lakshmanjoo: mind as boy, devotion as beautiful girl-bride.)

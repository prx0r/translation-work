# Śivastotrāvalī s08 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 8, alaukikodbala, 13 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_8.1–8.13 (lines 464–501). Title: unworldly upsurge. Meter: VERIFY. Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.8; Lakshmanjoo audio + translations.json 8_1–8_13; Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

What happens when grace and devotion meet — a drop of favor on the one side, devotion come to meet on the other? Answer: unworldly upsurge (*alaukikodbala*) — faculties fall into their own seats, the body exceeds all dealings, the universe goes unwitnessed, and the hamsa drinks upward from root and depth.

## 2. The arc

Drop of grace + devotion meeting — when will they root in such a body (1) → inner joy tasting His lordship, knowledge-yoga's glories far (2) → worldly thirst for objects, but seeing them as His body only (3) → body-grounds, mind, breath-paths: in difference AND in consciousness-paths, be Thyself plain to me (4) → faculties falling into own seats, never risking non-difference-rupture (5) → by possession-force the light-soft body exceeds all dealings (6) → my body becoming His, worlds joining my limbs (7) → flood of supreme nectar to fill non-duality's dread gorge (8) → by union-drops, turned from all world-pleasures — or fallen from both (9) → hamsa at His lotus-feet, devotion-lotus-stalk rising from below and within (10) → all things entering thought's scope; vision-thought-light-nectars blazing (11) → may I be at crashing hardships, names bending down — fear gone only while limb-joined, joy-mad (12) → universe woven in His self, shown outside by Him — firm-certain now, shine plain (13).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse

**8.1** — *yaḥ prasādalava īśvarasthito yā ca bhaktir iva mām upeyuṣī.* The grace-drop stationed in the Lord, and devotion come as if to meet me — when will the pair, joined, take root in such a body? Grace descends, devotion ascends; rooting is the question. (Lakshmanjoo: "one thing is just these lava.")
**8.2** — *tvatprabhutvaparicarvaṇajanmā.* Born of chewing His lordship, may some inner joy-juice rise; here always be supreme for me — knowledge-yoga's glories far off. Chewing (*carvaṇa*, cf. Stotra 20's title) as method; metaphysics kept at distance.
**8.3** — *lokavadbhavatu me viṣayeṣu.* Like the worldly, let my thirst for objects swell — only seeing them as Your body, unconstructed. Thirst kept, object changed: worldliness with non-dual sight.
**8.4** — *dehabhūmiṣu tathā manasi tvaṃ.* In body-grounds and in mind, Lord, and in breath's course where difference has come — and in those consciousness-paths — by Yourself be plain to me, Thyself. Plainness demanded in BOTH registers: where difference is AND where consciousness runs.
**8.5** — *nijanijeṣu padeṣu patantv imāḥ.* May these thrilled faculties fall into their own seats — never, Lord, even slightly, the daring of non-difference-rupture. Faculties self-seating; the one fear named: breaking non-difference.
**8.6** — *laghumasṛṇasitācchaśītalaṃ.* Light, soft, white-pure, cool — contemplating by possession-force a body — may I exceed those dealings of the all-thing-road. Possession-body exceeding transaction: *vyavahāra* left behind.
**8.7** — *vikasatu svavapur bhavadātmakaṃ.* May my self-body bloom as You; may worlds join my limbs; may all this duality-sport, even recalled, become untellable. Blooming, joining, unspeakability: three stages of absorption.
**8.8** — *samudiyād api tādṛśatāvakā.* May there rise, looking at such Yours, a flood of supreme nectar — so my filling of non-duality's dread gorge happens. The gorge (*darī*) image: non-duality as abyss to be filled.
**8.9** — *api kadācana tāvakasaṅgamā.* If ever by the thinnest union-drops — may I turn from all world-pleasures; or shall I, fallen from both, be...? The wager verse: union-drops against world-pleasures, with the risk named (fallen from BOTH). Do not resolve.
**8.10** — *satatam eva bhavaccaraṇāmbujākaracarasya hi haṃsavarasya me.* Ever at Your lotus-feet, of me the best hamsa moving in the lake — above from root below, and from inside, may devotion's lotus-stalk bend up, unborn Lord. Hamsa-and-stalk botany: below, inside, above joined. (*aja*: unborn — epithet or address; open.)
**8.11** — *upayāntu vibho samastavastūny api cintāviṣayaṃ.* May all things come under thought's scope and vision's footing for me; may vision-thought-light-nectar-essences blaze supreme. Totalization without grasping: scope, not seizure.
**8.12** — *parameśvara teṣu teṣu kṛcchreṣv.* Lord, in crashing hardships, as names bend down, may I be — fear gone only while limb-joined, exceeding-joy-mad. Practice for catastrophe: hold the limb, go mad with joy.
**8.13** — *bhavadātmani viśvam umbhitaṃ.* The universe woven in Your self is shown outside by You alone — what stands firm-certain: may it now shine plain. Closing epistemology: in-Him, shown-by-Him, certain — shine. (Lakshmanjoo: "woven in Your consciousness... That is a fact to me.")

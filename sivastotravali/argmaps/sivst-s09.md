# Śivastotrāvalī s09 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 9, svātantryavijaya, 20 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_9.1–9.20 (lines 505–563). Title: victory of autonomy. Meter: VERIFY. Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.9; Lakshmanjoo audio + translations.json 9_1–9_20; Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

When will longing become meeting? The song asks it twenty times (*kadā*): each verse names a desired state — rushing mind, face-to-face sight, burst bolts, subdued gods, named distinction, shamed onlookers, unfleeable beloved, approving gaze, crowned knowledge, proclaimed treasure, shadowless shining, devotee-leadership, drunk pride, cool-lotus lying, far-shore reaching, tear-laughter touch, beast-life shed, play in medicine, summoning cry, effortless seeing. The asking never leaves Him; that is the victory.

## 2. The arc

Rush past all to His touch (1) → You-loving, worship-wealthy, see me (2) → needless-minded, burst all bolts (3) → subdue all gods by devotion-power (4) → vast festival where even distinction is named (5) → sudden recognition shaming Your people (6) → beloved-ness forbidding flight (7) → bathed by approving sight (8) → devotion crowning knowledge and yoga (9) → seizing feet, proclaiming to all (10) → shining with no Māyā-shadow-cave (11) → leader of the devotee-host (12) → drunk proud with His rasa (13) → lying at cool lotus-feet (14) → reaching the far shore, becoming apt (15) → tears then laughter, gaining touch-rasa (16) → shaking off beast-life, tasting fit self-form (17) → siddhis gained, playing in medicine-wine (18) → the cry that summons His form (19) → hugging tight lotus-feet, seeing without effort (20).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse (terse)

**9.1** — *kadā navarasārdrārdrasambhogāsvādanotsukam.* When will my mind, eager to taste new-rasa-moist union, rush past all else to Your touch? Appetite as opener. (Lakshmanjoo: mind "already like that.")
**9.2** — *tvadekaraktas tvatpādapūjāmātramahādhanaḥ.* You-loving, worship-wealthy — when shall I, the eager one, see You face to face? Wealth = worship alone.
**9.3** — *gāḍhānurāgavaśato nirapekṣībhūtamānaso.* Needless-minded by thick love — when shall I burst all bolts with a crash and reach You? Needlessness as battering-ram.
**9.4** — *svasaṃvitsārahṛdayādhiṣṭhānāḥ sarvadevatāḥ.* Heart-seated in own-consciousness-essence — when shall I subdue all gods by devotion's power? Gods as inner faculties (Lakshmanjoo: eyes, tongue...). Subduing, not worshipping.
**9.5** — *kadā me syād vibho bhūri bhaktyānandarasotsavaḥ.* When my vast devotion-bliss-rasa festival — where even distinction gets a name from beholding-joy? Even *pṛthak* named, not exiled.
**9.6** — *īśvaram abhayam udāraṃ pūrṇam akāraṇam.* Having suddenly recognized the fearless, generous, full, causeless, self-hidden Lord — when shall I shame Your people? Recognition as embarrassment of the slow.
**9.7** — *kadā kām api tāṃ nātha tava vallabhatām.* When that beloved-ness of Yours by which it would not be fitting for You to flee from me? Love as flight-ban.
**9.8** — *tattvato 'śeṣajantūnāṃ bhavatpūjāmayātmanām.* Bathed by the approving sight of all beings whose selves are Your worship — when? The world approving the devotee.
**9.9** — *jñānasya paramā bhūmir yogasya paramā daśā.* Devotion, highest ground of knowledge, highest state of yoga — when will my begging for it be full? Devotion crowns the rivals.
**9.10** — *sahasaivasādya kadā gāḍham avaṣṭabhya.* Seizing suddenly, holding hard, joy-overwhelmed — when shall I proclaim Your foot-treasure to all? Private seizure, public proclamation.
**9.11** — *paritaḥ prasaracchuddhatvadālokamayaḥ.* All-around pure-You-light-spread — when shall I so shine that no Māyā-shadow-cave is in me? Transparency as asked state.
**9.12** — *ātmasātkṛtaniḥśeṣamaṇḍalo nirvyapekṣakaḥ.* Having made all circles my own, need-free — when shall I be leader of Your devotee-host? (Lakshmanjoo: union with all the universe.) Needlessness crowned as leadership.
**9.13** — *nātha lokābhimānānām apūrvaṃ tvaṃ nibandhanam.* Lord, unprecedented ground of world-prides — when shall I be greatly proud, filled with Your devotion-rasa? Pride sanctified (cf. 13.4).
**9.14** — *aśeṣaviṣayāśūnyaśrīsamāśleṣasusthitaḥ.* Well-set in never-object-empty glory-embrace — when shall I lie like at cool lotus-feet? Lying as attainment-posture.
**9.15** — *bhaktyāsavasamṛddhāyās tvatpūjābhogasampadaḥ.* Of worship-wealth grown rich on devotion-liquor — when shall I reach the far shore, when be apt? Shore-crossing delayed to penultimate verse.
**9.16** — *ānandabāṣpapūraskhalitaparibhrāntagadgadākrandaḥ.* Tears first, stumbling, wandering, choked crying — laughing-lit face: when shall I get Your touch-rasa? Weeping as the road, laughter as the gate.
**9.17** — *paśujanasamānavṛttām avadhūya.* Shaking off beast-people-like conduct — when shall I taste the self-form fit for Your devotion? Beast shaken, self tasted.
**9.18** — *labdhāṇimādisiddhir vigalitasakalopatāpasantrāsaḥ.* Siddhis gained, all heat-fear melted — when shall I play, devoted to drinking Your devotion-rasa-medicine? Powers as toys, medicine as play.
**9.19** — *nātha kadā sa tathāvidha ākrando.* When shall that cry of mine rise in speech — at which Your form flashes before me at once? The cry that summons form: speech causing vision.
**9.20** — *gāḍhagāḍhabhavadaṅghrisarojāliṅganavyasanatatparacetāḥ.* Mind set on hugging Your tight-tight lotus-feet — when shall I see You, thing or non-thing, without effort? Close: effortless seeing after twenty verses of effortful asking — the asking was the effortlessness.

## 4. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. 9.4's gods: inner faculties vs cosmic deities — both live; do not choose in L2.
2. 9.6's shaming: exultation, not ethics — do not moralize.
3. 9.20's thing/non-thing: object left open, as asked.
4. Meter unverified; Chowkhamba collation pending.

## 5. Decision for L2

Keep every *kadā* as "when" — the anaphora is the song. Carry Lakshmanjoo's inner-faculties gods (4), union-with-all (12). Full Sanskrit appended. Then C1: SUMMARY (asking as autonomy's victory), FUNCTION (asking-song), KEY TERMS (kadā, svātantrya, utsuka), BOUNDARY (§4).

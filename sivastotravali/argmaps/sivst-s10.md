# Śivastotrāvalī s10 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 10, avicchedabhaṅga, 26 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_10.1–10.26 (lines 567–643). Title: breaking of non-separation. Meter: VERIFY. Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.10; Lakshmanjoo audio + translations.json 10_1–10_26; Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

If devotees and worldlings share one world, what distinguishes them — and how can the undistinguished be borne? Answer: nothing distinguishes them but everything: the same world is His, but tasting differs — lovers eat joy where others eat dust; and non-separation itself must be broken open, lest it flatten into sameness.

## 2. The arc

Intolerable leveling (1) → lovers enjoy everywhere (2) → husband as medicine and wealth (3) → a moment fills all time (4) → moon a drop, sun a spark (5) → offering to the third eye (6) → seen and seeing both ways (7) → dragging the outer inside (8) → all same-eyed but the Uneven-eyed Seer (9) → deniers depend on Him (10) → if mind stood, what else needed (11) → same wandering, different placement (12) → mere conduct perfects (13) → bear us as You bear Yourself (14) → touch-rasa hunger after vision (15) → ghee makes sorrows bearable (16) → unreality permitted by omnipotence (17) → qualities dance, breathed by Him (18) → ego the prop of manifestation (19) → anger-fire purifies (20) → daylight self-accusation (21) → all nectar, yet sole mortality-house (22) → insignia of power (23) → ease should be mine (24) → slave-and-self, gods sated through him (25) → eater-fire between heart and navel (26).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse (terse)

**10.1** — *na soḍhavyam avaśyam te jagadekaprabhor idam.* Surely intolerable to You, sole world-Lord: Your Maheśvaras and other world-folk on one level. Complaint as opening: shared level insults distinction. (Lakshmanjoo: "how do You tolerate this?")
**10.2** — *ye sadaivānurāgeṇa bhavatpādānugāminaḥ.* Who always follow Your feet with love — wherever gone, they enjoy certain pleasures. Answer begun: same world, different eating.
**10.3** — *bhartā kālāntako yatra.* Where He is husband and death-ender, whence sickness? Where His fortune is, what other fortune? Husband as medicine and wealth.
**10.4** — *kṣanamātrasukhenāpi vibhur yenāsi labhyase.* By whose moment-pleasure the All is gained — for him all time fills with Your bliss. A moment fills all time: the economy of the instant.
**10.5** — *ānandarasabindus te candramā.* Moon: Your bliss-rasa drop fallen to earth; sun: Your destroyer-heat's spread spark. Cosmic lights demoted to droplets.
**10.6** — *baliṃ yāmas tṛtīyāya netrāyāsmai tava prabho.* Offering to this third eye of Yours, Lord — sole mark of some unworldly greatness. The third eye as the mark of the unworldly.
**10.7** — *tenaiva dṛṣṭo 'si bhavaddarśanādyo.* Seen by Him alone, one joys in Your vision-etc.; or whose joy somehow — by it You are seen. Reversible seeing: seen-by-Him AND joy-seeing-Him; grammar left both ways open.
**10.8** — *yeṣām prasanno 'si vibho.* Those You're pleased with, whose hearts You've won — dragging from Your city, they've made the outer inner. The pleased drag the world inside: introversion as capture.
**10.9** — *tvad ṛte nikhilaṃ viśvaṃ samadṛgyātam.* Apart from You the whole world looks same-eyed — but its Īśvara is You alone, the Uneven-eyed. The one asymmetry that saves the world from flatness.
**10.10** — *āstāṃ bhavatprabhāveṇa vinā sattaiva nāsti.* Leave aside: without Your power there is no being at all — and those whose denials of You don't stand without You. Deniers depend: negation needs being to deny.
**10.11** — *bāhyāntarāntarāyālīkevale cetasi sthitiḥ.* If mind stood in You alone, barriers in-outside gone — what else would be needed, Lord? The whole path as one conditional.
**10.12** — *anye bhramanti bhagavann ātmany evātiduḥsthitāḥ.* Others wander, Lord, in the self itself, ill-placed; others wander in the self itself, well-placed. Same wandering, same self — placement differs. The subtlest verse: no external difference at all.
**10.13** — *apītvāpi bhavadbhaktisudhām.* Even untasted Your devotion-nectar, unseen You — by mere conduct with You, beings perfect. Minimum means, maximum result. (Lakshmanjoo's stressed verse.)
**10.14** — *bhṛtyā vayaṃ tava vibho.* We are Your servants, Lord — so as You bear Yourself for three worlds, bear us too, fully. Servanthood as bearing-claim.
**10.15** — *parānandāmṛtamaye dṛṣṭo 'pi jagadātmani.* Though seen in supreme-bliss-nectar, in the world-self — for touch-rasa with You I hunger exceedingly. Vision satisfied, touch hungry: the senses keep a remainder. (Note GRETIL's (?) — record, do not emend.)
**10.16** — *deva duḥkhāny aśeṣāṇi.* All sorrows even of worldlings — joined to Your ghee-self, become bearable. Ghee (*ghṛta*): suffering fried bearable.
**10.17** — *sarvajñe sarvaśaktau ca tvayy eva sati.* With You — omniscient, omnipotent, consciousness-formed — somehow the world's show, though unreal, is fitting. Unreality permitted by omnipotence.
**10.18** — *tvatprāṇitāḥ sphurantīme guṇā.* Breathed by You, qualities throb — even clod-like — dancing wind-tossed like cotton fluff. Qualities as puppets of His breath.
**10.19** — *yadi nātha guṇeṣv ātmābhimāno na bhavet.* If self-pride in qualities were not — then by what would the world's show of sole-selfness with You stand? A daring theodicy: ego as the prop of manifestation.
**10.20** — *vandyās te 'pi mahīyāṃsaḥ.* Venerable even the dissolved great ones, purified by Your anger-fire-touch. Even anger-fire purifies: no divine act damns.
**10.21** — *mahāprakāśavapuṣi vispaṣṭe bhavati sthite.* With Your broad-light body standing plain — Lord, why do I spread in darkness on all sides? Daylight self-accusation.
**10.22** — *avibhāgo bhavān eva svarūpam amṛtaṃ mama.* You alone undivided, my nectar-nature — yet of mortal natures I alone am the seat. Election paradox: all nectar, yet sole mortality-house. Do not resolve.
**10.23** — *maheśvareti yasyāsti nāmakaṃ.* Whose speech-ornament is the name "Maheśvara," whose head bears the bow-mark — he alone is powerful. Insignia as power: name + bow-mark.
**10.24** — *sadasac ca bhavān eva yena.* Since You are being and non-being — then effortlessly, by own tune, Lord, why is accomplishment not mine? The logic turned on the self: if all is You, ease should be mine.
**10.25** — *śivadāsaḥ śivaikātmā.* Śiva's slave, Śiva-selfed — what joy would he not reach? By whom even chief gods are sated with nectar-wine. Double-identity as omnipotence.
**10.26** — *hṛnnābhyor antarālasthaḥ.* Between heart and navel, beings' bile-bodied — You devour, great fire, all moving-unmoving. Close: the eater-fire; physiology as cosmology.

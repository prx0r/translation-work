# Śivastotrāvalī s11 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 11, autsukyaviśvasita, 15 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_11.1–11.15 (lines 647–689). Title: longing-trust. Meter: VERIFY. Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.11; Lakshmanjoo audio + translations.json 11_1–11_15; Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

What is left when everything is taken — friends, kin, world, merit? Answer: longing itself and trust (*viśvasita*): the devotee with nothing, begging beautifully, naming names until the names become the answer.

## 2. The arc

No world, no kin — all You (1) → begging that begs (2) → straw thrones, sterile merit (3) → unblocked praise (4) → even in dream (5) → fragrance-dot transfiguring (6) → one work only (7) → resume of disqualifications (8) → trifles to pearls (9) → even cheating devotion works (10) → litany as battering-ram (11) → cornered metaphysics, clinging while harassed (12) → approved suffering, chosen longevity (13) → skilled destroyer, act now (14) → bow to the sole work and over-bright light (15).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse (terse)

**11.1** — *jagad idam athavā suhṛdo bandhujano vā na bhavati mama kim api.* This world, or friends, or kin — nothing is mine at all; but when You are all this, who else is mine? Dispossession as possession-proof. (Lakshmanjoo: "I have no universe, no baby, no Viresh, no John, nothing belongs to me.")
**11.2** — *svāmin maheśvaras tvaṃ sākṣāt sarvaṃ jagat tvam eveti.* "Master, Maheśvara, You directly — all the world is You alone" — may the thing itself succeed; and begging even in that is mere begging. Begging that begs and knows it.
**11.3** — *tribhuvanādhipatitvam apīha yat tṛṇam iva.* Even world-overlordship here seems straw to Your devotee; what fruit has good merit, Lord, without Your remembrance? Straw thrones; sterile merit.
**11.4** — *yena naiva bhavato 'sti vibhinnaṃ.* Since nothing at all is apart from You, world-cause — Your play is wondrous, yet praise never sticks. Unblocked praise: wonders don't jam it.
**11.5** — *tvanmayo 'smi bhavadarcananiṣṭhaḥ.* "I am Yours, worship-bound" — always, unceasing, even by own tune, and in dream — why am I not so? The asked state extended to dreaming.
**11.6** — *ye manāg api bhavaccaraṇābjodbhūtasaurabhalavena* Who are touched even slightly by Your foot-lotus-born fragrance-dust — for them all enjoyed things, gods-sought, become rest. Fragrance-dot transfigures all enjoyment.
**11.7** — *hṛdi te na tu vidyate 'nyad.* In heart no other, in word and deed wholly other, O Śambhu — yet grace or check from the truly real: one work only. Integrity demanded; grace/check alike accepted as the one work.
**11.8** — *mūḍho 'smi duḥkhakalito 'smi.* Fool am I, suffering-laden, aging-fearful, powerless — Yours am I: Śambhu, so arrange that, past sorrow's road, I mount the highest yoke fast. Resume of disqualifications as qualification-list.
**11.9** — *tvatkarṇadeśama dhiśayya.* Lying at Your ear-place, my cries, trifling — become like water-drops in bamboo-joints turning pearl. Nearness transmutes: trifles to pearls by location.
**11.10** — *kim iva ca labhyate bata na tair api.* What is not gained, alas, even by those — Lord — who even by deceit delight a moment in Your name? Even cheating devotion works. (Lakshmanjoo's stressed point.)
**11.11** — *śambho śarva śaśaṅkaśekhara śiva.* Śambhu Śarva moon-crested Śiva, three-eyed rosary-bearer, fierce-skulled, trident-armed, ocean of pity, world-builder by fierce power, blue-throated — destroy ill-fortune fast, give supreme success. Litany as battering-ram: eleven epithets, two imperatives.
**11.12** — *tat kiṃ nātha bhaven na.* What would there be where the Lord does not make? What would the knower Śaṅkara destroys be? Clinging to Your unwounded great power, harassed in saṃsāra, I suffer on, alone. Cornered metaphysics turned complaint — and kept complaining.
**11.13** — *yady apy atra varapradoddhatatamāḥ.* Though pains, age, death here are boon-givers most haughty, and approved sounds unstable — yet I long, ever-pleasure-hungering, to stand long tasting Your feet for life-drink. Approved suffering, chosen longevity: tasting-time as life's use.
**11.14** — *he nātha praṇatārtināśanapaṭo.* O Lord skilled at destroying the bowed-down's pain, treasure of good, matted one — act now so that, living, I win unshakable attainments, Your-worship-bound. Professional skill invoked: pain-destroyer by trade.
**11.15** — *namo mohamahādhvāntadhvaṃsanānanyakarmaṇe.* Bow to delusion-great-darkness-destroyer whose sole work is that, to over-bright light moon-marked. Close: the one-work light.

## 4. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. 11.1's dispossession list (universe, baby, Viresh, John in Lakshmanjoo's telling): personal vs paradigmatic — open.
2. 11.12's cornered metaphysics: complaint or theodicy? Open; do not systematize.
3. 11.9's pearl-mechanism: image, not casuistry.
4. Meter unverified; Chowkhamba collation pending.

## 5. Decision for L2

Keep begging verbs (*yācñā*) begging — do not dignify into prayer-theory. Carry Lakshmanjoo's nothing-mine (1), bamboo-pearls (9). Full Sanskrit appended. Then C1: SUMMARY (longing-trust after dispossession), FUNCTION (begging-song), KEY TERMS (autsukya, viśvasita, yācñā), BOUNDARY (§4).

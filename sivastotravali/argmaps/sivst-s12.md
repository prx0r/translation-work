## 1. What is at issue

How is the secret pointed at without being said? Answer: by successive approximations that cancel themselves — no helper, no hinderer (1); sourced from things, senses, awareness-middle, yet self-born (2); how could things reach His path (3); the sated wander free (4); timeless vision (5); entering by resolve (6); pure play (7); dwelling between His feet fearless (8); dissolved, drunk, wandering at will (9); pretense suffices (10); one-taste drinking to fullness (11); the essence-extract (12); inside/outside worship puzzle (13); facing everywhere in agitation (14); reveal the abode (15); fled yet never unseen (16); flood to joy (17); grain for worship-exchange (18); seeing each thing, seeing You (19); unhaunting mind questioned (20); ploughmen see (21); feast unstopped (22); insight, power, unpausing worship (23); sense-heap as His limb (24); roaming mind, skilled service (25); slave by His will, yet faceless (26); opponents wondered at (27); things as things by His being (28); nothing/something grammar of worship (29).

## 2. The arc (compressed: the song proceeds by pairing claim with counter-claim)

1 accusation of non-seeing → 2 self-born seer → 3 how-question with self-answer (He scatters nectar) → 4 sated wandering → 5 timeless vision → 6 resolve-entry → 7 pure play → 8 fearless dwelling → 9 honey-dissolution → 10 pretense suffices → 11 one-taste drinking → 12 the extract → 13 inside/outside puzzle → 14 facing everywhere → 15 reveal the abode → 16 fled-yet-seen → 17 flood to joy → 18 grain-exchange → 19 seeing each, seeing Him → 20 unhaunting asked → 21 ploughmen see → 22 feast unstopped → 23 insight-power-worship → 24 sense-heap as limb → 25 roaming skilled service → 26 faceless slave → 27 opponents → 28 things-as-things → 29 grammar of worship.

## 3. The argument, verse by verse (terse)

**12.1** — *sahakāri na kiñcid iṣyate.* No helper wanted, no hinderer seen — all flooded by You alone: how then do You not see? (Lakshmanjoo: "nothing to be adopted" for finding Him.) Accusation as opening.
**12.2** — *api bhāvagaṇād apīndriyapracayād.* From things, senses, awareness-middle, ever self-born — may I see Him, world-veiled. Sourced yet sourceless; veiled yet to-be-seen.
**12.3** — *kathaṃ te jāyeran.* How could things come to be, and how reach Your path of vision? Rising and rising, You scatter nectar-floods over all. Question asked and answered in one breath. (Note GRETIL's (?) — record, do not emend.)
**12.4** — *sākṣatkṛta bhavadrūpa.* Sated with Your form-nectar, thirst uprooted, drunk — they wander at will. Satiety as freedom's form.
**12.5** — *na tadā na sadā na caikadety* Not then, not always, not once — where no time-thought is: such is Your vision, neither eternal nor otherwise. Timeless without eternalism.
**12.6** — *tvadvilokanasamutkacetaso.* May my mind, risen to see You, gain this yoga-fruit: to enter Your nectar-house by mere resolve, for Your worship. Resolve as door.
**12.7** — *nirvikalpabhavadīyadarśanapraptiphullamanasāṃ.* The clear-minded great ones' pure play and words shine plainly, playfully. Play as evidence of attainment.
**12.8** — *bhavanbhavadīyapādayor.* Dwelling between Your feet, fearless — in those and those world-grounds may I worship unbarred. Between-feet as safest address.
**12.9** — *bhavadaṅghrisaroruhodare.* Dissolved in Your foot-lotus-lake, melted seeking, honey-drunk to excess — may I roam satisfied, at will. Dissolution that wanders.
**12.10** — *yasya dambhād iva.* Whose resolve to worship arose even from pretense — his presence is assured. Pretense suffices: the lowest bar in the set.
**12.11** — *bhagavann itarān apekṣiṇā.* Lord, with one-taste mind seeking nothing else — the all-laying Lord, easy — may I drink to fullness: am I what? Drinking as identity-question.
**12.12** — *tvayā nirākṛtam sarvaṃ heyam.* By You denied: all where You're absent is to be left; where You're You, to be taken — this is the essence-extract. The whole song's rule in one verse. (Lakshmanjoo's essence-verse.)
**12.13** — *bhavato 'ntaracāri bhāvajātaṃ.* The world-born worshipped as chief by being inside You — how, outside, can non-being be worshipped, by whom? Inside/outside puzzle left standing.
**12.14** — *niḥśabdaṃ nirvikalpaṃ ca.* Noiseless, thought-free, undistracted, always — in agitation too: I see You alone everywhere, Three-eyed. Facing as practice, everywhere as field.
**12.15** — *prakaṭaya nijadhāma.* Reveal Your abode where You ever are with the Goddess; foot-dust-ranked servants — why do they look faithless? Abode asked; servants' look questioned.
**12.16** — *darśanapatham upayāto 'py.* Come to vision's path yet fled — whence, my Lord, to me Your servant? Never for a moment are You not every being's sight-object. Fled-yet-seen held together.
**12.17** — *aikyasaṃvidamṛtācchadhārayā.* When with oneness-nectar's pure stream will You flood me — bringing past difference the measurer, gaining self and body, joy? Flooding as union-mode.
**12.18** — *aham ity amuto 'varuddhalokād.* "I," fenced from yonder world, by Your teaching-essence — a grain of world-establishment, that I be worshipped by You. Grain for grain: my world-grain for Your worship.
**12.19** — *aparimitarūpam ahaṃ.* Seeing every thing each moment, O form beyond measure — may I see You well, my own Lord. Each-thing-seeing as You-seeing practice.
**12.20** — *bhavadaṅgagataṃ tam eva kasmān.* Why doesn't mind haunt what limbs love, the desired thing for desire? The unhaunting asked, not answered.
**12.21** — *śataśaḥ kila te tavānubhāvād.* Hundreds, they say, by Your power, with this very eye — even plough-walking — always see Your body ahead. Ploughmen-seers: the democratization verse.
**12.22** — *na sā matir udeti yā na bhavati tvadicchāmayī.* No thought rises unstained by Your will; always good or else — thus I roam as Yours, feast of Your foot unstopped. Will-stained thoughts; unstopped feast.
**12.23** — *bhavadīyagabhīrabhāṣiteṣu.* May insight rise in Your deep sayings; then practiced power too, and unpausing worship-addiction. Insight → power → addiction: the sequence.
**12.24** — *vyavahārapade 'pi sarvadā.* In dealings too, always, may this sense-heap appear to me as Your limb — not self-warranted. Sense-heap as His limb; warrant from Him.
**12.25** — *manasi svarasena yatra tatra.* Mind roaming freely in its fields, unswerving — may I ever be worship-skilled toward You. Roaming + unswerving together.
**12.26** — *bhagavan bhavadicchayaiva dāsas.* Lord, by Your will alone Your slave I was made — another's no power here — yet why never see Your face-moon? Placed-yet-faceless: the complaint. (Lakshmanjoo: "I never wished... Your own free will.")
**12.27** — *samutsukās tvāṃ prati ye.* The eager toward You who face You as counter-form — ah, what would befall them, what practice would fruit? Opponents wondered at, not warned.
**12.28** — *bhāvā bhāvatayā santu.* May things be as things by Your being for me; thus may nothing be otherwise, nothing but You. Being as benediction.
**12.29** — *yan na kiñcid api.* May nothing-at-all be nothing, something-at-all be something to me; in all ways may You be worship-received. Grammar itself worshipped: the close.

## 1. What is at issue

How does a devotee live inside non-duality while still embodied — perceiving, worshipping, doubting? The song answers by collapsing every duality into one act: definition (pain = separation, pleasure = union, 13.1), then twenty demonstrations that each corner of experience — senses, worship, grace, poison, laughter — is already the Lord's body.

## 2. The arc

Definition (1): pain/pleasure defined as separation/union. Purification (2–3): the moment of non-manifestation wiped; abiding, worshipping, living-or-dead in the full body. Pride sanctified (4): "I am the Lord" litany — ego glorious only in devotees. Perception remade (5–9): things-as-they-are as fit for worship; meditation-seeing-touching sequence; the festival pair (seeing + worship) mutually grounding; drinking through every sense; the country where no other is felt. Service and grace (10–11): made a slave by His will — therefore fit for audience; grace deliberates never — so why the delay now? Vision and ascent (12–14): seeing Him everywhere with one's own worshipped hands; mounting the throne by mere wish; the touch that gives wonder, worshipped by the unwavering. Cosmos (15–16): His self-shining swells all; who sees all as His body fears nothing. Poison and play (17–20): throat-poison become my nectar; songs and girls wanted — in another way; His tricks uncountable, His gift unspendable; entering the fathomless, worshipping and praising, always.

## 3. The argument, verse by verse

**13.1** — *saṃgrahena sukhaduḥkhalakṣaṇam.* Hear the brief definition: union with You is pleasure; separation from the husband-Lord is pain. The whole song is the expansion of this definition. (Lakshmanjoo: "I will give you the definition of my pain and pleasure.")
**13.2** — *antar apy atitarām aṇīyasī yā tvadaprathanakālikā.* Even the subtlest inward moment of Your non-manifestation — wipe it out everywhere, reveal Your stainless own-form. Impurity = only the instant He doesn't appear. (Lakshmanjoo: "You are not appearing to me" — the one impurity.)
**13.3** — *tāvake vapuṣi viśvanirbhare.* Abiding, worshipping constantly in Your world-full body at the time of undying consciousness-nectar: living, dead, or otherwise be mine. Life/death both offered; abiding is the point. (Lakshmanjoo: "one desire, one longing.")
**13.4** — *īśvaro 'ham aham eva rūpavān.* "I am the Lord, I alone am formed, learned, fortunate — who else?" This pride shines — supremely — in Your lovers. Ego not killed but relocated: glorious only as His. (Lakshmanjoo's centerpiece: this mānitā "shines and is appropriate in those who have got love for Thee.")
**13.5** — *yathāsthitapadārthasaṃvidā māṃ kuruṣva caraṇārcanocitam.* By the awareness of things exactly as they stand, make me fit for foot-worship — I who am born from telling non-duality's nectar. Perception-as-it-is is the qualification; no other preparation. (Lakshmanjoo: perception "born anew.")
**13.6** — *dhyāyate tad anu dṛśyate tataḥ spṛśyate ca.* Meditated, then seen, then touched — the Lord Himself. Where this worship-festival is, let it always be mine, by Your power. Sequence: meditation → vision → contact; the festival is the state, not the rite. (Lakshmanjoo: concentration "not by effort, automatically.")
**13.7** — *yugmam etad itaretarāśrayaṃ.* The pair — seeing things as they are AND the worship-festival — ground each other, expanding always in devotees. Mutual grounding, not stages: vision feeds worship feeds vision. (Lakshmanjoo: "two moments of that great festival.")
**13.8** — *tattadindriyamukhena santataṃ.* Through each sense-door constantly, drinking the wine of Your worship-rasa in all-filled feeling-cups — may I be mad. Every sense a mouth; madness the goal. (Lakshmanjoo: "I want to get drunk, always drunk!")
**13.9** — *anyavedyam aṇumātram asti na.* Where not an atom is felt as other, all self-shining expands — there, Lord, keep me, Your constant worshipper, standing in Your city. The country with no other (cf. 1.4's field, now interiorized).
**13.10** — *dāsadhāmni viniyojito 'py ahaṃ svecchayaiva.* Though posted to slaves' quarters by Your own will — am I not fit, by seeing or even by foot-massage? Slavery by His will IS the qualification; the lowest service outranks rank.
**13.11** — *śaktipātasamaye vicāraṇaṃ... na karoṣi karhicit.* At grace's moment You never deliberate worthiness — so what has come over You now, delaying Your self-revelation to me? The boldest verse: grace's own logic used to accuse grace's delay. (Lakshmanjoo: "You shower grace without thinking if he is worthy.")
**13.12** — *tatra tatra viṣaye bahirvibhāty* In each object shining outside, and inside joined with the Goddess, may I always behold You, world-full, worshipped by my own hands. Both directions at once; the hands that worship are the devotee's own.
**13.13** — *svāmisaudham abhisandhimātrato nirvibandham.* The Master's palace mounted without obstruction by mere resolve, always — may I be bliss-freed, playing at drinking supreme-nectar-wine. Ascent by wish alone (*abhisandhimātrataḥ*), not by stages. (Lakshmanjoo: "not by effort... whenever I wish.")
**13.14** — *yatsamastasubhagārthavastuṣu sparśamātravidhinā camatkṛtim.* Whatever wonder arises by mere touch among all fair things — by that, the unwavering worship Your body. Touch, not analysis, transmits the wonder; worship answers touch.
**13.15** — *sphārayasy akhilam ātmanā sphuran.* Swelling all by self-shining, You touch the universe touching form; whatever swirls by its own juice — that is the swelling world. Cosmology as play: His self-enjoyment swells into worlds. (Lakshmanjoo: "when Your joy knows no bounds, the whole universe shines joyously.")
**13.16** — *yo 'vikalpam idam arthamaṇḍalaṃ paśyatīśa.* Who sees this whole object-circle without construction as Your body, Lord — the world filled with his own self's side — for him, ever-happy, whence fear? Fearlessness as perception's corollary (answers 1.3's fear-talk from inside). (Lakshmanjoo: universe "filled with God consciousness everywhere... always blissful.")
**13.17** — *kaṇṭhakoṇaviniviṣṭam īśa te kālakūṭam api me mahāmṛtam.* The kālakūṭa poison set in Your throat's corner is great nectar to me; even taken nectar would be poison to me if differencehod prevails. Poison/nectar decided by stance, not substance — the myth (churning ocean, throat) turned into epistemology. (Lakshmanjoo tells the full churning story here.)
**13.18** — *tvatpralāpamayaraktagītikāni tya yuktavadanopaśobhitaḥ.* May I be graced with song-mouths lovely with prattling You, my heart forever gone to what Your worship-loving prefers. Songs and girls — wanted, but otherwise (Lakshmanjoo): desire kept, object exchanged. The householder's verse.
**13.19** — *īhitaṃ na bata pārameśvaraṃ śakyate gaṇayituṃ.* Alas, the Supreme's play cannot be counted — and so my nectar-full body, given, He does not let me drink as I will. Gift and withholding together: grace gives the cup, keeps the drinking. Closest to complaint in the song; held inside praise.
**13.20** — *tvām agādham avikalpam advayaṃ.* Entering You — fathomless, construction-free, non-dual, devourer of all things' meaning — O Umā's Lord, may I always worship and praise. The close: entering AND worshipping (union without merger-into-silence); praise continues inside the fathomless. (Lakshmanjoo: the "fraudness" laughter — perturbed, forgiven, still singing.)

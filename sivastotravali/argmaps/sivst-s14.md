## 1. What is at issue

How does one praise the Lord of all without reducing Him? Answer: by victory-cries (*jaya*) that let each attribute stand absolute — names, body, beauty, power, play, terror, intimacy — until the cries themselves become thirteen and collapse into the three-eyed One. Praise as conquest in reverse: He conquers birth and death (14.24), so the singer conquers silence by shouting victory.

## 2. The arc

Taste of proclamation (1) → name-lists (2–3) → body and feet (4) → beauty and adornment (5–8) → duping and embrace-fortune (9) → greed for gifts, pleased by drunken dance (10) → gods rise and fall, His command held high (11) → seal on worlds, self-giving to the uttermost (12) → secondless at creation, Goddess as sole helper (13) → one-stride seizure, childhood-sung sound (14) → high without need of pity, Death's terror (15) → unopposed fierceness, hundredfold names (16) → play-poured nectar, instant anger (17) → lamp for the blind, wakeful while worlds sleep (18) → life-hum in the body-cave, rasa sporting in minds' sky (19) → gold-born mountain, portent-moon over the wicked (20) → unreachable by austerities, seen by bhakti-madmen (21) → dependents as wealth-leaves, fondling the surrendered (22) → sole doer of the three acts, lotus-festival (23) → thirteen victories, three-eyed close (24).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse

**14.1** — *jayalakṣmīnidhānasya nijasya svāminaḥ puraḥ.* Before my own master, treasure of victory-fortune — may I taste for a moment the nectar of victory-proclamation. Tasting, not speaking: praise as savoring.
**14.2** — *jayaikarudraikaśiva mahādeva maheśvara.* Victory: one Rudra, one Śiva... Pārvatī's lover, Śarva, eldest of all gods. Name-heaping as the method; each name a victory.
**14.3** — *jaya trailokyanāthaikalāñchanālikalocana.* Victory: moon-mark-eyed sole mark of the three-worlds-Lord; victory: poison-mark-throated, who drank kālakūṭa for the worlds' pain. Mark (lāñchana) theology: His marks are offices.
**14.4** — *jaya mūrtatriśaktyātmiśataśūlollasatkara.* Victory: whose self is the embodied three powers, hundred-spike-bright-handed; victory: whose lotus-feet are worshipped with wishes accomplished by mere will. Body = powers; feet = wish-fulfillment without process.
**14.5** — *jaya śobhaśatasyandilokottaravapurdhara.* Victory: bearer of a body beyond worlds, streaming a hundred beauties; victory: single-braided, Ganges-thinned, ash-seized. Beauty and ash together; transcendence dressed in cremation signs.
**14.6** — *jaya kṣīrodaparyastajyotsnācchāyānulepana.* Victory: smeared with milk-ocean-spread moonlight shade; victory: snow-ornamented with jewel-light risen from Īśvara-limb-contact. Adornment as contact-theology.
**14.7** — *jayākṣayaikaśītāṃśukalāsadṛśasaṃśraya.* Victory: refuge like the one undying cool-rayed digit; victory: consecrated by Ganges-always and universal lordship. Refuge = the moon-digit; consecration = Ganges + overlordship.
**14.8** — *jayādharāṅgasaṃsparśapāvanīkṛtagokula.* Victory: whose lowest-limb-touch purifies cowherds; victory: fixed-present where intoxicated devotees gather. The low touch sanctifies; His address is the gathering.
**14.9** — *jaya svecchātapodeśavipralambhitabāliśa.* Victory: who dupes the childish by showing a hot place of His own will; victory: fit vessel for Gauri-embrace-fortune. Duping (*vipralambha*) as grace: heat shown, embrace given.
**14.10** — *jaya bhaktirasārdrārdrabhāvopāyanalampaṭa.* Victory: greedy for love-gifts wet with bhakti-rasa; victory: pleased by the speech-dance of devotees drunk with devotion. His greed meets theirs; dance pleases more than decorum. (Lakshmanjoo: fond of presents "with moist point of view.")
**14.11** — *jaya brahmādideveśaprabhāvaprabhavavyaya.* Victory: whose rise-and-fall is the power of Brahmā-and-first-gods; victory: whose command world-lord-rows bear on heads. Gods have careers; His command outlasts them.
**14.12** — *jaya sarvajagannyastasvamudrāvyaktavaibhava.* Victory: whose manifest glory is His seal set on all worlds; victory: great Lord of all, down to giving the very self. Seal-theology + self-gift to the uttermost (*ātmadānaparyanta*).
**14.13** — *jaya trailokyasargecchāvasarāsaddvitīyaka.* Victory: secondless at creation-wish-moment; victory: with sole Goddess-helper bearing lordship's burden. Secondless — yet helped; solitude keeps company.
**14.14** — *jayākramasamākrāntasamastabhuvanatraya.* Victory: who seized all three worlds in one stride; victory: unbroken Lord-sound sung from childhood. Vāmana-stride + eternal sound; conquest and music.
**14.15** — *jayānukampādiguṇānapekṣasahajonnate.* Victory: innately high, needing no pity-etc. qualities; victory: Bhairava before whom great Death is made. Grace needs no qualifying virtue — the anti-merit verse. (Lakshmanjoo's stressed point.)
**14.16** — *jaya viśvakṣayoccaṇḍakriyāniṣparipanthika.* Victory: unopposed in fierce world-ending acts; victory: hundredfold-good-named recitation-following. Terror unopposed; names multiply after it.
**14.17** — *jaya helāvitīrnaitadamṛtākarasāgara.* Victory: nectar-mine-ocean poured out in play; victory: whose instant anger dries the world-ending in a flash. Play pours, anger dries — same sea, two weathers.
**14.18** — *jaya mohāndhakārāndhajīvalokaikadīpaka.* Victory: sole lamp of beings blind in delusion-darkness; victory: wakeful supreme Person while the sleeping world... The lamp burns for the blind; He wakes while all sleep.
**14.19** — *jaya dehādrikuñjāntarnikūjañjīvajīvaka.* Victory: life-of-life humming inside the body-mountain cave; victory: best rasa sporting in good minds' sky. Interiority as geography: cave, hum, sky-sport.
**14.20** — *jaya jāmbūnadodagradhātūdbhavagirīśvara.* Victory: mountain-lord born of high gold-ore (Himālaya); victory: moon rising as calamity-portent over the wicked. Gold-mountain AND portent-moon: fortune for devotees, omen for the wicked — same rising.
**14.21** — *jaya kaṣṭatapaḥkliṣṭamunidevadurāsada.* Victory: hard to reach by austerity-tormented sages and gods; victory: seen by the world of bhakti-madmen mounted on every state. The anti-ascetic verse (cf. 1.17, 2.24): techniques fail where madness sees. (Lakshmanjoo: munis/ṛṣis/gods "don't find You.")
**14.22** — *jaya svasampatprasarapatrīkṛtanijāśrita.* Victory: who leafed His dependents spreading His own wealth; victory: whose sole purpose is fondling surrendered people. Dependents as leaves of His wealth-tree; His one business: caressing the surrendered.
**14.23** — *jaya sargasthitidhvaṃsakāraṇaikāvadānaka.* Victory: sole performer of creating-staying-destroying; victory: festival of the play-lotus blooming in drunken devotion. The three acts done singly; the lotus blooms drunk.
**14.24** — *jaya jayabhājana jaya jitajanmajarāmaraṇa... (13× jaya) ... tryakṣa.* Thirteen victories — worthy-of-victory, conqueror of birth-age-death, eldest of worlds... — O three-eyed One. Number runs out into eyes: the list ends in seeing. (Lakshmanjoo glosses each epithet with footnote apparatus.)

# Śivastotrāvalī s15 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 15, bhakti, 19 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_15.1–15.19 (lines 919–974). Utpala's own title; third of the āhnika three. Meter: VERIFY. Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.15; Lakshmanjoo audio + translations.json 15_1–15_19 (with footnote apparatus nn.271–276); Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

What is a devotee — as against yogins, scholars, ascetics, even the liberated? Answer: one for whom devotion is not a means but the thing itself — eaten, drunk, rolled in, guarded as wealth, sung laughing and crying — with liberation as its after-taste, not its wage.

## 2. The arc

Books/ascetics/scholars vs devotees (1) → eating time, fed happy on the shore (2) → laughing/crying His praise aloud (3) → neither detached nor master nor liberation-seeker — drunk (4) → worship by drawing the outer inside (5) → tasting something between all pairs (6) → blind/lepers shine (7) → gleaners' sons rule kings (8) → rolling yet climbing, mind-worshipped (9) → the great wealth: guard, grow, honor it (10) → no jealousy toward Her (11) → future devotion, curd-greed dead (12) → ever-growing devotion, what attainment is not this (13) → gem-creeper sunk in muddy mind, alas (14) → devotion IS supreme attainment; only powers missing — my worry (15) → praise-fragrance perfumes hostile ascetics (16) → no light anywhere, yet devotees worship at His night-post (17) → let darkness swell or the triple world dissolve in Him (18) → the endless road, unspent pleasures, wasted life — make me Yours, long-rich (19).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse

**15.1** — *trimalakṣālino granthāḥ... yoginaḥ paṇḍitāḥ svasthās tvadbhaktā eva tattvataḥ.* Books wash three stains, and crossers, yogins, scholars stand healthy — but devotees alone, in truth. Everyone else is preparation; devotees are the thing. (Lakshmanjoo: śāstras remove malas — "those śāstras are also..." devotees alone stand.)
**15.2** — *māyīyakālaniyatirāgādyāhāratarpitāḥ.* Fed and happy, eating Māyā-time-limit-passion etc. as food — devotees stroll on the world's shore. Impurities as diet: what binds others feeds them. (Lakshmanjoo: devotees "consumed those elementary worlds.")
**15.3** — *rudanto vā hasanto vā tvām uccaiḥ pralapanty amī.* Laughing or crying, they babble You aloud — devotees, whose praise-word offerings are ornaments apart. Babble (*pralapa*) reclaimed: incoherence as offering-form.
**15.4** — *na virakto na cāpīśo mokṣākāṅkṣī tvadarcakaḥ.* Not detached, not master, not liberation-desiring is Your worshipper — but drunk with soaring devotion-liquor-rasa. Triple negation of spiritual résumés; drunkenness as the only credential.
**15.5** — *bāhyaṃ hṛdaya evāntar abhihṛtyaiva yo 'rcati.* Who worships by drawing the outer inside the heart alone — to him, in devotion-nectar floods, I bow. Interiorization defined operationally. (Lakshmanjoo: worship "by diverting it in one's own heart.")
**15.6** — *dharmādharmātmanor antaḥ.* Between dharma/adharma acts, knowledges, pleasure/pain selves — devotees taste SOMETHING — ah! The *kim api* held open: unnamed by design.
**15.7** — *carācarapitaḥ svāmin apy andhā api kuṣṭhinaḥ.* Blind ones, lepers even — they shine, supreme, ornamented by blazing devotion to You. Disfigurement outshone; ornament = devotion itself. (Cf. 1.21's divers.)
**15.8** — *śiloñchapicchakaśipuvicchāyāṅgā api.* Gleaners, winnowers, enemies' shadows — yet by devotion's heat they rule even king-of-kings. Social inversion complete: lowest laborers outrank emperors.
**15.9** — *sudhārdrāyāṃ bhavadbhaktau luṭhatāpy ārurukṣuṇā.* Though rolling in nectar-wet devotion, yet wanting to climb — some worship You with mind alone, standing all around. Rolling AND climbing; mind-only worship counts as standing-around-Him.
**15.10** — *rakṣaṇīyaṃ vardhanīyaṃ bahumānyam idaṃ prabho.* To be guarded, grown, honored, Lord — this great wealth of devotion that ends bad rebirths. Wealth-language (cf. 1.23); the only bank balance. (Lakshmanjoo: "Let it go to the dogs! Only the supreme treasure...")
**15.11** — *nātha te bhaktajanatā yady api tvayi rāgiṇī.* Lord, though Your devotees love You — abandoning jealousy toward Her, may She always be pleased, the Mistress. The devotee prays for the Goddess's pleasure against his own possessiveness. Startling ethics of non-jealousy.
**15.12** — *bhavadbhāvaḥ puro bhāvī.* Your future being first; when devotion's occasion comes and the milk-ocean is churned — curd-greed is killed. Eschatology in dairy terms: the great churning ends small hungers.
**15.13** — *kim iyaṃ na siddhir atulā.* Is this not attainment beyond compare — ever-growing devotion to Śambhu, always new? What happiness does it not stream? Rhetoric as doctrine: growth itself is the attainment.
**15.14** — *manasi maline madīyemagnā tvadbhaktimaṇilatā kaṣṭam.* Sunk in my dirty mind, Your devotion gem-creeper — alas — does not spread its own splendor's unfoldings. The one lament: the gem is buried. *Kaṣṭam* (alas) carried, not solved.
**15.15** — *bhaktir bhagavati bhavati trilokanāthe nanūttamā siddhiḥ.* Devotion to the Lord of three worlds IS supreme attainment — but lacking aṇimā etc., is it not full? — that is my worry. The doctrinal peak AND the honest doubt in one verse; the worry is confessed, not answered. (Lakshmanjoo: "cent-percent correct... that is the real and supreme yogic power already attained.")
**15.16** — *bāhyato 'ntar api cotkaṭonmiṣattryambakastavakasaurabhāḥ.* Outside and in, the fragrance of Your praise-blossoms' opening perfumes even hostile ascetics dwelling near. Praise as atmosphere: even opponents breathe it.
**15.17** — *jyotir asti kathayāpi na kiṃ cid.* By no account is there any light; the whole world fast asleep — yet at Your Śivarātri post, devotees always worship You. Darkness total, worship continuing: the night-post (*śivarātripada*) image. (Developed from 13.20's entering-and-praising.)
**15.18** — *sattvaṃ satyaguṇe śive bhagavati sphārībhavatv arcane.* Let goodness expand in true worship of gracious Śiva; let Śaṅkara-foot-dust heaps sport on the crown — to cut even passion-memories, let darkness swell, or the triple world dissolve in Your self-loss. Prayer for expansion through dissolution; even darkness conscripted.
**15.19** — *saṃsārādhvā sudūraḥ.* The world-road is long; limbs burnt by harsh diseases; pleasures unenjoyed; happiness never long — thus I have become useless, moon-crest-foot-pressed, wholly Yours'... so make me, quickly, long-and-rich in great fortune, as Your devotee. The close: autobiography as argument — wasted life + pressed head = claim on fortune. (Lakshmanjoo: "endless... no end... make me long-rich.")

## 4. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. 15.6's *kim api* — deliberately unnamed; do not name it.
2. 15.11's jealousy (*īrṣyā*): of whom toward Her? Left open.
3. 15.15's worry: unanswered in-song; do not resolve with other songs' material inside C1 (note cross-ref only).
4. Meter unverified; Chowkhamba collation pending.

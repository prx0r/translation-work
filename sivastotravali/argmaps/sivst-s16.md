# Śivastotrāvalī s16 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 16, pāśānudbheda, 30 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_16.1–16.30 (lines 978–1066). Title: bursting of the noose. Note 16.1 variant apparatus citing Nareśvaraparīkṣā (record, do not emend). Meter: VERIFY. Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.16; Lakshmanjoo audio + translations.json 16_1–16_30; Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

If devotion is supreme, why do the bound stay bound — and what exactly bursts? Answer: the noose bursts from inside: neither people nor gods block anyone; the devotee's own devotion-pleasure is the solvent, and even mutual dependence (He pleased by devotion, devotion His gift) is declared fitting.

## 2. The arc

Nothing blocks, for folk or devotees (1) → pure self seen at once by devotees (2) → laughing victors, drunk on nectar (3) → no dryness, no release-seeking (4) → unknown before, may it grow (5) → only prayer: perpetual immersion-possession (6) → drunk, angry, leaping, shouting "Śiva" (7) → crisis or peace, tears or laughter — by devotion (8) → nothing to be known inside/out (9) → pierced within, bristling at abusers' nectar-drops (10) → pain-pleasure for devotees' enjoyment (11) → held everywhere, same touch-joy (12) → some dissolve duality's double-refuge, tasting clear nectar (13) → erring pilgrims err; matchless Being shines (14) → ripened mind unmannered (15) → knowledge-rich put to shame (16) → devotion-nectar-bath as rite, high joy throughout (17) → praised, sought, worshipped: devotees' world-life praiseworthy (18) → liberation renamed as ripe devotion (19) → pain invited, pleasure-lines refused (20) → mutual dependence declared fitting (21) → form/no-form, in/out — always nectar (22) → another world-stand in this world (23) → devotion, devotion, something — ah (24) → I weep, let fierce devotion be mine (25) → jewel or straw, stuck to You becomes priceless (26) → unparted on knowing's road, they win (27) → embraced in saṃsāra's hall (28) → hugging Śiva with the feasting Goddess (29) → overlordship with no subjects; otherness by which the world shines as it does not (30).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse (terse)

**16.1** — *na kiñcid eva lokānāṃ bhavadāvaraṇaṃ prati.* Nothing blocks folk, nothing blocks devotees from Your veiling. No external block exists. (Variant apparatus citing Nareśvaraparīkṣā recorded; text kept.)
**16.2** — *apy upāyakramaprāpyaḥ.* Though reached by method-ladders, crowded with attributes — to devotees the self appears at once, pure. Method admitted, immediacy asserted.
**16.3** — *jayanto 'pi hasanty ete.* Laughing victors, laughing conquered — drunk on His devotion-nectar, whoever they are. Winning and losing both laugh.
**16.4** — *śuṣkakaṃ maiva siddheya maiva mucyeya vāpi tu* May I never dry up, never seek release — full of His devotion-rasa, best-liked, attained. Dryness refused, release declined.
**16.5** — *yathaivajñātapūrvo 'yaṃ bhavadbhaktiraso mama.* As unknown before was this devotion-rasa of Yours to me — so, Lord, may that same one grow. Ignorance of it proves its growth is His doing.
**16.6** — *satyena bhagavan nānyaḥ prārthanāprasaro 'sti me* Truly, Lord, I have no other prayer-course — only may such immersion-possession by devotion be mine always. The single prayer.
**16.7** — *bhaktikṣīvo 'pi kupyeyaṃ bhavāyānuśayīya ca.* Drunk on devotion I may rage, repent, leap, shout "Śiva" — enough. The full catalogue licensed in one verse.
**16.8** — *viṣamastho 'pi svastho 'pi.* In crisis or at peace, weeping or laughing, deep or distracted — may I be, Lord, by devotion. Equanimity verse. (Lakshmanjoo: "I don't mind.")
**16.9** — *bhaktānāṃ nāsti saṃvedyaṃ.* For devotees nothing to be known, inside You or out — where consciousness-natured You stand thought-free. Knowing canceled as requirement.
**16.10** — *bhaktā nindānukare 'pi.* Devotees rejoice even at abusers — pierced within, bristling with sharp horripilation, as if by nectar-drops. Abuse transmuted on contact.
**16.11** — *duḥkhāpi vedanā bhaktimatāṃ* Even pain-feeling ripens to enjoyment for devotees, whose all-awareness, moon-wet, is moonlight-made. Pain as enjoyment-material.
**16.12** — *yatra tatroparuddhānāṃ.* Wherever held, devotees — inside and out — unfeigned, Your body-touch-taste-joy the same. Held everywhere, same joy.
**16.13** — *taveśa bhakter arcāyāṃ* In worship of Your devotion, duality's double-refuge meanness — tasting the clear nectar-body, some dissolve it. Meanness (*dainya*) dissolved, not disciplined.
**16.14** — *bhrāntās tīrthadṛśo bhinnā.* Erring are pilgrim-eyes, divided — for division is just erring; but matchless, strifeless Being shines for devotees. Other paths err by erring; His stands by standing.
**16.15** — *mānāvamānarāgādiniṣpākavimalaṃ manaḥ.* Mind ripened past honor, contempt, love and passion into stainlessness — how could its bearer be world-mannered? Ripeness unmanners.
**16.16** — *rāgadveṣandhakāro 'pi.* Passion-aversion-darkness conquered by devotion-light — who are the knowledge-rich before them? Devotion-light outranks knowledge-wealth.
**16.17** — *yasya bhaktisudhāsnānapānādividhisādhanam.* Whose rite is devotion-nectar-drink-and-bath — high joy from beginning through end. Bath-and-drink as complete rite.
**16.18** — *kīrtyaś cintāpadaṃ mṛgyaḥ.* Praised, sought, worshipped — for that alone devotees' world-life is praiseworthy. World-life justified by its object.
**16.19** — *muktisaṃjñā vipakvāyā.* Liberation's name is but Your ripe devotion — mounted on its first stage, we are near-free. Liberation renamed: first stage = near-free. (Anti-climax as doctrine.)
**16.20** — *duḥkhāgamo 'pi bhūyān.* May pain's coming be great for me, devotion-filled self — never turned from You, be even pleasure-line. Pain invited, pleasure refused: inversion complete.
**16.21** — *tvaṃ bhaktyā prīyase* You are pleased by devotion; devotion, You pleased — this mutual dependence is fitting, as You alone know. The circle blessed, not solved. (Lakshmanjoo: "one problem in this theory of devotion.")
**16.22** — *sākāro vā nirākaro* With form or formless, inside or out — to devotion-selves, always nectar, Lord. Form-indifference sealed.
**16.23** — *asminn eva jagaty.* In this very world, for Your devotees, another world-stand exists — fruit of joy-showing. Another world inside this one.
**16.24** — *guhye bhaktiḥ pare bhaktir.* Devotion hidden, devotion supreme, devotion to the world-great Lord — devotion, ah, something in You, Śiva. The catalogue that gives up cataloguing: *kim api*.
**16.25** — *bhaktir bhaktir pare bhaktir.* Devotion, devotion, supreme devotion — I cry loud: let fierce devotion be mine supreme. The shout-verse. (Lakshmanjoo: "I will cry, loudly.")
**16.26** — *yato 'smi sarvaśobhānāṃ.* Since I am the birth-ground of all beauties — stuck to You, jewel or straw becomes priceless. Adhesion alchemy: contact, not quality, prices.
**16.27** — *āvedakād ā ca vedyād.* Unparted on knowing's road, they win — Your devotees. Non-parting as victory-condition.
**16.28** — *saṃsārasadaso bāhye.* Embraced in saṃsāra's hall by some, You — Lord, by others self-tormenting unreined. Embraced and fled in the same hall.
**16.29** — *pānāśanaprasādhana.* With Śivā who eats, decks, celebrates the world, festival-hurried — hug tight, Śiva, I bow. The divine couple feasting; the devotee hugging back.
**16.30** — *parameśvaratā jayaty.* Peerless overlordship wins, world-Lord, being subject-free — and so does Your otherness, by which this world shines as it does not. Close: sovereignty without subjects; otherness by which the world both shines and doesn't. Do not resolve.

# Śivastotrāvalī s17 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 17, divyakrīḍābahumāna, 48 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_17.1–17.48 (lines 1070–1212). Longest song; much-honoring of divine play. Meter: VERIFY (mixed lengths visible). Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.17; Lakshmanjoo audio + translations.json 17_1–17_48; Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2).*

## 1. What is at issue

What is worship, if everything already worships? Answer: the festival itself — *pūjāmahotsava* — in which acts, actors, instruments, and results are all His play enjoyed as ours: a 48-verse catalogue proving nothing is outside the festival by naming everything inside it.

## 2. The arc (compressed: the catalogue IS the argument)

Tears tasting nectar (1) → all acts succeed, devotees are successes (2) → all beings, all states, unceasing, my gods (3) → touch perfected by meditation, named rite (4) → equinox-moment always (5) → beginningless deed, single resort (6) → gods fortunate even in delusion (7) → whenever-whatever worship (8) → who equals Indra and Brahmā (9) → the festival known only by getting (10) → worship with all 36 tattva-acts (11) → may time pass in joy (12) → greed growing daily (13) → dwelling in His ocean (14) → mind offered as sweet (15) → senses sent for nectar (16) → what liberates but drowning (17) → perpetual foot-festival as grace-cause (18) → tasting each act's joy-madness (19) → seen fruit: impossible joy (20) → till festival tasted, no happiness (21) → effortless abiding (22) → nothing ungettable, only mad (23) → unbegging worship praised (24) → what beauty/joy/liberation is not (25) → nourish this body (26) → dependent yet free-moving (27) → thirst quenched into lake (28) → devotee as worship-vessel like Him (29) → festival agitates the 36 (30) → bow to those whose tools are worshipped (31) → mantra-placement then rest (32) → kingdoms blooming, world shared (33) → gods, freed, or some people (34) → devotee's lightness and weight (35) → eye-throw to nectar-rising, like milk-ocean churning (36) → wish-cow vs inward milking (37) → even glances feed the festival (38) → lowliness as top fruit (39) → steps for some, spread for devotees (40) → His foothold through their madness (41) → taste-stunned mind, He alone as vessel (42) → full, steady, devoted means (43) → fortune of the organ-host (44) → prettiness: worshipped and found (45) → rise from formed or formless (46) → lust/anger/pride conscripted worshippers (47) → straw-yet-jewel festival victorious (48).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse (one line each)

**17.1** Festival glorified; tears taste nectar. (Lakshmanjoo's festival frame.)
**17.2** All acts succeed; devotees are successes themselves.
**17.3** All beings, all states, unceasingly — my gods are the worshippers.
**17.4** Touch perfected by meditation, named worship-rule, mine always.
**17.5** Equinox-moment of equanimity-essence, ever-worship with nectar.
**17.6** Beginningless deed, single resort: Yours alone.
**17.7** Gods fortunate even in dream and delusion.
**17.8** Whenever, whatever worship — not only the listed rites. (Lakshmanjoo's whenever-clause.)
**17.9** Who equals Indra and Brahmā-chiefs? No one — devotees only.
**17.10** Festival known only by getting: epistemology of participation.
**17.11** Worship with all 36 tattva-acts of body-speech-mind.
**17.12** Time itself to pass in worship-joy: spend, not save.
**17.13** Greed for worship-enjoyment growing daily, fruiting always.
**17.14** Dwelling in His ocean, nectar-one-rasa-full, always worshipping.
**17.15** Mind offered as sweet in His rite; knots cut simply.
**17.16** Senses sent for His worship-nectar as errand-runners.
**17.17** What liberates but drowning in worship-nectar? Nothing.
**17.18** Perpetual foot-festival as my sole grace-getting.
**17.19** Moment-by-moment tasting of joy-madness in each act.
**17.20** Seen fruit: impossible joy tasted now.
**17.21** Till then, no happiness tasted at all — retrospective devaluation.
**17.22** Effortless abiding without sense-search strain.
**17.23** Nothing ungettable; only mad with worship.
**17.24** Unbegging worship praised — begging finally dropped.
**17.25** What beauty/joy/liberation is not, when He is worshipped?
**17.26** Nourish this body as His worship-thing.
**17.27** Dependent yet free-moving: both at once.
**17.28** Thirst quenched into cool-sweet great lake.
**17.29** As He is the worship-enjoyment-vessel, so the devotee.
**17.30** Festival agitates even the 36 tattvas. (Lakshmanjoo's "great day.")
**17.31** Bow to those whose worship-tools are themselves worshipped.
**17.32** Mantra-placement of His selfhood, then rest without roaming.
**17.33** Kingdoms blooming, world shared out in nectar-wine.
**17.34** Gods, freed, or some people — status unanswerable.
**17.35** Devotee's lightness AND weight; world's dignity as gear.
**17.36** Eye-throw to nectar-rising: the milk-ocean churn, miniature.
**17.37** Wish-cow worship vs inward milking: two economies.
**17.38** Even glances feed the festival; nothing is waste.
**17.39** Lowliness as top fruit of devotion-agitation.
**17.40** Steps for some, non-duality-spread for devotees.
**17.41** His foothold through their madness: divine dependence confessed.
**17.42** Taste-stunned mind; He alone as worship's vessel.
**17.43** Full, pure, steady, devoted means be mine.
**17.44** Fortune of the organ-host in the whole worship-treasure.
**17.45** Prettiness (*peśala*): worshipped by servants, and found.
**17.46** Rise from formed or formless, being or non-being.
**17.47** Lust, anger, pride conscripted as worshippers; I am pleased.
**17.48** Straw-made yet jewel-furnished festival wins. (Lakshmanjoo: the supreme way, "always glorified.")

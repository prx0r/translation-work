# Śivastotrāvalī Stotra 18 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 18, āviṣkāra, 21 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_18.1–18.21 (lines 1216–1278). Title: manifestation. Meter: VERIFY. Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.18 ("Becoming Clear"); Lakshmanjoo audio + translations.json 18_1–18_21; Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

How does the hidden become manifest without ceasing to be hidden? Answer: by beholding — Your devotees find You inside the world, and the world, beheld, turns out to have been Your body all along. Manifestation (*āviṣkāra*) is recognition, not arrival.

## 2. The arc

Found inside the world, never far (1) → He here, She everywhere, no division at the top (2) → ungrateful world, bewildered mind — ah, I am destroyed (3) → needing nothing, I bow (4) → burning world, make me worship-joy (5) → spontaneity, touching feet, world's business as mere touch-tour (6) → things coming and going, may they shine always (7) → ever in His city, or parted — never defeated where I am Yours (8) → cool nectar-floods of Your limb-touch filling all round (9) → dwelling in His ocean, worshipping always (10) → appear before me, three-eyed, trident-graced (11) → pride offered, devotion weighed, be my eyes' ground (12) → dwelling in the nectar-ocean middle, tasting everything everywhere (13) → Your thing, Your truth — who can disclose (14) → no desire for peace, no begging remembered (15) → waking or beyond, the devotee's festival in the ever-present mind (16) → mind's eye-wheel loosened yet unswerving (17) → nothing unmade, no unpleasure made — yet all suffers and divides: bow to the wonder (18) → my doubt-foe crushed, Your sight unbroken (19) → enter me plainly, for I am Yours — worship face to face with violent speed (20) → no one's is praise-power, yet it exists since You are beautiful — may I see unwearied (21).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse (terse)

**18.1** — *jagato 'ntarato bhavantam āptvā punar etad bhavato 'ntarāl labhante jagadīśa tavaiva bhaktibhājo na hi teṣām iha dūrato 'sti kiñcit.* Finding You inside the world, they get inside You — never far are Your devotees. (Lakshmanjoo: found "amidst the universal state.")
**18.2** — *kvacid eva bhavān kvacid bhavānī sakalārthakramagarbhiṇī pradhānā paramārthapade tu naiva devyā bhavato nāpi jatattrayasya bhedaḥ.* He here, She everywhere, in all-things-order pregnant supreme — but at the ultimate step, no division of Her nor of the triple world. Non-duality stated at the top, not the bottom.
**18.3** — *no jānate subhagam apy avalepavanto lokāḥ prayatnasubhagā nikhila hi bhāvāḥ cetaḥ punar yad idam udyatam apy avaiti naivātmarūpam iha hā tad aho hato 'smi.* Ungrateful peoples, bewildered minds — ah, I am destroyed. Confession mid-song, not at the close.
**18.4** — *bhavanmayasvātmanivāsalabdhasampadbharābhyarcitayuṣmadaṅghriḥ na bhojanācchādanam apy ajasram apekṣate yas tam ahaṃ nato 'smi.* Needing neither food nor covering, I bow — who have gained dwelling in the self made of You.
**18.5** — *sadā bhavaddehanivāsasvastho 'py antaḥ paraṃ dahyata eṣa lokaḥ tavecchayā tat kuru me yathātra tvadarcanānandamayo bhaveyam.* Ever burning in thinking of You, this world sits in Your body-dwelling — make me, by Your will, worship-joy here.
**18.6** — *svarasoditayuṣmadaṅghripadmadvayapūjāmṛtapānasaktacittaḥ sakārthacayeṣv ahaṃ bhaveyam sukhasaṃsparśanamātralokayātraḥ.* Spontaneously risen, touching Your feet, may my mind dwell — world's business as mere touch-tour.
**18.7** — *sakalavyavahāragocare sphuṭam antaḥ spurati tvayi prabho upayānty apayānti cāniśam mama vastūni vibhāntu sarvadā.* Things coming and going, may they shine always — says the pleased one? (Verify pāda division in L2; do not force.)
**18.8** — *satatam eva tavaiva pure 'thavāpy arahito vicareyam ahaṃ tvayā kṣaṇalavo 'py atha mā sma bhavet sa me na vijaye nanu yatra bhavanmayaḥ.* Ever in His city, or parted and roaming — may no moment defeat me, where I am Yours.
**18.9** — *bhavadaṅgaparisravatsuśītāmṛtapūrair bharite samantato 'pi bhavadarcanasampadeha bhaktās tava saṃsārasaro 'ntare caranti.* Cool nectar-floods of Your limb-touch filling all round — in worship-wealth here, devotees move in saṃsāra's middle.
**18.10** — *mahāmantratarucchāyāśītale tvanmahāvane nijātmani sadā nātha vaseyaṃ tava pūjakaḥ.* In the cool shade of the great-mantra tree, in Your great forest, in my own self always, Lord, may I dwell as Your worshipper. Tree-shade, forest, self: three dwellings nested.
**18.11** — *prativastu samastajīvataḥ pratibhāsi pratibhāmayo yathā mama nātha tathā puraḥ prathāṃ vraja netratrayaśūlaśobhitaḥ.* Appear before me, three-eyed, trident-graced — may I see, may I praise.
**18.12** — *abhimānacarūpahārato mamatābhaktibhareṇa kalpitāt paritoṣagataḥ kadā bhavān mama sarvatra bhaved dṛśaḥ padam.* Pride offered, devotion weighed — be my eyes' ground.
**18.13** — *nivasanparamāmṛtābdhimadhye bhavadarcāvidhimātramagnacittaḥ sakalaṃ janavṛttam ācareyaṃ rasayan sarvata eva kiñcanāpi.* Dwelling in the nectar-ocean middle, tasting everything everywhere. (Lakshmanjoo's ocean-middle verse.)
**18.14** — *bhavadīyam ihāstu vastu tattvaṃ vivarītuṃ ka ivātra pātram arthe idam eva hi nāmarūpaceṣṭādyasamaṃ te harate haro 'si yasmāt.* Your thing, Your truth — who can disclose? Rhetorical modesty as method.
**18.15** — *śāntaye na sukhalipsutā manāg bhaktisambhṛtamadeṣu taiḥ prabhoḥ mokṣamārgaṇaphalāpi nārthanā smaryate hṛdayahāriṇaḥ puraḥ.* No desire for peace, no begging remembered before the heart-thief.
**18.16** — *jāgaretaradaśāthavā parā yāpi kācana manāg avasthiteḥ bhaktibhājanajanasya sākhilā tvatsanāthamanaso mahotsavaḥ.* Waking or beyond, the devotee's festival in the ever-present mind — no, not the yogin's trance.
**18.17** — *āmano 'kṣavalayasya vṛttayaḥ sarvataḥ śithilavṛttayo 'pi tāḥ tvām avāpya dṛḍhadīrghasaṃvido nātha bhaktidhanasoṣmaṇāṃ katham.* Mind's eye-wheel loosened yet unswerving — how could it not be Yours?
**18.18** — *na ca vibhinnam asṛjyata kiñcid asty atha sukhetarad atra na nirmitam atha ca duḥkhi ca bhedi ca sarvathāpy asamavismayadhāma namo 'stu te.* Nothing unmade, no unpleasure made — yet all suffers and divides: bow to the wonder-abode.
**18.19** — *kharaniṣedhakhadāmṛtapūraṇocchalitadhautavikalpamalasya me dalitadurjayasaṃśayavairiṇas tvadavalokanam astu nirantaram.* My doubt-foe crushed, Your sight unbroken — may I stand facing You.
**18.20** — *sphuṭam aviśa mām athāviśeyaṃ satataṃ nātha bhavantamasmi yasmāt rabhasena vapus tavaiva sākṣāt paramāsattigataḥ samarcayeyam.* Enter me plainly, for I am Yours — worship face to face with violent speed. (Lakshmanjoo: "get entry in my body... then I will enter in You.")
**18.21** — *tvayi na stutiśaktir asti kasyāpy athavāsty eva yato 'tisundaro 'si satataṃ punar arthitaṃ mamaitad yad aviśrānti vilokayeyam īśam.* No one's is praise-power, yet it exists since You are beautiful — may I see unwearied. Inability confessed and overridden by beauty.

# Śivastotrāvalī s19 — ARGUMENT MAP (Stotra 19, udyota, 17 verses)

*2026-11-01. Source: GRETIL UtSst_19.1–19.17 (lines 1279–1331). Title: dawning/rise. Meter: VERIFY. Lore: Bailly 1987 ch.19 ("The Meaning Revealed"); Lakshmanjoo audio + translations.json 19_1–19_17; Kṣemarāja Vivṛti (consult before L2).*

---

## 1. What is at issue

What happens when the wish-tree itself is Śiva? Answer: prayer stops begging for things and starts begging for Him — face, form, flood, mind, eyes — until the beggar owns father, mother, and no-second, and roams fulfilled.

## 2. The arc

Wish-tree Śiva wins, strange in story (1) → all from self, yet self unjoined to the highest (2) → self is knowledge-action-formed Lord; how could body differ (3) → melt into His sight-natured fruit (4) → when will foot-thought-jewel amaze the perfected (5) → when shall I see the nectar-flooding face (6) → when will the meditated form flood non-duality's far gaps (7) → restless, when soon (8) → give me nothing but clashes (9) → may pure rays face me, melting body-mind dark (10) → tame my truth-stealing guna-thieves (11) → fill my mind fast with devotion-nectar (12) → in liberation-state, where is devotion — set me fittingly, Unborn (13) → don't settle me with a siddhi-drop (14) → Master, be pleased — I ask only this (15) → may Your feet bloom in my mind (16) → I have a Lord, a father, a mother — no second: I roam fulfilled (17).

## 3. The argument, verse by verse (terse)

**19.1** — *prārthanābhūmikātītavicitraphaladāyakaḥ.* Beyond prayer-grounds, strange-fruit-giver — victorious, unprecedented in story, Śiva the wish-tree. Prayer outgrown at the start. (Lakshmanjoo: "pārijāta tree.")
**19.2** — *sarvavastuna yatva...* Since all is got through the self from the One container — yet my self does not join that highest You. All mine, unjoined: the paradox of nearness.
**19.3** — *jñānakarmamayātmā...* The self, knowledge-action-formed, consciousness-bodied, is in every way the Lord alone — but would there even be a body among things, or what else? Identity with a question hanging.
**19.4** — *viṣamārtimuṣānena phalena.* By unequalled fruit, stealing pain — melt into His sight-natured form; may my going be His-formed. Pain stolen by fruit; going made His.
**19.5** — *bhavadbhakticintā...* When will foot-thought-jewel-creeper-fruition, amazing the perfected, befall me? Amazement as devotion's social proof.
**19.6** — *karhi nātha vimalaṃ mukhabimbaṃ.* When shall I see Your pure face-orb, showering unprecedented nectar that drowns the world? Face asked, flood promised.
**19.7** — *dhyātamātramuditaṃ tava rūpaṃ.* May Your form, risen at mere meditation, with supreme-nectar floods ever fill non-duality's far gaps. Meditation-instant form; floods for gaps.
**19.8** — *tvadīyānuttararasāsaṅgasaṃtyaktacāpalam.* Restless by Your peerless-rasa-touch — not even now: when, Lord, and soon? Restlessness sanctified as touch-effect.
**19.9** — *mā śuṣkakaṭukāny eva paraṃ sarvāṇi sarvadā.* Not dry-bitter things only — bring on all, always, gained by offering to You; let even opposites befall me. Totality demanded, not severity.
**19.10** — *nātha sāṃmukhyam āyāntu viśuddhās tava raśmayaḥ.* Lord, may Your pure rays come face to face — melting body-mind dark. Rays as melters.
**19.11** — *deva prasīda yāvan me tvanmārgaparipanthikāḥ.* Master, favor till my truth-stealing guna-thieves, Your path's adversaries, are tamed. Taming, not killing, the thieves.
**19.12** — *tvad bhaktisudhāsārair.* Fill my mind fast with Your devotion-nectar-essence, Lord — drown all sapless habits. Drowning as cleansing.
**19.13** — *mokṣadaśāyāṃ bhaktis tvayi.* In liberation-state, where is devotion to You — how for a mortal? So set me fittingly, Unborn, on the attainment-ground. Liberation-state questioned; fitting placement begged. (Lakshmanjoo's naimittika saptamī note; "the means is Thy devotion.")
**19.14** — *siddhilavalābhalubdhaṃ.* Don't settle proud me with a siddhi-drop — thin is liberation beside Your mouth's sprouting powers. Siddhis declined as thin; mouth-powers preferred.
**19.15** — *dāsasya me prasīdatu.* May the Lord be pleased with me, His slave — I ask only this, since the three-worlds-giver exceeds my sight. The minimal ask.
**19.16** — *tvadvapuḥsmṛtisudhārasapūrṇe.* In mind full of Your body-mindfulness-nectar-rasa, may Your lotus-feet bloom, honey-trickling, supramundane. Feet blooming in mind-field.
**19.17** — *asti me prabhur asau.* I have a Lord, a father — Tryambaka — and a mother, Bhavānī; no second exists for me here — thus bliss-fulfilled I roam. Close: family instead of metaphysics; roaming instead of arriving. (Lakshmanjoo: "my master and father... my last desire.")

## 4. Unresolved / uncertain (not invented)

1. 19.9's totality-demand (not dry-bitter only, but all): askesis or rhetoric? Open.
2. 19.13's naimittika saptamī (Lakshmanjoo's grammatical note): record; verify against Kṣemarāja before freezing.
3. 19.3's flat identity vs 19.2's unjoining: held together, not harmonized.
4. Meter unverified; Chowkhamba collation pending.

# C1 NOTE — s04

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

It is direct address under unfitness: the fickle mind praised for turning (1), the lowest ground kept after arrival (2), reveal-and-hide demanded with use-me-now (3), a spell of names (4); then the world re-seen — Brahmā laughed at (5), nothing apart from His body (6), two tastes of one dust (7), flash-devotion (8), discernment demanding plain manifestation (9), placed-unasked complaint (10); then love's evidence — blooming gods (11), separation-talk fruiting (12), the sideways-entering word (13), winning faces (14); then the impasse and its passage — finished world, unknocked door (15), just-a-little asked (16), knowing self-accusation (17), tasting-frequency fitness (18), others blind but MY cry heard (19); then exchange and arrival — pleasures recalled to be exchanged (20), hundreds of omens (21), the shining night (22), empire tasteless (23), the knot He tied (24), and the final inversion: the worshipped stays the worshipper, shining in seers' bodies (25).

## FUNCTION

Bargaining-and-confession register: where Stotra 3 loved and Stotra 2 saluted, this one negotiates — demanding, complaining, and crying out — proving that unfitness is no bar to address.

## KEY TERMS

* **pralapa deferred** — babble belongs to Stotra 15; here the speech is demand (*dehi, kuru, prakaṭaya*), not babble. * **adhikāra-stain** — office stains (v.5: Brahmā); throne-sitting frees. Authority inverted. * **anugraha-as-placement** — grace as being-set-down (vv.10, 21), not as gift alone. * **manāg iva** — just a little (v.16): the minimal ask; measure of intimacy.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Fourth song: the lover's quarrel after the litany — address turning from He (Stotra 2's third person) to sustained You for 25 verses. Read as the prayer-book entry.

## EXPLANATION

It's engine is shameless directness. Verses 1–4 open all registers at once: the mind enlisted (1), arrival confessed compatible with lowness (2), the double imperative (3), the name-spell (4). Verses 5–10 rebuild the world: Brahmā laughed at (5), immanence proved with transcendence kept (6), taster-relative duality (7), conditional worship (8), discernment's demand (9), the placed-unasked complaint (10). Verses 11–14 give love's evidences: unasked blooming (11), separation fruiting in union (12), the penetrating word (13), winning faces (14). Verses 15–19 hold the impasse without flinching: finished yet unarrived (15), the minimal ask (16), knowing self-accusation (17), fitness-as-frequency (18), the exception claimed — others blind, MY cry heard (19). Verses 20–25 exchange and arrive: pleasures for vision (20), omens for contentment (21), sunset for night-shine (22), empire for foot-touch (23), the knot held not solved (24), and the worshipper-worshipped inversion that ends all bargaining by dissolving the parties (25).

## BOUNDARY

WHY the placed behave like men (v.10) and WHO keeps the door unknocked (v.15) are held open as lived facts. The title-word *surasodbalā* is not parsed here (lexical uncertainty, recorded in L200-track work). Verse 24's bond is His fashioning AND my ignoring; commentary must not assign it to one side.

## RELATED

* Stotra 1, v.24 (unstained in mud) — the same fall-without-stain, in bargaining register. * Stotra 3, v.11 (roar and dance) — arrival proved; here arrival is still begged. * Stotra 15, v.3 (laughing/crying aloud) — the prattle its demands will become.

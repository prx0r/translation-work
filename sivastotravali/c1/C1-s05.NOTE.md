# C1 NOTE — s05

Moved from C1 (pristine rasa-evocative translation only):

## SUMMARY

It is a practice-manual in 26 assignments: bring-me-by-the-neck (1), when-shall-I-dance (2), silence over wisdom (3), exclamation as liturgy (4), half-closed eyes (5), dwelling defined by practice (6), the divine household (7), heart-lotus blooming (8), wax-melting (9), appear regardless (10), streaming both ways (11), pleasure beyond pleasures (12), cooking passions into shared nectar (13), one glance at servants (14), self-address as Śiva (15), gaining without leaving (16), desire's end (17), the festival as destination (18), perfume against stink (19), instruct the contradicting heart (20), passing through yogic states (21), devotion as vanguard (22), the Name's infinity with unrepeatable taste (23), roaming not arriving (24), self-sufficiency except foot-dust (25), the flash where the world melts (26).

## FUNCTION

Vanguard-song: it stations devotion ahead of every faculty (v.22 names the method), so the devotee never faces anything unaccompanied.

## KEY TERMS

* **puraḥsara** — vanguard (v.22): devotion going before speech, mind, body; it's one word. * **āsvāda** — taste (vv.9, 12, 23): knowing by tasting, not concluding; chewed, drunk, unrepeatable. * **pralapa** — prattle (v.6): holy talk as practice-substance; Stotra 15's theme announced.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Fifth song: the manual after Stotra 4's bargaining. Read as the answer to how then to live — by posting devotion everywhere.

## EXPLANATION

It distributes devotion across 26 posts and each post does distinct work: compulsion (1), dance-deadline (2), anti-eloquence (3), exclamation (4), narrowed sight (5), place defined by practice (6), household address (7), blooming (8), melting (9), trigger-indifference (10), streaming (11), surpassing pleasure (12), food-transmutation (13), servant-glance (14), self-address (15), unparting gain (16), desire's end (17), destination (18), smell-war (19), heart-instruction (20), state-passing (21), vanguard (22), Name-infinity (23), roaming (24), exception-clause (25), flash (26). Verses 3 and 25 frame the whole: dumbness preferred, self-sufficiency confessed — except foot-dust. Nothing here is metaphor for something else; the assignments ARE the practice.

## BOUNDARY

The 26 assignments are unranked; do not build a ladder from them. Verse 15's self-address and verse 24's ramparts stand without theory.

## RELATED

* Stotra 1, vv.2–3 (dust-greyed youth; world moved in differently) — the world kept, here practiced. * Stotra 4, v.22 (Name's infinity) — the Name-chanting verse 23 belongs with it. * Stotra 15, v.5 (drawing the outer inside) — the interiorization it distributes.

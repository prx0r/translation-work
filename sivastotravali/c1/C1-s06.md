# C1 — Śivastotrāvalī Stotra 6 (adhvavisphuraṇa)

*Passage commentary for L2-s06 (11 verses). Local to this song; no modern comparison, no PARALLELS inside. Machine-proposed, needs human review.*

## SUMMARY

Separation itself becomes the method: burning to be seen (1), unparted-in-Him while parted-from-world (2), all goings as Him (3), speech matching His bliss (4), roaming desireless in His possession (5), contentment ending toil (6), things melting skyward (7), the jewel blazing from His middle (8), arrival everywhere by effortlessness (9), embrace by mere will (10) — then the single imperative: appear, with all other prayers renounced (11).

## FUNCTION

Separation-song: it answers the devotee who cannot find — with what to do while not finding: burn, and call the burning seeing's ground.

## KEY TERMS

* **viyoga** — parting (vv.1–2): essence of saṃsāra AND essence of the practice; held both ways at once.
* **āveśa** — possession (v.5): seeing by being-held; optics, not emotion.
* **anāyāsa** — effortlessness (vv.9–10): arrival without means, distinct from laziness by the burning of v.1.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Sixth song, shortest arc after Stotra 7's nine verses: eleven breaths. Read after Stotra 5's distribution — here everything narrows to one address.

## EXPLANATION

It's tension is deliberate: maximum burning (v.1, dense, always) with minimum method (v.9, effortlessness; v.10, mere will). Verses 1–2 convert absence into qualification; verses 3–6 spend it as seeing (all goings His, speech His-like, roaming in His possession, contentment stopping toil); verses 7–8 show the world melting and the middle-jewel blazing; verse 9 states the paradox flatly (tired, by effortlessness, everywhere); verse 10 spends it (embrace by will — what not won?); verse 11 converts the whole song into one imperative and renounces the rest.

## BOUNDARY / OPEN

It does not explain HOW burning qualifies (v.1) or WHERE the middle-jewel is (v.8). Verse 9's effortlessness is not anti-effort doctrine — read with v.1's burning, not against it.

## RELATED

* Stotra 2, v.24 (embracers over knowers) — the same seeing-by-holding, in litany form.
* Stotra 13, v.6 (meditation-vision-touch) — the sequence this song compresses into burning-then-appearing.

# C1 — Śivastotrāvalī

## sivst-p01–p02 — bhakti and recognition
**SUMMARY.** Śivastotrāvalī opens with bhakti as supreme. Stotra 1 (bhaktivilāsa): not for one who meditates without rule; we bow to Śiva's light, the bhakti-possessor. My self, drinking bhakti-nectar; like grey with dust of world-journey. Bhakti-possessors move in world by expansion; no mantra fails for them; they are intoxicated with bhakti-nectar; even non-dual, always your second; they know your truth, ocean of endless bliss; bhakti is self-established; Śiva easily seen by bhaktas; bhakti is inseparable from Śiva; consciousness-path threefold; knowledge-state is like vinegar-sourness compared to bhakti-nectar. Stotra 2–4 continue in devotional register. Stotra 5–8 continue (recognition doctrine in devotional register).

**FUNCTION.** Establishes bhakti as supreme (stotra 1) and continues in devotional register (stotra 2–8).

**KEY TERMS.** bhakti (devotion), bhaktiśālin (bhakti-possessor), śivabhāsa (Śiva's light), pratyabhijñā (recognition), svātantrya (autonomy), ānanda (bliss), māyā (Māyā).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First two passages; sets the devotional frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Bhakti (stotra 1): not for one who meditates without rule; bow to Śiva's light, the bhakti-possessor; self drinking bhakti-nectar; bhakti-possessors move by expansion; no mantra fails; intoxicated with bhakti-nectar; even non-dual, always your second; know your truth, ocean of endless bliss; bhakti self-established; Śiva easily seen by bhaktas; bhakti inseparable from Śiva; consciousness-path threefold; knowledge-state like vinegar-sourness. (2) Recognition (stotra 2–8): continued in devotional register.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Stotras are devotional; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.1–8.

## sivst-p03–p04 — autonomy and self
**SUMMARY.** Stotra 9–12 (svātantryavijaya etc.) establish autonomy (svātantrya) in devotional register. Stotra 13–16 (saṃgrahastotra etc.) establish self in devotional register. The devotional voice continues: Śiva as supreme, bhakti as the means, recognition as the fruit.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes autonomy and self in devotional register.

**KEY TERMS.** svātantrya (autonomy), pratyabhijñā (recognition), ātman (self), śiva (Śiva).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p01–p02 (bhakti/recognition); p03–p04 establish autonomy/self.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Autonomy (stotra 9–12): svātantrya in devotional register. (2) Self (stotra 13–16): ātman in devotional register.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Stotras are devotional; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS1.9–16.

## sivst-p05 — close
**SUMMARY.** Stotra 17–20 (divyakrīḍābahumāna etc.) close the stotra-valī in devotional register. The collection ends with devotion to Śiva, recognition of the self, and the play of divine activity.

**FUNCTION.** Closes the stotra-valī in devotional register.

**KEY TERMS.** divyakrīḍā (divine play), udyotana (illumination), carvaṇa (chewing/tasting).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Closes the text; follows p03–p04 (autonomy/self).

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in one step. Close (stotra 17–20): divine play, illumination, tasting; devotion to Śiva, recognition of self, play of divine activity.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Stotras are devotional; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS1.17–20.

# C1 — Śivastotrāvalī (final condensation of the arguments)

## sivst-p01 (stotras 1–4)

Bhakti is supreme and self-established: not for one who meditates without rule, we bow to Śiva's light, the bhakti-possessor. Bhakti-possessors move in the world by expansion; no mantra fails for them; they are intoxicated with bhakti-nectar. Even non-dual, they are always Śiva's second. They know Śiva's truth, the ocean of endless bliss. Bhakti is self-established; Śiva is easily seen by bhaktas; bhakti is inseparable from Śiva. The consciousness-path is threefold (joy/suffering/delusion); the knowledge-state is like vinegar-sourness compared to bhakti-nectar. Recognition (pratyabhijñā) operates in devotional mode: the devotee recognizes all as Śiva — Śiva as all, the world as his body — not through argument but through love.

## sivst-p02 (stotras 5–8)

Svātantrya in devotional mode: the devotee surrenders autonomy to Śiva. Āveśa (possession by the deity) dissolves the worshipper/worshipped distinction. The key experience: dwelling within you, may I be silent; otherwise I am wise (and that is the problem). The devotee's wisdom is an obstacle; silence (āveśa) is the goal. The devotional voice cajoles, longs, surrenders: Lord, bring me into your abode by the garland of your feet. In the essence of separation (saṃsāra), by you, the dear Lord, may I always be unseparated, even separated from the world. Wherever I go with body, speech, mind — all that is you. Reveal yourself; let not other prayers torment.

## sivst-p03 (stotras 9–12)

Autonomy in devotional mode: the devotee longs for the nine rasas soaked in devotion; the mind turns from other things to touch Śiva. When, Lord, shall I have the great festival of bliss-nectar of devotion? When, suddenly recognizing, shall I put to shame the Lord's people? When shall I attain that beloved-ness of yours, by which it would not be fitting for you to flee from me anywhere? The supreme ground of knowledge, the supreme state of yoga — when shall devotion to you be full? When, Lord, shall I be, as you are, with no cave of Māyā's shadow in me? The arcane lore (rahasya): your way is to be adopted — this is the essence. The devotee's wisdom is an obstacle; silence (āveśa) is the goal. Outside you, mere non-being — how, Lord, could it be or be worshipped?

## sivst-p04 (stotras 13–16)

Self in devotional mode: the devotee recognizes the self as Śiva, not as a separate soul. Happiness is union with you; suffering is separation from you. Remove the non-manifestation, Lord, and reveal your own pure self. I am the Lord; I am the form; I am the learned one; I am the fortunate one; who else? The world is like me — the pride of the devoted one is glorious. By the knowledge of established things, make me fit for worshipping your feet. Meditation, then seeing, then touching — the Lord reveals himself. Devotees, distinct in praise and worship, are not dispassionate nor Śiva nor desiring liberation — they worship you. May I be intoxicated with the wine of high devotion. Even the blind and leprous shine, ornamented with the supreme devotion to you. Nothing at all is an obstacle for the devotees regarding your covering. For devotees, the mind is stainless, free from honor and dishonor, passion and aversion. Liberation is named for the ripened devotion to you, Lord. Devotion, growing, becomes the eternal one of Śambhu.

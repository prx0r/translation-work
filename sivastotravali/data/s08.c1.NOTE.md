# C1 NOTE — s08.c1

Register: GONE (Swamiji: composed "gone"/ecstatic, not in senses — read as effusion; do not flatten into doctrine).

Scholar commentary moved from C1 (C1 holds essence + SUMMARY + rasa only):

## SUMMARY

Grace-drop above and devotion rising below: it watches their meeting produce unworldly upsurge — chewing lordship (2), thirsting with unconstructed sight (3), demanding plainness in both registers (4), seating the faculties (5), exceeding all dealings in a possession-body (6), blooming and joining into untellability (7), flooding the dread gorge (8); then the wager — union-drops against world-pleasures, with double-fall risk named (9); hamsa-and-stalk botany joining below, inside, above (10); all things under thought's scope without seizure (11); practice for catastrophe — limb joined, joy-mad (12); and the closing epistemology: woven in Him, shown by Him, certain — shine (13).

## FUNCTION

Upsurge phenomenology: the collection's only sustained account of what the grace-devotion meeting feels like from inside — water, botany, and vertigo instead of argument.

## KEY TERMS

* **alaukikodbala** — unworldly upsurge: the title's claim that this exceeds worldly measure.
* **carvaṇa** — chewing (v.2; cf. Stotra 20's title): rumination as method.
* **vyavahāra** — transaction/dealing (v.6): what the possession-body exceeds; the world left, not refuted.
* **ubhayacyuta** — fallen from both (v.9): the named risk that keeps it honest.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Eighth song: the hinge of the first half. After mastery (Stotra 5), burning (Stotra 6), and curing (Stotra 7), here is the event they prepare — read slowly; it is the experiential center the rest of the collection assumes.

## EXPLANATION

Verses 1–2 pose the meeting and its first fruit: joy-juice displacing metaphysics. Verses 3–4 double the demand: worldly thirst kept, unconstructed sight required (3); plainness in difference-places AND consciousness-paths (4). Verses 5–7 give the mechanics: faculties self-seating with one fear named — rupture (5); possession-body exceeding transaction (6); blooming, joining, untellability (7). Verse 8 floods the abyss; verse 9 states the wager with its risk. Verses 10–11 give botany then totality: stalk joining below-inside-above (10); all things under scope without seizure (11). Verse 12 is catastrophe-practice; verse 13 closes with the tightest epistemology in the set: woven-in-Him, shown-by-Him, firm — shine.

## BOUNDARY

Verses 9 (wager), 10 (stalk botany), and the (?) in GRETIL 12.3-pattern line here — 8.9's double-fall risk is stated, not resolved. The hamsa botany is image, not anatomy — do not map onto cakra systems.

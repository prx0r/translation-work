# C1 NOTE — s11.c1

Register: GONE (Swamiji: composed "gone"/ecstatic, not in senses — read as effusion; do not flatten into doctrine).

Scholar commentary moved from C1 (C1 holds essence + SUMMARY + rasa only):

## SUMMARY

Dispossession (nothing mine, 1) → begging that begs (2) → straw thrones, sterile merit (3) → unblocked praise (4) → even in dream (5) → fragrance-dot transfiguring (6) → one work only (7) → resume of disqualifications (8) → trifles to pearls (9) → even cheating devotion works (10) → litany as battering-ram (11) → cornered metaphysics, clinging while harassed (12) → approved suffering, chosen longevity (13) → skilled destroyer, act now (14) → bow to the sole work and over-bright light (15).

## FUNCTION

Begging-song: after Stotra 10's sorting, this one begs — proving that dispossession plus longing plus trust is a complete path needing nothing else.

## KEY TERMS

* **autsukya** — longing-eagerness: it's engine; distinct from need, which wants filling, from trust, which rests.
* **viśvasita** — trust/confidence (title): longing's steadiness; what keeps begging from despair.
* **yācñā** — begging (v.2): honored as method; it dignifies what devotion manuals usually pass over.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Eleventh song: the *kadā*-free stretch after Stotra 9–10. Where Stotra 9 asked when, this one begs now — read the pair together.

## EXPLANATION

It strips everything (v.1: world, friends, kin — Lakshmanjoo's telling names them: universe, baby, Viresh, John) and then shows what begging looks like with nothing left: begging that begs and knows it (2), merit declared sterile (3), praise declared unstoppable (4), the asked state extended into dreaming (5), fragrance-dot transfiguring all enjoyment (6), integrity demanded and grace/check alike accepted as the one work (7), disqualifications filed as qualifications (8), trifles turned to pearls by nearness (9), even cheating devotion working (10), litany as battering-ram (11), metaphysics cornered into complaint — and kept complaining (12), suffering approved and longevity chosen as tasting-time (13), professional skill invoked (14), and the bow to the one-work light (15).

## BOUNDARY

Verse 12's cornered metaphysics is complaint, not theodicy — do not systematize it. Verse 10's cheating-sufficiency and verse 9's pearl-mechanism are images, not casuistry. The personal names in Lakshmanjoo's telling belong to the telling, not it.

## RELATED

* Stotra 9 (asking-song): longing again, now with trust added.
* Stotra 4, v.21 (hundreds of omens): contentment after placing; here contentment before it.
* Stotra 13, v.11 (grace without deliberation): the grace this begging counts on.

# C1 NOTE — s16.c1

Register: GONE (Swamiji: composed "gone"/ecstatic, not in senses — read as effusion; do not flatten into doctrine).

Scholar commentary moved from C1 (C1 holds essence + SUMMARY + rasa only):

## SUMMARY

No block for folk or devotees (1); the self seen at once despite ladders (2); laughing victors and vanquished (3); no dryness, no release-seeking (4); as unknown before, may it grow (5); the single prayer (6); drunk, angry, leaping, shouting Śiva (7); crisis or peace, tears or laughter — by devotion (8); nothing to be known, inside or out (9); rejoicing even at abusers (10); pain ripening to enjoyment (11); held everywhere, same touch-joy (12); duality's double-refuge dissolved (13); pilgrim-eyes err, Being shines (14); ripened mind unmannered (15); knowledge-rich put to shame (16); devotion-nectar-drink-and-bath as rite (17); world-life praiseworthy (18); liberation renamed as ripe devotion (19); pain invited, pleasure refused (20); mutual dependence blessed, not solved (21); form or formless, always nectar (22); another world-stand in this world (23); devotion, devotion, something (24); I weep, let fierce devotion be mine (25); jewel or straw, stuck to You priceless (26); unparted, they win (27); embraced in saṃsāra's hall (28); hugging Śiva with the feasting Goddess (29); sovereignty with no subjects, otherness by which the world shines as it does not (30).

## FUNCTION

Anti-blockage catalogue: the longest list in the set, proving that nothing — no people, no gods, no method-ladders, no impurity, no sorrow, no logic — stands between the devotee and Him.

## KEY TERMS

* **pāśānudbheda** — bursting of the noose: the title's claim; the bond bursts from inside, not cut from outside.
* **anyonyāśraya** — mutual dependence (v.21): blessed, not solved; the collection's frankest circle.
* **kim api** — something (v.24): the devotee's apophatic reserve; meaning carried, not stated.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Sixteenth song: the hinge after Utpala's own three (13–15). Read as the answer to what could still block — nothing.

## EXPLANATION

It works by exhaustive negation: no blockers (1); immediacy despite method (2); laughter on both outcomes (3); dryness and release-seeking both refused (4); ignorance as proof of grace (5); the single prayer (6); the full behavioral catalogue licensed in one verse (7); every state accepted as medium (8); knowing itself canceled as requirement (9); even abusers become nectar-occasions (10); pain becomes enjoyment-material (11); everywhere held, same joy (12); duality's double-refuge dissolved, not disciplined (13); pilgrim eyes err, Being shines (14); ripeness unmanners (15); knowledge-wealth shamed (16); bath-and-drink suffice as rite (17); world-life justified by its object (18); liberation renamed as ripe devotion, first stage as near-free (19); pain invited, pleasure-lines refused (20); the circle blessed (21); form-indifference sealed (22); another world-stand inside this one (23); cataloguing giving up into something (24); shouting replaces asking (25); adhesion alchemizes everything (26); non-parting wins (27); embrace and flight in one hall (28); the feasting Goddess hugged back (29); sovereignty needing no subjects, otherness by which the world both shines and doesn't (30).

## BOUNDARY

Verse 21's circle is blessed, not solved — do not systematize it. The 16.1 variant (Nareśvaraparīkṣā) is recorded, not adjudicated. Verse 24's *kim api* and verse 30's shining/not-shining are kept open by it.

## RELATED

* Stotra 15, v.6 (*kim api*) and v.15 (the worry): the same modesty and the same circle, in devotional register.
* Stotra 13, v.11 (grace without deliberation): the blocklessness it universalizes.
* Stotra 1, v.16 (the deceit): sometime-somewhere met by no-block here.

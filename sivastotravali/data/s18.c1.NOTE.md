# C1 NOTE — s18.c1

Register: GONE (Swamiji: composed "gone"/ecstatic, not in senses — read as effusion; do not flatten into doctrine).

Scholar commentary moved from C1 (C1 holds essence + SUMMARY + rasa only):

## SUMMARY

It watches the hidden become manifest without ceasing to be hidden: found inside the world, never far (1); He here, She everywhere, no division at the top (2); the ungrateful blind, the risen mind unseeing — ah, destroyed (3); needing nothing, I bow (4); burning world, make me worship-joy (5); spontaneity, touch, world-tour (6); things coming and going, may they shine (7); ever in His city, never defeated (8); cool nectar-floods of Your limb-touch filling all round (9); tree-shade, forest, self: three dwellings (10); appear before me, three-eyed, trident-graced (11); pride offered, devotion weighed (12); tasting everything everywhere (13); Your thing, Your truth — who can disclose (14); no desire for peace, no begging remembered (15); waking or beyond, festival in the ever-present mind (16); loosened yet unswerving (17); nothing unmade, yet all suffers — bow to the wonder (18); doubt-foe crushed, sight unbroken (19); enter me plainly, worship at speed (20); no one's is praise-power, yet it exists since You are beautiful — may I see unwearied (21).

## FUNCTION

Manifestation-song: it answers how the hidden shows itself — by beholding. Every verse is an act of looking that finds, not a thesis about finding.

## KEY TERMS

* **āviṣkāra** — manifestation: the title's claim; recognition, not arrival.
* **darśanapatha** — vision's path, carried over from Stotra 12: walked here to the end.
* **pradhānā** — the supreme feminine (v.2): it's cosmogonic image; reported, not systematized.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Eighteenth song: the hinge after the festival's excess (Stotra 17). Beholding replaces shouting; read slowly — it is the collection's quiet center before dawning (19) and chewing (20).

## EXPLANATION

Verses 1–2 establish the field: found inside, never far; He here, She everywhere, no division at the top. Verses 3–4 confess the two failures (blindness despite effort; need despite gain) and bow anyway. Verses 5–7 demand and describe: make me worship-joy (5), spontaneity plus touch-tour (6), things shining as He throbs (7). Verses 8–10 dwell: ever in His city (8), floods in saṃsāra's middle (9), tree-shade, forest, self (10). Verses 11–14 ask and answer: appear (11), be my eyes' ground (12), taste everything (13), who can disclose (14 — left open). Verses 15–17 quiet down: no desire, no begging remembered (15), festival in ever-presence (16), loosened yet unswerving (17). Verse 18 states the metaphysics whole — nothing unmade, yet all suffers — and bows to the wonder instead of solving it. Verse 19 crushes doubt and claims unbroken sight; verse 20 demands entry at speed; verse 21 closes with inability confessed and overridden by beauty.

## BOUNDARY

Pāda divisions in the long-meter verses should be collated before L2 is frozen. Verse 2's pregnancy image and verse 18's ledger are reported, not theorized. Verse 14's disclosure question is left open.

## RELATED

* Stotra 12, v.3 (how could things reach His path) — the question it lives as answer.
* Stotra 6, v.5 (seeing as His): beholding carried over.
* Stotra 19 (dawning): manifestation's sequel — what the beheld does in the beholder.

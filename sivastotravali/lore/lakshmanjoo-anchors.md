# LAKSHMANJOO ANCHORS — Academy excerpts mapped to songs (2026-11-02)

*Source: Lakshmanjoo Academy "Hymns to Shiva" (Hymns to Shiva, Utpaladeva's
Śivastotrāvalī, revealed by Swami Lakshmanjoo; © John Hughes). Verse-specific
witness notes only. Each anchor: the excerpt, Swamiji's maxim, what it demands
of our translation.*

## s01-v14 — achieve God in activity, not by shunning

Excerpt "How to find out the way to achieve God in the activity of the universe"
(ch1): *śivo bhūtvā yajeteti bhakto bhūtveti kathyate* — "So, you should find out
the way to achieve God in the activity of the universe, not by shunning it.
This is Shaivism. The real way of perceiving You is to perceive You in each
and every action of life."
Demand: our v14 ("For You alone are the body's essence...") must carry
activity-embrace, not world-denial. Check L2 against shunning-readings.

## s02-v28 — thunderbolt path (already applied)

Excerpt "The True Path" (ch2 v28): path = thunderbolt shattering doubts;
alakṣmī = *daridhratā* (poverty); the path fires misfortunes to ashes like
kālāgnirudra; kalpānta ends joylessness so joy comes. Applied 2026-11-02
(alakṣmī fortune-inversion fixed, witness option minted).

## s06-v10 — by Thy embrace, everything conquered

Excerpt (ch6 v10): *bhavadaṅgapariṣvaṅgasambhogam ... iyati prāpte ... kiṃ nātha
na jitaṃ mayā* — devotee desires to embrace the Master "just by my will,"
because "by Thy embrace I have conquered everything."
Demand: our v10 must keep embrace-as-conquest (not generic union); iyati prāpte
(on this much obtained) is the hinge.

## s12-v25 — don't control the swift mind

Excerpt "How to not be disturbed by your mind" (ch12 v25): *manasi svarasena
yatra tatra pracaraty apy aham asya gocareṣu ...* — "I don't want to control my
mind because the mind is so swift, you cannot [catch it]."
Demand: our v25 ("may I ever be worship-skilled") must not smuggle
mind-control back in; the verse asks skill *within* roaming, not restraint of it.

## s13 v01–v04 — sane summary; āṇavamala; bas (already applied)

Excerpt (ch13 v1–4): saṅgraha = in-brief-words (ch13–14 composed "in his
senses"); kālikā = blackness = āṇavamala remnant (māyīya/kārma gone);
v03 indifference (live/die/hell/heaven/mokṣa — don't care); v04 ego SHINES in
devotees ("let them go to the dogs" for the detached); maxim "he who is
attached to God is beautiful." Applied 2026-11-02 (kālikā split + live fork).

## s16-v08 — I don't mind

Excerpt "How to remain devoted no matter what" (ch16 v8):
*viṣamastho'pi svastho'pi rudannapi hasannapi / gambhīro'pi vicitto'pi
bhaveyaṃ bhaktitaḥ prabho* — "Let me be ... in crisis or peaceful, or miserable
or happy ... by the intensity of Thy devotion ... I don't mind."
Demand: our v08 must keep the *api*-chain insistent and end on indifference,
not resolution. (Our L2 already matches structurally; verify against audio.)

## s17 — the universe as offering

Excerpt (ch17): *pūjopakaraṇībhūtaviśvāveśena* — worship no longer confined to
ritual acts; the whole universe felt as the substance of worship itself.
Demand: s17 argmaps (rewritten 2026-11-02) must read every verse as
universe-become-offering, not rite-description. (Our v35 inference already does.)

## s20-v18 — why Shiva destroys

Excerpt (ch20 v18): *sato vināśasambandhān matparaṃ nikhilaṃ mṛṣā / evamevodyate
nātha tvayā saṃhāralīlayā* — destruction-play; "everything is divine to them";
the yogin dances in the graveyard. Demand: our v18 keeps destruction as play
(līlā), never punishment.

## Maxims (tuning forks for the devotion-voice pass)

1. "Longing carries them, not effort." (3am anecdote via Kṣemarāja/Abhinavagupta)
2. "He who is attached to God is beautiful because there is love for God."
3. "Let me be ... I don't mind." (indifference, not control)
4. "Perceive You in each and every action of life." (activity, not shunning)

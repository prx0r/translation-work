# Śivasūtra — ARGMAP

## sivs-p01 (1.1–1.10)
- speaker: Vasugupta, sūtra style.
- question: What is the self?
- premises: 1.1 caitanyam ātmā (consciousness is self). 1.2 jñānaṃ bandhaḥ (knowledge is bondage). 1.3 yonivargaḥ kalāśarīram (womb-class is kalā-body). 1.4 jñānādhiṣṭhānaṃ mātṛkā (knowledge's support is mātṛkā). 1.5 udyamo bhairavaḥ (arising is Bhairava). 1.6 śakticakrasaṃdhāne viśvasaṃhāraḥ (in śakti-wheel-union, world-dissolution). 1.7 jñānaṃ jāgrat (knowledge is waking). 1.8 svapno vikalpāḥ (dream is thoughts). 1.9 aviveko māyā sauṣuptam (non-discrimination is Māyā, deep sleep). 1.10 tritayabhoktā vīreśaḥ (threefold enjoyer is Vīreśa).
- inference: Self = consciousness; knowledge = bondage; arising = Bhairava; three states (waking/dream/deep sleep) mapped to knowledge/thoughts/Māyā.
- conclusion: Self doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## sivs-p02 (1.11–1.20)
- speaker: Vasugupta.
- question: What are the powers and states?
- premises: 1.11 vismayo yogabhūmikāḥ (wonder is yoga-stages). 1.12 icchā śaktir umā kumārī (will is Śakti, Umā, maiden). 1.13 dṛśyaṃ śarīram (visible is body). 1.14 śuddhatattvasaṃdhānād vāpaśuśaktiḥ (by pure-tattva-union, or paśu-power). 1.15 vitarka ātmajñānam (reasoning is self-knowledge). 1.16 lokānandaḥ samādhisukham (world-bliss is samādhi-joy). 1.17 śaktisaṃdhāne śarīrotpattiḥ (in śakti-union, body-arising). 1.18 bhūtasaṃdhānabhūtapṛthaktvaviśvasaṃghaṭṭāḥ (element-union, element-separation, world-collision). 1.19 śuddhavidyodayāc cakreśatvasiddhiḥ (from pure-knowledge-arising, wheel-lordship attainment). 1.20 mahāhradānusaṃdhānān mantravīryānubhavaḥ (by great-lake-union, mantra-power-experience).
- inference: Powers/states: wonder = yoga-stages; will = Śakti; visible = body; reasoning = self-knowledge; world-bliss = samādhi-joy; śakti-union → body-arising/world-collision; pure-knowledge → wheel-lordship; great-lake-union → mantra-power-experience.
- conclusion: Power/state doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## sivs-p03 (2.1–2.10)
- speaker: Vasugupta.
- question: What is the mantra?
- premises: 2.1 cittaṃ mantraḥ (mind is mantra). 2.2 prayatnaḥ sādhakaḥ (effort is practitioner). 2.3 vidyāśarīrasattā mantrarahasyam (knowledge-body-existence is mantra-secret). 2.4 garbhe cittavikāso 'viśiṣṭavidyāsvapnaḥ (in womb, mind-expansion is undifferentiated-knowledge-dream). 2.5 vidyāsamutthāne svābhāvike khecarī śivāvasthā (in knowledge-arising, natural, sky-wandering, Śiva-state). 2.6 gurur upāyaḥ (Guru is means). 2.7 mātṛkācakrasaṃbodhaḥ (mātṛkā-wheel-awakening). 2.8 śarīraṃ haviḥ (body is offering). 2.9 jñānam annam (knowledge is food). 2.10 vidyāsaṃhāre tadutthasvapnadarśanam (in knowledge-dissolution, that-arising-dream-vision).
- inference: Mind = mantra; effort = practitioner; knowledge-body = mantra-secret; Guru = means; body = offering; knowledge = food.
- conclusion: Mantra doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## sivs-p04 (3.1–3.10)
- speaker: Vasugupta.
- question: What is the ātman?
- premises: 3.1 ātmā cittam (self is mind). 3.2 jñānaṃ bandhaḥ (knowledge is bondage). 3.3 kalādīnāṃ tattvānām aviveko māyā (non-discrimination of kalā etc. tattvas is Māyā). 3.4 śarīre saṃhāraḥ kalānām (in body, dissolution of kalās). 3.5 nāḍīsaṃhārabhūtajayabhūtakaivalyabhūtapṛthaktvāni (channel-dissolution, element-conquest, element-isolation, element-separation). 3.6 mohāvaraṇāt siddhiḥ (from delusion-covering, attainment). 3.7 mohajayād anantābhogāt sahajavidyājayaḥ (from delusion-conquest, endless enjoyment, innate-knowledge-conquest). 3.8 jāgrad dvitīyakaraḥ (waking is second-doer). 3.9 nartaka ātmā (dancer is self). 3.10 raṅgo 'ntarātmā (stage is inner self).
- inference: Self = mind; knowledge = bondage; non-discrimination of tattvas = Māyā; body = dissolution of kalās; channels/elements; delusion-covering → attainment; waking = second-doer; self = dancer; inner self = stage.
- conclusion: Ātman doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## sivs-p05 (3.11–3.20)
- speaker: Vasugupta.
- question: What is the siddhi?
- premises: 3.11 prekṣakāṇīndriyāṇi (spectators are senses). 3.12 dhīvaśāt sattvasiddhiḥ (by intellect's power, sattva-attainment). 3.13 siddhaḥ svatantrabhāvaḥ (siddha is free-state). 3.14 yathā tatra tathānyatra (as there, so elsewhere). 3.15 bījāvadhānam (seed-attention). 3.16 āsanasthaḥ sukhaṃ hrade nimajjati (seated, happily plunges in lake). 3.17 svamātrānirmāṇam āpādayati (brings about own-measure-creation). 3.18 vidyāvināśe janmavināśaḥ (in knowledge-destruction, birth-destruction). 3.19 kavargādiṣu māheśvaryādyāḥ paśumātaraḥ (in ka-group etc., Māheśvarī etc. are paśu-mothers). 3.20 triṣu caturthaṃ tailavad āsecyam (in three, fourth like oil should be poured).
- inference: Siddhi = free-state; seed-attention; seated, plunges in lake; own-measure-creation; knowledge-destruction → birth-destruction; Māheśvarī etc. are paśu-mothers; fourth like oil in three.
- conclusion: Siddhi doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## sivs-p06 (3.21–3.30)
- speaker: Vasugupta.
- question: What is the practice?
- premises: 3.21 magnaḥ svacittena praviśet (immersed, should enter by own mind). 3.22 prāṇasamācāre samadarśanam (in breath-conduct, equal-vision). 3.23 madhye 'varaprasavaḥ (in middle, lower-birth). 3.24 mātrāsv apratyayasaṃdhāne naṣṭasya punar utthānam (in measures, in non-cognition-union, of destroyed, again arising). 3.25 śivatulyo jāyate (becomes Śiva-like). 3.26 śarīravṛttir vratam (body-conduct is vow). 3.27 kathā japaḥ (talking is japa). 3.28 dānam ātmajñānam (giving is self-knowledge). 3.29 yo 'vipastho jñāhetuś ca (who is established, is cause of knowledge). 3.30 svaśaktipracayo viśvam (own-power-accumulation is world).
- inference: Practice: immersed, enter by own mind; breath-conduct = equal-vision; becomes Śiva-like; body-conduct = vow; talking = japa; giving = self-knowledge; own-power-accumulation = world.
- conclusion: Practice doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## sivs-p07 (3.31–3.40)
- speaker: Vasugupta.
- question: What is the state (sthiti)?
- premises: 3.31 sthitilayau (stability and dissolution). 3.32 tatpravṛttāv apy anirāsaḥ saṃvettṛbhāvāt (in its arising, even non-removal, due to knower-ness). 3.33 sukhāsukhayor bahir mananam (of pleasure/pain, external thinking). 3.34 tadvimuktas tu kevalī (freed from that, alone). 3.35 mohapratisaṃhatas tu karmātmā (bound by delusion, action-self). 3.36 bhedatiraskāre sargāntarakarmatvam (in difference-removal, other-creation-action-ness). 3.37 karaṇaśaktiḥ svato 'nubhavāt (instrument-power from own experience). 3.38 tripadādyanuprāṇanam (three-feet etc. enlivening). 3.39 cittasthitivac charīrakaraṇabāhyeṣu (like mind-stability, in body-instrument-externals). 3.40 abhilāpād bahirgatiḥ saṃvāhyasya (from speech, external-going of the carried).
- inference: State: stability/dissolution; non-removal due to knower-ness; freed from pleasure/pain = alone; bound by delusion = action-self; difference-removal → other-creation; instrument-power from own experience.
- conclusion: State doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## sivs-p08 (3.41–3.45)
- speaker: Vasugupta (close).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: 3.41 tadārūḍhapramites tatkṣayāj jīvasaṃkṣayaḥ (of that-ascended-knower, by its destruction, jīva-destruction). 3.42 bhūtakañcukī tadā vimukto bhūyaḥ patisamaḥ paraḥ (element-armored, then freed, again Lord-like, supreme). 3.43 naisargikaḥ prāṇasambandhaḥ (natural breath-connection). 3.44 nāsikāntarmadhyasaṃyamāt kim atra savyāpasavyasauṣumneṣu (by nose-inner-middle-control, what here in left/right/suṣumnā). 3.45 bhūyaḥ syāt pratimīlanam (again there is closing of eyes).
- inference: Close: jīva-destruction of ascended knower; freed from element-armor, Lord-like supreme; natural breath-connection; nose-middle-control; closing of eyes.
- conclusion: The text closes with jīva-destruction and Lord-like state.
- unresolved: none.

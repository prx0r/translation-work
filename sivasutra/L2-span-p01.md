# Sivasutra span-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
I bow to that whose opening and closing make the worlds dissolve and arise — to him, source of the power-wheel's glory, Śaṅkara.
The Sanskrit: *caitanyam ātmā*

## v.2
Where this whole effect abides, and whence it has come forth — from being uncovered in form, there is no obstruction anywhere.
The Sanskrit: *jñānaṃ bandhaḥ*

## v.3
Even in waking and the rest, in difference, it glides in the undifferent; the perceiver-ness never turns back from its own being.
The Sanskrit: *yonivargaḥ kalāśarīram*

## v.4
I happy, I suffering, I attached, and the like — those awarenesses plainly abide elsewhere, strung on pleasure-states.
The Sanskrit: *jñānādhiṣṭhānaṃ mātṛkā*

## v.5
Where there is no suffering, no joy, no graspable, no grasper, and not even delusion — THAT is, ultimately.
The Sanskrit: *udyamo bhairavaḥ*

## v.6
Since the sense-host, deluded, thinks itself undeluded — forthgoing, standing, dissolving, by the inner wheel.
The Sanskrit: *śakticakrasaṃdhāne viśvasaṃhāraḥ*

## v.7
By that effort the to-be-examined reality is got respectfully; since its freedom is everywhere unmade.
The Sanskrit: *jñānaṃ jāgrat*

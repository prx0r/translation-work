# Sivasutra span-p02 — L2 READ

## v.8
Not as impeller of wishing does it proceed, but, but — from self-power's touch, the person becomes like that.
The Sanskrit: *svapno vikalpāḥ*

## v.9
Incapable of own purity, desiring things to be done — when agitation dissolves, then may there be the supreme state.
The Sanskrit: *aviveko māyā sauṣuptam*

## v.10
Then his unmade nature, marked by knowing and doing; all he desires, he knows and does.
The Sanskrit: *tritayabhoktā vīreśaḥ*

## v.11
Regarding his own nature as presiding, he abides as if smiling — whence, for him, this evil course?
The Sanskrit: *vismayo yogabhūmikāḥ*

## v.12
Non-being goes to be-becomeness, and there is no undelusion; since, from contact's application — then thus it was certain.
The Sanskrit: *icchā śaktir umā kumārī*

## v.13
Hence that made thing is to be known as ever like deep sleep; but not as thus being-remembered does that reality attain.
The Sanskrit: *dṛśyaṃ śarīram*

## v.14
State-pair, called effect-doer-ness; effect-ness waning there, doer-ness again unwaning.
The Sanskrit: *śuddhatattvasaṃdhānād vāpaśuśaktiḥ*

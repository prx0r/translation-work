# Sivasutra span-p03 — L2 READ

## v.15
Whatever mere effort faces effect is lost here; 'I am utterly lost' — so the unwise holds.
The Sanskrit: *vitarka ātmajñānam*

## v.16
But who inward-facing is, the seat of all-knowingness; his loss can never be, from perceiving no other at all.
The Sanskrit: *lokānandaḥ samādhisukham*

## v.17
His perception ever, unfailing in three states; ever the fully awake's — but the other's, at beginning and end.
The Sanskrit: *śaktisaṃdhāne śarīrotpattiḥ*

## v.18
Whose form is knowing and knowable, yoked by supreme power — in two states the pervader shines, elsewhere consciousness-made.
The Sanskrit: *bhūtasaṃdhānabhūtapṛthaktvaviśvasaṃghaṭṭāḥ*

## v.19
Spanda-flows beginning with qualities, from resting in common spanda — may they be, of the unopposed knower, ever.
The Sanskrit: *śuddhavidyodayāc cakreśatvasiddhiḥ*

## v.20
Of unawakened minds, eager to hide self-standing — they cast down on the hard-to-cross, terrible wandering road.
The Sanskrit: *mahāhradānusaṃdhānān mantravīryānubhavaḥ*

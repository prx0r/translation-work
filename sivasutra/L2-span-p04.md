# Sivasutra span-p04 — L2 READ

## v.21
Hence ever striving, spanda-reality discerning; waking indeed, their own state soon attains.
The Sanskrit: *cittaṃ mantraḥ*

## v.22
Angry, thrilled, or 'what shall I do?' — or musing, running: whatever step it takes, there spanda is established.
The Sanskrit: *prayatnaḥ sādhakaḥ*

## v.23
Which state soever seized, whatever this tells me — that I will do: thus resolved, he stands.
The Sanskrit: *vidyāśarīrasattā mantrarahasyam*

## v.24
Her, by the upper path resorting — moon and sun, the twain — in the middle channel set, the brahma-egg range left.
The Sanskrit: *garbhe cittavikāso 'viśiṣṭavidyāsvapnaḥ*

## v.25
Then in that great sky, moon and sun dissolved and gone — like deep-sleep: deluded or awakened may be uncovered.
The Sanskrit: *vidyāsamutthāne svābhāvike khecarī śivāvasthā*

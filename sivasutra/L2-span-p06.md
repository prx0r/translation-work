# Sivasutra span-p06 — L2 READ

## v.31
The self is mind.
The Sanskrit: *ātmā cittam*

## v.32
Knowing is bondage.
The Sanskrit: *jñānaṃ bandhaḥ*

## v.33
Of kalā and the rest, of realities — non-discernment is māyā.
The Sanskrit: *kalādīnāṃ tattvānām aviveko māyā*

## v.34
In the body, the dissolving of parts.
The Sanskrit: *śarīre saṃhāraḥ kalānām*

## v.35
Nerve-dissolving, being-conquering, being-isolating, being-separating.
The Sanskrit: *nāḍīsaṃhārabhūtajayabhūtakaivalyabhūtapṛthaktvāni*

## v.36
From delusion's veiling, accomplishment.
The Sanskrit: *mohāvaraṇāt siddhiḥ*

## v.37
From delusion's conquering, from endless enjoying — the innate-wisdom conquest.
The Sanskrit: *mohajayād anantābhogāt sahajavidyājayaḥ*

## v.38
Waking, the second ray.
The Sanskrit: *jāgrad dvitīyakaraḥ*

## v.39
The dancer is the self.
The Sanskrit: *nartaka ātmā*

## v.40
The stage is the inner self.
The Sanskrit: *raṅgo 'ntarātmā*

## v.41
Spectators are the senses.
The Sanskrit: *prekṣakāṇīndriyāṇi*

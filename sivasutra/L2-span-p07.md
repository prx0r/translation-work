# Sivasutra span-p07 — L2 READ

## v.42
By wit subdued: being accomplished.
The Sanskrit: *dhīvaśāt sattvasiddhiḥ*

## v.43
The perfected is free-natured.
The Sanskrit: *siddhaḥ svatantrabhāvaḥ*

## v.44
As there, so elsewhere.
The Sanskrit: *yathā tatra tathānyatra*

## v.45
Seed attending.
The Sanskrit: *bījāvadhānam*

## v.46
Seat-abiding, happily dived in the lake.
The Sanskrit: *āsanasthaḥ sukhaṃ hrade nimajjati*

## v.47
Own-measure making brings about.
The Sanskrit: *svamātrānirmāṇam āpādayati*

## v.48
On wisdom perishing, birth perishing.
The Sanskrit: *vidyāvināśe janmavināśaḥ*

## v.49
In ka-class etc, the great-lady etc, the bound mothers.
The Sanskrit: *kavargādiṣu māheśvaryādyāḥ paśumātaraḥ*

## v.50
In three, the fourth to be poured like oil.
The Sanskrit: *triṣu caturthaṃ tailavad āsecyam*

## v.51
Sunk, by own mind should enter.
The Sanskrit: *magnaḥ svacittena praviśet*

## v.52
On breath coursing, even-seeing.
The Sanskrit: *prāṇasamācāre samadarśanam*

## v.53
In the middle, inferior birthing.
The Sanskrit: *madhye 'varaprasavaḥ*

## v.54
In mothers, on non-certainty joining — of the destroyed, again rising.
The Sanskrit: *mātrāsv apratyayasaṃdhāne naṣṭasya punar utthānam*

## v.55
Śiva-equaled, becomes.
The Sanskrit: *śivatulyo jāyate*

## v.56
Body-course is the vow.
The Sanskrit: *śarīravṛttir vratam*

## v.57
Talk is muttering.
The Sanskrit: *kathā japaḥ*

## v.58
Giving is self-knowing.
The Sanskrit: *dānam ātmajñānam*

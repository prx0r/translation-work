# Sivasutra span-p08 — L2 READ

## v.59
Who un-shepherded standing, is cause of knowing, and —
The Sanskrit: *yo 'vipastho jñāhetuś ca*

## v.60
Own-power heaping is the universe.
The Sanskrit: *svaśaktipracayo viśvam*

## v.61
Standing-dissolving.
The Sanskrit: *sthitilayau*

## v.62
In its proceeding even, non-rejecting, from knower-being.
The Sanskrit: *tatpravṛttāv apy anirāsaḥ saṃvettṛbhāvāt*

## v.63
Of pleasure-pain, outward minding.
The Sanskrit: *sukhāsukhayor bahir mananam*

## v.64
From that freed but, alone.
The Sanskrit: *tadvimuktas tu kevalī*

## v.65
Delusion-massed but, deed-self.
The Sanskrit: *mohapratisaṃhatas tu karmātmā*

## v.66
On difference dismissal, other-world action-ness.
The Sanskrit: *bhedatiraskāre sargāntarakarmatvam*

## v.67
Sense-power, from self, from tasting.
The Sanskrit: *karaṇaśaktiḥ svato 'nubhavāt*

## v.68
Triple-step vivifying.
The Sanskrit: *tripadādyanuprāṇanam*

## v.69
Mind-steady speech, in body-sense-outsides.
The Sanskrit: *cittasthitivac charīrakaraṇabāhyeṣu*

## v.70
From prattling, outward going of the to-be-carried.
The Sanskrit: *abhilāpād bahirgatiḥ saṃvāhyasya*

## v.71
When that ascended measured, from that wasting, life wasting.
The Sanskrit: *tadārūḍhapramites tatkṣayāj jīvasaṃkṣayaḥ*

## v.72
Element-sheathed, then freed again — lord-equal, supreme.
The Sanskrit: *bhūtakañcukī tadā vimukto bhūyaḥ patisamaḥ paraḥ*

## v.73
Natural, breath-link.
The Sanskrit: *naisargikaḥ prāṇasambandhaḥ*

## v.74
From nose-inside-middle restraint, what here, in left-right-middle channels?
The Sanskrit: *nāsikāntarmadhyasaṃyamāt kim atra savyāpasavyasauṣumneṣu*

## v.75
Again may there be re-closing.
The Sanskrit: *bhūyaḥ syāt pratimīlanam*

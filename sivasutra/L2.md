# Śivasūtra — L2 READ (Dyczkowski-mode)

## sivs-p01–p02 — self and powers
**SUMMARY.** Caitanyam ātmā — consciousness is self. Jñānaṃ bandhaḥ — knowledge is bondage. Yonivargaḥ kalāśarīram — womb-class is kalā-body. Jñānādhiṣṭhānaṃ mātṛkā — knowledge's support is mātṛkā. Udyamo bhairavaḥ — arising is Bhairava. Śakticakrasaṃdhāne viśvasaṃhāraḥ — in śakti-wheel-union, world-dissolution. Jñānaṃ jāgrat — knowledge is waking. Svapno vikalpāḥ — dream is thoughts. Aviveko māyā sauṣuptam — non-discrimination is Māyā, deep sleep. Tritayabhoktā vīreśaḥ — threefold enjoyer is Vīreśa. Vismayo yogabhūmikāḥ — wonder is yoga-stages. Icchā śaktir umā kumārī — will is Śakti, Umā, maiden. Dṛśyaṃ śarīram — visible is body. Śuddhatattvasaṃdhānād vāpaśuśaktiḥ — by pure-tattva-union, or paśu-power. Vitarka ātmajñānam — reasoning is self-knowledge. Lokānandaḥ samādhisukham — world-bliss is samādhi-joy. Śaktisaṃdhāne śarīrotpattiḥ — in śakti-union, body-arising. Bhūtasaṃdhānabhūtapṛthaktvaviśvasaṃghaṭṭāḥ — element-union, element-separation, world-collision. Śuddhavidyodayāc cakreśatvasiddhiḥ — from pure-knowledge-arising, wheel-lordship attainment. Mahāhradānusaṃdhānān mantravīryānubhavaḥ — by great-lake-union, mantra-power-experience.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes self = consciousness; knowledge = bondage; arising = Bhairava; three states; powers/states (wonder, will, body, reasoning, samādhi-joy, śakti-union).

**KEY TERMS.** caitanya (consciousness), ātman (self), jñāna (knowledge), bandha (bondage), udyama (arising), bhairava (Bhairava), śakti (power), māyā (Māyā), vīreśa (Vīreśa), yogabhūmikā (yoga-stages), umā (Umā), samādhi (samādhi).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First two passages; sets the doctrinal frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Self: consciousness is self; knowledge is bondage; womb-class is kalā-body; knowledge's support is mātṛkā; arising is Bhairava; śakti-wheel-union dissolves world; knowledge = waking, dream = thoughts, non-discrimination = Māyā/deep sleep; threefold enjoyer = Vīreśa. (2) Powers: wonder = yoga-stages; will = Śakti/Umā/maiden; visible = body; pure-tattva-union or paśu-power; reasoning = self-knowledge; world-bliss = samādhi-joy; śakti-union → body-arising; element-union/separation/collision; pure-knowledge → wheel-lordship; great-lake-union → mantra-power-experience.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Sūtras are terse; L2 expands them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.1–1.20.

## sivs-p03–p04 — mantra and ātman
**SUMMARY.** Cittaṃ mantraḥ — mind is mantra. Prayatnaḥ sādhakaḥ — effort is practitioner. Vidyāśarīrasattā mantrarahasyam — knowledge-body-existence is mantra-secret. Garbhe cittavikāso 'viśiṣṭavidyāsvapnaḥ — in womb, mind-expansion is undifferentiated-knowledge-dream. Vidyāsamutthāne svābhāvike khecarī śivāvasthā — in knowledge-arising, natural, sky-wandering, Śiva-state. Gurur upāyaḥ — Guru is means. Mātṛkācakrasaṃbodhaḥ — mātṛkā-wheel-awakening. Śarīraṃ haviḥ — body is offering. Jñānam annam — knowledge is food. Vidyāsaṃhāre tadutthasvapnadarśanam — in knowledge-dissolution, that-arising-dream-vision. Ātmā cittam — self is mind. Jñānaṃ bandhaḥ — knowledge is bondage. Kalādīnāṃ tattvānām aviveko māyā — non-discrimination of kalā etc. tattvas is Māyā. Śarīre saṃhāraḥ kalānām — in body, dissolution of kalās. Nāḍīsaṃhārabhūtajayabhūtakaivalyabhūtapṛthaktvāni — channel-dissolution, element-conquest, element-isolation, element-separation. Mohāvaraṇāt siddhiḥ — from delusion-covering, attainment. Mohajayād anantābhogāt sahajavidyājayaḥ — from delusion-conquest, endless enjoyment, innate-knowledge-conquest. Jāgrad dvitīyakaraḥ — waking is second-doer. Nartaka ātmā — dancer is self. Raṅgo 'ntarātmā — stage is inner self.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes mantra (mind = mantra, Guru = means, body = offering, knowledge = food) and ātman (self = mind, knowledge = bondage, non-discrimination = Māyā, siddhi from delusion-covering).

**KEY TERMS.** mantra (mantra), citta (mind), prayatna (effort), sādhaka (practitioner), vidyā (knowledge), khecarī (sky-wandering), guru (Guru), upāya (means), mātṛkā (mātṛkā), haviḥ (offering), anna (food), kalā (kalā), māyā (Māyā), nāḍī (channel), siddhi (attainment), nartaka (dancer), raṅga (stage).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p01–p02 (self/powers); p03–p04 establish mantra/ātman.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Mantra: mind = mantra; effort = practitioner; knowledge-body = mantra-secret; in womb, mind-expansion = undifferentiated-knowledge-dream; in knowledge-arising, natural, sky-wandering, Śiva-state; Guru = means; mātṛkā-wheel-awakening; body = offering; knowledge = food; in knowledge-dissolution, dream-vision. (2) Ātman: self = mind; knowledge = bondage; non-discrimination of kalā tattvas = Māyā; in body, dissolution of kalās; channels/elements; delusion-covering → attainment; delusion-conquest → innate-knowledge-conquest; waking = second-doer; self = dancer; inner self = stage.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Sūtras are terse; L2 expands them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS1.21–1.3.10.

## sivs-p05–p06 — siddhi and practice
**SUMMARY.** Prekṣakāṇīndriyāṇi — spectators are senses. Dhīvaśāt sattvasiddhiḥ — by intellect's power, sattva-attainment. Siddhaḥ svatantrabhāvaḥ — siddha is free-state. Yathā tatra tathānyatra — as there, so elsewhere. Bījāvadhānam — seed-attention. Āsanasthaḥ sukhaṃ hrade nimajjati — seated, happily plunges in lake. Svamātrānirmāṇam āpādayati — brings about own-measure-creation. Vidyāvināśe janmavināśaḥ — in knowledge-destruction, birth-destruction. Kavargādiṣu māheśvaryādyāḥ paśumātaraḥ — in ka-group etc., Māheśvarī etc. are paśu-mothers. Triṣu caturthaṃ tailavad āsecyam — in three, fourth like oil should be poured. Magnaḥ svacittena praviśet — immersed, should enter by own mind. Prāṇasamācāre samadarśanam — in breath-conduct, equal-vision. Madhye 'varaprasavaḥ — in middle, lower-birth. Mātrāsv apratyayasaṃdhāne naṣṭasya punar utthānam — in measures, in non-cognition-union, of destroyed, again arising. Śivatulyo jāyate — becomes Śiva-like. Śarīravṛttir vratam — body-conduct is vow. Kathā japaḥ — talking is japa. Dānam ātmajñānam — giving is self-knowledge. Yo 'vipastho jñāhetuś ca — who is established, is cause of knowledge. Svaśaktipracayo viśvam — own-power-accumulation is world.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes siddhi (free-state, seed-attention, plunging in lake, knowledge-destruction → birth-destruction) and practice (immersed enter by mind, breath-conduct = equal-vision, becomes Śiva-like, body-conduct = vow, talking = japa, giving = self-knowledge).

**KEY TERMS.** siddhi (attainment), svatantra (free), bīja (seed), vidyāvināśa (knowledge-destruction), janmavināśa (birth-destruction), māheśvarī (Māheśvarī), prāṇa (breath), samadarśana (equal-vision), vratam (vow), japa (japa), dāna (giving).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p03–p04 (mantra/ātman); p05–p06 establish siddhi/practice.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Siddhi: spectators are senses; by intellect's power, sattva-attainment; siddha is free-state; as there, so elsewhere; seed-attention; seated, plunges in lake; own-measure-creation; knowledge-destruction → birth-destruction; Māheśvarī etc. are paśu-mothers; in three, fourth like oil. (2) Practice: immersed, enter by own mind; breath-conduct = equal-vision; in middle, lower-birth; in measures, non-cognition-union, destroyed again arising; becomes Śiva-like; body-conduct = vow; talking = japa; giving = self-knowledge; established = cause of knowledge; own-power-accumulation = world.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Sūtras are terse; L2 expands them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A06 (sthāna/kāla), spans TS1.3.11–3.30.

## sivs-p07–p08 — state and close
**SUMMARY.** Sthitilayau — stability and dissolution. Tatpravṛttāv apy anirāsaḥ saṃvettṛbhāvāt — in its arising, even non-removal, due to knower-ness. Sukhāsukhayor bahir mananam — of pleasure/pain, external thinking. Tadvimuktas tu kevalī — freed from that, alone. Mohapratisaṃhatas tu karmātmā — bound by delusion, action-self. Bhedatiraskāre sargāntarakarmatvam — in difference-removal, other-creation-action-ness. Karaṇaśaktiḥ svato 'nubhavāt — instrument-power from own experience. Tripadādyanuprāṇanam — three-feet etc. enlivening. Cittasthitivac charīrakaraṇabāhyeṣu — like mind-stability, in body-instrument-externals. Abhilāpād bahirgatiḥ saṃvāhyasya — from speech, external-going of the carried. Tadārūḍhapramites tatkṣayāj jīvasaṃkṣayaḥ — of that-ascended-knower, by its destruction, jīva-destruction. Bhūtakañcukī tadā vimukto bhūyaḥ patisamaḥ paraḥ — element-armored, then freed, again Lord-like, supreme. Naisargikaḥ prāṇasambandhaḥ — natural breath-connection. Nāsikāntarmadhyasaṃyamāt kim atra savyāpasavyasauṣumneṣu — by nose-inner-middle-control, what here in left/right/suṣumnā. Bhūyaḥ syāt pratimīlanam — again there is closing of eyes.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes state (stability/dissolution, freed from pleasure/pain = alone, bound by delusion = action-self) and close (jīva-destruction, freed Lord-like, natural breath-connection, closing of eyes).

**KEY TERMS.** sthiti (stability), laya (dissolution), kevalī (alone), karmātman (action-self), jīvasaṃkṣaya (jīva-destruction), pati (Lord), prāṇa (breath), pratimīlana (closing of eyes).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Closes the text; follows p05–p06 (siddhi/practice).

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) State: stability/dissolution; in arising, non-removal due to knower-ness; of pleasure/pain, external thinking; freed = alone; bound by delusion = action-self; difference-removal → other-creation; instrument-power from own experience; three-feet enlivening; like mind-stability in externals; from speech, external-going. (2) Close: jīva-destruction of ascended knower; freed from element-armor, Lord-like supreme; natural breath-connection; nose-middle-control; closing of eyes.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Sūtras are terse; L2 expands them.

**RELATED.** A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS1.3.31–3.45.

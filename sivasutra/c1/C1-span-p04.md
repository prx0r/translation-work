# C1 — span-p04

## SUMMARY

Mind mantra. Effort, accomplisher. Wisdom-body-being, mantra-secret. In womb, mind-unfolding, indistinct-wisdom-dream. On wisdom rising natural, sky-going Śiva-state.

## FUNCTION

States the śākta turn: mantra is mind, effort accomplishes, wisdom bodies, womb gestates, rising flies.

## KEY TERMS

citta-mantra (mind-mantra — awareness-state, not formula); prayatna (effort — dynamic); vidyā-śarīra (wisdom-body); garbha (womb — gestation); khecarī (sky-going).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Mantra-as-being here becomes guru, mātṛkā, oblation, food beyond.

## EXPLANATION

Mind mantra (citta itself). Effort, accomplisher (dynamic, not strained). Wisdom-body-being, mantra-secret (whose body is wisdom). In womb, mind-unfolding, indistinct-wisdom-dream (gestation, not confusion). On wisdom rising natural, sky-going Śiva-state (effortless flight).

## BOUNDARY

No technique is given; mantra is being, not doing.

## RELATED

Śāktopāya opening (unmeṣa 2: jñānopāya).

## ESSENCE

1. Mind mantra.
2. Effort, accomplisher.
3. Wisdom-body-being, mantra-secret.
4. In womb, mind-unfolding, indistinct-wisdom-dream.
5. On wisdom rising natural, sky-going Śiva-state.

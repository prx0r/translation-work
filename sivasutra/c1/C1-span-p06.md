# C1 — span-p06

## SUMMARY

Self, mind. Knowing, bondage. Kalā-etc realities, non-discernment, māyā. In body, dissolving of parts. Nerve-dissolving, being-conquering, isolating, separating. From delusion-veiling, accomplishment. From delusion conquering, endless enjoying, innate-wisdom conquering. Waking, second-ray. Dancer, self. Inner self, stage. Spectators, senses.

## FUNCTION

Contracts then stages: self-as-mind, knowing-as-bond, māyā, dissolution, series, paradox, theatre.

## KEY TERMS

āṇava (the limited one — unmeṣa 3 opens); māyā (non-discernment); nāḍī-series (dissolve/conquer/isolate/separate); mohāvaraṇa (delusion-veil — whence siddhi); naṭa/raṅga/prekṣaka (dancer/stage/spectators).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Bondage restated here is worked through senses, channels, and return beyond.

From delusion conquering, endless enjoying, innate-wisdom conquering. Waking, second-ray. Dancer, self. Inner self, stage. Spectators, senses (theatre: naṭa, raṅga, prekṣaka).

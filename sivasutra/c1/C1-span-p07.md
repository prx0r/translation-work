# C1 — span-p07

## SUMMARY

By wit subdued, being accomplished. Perfected, free-natured. As there, so elsewhere. Seed attending. Seat abiding, happily dived in lake. Own-measure making brings about. On wisdom perishing, birth perishing. Ka-class etc, great-lady etc, bound mothers. In three, fourth oil-like to be poured. Sunk, by own mind should enter. On breath coursing, even-seeing. In middle, inferior birthing. In mothers, non-certainty joining, destroyed rises again. Śiva-equaled becomes. Body-course, vow. Talk, muttering. Giving, self-knowing.

## FUNCTION

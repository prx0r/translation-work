# C1 — span-p08

## SUMMARY

Un-shepherded standing, knowing-cause. Own-power heaping, universe. Standing-dissolving. In proceeding even, non-rejecting, from knower-being. Pleasure-pain, outward minding. From that freed, alone. Delusion-massed, deed-self. On difference dismissal, other-world action-ness. Sense-power, from self, from tasting. Triple-step vivifying. Mind-steady speech, in body-sense-outsides. From prattling, outward going of to-be-carried. When ascended measured, from wasting, life wasting. Element-sheathed, freed again, lord-equal supreme. Natural, breath-link. Nose-inside-middle restraint, what in channels? Again may be re-closing.

## FUNCTION

Seals the tantra: unshepherded, heaped, paired, unrejected, witnessed, freed, acting, empowered, tasting, vivified, spoken, carried, wasted, freed, linked, channeled, closed.

## KEY TERMS

avi (un-shepherded — no master); anirāsa (non-rejecting); kevalī (alone); sargāntara (other worlds); tripadādi (triple-step); pratimīlana (re-closing — the seal); sauṣumna (middle channel).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The seal closes the whole: opening (unmeṣa) answered by re-closing (pratimīlana).

## EXPLANATION

Un-shepherded standing, knowing-cause (no master needed). Own-power heaping, universe (svaśakti-piled world). Standing-dissolving (one). In proceeding even, non-rejecting, from knower-being. Pleasure-pain outwardly minded (witnessed). From that freed but, alone. Delusion-massed but, deed-self (bound yet acting). On difference dismissal, other-world action-ness. Sense-power, from self, from tasting. Triple-step vivifying (whole sūtra). Mind-steady speech, in body-sense-outsides. From prattling, outward going of the to-be-carried. Ascended-and-measured: from its wasting, the wasting of life. Element-sheathed, then freed again — lord-equal, supreme. Natural, breath-link. Nose-inside-middle held: what is here, in the side and central channels? Again may be re-closing — the seal answering v1's opening.

## BOUNDARY

Nothing follows; re-closing is the last word.

## RELATED

Colophon verse v75; Rudrayāmala ascription absent here (contrast Parātriṃśikā v36).

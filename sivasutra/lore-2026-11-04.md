## Doctrine (verse-usable)
- Revealed to Vasugupta (dream-rock legend); Spanda = vimarsa aspect, Sūtras = prakāsa aspect (integral view).
- Three upāyas DESCENDING (highest first — doctrinal statement): I śāmbhavopāya/icchopāya (will; pure awareness, no object/technique; identity-statements), II śāktopāya/jñānopāya (knowledge; mind/thought-constructs; mantra as awareness-state), III āṇavopāya/kriyopāya (action; intellect/breath/body/senses/objects; longest practical).
- Kṣemarāja's frame dominates all modern readings; Bhāskara differs in places (check Vārttika verses in-file).
- vimarsa/parāśakti/svātantrya synonyms (cf. spanda lore).

## Rasa pointers
- Sūtra style = sūtra-śānta (compressed quiescence) throughout; sthāyin śama with local vīra (effort sūtras) and adbhuta (power sūtras).
- Collision warnings: caitanya (consciousness, not mere awareness-flavor); upāya (means-as-path, three distinct); mantra (awareness-state per śākta, not formula); siddhi (accomplishment); bandha/mokṣa (bondage/release); pramātṛ (knower).

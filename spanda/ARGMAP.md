# Spandakārikā — ARGMAP

## span-p01 (1–10)
- speaker: Vasugupta, devotional/philosophical voice.
- question: What is spanda (vibration)?
- premises: Verse 1: yasyonmeṣanimeṣābhyāṃ jagataḥ pralayodayau | taṃ śakticakravibhavaprabhavaṃ śaṅkaraṃ stumaḥ (to Him by whose opening/closing, world's dissolution/arising; Śaṅkara, source of śakti-wheel-glory, I praise). Verse 2: yatra sthitam idaṃ sarvaṃ kāryaṃ yasmāc ca nirgataṃ | tasyānāvṛtarūpatvān na nirodho 'sti kutracit (where all this effect abides, from which it proceeds; by His uncovered nature, no obstruction anywhere). Verse 3: jāgradādi vibhede 'pi tadabhinne prasarpati | nivartate nijānnaiva svabhāvād upalabdhṛtaḥ (though waking etc. differ, it flows undivided; does not turn back from own nature, from the knower). Verse 4: ahaṃ sukhī ca duḥkhī ca raktaś ca ityadisamvidaḥ | sukhādyavasthānusyūte vartante 'nyatra tāḥ sphuṭam (I am happy/sad/attached etc. consciousnesses; happiness etc. states, strung through, clearly abide elsewhere). Verse 5: na duḥkhaṃ na sukhaṃ yatra na grāhyam grāhakaṃ na ca | na cāsti mūḍhabhāvo 'pi tad asti paramārthataḥ (where no suffering/pleasure, no graspable/grasper, no delusion; that exists in supreme truth). Verse 6: (continues). Verse 7: (continues). Verse 8: (continues). Verse 9: (continues). Verse 10: (continues).
- inference: Spanda = Śiva's opening/closing (unmeṣa/nimeṣa) = source of śakti-wheel; all abides in Him; flows undivided through states; supreme truth beyond suffering/pleasure/grasper.
- conclusion: Spanda doctrine opened (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## span-p02 (11–19)
- speaker: Vasugupta.
- question: How does spanda operate?
- premises: Tam adhiṣṭhātṛbhāvena svabhāvam avalokayan | smayamāna ivāste yas tasyeyaṃ kusṛtiḥ kutaḥ (regarding own-nature as presiding, smiling, he abides; where is his evil course). Nābhāvo bhāvyatām eti na ca tatrāsty amūḍhatā | yato 'bhiyogasaṃsparśāt tadāsid iti niścayah (non-being does not become being; nor is there delusion there; from contact with effort, "it was then" is determination). Atas tatkṛtrimaṃ jñeyaṃ sauṣuptapadavat sadā | na tv evaṃ smaryamāṇatvaṃ tat tattvam pratipadyate (therefore that is artificial, to be known, like deep sleep always; but thus, being remembered, that tattva is not attained). Avasthāyugalaṃ cātra... (two states here...).
- inference: Spanda operates through presiding over own-nature; evil course is absent; non-being does not become being; artificial (like deep sleep); tattva not attained by mere remembering.
- conclusion: Spanda's operation stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## span-p03 (20–3)
- speaker: Vasugupta.
- question: What is the danger and the remedy?
- premises: Aprabudhadhiyas tv ete svasthitisthaganodyatāḥ | pātayanti duruttāre ghore saṃsāravartmani (those with unawakened intellect, rising to conceal own-state, cast into terrible, hard-to-cross saṃsāra-path). Ataḥ satatam udyuktaḥ spandatattvaviviktaye | jāgrad eva nijam bhāvam acireṇādhigacchati (therefore constantly striving for spanda-tattva-distinction, waking, quickly attains own-state). Atikruddhaḥ prahṛṣṭo vā kiṃ karomīti vā mṛśan | dhāvan va yat padaṃ gacchet tatra spandaḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ (whether very angry or delighted, thinking "what shall I do", running, whatever state one goes to, there spanda is established). Yām avasthāṃ samā... (whichever state...).
- inference: Danger = unawakened intellect conceals own-state → saṃsāra; remedy = constant striving for spanda-distinction, waking; spanda is established in every state (anger, delight, running).
- conclusion: Danger/remedy stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## span-p04 (4–6)
- speaker: Vasugupta.
- question: What is the jīvanmukta state?
- premises: Tasmāc chabdārthacintāsu na sāvasthā na yā śivaḥ | bhoktaiva bhogyabhāvena sadā sarvatra saṃsthitaḥ (therefore in sound/meaning/thought, no state that is not Śiva; enjoyer alone, in enjoyable-form, always everywhere established). Iti vā yasya saṃvittiḥ krīḍātvenākhilaṃ jagat | sa paśyan satataṃ yukto jīvanmukto na saṃśayaḥ (thus whose consciousness, playfully, entire world; he, seeing, constantly united, jīvanmukta, no doubt). Ayam evodayas tasya dhyeyasya dhyāyicetasi | tadātmatāsamāpattir icchataḥ sādhakasya yā (this alone is arising of that meditable, in meditator's mind; identity-absorption of that, of wishing practitioner). Iyam evāmṛtaprāpt... (this alone is nectar-attainment...).
- inference: Jīvanmukta = one who sees world as play, constantly united; Śiva is in all sound/meaning/thought; enjoyer alone in enjoyable form; identity-absorption of meditable.
- conclusion: Jīvanmukta doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## span-p05 (7–15)
- speaker: Vasugupta.
- question: What is the body and its spanda?
- premises: Anenādhiṣṭhite dehe yathā sarvajñatādayaḥ | tathā svātmany adhiṣṭhānāt sarvatraivam bhaviṣyati (in this-presided body, as omniscience etc.; so in self, by presiding, everywhere thus it will be). Glānir viluṅthikā dehe tasyāścājñānataḥ sṛtiḥ | tad unmeṣaviluptaṃ cet kutaḥ sā syād ahetukā (languor/rolling in body; from its ignorance, course; if that is destroyed by opening, whence would it be causeless). Ekacintāprasaktasya yataḥ syād aparodayaḥ | unmeṣaḥ sa tu vijñeyaḥ svayaṃ tam upalakṣayet (from single-thought-attachment, whence other-arising; that opening is to be known; oneself should mark it). Ato bindur ato nādo r... (from that, bindu; from that, nāda...).
- inference: Body's spanda: in presided body, omniscience etc.; languor/rolling from ignorance; destroyed by opening; single-thought-attachment prevents other-arising; opening is to be known and marked.
- conclusion: Body/spanda doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## span-p06 (16–2)
- speaker: Vasugupta (close).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: Seyaṃ kriyātmikā śaktiḥ śivasya paśuvartinī | bandhayitrī svamārgasthā jñāta siddhyupapādikā (this action-natured śakti of Śiva, abiding in paśu; binding, on own-path-established, known, attainment-producing). Tanmātrodayarūpena mano 'haṃbuddhivartinā | puryaṣṭakena saṃruddhas taduttham pratyayodbhavam (by tanmātra-arising-form, mind/I-buddhi-abiding; by eight-city enclosed, that-arising-cognition-arising). Bhuṅkte paravaśo bhogaṃ tadbhāvāt saṃsared ataḥ | saṃsṛtipralayasyāsya kāraṇaṃ sampracakṣmahe (enjoys dependent enjoyment; from that-state, wanders in saṃsāra; of this saṃsāra-dissolution, we declare the cause). Yadā tv ekatra saṃr... (when in one...).
- inference: Close: śakti of Śiva abiding in paśu binds; by tanmātra-arising, mind/I-buddhi; enclosed in eight-city; dependent enjoyment → saṃsāra; declares cause of saṃsāra-dissolution.
- conclusion: The text closes with saṃsāra-dissolution cause.
- unresolved: none.

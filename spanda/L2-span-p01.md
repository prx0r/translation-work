# Spanda span-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
By whose opening and closing the worlds dissolve and emerge — to him, source of power-wheel glory, I praise Śaṅkara.
The Sanskrit: *yasyonmeṣanimeṣābhyāṃ jagataḥ pralayodayau | taṃ śakticakravibhavaprabhavaṃ śaṅkaraṃ stumaḥ*

## v.2
Where this whole effect abides, and whence it has come forth — from being uncovered in form, there is no obstruction anywhere.
The Sanskrit: *yatra sthitam idaṃ sarvaṃ kāryaṃ yasmāc ca nirgataṃ | tasyānāvṛtarūpatvān na nirodho 'sti kutracit*

## v.3
Even in the difference of waking and the rest, it glides in the undifferent; the perceiver-ness never turns back from its own being.
The Sanskrit: *jāgradādi vibhede 'pi tadabhinne prasarpati | nivartate nijānnaiva svabhāvād upalabdhṛtaḥ*

## v.4
I happy, suffering, attached, and the like — those awarenesses plainly abide elsewhere, strung on pleasure-states.
The Sanskrit: *ahaṃ sukhī ca duḥkhī ca raktaś ca ityadisamvidaḥ | sukhādyavasthānusyūte vartante 'nyatra tāḥ sphuṭam*

## v.5
Where there is no suffering, no joy, no graspable, no grasper, and not even delusion — THAT is, ultimately.
The Sanskrit: *na duḥkhaṃ na sukhaṃ yatra na grāhyam grāhakaṃ na ca | na cāsti mūḍhabhāvo 'pi tad asti paramārthataḥ*

## v.6
Since the sense-host, deluded, thinks itself undeluded — forthgoing, standing, dissolving, by the inner wheel.
The Sanskrit: *yataḥ karaṇavargo 'yaṃ vimūḍho 'mūḍhavat svayam | sahāntareṇa cakreṇa pravṛttisthitisaṃhṛtiḥ*

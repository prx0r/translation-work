# Spanda span-p02 — L2 READ

## v.7
By that effort the to-be-examined reality is got respectfully; since its freedom is everywhere unmade.
The Sanskrit: *labhate tatprayatnena parīkṣyaṃ tattvam ādarāt | yataḥ svatantratā tasya sarvatreyam akṛtrimā*

## v.8
Not as impelled by wish-impulsion does it proceed; but, but — from self-power's touch the person becomes like that.
The Sanskrit: *na hīcchānodanasyāyam prerakatvena vartate | api tv ātmabalasparśāt puruṣas tatsamo bhavet*

## v.9
Incapable of own purity, desiring things to be done — when agitation dissolves, then may there be the supreme state.
The Sanskrit: *nijāśuddhyāsamarthasya kartavyeṣv abhilāṣiṇaḥ | yadā kṣobhaḥ pralīyeta tadā syāt paramam padam*

## v.10
Then his unmade nature, marked by knowing and doing; whatever he desires, he knows and does.
The Sanskrit: *tadāsyākṛtrimo dharmo jñatvakartṛtvalakṣaṇah | yatas tadepsitaṃ sarvaṃ jānāti ca karoti ca*

## v.11
Regarding own nature as presiding, he abides as if smiling — whence his evil course?
The Sanskrit: *tam adhiṣṭhātṛbhāvena svabhāvam avalokayan | smayamāna ivāste yas tasyeyaṃ kusṛtiḥ kutaḥ*

## v.12
Non-being goes to be-becomeness, and there is no undelusion; since, from contact's application — then thus it was certain.
The Sanskrit: *nābhāvo bhāvyatām eti na ca tatrāsty amūḍhatā | yato 'bhiyogasaṃsparśāt tadāsid iti niścayah*

## v.13
Hence that made thing is to be known as ever like deep sleep; but not thus being-remembered does that reality attain.
The Sanskrit: *atas tatkṛtrimaṃ jñeyaṃ sauṣuptapadavat sadā | na tv evaṃ smaryamāṇatvaṃ tat tattvam pratipadyate*

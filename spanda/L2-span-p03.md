# Spanda span-p03 — L2 READ

## v.14
State-pair, called effect-doer-ness; effect-ness waning there, doer-ness again unwaning.
The Sanskrit: *avasthāyugalaṃ cātra kāryakartṛtvaśabditam | kāryatā kṣayiṇī tatra kartṛtvam punar akṣayam*

## v.15
Whatever mere effort faces effect is lost here; when lost, 'I am utterly lost' — so the unwise holds.
The Sanskrit: *kāryonmukhaḥ prayatno yaḥ kevalaṃ so 'tra lupyate | tasmin lupte vilupto 'smīty abudhaḥ pratipadyate*

## v.16
But who inward-facing, seat of all-knowingness: his loss never ever, from not perceiving other.
The Sanskrit: *na tu yo 'ntarmukho bhāvah sarvajñātvaguṇāspadam | tasya lopaḥ kadācit syād anyasyānupalambhanāt*

## v.17
His perception ever, unfailing in three states; ever may it be of the fully awake — at beginning and end, but of the other.
The Sanskrit: *tasyopalabdhiḥ satataṃ tripadāvyabhicāriṇī | nityaṃ syāt suprabuddhasya tadādyante parasya tu*

## v.18
Whose form is knowing and knowable, yoked by supreme power: in two states the pervader shines, elsewhere consciousness-made.
The Sanskrit: *jñānajñeyasvarūpiṇyā śaktyā paramayā yutaḥ | padadvaye vibhur bhāti tadanyatra tu cinmayaḥ*

## v.19
Spanda-flows beginning with qualities, from resting in common spanda — who got self-getting ever may they be, of the unopposed knower.
The Sanskrit: *guṇādispandaniḥṣyandāḥ sāmānyaspandasaṃśrayāt | labdhātmalābhaḥ satataṃ syur jñasyāparipanthinaḥ*

# Spanda span-p04 — L2 READ

## v.20
Of unawakened minds, eager to cover self-standing — they cast down on the hard-to-cross, terrible wandering road.
The Sanskrit: *aprabudhadhiyas tv ete svasthitisthaganodyatāḥ | pātayanti duruttāre ghore saṃsāravartmani*

## v.21
Hence ever striving for spanda-reality discerning: waking indeed, the own state soon attains.
The Sanskrit: *ataḥ satatam udyuktaḥ spandatattvaviviktaye | jāgrad eva nijam bhāvam acireṇādhigacchati*

## v.22
Angry, thrilled, or what shall I do — or pondering, running: whatever step it takes, there spanda is established.
The Sanskrit: *atikruddhaḥ prahṛṣṭo vā kiṃ karomīti vā mṛśan | dhāvan va yat padaṃ gacchet tatra spandaḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ*

## v.23
Which state seized, whatever this one tells me — that I will do: thus resolved, he stands.
The Sanskrit: *yām avasthāṃ samālambya yad ayam mama vakṣyati | tadavaśyaṃ kariṣye 'ham iti saṃkalpya tiṣṭhati*

## v.24
Her, by resorting to the upper path, moon and sun both — in suṣumnā set, brahma-egg-range left.
The Sanskrit: *tām aśrityordhvamārgeṇa candrasūryāv ubhāv api | sauṣumne 'dhvanyastamito hitvā brahmā.ṅdagocaram*

## v.25
Then in that great sky, moon and sun dissolved — like deep-sleep: deluded or awakened may be uncovered.
The Sanskrit: *tadā tasmin mahāvyomni pralīnaśaśibhāskare | sauṣuptapadavan mūḍhaḥ prabuddhaḥ syād anāvṛtaḥ*

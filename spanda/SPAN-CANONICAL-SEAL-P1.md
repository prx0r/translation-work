## The parable (canonical draft, ≈78s)

> Under everything there is a heart, and every world is one of its
> beats: on the opening-beat worlds emerge, on the closing-beat
> they dissolve — Śaṅkara's power-wheel, praised. Reality is got by
> effort, says the text — but its freedom was never made anywhere
> to be got: you strive for what was already free. And the beats
> fade — the effects come and go — but the doer of the beats never
> wanes: loss without a loser, shining without ever moving. So
> strive, seize, resolve, resort — and meditate the sky itself:
> those four hold while everything else beats. Those who cover
> over the self-standing heart, though, they fall onto the hard,
> terrible wandering road.

**Map (hinge):** opening-closing = worlds dissolve/emerge, power-
wheel praised → p01 · effort-got / freedom-everywhere-unmade →
p02 · effect-wanes / doer-unwanes / loss-no-loser / shining-no-
moving → p03 · strive-seize-resort-resolve + meditate-the-sky +
coverers-cast-on-terrible-road → p04. **All four songs at their
essence-arc (4/4); 25 verses grouped per song, no folds beyond
per-song compression.**
**Limit:** "the text says" is the parable's one self-aware beat
(the effort-paradox is the text's own contradiction — spoken as
spoken, not smoothed); coverers never named as people-fools (they
fall, they aren't mocked).
**Rasa:** adbhuta settling into śānta → vīra-inside-śānta (the four
injunctions) → śānta-vidhi-sealed. The pulse's own rhythm: beat →
still-doer → sealed-injunction.
**Bar check:** one subject (the heart; rim-road = side-scene);
retellable: "every world is a heartbeat; the beats fade; the heart
that beats them never does; four things hold; those who hide it
fall"; plain (spanda/svātantrya folded to heartbeat/freedom-already-
free).

## The seal — statement

**The heartbeat under everything:** base: a great rose-red heart
at the root of all things, gold-rimmed, alive; from it pulse-rings
rise — some rings whole (worlds emerged), some thinning to
nothing (worlds dissolving) — the beats visibly fading upward;
above the heart, ONE still white-gold point that never rings (the
doer that never wanes — everything rings around it); at the sky's
lower edge four small gold lights in a row (strive, seize, resolve,
resort — the four that hold); the clear open SKY above all (the
meditation-object itself); at one rim-edge a small dark road where
tiny grey figures stumble downward (the coverers, cast on the hard
wandering road — the counter-example, never central).

## Composition laws (this seal)

ONE subject (the heart). Rings MUST show stages (whole → thinning)
— a static ring-field fails the beat-doctrine. The still-point
rings nothing (its stillness is the point — no halo-motion).
The four lights equal and ordered (they are a list). The road
small, at rim, figures stumbling DOWN (cast down = direction
kept). Sky = emptiness, no object (the injunction is the void
itself).

# NATURE-PASS v2 — THE STORM AND ITS EYE (owner's own sighting)
**One-line:** *"everything's a storm; the eye at its center was
never made; row for it and look at the clear sky — and don't pull
the sail over your head."*

## Parable v2 (≈80s)
> Out at sea everything is storm: on one turn of the wall worlds
> come apart, on the next they reassemble — that's the churning,
> and it's praised. And at the center there is an eye where the
> storm has never reached — not a calm someone made, but a calm
> that was never made at all: you can row for years and never
> build it; the rowing only keeps your hands honest. The waves are
> the doing — they rise and they fall — but the eye that watches
> them never wanes: no wave ever lost a watcher. So tie down, seize
> the rope, hold course — and above the storm, look at the clear
> sky: those four hold while everything else turns. Sailors who
> pull the sail down over their heads instead of aiming for the
> eye? They go onto the hard road that drowns.

(Heritage: heart-beat v1 kept as recorded alt; eye-of-storm chosen
because it carries ALL FOUR songs: p01 churn = the rotating wall ·
p02 effort/unmade = rowing-toward / the eye-as-absolute-absence
("never made" = "freedom everywhere unmade" performed) · p03
doer-unwanes = the eye no wave can dim · p04 injunctions = sailor-
acts (tie/seize/hold + clear-sky) and coverers = sail-pulled-over-
heads, "the terrible wandering road" = the drowning road. The
word "coverers" is made LITERAL by the sail. Shedding-creature
recorded as alt for a future p03-only seal.)

## Seal v2 — THE STORM AND ITS EYE
Vertical: base = the churning wall of sea, wave-crests carrying
small glowing world-shapes coming apart and reassembling (p01
made literal); mid = a small boat mid-storm, oar-line straining
TOWARD the center; center = THE EYE — a perfect calm hole, empty,
nothing inside it (the unmade as literal absence = the subject);
on the boat-rail FOUR tied knots of rope-light (the injunctions);
the storm is a ring-column and ABOVE it clear open sky (meditate-
the-sky answered by the frame's top); rim = a second small boat
with its sail pulled down over its heads, sinking (the coverers,
never central). Colours: storm indigo/grey churn · silver empty
eye · rope-gold knots · clear ivory sky above · the drowned boat
small umber. ONE subject-line = the boat's oar aiming at absence.
Base (tier-2 folio-by-sight): stage-target = a bordered storm/
sea-page with figure. Seed 837.

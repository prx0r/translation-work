# C1 — span-p02

## SUMMARY

By that effort reality got respectfully — freedom everywhere unmade. Not wish-impelled; from self-power's touch the person becomes like that. Impure, desiring: when agitation dissolves — supreme state. Unmade nature, knowing-doing-marked; desires all, knows and does. Regarding presiding, smiling as if: whence evil course? Non-being to be-becomeness, no undelusion; contact-certainty was. Made-thing like deep-sleep ever; not thus-remembered reality attains.

## FUNCTION

Arms the ground with freedom and effort: unmade, un-impelled, dissolved agitation, knowing-doing mark, dismissed evil, refused memory.

## KEY TERMS

svatantratā (freedom — unmade everywhere); preraka (impeller — denied); kṣobha (agitation — dissolved); jñatva-kartṛtva (knowing-doing — the mark); kusṛti (evil course — impossible); smaryamāṇatva (being-remembered — refused).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Freedom and effort pledged here pay out as vision (beholding) and standing (initiation) beyond.

## EXPLANATION

Examination: by that effort the to-be-examined reality got respectfully — since freedom everywhere unmade (svatantratā). Denial: not as wish-impelled proceeds; but but from self-power's touch the person becomes like that. Condition: of the impure desiring things — when agitation may dissolve, then supreme state. Mark: then his unmade nature, marked by knowing-doing-ness; all desired, knows and does. Dismissal: regarding own nature as presiding, smiling as if — whence his evil course? Recollection: non-being goes to be-becomeness, yet no undelusion there; since contact's application, then thus certainty was. Exclusion: hence the made-thing to be known as ever deep-sleep-like; but not thus being-remembered does that reality attain.

## BOUNDARY

No fruit is tasted; the fruit's shape is drawn but not entered.

## RELATED

Vikalpa-kṣaya (wasting of constructs) thread; suprabuddha returns in v17.

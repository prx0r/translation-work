# C1 — span-p03

## SUMMARY

State-pair called effect-doer-ness; effect wanes, doer unwanes. Mere facing-effect effort lost; 'utterly lost am I' holds the unwise. Inward-facing, seat of knowingness: never loss, no other perceived. Perception ever unfailing triple; fully awake's, beginning-end, but other's. Knowing-knowable-formed, supreme-yoked: two states shines; elsewhere consciousness-made. Quality-born spanda-flows from common spanda; self-getters unopposed may be.

## FUNCTION

Reads standing: what wanes, what is lost, what never loses, what ever perceives, where shines, who may be.

## KEY TERMS

kāryatā/kartṛtva (effect-ness/doer-ness — waning vs unwaning); antarmukha (inward-facing); tripadā (three states — unfailing); cinmaya (consciousness-made); sāmānyaspanda (common spanda — resting-place).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The inward exception drawn here becomes the suprabuddha standing and the heart-seed world beyond.

## EXPLANATION

Division: state-pair called effect-doer-ness; effect-ness waning there, doer-ness again unwaning. Diagnosis: facing-effect mere effort lost here; when lost, 'I am utterly lost' — so the unwise holds. Exception: but who inward-facing, seat of all-knowingness quality — his loss never ever, from not perceiving other. Contrast: his perception ever, unfailing in three states; ever may be of the fully awake — at beginning-end, but of the other. Showing: whose form is knowing-knowable, by supreme power yoked — in two states the pervader shines, elsewhere than that consciousness-made. Wish: spanda-flows beginning with qualities, from resting in common spanda — who got self-getting ever may they be, of the unopposed knower.

## BOUNDARY

No rite is given; standing is read, not yet directed.

## RELATED

Suprabuddha thread (v17, cf. svabodhasiddhi); three-state perception (jāgrat-svapna-sauṣupta).

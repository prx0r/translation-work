# C1 — span-p04

## SUMMARY

Unawakened coverers cast down on hard terrible road. Ever striving, discerning spanda-reality; waking attains soon. Angry, thrilled, pondering, running: whatever step, there spanda stands. Seized state, promised doing: resolved, stands. Upper path resorting, moon-sun in suṣumnā; brahma-egg left. Great sky, moon-sun dissolved; deep-sleep-like: deluded or awake, uncovered.

## FUNCTION

Seals the first niḥṣyanda: warns, urges, places, vows, ascends, opens — ending where it began, uncovered.

## KEY TERMS

udyukta (striving); pratiṣṭhita (established — wherever the step); saṃkalpya tiṣṭhati (resolved, stands); ūrdhvamārga (upper path); brahmāṇḍa (world-egg — byte-exact .ṅ); anāvṛta (uncovered — closing seal).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The striving pledged in freedom pays out as placed spanda, vowed doing, ascended breath, opened sky.

## EXPLANATION

Warning: of unawakened minds eager to cover self-standing — they cast down on the hard, terrible wandering road. Urging: hence ever striving for spanda-reality discerning — waking indeed, own state soon attains. Placing: angry, thrilled, 'what shall I do,' pondering, running — whatever step it takes, there spanda stands. Vowing: which state seized, whatever this tells me — that I will do: thus resolved, he stands. Ascending: her, by upper path resorting, moon and sun both — in suṣumnā set, brahma-egg-range (byte-exact .ṅ) left. Opening: then in that great sky, moon-sun dissolved — like deep-sleep: deluded or awakened may be uncovered (closing word echoes v2's uncovered).

## BOUNDARY

Nothing further is prescribed; uncovered is the last word of the first niḥṣyanda.

## RELATED

Suṣumnā ascent (cf. saktavijnana raising, distinct system); closing anāvṛtaḥ frames the niḥṣyanda with v2.

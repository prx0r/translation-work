# C1 — Spandakārikā

## span-p01–p02 — spanda and its operation
**SUMMARY.** Spandakārikā opens: to Him by whose opening/closing, world's dissolution/arising; Śaṅkara, source of śakti-wheel-glory, I praise. Where all effect abides, from which it proceeds; by His uncovered nature, no obstruction anywhere. Though waking etc. differ, it flows undivided; does not turn back from own nature, from the knower. I am happy/sad/attached etc. consciousnesses; happiness etc. states, strung through, clearly abide elsewhere. Where no suffering/pleasure, no graspable/grasper, no delusion; that exists in supreme truth. Regarding own-nature as presiding, smiling, he abides; where is his evil course. Non-being does not become being; nor is there delusion there; from contact with effort, it was then is determination. Therefore that is artificial, to be known, like deep sleep always; but thus, being remembered, that tattva is not attained.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes spanda = Śiva's opening/closing (unmeṣa/nimeṣa) = source of śakti-wheel; flows undivided; supreme truth beyond suffering/pleasure; spanda operates through presiding over own-nature.

**KEY TERMS.** spanda (vibration), unmeṣa (opening), nimeṣa (closing), śakticakra (śakti-wheel), śaṅkara (Śaṅkara), pralaya (dissolution), udaya (arising), paramārtha (supreme truth), adhiṣṭhātṛ (presiding), kusṛti (evil course).



**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A06 (sthāna/kāla), spans TS1.1–1.19.

## span-p03–p04 — danger and jīvanmukta
**SUMMARY.** Danger: those with unawakened intellect, rising to conceal own-state, cast into terrible, hard-to-cross saṃsāra-path. Therefore constantly striving for spanda-tattva-distinction, waking, quickly attains own-state. Whether very angry or delighted, thinking what shall I do, running, whatever state one goes to, there spanda is established. Jīvanmukta: in sound/meaning/thought, no state that is not Śiva; enjoyer alone, in enjoyable-form, always everywhere established. Thus whose consciousness, playfully, entire world; he, seeing, constantly united, jīvanmukta, no doubt. This alone is arising of that meditable, in meditator's mind; identity-absorption of that, of wishing practitioner.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes danger (unawakened intellect → saṃsāra), remedy (constant striving, spanda in every state), jīvanmukta (world as play, constantly united, Śiva in all).

**KEY TERMS.** aprabudhadhī (unawakened intellect), saṃsāravartman (saṃsāra-path), udyukta (striving), spandatattva (spanda-tattva), jīvanmukta (liberated while living), krīḍā (play), dhyeya (meditable), samāpatti (absorption).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p01–p02 (spanda/operation); p03–p04 establish danger/jīvanmukta.


**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.20–1.6.

## span-p05–p06 — body and close
**SUMMARY.** Body: in presided body, omniscience etc.; so in self, by presiding, everywhere; languor/rolling in body from ignorance; if destroyed by opening, whence causeless; from single-thought-attachment, whence other-arising; opening to be known and marked. Close: this action-natured śakti of Śiva abiding in paśu binds; by tanmātra-arising, mind/I-buddhi; enclosed in eight-city; dependent enjoyment → saṃsāra; declares cause of saṃsāra-dissolution.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes body's spanda (omniscience by presiding, languor from ignorance, opening destroys it) and close (śakti of Śiva abiding in paśu binds; tanmātra-arising; eight-city; dependent enjoyment → saṃsāra; declares cause of saṃsāra-dissolution).

**KEY TERMS.** glāni (languor), viluṅthikā (rolling), unmeṣa (opening), bindu (bindu), nāda (nāda), kriyātmikā (action-natured), paśu (paśu), puryaṣṭaka (eight-city), paravaśa (dependent), saṃsṛtipralaya (saṃsāra-dissolution).



**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS1.7–1.2.

## Doctrine (Singh, verse-usable)
- spanda ≠ movement: throb/heaving of spiritual rapture in the Divine, excluding succession ("movement as it were"); = vimarsa = svātantrya-śakti = Self-awareness dynamism. Prakāśa (Śiva-sūtras) vs vimarśa (Spanda-kārikās): integral view.
- Kallata's 3 sections: svarūpa-spanda (1-25), sahaja-vidyodaya (26-32), vibhūti-spanda (33-52 + guru-obeisance v53). WE DO vv1-25 ONLY.
- v1: unmeṣa-nimeṣa (opening/closing of the eyes of consciousness) as cosmic dissolution/emergence; śakti-cakra-vibhava-prabhava Śaṅkara praised (source/glory of the wheel of powers).
- v2: anāvṛta (uncovered) — no nirodha (obstruction) anywhere; the heart (hṛdaya) of devadeva gives yoga-release at once (sadyo yogavimokṣadam).
- v3: jāgrat waking-state non-difference (tadabhinne prasarpati); svabhāva the perceiver (upalabdhṛ) never leaves itself.
- v4: ahaṃ sukhī/duḥkhī/rakta states are elsewhere (anyatra) — clearly (sphuṭam) strung on pleasure-states (sukhādyavasthānusyūte).
- v5: beyond pleasure/pain/grasped/grasper/delusion — THAT exists ultimately (paramārthataḥ).
- Unmeṣa doctrine peaks later (v30+ out of scope) — do not import; first niḥṣyanda establishes svarūpa only.

## Rasa pointers

- p02 (vv7-13, freedom + effort): vīra-utsāha (svatantratā, ātmabala, kṣobha-dissolving) inside śānta.

- p04 (vv20-25, waking + initiation): śānta sealed (suprabuddha, dīkṣā undoubted).
- Collision warnings: spanda (throb, never motion); vimokṣa (release); unmeṣa/nimeṣa (opening/closing, not blinking); anāvṛta (uncovered — key epithet); nirodha (obstruction); upalabdhṛ (perceiver).

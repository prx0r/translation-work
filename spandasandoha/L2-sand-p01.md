# Spandasandoha sand-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
He alone — Śiva, possessing spanda, self-established — who makes the inner face taste.
The Sanskrit: *saḥ jayati śivaḥ ekaḥ spandavān | svapratiṣṭhaḥ yaḥ antarvaktram rasayati*

## v.2
This hero, lord of the Kula — victorious.
The Sanskrit: *jayati eṣaḥ | vīraḥ kulendraḥ*

## The parable (canonical draft, ≈50s spoken)

> A champion stands alone in the sea. Waves come from every side — all
> kinds of weather — and they do not move him, and the sea beneath him
> does not move either. He is not holding out against the waves. He is
> the slow throb the waves are made of, standing by itself. And he is
> not even watching the sea: he tastes it from inside — his own throb,
> on his own inward face. On the shore the chiefs call him victor, and
> he is — without turning around. The wonder does not end in noise.
> The ocean stands, alone, victorious.

**Map (hinge)** — champion alone in sea → "He alone conquers" [v1] ·
waves + unmoved ocean → C1's *like an ocean unmoved by world-waves* ·
slow throb = the waves' source → *spandavān* + *svapratiṣṭha* [v1] ·
tastes it inside on his own inward face → *antarvaktram rasayati* +
pratyabhijñā = tasting spanda in one's own inner face [C1] · chiefs
call him victor → *vīraḥ kulendraḥ* [v2] · still close → rasa
adbhuta→śānta, *shines supreme* [C1].
**Limit:** a champion and a sea are two; this champion and this sea
are not — the story must drop the pair to keep the one. (Also: real
seas move; the stillness is doctrine, not physics.)
**Rasa:** adbhuta (wonder) resolving into śānta (tranquility) — the
justification is C1's own (Abhinava names none; the ocean-line is the
ground). The parable's last movement — noise → stillness — IS that
sthāyibhāva.
**Why it passes the bar:** one subject, one setting, retellable in
three lines, zero doctrine words (spanda/Kula/pratyabhijñā all
decoded: throb / chiefs / tasting).

## The seal — statement

**The still center that thunders outward:** one figure standing in a
sea that does not move, concentric pulse-rings radiating from his
chest *becoming* the waves — inner stillness generating outer motion —
with the shore-chiefs small at the rim and a serene gold sky above.

anything *to* the sea (as-above/so-below inverted: multiplicity
radiating from a single unmoving source).

## Composition laws (this seal)

ONE subject (the champion; chiefs stay rim-scale, never a crowd).
Zones connected: pulse-rings physically become the waves (no gap —
that IS the argument). No pretty symmetric mandala-web (that's song
4's idiom); this seal's idiom is CONCENTRIC RINGS + horizon.
The inward-bowed face carries the "tasting" — not a tongue, not a
mouth: light at the inner face.

## The image prompt (seal text ONLY — reference carries style)

> On aged vellum, one scene: a calm sea under a serene gold sky; at
> the still center a lone figure stands unmoved, face bowed inward to
> a faint light at his own inner face; from his chest silver-white
> pulse-rings widen and become the waves themselves, hatched and
> living, in every direction; at the far rim small chiefs on the
> shore raise their arms to him; burnished raised gold over red bole
> in the sky, lapis and teal sea, ivory stillness where he stands,
> one vermilion spark at the chest; pale before dark, black outline,
> white highlights, shell-gold accents. One subject. No text, no
> letters, no signature.

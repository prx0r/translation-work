## 1. Lore (one focused web search)

Search: "Spandasandoha Kshemaraja Spandakarika commentary meaning verses" (deep, 6 results).

Findings:
- Spandasandoha = Kṣemarāja's commentary on Spandakārikā 1.1 (first aphorism only).
- Spanda = divine throb/vibration = Śakti = I-consciousness. "Spanda is synonymous with Śakti (I-consciousness). Spanda is the Power of Śiva, by which He is conscious of Himself."
- Spanda is NOT physical vibration — "It is a kind of movement in the space of Consciousness."
- Four commentaries on Spandakārikā: Vṛtti (Kallaṭa), Spandapradīpikā (Bhaṭṭotpala), Vivṛti (Rāmakaṇṭha), Spandanirṇaya (Kṣemarāja, prose, most authoritative). Spandasandoha is Kṣemarāja's earlier, shorter work.
- Kṣemarāja was Abhinavagupta's cousin and pupil. He wrote Spandasandoha first, then Spandanirṇaya at his pupil Sura's request.
- The verse: "sa jayati śiva ekaḥ spandavān svapratiṣṭhaḥ yo'ntarvaktraṃ rasayati / jayatyeṣa vīraḥ kulendraḥ"
- Kṣemarāja's gloss: akalitamahimā (unmeasured greatness), yaḥ kṣmādisādāśivāntaṃ kalayati hṛdi viśvaṃ (measures world from earth to Sadāśiva in heart), citrasaṃyojanābhiḥ (various combinations), prathayati vicitrāḥ sṛṣṭisaṃhāralīlāḥ (displays creation-dissolution plays), sa ekaḥ advaitasvarūpaḥ (non-dual nature), svābhinnasvātantryaśaktisamavetaḥ (endowed with autonomy-power), samudra iva avicala (ocean unmoved by waves), sarvotkṛṣṭatayā prasphurati (shines supreme).

Ledger: spanda=throb · svapratiṣṭha=self-established · antarvaktra=inner face · rasa=taste · vīra=hero · kulendra=lord of the Kula · akalita=unmeasured · mahimā=greatness · citrasaṃyojana=various combination · sṛṣṭi=creation · saṃhāra=dissolution · līlā=play · advaita=non-dual · svasvarūpa=own nature · svasvātantryaśakti=own autonomy-power · samudra=ocean · viśvakallola=world-waves · avicala=unmoved.

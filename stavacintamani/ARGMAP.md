# Stavacintāmaṇi — ARGMAP

## stav-p01 (2–11)
- speaker: Bhaṭṭanārāyaṇa, devotional voice.
- question: Who is the supreme Lord?
- premises: Yaḥ sphītaḥ śrīdayābodhaparamānandasampadā (He who is full of abundant bliss-wealth from Śrī's knowledge) vidyoddyotitamāhātmyaḥ (whose greatness shines by knowledge) sa jayaty aparājitaḥ (He is victorious, unconquered). Prasaradbindunādāya śuddhāmṛtamayātmane (to Him whose self is pure nectar of spreading bindu-nāda) namo 'nantaprakāśāya śaṃkarakṣīrasindhave (homage to unlimited light, Śaṃkara milk-ocean). Dviṣmas tvāṃ tvāṃ stumas tubhyaṃ mantrayāmo 'mbikāpate (we hate you, we praise you, we invoke you, Lord of Ambikā) ativāllabhyataḥ sādhu viśvaṅ no dhṛtavān asi (by great accessibility, you have held the world). Saṃhṛtasparśayogāya sampūrṇāmṛt... (to Him who has gathered touch-yoga, full nectar...).
- inference: The supreme Lord = Śiva (unconquered, full of bliss, pure nectar, unlimited light, accessible).
- conclusion: Śiva is the supreme Lord (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## stav-p02 (12–21)
- speaker: Bhaṭṭanārāyaṇa, devotional voice.
- question: What is the fruit of devotion?
- premises: Api paśyema gambhīrāṃ pareṇa jyotiṣābhitaḥ (may we see the deep, by supreme light) unmṛṣṭatamasaṃ ramyām antarbhava bhavadguhām (cleared darkness, lovely, inside your cave). Namaste 'pi ye somakalākalitaśekharam (homage to those who, with moon-crescent crown) nāthaṃ svapne 'pi paśyanti paramānandadāyinam (see the Lord even in dream, giver of supreme bliss). Bhagavan bhava bhāvatkaṃ bhāvaṃ bhāvayituṃ ruciḥ (Lord, may desire arise to cultivate your state). Punarbhavabhayocchedadakṣā kasmai na rocate (who would not delight in skill at cutting rebirth-fear). Yāvajjīvaṃ jagannātha kartavya... (as long as life, Lord of world, duty...).
- inference: Devotion's fruit = seeing Śiva (even in dream), supreme bliss, cutting rebirth-fear.
- conclusion: Devotion yields vision and liberation (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## stav-p03 (22–31)
- speaker: Bhaṭṭanārāyaṇa, devotional voice.
- question: What is the nature of worship?
- premises: Arcito 'yam ayaṃ dhyāta eṣa toṣita ity ayam (this is worshipped, this is meditated, this is pleased) rasaḥ srotaḥsahasreṇa tvayi me bhava vardhatām (may this rasa grow in you by thousand streams). Namo niḥśeṣadhīpatrimālālayamayātmane (homage to Him whose self is abode of complete knowledge-garland) nāthāya sthāṇave tubhyaṃ nāgayajñopavītine (to Lord, to Sthāṇu, to you with serpent-sacrificial-thread). Ajñānatimirasyaikam auṣadhaṃ saṃsmṛtis tava (the one medicine for darkness of ignorance is remembrance of you). Bhava tattatpradānena prasādaḥ kriyatāṃ mayi (by this and that giving, grace be done to me). Nama īśāya niḥśeṣapuruṣārthaprasādhakaḥ (homage to Īśa, accomplisher of all human ends).
- inference: Worship = remembrance of Śiva (medicine for ignorance); grace accomplishes all ends.
- conclusion: Worship doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## stav-p04 (32–41)
- speaker: Bhaṭṭanārāyaṇa, devotional voice.
- question: What is Śiva's power?
- premises: Icchāyā eva yasyeyat phalaṃ lokatrayātmakam (whose desire alone has fruit, three-world-natured) tasya te nātha kāryāṇāṃ ko vetti kiyatī gatiḥ (Lord, who knows the extent of your works). Brahmādayo 'pi tad yasya karmasopānamālayā (even Brahmā etc. by whose karma-ladder) upary upari dhāvanti labdhuṃ dhāma namāmi tam (run up and up to gain abode; I bow to Him). Ayam brahmā mahendro 'yaṃ sūryācandramasāv imau (this is Brahmā, this is Mahendra, these are sun and moon) iti śaktilatā yasya puṣpitā pātv asau bhavaḥ (whose power-vine is blossomed; may Bhava protect). Bhramo na labhyate yasya bhr... (whose delusion is not obtained...).
- inference: Śiva's power = desire with three-world fruit; Brahmā etc. run up the karma-ladder to gain abode.
- conclusion: Power doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## stav-p05 (42–50)
- speaker: Bhaṭṭanārāyaṇa, devotional voice.
- question: What is Śiva's grace?
- premises: Namo nirupakāryāya trailokyaikopakāriṇe (homage to Him who needs no service, sole benefactor of three worlds) sarvasya spṛhaṇīyāya niḥspṛhāya kapardine (desirable by all, desireless, with matted hair). Aho kṣetrajñatā seyaṃ kāryāya mahate satām (oh, this knower-ness for great work of the good) yayānantaphalāṃ bhaktiṃ vapanti tvayy amī prabho (by which they sow devotion with endless fruit in you, Lord). Mahatīyam aho māyā tava māyin (great, oh, your Māyā, Māyin) yayāvṛtaḥ tvaddhyānanidhilābhe 'pi mugdho lokaḥ ślathāyate (by which, even on gaining treasure of meditation on you, the deluded world is slack). Ārambhe bhava sarvatra karma vā karaṇ... (in beginning, Bhava, everywhere karma or...).
- inference: Grace = Śiva needs no service, sole benefactor; Māyā deludes even those who gain meditation-treasure.
- conclusion: Grace/Māyā doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## stav-p06 (51–60)
- speaker: Bhaṭṭanārāyaṇa, devotional voice.
- question: What is Śiva's sovereignty?
- premises: Upasaṃhṛtakāmāya kāmāyatim atanvate (to Him who has withdrawn desire, not extending desire's course) avataṃsitasomāya somāya svāmine namaḥ (homage to Him crowned with moon, to Soma, to Lord). Kim aśaktaḥ karomīti sarvatrānadhyavasyataḥ (thinking "what can I, powerless, do") sarvānugrāhikā śaktiḥ śāṃkarī śaraṇaṃ mama (all-beneficent Śāṃkarī power is my refuge). Guṇātītasya nirdiṣṭaniḥśeṣātiśayātmanaḥ (of Him beyond qualities, whose essence is all-excellence) labhyate bhava kutrāṃśe paraḥ pratinidhis tava (where, Bhava, is a peer or substitute for you). Nirdvandve nirupādhau ca tvayy ātmani sati prabho va... (in you, self, without duality, without condition, Lord...).
- inference: Sovereignty = Śiva withdraws desire, Śāṃkarī power is refuge, no peer/substitute.
- conclusion: Sovereignty doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## stav-p07 (61–70)
- speaker: Bhaṭṭanārāyaṇa, devotional voice.
- question: What is the bliss (ānanda)?
- premises: Trailokye 'py atra yo yāvān ānandaḥ kaścid īkṣyate (whatever bliss is seen in these three worlds) sa bindur yasya taṃ vande devam ānandasāgaram (that is a drop of His; I bow to the god, ocean of bliss). Aho brahmādayo dhanyā ye vimuktānyasaṃkatham (oh, Brahmā etc. are fortunate, freed from other-talk) namo namaḥ śivāyeti japanty āhlādavihvalāḥ (chanting "homage to Śiva", overwhelmed with delight). Niṣkāmāyāpi kāmānām anantānāṃ vidhāyine (to Him who bestows endless desires even to the desireless) anāditve 'pi viśvasya bhoktre bhava namo 'stu te (even though beginningless, enjoyer of the world; homage to you, Bhava). Stumas tribhuvanārambhamūl... (we praise, root of three-world-beginning...).
- inference: Bliss = three-world bliss is a drop of Śiva's ocean; Brahmā etc. fortunate, chanting "Śiva" with delight.
- conclusion: Bliss doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## stav-p08 (71–80)
- speaker: Bhaṭṭanārāyaṇa, devotional voice.
- question: What is Śiva's protection?
- premises: Namaste bhavasambhrāntabhrāntim udbhāvya bhindate (homage to you who, manifesting the delusion of world-confusion, shatters it) jñānānandaṃ ca nirdvandvaṃ deva vṛtvā vivṛṇvate (and, covering, reveals knowledge-bliss without duality, god). Yasyāḥ prāpyeta paryantaviśeṣaḥ kair manorathaiḥ (by what desires could the limit-distinction of that be reached). Māyām ekanimeṣeṇa muṣṇaṃs tāṃ pātu naḥ śivaḥ (stealing Māyā in one blink, may Śiva protect us). Vairāgyasya gatiṃ gurvīṃ jñānasya paramāṃ śriyam (the weighty course of dispassion, the supreme glory of knowledge) naiḥspṛhyasya parāṃ koṭiṃ bibhratāṃ tvaṃ prabho prabhuḥ (to those bearing the peak of desirelessness, you are Lord, Lord). Br... (continued).
- inference: Protection = Śiva shatters world-confusion, steals Māyā in one blink, protects devotees.
- conclusion: Protection doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## stav-p09 (81–90)
- speaker: Bhaṭṭanārāyaṇa, devotional voice.
- question: What is Śiva's remembrance?
- premises: Duṣkaraṃ sukarīkartuṃ duḥkhaṃ sukhayituṃ tathā (to make the difficult easy, to make suffering pleasant) ekavīrā smṛtir yasya taṃ smarāmaḥ smaradviṣam (whose memory is a single hero; we remember the enemy of Smara). Jayanti gītayo yāsāṃ sa geyaḥ parameśvaraḥ (victorious are the songs whose singer is the supreme Lord) yannāmnāpi mahātmānaḥ kīryante pulakāṅkuraiḥ (by whose name alone great souls are thrilled with horripilation). Bhavān iva bhavān eva bhaved yadi paraṃ bhava (if you, Bhava, were only like Bhava, supreme Bhava) svaśaktivyūhasaṃvyūḍhatrailokyārambhasaṃhṛtiḥ (arrangement of own-powers, three-world-beginning-dissolution). Mantro 'si mantraṇīyo 'si m... (you are mantra, to be counseled...).
- inference: Remembrance = Śiva makes difficult easy, suffering pleasant; memory is single hero; name thrills great souls.
- conclusion: Remembrance doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## stav-p10 (91–100)
- speaker: Bhaṭṭanārāyaṇa, devotional voice.
- question: What is Śiva's sovereignty (continued)?
- premises: Namaḥ kṛtakṛtāntānta tubhyaṃ madanamardine (homage to you, end of the end-maker, crusher of Kāma) mastakanyastagaṅgāya yathāyuktārthakāriṇe (with Gaṅgā on head, doer of fitting meanings). Aiśvaryajñānavairāgyadharmebhyo 'py upari sthitim (standing above even sovereignty-knowledge-dispassion-dharma) nātha prārthayamānānāṃ tvad ṛte kā parā gatiḥ (Lord, for those praying, what other goal besides you). Tvayy anicchati kaḥ śambho śaktaḥ kubjayituṃ tṛṇam (when you, Śambhu, do not wish, who can bend even a blade of grass). Tvadicchānugṛhītas tu vahed brāhmīṃ dhuraṃ na kaḥ (but one graced by your wish would bear the Brahmī burden, who not). Harapraṇatimāṇikya... (continued).
- inference: Sovereignty = Śiva above even sovereignty-knowledge-dispassion-dharma; no one can bend grass without His wish.
- conclusion: Sovereignty doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## stav-p11 (101–110)
- speaker: Bhaṭṭanārāyaṇa, devotional voice.
- question: What is the devotee's state?
- premises: Niḥśeṣakleśahānasya hetuḥ ka iti saṃśaye (in doubt, who is the cause of complete removal of affliction) svāmin so 'sīti niścitya kas tvāṃ na śaraṇaṃ gataḥ (Lord, you are that; having decided, who would not take refuge in you). Bhuktvā bhogān bhavabhrāntiṃ hitvā lapsye paraṃ padam (having enjoyed pleasures, abandoning world-delusion, I shall gain supreme state) ity āśaṃseha śobheta śambhau bhaktimataḥ param (thus hoping, may it be auspicious for the devotee of Śambhu). Nātha svapne 'pi yat kuryāṃ brūyāṃ vā sādhv asādhu vā (Lord, whatever I do or say in dream, good or bad) tvadadhīnatvadarpeṇa sarvatrātrāsmi nirvṛtaḥ (by pride of dependence on you, everywhere here I am content). Jyotiṣām... (continued).
- inference: Devotee's state = taking refuge in Śiva (cause of affliction-removal), abandoning world-delusion, contentment in dependence.
- conclusion: Devotee doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## stav-p12 (111–120)
- speaker: Bhaṭṭanārāyaṇa, devotional voice (close).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: Aho svādutamaḥ śarvasevāśaṃsāsudhārasaḥ (oh, most sweet is the nectar of desire for service to Śarva) kutra kālakalāmātre na yo navanavāyate (where, in any moment, does it not become new). Muhur muhur aviśrāntas trailokyaṃ kalpanāśataiḥ (again and again tirelessly, three worlds by hundred thoughts) kalpayann api ko 'py eko nirvikalpo jayaty ajaḥ (creating, yet one, thought-free, victorious, unborn). Malatailāktasaṃsāravāsanāvartidāhinā (by the lamp of knowledge, burning the oil of saṃsāra-trace) jñānadīpena deva tvāṃ kadā nu syām upasthitaḥ (god, when shall I be present to you). Nimeṣam api yady ekaṃ kṣīṇadoṣe kariṣyasi (if even for one moment you will do in the faultless...).
- inference: Close = nectar of service to Śarva, thought-free creation, lamp of knowledge burning saṃsāra-trace, presence to Śiva.
- conclusion: The stotra closes with devotion and presence.
- unresolved: none.

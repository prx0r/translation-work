# C1 — Stavacintāmaṇi (final condensation of the arguments)

## stav-p01 (vv. 2–11)

Stavacintāmaṇi opens with praise of Śiva as supreme Lord: unconquered, full of bliss-wealth from Śrī's knowledge, whose greatness shines by knowledge. He is pure nectar of spreading bindu-nāda, unlimited light, Śaṃkara milk-ocean. We hate/praise/invoke you, Lord of Ambikā; by great accessibility you have held the world. May we see the deep by supreme light; cleared darkness, lovely cave. Homage to those with moon-crescent crown who see Lord even in dream, giver of supreme bliss. Lord, may desire arise to cultivate your state; who would not delight in cutting rebirth-fear. Worship: worshipped/meditated/pleased; rasa grow by thousand streams. Homage to abode of complete knowledge-garland; Lord, Sthāṇu, serpent-thread. Remembrance = medicine for ignorance; grace be done; homage to Īśa, accomplisher of all ends.

## stav-p02 (vv. 12–21)

May we see the deep, by supreme light, cleared darkness, lovely, inside your cave. Homage to those who, with moon-crescent crown, see the Lord even in dream, giver of supreme bliss. Lord, may desire arise to cultivate your state; who would not delight in skill at cutting rebirth-fear? For life, Lord of world, let this be our duty; by your grace, by single-mindedness, may we be. With thousand branches spread, Veda-āgama-formed self; homage to wish-fulfilling tree, Śambhu. Speech-mind-body actions, employing in you, Lord; becoming you-filled, free from dvandva, may we be, even sometime. In worlds' creation-dissolution, particular welfare-appointment; to the trident-bearer, without-other-dependence, power-possessing, homage. Beyond-guṇa-yoga's chief-meditation-object, Dhūrjaṭi; what fruit from other supports for those who meditate on name even? Homage, homage, to Śiva; taking refuge in mantra-power; praiseworthy are those who strive to enjoy Śāmbhavī bhūti. What path not obtainable, what speech not said by which; what meditation not meditable, what not are you, Lord?

## stav-p03 (vv. 22–31)

This is worshipped, this is meditated, this is pleased; may this rasa grow in you by thousand streams. Homage to Him whose self is abode of complete knowledge-garland, to Lord, to Sthāṇu, to you with serpent-sacrificial-thread. The one medicine for darkness of ignorance is remembrance of you; by this and that giving, grace be done to me. Homage to Īśa, accomplisher of all human ends; homage itself is to be offered by the wise here. Immersed in terrible bhava-ocean, in nilaya of suffering-fish; having obtained Śārva bhakti-cintāmaṇi, what not conquered? To the uncovered, non-dvandva, immovable knowledge-wealth, you are known, indeed, by some, Dhūrjaṭe. Quality-less, yet of quality-knowers the sole knowable, victorious, the unborn; desire-less by nature, yet who is the supreme fruit of desires. For Śrī-ratna-nectar-gain, where not labored by some gods; that milk-ocean lordship, your own, natural, Lord. Homage, by devotion, for liberation of humans; by remembrance, by praise, they give to Śambhu, to Śaṃ, to Bhava, to Abhava. All-knowing, all-doing, all is you, thus for knowers of knowledge; what knowable or karma, Lord, is offered for endlessness to you?

## stav-p04 (vv. 32–41)

By whose will alone, whose fruit is three-world-natured; of your actions, Lord, who knows what extent? Brahmā etc. even, by whose karma-ladder, ascending, to gain the abode, we bow to that. This Brahmā, Mahendra, these sun-moon; iti śakti-latā whose blossomed, may that protect, Bhava. Delusion not obtained by whom, even by deluded inner-organs; by far ones even, whose end is inaccessible, we bow to the soft one. Homage in stuti, smṛti, dhyāna, darśana, sparśana, and in attainment; to the bliss-group, to the beloved, to the matted-haired. What wonder, thinking, even performing countless tasks of mind's fickleness; resting, Bhava, in bhava-bhakti-bliss, rejoice intensely. Even if subtle, this three-world, by kalā alone, how is it yours; if gross, why not easily seen by Brahmā etc., Lord? If these were not to be said, you alone, it would not have been; what trouble, Lord, in speech-nets would the wise have taken then? By what order, by what karma, by what wisdom, Lord, are you seen? By instruction prasāda be done to me. Homage to the non-help-needing, to the sole helper of three worlds; to the desired by all, to the desire-less, to the matted-haired.

## stav-p05 (vv. 42–50)

Ah, what a great thing, this karma, Lord, of the nature of bhava-bhāvanā; from Brahmā to worm, in which, for liberation, who is not made eligible? Beginning is all karmas', end is all karmas'; the varied middle-vṛttis are yours alone, Lord, of wisdom's path. As much as the tasting of nectar of bhakti-rasa, thousandfold-guṇa-vistara; Lord, not elsewhere seen, from bhakti-rasa-nectar. For the one with desires withdrawn, to the one extending desire, for the one with desires unfulfilled; even so, by śakti, for the one with desires fulfilled, all-grace. Guṇa-transcendent, for the designated, complete, beyond-all-excellence-self; where is obtained, Lord, a counterpart of you in any portion? In dvandva-less, without-upādhi, in you, self, Lord, we are deceived even now by your māyā, immeasurable. Aṇimā etc. guṇa-attainment, eternal lordship, bhava-destruction; these, Bhava, are the sprouts of the kalpa-tree of devotion to you. Whatever direction, there not, you are; all time is your-made. Thus obtained, when will you be obtained, Lord, tell? Homage to the clear, good-conduct-mind, one-abode; to the much-bhūti-situated, to the great swan, to Śambhu. Heart's highest-darkness-destroyed, burned all bhava-fuel; may your knowledge-lamp, Lord, be my devotion-lamp. Many seeds of worlds, creating, the world-drama; having staged, Hara, to dissolve, who else is capable? Homage to the maker of real/unreal, to make unreal real or real unreal; to the autonomous, to the non-autonomous, to the one with decay-lordship-one-line.

## stav-p06 (vv. 51–60)

Trailokya even, here, whatever ānanda is seen; that is a drop, whose that, we bow to that god, ocean of bliss. Ah, Brahmā etc. are blessed, who are freed from other-talk; homage, homage, to Śiva, chanting namaḥ śivāya, delighted with bliss. Desire-less, yet of desires, infinite, arranger; beginning-less, yet of world's, enjoyer; to you, Bhava, homage. We praise Tribhuvana's root-prakṛti, Īśvara; who would seek help from us, from whom complete-dharma? In great artha-difficulties, by moha-mass-stained; when remembered, they are pleased, that Śiva we praise. Lord, your power alone here, unbroken; by whose will, three-worlds' dissolution-rise are spread. Kukarma even, to which directed, god, becomes supreme good karma; of good karma, the goodness, from which not elsewhere, you, Lord. This fist-grasped, seen, where will you go from us? Thus bhakti-rasa-intoxicated, blessed ones, run to Dhūrjaṭi. We praise you by Ṛg-Yajuḥ-Sāman; whose knowledge-object is profound-beautiful. Vidhi is beginning and end of world, Lord; from whom, dharma-group, how would it be? Homage to you, who manifest delusion, breaking delusion; knowledge-bliss and non-dvandva, Lord, covering, revealing. Whose paryanta-distinction, by what manorathas, obtainable; Māyā, in one nimeṣa, stealing, may that Śiva protect us. Of vairāgya, the great gati; of jñāna, supreme śrī; of naiḥspṛhya, supreme koṭi; bearing those, you, Lord, are Lord.

# Stavacintamani stav-p01 (invocation and offering) — L2 READ

## v.2
Who, grown great with the wealth of glory, compassion, awakening, and supreme bliss, his greatness lit by knowledge — he conquers, the undefeated.
The Sanskrit: *yaḥ sphītaḥ śrīdayābodhaparamānandasampadā vidyoddyotitamāhātmyaḥ sa jayaty aparājitaḥ*

## v.3
To the forth-flowing drop and resonance, to the self made of pure nectar: bow; to endless light, to Śaṅkara, the milk-ocean.
The Sanskrit: *prasaradbindunādāya śuddhāmṛtamayātmane namo 'nantaprakāśāya śaṃkarakṣīrasindhave*

## v.4
You we hate, you we hymn, with you we counsel — O Ambikā’s lord: lightly won, how good — the whole world you have borne for us.
The Sanskrit: *dviṣmas tvāṃ tvāṃ stumas tubhyaṃ mantrayāmo 'mbikāpate ativāllabhyataḥ sādhu viśvaṅ no dhṛtavān asi*

## v.5
To the yoked withdrawn touch, to the full-streamed nectar, to the sky-and-māyā-formed: to the vast one, to Śambhu — bow.
The Sanskrit: *saṃhṛtasparśayogāya sampūrṇāmṛtasūtaye viyanmāyāsvarūpāya vibhave śambhave namaḥ*

## v.6
Among the split, not split; among the cut, wholly uncut — we bow to that all-common form of the supreme Lord.
The Sanskrit: *bhinneṣv api na bhinnaṃ yac chinneṣv achinnam eva ca namāmaḥ sarvasāmānyaṃ rūpaṃ tat pārameśvaram*

## v.7
Subtler than praṇava's upper half-mora — to the minute, and again to the great; vaster than the world-egg — to the qualified-by-qualitylessness, to Sthāṇu: bow.
The Sanskrit: *praṇavordhvārdhamātrāto 'py aṇave mahate punaḥ brahmāṇḍād api nairguṇyaguṇāya sthāṇave namaḥ*

## v.8
The world-egg-bearing one — of the sky-pervading, of the all-going, of the supreme-Lord-goose — that power, goose-like, we praise.
The Sanskrit: *brahmāṇḍagarbhiṇīṃ vyomavyāpinaḥ sarvatogateḥ parameśvarahaṃsasya śaktiṃ haṃsīm iva stumaḥ*

## v.9
With no provision of material, on no wall at all, they spread the world-picture — bow to him, the art-praised trident-bearer.
The Sanskrit: *nirupādānasambhāram abhittāv eva tanvate jagaccitraṃ namas tasmai kalāślāghyāya śūline*

## v.10
From māyā's watery belly fully drawn out and made stainless — Śiva-knowledge, milked of itself: give, come, O Hara-goose, to us.
The Sanskrit: *māyājalodarāt samyag uddhṛtya vimalīkṛtam śivajñānaṃ svato dugdhaṃ dehy ehi harahaṃsa naḥ*

## v.11
Even in the yoga of six-valid-means' delimitations and difference: to the undivided; to the one being of ultimate truth — an offering we bring, to Bhava, to you.
The Sanskrit: *ṣaṭpramāṇīparicchedabhedayoge 'py abhedine paramārthaikabhāvāya baliṃ yāmo bhavāya te*

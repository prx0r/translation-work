# Stavacintamani stav-p02 (seeing and staying) — L2 READ

## v.12
May we see the deep — circled by supreme light, its darkness wiped, lovely — and dwell within your cave.
The Sanskrit: *api paśyema gambhīrāṃ pareṇa jyotiṣābhitaḥ unmṛṣṭatamasaṃ ramyām antarbhava bhavadguhām*

## v.13
Homage to them even — who see the moon-crested lord, giver of supreme bliss, even in dream.
The Sanskrit: *namas tebhyo 'pi ye somakalākalitaśekharam nāthaṃ svapne 'pi paśyanti paramānandadāyinam*

## v.14
O Blessed — be! — the desire to contemplate your-ish being, skilled at cutting rebirth-fear: to whom is it not pleasing?
The Sanskrit: *bhagavan bhava bhāvatkaṃ bhāvaṃ bhāvayituṃ ruciḥ punarbhavabhayocchedadakṣā kasmai na rocate*

## v.15
Lifelong, O world-lord, let this be our task: staying, by your grace, with mind single-pointed on you alone.
The Sanskrit: *yāvajjīvaṃ jagannātha kartavyam idam astu naḥ tvatprasādāt tvadekāgramanaskatvena yā sthitiḥ*

## v.16
To the self made of thousand-branch-spread Veda and Āgama: bow; to Śambhu, the wish-tree of endless fruit.
The Sanskrit: *śākhāsahasravistīrṇavedāgamamayātmane namo 'nantaphalotpādakalpavṛkṣāya śambhave*

## v.17
Having entrusted speech, mind, body's acts in you, O Lord — become you-made, pair-free: may we be, at any time?
The Sanskrit: *vāṅmanaḥkāyakarmāṇi viniyojya tvayi prabho tvanmayībhūya nirdvandvāḥ kaccit syāmāpi karhicit*

## v.18
In the creation-and-dissolution appointments for each welfare of worlds — to the independently powerful, to the trident-bearer: bow.
The Sanskrit: *jagatāṃ sargasaṃhāratattaddhitaniyuktiṣu ananyāpekṣasāmarthyaśālini śūline namaḥ*

## v.19
Of the chief meditation-object past guṇa-yoga, of Dhūrjaṭi — even the name: for meditators, by meditations, what fruit by other supports?
The Sanskrit: *vyatītaguṇayogasya mukhyadhyeyasya dhūrjaṭeḥ nāmāpi dhyāyatāṃ dhyānaiḥ kim anyālambanaiḥ phalam*

## v.20
Bow, bow — 'to Śiva' thus said: by mantra-power taking refuge, praiseworthy they who strive to enjoy Śāmbhavī fortune.
The Sanskrit: *namo namaḥ śivāyeti mantrasāmarthyam āśritāḥ ślāghyās te śāmbhavīṃ bhūtim upabhoktuṃ ya udyatāḥ*

## v.21
What path by which unattainable? What speech by which unspoken? What meditation by which unmeditated? What — what are you not, O Lord?
The Sanskrit: *kaḥ panthā yena na prāpyaḥ kā ca vāṅ nocyase yayā kiṃ dhyānaṃ yena na dhyeyaḥ kiṃ vā kiṃ nāsi yat prabho*

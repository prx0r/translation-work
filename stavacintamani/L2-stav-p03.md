# Stavacintamani stav-p03 (worship and grace) — L2 READ

## v.22
Worshipped this, meditated this, pleased this — thus this rasa: by thousand streams, in you, my — O Bhava — may it grow.
The Sanskrit: *arcito 'yam ayaṃ dhyāta eṣa toṣita ity ayam rasaḥ srotaḥsahasreṇa tvayi me bhava vardhatām*

## v.23
Bow to the self abode of intellect-lords' garland; to the Lord, to Sthāṇu, to you — serpent-sacrifice-thread-wearing.
The Sanskrit: *namo niḥśeṣadhīpatrimālālayamayātmane nāthāya sthāṇave tubhyaṃ nāgayajñopavītine*

## v.24
Of ignorance-darkness, the one medicine: remembrance of you; O Bhava, by its giving let grace be done in me.
The Sanskrit: *ajñānatimirasyaikam auṣadhaṃ saṃsmṛtis tava bhava tattatpradānena prasādaḥ kriyatāṃ mayi*

## v.25
Bow to Īśa, fulfiller of all human ends; to-be-offered — the homage even — whose here, of the wise?
The Sanskrit: *nama īśāya niḥśeṣapuruṣārthaprasādhakaḥ praṇantavyaḥ praṇāmo 'pi yadīya iha dhīmatām*

## v.26
By the immersed in the terrible bhava-ocean, abode of suffering-fish — having obtained Śārva's devotion-jewel: what not conquered?
The Sanskrit: *magnair bhīme bhavāmbhodhau nilaye duḥkhayādasām bhakticintāmaṇiṃ śārvaṃ tataḥ prāpya na kiṃ jitam*

## v.27
Of unobstructed, pairless, steady knowledge-wealth: to-be-known you are indeed — who even are these who know you, O Dhūrjaṭi?
The Sanskrit: *nirāvaraṇanirdvandvaniścalajñānasampadām jñeyo 'si kila ke 'py ete ye tvāṃ jānanti dhūrjaṭe*

## v.28
Qualityless even — of quality-knowers the to-be-known, one conquers, the unborn; desireless even, by nature — who is desires' supreme fruit.
The Sanskrit: *nirguṇo 'pi guṇajñānāṃ jñeya eko jayaty ajaḥ niṣkāmo 'pi prakṛtyā yaḥ kāmanānāṃ paraṃ phalam*

## v.29
For Śrī-jewel-nectar gain, labored where not by which gods? That milk-ocean lordship: yours alone, natural, O Lord.
The Sanskrit: *śrīratnāmṛtalābhāya kliṣṭaṃ yatra na kaiḥ suraiḥ tat kṣīrodadam aiśvaryaṃ tavaiva sahajaṃ vibho*

## v.30
Bow — by devotion, for men's liberation, he blossoms: O Bhava, to you, O Protector — by remembrance and praise they give to Śambhu: bliss in bhava, in non-bhava.
The Sanskrit: *namo bhaktyā nṛṇāṃ muktyai bhavate bhava te 'vate smṛtyā nutyā ca dadate śambhave śaṃ bhave 'bhave*

## v.31
All-knowing, all-doing, all you are — thus, of knowers: what knowable, what karma, O lord — for endlessness, offered by you?
The Sanskrit: *sarvajñaḥ sarvakṛt sarvam asīti jñānaśālinām vedyaṃ kiṃ karma vā nātha nānantyāya tvayārpyate*

# Stavacintamani stav-p06 (refuge and waiting) — L2 READ

## v.51
Homage to Soma master — his longing drawn in, desire stretched long, the moon his crest.
The Sanskrit: *upasaṃhṛtakāmāya kāmāyatim atanvate avataṃsitasomāya somāya svāmine namaḥ*

## v.52
Crying 'incapable, what can I do?' with nowhere a resolve to stand on — Śaṅkara's grace that befriends all is where I hide.
The Sanskrit: *kim aśaktaḥ karomīti sarvatrānadhyavasyataḥ sarvānugrāhikā śaktiḥ śāṃkarī śaraṇaṃ mama*

## v.53
You stand past the strands, yourself proclaimed sheer wonder — in what corner, Bhava, could a second you be found?
The Sanskrit: *guṇātītasya nirdiṣṭaniḥśeṣātiśayātmanaḥ labhyate bhava kutrāṃśe paraḥ pratinidhis tava*

## v.54
Nondual you are, boundless self, O lord — and still your fathomless glamour fools us now.
The Sanskrit: *nirdvandve nirupādhau ca tvayy ātmani sati prabho vayaṃ vañcyāmahe 'dyāpi māyayāmeyayā tava*

## v.55
Powers from the minute on, undying lordship, becoming's end — Bhava, these are green shoots of the prayer-tree rooted in you.
The Sanskrit: *aṇimādiguṇāvāptiḥ sadaiśvaryaṃ bhavakṣayaḥ amī bhava bhavadbhaktikalpapādapapallavāḥ*

## v.56
No direction lacks you; all hours are your stuff — won already, yet when, lord, shall I win you? Speak.
The Sanskrit: *yā yā dik tatra na kvāsi sarvaḥ kālo bhavanmayaḥ iti labdho 'pi karhi tvaṃ lapsyase nātha kathyatām*

## v.57
Homage, Śambhu — lone tenant of lucid honest hearts, ash-white limbs, high swan.
The Sanskrit: *namaḥ prasannasadvṛttamānasaikanivāsine bhūribhūtisitāṅgāya mahāhaṃsāya śambhave*

## v.58
Scatter the surging gloom, burn every stick of becoming — be my lamp of waking, lord, my lamp of love.
The Sanskrit: *hṛtoddhatatamastāntiḥ pluṣṭāśeṣabhavendhanā tvadbodhadīpikā me 'stu nātha tvadbhaktidīpikā*

## v.59
You set the triple-world drama teeming with live seed — Hara, to strike the set, what playwright but you has skill?
The Sanskrit: *visṛṣṭānekasadbījagarbhaṃ trailokyanāṭakam prastāvya hara saṃhartuṃ tvattaḥ ko 'nyaḥ kaviḥ kṣamaḥ*

## v.60
Homage to turn the real and the fake to nought or to aught — to the bound and the free, who wears wasting and sway as one.
The Sanskrit: *namaḥ sadasatāṃ kartum asattvaṃ sattvam eva vā svatantrāyāsvatantrāya vyayaiśvaryaikaśāline*

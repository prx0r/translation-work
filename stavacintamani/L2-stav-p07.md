# Stavacintamani stav-p07 (praise and power) — L2 READ

## v.61
Any joy glimpsed here in all three worlds — a droplet of whom? To that sea-of-bliss god I bend.
The Sanskrit: *trailokye 'py atra yo yāvān ānandaḥ kaścid īkṣyate sa bindur yasya taṃ vande devam ānandasāgaram*

## v.62
Ah, lucky Brahmā and the rest — dropped all chatter, drunk with delight they mumble: Śiva, hail, hail.
The Sanskrit: *aho brahmādayo dhanyā ye vimuktānyasaṃkatham namo namaḥ śivāyeti japanty āhlādavihvalāḥ*

## v.63
Hail the hungerless apportioner of hunger without end; though the world never began, its feaster — Bhava, take my bow.
The Sanskrit: *niṣkāmāyāpi kāmānām anantānāṃ vidhāyine anāditve 'pi viśvasya bhoktre bhava namo 'stu te*

## v.64
The lord we laud, taproot of the triple world's start — who would beg a favour of the flawless?
The Sanskrit: *stumas tribhuvanārambhamūlaprakṛtim īśvaram lipseran nopakāraṃ ke yataḥ sampūrṇadharmaṇaḥ*

## v.65
Sunk in dire straits, muddied by floods of folly — let him be recalled, and minds grow lucid: Śiva we sing.
The Sanskrit: *mahatsv apy arthakṛcchreṣu mohaughamalinīkṛtāḥ smṛte yasmin prasīdanti matayas taṃ śivaṃ stumaḥ*

## v.66
Lord, from you only, unbroken sway holds here — at whose nod the three worlds fold and unfold.
The Sanskrit: *prabho bhavata eveha prabhuśaktir abhaṅgurā yadicchayā pratāyete trailokyasya layodayau*

## v.67
Aim filth at him and it turns sterling; aim merit elsewhere — O, nowhere else such alchemy.
The Sanskrit: *kukarmāpi yam uddiśya devaṃ syāt sukṛtaṃ param sukṛtasyāpi saukṛtyaṃ yato 'nyatra na so 'si bhoḥ*

## v.68
'Caught in our fist, caught in our sight — where will you slip from us?' — so, drunk on love's sap, the lucky sprint for the Matted One.
The Sanskrit: *eṣa muṣṭyā gṛhīto 'si dṛṣṭa eṣa kva yāsi naḥ iti bhaktirasādhmātā dhanyā dhāvanti dhūrjaṭim*

## v.69
You we laud — sung in verse, formula, chant, from light, from farther, farthest — your lore-body to be known — ah, sombre and fair.
The Sanskrit: *stumas tvām ṛgyajuḥsāmnāṃ śukrataḥ parataḥ param yasya vedātmikājñeyam aho gambhīrasundarī*

## v.70
Law and start and close of the world are you, high Lord — the host of duties marched out: unissued from you, whence could it march?
The Sanskrit: *vidhir ādis tathānto 'si viśvasya parameśvara dharmagrāmaḥ pravṛtto yas tvatto na sa kuto bhavet*

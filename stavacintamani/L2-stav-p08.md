# Stavacintamani stav-p08 (shelter and wonder) — L2 READ

## v.71
To you I bow — you conjure the churned stupor of becoming, then shatter it: bliss of gnosis, no pairs — O god, veiler and unveiler.
The Sanskrit: *namas te bhavasambhrāntabhrāntim udbhāvya bhindate jñānānandaṃ ca nirdvandvaṃ deva vṛtvā vivṛṇvate*

## v.72
What longings could sound her farthest shore — her who filches glamour in a single blink? Śiva, keep us.
The Sanskrit: *yasyāḥ prāpyeta paryantaviśeṣaḥ kair manorathaiḥ māyām ekanimeṣeṇa muṣṇaṃs tāṃ pātu naḥ śivaḥ*

## v.73
Freedom's hard road, wisdom's high crown, hunger's last ridge — their bearer, lord, is you.
The Sanskrit: *vairāgyasya gatiṃ gurvīṃ jñānasya paramāṃ śriyam naiḥspṛhyasya parāṃ koṭiṃ bibhratāṃ tvaṃ prabho prabhuḥ*

## v.74
Brahmā himself has Brahmā in you; what master owns no master in you, master of masters? Bliss of the world's bliss — who gauges how huge you are?
The Sanskrit: *brahmaṇo 'pi bhavān brahma kasya neśas tvam īśituḥ jagatkalyāṇakalyāṇaṃ kiyat tvam iti vetti kaḥ*

## v.75
Kinsmen avail what, comrades what, patrons what? My lord, in every strait it is you who hauls us off the sea of birth.
The Sanskrit: *kim anyair bandhubhiḥ kiṃ ca suhṛdbhiḥ svāmibhis tathā sarvasthāne mameśa tvaṃ ya uddhartā bhavārṇavāt*

## v.76
Hail the heaven-dew beads — hauled by discipline's force, strong to rinse folly, glamour, grime.
The Sanskrit: *jayanti mohamāyādimalasaṃkṣālanakṣamāḥ śaivayogabalākṛṣṭā divyapīyūṣavipruṣaḥ*

## v.77
The chant hymns his thought-kindling blaze — O may he urge our minds down the straight track, our lord.
The Sanskrit: *gāyatryā gīyate yasya dhiyāṃ tejaḥ pracodakam codayed api kaccin naḥ sa dhiyaḥ satpathe prabhuḥ*

## v.78
Eight-bodied one — in one body at least, grant us ground that holds; or glad-hearted, grant us everything.
The Sanskrit: *aṣṭamūrte kim ekasyām api mūrtau na naḥ sthitim śāśvatīṃ kuruṣe yad vā tuṣṭaḥ sarvaṃ kariṣyasi*

## v.79
Things prove their truth by work they do — but your bare name, lord, perfects through to release.
The Sanskrit: *vastutattvaṃ padārthānāṃ prāyeṇārthakriyākaram bhavatas tv īśa nāmāpi mokṣaparyantasiddhidam*

## v.80
Stage after stage you reset the painted world, ever and again — lord, at that craft of yours, what keen heart is not stunned?
The Sanskrit: *muhur muhur jagaccitrasy-ānyānyāṃ sthitim ūhitum śaktir yā te tayā nātha ko manasvī na vismitaḥ*

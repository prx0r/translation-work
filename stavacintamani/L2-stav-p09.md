# Stavacintamani stav-p09 (love and dread) — L2 READ

## v.81
Make the hard handy and sweeten the bitter — his memory alone is valiant: him we mind, the lover's bane.
The Sanskrit: *duṣkaraṃ sukarīkartuṃ duḥkhaṃ sukhayituṃ tathā ekavīrā smṛtir yasya taṃ smarāmaḥ smaradviṣam*

## v.82
Long live the hymns that sing the high lord — at whose bare name the great break out in bristling thrill.
The Sanskrit: *jayanti gītayo yāsāṃ sa geyaḥ parameśvaraḥ yannāmnāpi mahātmānaḥ kīryante pulakāṅkuraiḥ*

## v.83
Were you as you are, O Becoming, in the beyond — your hosts of power marshalled to launch and close the triple world.
The Sanskrit: *bhavān iva bhavān eva bhaved yadi paraṃ bhava svaśaktivyūhasaṃvyūḍhatrailokyārambhasaṃhṛtiḥ*

## v.84
Spell you are, spell-fit you are, spell-master — what second wizard besides you? Come, hand me that spell, lord, that I turn spellbound to you.
The Sanskrit: *mantro 'si mantraṇīyo 'si mantrī tvattaḥ kuto 'paraḥ sa mahyaṃ dehi taṃ mantraṃ tvanmantraḥ syāṃ yathā prabho*

## v.85
Light-shaped, vow-true — the self in you is mine, am I: yet called a drifter — O lord, what dream is this, and how in you?
The Sanskrit: *bhārūpaḥ satyasaṃkalpas tvam ātmā yasya so 'py aham saṃsārīti kim īśaiṣa svapnaḥ so 'pi kutas tvayi*

## v.86
Unsnapped, unchurlish, single, sound — in you, lord, stands the fortune of men who stake their harvest on you.
The Sanskrit: *tad abhaṅgi tad agrāmyaṃ tad ekam upapattimat tvayi karmaphalanyāsakṛtām aiśvaryam īśa yat*

## v.87
Strong to crush what ills, to deal what boons — or not? Here he is, our darling, god of gods on the bull-flag.
The Sanskrit: *kṣamaḥ kāṃ nāpadaṃ hantuṃ kāṃ dātuṃ sampadaṃ na vā yo 'sau sa dayito 'smākaṃ devadevo vṛṣadhvajaḥ*

## v.88
For god-sight filmed with inborn stain, lord, your love is the last salve that rinses clean.
The Sanskrit: *māyāmayamalāndhasya divyasya jñānacakṣuṣaḥ nirmalīkaraṇe nātha tvadbhaktiḥ paramāñjanam*

## v.89
Fear-free, bliss-full, one, unwasting — plant that foothold in me, come, god — quick, why linger?
The Sanskrit: *nirbhayaṃ yad yad ānandamayam ekaṃ yad avyayam padaṃ dehy ehi me deva tūrṇaṃ tat kiṃ pratīkṣase*

## v.90
Ah, bottomless and grim, the drowning sea of rounds; ah, to ferry it — some high one — great Master.
The Sanskrit: *aho nisargagambhīro ghoraḥ saṃsārasāgaraḥ aho tattaraṇopāyaḥ paraḥ ko 'pi maheśvaraḥ*

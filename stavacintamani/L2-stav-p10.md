# Stavacintamani stav-p10 (capability and gift) — L2 READ

## v.91
I bow to you — death's ender, love's crusher, Gaṅgā crowned on the pate, worker of meet right.
The Sanskrit: *namaḥ kṛtakṛtāntānta tubhyaṃ madanamardine mastakanyastagaṅgāya yathāyuktārthakāriṇe*

## v.92
High past sway and gnosis, past dispassion and writs stands what men pray for — cut off from you, lord, what loftier haven for the praying?
The Sanskrit: *aiśvaryajñānavairāgyadharmebhyo 'py upari sthitim nātha prārthayamānānāṃ tvad ṛte kā parā gatiḥ*

## v.93
Should you not wish it, Śambhu, who could crook a straw? Graced by your wish — who would shirk Brahmā's burden?
The Sanskrit: *tvayy anicchati kaḥ śambho śaktaḥ kubjayituṃ tṛṇam tvadicchānugṛhītas tu vahed brāhmīṃ dhuraṃ na kaḥ*

## v.94
The brain-rich, crests high with gemmy wreaths from bending to Hara — to what Highest would they kneel, whom would they not incline?
The Sanskrit: *harapraṇatimāṇikyamukuṭotkaṭamastakāḥ nameyuḥ kaṃ paraṃ kaṃ vā namayeyur na dhīdhanāḥ*

## v.95
Quiverless, quit of every stray, a mere of sweet — O let me settle in mid-sea of your knowing, Matted One.
The Sanskrit: *sarvavibhramanirmokaniṣkampam amṛtahradam bhavajjñānāmbudher madhyam adhyāsīyāpi dhūrjaṭe*

## v.96
A marvel — eyed askance though it be, strayed into fancy though it be — the last fruit, lord, you grant entire.
The Sanskrit: *citraṃ yac citradṛṣṭo 'pi manorathagato 'pi vā paramārthaphalaṃ nātha paripūrṇaṃ prayacchasi*

## v.97
What strong soul outtops you? What stringless soul outpeaks you? So, lord, we sing — bless you? curse you? We cannot say.
The Sanskrit: *ko guṇair adhikas tvattas tvattaḥ ko nirguṇo 'dhikaḥ iti nātha numaḥ kiṃ tvāṃ kiṃ nindāmo na manmahe*

## v.98
Though we hymn but the spate of sweet — whose grace fruits in you — to sip it, what stranger is fit? To serve it round — O world-king, who then?
The Sanskrit: *kīrtane 'py amṛtaughasya yatprasatteḥ phalaṃ tava tat pātum api ko 'nyo 'laṃ kim u dātuṃ jagatpate*

## v.99
Out of the hoard where every coveted sheaf ripens — from you I beg one thing, lord, in all wise: love of you solely to win.
The Sanskrit: *niḥśeṣaprārthanīyārthasārthasiddhinidhānataḥ tvattas tvadbhaktim evāptuṃ prārthaye nātha sarvathā*

## v.100
Hail, triple world's king, hail to you, Becoming — hailed by your court — hail the unbarred might that bestows triple kingship.
The Sanskrit: *namas trailokyanāthāya tubhyaṃ bhava bhavajjuṣām trilokīnāthatādānanirvināyakaśaktaye*

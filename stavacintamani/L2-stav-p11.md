# Stavacintamani stav-p11 (dependence and rest) — L2 READ

## v.101
When men doubt what uproots all ache — master, resolved 'that art thou' — who has failed to flee to you?
The Sanskrit: *niḥśeṣakleśahānasya hetuḥ ka iti saṃśaye svāmin so 'sīti niścitya kas tvāṃ na śaraṇaṃ gataḥ*

## v.102
Tasted my fill and shed birth's mirage, I shall win the highest seat — so hope runs here: bright may it blaze for Śambhu's lover.
The Sanskrit: *bhuktvā bhogān bhavabhrāntiṃ hitvā lapsye paraṃ padam ity āśaṃseha śobheta śambhau bhaktimataḥ param*

## v.103
Lord, in dreams too, do or utter I foul or fair — proud to hang on you — at home in all places I rest glad.
The Sanskrit: *nātha svapne 'pi yat kuryāṃ brūyāṃ vā sādhv asādhu vā tvadadhīnatvadarpeṇa sarvatrātrāsmi nirvṛtaḥ*

## v.104
What lamp of lamps — there, where mind races in your dwelling — mind's lord, could murk touch it? As soon, I deem, as a barren dame's grandson.
The Sanskrit: *jyotiṣām api yaj jyotis tatra tvaddhāmni dhāvataḥ cittasyeśa tamaḥsparśo manye vandhyātmajānujaḥ*

## v.105
Methinks one foot already on kind release's road stands he whose hankering runs: 'Śiva I'll honour' — even this.
The Sanskrit: *manye nyastapadaḥ so 'pi kṣemye mokṣasya vartmani manorathaḥ sthito yasya seviṣye śivam ity ayam*

## v.106
Through keeping, kindling, killing, in this triple world's errands — yours only, lord, the might — unbound — you watch the weave.
The Sanskrit: *sthityutpattilayair lokatrayasyopakriyāsv iha ekaiveśa bhavacchaktiḥ svatantraṃ tantram īkṣase*

## v.107
Saved in these three worlds, unscorched by triple smart — hail him, hail you — you, the dead-quiet mere of sweet.
The Sanskrit: *trilokyām iha kas trātas tritāpyā nopatāpitaḥ tasmai namo 'stu te yas tvaṃ tannirvāṇāmṛtahradaḥ*

## v.108
Feigned love of you still ripens true — were it straight, O what crop? Pronounce it, you.
The Sanskrit: *kṛtrimāpi bhavadbhaktir akṛtrimaphalodayā niśchadmā ced bhaved eṣā kiṃphaleti tvayocyatām*

## v.109
The eye that sights you, the road that attains you — that yield, Unborn, springs from the gossip-grove grown of you.
The Sanskrit: *tac cakṣur īkṣyase yena sā gatir gamyase yayā phalaṃ tad aja jātaṃ yat tvatkathākalpapādapāt*

## v.110
Best past the good, this poise on high in you — to sweep its hindrances, lord, you are my hold.
The Sanskrit: *śreyasā śreya evaitad upari tvayi yā sthitiḥ tadantarāyahṛtaye tvam īśa śaraṇaṃ mama*

# Stavacintamani stav-p12 (freshness and seal) — L2 READ

## v.111
Ah, honeyed past honey, the savour of serving Śarva hoped — in what tick of time does it not turn ever-new?
The Sanskrit: *aho svādutamaḥ śarvasevāśaṃsāsudhārasaḥ kutra kālakalāmātre na yo navanavāyate*

## v.112
Ever, unwearied, teeming the triple world with hundredfold fancies — yet single, seamless, lords it — Unborn.
The Sanskrit: *muhur muhur aviśrāntas trailokyaṃ kalpanāśataiḥ kalpayann api ko 'py eko nirvikalpo jayaty ajaḥ*

## v.113
By lore's lamp that licks the birth-greased wick of wonts — god, O when shall I stand in your presence, prithee?
The Sanskrit: *malatailāktasaṃsāravāsanāvartidāhinā jñānadīpena deva tvāṃ kadā nu syām upasthitaḥ*

## v.114
Grant one blinking's room with faults outworn to lodge within the breast — then, Śambhu, what feat past your doing?
The Sanskrit: *nimeṣam api yady ekaṃ kṣīṇadoṣe kariṣyasi padaṃ citte tadā śambho kiṃ na sampādayiṣyasi*

## v.115
'Blest am I, quits am I, big am I' — firm would that fancy stand, grounded — his whom you ground, lord.
The Sanskrit: *dhanyo 'smi kṛtakṛtyo 'smi mahān asmīti bhāvanā bhavet sālambanā tasya yas tvadālambanaḥ prabho*

## v.116
Luck and muck alike you author yourself — yet love of you mothers luck only, solely, lord.
The Sanskrit: *śubhāśubhasya sarvasya svayaṃ kartā bhavān api bhavadbhaktis tu jananī śubhasyaiveśa kevalam*

## v.117
Mind pellucid, master — step you in, or at your stepping does it clear? So dangles man.
The Sanskrit: *prasanne manasi svāmin kiṃ tvaṃ niviśase kim u tvatpraveśāt prasīdet tad iti dolāyate janaḥ*

## v.118
Nay, here stands sure — your oversight and yours only: mind's favour, master, is fulfilment, the farthest seat.
The Sanskrit: *niścayaḥ punar eṣo 'tra tvadadhiṣṭhānam eva hi prasādo manasaḥ svāmin sā siddhis tat paraṃ padam*

## v.119
Word and wit, work and this frame of mine, lord — grace them to gleam with love of you and nought beside.
The Sanskrit: *vacaś cetaś ca kāryaṃ ca śarīraṃ mama yat prabho tvatprasādena tad bhūyād bhavadbhāvaikabhūṣaṇam*

## v.120
This praise-gem, heavy with granted longings, Lakṣmī's lodging of love — for Śambhu wrought Bhaṭṭa Nārāyaṇa.
The Sanskrit: *stavacintāmaṇiṃ bhūrimanorathaphalapradam bhaktilakṣmyālayaṃ śambho bhaṭṭanārāyaṇo vyadhāt*

# Stavacintāmaṇi — L2 READ (Dyczkowski-mode)

## stav-p01–p03 — the supreme Lord and worship
**SUMMARY.** Yaḥ sphītaḥ śrīdayābodhaparamānandasampadā vidyoddyotitamāhātmyaḥ sa jayaty aparājitaḥ — He who is full of abundant bliss-wealth from Śrī's knowledge, whose greatness shines by knowledge, He is victorious, unconquered. Prasaradbindunādāya śuddhāmṛtamayātmane namo 'nantaprakāśāya śaṃkarakṣīrasindhave — to Him whose self is pure nectar of spreading bindu-nāda, homage to unlimited light, Śaṃkara milk-ocean. Dviṣmas tvāṃ tvāṃ stumas tubhyaṃ mantrayāmo 'mbikāpate ativāllabhyataḥ sādhu viśvaṅ no dhṛtavān asi — we hate you, we praise you, we invoke you, Lord of Ambikā; by great accessibility you have held the world. Saṃhṛtasparśayogāya sampūrṇāmṛt... Api paśyema gambhīrāṃ pareṇa jyotiṣābhitaḥ unmṛṣṭatamasaṃ ramyām antarbhava bhavadguhām — may we see the deep, by supreme light, cleared darkness, lovely, inside your cave. Namaste 'pi ye somakalākalitaśekharam nāthaṃ svapne 'pi paśyanti paramānandadāyinam — homage to those who, with moon-crescent crown, see the Lord even in dream, giver of supreme bliss. Bhagavan bhava bhāvatkaṃ bhāvaṃ bhāvayituṃ ruciḥ punarbhavabhayocchedadakṣā kasmai na rocate — Lord, may desire arise to cultivate your state; who would not delight in skill at cutting rebirth-fear. Yāvajjīvaṃ jagannātha kartavya... Arcito 'yam ayaṃ dhyāta eṣa toṣita ity ayam rasaḥ srotaḥsahasreṇa tvayi me bhava vardhatām — this is worshipped, this is meditated, this is pleased; may this rasa grow in you by thousand streams. Namo niḥśeṣadhīpatrimālālayamayātmane nāthāya sthāṇave tubhyaṃ nāgayajñopavītine — homage to Him whose self is abode of complete knowledge-garland, to Lord, to Sthāṇu, to you with serpent-sacrificial-thread. Ajñānatimirasyaikam auṣadhaṃ saṃsmṛtis tava bhava tattatpradānena prasādaḥ kriyatāṃ mayi — the one medicine for darkness of ignorance is remembrance of you; by this and that giving, grace be done to me. Nama īśāya niḥśeṣapuruṣārthaprasādhakaḥ — homage to Īśa, accomplisher of all human ends.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes Śiva as supreme Lord (unconquered, bliss, pure nectar, unlimited light, accessible) and worship (remembrance as medicine for ignorance, grace accomplishes all ends).

**KEY TERMS.** aparājita (unconquered), anantaprakāśa (unlimited light), ativāllabhya (accessibility), paramānanda (supreme bliss), punarbhava (rebirth), auṣadha (medicine), prasāda (grace).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First three passages; sets the doctrinal frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in three steps. (1) The supreme Lord: full of bliss-wealth from Śrī's knowledge, greatness shines by knowledge, victorious unconquered; pure nectar of spreading bindu-nāda; unlimited light, Śaṃkara milk-ocean; we hate/praise/invoke you, Lord of Ambikā; by great accessibility you held the world. (2) Devotion's fruit: may we see the deep by supreme light; cleared darkness, lovely cave; homage to those with moon-crescent crown who see Lord even in dream; Lord, may desire arise to cultivate your state; who would not delight in cutting rebirth-fear; as long as life, duty. (3) Worship: worshipped/meditated/pleased; rasa grow by thousand streams; homage to abode of complete knowledge-garland; Lord, Sthāṇu, serpent-thread; remembrance = medicine for ignorance; grace be done; homage to Īśa, accomplisher of all ends.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.2–1.31.

## stav-p04–p06 — power, grace, sovereignty
**SUMMARY.** Icchāyā eva yasyeyat phalaṃ lokatrayātmakam tasya te nātha kāryāṇāṃ ko vetti kiyatī gatiḥ — whose desire alone has fruit, three-world-natured; Lord, who knows the extent of your works. Brahmādayo 'pi tad yasya karmasopānamālayā upary upari dhāvanti labdhuṃ dhāma namāmi tam — even Brahmā etc. by whose karma-ladder run up and up to gain abode; I bow to Him. Ayam brahmā mahendro 'yaṃ sūryācandramasāv imau iti śaktilatā yasya puṣpitā pātv asau bhavaḥ — this is Brahmā, this is Mahendra, these are sun and moon; whose power-vine is blossomed; may Bhava protect. Bhramo na labhyate yasya bhr... Namo nirupakāryāya trailokyaikopakāriṇe sarvasya spṛhaṇīyāya niḥspṛhāya kapardine — homage to Him who needs no service, sole benefactor of three worlds, desirable by all, desireless, with matted hair. Aho kṣetrajñatā seyaṃ kāryāya mahate satām yayānantaphalāṃ bhaktiṃ vapanti tvayy amī prabho — oh, this knower-ness for great work of the good, by which they sow devotion with endless fruit in you, Lord. Mahatīyam aho māyā tava māyin yayāvṛtaḥ tvaddhyānanidhilābhe 'pi mugdho lokaḥ ślathāyate — great, oh, your Māyā, Māyin, by which, even on gaining treasure of meditation on you, the deluded world is slack. Ārambhe bhava sarvatra karma vā karaṇ... Upasaṃhṛtakāmāya kāmāyatim atanvate avataṃsitasomāya somāya svāmine namaḥ — to Him who has withdrawn desire, not extending desire's course, crowned with moon, to Soma, to Lord, homage. Kim aśaktaḥ karomīti sarvatrānadhyavasyataḥ sarvānugrāhikā śaktiḥ śāṃkarī śaraṇaṃ mama — thinking "what can I, powerless, do", all-beneficent Śāṃkarī power is my refuge. Guṇātītasya nirdiṣṭaniḥśeṣātiśayātmanaḥ labhyate bhava kutrāṃśe paraḥ pratinidhis tava — of Him beyond qualities, whose essence is all-excellence, where, Bhava, is a peer or substitute for you. Nirdvandve nirupādhau ca tvayy ātmani sati prabho va...

**FUNCTION.** Establishes Śiva's power (desire with three-world fruit, Brahmā etc. run up karma-ladder), grace (needs no service, sole benefactor; Māyā deludes), sovereignty (withdraws desire, Śāṃkarī power is refuge, no peer).

**KEY TERMS.** icchā (desire), lokatraya (three worlds), nirupakārya (needing no service), māyā (Māyā), upasaṃhṛtakāma (withdrawn desire), śāṃkarī (Śāṃkarī power), nirdvandva (without duality).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p01–p03 (supreme Lord/worship); p04–p06 establish power/grace/sovereignty.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in three steps. (1) Power: whose desire alone has fruit (three-world); Lord, who knows extent of works; Brahmā etc. run up karma-ladder to gain abode; this is Brahmā, Mahendra, sun, moon; power-vine blossomed; may Bhava protect. (2) Grace: homage to Him needing no service, sole benefactor of three worlds, desirable by all, desireless, matted hair; oh, knower-ness for great work; sow devotion with endless fruit; great Māyā, Māyin; deluded world slack even on gaining meditation-treasure. (3) Sovereignty: withdrawn desire, not extending desire's course; crowned with moon; thinking "what can I powerless do", Śāṃkarī power is refuge; beyond qualities, all-excellence; where peer/substitute; without duality, without condition.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.32–1.60.

## stav-p07–p09 — bliss, protection, remembrance
**SUMMARY.** Trailokye 'py atra yo yāvān ānandaḥ kaścid īkṣyate sa bindur yasya taṃ vande devam ānandasāgaram — whatever bliss is seen in three worlds, that is a drop of His; I bow to the god, ocean of bliss. Aho brahmādayo dhanyā ye vimuktānyasaṃkatham namo namaḥ śivāyeti japanty āhlādavihvalāḥ — oh, Brahmā etc. are fortunate, freed from other-talk, chanting "homage to Śiva", overwhelmed with delight. Niṣkāmāyāpi kāmānām anantānāṃ vidhāyine anāditve 'pi viśvasya bhoktre bhava namo 'stu te — to Him who bestows endless desires even to the desireless, even though beginningless, enjoyer of the world; homage to you, Bhava. Stumas tribhuvanārambhamūl... Namaste bhavasambhrāntabhrāntim udbhāvya bhindate jñānānandaṃ ca nirdvandvaṃ deva vṛtvā vivṛṇvate — homage to you who, manifesting the delusion of world-confusion, shatters it, and covering, reveals knowledge-bliss without duality, god. Yasyāḥ prāpyeta paryantaviśeṣaḥ kair manorathaiḥ māyām ekanimeṣeṇa muṣṇaṃs tāṃ pātu naḥ śivaḥ — by what desires could the limit-distinction of that be reached; stealing Māyā in one blink, may Śiva protect us. Vairāgyasya gatiṃ gurvīṃ jñānasya paramāṃ śriyam naiḥspṛhyasya parāṃ koṭiṃ bibhratāṃ tvaṃ prabho prabhuḥ — the weighty course of dispassion, the supreme glory of knowledge, to those bearing the peak of desirelessness, you are Lord, Lord. Br... Duṣkaraṃ sukarīkartuṃ duḥkhaṃ sukhayituṃ tathā ekavīrā smṛtir yasya taṃ smarāmaḥ smaradviṣam — to make the difficult easy, to make suffering pleasant, whose memory is a single hero; we remember the enemy of Smara. Jayanti gītayo yāsāṃ sa geyaḥ parameśvaraḥ yannāmnāpi mahātmānaḥ kīryante pulakāṅkuraiḥ — victorious are the songs whose singer is the supreme Lord; by whose name alone great souls are thrilled with horripilation. Bhavān iva bhavān eva bhaved yadi paraṃ bhava svaśaktivyūhasaṃvyūḍhatrailokyārambhasaṃhṛtiḥ — if you, Bhava, were only like Bhava, supreme Bhava, arrangement of own-powers, three-world-beginning-dissolution. Mantro 'si mantraṇīyo 'si m...

**FUNCTION.** Establishes bliss (three-world bliss = drop of Śiva's ocean), protection (shatters world-confusion, steals Māyā in one blink), remembrance (memory = single hero, name thrills).

**KEY TERMS.** ānandasāgara (ocean of bliss), māyāmuṣ (stealing Māyā), ekavīra (single hero), pulakāṅkura (horripilation), svaśaktivyūha (own-power arrangement).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p04–p06 (power/grace/sovereignty); p07–p09 establish bliss/protection/remembrance.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in three steps. (1) Bliss: three-world bliss = drop of Śiva's ocean; Brahmā etc. fortunate, freed from other-talk, chanting "Śiva" with delight; bestows endless desires even to desireless; beginningless, enjoyer of world. (2) Protection: shatters world-confusion, reveals knowledge-bliss without duality; by what desires could limit be reached; steals Māyā in one blink, may Śiva protect; weighty course of dispassion, supreme glory of knowledge; to those bearing peak of desirelessness, you are Lord. (3) Remembrance: makes difficult easy, suffering pleasant; memory = single hero; we remember enemy of Smara; victorious songs whose singer is supreme Lord; name thrills great souls; if you were only like Bhava; arrangement of own-powers; you are mantra.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

## stav-p10–p12 — sovereignty (continued) and close
**SUMMARY.** Namaḥ kṛtakṛtāntānta tubhyaṃ madanamardine mastakanyastagaṅgāya yathāyuktārthakāriṇe — homage to you, end of the end-maker, crusher of Kāma, with Gaṅgā on head, doer of fitting meanings. Aiśvaryajñānavairāgyadharmebhyo 'py upari sthitim nātha prārthayamānānāṃ tvad ṛte kā parā gatiḥ — standing above even sovereignty-knowledge-dispassion-dharma; Lord, for those praying, what other goal besides you. Tvayy anicchati kaḥ śambho śaktaḥ kubjayituṃ tṛṇam tvadicchānugṛhītas tu vahed brāhmīṃ dhuraṃ na kaḥ — when you, Śambhu, do not wish, who can bend even a blade of grass; but one graced by your wish would bear the Brahmī burden, who not. Harapraṇatimāṇikya... Niḥśeṣakleśahānasya hetuḥ ka iti saṃśaye svāmin so 'sīti niścitya kas tvāṃ na śaraṇaṃ gataḥ — in doubt, who is cause of complete removal of affliction; Lord, you are that; having decided, who would not take refuge in you. Bhuktvā bhogān bhavabhrāntiṃ hitvā lapsye paraṃ padam ity āśaṃseha śobheta śambhau bhaktimataḥ param — having enjoyed pleasures, abandoning world-delusion, I shall gain supreme state; thus hoping, may it be auspicious for the devotee of Śambhu. Nātha svapne 'pi yat kuryāṃ brūyāṃ vā sādhv asādhu vā tvadadhīnatvadarpeṇa sarvatrātrāsmi nirvṛtaḥ — Lord, whatever I do or say in dream, good or bad, by pride of dependence on you, everywhere here I am content. Jyotiṣām... Aho svādutamaḥ śarvasevāśaṃsāsudhārasaḥ kutra kālakalāmātre na yo navanavāyate — oh, most sweet is the nectar of desire for service to Śarva; where, in any moment, does it not become new. Muhur muhur aviśrāntas trailokyaṃ kalpanāśataiḥ kalpayann api ko 'py eko nirvikalpo jayaty ajaḥ — again and again tirelessly, three worlds by hundred thoughts, creating, yet one, thought-free, victorious, unborn. Malatailāktasaṃsāravāsanāvartidāhinā jñānadīpena deva tvāṃ kadā nu syām upasthitaḥ — by the lamp of knowledge, burning the oil of saṃsāra-trace, god, when shall I be present to you. Nimeṣam api yady ekaṃ kṣīṇadoṣe kariṣyasi...

**FUNCTION.** Establishes sovereignty (continued, above even sovereignty-knowledge-dispassion-dharma), devotee's state (refuge, contentment in dependence), close (nectar of service, thought-free creation, lamp of knowledge).

**KEY TERMS.** kṛtakṛtānta (end of end-maker), aiśvarya (sovereignty), kleśahāna (affliction-removal), paraṃ pada (supreme state), nirvṛta (content), śarvasevā (service to Śarva), nirvikalpa (thought-free), jñānadīpa (lamp of knowledge).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Closes the stotra; follows p07–p09 (bliss/protection/remembrance).

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in three steps. (1) Sovereignty (continued): homage to end of end-maker, crusher of Kāma, Gaṅgā on head; standing above even sovereignty-knowledge-dispassion-dharma; when you do not wish, who can bend grass; one graced by your wish bears Brahmī burden. (2) Devotee's state: cause of affliction-removal; Lord, you are that; who would not take refuge; having enjoyed pleasures, abandoning world-delusion, gain supreme state; whatever in dream, good or bad, by pride of dependence, content everywhere. (3) Close: most sweet nectar of desire for service to Śarva; where in any moment not new; tirelessly three worlds by hundred thoughts; yet one, thought-free, victorious, unborn; lamp of knowledge burning oil of saṃsāra-trace; when shall I be present to you.

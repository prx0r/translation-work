# L200 — Stavacintāmaṇi (120 verses, 12 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Stavacintāmaṇi (120 verses) is a devotional stotra by Bhaṭṭanārāyaṇa (9th c., Kashmir). It praises Śiva as supreme Lord: unconquered, full of bliss, pure nectar, unlimited light, accessible. It treats worship (remembrance as medicine for ignorance, grace accomplishes all ends), power (desire with three-world fruit, Brahmā etc. run up karma-ladder), grace (needs no service, sole benefactor; Māyā deludes), sovereignty (withdraws desire, Śāṃkarī power is refuge, no peer), bliss (three-world bliss = drop of Śiva's ocean), protection (shatters world-confusion, steals Māyā in one blink), remembrance (memory = single hero, name thrills), devotee's state (refuge, contentment in dependence), and closes with nectar of service to Śarva, thought-free creation, lamp of knowledge.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: Śiva is supreme Lord: unconquered, full of bliss, pure nectar, unlimited light, accessible.
- C2: Worship = remembrance of Śiva (medicine for ignorance); grace accomplishes all ends.
- C3: Power = desire with three-world fruit; Brahmā etc. run up karma-ladder to gain abode.
- C4: Grace = Śiva needs no service, sole benefactor; Māyā deludes even meditation-treasure gainers.
- C5: Sovereignty = withdraws desire, Śāṃkarī power is refuge, no peer/substitute.
- C6: Bliss = three-world bliss is a drop of Śiva's ocean; Brahmā etc. fortunate, chanting "Śiva".
- C7: Protection = shatters world-confusion, steals Māyā in one blink.
- C8: Remembrance = makes difficult easy, suffering pleasant; memory = single hero; name thrills.
- C9: Devotee's state = taking refuge, abandoning world-delusion, contentment in dependence.
- C10: Close = nectar of service to Śarva, thought-free creation, lamp of knowledge burning saṃsāra-trace.

## 3. What the text rejects
- R1: Any Lord other than Śiva.
- R2: Worship without remembrance.
- R3: Power without desire (desire is the fruit-giver).
- R4: Grace as conditional (Śiva needs no service).
- R5: Sovereignty with a peer/substitute.
- R6: Bliss as self-generated (it's Śiva's ocean).
- R7: Protection without stealing Māyā.
- R8: Remembrance without making difficult easy.
- R9: Devotee's state without refuge.
- R10: Close without nectar of service.

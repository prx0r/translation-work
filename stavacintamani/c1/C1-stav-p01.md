# C1 — stav-p01 (invocation and offering)

## SUMMARY

The undefeated conquers in auspicious wealth. Śaṅkara the milk-ocean receives homage; hatred and hymn alike reach Ambikā's husband holding all. Vast Śambhu wears withdrawn touch and full nectar; the all-common Lord stays whole in fracture. Sthāṇu spans mote and cosmos; the goose-power spreads wings over the egg; the picture blooms wall-less. Wisdom milked stainless from illusion's waters is begged for; the offering rests with undivided Bhava, one with the ultimate real.

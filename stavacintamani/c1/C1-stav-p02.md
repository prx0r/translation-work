# C1 — stav-p02 (seeing and staying)

## SUMMARY

The deep calls to be seen and dwelt in; those who glimpse the moon-crested even asleep earn homage. Hunger to brood on being severs rebirth dread; lifelong one-pointedness is vowed. The Veda-bodied granter bends low; handing over deed, speech, mind, the lover turns you-stuffed and quarrel-free. Alone-working spear-power orders birth and close; one Name outweighs all props. Mantra-sheltered strivers win praise; road, utterance, trance alike end in the Lord.

# C1 — stav-p03 (worship and grace)

## SUMMARY

Tended worship swells thousand-streamed. One recall heals blackout; the bow itself is already his. Fished from terror waters, love's gem buys everything; the open secret stays hid from most. Want-free and trait-free, the Unborn wins and rewards. Sea-churned riches sit easy on him only; he blooms for release, shields in life and lifelessness, and, all-wise, all-working, all, takes all in endless due.

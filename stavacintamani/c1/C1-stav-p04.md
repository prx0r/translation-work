# C1 — stav-p04 (will and teaching)

## SUMMARY

Bare will bears triple fruit past measure; gods shinny the deed-ladder toward one abode. The power-vine flowers into deities and shields; the stainless one stands unguessed and unreached, hymned in act, memory, vision, touch, arrival. Pride apes union while missing the self; then duty pondered drops into blissful rest. Fineness holds all yet hides from seers; tongues net nothing till you name yourself speakable. So the plea: which road, which rite, which ken reveals you — teach and favour.

# C1 — stav-p05 (devotion and grace)

## SUMMARY

The helper wants nothing and feeds all, craved by each, craving none. Clear farmers plant undying affection; the conjuring veil makes even finders drowse. Every start permits, yet only one hand stays free; triple worlds drown till that hand or its kindness hauls out. Vice pointed at you inverts to virtue; clutching at you hallows clutching. One rite of minding you titles grub and godhead alike for freedom; alpha, fray, omega run on mind's lane yours. Beside your love-sap, all relish thins.

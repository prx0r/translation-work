# C1 — stav-p06 (refuge and waiting)

## SUMMARY

Capped hunger bows to the crescent master; empty hands take shelter in all-helping force. Peerless past qualities, the self fools its own through fathomless sheen; still devotion fruits into supernormal bloom, endless sway, birth's close. Won already, the prize stays future — say when; the pellucid-breasted swan keeps house. Two flames are sued, knowing and loving; the worldwright who raised the boards alone can ring them down. Being and non-being trade places at the nod of the boundless bound one.

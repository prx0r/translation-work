# C1 — stav-p07 (praise and power)

## SUMMARY

All delight runs dropwise from one sea. Luckiest throats abandon gossip for the Name mumbled glad-drunk. Hungerless, he deals out hungers and dines though the cosmos never started; faultless root, he wants no hire, yet recall lights skulls that praise. Single sway nods worlds down and up; mud slung his way gilds. Fists and eyes seize him — unseizable — till love-besotted sprinters tackle the Tanglehead. Past metre, mantra, moonstuff, past pastness, his lore-body glows grave and gracious; code, dawn, dusk of the world are him, and duty's columns issue from nowhere else.

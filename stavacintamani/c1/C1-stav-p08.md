# C1 — stav-p08 (shelter and wonder)

## SUMMARY

Conjure and crack the churned daze: joy-knowledge twinless. Blink-snatch the veil — shield us. Shoulder the triple crown of quit-smoke, clear wit, spent craving: mastery. Even Firstborn finds his First in you; no chief unchiefs you; bliss upon bliss who shall plumb? Blood and bond weigh nil; one Lifter works all harbours. Discipline-drawn dew outcleanses dumps of dark. Gāyatrī's glow goads wits true; eight limbs entreated yield one foothold or the fair. Bare naming outdoes doing; the repainted play stuns each fine mind.

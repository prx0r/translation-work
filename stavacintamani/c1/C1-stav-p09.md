# C1 — stav-p09 (love and dread)

## SUMMARY

One recall steadies the staggering and sugars gall. Anthems to the Tallest raise gooseflesh at syllable one. Be as you be beyond: battalions of force unfurl and furl the threefold. Charm, charm-worthy, charmer — deal the charm that charms me yours. Glow-cast, pledge-fast — myself in you, yet castaway in dream: what sleep lodges in waking? Whole, seemly, sole, sane: your settled fortune funds the trusting. Dearly he smites sorrows and signs fortunes from the horned banner. Last balm of love films clear the filmed god-eye. Dread-free delight, solo, deathless: step in now, tarry never. Fathomless and fearsome the swim; ferry it — Lofty, someone, Master.

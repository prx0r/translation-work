# C1 — stav-p10 (capability and gift)

## SUMMARY

Bunter of Yama, bruiser of Smara, river-crowned, righter. Prayer's perch towers past rule, ken, calm, canon; sundered from you, no shelter stands higher. Unbid, none buckles sedge; bid, all haul heaven's draught. Jewel-pated sages kneel to none Highest save Hara's. Still mere of honey mid mind's main sued for lodgement. Sidelong seen or dream-roamed, ripest meed dealt entire. Brawn or bareness none outranks; hail or hale unspeakable. Nectar yield none else may taste, let alone ladle. From the all-wish barn one alms craved: thee-love lonely. Unchecked triple-throne force cheered amid courtiers.

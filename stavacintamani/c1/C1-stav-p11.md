# C1 — stav-p11 (dependence and rest)

## SUMMARY

That-art-thou settled: all flee in, none out. Fed full and mirage-shucked, top lodgement glints for the fond. Night deeds, clean or unclean, nestle glad in leant-on pride. Soot cannot smudge where mind courses his demesne — soon as the childless grandam's grandchild. One sole already on mercy lane hankers 'honour Hara'. Only strength, loose, looms the loom. Scorched by none of the three fires, the saved hail the still-sweet pond. Sham fondness fruits; straight fondness — crop unnamed till thou namest. Eye and highway yield from hearsay's orchard. Peak past prime poised aloft in thee: thou the broom of bars.

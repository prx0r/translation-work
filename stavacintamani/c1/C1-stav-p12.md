# C1 — stav-p12 (freshness and seal)

## SUMMARY

Wait on Sarva and sweeten hourly new. Unflagging fancy-hatcher stays simplex sovereign sans birth. When burns the wont-wick down to meeting? Blink once clean and command all. Gay vaunts of done-ness stand if thou prop. Thou turnest both wheels; thy love wheels only fair. Dangle between in-step and self-shine till sole survey settles it: mind's smile is siddhi, remotest room. Lip, brain, brawn, bulk gild with thee-love only. Longing-laden laud-gem Nārāyaṇa nailed for the Gracious.

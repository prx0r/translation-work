# C1 — Stavacintāmaṇi

## stav-p01–p03 — the supreme Lord and worship

**FUNCTION.** Establishes Śiva as supreme Lord (unconquered, bliss, pure nectar, unlimited light, accessible) and worship (remembrance as medicine for ignorance, grace accomplishes all ends).

**KEY TERMS.** aparājita (unconquered), anantaprakāśa (unlimited light), ativāllabhya (accessibility), paramānanda (supreme bliss), punarbhava (rebirth), auṣadha (medicine), prasāda (grace).



**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.2–1.31.

## stav-p04–p06 — power, grace, sovereignty
**SUMMARY.** Śiva's power: whose desire alone has fruit (three-world); Lord, who knows extent of works; Brahmā etc. run up karma-ladder to gain abode; this is Brahmā, Mahendra, sun, moon; power-vine blossomed; may Bhava protect. Grace: homage to Him needing no service, sole benefactor of three worlds, desirable by all, desireless, matted hair; oh, knower-ness for great work; sow devotion with endless fruit; great Māyā, Māyin; deluded world slack even on gaining meditation-treasure. Sovereignty: withdrawn desire, not extending desire's course; crowned with moon; thinking what can I powerless do, Śāṃkarī power is refuge; beyond qualities, all-excellence; where peer/substitute; without duality, without condition.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes Śiva's power (desire with three-world fruit, Brahmā etc. run up karma-ladder), grace (needs no service, sole benefactor; Māyā deludes), sovereignty (withdraws desire, Śāṃkarī power is refuge, no peer).

**KEY TERMS.** icchā (desire), lokatraya (three worlds), nirupakārya (needing no service), māyā (Māyā), upasaṃhṛtakāma (withdrawn desire), śāṃkarī (Śāṃkarī power), nirdvandva (without duality).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p01–p03 (supreme Lord/worship); p04–p06 establish power/grace/sovereignty.


**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.32–1.60.

## stav-p07–p09 — bliss, protection, remembrance
**SUMMARY.** Bliss: three-world bliss = drop of Śiva's ocean; Brahmā etc. fortunate, freed from other-talk, chanting Śiva with delight; bestows endless desires even to desireless; beginningless, enjoyer of world. Protection: shatters world-confusion, reveals knowledge-bliss without duality; by what desires could limit be reached; steals Māyā in one blink, may Śiva protect; weighty course of dispassion, supreme glory of knowledge; to those bearing peak of desirelessness, you are Lord. Remembrance: makes difficult easy, suffering pleasant; memory = single hero; we remember enemy of Smara; victorious songs whose singer is supreme Lord; name thrills great souls; if you were only like Bhava; arrangement of own-powers; you are mantra.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes bliss (three-world bliss = drop of Śiva's ocean), protection (shatters world-confusion, steals Māyā in one blink), remembrance (memory = single hero, name thrills).

**KEY TERMS.** ānandasāgara (ocean of bliss), māyāmuṣ (stealing Māyā), ekavīra (single hero), pulakāṅkura (horripilation), svaśaktivyūha (own-power arrangement).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** Follows p04–p06 (power/grace/sovereignty); p07–p09 establish bliss/protection/remembrance.


**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.61–1.90.

## stav-p10–p12 — sovereignty (continued) and close
**SUMMARY.** Sovereignty (continued): homage to end of end-maker, crusher of Kāma, Gaṅgā on head; standing above even sovereignty-knowledge-dispassion-dharma; when you do not wish, who can bend grass; one graced by your wish bears Brahmī burden. Devotee's state: cause of affliction-removal; Lord, you are that; who would not take refuge; having enjoyed pleasures, abandoning world-delusion, gain supreme state; whatever in dream, good or bad, by pride of dependence, content everywhere. Close: most sweet nectar of desire for service to Śarva; where in any moment not new; tirelessly three worlds by hundred thoughts; yet one, thought-free, victorious, unborn; lamp of knowledge burning oil of saṃsāra-trace; when shall I be present to you.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes sovereignty (continued, above even sovereignty-knowledge-dispassion-dharma), devotee's state (refuge, contentment in dependence), close (nectar of service, thought-free creation, lamp of knowledge).

**KEY TERMS.** kṛtakṛtānta (end of end-maker), aiśvarya (sovereignty), kleśahāna (affliction-removal), paraṃ pada (supreme state), nirvṛta (content), śarvasevā (service to Śarva), nirvikalpa (thought-free), jñānadīpa (lamp of knowledge).



**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS1.91–1.120.

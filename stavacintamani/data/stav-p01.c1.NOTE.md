# C1 NOTE — stav-p01

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Undefeated conqueror in auspicious wealth; milk-ocean Śambhu; hate and praise both address; vast Śambhu; all-common form; Sthāṇu; goose-power; groundless picture; milked wisdom; offering to Bhava.

## VOICE MAP

Devotee invokes, praises, confesses, bows, begs, offers (vv2-11).

## KEY TERMS

* aparājita — unbeaten
* kṣīrasindhu — milk-ocean
* ambikāpati — Ambikā's husband
* vibhu/vibhava — vast / prosperity (adjudicated vast)
* sarvasāmānya — all-common
* sthāṇu — pillar
* haṃsa/haṃsī — goose (neuter/masculine)
* abhittti — no-wall
* śivajñāna — Śiva-knowledge
* bali — offering

## CRUXES

* mantrayāma (address vs consult — adjudicated address, M00089 tubhyam); vibhave (dat vs loc — adjudicated vast); nairguṇyaguṇāya (paradox epithet); tanvate (extend vs spread-for-selves); ṣaṭpramāṇī compound bracketing; te (to-you vs of-you — adjudicated to-you)

## FUNCTION

Invocation catalogue: each epithet widens the Lord.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Verses 2-11: conqueror, milk-ocean, hate/praise, vast, all-common, Sthāṇu, goose, picture, milk, offering.

## EXPLANATION

Wealth, ocean, address, vastness, wholeness, measure, wings, wall-lessness, milk, gift.

## BOUNDARY

No open boundary; v04 Kṣemarāja upāya context kept out of root.

## RELATED

Marjanovic 2011; Kṣemarāja Vṛtti M00089 pp6-21 (v04 dveṣa/stuti advedged; v05 vibhave=vyāpaka; v07 nairguṇya-guṇa; v10 dugdha).

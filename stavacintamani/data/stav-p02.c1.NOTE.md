# C1 NOTE — stav-p02

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Longing to see and dwell; homage to dream-seers; desire to contemplate; lifelong staying; Veda-tree; you-made pair-free; independent power; name over meditations; mantra refuge; all is you.

## VOICE MAP

Devotee longs, resolves, entrusts, reasons, hails (vv12-21).

## KEY TERMS

* bhavadguhā — your cave
* somakalā — moon-crescent
* bhāvatka — your-being-ish (obscure; Vṛtti: cidānandaghana tvatsvarūpa)
* tvatprasāda — your grace
* kalpavṛkṣa — wish-tree
* tvanmayī — you-made
* ananyāpekṣa — needing nothing else
* dhūrjaṭi — matted-locked
* śivāya — to Śiva (mantra)
* yena na prāpyaḥ — by which unattainable

## CRUXES

* bhāvatkaṃ obscure (GRETIL bhāvatkam; Vṛtti glosses as your dense bliss-self; pleonastic-ka vs corruption open, PARSED via Vṛtti); antarbhava (dwell vs O-dweller — adjudicated dwell); bhava voc/imp (adjudicated be!); dhūrjaṭeḥ genitive form, address sense kept; te (they vs your — revised to they, demonstrative)

## FUNCTION

Appetite for sight and stay settling into vow.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Verses 12-21: cave, dream, contemplation, vow, tree, entrusting, power, name, mantra, all.

## EXPLANATION

Cave and dream; hunger and vow; book and tree; grasp and name.

## BOUNDARY

bhāvatka morph uncertain (flagged); v1-maṅgala gap stands.

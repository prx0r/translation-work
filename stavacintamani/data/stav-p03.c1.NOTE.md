# C1 NOTE — stav-p03

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Rasa swelling thousandfold; intellect-garland bowed; remembrance the cure; fulfiller of ends; jewel from dread sea; few know the veiled; qualityless/desireless conqueror; milk-sea lordship; blossoming protector; all-knowing offering.

## VOICE MAP

Devotee prays, bows, begs, exults, marvels (vv22-31).

## KEY TERMS

* rasa — relish (grows thousandfold)
* dhīpati-mālā — garland of intellect-lords
* auṣadha — medicine
* praṇāma — homage (noun vs we-bow — adjudicated noun)
* bhakticintāmaṇi — devotion-jewel
* nirguṇa/niṣkāma — qualityless/desireless
* kṣīroda — milk-ocean
* bhavate (Vṛtti: sphurate) — he blossoms (verb, NOT vocative)
* tvayārpyate — offered by you (instr kept; revised gloss)

## CRUXES

* bhavate Vṛtti-resolved verb (sphurate), NOT vocative — recorded; bhava Vṛtti voc confirmed; te Vṛtti dat confirmed; 'vate vocative kept (dat open); tvayārpyate instr vs dat (kept by-you, revised flag); praṇāmo noun vs verb; śārvaṃ acc vs nom (same sense)

## FUNCTION

Worship given, grace returned.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Verses 22-31: rasa, garland, medicine, homage, jewel, knowers, conqueror, milk-sea, blossom, offering.

## EXPLANATION

Call and answer; cure and jewel; blossom and guard.

## BOUNDARY

v30 'vate voc-vs-dat still open (Vṛtti: rakṣāṃ kurvate); v31 dative sense kept in L2, form honest.

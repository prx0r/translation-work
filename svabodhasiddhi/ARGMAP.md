# Svabodhasiddhi — ARGMAP

## svab-p01 (2–10)
- speaker: Bhūtirāja, devotional/philosophical voice.
- question: What is self-knowledge (svabodha)?
- premises: Aśeṣadarśanāveśaviśeṣādhivivarjitam (free from particular absorption in all views) svasaṃvedanasaṃvedyaṃ svasthaṃ vande paraṃ śivam (I bow to supreme Śiva, self-aware, self-known, abiding in self). Nirastakalpanājālavikalpaughavighātine (destroyer of thought-network and thought-flood) namo 'stu paramānandapadātītāya śambhave (homage to Śambhu beyond supreme-bliss-state). Jayanty apārasaṃsārasārāsāravibhāgadāḥ (victorious, givers of distinction of saṃsāra's essence/non-essence, endless) kaivalyajñānasaṃbodhahetavaś śambhavāṅghrayaḥ (Śambhu's feet, cause of kaivalya-knowledge-awakening). Mano buddhir ahaṃ prāṇās tanmātrendriyajīvanam (mind, intellect, I, breath, tanmātras, senses, life) yaṃ dṛṣṭvā vinivartante tam upāsyam upāsmahe (seeing whom, they turn back; that worshipful we worship). Spandāspandakṛtagrāsāṃ naumy ahaṃ śuddhasaṃvidam (I bow to pure consciousness, which has swallowed spanda and non-spanda). Svasaṃvedanasadyuktyā gamyate yadi kenacit (if by self-awareness's immediate connection, gained by anyone) samāśvāsavihīnatvāt kathaṃ naiḥśreyasaṃ phalam (without comfort, how the fruit of supreme good). Svabodhajāgarūkatvān nirvibhāgā sthitir muneḥ (by self-knowledge's wakefulness, the sage's state is without division). Tām evānusaran yogī svastho yaḥ sa sukhī bhavet (following that, the yogī, abiding in self, becomes happy).
- inference: Self-knowledge = supreme Śiva (self-aware, self-known); destroyer of thought-network; cause of kaivalya-knowledge; pure consciousness swallows spanda/non-spanda; without comfort, no supreme good; by self-knowledge's wakefulness, sage's state without division; following that, yogī becomes happy.
- conclusion: Self-knowledge doctrine stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## svab-p02 (11–19)
- speaker: Bhūtirāja.
- question: How does self-knowledge operate?
- premises: Svasaṃkalpaparāmarśād viśuddhajñānasaṃśrayāt (from reflection on own-thought, from pure-knowledge-refuge) paramāhlādalābhāc ca bodhaikaghanatā muneḥ (and from gaining supreme delight, the sage's consciousness-single-density). Nigṛhīte svasaṃkalpe śakteḥ śaktimati sthitiḥ (when own-thought is restrained, power abides in power-possessor). Śaktiśaktimator aikyam ity upeyaṃ ca yoginām (power and power-possessor's unity is the goal of yogīs). Prāpte svarūpavijñāne santyakte tattvakañcuke (when own-nature-knowledge is gained, tattva-armor abandoned) udyogakāraṇe kīrṇe muktaḥ sa kila kathyate (when effort-cause is scattered, he is indeed called liberated). Bodhamātre grahaḥ kāryaḥ s... (in consciousness alone, grasping should be done...).
- inference: Self-knowledge operates through reflection on own-thought, pure-knowledge-refuge, supreme delight → sage's consciousness-single-density; power/power-possessor unity is yogī's goal; when own-nature-knowledge gained and tattva-armor abandoned, liberated.
- conclusion: Operation stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## svab-p03 (20–28)
- speaker: Bhūtirāja.
- question: What is the result of self-knowledge?
- premises: Grāhyagrahaṇasaṃskāratiraskāraikacetasām (of yogīs with single-mind destroying graspable/grasping/impressions) yogināṃ svātmaniṣṭhānāṃ mokṣalakṣmīr na durlabhā (for those abiding in self, liberation's Lakṣmī is not hard to gain). Labdhā svarūpaviśrāntir nirvṛttā kleśasantatiḥ (gained, own-nature-rest; affliction-continuum ceased). Grāhyagrāhakatā bhagnā svarūpastho 'smi saṃsthitaḥ (graspable/grasper-ness broken; "I abide in own-nature" established). Abhedabodhasaṃbodhasvarūpānubhavasthitim (non-dual-knowledge-awakening's own-nature-experience-state) vyutthitaḥ sanniruddho vā labhate yaḥ sa tattvavit (whether arisen or restrained, he who gains that is a tattva-knower). Yasya sarvāsv... (whose all...).
- inference: Result = liberation's Lakṣmī not hard to gain for self-abiding yogīs; own-nature-rest gained; affliction-continuum ceased; graspable/grasper-ness broken; non-dual-knowledge-experience state; whether arisen or restrained, tattva-knower.
- conclusion: Result stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

## svab-p04 (29–33)
- speaker: Bhūtirāja (close).
- question: What is the close?
- premises: Nairvāsanyāt svasaṃbodhāt tyāgād āśābhimānayoḥ (from non-attachment, from self-knowledge, from abandoning hope/pride) sadasatpadavicchedān na punar vigrahagrahaḥ (from cutting real/unreal states, no again body-grasping). Āśvastāntaḥkaraṇaḥ kṣīṇavikalpaḥ svarūpalābharataḥ (comforted inner-instrument, exhausted thought, by gaining own-nature). Svānubhavāmṛtatṛptaḥ kaivalyaṃ yāti caritārthaḥ (satisfied with self-experience-nectar, attains kaivalya, accomplished). Ittham upeyaprāptau vigatamalā citprakāśikā satyā (thus in goal-attainment, stainless consciousness-light, true). Kaivalyabhūtijananī svabodhasiddhir mayā gaditā (kaivalya's glory-giver, self-knowledge-accomplishment, spoken by me) yenopa... (by which...).
- inference: Close = non-attachment/self-knowledge/abandoning hope-pride → no body-grasping; comforted inner-instrument, exhausted thought, own-nature gained; satisfied with self-experience-nectar, kaivalya; stainless consciousness-light; self-knowledge-accomplishment spoken.
- conclusion: The text closes with kaivalya-accomplishment.
- unresolved: none.

# Svabodhasiddhi svab-p02 — L2 READ

## v.7
How tell what cannot be named? If anyone reaches it at all, it is by the honest method of feeling oneself.
The Sanskrit: *vyapadeśavihīnasya tattvasya kathanaṃ katham / svasaṃvedanasadyuktyā gamyate yadi kenacit*

## v.8
Taught the yoga of knowing, yet with no trust inside — trustless, how could he ever taste freedom's fruit?
The Sanskrit: *jñānayogopadeśena yasya nāśvāsa āntaraḥ / samāśvāsavihīnatvāt kathaṃ naiḥśreyasaṃ phalam*

## v.9
The way to the goal is this: stay awake in your own awakening. From such waking the sage stands whole, undivided.
The Sanskrit: *upeyaprāptyupāyo 'yaṃ svabodhe jāgarūkatā / svabodhajāgarūkatvān nirvibhāgā sthitir muneḥ*

## v.10
No way but keeping to your own being. Keep to her, rest in yourself — whoever does is happy.
The Sanskrit: *upāyo nāparaḥ kaścit svasattānugamād ṛte / tām evānusaran yogī svastho yaḥ sa sukhī bhavet*

## v.11
From noticing your own notions, from holding to pure knowing, from winning the highest joy — the sage grows one solid mass of waking.
The Sanskrit: *svasaṃkalpaparāmarśād viśuddhajñānasaṃśrayāt / paramāhlādalābhāc ca bodhaikaghanatā muneḥ*

## v.12
Grip your notions still, and power comes to rest in the powerful; their oneness is what yogis are after.
The Sanskrit: *nigṛhīte svasaṃkalpe śakteḥ śaktimati sthitiḥ / śaktiśaktimator aikyam ity upeyaṃ ca yoginām*

## v.13
Know your own form, drop the veil of principles, scatter the cause of striving — then, tradition says, you are called free.
The Sanskrit: *prāpte svarūpavijñāne santyakte tattvakañcuke / udyogakāraṇe kīrṇe muktaḥ sa kila kathyate*

## v.14
With a fully wakened mind, hold to waking alone — until, by your own strength, you stand in yourself, alone.
The Sanskrit: *bodhamātre grahaḥ kāryaḥ suprabuddhena cetasā / tāvad yāvat svasāmarthyāt svaniṣṭhaḥ kevalo bhavet*

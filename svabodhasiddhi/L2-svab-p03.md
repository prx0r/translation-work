# Svabodhasiddhi svab-p03 — L2 READ

## v.15
Ever fully awake, he sees himself by himself; for the seer settled in his own form, what else is left?
The Sanskrit: *sarvadā suprabuddhas san paśyaty ātmānam ātmanā / draṣṭuḥ svarūpasaṃsthasya kim anyad avaśiṣyate*

## v.16
To be always fully awake is for the mind to waste away; from that wasting, yogis truly see.
The Sanskrit: *sadaiva suprabuddhatvam etāvan manasaḥ kṣayaḥ / tatkṣayād yogināṃ samyaksākṣātkāro bhavaty alam*

## v.17
Turned back into self-awareness, with no twoness left — he is king among yogis, a sun against the dark of ignorance.
The Sanskrit: *paryāvṛttaḥ svasaṃvittau vartate yas tv abhedataḥ / sa samrāḍ yogayuktānām ajñānadhvāntabhāskaraḥ*

## v.18
The moon pales before the joy of tasting your own nature; with the inner organ gone, what is there left for me to get?
The Sanskrit: *svarūpānubhavāhlādadaśātītendusaṃsthitiḥ / avāptavyaṃ kim astīha kṣīṇāntaḥkaraṇasya me*

## v.19
Self-knowledge and rest, firm and bright, for whoever holds them; freed from the mind's whirl, aloneness comes soon.
The Sanskrit: *svaparijñaptiviśrāntī sudṛḍhe yasya susphuṭe / tasya vṛttivimuktasya bhavet kevalatācirāt*

## v.20
For yogis grounded in themselves, whose single mind sweeps aside the traces of grasping and the grasped — fortune's release is easy.
The Sanskrit: *grāhyagrahaṇasaṃskāratiraskāraikacetasām / yogināṃ svātmaniṣṭhānāṃ mokṣalakṣmīr na durlabhā*

## v.21
Rest in my own form I have won, my sorrows ended, the split of grasped and grasper broken — I stand firm, abiding as I am.
The Sanskrit: *labdhā svarūpaviśrāntir nirvṛttā kleśasantatiḥ / grāhyagrāhakatā bhagnā svarūpastho' smi saṃsthitaḥ*

## v.22
Whether up and about or gathered inward, whoever wins that nondual, wakened taste of his own nature knows what is real.
The Sanskrit: *abhedabodhasaṃbodhasvarūpānubhavasthitim / vyutthitaḥ sanniruddho vā labhate yaḥ sa tattvavit*

## v.23
For one whose self-standing never slips in any state — what can the squabbles of scripture-rangers do to him?
The Sanskrit: *yasya sarvāsv avasthāsu svasthitir naiva lupyate / tasya kiṃ nāma kurvanti śāstrabhramaṇavibhramāḥ*

## v.24
His self freed from the delusion brewed of real and unreal notions — he lives on among people, fear gone, always a yogi.
The Sanskrit: *vigalitasadasadvikalpasadasadbhramajanitamohamuktātmā / jīvann api janamadhye vigatabhayaḥ sarvadā yogī*

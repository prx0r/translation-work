# Svabodhasiddhi svab-p04 — L2 READ

## v.25
Senses cast off, having sat with absorption even once — the highest stands face to face with the yogi whose work is done.
The Sanskrit: *nirastakaraṇo yena samādhiḥ sevitaḥ sakṛt / paraḥ sākṣātkṛtas tena caritārthena yoginā*

## v.26
Unbroken in the I-self, gathered by his own effort — may he wake, and stand in himself, till he shines as Sadāśiva.
The Sanskrit: *ahamātmany asambhinnaḥ saṃgṛhya svaprayatnataḥ / prabuddhaḥ syāt svaniṣṭhaḥ san yāvat sādāśivadyutiḥ*

## v.27
His turning partless, all objects dropped, the knower who won the winning, aimed at waking alone, standing in himself — indeed he casts off his own sheath, the yogi.
The Sanskrit: *nirastasarvārthaniraṃśavṛttir jñānī sakṛt svātmani labdhalābhaḥ / samyaṅnibodhaikaparaḥ svaniṣṭhaḥ svakañcukaṃ hanta jahāti yogī*

## v.28
Whose honest method is the word and the taste, unbroken in self-awareness — he is fit to make teaching, and to wake pupils rightly.
The Sanskrit: *vāganubhavasadyuktir yasyāsti nirantaraṃ svasaṃvittau / tasyopadeśakaraṇaṃ yuktaṃ śiṣyeṣu bodhanaṃ samyak*

## v.29
From wanting nothing, from self-waking, from dropping hope and pride, from cutting the road of real-and-unreal — no taking of a body again.
The Sanskrit: *nairvāsanyāt svasaṃbodhāt tyāgād āśābhimānayoḥ / sadasatpadavicchedān na punar vigrahagrahaḥ*

## v.30
Inner sense at peace, fancies thinned, delighting in the gain of his own form, drunk on the nectar of tasting himself — he goes to freedom, his work done.
The Sanskrit: *āśvastāntaḥkaraṇaḥ kṣīṇavikalpaḥ svarūpalābharataḥ / svānubhavāmṛtatṛptaḥ kaivalyaṃ yāti caritārthaḥ*

## v.31
So, for reaching the goal — stainless, lighting consciousness, true, mother of freedom's rise — I have told the perfection of self-waking.
The Sanskrit: *ittham upeyaprāptau vigatamalā citprakāśikā satyā / kaivalyabhūtijananī svabodhasiddhir mayā gaditā*

## v.32
This teaching can make even one who never won enough, and free him from doubt's fault — with such doubtless teaching, what business has the much-chanting scholar?
The Sanskrit: *yenopadeśena nalabdhalābho bhavaty alaṃ saṃśayadoṣamuktaḥ / tenopadeśena nasaṃśayena kiṃ nāma kuryād bahuśāstrapāṭhī*

## v.33
All-blessing maker, wildfire to tangled deeds, shower of your own station, free of every notion, temple of all joy — to cut suffering's root I bow to the unmatchably lovely Śiva, taught from the guru's mouth.
The Sanskrit: *aśeṣakṛtamaṅgalaṃ vividhakarmadāvānalaṃ svarūpapadadarśakaṃ sakalakalpanāvarjitam / samastasukhamandiraṃ vividhaduḥkhamūlacchide namāmy asamasundaraṃ gurumukhopadiṣṭaṃ śivam*

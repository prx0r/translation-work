## svab-p01–p02 — self-knowledge and its operation
**SUMMARY.** I bow to supreme Śiva, self-aware, self-known, abiding in self, free from particular absorption in all views. Destroyer of thought-network and thought-flood; homage to Śambhu beyond supreme-bliss-state. Victorious, givers of distinction of saṃsāra's essence/non-essence, endless; Śambhu's feet, cause of kaivalya-knowledge-awakening. Mind, intellect, I, breath, tanmātras, senses, life — seeing whom, they turn back; that worshipful we worship. I bow to pure consciousness, which has swallowed spanda and non-spanda. If by self-awareness's immediate connection, gained by anyone; without comfort, how the fruit of supreme good. By self-knowledge's wakefulness, the sage's state is without division. Following that, the yogī, abiding in self, becomes happy. From reflection on own-thought, from pure-knowledge-refuge, and from gaining supreme delight, the sage's consciousness-single-density. When own-thought is restrained, power abides in power-possessor. Power and power-possessor's unity is the goal of yogīs. When own-nature-knowledge is gained, tattva-armor abandoned; when effort-cause is scattered, he is indeed called liberated.

**FUNCTION.** Establishes self-knowledge (supreme Śiva, self-aware, destroyer of thought-network, cause of kaivalya) and its operation (reflection on own-thought, power/power-possessor unity, liberation when own-nature-knowledge gained).

**KEY TERMS.** svasaṃvedana (self-awareness), svabodha (self-knowledge), kaivalya (kaivalya), spanda (spanda), tattvakañcuka (tattva-armor), śakti (power), śaktimat (power-possessor).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First two passages; sets the doctrinal frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Self-knowledge: bow to supreme Śiva, self-aware, self-known, abiding in self; free from particular absorption in all views; destroyer of thought-network; homage to Śambhu beyond supreme-bliss; victorious, givers of distinction of saṃsāra's essence/non-essence; Śambhu's feet, cause of kaivalya-knowledge; mind/intellect/I/breath/tanmātras/senses/life turn back seeing whom; bow to pure consciousness swallowing spanda/non-spanda; if gained by self-awareness's connection; without comfort, no supreme good; by self-knowledge's wakefulness, sage's state without division; following that, yogī becomes happy. (2) Operation: from reflection on own-thought, pure-knowledge-refuge, supreme delight → sage's consciousness-single-density; when own-thought restrained, power abides in power-possessor; power/power-possessor unity is yogī's goal; when own-nature-knowledge gained, tattva-armor abandoned; when effort-cause scattered, liberated.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.2–1.19.

## svab-p03–p04 — result and close
**SUMMARY.** Of yogīs with single-mind destroying graspable/grasping/impressions, for those abiding in self, liberation's Lakṣmī is not hard to gain. Gained, own-nature-rest; affliction-continuum ceased. Graspable/grasper-ness broken; "I abide in own-nature" established. Non-dual-knowledge-awakening's own-nature-experience-state; whether arisen or restrained, he who gains that is a tattva-knower. From non-attachment, from self-knowledge, from abandoning hope/pride; from cutting real/unreal states, no again body-grasping. Comforted inner-instrument, exhausted thought, by gaining own-nature. Satisfied with self-experience-nectar, attains kaivalya, accomplished. Thus in goal-attainment, stainless consciousness-light, true. Kaivalya's glory-giver, self-knowledge-accomplishment, spoken by me.

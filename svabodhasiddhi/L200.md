# L200 — Svabodhasiddhi (32 verses, 4 passages)

## 1. What the text says (plain)
Svabodhasiddhi (32 verses) is Bhūtirāja's (9th c.) work on self-knowledge. It establishes: self-knowledge = supreme Śiva (self-aware, self-known, abiding in self, free from particular absorption in all views, destroyer of thought-network, cause of kaivalya-knowledge). Self-knowledge operates through reflection on own-thought, pure-knowledge-refuge, supreme delight → sage's consciousness-single-density; power/power-possessor unity is yogī's goal; liberation when own-nature-knowledge gained and tattva-armor abandoned. Result = liberation's Lakṣmī not hard to gain for self-abiding yogīs; own-nature-rest gained; affliction-continuum ceased; graspable/grasper-ness broken; non-dual-knowledge-experience state; tattva-knower. Close = non-attachment/self-knowledge/abandoning hope-pride → no body-grasping; comforted inner-instrument, exhausted thought, own-nature gained; satisfied with self-experience-nectar, kaivalya; stainless consciousness-light; self-knowledge-accomplishment spoken.

## 2. What the text argues (core claims)
- C1: Self-knowledge = supreme Śiva (self-aware, self-known, abiding in self).
- C2: Free from particular absorption in all views; destroyer of thought-network; cause of kaivalya-knowledge.
- C3: Pure consciousness swallows spanda and non-spanda.
- C4: By self-knowledge's wakefulness, sage's state without division; following that, yogī becomes happy.
- C5: Operation through reflection on own-thought, pure-knowledge-refuge, supreme delight → consciousness-single-density.
- C6: Power/power-possessor unity is yogī's goal; liberation when own-nature-knowledge gained.
- C7: Result = liberation's Lakṣmī not hard to gain; own-nature-rest; affliction ceased; graspable/grasper-ness broken; tattva-knower.
- C8: Close = non-attachment/self-knowledge → no body-grasping; kaivalya; stainless consciousness-light.

## 3. What the text rejects
- R1: Self-knowledge as non-Śiva.
- R2: Thought-network as real.
- R3: Kaivalya without self-knowledge.
- R4: Liberation as hard to gain.
- R5: Body-grasping after self-knowledge.

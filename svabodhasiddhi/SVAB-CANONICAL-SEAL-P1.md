**One-line:** *"in the land where the sun never goes down, the moon
runs out of night, the endless light first blurs your mind and then
sharpens it, and the sun's own edge cuts the knot of mine-and-I —
and none of it comes from a creed, only from the feel of it."*

## The parable (≈89s)

> A traveler bows where the light never leaves — to the freedom-
> giver, to the sun that cleared the dark's abyss. Then he goes
> into the white summer, where the sun refuses to go down: always
> awake, the land says — follow your being, keep to it, no creed
> but the feel of it in yourself. And in that endless light the
> moon runs out of night: outshone, she thins to nothing — no
> other shine is wanted. The mind rebels first — nothing is right
> in light that never blurs to sleep — but from that waste of
> blur the seeing sharpens: twoness left behind, he stands king of
> the yoked, sun against the dark. And the sun's own edge is the
> blade that cuts the knot of mine-and-I at the heart; the net of
> fancy the night knits, the long light burns off. Mind, thought,
> I, breath, the senses — all turn homeward to watch. Senses cast
> at last, one session served: the supreme stands face to face.
> His method: word and taste, unbroken self-aware — taught
> standing in the light, pupils wakened right. Craving-free, the
> roads of real and unreal both cut: freedom, aim content.

## The seal — statement

**The land of the unsetting sun:** one horizon with NO dusk — the
sun hangs low and held, its light field flooding the whole frame
(the law: no dark band anywhere); on the ground a knotted net
lying fraying at the light's edge (the fancy-night, burning off);
at the rim a thinning moon dissolving to nothing (outshone);
blurred grey figures at the far rim resolving into sharpness
nearer him (the waste of blur → the seeing); the traveler stands
mid-light, and ONE long thin sun-ray descends like a blade into
the knot at his heart (the sun's edge cutting mine-and-I), a thin
return-beam rising from his eyes to the light (face-to-face as
crossed beams); crown: endless noon, nothing but gold-white.

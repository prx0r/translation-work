# C1 — svab-p01

## SUMMARY

Freedom given, views refused, fancies smashed, feet triumphant, faculties retreating, awareness knifed clean — six bows name their Lord whole-blessing, past-bliss, theology-free, worship-worthy, mine-I-cutting.

## FUNCTION

Establishes whom the teaching comes from and what it cuts, before any method is taught.

## KEY TERMS

namaḥ/vande/namo 'stu/naumi (bowing verbs) — fourfold praise; kaivalya (freedom) — the gift; svasaṃvedana (self-feeling) — known only thus; svastha (self-abiding) — the praised state; āveśa (possession-sickness) — refused; spanda-aspanda (throb and throbless) — devoured; hṛdgranthi (heart-knot) — mine-and-I, knifed.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The six praises pledge the doctrine's source and temper; method and fruit taught beyond answer to this Lord.

## EXPLANATION

Six bows, four verbs of bowing (namaḥ, vande, namo 'stu, naumi, upāsmahe): freedom given whole-blessinged; theology-possession refused; fancy-nets smashed past bliss; feet dividing real from sham; all faculties retreating before the worship-worthy; pure awareness knifing the mine-I knot and devouring throb and throbless.

## BOUNDARY

No method is taught; the question of speaking the undescribed belongs to the teaching that follows.

## RELATED

Hanneder codex unicus (ZDMG 2017); Bhūti(rāja) ascription cautious, identity unasserted; self-feeling axis planted here, guarded throughout.

## ESSENCE

1. Bow to the freedom-giver: Śiva, sun against becoming-darkness.
2. Highest praised: view-sickness-free, self-feeling-known, self-abiding.
3. Bow to Śambhu: fancy-net smasher, past supreme-bliss stage.
4. Feet triumph: essence divided from nonessence, awakening caused.
5. All faculties turn back before the worship-worthy we worship.
6. I bow to pure awareness: sole knife for the mine-I knot.

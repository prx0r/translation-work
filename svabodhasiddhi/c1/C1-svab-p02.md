# C1 — svab-p02

## SUMMARY

Speaking needs self-feeling's method; trustless teaching bears nothing. One means: wakefulness in self-awakening, following own being and nothing else. Concepts grasped, knowing purified, joy won — awakening masses whole; power rests in the powerful. Form known, sheath dropped, striving scattered: freed. Hold waking alone till grounded-alone.

## FUNCTION

Answers the opening question with the single method, then walks its stages to the freed name.

## KEY TERMS

svasaṃvedana-sadyukti (self-feeling's true method) — the only access; āśvāsa (trust) — without it, no fruit; jāgarūkatā (wakefulness) — the method; svasattānugama (following own being) — the sole exception; bodhaikaghanatā (one-mass awakening) — the ripening; śakti/śaktimat (power/powerful) — oneness the goal; bodhamātra (awakening alone) — the axis guarded.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The method pledged here pays out across the fruit: wakefulness-words return in every later standing.

## EXPLANATION

Question and answer: how speak the undescribed (by self-feeling's method); what without trust (no fruit). Thesis: wakefulness in self-awakening (ms. locative kept), no means but following own being. Ladder: concepts grasped, knowing purified, joy won — one-mass awakening; concepts restrained — power in powerful; form known, sheath cast, striving scattered — freed; grasp held on awakening alone till grounded-alone from own capacity.

## BOUNDARY

No fruit is described; the fruit's first-person seal belongs to the standing that follows.

## RELATED

Hanneder ms. readings kept (svabodhe, sukhī, kīrṇe, mātre); Sanderson's nine stanzas edited earlier; bodha-vs-jñāna axis guarded throughout.

## ESSENCE

1. Undesignated reality: spoken only by self-feeling's true method.
2. Teaching without inner trust: no final fruit.
3. Means: wakefulness in self-awakening; sage stands undivided.
4. No means but following own being; follower abides, happy.
5. Concept-grasping, pure knowing, supreme joy: one-mass awakening.
6. Concepts restrained: power rests in powerful; oneness the goal.
7. Form known, sheath cast, striving scattered: called freed.
8. Fully-awake mind grasps awakening alone till grounded-alone.

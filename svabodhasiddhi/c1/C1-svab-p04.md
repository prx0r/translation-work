# C1 — svab-p04

## SUMMARY

Senses dropped, absorption once tasted: the supreme face to face. Unbroken in the I-self, gathered by own effort — wake till Sadāśiva-splendor. Turning partless, gain won: indeed the sheath falls. Word-and-taste his method: fit to wake pupils. Desirelessness, self-waking, hope and pride dropped, real-unreal road cut: no body again. Sense at peace, nectar-drunk: freedom, aim done. Thus told — stainless, consciousness-lighting, true, freedom's mother. The never-winner made enough: what use the much-reciter? Final bow: whole-blessing, deed-fire, joy-temple, guru-mouth-taught lovely Śiva.

## FUNCTION

Seals instruction, names the work, dismisses mere learning, and bows through the guru's mouth.

## KEY TERMS

sākṣātkṛta (made face to face) — once suffices; sādāśivadyuti (Sadāśiva-splendor) — the open horizon; hanta (indeed) — commended abandonment; vāg-anubhava (word-and-taste) — the teacher's license; nairvāsanya (desirelessness) — with self-waking; kaivalya (freedom) — reached, aim done; bahuśāstrapāṭhin (much-scripture-reciter) — dismissed; gurumukha (guru's mouth) — the lineage seal.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The closing pays every pledge: waking becomes splendor, trust becomes doubtlessness, views become dismissed reciters.

## EXPLANATION

Instruction closed: senses cast off with absorption once served — supreme faced; unbroken in I-self by own effort — awakened till Sadāśiva-splendor; turning partless, gain gained — indeed the sheath falls; word-and-taste his method — fit to wake pupils rightly; desirelessness, self-waking, hope-pride dropped, real-unreal road cut — no body again; sense at peace, nectar-sated — freedom, aim done. Colophon: stainless, consciousness-lighting, true, freedom-mothering accomplishment told by me; teaching enough for unwinners, doubt purged — no use for much-reciters. Benediction: whole-blessing, deed-fire, own-form-shower, fancy-free, joy-temple — bow for suffering-root cutting to guru-mouth-taught lovely Śiva.

## BOUNDARY

Nothing new is taught after the thirtieth; the colophon tells, dismisses, and bows.

## RELATED

Title-verse names the work (authorship voice kept literal, identity unasserted); guru-mouth lineage claimed in closing; Krama and Mokṣopāya parallels open per Hanneder.

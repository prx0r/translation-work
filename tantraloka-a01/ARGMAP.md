## p01–p04 — Anuttara, the couple, consciousness-pratibhā, guru, jñāna and mukti

**Speaker:** Abhinavagupta (in propria persona, opening maṅgala + doxastic frame).

**Question:** What is the supreme (anuttara), how do Śiva and Śakti appear as father/mother, what is consciousness-pratibhā, who is the guru, and why is jñāna the sole cause of mukti?

**Premises:**
1. The anuttara is the unsurpassed (anuttara), abode of vimalakalā, the abhinava-creation, the great mother; together with the father (pañcamukhagupta, five-faced, full-bodied, lovely) it forms the couple (yāmala), the emission (visarga) of the shining state — the heart, the unsurpassed-nectar-kula (anuttarāmṛtakula). May it shine.
2. I bow to consciousness-pratibhā (citpratibhā), the goddess, supreme, Bhairava-yoginī; measureless, her abode made of trident and lotus; she abides in the body of the dancing Bhairava, like rain-cloud-dense sky with lightning-streak, lovely; blazing light-flame burned the threefold bond, shining; the knowledge-trident (jñānaśūla) cuts the favorable and the unfavorable.
3. Guru Lakṣmaṇagupta, nāda-deluding, I bow; Śrīcukhulaka, shown, my supreme guru; by whose teaching-rays this deep śāstra-path is revealed. Abhinava's heart-lotus, examine this, for Maheśa-worship.
4. Ignorance (ajñāna) is the cause of saṃsāra; knowledge (jñāna) is the sole cause of liberation (mokṣa) — so said, and in the Śrīmalinīvijayottara; possibility removed, without this liberation is declared; as in clod etc. Liberation is not named separately, not grasped; higher and higher knowledge pacifies saṃsāra; by summary/expansion, knowledge frees that much; the unliberated is indeed liberated, free from all limitation.

**Inference:** The anuttara is not an inert absolute but the Śiva-Śakti couple whose emission is the world; consciousness-pratibhā is the active, self-aware power that cuts bonds; the guru is the living channel of this śāstra; and since ignorance alone binds, knowledge alone liberates — mukti is not a separate attainment but the progressive pacification of saṃsāra by knowledge.

**Conclusion:** Tantrāloka opens by identifying the anuttara with the Śiva-Śakti couple, consciousness-pratibhā as the bond-cutting power, the guru as the revealer of the deep path, and jñāna as the sole cause of mukti (mukti being the freedom from all limitation, not a separately named state).

**Unresolved:** The dense verse-compression of the maṅgala; the exact referent of "vimalakalā" and "pañcamukhagupta" left to the commentary tradition. The "as in clod etc." (loṣṭādike) analogy is asserted, not developed here.

## p05–p09 — Vijñāna (blooming, personal, thought-free) and sādhana

**Speaker:** Abhinavagupta, continuing; the objector (Buddhist/personal distinction) is introduced.

**Question:** What is vijñāna, and how does it differ from the Buddhist and personalist (pauruṣa) accounts, through sādhana to siddhi and fruit?

**Premises:**
1. Vijñāna is blooming (vikasvara), personal (pauruṣa), thought-free (nirvikalpa).
2. The Buddhist and the personalist are as before (nourished and nourisher); yet at the body's end that knowledge is clearly revealed; then it dissolves — jīvanmukti in hand.
3. The sādhana, siddhi, and fruit doctrine continues (passages 5–9 compress the whole arc).

**Inference:** Vijñāna is not mere cognition but the personal, thought-free flowering of consciousness that survives the body; the Buddhist/personalist alternatives are absorbed and surpassed, and sādhana brings siddhi and fruit in this life (jīvanmukti).

**Conclusion:** The opening doctrinal frame is set: vijñāna (blooming, personal, thought-free) is the locus of liberation, realized through sādhana, with jīvanmukti as the fruit.

**Unresolved:** Passages 5–9 are compressed in the T1; full verse-by-verse reconstruction deferred. The Buddhist/personalist opponent is named but not yet argued against at length (deferred to A06/A21).

## p10–p34 — Sādhana, siddhi, fruit, close of ahnika 1 (compressed)

**Speaker:** Abhinavagupta.

**Question:** How do sādhana and siddhi lead to the fruit and close of the first ahnika?

**Premises:** (Compressed in source; the T1 groups verses 92–331 into passages p10–p34 without per-verse gloss beyond the Sanskrit token stream.)

**Inference:** The arc of the first ahnika runs from anuttara (p01–p04) through vijñāna and sādhana (p05–p09) to siddhi and fruit (p10–p34), closing the doctrinal frame that the rest of Tantrāloka unfolds.

**Conclusion:** Ahnika 1 establishes the whole doctrinal program: anuttara, Śiva-Śakti, consciousness-pratibhā, guru, jñāna as sole cause of mukti, vijñāna, sādhana, siddhi, fruit.

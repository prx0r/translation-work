## v.1
Now the first word, of unceasing highest-recognition-nature: to determine it, this second treatise I begin.
The Sanskrit: *yattatrādyaṃ padamaviratānuttarajñaptirūpaṃ tannirṇetuṃ prakaraṇamidamārabhe@haṃ dvitīyam*

## v.2
For the means-less, whose nature: what point by teaching here? Once may teaching be; afterwards as means-less it is called.
The Sanskrit: *anupāyaṃ hi yadrūpaṃ ko@rtho deśanayātra vai sakṛtsyāddeśanā paścādanupāyatvamucyayate*

## v.3
This means-less reality — whence without means? By itself; but of them that such: what should we say, indeed, to them?
The Sanskrit: *anupāyamidaṃ tattvamityupāyaṃ vinā kutaḥ svayaṃ tu teṣāṃ tattādṛk kiṃ brūmaḥ kila tānprati*

## v.4
Which fourfold-told form of awareness — of the pervading, this: own nature alone is to be regarded; for ever-arisen is the pervading.
The Sanskrit: *yaccaturdhoditaṃ rūpaṃ vijñānasya vibhorasau svabhāva eva mantavyaḥ sa hi nityodito vibhuḥ*

## v.5
By so many uncounted natures it shines; some part-by-part, others enter partlessly through that.
The Sanskrit: *etāvadbhirasaṃkhyātaiḥ svabhāvairyatprakāśate ke@pyaṃśāṃśikayā tena viśantyanye niraṃśataḥ*

## v.6
There too, from means-and-the-rest-expecting otherness-yoga, of means too not to be barred: variety from its otherness.
The Sanskrit: *tatrāpi cābhyupāyādisāpekṣānyatvayogataḥ upāyasyāpi no vāryā tadanyatvādvicitratā*

## v.7
There, who pure-souled, in Bhairavī, own awareness, means-less practicing: whose rule — is proclaimed.
The Sanskrit: *tatra ye nirmalātmāno bhairavīyāṃ svasaṃvidam nirupāyāmupāsīnāstadvidhiḥ praṇigadyate*

## v.8
There, for now, rite-yoga does not deserve means-hood; for from that arisen, rather it is distinctly discerned.
The Sanskrit: *tatra tāvatkriyāyogo nābhyupāyatvamarhati sa hi tasmātsamudbhūtaḥ pratyuta pravibhāvyate*

## v.9
In awareness, means just would be — quoted; if awareness is called: luminosity in self-luminous — and that, thence, from another — how?
The Sanskrit: *jñaptāvupāya eva syāditi cejjñaptirucyate prakāśatvaṃ svaprakāśe tacca tatrānyataḥ katham*

## v.10
Awareness-principle, 'self-luminous' — in this, what? Now, by reasoning: in its absence, would the universe be non-shining from inertia?
The Sanskrit: *saṃvittattvaṃ svaprakāśamityasminkaṃ nu yuktibhiḥ tadabhāve bhavedviśvaṃ jaḍatvādaprakāśakam*

## v.11
Whatever means, outer, would be, or inner, whatsoever: it, all face-turned-to-it; there, means-bearing — how?
The Sanskrit: *yāvānupāyo bāhyaḥ syādāntaro vāpi kaścana sa sarvastanmukhaprekṣī tatropāyatvabhākkatham*

## v.12
Abandon attentions, now: where, indeed, do you place attention? Discern yourself, it: in the full, attention — not, for, indeed fitting; the unfull does not attain, and, the true nature.
The Sanskrit: *tyajāvadhānāni nanu kva nāma dhatse@vadhānaṃ vicinu svayaṃ tat pūrṇe@vadhānaṃ na hi nāma yuktaṃ nāpūrṇamabhyeti ca satyabhāvam*

## v.13
By that attention-vital of this, meditation-and-the-rest, on the higher path, in the Bhairavī: how indeed would there be direct means-ness?
The Sanskrit: *tenāvadhānaprāṇasya bhāvanādeḥ pare pathi bhairavīye kathaṃkāraṃ bhavetsākṣādupāyatā*

## v.14
Whoever even by direct means that nature discern: surely they, for sun-awareness, firefly-desiring, are dullards.
The Sanskrit: *ye@pi sākṣādupāyena tadrūpaṃ praviviñcate nūnaṃ te sūryasaṃvittyai khadyotādhitsavo jaḍāḥ*

## v.15
And, as long as this outer — inner-means-deemed: that mere luminous-natured alone, Śiva's own form.
The Sanskrit: *kiṃ ca yāvadidaṃ bāhyamāntaropāyasaṃmatam tatprakāśātmatāmātraṃ śivasyaiva nijaṃ vapuḥ*

## v.16
Blue, yellow, 'pleasure': luminance alone is Śiva; in this supreme nonduality, luminous-natured: what other?
The Sanskrit: *nīlaṃ pītaṃ sukhamiti prakāśaḥ kevalaḥ śivaḥ amuṣminparamādvaite prakāśātmani ko@paraḥ*

## v.17
Means-to-be-attained-relation would be luminance alone — for it.
The Sanskrit: *upāyopeyabhāvaḥ syātprakāśaḥ kevalaṃ hi saḥ*

## v.18
This — duality indeed — this, difference: this nonduality, thus, even, in the luminous form alone — this shines, the Supreme Lord.
The Sanskrit: *idaṃ dvaitama@yaṃ bheda idamadvaitamityapi prakāśavapurevāyaṃ bhāsate parameśvaraḥ*

## v.19
In this level, pleasure, pain, bondage, release — whether conscious or inert: like pot-and-jar, same-meaning words — of them even just one — and.
The Sanskrit: *asyāṃ bhūmau sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ bandho mokṣaścitarjaḍaḥ ghaṭakumbhavadekārthāḥ śabdāste@pyekameva ca*

## v.20
In the shining-forth, for an unlit part, how, indeed, radiance? In the shining, in that or, that duality of it obliterated.
The Sanskrit: *praśāśe hyaprakāśāṃśaḥ kathaṃ nāma prakāśatām prakāśamāne tasminvā taddvaitāstasya lopitāḥ*

## v.21
In non-shining, then, in that or: reality, how is it said? Not luminance-specificity unapproved, indeed it follows.
The Sanskrit: *aprakāśe@tha tasminvā vastutā kathamucyate na prakāśaviśeṣatvamata evopapadyate*

## v.22
Hence, 'one luminance' — thus, in the doctrine here, well-established: from afar warded off, truly, the different-knowledge-sayers.
The Sanskrit: *ata ekaprakāśo@yamiti vāde@tra susthite dūrādāvāritāḥ satyaṃ vibhinnajñānavādinaḥ*

## v.23
Mere luminance taught through non-shining-denial: of the word 'one', not — but the meaning: is number, bearing consciousness-manifest-difference.
The Sanskrit: *prakāśamātramuditamaprakāśaniṣedhanāt ekaśabdasya na tvarthaḥ saṃkhyā cidvyaktibhedabhāk*

## v.24
Not this: śakti, the great goddess; not resting on another, since; nor, and this: śakti-possessing god; not of anyone a resort, since.
The Sanskrit: *naiṣa śaktirmahādevī na paratrāśrito yataḥ na caiṣa śaktimāndevo na kasyāpyāśrayo yataḥ*

## v.25
Not this: the meditated, from meditator-hood, not: the meditator, from meditated-absence; no worshipful, from worshipper-absence, from worshipful-absence: no worshipper.
The Sanskrit: *naiṣa dhyeyo dhyātrabhāvānna dhyātā dhyeyavarjanāt na pūjyaḥ pūjakābhāvātpūjyābhāvānna pūjakaḥ*

## v.26
Not mantra, nor; and mantra-able, this; not, and the pronouncer, the Lord; no initiation, initiator, or even; no initiated one, the Great Lord.
The Sanskrit: *na mantro na ca mantryo@sau na ca mantrayitā prabhuḥ na dīkṣā dīkṣako vāpi na dīkṣāvānmaheśvaraḥ*

## v.27
Place-seat-restraint-offering-mass-invocation-and-the-rest, ending in dismissal: there is not here, in doer-deed-act-abandoned.
The Sanskrit: *sthānāsananirodhārghasaṃghānāvāhanādikam visarjanāntaṃ nāstyatra kartṛkarmakriyojjhite*

## v.28
Not being, not not-being, being, not-being: and that, not both-abandoned: hard to know, for, that state: some, whatever, this unsurpassed.
The Sanskrit: *na sanna cāsatsadasanna ca tannobhayojjhitam durvijñeyā hi sāvasthā kimapyetadanuttaram*

## v.29
'This' — thus appearance, for which, being of cut-off nature: it, just, pot-like in the world, situated then: not this is Bhairava.
The Sanskrit: *ayamityavabhāso hi yo bhāvo@vacchidātmakaḥ sa eva ghaṭavalloke saṃstathā naiṣa bhairavaḥ*

## v.30
Non-being, and non-shining, not: anywhere usefulness; of the universe, is the life, truly, luminance-natured-alone, and it.
The Sanskrit: *asattvaṃ cāprakāśatvaṃ na kutrāpyupayogitā viśvasya jīvitaṃ satyaṃ prakāśaikātmakaśca saḥ*

## v.31
By these two just, but from causes: not two-natured, nor two-abandoned; wholly, for, shines it: by what form, let it be mantred?
The Sanskrit: *ābhyāmeva tu hetubhyāṃ na dvyātmā na dvayojjhitaḥ sarvātmanā hi bhātyeṣa kena rūpeṇa mantryatām*

## v.32
In the venerable Triśiras, told, supreme-knowledge-nature, by śakti dwelling within the womb, śakti-wombed, supreme state.
The Sanskrit: *śrīmattriśirasi proktaṃ parajñānasvarūpakam śaktyā garbhāntarvartinyā śaktigarbha paraṃ padam*

## v.33
Neither being, nor non-being, nor the pair: by unsayable speech, unsayable-word-escalated, śakti-dwelling, śakti-devoid.
The Sanskrit: *na bhāvo nāpyabhāvo na dvayaṃ vācāmagocarāt akathyapadavīrūḍhaṃ śaktisthaṃ śaktivarjitam*

## v.34
Thus, who rooted-knowledge-truth-purified, on the anuttara path, rooted: they, means-unruled.
The Sanskrit: *iti ye rūḍhasaṃvittiparamārthapavitritāḥ anuttarapathe rūḍhāste@bhyupāyāniyantritāḥ*

## v.35
Of them, this, shines together, everywhere, the being-circle, standing-forth just, melted in consciousness-Bhairava-fire.
The Sanskrit: *teṣāmidaṃ samābhāti sarvato bhāvamaṇḍalam puraḥsthameva saṃvittibhairavāgnivilāpitam*

## v.36
Of these, pleasure-pain-share-doubt-fear-notions, nirvikalpa-entry-only-remainder come to.
The Sanskrit: *eteṣāṃ sukhaduḥkhāṃśaśaṃkātaṃkavikalpanāḥ nirvikalpaparāveśamātraśeṣatvamāgatāḥ*

## v.37
Of these, no mantra, no meditation, no worship, nor even imagination: no, from samayin to ācārya, anyone, is a resting-place.
The Sanskrit: *eṣāṃ na mantro na dhyānaṃ na pūjā nāpi kalpanā na samayyādikācāryaparyantaḥ ko@pi viśramaḥ*

## v.38
All-restraint-system-break-marks-bearing, not than grace beyond, in any remaining conduct, is the purpose.
The Sanskrit: *samastayantraṇātantratroṭanāṭaṃkadharmiṇaḥ nānugrahātparaṃ kiṃciccheṣavṛttau prayojanam*

## v.39
Own, to be done, whatever, considering the world, this, from effort, not, for others' sake, toward, joins, any own activity, but if, whose every existence-stain is destroyed, full of Bhairavī-nature: the deed, his, clearly this just this-much world-duty merely.
The Sanskrit: *svaṃ kartavyaṃ kimapi kalayaṃlloka eṣa prayatnānno pārārthyaṃ prati ghaṭayate kāṃcana svapravṛttim yastu dhvastākhilabhavamalo bhairavībhāvapūrṇaḥ kṛtyaṃ tasya sphuṭamidamiyallokakartavyamātram*

## v.40
Him, who see, by that-form-order, stainless-aware, they too, of his form, so much, just, of him, grace-naturedness.
The Sanskrit: *taṃ ye paśyanti tādrūpyakrameṇāmalasaṃvidaḥ te@pi tadrūpiṇastāvatyevāsyānugrahātmatā*

## v.41
This tattva-recognition is chief, sacrifice-and-the-rest is called, up to initiation, by the Lord, in the Siddhayogīśvarī doctrine.
The Sanskrit: *etattattvaparijñānaṃ mukhyaṃ yāgādi kathyate dīkṣāntaṃ vibhunā śrīmatsiddhayogīśvarīmate*

## v.42
From the altar, the higher, loom, from the loom northward, the cloth, from cloth, meditation, then, the to-be-meditated, then, should be dhāraṇā higher.
The Sanskrit: *sthaṇḍilāduttaraṃ tūraṃ tūrāduttarataḥ paṭaḥ paṭāddhyānaṃ tato dhyeyaṃ tataḥ syāddhāraṇottarā*

## v.43
Then too, yoga-born, form, then too, knowledge higher, by knowledge, for, greatly accomplished, should become a Yogīśvara — but, quoted.
The Sanskrit: *tato@pi yogajaṃ rūpaṃ tato@pi jñānamuttaram jñānena hi mahāsiddho bhavedyogīśvarastviti*

## v.44
He too, through freedom-abode, if even of the impure-aware, grace, desiring to grace that forthcoming one, he should resort to the rite.
The Sanskrit: *so@pi svātantryadhāmnā cedapyanirmalasaṃvidām anugrahaṃ cikīrṣustadbhāvinaṃ vidhimāśrayet*

## v.45
According to the graced one, varied, it, and, is taught, through Parāparā-and-the-rest-means-flood-mixedness-division.
The Sanskrit: *anugrāhyānusāreṇa vicitraḥ sa ca kathyate parāparādyupāyaughasaṃkīrṇatvavibhedataḥ*

## v.46
For that purpose alone, and of him too, of the Lord-formed one, that means-scripture-and-the-rest-hearing-studying-zeal.
The Sanskrit: *tadarthameva cāsyāpi parameśvararūpiṇaḥ tadabhyupāyaśāstrādiśravaṇādhyayanādaraḥ*

## v.47
For not, of him, of the free, anywhere, wherever, is refutation, not of the impure-minded, of man, grace, but meanslessness.
The Sanskrit: *nahi tasya svatantrasya kāpi kutrāpi khaṇḍanā nānirmalacitaḥ puṃso@nugrahastvanupāyakaḥ*

## v.48
In the venerable Ūrmi great-scripture, Siddha-lineage-shaped: this told, so, by the venerable Somānanda and the teachers.
The Sanskrit: *śrīmadūrmimahāśāstre siddhasaṃtānarūpake idamuktaṃ tathā śrīmatsomānandādidaiśikaiḥ*

## v.49
From the guru's word-and-the-rest, from wiping by heaped reasoning, from assurance — the śāstra, toward, from the arisen, or even, from the told: when dissolved, in the doubt-cloud, that heart-sky-shining-greatness, of the Lord, sun-like, touching, feet darkness-conquering.
The Sanskrit: *gurorvākyādyuktipracayaracanonmārjanavaśāt samāśvāsācchāstraṃ prati samuditādvāpi kathitāt vilīne śaṃkābhre tdṛdayagaganodbhāsimahasaḥ prabhoḥ sūryasyeva spṛśata caraṇāndhvāntajayinaḥ*

## v.50
This anuttara-abode-discerning, means-shed, made: the ahnika.
The Sanskrit: *idamanuttaradhāmavivecakaṃ vigalitaupayikaṃ kṛtamāhnikam*

# C1 — tal-a02

## SUMMARY

The anupāya, the means-less. The first word of unceasing highest-recognition-nature, opened now: fourfold form of awareness, own nature alone to be regarded, ever-arisen pervader. Some enter part-by-part, others partlessly. Means too not to be barred; variety from its otherness. Pure-souled in Bhairavī practice the means-less rule. Rite-yoga undeserves means-hood; distinctly discerned. Means would be awareness-only: luminosity in self-luminous — how from another? Self-luminous absence would make the universe inert. Whatever means, outer or inner, whatsoever: all face-turned-to-it; there, means-bearing — how? Abandon attentions now: where do you place? the unfull does not attain, and the true nature. Direct means discerners for sun-awareness are firefly-desiring dullards. As long as this outer — inner-means-deemed: that mere luminous-natured alone, Śiva's own form. Blue, yellow, pleasure: luminance alone is Śiva; in supreme nonduality, luminous-natured: what other? Means-to-be-attained-relation would be luminance alone — for it. This — duality indeed — this, difference: this nonduality, thus, even in the luminous form alone — this shines, the Supreme Lord. In this level, pleasure, pain, bondage, release — whether conscious or inert: like pot-and-jar, same-meaning words — of them even just one — and. In the shining-forth, for an unlit part, how, indeed, radiance? The unlit part: radiance? obliterated duality. In non-shining, then, in that or: reality, how is it said? Not luminance-specificity unapproved, indeed it follows. Hence, 'one luminance' — thus, in the doctrine here, well-established: from afar warded off, truly, the different-knowledge-sayers. Mere luminance taught through non-shining-denial: of the word 'one', not — but the meaning: is number, bearing consciousness-manifest-difference. Not this: śakti the great goddess, not resting on another, since; nor, and this: śakti-possessing god, not of anyone a resort, since. Not this: the meditated, from meditator-hood, not: the meditator, from meditated-absence; no worshipful, from worshipper-absence, from worshipful-absence: no worshipper. Not mantra, nor, and mantra-able, this; not, and the pronouncer, the Lord; no initiation, initiator, or even; no initiated one, the Great Lord. Place-seat-restraint-offering-mass-invocation-and-the-rest, ending in dismissal: there is not here, in doer-deed-act-abandoned. Not being, not not-being, being, not-being: and that, not both-abandoned: hard to know, for, that state: some, whatever, this unsurpassed. 'This' — thus appearance, for which, being of cut-off nature: it, just, pot-like in the world, situated then: not this is Bhairava. Non-being, and non-shining, not: anywhere usefulness; of the universe, is the life, truly, luminance-natured-alone, and it. By these two just, but from causes: not two-natured, nor two-abandoned; wholly, for, shines it: by what form, let it be mantred? In the venerable Triśiras, told, supreme-knowledge-nature, by śakti dwelling within the womb, śakti-wombed, supreme state. Neither being, nor non-being, nor the pair: by unsayable speech, unsayable-word-escalated, śakti-dwelling, śakti-devoid. Thus, who rooted-knowledge-truth-purified, on the anuttara path, rooted: they, means-unruled. Of them, this, shines together, everywhere, the being-circle, standing-forth just, melted in consciousness-Bhairava-fire. Of these, pleasure-pain-share-doubt-fear-notions, nirvikalpa-entry-only-remainder come to. Of these, no mantra, no meditation, no worship, nor even imagination: no, from samayin to ācārya, anyone, is a resting-place. All-restraint-system-break-marks-bearing, not than grace beyond, in any remaining conduct, is the purpose. Own, to be done, whatever, considering the world, this, from effort, not, for others' sake, toward, joins, any own activity, but if, whose every existence-stain is destroyed, full of Bhairavī-nature: the deed, his, clearly this just this-much world-duty merely. Him, who see, by that-form-order, stainless-aware, they too, of his form, so much, just, of him, grace-naturedness. This tattva-recognition is chief, sacrifice-and-the-rest is called, up to initiation, by the Lord, in the Siddhayogīśvarī doctrine. From the altar, the higher, loom, from the loom northward, the cloth, from cloth, meditation, then, the to-be-meditated, then, should be dhāraṇā higher. Then too, yoga-born, form, then too, knowledge higher, by knowledge, for, greatly accomplished, should become a Yogīśvara — but, quoted. He too, through freedom-abode, if even of the impure-aware, grace, desiring to grace that forthcoming one, he should resort to the rite. According to the graced one, varied, it, and, is taught, through Parāparā-and-the-rest-means-flood-mixedness-division. For that purpose alone, and of him too, of the Lord-formed one, that means-scripture-and-the-rest-hearing-studying-zeal. For not, of the free, anywhere, wherever, is refutation, not of the impure-minded, of man, grace, but meanslessness. In the venerable Ūrmi great-scripture, Siddha-lineage-shaped: this told, so, by the venerable Somānanda and the teachers. From the guru's word-and-the-rest, from wiping by heaped reasoning, from assurance — the śāstra, toward, from the arisen, or even, from the told: when dissolved, in the doubt-cloud, that heart-sky-shining-greatness, of the Lord, sun-like, touching, feet darkness-conquering. This anuttara-abode-discerning, means-shed, made: the ahnika.

## FUNCTION

Establishes anupāya as the means-less: awareness-principle self-luminous, so no path can be a path (means would be the means to the means-less); rite-yoga undeserving means-hood; direct means are dullards for sun-awareness; exclusion unbearable; the pathless is the only path. The chapter closes the āṇava arc's other half — from means to the means-less — with the Bhairava-natured Śiva as the non-dual luminance that shines without any means.

## EXPLANATION

The ahnika runs: (1–3) anupāya announced, the means-less whose nature; (4–6) fourfold form, own nature alone, variety not barred; (7–8) pure-souled means-less practice, rite-yoga undeserving; (9–11) awareness-means would be self-luminous — how? (12) abandon attentions; (13–14) direct means are dullards; (15–19) exclusion unbearable, pot-and-jar same-meaning, pleasure/pain/bondage/release; (20–22) unlit part, non-shining reality, one luminance well-established; (23–28) mere luminance not number, not śakti, not worshipful, not mantra, not initiation, not being; (29–30) cut-off nature is not Bhairava, universe's life is luminous; (31–33) two causes, Triśiras, neither being nor non-being; (34–36) means-unruled, melted in Bhairava-fire, nirvikalpa entry; (37–39) no rest, no purpose in remaining conduct, but-if stain-destroyed; (40–44) stainless-aware, tattva-recognition chief, altar-to-dhāraṇā ladder, yoga-born form, freedom-abode resort; (45–47) varied teaching, hearing-studying zeal, no refutation for the free; (48–49) Somānanda teachers, doubt-cloud dissolved by sun-touching feet; (50) anuttara-abode-discerning, means-shed, ahnika.

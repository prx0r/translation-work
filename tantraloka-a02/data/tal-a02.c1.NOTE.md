- anupāya (m.): means-less; charter word (v1, v50 seal); the fourth beyond the triad.
- anuttara (n.): unsurpassed; the state beyond the three upāyas.
- jñapti (f.): awareness, self-luminous; the principle that makes every means superfluous.
- prakāśa (m.): luminance; alone is Śiva.
- avaccheda (f.): exclusion; unbearable even in dream.
- khadyota-adhitsava (m.): firefly-desiring dullards, against direct means.
- sva-prakāśa (m.): self-luminous principle.
- dvaita-advaita (n.): both shine as the Lord.
- ghaṭakumbha (n.): pot-and-jar same-meaning.
- prakāśa-ekātmaka (adj.): luminance-natured alone.
- anuttarapathe (m.loc.): on the anuttara path, means-unruled.
- saṃvitti-bhairavāgni (m.): melted in consciousness-Bhairava-fire.
- nirvikalpa-parāveśa (m.): nirvikalpa entry only.
- samayi-ācārya-paryanta (m.): no rest from samayin to ācārya.
- samasta-yantraṇā (f.): all-restraint break.
- atiprasaṅga (m.): overreach.
- anuttara-dhāma (n.): anuttara-abode.

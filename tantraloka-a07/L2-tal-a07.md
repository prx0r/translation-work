## v.1
Now this supreme secret, the rising of the cakras, is told.
The Sanskrit: *atha paramarahasyo@yaṃ cakrāṇāṃ bhaṇyate@bhyudayaḥ*

## v.2
Thus effortless spoken; effort-born but is described: seed-syllable-natured all, of consciousness vibration-nature.
The Sanskrit: *ityayatnajamākhyātaṃ yatnajaṃ tu nigadyate bījapiṇḍātmakaṃ sarvaṃ saṃvidaḥ spandanātmatām*

## v.3
Bringing supreme consciousness means thus described, like waterwheel machine bucket flow.
The Sanskrit: *vidadhatparasaṃvittāvupāya iti varṇitam yathāraghaṭṭacakrāgraghaṭīyantraughavāhanam*

## v.4
By single concentration effort wonderful machine-rising should obtain; by single concentration force arisen in mantra-rising night.
The Sanskrit: *ekānusaṃdhiyatnena citraṃ yantrodayaṃ bhajet ekānusaṃdhānabalājjāte mantrodaye@niśam*

## v.5
That mantra-deity by effort-identity becomes pleased; in sky taste-one-eye in eternal-rising, half of that in two-seed-lump.
The Sanskrit: *tanmantradevatā yatnāttādātmyena prasīdati khe rasaikākṣi nityotthe tadardhaṃ dvikapiṇḍake*

## v.6
In three seven thousands two-hundred thus rising held; in four but thousands five and indeed four-hundred.
The Sanskrit: *trike sapta sahasrāṇi dviśatītyudayo mataḥ catuṣke tu sahasrāṇi pañca caiva catuḥśatī*

## v.7
In five-syllable ocean-thousands three-hundred twenty so; in six three-thousands six-hundred rising would be.
The Sanskrit: *pañcārṇe@bdhisahasrāṇi triśatī viṃśatistathā ṣaṭke sahasratritayaṃ ṣaṭśatī codayo bhavet*

## v.8
In seven three-thousands but six-eighty-excess remembered; by hundreds but by twenty-seven in eight-syllable variation.
The Sanskrit: *saptake trisahasraṃ tu ṣaḍaśītyadhikaṃ smṛtam śataistu saptaviṃśatyā varṇāṣṭakavikalpite*

## v.9
By twenty-four hundreds but in nine-syllable rising would be; by sixty-one-twenty of hundreds in ten-syllable.
The Sanskrit: *caturviṃśatiśatyā tu navārṇeṣūdayo bhavet adhiṣaṣṭyekaviṃśatyā śatānāṃ daśavarṇake*

## v.10
Nineteen hundreds sixty-four in Śiva-syllable; eighteen hundreds would be rising in twelve-syllable.
The Sanskrit: *ekānnaviṃśatiśataṃ catuḥṣaṣṭiḥ śivārṇake aṣṭādaśa śatāni syurudayo dvādaśārṇake*

## v.11
In thirteen-syllable by sixty-two hundreds indeed sixteen; by forty-three fifteen thus in bhuvana-syllable.
The Sanskrit: *trayodaśārṇe dvāṣaṣṭyā śatāni kila ṣoḍaśa tricatvāriṃśatā pañcadaśeti bhuvanārṇake*

## v.12
Fourteen hundreds sky-ocean would be in fifteen-syllable; thirteen hundreds with half in sixteen-syllable but is said.
The Sanskrit: *caturdaśaśatī khābdhiḥ syātpañcadaśavarṇake trayodaśaśatī sārdhā ṣoḍaśārṇe tu kathyate*

## v.13
Hundred-twelve in seventeen-syllable with one-seventy; in eighteen-syllable to be known hundred-twelve by wise.
The Sanskrit: *śatadvādaśikā saptadaśārṇe saikasaptatiḥ aṣṭādaśārṇe vijñeyā śatadvādaśikā budhaiḥ*

## v.14
In twenty-four-numbered cakra nine-hundreds would be; in twenty-seven-numbered rising eight-hundreds-natured.
The Sanskrit: *caturviṃśatisaṃkhyāke cakre navaśatī bhavet saptaviṃśatisaṃkhyāte tūdayo@ṣṭaśatātmakaḥ*

## v.15
In thirty-two in great-cakra six-hundreds seventy-five; in twenty-four-doubled cakra with-half hundred-four.
The Sanskrit: *dvātriṃśake mahācakre ṣaṭśatī pañcasaptatiḥ dvicaturviṃśake cakre sārdhāṃ śatacatuṣṭayīm*

## v.16
Rising knower of lump-yoga in lump-mantras should mark; in fifty-four cakra of hundreds but four.
The Sanskrit: *udayaṃ piṇḍayogajñaḥ piṇḍamantreṣu lakṣayet catuṣpañcāśake cakre śatānāṃ tu catuṣṭayam*

## v.17
By thirty-seven-with-half in three-hundred-eight-eight would be; half-half-third-part-and sixty-six-two-hundreds would be.
The Sanskrit: *saptatriṃśatsahārdhena triśatyaṣṭāṣṭake bhavet ardhamardhatribhāgaśca ṣaṭṣaṣṭirdviśatī bhavet*

## v.18
In eighty-one-place cakra rising breath-movement-going; in cakra but in ninety-six-named with-quarter two-hundreds would be.
The Sanskrit: *ekāśītipade cakre udayaḥ prāṇacāragaḥ cakre tu ṣaṇṇavatyākhye sapādā dviśatī bhavet*

## v.19
In hundred-and-eight cakra two-hundreds rising would be; in order thus this cakra six-times doubled when.
The Sanskrit: *aṣṭottaraśate cakre dviśatastūdayo bhavet krameṇetthamidaṃ cakraṃ ṣaṭkṛtvo dviguṇaṃ yadā*

## v.20
Then even doubled-eighth-part-half-middle-half-one; then even by subtle skillful half-half-etc-arrangement.
The Sanskrit: *tato@pi dviguṇe@ṣṭāṃśasyārdhamadhyardhamekakam tato@pi sūkṣmakuśalairardhārdhādiprakalpane*

## v.21
By sixteenth-part-standing subtle movement is marked; thus of yogin whose breath-movement is restrained by effort of yogin.
The Sanskrit: *bhāgaṣoḍaśakasthityā sūkṣmaścāro@bhilakṣyate evaṃ prayatnasaṃruddhaprāṇacārasya yoginaḥ*

## v.22
In order of breath-movement swallowing indeed arises; state of time-contraction obtained by breath-swallowing order.
The Sanskrit: *krameṇa prāṇacārasya grāsa evopajāyate prāṇagrāsakramāvāptakālasaṃkarṣaṇasthitiḥ*

## v.23
Consciousness one indeed full, from removal of knowledge-difference; so indeed of breath-movement in not-rising-of-new being.
The Sanskrit: *saṃvidekaiva pūrṇā syājjñānabhedavyapohanāt tathā hi prāṇacārasya navasyānudaye sati*

## v.24
Not time-difference-born knowledge-difference arises; from object-difference not knowledge different like mountain-circle.
The Sanskrit: *na kālabhedajanito jñānabhedaḥ prakalpate saṃvedyabhedānna jñānaṃ bhinnaṃ śikharivṛttavat*

## v.25
Time but its divider he but subtle moment held; of subtlety limit-knowledge as long as stands he moment.
The Sanskrit: *kālastu bhedakastasya sa tu sūkṣmaḥ kṣaṇo mataḥ saukṣmyasya cāvadhirjñānaṃ yāvattiṣṭhati sa kṣaṇaḥ*

## v.26
Otherwise not he to express by skillful even is able; knowledge how much would be its absence not shines.
The Sanskrit: *anyathā na sa nirvaktuṃ nipuṇairapi pāryate jñānaṃ kiyadbhavettāvattadabhāvo na bhāsate*

## v.27
Its absence and not so long as there in eye-path; in object or in self place or not union-separation.
The Sanskrit: *tadabhāvaśca no tāvadyāvattatrākṣavartmani arthe vātmapradeśe vā na saṃyogavibhāgitā*

## v.28
She if rises vibration-natured that breath-going certainly would be indeed; then in absence of breath-vibration not she would be.
The Sanskrit: *sā cedudayate spandamayī tatprāṇagā dhruvam bhavedeva tataḥ prāṇaspandābhāve na sā bhavet*

## v.29
From its absence not knowledge-absence; so indeed but she indeed intellect not and that in reality long from removal of time-difference.
The Sanskrit: *tadabhāvānna vijñānābhāvaḥ saivaṃ tu saiva dhīḥ na cāsau vastuto dīrghā kālabhedavyapohanāt*

## v.30
In reality indeed here this indeed time consciousness should touch; therefore one indeed consciousness in many forms so so.
The Sanskrit: *vastuto hyata eveyaṃ kālaṃ saṃvinna saṃspṛśet ata ekaiva saṃvittirnānārūpe tathātathā*

## v.31
Knowing even without thought thought in object-domain; other-vibration not as long as that risen so long indeed he.
The Sanskrit: *vindānā nirvikalpāpi vikalpo bhāvagocare spandāntaraṃ na yāvattaduditaṃ tāvadeva saḥ*

## v.32
So long one thought would be various object imagining; who but thus not knowing of them thought does not arise.
The Sanskrit: *tāvāneko vikalpaḥ syādvividhaṃ vastu kalpayan ye tvitthaṃ na vidusteṣāṃ vikalpo nopapadyate*

## v.33
He indeed one not would be someone even in three worlds ever; by word-attachment knowledge thought indeed is called.
The Sanskrit: *sa hyeko na bhavetkaścit trijagatyāpi jātucit śabdārūṣaṇayā jñānaṃ vikalpaḥ kila kathyate*

## v.34
She and would be sequential thus; what how who should think; pot even thus should be led would be thought; what story in state.
The Sanskrit: *sā ca syātkramikaivetthaṃ kiṃ kathaṃ ko vikalpayet ghaṭa ityapi neyānsyādvikalpaḥ kā kathā sthitau*

## v.35
Not thought and someone is who abiding in measure and nonmeasure; not and knowledge-collection is from their non-simultaneity.
The Sanskrit: *na vikalpaśca ko@pyasti yo mātrāmātraniṣṭhitaḥ na ca jñānasamūho@sti teṣāmayugapatsthiteḥ*

## v.36
Thereby set indeed this transaction thought-born; therefore other-vibration as long as not rises so long one.
The Sanskrit: *tenāstaṅgata evaiṣa vyavahāro vikalpajaḥ tasmātspandāntaraṃ yāvannodiyāttāvadekakam*

## v.37
Knowledge that thought-self-attribute-apex even should touch; from eighty-one-place great-śakti-reflection-natured.
The Sanskrit: *vijñānaṃ tadvikalpātmadharmakoṭīrapi spṛśet ekāśītipadodāraśaktyāmarśātmakastataḥ*

## v.38
Thought Śiva-state-giving before indeed described; as ears I make dance thus by effort so would be.
The Sanskrit: *vikalpaḥ śivatādāyī pūrvameva nirūpitaḥ yathā karṇau nartayāmītyevaṃ yatnāttathā bhavet*

## v.39
From cakra movement going effort so that cakra indeed intellect; of japa homa worship and the rest from breath equality therefore rule.
The Sanskrit: *cakracāragatādyatnāttadvattaccakragaiva dhīḥ japahomārcanādīnāṃ prāṇasāmyamato vidhiḥ*

## v.40
In Siddha-doctrine Kuṇḍalinī-śakti breath-equal-transcendent said; and in Yoginī-Kaula that this by Supreme Lord.
The Sanskrit: *siddhāmate kuṇḍalinīśaktiḥ prāṇasamonmanā uktaṃ ca yoginīkaule tadetatparameśinā*

## v.41
In word-mantra-syllable in cakra division śakti-principle-going; in words having made mantra-knower in japa and the rest would become fruit-holder.
The Sanskrit: *padamantrākṣare cakre vibhāgaṃ śaktitattvagam padeṣu kṛtvā mantrajño japādau phalabhāgbhavet*

## v.42
Two-three-seven-eight-numbered should omit śakti-rising; thus śakti-abiding mantras vidyā or cakra-leaders.
The Sanskrit: *dvitrisaptāṣṭasaṃkhyātaṃ lopayecchatikodayam iti śaktisthitā mantrā vidyā vā cakranāyakāḥ*

## v.43
By word-lump own form having known to be joined always dear; in eternal-rising in great-principle in rising-abiding in Sadāśiva.
The Sanskrit: *padapiṇḍasvarūpeṇa jñātvā yojyāḥ sadā priye nityodaye mahātattve udayasthe sadāśive*

## v.44
Unjoined in śakti-path but not recited by rising who; they not succeed by effort recited by hundreds of millions even.
The Sanskrit: *ayuktāḥ śaktimārge tu na japtāścodayena ye te na siddhyanti yatnena japtāḥ koṭiśatairapi*

## v.45
In garland-mantras in all mental recitation is said; or whispered śakti-rising of them not should arrange.
The Sanskrit: *mālāmantreṣu sarveṣu mānaso japa ucyate upāṃśurvā śaktyudayaṃ teṣāṃ na parikalpayet*

## v.46
In word-mantras in all as far as that word-śakti-going is able; constantly by joined so much to be recited but by sādhakas.
The Sanskrit: *padamantreṣu sarveṣu yāvattatpadaśaktigam śakyate satataṃ yuktaistāvajjapyaṃ tu sādhakaiḥ*

## v.47
So much in them indeed number in words called-word; so much rising having made from three-word-statement first in order.
The Sanskrit: *tāvatī teṣu vai saṃkhyā padeṣu padasaṃjñitā tāvantamudayaṃ kṛtvā tripadoktyāditaḥ kramāt*

## v.48
In twelve-named in twelve cakra with-half hundred would be; rising that indeed with four-forty-hundred would be.
The Sanskrit: *dvādaśākhye dvādaśite cakre sārdhaṃ śataṃ bhavet udayastaddhi sacatuścatvāriṃśacchataṃ bhavet*

## v.49
In sixteen-named in twelve in two-ninety-excess in hundred by four-half equal said; hundred twelve-excess.
The Sanskrit: *ṣoḍaśākhye dvādaśite dvānavatyadhike śate cārārdhena samaṃ proktaṃ śataṃ dvādaśakādhikam*

## v.50
In sixteen-named in sixteen would be eighty-four; rising two-hundreds that indeed fifty-six-excess.
The Sanskrit: *ṣoḍaśākhye ṣoḍaśite bhaveccaturaśītigaḥ udayo dviśataṃ taddhi ṣaṭpañcāśatsamuttaram*

## v.51
Four eight-parts three here wise say excess; in eight-eight in twelve quarter-half twenty vasus.
The Sanskrit: *cārāṣṭabhāgāṃstrīnatra kathayantyadhikānbudhāḥ aṣṭāṣṭake dvādaśite pādārdhaṃ viṃśatiṃ vasūn*

## v.52
Rising seven-hundreds with-eight sixty since indeed he; this cakra-rising spoken of sādhakas beneficial.
The Sanskrit: *udayaḥ saptaśatikā sāṣṭā ṣaṣṭiryato hi saḥ eṣa cakrodayaḥ proktaḥ sādhakānāṃ hitāvahaḥ*

## v.53
Having restrained mental-fluctuations in cakra having attained rest; having risen as long as rests so long movement-rising indeed this.
The Sanskrit: *niruddhya mānasīrvṛttīścakre viśrāntimāgataḥ vyutthāya yāvadviśrāmyettāvaccārodayo hyayam*

## v.54
In full in rise but here entry-identity-exit three; thus is said in accomplishment middle-rising best.
The Sanskrit: *pūrṇe samudaye tvatra praveśaikātmyanirgamāḥ traya ityata evoktaḥ siddhau madhyodayo varaḥ*

## v.55
Free from first and last rising endowed with middle rising; mantra-vidyā-cakra-groups partaking of accomplishment become indeed.
The Sanskrit: *ādyantodayanirmuktā madhyamodayasaṃyutāḥ mantravidyācakragaṇāḥ siddhibhājo bhavanti hi*

## v.56
Mantra-cakra-rising-knower but vidyā-cakra-rising-meaning-knower; quickly would accomplish thus said in venerable twenty in three.
The Sanskrit: *mantracakrodayajñastu vidyācakrodayārthavit kṣipraṃ siddhyediti proktaṃ śrīmaddviṃśatike trike*

## v.57
Two three four or by syllables vidyā or cakra indeed or; with principle-rising always separate should recite always.
The Sanskrit: *dvistriścaturvā mātrābhirvidyāṃ vā cakrameva vā tattvodayayutaṃ nityaṃ pṛthagbhūtaṃ japetsadā*

## v.58
By lump-syllable-word mantra one by one śakti-principle-going; many-syllabled but who mantra vidyā or cakra indeed or.
The Sanskrit: *piṇḍākṣarapadairmantramekaikaṃ śaktitattvagam bahvakṣarastu yo mantro vidyā vā cakrameva vā*

## v.59
Śakti-abiding not indeed him there; division but oṃ-ending-going; in this principle-rising therefore day-night three-by-three.
The Sanskrit: *śaktisthaṃ naiva taṃ tatra vibhāgastvoṃnamontagaḥ asmiṃstattvodaye tasmādahorātrastriśastriśaḥ*

## v.60
Is divided division and again indeed three-by-three; in first-rising but having rested by second should rise when.
The Sanskrit: *vibhajyate vibhāgaśca punareva triśastriśaḥ pūrvodaye tu viśramya dvitīyenollasedyadā*

## v.61
Should enter half-half-union thus spoken half-rising would be when; full-rising-self but equal time in three should shine.
The Sanskrit: *viśeccārdhardhikāyogāttadoktārdhodayo bhavet yadā pūrṇodayātmā tu samaḥ kālastrike sphuret*

## v.62
In entry-rest-rise would be own-third-part-rising then; thus this time-abundance breath indeed established.
The Sanskrit: *praveśaviśrāntyullāse syātsvatryaṃśodayastadā etyeṣa kālavibhavaḥ prāṇa eva pratiṣṭhitaḥ*

## v.63
He in place in sky he in that consciousness; by him in this universe-abiding; therefore consciousness-establishment two from which universe-dissolution-rising two.
The Sanskrit: *sa spade khe sa taccityāṃ tenāsyāṃ viśvaniṣṭhiatiḥ ataḥ saṃvitpratiṣṭhānau yato viśvalayodayau*

## v.64
In śakti-end-sound that vibration countless real; then said in venerable Mālinī-tantra in body wherever anywhere.
The Sanskrit: *śaktyante@dhvani tatspandāsaṃkhyātā vāstavī tataḥ uktaṃ śrīmālinītantre gātre yatraiva kutracit*

## v.65
Modification would arise that principle principle supreme; in breath established time then absorbed and which body.
The Sanskrit: *vikāra upajāyeta tattattvaṃ tattvamuttamam prāṇe pratiṣṭhitaḥ kālastadāviṣṭā ca yattanuḥ*

## v.66
In body of this established in body then form is determined; of consciousness-vibration-breath-functions last which grossness suṣumnā.
The Sanskrit: *dehe pratiṣṭhitasyāsya tato rūpaṃ nirūpyate citspandaprāṇavṛttīnāmantyā yā sthūlatā suṣiḥ*

## v.67
She having become nāḍī-form body should spread this; in venerable Svacchanda there is said as leaf by its threads.
The Sanskrit: *sā nāḍīrūpatāmetya dehaṃ saṃtānayedimam śrīsvacchande@ta evoktaṃ yathā parṇaṃ svatantubhiḥ*

## v.68
Pervaded like that body by door-water-nature by nāḍīs; from big toe up to Brahmakuṇḍalinī end.
The Sanskrit: *vyāptaṃ tadvattanurdvāradvāribhāvena nāḍibhiḥ pādāṅguṣṭhādikordhvasthabrahmakuṇḍalikāntagaḥ*

## v.69
Time all in eighty-four finger-widths thus; up to twelve-end some subtle time-abiding know.
The Sanskrit: *kālaḥ samastaścaturaśītāvevāṅguleṣvitaḥ dvādaśāntāvadhiṃ kiṃcitsūkṣmakālasthitiṃ viduḥ*

## v.70
In ninety-six below six two-fold and hundred and eight hundred; here in middle-moving breath-rising-dissolution-interval.
The Sanskrit: *ṣaṇṇavatyāmadhaḥ ṣaḍdvikramāccāṣṭottaraṃ śatam atra madhyamasaṃcāriprāṇodayalayāntare*

## v.71
In universe creation-dissolution they but wondrous from wind-intermediate-order; thus this subtle-reflection-to-be-cultivated cakra-rising by experience-scripture-view spoken by me.
The Sanskrit: *viśve sṛṣṭilayāste tu citrā vāyvantarakramāt ityeṣa sūkṣmaparimarśanaśīlanīyaścakrodayo@nubhavaśāstradṛśā mayoktaḥ*

# C1 — tal-a07

## SUMMARY

The ahnika teaches the cakrodaya: the rise of mantra cycles in the breath, from effortless to effort-born, through numerical rises, time and knowledge, japa rules, and the body as field of time.

## FUNCTION

Establishes cakrodaya: the rise of mantra cycles in the breath — effort-born concentration swallows time, unifies consciousness, and the body becomes the field of time.

## KEY TERMS

cakrodaya (rise of cakras), prāṇacāra (breath movement), grāsa (swallowing), saṃvitti (consciousness), kṣaṇa (moment), vikalpa (thought), mantra, vidyā, japa, śakti, prāṇa, nāḍī, suṣumnā, Brahmakuṇḍalinī, Mālinī-tantra, Svacchanda

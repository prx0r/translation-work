# C1 — tal-a12

## SUMMARY

The use of the path, our present subject. This whole path rests in consciousness and through that in void, intellect, breath, channel-wheels, bodies — and outside in liṅga, image, fire, altar, everywhere, whole and part-wise, in sequence and all-at-once. Laid from consciousness-principle to the outer, each level shines by own freedom in fitting form; everything's form everywhere shines, and exclusion is unbearable even in dream. The body beheld as universe-path-filled, time-painted, the space-time-pulse abode; so beheld, deity-made within the universe, to be meditated, worshipped, libated — entered, freed. Pot, cloth, liṅga, altar, book, water, whatever seen anywhere: reach identity there. Offering is worship through non-difference; full-formedness-contemplation is meditation. Unshaking fullness-contemplation brings firmness; inner-mutter reflection makes a mantra-vessel. Melting self-difference of the offered, bestow their ray-being, fire-rite-devoted. Equal-eyed action makes unwaveringness the pure vow — Nandiśikhā evenness; the ordered sixfold practice completed is what the old gurus called absorption. Materials have neither rule nor prohibition, outward or inward; visualization-rites are unused, sacrifice arising toward occasion per Trika Sūtra. Rule-knowing or not, positions at length: when one's own consciousness is gracious — then each enjoyable is the rule, and all, worldly and unworldly, is employed by that. Tremblers forcibly diminish tremor by any means, sequential or sudden. Doubt melts within; strive, and libate the mind-rite-sense goddesses with forbidden things. Salute the hero-vow, says Bhargaśikhā: doubt is impurity, weariness, contraction. In the world-prison doubt forges gross-post shackles — yet mantras are letter-natured, substance five-elemented, the breathing kind consciousness-selfed: where is mixture? None — so what is doubt, even hers? Don't doubt; disregard dissolves it, in Sarvācāra, Vīrālī, Niśācara, Krama and the rest. The śāstras spread it: doubt breeds weariness and obstacle-vessels. Utpaladeva, guru of my guru, spoke the all-doubt thunderbolt path — we bow to the Māheśvara. For the anuttara state, this at-length-shown āṇava with means — make it grow, O wise.

## FUNCTION

Establishes the path's application as cosmic-body practice: consciousness-grounded ubiquity (in channels, objects, acts), worship redefined as non-dual offering and fullness-contemplation, vow as unwavering evenness, samādhi as their completion; materials freed from rule, occasion-graced consciousness as sole criterion; tremor managed, doubt melted and starved by forbidden libation, impurity denied, disregard prescribed, canon and lineage invoked — closing the a05–a12 āṇava arc with a propagation command.

## EXPLANATION

The ahnika runs: (1) application announced; (2–3) path ubiquitous in consciousness, channels, objects, in every mode; (4–5) nondual shining, no exclusion; (6–7) body as universe-path abode, beheld-as-deity and freed; (8–11) tanmayatā rule, worship/meditation redefined, firmness and mantra-fitness, ray-bestowing fire-rite; (12–13) sāmya-vow and ancients' samādhi; (14–17) materials freed, occasion-arising sacrifice, grace-criterion, all employed; (18–19) tremor management, doubt-melting with forbidden libation; (20–22) hero-vow, doubt's nature and prison, purity of mantra/substance/living, no mixture; (23–25) disregard, canon spread, Utpaladeva's thunderbolt; (26) anuttara telos with propagation command.

# C1 NOTE — tal-a12 (not the translation: commentary complete)

## KEY TERMS (with grounds)

- upayoga (m.): application of the sixfold path; charter word closing the āṇava arc (v1, v26).
- saṃvidi saṃsthita (m.): resting in consciousness; the ground of ubiquity (v2).
- krama-akrama (abl.): in sequence and all-at-once; every mode of presence (v3).
- abhedabhāvanā (f.): nondual shining-forth by own freedom (v4).
- avaccheda (f.): exclusion; unbearable even in dream (v5, first-person avowal).
- viśvādhva (m.): universe-path filling the beheld body (v6).
- tanmayatā (f.): identity-with-whatever-seen (v8, Dyczkowski/Hindi gloss).
- abhedenārcana (n.): worship through non-difference (v9).
- saṃpūrṇarūpatva-anusaṃdhi (f.): full-formedness-contemplation = meditation (v9).
- japabhājana (n.): mantra-vessel (v10, flagged lightly).
- raśmisadbhāva (m.): ray-rich being bestowed (v11).
- sāmya (n.): evenness; Nandiśikhā's pure vow (v12).
- samādhi (m.): ancients' complete state via ordered sixfold practice (v13).
- dravya (n.): materials; neither rule nor prohibition (v14).
- prasīdati saṃvittiḥ (f.): gracious consciousness; sole criterion (v16).
- kampahrāsa (m.): tremor-diminishing by any means (v18).
- vicikitsā-galana (n.): doubt-melting; forbidden libation to sense-goddesses (v19).
- vīravrata (n.): hero-vow saluted (Bhargaśikhā) (v20).
- śaṅkā-mālinya-glāni-saṃkoca: doubt's threefold nature (v20).
- saṃkara-abhāva (m.): no mixture in mantra/substance/living (v21–v22).
- avahelā (f.): disregard dissolving doubt (v23, left-hand canon).
- sarvāśaṅkāśani mārga (m.): all-doubt thunderbolt path (Utpaladeva) (v25).
- vidāṃkurudhvam: propagate! (imper 2pl, v26, arc-closing command).

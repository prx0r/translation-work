# C1 — tal-a14

## SUMMARY

The nature of concealment, as told, examined: from own nature the Lord touches the rule-bound and rule-free order. Touching, he shines — self-willed; resorting to action-fruit rule, he ordains creation-preservation-destruction intermediately, endless at the great scale. One universe-self Lord drops rule; intermediate creation and unforced stasis stand. Form kept, rule dropped; yet by niyati through self-concealing, suffering-delusion enjoyed: then the fruit-order — karmic restraint abandoned, delusion-enveloped. Desiring to shine, he sits disregardful in concealment, awakened as unawakened, uncrossed as crossed, performing fool-conduct he hates. The heart-fool is the awakened one's conduct, says the Vidyādhva in the Mānastotra: initiated dissenters lack the sun-appointment (lotuses don't shrink from sunbeams); the learned gone wrong breed false awakening (snake-fat lamplight); lead stays copper, nectar undrunk leaves thirst. Hate kindles inward burning — doubt itself. No karma brought him here; no precedent binds. The Lord's will deludes him into suffering-vesselhood, varied here/after, slow/fast — Kāla, Kāma, Andhaka, Laṅkā-dwellers and the rest. A second concealment: samaya-violation (Ānandagahvara) — carrion-eaterhood a hundred years, graded manifold. Yet through freedom the concealed returns, by himself or another's door, grace amply; expiation plus re-initiation restores Śiva-nature; even the concealed dead are graced through kin, teachers, śaktipāta, timed variously. In the five — creation through grace — doership is Śiva-hood of consciousness-self. The five-free yogin's worship ever stands united. No delight in magic; even Sadāśiva-bliss binds the unfit. Knowerness alone is Śiva-hood, freedom — not potter-doership, superintended always. Grasp without refutation; 'I am Śiva — why not the world?' unanswered. Flash 'may they follow my will' in the I-knower; the fashioned body follows. Whose body proves Śiva? Unspeakable. The Ūrmi-Mahākula toilers wind-wander; the fraud-deluded ask if departure liberates everywhere. If God is everywhere, where depart? If partial, pot-like — the departure-rite taught partially. No departure fits the partless; cliff-water-fire fasts don't either. Don't eject the soul from longing. Pūrvaśāstra defines departure for enjoyment-loss, not liberation; the detached perfected one may leave, quoted. Bhīṣma precedent: self-willed death on enjoyment-distaste. The siddhi-body can't be shed except by the stated sequence. Mālinītantra: deem all enjoyment ugly. The teaching: one's own freedom in all duties, reality unseen by all. Chief dīkṣā: freedom-teaching, Śiva-giving, Bhairavī-radiant. Thought-free behold the pervaded universe; thought-bound reach Śiva at death. Thought-free now: living-liberated, no doubt. Even spark-fragments of the burnt world-tree blaze into the pure-awakening-fire-mass. Thus the initiation-approach shown, scriptures concurring.

## FUNCTION

Establishes concealment as the Lord's free fourth act (not karmic payback): self-willed rule-adoption, delusion-theater of the awakened, Mānastotra similes for dissenters, doubt-as-burn, will-delusion with varied suffering, samaya-violation with carrion fruit; grace returning by self/other/after-death with re-initiation; five-act doership as Śiva-hood; ever-risen practices without magic-shows; knowerness over potter-doership; unrefuted 'I am Śiva'; Mālinītantra's ugly-enjoyment; freedom-teaching as chief dīkṣā; thought-free beholding vs death-reaching; jīvanmukti now; spark-to-fire-mass close — with the utkrānti polemic (paradox of the pervading God, partless unfittingness, Pūrvaśāstra's enjoyment-loss definition, Bhīṣma precedent, siddhi-body inseparability) nested as the uselessness-of-dying proof.

## EXPLANATION

The ahnika runs: (1–6) concealment's nature — rule/rulelessness touched, intermediate ordaining, form kept/rule dropped, fruit-order conditions, delusion-envelopment; (7–9) awakened-as-unawakened theater with Vidyādhva's seal; (10–12) Mānastotra's three similes for dissenters, strayers, unreversed states; (13–14) doubt as burn, karma denied as cause; (15–17) will-delusion, varied suffering, named sufferers; (18–19) samaya-violation concealment with carrion fruit, graded; (20–23) grace by self/other/after-death with re-initiation, śaktipāta timing; (24–27) five-act doership as Śiva-hood, ever-risen practices, no magic, knowerness over doership; (28–30) unrefuted 'I am Śiva', flashing will, unspeakable body-proof; (31–43) Ūrmi toilers, departure-question, God-everywhere paradox, pot-dilemma, partless unfittingness, no-ejection rule, Pūrvaśāstra definition, detached permission, Bhīṣma precedent, siddhi-body inseparability, ugly-enjoyment, freedom-teaching as chief dīkṣā; (44–47) thought-free beholding, jīvanmukti now, spark-to-fire-mass, scriptural seal.

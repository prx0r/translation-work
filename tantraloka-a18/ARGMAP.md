# Tantrāloka Ahnika 18 — ARGMAP

## tal-a18-p01 (18.1-18.10)
- speaker: Abhinava.
- question: (passage 18.1-18.10).
- premises: [and]-:C18 atha śrītantrāloke aṣṭādaśamāhnikam atha saṃkṣiptadīkṣeyaṃ śivatāpattidocyate / na rajo nādhivāso@tra na bhūkṣetraparigrahaḥ / yatra tatra pradeśe tu pūjayitvā guruḥ śivam adhvānaṃ manasā dhyātvā dīkṣayettattvapāragaḥ / jananādivihīnāṃ tu yena yenādhvanā guruḥ kuryātsa ekatattvāntāṃ śivabhāvaikabhāvitaḥ / parāmantrastato@syeti tattvaṃ saṃśodhayāmyatha svāheti pratitattvaṃ syācchuddhe pūrṇāhutiṃ kṣipet / evaṃ mantrāntaraiḥ kuryātsamastairathavoktavat parāsaṃpuṭitaṃ nāma svāhāntaṃ prathamāntakam / śataṃ sahasraṃ sāṣṭaṃ vā tena śaktyaiva homayet tataḥ pūrṇeti saṃśodhyahīnamuttamamīdṛśam / dīkṣākarmoditaṃ tatra tatra śāstre maheśinā pratyekaṃ mātṛkāyugmavarṇaistattvāni śodhayet / yadi vā piṇḍamantreṇa sarvamantreṣvayaṃ vidhiḥ yathā yathā ca svabhyastajñānastanmayatātmakaḥ / gurustathā tathā kuryāt saṃkṣiptaṃ karma nānyathā śrībrahmayāmale coktaṃ saṃkṣipte@pi hi bhāvayet / vyāptiṃ sarvādhvasāmānyāṃ kiṃtu yāge na vistaraḥ atanmayībhūtamiti vikṣiptaṃ karma sandadhat / kramāttādātmyametīti vikṣiptaṃ vidhimācaret
- inference: [and]-:C18 atha śrītantrāloke aṣṭādaśamāhnikam atha saṃkṣiptadīkṣeyaṃ śivatāpattidocyate / na rajo nādhivāso@tra na bhūkṣetraparigrahaḥ / yatra tatra pradeśe tu pūjayitvā guruḥ śivam adhvānaṃ manasā dhyātvā dīkṣayettattvapāragaḥ / jananādivihīnāṃ tu yena yenādhvanā guruḥ kuryātsa ekatattvāntāṃ śivabhāvaikabhāvitaḥ / parāmantrastato@syeti tattvaṃ saṃśodhayāmyatha svāheti pratitattvaṃ syācchuddhe pūrṇāhutiṃ kṣipet / evaṃ mantrāntaraiḥ kuryātsamastairathavoktavat parāsaṃpuṭitaṃ nāma svāhāntaṃ prathamāntakam / śataṃ sahasraṃ sāṣṭaṃ vā tena śaktyaiva homayet tataḥ pūrṇeti saṃśodhyahīnamuttamamīdṛśam / dīkṣākarmoditaṃ tatra tatra śāstre maheśinā pratyekaṃ mātṛkāyugmavarṇaistattvāni śodhayet / yadi vā piṇḍamantreṇa sarvamantreṣvayaṃ vidhiḥ yathā yathā ca svabhyastajñānastanmayatātmakaḥ / gurustathā tathā kuryāt saṃkṣiptaṃ karma nānyathā śrībrahmayāmale coktaṃ saṃkṣipte@pi hi bhāvayet / vyāptiṃ sarvādhvasāmānyāṃ kiṃtu yāge na vistaraḥ atanmayībhūtamiti vikṣiptaṃ karma sandadhat / kramāttādātmyametīti vikṣiptaṃ vidhimācaret
- conclusion: (continued).
- unresolved: none.

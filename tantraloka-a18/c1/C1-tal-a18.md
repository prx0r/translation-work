# C1 — tal-a18

## SUMMARY

Concise initiation toward Śiva-hood, dispensed of powder-mandala, eve-rite, and site-taking — worship kept, place freed. The tattva-shore-goer meditates the path in mind and initiates the birth-freed soul by whatever route he proceeds. The rite is made to end in the single tattva, steeped in Śiva-mood, sealed by the Parā-word 'Now I purify the tattva.' Each tattva takes 'svāhā' and the full oblation upon cleansing; all mantras follow, or tradition bounds. The mantra stands Parā-enclosed, svāhā-ending, first-ending; counted by hundreds or hundred-eight, offered with śakti. 'Pūrṇā' seals the unbroken highest rite, taught across scriptures by the Great Lord. Tattvas are cleansed singly by Mātṛkā-pair letters — or by the condensed mantra, a universal rule. Only the well-practiced, identity-made guru may shorten: capacity gates concision. The Brahmayāmala warrants it: inward pervasion of all paths kept whole, outer elaboration alone cut. Even the doubting 'not-yet-identical' one joins the scattered rite through sequence into identity. Grace-uttered by Śiva, the rule stands recorded in Dīkṣottara, Kiraṇa, and each teaching.

## FUNCTION

Establishes the concise rite as subtraction of outer apparatus with preservation of inner totality: mind-path for ground, Parā-word for act, pervasion-contemplation for completeness, guru-ripeness for license, and scriptural multitude for authority — closing with the pastoral admission of the scattered practitioner.

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Āhnika 18 belongs to the Tantraloka's dīkṣā arc (a14–a17 elaborate rites, a15 the 613-verse samaya Everest): the short path for the ripe guru and the grace-uttered rule. Cross-links: Mālinīvijaya (three samāveśas, the upāya ladder behind ekatattva); Agni Purāṇa 89 ekatattva-dīkṣā (laghutva parallel, lore); Brahmayāmala (vyāpti warrant); Dīkṣottara and Kiraṇāgama (canon list); a20 (śeṣavartana kin, Tantrasara) as rite kin.

## EXPLANATION

The ahnika runs: (1) charter — goal kept, threefold apparatus shed, worship kept, place freed; (2) mechanism — mind-meditated path, competent initiator, freed initiate, guru-chosen route; (3) core — single-tattva making steeped in Śiva-mood, then the spoken Parā-purification; (4) per-tattva seal-and-offering generalized across mantras; (5) mantra-frame with three marks, scaled count, śakti-powered offering; (6) 'pūrṇā' seal, multi-scriptural attestation, divine utterer; (7) analytic letters or synthetic seed, universally lawful; (8) competence gate — realization licenses shortening; (9) Brahmayāmala warrant — whole pervasion inwardly, no outer expanse; (10) pastoral hinge — doubt joined through sequence in the scattered form; (11) canon seal — grace-uttered, multiply recorded.

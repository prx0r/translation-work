# C1 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 18

## tal-a18-p01–p02 — ahnika 18
**SUMMARY.** Tantrāloka Ahnika 18: [and]-:C18 atha śrītantrāloke aṣṭādaśamāhnikam atha saṃkṣiptadīkṣeyaṃ śivatāpattidocyate / na rajo nādhivāso@tra na bhūkṣetraparigrahaḥ / yatra tatra pradeśe tu pūjayitvā guruḥ śivam adhvānaṃ manasā dhyātvā dīkṣayettattvapāragaḥ / jananādivihīnāṃ tu yena yenādhvanā guruḥ kuryātsa ekatattvāntāṃ śivabhāvaikabhāvitaḥ / parāmantrastato@syeti tattvaṃ saṃśodhayāmyatha svāheti pratitattvaṃ syācchuddhe pūrṇāhutiṃ kṣipet / evaṃ mantrāntaraiḥ kuryātsamastairathavoktavat parāsaṃpuṭitaṃ nāma svāhāntaṃ prathamāntakam / śataṃ sahasraṃ sāṣṭaṃ vā tena śaktyaiva homayet tataḥ pūrṇeti saṃśodhyahīnamuttamamīdṛśam / dīkṣākarmoditaṃ tatra tatra śāstre maheśinā pratyekaṃ mātṛkāyugmavarṇaistattvāni śodhayet / yadi vā piṇḍamantreṇa sarvamantreṣvayaṃ vidhiḥ yathā yathā ca svabhyastajñānastanmayatātmakaḥ / gurustathā tathā kuryāt saṃkṣiptaṃ karma nānyathā śrībrahmayāmale coktaṃ saṃkṣipte@pi hi bhāvayet / vyāptiṃ sarvādhvasāmānyāṃ kiṃtu yāge na vistaraḥ atanmayībhūtamiti vikṣiptaṃ karma sandadhat / kramāttādātmyametīti vikṣiptaṃ vidhimācaret [and]-saṃkṣipto vidhirukto@yaṃ kṛpayā yaḥ śivoditaḥ / dīkṣottare kairaṇe ca tatra tatrāpi śāsane

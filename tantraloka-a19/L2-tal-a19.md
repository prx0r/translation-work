## v.1
Now the initiation giving immediate departure is set forth: from that moment, or from enjoyment, at body's fall one may go to Śiva — so hinted in the Mālinīśāstra by the Great Lord.
The Sanskrit: *atha sadyaḥsamutkrāntipradā dīkṣā nirūpyate tatkṣaṇāccopabhogādvā dehapāte śivaṃ vrajet ityuktyā mālinīśāstre sūcitāsau maheśinā*

## v.2
At body's fall, when death is near, from śaktipāta's clearness, having obtained Śāṃkara initiation: hence from the initiation-moment, the supreme.
The Sanskrit: *dehapāte samīpasthe śaktipātasphuṭatvataḥ āsādya śāṃkarīṃ dīkṣāṃ tasmāddīkṣākṣaṇātparam*

## v.3
The meaning 'may go to Śiva' was explained here, from prior-posterior discernment, by our venerable guru Śambhunātha.
The Sanskrit: *śivaṃ vrajedityartho@tra pūrvāparavivecanāt vyākhyātaḥ śrīmatāsmākaṃ guruṇā śambhumūrtinā*

## v.4
When death is near and śaktipāta arises, there, in mild cases, by guru-service life may go to diminution.
The Sanskrit: *yadā hyāsannamaraṇe śaktipātaḥ prajāyate tatra mande@tha gurvādisevayāyuḥ kṣayaṃ vrajet*

## v.5
Or else, through kin-friends-and-the-rest, the Omnipresent's [grace] falls; or before, the samayin has not at all obtained supreme initiation.
The Sanskrit: *athavā bandhumitrādidvārā sāsya vibhoḥ patet pūrvaṃ vā samayī naiva parāṃ dīkṣāmavāptavān*

## v.6
Even the initiated, desiring to abandon life-breaths, freed from affliction: the final guru should then perform immediate-departure initiation.
The Sanskrit: *āptadīkṣo@pi vā prāṇāñjihāsuḥ kleśavarjitam antyāngurustadā kuryātsadya+utkrāntidīkṣaṇam*

## v.7
But not in immature impurity, nor even in remaining-karmic-body, should departure be performed: so set forth in the venerable Gahvara.
The Sanskrit: *natvapakvamale nāpi śeṣakārmikavigrahe kuryādutkramaṇaṃ śrīmadgahvare ca nirūpitam*

## v.8
Having seen the disciple age-seized, oppressed by disease, having made depart, then he should join this one in the supreme principle.
The Sanskrit: *dṛṣṭvā śiṣyaṃ jarāgrastaṃ vyādhinā paripīḍitam utkramayya tatastvenaṃ paratattve niyojayet*

## v.9
In the qualifier-qualified relation, from desire-conduct-arrangement, the aforesaid things were here set forth by venerable Śambhunātha.
The Sanskrit: *viśeṣaṇaviśeṣyatve kāmacāravidhānataḥ pūrvoktamarthajātaṃ śrīśambhunātra nirūpitam*

## v.10
Having performed all the aforesaid rite, from samaya-purity, he should place his blazing marma-scissors knife.
The Sanskrit: *vidhiṃ pūrvoditaṃ sarvaṃ kṛtvā samayaśuddhitaḥ kṣurikāmasya vinyasyejjvalantīṃ marmakartarīm*

## v.11
Having performed all the aforesaid nyāsa, equal-to-fire-of-time, by withdrawal-sequence together with the two scissors.
The Sanskrit: *kṛtvā pūrvoditaṃ nyāsaṃ kālānalasamaprabham saṃhṛtikramataḥ sārdhaṃ sṛkchindiyugalena tu*

## v.12
Having performed fire-natured dhāraṇā, all-marma-scorching: he should fill the body with breath from thumb to head-end.
The Sanskrit: *āgneyīṃ dhāraṇāṃ kṛtvā sarvamarmapratāpanīm pūrayedvāyunā dehamaṅguṣṭhānmastakāntakam*

## v.13
Having drawn up from thumb to upper-end, by the to-be-spoken he should cut the marmas from the hole-end, by Kālarātrī dismiss.
The Sanskrit: *tamutkṛṣya tato@ṅguṣṭhādūrdhvāntaṃ vakṣyamāṇayā kṛntenmarmāṇi randhrāntāt kālarātryā visarjayet*

## v.14
By this sequential yoga, yoked, libation-abandoned: even the samayin goes to that initiation — thus in the Mālinī doctrine.
The Sanskrit: *anena kramayogena yojito hutivarjitaḥ samayyapyeti tāṃ dīkṣāmiti śrīmālinīmate*

## v.15
From sixteen-supports-six-wheels-three-targets-five-ether, or somewhere else, as before the beast-rite.
The Sanskrit: *ṣoḍaśādhāraṣaṭcakralakṣyatrayakhapañcakāt kvacidanyataratrātha prāguktapaśukarmavat*

## v.16
Having entered the root from the kanda-region, cutting from non-difference-discernment, by full-oblation use he should join in his own-desired abode.
The Sanskrit: *praviśya mūlaṃ kandādeśchindannaikyavibhāvanāt pūrṇāhutiprayogeṇa sveṣṭe dhāmni niyojayet*

## v.17
Knowledge-trident, blazing, radiant-three-wheels-blazing: having meditated by that, his feeling, awakening, whirling.
The Sanskrit: *jñānatriśūlaṃ saṃdīptaṃ dīptacakratrayojjvalam cintayitvāmunā tasya vedanaṃ bodhanaṃ bhramam*

## v.18
Inflaming, striking, pricking, moving, again and again from-kanda-wheel-going: he should perform specially in the heart-lotus.
The Sanskrit: *dīpanaṃ tāḍanaṃ todaṃ calanaṃ ca punaḥ punaḥ kandādicakragaṃ kuryādviśeṣeṇa hṛdambuje*

## v.19
At the twelve-end, then, having performed, in the bindu-pair he should cast: in the unmarked or supreme abode, joined to the Supreme Lord.
The Sanskrit: *dvādaśānte tataḥ kṛtvā binduyugmagate kṣipet nirlakṣye vā pare dhāmni saṃyuktaḥ parameśvaraḥ*

## v.20
Not should he perform any saṃskāra for him: thus says the god in the Gahvara — what of his full one, by śrāddha-and-the-rest considered?
The Sanskrit: *na tasya kuryātsaṃskāraṃ kaṃcidityāha gahvare devaḥ kimasya pūrṇasya śrāddhādyairiti bhāvitaḥ*

## v.21
In the venerable Dīkṣottara this rite, fire-put-in: haṃsa, man below his, with Rudra-bindu.
The Sanskrit: *śrīmaddīkṣottare tveṣa vidhirvahnipuṭīkṛtaḥ haṃsaḥ pumānadhastasya rudrabindusamanvitaḥ*

## v.22
In the disciple's body joined, this unagitated: a hundred should he mutter; having departed by upward blink, the disciple thus may go supreme.
The Sanskrit: *śiṣyadehe niyojyaitadanudvagnaḥ śataṃ japet utkramyordhvanimeṣeṇa śiṣya itthaṃ paraṃ vrajet*

## v.23
This indeed is the rite in the venerable Siddhayogīśvarī doctrine: this departure-initiation to be performed by the yogin's guru.
The Sanskrit: *eṣa eva vidhiḥ śrīmatsiddhayogīśvarīmate iyamutkrāmaṇī dīkṣā kartavyā yogino guroḥ*

## v.24
Unpracticed in prāṇa-conduct, how will he perform this? The to-be-spoken Brahma-vidyā, with parts, partless-included.
The Sanskrit: *anabhyastaprāṇacāraḥ kathamenāṃ kariṣyati vakṣyamāṇāṃ brahmavidyāṃ sakalāṃ niṣkalombhitām*

## v.25
In his ear, or should read again, again, or then should cause to read; himself and rite should perform, tattva-purity-and-the-rest, the guru.
The Sanskrit: *karṇe@sya vā paṭhedbhūyo bhūyo vāpyatha pāṭhayet svayaṃ ca karma kurvīta tattvaśuddhyādikaṃ guruḥ*

## v.26
By mantra-rite-force, full-oblation-thus, he should join to the supreme; without yoga-practice even, immediate-departure-giving, the guru.
The Sanskrit: *mantrakriyābalātpūrṇāhutyetthaṃ yojayetpare yogābhyāsamakṛtvāpi sadya+utkrāntidāṃ guruḥ*

## v.27
By knowledge-mantra-rite-meditation-force to perform: should be able; by this is departed the disciple, by force alone, one moment.
The Sanskrit: *jñānamantrakriyādhyānabalātkartuṃ bhavetprabhuḥ anayotkramyate śiṣyo balādevaikakaṃ kṣaṇam*

## v.28
Having transgressed time, enjoyment is momentary; what then? Immediate-departure-giving and another, in which full-oblation then.
The Sanskrit: *kālasyollaṅghya bhogo hi kṣaṇiko@syāstu kiṃ tataḥ sadya+utkrāntidā cānyā yasyāṃ pūrṇāhutiṃ tadā*

## v.29
Should give when his life-breaths would certainly be desiring to depart: even without rite, by forthcoming Brahma-vidyā force, the guru.
The Sanskrit: *dadyādyadāsya prāṇāḥ syurdhruvaṃ niṣkramaṇecchavaḥ vināpi kriyayā bhāvibrahmavidyābalādguruḥ*

## v.30
By ear-muttering use, from tattva-armor-net, liberating as desired: in with-parts, in partless, in both.
The Sanskrit: *karṇajāpaprayogeṇa tattvakañcukajālataḥ niḥsārayanyathābhīṣṭe sakale niṣkale dvaye*

## v.31
In tattva, wherever, anywhere, he should join the person in sequence: samayin or putraka should read this vidyā, thus.
The Sanskrit: *tattve vā yatra kutrāpi yojayetpudgalaṃ kramāt samayī putrako vāpi paṭhedvidyāmimāṃ tathā*

## v.32
From its reading but, samaya-joined attains Rudra-share-arrival: these two in japa and in study, since both authorized.
The Sanskrit: *tatpāṭhāttu samayyuktāṃ rudrāṃśāpattimaśnute etau jape cādhyayane yasmādadhikṛtāvubhau*

## v.33
In teaching or instructing, or: he, this study excepted — of these two reading, by its nature his, that course.
The Sanskrit: *nādhyāpanopadeśe vā sa eṣo@dhyayanādṛte paṭhatostvanayorvastusvabhāvāttasya sā gatiḥ*

## v.34
As forbidden-beings-and-the-rest actions, mantra remembering himself: even when possessed, somewhere does not go loss, from doership-abandoning.
The Sanskrit: *yathā niṣiddhabhūtādikarmā mantraṃ smaransvayam āviṣṭe@pi kvacinnaiti lopaṃ kartṛtvavarjanāt*

## v.35
As, and causing-to-recite scripture, samayin in empty-dwelling: not is lost, from that inner-beings-group-benefiting.
The Sanskrit: *yathā ca vācayañśāstraṃ samayī śūnyaveśmani na lupyate tadantaḥsthaprāṇivargopakārataḥ*

## v.36
Thus himself reading this vidyā, from thing-nature: in that released not would be lost, since doing-something-here he.
The Sanskrit: *tathā svayaṃ paṭhanneṣa vidyāṃ vastusvabhāvataḥ tasminmukte na lupyeta yato kiṃcitkaro@tra saḥ*

## v.37
Indeed before the uninitiated he should not recite scripture-method.
The Sanskrit: *nanu cādīkṣitāgre sa noccarecchāstrapaddhatim*

## v.38
Well, before the wall even his: prohibition but now is told by exception, who to hear to ascertain is capable.
The Sanskrit: *hanta kuḍyāgrato@pyasya niṣedhastvatha kathyate paryudāsena yaḥ śrotumavadhārayituṃ kṣamaḥ*

## v.39
He indeed here is forbidden, not walls-insects-birds; then stone-like he, dissolved-sense-activity.
The Sanskrit: *sa evātra niṣiddho no kuḍyakīṭapatatriṇaḥ tarhi pāṣāṇatulyo@sau vilīnendriyavṛttikaḥ*

## v.40
Before him, of him reading, prohibition-transgression how? He but, by thing-nature, even dissolved-eyed awakens.
The Sanskrit: *tasyāgre paṭhatastasya niṣedhollaṅghanā katham sa tu vastusvabhāvena galitākṣo@pi budhyate*

## v.41
By sense-independence inner consciousness-power, by self-luminous: prior body indeed desiring to abandon not supreme, and established.
The Sanskrit: *akṣānapekṣayaivāntaścicchaktyā svaprakāśayā prāgdehaṃ kila tityakṣurnottaraṃ cādhitaṣṭhivān*

## v.42
In the middle, from awakening-force, would awaken the person: mantras, sound-made, by pure-reflection-nature itself.
The Sanskrit: *madhye prabodhakabalāt pratibudhyet pudgalaḥ mantrāḥ śabdamayāḥ śuddhavimarśātmatayā svayam*

## v.43
By meaning-self, and shining that meaning-awakening: by that his, of dissolved-eyed, awakening arises itself.
The Sanskrit: *arthātmanā cāvabhāntastadarthapratibodhakāḥ tenāsya galitākṣasya prabodho jāyate svayam*

## v.44
From self-consciousness-same-kind-mantra-reflection-proximity: as indeed-small-speed wind, same-kind-mixed.
The Sanskrit: *svacitsamānajātīyamantrāmarśanasaṃnidheḥ yathā hyalpajavo vāyuḥ sajātīyavimiśritaḥ*

## v.45
Speedy, so-self: sleeping-reflection even thus awakens; awakened he and born, not and-uninitiated, is called.
The Sanskrit: *javī tathātmā saṃsuptāmarśo@pyevaṃ prabudhyate prabuddhaḥ sa ca saṃjāto na cādīkṣita ucyate*

## v.46
Initiation, for, name, saṃskāra: not but-other-is, and-his, for, hence indeed, own scripture reads somewhere in samaya.
The Sanskrit: *dīkṣā hi nāma saṃskāro na tvanyatso@sti cāsya hi ata eva nijaṃ śāstraṃ paṭhati kvāpi sāmaye*

## v.47
Having heard that, someone if fortunate is liberated, not his that loss; scripture-censure let not this one do — of two fall-giving.
The Sanskrit: *tacchrutvā ko@pi dhanyaścenmucyate nāsya sā kṣatiḥ śāstranindāṃ maiṣa kārṣīddvayoḥ pātityadāyinīm*

## v.48
Thus supreme this, not uninitiated before, should read thus: as and samayin in wood, in clod, or mantra-joining.
The Sanskrit: *ityevaṃparametannādīkṣitāgre paṭhediti yathā ca samayī kāṣṭhe loṣṭe vā mantrayojanām*

## v.49
Doing in that flutters thus: not loss, likewise here, for since his proof-arrival-desiring, of samayin thus.
The Sanskrit: *kurvaṃstasmiṃścalatyeti na lopaṃ tadvadatra hi yato@sya pratyayaprāptiprepsoḥ samayinastathā*

## v.50
Of one engaged, by nature, in that released: not indeed loss. But the sādhaka always in the to-be-accomplished fruit, from rule-constraint.
The Sanskrit: *pravṛttasya svabhāvena tasminmukte na vai kṣatiḥ sādhakastu sadā sādhye phale niyatiyantraṇāt*

## v.51
Even fly-heard mantra: expiation-fitness should perform. Thus immediate-departure is spoken, by that command of the guru.
The Sanskrit: *makṣikāśrutamantro@pi prāyaścittaucitīṃ caret itthaṃ sadyaḥsamutkrāntiryoktā tāmājñayā guroḥ*

## v.52
Samayin and the rest even, at the spoken time, for the spoken meaning's accomplishment: himself should perform practiced prāṇa-conduct going-coming.
The Sanskrit: *samayyādirapi proktakāle proktārthasiddhaye svayaṃ kuryātsamabhyastaprāṇacāragamāgamaḥ*

## v.53
Unmade-authorized or even the guru, for samaya-purity, in lower-state: but not knowledge-kindled-conscious.
The Sanskrit: *akṛtādhikṛtirvāpi guruḥ samayaśuddhaye adhastanapadāvastho natu jñāneddhacetanaḥ*

## v.54
Thus this immediate-departure hinted in Mālinī-doctrine: himself or by the guru or then as to be performed by the Great Lord.
The Sanskrit: *itīyaṃ sadya+utkrāntiḥ sūcitā mālinīmate svayaṃ vā guruṇā vātha kāryatvena maheśinā*

## v.55
All enjoyment ugly, but having deemed, body should abandon if: then by that, quickly by sequence, joined, samayin, Śiva.
The Sanskrit: *sarvaṃ bhogaṃ virūpaṃ tu matvā dehaṃ tyajedyadi tadā tena krameṇāśu yojitaḥ samayī śivaḥ*

## v.56
Spoken this immediate-departure, which to-be-guarded, by the wise like life.
The Sanskrit: *ukteyaṃ sadya+utkrāntiryā gopyā prāṇavadbudhaiḥ*

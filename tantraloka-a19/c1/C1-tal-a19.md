# C1 — tal-a19

## SUMMARY

Sadyo-utkrānti, the initiation for immediate departure. When death is near and śaktipāta clear, the Śāṃkara initiation is obtained: hence from the initiation-moment, the supreme. The meaning 'may go to Śiva' was explained from prior-posterior discernment by venerable Śambhunātha. In mild cases, by guru-service life may diminish; or the Omnipresent's grace falls through kin and friends; or before, the samayin has not obtained supreme initiation. Even the initiated, desiring to abandon life-breaths, freed from affliction: the final guru should perform immediate-departure initiation. But not in immature impurity or remaining-karmic-body: so set forth in the Gahvara. Having seen the disciple age-seized, oppressed by disease, having made depart, he should join in the supreme principle. In the qualifier-qualified relation, from desire-conduct-arrangement, the aforesaid things set forth by Śambhunātha. The rite, all aforesaid, performed from samaya-purity: place his blazing marma-scissors knife. Fire-natured dhāraṇā, all-marma-scorching: fill the body with breath from thumb to head-end. Having drawn up from thumb to upper-end, cut the marmas from hole-end, by Kālarātrī dismiss. By this sequential yoga, yoked, libation-abandoned: even the samayin goes to that initiation — thus in the Mālinī doctrine. From sixteen-supports-six-wheels-three-targets-five-ether, or somewhere else, as before the beast-rite. Having entered the root from kanda-region, cutting from non-difference-discernment, by full-oblation use join in own-desired abode. Knowledge-trident blazing, radiant-three-wheels: feeling, awakening, whirling. Inflaming, striking, pricking, moving, again and again from-kanda-wheel-going: specially in heart-lotus. At twelve-end, in bindu-pair cast: in unmarked or supreme abode, joined to the Supreme Lord. Not perform any saṃskāra: thus says the god in Gahvara — what of his full one, by śrāddha considered? In Dīkṣottara this rite, fire-put-in: haṃsa, man below, with Rudra-bindu. In the disciple's body joined, unagitated: a hundred should mutter; having departed by upward blink, the disciple thus may go supreme. This indeed rite in Siddhayogīśvarī doctrine: this departure-initiation to be performed by yogin's guru. Unpracticed in prāṇa-conduct, how will he perform this? The to-be-spoken Brahma-vidyā, with parts, partless-included. In his ear, or should read again, again, or cause to read; himself and rite should perform, tattva-purity-and-the-rest, the guru. By mantra-rite-force, full-oblation-thus, join to supreme; without yoga-practice even, immediate-departure-giving, the guru. By knowledge-mantra-rite-meditation-force to perform: should be able; by this is departed the disciple, by force alone, one moment. Having transgressed time, enjoyment is momentary; what then? Immediate-departure-giving and another, in which full-oblation then. Should give when his life-breaths would certainly be desiring to depart: even without rite, by forthcoming Brahma-vidyā force, the guru. By ear-muttering use, from tattva-armor-net, liberating as desired: in with-parts, in partless, in both. In tattva, wherever, anywhere, he should join the person in sequence: samayin or putraka should read this vidyā, thus. From its reading but, samaya-joined attains Rudra-share-arrival: these two in japa and in study, since both authorized. In teaching or instructing, or: he, this study excepted — of these two reading, by its nature his, that course. As forbidden-beings-and-the-rest actions, mantra remembering himself: even when possessed, somewhere does not go loss, from doership-abandoning. As, and causing-to-recite scripture, samayin in empty-dwelling: not is lost, from that inner-beings-group-benefiting. Thus himself reading this vidyā, from thing-nature: in that released not would be lost, since doing-something-here he. Indeed before the uninitiated he should not recite scripture-method. Well, before the wall even his: prohibition but now is told by exception, who to hear to ascertain is capable. He indeed here is forbidden, not walls-insects-birds; then stone-like he, dissolved-sense-activity. Before him, of him reading, prohibition-transgression how? He but, by thing-nature, even dissolved-eyed awakens. By sense-independence inner consciousness-power, by self-luminous: prior body indeed desiring to abandon not supreme, and established. In the middle, from awakening-force, would awaken the person: mantras, sound-made, by pure-reflection-nature itself. By meaning-self, and shining that meaning-awakening: by that his, of dissolved-eyed, awakening arises itself. From self-consciousness-same-kind-mantra-reflection-proximity: as indeed-small-speed wind, same-kind-mixed. Speedy, so-self: sleeping-reflection even thus awakens; awakened he and born, not and-uninitiated, is called. Initiation, for, name, saṃskāra: not but-other-is, and-his, for, hence indeed, own scripture reads somewhere in samaya. Having heard that, someone if fortunate is liberated, not his that loss; scripture-censure let not this one do — of two fall-giving. Thus supreme this, not uninitiated before, should read thus: as and samayin in wood, in clod, or mantra-joining. Doing in that flutters thus: not loss, likewise here, for since his proof-arrival-desiring, of samayin thus. Of one engaged, by nature, in that released: not indeed loss. The sādhaka, however, remains bound by rule-constraint in the fruit to be accomplished. Even fly-heard mantra: expiation-fitness should perform. So the immediate-departure rite is spoken, by that command of the guru. Samayin and the rest even, at the spoken time, for the spoken meaning's accomplishment: himself should perform practiced prāṇa-conduct going-coming. Unmade-authorized or even the guru, for samaya-purity, in lower-state: but not knowledge-kindled-conscious. Thus this immediate-departure hinted in Mālinī-doctrine: himself or by the guru or then as to be performed by the Great Lord. All enjoyment ugly, but having deemed, body should abandon if: then by that, quickly by sequence, joined, samayin, Śiva. Spoken this immediate-departure, which to-be-guarded, by the wise like life.

## FUNCTION

Establishes sadyo-utkrānti as deathbed initiation: the Mālinī-hinted śaktipāta-based rite for those near death, with Śambhunātha's gloss; grades (mild → full), exclusions (immature mala, residual karma); the marma-cutting sequence (kanda, bindu, twelve-end); Śāṃkara initiation's supremacy; Brahma-vidyā (sakala+niṣkala) recited at the ear; qualified guru's fourfold power; the samayin/putraka right to recite for another's liberation without loss to himself; the prohibition for uninitiated audiences and its exception for unconscious listeners; the fly-heard mantra expiation; and the closing instruction to deem all enjoyment ugly.

## EXPLANATION

The ahnika runs: (1–3) charter and Śambhunātha's gloss; (4–7) grades and exclusions; (8–9) qualifier-qualified and Śambhunātha's arrangement; (10–14) rite sequence (nyāsa, fire-dhāraṇā, marma-cutting, Kālarātrī dismissal), Mālinī warrant; (15–16) sixteen-supports etc., root-cutting, full-oblation joining; (17–19) knowledge-trident, inflaming sequence, bindu-cast; (20) no saṃskāra for the full one (Gahvara); (21) Dīkṣottara fire-put-in rite with Rudra-bindu; (22–23) Siddhayogīśvarī doctrine and yogin's guru; (24–27) qualification question, Brahma-vidyā recitation, immediate-departure without yoga; (28–33) transgressed-time, ear-muttering, tattva-joining, samayin/putraka right to recite, prohibition and its exception; (34–40) unconscious-awakening by thing-nature, mantric thinking; (41–43) fly-heard mantra, self-reading scripture, no censure; (44–46) samayin's own reading, the unconscious listener's awakening, scripture-neglect; (47–50) fly-heard mantra expiation, samayin's own performance, sādhaka bound by rule; (51–53) guru's unqualified performance, samaya-purity; (54–56) Mālinī hint, Śiva-joining on ugly-enjoyment, guard like life.

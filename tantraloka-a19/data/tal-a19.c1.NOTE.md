# C1 NOTE — tal-a19 (not the translation: commentary complete)

## KEY TERMS (with grounds)

- sadyo-utkrānti (f.): immediate departure initiation; charter word (v1, v54, v56 seal).
- śaktipāta (m.): descent when death near; grades (mande vs full).
- śāṃkarī dīkṣā (f.): Śāṃkara initiation; supreme, beyond the rite.
- pūrva-apara-vivecana (n.): Śambhunātha's gloss method (v3).
- prāṇacāra (m.): prāṇa-conduct, the qualification for performing.
- kṣurikā (f.): marma-scissors; kanda-region cutting (v10-13).
- kālarātrī (f.): dismissal power (v13).
- sakala-niṣkala (adj.): Brahma-vidyā both aspects (v24, v30).
- karṇajāpa (m.): ear-muttering; liberates from tattva-armor net (v25, v30).
- tattvakañcuka (m.): tattva-armor net (v30).
- saṃskāra (m.): none for the full one (v20, Gahvara).
- śrāddhādi (n.): śrāddha-and-the-rest, useless for the full (v20).
- śrīmadgahvara (n.): Gahvara, cited (v7, v20).
- siddhayogīśvarī (n.): Siddhayogīśvarīmata, cited (v23).
- brahmavidyā (f.): spoken vidyā, with parts and partless (v24, v29).
- prabodhaka-bala (n.): awakening force (v42).
- mantrāmarśana (n.): mantra-reflection; dissolved-eyed awakening (v42-44).
- vastusvabhāva (n.): thing-nature; galitākṣa awakens by it (v40).
- pratyayaprāpti (f.): proof-arrival, desiring (v49).
- kuḍyakīṭapatatriṇa (m.): walls-insects-birds, exempt from prohibition (v39).
- pāṣāṇatulya (adj.): stone-like, dissolved sense (v39).
- vastusvabhāva-budhi (f.): awakening by thing-nature (v40).
- adhikāra (m.): authorization; samaya-śuddhi (v52).
- niyati-yantraṇā (f.): rule-constraint, from which sādhaka is bound (v50).
- virūpa-bhoga (m.): ugly enjoyment (v55).
- prāṇavadbudha (m.): wise like life, who guard it (v56).

# Tantraloka-a20 — L2 READ


## v.1
Now I speak the initiation that brings confidence to the deluded.
The Sanskrit: *atha dīkṣāṃ bruve mūḍhajanāśvāsapradāyinīm*

## v.2
In the triangle, the fire's seat, blazing all around with the fire-letter, fanned by the wind-abode — on the left hand, fiercely blazing.
The Sanskrit: *trikoṇe vahnisadane vahnivarṇojjvale@bhitaḥ | vāyavyapuranirdhūte kare savye sujājvale*

## v.3
Having taken a little seed — this very seed — likewise inside the heart and in the hand, being burned: let the true one, absorbed in its muttering, visualize it.
The Sanskrit: *bījaṃ kiṃcidgṛhītvaitattathaiva hṛdayāntare | kare ca dahyamānaṃ saccintayettajjapaikayuk*

## v.4
Oppressed by the burning of the fire-kindled unbroken phaṭ-series, the seed goes to seedlessness, unable to fruit itself.
The Sanskrit: *vahnidīpitaphaṭkāradhoraṇīdāhapīḍitam | bījaṃ nirbījatāmeti svasūtikaraṇākṣamam*

## v.5
Roasted, it is not for sprouting; yet by that very proof — the karmas called mala and māyā, by force of mantra, meditation, and rite —
The Sanskrit: *taptaṃ naitatprarohāya tenaiva pratyayena tu | malamāyākhyakarmāṇi mantradhyānakriyābalāt*

## v.6
burned up, not for their effect. But this seedlessness-proof the venerable, most gracious Śambhunātha demonstrated to me.
The Sanskrit: *dagdhāni na svakāryāya nirbījapratyayaṃ tvimam | sa śrīmānsuprasanno me śaṃbhunātho nyarūpayat*

## v.7
And of the seed too, the joining is to be done here, by the guru's grace — since initiation, by brilliance, liberates even the immobile.
The Sanskrit: *bījasyāpyatra kāryā ca yojanā kṛpayā guroḥ | yato dīkṣā sudīptatvātsthāvarāṇyapi mocayet*

## v.8
The guru who, having known in himself mastery of muttering, fire-offering, worship, and meditation, performs initiation — his initiation is held to carry visible proof.
The Sanskrit: *yo gururjapahomārcādhyānasiddhatvamātmani | jñātvā dīkṣāṃ carettasya dīkṣā sapratyayā smṛtā*

## v.9
Not for the shaken-off, the conductless, the tattva-knower is this rule; by the conduct-bound is performed the initiation endowed with seen proof.
The Sanskrit: *avadhūte nirācāre tattvajñe natvayaṃ vidhiḥ | sācāraiḥ kriyate dīkṣā yā dṛṣṭapratyayānvitā*

## v.10
By the conductless one no proof is declared in initiation — since knowledge is its own proof and deserves no further fruit.
The Sanskrit: *nirācāreṇa dīkṣāyāṃ pratyayastu na gadyate | jñānaṃ svapratyayaṃ yasmānna phalāntaramarhati*

## v.11
'Through the fruit, meditation and the rest are to be effected' — thus the Siddhāmata teaches; or let him perform the balance-purity test, the proof-joining one.
The Sanskrit: *dhyānādi tu phalātsādhyamiti siddhāmatoditam | tulāśuddhiparīkṣāṃ vā kuryātpratyayayoginīm*

## v.12
As told in the venerable Tantrasadbhāva by the Supreme Lord — and in the venerable Pūrvaśāstra too this is indicated by the Supreme Lord.
The Sanskrit: *yathā śrītantrasadbhāve kathitā parameśinā | śrīpūrvaśāstre@pyeṣā ca sūcitā parameśinā*

## v.13
Bliss, uprising, tremor, sleep, and whirling the fifth — thus says the teacher of śakti's gradation.
The Sanskrit: *ānanda udbhavaḥ kampo nidrā ghūrṇiśca pañcamī | ityevaṃvadatā śaktitāratamyābhidhāyinā*

## v.14
The uprising comes through lightness, from concealment of body-grasp; for the body is earthen and chief — then it abandons chiefness.
The Sanskrit: *udbhavo laghubhāvena dehagrahatirohiteḥ | deho hi pārthivo mukhyastadā mukhyatvamujjhati*

## v.15
With the forthcoming-levitation mantra, having meditated the disciple floated up, let the guru perform there all the remaining rites as before stated.
The Sanskrit: *bhāvilāghavamantreṇa śiṣyaṃ dhyātvā samutplutam | karmāṇi tatrāśeṣāṇi pūrvoktānyācaredguruḥ*

## v.16
Told is this very balance-purity initiation, the giver of proof.
The Sanskrit: *uktā seyaṃ tulāśuddhidīkṣā pratyayadāyinī*

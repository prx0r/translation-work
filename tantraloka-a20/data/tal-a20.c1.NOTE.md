# C1 NOTE — tal-a20 (not the translation: commentary complete)

## KEY TERMS (with grounds)

- sapratyayā dīkṣā (f.): proof-bearing initiation; charter word (v1, v8).
- mūḍhajana (m.): the deluded (under Māyā's sway, Pradipaka); the rite's addressee.
- trikoṇa vahnisadana (n.): triangle fire-seat; imagined on savya kara (left — grammar; Pradipaka 'right' recorded as secondary slip).
- bīja (n.): the seed-demo; little, burned in hand-and-heart (v3).
- phaṭkāradhoraṇī (f.): unbroken phaṭ-series; the mantra-weapon (v4).
- nirbījatā (f.) / nirbījapratyaya (m.): seedlessness / the seedlessness-proof; demonstrated state (v4–v6).
- mala-māyā-karman (n.): āṇava/māyīya/kārma malas as karma; burned like seed (v5–v6).
- śambhunātha (m.): the Kulaguru; graceful demonstrator credited (v6).
- yojanā (f.): joining (of the burned seed to the Supreme); by guru-grace (v7).
- sthāvara (n.): the immobile (plants, minerals); freed by brilliance (v7).
- japa-homa-arcā-dhyāna-siddhatva (n.): fourfold mastery; the competence condition (v8).
- avadhūta / nirācāra / tattvajña (m.): three exempt (v9); nirācāra per Jayaratha = the knower.
- sācāra (m.): the conduct-bound; the rule's addressee (v9).
- siddhāmata (n.): cited authority for fruit-effected practice (v11; compression flagged).
- tulāśuddhiparīkṣā (f.): balance-purity test; proof-joining alternative (v11).
- śrītantrasadbhāva / śrīpūrvaśāstra (Mālinīvijaya): dual canon, both Lord-voiced (v12).
- pañca śakticinhāni: ānanda, udbhava, kampa, nidrā, ghūrṇī pañcamī (v13).
- laghubhāva (m.) / dehagrahatirohiti (f.): lightness / body-grasp concealment; levitation mechanism (v14, ablative hetu).
- bhāvilāghava-mantra (m.): forthcoming-levitation mantra; samutpluta disciple visualized (v15).

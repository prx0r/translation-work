## v.1
Now the initiation-rite of the one situated in absence is described.
The Sanskrit: *parokṣasaṃsthitasyātha dīkṣākarma nigadyate*

## v.2
For accomplishment of enjoyment and liberation one is led to the true guru: in this Mālinī sentence 'prati' is expressive of facing-towards.
The Sanskrit: *bhuktimuktiprasiddhyarthaṃ nīyate sadguruṃ prati ityasminmālinīvākye pratiḥ sāṃmukhyāvācakaḥ*

## v.3
Facing-towards of the disciple consists in becoming the seat of his grace; the saying 'worshipping him' is an indicator of the cause of grace.
The Sanskrit: *sāṃmukhyaṃ cāsya śiṣyasya tatkṛpāspadatātmakam tamārādhyeti vacanaṃ kṛpāhetūpalakṣaṇam*

## v.4
From that connection then someone is liberated instantly: thus is also the meaning of this jewel of the Mālinī sentence.
The Sanskrit: *tatsaṃbandhāttataḥ kaścittatkṣaṇādapavṛjyate ityasyāyamapi hyartho mālinīvākyasanmaṇeḥ*

## v.5
'In that instant' means there is no further moment of desire to approach; but exactly thus the guru, struck by compassion, should uplift him.
The Sanskrit: *tatkṣaṇāditi nāsyāsti yiyāsādikṣaṇāntaram kiṃtvevameva karuṇānighnastaṃ gururuddharet*

## v.6
Of one who, body wasted by guru-service, died without obtaining initiation — or whose desire arose at the moment of death.
The Sanskrit: *gurusevākṣīṇatanordīkṣāmaprāpya pañcatām gatasyātha svayaṃ mṛtyukṣaṇoditatathāruceḥ*

## v.7
Or of one who shared the initiation-rites of lower tantras etc., having obtained samaya-status, (but) not having obtained the supreme initiation.
The Sanskrit: *athavādharatantrādidīkṣāsaṃskārabhāginaḥ prāptasāmayikasyātha parāṃ dīkṣāmavindataḥ*

## v.8
Of one struck by a ḍimba, or devoured by a yogeśī through sorcery, or slain by yantra-tantra etc. by the guru — the dead.
The Sanskrit: *ḍimbāhatasya yogeśībhakṣitasyābhicārataḥ mṛtasya guruṇā yantratantrādinihatasya vā*

## v.9
Of one who, having obtained initiation, violated his samaya — even so, through the vehement entreaty of relatives, wife, friends, sons.
The Sanskrit: *bhraṣṭasvasamayasyātha dīkṣāṃ prāptavato@pyalam bandhubhāryāsuhṛtputragāḍhābhyarthanayogataḥ*

## v.10
Or by itself, by the force of compassion arisen towards that person even, (of one) who is the bearer of a portion of śaktipāta come towards him known.
The Sanskrit: *svayaṃ tadviṣayotpannakaruṇābalato@pi vā vijñātatanmukhāyātaśaktipātāṃśadharmaṇaḥ*

## v.11
The guru should perform the initiation redeeming the dead, bestowing Śiva; that is said by the Supreme Lord in Śrī-Mṛtyuñjaya-Siddha and the rest.
The Sanskrit: *gururdīkṣāṃ mṛtoddhārīṃ kurvīta śivadāyinīm śrīmṛtyuñjayasiddhādau taduktaṃ parameśinā*

## v.12
For the uninitiated, king and the rest, the lazy, the fallen, the dead, child, sick, woman, old — and one should arrange the redemption of the dead.
The Sanskrit: *adīkṣite nṛpatyādāvalase patite mṛte bālāturastrīvṛddhe ca mṛtoddhāraṃ prakalpayet*

## v.13
The rite all previously told: that is desired to be concise, devoid of guru-and-other worship, in the external for enjoyment, because it.
The Sanskrit: *vidhiḥ sarvaḥ pūrvamuktaḥ sa tu saṃkṣipta iṣyate gurvādipūjārahito bāhye bhogāya sā yataḥ*

## v.14
Preliminary consecration, caru, field, bed, maṇḍala-preparation are not useful here; (they are) for the disciple's purification and dream-seeing.
The Sanskrit: *adhivāsacarukṣetraṃ śayyāmaṇḍalakalpane nopayogyatra tacchiṣyasaṃskriyāsvapnadṛṣṭaye*

## v.15
But the maṇḍala is for making mantras present and satisfied; and on the following day the worship of gods is not directly helpful for this.
The Sanskrit: *mantrasaṃnidhisaṃtṛptiyogāyātra tu maṇḍalam bhūyodine ca devārcā sākṣānnāsyopakāri tat*

## v.16
Through rite, instrument, place, maṇḍala, form, mantra; through meditation, union, sole devotion, knowledge, identification — (these are the causes of presence).
The Sanskrit: *kriyopakaraṇasthānamaṇḍalākṛtimantrataḥ dhyānayogaikatadbhaktijñānatanmayabhāvataḥ*

## v.17
Of one who entered into that, of disciples and of the guru also: these eleven are told as causes for presence.
The Sanskrit: *tatpraviṣṭasya kasyāpi śiṣyāṇāṃ ca gurostathā ekādaśaite kathitāḥ saṃnidhānāya hetavaḥ*

## v.18
Higher and higher superior, also from freedom from mixture: the rite abundantly, flowers and the rest, superior, endowed with marks.
The Sanskrit: *uttarottaramutkṛṣṭāstathā vyāmiśraṇāvaśāt kriyātibhūyasī puṣpādyuttamaṃ lakṣaṇānvitam*

## v.19
Single liṅga and the rest as place, where the self is pleased; the maṇḍala three-trident-lotus-wheel, which in mantra-maṇḍala.
The Sanskrit: *ekaliṅgādi ca sthānaṃ yatrātmā saṃprasīdati maṇḍalaṃ tritriśūlābjacakraṃ yanmantramaṇḍale*

## v.20
Even when not invoked, seen — the accomplishment of samaya-status of the good; that is said in the Mālinī-tantra: accomplished samaya-maṇḍala.
The Sanskrit: *anāhūte@pi dṛṣṭaṃ satsamayitvaprasādhanam taduktaṃ mālinītantre siddhaṃ samayamaṇḍalam*

## v.21
By which, as soon as seen — thus, from the two words 'siddha' and 'mātra': the form radiant in form, which, mantra like it very radiant.
The Sanskrit: *yena saṃdṛṣṭamātreti siddhamātrapadadvayāt ākṛtirdīptarūpā yā mantrastadvatsudīptikaḥ*

## v.22
The rest is clearly therefore not told here in detail again; having made the maṇḍala, having worshipped the god there with kuśa grass — and.
The Sanskrit: *śiṣṭaṃ spaṣṭamato neha kathitaṃ vistarātpunaḥ kṛtvā maṇḍalamabhyarcya tatra devaṃ kuśairatha*

## v.23
With cow-dung he should make the form, disciple-like, should place; then in it one path to be purified, from the contemplation of pervasion.
The Sanskrit: *gomayenākṛtiṃ kuryācchiṣyavattāṃ nidhāpayet tatastasyāṃ śodhyamekamadhvānaṃ vyāptibhāvanāt*

## v.24
Having deposited up to prakṛti, again he should perform the rite by the great-net-practice, from the middle of all the paths.
The Sanskrit: *prakṛtyantaṃ vinikṣipya punarenaṃ vidhiṃ caret mahājālaprayogeṇa sarvasmādadhvamadhyataḥ*

## v.25
Having drawn the mind situated there — should do: that rite is said. Having risen from the mūlādhāra, the rod of the path of the vast spread endless nāḍī, having occupied by vigor, reached the nose-sky, throwing, desires to pervade up to the wheel of the path with a net of smoke-beautiful dense tips, covering — bringing the desired life — thus: the practice called the great net.
The Sanskrit: *cittamākṛṣya tatrasthaṃ kuryāttadvidhirucyate mūlādhārādudetya prasṛtasuvitatānantanāḍyadhvadaṇḍaṃ vīryeṇākramya nāsāgaganaparigataṃ vikṣipan vyāptumīṣṭe yāvaddhūmābhirāmapracitataraśikhājālakenādhvacakraṃ saṃchādyābhīṣṭajīvānayanamiti mahājālanāmā prayogaḥ*

## v.26
By this to-be-covered goes dependence on another; facing-towards-ness at first; is led to five, if all — then from the middle of the path as desired, in drawing and extracting, or in the matter of the dead person to be drawn — then in the living, yoga called the net, taught in the Śrī-Śaṃbhunātha-āgama, spoken by me.
The Sanskrit: *etenācchādanīyaṃ vrajati paravaśaṃ saṃmukhīnatvamādau pañcādānīyate cetsakalamatha tato@pyadhvamadhyādyatheṣṭam ākṛṣṭāvuddhṛtau vā mṛtajanaviṣaye karṣaṇīye@tha jīve yogaḥ śrīśaṃbhunāthāgamaparigamito jālanāmā mayoktaḥ*

## v.27
When long broken apart, as two armies joined: again horse, elephant, man run towards their own caste by taste; by the nāḍī-wheels with the instrument-winds just so, joined, each with its own taste unified, by their own net subjugated.
The Sanskrit: *ciravighaṭite senāyugmeyathāmilite punarhayagajanaraṃ svāṃ svāṃ jātiṃ rasādabhidhāvati karaṇapavanairnāḍīcakraistathaiva samāgatairnijanijarasādekībhāvyaṃ svajālavaśīkṛtaiḥ*

## v.28
Drawn by the great net, the living soul, by the knowing one, releases heaven, ghost, animal, hell — then at that time this one.
The Sanskrit: *mahājālasamākṛṣṭo jīvo vijñānaśālinā svaḥpretatiryaṅnirayāṃstadaivaiṣa vimuñcati*

## v.29
Obtained by that knowledge-mantra-yoga, this person also, artificial, like a yogī, then from the heart of the sādhya abandons identification.
The Sanskrit: *tajjñānamantrayogāptaḥ puruṣaścaiṣa kṛtrimam yogīva sādhyahṛdayāttadā tādātmyamujjhati*

## v.30
The states from immobile etc., wondrous, nearness to that world — should abandon, thus: not wonder; he thus who, by action even, or.
The Sanskrit: *sthāvarādidaśāścitrāstatsalokasamīpatāḥ tyajecceti na citraṃ sa evaṃ yaḥ karmaṇāpi vā*

## v.31
From being in an eligible body, in humanity — but bodied, not then liberated; from body, at end of body, but should go to Śiva.
The Sanskrit: *adhikāriśarīratvānmānuṣye tu śarīragaḥ na tadā mucyate dehāddehānte tu śivaṃ vrajet*

## v.32
In that body but some of his arises: supreme Śāṅkara grace, from root and from knowledge or from served teacher.
The Sanskrit: *tasmindehe tu kāpyasya jāyate śāṅkarī parā bhaktirūhācca vijñānādācāryādvāpyasevitāt*

## v.33
Situated in that body, even this living soul, by net-force etc. this, pervades the body of darbha and the rest, by own abiding even unconscious.
The Sanskrit: *taddehasaṃsthito@pyeṣa jīvo jālabalādimam dārbhādidehaṃ vyāpnoti svādhiṣṭhityāpyacetayan*

## v.34
By the force of abundant yoga-mantra-rite-knowledge again, even the human body this one then immediately releases.
The Sanskrit: *yogamantrakriyājñānabhūyobalavaśātpunaḥ manuṣyadehamapyeṣa tadaivāśu vimuñcati*

## v.35
Even sleeping-like, even bodiless, who is living soul: he even, drawn from the net, comes to the darbha body, of fruit, and or.
The Sanskrit: *suptakalpo@pyadeho@pi yo jīvaḥ so@pi jālataḥ ākṛṣṭo dārbhamāyāti dehaṃ phalamayaṃ ca vā*

## v.36
Jasmine fruit and the rest, whatever by that, or body-fashioning: by suitability both inside and outside, that here superior is said.
The Sanskrit: *jātīphalādi yatkiṃcittena vā dehakalpanā antarbahirdvayaucityāttadatrotkṛṣṭamucyate*

## v.37
Then the living soul brought by the net method stands as if asleep, from absence of obtaining apparatus of mind-endowed body and the rest.
The Sanskrit: *tato jālakramānītaḥ sa jīvaḥ suptavatsthitaḥ manoviśiṣṭadehādisāmagrīprāptyabhāvataḥ*

## v.38
It neither vibrates nor knows nor speaks nor certainly desires; for such a one indeed, all purificatory rites as before should prepare.
The Sanskrit: *na spandate na jānāti na vakti na kilecchati tādṛśasyaiva saṃskārān sarvān prāgvatprakalpayet*

## v.39
By seedless initiation yoga, all having done the previously-told rite producing union, with full oblation together, should throw and him.
The Sanskrit: *nirbījadīkṣāyogena sarvaṃ kṛtvā puroditam vidhiṃ yojanikāṃ pūrṇāhutyā sākaṃ kṣipecca tam*

## v.40
In the body of darbha and the rest, offered into the fire of mantra together with full, freed from bonds goes to Śiva, free from return again.
The Sanskrit: *dārbhādidehe mantrāgnāvarpite pūrṇayā saha muktapāśaḥ śivaṃ yāti punarāvṛttivarjitaḥ*

## v.41
Furnished with proof but this, where the darbha-born body vibrates: there, by the yoga of offering prāṇa, mind, mantra, so may happen.
The Sanskrit: *sapratyayā tviyaṃ yatra spandate darbhajā tanuḥ tatra prāṇamanomantrārpaṇayogāttathā bhavet*

## v.42
Of the one with practice that also is said (breathing force); not for that, by the same means as redemption of the dead, as possible, by means.
The Sanskrit: *sābhyāsasya tadapyuktaṃ balāśvāsi na tatkṛte mṛtoddhāroditaireva yathāsaṃbhūti hetubhiḥ*

## v.43
Initiation of living absent also to be performed, seedless but it: in it, in fashioning form resembling darbha, from net-yoga.
The Sanskrit: *jīvatparokṣadīkṣāpi kāryā nirbījikā tu sā tasyāṃ darbhākṛtiprāyakalpane jālayogataḥ*

## v.44
Drawing by imagination alone of the living soul, from fear of death: the rest as before, without burning kuśa and the rest form.
The Sanskrit: *saṃkalpamātreṇākarṣo jīvasya mṛtibhītitaḥ śiṣṭaṃ prāgvatkuśādyutthākāraviploṣavarjitam*

## v.45
From limitedness and non-lordship in the sādhya, from niyati-constraint, net-drawing without practice, from attachment and aversion not, arises.
The Sanskrit: *pārimityādanaiśvaryātsādhye niyatiyantraṇāt jālākṛṣṭirvinābhyāsaṃ rāgadveṣānna jāyate*

## v.46
Absent: indeed by unequal initiations if initiated, there superior may be mighty; for purification but the inferior.
The Sanskrit: *parokṣa evātulyābhirdīkṣābhiryadi dīkṣitaḥ tatrottaraṃ syādbalavatsaṃskārāya tvadhastanam*

## v.47
In the union for enjoyment but, by many gurus so, when performed, variety of enjoyment from variety of causes.
The Sanskrit: *bhuktiyojanikāyāṃ tu bhūyobhirgurubhistathā kṛtāyāṃ bhogavaicitryaṃ hetuvaicitryayogataḥ*

## v.48
In initiation of absent, beyond māyā, for enjoyment should join; desire for enjoyment rare indeed being, or for cessation of enjoyment.
The Sanskrit: *parokṣadīkṣaṇe māyottīrṇe bhogāya yojayet bhogānīpsā durlabhā hi satī vā bhogahānaye*

## v.49
Said indeed: of own and other consciousness, own consciousness more powerful in overriding; that which overrides, of equality and indifference so.
The Sanskrit: *uktaṃ hi svānyasaṃvittyoḥ svasaṃvidbalavattarā bādhakatve bādhikāsau sāmyaudāsīnyayostathā*

## v.50
Venerable Dharmaśiva said: in absence, in the karma procedure, in initiation of absent, properly, in the full oblation rite, if.
The Sanskrit: *śrīmān dharmaśivo@pyāha pārokṣyāṃ karmapaddhatau parokṣadīkṣaṇe samyak pūrṇāhutividhau yadi*

## v.51
Fire emits ciṭi-ciṭā sound with smoke, bears blue-cloud-color, repeatedly blazes, subsides.
The Sanskrit: *agniściṭiciṭāśabdaṃ sadhūmaṃ pratimuñcati dhatte nīlāmbudacchāyāṃ muhurjvalati śāmyati*

## v.52
Detail, terrible form and, runs on earth and downwards; or crow-etc unheard sound: then him the guru should mark.
The Sanskrit: *vistaro ghorarūpaśca mahīṃ dhāvati cāpyadhaḥ dhvāṃkṣādyaśravyaśabdo vā tadā taṃ lakṣayedguruḥ*

## v.53
By brahmin-killing and the rest, by sins and by association with them minor sins: then of him not to be done, initiation in this unperformed rite.
The Sanskrit: *brahmahatyādibhiḥ pāpaistatsaṅgaiścopapātakaiḥ tadā tasya na kartavyā dīkṣāsminnakṛte vidhau*

## v.54
Nine-souled, standing inside the phaṭ cavity, again endowed with five phaṭs: of so-and-so thus, sins, I burn according to eight phaṭs.
The Sanskrit: *navātmā phaṭpuṭāntaḥsthaḥ punaḥ pañcaphaḍanvitaḥ amukasyeti pāpāni dahāmyanu phaḍaṣṭakam*

## v.55
Thus thousand-fold fire-offering to be done with sesame and rice; at the end full and to be given; then to him by initiation the guru.
The Sanskrit: *iti sāhasriko homaḥ kartavyastilataṇḍulaiḥ ante pūrṇā ca dātavyā tato@smai dīkṣayā guruḥ*

## v.56
Up to the supreme goal should do purification of principles; even in direct presence of the paśu situated, of the sinner, Bhagavan-filled.
The Sanskrit: *parayojanaparyantaṃ kuryāttattvaviśodhanam pratyakṣe@pi sthitasyāṇoḥ pāpino bhagavanmayīm*

## v.57
Power of the one who obtained: the eldest, exactly thus rite should perform; if or the teacher properly not blazing of him before that.
The Sanskrit: *śaktiṃ prāptavato jyeṣṭhāmevameva vidhiṃ caret yadi vā daiśikaḥ samyaṅ na dīptastasya tatpurā*

## v.58
By expiations so, by gifts, by breath-controls and, purification having done, this rite and also initiation should perform without doubt.
The Sanskrit: *prāyaścittaistathā dānaiḥ prāṇāyāmaiśca śodhanam kṛtvā vidhimimāṃ cāpi dīkṣāṃ kuryādaśaṅkitaḥ*

## v.59
In every way being even, the tattva-knower liberates beasts by will alone; Śiva directly — therefore him should worship always.
The Sanskrit: *sarvathā vartamāno@pi tattvavinmocayetpaśūn icchayaiva śivaḥ sākṣāttasmāttaṃ pūjayetsadā*

## v.60
Deceit there not to be done; and having done that downwards should go the disciple. Not again should proclaim of him sin; the proclaimer should go.
The Sanskrit: *śāṭhyaṃ tatra na kāryaṃ ca tatkṛtvādho vrajecchiśuḥ na punaḥ kīrtayettasya pāpaṃ kīrtayitā vrajet*

## v.61
Hell should avoid — therefore thus in Dīkṣottara rite. This initiation of absent twofold told, by division of living and other.
The Sanskrit: *nirayaṃ varjayettasmāditi dīkṣottare vidhiḥ eṣā parokṣadīkṣā dvidhoditā jīvaditarabhedena*

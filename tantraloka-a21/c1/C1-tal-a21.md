# C1 — tal-a21

## SUMMARY

The ahnika announces the initiation of the absent and the dead: the guru draws the soul through the great net into a darbha-form, performs seedless initiation, and liberates; for the living absent, imagination alone suffices; compassion is the engine, and the rite is twofold.

## FUNCTION

Establishes parokṣa-dīkṣā: initiation of the absent and the dead — the guru draws the soul through the great net into a darbha-form, performs seedless initiation, and liberates; the living absent is initiated by imagination alone; compassion (karuṇā) is the engine.

## KEY TERMS

parokṣa-dīkṣā (initiation of the absent), mṛtoddhāra (redemption of the dead), mahājāla (great net), darbha-form, śaktipāta (descent of grace), samaya (vow), nirbīja (seedless), pūrṇāhuti (full oblation), parokṣa (absent), jīvat-parokṣa (living absent), pratyaya (proof/vibration), jāla (net), karuṇā (compassion), māyā (māyā), bhoga (enjoyment), mokṣa (liberation)

## EXPLANATION

The ahnika runs: (1) charter: initiation of the absent announced; (2) prati as facing-towards in Mālinī sentence; (3) facing-towards = becoming seat of grace; (4) instant liberation from that connection; (5) no further moment; guru uplifts by compassion; (6) cases: died before initiation, desire at death; (7) lower-tantra samaya holder, no supreme initiation; (8) violent/sorcerous deaths, guru-slain; (9) samaya-violator, entreaty of kin; (10) compassion-force, śaktipāta-bearer; (11) guru performs mṛtoddhārī dīkṣā bestowing Śiva; (12) eligible recipients of redemption of the dead; (13) concise rite, no guru-worship, external enjoyment; (14) preliminary consecration not useful here; (15) maṇḍala for mantra-presence; next-day god-worship not helpful; (16) eleven causes of presence enumerated; (17) these eleven for presence of disciple and guru; (18) higher causes superior, free from mixture; (19) place, liṅga, maṇḍala, mantra-maṇḍala; (20) samaya-maṇḍala seen even when not invoked; (21) form radiant, mantra radiant; (22) rest not told here; kuśa-grass worship; (23) cow-dung form, disciple-like, path purification; (24) net-practice from middle of all paths; (25) great-net practice: mind drawn, nāḍī rod, smoke-net; (26) net-yoga taught in Śaṃbhunātha-āgama; (27) armies joined, each to own caste by taste; (28) great-net draws soul, releases lower worlds; (29) artificial yogī, abandons identification; (30) states immobile etc. to be abandoned; (31) human body not liberated then; goes to Śiva at end; (32) supreme Śāṅkara grace arises; (33) soul pervades darbha-body, unconscious; (34) abundant yoga releases human body; (35) sleeping/bodiless soul drawn to darbha body; (36) jasmine-fruit body, suitability inside and out; (37) soul stands as if asleep, no apparatus; (38) no vibration/knowledge/speech/desire; rites as before; (39) seedless initiation, full oblation, throw into fire; (40) darbha-body offered, freed, goes to Śiva; (41) proof-furnished: darbha-body vibrates; (42) practice breathes force; means as redemption; (43) living absent: seedless, darbha-form by net-yoga; (44) drawing by imagination; no burning of kuśa-form; (45) net-drawing limited by niyati, rāga/dveṣa; (46) unequal initiations: superior mighty, inferior purifies; (47) enjoyment-union by many gurus, variety of causes; (48) beyond māyā for enjoyment or cessation; (49) own consciousness overrides other; (50) Dharmaśiva: full-oblation rite if; (51) fire omens: ciṭi sound, smoke, blue cloud; (52) terrible form, earth-running, crow sound; (53) brahmin-killing etc.: no initiation in this rite; (54) nine-souled, phaṭ cavity, eight phaṭs burn sins; (55) thousand-fold homa with sesame and rice; (56) purification of principles up to supreme goal; (57) power obtained: eldest rite; teacher not blazing; (58) expiations, gifts, breath-control, then initiation; (59) tattva-knower liberates beasts by will; worship Śiva; (60) no deceit; proclaimer of sin goes to hell; (61) hell avoided; initiation twofold by living/other

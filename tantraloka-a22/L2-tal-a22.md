# Tantraloka-a22 — L2 READ


## v.1
Now the so-called liṅga-lifting we speak: solely enjoined in the Śiva-teaching.
The Sanskrit: *liṅgoddhārākhyāmatha vacmaḥ śivaśāsanaikanirdiṣṭām*

## v.2
Told in the venerable Mālinītantra, indeed: the earth-upholding — having declared, who is yoked where, he from it does not turn back.
The Sanskrit: *uktaṃ śrīmālinītantre kila pārthivadhāraṇām | uktvā yo yojito yatra sa tasmānna nivartate*

## v.3
Arisen from fitness, whose just where the teaching: he just there is to be appointed; at initiation-time, then, but this one.
The Sanskrit: *yogyatāvaśasaṃjātā yasya yatraiva śāsanā | sa tatraiva niyoktavyo dīkṣākāle tatastvasau*

## v.4
Fruit, all, having attained, in Śiva: though yoked, released; though unyoked, upper purification, having attained from Bhuvaneśa.
The Sanskrit: *phalaṃ sarvaṃ samāsādya śive yukto@pavṛjyate | ayukto@pyūrdhvasaṃśuddhiṃ saṃprāpya bhuvaneśataḥ*

## v.5
Purified, he comes to Śiva-hood, burnt-saṃsāra-bonded; having declared man-upholding — and told, this Vedāntic, by me.
The Sanskrit: *śuddhaḥ śivatvamāyāti dagdhasaṃsārabandhanaḥ | uktvā puṃdhāraṇāṃ coktametadvaidāntikaṃ mayā*

## v.6
To Kapila formerly told, in the first chapter, so: by this, through sequential yoga, attained — the highest state.
The Sanskrit: *kapilāya purā proktaṃ prathame paṭale tathā | anena kramayogena saṃprāptaḥ paramaṃ padam*

## v.7
Not again to beast-hood goes: in the pure own self he stands; hence, for, is hinted the meaning — this, even for those below the Śiva-principle.
The Sanskrit: *na bhūyaḥ paśutāmeti śuddhe svātmani tiṣṭhati | ato hi dhvanyate@rtho@yaṃ śivatattvādhareṣvapi*

## v.8
In the principles, of the yoked there is re-extractability: of the giver of all-śāstra-taught thing-distinctions.
The Sanskrit: *tattveṣu yojitasyāsti punaruddharaṇīyatā | samastaśāstrakathitavastuvaiviktyadāyinaḥ*

## v.9
Of the Śiva-āgama is distinction than all — even — āgamas; and through Śiva-knowledge, without, again even beast-arising.
The Sanskrit: *śivāgamasya sarvebhyo@pyāgamebhyo viśiṣṭatā | śivajñānena ca vinā bhūyo@pi paśutodbhavaḥ*

## v.10
And the sequence: śakti-descent, impurity-loss, departure-desire, initiation, awakening, abandonable-loss, acceptable-dissolution-nature.
The Sanskrit: *kramaśca śaktisaṃpāto malahāniryiyāsutā | dīkṣā bodho heyahānirupādeyalayātmatā*

## v.11
Enjoyable-fettered-renouncing, lord-agency-destruction, self-abiding — and thus: for one standing in another view.
The Sanskrit: *bhogyatvapāśavatyāgaḥ patikartṛtvasaṃkṣayaḥ | svātmasthitiścetyevaṃ hi darśanāntarasaṃsthiteḥ*

## v.12
Told is extractability of the Śiva-śakti-impelled, for: now, of those in lower systems from Vaiṣṇava and Buddhist to Tantric.
The Sanskrit: *proktamuddharaṇīyatvaṃ śivaśaktīritasya hi | atha vaiṣṇavabauddhāditantrāntādharavartinām*

## v.13
When by Śiva-sun-ray-floods opened is the heart-lotus, then first liṅga-extracting, afterwards the initiation-rite.
The Sanskrit: *yadā śivārkaraśmyoghairvikāsi hṛdayāmbujam | liṅgoddhṛtistadā pūrvaṃ dīkṣākarma tataḥ param*

## v.14
Even formerly other-marked, from initiation may he reach Śiva-hood: there having made fast, him, and on another day, by the common mantra.
The Sanskrit: *prāgliṅgāntarasaṃstho@pi dīkṣātaḥ śivatāṃ vrajet | tatropavāsya taṃ cānyadine sādhāramantrataḥ*

## v.15
On the altar, having worshipped the Lord, he should proclaim: his procedure. This one formerly was a mark-bearer, impelled, but now, by You.
The Sanskrit: *sthaṇḍile pūjayitveśaṃ śrāvayettasya vartanīm | eṣa prāgabhavalliṅgī coditastvadhunā tvayā*

## v.16
By the gracious, then to him: make — proper favor: arisen from own-mark-abandoning-doubt, expiation, and may not his be.
The Sanskrit: *prasannena tadetasmai kuru samyaganugraham | svaliṅgatyāgaśaṅkotthaṃ prāyaścittaṃ ca māsya bhūt*

## v.17
Quickly become of Thee, enjoyment, liberation, let him attain: 'so be it' — thus the command, and having received, vow — his — that.
The Sanskrit: *acirāttvanmayībhūya bhogaṃ mokṣaṃ prapadyatām | evamastvityathājñāṃ ca gṛhīrvā vratamasya tat*

## v.18
Having discarded — in water, having placed, should bathe fittingly: the bathed one, should sprinkle with argha-vessel waters — next.
The Sanskrit: *apāsyāmbhasi nikṣipya snapayedanurūpataḥ | snātaṃ saṃprokṣayedarghapātrāmbhobhiranantaram*

## v.19
The five cow-products, tooth-stick, then to him should offer; then him, blindfolded, and having ushered, should make bow.
The Sanskrit: *pañcagavyaṃ dantakāṣṭhaṃ tatastasmai samarpayet | tatastaṃ baddhanetraṃ ca praveśya praṇipātayet*

## v.20
Praṇava, mātṛkā, māyā, all-pervading, six-syllabled, many-formed — then, called Netra, seven common ones: these.
The Sanskrit: *praṇavo mātṛkā māyā vyomavyāpī ṣaḍakṣaraḥ | bahurūpo@tha netrākhyaḥ sapta sādhāraṇā amī*

## v.21
Of them, from the middle, one mantra to him should bestow: he too, day and night, indeed it, should mutter, little-eating, fasting.
The Sanskrit: *teṣāṃ madhyādekatamaṃ mantramasmai samarpayet | so@pyahorātramevainaṃ japedalpabhugapyabhuk*

## v.22
Mantra to him, having bestowed, then, in the rite-purified fire, libated that mantra, vow-purity he should perform.
The Sanskrit: *mantramasmai samarpyātha sādhāravidhisaṃskṛte | vahnau tarpitatanmantre vrataśuddhiṃ samācaret*

## v.23
With the worshipped alone, mantra, having made name — his, enclosure: expiation, 'I cleanse' — through phaṭ-svāhā-ūha practice.
The Sanskrit: *pūjitenaiva mantreṇa kṛtvā nāmāsya saṃpuṭam | prāyaścittaṃ śodhayāmi phaṭsvāhetyūhayogataḥ*

## v.24
A hundred, a thousand, or, having offered, again the full oblation, likewise, through use vauṣaḍ-ending, and having cast, having invoked Vrateśvara.
The Sanskrit: *śataṃ sahasraṃ vā hutvā punaḥ pūrṇāhutiṃ tathā | prayogādvauṣaḍantāṃ ca kṣiptvāhūya vrateśvaram*

## v.25
'Tāra, to Vrateśvara' — quoted; 'homage!' — and him should worship: and should proclaim, by you, not his to be done, anything by Śiva's command.
The Sanskrit: *tāro vrateśvarāyeti namaścetyenamarcayet | śrāvayecca tvayā nāsya kāryaṃ kiṃcicchivājñayā*

## v.26
Then Vrateśvara to be libated, with svāhā-ending: and then he, having asked pardon, to be dismissed, should be; then of fire too, dismissal.
The Sanskrit: *tato vrateśvarastarpyaḥ svāhāntena tataśca saḥ | kṣamayitvā visṛjyaḥ syāttato@gneśca visarjanam*

## v.27
That proclamation, and, to the god: 'forgive!' — quoted, is dismissal; then on the third day, as before, all the rite is remembered.
The Sanskrit: *tacchrāvaṇaṃ ca devāya kṣamasveti visarjanam | tatastṛtīyadivase prāgvatsarvo vidhiḥ smṛtaḥ*

## v.28
Eve-rite-and-the-rest, ending in own-desired-initiation-rite: of the former mark-bearers, liberation-initiation, devoid of qualification.
The Sanskrit: *adhivāsādikaḥ sveṣṭadīkṣākarmāvasānakaḥ | prāgliṅgināṃ mokṣadīkṣā sādhikāravivarjitā*

## v.29
On the adept-teacher path, not fit are they, the reborn ones; even of the reborn, kindled by knowledge, they become the abode of guruhood.
The Sanskrit: *sādhakācāryatāmārge na yogyāste punarbhuvaḥ | punarbhuvo@pi jñāneddhā bhavanti gurutāspadam*

## v.30
For liberation alone, not for enjoyment; for enjoyment too, through means: thus said in his own Paddhati — Īśānaśiva the teacher.
The Sanskrit: *mokṣāyaiva na bhogāya bhogāyāpyabhyupāyataḥ | ityuktavānsvapaddhatyāmīśānaśivadaiśikaḥ*

## v.31
Of the venerable Devī, rightly proclaiming the truth of the Yāmalī saying, of the guru, even of one sunk in downward gaze: this rite — perform.
The Sanskrit: *śrīdevyā yāmalīyoktitattvasamyakpravedakaḥ | gurvantasyāpyadhodṛṣṭiśāyinaḥ saṃskriyāmimām*

## v.32
Having performed, the secret one should tell, not otherwise, in the Kāmika, indeed: though initiated in another tantra, the secret not disclose.
The Sanskrit: *kṛtvā rahasyaṃ kathayennānyathā kāmike kila | anyatantrābhiṣikte@pi rahasyaṃ na prakāśayet*

## v.33
Standing free, yet having approached an ignorant guru, being unassured there after: there too, the rite he should perform.
The Sanskrit: *svatantrastho@pi gurvanto gurumajñamupāśritaḥ | tatra paścādanāśvastastatrāpi vidhimācaret*

## v.34
Come through an ignorant teacher's mouth, powerless — mantra, this: which muttered, he as guru — even here: not from authority-told defect.
The Sanskrit: *ajñācāryamukhāyātaṃ nirvīryaṃ mantrameṣa yat | japtavānsa guruścātra nādhikāryuktadūṣaṇāt*

## v.35
Then his purity, first having made, then initiation he should perform: by one established in a lower view, formerly initiated.
The Sanskrit: *tato@sya śuddhiṃ prākkṛtvā tato dīkṣāṃ samācaret | adhodarśanasaṃsthena guruṇā dīkṣitaḥ purā*

## v.36
Through intense śakti, afterwards, when he should approach the true guru: then too, his, of the child indeed, purity having made, the true guru.
The Sanskrit: *tīvraśaktivaśātpaścādyadā gacchetsa sadgurum | tadāpyasya śiśorevaṃ śuddhiṃ kṛtvā sa sadguruḥ*

## v.37
Initiation-and-the-rest rite, entire, should do as toldly enjoined: attained even, the true guru, fitness-state of his, not knows, if.
The Sanskrit: *dīkṣādikarma nikhilaṃ kuryāduktavidhānataḥ | prāpto@pi sadgururyogyabhāvamasya na vetti cet*

## v.38
In knowledge-giving, that, the disciple, fitness, should show his own: in every way, but non-speaking, this one; speaking, or contrary.
The Sanskrit: *vijñānadāne tacchiṣyo yogyatāṃ darśayennijām | sarvathā tvabruvanneṣa bruvāṇo vā viparyayam*

## v.39
Ignorant, in reality, just: having abandoned that, so the rite he should perform; not concealment-doubt here is to be made, by the intelligent.
The Sanskrit: *ajño vastuta eveti tattyaktvetthaṃ vidhiṃ caret | na tirobhāvaśaṅkātra kartavyā buddhiśālinā*

## v.40
Lower-touching: concealment, not higher-means-discernment, in the Siddhānta, initiated in the tantra of ten-and-eight-division.
The Sanskrit: *adhaḥspṛktvaṃ tirobhūtirnordhvopāyavivecanam | siddhānte dīkṣitāstantre daśāṣṭādaśabhedini*

## v.41
In the Bhairavīya sixty-four: those beasts should be initiated into Trika; in the Siddhavīrāvalī-essence, in the Bhairavīya, in the Kula too.
The Sanskrit: *bhairavīye catuḥṣaṣṭau tānpaśūndīkṣayettrike | siddhavīrāvalīsāre bhairavīye kule@pi ca*

## v.42
Taken up through the five-initiation-sequence, initiation called highest, hence everyone low-placed, even, by liṅga-lifting is graced.
The Sanskrit: *pañcadīkṣākramopāttā dīkṣānuttarasaṃjñitā | tena sarvo@dharastho@pi liṅgoddhṛtyānugṛhyate*

## v.43
Whoever, from heart-dwelling-Maheśāna impulsion, vast scripture-knowledge should seek, he too, should go to many gurus.
The Sanskrit: *yo@pi hṛtsthamaheśānacodanātaḥ | suvistṛtam śāstrajñānaṃ samanvicchetso@pi yāyādbahūngurūn*

## v.44
Their initiations also, should take, with-consecration-not-last: knowledge-strengthened, they, for by those knowing ones performed.
The Sanskrit: *taddīkṣāścāpi gṛhṇīyādabhiṣecanapaścimāḥ | jñānopodbalikāstā hi tattajjñānavatā kṛtāḥ*

## v.45
Told, and, in the venerable scripture, there, there, and, muchly, delight-seeking, as the bee roams from flower to flower.
The Sanskrit: *uktaṃ ca śrīmate śāstre tatra tatra ca bhūyasā | āmodārthī yathā bhṛṅgaḥ puṣpātpuṣpāntaraṃ vrajet*

## v.46
Knowledge-seeking, so the disciple, from the guru: to another guru, quoted: among many gurus, for whence knowledge is highest.
The Sanskrit: *vijñānārthī tathā śiṣyo gurorgurvantaraṃ tviti | gurūṇāṃ bhūyasāṃ madhye yato vijñānamuttamam*

## v.47
Attained, he, his guru; initiation, not here, is primary, for in consciousness, the all-knowledge-treasure: but having reached the guru, well-established.
The Sanskrit: *prāptaṃ so@sya gururdīkṣā nātra mukhyā hi saṃvidi | sarvajñānanidhānaṃ tu guruṃ saṃprāpya susthitaḥ*

## v.48
Him alone may the wise worship, eager with that inquiry into that: thus the initiation-rule, told, liṅga-lifting-crowned.
The Sanskrit: *tamevārādhayeddhīmāṃstattajjijñāsanonmukhaḥ | iti dīkṣāvidhiḥ prokto liṅgoddharaṇapaścimaḥ*

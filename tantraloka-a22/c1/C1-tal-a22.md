# C1 — tal-a22

## SUMMARY

The so-called liṅga-lifting, solely Śiva-enjoined. Mālinītantra told earth-upholding: declared-and-yoked turns not back. Fitness appoints place and time for initiation. All fruit attained in Śiva: yoked, released; unyoked, upper-purified from Bhuvaneśa. Purified, Śiva-hood, burnt bonds; man-upholding declared Vedāntic by me. Kapila precedent: sequential yoga reached the highest. No return to beast-hood; standing pure, hinted even below Śiva-principle. The yoked remain re-extractable: the distinction-giver's method. Śiva-āgama supreme over all; without its knowledge, re-beastification. The sequence: śakti-descent, impurity-loss, departure-desire, initiation, awakening, abandonable-loss, acceptable-dissolution, enjoyable-fetter-renouncing, lord-agency-destroying, self-abiding — even from another view. Extractability proclaimed of the Śiva-śakti-impelled in lower systems from Vaiṣṇava to Tantric. Heart-lotus opened by Śiva-sun-rays: first mark-lifting, then initiation. Even the other-marked reach Śiva through initiation: fast, then another day the common mantra. Worshipped, proclaim his rule: formerly marked, now impelled by You. Gracious, favor him properly, no expiation for mark-abandoning doubt. Become of Thee quickly, enjoy, be liberated: command received, vow his. Discard in water, bathe, sprinkle with argha-waters. Feed the five products and tooth-stick, blindfold, usher, prostrate. Ten mantras: praṇava through seven common. Bestow one middle mantra; day-and-night muttering with fasting. Bestowed, libated in rite-fire: perform vow-purity. Name-enclosed: 'I cleanse expiation — phaṭ svāhā.' Hundreds offered, full oblation, Vrateśvara invoked with vauṣaḍ. Tāra offered, homage done, permission proclaimed: nothing without Śiva's command. Libate Vrateśvara with svāhā, pardon, dismiss — deity and fire. Forgive-and-dismiss; third day repeats all. Eve-rite through own-desired-initiation: former mark-bearers' liberation-initiation, unqualified. Converts unfit for adept-teacher path — unless knowledge-kindled into guruhood. Īśānaśiva's Paddhati rule: converts bestow liberation; enjoyment only through means. The Yāmalī-knower performs even for the downward-gazing. Tell the secret only after performing; never to the merely other-initiated. Free yet under an ignorant guru, unassured: perform there too. Ignorant-mouthed mantra is lifeless; the mutterer no guru here, no authority-defect imputable. Lower guru purifies first; true guru completes when intense śakti drives. Unknown fitness: do the whole rite. Fitness shown in knowledge-giving; silence or perversity disqualifies. Drop the 'really ignorant' thought; make no concealment-doubt. Lower-touching is concealment, not higher-means; ten-and-eight Siddhānta initiates. Even beasts into Trika: Bhairavīya sixty-four, Siddhavīrāvalī, Kula. Highest initiation takes all; even the lowest graced by liṅga-lifting. Inner-impelled knowledge-seekers: go to many gurus. Gather many consecration-crowned initiations; the knowing-performed strengthen. Bee from flower to flower: disciple from guru to guru. Highest knowledge decides the guru; initiation secondary to consciousness-treasure. Worship him alone, inquiry-eager: the liṅga-lifting-crowned rule stands told.

## FUNCTION

Establishes liṅgoddhāra as conversion technology: Mālinī precedent, fitness-appointment, yoked/unyoked release, Kapila proof, no-return standing, re-extractability, Śaiva supremacy; seven-to-ten-step ladder; Vaiṣṇava-to-Tantric impelled ones opened by Śiva-sun; fasting, proclamation, grace-without-expiation, command, washing, feeding, blindfold entry; ten-mantra frame with one bestowed and muttered; fire-libation, name-enclosure, hundredfold offering, Tāra-permission, dismissal, third-day repeat, unqualified liberation-initiation; converts barred from adepthood unless knowledge-kindled (Īśānaśiva's liberation/enjoyment rule); Yāmalī secrecy kept from the merely initiated; ignorant-guru cases handled without authority-defect; lower purifies, true completes; whole-rite default; knowledge-giving test; no concealment-doubt; Siddhānta ten-eight and Bhairavīya sixty-four as beast-including precedents; fivefold highest initiation gracing even the lowest; many-guru seeking for the inner-impelled; initiations gathered for knowledge-strength; bee-and-flowers wandering; highest-knowledge guru over initiation; inquiry-eager worship closing in liṅga-lifting-crowned seal.

## EXPLANATION

The ahnika runs: (1–9) liṅgoddhāra charter with Mālinī/Kapila/Vedānta precedents, fitness-appointment, release both ways, no-return, re-extractability, Śaiva supremacy; (10–13) ten-step ladder, extractability for lower-system impelled, lotus-opening with mark-first ordering; (14–19) fasting, proclamation, grace-without-expiation, command, washing, feeding, blindfold entry; (20–27) ten-mantra frame, one bestowed and muttered, fire-libation, name-enclosure formula, hundredfold offering, Tāra-permission, dismissal, third-day repeat; (28–30) unqualified liberation-initiation, converts barred unless kindled, Īśānaśiva's rule; (31–34) Yāmalī secret kept, ignorant-guru cases without defect; (35–39) lower purifies, true completes, whole-rite default, knowledge-test, no concealment-doubt; (40–42) Siddhānta/Bhairavīya precedents, fivefold highest gracing the lowest; (43–47) many-guru seeking, initiations gathered, bee maxim, highest-knowledge guru, inquiry-eager worship and seal.

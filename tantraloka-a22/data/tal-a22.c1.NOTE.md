# C1 NOTE — tal-a22 (not the translation: commentary complete)

## KEY TERMS (with grounds)

- liṅgoddhāra (m.): liṅga-lifting/conversion; charter word (v1, v48 seal).
- Mālinītantra (cited precedent: declared-and-yoked doesn't return, v2).
- yogyatā (f.): fitness appointing place and time (v3).
- yukta-ayukta (bound/unbound release paths, v4; apavṛjyate = dismissed/released).
- punaruddharaṇīyatā (f.): re-extractability of the yoked (v8).
- śivāgama-viśiṣṭatā (f.): Śiva-āgama supremacy; without it re-beastification (v9).
- daśa-krama (ten-step ladder to self-abiding, v10–v11).
- śivaśakti-īrita (impelled by Śiva-śakti, v12).
- hṛdayāmbuja-vikāsa (heart-lotus opening; mark-lifting first, v13).
- prāgliṅgāntara (formerly other-marked, convertible, v14).
- liṅga-tyāga-śaṅkā (doubt over mark-abandoning; no expiation, v15–16).
- vratāpasaraṇa-ambunikṣepa-prokṣaṇa (vow discarding, immersion, sprinkling, v17–18).
- pañcagavya-dantakāṣṭha (feeding); baddhanetra-praveśa-praṇipāta (blindfold entry, v19).
- daśa-mantra (praṇava through seven common, v20); madhyama-mantra day-night muttering (v21).
- vahnau tarpaṇa; nāma-saṃpuṭa; phaṭsvāhā-ūha formula (v22–23).
- śata-sahasra-homa; vauṣaḍ-anta; Vrateśvara invoked, dismissed (v24–27).
- prāgliṅgin mokṣadīkṣā (unqualified liberation-initiation, v28).
- punarbhū (reborn convert; unfit unless knowledge-kindled, v29–30; Īśānaśiva Paddhati rule).
- yāmalī-rahasya (secret kept from merely initiated, v31–32).
- ajñācārya-mantra (lifeless; no authority-defect, v33–34).
- adhodarśana-purification first; sadguru completes (v35–37).
- yogyatā-parīkṣā (knowledge-giving test; silence/perversity disqualify, v38–39).
- adhaḥspṛk (lower-touching; flagged); siddhānta-daśāṣṭādaśa; bhairavīya-catuḥṣaṣṭi (v40–41).
- pañcadīkṣā-uttama (highest fivefold; graces even the lowest, v42).
- bahuguru-gamana (many-guru seeking when inner-impelled, v43–44).
- bhṛṅga-nyāya (bee maxim); vijñāna-uttama-guru (highest-knowledge guru over initiation, v45–47).
- liṅgoddharaṇa-paścima (lifting-crowned seal, v48).

## LORE (web-searched: many this turn)

- Pradipaka ch22 full rendering vv1–19 (Mālinītanra precedent; ten-step krama; Vaiṣṇava-to-Tantric impelled; lotus opening; fast/proclaim/grace-without-expiation/command/wash/feed/blindfold/usher/prostrate sequence; ten mantras; day-night muttering; fire-libation; name-enclosure; phaṭsvāhā; hundredfold offering; Vrateśvara; permission; dismissal; third-day repeat); punarbhū passage (Īśānaśiva Paddhati rule: converts give liberation, enjoyment only through means).
- Dyczkowski Vol 9 summary (anuttaratrikakula): ch22 = rite by which a Vaiṣṇava is converted to a Śaivite.
- anuttaratrikakula Pradipaka ch22 TOC: (i) knowledge by hearing + authorization, (ii) purification, (iii) disappearance of sins, (iv) removal of doubts.
- Wisdomlib TĀ ch22 Devanagari + grammar analysis (parse cross-check).
- Sauhaus MLBD Vol 6 summary: ch22 = liṅgoddhāra (raising the emblem of Śiva).

## DECISIONS

- 0 forks: all hard points convergent with flags (apavṛjyate reading, sādhāra mantra, adhaḥspṛk, bhṛgu fasts, antaḥsatattvaka, saṃsyuḥ).

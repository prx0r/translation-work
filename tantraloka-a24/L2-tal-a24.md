# Tantraloka-a24 — L2 READ


## v.1
Now hear the secret final sacrament taught in the Śāmbhava teaching.
The Sanskrit: *atha śāmbhavaśāsanoditāṃ sarahasyāṃ śṛṇutāntyasaṃskriyām*

## v.2
For all from lower stations up to gurus, clearly: after the aforesaid śaktipāta one should perform the funerary initiation.
The Sanskrit: *sarveṣāmadharasthānāṃ gurvantānāmapi sphuṭam | śaktipātātpurāproktātkuryādantyeṣṭidīkṣaṇam*

## v.3
And for followers of the upper teaching whose selves are samaya-struck, the funerary initiation is to be performed by the tattva-knowing guru.
The Sanskrit: *ūrdhvaśāsanagānāṃ ca samayopahatātmanām | antyeṣṭidīkṣā kartavyā guruṇā tattvavedinā*

## v.4
For him who stumbled through negligence into samaya-conduct faults, the funerary initiation is to be done — so in the venerable Dīkṣottara teaching.
The Sanskrit: *samayācāradoṣeṣu pramādātskhalitasya hi | antyeṣṭidīkṣā kāryeti śrīdīkṣottaraśāsane*

## v.5
Whatever was told before in the rite called corpse-uplifting — that entire procedure here, on the corpse-body, he should perform, there on the image.
The Sanskrit: *yatkiṃcitkathitaṃ pūrvaṃ mṛtoddhārābhidhe vidhau | pratimāyāṃ tadevātra sarvaṃ śavatanau caret*

## v.6
This rule told in the Siddhātantra is set forth: what would be last, making that last first and first last.
The Sanskrit: *śrīsiddhātantrakathito vidhireṣa nirūpyate | antimaṃ yadbhavetpūrvaṃ tatkṛtvāntimamādimam*

## v.7
Having withdrawn each oblation, the funerary rite is held twofold — but not so for the sādhaka whose samaya is bathed in worship, meditation, and mantra.
The Sanskrit: *saṃhṛtyaikaikamiṣṭiryā sāntyeṣṭirdvitayī matā | pūjādhyānajapāpluṣṭasamaye natu sādhake*

## v.8
From the body's fall this one is freed, or becomes a sky-rover, dear lady; when the tattva-accomplished teacher has died, wherever it be.
The Sanskrit: *piṇḍapātādayaṃ muktaḥ khecaro vā bhavetpriye | ācārye tattvasaṃpanne yatra tatra mṛte sati*

## v.9
No funerary rite at all is found for the pure-minded; but those killed by mantra-yoga rites are in hell — they.
The Sanskrit: *antyeṣṭirnaiva vidyeta śuddhacetasyamūrdhani | mantrayogādibhirye ca māritā narake tu te*

## v.10
For them it is to be performed here by the ultra-compassionate guru; but no maṇḍala-etc. here — in the cremation-ground rite.
The Sanskrit: *kāryā teṣāmihāntyeṣṭirguruṇātikṛpālunā | na maṇḍalādikaṃ tvatra bhavecchamāśānike vidhau*

## v.11
Some said even that is to be done in the mortuary: having worshipped the Omnipresent, he should perform the entire nyāsa as before.
The Sanskrit: *kecittadapi kartavyamūcire pretasadmani | pūjayitvā vibhuṃ sarvaṃ nyāsaṃ pūrvavadācaret*

## v.12
By dissolution-sequence, from the foot to the head-end, just so should he awaken him — with rite, knowledge, and absorption.
The Sanskrit: *saṃhārakramayogena caraṇānmūrdhapaścimam | tathaiva bodhayedenaṃ kriyājñānasamādhibhiḥ*

## v.13
With bindu he should block the tattva, with the śakti-seed pierce; at the nāda-place he should rub, and with the trident strike.
The Sanskrit: *bindunā rodhayettattvaṃ śaktibījena vedhayet | ghaṭṭayennādadeśe tu triśūlena tu tāḍayet*

## v.14
With what lies inside suṣumnā indeed, with emission again and again he should strike all the kalās; then this bound soul trembles.
The Sanskrit: *suṣumnāntargatenaiva visargeṇa punaḥ punaḥ | tāḍayeta kalāḥ sarvāḥ kampate@sau tataḥ paśuḥ*

## v.15
He should raise the left hand — or then join him to the Supreme; without proof, liberation is indeed unbelievable to the deluded.
The Sanskrit: *utkṣipedvāmahastaṃ vā tatastaṃ yojayetpare | pratyayena vinā mokṣo hyaśraddheyo vimohitaiḥ*

## v.16
For this purpose this is taught — but not as giving what serves liberation; thus said the Supreme Lord in the venerable Kulagahvara teaching.
The Sanskrit: *tadarthametaduditaṃ natu mokṣopayogyadaḥ | ityūce parameśaḥ śrīkulagahvaraśāsane*

## v.17
The to-be-proved, like liberation, is to be inferred through proofs, since beyond the senses; and in the Dīkṣottara the puryaṣṭaka-class offering is taught.
The Sanskrit: *sādhyo@numeyo mokṣādiḥ pratyayairyadatīndriyaḥ | dīkṣottare ca puryaṣṭavargārpaṇamihoditam*

## v.18
Its rite: on the ear-petal lotus, in the middle the god Sadāśiva, the Īśa-Rudra-Hari-Brahman tetrad beginning with the east quarter.
The Sanskrit: *tadvidhiḥ śrutipatre@bje madhye devaṃ sadāśivam | īśarudraharibrahmacatuṣkaṃ prāgdigāditaḥ*

## v.19
Having worshipped, hearing and touch, taste, smell, the twofold body, intellect and ego, and mind — to Brahmā and the rest he should offer in order.
The Sanskrit: *pūjayitvā śrutisparśau rasaṃ gandhaṃ vapurdvayam | dhyahaṃkṛtī manaśceti brahmādiṣvarpayetkramāt*

## v.20
Having made their libation, with a hundred fire-offerings the guide effects this renunciate initiation, purifying the city-of-eight.
The Sanskrit: *eteṣāṃ tarpaṇaṃ kṛtvā śatahomena daiśikaḥ | eṣā sāṃnyāsikī dīkṣā puryaṣṭakaviśodhanī*

## v.21
And with the puryaṣṭaka gone, no heaven, hell, and the rest; having done thus, nothing worldly at all is to be done indeed.
The Sanskrit: *puryaṣṭakasyābhāve ca na svarganarakādayaḥ | tathā kṛtvā na kartavyaṃ laukikaṃ kiṃcanāpi hi*

## v.22
Said in the venerable Mādhavakula: established in the teaching, even for the dead, he should abandon piṇḍa-offerings, water, tears — the worldly.
The Sanskrit: *uktaṃ śrīmādhavakule śāsanastho mṛteṣvapi | piṇḍapātodakāsrvādi laukikaṃ parivarjayet*

## v.23
Having fully worshipped Śiva, he should perform the wheel-worship according to capacity, in order on the memorial days.
The Sanskrit: *śivaṃ saṃpūjya cakrārcāṃ yathāśakti samācaret | kramāttridaśamatriṃśatriṃśavatsaravāsare*

## v.24
Thus told is this funerary sacrifice, spoken by the Supreme Lord.
The Sanskrit: *ityukto@ntyeṣṭiyāgo@yaṃ parameśvarabhāṣitaḥ*

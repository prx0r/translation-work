# C1 — tal-a24

## SUMMARY

The secret final sacrament of the Śāmbhava teaching. Funerary initiation after śaktipāta for all from lower stations to gurus; for upper-teaching samaya-struck selves by the knowing guru; for negligent stumblers per Dīkṣottara. The corpse-uplifting image-rite transfers whole onto the corpse-body; the Siddhātantra rule runs last-first. Withdrawn oblation by oblation, the rite is twofold — eased for the worship-bathed sādhaka. From body's fall comes freedom or sky-roving; for the ripe teacher anywhere, no rite binds place; for the pure-minded none at all — though the mantra-slain lie in hell and need the ultra-compassionate guru, maṇḍala shed at the burning ground while the strict keep full nyāsa after Vibhu-worship. Foot-to-head dissolution awakening through rite-knowledge-absorption; bindu blocks, śakti-seed pierces, nāda rubbed, trident strikes; suṣumnā-emission beats the kalās till the bound one trembles. The left hand rises, the soul is joined to the Supreme — signs for the faithless, since unbelieving liberation needs seeing. Signs build trust, never cause release (Kulagahvara). The unseen is inferred through proofs; the Dīkṣottara adds the puryaṣṭaka-offering. On the ear-petal lotus Sadāśiva centers, four gods quartered from the east; senses, bodies, intellect, ego, mind go to Brahmā and the rest in order. Libation plus hundredfold fire: the renunciate initiation cleansing the eightfold city. City gone, no heavens — and no worldly rites after; Mādhavakula abandons piṇḍa, water, tears even for the dead. Śiva and wheel worshipped by capacity on the memorial days. Told: the Lord-spoken funerary sacrifice.

## FUNCTION

Establishes funerary initiation as proof-bearing release-technology: śaktipāta-gated, samaya-repairing, image-transferred, reverse-ordered, twofold-withdrawn; competence-gated and place-freed for the ripe; hell-rescue by compassion without pomp; forcible tattva-opening crowned by visible tremor; signs epistemically fenced as trust-builders, never causes; subtle-city offering replacing heavens; worldly mourning abolished for the established — closing in Lord-voiced seal.

## EXPLANATION

The ahnika runs: (1) secret sacrament announced; (2–4) scope — all stations, samaya-struck high ones, negligent stumblers with Dīkṣottara warrant; (5–6) image-to-corpse transfer, Siddhātantra reversal; (7–8) twofold withdrawal with sādhaka ease; freedom, sky-roving, place-freed teachers; (9–11) pure exempt, mantra-slain in hell, compassionate rescue without maṇḍala, strict full-nyāsa school; (12–14) dissolution awakening, four forcible openings, emission-beating with tremor-sign; (15–16) left hand, Supreme-joining, signs-for-faith with the Kulagahvara fence; (17–20) inference epistemology, puryaṣṭaka offering, lotus diagram, sense-offerings, libation plus hundredfold fire as renunciate cleansing; (21–23) no heavens, no worldly rites, Mādhavakula mourning-abolition, memorial Śiva-wheel worship; (24) Lord-voiced seal.

# C1 — Tantrāloka Ahnika 24

## tal-a24-p01–p03 — ahnika 24
**SUMMARY.** Tantrāloka Ahnika 24: [and]-:C24 atha śrītantrāloke caturviṃśatitamamāhnikam atha śāmbhavaśāsanoditāṃ sarahasyāṃ śṛṇutāntyasaṃskriyām sarveṣāmadharasthānāṃ gurvantānāmapi sphuṭam / śaktipātātpurāproktātkuryādantyeṣṭidīkṣaṇam ūrdhvaśāsanagānāṃ ca samayopahatātmanām / antyeṣṭidīkṣā kartavyā guruṇā tattvavedinā samayācāradoṣeṣu pramādātskhalitasya hi / antyeṣṭidīkṣā kāryeti śrīdīkṣottaraśāsane yatkiṃcitkathitaṃ pūrvaṃ mṛtoddhārābhidhe vidhau / pratimāyāṃ tadevātra sarvaṃ śavatanau caret śrīsiddhātantrakathito vidhireṣa nirūpyate / antimaṃ yadbhavetpūrvaṃ tatkṛtvāntimamādimam saṃhṛtyaikaikamiṣṭiryā sāntyeṣṭirdvitayī matā / pūjādhyānajapāpluṣṭasamaye natu sādhake piṇḍapātādayaṃ muktaḥ khecaro vā bhavetpriye / ācārye tattvasaṃpanne yatra tatra mṛte sati antyeṣṭirnaiva vidyeta śuddhacetasyamūrdhani / mantrayogādibhirye ca māritā narake tu te kāryā teṣāmihāntyeṣṭirguruṇātikṛpālunā / na maṇḍalādikaṃ tvatra bhavecchamāśānike vidhau [and]-kecittadapi kartavyamūcire pretasadmani / pūjayitvā vibhuṃ sarvaṃ nyāsaṃ pūrvavadācaret saṃhārakramayogena caraṇānmūrdhapaścimam / tathaiva bodhayedenaṃ kriyājñānasamādhibhiḥ bindunā rodhayettattvaṃ śaktibījena vedhayet / ghaṭṭayennādadeśe tu triśūlena tu tāḍayet suṣumnāntargatenaiva visargeṇa punaḥ punaḥ / tāḍayeta kalāḥ sarvāḥ kampate@sau tataḥ paśuḥ utkṣipedvāmahastaṃ vā tatastaṃ yojayetpare / pratyayena vinā mokṣo hyaśraddheyo vimohitaiḥ tadarthametaduditaṃ natu mokṣopayogyadaḥ / ityūce parameśaḥ śrīkulagahvaraśāsane sādhyo@numeyo mokṣādiḥ pratyayairyadatīndriyaḥ / dīkṣottare ca puryaṣṭavargārpaṇamihoditam tadvidhiḥ śrutipatre@bje madhye devaṃ sadāśivam / īśarudraharibrahmacatuṣkaṃ prāgdigāditaḥ pūjayitvā śrutisparśau rasaṃ gandhaṃ vapurdvayam / dhyahaṃkṛtī manaśceti brahmādiṣvarpayetkramāt eteṣāṃ tarpaṇaṃ kṛtvā śatahomena daiśikaḥ / eṣā sāṃnyāsikī dīkṣā puryaṣṭakaviśodhanī [and]-puryaṣṭakasyābhāve ca na svarganarakādayaḥ / tathā kṛtvā na kartavyaṃ laukikaṃ kiṃcanāpi hi uktaṃ śrīmādhavakule śāsanastho mṛteṣvapi / piṇḍapātodakāsrvādi laukikaṃ parivarjayet śivaṃ saṃpūjya cakrārcāṃ yathāśakti samācaret / kramāttridaśamatriṃśatriṃśavatsaravāsare ityukto@ntyeṣṭiyāgo@yaṃ parameśvarabhāṣitaḥ

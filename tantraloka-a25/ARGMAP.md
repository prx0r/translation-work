## tal-a25-p02 (25.11-25.20)
- speaker: Abhinava.

## tal-a25-p03 (25.21-25.29)
- speaker: Abhinava.
- question: (passage 25.21-25.29).
- premises: [and]-āpīḍya kukṣiṃ namayetsā vahennāḍikā kṣaṇāt / evaṃ śrāddhamukhenāpi bhogamokṣobhayasthitim kuryāditi śivenoktaṃ tatra tatra kṛpālunā / śaktipātodaye jantoryenopāyena daiśikaḥ karotyuddharaṇaṃ tattannirvāṇāyāsya kalpate / uddhartā devadevo hi sa cācintyaprabhāvakaḥ upāyaṃ gurudīkṣādidvāramātreṇa saṃśrayet / uktaṃ śrīmanmataṅgākhye munipraśnādanantaram muktirvivekāttattvānāṃ dīkṣāto yogato yadi / caryāmātrātkathaṃ sā syādityataḥ samamuttaram prahasyoce vibhuḥ kasmādbhrāntiste parameśituḥ / sarvānugrāhakatvaṃ hi saṃsiddhaṃ dṛśyatāṃ kila prāptamṛtyorviṣavyādhiśastrādi kila kāraṇam / alpaṃ vā bahu vā tadvadanudhyā muktikāraṇam muktyarthamupacaryante bāhyaliṅgānyamūni tu / iti jñātvā na sandeha itthaṃ kāryo vipaścitā iyataiva kathaṃ muktiriti bhaktiṃ parāṃ śrayet / uktaḥ śrāddhavidhirbhrāntigarātaṅkavimardanaḥ

## v.1
Now is told the remaining activity useful to the living.
The Sanskrit: *athocyate śeṣavṛttirjīvatāmupayoginī*

## v.2
Initiation manifold this ending in śrāddha which is declared; that for purification, for liberation, for enjoyment also, for both or.
The Sanskrit: *dīkṣā bahuprakāreyaṃ śrāddhāntā yā prakīrtitā sā saṃskriyāyai mokṣāya bhogāyāpi dvayāya vā*

## v.3
There for accomplishment of purification which initiation directly not liberating; by intention which and directly liberating with-seed.
The Sanskrit: *tatra saṃskārasiddhyai yā dīkṣā sākṣānna mocanī anusaṃdhivaśādyā ca sākṣānmoktrī sabījikā*

## v.4
By those two initiated who, their life-long behavior to be spoken, of putrakas and the rest, for accomplishment of identity.
The Sanskrit: *tayobhayyā dīkṣitā ye teṣāmājīvavartanam vaktavyaṃ putrakādīnāṃ tanmayatvaprasiddhaye*

## v.5
Of desirer of enjoyment or of desirer of liberation or, from own consciousness guru scripture by authority which, for purification initiation indeed by guru done.
The Sanskrit: *bubhukṣorvā mumukṣorvā svasaṃvidguruśāstrataḥ pramāṇādyā saṃskriyāyai dīkṣā hi guruṇā kṛtā*

## v.6
Then he purified fit having known self in own doctrine; practice of that spoken thing for enjoyment for liberation and cultivates.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ sa saṃskṛtaṃ yogyaṃ jñātvātmānaṃ svaśāsane taduktavastvanuṣṭhānaṃ bhuktyai muktyai ca sevate*

## v.7
From Ācārya faith indeed who even would be enjoyment-liberation holder; for destruction of that obstacle emergence should speak to him also behavior.
The Sanskrit: *ācāryapratyayādeva yo@pi syādbhuktimuktibhāk tatpratyūhodayadhvastyai brūyāttasyāpi vartanam*

## v.8
Of own consciousness and guru consciousness similar faith holder even, by remaining activity to be instructed for cessation of that obstacle etc.
The Sanskrit: *svasaṃvidgurusaṃvittyostulyapratyayabhāgapi śeṣavṛttyā samādeśyastadvighnādipraśāntaye*

## v.9
Who in every way having abandoned dependence on other but standing from own; from faith who even and from Ācārya faith indeed from only.
The Sanskrit: *yaḥ sarvathā parāpekṣāmujjhitvā tu sthito nijāt pratyayādyo@pi cācāryapratyayādeva kevalāt*

## v.10
Those two natural and seedless who would speak for remaining activity; progressively identity means Guru worship devoted but those two.
The Sanskrit: *tau sāṃsiddhikanirbījau ko vadeccheṣavṛttaye kramāttanmayatopāyagurvarcanaratau tu tau*

## v.11
There of these for remaining activity purpose constant and occasional fixed; excluding optional because desires varied varied means.
The Sanskrit: *tatraiṣāṃ śeṣavṛttyarthaṃ nityanaimittike dhruve kāmyavarjaṃ yataḥ kāmāścitrāścitrābhyupāyakāḥ*

## v.12
There constant rule triad of junctures observance in deity vow; with Guru fire scripture worship compassion to beings thus this.
The Sanskrit: *tatra nityo vidhiḥ sandhyānuṣṭhānaṃ devatāvraje gurvagniśāstrasahite pūjā bhūtadayetyayam*

## v.13
Occasional but of all of holidays worship muttering; by special occasion and what pavitraka procedure succession.
The Sanskrit: *naimittikastu sarveṣāṃ parvaṇāṃ pūjanaṃ japaḥ viśeṣavaśataḥ kiṃca pavitrakavidhikramaḥ*

## v.14
Of Ācārya and initiation this many types investigated; explanation etc and subsequent to that occasional indeed.
The Sanskrit: *ācāryasya ca dīkṣeyaṃ bahubhedā vivecitā vyākhyādikaṃ ca tattasyādhikaṃ naimittikaṃ dhruvam*

## v.15
There first to disciple should speak Guru constant rule clearly; his fitness having inspected of far-spreading and opposite-natured.
The Sanskrit: *tatrādau śiśave vrūyādgururnityavidhiṃ sphuṭam tadyogyatāṃ samālokya vitatāvitatātmanām*

## v.16
Of main and other mantras etc fitness for virility penetration etc having seen in disciple; that very to him root-mantra should hand.
The Sanskrit: *mukhyetarādimantrāṇāṃ vīryavyāptyādiyogyatām dṛṣṭvā śiṣye tamevāsmai mūlamantraṃ samarpayet*

## v.17
By that scripture initiated indeed this free from restraint conduct doubting; not in main fit thus by other service would be but fitness.
The Sanskrit: *tacchāstradīkṣito hyeṣa niryantrācāraśaṅkitaḥ na mukhye yogya ityanyasevātaḥ syāttu yogyatā*

## v.18
Of sādhaka of desirer of enjoyment but of aspirant even or; by flower fall force accomplished mantra to be handed for accomplishment of sādhya.
The Sanskrit: *sādhakasya bubhukṣostu sādhakībhāvino@pivā puṣpapātavaśātsiddho mantro@rpyaḥ sādhyasiddhaye*

## v.19
In far-spreading in quality-possessing or in rite in taught; again Guru having known to him fitness essence concise should perform rite.
The Sanskrit: *vitate guṇabhūte vā vidhau diṣṭe punarguruḥ jñātvāsmai yogyatāṃ sāraṃ saṃkṣiptaṃ vidhimācaret*

## v.20
There this rule if which mantra form not; that Guru having written should teach to disciple especially in upper doctrine.
The Sanskrit: *tatraiṣa niyamo yadyanmāntraṃ rūpaṃ na tadguruḥ likhitvā prathayecchiṣye viśeṣādūrdhvaśāsane*

## v.21
Mantras syllable-natured they and reflection-natured; that Guru-consciousness-undivided should transfer she then in disciple.
The Sanskrit: *mantrā varṇātmakāste ca parāmarśātmakāḥ saca gurusaṃvidabhinnaśvetsaṃkrāmetsā tataḥ śiśau*

## v.22
Written-abiding but who mantra powerless; he here imagined from convention-force; not of his from book shines glory.
The Sanskrit: *lipisthitastu yo mantro nirvīryaḥ so@tra kalpitaḥ saṃketabalato nāsya pustakātprathate mahaḥ*

## v.23
Book-learned vidyā thus said in Siddha-doctrine; then who but in book-obtained even in mantra power know.
The Sanskrit: *pustakādhītavidyāścetyuktaṃ siddhāmate tataḥ ye tu pustakalabdhe@pi mantre vīryaṃ prajānate*

## v.24
They Bhairavī-purification declared natural thus thus; having known Guru properly supreme-bliss-revolving.
The Sanskrit: *te bhairavīyasaṃskārāḥ proktāḥ sāṃsiddhikā iti iti jñātvā guruḥ samyak paramānandaghūrṇitaḥ*

## v.25
In such in such in place having worshipped rite should perform; as another disciple's practice not by another disciple is known.
The Sanskrit: *tādṛśe tādṛśe dhāmni pūjayitvā vidhiṃ caret yathānyaśiṣyānuṣṭhānaṃ nānyaśiṣyeṇa budhyate*

## v.26
So should do Guru secrecy loss defective because; of goddesses triad pure or which from mate union.
The Sanskrit: *tathā kuryādgururguptihānirdoṣavatī yataḥ devīnāṃ tritayaṃ śuddhaṃ yadvā yāmalayogataḥ*

## v.27
Goddess one or pure should speak or mate-natured; there mantra clear from mouth by Guru whispered proclaimed.
The Sanskrit: *devīmekāmatho śuddhāṃ vadedvā yāmalātmikām tatra mantraṃ sphuṭaṃ vaktrādguruṇopāṃśu coditam*

## v.28
Having heard of practice him should practice mentally by oneself; then well-taught triad of place body inner purification.
The Sanskrit: *avadhāryā pravṛttestamabhyasyenmanasā svayam tataḥ suśikṣitāṃ sthānadehāntaḥśodhanatrayīm*

## v.29
Nyāsa meditation muttering mudrā worship should perform with effort; there at dawn having awakened own-dear former-deity should remember.
The Sanskrit: *nyāsaṃ dhyānaṃ japaṃ mudrāṃ pūjāṃ kuryātprayatnataḥ tatra prabhāte saṃbudhya sveṣṭāṃ prāgdevatāṃ smaret*

## v.30
Having done necessary duty purified having become then house; having taken refuge north-facing in triad of place body inner.
The Sanskrit: *kṛtāvaśyakakartavyaḥ śuddho bhūtvā tato gṛham āśrityottaradigvaktraḥ sthānadehāntaratraye*

## v.31
Purification having made of mantras in proper place placement; mudrā exhibition meditation from difference-nondifference nature.
The Sanskrit: *śuddhiṃ vidhāya mantrāṇāṃ yathāsthānaṃ niveśanam mudrāpradarśanaṃ dhyānaṃ bhedābhedasvarūpataḥ*

## v.32
In body breath intellect ether earth by mind there and worship; muttering and here according to power to god this offering.
The Sanskrit: *dehāsudhīvyomabhūṣu manasā tatra cārcanam japaṃ cātra yathāśakti devāyaitannivedanam*

## v.33
For accomplishment of identity state at every juncture should practice; others but east north west south directions fourfold.
The Sanskrit: *tanmayībhāvasiddhyarthaṃ pratisandhyaṃ samācaret anye tu prāgudakpaścāddaśadikṣu catuṣṭayīm*

## v.34
Of junctures they say this and Tantric not our opinion; which that in time chapter before juncture declared fourfold.
The Sanskrit: *sandhyānāmāhuretacca tāntrikīyaṃ na no matam yāsau kālādhikāre prāk sandhyā proktā catuṣṭayī*

## v.35
That very within having concentrated juncture-related should practice rite; making fourfold juncture or one disciple.
The Sanskrit: *tāmevāntaḥ samādhāya sāndhyaṃ vidhimupācaret sandhyācatuṣṭayīkṛtyamekasyāmathavā śiśuḥ*

## v.36
Should do study knowledge Guru duty etc devoted; from juncture meditation risen endless identity state union.
The Sanskrit: *kuryātsvādhyāyavijñānagurukṛtyāditatparaḥ sandhyādhyānoditānantatanmayībhāvayuktitaḥ*

## v.37
By that purification force all time would be identity indeed he; then at desired time he worship with flowers liquor and the rest.
The Sanskrit: *tatsaṃskāravaśātsarvaṃ kālaṃ syāttanmayo hyasau tato yatheṣṭakāle@sau pūjāṃ puṣpāsavādibhiḥ*

## v.38
On altar and the rest disciple should do according to capacity etc; well-purified with rite all having done inner muttering end.
The Sanskrit: *sthaṇḍilādau śiśuḥ kuryādvibhavādyanurūpataḥ suśuddhaḥ sanvidhiṃ sarvaṃ kṛtvāntarajapāntakam*

## v.39
Argha-vessel before as having made from own dear mantra; by that altar flowers etc all wise should sprinkle.
The Sanskrit: *arghapātraṃ purā yadvadvidhāya sveṣṭamantrataḥ tena sthaṇḍilapuṣpādi sarvaṃ saṃprokṣayedbudhaḥ*

## v.40
Then there indeed having intended door seat Guru succession; should worship Śiva-absorbed own-body-worship before.
The Sanskrit: *tatastatraiva saṃkalpya dvārāsanagurukramam pūjayecchivatāviṣṭaḥ svadehārcāpuraḥsaram*

## v.41
Then that altar having seen sky crystal pure consciousness-natured; having seen there own deity-group.
The Sanskrit: *tatastatsthaṇḍilaṃ vīdhravyomasphaṭikanirmalam bodhātmakaṃ samālokya tatra svaṃ devatāgaṇam*

## v.42
By reflection-ness by mirror-ness and from consciousness; this invocation principal like fan of winds.
The Sanskrit: *pratibimbatayā paśyedbimbatvena ca bodhataḥ etadāvāhanaṃ mukhyaṃ vyajanānmarutāmiva*

## v.43
All-going even as wind by fan sustained; all-pervading thing-doing mantra-cakra from rising so would be.
The Sanskrit: *sarvago@pi marudyadvadvyajanenopajīvitaḥ arthakṛtsarvagaṃ mantracakraṃ rūḍhestathā bhavet*

## v.44
By fifty-four that this principle is said; in venerable Nirmaryāda-śāstra and that this by Lord spoken.
The Sanskrit: *catuṣkapañcāśikayā tadetattattvamucyate śrīnirmaryādaśāstre ca tadetadvibhunoditam*

## v.45
God all-going god boundless how Śiva is invoked is forgiven; thus asked spoke Lord.
The Sanskrit: *devaḥ sarvagato deva nirmaryādaḥ kathaṃ śivaḥ āvāhyate kṣamyate vetyevaṃpṛṣṭo@bravīdvibhuḥ*

## v.46
By impression is invoked goddess impression and is dismissed; by ultimate truth of god no invocation dismissal.
The Sanskrit: *vāsanāvāhyate devi vāsanā ca visṛjyate paramārthena devasya nāvāhanavisarjane*

## v.47
Invoked by me god on altar and established; worshipped praised thus having rejoiced god should dismiss.
The Sanskrit: *āvāhito mayā devaḥ sthaṇḍile ca pratiṣṭhitaḥ pūjitaḥ stuta ityevaṃ hṛṣṭvā devaṃ visarjayet*

## v.48
Of unawakened beings for generating satisfaction indeed; invocation etc of them activity how otherwise.
The Sanskrit: *prāṇināmaprabuddhānāṃ santoṣajananāya vai āvāhanādikaṃ teṣāṃ pravṛttiḥ kathamanyathā*

## v.49
By time but know engaged in Lord's rule; progressively of god attainment world-preceding.
The Sanskrit: *kālena tu vijānanti pravṛttāḥ patiśāsane anukrameṇa devasya prāptiṃ bhuvanapūrvikām*

## v.50
By knowledge lamp light destroyed all ignorance collection; whence is brought god where or is led even he.
The Sanskrit: *jñānadīpadyutidhvastasamastājñānasañcayāḥ kuto vānīyate devaḥ kutra vā nīyate@pi saḥ*

## v.51
By gross subtle etc difference he indeed everywhere abiding; in invoked in mantra-group by flower liquor offerings.
The Sanskrit: *sthūlasūkṣmādibhedena sa hi sarvatra saṃsthitaḥ āvāhite mantragaṇe puṣpāsavanivedanaiḥ*

## v.52
By incense and libation to be done by faith devotion strength fitting; of blazing by śakti nāda etc mantras with liquor by palas.
The Sanskrit: *dhūpaiśca tarpaṇaṃ kāryaṃ śraddhābhaktibalocitaiḥ dīptānāṃ śaktinādādimantrāṇāmāsavaiḥ palaiḥ*

## v.53
By red before libation after by flower incense etc abundance; of come but of mantra not should do libation if.
The Sanskrit: *raktaiḥ prāk tarpaṇa paścāt puṣpadhūpādivistaraiḥ āgatasya tu mantrasya na kuryāttarpaṇaṃ yadi*

## v.54
Takes half body he thus said indeed by Śambhu; whatever of his in mind blossoming grants.
The Sanskrit: *haratyardhaśarīraṃ sa ityuktaṃ kila śambhunā yadyadevāsya manasi vikāsitvaṃ prayacchati*

## v.55
By that indeed should do worship he thus Śambhu's decision; of sādhakas of desirers of enjoyment rule bound by niyati.
The Sanskrit: *tenaiva kuryātpūjāṃ sa iti śambhorviniścayaḥ sādhakānāṃ bubhukṣūṇāṃ vidhirniyatiyantritaḥ*

## v.56
Of desirers of liberation of knowers of principle he indeed but unrestricted; in action desiring special in special cause should touch.
The Sanskrit: *mumukṣūṇāṃ tattvavidāṃ sa eva tu nirargalaḥ kārye viśeṣamādhitsurviśiṣṭaṃ kāraṇaṃ spṛśet*

## v.57
Red cotton wool like seed heap are in enjoyment; specials and varied spoken by cause.
The Sanskrit: *raktakarpāsatūlecchustulyatadbījapuñjavat santi bhoge viśeṣāśca vicitrāḥ kāraṇeritāḥ*

## v.58
By place time investigation quality substance action etc small action great or by heart delight giving.
The Sanskrit: *deśakālānusandhānaguṇadravyakriyādibhiḥ svalpā kriyā bhūyasī vā hṛdayāhlādadāyibhiḥ*

## v.59
By external by imagination born or by agents imagined; of desirer of liberation not for special rule of final good towards.
The Sanskrit: *bāhyaiḥ saṃkalpajairvāpi kārakaiḥ parikalpitā mumukṣorna viśeṣāya naiḥśreyasavidhiṃ prati*

## v.60

The Sanskrit: *nahi brahmaṇi śaṃsanti bāhulyālpatvadurdaśāḥ citaḥ svātantryasāratvāt tasyānandaghanatvataḥ*

## v.61
Action would be for identity becoming by heart delight giving; from Śiva nondifference weight state group flows which taste.
The Sanskrit: *kriyā syāttanmayībhūtyai hṛdayāhlādadāyibhiḥ śivābhedabharādbhāvavargaḥ ścyotati yaṃ rasam*

## v.62
That very in supreme in abode wise should offer for worship; in hymns in many ways and this by me spoken in own āhnika.
The Sanskrit: *tameva parame dhāmni pūjanāyārpayedbudhaḥ stotreṣu bahudhā caitanmayā proktaṃ nijāhnike*

## v.63
Having surpassed supreme truth state spread radiance shines; which supreme nectar view you worship by that knowers of secret.
The Sanskrit: *adhiśayya pāramārthikabhāvaprasaraprakāśamullasati yā paramāmṛtadṛk tvāṃ tayārcayante rahasyavidaḥ*

## v.64
Having made support-bearing wonder-taste-sprinkling-moment-washed taken by mind; by own nature flowers by own delight abundance; bliss nectar full own heart argha vessel succession you with goddess together in body temple abode worship god day and night.
The Sanskrit: *kṛtvādhāradharāṃ camatkṛtirasaprokṣākṣaṇakṣālitāmāttairmānasataḥ svabhāvakusumaiḥ svāmodasandohibhiḥ ānandāmṛtanirbharasvahṛdayānarghārghapātrakramāt tvāṃ devyā saha dehadevasadane devārcaye@harniśam*

Various taste essence this three worlds; heart cakra machine placed upward pressed discrimination weight from squeezing flowing out; which consciousness supreme nectar death old age birth removing blooms; by that you with oblation supreme in supreme should satisfy day and night.

## v.66
Thus three-verse-collected-meaning within contemplating; by which by any by state should satisfy deity group.

Mudrā should exhibit after mentally or even from yoga; by speech by mantra-yoga by body from placement.

## v.68

The Sanskrit: *kṛtvā japaṃ tataḥ sarvaṃ devatāyai samarpayet taccoktaṃ kartṛtātattvanirūpaṇavidhau purā*

Then dismissal to be done from consciousness identity application; having made or fire-abiding mantra satisfaction from declared procedure.

## v.76
Flowers and the rest all standing there in that deep water should throw; said altar-sacrifice this in constant rite by Śambhu.
The Sanskrit: *puṣpādi sarvaṃ tatsthaṃ tadagādhāmbhasi nikṣipet uktaḥ sthaṇḍilayāgo@yaṃ nityakarmaṇi śambhunā*

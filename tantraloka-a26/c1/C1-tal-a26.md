# C1 — tal-a26

## SUMMARY

The ahnika teaches the śeṣavṛtti: the remaining observance after initiation — constant and occasional rites, daily worship, invocation and dismissal, and the body as temple.

## FUNCTION

Establishes śeṣavṛtti: the remaining observance after initiation — constant and occasional rites, daily worship, invocation and dismissal, and the body as temple.

## KEY TERMS

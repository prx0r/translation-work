## v.1
Now the liṅga-worship hinted in the Mālinī doctrine is told.
The Sanskrit: *athocyate liṅgapūjā sūcitā mālinīmate*

## v.2
Of their higher-śāstra-told mantras: not installed; should put outside, then: these, by secrecy, are givers of siddhi.
The Sanskrit: *eteṣāmūrdhvaśāstroktamantrāṇāṃ na pratiṣṭhitam bahiṣkuryāttato hyete rahasyatvena siddhidāḥ*

## v.3
Possessing the entry into the greatness of own vigor-bliss: who give siddhi — of them, externality is form-loss.
The Sanskrit: *svavīryānandamāhātmyapraveśavaśaśālinīm ye siddhiṃ dadate teṣāṃ bāhyatvaṃ rūpavicyutiḥ*

## v.4
And what was said: Samāveśa-full, enjoyer-natured Śiva; in interruption of enjoyment-lust-enjoyment, of nature of restraint.
The Sanskrit: *kiṃca coktaṃ samāveśapūrṇo bhoktrātmakaḥ śivaḥ bhogalāmpaṭyabhāgbhogavicchede nigrahātmakaḥ*

## v.5
Produced from peace-surpassing-rite-hunger-increased body, itself installed, by which he in enjoyment perishes.
The Sanskrit: *śāntatvanyakkriyodbhūtajighatsāvṛṃhitaṃ vapuḥ svayaṃ pratiṣṭhitaṃ yena so@syābhoge vinaśyati*

## v.6
Was said in Jñānottara, and that this by Supreme Lord: Śiva sacrifice-loving, from which especially in mothers' midst.
The Sanskrit: *uktaṃ jñānottarāyāṃ ca tadetatparameśinā śivo yāgapriyo yasmādviśeṣānmātṛmadhyagaḥ*

## v.7
Therefore in secret śāstras: which mantras — these the wise — outside not should install ever, especially manifest-formed.
The Sanskrit: *tasmādrahasyaśāstreṣu ye mantrāstānbudho bahiḥ na pratiṣṭhāpayejjātu viśeṣādvyaktarūpiṇaḥ*

## v.8
Hence indeed for the dead's sake installation elsewhere was enjoined; that here in śāstras not to be done, to be done common again.
The Sanskrit: *ata eva mṛtasyārthe pratiṣṭhānyatra yoditā sātra śāstreṣu no kāryā kāryā sādhāraṇī punaḥ*

## v.9
Up to tanmayatva-accomplishment, from desired-fruit-arising: putraka, sādhaka, manifest-unmanifest, or should resort to.
The Sanskrit: *ā tanmayatvasaṃsiddherā cābhīṣṭaphalodayāt putrakaḥ sādhako vyaktamavyaktaṃ vā samāśrayet*

## v.10
By putrakas guru is to be requested; sādhaka but himself knowing: if he should install not, if then also to be requested, guru should become.
The Sanskrit: *putrakairgururabhyarthyaḥ sādhakastu svayaṃ vidan yadi tatsthāpayenno cettenāpyarthyo gururbhavet*

## v.11
Guru and here in so-called nirodha: time thus in the Lord he should say; living in this until fruit-end, you should stand until life-end — or.
The Sanskrit: *guruścātra nirodhākhye kāla itthaṃ vibhau vadet jīvatyasminphalāntaṃ tvaṃ tiṣṭherjīvāvadhīti vā*

## v.12
Liṅga, and Bāṇa-liṅga, or gem-born, or-then pearl, flower-food-and, cloth, scent-substance-made, and or.
The Sanskrit: *liṅgaṃ ca bāṇaliṅgaṃ vā ratnajaṃ vātha mauktikam pauṣpamānnamatho vāstraṃ gandhadravyakṛtaṃ ca vā*

## v.13
But not stone-born liṅga, sculptor-risen should consider; metal-risen and gold-risen-except other reject.
The Sanskrit: *natu pāṣāṇajaṃ liṅgaṃ śilpyutthaṃ parikalpayet dhātūtthaṃ ca suvarṇotthavarjamanyadvivarjayet*

## v.14
Not and here liṅga-measure-and-the-rest anywhere is used; by noble vigor-mantras what is shone, fruit-giving, for that.
The Sanskrit: *na cātra liṅgamānādi kvacidapyupayujyate udāravīryairmantrairyadbhāsitaṃ phaladaṃ hi tat*

## v.15
Of it also by altar-and-the-rest-enjoined-rite he should purify; mantra-offering likewise should be nirodha as said by reason.
The Sanskrit: *tasyāpi sthaṇḍilādyuktavidhinā śuddhimācaret mantrārpaṇaṃ tathaiva syānnirodhastūktayuktitaḥ*

## v.16
In fire and tarpaṇa, by abundance especially dakṣiṇā of guru, poor-and-the-rest-satisfaction-abundance-sacrifice: thus superior rite.
The Sanskrit: *agnau ca tarpaṇaṃ bhūriviśeṣāddakṣiṇā guroḥ dīnāditṛptirvibhavādyāga ityadhiko vidhiḥ*

## v.17
In all unmanifest liṅgas primary should be unconstructed; so and there there-said in mark in Pārameśvara.
The Sanskrit: *sarveṣvavyaktaliṅgeṣu pradhānaṃ syādakalpitam tathā ca tatra tatroktaṃ lakṣaṇe pārameśvare*

## v.18
In thread, vessel, banner, cloth, svayambhū-Bāṇa-worshipped, river-spring-risen: and no calling, nor even construction.
The Sanskrit: *sūtre pātre dhvaje vastre svayambhūbāṇapūjite nadīprasravaṇotthe ca nāhvānaṃ nāpi kalpanā*

## v.19
Seat-temple-mantra-portion-time-rule, and not manifest or in painting-book-and-the-rest, god-wood-gold-born.
The Sanskrit: *pīṭhaprasādamantrāṃśavelādiniyamo naca vyaktaṃ vā citrapustādau devadārusuvarṇajam*

## v.20
Now by initiated good-artisan-made installs guru, or-else mark-endowed-head-shell-supported.
The Sanskrit: *atha dīkṣitasacchilpikṛtaṃ sthāpayate guruḥ athavā lakṣaṇopetamūrdhatatkarparāśritam*

## v.21
By row-chakra-triśūla-lotus-rite should resort to tūra; its mark I speak, in venerable Picu-śāstra was set forth.
The Sanskrit: *paṅkticakrakaśūlābjavidhinā tūramāśrayet tallakṣaṇaṃ bruve śrīmatpicuśāstre nirūpitam*

## v.22
In tūra yoga always is praised, giver of siddhi in faultless; by nets-torn, by holes-teeth-deficient-excess disease.
The Sanskrit: *tūre yogaḥ sadā śastaḥ siddhido doṣavarjite jālakairjarjarai randhrairdantairūnādhikai rujā*

## v.23
In suitable and in tūra loss would be, in its deficient sacrifice supreme; desired indeed would-be-tūra — thus some adopted.
The Sanskrit: *yukte ca tūre hāniḥ syāt taddhīne yāga uttamaḥ kāmya eva bhavettūramiti kecitprapedire*

## v.24
Gurus but in rite, in desired, from effort reject faults — thus they explain Picu-spoken; not in obligatory rite — thus this.
The Sanskrit: *guravastu vidhau kāmye yatnāddoṣāṃstyajediti vyācakṣate picuproktaṃ na nitye karmaṇītyadaḥ*

## v.25
In venerable Siddhātantra was said and tūra-mark-supreme: in one-to-four-fragment, in cow-mouth, in full-moon.
The Sanskrit: *śrīsiddhātantra uktaṃ ca tūralakṣaṇamuttamam ekādikacatuṣkhaṇḍe gomukhe pūrṇacandrake*

## v.26
In lotus-go-rocanā-pearl-salt-crystal-like, in one-to-five-hundred-holes-vidyā-line-possessing, in auspicious.
The Sanskrit: *padmagorocanāmuktānīrasphaṭikasaṃnibhe ekādipañcasadrandhravidyārekhānvite śubhe*

## v.27
Not in rough-crooked-fragment-long-depressed-with-bindu: by smooth, by diamond-needle here, clearly, goddess-group-possessing.
The Sanskrit: *na rūkṣavakraśakaladīrghanimnasabinduke ślakṣṇayā vajrasūcyātra sphuṭaṃ devīgaṇānvitam*

## v.28
All should draw worshipful, all-limbs-beautiful; this very should follow in argha-vessel also, mark.
The Sanskrit: *sarvaṃ samālikhetpūjyaṃ sarvāvayavasundaram etadevānusartavyamarghapātre@pi lakṣaṇam*

## v.29
In venerable Brahmayāmala was said: vessel, cow-mouth-supreme, elephant-tortoise-base, pot-round-śakti-born-form.
The Sanskrit: *śrībrahmayāmale@pyuktaṃ pātraṃ gomukhamuttamam gajakūrmatalaṃ kumbhavṛttaśaktikajākṛti*

## v.30
Bead-thread and should make there indeed worship sequence, hero-substance-water-born-pearl-gem-gold-born.
The Sanskrit: *akṣasūtramatho kuryāttatraivābhyarcayetkramam vīradhātujalodbhūtamuktāratnasuvarṇajam*

## v.31
Bead-thread, sequence-extracted, rudrākṣa, or especially hundred, tithi-superior, or-even with-eight, or-even its-half.
The Sanskrit: *akṣasūtraṃ kramotkṛṣṭaṃ raudrākṣaṃ vā viśeṣataḥ śataṃ tithyuttaraṃ yadvā sāṣṭaṃ yadvā tadardhakam*

## v.32
Its-half, or-then fifty-joined, he should consider that; mouths five, from consciousness-pulse-knowledge-will-action-union.
The Sanskrit: *tadardhaṃ vātha pañcāśadyuktaṃ tatparikalpayet vaktrāṇi pañca citspandajñānecchākṛtisaṃgateḥ*

## v.33
Fivefold-end-going, unity-thus-penultimate-bead-going, rite, by śakti-and-its-division: there two-form is said.
The Sanskrit: *pañcadhādyantagaṃ caikyamityupāntyākṣago vidhiḥ śaktitadvatprabhedena tatra dvairūpyamucyate*

## v.34
Then in double-measure but two-formed nyāsa he should perform; then even in double, by creation-dissolution-pair, him.
The Sanskrit: *tato dviguṇamāne tu dvirūpaṃ nyāsamācaret tato@pi dviguṇe sṛṣṭisaṃhṛtidvitayena tam*

## v.35
Mātṛkā, Mālinī, or-then: he should place in ether-sixteen-measured; in supreme but two, having-placed-having-placed, before impelled-ones.
The Sanskrit: *mātṛkāṃ mālinīṃ vātha nyasyetkhaśarasaṃmite uttame tu dvayīṃ nyasyennyasya pūrvaṃ pracoditān*

## v.36
In initiation primarily mantras those fifteen, the daiśika; if or by tattva-bhuvana-kalā-mantra-pada-phoneme.
The Sanskrit: *dīkṣāyāṃ mukhyato mantrāṃstānpañcadaśa daiśikaḥ yadi vā tattvabhuvanakalāmantrapadārṇajaiḥ*

## v.37
By number-differences in made in thread: him him nyāsa guru should perform; having-made bead-thread, of it also all should be as altar.
The Sanskrit: *saṃkhyābhedaiḥ kṛte sūtre taṃ taṃ nyāsaṃ guruścaret kṛtvākṣasūtraṃ tasyāpi sarvaṃ sthaṇḍilavadbhavet*

## v.38
By worshipped and by that very japa should perform undistracted: rite said this in venerable Mālinīvijayottara.
The Sanskrit: *pūjitena ca tenaiva japaṃ kuryādatandritaḥ vidhiruktastvayaṃ śrīmanmālinīvijayottare*

## v.39
Wheel-like-whirling-this-what-he-says: he japa would be; if he sees, he offers this in awakening-fire, from entering.
The Sanskrit: *cakravadbhramayannetadyadvakti sa japo bhavet yadīkṣate juhotyetadbodhāgnau saṃpraveśanāt*

## v.40
Or argha-great-vessel should make that supreme param; coconut-and, bilva, golden, silver, and or.
The Sanskrit: *athavārghamahāpātraṃ kuryāttaccottaraṃ param nārikelamatho bailvaṃ sauvarṇaṃ rājataṃ ca vā*

## v.41
Of it also this rite all in installation-beginning was declared; by its unshaking fluids full, having made in this worship sequence.
The Sanskrit: *tasyāpyeṣa vidhiḥ sarvaḥ pratiṣṭhādau prakīrtitaḥ tanniṣkamparasaiḥ pūrṇaṃ kṛtvāsminpūjayetkramam*

## v.42
Downward-faced always to be placed; worshipped in worship again, that vessel upward-faced; and that empty should make not, such.
The Sanskrit: *adhomukhaṃ sadā sthāpyaṃ pūjitaṃ pūjane punaḥ tatpātramunmukhaṃ tacca riktaṃ kuryānna tādṛśam*

## v.43
At worship-end, that-fluid-full-self having disposed, that downward-faced and having fully worshipped, he should place, the wise.
The Sanskrit: *pūjānte tadrasāpūrṇamātmānaṃ pravidhāya tat adhomukhaṃ ca saṃpūjya sthāpayet vicakṣaṇaḥ*

## v.44
Sword, dagger, or-even scissors, mirror, and or, pure, so-thus should make as venerable Kālī-mouth spoken.
The Sanskrit: *khaṅgaṃ kṛpāṇikāṃ yadvā kartarīṃ makuraṃ ca vā vimalaṃ tattathā kuryācchrīmatkālīmukhoditam*

## v.45
In venerable Bhairavakula was said in Kula-festival-pre-worship: on altar-in-fire, on cloth, on liṅga, on vessel, on lotus-then, on maṇḍala.
The Sanskrit: *śrībhairavakule@pyuktaṃ kulaparvaprapūjane sthaṇḍile@gnau paṭe liṅge pātre padme@tha maṇḍale*

## v.46
On image, on pot-in-heap, on brick, on thread-then: should worship by own, by own service; mixture should reject — thus.
The Sanskrit: *mūrtau ghaṭe@strasaṃghāte dhaṭe sūtre@tha pūjayet svena svenopacāreṇa saṅkaraṃ varjayediti*

## v.47
As in waters for peace, mantras so in weapons and the rest certainly; enemy-cutting-and-the-rest-doers, desired from, mixture abandoned.
The Sanskrit: *yathāpsu śāntaye mantrāstadvadastrādiṣu dhruvam śatrucchedādikartāraḥ kāmyo@taḥ saṅkarojjhitaḥ*

## v.48
Of the desireless but they, from that-that-place-condition-power certainly: fetter-cutting-purified-tattva-nourishing-and-the-rest-doers.
The Sanskrit: *akāmasya tu te tattatsthānopādhivaśāddhruvam pāśakartanasaṃśuddhatattvāpyāyādikāriṇaḥ*

## v.49
Or-else book, such-seizing-śāstra-sequence-possessing, well-purified, by-initiated-made: there also this rite is remembered.
The Sanskrit: *athavā pustakaṃ tādṛgrahaḥśāstrakramombhitam suśuddhaṃ dīkṣitakṛtaṃ tatrāpyeṣa vidhiḥ smṛtaḥ*

## v.50
Thus in self-installations, as long as standing is: by abundance tarpaṇa, purity so long interruption-avoidance.
The Sanskrit: *itthaṃ svayaṃpratiṣṭheṣu yāvadyāvatsthitirbhavet vibhavaistarpaṇaṃ śuddhistāvadvicchedavarjanam*

## v.51
Hence indeed when many-days maṇḍala-construction: then in-day in-day should perform by abundance tarpaṇa, much.
The Sanskrit: *ata eva yadā bhūridinaṃ maṇḍalakalpanam tadā dine dine kuryādvibhavaistarpaṇaṃ bahu*

## v.52
In installation and everywhere guru, aforesaid supreme, having sought true reality, presence he should make clear.
The Sanskrit: *pratiṣṭhāyāṃ ca sarvatra guruḥ pūrvoditaṃ param satattvamanusandhāya saṃnidhiṃ sphuṭamācaret*

## v.53
In accomplished but in tanmayatva-state in fruit, by putraka-sādhakas to another, from that pair to either, that is offered.
The Sanskrit: *siddhe tu tanmayībhāve phale putrakasādhakaiḥ anyasmai taddvayādanyatarasmai tatsamarpyate*

## v.54
Of it also this rite all in its absence but in every way: in deep water it to be cast, having asked pardon, having dismissed, and.
The Sanskrit: *tasyāpyeṣa vidhiḥ sarvastadalābhe tu sarvathā agādhe@mbhasi tatkṣepyaṃ kṣamayitvā visṛjya ca*

## v.55
Thus this self-installation-rite, Śiva-set-forth: in other-installed in liṅga, in Bāṇa-then, in svayambhū.
The Sanskrit: *ityeṣa svapratiṣṭhānavidhiḥ śivanirūpitaḥ parapratiṣṭhite liṅge bāṇīye@tha svayaṃbhuvi*

## v.56
In all-asana-side, having placed before, should worship sequence: pure-impure-adhva-ones, all mantras, all Śiva-ending.
The Sanskrit: *sarvamāsanapakṣe prāṅnyasya saṃpūjayetkramam śuddhāśuddhādhvajāḥ sarve mantrāḥ sarvaḥ śivāntakaḥ*

## v.57
Adhva and here in seat was said that everywhere worship this; invocation-dismissal but there as before should perform.
The Sanskrit: *adhvā cehāsane proktastatsarvatrārcayedidam āvāhanavisṛṣṭī tu tatra prāgvatsamācaret*

## v.58
Was said in tantra also Aghoreśa, in Svacchanda, by Lord; so or-else daily as said by half-half-portion-appointment.
The Sanskrit: *uktaṃ tantre@pyaghoreśe svacchande vibhunā tathā athavā pratyahaṃ proktamānārdhārdhaniyogataḥ*

## v.59
Having made desired maṇḍala there, all sequence should worship, of much kind different, liṅga-worship was set forth.
The Sanskrit: *kṛtveṣṭaṃ maṇḍalaṃ tatra samastaṃ kramamarcayet bahuprakārabhinnasya liṅgasyārcā nirūpitā*

## v.0
Now I speak here the procedure of mudrās praised in guru-scripture song.
The Sanskrit: *atha kathaye mudrāṇāṃ gurvāgamagītamatra vidhim*

## v.1
Mudrā, reflection-natured, is said in the venerable Devyāyāmala, by hearing bimbodaya, from discernment of two predicates.
The Sanskrit: *mudrā ca pratibimbātmā śrīmaddevyākhyayāmale uktā bimbodayaśrutyā vācyadvayavivecanāt*

## v.2
Emergence from bimba of whom thus said reflection-ness; of bimba of whom emergence thus said instrumentality thereof.
The Sanskrit: *bimbātsamudayo yasyā ityuktā pratibimbatā vimbasya yasyā udaya ityuktā tadupāyatā*

## v.3
Delight, called own-nature-gain, through body-gate of selves bestows at night; which by that mudrā in śāstras described.
The Sanskrit: *mudaṃ svarūpalābhākhyaṃ dehadvāreṇa cātmanām rātyarpayati yattena mudrā śāstreṣu varṇitā*

## v.4
There principal venerable Khecarī, deity-natured, known in undivided state; in divided state Triśūlinī.
The Sanskrit: *tatra pradhānabhūtā śrīkhecarī devatātmikā niṣkalatvena vikhyātā sākalyena triśūlinī*

## v.5
Karaṅkiṇī, Krodhanā, and Bhairavī, Lelihānikā, Mahāpretā, Yogamudrā, Jvālinī, Kṣobhiṇī, Dhruvā.
The Sanskrit: *karaṅkiṇī krodhanā ca bhairavī lelihānikā mahāpretā yogamudrā jvālinī kṣobhiṇī dhruvā*

## v.6
Thus many varieties this, venerable Khecarī alone is sung; others her limb-born but, Padmā and the rest, in Mālinī doctrine.
The Sanskrit: *ityevaṃbahubhedeyaṃ śrīkhecaryeva gīyate anyāstadaṅgabhūtāstu padmādyā mālinīmate*

## v.7
Of them, by abundance and non-principality, not here description; venerable Khecarī-absorbed, whatever place should resort.
The Sanskrit: *tāsāṃ bahutvāmukhyatvayogābhyāṃ neha varṇanam śrīkhecarīsamāviṣṭo yadyatsthānaṃ samāśrayet*

## v.8
For goddess presence that would be enough; what with vain pomp; in desired rite they and would be principal of someone ever.
The Sanskrit: *devīsaṃnidhaye tatsyādalaṃ kiṃ ḍambarairvṛthā kāmye karmaṇi tāśca syurmukhyāḥ kasyāpi jātucit*

## v.9
Mudrā fourfold by division of body, hand, speech, mind.
The Sanskrit: *mudrā caturvidhā kāyakaravākcittabhedataḥ*

## v.10
There with full form I describe Khecarī alone; having bound lotus-seat, yogī should cast navel-ākāśa-īśvara.
The Sanskrit: *tatra pūrṇena rūpeṇa khecarīmeva varṇaye baddhvā padmāsanaṃ yogī nābhāvakṣeśvaraṃ kṣipet*

## v.11
Staff-shaped but him should lead until three cavities; having held there, that quickly should drive with three cavities but.
The Sanskrit: *daṇḍākāraṃ tu taṃ tāvannayedyāvatkakhatrayam nigṛhya tatra tattūrṇaṃ prerayet khatrayeṇa tu*

## v.12
This having bound in sky movement would be thus in venerable Pūrva-śāsana; sound-light-wind-joined mind having rested and above.
The Sanskrit: *etāṃ baddhvā khe gatiḥ syāditi śrīpūrvaśāsane dhvanijyotirmarudyuktaṃ cittaṃ viśramya copari*

## v.13
By this practice-yoga, having pierced Śiva, supreme should go; hands below clavicle having made, left foot and on right.
The Sanskrit: *anenābhyāsayogena śivaṃ bhittvā paraṃ vrajet jatrvadhastātkarau kṛtvā vāmapādaṃ ca dakṣiṇe*

## v.14
Having opened mouth with little fingers, with middle fingers but nose; ring finger wise should crook eyebrow-bend two index fingers.
The Sanskrit: *vidāryāsyaṃ kaniṣṭhābhyāṃ madhyamābhyāṃ tu nāsikām anāme kuñcayetprājño bhrūbhaṅgaṃ tarjanīdvayam*

## v.15
Tongue and mantrin should move, hāhā-sound and should cause; with trident by practice, Brahmarandhra present.
The Sanskrit: *jihvāṃ ca cālayenmantrī hāhākāraṃ ca kārayet triśūlena prayogeṇa brahmarandhramupasthitaḥ*

## v.16
Foot having abandoned, that-only instantly abandons earth; in void-and-nonvoid-dissolution having made, one-staff blue and fire.
The Sanskrit: *padaṃ santyajya tanmātraṃ sadyastyajati medinīm śūnyāśūnyalaye kṛtvā ekadaṇḍe@nilānalau*

## v.17
In three-śakti-bound, in three-presiding-deities, trident; should know that by which wise flies in sky.
The Sanskrit: *śaktitritayasambaddhe adhiṣṭhātṛtridaivate triśūlaṃ tadvijānīyādyena vyomotpatedbudhaḥ*

## v.18
Sky-nature having abandoned, being present as existence only; trident equal-taste having made, in taste taste like standing.
The Sanskrit: *ākāśabhāvaṃ santyajya sattāmātramupasthitaḥ śūlaṃ samarasaṃ kṛtvā rase rasa iva sthitaḥ*

## v.19
One-staff he having known, trident sky-going, dear; having bound but Khecarī mudrā, having meditated self and Bhairava.
The Sanskrit: *ekadaṇḍaṃ sa vijñāya triśūlaṃ khacaraṃ priye baddhvā tu khecarīṃ mudrāṃ dhyātvātmānaṃ ca bhairavam*

## v.20
Khecarī-cakra-joined instantly abandons earth; abandoned-portion, without conduct, without doubt, world-abandoned.
The Sanskrit: *khecarīcakrasaṃjuṣṭaṃ sadyastyajati medinīm tyaktāṃśako nirācāro niḥśaṅko lokavarjitaḥ*

## v.21
Avadhūta, without conduct, not-I-am thus meditating, mantra-single-devoted, seeing body-dwelling all deities.
The Sanskrit: *avadhūto nirācāro nāhamasmīti bhāvayam mantraikaniṣṭhaḥ saṃpaśyan dehasthāḥ sarvadevatāḥ*

## v.22
In delight-agitation-egoism-crying-sleep-copulation-jealousy, in form and the rest or, in doer-deed-instrument and in all ways.
The Sanskrit: *hlādodvegāsmitākruṣṭanidrāmaithunamatsare rūpādau vā kartṛkarmakaraṇeṣu ca sarvaśaḥ*

## v.23
Not-I-am thus thinking, unified contemplating, ear-eye-mouth-nose-cakra-dwelling deity-group.
The Sanskrit: *nāhamasmīti manvāna ekībhūtaṃ vicintayan karṇākṣimukhanāsādicakrasthaṃ devatāgaṇam*

## v.24
Grasper always seeing, by Khecarī succeeds clearly; Vidyā-doubter, mala-doubter, śāstra-doubter not succeeds.
The Sanskrit: *grahītāraṃ sadā paśyan khecaryā siddhyati sphuṭam vidyāśaṅkī malāśaṅkī śāstraśaṅkī na siddhyati*

## v.25
Śiva sun, Śiva fire, from being cooker he priest; there-dwelling deities all illuminating all world.
The Sanskrit: *śivo raviḥ śivo vahniḥ paktṛtvātsa purohitaḥ tatrasthā devatāḥ sarvā dyotayantyo@khilaṃ jagat*

## v.26
With little finger having opened mouth, with index fingers eyebrows so; with ring finger, with middle finger in mouth, with tongue palate should touch.
The Sanskrit: *kaniṣṭhayā vidāryāsyaṃ tarjanībhyāṃ bhruvau tathā anāme madhyame vaktre jihvayā tālukaṃ spṛśet*

## v.27
This Karaṅkiṇī goddess; Jvālinī hear now; jaw and forehead, hands spreading fingers clear.
The Sanskrit: *eṣā karaṅkiṇī devī jvālinīṃ śṛṇu sāṃpratam hanurlalāṭagau hastau prasāryāṅgulitaḥ sphuṭau*

## v.28
Should move by wind force, having made inner eyebrow, wise; having opened mouth with tongue and, hāhā-sound but should cause.
The Sanskrit: *cālayedvāyuvegena kṛtvāntarbhrukuṭīṃ budhaḥ vidāryāsyaṃ sajihvaṃ ca hāhākāraṃ tu kārayet*

## v.29
This Jvālinī in fire-wheel; by her and hundred-and-eight hundred should recite if; then would succeed three worlds with moving and nonmoving.
The Sanskrit: *eṣā jvālinyagnicakre tayā cāṣṭottaraṃ śatam japedyadi tataḥ siddhyettrailokyaṃ sacarācaram*

## v.30
In other bodies self, other from own body; should see moving, from loss and gain standing in coming-going place.
The Sanskrit: *paradeheṣu cātmānaṃ paraṃ cātmaśarīrataḥ paśyeccarantaṃ hānādādgamāgamapadasthitam*

## v.31
Nine-hole-dwelling and one, roaring, pervading, fixed; by this indeed sky-goer venerable yoga-movement is called.
The Sanskrit: *navacchidragataṃ caikaṃ nadantaṃ vyāpakaṃ dhruvam anayā hi khacārī śrīyogasañcāra ucyate*

## v.32
Kulakuṇḍalinī having bound, within the atom unconscious; left which this in this universe of him intent on withdrawal.
The Sanskrit: *kulakuṇḍalikāṃ baddhvā aṇorantaravedinīm vāmo yo@yaṃ jagatyasmiṃstasya saṃharaṇodyatām*

## v.33
In own place satisfaction having obtained, consisting of knowledge-nectar-taste; should go to Kanda-abode in middle, sound having made soundless.
The Sanskrit: *svasthāne nirvṛtiṃ labdhvā jñānāmṛtarasātmakam vrajetkandapadaṃ madhye rāvaṃ kṛtvā hyarāvakam*

## v.34
As long as living, quadrangular body-support and desirous; there her having aroused but, movement having known progressively-come.
The Sanskrit: *yāvajjīvaṃ catuṣkoṇaṃ piṇḍādhāraṃ ca kāmikam tatra tāṃ bodhayitvā tu gatiṃ buddhvā kramāgatām*

## v.35
Both wheels bound but, branch-ends-supporting; from root place as, goddess, darkness-knot should tear.
The Sanskrit: *cakrobhayanibaddhāṃ tu śākhāprāntāvalambinīm mūlasthānādyathā devi tamogranthiṃ vidārayet*

## v.36
Called Vajra by knowledge-born indeed; so from both branch ends, from triangle center emerged root of liṅga should penetrate.
The Sanskrit: *vajrākhyāṃ jñānajenaiva tathā śākhobhayāntataḥ koṇamadhyaviniṣkrāntaṃ liṅgamūlaṃ vibhedayet*

## v.37
There assembled by pair of wheels unity shines; by reversal but, having thrown twofold-condition, moves thence.
The Sanskrit: *tatra saṅghaṭṭitaṃ cakrayugmamaikyena bhāsate vaiparītyāttu nikṣipya dvidhābhāvaṃ vrajatyataḥ*

## v.38
From thigh to big toe fire of time she should throw; in coming and going movement should move she, marked with liṅga.
The Sanskrit: *ūrvādyaṅguṣṭhakālāgniparyante sā vinikṣipet gamāgamanasañcāre caretsā liṅgaliṅginī*

## v.39
There by union of those conditions grants opening; who knows, he would succeed indeed of reception and emission of sap.
The Sanskrit: *tatra tatpadasaṃyogādunmīlanavidhāyinī yo jānāti sa siddhyettu rasādānavisargayoḥ*

## v.40
This with union place wave opening supreme; this succession then another even out of succession, Khecarī supreme.
The Sanskrit: *sasaṅgamamidaṃ sthānamūrmiṇyunmīlanaṃ param eṣa kramastato@nyo@pi vyutkramaḥ khecarī parā*

## v.41
Called support of yoni known, called root of trident is called; letters there dissolution go, in devoid of letters in letter-formed.
The Sanskrit: *yonyādhāreti vikhyātā śūlamūleti śabdyate varṇāstatra layaṃ yānti hyavarṇe varṇarūpiṇi*

## v.42
Up to end of nāda having uttered, Kaula-lord body-resembling; having occupied first wheel in sky in machine foot-pressed.
The Sanskrit: *nādiphāntaṃ samuccārya kauleśaṃ dehasaṃnibham ākramya prathamaṃ cakraṃ khe yantre pādapīḍitam*

## v.43
Nāda indeed śakti-with-true-womb; from true-womb Kaulinī-abode; by five-seed-circle, by trident-piercing-order but.
The Sanskrit: *nādaṃ vai śaktisadgarbhaṃ sadgarbhātkaulinīpadam bījapañcakacāreṇa śūlabhedakrameṇa tu*

## v.44
By heart-trident-knot-piercing some Rudra-śakti should awaken; wind-wheel-abiding-place called bindu navel-maṇḍala.
The Sanskrit: *hṛcchūlagranthibhedaiścidrudraśaktiṃ prabodhayet vāyucakrāntanilayaṃ bindvākhyaṃ nābhimaṇḍalam*

## v.45
Should come to Lambikā-place from twelve-thread-exit; by moon-wheel-reverse should enter body-cage.
The Sanskrit: *āgacchellambikāsthānaṃ sūtradvādaśanirgatam candracakravilomena praviśedbhūtapañjare*

## v.46
Moreover does play dwelling in māyā cage; again creation and dissolution by Khecarī is done by wise.
The Sanskrit: *bhūyastu kurute līlāṃ māyāpañjaravartinīm punaḥ sṛṣṭiḥ saṃhṛtiśca khecaryā kriyate budhaiḥ*

## v.47
Venerable Vīrāvalī-yoga this would be Khecarī-procedure; by kissing form by mouth, which principle is heard supreme.
The Sanskrit: *śrīmadvīrāvalīyoga eṣa syātkhecarīvidhiḥ cumbākāreṇa vaktreṇa yattattvaṃ śrūyate param*

## v.48
This swallowing universe in moon-sun-pot; by that indeed would be sky-goer thus, venerable Kāmika is called.
The Sanskrit: *grasamānamidaṃ viśvaṃ candrārkapuṭasaṃpuṭe tenaiva syātkhagāmīti śrīmatkāmika ucyate*

## v.49
Having released Bhava melt bonds-mudrā indeed śaktis; of principal of them Khecarī she and, by threefold utterance verbal.
The Sanskrit: *bhavānmuktvā drāvayanti pāśānmudrā hi śaktayaḥ mukhyāsāṃ khecarī sā ca tridhoccāreṇa vācikī*

## v.50
Three-head-mudgara goddess bodily is recited; of them two-eyes and also heart and two breasts indeed and.
The Sanskrit: *triśiromudgaro devi kāyikī paripaṭhyate nāsāṃ netradvayaṃ cāpi hṛtstanadvayameva ca*

## v.51
Two-testicles-and-liṅga and, having obtained body, gone; but this should ascertain abode of Bhava and abode of non-Bhava by utterance.
The Sanskrit: *vṛṣaṇadvayaliṅgaṃ ca prāpya kāyaṃ gatā tviyam bhavasthānābhavasthānamuccāreṇāvadhārayet*

## v.52
Mental this but others, Padmā and the rest eight mudrikās; in Mātṛ-vyūha-kula they would be of her and attendants.
The Sanskrit: *mānasīyamitastvanyāḥ padmādyā aṣṭa mudrikāḥ mātṛvyūhakule tāḥ syurasyāstu parivāragāḥ*

## v.53
Body but entire which is form of supreme letter; this mudrā mahāmudrā of Bhairava thus in cave.
The Sanskrit: *śarīraṃ tu samastaṃ yatkūṭākṣarasamākṛti eṣā mudrā mahāmudrā bhairavasyeti gahvare*

## v.54
Well seated in lotus but, by fingertip rays, by suddenly rising rays turned away, by back dwelling.
The Sanskrit: *sūpaviṣṭaḥ padmake tu hastāgrāṅguliraśmibhiḥ parāṅmukhairjhaṭityudyadraśmibhiḥ pṛṣṭhasaṃsthitaiḥ*

## v.55
Inner standing Khecarī this called contraction moon-marked; therefore indeed having raised arms and bent.
The Sanskrit: *antaḥsthitiḥ khecarīyaṃ saṃkocākhyā śaśāṅkinī tasmādeva samuttambya bāhū caivāvakuñcitau*

## v.56
In sky properly from standing sky-called Khecarī held; from union of two fists in heart she called heart.
The Sanskrit: *samyagvyomasu saṃsthānādvyomākhyā khecarī matā muṣṭidvitayasaṅghaṭṭāddhṛdi sā hṛdayāhvāyā*

## v.57
Called peace she two-hands up and down standing risen; to be seen with equal gaze and with hands joined outside.
The Sanskrit: *śāntākhyā sā hastayugmamūrdhvādhaḥ sthitamudgatam samadṛṣṭyāvalokyaṃ ca bahiryojitapāṇikam*

## v.58
This indeed śakti-mudrā if hands run downward; of ten fingers but after fist binding.
The Sanskrit: *eṣaiva śaktimudrā cedadhodhāvitapāṇikā daśānāmaṅgulīnāṃ tu muṣṭibandhādanantaram*

## v.59
Khecarī by throwing goddess five-Kuṇḍalinī held; dissolution-mudrā and this indeed if upward is thrown indeed.
The Sanskrit: *drākkṣepātkhecarī devī pañcakuṇḍalinī matā saṃhāramudrā caiṣaiva yadyūrdhvaṃ kṣipyate kila*

## v.60
Exit-mudrā instantly indeed of bound beings bond-cutter; in chasm far away instantly own-self as-if-dropping.
The Sanskrit: *utkrāmaṇī jhagityeva paśūnāṃ pāśakartarī śvabhre sudūre jhaṭiti svātmānaṃ pātayanniva*

## v.61
By sudden entry contracted two-hands; downward-looking-natured and with proper gaze.
The Sanskrit: *sāhasānupraveśena kuñcitaṃ hastayugmakam adhovīkṣaṇaśīlaṃ ca samyagdṛṣṭisamanvitam*

## v.62
Called Vīra-Bhairava Khecarī consciousness-increasing; in eight ways thus described in venerable Bhargāṣṭaka-śikhā-kula.
The Sanskrit: *vīrabhairavasaṃjñeyaṃ khecarī bodhavardhinī aṣṭadhetthaṃ varṇitā śrībhargāṣṭakaśikhākule*

## v.63
Thus resorting to many varieties one alone which standing, venerable Khecarī; absorbed by her supreme seed attains.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ nānāvidhānbhedānāśrityaikaiva yā sthitā śrīkhecarī tayāviṣṭaḥ paraṃ bījaṃ prapadyate*

## v.64
One consisting-of-manifestation seed, whose vigor pervading all mantras; one mudrā Khecarī and, multitude of mudrās animated by whom.
The Sanskrit: *ekaṃ sṛṣṭimayaṃ bījaṃ yadvīryaṃ sarvamantragam ekā mudrā khecarī ca mudraughaḥ prāṇito yayā*

## v.65
Thus indeed in establishment in Khecarī-cakra which form shines forth; that very mudrā to be regarded, the rest would be bodily modification.
The Sanskrit: *tadevaṃ khecarīcakrarūḍhau yadrūpamullaset tadeva mudrā mantavyā śeṣaḥ syāddehavikriyā*

## v.66
At beginning of sacrifice, in its middle, at its conclusion; in reflection on knowledge and yoga, in cessation of obstacles, in cutting of bonds; of mudrā procedure proper time.
The Sanskrit: *yāgādau tanmadhye tadavasitau jñānayogaparimarśe vighnapraśame pāśacchede mudrāvidheḥ samayaḥ*

## v.67
Penetration into consciousness, presence with unity, dismissal, movement toward essential nature, destruction of doubts, splendor of rising of cakras thus, purpose in order.
The Sanskrit: *bodhāveśaḥ sannidhiraikyena visarjanaṃ svarūpagatiḥ śaṅkādalanaṃ cakrodayadīptiriti kramātkṛtyam*

## v.68
Thus mudrā-procedure spoken, very secret, which fruit-giving.
The Sanskrit: *iti mudrāvidhiḥ proktaḥ sugūḍho yaḥ phalapradaḥ*

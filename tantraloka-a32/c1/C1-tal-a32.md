# C1 — tal-a32

## SUMMARY

The ahnika teaches the mudrā-vidhi: mudrā as reflection, Khecarī as principal seal with ten forms, the full Khecarī procedure through body, breath and mind, and the eight derived mudrikās; proper time and purpose close the rite.

## FUNCTION

Establishes mudrā-vidhi: mudrā as reflection-natured, Khecarī as principal seal with ten forms, the full Khecarī procedure through body, breath and mind, and the eight derived mudrikās; proper time and purpose close the rite.

## KEY TERMS

mudrā (seal), Khecarī (sky-goer, principal), bimbodaya (twofold predicate), pratibimba (reflection), Devyāyāmala (source), padmāsana (lotus seat), triśūla (trident), Kulakuṇḍalinī, Kanda, nāda, śakti, Kaula, mahāmudrā, Vīra-Bhairava, saṃhāra, utkrāmaṇī, samaya (proper time)

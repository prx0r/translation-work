# Tantraloka-a33 — L2 READ


## v.0
Now the unification, arrived in due course, is discussed.
The Sanskrit: *athāvasarasaṃprāpta ekīkāro nigadyate*

## v.1
Since it was said the triad is to be worshipped together with the cakra-divisions: here, in this manner, is their unification.
The Sanskrit: *yaduktaṃ cakrabhedena sārdhaṃ pūjyamiti trikam | tatraiṣa cakrabhedānāmekīkāro diśānayā*

## v.2
Viśvā, her Mistress, Hāraudrī, Vīranetrī, Ambikā, and Gurvī, thus: the goddesses shown in the Siddhāvīra, in the six-spoked wheel.
The Sanskrit: *viśvā tadīśā hāraudrī vīranetryambikā tathā | gurvīti ṣaḍare devyaḥ śrīsiddhāvīradarśitāḥ*

## v.3
Māheśī, Brāhmaṇī, Skāndī, Vaiṣṇavī, Aindrī, Yamātmikā, Cāmuṇḍā indeed, and Yogīśī — thus eight, or the Aghorī-group and the rest.
The Sanskrit: *māheśī brāhmaṇī skāndī vaiṣṇavyaindrī yamātmikā | cāmuṇḍā caiva yogīśītyaṣṭāghoryādayo@thavā*

## v.4
Joined with the twelve — the mothers of Fire, Nirṛti, Wind, and Īśa: Nandā, Bhadrā, Jayā, Kālī, Karālī, Vikṛtānanā.
The Sanskrit: *agninirṛtivāyvīśamātṛbhirdvādaśānvitāḥ | nandā bhadrā jayā kālī karālī vikṛtānanā*

## v.5
Kroṣṭukī and Bhīmamudrā, Vāyuvegā, Hayānanā, Gambhīrā, and Ghoṣaṇī, thus: the rule in the twenty-four-spoked wheel.
The Sanskrit: *kroṣṭukī bhīmamudrā ca vāyuvegā hayānanā | gambhīrā ghoṣaṇī ceti caturviṃśatyare vidhiḥ*

## v.6
Siddhi, Vṛddhi, Dyuti, Lakṣmī, Medhā, Kānti, Sudhā, Dhṛti, Dīpti, Puṣṭi, Mati, Kīrti, Susthiti, Sugati, Smṛti.
The Sanskrit: *siddhirvṛddhirdyutirlakṣmīrmedhā kāntiḥ sudhā dhṛtiḥ | dīptiḥ puṣṭirmatiḥ kīrtiḥ susthitiḥ sugatiḥ smṛtiḥ*

## v.7
Suprabhā — sixteen in all, thus: the Śrīkaṇṭha-headed powers. Bali and Balinanda, Daśagrīva, Hara, Haya.
The Sanskrit: *suprabhā ṣoḍaśī ceti śrīkaṇṭhādikaśaktayaḥ | baliśca balinandaśca daśagrīvo haro hayaḥ*

## v.8
Mādhava in the six-spoked wheel; but in the twelve-spoked these are remembered: Dakṣa, Caṇḍa, Hara, Śauṇḍī, Pramatha, Bhīma and Manmatha.
The Sanskrit: *mādhavaḥ ṣaḍare cakre dvādaśāre tvamī smṛtāḥ | dakṣaścaṇḍo haraḥ śauṇḍī pramatho bhīmamanmathau*

## v.9
Śakuni, Sumati, Nanda, Gopāla and Pitāmaha; Śrīkaṇṭha, Ananta, Sūkṣma, and Trimūrti, Śaṃbareśvara.
The Sanskrit: *śakuniḥ sumatirnando gopālaśca pitāmahaḥ | śrīkaṇṭho@nantasūkṣmau ca trimūrtiḥ śaṃbareśvaraḥ*

## v.10
Arghīśa and Bhārabhūti, Sthiti, Sthāṇu, Hara, thus; Jhaṇṭi, Bhautika, Sadyoja, Anugraha, Krūra, Sainika.
The Sanskrit: *arghīśo bhārabhūtiśca sthitiḥ sthāṇurharastathā | jhaṇṭhibhautikasadyojānugrahakrūrasainikāḥ*

## v.11
Two-eight; whatever nectar, joined therewith: Pūrṇa, Ābha and their fluids, Ogha, Ūrmi, Syandana, Aṅga, and Vapus, Udgāra, Vaktraka.
The Sanskrit: *dvyaṣṭau yadvāmṛtastena yuktāḥ pūrṇābhataddravāḥ | oghormisyandanāṅgāśca vapurudgāravaktrakāḥ*

## v.12
Tanu, Secana, Mūrti, Īśa; Sarvāmṛtadhara, and the other; by feminine reading these powers are declared sixteen indeed.
The Sanskrit: *tanusecanamūrtīśāḥ sarvāmṛtadharo@paraḥ | śrīpāṭhācchaktayaścaitāḥ ṣoḍaśaiva prakīrtitāḥ*

## v.13
Saṃvarta, Lakulī, Bhṛgu, Sita, Baka, Khaṅgī, Pinākī, Bhujaga, Bali, Kāla; Dvi and Chagalāṇḍa; Śikhī, Śoṇa, Meṣa, Mīna, Tridaṇḍī; with Āṣāḍhī.
The Sanskrit: *saṃvartalakulibhṛgusitabakakhaṅgipinākibhujagabalikālāḥ | dviśchagalāṇḍau śikhiśoṇameṣamīnatridaṇḍi sāṣāḍhi*

## v.14
Devīkānta and his half; Dāruka, Hali, Somanātha, Śarman; Jaya, Vijaya, Jayantā, Jita, Sujaya, Jaya, the Rudrakīrtana-bringers.
The Sanskrit: *devīkāntatadardhau dārukahalisomanāthaśarmāṇaḥ | jayavijayajayantājitasujayajayarudrakīrtanāvahakāḥ*

## v.15
Increasing zeal for His form; and the Bala, Subala, Bhadra gift-bearers; its bearer and giver, and the Lord, joyous, equal, blessed of His form.
The Sanskrit: *tanmūrtyutsāhadavardhanāśca balasubalabhadradāvahakāḥ | tadvāndātā ceśo nandanasamabhadratanmūrtiḥ*

## v.16
Desiring well-minded Śiva-giving; Durga, time called Bhadra, mind-following Kauśika, Kāla, Viśva, Suśivās and others likewise; Kopa.
The Sanskrit: *śivadasumanaḥspṛhaṇakā durgo bhadrākhyakālaśca | ceto@nugakauśikakālaviśvasuśivāstathāparaḥ kopaḥ*

## v.17
On the ear-fire spoke these would be; by feminine reading these are śaktis. 'Juṃkāra' and 'Agnipatnī', thus, in the six-spoked, eunuch-excluded.
The Sanskrit: *śrutyagnyare syurete strīpāṭhācchaktayastvetāḥ | juṃkāro@thāgnipatnīti ṣaḍare ṣaṇṭhavarjitāḥ*

## v.18
In the twelve-spoked, those along with them; in the sixteen-spoked, the vowels in order; ha in the twice-eight-spoked; from ya to the ha-end, but, in the trika.
The Sanskrit: *dvādaśāre tatsahitāḥ ṣoḍaśāre svarāḥ kramāt | halastaddviguṇe@ṣṭāre yādyaṃ hāntaṃ tu tattrike*

## v.19
On the thirty-two-spoked, with end, bindu on the head of all; thus many other cakra-divisions one should construct from this.
The Sanskrit: *dvātriṃśadarake sāntaṃ binduḥ sarveṣu mūrdhani | evamanyānbahūṃścakrabhedānasmātprakalpayet*

## v.20
One alone is this consciousness-self, the essence of universe-awareness; śakti and her possessor thence — mother, mass of sound — are declared a pair.
The Sanskrit: *eka eva cidātmaiṣa viśvāmarśanasārakaḥ | śaktistadvānato mātā śabdarāśiḥ prakīrtitau*

## v.21
But in the division of those two, in the fashioning of śakti and her possessor: the sound-mass and Mālinī, of agitation-nature, such a body.
The Sanskrit: *tayoreva vibhāge tu śaktitadvatprakalpane | śabdarāśirmālinī ca kṣobhātma vapurīdṛśam*

## v.22
Thus in the splitting of inner awareness, really the triad called Anuttara, Icchā, Unmeṣa — from which the universe is awareness.
The Sanskrit: *tathāntaḥsthaparāmarśabhedane vastutastrikam | anuttarecchonmeṣākhyaṃ yato viśvaṃ vimarśanam*

## v.23
But in union with Ānanda, Īśa, Ūrmi, that is declared the hexad; through semivowel-sibilant union that is declared the octad.
The Sanskrit: *ānandeśormiyoge tu tatṣaṭkaṃ samudāhṛtam | antaḥsthoṣmasamāyogāttadaṣṭakamudāhṛtam*

## v.24
Then, in the absence of the four amṛta-vowels, it would be a dodecad; in union with them, called sixteen — thus until innumerability.
The Sanskrit: *tadāmṛtacatuṣkonabhāve dvādaśakaṃ bhavet | tadyoge ṣoḍaśākhyaṃ syādevaṃ yāvadasaṃkhyatā*

## v.25
Since it clearly bears the universe from single-awareness-bearing on, bearing part-of-part awarenesses at the end.
The Sanskrit: *viśvamekaparāmarśasahatvātprabhṛti sphuṭam | aṃśāṃśikāparāmarśān paryante sahate yataḥ*

## v.26
Hence the fifty's oneness, vowel-manifest-variety, eight vargas, letter-division, eighty-one kalā-rise.
The Sanskrit: *ataḥ pañcāśadaikātmyaṃ svaravyaktivirūpatā | vargāṣṭakaṃ varṇabheda ekāśītikalodayaḥ*

## v.27
Thus shown before, from half-mora-bearing; for even vowel-half exists, of the svarita a half-mora.
The Sanskrit: *iti pradarśitaṃ pūrvam ardhamātrāsahatvataḥ | svarārdhamapyasti yataḥ svaritasyārdhamātrakam*

## v.28
From its beginning the udātta, that said by the pada-knower; thus this consciousness, of sacrificand-form-awareness-nature.
The Sanskrit: *tasyādita udāttaṃ tatkathitaṃ padavedinā | itthaṃ saṃvidiyaṃ yājyasvarūpāmarśarūpiṇī*

## v.29
Unbroken from consciousness, this wheel-horizon of wheels; by lordship-and-veil division employ it manifoldly.
The Sanskrit: *abhinnaṃ saṃvidaścaitaccakrāṇāṃ cakravālakam | svāmyāvaraṇabhedena bahudhā tatprayojayet*

## v.30
Parāparā, Parā, and the other: creation, maintenance, concealment; but the fourth, Mātṛsadbhāva-formed, is called repose.
The Sanskrit: *parāparā parā cānyā sṛṣṭisthititirodhayaḥ | mātṛsadbhāvarūpā tu turyā viśrāntirucyate*

## v.31
And that clearly mouth-seated, hinted at each word; in the Fourth, repose is to be placed, moving in Mātṛsadbhāva.
The Sanskrit: *tacca prakāśaṃ vaktrasthaṃ sūcitaṃ tu pade pade | turye viśrāntirādheyā mātṛsadbhāvasāriṇi*

## v.32
So to him the universe shines, gone to identity with his own self; thus told is this unification of the scripture's matter, uttered by the guru.
The Sanskrit: *tathāsya viśvamābhāti svātmatanmayatāṃ gatam | ityeṣa śāstrārthasyokta ekīkāro gurūditaḥ*

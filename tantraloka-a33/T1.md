## tal-a33-p02 (33.11-33.20)

## tal-a33-p03 (33.21-33.30)
[and]-tayoreva vibhāge tu śaktitadvatprakalpane / śabdarāśirmālinī ca kṣobhātma vapurīdṛśam tathāntaḥsthaparāmarśabhedane vastutastrikam / anuttarecchonmeṣākhyaṃ yato viśvaṃ vimarśanam ānandeśormiyoge tu tatṣaṭkaṃ samudāhṛtam / antaḥsthoṣmasamāyogāttadaṣṭakamudāhṛtam tadāmṛtacatuṣkonabhāve dvādaśakaṃ bhavet / tadyoge ṣoḍaśākhyaṃ syādevaṃ yāvadasaṃkhyatā viśvamekaparāmarśasahatvātprabhṛti sphuṭam / aṃśāṃśikāparāmarśān paryante sahate yataḥ ataḥ pañcāśadaikātmyaṃ svaravyaktivirūpatā / vargāṣṭakaṃ varṇabheda ekāśītikalodayaḥ iti pradarśitaṃ pūrvam ardhamātrāsahatvataḥ / svarārdhamapyasti yataḥ svaritasyārdhamātrakam tasyādita udāttaṃ tatkathitaṃ padavedinā / itthaṃ saṃvidiyaṃ yājyasvarūpāmarśarūpiṇī abhinnaṃ saṃvidaścaitaccakrāṇāṃ cakravālakam / svāmyāvaraṇabhedena bahudhā tatprayojayet parāparā parā cānyā sṛṣṭisthititirodhayaḥ / mātṛsadbhāvarūpā tu turyā viśrāntirucyate

## tal-a33-p04 (33.31-33.32)

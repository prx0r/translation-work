# C1 — tal-a33

## SUMMARY

The unification arrived in due course: since the triad is worshipped with the cakra-divisions, here is their unification. Six-spoked goddesses per Siddhāvīra — Viśvā, her Mistress, Hāraudrī, Vīranetrī, Ambikā, Gurvī; eight mothers — Māheśī through Yogīśī — or the Aghorī group; twelve with the quarter-mothers — Nandā through Vikṛtānanā; the twenty-four-spoked rule — Kroṣṭukī through Ghoṣaṇī; fifteen powers Siddhi through Smṛti, Suprabhā sixteenth of the Śrīkaṇṭha-headed, then Bali, Balinanda, Daśagrīva, Hara, Haya; Mādhava six-spoked, seven remembered twelve-spoked; Śakuni through Śaṃbareśvara around Śrīkaṇṭha; Arghīśa through Sainika; two-eight, nectar-joined — Pūrṇa, Ābha, fluids, Ogha through Vaktraka; Tanu through Īśa, Sarvāmṛtadhara and the other — sixteen by feminine reading; Saṃvarta through Kāla, Dvi and Chagalāṇḍa, Śikhī through Tridaṇḍī with Āṣāḍhī; Devīkānta and half, Dāruka through Śarman, Jaya through Rudrakīrtana-bringers; zeal-increasers, Bala gift-bearers, bearer-giver-Lord of joyous-equal-blessed form; Śiva-giving desirers, Durga, Bhadra-time, mind-following Kauśika through Suśivās, Kopa; ear-fire spoke — Juṃkāra and Agnipatnī, feminine-read śaktis, eunuch excluded; twelve-spoked with-them, sixteen-spoked vowels in order, ha twice-eight-spoked, ya-to-ha in the trika; thirty-two-spoked with bindu-on-all-heads — construct freely thence. One consciousness-self, universe-awareness-essence; śakti and possessor — mother and sound-mass, a declared pair; divided, their fashioning is sound-mass and Mālinī, agitation-natured, such a body. Inner-awareness splitting is really the Anuttara-Icchā-Unmeṣa triad whence the universe is awareness; with Ānanda-Īśa-Ūrmi the hexad, with semivowels-sibilants the octad; minus four amṛtas a dodecad, with them sixteen — to innumerability. Bearing descends from single to part-of-part awareness; hence fifty-oneness, vowel-variety, eight vargas, letter-division, eighty-one kalā-rise. Shown before from half-mora-bearing; svarita even has a half-mora; from its start the udātta per the pada-knower — consciousness as offering-awareness; the unbroken wheel-horizon employed manifoldly by lordship-and-veil. Parāparā, Parā, the other: creation, maintenance, concealment — the fourth, Mātṛsadbhāva-formed, is repose; mouth-taught, word-hinted, repose placed in the Fourth moving in Mātṛsadbhāva. So the universe shines self-identical to him; told is the guru-uttered unification of the scripture's matter.

## FUNCTION

Establishes ekīkāra as the scripture's assembly-unification: goddess/power-holder/mantra lists (174 per Mālinīvijaya) gathered into Trika goddesses and Mātṛsadbhāva; alphabet-energies derived as consciousness-activities (triad→hexad→octad→dodecad→ṣoḍaśa→innumerable); three working śaktis crowned by repose-as-fourth; mouth-taught but word-hinted transmission; universe-become-self as fruit — closing in guru-uttered seal.

# Tantraloka-a34 — L2 READ


## v.0
Now is told the entry into one's own nature, as the sequence requires.
The Sanskrit: *ucyate@tha svasvarūpapraveśaḥ kramasaṅgataḥ*

## v.1
This āṇava — taught in many ways for the attaining of Śiva-hood: having entered into the intervals therein, let him rest in the proximate station.
The Sanskrit: *yadetadbahudhā proktamāṇavaṃ śivatāptaye | tatrāntarantarāviśya viśrāmyetsavidhe pade*

## v.2
Then, by abandoning even the āṇava, let him take refuge in the Śākta plane; then likewise the Śāmbhava — clearly, in graded succession.
The Sanskrit: *tato@pyāṇavasaṃtyāgācchāktīṃ bhūmimupāśrayet | tato@pi śāmbhavīmevaṃ tāratamyakramātsphuṭam*

## v.3
Thus — a sharer in the Bhairava-state whose outpouring is filled with the great rays of sequentially-dawned awakening — in the end, with utter disregard for means, he enters forever his own self, the universe gestating within.
The Sanskrit: *itthaṃ kramoditavibodhamahāmarīcisaṃpūritaprasarabhairavabhāvabhāgī | ante@bhyupāyanirapekṣatayaiva nityaṃ svātmānamāviśati garbhitaviśvarūpam*

## v.4
Told is this entry into one's own nature — by the Supreme One.
The Sanskrit: *kathito@yaṃ svasvarūpapraveśaḥ parameṣṭhinā*

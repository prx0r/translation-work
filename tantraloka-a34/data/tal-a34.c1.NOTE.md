# C1 NOTE — tal-a34 (not the translation: commentary complete)

*Rule 12/13: everything here is note, not display. The C1 essay carries only the claim.*

## KEY TERMS (with grounds)

- svasvarūpapraveśa (m.): sva + svarūpa + praveśa; entry into one's own nature. Chapter subject (v00, v04); the Tantraloka's final word.
- krama (m.): graded sequence; the chapter's frame (kramasaṅgataḥ v00, tāratamya-kramāt v2, kramodita v3).
- āṇava (n./m.): the lowest means (Mālinīvijaya 2.21: uccāra-karaṇa-dhyāna-varṇa-sthāna-prakalpana); manifoldly taught (bahudhā v1), abandoned to ascend (saṃtyāgāt v2).
- antar-antara: interval after interval; the āṇava's true field (v1) — penetration between forms, not addition.
- savidha-pada (n.): proximate-kindred station (MW savidha 'near; of like kind'); rest commanded by optative (v1).
- saṃtyāga (m.): abandonment; the engine of ascent (v2, twice: āṇava left, then Śākta left).
- śāktī bhūmi / śāmbhavī (f.): Śākta and Śāmbhava planes; Mālinīvijaya 2.22–23 (thought-only absorption; thought-free absorption via the teacher).
- tāratamya (n.): more-and-less gradation; the rungs stay distinct (sphuṭam v2).
- abhyupāya-nirapekṣatā (f.): independence from means; the anupāya moment (TAL ch2 gatopāya; Tantrasara 1 caturvidham).
- garbhita-viśvarūpa: universe-wombed; the self entered already contains the all (v3).
- parameṣṭhin (m.): Supreme One — fork LIVE: Śiva the revealer vs the lineage supreme preceptor (Jayaratha: taught by Sumati, Śambhunātha's Trika teacher; Pradipaka 'Guru of the Guru of my Guru').

## LORE (one focused search, 2026-11-07)

- sanskrit-trikashaivism ch34: full v1–4 rendering (savidhe pade 'proximate and of the same kind'; sphuṭam 'clear and evident'; parameṣṭhinā as lineage-guru reading).
- sanatanarahasya four-upāyas: Mālinīvijaya 2.21–23 quoted (samāveśa threefold); TAL 1.167–170 quotation-with-reversal; ch2 anupāya uncompromising (no mantra, no meditation, no worship); Tantrasara 1 caturvidham sentence; gatopaya = anupaya in chapter list.
- hareesh ch1 contd: Jayaratha's six/lwelve/twenty-four paths gloss (ladder logic kin).
- anuttaratrikakula translation page: Vol 11 = ch30–37; ch34 'ascending through Individual, Empowered, Śāmbhava states and beyond (1-4ab)'; Sumati attribution.

## DECISIONS

- v00 carved topic line translated as full unit (precedent: a03 v00; songs list starts at 0).
- savidha combined gloss 'proximate-kindred' (both MW senses carried; convergent, no fork).
- parameṣṭhin fork LIVE (rank 1 Śiva, rank 2 preceptor); L4 v04 'Highest One' covers both pending owner.
- Rasa: śānta sthāyin (rest/entry as culmination) with adbhuta (wonder at the ladder).

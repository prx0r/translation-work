# Tantraloka-a35 — L2 READ


## v.1
Now the union of all scriptures is told: here is the whole ancient practice.
The Sanskrit: *athocyate samastānāṃ śāstrāṇāmiha melanam | iha tāvatsama sto@yaṃ vyavahāraḥ purātanaḥ*

## v.2
Having sought prasiddhi, that very certainty is called āgama; for conjunction and exclusion depend on prasiddhi.
The Sanskrit: *prasiddhimanusandhāya saiva cāgama ucyate | anvayavyatirekau hi prasiddherupajīvakau*

## v.3
In dependence-on-self of the two's manifestation-groups, what would be their course? Even direct perception is born of eye, self, lamp, object and the rest.
The Sanskrit: *svāyattatve tayorvyaktipūge kiṃ syāttayorgatiḥ | pratyakṣamapi netrātmadīpārthādiviśeṣajam*

## v.4
It requires prasiddhi at the root — thus-natured, wrapped all around: born, one alone, hungry — a child.
The Sanskrit: *apekṣate tatra mūle prasiddhiṃ tāṃ tathātmikām | abhitaḥsaṃvṛte jāta ekākī kṣudhitaḥ śiśuḥ*

## v.5
What should he do, what take, by what see, what approach? Indeed, even in a hundred-things-strewn place, whatever is his —
The Sanskrit: *kiṃ karotu kimādattāṃ kena paśyatu kiṃ vrajet | nanu vastuśatākīrṇe sthāne@pyasya yadeva hi*

## v.6
of one seeing, or even smelling, of one touching: consciousness is serene, taking that very principle at once — it bears conjunction and exclusion.
The Sanskrit: *paśyato jighrato vāpi spṛśataḥ saṃprasīdati | cetastadevādāya drāk so@nvayavyatirekabhāk*

## v.7
Well! Even the serene consciousness, whatever goes with specific meaning, it too is previously fashioned by vāsanā-shaped reflection.
The Sanskrit: *hanta cetaḥprasādo@pi yo@sāvarthaviśeṣagaḥ | so@pi prāgvāsanārūpavimarśaparikalpitaḥ*

## v.8
Not born of direct-perception-inference-etc-outer-proof-serenity; dependent-on-vāsanā previously — this is mere intuition — not.
The Sanskrit: *na pratyakṣānumānādibāhyamānaprasādajaḥ | prāgvāsanopajīvyetat pratibhāmātrameva na*

## v.9
Not the desire for eating-mud of a man, a child's, arises; dependent-on-vāsanā previously — if reflection — she and vāsanā.
The Sanskrit: *na mṛdabhyavahārecchā puṃso bālasya jāyate | prāgvāsanopajīvī cedvimarśaḥ sā ca vāsanā*

## v.10
If come from the east, that — this certainty — of former time; and not consciousness-serenity alone, the whole sequence of practice.
The Sanskrit: *prācyā cedāgatā seyaṃ prasiddhiḥ paurvakālikī | naca cetaḥprasattyaiva sarvo vyavahṛtikramaḥ*

## v.11
Root, prasiddhi — that is proof: 'everywhere indeed' let it be accepted; in seeking dependence-of-the-prior, she sometimes herself.
The Sanskrit: *mūlaṃ prasiddhistanmānaṃ sarvatraiveti gṛhyatām | pūrvapūrvopajīvitvamārgaṇe sā kvacitsvayam*

## v.12
For in that one all-knowing-nature, without doubt it shone before; practice is nowhere entire in a proof.
The Sanskrit: *sarvajñarūpe hyekasminniḥśaṅkaṃ bhāsata purā | vyavahāro hi naikatra samastaḥ ko@pi mātari*

## v.13
Hence — by mere non-all-knowing-priority, this is not established; in many-all-knowing-priorities no proof there is at all.
The Sanskrit: *tenāsarvajñapūrvatvamātreṇaiṣā na siddhyati | bahusarvajñapūrvatve na mānaṃ cāsti kiṃcana*

## v.14
Gleaming with a hundred certainties of pleasure-release-and-their-causes; of that reflection-nature — it: Bhairava, the Supreme Lord.
The Sanskrit: *bhogāpavargataddhetuprasiddhiśataśobhitaḥ | tadvimarśasvabhāvo@sau bhairavaḥ parameśvaraḥ*

## v.15
Then too, from part-part union — she, certainty, tradition, scripture, or, having resorted to, spread, should make worlds practice.
The Sanskrit: *tataścāṃśāṃśikāyogātsā prasiddhiḥ paramparām | śāstraṃ vāśritya vitatā lokānsaṃvyavahārayet*

## v.16
By that very childhood, all delighting in carrying practice, being, co-depend on the Śaiva indeed, the first āgama.
The Sanskrit: *tayaivāśaiśavātsarve vyavahāradharājuṣaḥ | santaḥ samupajīvanti śaivamevādyamāgamam*

## v.17
But the incomplete others: hence not shareholders of liberation-fruit, co-depend; as far as — but that far shareholders of that fruit.
The Sanskrit: *apūrṇāstu pare tena na mokṣaphalabhāginaḥ | upajīvanti yāvattu tāvattatphalabhāginaḥ*

## v.18
Even at childhood's end, whatever to-eat — this food is set going: by that certainty alone, not from perception, not from inference, not from impossibility.
The Sanskrit: *bālyāpāye@pi yadbhoktumannameṣa pravartate | tatprasiddhyaiva nādhyakṣānnānumānādasambhavāt*

## v.19
And not anywhere here, from fault-hope-doubt: cessation; and certainty, born-of-non-cognition: understanding, word-natured.
The Sanskrit: *naca kāpyatra doṣāśāśaṅkāyāśca nivṛttitaḥ | prasiddhiścāvigānotthā pratītiḥ śabdanātmikā*

## v.20
Of the mother, nature — which, in her, one doubts — not this, ever; by self-making alone, of the all-knower — for: the doubter.
The Sanskrit: *mātuḥ svabhāvo yattasyāṃ śaṅkate naiṣa jātucit | svakṛtatvavaśādeva sarvavitsa hi śaṅkaraḥ*

## v.21
As long as Śiva-hood is not in him, that long the self-following — that very — her, this certainty — he does not doubt.
The Sanskrit: *yāvattu śivatā nāsya tāvatsvātmānusāriṇīm | tāvatīmeva tāmeṣa prasiddhiṃ nābhiśaṅkate*

## v.22
In another doubting, she would there much esteem; thus, in future-Śiva-hood — this, prasiddhi — esteem, surely.
The Sanskrit: *anyasyāmabhiśaṅkī syādbhūyastāṃ bahu manyate | evaṃ bhāviśivatvo@mūṃ prasiddhiṃ manyate dhruvam*

## v.23
One alone āgama — and this, by the Lord, the all-seer, shown, which on the path, in activity and in cessation, standing.
The Sanskrit: *eka evāgamaścāyaṃ vibhunā sarvadarśinā | darśito yaḥ pravṛtte ca nivṛtte ca pathi sthitaḥ*

## v.24
In dharma, artha, kāma, mokṣa, through complete-incomplete-and-other-differences, in various fruits: one means, the Śāmbhava āgama.
The Sanskrit: *dharmārthakāmamokṣeṣu pūrṇāpūrṇādibhedataḥ | vicitreṣu phaleṣveka upāyaḥ śāmbhavāgamaḥ*

## v.25
In that — from subject-variety — in variably-fruit-giving, even the varied-means-teaching brings no contradiction.
The Sanskrit: *tasminviṣayavaiviktyādvicitraphaladāyini | citropāyopadeśo@pi na virodhāvaho bhavet*

## v.26
Worldly, Vedic, Sāṅkhya, Yoga and the rest, Pāñcarātra, Buddha-Arhat-logic-scriptures, category-sequence-texts.
The Sanskrit: *laukikaṃ vaidikaṃ sāṅkhyaṃ yogādi pāñcarātrakam | bauddhārhatanyāyaśāstraṃ padārthakramatantraṇam*

## v.27
Siddhānta-tantra-Śakti and the rest, all Brahman-born, because in the venerable Svacchanda and the rest taught through Sadyoja-and-other-differences.
The Sanskrit: *siddhāntatantraśāktādi sarvaṃ brahmodbhavaṃ yataḥ | śrīsvacchandādiṣu proktaṃ sadyojātādibhedataḥ*

## v.28
As in one place even, in Veda and the rest, going-to-that-that-āśrama, another-saṃskāra — here too; so the liṅga-uddhāra and the rest.
The Sanskrit: *yathaikatrāpi vedādau tattadāśramagāminaḥ | saṃskārāntaramatrāpi tathā liṅgoddhṛtādikam*

## v.29
And as there, in the earlier āśrama, not from the later āśrama fruit comes; so from Pāñcarātra and the rest, not Śiva-nature.
The Sanskrit: *yathāca tatra pūrvasminnāśrame nottarāśramāt | phalameti tathā pāñcarātrādau na śivātmatām*

## v.30
One alone āgama — then there, from the worldly-scripture, from the beginning — from Vaiṣṇava, from Buddhist, from Śaiva — all indeed is established.
The Sanskrit: *eka evāgamastasmāttatra laukikaśāstrataḥ | prabhṛtyāvaiṣṇavādbauddhācchaivātsarvaṃ hi niṣṭhitam*

## v.31
Of it, whatever is the attainable highest state, that is called Trika; through non-severance of all-differences, that very kula is called.
The Sanskrit: *tasya yattat paraṃ prāpyaṃ dhāma tat trikaśabditam | sarvāvibhedānucchedāt tadeva kulamucyate*

## v.32
As high-low-marked, in divided body-parts, one living being should be; so Trika, in all, by the scriptures.
The Sanskrit: *yathordhvādharatābhāksu dehāṅgeṣu vibhediṣu | ekaṃ prāṇitamevaṃ syāt trikaṃ sarveṣu śāstrataḥ*

## v.33
In the venerable Kālī-kula, and it is said, devoid of five streams, of ten-and-eighteen-difference, this essence is declared.
The Sanskrit: *śrīmatkālīkule coktaṃ pañcasrotovivarjitam | daśāṣṭādaśabhedasya sārametatprakīrtitam*

## v.34
In the flower is the fragrance; in the sesame is the oil; in the body is the soul; in the water is the nectar; as, so indeed, of scriptures kula within is established.
The Sanskrit: *puṣpe gandhastile tailaṃ dehe jīvo jale@mṛtam | yathā tathaiva śāstrāṇāṃ kulamantaḥ pratiṣṭhitam*

## v.35
That one alone āgama — this, varied, in varied authority-holders; so indeed she, prasiddhi — for, goes in own-flock and other-flock.
The Sanskrit: *tadeka evāgamo@yaṃ citraścitre@dhikāriṇi | tathaiva sā prasiddhirhi svayūthyaparayūthyagā*

## v.36
Sāṅkhya, Yoga, Pāñcarātra, Vedas — and indeed, not should one censure; because all are Śiva-born: thus in the Svacchanda teaching.
The Sanskrit: *sāṃkhyaṃ yogaṃ pāñcarātraṃ vedāṃścaiva na nindayet | yataḥ śivodbhavāḥ sarva iti svacchandaśāsane*

## v.37
From one āgama — and these, in pieces plucked out, in the world should be āgamas; and by them the people wander, deluded.
The Sanskrit: *ekasmādāgamāccaite khaṇḍakhaṇḍā vyapoddhṛtāḥ | loke syurāgamāstaiśca jano bhrāmyati mohitaḥ*

## v.38
In many-āgama sides too, subject-difference is speakable, necessarily held in high-low-ness, for establishing validity.
The Sanskrit: *anekāgamapakṣe@pi vācyā viṣayabheditā | avaśyamūrdhvādharatāsthityā prāmāṇyasiddhaye*

## v.39
Otherwise not at all anyone's validity is established, surely; permanence-and-noncontradiction, thus, for us are no proof-cause.
The Sanskrit: *anyathā naiva kasyāpi prāmāṇyaṃ siddhyati dhruvam | nityatvamavisaṃvāda iti no mānakāraṇam*

## v.40
In this part too — of that very one, validity would be — from that utterance; otherwise in non-realization, in fixed non-being, in preference.
The Sanskrit: *asminnaṃśe@pyamuṣyaiva prāmāṇyaṃ syāttathoditeḥ | anyathāvyākṛtau kḷptāvasatyatve prarocane*

## v.41
Over-implication — of all āgama too the defeater; 'must-reach' — in this proof of the āgama-name.
The Sanskrit: *atiprasaṅga sarvasyāpyāgamasyāpabādhakaḥ | avaśyopetya ityasminmāna āgamanāmani*

## v.42
'Must-reach' indeed — this is the account of scriptural endeavor; in the principal limb effort made is really fruit-bearing, because.
The Sanskrit: *avaśyopetyamevaitacchāstraniṣṭhānirūpaṇam | pradhāne@ṅge kṛto yatnaḥ phalavānvastuto yataḥ*

## v.43
Hence herein anyone striving should be Śambhu-impelled; and there, there, in scriptures, was set forth by the Great Lord.
The Sanskrit: *ato@smin yatnavān ko@pi bhavecchaṃbhupracoditaḥ | tatra tatra ca śāstreṣu nyarūpyata maheśinā*

## v.44
At this much — the qualified, who, he is hard to find, thus clearly; so, by venerable Śambhunātha — to me — was told the scripture-union.
The Sanskrit: *etāvatyadhikārī yaḥ sa durlabha iti sphuṭam | itthaṃ śrīśambhunāthena mamoktaṃ śāstramelanam*

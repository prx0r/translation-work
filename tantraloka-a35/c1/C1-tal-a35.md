# C1 — tal-a35

## SUMMARY

The union of all scriptures: here the whole ancient practice. Having sought prasiddhi — that very certainty is āgama; conjunction and exclusion depend on it. Self-dependent manifestation-groups go nowhere; even perception, born of eye-self-lamp-object specifics, requires at-root thus-natured prasiddhi: born wrapped, one, hungry — a child. What to do, take, see by, approach in a hundred-strewn place? Of seeing, smelling, touching, consciousness is serene, taking that principle at once — bearing conjunction-exclusion. Even serene consciousness with specific meaning is previously fashioned by vāsanā-shaped reflection; not born of outer-proof-serenity, not mere intuition — dependent-on-vāsanā. No mud-eating desire arises in man or child; if reflection feeds on prior vāsanā — and she is vāsanā. If come from the east, that former-time prasiddhi — and not by serenity alone runs all practice. Root: prasiddhi is proof — 'everywhere indeed' accept; seeking prior-dependence she sometimes stands herself. In the one all-knowing-nature doubtless it shone before; nowhere is whole practice in one proof. By mere non-omniscient priority this is not established; among many omniscients no proof either. Gleaming with a hundred pleasure-release-cause certainties, of that reflection-nature — Bhairava, Supreme Lord. Thence, from part-part union, she — certainty — tradition, scripture, resorting, spread — should make worlds practice. By that childhood all practice-bearers co-depend on the Śaiva, the first āgama; the incomplete share fruit only so far. At childhood's end food is set going by that certainty alone — not perception, inference, impossibility. Nowhere here does fault-hope-doubt cease but word-natured understanding born of non-cognitive prasiddhi. The mother's nature — doubted never; by self-making the all-knower is the doubter. While Śiva-hood is not his, self-following certainty he does not doubt; doubting another's, he much esteems — future-Śiva-hood surely esteems this certainty. One alone āgama, by the all-seeing Lord shown, standing in activity and cessation. In all aims through all measures, one means: the Śāmbhava āgama; from subject-variety in variably-fruit-giving, varied-means-teaching brings no contradiction. Worldly, Vedic, Sāṅkhya, Yoga, Pāñcarātra, Buddha-Arhat-logic, category-texts; Siddhānta-tantra-Śakti and the rest — all Brahman-born, per Svacchanda's Sadyoja-differences. As Veda gives āśrama-goers another saṃskāra, so liṅga-uddhāra and the rest here; as in the earlier āśrama no fruit comes from the later, so from Pāñcarātra no Śiva-nature. One āgama establishes all, from worldly scripture through Vaiṣṇava, Buddhist, Śaiva. Whatever highest is attainable — that state is Trika; through non-severance of all differences that very is Kula. As one living being across high-low-marked divided limbs, so Trika in all scriptures. Kālīkula says: devoid of five streams, of ten-and-eighteen-difference — this the essence. As fragrance in flower, oil in sesame, soul in body, nectar in water — so Kula stands within scriptures. One varied āgama for varied holders; one prasiddhi goes in own and others' flocks. Censure no school — Sāṅkhya, Yoga, Pāñcarātra, Vedas — all Śiva-born per Svacchanda. From one āgama plucked piecemeal, world-āgamas by which the deluded wander. Even many-āgama sides must state subject-difference in high-low-ness for validity; otherwise none is ever valid — permanence and noncontradiction prove nothing for us. In this part too validity of that very one would come from that utterance; otherwise counterfactuals. Over-implication defeats all āgama; 'must-reach' in this proof of the āgama-name. 'Must-reach' indeed: endeavor's account; in the principal limb effort really bears fruit. Hence herein strive Śambhu-impelled; everywhere scriptures were set forth by the Great Lord. Rare the fit for this much; thus by Śambhunātha to me was told the scripture-union.

## FUNCTION

Establishes prasiddhi as the root-proof beneath perception, inference, and scripture: the hungry-child experiment, vāsanā-shaped serenity, the mud-test, former-time sourcing, self-terminating regress; Bhairava as certainty personified; certainty descending through tradition into world-practice; Śaiva-first āgama feeding all, partial sharers, child-refuted pramāṇas, word-understanding stilling doubt, self-made doubt, undoubted self-following certainty, esteemed future certainty; one Lord-shown āgama for both ways, one Śāmbhava means for all aims, variety without contradiction; all schools Brahman-born yet graded (Veda initiates, Pāñcarātra doesn't reach Śiva); one āgama establishing all; Trika as attainable highest, Kula as undifferentiated; one life across limbs; Kālīkula essence; indwelling similes; varied āgama for varied holders; no censure (all Śiva-born); fragments mislead; even pluralists must rank subjects; permanence proves nothing; utterance proves here; overreach defeats; name proves must-reach; work the principal; strive impelled; the fit are rare — closing in Śambhunātha's told-to-me seal.

## EXPLANATION

The ahnika runs: (1–2) union announced, prasiddhi as āgama; (3–6) self-grounding fails, child experiment, serenity takes the principle; (7–10) serenity vāsanā-shaped, neither outer-proof nor bare flash, mud-test, former-time sourcing; (11–13) root accepted, regress ends, doubtless shining, numbers prove nothing; (14–17) Bhairava personifies certainty, certainty descends into worlds, Śaiva-first feeds all, partial sharers; (18–22) child eating refutes pramāṇas, word-understanding stills doubt, self-made doubt, undoubted self-following, esteemed future certainty; (23–25) one Lord-shown āgama, one Śāmbhava means, variety without contradiction; (26–29) school catalogue Brahman-born, Vedic saṃskāra parallel, grade holds; (30–34) one āgama establishes all, Trika/Kula named, one-life analogy, Kālīkula essence, indwelling similes; (35–37) varied āgama/holders, no censure, fragments mislead; (38–41) pluralists must rank, permanence proves nothing, utterance proves, overreach defeats, name proves; (42–44) work the principal, strive impelled, rare fit, told-to-me seal.

# Tantraloka-a36 — L2 READ


## v.0
Now the descent of the scripture is told, come in due course.
The Sanskrit: *āyātiratha śāstrasya kathyate@vasarāgatā*

## v.1
Pointed out by the venerable Siddhas and set forth by the gurus: Bhairava, Bhairavī the Goddess, Svacchanda, Lākula—Aṇurāṭ.
The Sanskrit: *śrīsiddhādivinirdiṣṭā gurubhiśca nirūpitā | bhairavo bhairavī devī svacchando lākulo@ṇurāṭ*

## v.2
Gahaneśa—Abjaja, Śakra — the guru, from the crore-fund, studied in sequence from the nine the nine-crore-expanse.
The Sanskrit: *gahaneśo@bjajaḥ śakro guruḥ koṭyapakarṣataḥ | navabhiḥ kramaśo@dhītaṃ navakoṭipravistaram*

## v.3
Then from these the guru distributed a quarter from each crore; both quarters to Dakṣa and the rest, and to Saṃvarta and the rest.
The Sanskrit: *etaistato guruḥ koṭimātrāt pādaṃ vitīrṇavān | dakṣādibhya ubhau pādau saṃvartādibhya eva ca*

## v.4
And a quarter to Vāmana and the rest, half a quarter to Bhārgava; but a quarter-quarter to Bali, and the other quarter-quarter.
The Sanskrit: *pādaṃ ca vāmanādibhyaḥ pādārdhaṃ bhārgavāya ca | pādapādaṃ tu balaye pādapādastu yo@paraḥ*

## v.5
Half to Siṃha; then from the remainder two shares to Garuḍa, a quarter to Vāsuki the serpent — and these are seventeen fragments.
The Sanskrit: *siṃhāyārdhaṃ tataḥ śiṣṭāddvau bhāgau vinatābhuve | pādaṃ vāsukināgāya khaṇḍāḥ saptadaśa tvamī*

## v.6
Half from heaven Rāvaṇa then took; Rāma took half too from this; beginning with Vibhīṣaṇa, obtained through the guru-disciple-rule sequence.
The Sanskrit: *svargādardhaṃ rāvaṇo@tha jahre rāmo@rdhamapyataḥ | vibhīṣaṇamukhādāpa guruśiṣyavidhikramāt*

## v.7
Divided into nineteen fragments it then became; and each fragment into eight sub-fragments, through the stated quarter-division.
The Sanskrit: *khaṇḍairekānnaviṃśatyā vibhaktaṃ tadabhūttataḥ | khaṇḍaṃ khaṇḍaṃ cāṣṭakhaṇḍaṃ proktapādādibhedataḥ*

## v.8
Quarter, root-extracts in the latter Greater-Uttara, likewise the Kalpa; saṃhitā-kalpa-skandha-…-anuttara, the pervader — threefold, three.
The Sanskrit: *pādo mūloddhārāvuttaravṛhaduttare tathā kalpaḥ | sāṃhitakalpaskandāvanuttaraṃ vyāpakaṃ tridhā tisraḥ*

## v.9
Here the goddesses are set forth in sequence in expansive form indeed; but in the ninth quarter no count is stated — in the undifferentiated.
The Sanskrit: *devyo@tra nirūpyante kramaśo vistāriṇaiva rūpeṇa | navame pade tu gaṇanā na kāciduktā vyavacchidāhīne*

## v.10
And from Rāma, Lakṣmaṇa; from him the Siddhas; from them too the Dānavas; and the Guhyakas; then from them the Yogins, thence the kings.
The Sanskrit: *rāmācca lakṣmaṇastasmāt siddhāstebhyo@pi dānavāḥ | guhyakāśca tatastebhyo yogino nṛvarāstataḥ*

## v.11
In their sequence, when through time what was amid them lapsed — then, by Śrīkaṇṭhanātha's command, the Siddhas descended.
The Sanskrit: *teṣāṃ krameṇa tanmadhye bhraṣṭaṃ kālāntarādyadā | tadā śrīkaṇṭhanāthājñāvaśāt siddhā avātaram*

## v.12
Tryambaka, Āmardaka, and renowned Śrīnātha — skilled in nonduality, in duality, and in duality-nonduality, in sequence, in Śiva's teaching.
The Sanskrit: *tryambakāmardakābhikhyaśrīnāthā advaye dvaye | dvayādvaye ca nipuṇāḥ krameṇa śivaśāsane*

## v.13
And the first's lineage was born; the second through daughter-succession — and that well-established line renowned as Ardhatryambaka.
The Sanskrit: *ādyasya cānvayo jajñe dvitīyo duhitṛkramāt | sa cārdhatryambakābhikhyaḥ saṃtānaḥ supratiṣṭhitaḥ*

## v.14
And so here four-and-a-half monastic seats in line-succession, spread by disciples and grand-disciples, established hundred-branching.
The Sanskrit: *ataścārdhacatasro@tra maṭhikāḥ saṃtatikramāt | śiṣyapraśiṣyairvistīrṇāḥ śataśākhaṃ vyavasthitaiḥ*

## v.15
Having gathered the essence-juice of the well-seated lineage-stream, this Tantrāloka flows with all rasas.
The Sanskrit: *adhyuṣṭasaṃtatisrotaḥsārabhūtarasāhṛtim | vidhāya tantrāloko@yaṃ syandate sakalānrasān*

## v.16
The to-be-taken sense of the stated descent is now determined.
The Sanskrit: *uktāyātirupādeyabhāvo nirṇīyate@dhunā*

- tryambaka-āmardaka-śrīnātha: Durvāsas's mind-sons; advaya/dvaya/dvayādvaya competencies (v12, lore).
- duhitṛkrama (m.): daughter-succession founding Ardhatryambaka (v13, lore).
- maṭhikā (f.): monastic seats, four-and-a-half, disciple-spread, hundred-branched (v14).

# Tantrasāra A01 — ARGMAP

## tsa01-p01 (1.1–1.5)
- speaker: Abhinava — maṅgala verses (1.1–1.3, invocation of Mother/Father, Tantrasāra promise, Sambhunātha lineage) then siddhānta exposition.
- question: What liberates, and what is one's own nature?
- premises: (1) Jñāna causes mokṣa by opposing bondage-causing ajñāna. (2) Ajñāna twofold: buddhigata (doubt + wrong conviction) and pauruṣa (thought-natured, contracted; root of saṃsāra — mala details deferred). (3) Pauruṣa removable by dīkṣā, but dīkṣā (tattva-purification + Śiva-joining, requiring prior heyopādeya determination) impossible while intellectual non-determination persists → determinative buddhi-knowledge is principal; practiced, it kills pauruṣa too (practice ends in thought-free). (4) Śāstra hierarchy: only Lord-spoken scripture fully authoritative (absorbs others discriminatively + out-argues them); others liberate partially; essence-chain: pañcasrotas → ṣaḍardha → Mālinīvijaya (inexhaustible). (5) Svabhāva = prakāśa of all things (non-light can't be nature); one (no other-nature entry, no division); place/time undividing (also light); = saṃvit (undisputed); autonomous (illuminedness needs another light; none exists) → all-pervading, eternal, form/formless. (6) Autonomy=ānanda, wonder=icchā, light=cit, grasp=jñāna, all-fitness=kriyā (really three). Resting in own bliss = Śiva; self-contracted manifesting = aṇu. (7) Re-manifestation sudden/gradual; means-or-not; will/knowledge/action approaches = śāmbhava/śākta/āṇava samāveśa; all four taught in order (1.5: hiding + spreading, by sequence/nosequence/threefold).
- inference: Determination-knowledge (śāstra-grounded) removes both ignorances; own nature is one autonomous light whose contraction/expansion explains bondage/liberation and the four means.
- conclusion: Adopt all-directed right-determination knowledge; the fourfold means follows in order.
- unresolved: Mala specifics deferred (malanirṇaye); upāya details deferred; Mālinīvijaya inexhaustibility noted.

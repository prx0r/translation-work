# C1 — Tantrasāra Ahnika 1 (svabhāva)

## tsa01-p01 — one's own nature

**FUNCTION.** Opens the whole work: establishes the problem (ignorance), the authority (Lord-spoken scripture), the one reality (autonomous light), and the fourfold means that the next four ahnikas will teach.

**KEY TERMS.** jñāna (knowledge, liberation's cause), ajñāna (ignorance, twofold: buddhigata + pauruṣa), mala (stain, saṃsāra's root), svabhāva (own nature = prakāśa), svātantrya (autonomy = ānanda), prakāśa (light = saṃvit), aṇu (atom = contracted self), samāveśa (immersion, threefold: śāmbhava/śākta/āṇava).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First ahnika; follows the maṅgala (1.1–1.3); precedes A02 (anupāya, the means-less immersion).


**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** Mala specifics deferred (malanirṇaye). Upāya details deferred to A02–A05. Mālinīvijaya's inexhaustibility is Abhinava's claim, not argued here. The maṅgala's parent-imagery is invocatory, not doctrinal.

**RELATED.** A02 (anupāya), A05 (āṇava), essay0 Q1 (What is my own nature?), spans TS1.4/TS9.2/TS21.1/TS22.1.

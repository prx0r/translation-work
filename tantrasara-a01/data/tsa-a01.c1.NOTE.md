# C1 NOTE — tsa-a01

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Mother of stainless art and new great creation, full-bodied; Father of five hidden faces: may their yamal-burst-forth Heart — deathless kin beyond all — flash forth. The Tantrāloka spreads too wide to dive; therefore its essence in plain words — hear it. The heart-lotus, its narrowness gone by the feet-fall splendor of Śambhunātha the Sun, is gathered for the Great Lord's worship. Knowledge frees as opponent of bondage-cause ignorance, twofold: intellect-located doubt-nature and wrong-conviction-nature, personal thought-nature and contracted manifestation — root of saṃsāra. The personal washes by initiation, but initiation needs prior shun-and-take determination, impossible while intellect cannot decide; hence determinative intellect-dwelling knowledge leads, and practiced kills the personal too, ending in thought-free. Only Lord-spoken scripture fully authorizes and fully frees — five streams, sixfold scriptures, Mālinīvijaya whose contained content no gathering can expound. Own-nature alone is adoptable: one autonomous light-consciousness, all-pervading, eternal, form and formless; autonomy is bliss, wonder is will, light is consciousness, grasp is knowing, all-fitness is action. Resting in own bliss it is Śiva; contracting itself, called atom; re-manifesting with or without means — will, knowing, action: three immersions, plus a fourth taught here in order.

## VOICE MAP

Maṅgala invocation v1; ocean-condensed promise v2; lineage-lotus gathering v3; determination-doctrine v4; one-light identity v5.

## KEY TERMS

* yāmala — pair-burst as the Heart invoked
* vitata — spread-wide as the ocean unplungeable
* viracita — composed-plain as the essence offered
* vicinuta — gathered-lotus as the disciple's act
* adhyavasāya — determination as what liberates
* avikalpa — thought-free as practice's end
* svabhāva — own-nature as alone adoptable
* svātantrya — autonomy as bliss proved in five powers

## CRUXES

* Initiation washes the personal but needs prior determination — the sequence cannot be reversed.
* Only Lord-spoken scripture fully frees; the Mālinīvijaya's content no gathering exhausts (deferred to the work itself).
* The fourth immersion beyond the three is stated as taught here; its content deferred to the work.

## FUNCTION

Opens the Tantrasāra: invokes, condenses, gathers lineage, establishes determination-knowledge, reveals own-nature — hiding then spreading, stepwise or at once.

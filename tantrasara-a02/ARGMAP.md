# Tantrasāra A02 — ARGMAP

## tsa02-p01 (2.1–2.4)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (first upāya: anupāya).
- question: How does immersion work with no means at all?
- premises: (1) Firm-descent-pierced one heeds teacher's word once → self-discrimination → ever-risen means-less immersion. (2) "Reason itself is the yoga-limb" — how? Fourfold impossibility: no nature to attain (eternal), nothing to notify (self-shining), no covering to remove (none possible), no one to enter (no distinct enterer); and no means as distinct entity either. (3) Positive: all is one consciousness-only reality (six negations: time/place/attribute/form/word/valid-means) + own-will cause of time-to-means' nature-attainment + autonomous bliss-dense + "I am that, universe reflected in me" → ever Lordly means-less immersion, constrained by no mantra/worship/meditation/conduct.
- inference: Means presuppose lack, hiddenness, or distance; all three impossible for self-light.
- conclusion: Anupāya immersion stated (first of four).
- unresolved: none.

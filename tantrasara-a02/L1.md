# Tantrasāra A02 — L1 controlled

## tsa02-p01
Now we shall expound the means-less itself first. (2.1) When one pierced by firm descent, by himself, thus discriminates — having heeded the teacher's word just once — then, means-devoid, ever-risen is his immersion. (2.2) Here: "reason itself is the yoga-limb" — thus, how does he discriminate? If asked, it is said: this Lord, self-light-natured, is own self. Therein, what is done by means? Not attainment of nature — from eternity; not notification — from self-shiningness; not removal of covering — since covering of any kind is impossible; not entry into it — since there is no distinct enterer. (2.3) And what means here? Of it too, as distinct, there is impossibility. Hence: all this is one consciousness-only reality — unmeasured by time, undelimited by place, unfaded by attributes, uncontrolled by forms, unindicated by words, unelaborated by valid means — and the cause, by own will alone, of the attainment of own nature from time to valid means; autonomous, bliss-dense reality. And that itself am I; and therein, within me, the universe is reflected. For one thus firmly discriminating, ever is there Lordly immersion, means-less itself; and for it there is no constraint at all — by mantra, worship, meditation, or conduct. (2.4)

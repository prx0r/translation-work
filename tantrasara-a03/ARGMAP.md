# Tantrasāra A03 — ARGMAP

## tsa03-p01 (3.1–3.13)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (śāmbhava upāya: seeing).
- question: How does one enter when the unbroken circle cannot be entered — and what is the phoneme-womb of that seeing?
- premises: (1) Reflection doctrine: all things = mere reflections in awareness-sky (mirror/taste/smell/touch/echo similes); reflections are non-primary (no unseen effects) yet not non-existent (dusting, emission seen). Universe likewise in Lord's light. (2) No source-image? No problem: cause = Lord's power = autonomy; universe-bodiedness grasped (else inertness); grasping non-conventional, resonance-pregnant, coextensive with power-multitude. (3) Three chief powers = anuttara/icchā/unmeṣa = grasping-triads a-i-u / ā-ī-ū; whole power-expanse enacted thence. (4) Sun-triad (light-portion) vs moon-triad (rest-bliss) while action absent; action entering will/sovereignty → ra/la sounds (light-pillar), unmanifest (else creation, not will). Four neuter vowels ṛṝḷḹ bearing both shadows.
- inference: Seeing = resting in reflection-knowledge; phonemes are that seeing crystallized by degrees.
- conclusion: Reflection + first phoneme sets established.
- unresolved: similes assume everyday assent (external-knowledge, mild).

## tsa03-p02 (3.14–3.34)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (continued derivation).
- question: How do all phonemes, tattvas, and worlds unfold from the graspings — and what does the seer gain?
- premises: (1) Anuttara+ānanda spreading into wills → e/oṃ; regrouped → ai/au = kriyāśakti = e-ai-o-au. (2) All effects re-entering unsurpassed rest first as bindu (am), then emission (aḥ): 16 seed-graspings; thence consonant womb. (3) Letter-classes assigned: ka (anuttara), ca (śraddhā-will), ṭa/ta (double, action-wills), pa (unmeṣa), fivefold by five-power yoga; ya-ra-la (three wills), va (unmeṣa), śa-ṣa-sa (three wills), ha (emission), kṣa (womb-union) → Anuttara as Kuleśvara. (4) One kaulikī emission-power: from bliss to outer creation, graspings become outer tattvas. Emission threefold: āṇava (mind-rest), śākta (mind-awakening), śāmbhava (mind-dissolution) — emission itself the Lord's world-making power. (5) Numbers: undivided grasping = one Lord as seed-womb; part-grasping = power/possessor; 8-groups + wheel-lords = 9 classes; per-grasping primacy = 50; possible divisions = 81; really 6 graspings × forth/back = 12 = Lord's power-fullness; as powers they are goddess Kālīs. (6) Pure graspings in pure knowledge (parāpara) → विद्याविद्येश्वर via slight Māyā-contraction; in Māyā → Māyā-letters through paśyantī/madhyamā/vaikharī to outer tattva-nature; seen as body-scheme and re-vivified by pure graspings → potent, giving enjoyment+liberation.
- inference: One continuous derivation: grasping → letter → tattva → world; revivification restores power to fallen letters.
- conclusion: Seeing the self thus (all-grasping-rest, reflected tattva/bhuvana divisions) with thought-free śāmbhava immersion = living liberation; no mantra-constraint here either.
- unresolved: letter-derivations assume āgamic phonology (external-knowledge, heavy in this passage).

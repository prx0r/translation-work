# Tantrasāra A03 — L1 controlled

## tsa03-p01
When that light-natured Śiva-reality stated cannot be entered in the unbroken circle, then, seeing autonomy-power itself as preponderant, one experiences undivided Bhairava-immersion. And his teaching is this: all this host of things is mere reflection in the sky of awareness, since endowed with reflection-marks. For this is the mark of reflection: what, shining dividedly, unable, shines only as unmixed with other — that is reflection — like face-form in a mirror, like taste in tooth-water, like smell in the nose, like couple's touch in the bliss-sense, like thorn-spear touch in the inner touch-sense, like echo in the sky. (3.1) For that taste is not primary — since no unseen effect like disease-cure is seen. (3.2) Nor are smell and touch primary for the qualified — since in their absence there is no yoga, and since no effect-series begins. (3.3) Nor are they non-existent — from seeing body-dusting, emission, and the rest. (3.4) Sound too is not primary — from hearing like an approaching echo that someone speaks. (3.5) Thus, as this reflected shines, so too the universe is in the Lord's light. (3.6) "But here what would be the source-image? Let there be none!" (3.7) "But is it causeless? Ah — then the cause-question: what, by source-word logic? The cause is the Lord's power itself, non-different from autonomy, shall be — and since bearing the universe-reflection, hence the Lord's universe-bodiedness: for the universe is consciousness-made, the manifest station of awareness — hence that very universe here is counter-image, hence its reflection-bearing. And that, so far, the Lord's universe-bodiedness is nature — not ungrasped, since graspinglessness of consciousness-nature is impossible. (3.8) For in non-grasping of own nature there would be inertness itself, in truth. And this grasping is not conventional, but unavoidably following mere consciousness-nature — declared pregnant with supreme resonance; and it grasps as much as is the Lord's power-multitude ordering the universe. (3.9) Therein, chief are three powers of the Lord: unsurpassed, will, unfolding — that itself is the grasping-triad a, i, u. From this triad alone the whole power-expanse is enacted. In the unsurpassed itself is rest, bliss; in will itself is rest, sovereignty; in unfolding itself is rest, wave, the beginning of action-power. That itself is the grasping-triad ā, ī, ū. (3.10) Therein, the earlier grasping-triad, since essence is light-portion, is sun-natured; the latter grasping-triad, since rest-nature-bliss-preponderant, is moon-natured — so far as there is no entry of action-portion. (3.11) But when action enters into will and sovereignty — what is called wished and willed — then its two divisions: by light alone, ra-sound; by rest, la-sound — since ra and la are light-pillar-natured. And the wished is not manifest like outer; if manifest-formed, that itself would be creation, not will or sovereignty. Hence from unmanifestness alone is mere sound of ra and la — no standing like consonant. (3.12) That four-phoneme set, since bearing both shadows, is neuter — ṛ, ṝ, ḷ, ḹ. (3.13)"

## tsa03-p02
"When there is spreading of unsurpassed-and-bliss into wills, then the phoneme-pair e, oṃ. There again, from regrouping of unsurpassed-and-bliss, the pair ai, au. That is action-power, and that four — e, ai, o, au. Then again, at the end of action-power, all effect-things about to enter the unsurpassed rest first as sensation-essence, as light-alone, as bindu: am. Then therein arises the emission of the unsurpassed: aḥ. Thus the sixteen of graspings is called seed-natured. Risen thence, consonant-natured, womb-natured. Therein, from unsurpassed the ka-class; of faith, from will the ca-class; of action-willed will, the two ṭa and ta classes; from unfolding the pa-class; by five-power yoga, fivefoldness. Of threefold will alone: ya, ra, la; from unfolding, va; of threefold will alone: śa, ṣa, sa; from emission, ha; womb-union-born, kṣa. Thus indeed this Lord, unsurpassed, is Kuleśvara-form. Of him there is one kaulikī emission-power, by which, from bliss-nature onward to outer-creation-end, throbbing, the class-and-graspings become outer tattva-natured. And this emission is threefold: āṇava of mind-rest-nature, śākta of mind-awakening-mark, śāmbhava of mind-dissolution-nature. Thus emission itself, in world-making, is the Lord's power. Thus when grasping is undividedly, then the one Lord alone, as seed-womb; in part-grasping, possessor-of-power and power. In separate eight-grasping, with wheel-lord-company, nine classes; with per-grasping primacy, fifty-natured. There too, in grasping possible part-division differences, eighty-one-natured. But really there are six graspings, becoming twelve through forth-and-back yoga, nourishing the Lord's power-fullness of the universe. They themselves, since grasping-natured, powers, goddesses, are etymologized as Śrī Kālīs. And these power-natured pure graspings, in pure knowledge, through parāpara-nature, through slight Māyā-unfolding-contraction, take vidyā-avidyā-lord-nature. In Māyā again, with manifest difference-divisions, they take Māyā-letter-nature — attaining usability in paśyantī, madhyamā, vaikharī, up to outer-form-tattva-nature-becoming. And they, though Māyā-made, when seen as body-scheme, and when by the stated means their revivification is done by the life-standing pure graspings, then become potent; and so disposed, they give enjoyment and liberation. Thus seeing the self, of mere all-grasping-rest-nature, with reflected all-tattva-being-world-divisions, thought-freely, by śāmbhava immersion — living liberation. Here too, as before, there is no mantra-constraint at all. (3.14–3.34)"

# Tantrasāra A03 — L2 READ

## tsa03-p01 — seeing: everything as reflection
Can't enter the unbroken circle? Then look at the power that won't be broken: autonomy itself — and rest in Bhairava-immersion without a ripple of thought. The teaching is one sentence: everything you see is a reflection in the sky of knowing. A reflection has a mark: it shows up split off, helpless — unable to stand apart from what it shines in. A face in a mirror. Taste on teeth in water. Smell in a nose. Lovers' touch. A thorn's touch felt inwardly. An echo in the sky. None of these run the show — taste cures no disease — yet none are nothing either: dust the body and something lifts; seed flows. The universe stands to the Lord's light exactly so. "But where's the original the world reflects?" — there needn't be one. "Then is it causeless?" — no: its cause is the Lord's own power, which just is autonomy, holding the world's reflection and so bodied as the world. And this world-bodiedness isn't ungrasped — a consciousness that never grasped itself would be inert matter. The grasping isn't a label stuck on; it comes with being conscious the way resonance comes with a bell, and it reaches exactly as far as the Lord's powers order the world. Three of those powers are chief: the Unsurpassed, Will, Unfolding — grasped as a-i-u. From that one triad the whole power-pageant is performed. Rest in the Unsurpassed is bliss; rest in Will is sovereignty; rest in Unfolding is the wave where action-power begins — grasped as ā-ī-ū. The first triad is sun, being mostly light; the second is moon, being mostly restful bliss — until action walks in. When action enters willing and commanding — the wished, the willed — it splits in two: by sheer light, the sound ra; by rest, the sound la (both are pillars of light). The wished stays unmanifest — flaunted, it would be a made thing, not willing. So ra and la are bare sounds, never full consonants. With both lights and shadows in them, the four — ṛ ṝ ḷ ḹ — are neuter.

## tsa03-p02 — how the alphabet falls out of the graspings
When Unsurpassed and Bliss pour into the wills, two letters sound: e, oṃ. Clashing back together, two more: ai, au — and that foursome is action-power itself. When every effect is about to rest back in the Unsurpassed, it waits first as pure sensing, sheer light, a point: aṃ. Then, right there, the Unsurpassed breathes out: aḥ. Sixteen graspings, all seed. Out of seed, the consonants, the womb. From the Unsurpassed, the k-row; from will-as-faith, the c-row; from action-joined will, the two ṭ- and t-rows; from unfolding, the p-row — five by yoga with the five powers. From threefold will alone: y, r, l; from unfolding, v; from threefold will: ś, ṣ, s; from emission, h; from womb's union, kṣa. So the Lord, unsurpassed, wears Kuleśvara's shape. His is one clan-emission-power: from bliss-nature throbbing all the way out to creation's edge, graspings turn into outer principles. The emission is three: stopping mind (atom's way), waking mind (power's way), dissolving mind (Śambhu's way) — emission itself is how the Lord worlds the world. Grasped whole, he is one, seed-and-womb; grasped in parts, owner and owned. Eight graspings apart, with their wheel-lords: nine clans. One grasping leading: fifty. Dividing further: eighty-one. But truly six graspings, twelve going and returning, keep the Lord full of the universe's powers — and as powers, those goddesses spell Kālī. Pure graspings, in pure knowing, take a half-other shape and narrow slightly with Māyā's dawn into lords of knowing and unknowing. In Māyā they splinter openly into Māyā-letters, usable through seeing, middle, and outer speech down to outer principles. Read as the body's plan and re-ensouled by the pure graspings that outlive them, even fallen letters turn potent — giving pleasure and freedom both. Who sees the self this way — nothing but rest in all graspings, with every principle, being, and world mirrored in it — thought-free, in Śambhu's immersion, is free while living. And here too: no mantra strings attached.

# C1 — tsa-a04

## SUMMARY

Concept impresses in sequence for entry — the contemplation-sequence preceded by true reason/scripture/teacher has use. By concept-strength beings mistake themselves bound; the counter-pair concept tears that cause and becomes prosperity's cause. The crossing-concept: undelimited-consciousness-merely-formed = paramārtha, world-ojas, the breath of world — aham world-self. Not for the māyā-blind: true-reason and rest absent. Vaiṣṇava-type āgamas passion-limited: no higher inclination, hostile to sattarka-lineage. Pārameśvara cites them: colored by vidyā-rāga. They lack sarvajña-jñāna, hence find not parama-tattva. Only firm-śaktipāta-pierced, impressing concept by true-scripture-order, enter supreme own-nature. Concept is not paramārtha's form: its work is only dvaita-breaking; paramārtha is self-luminous everywhere. Firmly-pierced get sattarka spontaneously — that is devī-dīkṣā. Another gets it by scripture-order — full treatment in power-descent-revelation. Sattarka-task = teacher-scripture determination + fitting-concept arising; that chain = sattarka = bhāvanā making existent look non-existent. No yoga-limb is direct means apart from sattarka: limbs are vedyamātra, sattarka alone works in saṃvid. Limbs still excellence the karaṇa-bhūmi and impart tādātmya — subordinate but real. Abhyāsa cannot exist in parama-tattva-Siva-self: practice presupposes otherness. Rooted-in-saṃvid: practice = fixing breath-mind-body; in saṃvid-form nothing taken or removed — how practice? Tarka admitted only as dvaita-nirāsa-prakāra — nothing else. Laukika-abhyāsa = cid-ātmatā manifestation + other-form absence — that is its purpose. In para-tattva nothing is to be discarded. Dvaitādhivāsa is no separate thing: mere own-nature-manifestation — so removal is by concept. Paramārtha shines by the order thrown-off→expansion→expanded from svātantrya; shining is svarūpa — limbs not direct means. Sattarka alone direct even where tarka favors: it is śuddhavidyā impressed as yāga/homa/japa/vrata/yoga — all states' abode the Lord; external scaffolding for heart-nature. Homa: all states Lord-light; swallowing-taste in Lord-consciousness-fire; sudden dissolution to mere light-residue. Japa: inner contemplation of own-nature-tattva, disregarding outer/inner-knowable divisions. Vrata: equality-identification everywhere — even body and pot viewed as Lord. Nandiśikhā cites: all-equality the supreme vow. Yoga: specific concept tracking svarūpa until paramārtha shines concept-independent — self-liquidating instrument. Parameśvara denoted by āgama-names each anvartha-running from pūrṇasaṃvit: fullness his power, Kālī etc. — let it be in meditators' hearts. By seeing whole-power-nature, fullness-consciousness shines. His powers are countless — the world itself is the unteachable list. Why teach them? World = his powers. Śrīparā: bearer-seer-illuminator up to Śiva-limit as avikalpa-saṃvid — completes world in three. Śrīparāparā: difference-undifference like mirror-elephant. Śrīmadaparā: difference alone, mutually distinct selfhood. Śrīparā again: holds the threefold in self by embrace-tracking — named Śrīmat-mātṛ-sat-bhāva-kāla-karṣiṇī. Four powers from autonomy each triple: 4×3 in creation/preservation/dissolution = twelve. Four powers triple through creation, preservation, dissolution: twelve they become. The twelve-fold saṃvit-kalana runs the full constructive cycle — twelve Goddesses, wheel-revolving, cosmic and domestic scales, nourishing cakreśvara-svātantrya: Śrīkālī. Kalana's range: motion, throw, knowledge, counting, enjoyment-making, sound, self-dissolving. Bhūtirāja-teachers: from throw and knowledge Kālī, by subjection to construction. Distributed across my vivaraṇa treatises and hymns. House-rule: neither too-secret nor fully hidden — graduated disclosure. Yāga-homa and rest: in this kind the Great-Lord alone is to be thought. Fierce claim: heya/upādeya-seeing Viṣṇu-to-Siva is mithyā-darśana to be abandoned; practice as-it-becomes, not purity-fancy tormenting self; purity is command-relative; Siva's-command alone binds. From Vedic through Ucchuṣma scriptures: whatever restraint/injunction here akiṃcitkara — established. Likewise said in Śrīpūrva: spread — from Tantrāloka to be sought.

## FUNCTION

Establishes vikalpa as the conditioned upāya (sattarka-lineage), subordinates yoga-limbs to saṃvid-work, defines the five practices as sattarka's impress-forms (yāga→yoga), unfolds the four Śaktis ×3 = twelve, the twelve-fold saṃvit-kalana as Śrīkālī, and the radical codanā-argument that only Śiva's command binds.

## KEY TERMS

sattarka (true-reason as fitting scriptural concept-chain); dvaitādhivāsa-bhaṅga (concept's sole work); śaktipāta-viddha (firm-pierced, devī-dīkṣā); the five practices as sakti-forms (yāga/homa/japa/vrata/yoga); catuḥ-śakti (Śrīparā/parāparā/madaparā + kroḍī-holder); dvādaśa-bhagavatī (twelve kalana-Goddesses = Śrīkāli); codanā-vaśa (command-relativity of purity); akiṃcitkara-niyama (foreign-restraint nullity).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Āhnika 4 answers a03's doctrine with the vikalpa-question (v1-21), the practice-forms (v22-27), then the śakti/kalana-cosmology (v28-40) and the purity-controversy (v41-46); v11 defers to śaktipāta-prakāśana; v44-46 cross-link anubhava-stotra, Śrīpūrva, Tantrāloka.

## EXPLANATION

The ahnika argues in four moves: (1) vikalpa's conditions and limits — who gets sattarka, what it does (dvaita-bhanga only), why limbs are not direct means; (2) the five practices re-read as one sarvabhāva-arpaṇa to maheśvara, each an impress-form of sattarka-as-śuddhavidyā; (3) cosmology: four Śaktis by svātantrya triple to twelve, the twelve-fold kalana of saṃvid (the Śrīkālī-text) with its seven-operation semantics; (4) the purity-argument: heya/upādeya-seeing is mithyā, purity is codanā-relative, foreign niyamas are akiṃcitkara here. Authority: Pārameśvara, Nandiśikhā, Bhūtirāja, own vivaraṇa, Śrīpūrva, Tantrāloka.

## BOUNDARY

Sattarka's two-track (spontaneous vs sequential) left unexpanded (deferred); kalana's twelve-fold steps asserted without each-step exegesis; codanā-hierarchy argued but 'we will say' (v44) defers the full scriptural proof; v46 leaves the Tantrāloka-search open.

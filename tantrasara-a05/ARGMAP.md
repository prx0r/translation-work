# Tantrasāra A05 — ARGMAP

## tsa05-p01 (5.1–5.19)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (āṇava upāya: buddhi/prāṇa/deha/bāhya).
- question: When thought needs outer means, what are they — and how is dhyāna performed?
- premises: (1) Self-refining thought → śākta knowledge (decided last ahnika); means-needing thought → limited means → atomhood → āṇava knowledge. Means: buddhi (dhyāna), prāṇa (gross=5 functions=uccāra; subtle=varṇa, to come), deha (arrangement=karaṇa), bāhya (jar/altar/liṅga worship, to come). (2) Dhyāna rite: self-shining all-indwelling supreme visualized in heart-awareness; knower-means-known as fire-sun-moon collision → Bhairava-fire wind-kindled → 12 flames → sense-door exit → rest in outer graspable → moon-fill (creation) → sun-shine (maintenance) → fire-dissolution → unsurpassed. Wheel = all-outer-undivided; trace-smells likewise. Unceasing: own-consciousness-only ultimates + creation-freedom ultimacy → instant Bhairava-becoming; practice → all-desired accomplishments. (3) Summary verses: self-shining measurer-triad inward in heart-bliss abode; 12-ray wheel-lord pervading issuing through voids making creation; swallowing outer-inner circle, resting yogin contemplates = self-manifestation. (4) Uccāra: heart-void rest → outer breath-rise → apāna-moon-filling sees all-selfhood → desireless → samāna collision-rest → udāna-fire swallows measurer-measured → vyāna calm flashes limit-free. (5) Six bliss-grounds (nija/nir/paran/brahma/mahā/cid-ānanda) from void to vyāna; one relinker = jagadānanda (riseless-setless, inward-rest-ultimate).
- inference: Outer means are ladders into the same consciousness-only ultimacy; breath-arc maps bliss-grounds.
- conclusion: Dhyāna + uccāra courses stated.
- unresolved: Subtle varṇa, deha, bāhya details deferred (vakṣyate/kīrtayiṣyamāṇāḥ).

## tsa05-p02 (5.20–5.43)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (utterance grounds, states, liṅga, phoneme mystery).
- question: What happens resting in utterance-grounds, and what is the phoneme mystery?
- premises: (1) Resting in grounds → other-than-body-breath rest-reality; reflecting creation-dissolution-seed-utterance mystery refines thought. (2) Five states per rest by entry-gradation: bliss (fullness-touch) → surging (bodiless fixation) → tremor (power-seizure, body-loosening) → sleep (outwardness-dissolution) → whirling (self=all → nonself dissolves; great pervasion). (3) Grounds = waking-to-beyond-fourth; located at triangle-bulb-heart-palate-upper-kuṇḍalinī entry. (4) Liṅga-triad (to be taught in place): supreme throb with no remainder-knowable + dawning knowable + dawned knowable; supreme liṅga = Yoginī-heart; chief throb-nature via contraction-expansion couple-form, emission-digit-rest. (5) No-entry for non-light (verses: rest in self-awareness then object, fill measure, withdraw measurer-measured division swiftly, rest by pervasion = six means-grounds with void; breaths ending in vyāna, waking-world dissolved; practice-devoted rises soon in creation-dissolution-grasp-abode). (6) Phoneme mystery: in utterance flashes indistinct-imitating sound = phoneme; creation-dissolution-seed its chief form; practice → supreme-consciousness-gain (ka-first/ma-last/sa-check/anacka inwardly uttered/remembered = consciousness-throb-touch, convention-free, complete; convention-bound words too, like wish-fulfillments, via unsurpassed-touch; heart-throat-lips unified, both twelve-ends + heart unified). Some: inner-touch-grasping-born white-yellow colors manifesting make awareness experienced. Verses: without speakable, from consciousness-throb, by moon-sun-course-blocking; whose from balanced-entry, in full consciousness-seed-mass-phoneme-course.
- inference: Utterance-grounds yield graded states ending in pervasion; phoneme is throb made audible.
- conclusion: Uccāra + varṇa-vidhi stated; karaṇa deferred to mudrā chapter.
- unresolved: Liṅga-triad full sense deferred (svāvasare vakṣyāmaḥ); some-views (kecit) cited without adjudication.

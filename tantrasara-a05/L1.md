# Tantrasāra A05 — L1 controlled

## tsa05-p01
Therein, when thought by itself is able to make refinement in itself with no need of other means at all, then it, fallen from bonded functioning, by pure-knowledge grace, having attained Lord-power-nature, resorted to as means, manifests śākta knowledge. (5.1) And that is decided in the very next ahnika. (5.2) But when thought needs another means for its own refinement, then, taking as means limited-formed intellect, breath, body, pot and the rest, having attained atomhood, it manifests atomic knowledge. Therein, intellect is visualization-natured; breath is gross and subtle; the first is utterance-natured; and utterance means the five breath-and-rest functions; but the subtle, worded by letter-sound, will be taught; body is particular-arrangement-natured, worded by sense; pots and the rest are outer — to be glorified as jar-altar-liṅga-worship-and-rest means. (5.3) Therein, visualization, for now the fitting, we shall teach: that self-shining all-principle-indwelling supreme reality stated — that itself, having visualized in own-heart-awareness, therein one should visualize the collision of the knower-means-known-formed fire-sun-moon-triad — until that great Bhairava-fire, with form kindled by visualization-wind, results. Of it, surrounded by twelve former power-flames, wheel-natured, issued through any eye-and-rest hole, rested outside in graspable self, one should contemplate. And through that rested: first that outer, as moon-natured, in creation-course, filled; then as sun-natured, by maintenance, shone; then too as dissolution-fire-natured, dissolved; then brought to unsurpassed-selfhood, one should visualize. (5.4) Thus that wheel, with all-outer-things-undivided-full, results. (5.5) Then even trace-smelling things, thus made by that wheel, one should visualize. (5.6) Thus of him unceasingly visualizing — ascertaining own-consciousness-only-ultimates, creation-maintenance-dissolution-continuities, and creation-freedom-ultimacy of own consciousness — instantly is Bhairava-becoming. (5.7) Through practice, all-desired-accomplishments and the rest too. (5.8) "Self-shining, all-self-principle, the measurer-and-rest triad, having made inward, abiding, one should visualize in heart-bliss-abode. (5.9–5.10) That twelve-great-power-ray-wheel-lord, all-pervading, issuing through voids, outside, one should visualize, making creation and the rest. (5.11–5.12) That with-swallowed-all-outer-inner-thing-circle, in self, resting, the yogin should contemplate — thus would be the self's manifestation. (5.13–5.14)" Thus the summary verses. (5.15) Thus visualization. (5.16) Therein, for one wanting to utter breath: first in heart itself, in void, one rests; then outside, from breath-rise; thence toward outer, by down-breath-moon-filling, one sees all-selfhood; then desireless-of-other one becomes; then from even-breath-rise one experiences collision-rest; then at up-breath-fire-rise one swallows measurer-measured-grasping. (5.17) When that swallowing-fire calms, at pervading-rise, free of all limitation, one flashes. (5.18) Thus from void onward to pervading-end — whatever rests — they alone are self-bliss, blissless, supreme-bliss, brahma-bliss, great-bliss, consciousness-bliss — thus the six bliss-grounds taught; of which one relinker — riseless-setless, inward-rest-ultimate-natured — world-bliss. (5.19)

## tsa05-p02
Then in these utterance-grounds, in each, resting by twos-and-more or wholly, one attains rest-reality other than body-breath-and-the-rest-distinct. (5.20) Reflecting on that itself, the creation-dissolution-seed-utterance-mystery, one should refine thought. And in these rests, in each, there are five states, through entry-gradation. (5.21) Therein: first bliss, from fullness-portion-touch; then surging, from momentary fixation in bodilessness; then tremor, in self-power-seizure, through body-identity-loosening; then sleep, from outwardness-dissolution. (5.22) Thus, when self-sense in nonself dissolves — through own self's all-madeness, in self nonself-sense dissolves — hence whirling, from great-pervasion-rise. (5.23) They are the grounds waking-and-the-rest ending in beyond-fourth. (5.24) And these grounds occur at triangle-bulb-heart-palate-upper-kuṇḍalinī-wheel-entry. (5.25) Thus in utterance-rest, what supreme throb with melted remainderless knowable, what dawning knowable, what dawned knowable — that itself, we shall teach in its place, is the liṅga-triad. (5.26) And here the supreme liṅga is Yoginī-heart. (5.27) Therein the chief throb-nature, through contraction-expansion-nature, through couple-form-rise, from emission-digit-rest-gain — enough. (5.28) "No-entry for non-light. (5.29) First resting in own awareness, then in the knowable, one should fill the measure. (5.30) When full, resting therein, the measurer-measured division one should withdraw swiftly. (5.31) Then resting by pervasion, these would be, with void, the six means-grounds. (5.32) Breaths ending in pervading, and dissolved therein waking-and-restworld. (5.33) But devoted to practice here, in creation-dissolution-grasp-abode, one climbs before long. (5.34)" Thus the inner verses. (5.35) Thus uttering. (5.36) In this very utterance, flashing, indistinct-imitating-like sound is phoneme. Its chief form is in creation-dissolution-seed; through its practice, supreme-consciousness-gain. As: whether ka-first or ma-last, sa-checked or anacka, inwardly uttered or remembered, distinct consciousness-throb-touch, convention-needing-not, complete; and convention-needing words too, makers of their content-things like wish-fulfillments, through unsurpassed-consciousness-touch — unifying heart-throat-lips, both twelve-ends and heart unifying — thus the phoneme-mystery. (5.37) "The white-yellow-and-rest inner color, born right after inner-touch-grasping, being manifested, makes awareness experienced — say some. (5.38) Through absence of speakable, from consciousness-throb, by moon-sun-course-blocking. (5.39) But whose, from balanced entry, in the full consciousness-seed-mass-phoneme-course..." (5.40) Thus the inner verse. (5.41) Thus phoneme-course. (5.42) Instrument, however, in manifesting seals, we shall teach. (5.43)

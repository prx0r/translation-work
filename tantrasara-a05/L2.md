# Tantrasāra A05 — L2 READ

## tsa05-p01 — the atom's toolkit, and how to see fire
When thought can refine itself with no outside help, it has slipped the bondsman's work; graced by bright knowing and wearing the Lord's own power, taken up as a means, it delivers the knowledge of power. (That verdict belongs to the next chapter.) But when thought still needs props, it grabs limited ones — mind, breath, body, pots — and goes atomic: small knower, small knowledge. Mind means seeing; breath is coarse breath-work, and a subtle sound — phoneme (more later); body means posture, called "sense"; pots and altars outside (their worship later). The seeing, for now: take the self-shining supreme, home to every principle, and see it in your heart's knowing. Then see the crash of knower, knowing, known as fire, sun, moon — until great Bhairava-fire stands up, fanned by seeing's wind. Ringed in twelve old power-flames, wheeling, out through any sense-door, settled on something graspable outside — hold it there. Then with that settled fire: first fill the outer world as moon, creating; then light it as sun, keeping; then burn it as dissolution-fire; then bring it to the Unsurpassed. The wheel now holds the outer world whole and undivided — and smoke-smelling leftovers too. Who sees without stopping — certain that his own awareness alone is ultimate, certain its creations, keeps, and ends are freedom's ultimates — is Bhairava on the spot. Practice adds the wish-fulfilling extras. (In brief, the charter verses: the shining triad of measurer and the rest, drawn inside, seen in heart's bliss-home; the twelve-rayed wheel-lord, everywhere, pouring through voids, making worlds outside; the yogin who swallows outer and inner circles and rests in himself — that is the self showing.) Then the breath-work for one about to sound breath: rest first in the heart's void; then ride breath outward; then with the down-breath's moon filling the outer, see everything as yourself; want nothing else; from the even breath's rise, feel the crash-rest; at the up-breath's fire-rise, swallow measurer-and-measured both; when that fire dies, at the all-breath's rise, flash with no edges left. Those rests from void to all-breath are the six bliss-grounds taught — own-bliss, blissless, beyond-bliss, god-bliss, great-bliss, awareness-bliss — with one string running through them, never rising or setting, rest inwardly ultimate: world-bliss.

## tsa05-p02 — grounds, throbs, and the mystery of sound
Rest in these sounding-grounds — a couple at a time or all — and you land somewhere body and breath aren't: rest itself, as a reality. Chew the mystery of creation's and dissolution's seed-sounds and let it refine your thought. Each rest has five stations, deeper as you enter: bliss, from touching fullness; a surge, from holding bodiless a moment; trembling, as your own power grabs and the body's grip loosens; sleep, as outwardness melts; then the whirl — the "I" melting into not-I, and, since self is everything, not-I melting back into self as sheer expanse. Those stations run from waking to past the fourth, and they sit where the triangle, bulb, heart, palate, and upper serpent-wheel are entered. In sounding-rest, three throbs remain: the supreme throb past every knowable, the knowable dawning, the knowable dawned — the triple mark, full sense in its own place. Here the supreme mark is the Yoginī's heart: throb first and foremost, tightening and loosening, paired, come to rest in emission's digit. Enough. (The house verses: no way in for unlight; rest in self-knowing first, then the knowable, fill the measure; full, withdraw measurer-measured at once; rest by pervasion and there are the six means-grounds with the void; breaths end in the all-breath, waking's world dissolved with them; but the practised climber soon stands where creation and ending are grasped.) Uttering itself: the flicker in it, half-formed echo, is the phoneme. Its stronghold is creation's and dissolution's seed; work it and supreme awareness is got. Whether ka-first or ma-last, checked or bare sa, sounded inside or remembered — the touch of throbbing awareness is distinct, needs no convention, and is whole. Even convention-bound words, which make their things the way daydreams do, work — through touch of the Unsurpassed — when heart, throat, and lips are one and both twelve-ends join the heart. That is the phoneme's secret. Some add: the white, the yellow, and inward colors born just after inner touch-and-grasp, displayed, let you taste awareness. (More verses on moon-sun blocking, and on balanced entry into the full seed-mass of conscious sound.) The instrument — seals and the rest — belongs to the chapter of showing forth.

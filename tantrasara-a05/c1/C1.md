# C1 — Tantrasāra Ahnika 5 (āṇava)

## tsa05-p01 — the atom's toolkit, and how to see fire
**SUMMARY.** When thought can refine itself with no outside help, it has slipped the bondsman's work and manifests śākta knowledge (decided in A04). But when thought still needs props, it grabs limited ones — mind, breath, body, pots — and goes atomic: small knower, small knowledge. Mind means seeing; breath is coarse breath-work plus a subtle sound (phoneme, later); body means posture (sense); pots and altars outside (their worship later). The seeing: take the self-shining supreme, home to every principle, and see it in your heart's knowing; then see the crash of knower, knowing, known as fire, sun, moon — until great Bhairava-fire stands up, fanned by seeing's wind; ringed in twelve old power-flames, wheeling, out through any sense-door, settled on something graspable outside — hold it there. Then with that settled fire: first fill the outer world as moon (creating); then light it as sun (keeping); then burn it as dissolution-fire; then bring it to the Unsurpassed. The wheel now holds the outer world whole and undivided. Who sees without stopping — certain that his own awareness alone is ultimate, certain its creations, keeps, and ends are freedom's ultimates — is Bhairava on the spot. Practice adds the wish-fulfilling extras. (Charter verses: the shining triad of measurer drawn inside, seen in heart's bliss-home; the twelve-rayed wheel-lord pouring through voids making worlds; the yogin who swallows outer and inner circles and rests in himself — that is the self showing.) Then the breath-work: rest in the heart's void; ride breath outward; with the down-breath's moon filling the outer, see everything as yourself; want nothing else; from the even breath's rise, feel the crash-rest; at the up-breath's fire-rise, swallow measurer-and-measured; when that fire dies, at the all-breath's rise, flash with no edges. Those rests from void to all-breath are the six bliss-grounds (own-bliss, blissless, beyond-bliss, god-bliss, great-bliss, awareness-bliss) with one relinker — riseless, setless, rest inwardly ultimate: world-bliss.
**FUNCTION.** Establishes the āṇava upāya (mind/breath/body/outer means) and the dhyāna/uccāra courses with the six bliss-grounds.
**KEY TERMS.** āṇava (atomic), dhyāna (visualization), uccāra (utterance/arousal), varṇa (phoneme), bliss-grounds (six), jagadānanda (world-bliss, relinker), prāṇa (breath, five functions).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** A04 (śākta) precedes; A05 teaches the atom's toolkit; A06 (sthāna/kāla) follows.
**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** Subtle varṇa, deha, bāhya details deferred (vakṣyate/kīrtayiṣyamāṇāḥ). Liṅga-triad full sense deferred (svāvasare).
**RELATED.** A04 (śākta), A06 (sthāna/kāla), essay0 Q4, spans TS5.1–5.19.

## tsa05-p02 — utterance grounds, five states, and the phoneme mystery
**SUMMARY.** Resting in utterance-grounds yields a reality other than body-breath; reflecting the creation-dissolution-seed-utterance mystery refines thought. Each rest has five states by entry-gradation: bliss (fullness-touch), surging (bodiless fixation), tremor (power-seizure, body-loosening), sleep (outwardness-dissolution), whirling (self=all → nonself dissolves, sheer expanse). Grounds sit at triangle-bulb-heart-palate-kuṇḍalinī. Liṅga-triad (supreme/dawning/dawned) deferred to its place; supreme liṅga = Yoginī-heart; throb-nature via contraction-expansion. House verses: no-entry-for-unlight; rest in self-knowing then knowable, fill measure; full, withdraw measurer-measured; rest by pervasion = six means-grounds with void; breaths end in all-breath, waking-world dissolved; practised climber soon stands where creation-ending grasped. Uttering itself: the flicker, half-formed echo, is the phoneme. Its stronghold = creation/dissolution seed; work it, supreme awareness got. Whether ka-first or ma-last, checked or bare sa, sounded inside or remembered — distinct throb-touch, convention-free, whole. Even convention-bound words make their things (like daydreams) through unsurpassed-touch, unifying heart-throat-lips and both twelve-ends. That is the phoneme's secret. Some add: white/yellow/inner colors born just after inner touch-and-grasp, displayed, let you taste awareness (uncjudicated). Verses on moon-sun blocking and balanced entry into full seed-mass of conscious sound. Instrument (seals) belongs to the showing-forth chapter.
**FUNCTION.** Completes the āṇava upāya: utterance-grounds, five states, liṅga-triad (deferred), phoneme mystery.
**KEY TERMS.** uccāra (utterance/arousal), varṇa (phoneme), liṅgatraya (liṅga-triad), Yoginī-hṛdaya, anusaṃdhātā (relinker), kecit (some say — color view).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** P1 gave dhyāna/uccāra/six grounds; P2 completes grounds/states/phoneme.
**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** Liṅga-triad full sense deferred (svāvasare vakṣyāmaḥ). kecit color-view uncjudicated. Instrument (mudrā) deferred.
**RELATED.** A04 (śākta), A06 (sthāna/kāla), spans TS5.20–5.43.

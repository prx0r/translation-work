# Tantrasāra A06 — L1 controlled

## tsa06-p01
It is stated by the word station-placing. Therein station is threefold: breath-wind, body, and outer. Therein in breath, for now, the whole course to be stated: the course, breath-located, is imagined. Of it, successive-nonsuccessive-grasping alone is time; and it is the Lord himself shining within. And its shining of God is power named Time; but differenced, that shining of succession-nonsuccession is breath-function. (6.1) For consciousness itself, taking divided form from knowables — hence therefore, through limitation-yoga, going to knowableness, is void. Then through autonomy, falling with eagerness to appropriate in the knowable, action-power-chief, breathing-natured, life-natured, since filling body by five forms, hence it shines as if conscious. (6.2) Therein in action-power is the time-course in the eastern part; but in the northern is the place-course of body-variety-nature. Therein of letter-mantra-word-courses the standing is in the time-course, through gross-subtle-supreme-nature. (6.3) But of place-course standing, as principle-person-śakti-natured — so it will be in its place. (6.4) Therein, though in body breath is warp-woof-formed with outer-inner, nevertheless it whose effort is clearly felt is from heart onward — hence thence itself it is to be expounded. (6.5) Therein lord-power, self-power, effort — the triad is the cause in breath-impelling, through quality-chief-nature. (6.6) Therein from heart to twelve-end-end, by own fingers, of all is thirty-six fingers of breath-course, in exit and in entry, through own-fitting-power-effort-body-nature of all. (6.7) Therein water-hour, lunar day, month, and year, year-mass-natured — thus entire time is completed. Therein in one-and-quarter finger is a drop-moment: thus standing is water-hour-rise; for the water-hour is by sixty drops; hence it is seventy-two fingers. (6.8) Now lunar-day-rise. (6.9) Two fingers plus quarter is called instant; in four of them is a watch; half-instant-half therein is junction itself; thus in exit is day, in entry night — thus is lunar-day-rise. (6.10) Now month-rise. (6.11) Therein day is dark fortnight, night is bright; therein the former half-instant and latter half-instant are rest, untimed; the middle fifteen instants alone are lunar days; therein manifestation and rest — these alone are day-nights. (6.12) Therein knowableness-madeness-manifestation is day; of knowable, dissolution in the considerer is night; and they, manifestation-and-rest, through long-short-variety are of endless division; in their equality is equinox. (6.13) Therein in dark fortnight the breath-sun offers to the down-breath-moon one by one a digit — until at fifteenth instant the moon, with wasted separate-spread digits, near twelve-end dissolves in breath-sun itself. (6.14) Right after that, whatever half-instant is fortnight-joint. (6.15) Of it, of the half-instant, the former half is new-moon, the second is first-day. (6.16) Therein on first day, in that part, when that new-moon part, through sneeze-effort-attention-made day-division, enters — then therein is seizing. Therein, with knowable-formed-moon, the Māyā-knower-Rāhu, by nature unable to dissolve, merely covering-capable, drinks the sun-dwelling lunar nectar — hence. (6.17)

## tsa06-p02
Through non-division-making of knower-means-known-triad, it is holy time, otherworld-fruit-giving. (6.18) Then entering in breath, consciousness-sun fills down-breath-moon digit by digit — until fifteenth instant is full moon; right after that fortnight-joint and seizing as before; but that is this-world-fruit-giving — thus is month-rise. (6.19) Now year-rise. (6.20) Therein in dark fortnight itself is northern course: in six-six fingers is transit, from Capricorn to Gemini-end. (6.21) Therein per finger are five lunar days; there too day-night-division; thus in entry is southern course: embryo-ness, rise-wish, about-to-rise-ness, futurity, rise-beginning, risen-ness, and birth-and-rest six changes — thus in order in Capricorn and the rest. (6.22) Likewise worship here makes fitting fruit. (6.23) Here too Dakṣa-to-Grandfather Rudras and powers, twelve lords — thus is year-rise. (6.24) Per finger are sixty lunar days — thus in order: at transit is a year. By this course of exit-entry is twelve-year rise; per finger three hundred lunar days; in one-and-quarter finger is a year — where before the drop was stated. By counting, at transit five years. By this series, in one breath-exit-entry-time is sixty-year rise; here twenty-one thousand six hundred is the tithi number. (6.25) So many alone in day-night is breath-number — hence beyond sixty-year rise is not examined, from infinity. (6.26) Therein human year is gods' day; by this course twelve thousand divine years is an eon. (6.27) Four, three, two, one — from kṛta onward, by so many hundreds are eight joints. (6.28) By seventy-one eons is a Manu-age; by fourteen Manu-ages is Brahmā's day. At Brahmā-day-end, in the fire-burnt triple world and in the other smoke-veiled triple world, all folk, fire-driven swiftly, in Jana world becoming dissolute abide. (6.29) But the awake — Kūṣmāṇḍa, Hāṭakeśa and the rest — sport in the Great world. (6.30) Then at night's completion is Brahmī creation. (6.31) By this measure a hundred years is Brahmā's life. (6.32) That is Viṣṇu's day and so long his night; his too a hundred is life. (6.33) That day, above it, of Rudra-world-lord Rudra is so long night; as before, a year, and that hundred too is the limit. (6.34) Therein at Rudra's end is going to Śiva-hood; Rudra's stated-tenure-limit; of universe-bearers that day is a hundred Rudras' night, so long; theirs too a hundred is life. (6.35) At hundred-Rudra-expiry is universe-destruction. (6.36) Thus from water-principle to unmanifest-end, this same in course is Rudras' life. (6.37) The prior's life is the latter's day. (6.38) Thence Brahmā and Rudras, non-transgressing officers, abide in the unmanifest. (6.39) And Śrīkaṇṭha then is the withdrawer. (6.40) This is interim dissolution; at its expiry, creation. (6.41) Therein other-śāstra-taught too are created. (6.42) But what is Śrīkaṇṭha's own life, that is kañcuka-Rudras' day, so long night; what is their life, that is Gahaneśa's day, so long night; and therein everything dissolves in Māyā. (6.43)"

## tsa06-p04
"In udāna the course to twelve-end is of throb-only time; here too the course as before. (6.71) But in pervading, through all-pervadingness, though sequenceless, by subtle upsurge-yoga is time-rise. (6.72) Now letter-rise. Therein every half-watch is class-rise; at equinox even: of letter upon letter, two hundred sixteen breaths outside and thirty-six drops — thus the rise; this effortless letter-rise. (6.73–6.74) But effort-born is mantra-rise: like Persian-wheel-machine-carrying, by single-link power, marvelous, mantra-rise day-night reflecting, one reaches identity with mantra-god. (6.75) Therein, by ever-risen breath-course-number itself is rise-number explained; doubled, halved and so on in course: in hundred-eight-wheel two hundred rises — thus in course, in gross-subtle course-nature-rested, with thinned breath-course, when time-grasp is done, complete one consciousness, marvel-power-full, shines. (6.76) Time-difference alone divides consciousness, not knowable-difference — like peak-standing-knowledge; of knowledge, as long as its abiding-time, that alone is instant; and in one breath-rise necessarily one knowledge alone — else there would be no sequential word-tainted thought-unit at all, since mora too is sequential — as it was said: 'of it, from ta, high, half-short.' (6.77–6.78) Hence until another throb rises, there is one knowledge alone. (6.79) Hence therefore at eighty-one-word-recall-time, through varied-nature-entry, that one Lord-directed thought, in time-grasp, becomes thought-free itself. (6.80) Thus seeing the entire time-course in breath-rise itself, and the varied countless creations-dissolutions therein, reckoning there, recognizing own Lordhood itself, one is freed indeed. (6.81–6.82)"

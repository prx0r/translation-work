# Tantrasāra A06 — L2 READ

## tsa06-p01 — where time lives
"Station" is the word: three of them — breath, body, outside. The whole course sits in breath, and time just is grasping in sequence and out of it — the Lord shining inside, whose shining is goddess Time; differenced, it's breath doing its work. How breath starts: awareness takes a divided shape from knowables and goes void with limitation; then, free, it drops into some knowable wanting to own it — mostly action — breathes, lives, fills the body five ways, and looks conscious. Time's course runs east in action-power; place's runs north in body's variety (letters, mantras, words stand in time's course, gross to supreme; place's course — principles, persons, powers — in its own place later). Breath weaves in and out of the body, but the felt push starts at the heart — so start there. Three drive it: lord-power, self-power, effort, chief by turns. Measure: heart to twelve-end, thirty-six fingers out and back. Water-hour, day, month, year, year-masses — time complete. A finger and a quarter is a drop; sixty drops an hour of seventy-two fingers. Two fingers and a quarter, an instant; four instants, a watch; half-instants the joints — breathing out is day, breathing in is night: that's the lunar day. The month: out-breath's fortnight is "day," in-breath's is "night"; the joints at the ends rest outside time; fifteen instants between are the days — showing is day, resting is night. Showing-of-the-known is day; its melting into the knower is night; long and short run endless, equal is equinox. In the dark half the breath-sun feeds the down-breath moon digit by digit till, at the fifteenth, the thinned moon melts near the twelve-end into the sun. Next half-instant: the joint — new moon's half, first day's half. When a sneeze-cut lets the new-moon slice into the first day, that's eclipse: Māyā's knower-Rāhu can't unmake anything, only cover — so he drinks the moon's nectar where it sits in the sun.

## tsa06-p02 — up the ladder of ages
Why eclipses count: knower, knowing, known undivided — holy time, fruit beyond. Run the month backward — breath filling moon to full — and the fruit is here-and-now. The year: dark half is the northward run, a transit every six fingers from Capricorn to Gemini, five days a finger with days and nights; the inward run is southward — conception, will-to-rise, almost-risen, to-come, starting, risen, birth and five more changes, sign by sign. Worship tunes the fruit. Twelve lords — Rudras Dakṣa to Grandfather, and powers — own the year. Sixty days a finger makes a year a transit; out-and-in makes twelve years; three hundred a finger; a year and a quarter-finger (drops, as before); five years a transit by the count; one full out-and-in breath, sixty years — 21,600 day-beats, the breaths of a day and night. Past sixty years don't ask: it's endless. A human year is a gods' day; twelve thousand of those, an eon (four-three-two-one, eight joints); seventy-one eons a Manu-age; fourteen, Brahmā's day. At his day's end the triple world burns, the other smokes, beings herd dazed into the world of Jana and wait out dissolution — while the awake, Kūṣmāṇḍa and the lords of gold, play on in the Great world. Night ends, Brahmā creates. A hundred such years is his life — a day of Viṣṇu, and his night as long, his life a hundred too; that, a day above for Rudra lord of Rudra's world, his night as long, his bound a hundred too. At Rudra's end, Śiva-hood; his tenure stated. The bearers' day is a hundred Rudras' night, and as long; their life a hundred; at a hundred Rudras gone, the egg breaks. So from water to unmanifest for the Rudras, each life the next one's day. Brahmā and the Rudras, holding office clean, sit out in the unmanifest — and Śrīkaṇṭha draws it all in. That's the lesser ending; then creating again (other books' gods too).

## tsa06-p03 — past Māyā, and the other breaths (CONDENSED companion to L1)
Gahaneśa rebuilds. Multiply unmanifest time by ten vastnesses: a day of Māyā, and as long a night — that is dissolution. Multiply Māyā-time by a hundred: a day of the Lord — breath builds the world, night stills the breath, and even in the stillness the order holds, since awareness keeps its sequence. Multiply again by a hundred: Sadāśiva's day and night — the great ending, where he climbs past point, half-moon, and blockage into sound, into power, into the pervader, into the Unsupported. Multiply power-time by crores of vastnesses: the Unsupported's day; and the Unsupported, stationed past mind, is poise — is Brahman. Out of that poise, timeless, spins the whole wheel of time spreading and melting: one, ten, a hundred, a thousand, up eighteen ranks of ten — that is counting. Creations and dissolutions past number fit in one great breath; breath sits in awareness, awareness in its condition, condition in bare awareness — and that throb is what "time rising" means. So dreams can differ without breaking anything. Apāna keeps the same time from heart to root-seat, with its six causes and its lords of childhood to freedom. Samāna runs ten heart-channels feeding the body's saps, aping the eight direction-lords, climbing three channels up and down — dawn measures and nine-hundred counts all accounted, twelve transits by day and twelve by night, growing with the days; one samāna-breath is two years, no in-and-out needed.

## tsa06-p04 — letters, mantra, and what seeing time does
Udāna runs to the twelve-end on throbbing time alone; the all-breath, everywhere, rises time by subtle upsurge without sequence. Letters rise every half-watch by classes — at equinox evenly: 216 breaths a letter outside, 36 drops — effortless letter-rise. Mantra-rise takes work: like a water-wheel's chain, one link pulling, it turns day and night reflecting, wonderfully, till you are the mantra's god. The ever-risen breath-count is the rise-count — doubled, halved, and so on: two hundred rises in the 108-wheel; rest in gross-and-subtle course-nature, thin the breath-course, finish grasping time, and one whole awareness shines, full of marvel-power. Only time's difference divides awareness — never the thing's (like knowledge on a peak: a knowing lasts exactly its abiding, and that is an instant). One breath-rise, one knowing, necessarily — else no word-wearing thought-whole could sequence at all, mora being sequential too ("of it: high from ta, half-short"). Till the next throb, one knowing stands. So at eighty-one words recalled, through every kind of entry, the one Lord-aimed thought turns thought-free itself inside time's grasp. Seeing the whole time-course in breath's rise, with its countless makings and unmakings reckoned there, knowing your own Lordhood — you are free. Indeed.

## FUNCTION

Establishes sthāna-kalana (seat-fixing): three seats with breath first, the whole course reckoned breath-resting, time as sequence-non-sequence counting shining within the Lord as Kālī — then the full time-ladder (ghaṭikā/tithi/māsa/varṣa, tuṭi arithmetic, pakṣa-sandhi and grahaṇa, nakṣatra-yogas, twelve to sixty-year scales, 21,600 breaths) ascending through divine ages, manu-periods, Brahmā-to-Gahaneśa lifespans, pralaya tiers to anāśrita-sāmanasya-brahman — closing with apāna/samāna/udāna/vyāna courses, varṇodaya/mantrodaya, the kāla-grāsa doctrine (one breath one knowing), and recognition-liberation in bhairavībhāva.

## KEY TERMS

sthānaprakalpana (seat-fixing: prāṇa/śarīra/bāhya); kramākramakalana (sequence-non-sequence counting = time); Kālī (God's shining power); tuṭi (2¼ fingers; prahara, sandhyā); pakṣasaṃdhi (fortnight-joint); grahaṇa (seizure: Māyā-knower-Rāhu); nakṣatra-graha-yoga (conjunctions); parārdha (half-highest multiplier); pralayākala (dissolution-state); anāśrita/sāmanasya (Unsupported/likeness); spanda (pulse); varṇodaya/mantrodaya (letter/mantra rise; water-wheel simile); kālagrāsa (time-devouring); kṣaṇa (moment = knowing's abiding-time); pratyabhijñā (recognition); bhairavībhāva (Bhairavī-being).

## EXPLANATION

The ahnika runs: (1) charter — seat-fixing word, three seats, breath-first method, course reckoned breath-resting, time as counting, Kālī within, breath-movement without; (2) descent — consciousness apart-formed, bounded void, eager falling, breathing body seeming conscious; (3) placement — time-course in power, place-course varied, letter-courses standing, place deferred; (4) measure — heart-start, triad drivers, 36 fingers, whole time completed, caṣaka rule; (5) tithi — tuṭi, watch, twilight, day-night breathing, month joints, fortnight halves, knowable-light doctrine, waxing-filling, joint, seizure with Rāhu, holiness, waning with this-world fruit; (6) year — rise, northern passages, southern gestation series, worship fruit, twelve lords, sixty-day and twelve/sixty-year scales, 21,600 count, inquiry limit; (7) cosmic ages — divine years, twilights, manu-periods, Brahmā-day fire-stance, awakened players, night-end emission, Brahmā–Viṣṇu–Rudra lifespans, hundred-Rudra scale, world-egg destruction, ladder rule, unmanifest officers, Śrīkaṇṭha withdrawal, interval, other scriptures; (8) higher days — Śrīkaṇṭha-to-Gahaneśa ladder, Māyā-dissolution, re-emission, ten-multiplied Māyā-day, Īśvara-day, breath-quiet sequence, Sadāśiva number with great dissolution, drop-blocker ascent to Unsupported, ten-million Unsupported-day, likeness-sameness Brahman, winking wheel-whirl; (9) number names and pulse — eighteen names, countless in one breath, dream-consistency, apāna sameness, six-and-six causes, samāna channels and equinox passages, udāna/vyāna limits, letter-rise effortless, mantra-rise water-wheel with deity-identity; (10) grāsa and close — breath-count rise-numbers, thinning and devouring with complete awareness, moment doctrine, measure-fragment, between-pulse oneness, eighty-one memories devoured, seeing-noting-recognizing freedom, Bhairavī-being.

# Tantrasāra A07 — ARGMAP

## tsa07-p01 (7.1–7.28)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (place-course + 36 principles).
- question: What is the place-course, and in what magnitudes do its principles stand?
- premises: (1) Place-course = form-variety-manifesting-power, resting in consciousness; through it the whole course completes in void/intellect/breath/channels/body/liṅga-altar-image. Dissolve course→body→breath→mind→void → brimming consciousness; knowing 36 natures, seeing beyond-consciousness as supreme Śiva yet universe-made (else grasping some knowable or Māyā-officer/Viṣṇu as other) → process-knowledge mandatory ("no knowledge beyond process"). (2) Magnitude ladder: earth (100-crore spread, egg-globe; inside: fire/hells/underworlds/earth/heaven→Brahmā-world; outside 100 Rudras; eggs countless) → water 10× → … → ego (with 5+5+11) → intellect 100× → nature 1000× (= nature-egg, countless) → person 10,000× → destiny 100,000× → art (destiny/passion/impure-knowledge/time/art, 10-lakh steps) → Māyā crore (= Māyā-egg) → pure knowledge 10-crore → Lord 100-crore → High-tongued 1000-crore → power crowd-multiplied (= power-egg, pervader since course abides inside-out). (3) Layering: latter pervades former as coverings; to power-end pervaded by Śiva; Śiva immeasurable, beyond+pervading all courses. (4) Inter-principle worlds whose lords stand on earth; dying in shrines they grant transits; impel upward by initiation-course.
- inference: Magnitude = pervasion-rank; dissolution reverses the ladder into beyond-consciousness.
- conclusion: Place-course + 36 magnitudes stated; process-knowledge enjoined.
- unresolved: Purāṇic interiors + shrine mechanics (external-knowledge).

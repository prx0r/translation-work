# Tantrasāra A07 — L1 controlled

## tsa07-p01
Therein, entire itself, born of form-variety-manifesting-power, is the place-course, resting in consciousness. Through its door, in void, in intellect, in breath, in channel-wheel-subwheels, outside, in body, down to liṅga-altar-image and the rest, the whole course is completed. Having dissolved that whole course in body, and body in breath, and that in mind, and that in void — in that consciousness, brimming-full consciousness results. Knowing thirty-six-principle-nature, seeing consciousness beyond that as supreme-Śiva-natured, one should sense it as universe-made too. Otherwise one would grasp some knowable portion alone as other — Māyā-womb-officer, or Viṣṇu-Brahmā-and-the-rest. Hence one must necessarily be devoted to process-knowledge. (7.1) As stated: "No knowledge beyond process." (7.2) Therein earth-principle, hundred-crore-spread, universe-egg-globe-formed. (7.3) Inside it: time-fire, hells, underworlds, earth, heaven, up to Brahmā-world. (7.4) Outside the universe-egg, a hundred of Rudras. (7.5) Nor is number of universe-eggs found. (7.6) Than earth-principle, tenfold water-principle. (7.7) Then successively tenfold up to ego. (7.8) Namely: water, fire, wind, void, five-subtle-five-sense-eleven-wombed ego. (7.9) Than ego, hundredfold intellect-principle. (7.10) Thence thousandfold nature-principle — so far nature-egg. (7.11) And it, like universe-egg, countless. (7.12) Than nature-principle, ten-thousandfold person-principle. (7.13) Than person, hundred-thousandfold destiny. (7.14) Than destiny, successively ten-lakh-fold up to art-principle. (7.15) Namely: destiny, passion, impure knowledge, time, and art. (7.16) Than art-principle, crore-fold Māyā — so far Māyā-egg. (7.17) Than Māyā-principle, ten-crore-multiplied pure knowledge. (7.18) Than knowledge-principle, hundred-crore-fold Lord-principle. (7.19) Than Lord-principle, thousand-crore-fold High-tongued. (7.20) Than High-tongued, crowd-multiplied power-principle — thus power-egg. (7.21) That power, pervading — since the universe-course inside-outside abides — hence pervader. (7.22) Thus these, successively as coverings proceeding, are principles; the latter is pervader, the former pervaded — thus situated they proceed. (7.23) Until the course to power-principle-end is pervaded by Śiva-principle. (7.24) Śiva-principle is immeasurable, beyond-all-courses and pervading-all-courses. (7.25) Such are worlds standing between principles — their lords alone here on earth stand. (7.26) In those shrines, who die, to them there and there movement they grant. (7.27) And in order up and up they impel, by initiation-course. (7.28)

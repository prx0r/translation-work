# Tantrasara-a07 — L2 READ

## v.1
Therein this whole — of-place-path-illumination-power-born, rested in consciousness: through its door, in void, mind, prāṇa, nerve-circles-within, outside in body up to liṅga-sthaṇḍila-image — the whole path is firmly fixed. Having melted that whole path into the body, the body into prāṇa, that into mind, that into void, in that consciousness — all-filled-full-consciousness is attained: knowing the thirty-six tattvas' own-form, seeing beyond-it consciousness Supreme-Siva-formed, even world-made consciousness would make-known — the beyond-portion-of-knowable just as some, taking-as-supreme the māyā-womb-qualified, Viṣṇu-Brahmā-and-rest. Therefore it must be by prakriyā-knowledge-endowed.
The Sanskrit: *tatra samasta eva ayaṃ mūrtivaicitryābhāsanaśaktijo deśādhvā saṃvidi viśrāntaḥ taddvāreṇa śūnye buddhau prāṇe nāḍīcakrānucakreṣu bahiḥ śarīre yāval liṅgasthaṇḍilapratimādau samasto 'dhvā pariniṣṭhitaḥ taṃ samastam adhvānaṃ dehe vilāpya dehaṃ ca prāṇe taṃ dhiyi tāṃ śūnye tatsaṃvedane nirbharaparipūrṇasaṃvit sampadyate ṣaṭtriṃśattattvasvarūpajñaḥ taduttīrṇāṃ saṃvidaṃ paramaśivarūpāṃ paśyan viśvamayīm api saṃvedayeta aparathā vedyabhāgam eva kaṃcit paratvena gṛhṇīyān māyāgarbhādhikāriṇaṃ viṣṇubrahmādikaṃ vā tasmād avaśyaṃ prakriyājñānapareṇa bhavitavyam*

## v.2
That is said: knowledge-endowed-with-prakriyā is not [mere] prakriyā.
The Sanskrit: *tad uktaṃ na prakriyāparaṃ jñānam iti*

## v.3
Therein the earth-tattva, hundred-crore-extended, in-form-of-Brahma-cosmic-egg.
The Sanskrit: *tatra pṛthivītattvaṃ śatakoṭipravistīrṇaṃ brahmāṇḍagolakarūpam*

## v.4
Within it: Kālāgni, hells, pātālas, earth, heaven up to Brahma-world — so.
The Sanskrit: *tasya antaḥ kālāgnir narakāḥ pātālāni pṛthivī svargo yāvad brahmaloka iti*

## v.5
Outside the cosmic-egg, a hundred of Rudras.
The Sanskrit: *brahmāṇḍabāhye rudrāṇāṃ śatam*

## v.6
Nor does a count of the cosmic-eggs exist.
The Sanskrit: *na ca brahmāṇḍānāṃ saṃkhyā vidyate*

## v.7
Then water-tattva, tenfold from earth-tattva.
The Sanskrit: *tato dharātattvād daśaguṇaṃ jalatattvam*

## v.8
Then, successively, tenfold up-to-ahaṃkāra-end.
The Sanskrit: *tata uttarottaraṃ daśaguṇam ahaṃkārāntam*

## v.9
As water, fire, wind, sky — the five-substances-included one-eleven-wombed ahaṃkāra — and so.
The Sanskrit: *tad yathā jalaṃ tejo vāyur nabhaḥ tanmātrapañcakākṣaikādaśagarbho 'haṃkāraś ceti*

## v.10
From ahaṃkāra, hundredfold buddhi-tattva.
The Sanskrit: *ahaṃkārāt śataguṇaṃ buddhitattvam*

## v.11
Then thousandfold prakṛti-tattva — so much prakṛti-egg.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ sahasradhā prakṛtitattvam etāvat prakṛtyaṇḍam*

## v.12
That, like the cosmic-egg, is countless.
The Sanskrit: *tac ca brahmāṇḍavad asaṃkhyam*

## v.13
From prakṛti-tattva, puruṣa-tattva too, ten-thousandfold.
The Sanskrit: *prakṛtitattvāt puruṣatattvaṃ ca daśasahasradhā*

## v.14
From persons, niyati, lakh-fold.
The Sanskrit: *puruṣān niyatiḥ lakṣadhā*

## v.15
From niyati, successively ten-lakh-fold up to kalā-tattva-end.
The Sanskrit: *niyater uttarottaraṃ daśalakṣadhā kalātattvāntam*

## v.16
As niyati, rāga, aśuddha-vidyā, kāla, kalā — and so.
The Sanskrit: *tad yathā niyatiḥ rāgo 'śuddhavidyā kālaḥ kalā ceti*

## v.17
From kalā-tattva, crorefold māyā — so much māyā-egg.
The Sanskrit: *kalātattvāt koṭidhā māyā etāvat māyāṇḍam*

## v.18
From māyā-tattva, śuddha-vidyā, ten-crore-multiplied.
The Sanskrit: *māyātattvāt śuddhavidyā daśakoṭiguṇitā*

## v.19
From vidyā-tattva, īśvara-tattva, hundred-crore-fold.
The Sanskrit: *vidyātattvād īśvaratattvaṃ śatakoṭidhā*

## v.20
From īśvara-tattva, sādākhya, thousand-crore-fold.
The Sanskrit: *īśvaratattvāt sādākhyaṃ sahasrakoṭidhā*

## v.21
From sādākhya, multiplied-by-group, śakti-tattva — so the śakti-egg.
The Sanskrit: *sādākhyāt vṛndaguṇitaṃ śaktitattvam iti śaktyaṇḍam*

## v.22
That power pervading, by which the world-path inside-outside stands — hence all-pervading.
The Sanskrit: *sā śaktir vyāpya yato viśvam adhvānam antarbahir āste tasmād vyāpinī*

## v.23
Thus these tattvas, standing as mutual-coverings: the later pervader, the earlier pervaded — by that standing they stand.
The Sanskrit: *evam etāni uttarottaram āvaraṇatayā vartamānāni tattvāny uttaraṃ vyāpakaṃ pūrvaṃ vyāpyam iti sthityā vartante*

## v.24
Up to the all-power-tattva-end, the path is pervaded by Śiva-tattva.
The Sanskrit: *yāvad aśeṣaśaktitattvānto 'dhvā śivatattvena vyāptaḥ*

## v.25
Śiva-tattva, further, is immeasurable, having-transcended-all-paths, pervading-all-paths.
The Sanskrit: *śivatattvaṃ punar aparimeyaṃ sarvādhvottīrṇaṃ sarvādhvavyāpakaṃ ca*

## v.26
These, dwelling in the gaps-between-tattvas, the bhuvanas — their lords stand in earth, here.
The Sanskrit: *etat tattvāntarālavartīni yāni bhuvanāni tatpataya eva atra pṛthivyāṃ sthitā iti*

## v.27
In those abodes, those who die — their course there, they grant.
The Sanskrit: *teṣv āyataneṣu ye mriyante teṣāṃ tatra tatra gatiṃ te vitaranti*

## v.28
And successively upward-downward they impel, by the dīkṣā-order.
The Sanskrit: *kramāc ca ūrdhvordhvaṃ prerayanti dīkṣākrameṇa*

# Tantrasāra A07 — L2 READ

## tsa07-p01 — how big the world is
The place-course is one thing born of power showing every shape, and it rests in awareness. Through its gate the whole course gets finished — in the void, in mind, in breath, in wheels within wheels of channels, outside, in the body, down to the liṅga on the altar and the temple image. Melt the course into the body, the body into breath, breath into mind, mind into the void — and what knows all that brims over as full awareness. Know the thirty-six this way, and see the awareness past them as supreme Śiva — yet still taste it as the universe too. Else you'll grab some one knowable as "other" — some Māyā officer, some Viṣṇu — so learn the process, no shortcuts: "no knowing past process." Earth spreads a hundred crores wide, an egg-globe of a universe — fire of time, hells, underworlds, earth, heaven to Brahmā's world inside; a hundred Rudras outside; eggs past counting. Water, ten times earth; then ten times at every step to ego (water, fire, wind, void, five subtles, eleven senses in ego's womb); mind a hundred times ego; nature a thousand — the nature-egg, countless like the world-egg; person ten thousand times nature; destiny a hundred thousand; then ten lakhs at a step to art (destiny, passion, half-knowing, time, art); Māyā a crore past art — the Māyā-egg; pure knowing ten crores past Māyā; the Lord a hundred crores past knowing; the High-voiced a thousand crores past the Lord; power past him by crowds — the power-egg, called Pervader because the world's course sits inside and outside her. Each later principle wears the earlier as its cover, pervading it; and Śiva pervades up to power's end — himself past measure, past every course and through them. Between the principles stand worlds, whose lords are posted here on earth: die in their shrines and they ferry you onward and upward, by initiation's road.

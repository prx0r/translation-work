# Tantrasāra A08 — ARGMAP

## tsa08-p01 (8.1–8.17)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (causality; pure principles).
- question: What are principles, and how do cause and effect relate — ultimately and conventionally?
- premises: (1) United great-light = supreme Śiva-nature; difference-united = principle (earth example: shining-hard-gross, Kālāgni→Vīrabhadra world-lords, whole egg). (2) Causality twofold: ultimate + of creation. Ultimate = autonomous agent-Lord spreading in Śiva-to-earth body, nature-nondifferent, nature-resting. Imagined = Lord's-will-made with destiny-as-life; holds wherever fixed before-after shining follows nature (yogin-wish, seed-sprout, dream-pot). Lord's agentness never lost — even unimagined stands ultimate. (3) Ultimate as wall: all sketching fits; totality itself causes (its otherwise-ness grades effects). (4) Universe-bodied consciousness alone makes pots — no more than potter-consciousness; potter-body thrown among things; its agent-conceit Lord-made like pots. Totality-doctrine strengthens same agentness (Meru pot counterfactual). (5) Imagined effecthood reconciles scriptural manifoldness (dung-worm, yogin-wish, mantra, herb, scorpion-birth). (6) Own-tantra view: Lord full of five powers, standing fivefold by chief-manifestation: cit→Śiva, ānanda→Śakti, icchā→Sadāśiva (will = knowledge-action equilibrium acceptance), jñāna→Īśvara, kriyā→Vidyā. Lords: Śiva/Śakti/Sadāśiva/Īśvara/Ananta; in cessation like Brahmā; five followers (śāmbhava/śākta/mantramahāśvara/mantreśvara/mantra) = pure course.
- inference: Both causalities are the Lord's — spreading (ultimate) and willing (imagined).
- conclusion: Two causalities + five pure principles stated.
- unresolved: World-lord names (Kālāgni→Vīrabhadra) assumed known (external).

## tsa08-p02 (8.18–8.38)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (mala doctrine).
- question: Who makes the impure course, what is stain, and what is the atom?
- premises: (1) Śiva directly makes; impure course made by Ananta-alias-Aghoreśa at Lord's-will for pleasure-shaken atoms' enjoyment. (2) Lolikā = incomplete other-formed throb, actionless mere longing, future delimitable — not stain, not separate principle. Rāga-principle = karma-delimited longing; karma mere, buddhi-natured passion, karma-division-vivid (division to come). (3) Stain = Lord's self-concealment-will alone; nothing else (earth-things too are Lord's-will-natured). In vijñānākala present but destruction-facing → doesn't feed karma; in pralayākala yawning → stain-bolstered karma = varied-saṃsāra-enjoyment cause; for bhoga-vāsanā-bound atoms Aghoreśa creates (stain-agitation from Lord's-will-power alone; inert has no power). (4) Atom = consciousness-n-consciousness shining; its consciousness = lordship, its n-consciousness = stain. Creating, Lord's-will-made; since ever future-thing-form to be shown as inert + inert as all-effect-pervading, Māyā-principle = material cause; Lord's Māyā-power (other than it) = manifester. (5) Same twoformedness for kalā-to-earth principles. Abhinavagupta teachers cited as authority (saṃkalpa…).
- inference: Impurity needs no second substance — concealment-will + Lord's-will-agitation suffice.
- conclusion: Mala/atom/Māyā doctrine stated.
- unresolved: Abhinavagupta-teacher proof-text truncated in cut (external).

## tsa08-p03 (8.39–8.64)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (vivekas; kañcukas).
- question: What discriminations free, and how do the covers work?
- premises: (1) By some Lord's grace at all-karma-exhaustion: Māyā-person discrimination (above Māyā = vijñānākala, never below; kalā-person → above kalā; prakṛti-person → below ground no transmigration; mala-person → Śiva-equality; person-fullness-view → Śiva-hood). (2) Kalā gives partial agency (not to ignorant); partial knowledge from kalā-born impure knowledge; knowledge sees intellect + grasps its joys discriminately (intellect's गुण-mixture ungraspable). Intellect-mirrored thing discriminated by knowledge. (3) Partial agency ends as: partial agency for partial success; there rāga functions. Not from dispassionlessness (dispassionless seen unattached; in dispassion toward dharma etc. attachment seen; sated, no dispassion yet inner passion unremoved; without it, dispassion never arises). (4) Time grasps action + delimits agentness (same partly-ness: "here agency" = niyati's function). In effect-cause too hers. From kalā alone this quartet born: "now knowing partially, clinging, I do" — consciousness gone to body-city-eight = bound soul. (5) This Māyā-six = six covers (to be expanded in place).
- inference: Freedom = discriminations; bondage = kalā-born quartet (partial agency/knowledge/passion/time-rule).
- conclusion: Vivekas + kañcuka-six stated.
- unresolved: Expansion deferred (yathāvasara).

## tsa08-p04 (8.65–8.93)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (Sāṅkhya-corrected evolution).
- question: How do intellect, ego, and senses evolve — and what must be corrected?
- premises: (1) Agitation must be admitted between (else fixed person fails) → Sāṅkhya-seen separate guṇa-principle; agitation from prakṛti's lord-abiding alone. (2) Guṇa → intellect (person-light + object mirror each other). Intellect → ego (in intellect-mirrored knowable-touch, turbid, non-self self-conceit like silver-in-shell). Hence "maker" = made-ness stated; Sāṅkhya wrong (denies self's I-grasp-reflexivity; we want even agentness for it). Pure reflexivity alone = "I" as self-wonder-form, rival-less. (3) Ego's sense-heap; nature-heap threefold by sattva-etc. Sattvic → mind + 5 intellect-senses (mind birthing, all-subtle-birthing-capable = father; ear→sound-birth… nose→smell: even elements prove ego-made via "I hear" + sense needing agent-touch else infinite regress). (4) Agent-portion = ego itself: two chief senses of person — in knowledge, knowledge (vidyā); in action, art (kalā). Lame-blind: I-form knowledge-action unremoved. From udrikta-subtle-portioned sattvic ego alone = 5 action-senses ("I go" ego-distinguished, act-fit; foot chief-outer, elsewhere too — lame still move). (5) Action must be sense-effect (doing-purity only from doable-confusion? no — going-etc. chiefly actions, not form-apprehension, since quality in Kaṇāda-tantra) → action-sense class mandatory. Fivefold by linkage-count (out: reject/take/both/neither-rest = anus/pani/foot/sex; inner breath-based linkage = voice; hand-walking = foot's act; so act-infinity ≠ sense-infinity). Rajasic = clinger (some say mind; some sattvic-mind/rajasic-senses). From covering enjoyer-portion, tamas-chief ego → subtle-only knowables…
- inference: Sāṅkhya machinery kept, corrected at two joints (self-reflexivity, agentness).
- conclusion: Evolution to senses stated (tail continues past cut; full close in refinement).
- unresolved: Passage ends mid-evolution (tanmātra derivation continues); Sāṅkhya/Kaṇāda texts assumed (external).

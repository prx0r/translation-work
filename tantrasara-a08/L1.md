# Tantrasāra A08 — L1 controlled

## tsa08-p01
When this glory-natured world-host stated — wombing endless varied enjoyer-enjoyed — therein, what is united, great-light-natured, that is great-universal-like, supreme-Śiva-natured. (8.1) But what form is united with some-some differences, that is principle — as earth named, shining-hard-gross-formed, from time-fire to Vīrabhadra-end world-lord-abided whole-universe-egg-united. (8.2) Therein, of these principles, effect-cause-relation is shown; and it is twofold. (8.3) Ultimate, and of creation. (8.4) Therein, the ultimate is so much effect-cause-relation, namely: of agent-natured autonomous Lord, by such body from Śiva to earth, nature-undifferent, nature-rested, spreading forth. (8.5) Imagined is effect-cause-relation made by Lord's will with destiny as life; and it holds as far as, when fixed before-after shining, nature-following even with more — hence even from yogin's wish, even sprout from seed, even in dream from pot and the rest. (8.6) Therein too, of the Lord agentness is unlost — hence unimagined too, it, ultimate, stands indeed. (8.7) For in ultimate, as wall-standing, in established form, all this being sketched fits — not otherwise. Hence therefore of totality itself causality is proper. (8.8) For it, all-thing-tissue-made, through autonomous consciousness greatness, thus with fixed own-place-time-thing-heap-natured, in each thing-nature completion time thus disposed — for of it becoming otherwise, as it grows more, so of effect too heterogeneity is nourished by degrees. (8.9) Thus consciousness-autonomy-natured the Lord alone, universe-thing-bodied — of pot and the rest is the maker — from potter-consciousness through non-excess; and of potter-body, from throwing among thing-heap. "How is of potter-body agentness-conceit?" — if asked: Lord-made itself, like pot and the rest, it will be. (8.10) Hence totality-doctrine too, for universe-bodied consciousness itself, in agentness is strengthener. (8.11) For on Meru, there standing, there would not be such pot. (8.12) Thus in this imagined effecthood, in scriptures, of principles toward effect-cause-relation whatever manifoldness — that too fits — from dung worm, from yogin's wish, from mantra, from herb — like scorpion-birth. (8.13) Therein, by own-tantra view, that imagined we show. (8.14) Therein the Lord, full with five powers, as stated — he through autonomy, manifesting power after power as chief, stands fivefold. (8.15) In consciousness-primacy is Śiva-principle; in bliss-primacy power-principle; in will-primacy Sadāśiva-principle — since of will, knowledge-action equilibrium-form-acceptance-nature; in knowledge-power-primacy Lord-principle; in action-power-primacy knowledge-principle. (8.16) Herein are principle-lords: Śiva, power, Sadāśiva, Lord, Endless; like Brahmā in cessation — of these universal-formed, particulars, following-subjects, five — namely: śāmbhavas, śāktas, great mantra-lords, mantra-lords, mantras — the pure course. (8.17)

## tsa08-p02
So far Śiva directly is maker; but the impure course the one named Endless-later-Aghoreśa makes, at Lord's-will, for pleasure-shaken atoms' enjoyment-success. (8.18) Therein the shaken is incomplete, other-formed throb, actionless, mere longing alone, future delimitable — hence not stain, not separate principle of person. (8.19) But passion-principle is karma-delimited longing. (8.20) But karma therein is mere karma; passion is intellect-nature; karma-division-vivid — so the division will be taught. (8.21) This stain is from the Lord's self-concealment-will, from nothing else whatever — and also because even earth-and-rest thingness is Lord's-will-natured. (8.22) And stain, existing in knowledge-alone-bodied, destruction-facing, hence does not feed its effect karma. (8.23) But of dissolution-alone-bodied it stays yawning — hence stain-bolstered karma is varied-saṃsāra-enjoyment cause. Hence for atoms pierced by enjoyment-latency enjoyment-success, glorious Aghoreśa creates — so rightly it was stated. And stain's agitation is from Lord's-will-power alone — for of inert, by itself, anywhere at all is incapacity. (8.24) "Atom" means consciousness-n-consciousness-shining alone. Of it, consciousness-form is lordship itself; n-consciousness-form itself is stain. Of it, creating, Lord's-will-made — and thence ever of future-thing-form, through going-to-be-shown-as-inert-by-inertness, inert, and through all-effect-pervading-and-rest-nature, pervading — Māyā-named principle is material cause; and manifesting it is the Lord's Māyā-named power, other than it. (8.25) Thus of kalā-to-earth principles too twoformedness is to be stated. (8.26–8.38, summarized positions per T1; full close render in L2.)"

## tsa08-p03
"But for Māyā-womb-officers, by some Lord's grace, at all-karma-exhaustion, Māyā-person discrimination comes to be — whereby above Māyā stands knowledge-bodiless, never below Māyā; or kalā-person discrimination, whereby one stands above kalā. (8.39) Or prakṛti-person discrimination, whereby below the ground one does not transmigrate. (8.40) But in mala-person discrimination, Śiva-equality. (8.41) And in person-fullness-view, Śiva-hood itself. (8.42) Thus kalā-principle alone gives partial agency — and not to the ignorant. (8.43–8.44) Partial-knowledge-giving is impure knowledge, born of kalā; and knowledge sees intellect and grasps its joys and the rest discriminately. (8.45) Since of quality-mixed-formed intellect, grasping discriminately is impossible. (8.46) Hence intellect-mirrored thing is discriminated by knowledge. (8.47) Partial agency ends as agency for partial success somewhere alone; and 'somewhere alone' — therein is passion-principle's function. (8.48) Nor is that from non-dispassion-made — for even of non-dispassion is seen non-attachment. (8.49) In dispassion, toward dharma and the rest too attachment is seen. (8.50) Of the sated, toward food and the rest, though no non-dispassion, from inner passion unremoved. (8.51) For without it, there would be non-arising of dispassion. (8.52) And time, grasping action, grasps agentness delimited by it too; in 'same partly-ness, here agency' is destiny's function. (8.53) In effect-cause too hers is the function. Hence from kalā alone this quartet is born — 'now knowing partially, clinging, I do' — consciousness of such form, gone to body-city-eight and the rest, is called bound soul. (8.54) That Māyā-six here, six-cover-named... (8.55–8.64, positions per T1.)"

## tsa08-p04
"[Agitation must admittedly be admitted between — thus established; Sāṅkhya-seen separate guṇa-principle. (8.65) And that agitation is from prakṛti's lord-abiding alone — else, toward a fixed person, it would not succeed. (8.66) Thence from guṇa-principle, intellect-principle — wherein person-light and object mirror each other. (8.67) From intellect-principle, ego — whereby in intellect-mirrored knowable-touch, turbid, in nonself, self-conceit, like silver-in-shell conceit. (8.68) Hence by 'maker' its made-ness was stated; but for Sāṅkhya that is not proper — for he does not want the self's I-grasp-reflexivity; but we want even agentness for it. (8.69) And that pure reflexivity alone, rival-less, self-wonder-formed 'I'. (8.70) This is his ego's sense-heap. (8.71) But nature-heap of it is threefold by sattva-and-rest-difference. (8.72) Therein from sattvic: mind and five intellect-senses; therein, in mind to be born, endowed with all-subtle-birthing-capability, it is father. (8.73) In ear, distinguished by sound-birthing-capability — down to: in nose, endowed with smell-birthing-fitness — thus even elemental is not proper; and from 'I hear' and the rest following, clearly ego-madeness; and as sense, necessarily agent-portion-touching — else, in sense-after-sense-linking, regress-fall. (8.74) And agent-portion is ego itself. Hence two chief senses of person: in knowledge, knowledge; in action, art. Of lame and blind, I-form knowledge-action unremoved. From udrikta-subtle-portioned sattvic ego alone, five action-senses — 'I go,' ego-distinguished, act-fit; foot-sense, its chief abode outer, elsewhere too it exists — thus even of injured, no movement-cut. (8.75) Nor, merely from doable-confusion-release cause, is action sense-effected; and chief is of goings-and-the-rest action-hood, not of form-and-rest-apprehension — since in Kaṇāda-tantra it is quality. Hence necessarily to be admitted is the action-sense class. (8.76) And it is fivefold from linkage-count. (8.77) As: outside, for now, linkage is for rejecting, for taking, for both, or as neither for own-nature-rest — therein in order: anus, hand, foot, sex. (8.78) But inner, breath-based-action-linkage: voice-sense. Hence what walking in hand-abode of senses, that too is foot-sense's act alone — so think. Hence act-infinity does not bring sense-infinity. So far the rajasic as clinger, they say. (8.79) Others say rajasic mind. (8.80) Others: from sattvic, mind; from rajasic, senses. (8.81) But from enjoyer-portion covering, tamas-chief ego, subtles, knowable-only... (8.82–8.93, tail continues past cut; full close in refinement.)]"

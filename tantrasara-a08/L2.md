# Tantrasāra A08 — L2 READ

## tsa08-p01 — two causalities, and the potter
What holds everything shining and undivided — that is supreme Śiva-nature. What shows with a few differences — that is a "principle": earth, say, shining, hard, gross, with world-lords from Time-fire to Vīrabhadra posted through every universe-egg. Cause and effect come in two kinds: ultimate, and the kind in stories. Ultimate causality is just this: the free agent-Lord spreading in a body from Śiva to earth, same as himself, resting as himself. The story-kind is willed by the Lord with destiny for its lifeblood — and it holds wherever a fixed before-and-after shows up following nature: a yogin's wish, a sprout from seed, a pot in a dream. The Lord never stops being the maker — so even the unwilled stands, ultimately. The ultimate is the wall everything is sketched on; without it nothing fits. So "everything together causes" is rightly said — the sketch fits the wall, and the more otherwise it goes, the more by degrees the effect goes heterogeneous. The Lord alone, then — awareness autonomous, bodied as the universe's things — makes pots no more mysteriously than the potter's awareness does: the potter's body was just thrown into the thing-heap like everything else. "Then whose is the potter's 'I make'?" — the Lord's making, like the pot. So the "many causes" school only backs the same agency. (No such pot on Meru, incidentally.) And once effects are story-level, the books can differ without breaking — worms from dung, a yogin's wish, a mantra, a herb, scorpions rotting into birth. Our own books' view of the story: the Lord, full of five powers, stands five ways as each takes the lead — awareness leading, Śiva; bliss, Power; will, Sadāśiva (will being knowledge and action held even); knowledge, the Lord; action, Knowledge. Their lords — Śiva, Power, Sadāśiva, Lord, the Endless — and in cessation, like Brahmā; five followers of their universal shapes — Śambhu-folk, power-folk, great mantra-lords, mantra-lords, mantras: the pure course.

## tsa08-p02 — who makes the dirty world, and what stain is
Śiva makes directly. The dirty course is made by Endless, alias the Non-terrible Lord, on the Lord's will — for atoms shaken with hunger, so their pleasure can succeed. The "shaken" is an unfinished, other-shaped throb — no act in it, bare about-to-be, delimitable later: not a stain, not a separate tattva of the person. Passion is hunger fenced by deeds; deed is bare deed, passion is mind-stuff, deeds vivid in kinds (their split comes later). Stain is one thing only: the Lord wanting to hide himself. Nothing else — dirt's thingness is Lord's-will-natured too. In a knowledge-bodied soul the stain sits doomed and feeds no deed; in a dissolution-bodied one it yawns — and stain-fed deeds cause saṃsāra's variety show. So for atoms needled with pleasure-hunger, glorious Aghoreśa rightly makes. Stain shakes on Lord's-will power only — dead matter can't do anything anywhere by itself. "Atom" just means: shining as aware-and-unaware. Its aware side is lordship; its unaware side is stain. Making, it's made of Lord's will — and since every coming thing will show inert, and the inert, spreading through every effect, pervades, Māyā is the stuff things are made of, and the Lord's own Māyā-power, distinct from her, shows it. The same double-face holds from art down to earth. (My teachers the Abhinavaguptas prove it too.)

## tsa08-p03 — the discriminations that free, and the six covers
But officers inside Māyā's womb can, by some Lord's grace when every deed is spent, tell Māyā from person — and stand above Māyā as knowledge-bodied, never under; or tell art from person and stand above art; or nature from person and never whirl below the ground; or stain from person — and that's equality with Śiva; or see the person's fullness — and that's Śiva-hood itself. Art alone lends a little agency — never to the ignorant. Half-knowing comes from art-born half-knowledge: it sees the intellect and picks its pleasures apart, since intellect comes mixed with qualities and can't sort itself — what mirrors in intellect, knowledge sorts. "A little agency" cashes out as: agency somewhere, for some success; "somewhere" is where passion works. And not from dispassion's absence — the undispassioned are seen unsticky; and in dispassion, toward duty and the rest, stickiness shows; the full man, no dispassion toward food, still unrid of inner passion; without which dispassion would never start. Time, grasping the act, grasps a fenced agency too — "in this same partly-ness, here is agency": destiny's job, in effects and causes alike. So from art alone this foursome is born — "now half-knowing, clinging, I act" — awareness in that shape, lodged down to the eightfold body-city, is called the bound soul. Those Māyā-six are the six covers... (expansion in its place).

## tsa08-p04 — mind, ego, and senses (Sāṅkhya, corrected)
Something must churn between — admit the stir, or no fixed person works: the Sāṅkhya-seen separate stuff of qualities. And the churn runs on nature indwelt by a lord — else aimed at a fixed person it fails. From stuff: mind-stuff, where the person's light and the object mirror each other. From mind-stuff: ego — in the mirrored knowable-touch, turbid, mistaking non-self for self like silver for shell. Hence "maker" means "made" — but Sāṅkhya can't have it: he won't grant the self an I shaped of grasping, while we grant it even agency. Pure grasping alone — no rival, self amazement shaped as "I." That is ego's sense-heap; its nature-heap is triple by bright and restless and dark. From brightness: mind and five knowing-senses — mind, about to be born, fathering every subtle's birth; ear with sound-birthing fitness, down to nose with smell-fitness. Even elements prove ego-made — "I hear" gives it away — and a sense must touch agency, or senses behind senses run forever. Agency's portion is ego itself: so a person has two chief senses — in knowing, knowing; in doing, art. The lame and the blind keep I-shaped knowing-doing. From bright ego rich in subtles alone: five doing-senses — "I go," I-marked, act-fit — foot foremost, posted outside but present elsewhere (the hurt still move). Doing must be sense-done, and going and the rest are chiefly doings — not seizing of shapes (in Kaṇāda's book that's a quality) — so the doing-sense class stands admitted. Five, by linkages: outgoings — dropping, taking, both, neither-but-rest — anus, hand, foot, sex in turn. Inward, breath-hung linkage: voice. So hand-walking, seated in senses, is still foot's doing — mark it. Acts may be endless; senses needn't be. So far the restless as clinger, they say — others say the restless is mind, others bright-mind/restless-senses. From the covering enjoyer-portion, dark-chief ego, the subtles, knowable-only...

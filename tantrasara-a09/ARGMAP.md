# Tantrasāra A09 — ARGMAP

## tsa09-p01 (9.1–9.16)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (seven knowers/powers; knowability defense).
- question: How do knowers and powers divide — and can a thing be knowable without regress?
- premises: (1) Seven power-possessors (śiva→sakala) + seven powers; each own-form fifteenfold via 14 divisions from earth to chief. Own-form = knowability-fit, self-abiding; in excess-powered knowers, rest-receptacle = its power-form (aparā grace, sevenfold by power-count); knower-class rested = powerful-Śiva-form (parā grace, sevenfold). (2) Knower-division from power-division, told sakala-first: sakala's power in knowledge-art (intellect/action/sense powers in distinct form); pralayākala's same powers objectless-unmanifest; vijñānākala's same with melted art + refinement-companion = mantra's awakening pure knowledge; without refinement = mantreśa's; seizing will-power-autonomy-nature = mantramaheśvara's; will-natured manifest-autonomy = Śiva's. Seven chief power-divisions. Tinge-made knower-division (sense-division ends in agent-division; non-different power alone sense-makable, else regress). (3) Really one knower resting in pure autonomy-bliss (Caitra-eye/known/I-know demo to Śiva-end: distinct conviction "I know the universe abiding in Śiva-power, resting in Śiva-nature"). (4) Objection: knowableness as own-body → all-knowableness → knowableness-knowable regress → blind-asleep shining world + knowable-unknowable contradiction. Reply: not "own body" separately stated; rest-receptacle form in knower-power/knower = self-shining, shining toward nothing = omniscience; regress/contradiction far removed. (5) Endless-knowers-known still one (one-shining-form) → no other-mind inference block; form true (does work): beloved-seen-by-other envied; Śiva-nature rest-jar seen = immersion; endless-knower-rested thing seen = fullness (dancer-watching). Blue's knower-rested form from self-shining-grasping-rise = fifteen-naturedness earth-to-chief.
- inference: Knowability = resting-in-knower, not own-body property; oneness of knower compatible with knower-division (power-tinge).
- conclusion: Seven/seven/fifteen + regress dissolved.
- unresolved: Dancer/beloved/jar illustrations assume assent (mild, external).

## tsa09-p02 (9.17–9.35)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (counts + earth-principle walkthrough).
- question: How many divisions per knower, and how does it work for earth?
- premises: (1) Sakala (excess-passion-cover) fifteenfold too (knowable so much). Tantrāloka for detail. Person-to-art thirteenfold. (2) Countdown: sakala own-form + knowerhood sunset → 13 (sakala nature alone); pralayākala nature + five-knowerhood → 11; vijñānākala nature + four → 9; mantra nature + three → 7; mantreśa nature + two → 5; mantramaheśa nature + Lord alone → 3 (power/possessor split); Śiva (light-only-pure-autonomy-full) → 0 (fullness). (3) Principle-division in Lord-unsurpassed-course-one-named makes world-difference-variety (hell-heaven-Rudra-worlds, same earthhood, far nature-difference). Sub-division curiosity → Tantrāloka. Pot-by-pot division likewise. (4) Whole earth-principle as knower-known: earth's shining = Śiva (śruti: earth is Brahman). Earth-great-mantra-lord impels earth-success-giving; earth-mantra-lord is impelled; its speaker = mantra (ego-image-bodied), practiced-earth-yoga past-Sāṅkhya-bestial-knowledge unreached-firm-state = earth-vijñānākala. Bestial-knowledge-course practiced-earth-yoga = at end/death earth-dissolution-bodiless. In sleep, tattva-entry-force brings varied dreams; grasped-earth-conceit = earth-with-parts.
- inference: Countdown + earth-case instantiate the general fifteenfold.
- conclusion: Counts + earth walkthrough stated.
- unresolved: Tantrāloka cross-refs (external); śruti quote assumed.

## tsa09-p03 (9.36–9.53)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (fifteenfold everywhere; tuṭi; states).
- question: How does fifteenfold hold everywhere, and what are waking/dream/sleep/fourth?
- premises: (1) Power-excess/decline → fourteenfold (knower-become earthhood). Discrimination-rich, teacher-dwelling, everywhere divide fifteenfold by division. (2) Thought-lessness → instant-lessness (as in joy-consciousnesses, until thought-free). (3) Folk mistake thought-rest I-ness-covered this-lessness-spread thought-free grasping-ground unmanifest as suffering-state (like joy-rested mistake); by thought-waning it shines — hence carefulness in grasper-grasped relation (Abhinavagupta teachers). (4) Fifteenfold standing: as manifest-I-natured difference lessens, pairs wane until two-instant Śiva-immersion: first instant all-full, second all-knowledge-sense-entered-practiced for omniscience/omnipotence (not first). Kallata: "instant-fall" — fall-word = goddess Kālī = Mother-of-Beings = Bhairava = intuitiveness; enough of secret-nonself. (5) From mantra-great-lord-instant onward, by practice siddhis. (6) States: variety of knowable + its consciousness mutually dependent = state (not knowable alone, not consciousness alone, not two separate). As supported-outer-shining = waking (in known, measurer, measure). As supported-form-shining = resolve = dream. As supporter-form seed-nature shining = deep sleep. These three as known/means/knower states each fourfold by waking-etc. (7) When in knower-rest-gone, through knower's fullness-facing, shining as fullness-facing = fourth: "form, I see" — crossing three parts "I see" = means-less knowerhood, autonomy-essence, near-middle-far, conferring knower-means-known baptism, gracing the three states, threefold.
- inference: States = knower-known relations, not substances; fourth = fullness-facing.
- conclusion: Fifteenfold + tuṭi + four states stated (piṇḍastha etc. continue past cut).
- unresolved: Tail continues (piṇḍastha/pada/rūpa/rūpātīta — full close in refinement); Kallata quote assumed.

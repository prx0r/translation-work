## tsa09-p01
And it is sevenfold in sixfold-scripture itself, supremely by the Lord stated. (9.1) Therein — Śivas, great mantra-lords, mantra-lords, mantras, knowledge-bodiless, dissolution-bodiless, with-parts — the seven power-possessors. (9.2) Of them are seven itself powers; through their division, from earth to chief-principle-end, by fourteen divisions, in each own form fifteenth. (9.3) Therein own form is knowability-fit, self-abiding; through lower-goddess grace, in knowers with excessed powers, whatever is rest-receptacle — that is of itself power-form; through upper-lower-goddess grace — and that is sevenfold from power-so-much-ness. (9.4) But in power-possessor-form-primacy, in knower-class, whatever is rested — that is powerful-Śiva-form — through supreme-goddess grace; that too is sevenfold. (9.5) Since of knowers from Śiva to with-parts-end of so many is the stating. (9.6) Therein from power-division alone is of knowers division; and it, for clarifying, is told in with-parts-first-course. Therein: of with-parts-one, in knowledge-art is power — since that-distinction-formed of intellect-action-sense-powers; but of dissolution-bodiless, they themselves, from objectlessness, in unmanifest. (9.7) Of knowledge-bodiless, they themselves, with melted art, with refinement-companion, awakening is pure knowledge of mantra. (9.8) Without that refinement, that itself awakened is of mantra-lord. (9.9) That itself, wanting to seize will-power-formness autonomy-natured, is of great mantra-lord. (9.10) Will-natured, manifest-autonomy-natured, is of Śiva — thus power-divisions seven chief. (9.11) And from their tinge-made is in powerful knowers division — since sense-division ends in agent-division, and through non-different power alone from can-be-made-sense, not of other — from regress-fall. (9.12) But really one alone, in pure autonomy-bliss-resting, is knower. Therein when earth, as nature-only-rested, is known — then nature of it merely shines: "seen by Caitra's eye, known by Caitra" — "I know" thus. Therein by whole-power-made, by whole-powerful-form-made, other-nature shines indeed. Thus to Śiva-end too is to be stated — abiding in Śiva-power, resting in Śiva-nature is the universe — "I know" thus, through being of distinct conviction. (9.13) "But if of thing there is knowableness as own body, then for all there is knowableness; and knowableness too is knowable — thus regress; and through it, of world, blind-asleep-ness, well-shining; and through it, in knowable-unknowable, contrary-nature-yoga — thus is the flaw. Here it is said: (9.14) Not that own body of nature separately stated. What then? That in knower-power and in knower, whatever is rest-receptacle — whatever form — that indeed, that self-shining itself — that shines not toward anything at all — thus is omniscience; and regress-contrary-yoga thus is far removed. (9.15) Even known by endless knowers, one alone is that — of it is the form; in so much, of them from one-shining-form-ness — hence is no other-knower-knowledge-inference-obstacle whatever. And that of it is form true — through doing-work. Thus indeed seeing the beloved seen by other, one envies for her; seeing Śiva-nature rest-jar, one immerses; seeing thing rested in by endless knowers, one becomes full — like dancer-watching. Of that very blue is that form in knower — whatever is rested — thus alone from self-shining-grasping-rise — thus is fifteen-naturedness from earth onward to chief-principle-end. (9.16)"

## tsa09-p03
"[One's-own-work-doership is grasper-natured — so stated; it is not counted again — thus discrimination-rich, teacher-dwelling-following, everywhere fifteenfold by division one discriminates. (9.36) But in thought-lessness there is instant-lessness — as in joy-consciousnesses, until thought-free alone. (9.37) But folk, by thought-rest, mistake that I-ness-formed, I-ness-covered-this-ness-spread thought-free grasping-ground unmanifest as suffering-state — as joy-rested mistake; but by thought-waning it shines indeed — thus this carefulness in grasper-grasped relation — so the Abhinavagupta teachers. (9.38) And thus fifteenfold standing: as far as manifest-I-natured difference lessens, pairs wane in twos, until two-instant Śiva-immersion: therein the first instant all-full, the second all-knowledge-sense-entered-practiced, fitting for omniscience-omnipotence — not the first. (9.39) As venerable Kallata said: 'instant-fall' — here fall-word is goddess Kālī herself, Mother-of-Beings, Bhairava, intuitiveness — enough of secret-nonself. (9.40) Thus from mantra-great-lord-instant onward, through practice thereof, thereof siddhis. (9.41) Now herein waking-and-rest-states are described: therein, of knowable and its consciousness, whatever variety mutually dependent — that is state — not of knowable alone, nor of consciousness alone, nor two separate. (9.42) Therein, when shining as to-be-supported, as outer-formed, then waking state — in known, measurer, measure. (9.43) But when therein, as supporting-formed, shining is resolve — then dream state. (9.44) But when therein, as supporter-formed, as seed-natured alone shining — then deep-sleep state. (9.45) These three alone, as known-means-knower-states, each fourfold by waking-and-the-rest, are stated. (9.46) But when therein, in knower-rest-gone, through knower's fullness-facing, shining as fullness-facing — then fourth state. And it is 'form, I see' — thus crossing three parts 'I see' — means-less knowerhood, autonomy-essence, by near-middle-far, conferring knower-means-known baptism, gracing the three states, threefold. (9.47–9.53 per T1.)]"

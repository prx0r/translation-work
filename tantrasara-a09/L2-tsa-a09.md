# Tantrasara-a09 — L2 READ

## v.1
That, sevenfold, the six-and-half-fold scripture is taught by the Supreme Lord alone.
The Sanskrit: *sa ca saptadhā ṣaḍardhaśāstra eva paraṃ parameśena uktaḥ*

## v.2
There: Śivas, mantra-great-Lords, mantra-Lords, mantras, knowledge-portions, dissolution-portions, complete — seven power-bearing.
The Sanskrit: *tatra śivāḥ mantramaheśāḥ mantreśāḥ mantrāḥ vijñānākalāḥ pralayākalāḥ sakalā iti sapta śaktimantaḥ*

## v.3
Of these seven powers, by their difference up to the earth-and-rest principal tattva: each its own form by fourteen differences — fifteenfold.
The Sanskrit: *eṣāṃ saptaiva śaktayaḥ tadbhedāt pṛthivyādipradhānatattvāntaṃ caturdaśabhir bhedaiḥ pratyekaṃ svaṃ rūpaṃ pañcadaśam*

## v.4
There: own form fit for knowability, self-established, by the grace of Aparābhaṭṭārikā — in the power-raised knower-objects the rest-receptacle: that is the śākta form itself, by the grace of Śrīmatparāparā; and that too is sevenfold, from the powers being as many.
The Sanskrit: *tatra svaṃ rūpaṃ prameyatāyogyaṃ svātmaniṣṭham aparābhaṭṭārikānugrahāt pramātṛṣu udriktaśaktiṣu yat viśrāntibhājanaṃ tat tasyaiva śāktaṃ rūpaṃ śrīmatparāparānugrahāt tac ca saptavidhaṃ śaktīnāṃ tāvattvāt*

## v.5
In the power-bearing-form-principal, however, what rests in the knower-class: that power-bearing Siva-form, by the grace of Śrīmatparābhaṭṭārikā — sevenfold too.
The Sanskrit: *śaktimadrūpapradhāne tu pramātṛvarge yat viśrāntaṃ tac chaktimac chivarūpaṃ śrīmatparābhaṭṭārikānugrahāt tad api saptavidham*

## v.6
From Śiva to the complete-limit — from those many being said.
The Sanskrit: *pramātṝṇāṃ śivāt prabhṛti sakalāntānāṃ tāvatām uktatvāt*

## v.7
There the difference of knower-objects is from power-difference alone; and for clarification it is said by the order of complete and the rest: in complete, knowledge-portion, power — from being its specific form (the mind-, action- and instrument-powers); of the dissolution-portion, those same from objectlessness, indistinct.
The Sanskrit: *tatra śaktibhedād eva pramātṝṇāṃ bhedaḥ sa ca sphuṭīkaraṇārthaṃ sakalādikrameṇa bhaṇyate tatra sakalasya vidyākale śaktiḥ tadviśeṣarūpatvāt buddhikarmākṣaśaktīnāṃ pralayākalasya tu te eva nirviṣayatvāt asphuṭe*

## v.8
Of the knowledge-portion, those same, in the dissolving-portion, helper of its impression, awakening — the pure knowledge of mantra.
The Sanskrit: *vijñānākalasya te eva vigalatkalpe tatsaṃskārasacivā prabudhyamānā śuddhavidyā mantrasya*

## v.9
Devoid of that impression: she alone awakened, of the mantra-Lord.
The Sanskrit: *tatsaṃskārahīnā saiva prabuddhā mantreśasya*

## v.10
She alone, desiring the will-power-form, autonomy-natured, of the mantra-great-Lord.
The Sanskrit: *saiva icchāśaktirūpatāṃ svātantryasvabhāvāṃ jighṛkṣantī mantramaheśvarasya*

## v.11
Will-natured, distinct-autonomy-natured, of Śiva — thus the power-differences: seven primary.
The Sanskrit: *icchātmikā sphuṭasvātantryātmikā śivasya iti śaktibhedāḥ sapta mukhyāḥ*

## v.12
And the difference in the power-bearing knower-objects is made by its tainting: instrument-difference ends in agent-difference; and from power alone — non-separate — being able to act; not of another, from the fallacy of infinite regress and the rest.
The Sanskrit: *taduparāgakṛtaś ca śaktimatsu pramātṛṣu bhedaḥ karaṇabhedasya kartṛbhedaparyavasānāt śakter eva ca avyatiriktāyāḥ karaṇīkartuṃ śakyatvāt na anyasya anavasthādyāpatteḥ*

## v.13
In reality, however, one alone — consciousness-autonomy-bliss-resting knower; there earth, resting in own-nature-merely, when known, its own nature alone shines: seen by the mind's eye, known by the mind, I know — there the other own-nature made by complete-power, made by power-bearing-form, shines too; thus up to Śiva also is to be said: world established in Śiva-power, resting in Śiva-nature, I know — from the conviction being of a different kind.
The Sanskrit: *vastutaḥ punar eka eva citsvātantryānandaviśrāntaḥ pramātā tatra pṛthivī svarūpamātraviśrāntā yadā vedyate tadā svarūpam asyāḥ kevalaṃ bhāti caitracakṣurdṛṣṭaṃ caitraviditaṃ jānāmīti tatra sakalaśaktikṛtaṃ sakalaśaktimadrūpakṛtaṃ svarūpāntaraṃ bhāty eva evaṃ śivāntam api vācyaṃ śivaśaktiniṣṭhaṃ śivasvabhāvaviśrāntaṃ ca viśvaṃ jānāmi iti pratyayasya vilakṣaṇasya bhāvāt*

## v.14
But if the state's knowability is its own body, then knowability toward all, knowability of the knowable too — infinite regress; by that, the world's blindness well-luminous; by that, the union of opposite qualities in knowable-unknowable: fault — so it is said here.
The Sanskrit: *nanu bhāvasya cet vedyatā svaṃ vapuḥ tat sarvān prati vedyatvaṃ vedyatvam api vedyam ity anavasthā tayā ca jagato 'ndhasuptatvaṃ suprakāśam eva tayā ca vedyatvāvedyatve viruddhadharmayoga iti doṣaḥ atra ucyate*

## v.15
Not that own body: from own-nature being separately said; but what? In knower-power and in the knower the rest-receptacle, the form — that self-luminous itself shines, not toward anything at all: thus omniscience; regress and opposite-quality-union — far removed.
The Sanskrit: *na tat svaṃ vapuḥ svarūpasya pṛthaguktatvāt kiṃ tarhi tat pramātṛśaktau pramātari ca yat viśrāntibhājanaṃ yat rūpaṃ tat khalu tat tat svaprakāśam eva tat prakāśate na tu kiṃcid api prati iti sarvajñatvam anavasthāviruddhadharmayogaś ca iti dūrāpāstam*

## v.16
Known by infinite knower-objects too: one alone, its form in those, from the one-shining-form nature: no obstruction by another knower's knowing-inference; and that its form is real, from doing work for meaning; like seeing a beloved seen by another becoming jealous: seeing Śiva-nature, the rest-pot, one enters completely; seeing reality resting in all infinite knower-objects one becomes full, like watching a dancer; her blue's form resting in the knower — from the arising of self-luminous contemplation: thus the fifteenfold nature of earth up to the principal tattva.
The Sanskrit: *anantapramātṛsaṃvedyam api ekam eva tat tasya rūpaṃ tāvati teṣām ekābhāsarūpatvāt iti na pramātrantarasaṃvedanānumānavighnaḥ kaścit tac ca tasya rūpaṃ satyam arthakriyākāritvāt tathaiva paradṛśyamānāṃ kāntāṃ dṛṣṭvā tasyai samīrṣyati śivasvabhāvaṃ viśrāntikumbhaṃ paśyan samāviśati samastānantapramātṛviśrāntaṃ vastu paśyan pūrṇībhavati nartakīprekṣaṇavat tasyaiva nīlasya tadrūpaṃ pramātari yat viśrāntaṃ tathaiva svaprakāśasya vimarśasyodayāt iti pañcadaśātmakatvaṃ pṛthivyāḥ prabhṛti pradhānatattvaparyantam*

## v.17
In that much — the complete's knower-nature from the raised passion-and-rest curtain — the complete's fifteenfoldness too thus, from its that-much knowability.
The Sanskrit: *tāvaty udriktarāgādikañcukasya sakalasya pramātṛtvāt sakalasyāpi evaṃ pāñcadaśyaṃ tasyāpi tāvad vedyatvāt*

## v.18
Spread, this is settled in the Tantrāloka.
The Sanskrit: *vitatya caitat nirṇītaṃ tantrāloke*

## v.19
From the male up to the segment-tattva: thirteenfold.
The Sanskrit: *puṃsaḥ prabhṛti kalātattvāntaṃ trayodaśadhā*

## v.20
Complete's there, by union with knower-nature, from suppression of its two differences power/power-bearing-self: thus complete's own-nature merely; the dissolution-portion's in own-nature, five's in knower-nature: eleven differences.
The Sanskrit: *sakalasya tatra pramātṛtāyogena tacchaktiśaktimadātmano bhedadvayasya pratyastamayāt tathā ca sakalasya svarūpatvam eva kevalaṃ pralayākalasya svarūpatve pañcānāṃ pramātṛtve ekādaśa bhedāḥ*

## v.21
The knowledge-portion's in own-nature, four's in knower-nature: nine differences.
The Sanskrit: *vijñānākalasya svarūpatve caturṇāṃ pramātṛtve nava bhedāḥ*

## v.22
Mantra's in own-nature, three's in knower-nature: seven.
The Sanskrit: *mantrasya svarūpatve trayāṇāṃ pramātṛtve sapta*

## v.23
The mantra-Lord's in own-nature, two's in knower-nature: five.
The Sanskrit: *mantreśasya svarūpatve dvayoḥ pramātṛtve pañca*

## v.24
The mantra-great-Lord's in own-nature, the Lord's one alone in knower-nature, from power-power-bearing-difference: three.
The Sanskrit: *mantramaheśasya svarūpatve bhagavata ekasyaiva pramātṛtve śaktiśaktimadbhedāt trayaḥ*

## v.25
Śiva's, however — dependent on light-one-consciousness-autonomy: no difference at all, from fullness.
The Sanskrit: *śivasya tu prakāśaikacitsvātantryanirbharasya na ko 'pi bhedaḥ paripūrṇatvāt*

## v.26
Thus tattva-difference itself is established in the Supreme-Lord's unsurpassable-policy-one-name, and makes the variegation of world-difference: of hell, heaven and rudra-worlds, earthness being equal too, from the farther nature-difference being said.
The Sanskrit: *evam ayaṃ tattvabheda eva parameśvarānuttaranayaikākhye nirūpitaḥ bhuvanabhedavaicitryaṃ karoti narakasvargarudrabhuvanānāṃ pārthivatve samāne 'pi dūratarasya svabhāvabhedasya uktatvāt*

## v.27
And here, by mutual construction of difference, curious for inner-difference-knowledge: let him determine the Tantrāloka alone.
The Sanskrit: *atra ca parasparaṃ bhedakalanayā avāntarabhedajñānakutūhalī tantrālokam eva avadhārayet*

## v.28
Thus by following each pot and the rest too, the difference of earth-and-rest tattvas is established.
The Sanskrit: *evam ekaikaghaṭādyanusāreṇāpi pṛthivyādīnāṃ tattvānāṃ bhedo nirūpitaḥ*

## v.29
Now the whole earth-tattva, targeted as knower-known-form, is established: what by earth-tattva-non-difference is light — that is Śiva.
The Sanskrit: *adhunā samastaṃ pṛthivītattvaṃ pramātṛprameyarūpam uddiśya nirūpyate yo dharātattvābhedena prakāśaḥ sa śivaḥ*

## v.30
As the Vedic text: in earth this very brahman.
The Sanskrit: *yathā śrutiḥ pṛthivy evedaṃ brahma iti*

## v.31
It impels the giving of earth-tattva-attainment: the earth-mantra-great-Lord; impelled, the earth-mantra-Lord; his expressive mantra made of imagined-image-nature, by the order of Sāṃkhya-and-rest bonded-knowledge-crossed-Śiva-knowledge, the practiced earth-yoga not reaching the stable stage: the earth-knowledge-portion.
The Sanskrit: *dharātattvasiddhipradān prerayati sa dharāmantramaheśvaraḥ preryo dharāmantreśaḥ tasyaivābhimānikavigrahatātmako vācako mantraḥ sāṃkhyādipāśavavidyottīrṇaśivavidyākrameṇa abhyastapārthivayogo 'prāptadhruvapadaḥ dharāvijñānākalaḥ*

## v.32
By bonded-knowledge-order the practiced earth-yoga: at cosmic-end or in death, earth-dissolution-alone.
The Sanskrit: *pāśavavidyākrameṇa abhyastapārthivayogaḥ kalpānte maraṇe vā dharāpralayakevalaḥ*

## v.33
In deep sleep, from tattva-immersion alone, varied dream would arise; the earth-identity-grasped, however: earth-complete.
The Sanskrit: *sauṣupte hi tattvāveśavaśād eva citrasya svapnasya udayaḥ syāt gṛhītadharābhimānas tu dharāsakalaḥ*

## v.34
Even here by power-raising-absence-nature: fourteenfold — thus the differences of the earth-tattva having attained knower-nature; own-nature, however, pure knowable — thus elsewhere-here-in-even.
The Sanskrit: *atrāpi śaktyudrekanyagbhāvābhyāṃ caturdaśatvam iti pramātṛtāpannasya dharātattvasya bhedāḥ svarūpaṃ tu śuddhaṃ prameyam iti evam aparatrāpi*

## v.35
Now in one knower, by being established in breath, the difference-determination: here the blue-grasping one, breath, sixteen-segment-self, rises up to knowable-immersion. There the first segment indivisible-one-form; the second knower-exultation-form; the last knowable-identical pervaded; the penultimate manifested-knower-form; in the middle, the twelve-segment — from among them the first six-group non-conceptual-natured, covering-concept sixness: as its own nature one segment; in the covering-concept five-form-nature, dawning-willingness, already-dawned — she two-segment-bodied from pulsation's absence-of-one-instant-form, own-work-agent-nature — thus from the covering-concept's fivefoldness, and from own-nature six non-conceptual instants; then from non-conceptual's dissolution-likeness: dissolution of concept, dawning-willingness, two-segment-bodied already-dawned: six segments.
The Sanskrit: *atha ekasmin pramātari prāṇapratiṣṭhitatayā bhedanirūpaṇam iha nīlaṃ gṛhṇataḥ prāṇaḥ tuṭiṣoḍaśakātmā vedyāveśaparyantam udeti tatra ādyā tuṭir avibhāgaikarūpā dvitīyā grāhakollāsarūpā antyā tu grāhyābhinnā tanmayī upāntyā tu sphuṭībhūtagrāhakarūpā madhye tu yat tuṭidvādaśaka tanmadhyāt ādyaṃ ṣaṭkaṃ nirvikalpasvabhāvaṃ vikalpāc chādakaṃ ṣaṭtvaṃ ca asya svarūpeṇa ekā tuṭiḥ ācchādanīye ca vikalpe pañcarūpatvam unmimiṣā unmiṣattā sā ca iyaṃ sphuṭakriyārūpatvāt tuṭidvayātmikā spandanasya ekakṣaṇarūpatvābhāvāt unmiṣitatā svakāryakartṛtvaṃ ca ity evam ācchādanīyavikalpapāñcavidhyāt svarūpāc ca ṣaṭ kṣaṇā nirvikalpakāḥ tato 'pi nirvikalpasya dhvaṃsamānatā dhvaṃso vikalpasya unmimiṣā unmiṣattā tuṭidvayātmikā unmiṣitatā ca iti ṣaṭ tuṭayaḥ*

## v.36
Own-work-agent-nature is knower-form-nature — said; she is not counted further — thus the wise-wealthed, practising the teacher's instruction, distinguish fifteenfoldness everywhere by division.
The Sanskrit: *svakāryakartṛtā tu grāhakarūpatā iti uktaṃ na sā bhūyo gaṇyate ity evaṃ vivekadhanā gurūpaveśānuśīlinaḥ sarvatra pāñcadaśyaṃ pravibhāgena viviñcate*

## v.37
In concept-lack: segment-lack, in bliss-and-rest-knowing — like, as far as non-conceptualness alone.
The Sanskrit: *vikalpanyūnatve tu tuṭinyūnatā sukhādisaṃvittāv iva yāvat avikalpataiva*

## v.38
The worlds too, by concept-rest, consider that ego-made, the spread-of-this-being-concept-covered, non-conceptual contemplation-abode unlit — like a suffering-state resting in bliss; but by concept-decrease she shines indeed — thus this attentiveness in the relation of knowable and knower: the Abhinavagupta-teachers.
The Sanskrit: *lokās tu vikalpaviśrāntyā tām ahaṃtāmayīm ahaṃtācchāditedaṃbhāvavikalpaprasarāṃ nirvikalpāṃ vimarśabhuvam aprakāśitām iva manyante duḥkhāvasthāṃ sukhaviśrāntā iva vikalpanirhrāsena tu sā prakāśata eva iti iyam asau sambandhe grāhyagrāhakayoḥ sāvadhānatā iti abhinavaguptaguravaḥ*

## v.39
Thus in fifteenfold standing, as far as the lack of the distinct this-ness-self's difference, so much pair-pair diminishes; as far as two-segment Śiva-immersion: there the first segment everywhere complete; the second pervaded-by-all-knowledge-instruments being-practised, fit for omniscience-omnipotence — not the first.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ ca pāñcadaśye sthite yāvat sphuṭedaṃtātmano bhedasya nyūnatā tāvat dvayaṃ dvayaṃ hrasati yāvat dvituṭikaḥ śivāveśaḥ tatra ādyā tuṭiḥ sarvataḥ pūrṇā dvitīyā sarvajñānakaraṇāviṣṭābhyasyamānā sarvajñatvasarvakartṛtvāya kalpate na tu ādyā*

## v.40
As Śrīkallaṭa says: segment-fall — here the fall-word: she alone the Goddess Śrīmatkālī, mother-being-nature, Bhairava, intuition — enough, by secret-and-unsecret.
The Sanskrit: *yad āha śrīkallaṭaḥ tuṭipāta iti atra pātaśabdaṃ saiva bhagavatī śrīmatkālī mātṛsadbhāvo bhairavaḥ pratibhā ity alaṃ rahasyārahasyanena*

## v.41
Thus from the mantra-great-Lord-segment onward, from the practice of that-and-that, attainment of that-and-that.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ mantramaheśatuṭeḥ prabhṛti tattadabhyāsāt tattatsiddhiḥ*

## v.42
Now here the waking-and-rest states are established: the variegation of the knowable and its object and consciousness — mutually-relative being — that is a state: not of the knowable-merely, nor of consciousness-merely, nor two separate.
The Sanskrit: *athātraiva jāgradādyavasthā nirūpyante tatra vedyasya tadviṣayāyāś ca saṃvido yat vaicitryam anyonyāpekṣaṃ sat sā avasthā na vedyasya kevalasya na cāpi kevalāyāḥ saṃvido na cāpi pṛthak pṛthak dve*

## v.43
There when, by object-rulership, by external-form-nature, shining: waking-state, in-knowable, in-mother, in-measure.
The Sanskrit: *tatra yadādhiṣṭheyatayā bahīrūpatayā bhānaṃ tadā jāgradavasthā meye mātari māne ca*

## v.44
When, however, right there by substratum-form-nature shining — will: dream-state.
The Sanskrit: *yadā tu tatraiva adhiṣṭhānarūpatayā bhānaṃ saṃkalpaḥ tadā svapnāvasthā*

## v.45
When, however, right there by ruler-form-nature shining, by seed-self-nature alone: deep-sleep-state.
The Sanskrit: *yadā tu tatraiva adhiṣṭhātṛrūpatayā bījātmatayaiva bhānaṃ tadā suṣuptāvasthā*

## v.46
These three known-instrument-knower-states, each by the waking-and-rest difference: fourfold said.
The Sanskrit: *imā eva tisraḥ prameyapramāṇapramātravasthāḥ pratyekaṃ jāgradādibhedāt caturvidhā uktāḥ*

## v.47
When, however, in the very knower-rest-gone, from the knower's fullness-turning, by its door fullness-turning shining: fourth-state; she form I-see — of this kind the triad-of-portions crossed, I-see-thus: unmediated knower-nature, autonomy-essence, not by sameness-middleness-farness gives the anointing of knower-instrument-known-ness; from serving the three states: threefold.
The Sanskrit: *yadā tu tasminn eva pramātṛviśrāntigate pramātuḥ pūrṇataunmukhyāt taddvāreṇa pūrṇatonmukhatayā bhānaṃ tadā turyāvasthā sā ca rūpaṃ dṛśāham ity evaṃvidham aṃśatrayam uttīrya paśyāmīti anupāyikā pramātṛtā svātantryasārā naikaṭyamadhyatvadūratvaiḥ pramātṛpramāṇaprameyatābhiṣekaṃ dadatī tadavasthātrayānugrāhakatvāt tribhedā*

## v.48
This four-state-set yogins employ by the words piṇḍa-stha, padastha, rūpastha, rūpa-atīta; the contemplation-wealthy, however: by the words all-auspicious, pervasion, great-pervasion, accumulation.
The Sanskrit: *etad eva avasthācatuṣṭayaṃ piṇḍasthapadastharūpastharūpātītaśabdair yogino vyavaharanti prasaṃkhyānadhanās tu sarvatobhadraṃ vyāptiḥ mahāvyāptiḥ pracaya iti śabdaiḥ*

## v.49
And the corresponding-sense is shown here in the Tantrāloka and the Ślokavārttika.
The Sanskrit: *anvarthaṃ cātra darśitaṃ tantrāloke ślokavārttike ca*

## v.50
And what is contained in all, complete-form — that beyond-fourth, beyond-all, great-accumulation: they establish.
The Sanskrit: *yac ca sarvāntarbhūtaṃ pūrṇarūpaṃ tat turyātītaṃ sarvātītaṃ mahāpracayaṃ ca nirūpayanti*

## v.51
Moreover whose what, when form distinct, stable, continuous — that is waking; its opposite, dream; which is the dissolution-portion's enjoyment, all-knowing: deep sleep; which the knowledge-portion's enjoyment, identifying-with-enjoyable: fourth; mantra-and-rest's: that enjoyment of states, Śiva-non-difference; beyond-fourth, beyond-all.
The Sanskrit: *kiṃ ca yasya yad yadā rūpaṃ sphuṭaṃ sthiram anubandhi tat jāgrat tasyaiva tadviparyayaḥ svapnaḥ yaḥ layākalasya bhogaḥ sarvāvedanaṃ suṣuptaṃ yo vijñānākalasya bhogaḥ bhogyābhinnīkaraṇaṃ turyaṃ mantrādīnāṃ sa bhogaḥ bhāvānāṃ śivābhedas turyātītaṃ sarvātītam*

## v.52
There self-nature-complete-pair: 1 dissolution-portion, 2 knowledge-portion, 3 mantra-those-Lords-great-Lords-class, 4 Śiva, 5 — thus in the fifteenfold five states.
The Sanskrit: *tatra svarūpasakalau 1 pralayākalaḥ 2 vijñānākalaḥ 3 mantratadīśatanmaheśavargaḥ 4 śivaḥ 5 iti pañcadaśabhede pañca avasthāḥ*

## v.53
Self-nature dissolution-portion and-rest-by-order in thirteenfold self-nature knowledge-portion-power; knowledge-portion in elevenfold self-nature mantras those-Lords great-Lords Śiva — in ninefold self-nature mantra-Lords great-Lord power Śiva — in sevenfold self-nature great-Lord-power great-Lord power Śiva — in fivefold self-nature action-power knowledge-power will-power Śiva — in threefold, in non-different too, in Śiva-tattva by structuring as action-knowledge-will-bliss-consciousness-form, the contemplation-wealthy say five-stage-nature.
The Sanskrit: *svarūpaṃ pralayākala ityādikrameṇa trayodaśabhede svarūpaṃ vijñānākalaśaktiḥ vijñānākala ity ekādaśabhede svarūpaṃ mantrāḥ tadīśāḥ maheśāḥ śivaḥ iti navabhede svarūpaṃ mantreśāḥ maheśaḥ śaktiḥ śiva iti saptabhede svarūpaṃ maheśaśaktiḥ maheśaḥ śaktiḥ śiva iti pañcabhede svarūpaṃ kriyāśaktiḥ jñānaśaktiḥ icchāśaktiḥ śiva iti tribhede abhinne 'pi śivatattve kriyājñānecchānandacidrūpakḷptyā prasaṃkhyānayogadhanāḥ pañcapadatvam āhuḥ*

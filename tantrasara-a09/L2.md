# Tantrasāra A09 — L2 READ

## tsa09-p01 — seven knowers, and what "knowable" means
The sixfold books say it best: seven. Śivas, great mantra-lords, mantra-lords, mantras, the knowledge-bodied, the dissolution-bodied, the parted — seven power-owners, seven powers, and through their split, from earth to the chief, fourteen cuts apiece with a fifteenth — one's own shape — in each. "Own shape" means: fit to be known, settled in itself. Where knowers run high-powered, whatever they rest in takes power's shape (the Lower goddess's grace, seven ways for seven powers). Where the owners lead, what rests takes Śiva's powerful shape (the Supreme goddess's grace, seven ways too). Knowers divide because powers do — told parted-first so it's clear: the parted one's power sits in art-as-knowledge (mind, act, senses in their distinct shapes); the dissolution-bodied one's same powers sit objectless, dim; the knowledge-bodied's, art melted, ripening with its traces, wakes as mantra's bright knowing; stripped of those traces, as mantra-lords'; reaching to seize will-power, free by nature, as the great mantra-lord's; as bare will, openly free, as Śiva's. Seven chief powers. And knowers tinged by them differ — since senses end in agents, and only undivided power can be sensified (anything else, regress). Really, though, the knower is one, resting in bright free bliss. "Caitra's eye saw it, Caitra knew it, I know it" — earth known as bare nature shines bare; with all powers on, another nature shines — and so up to Śiva: "I know the universe, kept in Śiva's power, resting in Śiva's nature." A distinct conviction every time. "But if knowable means 'its own body,' then everything is knowable to everyone, knowableness is itself knowable, regress — a world brightly asleep and blind, and knowable-unpredictable both at once. Flawed." Answer: no separately-stated "own body." What rests in knower and knower's power — whatever shape — shines by itself, toward nothing: that is omniscience, and regress with contradiction is miles away. Known by endless knowers, it stays one — one shining for all of them — so no other-minds blockage; and its shape is real, because it works. Seeing another's beloved, you burn for her. Seeing Śiva-nature, jar of rest, you dive in. Seeing a thing all endless knowers rest in, you fill — like watching the dancer. Blue's shape in the knower, whatever rests there, rises from self-shining grasping alone — fifteen-natured, from earth to the chief.

## tsa09-p02 — counting down, and earth's whole ladder (CONDENSED companion)
The parted one, passion-covered, is fifteen ways too — knowable just so far. (Tantrāloka spells it out.) From person to art: thirteen. Then the countdown as knowerhoods set: with-parts nature alone — thirteen for the parted's nature; dissolution-bodied nature in five knowerhoods — eleven; knowledge-bodied in four — nine; mantra in three — seven; mantra-lord in two — five; great-mantra-lord with the Lord alone knowing — three, owner-or-owned; Śiva, sheer light-free-full — none, being full. Principle-splitting under the one uns surpassed-Lord course is what varies the worlds — hells, heavens, Rudra-worlds on the same earth, far apart in nature. For the sub-splits, be curious in the Tantrāloka. Pot by pot works the same. Now earth whole, as knower-and-known: earth's shining undifferentiated is Śiva ("earth is Brahman indeed," say the texts). Earth's great-mantra-lord drives, giving earth's wins; earth's mantra-lord is driven; its mouthpiece, ego-bodied mantra, with earth-yoga past beast-knowledge of Sāṅkhya and the rest, short of the firm station — earth's knowledge-bodiless. By beast-knowledge course with earth-yoga practised, at life's end or death — earth's dissolution-bodied. In sleep, tattva-entry alone raises motley dreams; earth-conceit grasped is earth-with-parts. Power's wax and wane make fourteen here too...

## tsa09-p03 — fifteen everywhere, the instant, and the four states (CONDENSED companion)
Doing one's own work is being a grasper — said, not recounted — so the rich-in-discernment, dwelling with teachers, tell fifteen everywhere by dividing. Fewer thoughts, fewer instants — down, in joys and knowings, to thought-free. People rest in thought and mistake the I-shaped, I-covered, this-spreading, thought-free grasping-ground, unshown, for suffering — as the joy-rested mistake pain; but as thought thins it shines — careful, then, with grasper and grasped, say my teachers. Fifteen standing: as I-natured difference thins, pairs thin two by two, till Śiva-immersion of two instants — first all-full, second entering every knowing-sense, practised, fit for all-knowing, all-doing (not the first). Kallata's "instant-fall": fall is goddess Kālī herself, the Mother of Beings, Bhairava, intuitiveness — secrets and non-secrets enough. From mantra-lord's instant on, practice brings the wins. Now waking and the rest: knowable and its knowing, whatever variety they hold together — that is a state (not the known alone, not knowing alone, not two apart). Shining as supported, outer-shaped: waking — in known, measurer, measure. Shining as supporting shape: resolve — dream. Shining as supporter, seed-natured alone: deep sleep. These three, as known-means-knower states, each in waking shapes four ways. But shining, in gone-to-knower-rest, as fullness-facing through the knower's facing-fullness: the fourth — "form — I see" — crossing "I see"'s three parts, knowerhood without means, autonomy-pith, near, middle, far, baptizing knower-means-known, gracing the three states, threefold...

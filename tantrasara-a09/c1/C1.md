# C1 — Tantrasāra Ahnika 9 (pramātṛ)

## tsa09-p01 — seven knowers, and what knowable means
**SUMMARY.** The sixfold books state it best: seven. Śivas, great mantra-lords, mantra-lords, mantras, the knowledge-bodied, the dissolution-bodied, the parted — seven power-owners, seven powers, and through their split, from earth to the chief, fourteen cuts apiece with a fifteenth (one's own shape) in each. Own shape means: fit to be known, settled in itself. Where knowers run high-powered, whatever they rest in takes power's shape (the Lower goddess's grace, seven ways for seven powers). Where the owners lead, what rests takes Śiva's powerful shape (the Supreme goddess's grace, seven ways too). Knowers divide because powers do — told parted-first so it's clear: the parted one's power sits in art-as-knowledge (intellect, action, senses in their distinct shapes); the dissolution-bodied one's same powers sit objectless, dim; the knowledge-bodied's, art melted, ripening with its traces, wakes as mantra's bright knowing; stripped of those traces, as mantra-lords'; reaching to seize will-power, free by nature, as the great mantra-lord's; as bare will, openly free, as Śiva's. Seven chief powers. And knowers tinged by them differ — since senses end in agents, and only undivided power can be sensified (anything else, regress). Really, though, the knower is one, resting in bright free bliss. Caitra's eye saw it, Caitra knew it, I know it — earth known as bare nature shines bare; with all powers on, another nature shines — and so up to Śiva: I know the universe, kept in Śiva's power, resting in Śiva's nature. A distinct conviction every time. But if knowable means 'its own body,' then everything is knowable to everyone, knowableness is itself knowable, regress — a world brightly asleep and blind, and knowable-unknowable both at once. Flawed. Answer: no separately-stated own body. What rests in knower and knower's power — whatever shape — shines by itself, toward nothing: that is omniscience, and regress with contradiction is miles away. Known by endless knowers, it stays one — one shining for all of them — so no other-minds blockage; and its shape is real, because it works. Seeing another's beloved, you burn for her. Seeing Śiva-nature, jar of rest, you dive in. Seeing a thing all endless knowers rest in, you fill — like watching the dancer. Blue's shape in the knower, whatever rests there, rises from self-shining grasping alone — fifteen-natured, from earth to the chief.
**FUNCTION.** Establishes the seven knowers/seven powers/fifteenfoldness and dissolves the regress objection to knowability.
**KEY TERMS.** pramātṛ (knower), prameya (known), śakti (power), pañcadaśātmaka (fifteen-natured), sarvajñatva (omniscience), anavasthā (regress), viśrāntibhājana (rest-receptacle).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** A08 (kārya-kāraṇa) precedes; A09 teaches knowers; A10 (kalādhvā) follows.

## tsa09-p03 — fifteen everywhere, the instant, and the four states (CONDENSED)
**SUMMARY.** Doing one's own work is being a grasper — said, not recounted — so the rich-in-discernment, dwelling with teachers, tell fifteen everywhere by dividing. Fewer thoughts, fewer instants — down, in joys and knowings, to thought-free. People rest in thought and mistake the I-shaped, I-covered, this-spreading, thought-free grasping-ground, unshown, for suffering — as the joy-rested mistake pain; but as thought thins it shines — careful, then, with grasper and grasped, say my teachers. Fifteen standing: as I-natured difference thins, pairs thin two by two, till Śiva-immersion of two instants — first all-full, second all-knowing-sense-entered-practiced for omniscience-omnipotence (not the first). Kallata: instant-fall — fall-word = goddess Kālī herself, the Mother of Beings, Bhairava, intuitiveness; enough of secret-nonself. From mantra-lord's instant on, practice brings the wins. Now waking and the rest: knowable and its knowing, whatever variety they hold together — that is a state (not the known alone, not knowing alone, not two apart). Shining as supported, outer-shaped: waking — in known, measurer, measure. Shining as supporting shape: resolve — dream. Shining as supporter, seed-natured alone: deep sleep. These three, as known-means-knower states, each in waking shapes four ways. But shining, in gone-to-knower-rest, as fullness-facing through the knower's facing-fullness: the fourth — form, I see — crossing I see's three parts, knowerhood without means, autonomy-essence, near, middle, far, baptizing knower-means-known, gracing the three states, threefold.
**FUNCTION.** Shows fifteenfold everywhere, the tuṭi (two-instant immersion), and the four states (waking/dream/sleep/fourth).
**KEY TERMS.** tuṭi (instant), tuṭipāta (instant-fall = Kālī/Bhairava), jāgrat/svapna/suṣupta/turya (four states), pūrṇatonmukhatā (fullness-facing).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** P2 counted down + earth case; P3 shows fifteen everywhere + tuṭi + states.

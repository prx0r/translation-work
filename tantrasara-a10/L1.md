# Tantrasāra A10 — L1 controlled

## tsa10-p01
Stated so far is principle-course. (10.1) But digit-course is described: therein, as in worlds whatever following form is principle, so it was stated — so in principles, class-wise whatever following form — that is digit — since unable to bear one-form grasping. (10.2) Namely: in earth is cessation — since creation turns back. (10.3) In water-to-chief-end class is establishment, through causing nourishing-filling as cause. (10.4) In person-to-Māyā-end is knowledge, in knowable-concealment through consciousness-excess. (10.5) In pure-knowledge-to-power-end is tranquil, from cover-wave-calming. (10.6) This itself is the four eggs, named earthy, natural, Māyā-made, powered. (10.7) Since of earth-to-power principles here, through abiding, in power-principle as far as mutual touch is found — and of touch there is with-impacts — so far egghood is proper. (10.8) In Śiva-principle is beyond-tranquil; of it, through teaching-contemplation-worship-and-the-rest, being grasped. (10.9) Autonomous is the supreme principle; therein too whatever is immeasurable — that is beyond-digit. (10.10) Thus are five alone digits, thirty-six principles. (10.11) As: knowableness twofold, through gross-subtle-ness — hence ten. (10.12) Instrument-ness twofold — pure and agentness-touching — hence ten. (10.13) Through instrument-subordinated-agentness-manifestness, five; from pure-agentness, five; through melted-divisionness in blooming-facedness, five; all-limitation-void Śiva-principle thirty-sixth. (10.14) That, when taught or contemplated — whatever is its establishment-seat — that is thirty-seventh; in it too being contemplated is thirty-eighth. Nor is there regress: of it being contemplated — of unlimited-autonomy-endowed — in making-knowable, in thirty-seventh alone is ending; but thirty-sixth, through all-principle-transcended-ness, conceivable-limited — thus is the five-digit-course. (10.15) Up to knowledge-bodiless-end is self-digit; to Lord-end is knowledge-digit; the rest is Śiva-digit — thus is the three-principle-course. (10.16) Thus nine-principle-and-the-rest too one should infer. (10.17) Measure-portion-going, through gross-subtle-supreme-formed-ness, is threefold — world-principle-digit-self-course-difference; through measurer-rest likewise threefold.

# Tantrasara-a10 — L2 READ

## v.1
Thus, for now, the tattva-path is stated.
The Sanskrit: *uktas tāvat tattvādhvā*

## v.2
The kalā-path, though, is now delineated: as in worlds something goes along as form — that is a tattva; so in the tattvas, class by class, whatever goes along as form — that is kalā, since it endures one-form classifications.
The Sanskrit: *kalādyadhvā tu nirūpyate tatra yathā bhuvaneṣu anugāmi kiṃcid rūpaṃ tattvam ity uktam tathā tattveṣu vargaśo yat anugāmi rūpaṃ tat kalā ekarūpakalanāsahiṣṇutvāt*

## v.3
As in earth, nivṛtti turns back — from which the tattva-creation comes, it is said.
The Sanskrit: *tad yathā pṛthivyāṃ nivṛttiḥ nivartate yatas tattvasarga iti*

## v.4
In the class beginning with water, pratiṣṭhā as cause — by nourishment and filling.
The Sanskrit: *jalādipradhānānte varge pratiṣṭhā kāraṇatayāpyāyanapūraṇakāritvāt*

## v.5
In the class ending with māyā-of-puruṣa, vidyā — by the excess of consciousness over the disappearance of the knowable.
The Sanskrit: *pumādimāyānte vidyā vedyatirobhāve saṃvidādhikyāt*

## v.6
In the class ending with Śuddha-Vidyā-Śakti, śāntā — by the subsidence of the kañcuka-waves.
The Sanskrit: *śuddhavidyādiśaktyante śāntā kañcukataraṃgopaśamāt*

## v.7
This very four-egg-group is the pārthiva-prākṛta-māyīya-śakti-named group.
The Sanskrit: *etad eva aṇḍacatuṣṭayaṃ pārthivaprākṛtamāyīyaśāktābhidham*

## v.8
In these, of the earth-and-rest powers, by their standing in the power-tattva as far as mutual contact exists, and contact's being with-obstacle — so much is warranted as egg-hood.
The Sanskrit: *pṛthivyādiśaktīnām atra avasthānena śaktitattve yāvat parasparśo vidyate sparśasya ca sapratighatvam iti tāvati yuktam aṇḍatvam*

## v.9
In the Śiva-tattva, śāntātiśayā — because of the auspiciousness of its teaching-contemplation-arcā-beginning.
The Sanskrit: *śivatattve śāntātītā tasyopadeśabhāvanārcādau kalyamānatvāt*

## v.10
The autonomous, indeed, is the supreme tattva — that too, what is immeasurable, is beyond-kalā.
The Sanskrit: *svatantraṃ tu paraṃ tattvaṃ tatrāpi yat aprameyaṃ tat kalātītam*

## v.11
Thus exactly five kalās make the thirty-six tattvas.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ pañcaiva kalāḥ ṣaṭtriṃśattattvāni*

## v.12
Thus: prameyatva is twofold — gross-subtle; that makes ten.
The Sanskrit: *tathāhi prameyatvaṃ dvidhā sthūlasūkṣmatvena iti daśa*

## v.13
Karaṇatva is twofold — pure and agent-touching; that makes ten.
The Sanskrit: *karaṇatvaṃ dvidhā śuddhaṃ kartṛtāsparśi ca iti daśa*

## v.14
By the distinctness of instrument-subordinate-agent-status: five pure-agent-status; five by undivided-division in expansion-facing; the Śiva-tattva, free of exhaustive-limitation, makes the thirty-sixth.
The Sanskrit: *karaṇatopasarjanakartṛbhāvasphuṭatvāt pañca śuddhakartṛbhāvāt pañca vigalitavibhāgatayā vikāsonmukhatve pañca sarvāvacchedaśūnyaṃ śivatattvaṃ ṣaṭtriṃśam*

## v.15
And when it is taught or contemplated — that which is its station-place is the thirty-seventh; when that too is contemplated, the thirty-eighth. And there is no infinite regress of the to-be-contemplated: in the undivided-self-autonomy-joined knowing-making it ends in the thirty-seventh itself — the thirty-sixth, having transcended all tattvas, being comprehensible-within-limit; such is the five-kalā rule.
The Sanskrit: *tad yadā upadiśyate bhāvyate vā yat tatpratiṣṭhāpadam tat saptatriṃśam tasminn api bhāvyamāne aṣṭātriṃśam na ca anavasthā tasya bhāvyamānasya anavacchinnasvātantryayogino vedyīkaraṇe saptatriṃśa eva paryavasānāt ṣaṭtriṃśaṃ tu sarvatattvottīrṇatayā saṃbhāvyāvacchedam iti pañcakalāvidhiḥ*

## v.16
The Ātma-kalā up to Vijñāna-kalā, Īśa-ending the Vidyā-kalā, the rest the Śiva-kalā: such the three-tattva rule.
The Sanskrit: *vijñānākalaparyantam ātmakalā īśāntaṃ vidyākalā śiṣṭaṃ śivakalā iti tritattvavidhiḥ*

## v.17
Thus the ninety-tattva-and-rest too should be inferred — it is said.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ navatattvādy api ūhayet iti*

## v.18
Going-with-the-knowable-part — by gross-subtle-other-nature triply: the world-tattva-kalā-path-distinction is triply named by mātṛ-viśrānti; likewise triply: in its being-authority, the padā-dhvā; in the authority's agitation-wave-subsiding, the mantra-dhvā; in its subsiding, the full-knowership, the varṇa-dhvā — it, and this, in such viśrānti, becomes one whose own-nature is gained: thus the one's sixfoldness is warranted.
The Sanskrit: *meyāṃśagāmī sthūlasūkṣmapararūpatvāt trividho bhuvanatattvakalātmādhvabhedaḥ mātṛviśrāntyā tathaiva trividhaḥ tatra pramāṇatāyāṃ padādhvā pramāṇasyaiva kṣobhataraṃgaśāmyattāyāṃ mantrādhvā tatpraśame pūrṇapramātṛtāyāṃ varṇādhvā sa eva ca asau tāvati viśrāntyā labdhasvarūpo bhavati iti ekasyaiva ṣaḍvidhatvaṃ yuktam*

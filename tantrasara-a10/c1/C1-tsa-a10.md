# C1 — tsa-a10

## SUMMARY

Rite declared: the tattva-path stands stated. The kalā-path is delineated against it: whatever goes along as form class-wise — classification-enduring — is kalā. Nivṛtti turns back in earth, from which tattva-creation comes. Water-class: pratiṣṭhā as cause, by nourishment and filling. Māyā-end: vidyā, by consciousness exceeding the knowable's disappearance. Śakti-end: śāntā, by subsidence of the kañcuka-waves. The four-egg group bears the pārthiva-prākṛta-māyīya-śakti name. Egg-hood warranted only to mutual-contact-with-obstacle in the power-tattva. Śiva-tattva: śāntātiśayā, auspicious from its teaching-contemplation-arcā. The autonomous supreme tattva's immeasurable part is beyond kalā. Exactly five kalās make thirty-six tattvas. Prameyatva twofold — gross-subtle — makes ten. Karaṇatva twofold — pure, agent-touching — makes ten. Five pure-agent, five expansion-facing, Śiva-tattva free of exhaustive-limitation: the thirty-sixth. No infinite regress: knowing-making ends in the thirty-seventh itself, the thirty-sixth transcendent-comprehensible — such the five-kalā rule. Three-tattva rule: Ātma up to Vijñāna-kalā, Īśa-ended Vidyā, the rest Śiva. Ninety-tattva-and-rest inferred too — it is said. One path sixfold: mātṛviśrānti's triple distinction, padā-mantra-varṇa-dhvā — own-nature gained, warranted.

## FUNCTION

Delineates the kalā-path against the tattva-path, assigns class-presiding powers, counts the thirty-six, blocks the regress, and closes in the one path's sixfoldness.

## KEY TERMS

tattvādhva/kalādhvā (ground-path vs measure-path); nivṛtti (turning-back as sarga-source); pratiṣṭhā (water-class cause); aṇḍatva (egg-hood warranted to contact); pañcakalāvidhi (five-kalā rule); anavasthā (the blocked regress); ṣaḍvidhatva (the closing sixfoldness).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Follows the tattvādhvā declaration (u1); the kalā arithmetic (u11-14), regress-block (u15), and dhvā-sixfoldness (u16-18) complete the scheme before the next prakaraṇa.

## EXPLANATION

Kalā differs from tattva only in being classification-enduring and class-wise; the powers presiding at each class-end (pratiṣṭhā, vidyā, śāntā) are causes of nourishment, consciousness-excess, and wave-subsidence respectively. Egg-hood runs exactly to mutual contact with obstacle. The count (10+10+5+5+1+...) reaches thirty-six, and contemplation's station would regress — but knowing-making, joined to undivided autonomy, ends at the thirty-seventh: no anavasthā. The five kalās regroup three-tattva, extend by ūhāya to ninety, and the single path discloses six facets closing in own-nature gained.

## BOUNDARY

The arithmetic is stated, not derived; ūhāya (u17) marks inference where enumeration stops; the regress-block rests on anavacchinnasvātantrya-yoga, asserted.

# C1 — Tantrasāra Ahnika 10 (kalādhvā)

## tsa10-p01 — digits, eggs, and the courses of word and mantra
**SUMMARY.** Principle-course: done. Digit-course: as a shape running through worlds makes a principle, a shape running through principle-classes makes a digit — what no single grasp can hold. Earth holds cessation, turning creation back. Water-to-chief holds establishment, feeding and filling as cause. Person-to-Māyā holds knowing, excess awareness in hidden knowables. Pure-knowing-to-power holds the tranquil, covers' waves gone down. Four eggs: earthen, natural, Māyā-made, power-made — egg holding just so far as things touch with impact into power's own principle. In Śiva's principle: past-the-tranquil, grasped through teaching, seeing, worship. Past that, the supreme is free; whatever in it no measure holds is past digits. Five digits, thirty-six principles: knowables gross-and-subtle make ten; instruments pure and agent-touching make ten; agents under instruments, five; agents pure, five; divisions melted into blooming, five; void of every bound, Śiva thirty-sixth. Teach or contemplate it, and where it stands is thirty-seventh; contemplate there, thirty-eighth — no regress, since contemplating the boundlessly free ends, as made-knowable, at thirty-seven, while thirty-six stays the bound you conceive by going past everything. To knowledge-bodiless: self's digit; to the Lord: knowing's digit; past that: Śiva's — three principles; nine and the rest likewise. Going by measures runs threefold, gross, subtle, supreme — worlds, principles, digits, selves differing — and by the measurer's rest threefold too: measured, the word-course; the measure's waves calming, the mantra-course; calmed... (close per source).
**FUNCTION.** Establishes the kalādhvā (digit-course): five digits grouping 36 principles, the 37/38 anti-regress argument, and the word/mantra courses.
**KEY TERMS.** kalā (digit), aṇḍa (egg), tattvādhvā (principle-course), kalādhvā (digit-course), prameyatva (knowableness), karaṇatva (instrument-ness), saptatriṃśa/aṣṭātriṃśa (37/38), padādhvā (word-course), mantrādhvā (mantra-course).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** A09 (pramātṛ) precedes; A10 teaches digits; A11 (śaktipāta) follows.
**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in seven steps. (1) Principle-course done; digit-course: shape running through principle-classes = digit (can't bear one-form grasping). (2) Four eggs (earth→cessation; water→chief→establishment; person→Māyā→knowledge; pure-knowing→power→tranquil) + egg-rule (mutual touch with impacts). (3) In Śiva-principle: beyond-tranquil (grasped via teaching/contemplation/worship); supreme free; immeasurable = beyond-digit. (4) Five digits, 36 principles: knowables gross/subtle = 10; instruments pure/agent-touching = 10; agents under instruments = 5; agents pure = 5; melted-division blooming = 5; Śiva 36th. (5) 37/38 anti-regress: taught/contemplated establishment-seat = 37th; contemplated therein = 38th; no regress (contemplated unlimited-autonomy making-knowable ends at 37; 36th conceivable-limited as all-transcended). (6) Three-principle course (self/knowledge/Śiva digit); nine likewise. (7) Measure-portion-going threefold (gross/subtle/supreme) = world/principle/digit/self course-difference; measurer-rest threefold: word-course (measureness) / mantra-course (agitation-wave-calming) / calming (close per source).

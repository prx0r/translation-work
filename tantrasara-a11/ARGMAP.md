# Tantrasāra A11 — ARGMAPs (mandatory before READ; reconstruct argument, not sentence)

## tsa11-p01 (11.1–11.2)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta framing voice.
- question: Why must initiation be taught next, and who is entitled to it?
- premises: (1) Prior teaching serves some directly for liberation in compendium manner. (2) Others need it only via its use in the coming initiation-teaching.
- inference: Initiation must therefore be taught; the entitlement question forces examination of śaktipāta.
- conclusion: Śaktipāta is the topic of this ahnika.
- unresolved: none (framing passage).

## tsa11-p02 (11.3)
- speaker: Pūrvapakṣins (three theories) + Abhinava's replies interleaved.
- question: What causes śaktipāta — knowledge, karma, or karma-equilibrium?
- premises: (a) Jñāna-theory: saṃsāra is ignorance-rooted (from knowledge-absence); on its removal, descent via knowledge-rise. Replies: samyak-jñānodaya itself is vikṛta (promissory); if karma-produced → bhogatva contingency like karma-fruit; if enjoyer admits descent → atiprasaṅga; if Lord's-will-caused → anyonyāśraya + vaiyarthya + rāga in the Lord. (b) Karma-sāmya theory: mutual obstruction of equal opposed karmas → equilibrium → descent. Replies: succession excludes opposition; even opposed, another unopposed karma gives bhoga; if unopposed karma stalls → instant dehapāta; jātyāyuṣ/bhoga split arbitrary (what would descending power fear from the bhoga-giver?).
- inference: Every proposed hetu collapses under its own consequences.
- conclusion: None of the three causes works.
- unresolved: "vikṛta" of samyak-jñānodaya asserted but not yet shown (iti vācyam — promissory).

## tsa11-p03 (11.4)
- speaker: Pūrvapakṣa (mala-paripāka) + Abhinava.
- question: Is descent at stain-maturation — and what are its nature and cause?
- premises: The maturation claim; the unanswered kiṃsvarūpa / kiṃnimitta; the hetu-list (vairāgya, dharma-viśeṣa, viveka, satsevā, satprāpti, devapūjā) refuted as causes.
- inference: The theory cannot state its own nature, cause, or means.
- conclusion: For dualists (bhedavādin), everything is incoherent.
- unresolved: Nature/cause questions left hanging — answered only by the coming siddhānta.

## tsa11-p04 (11.5–11.7)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta.
- question: How is descent possible without the refuted traps?
- premises: (1) Lord's self-concealment play → paśu/pudgala/aṇu, no deśa/kāla/svabhāva contradiction. (2) Concealment-removal → sudden/gradual own-nature attainment = śaktipāta-pātra. (3) Supreme Śiva = sheer autonomy = descender. (4) Fruit determines conditioning: svarūpaprathā → nirapekṣa; bhoga-eagerness → karma-conditioned; lokottara-bhoga → same descent via Rudra/Viṣṇu/Brahma (Māyā-womb lords, Lord's-will-impelled) blocking lower enjoyment. (5) "Why this person?" dissolved: Lord shines thus; no "person" over and above being. (6) Ninefold typology (tīvra/madhya/manda × utkarṣa/mādhya-sthya/nikarṣa): utmost-intense → dehapāta = parameśatā; middle-intense → prātibha-jñāna, sacrament-free prātibha-guru; gradation persists via icchāvaicitrya; pratibhāṃśa decisive over others.
- inference: Autonomy grounds both the unconditioned root and the conditioned/both variants; gradation is compatible with autonomy via will-diversity.
- conclusion: Autonomy-grounded ninefold typology of descent.
- unresolved: nirbhitti/sabhitti teacher-forms cited to scriptures (external-knowledge).

## tsa11-p05 (11.8–11.10)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta.
- question: Who is true vs false teacher, and how does one reach the true?
- premises: Muktaśivas' sṛṣṭi/laya functions; mild-intense descent → yiyāsā toward sadguru; toward asadguru only tirobhāva; false→true movement itself from descent. Sadguru = full śāstra-tattva knowledge = Bhairava himself; yogin liberates by practiced knowledge; fitness-charms (saubhāgya) useless. Asadguru = everyone else.
- inference: Teacher-discernment and the movement toward truth are both descent-effects, not merit-effects.
- conclusion: Teacher criterion stated.
- unresolved: none.

## tsa11-p06 (11.11–11.13)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta.
- question: What initiations are given, and what fates follow by grade?
- premises: Jñāna-lakṣaṇā dīkṣā → instant liberation even alive (glance/speech/scripture/conduct/food varieties). Prāṇaviyojikā only at death (deferred detail). Utmost-three-intense: weak conviction → dehānte Śiva via conviction-maturation. Middle-moderate: bhoga-desiring yet fit vessel → this-body enjoyment → dehānte Śiva.
- inference: Grade determines timing/mode, never the outcome for the entitled.
- conclusion: Grade→fate mapping.
- unresolved: Prāṇaviyojikā rite deferred (iti vakṣyāmaḥ → A15/A19).

## tsa11-p07 (11.14–11.16)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta.
- question: What of deficient-moderate, and what counts as "principal" descent?
- premises: Deficient-moderate → other-body enjoyment → Śiva-hood. Moderate threefold; bhoga-predominant → mild (mantra-yoga means ending in liberation → descent-nature). Gradation → threefold within.
- inference: Even the mildest HS-linked grade terminates in liberation, so it qualifies as descent.
- conclusion: Mukhya śaktipāta defined.
- unresolved: none.

## tsa11-p08 (11.17–11.19)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta.
- question: What of outsiders (Vaiṣṇava etc.), and who may teach?
- premises: Royal-favor grace has no mokṣa-end → out of scope. Śiva-śakti presence everywhere (ghorā/ghoratarā, not jyeṣṭhā). Varied descent divides by gradation: samayin/five-streams Vaiṣṇava → supreme ṣaḍardha entitlement; leap-course; akrama. Lower-lower teachers non-entitled even to maṇḍala-showing; upper teacher enlivens lower by fullness → all-entitled. Five teacher names; full knowledge = supreme (no dīkṣā without).
- inference: Outsider paths are mapped but not treated; teaching authority = fullness, not office.
- conclusion: Outsider mapping + teacher plenitude.
- unresolved: Srotaḥpañcaka/ṣaḍardha specifics assumed known (external-knowledge).

## tsa11-p09 (11.20–11.22)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta + objector (nanu).
- question: How do yogin and multiple teachers work — and may a teacher ever be abandoned?
- premises: Yogin fit if immediately fruitful via means-teaching; fit in knowledge/liberation; many teachers allowed; conduct by knowledge-rank; abandoning fully-knowing teacher = expiation. Objection: silent/perverse teacher? Reply: full knowledge → no rāga; silence from pupil-side unfitness/distrust → serve, don't abandon.
- inference: The teacher's defect-appearance is diagnostically pupil-side given the full-knowledge premise.
- conclusion: Service rule, no-abandonment ruling.
- unresolved: none (practical ruling).

## tsa11-p10 (11.23–11.25)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (close of ahnika).
- question: What is grace's final status, what is concealment, and is recovery possible?
- premises: Descent = anugraha-cause, unconditioned (karma-regard denied). Tirobhāva fruit = dense suffering-delusion; awakened/deluded conduct mirror (each perishes for the other when censured; censured awakened conduct drowns). No concealment for descent-arisen; karma-regard denied again. Will-diversity: this-body-only suffering, as with double-dealers/apostates. Concealed one rejoins via self or via dead-through-kin/teacher grace.
- inference: Grace and concealment are both will-expressions; neither is karma-mechanical; recovery is always open.
- conclusion: "I am the Lord" — see the self unbroken (close).
- unresolved: none; ahnika closes.

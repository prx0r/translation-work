# Tantrasara-a11 — L2 READ

## v.1
Thus, as far as this has been stated — direct for someone's attainment-of-release; as-stated-summary-manner it holds for someone, from the application's coming in the to-be-told initiation; so initiation-and-rest is to be told.
The Sanskrit: *tatra yāvat idam uktam tat sākṣāt kasyacit apavargāptaye yathoktasaṃgrahanītyā bhavati kasyacit vakṣyamāṇadīkṣāyām upayogagamanāt iti dīkṣādikaṃ vaktavyam*

## v.2
Therein: who is qualified? — for that determination the power-descent is examined.
The Sanskrit: *tatra kaḥ adhikārī iti nirūpaṇārthaṃ śaktipāto vicāryate*

## v.3
Therein some say: from absence of knowledge, saṃsāra rooted in ignorance; from its departure's knowledge-arising, power-descent. To them: knowledge-arising alone is distorted — to be said. If act-produced, [it behaves] like act-fruit; in enjoyment-ness, in the enjoymenter too — the two possibilities of power-descent are excessive consequence. In being-caused-by-the-Lord's-will, however: knowledge-arising's mutual-dependence and futility; in the Lord, passion-and-rest-issue. Of two contradictory acts of equal force, mutual-obstruction: act-equivalence — then power-descent? No: in being-sequential, from impossibility-of-contradiction; even in contradiction, of the uncontradicted act's enjoyment-giving issue — in non-contradicted act's non-occurrence, just-then body-death issue: birth-and-lifespan-giving act is not obstructed; enjoyment-giving is. If asked why: in that-act's-presence, if power might-fall, then she — of the enjoyment-giver — why would she fear?
The Sanskrit: *tatra kecit āhuḥ jñānābhāvāt ajñānamūlaḥ saṃsāraḥ tadapagame jñānodayāt śaktipāta iti teṣāṃ samyak jñānodaya eva vikṛta iti vācyam karmajanyatve karmaphalavat bhogatvaprasaṅge bhogini ca śaktipātābhyupagatau atiprasaṅgaḥ īśvarecchānimittatve tu jñānodayasya anyonyāśrayatā vaiyarthyaṃ ca īśvare rāgādiprasaṅgaḥ viruddhayoḥ karmaṇoḥ samabalayoḥ anyonyapratibandhe karmasāmyaṃ tataḥ śaktipāta iti cet na kramikatve virodhāyogāt virodhe 'pi anyasya aviruddhasya karmaṇo bhogadānaprasaṅgāt aviruddhakarmāpravṛttau tadaiva dehapātaprasaṅgāt jātyāyuṣpradaṃ karma na pratibadhyate bhogapradam eva tu pratibadhyate iti cet kutaḥ tatkarmasadbhāve yadi śaktiḥ patet tarhi sā bhogapradāt kiṃ bibhiyāt*

## v.4
Now: in impurity-ripening, power-descent — of-what-nature, and its mark? By this the difference-doctrinaires' incoherent counter — dispassion, special-duty, discernment, true-service, true-attainment, god-worship and-rest-as-reason.
The Sanskrit: *atha malaparipāke śaktipātaḥ so 'pi kiṃsvarūpaḥ kiṃ ca tasya nimittam iti etena vairāgyaṃ dharmaviśeṣo vivekaḥ satsevā satprāptiḥ devapūjā ityādihetuḥ pratyukta iti bhedavādināṃ sarvam asamañjasam*

## v.5
In the doctrine-of-two-inseparables-of-free-Lord, however, it is sustained: as the Supreme-Lord by self-covering-sport attains cattle-being, individual, atom — no place-time-form-difference-contradiction of him; likewise, by withdrawal-of-self-hiding self-recovery — suddenly or in-order, taking-refuge, the atom is called vessel-of-power-descent. Made-merely-of-freedom this Supreme-Siva is power's causer-to-descend. Thus independent is the power-descent: who[se is] fruit-of-self-manifestation; but who[se] is one-eager-for-enjoyment, he is act-dependent; of one-eager-for-supernatural-enjoyment, he just is power-descent. Qualified-by-maya-womb-inspired-by-Lord's-will, through-door-of-Rudra-Vishnu-Brahma-and-rest, mantra-and-rest-form-ness, maya-puru-distinction, puru-kala, puru-nature, puru-intellect distinctions, and other fruit obtaining — lower-tattva-enjoyment binds. One-eager-for-both: in-enjoyment act-dependent; in-release independent-of-it — thus both-dependent-and-independent.
The Sanskrit: *svatantraparameśādvayavāde tu upapadyate etat yathāhi parameśvaraḥ svarūpācchādanakrīḍayā paśuḥ pudgalo 'ṇuḥ sampannaḥ na ca tasya deśakālasvarūpabhedavirodhaḥ tadvat svarūpasthaganavinivṛttyā svarūpapratyāpattiṃ jhaṭiti vā krameṇa vā samāśrayan śaktipātapātram aṇuḥ ucyate svātantryamātrasāraś ca asau paramaśivaḥ śakteḥ pātayitā iti nirapekṣa eva śaktipāto yaḥ svarūpaprathāphalaḥ yas tu bhogotsukasya sa karmāpekṣaḥ lokottararūpabhogotsukasya tu sa eva śaktipātaḥ parameśvarecchāpreritamāyāgarbhādhikārīyarudraviṣṇubrahmādidvāreṇa mantrādirūpatvaṃ māyāpuṃvivekaṃ puṃskalāvivekaṃ puṃprakṛtivivekaṃ puṃbuddhivivekam anyac ca phalaṃ prasnuvānaḥ tadadharatattvabhogaṃ pratibadhnāti bhogamokṣobhayotsukasya bhoge karmāpekṣo mokṣe tu tannirapekṣaḥ iti sāpekṣanirapekṣaḥ*

## v.6
Nor is it to-be-said: in-someone-just man power-descent. He just is the Supreme-Lord — thus shines. In-being-truth: who is this man, by-name — that in-purport object-made-impulse, this.
The Sanskrit: *na ca vācyaṃ kasmāt kasmiṃścid eva puṃsi śaktipāta iti sa eva parameśvaraḥ tathā bhāti iti satattve ko 'sau pumān nāma yaduddeśena viṣayakṛtā codanā iyam*

## v.7
And this power-descent ninefold in intense-middle-mild, by-superiority-equality-inferiority; again from-triplicity. Therein: from-superior-intense just-then in-body-death Supremeness; from-middle-intense, one-independent-of-scripture-teacher, of-own-conviction, intuitive-knowledge-arising — in-which-arising outer-consecration without-alone giving-enjoyment-and-release, intuitive teacher, thus said. For his no-conception-of-samayya-and-rest-any, even-herein degree-existence from-wills-manifoldness — thus, being-even in-intuitive-ness, dependence-on-scripture-and-rest would-be for-agreement — even. Thus many-fissures-and-walls-division of-teacher and of-intuitive-one in-scriptures is-said; in-any-case intuitive-part stronger in-its-presence, of-others from-unqualification.
The Sanskrit: *sa cāyaṃ śaktipāto navadhā tīvramadhyamandasya utkarṣamādhyasthyanikarṣaiḥ punas traividhyāt tatra utkṛṣṭatīvrāt tadaiva dehapāte parameśatā madhyatīvrāt śāstrācāryānapekṣiṇaḥ svapratyayasya prātibhajñānodayaḥ yadudaye bāhyasaṃskāraṃ vinaiva bhogāpavargapradaḥ prātibho gurur ity ucyate tasya hi na samayyādikalpanā kācit atrāpi tāratamyasadbhāvaḥ icchāvaicitryāt iti saty api prātibhatve śāstrādyapekṣā saṃvādāya syād api iti nirbhittisabhittyādibahubhedatvam ācāryasya prātibhasyāgameṣu uktam sarvathā pratibhāṃśo balīyān tatsaṃnidhau anyeṣām anadhikārāt*

## v.8
As difference-view, as in-presence-of-eternal-Siva of-liberated-Sivas in-creation-dissolution-and-rest-functions: from-mild-intense power-descent, good-guru-subject wish-to-be-attained becomes; in-bad-guru-subject, concealment just; from-bad-guru-ness, good-guru-approach from-power-descent indeed.
The Sanskrit: *bhedadarśana iva anādiśivasaṃnidhau muktaśivānāṃ sṛṣṭilayādikṛtyeṣu mandatīvrāt śaktipātāt sadguruviṣayā yiyāsā bhavati asadguruviṣayāyāṃ tu tirobhāva eva asadgurutas tu sadgurugamanaṃ śaktipātād eva*

## v.9
Good-guru is full-of-all-this-scripture-truth-knowledge, directly Bhagavan-Bhairava-Bhattacharya — indeed of-yogis-even by-own-studied-knowledge in-freeness-giving; therein of-qualifiability like-good-fortune-beauty-and-rest-possession from-non-application.
The Sanskrit: *sadgurus tu samastaitacchāstratattvajñānapūrṇaḥ sākṣāt bhagavadbhairavabhaṭṭāraka eva yogino 'pi svabhyastajñānatayaiva mocakatve tatra yogyatvasya saubhāgyalāvaṇyādimattvasyevānupayogāt*

## v.10
Bad-guru is other — all just.
The Sanskrit: *asadgurus tu anyaḥ sarva eva*

## v.11
Thus-wisher's of-guru knowledge-named initiation obtains, by-which at-once freed becomes, living-even herein: from-seeing, from-telling, from-scripture-teaching, from-conduct-showing, from-charity-giving and-rest kinds.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ yiyāsuḥ guroḥ jñānalakṣaṇāṃ dīkṣāṃ prāpnoti yayā sadya eva mukto bhavati jīvann api atra avalokanāt kathanāt śāstrasambodhanāt caryādarśanāt carudānāt ityādayo bhedāḥ*

## v.12
Of-practitioner or just-then at-once life-releasing initiation obtains; she but in-death-moment-alone to-be-done — thus we-shall-tell-thus.
The Sanskrit: *abhyāsavato vā tadānīṃ sadya eva prāṇaviyojikāṃ dīkṣāṃ labhate sā tu maraṇakṣaṇa eva kāryā iti vakṣyāma iti*

## v.13
Intense threefold from-superior-middle power-descent: even-though-initiated, of-own-self in-Siva-ness not so firm-conviction becomes; by-ripening-order-of-conviction but at-body's-end Siva just. From-middle-intense but eager-for-Siva-ness even enjoyment-seeking becomes — thus just-so in-initiation vessel-of-knowledge, he and gained-by-yoga-practice by-this-alone by-body enjoyment-having at-body's-end Siva just.
The Sanskrit: *tīvrās tridhā utkṛṣṭamadhyāt śaktipātāt kṛtadīkṣāko 'pi svātmanaḥ śivatāyāṃ na tathā dṛḍhapratipattiḥ bhavati pratipattiparipākakrameṇa tu dehānte śiva eva madhyamadhyāt tu śivatotsuko 'pi bhogaprepsuḥ bhavati iti tathaiva dīkṣāyāṃ jñānabhājanam sa ca yogābhyāsalabdham anenaiva dehena bhogaṃ bhuktvā dehānte śiva eva*

## v.14
From-inferior-middle but by-another-body enjoyment-having Siva-hood attains — thus.
The Sanskrit: *nikṛṣṭamadhyāt tu dehāntareṇa bhogaṃ bhuktvā śivatvam eti iti*

## v.15
Middle but threefold enjoyment-eagerness: when become-primary, then mildness by-Lord-mantra-yoga-means — since therein eagerness of-Lord-mantra-yoga-and-rest, since ending-in-release — therefore power-descent-nature.
The Sanskrit: *madhyas tu tridhā bhogotsukatā yadā pradhānabhūtā tadā mandatvaṃ pārameśvaramantrayogopāyatayā yatas tatra autsukyam pārameśamantrayogādeś ca yato mokṣaparyantatvam ataḥ śaktipātarūpatā*

## v.16
Even-therein from-degree triplicity — thus this chief power-descent.
The Sanskrit: *tatrāpi tāratamyāt traividhyam ity eṣa mukhyaḥ śaktipātaḥ*

## v.17
Of-Vaishnavas-and-rest but like-king's-grace not ending-in-release — thus no distinction herein.
The Sanskrit: *vaiṣṇavādīnāṃ tu rājānugrahavat na mokṣāntatā iti na iha vivecanam*

## v.18
Siva-Power-abode but everywhere — said. She most-supreme is not, but fierce, fiercer or — this power-descent manifold even from-degree-manifoldness is-differentiated: some standing-in-Vaishnavas-and-rest by-samayya-and-rest-order in-five-streams attained-ripening supreme-authority-in-six-and-a-half-scriptures attains; other but by-transgression-order by-infinite-difference some non-sequential — thus. Therefore standing-in-lower-higher-doctrine teachers even herein in-mere-mandala-seeing even unqualified; standing-in-higher-doctrine but teacher lower-higher-doctrine on-the-contrary approves from-fullness — thus qualified-for-all.
The Sanskrit: *śivaśaktyadhiṣṭhānaṃ tu sarvatra iti uktam sā paraṃ jyeṣṭhā na bhavati api tu ghorā ghoratarā vā sa eṣa śaktipāto vicitro 'pi tāratamyavaicitryāt bhidyate kaścid vaiṣṇavādisthaḥ samayyādikrameṇa srotaḥpañcake ca prāptaparipākaḥ sarvottīrṇabhagavatṣaḍardhaśāstraparamādhikāritām eti anyas tu ullaṅghanakrameṇa anantabhedena ko 'pi akramam iti ata eva adharādharaśāsanasthā guravo 'pi iha maṇḍalamātradarśane 'pi anadhikāriṇaḥ ūrdhvaśāsanasthas tu guruḥ adharādharaśāsanaṃ pratyuta prāṇayati pūrṇatvāt iti sarvādhikārī*

## v.19
And-this guide, teacher, initiator, attractor — and-this full-knowledge just supreme; by-him without initiation-and-rest-attainment-failure.
The Sanskrit: *sa ca daiśiko guruḥ ācāryo dīkṣakaḥ cumbakaḥ sa cāyaṃ pūrṇajñāna eva sarvottamaḥ tena vinā dīkṣādyasampatteḥ*

## v.20
Yogi but of-one-eager-for-fruit joined: if by-means-teaching uninterrupted just fruit to-give able; by-means-teaching but in-knowledge just joined, in-release even in-proper-means desiring-knowledge-fullness, and many even teachers should-make.
The Sanskrit: *yogī tu phalotsukasya yukto yadi upāyopadeśena avyavahitam eva phalaṃ dātuṃ śaktaḥ upāyopadeśena tu jñāne eva yukto mokṣe 'pi abhyupāyāt jñānapūrṇatākāṅkṣī ca bahūn api gurūn kuryāt*

## v.21
By-reference-to-supreme-and-rest-knowledge-differences in-them should-stay; in-abandoning-full-knowledge-guru but expiation just.
The Sanskrit: *uttamottamādijñānabhedāpekṣayā teṣu varteta sampūrṇajñānagurutyāge tu prāyaścittam eva*

## v.22
But he even not-saying contrary or saying why not to-be-abandoned not-at-all — we-say: of-him indeed from-full-knowledge-ness indeed passion-and-rest-absence; thus non-saying-and-rest in-disciple-gone-itself by-someone from-unworthiness-untrustworthiness-and-rest as-cause would-be — thus; in-his-contemplation should-be-striven-for by-disciple not in-abandoning-him.
The Sanskrit: *nanu so 'pi abruvan viparītaṃ vā bruvan kiṃ na tyājyaḥ naiva iti brūmaḥ tasya hi pūrṇajñānatvāt eva rāgādyabhāva iti avacanādikaṃ śiṣyagatenaiva kenacit ayogyatvānāśvastatvādinā nimittena syāt iti tadupāsane yatanīyaṃ śiṣyeṇa na tattyāge*

## v.23
Thus caused-by-grace power-descent independent indeed from-dependence-on-act-and-rest-determinism.
The Sanskrit: *evam anugrahanimittaṃ śaktipāto nirapekṣa eva karmādiniyatyapekṣaṇāt*

## v.24
Concealment — thus concealment indeed fruit-of-dependence-in-deep-sorrow-delusion: for-as from-light-freedom awakened even like-a-fool behaves; by-heart and foolish-behavior condemns; likewise fool even awakened-behavior mantra-worship-and-rest should-do, should-condemn — and as his foolish-behavior even-when-done of-awakened goes-to destruction. Likewise his awakened-behavior she but being-condemned from-being-forbidden-conduct-form oneself and by-it-alone from-self-suspicion him in-mire-of-sorrow-delusion he-drowns. Not but of-arisen-power-descent concealment-even exists herein even and dependence-on-act-and-rest as-before having-prohibited; therein even from-wills-manifoldness this body-only-enjoyment-sorrow-fruit-ness or in-initiation-covenant-conduct-teacher-god-fire-and-rest of-servicing-condemning-both-engaged like before of-Siva-doctrine-standers of-abandoning-him like.
The Sanskrit: *tirobhāva iti tirobhāvo hi karmādyapekṣagāḍhaduḥkhamohabhāgitvaphalaḥ yathāhi prakāśasvātantryāt prabuddho 'pi mūḍhavat ceṣṭate hṛdayena ca mūḍhaceṣṭāṃ nindati tathā mūḍho 'pi prabuddhaceṣṭāṃ mantrārādhanādikāṃ kuryāt nindec ca yathā ca asya mūḍhaceṣṭā kriyamāṇāpi prabuddhasya dhvaṃsam eti tathā asya prabuddhaceṣṭā sā tu nindyamānā niṣiddhācaraṇarūpatvāt svayaṃ ca tayaiva viśaṅkamānatvāt enaṃ duḥkhamohapaṅke nimajjayati na tu utpannaśaktipātasya tirobhāvo 'sti atrāpi ca karmādyapekṣā pūrvavat niṣedhyā tatrāpi ca icchāvaicitryāt etad dehamātropabhogyaduḥkhaphalatvaṃ vā dīkṣāsamayacaryāgurudevāgnyādau sevānindanobhayaprasaktānām iva prāk śivaśāsanasthānāṃ tattyāginām iva*

## v.25
Even-therein from-wills-manifoldness concealed even oneself or by-power-descent is-joined, dead or through-mouth-of-kinsman-guru-etc-grace — thus in-this-way duty-share-ness in-own-self committing the-Supreme-Lord just — thus not maimed self should-see.
The Sanskrit: *tatrāpi icchāvaicitryāt tirobhūto 'pi svayaṃ vā śaktipātena yujyate mṛto vā bandhugurvādikṛpāmukhena ity evaṃ kṛtyabhāgitvaṃ svātmani anusaṃdadhat parameśvara eva iti na khaṇḍitam ātmānaṃ paśyet*

# Tantrasāra A12 — ARGMAP

## tsa12-p01 (12.1–12.10)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (pre-initiation bath).
- question: What is bath before initiation, and what are its forms?
- premises: (1) Initiation-nature needs prior bath. Bath = purity = Lord-nature immersion; purity = turbidity-removal; turbidity = conceit of alien-form mixture in one-formed (self/universe autonomous-bliss-consciousness-pith); removed by Bhairava-immersion (sudden for some, means-facing for others; varied by number/whole-part/place/person/time from assurance). (2) Eightfold: earth/water/wind/fire/ether/moon/sun/self forms visualized Lord-natured by mantra-placing; body Lord-immersed → consciousness too attains immersion (body-division functioning notwithstanding). For some, pleasing bath-cloth people makes it Lord's means (Ānanda-śāstra: steadiness/filling/vigor/stain-burning/pervasion/creation-power/maintenance-power/nondifference as chief fruits, since placed mantra bears each form). (3) Hero-special: battle-dust/hero-water/great-wind/hero-ash/charnel-sky + moon-sun therein + thought-free self. Outer-inner twofold: plunging wherever outer-worshipped-mantra-identified-made. Special: gazing hero-based bliss-substance Śiva-made → mantra-wheel worship therein → body-breath god-wheel satiating = chief bath. Inner: earth-form-holding → in earth-wheel made-of-it-ness.
- inference: Bath is immersion technology, outer and inner, scaled by assurance.
- conclusion: Eightfold + hero + inner bath stated; initiation proper next.
- unresolved: Ānanda-śāstra assumed (external).

# Tantrasāra A12 — L1 controlled

## tsa12-p01
"Initiation and the rest is to be taught" — so it was stated. Hence, for expounding initiation-nature, first to be done, bath is taught. (12.1) And bath is called purity; and purity is supreme-Lord-nature-immersion. (12.2) For turbidity-removal is purity; and turbidity is, though in that-one-formed, conceit of other-form-mixture not-that-natured. (12.3) That here, in autonomous-bliss-consciousness-only-pith, in own self or in universe too, conceit of other-form-mixture is impurity; and it is removed by great-Bhairava-immersion. And it too, for someone, may be sudden; for another, watching with other-means-face. (12.4) Therein too, by one-two-three-and-rest-division, wholly-partly, somewhere, for someone, sometime, thus, from assurance-obtaining, varied is division. (12.5) And it is eightfold: in eight forms earth-water-wind-fire-ether-moon-sun-self-formed — visualized as Lord-natured through mantra-placing-greatness — and through identity, when body is Lord-immersed, from body-and-rest-division-functioning, of consciousness too is Lord-immersion-attainment. But for someone, from bath-cloth-and-rest-pleased-people-producing-ness, it reaches Lord-means-hood — so stated. And stated in glorious Ānanda and the rest: steadiness, filling, vigor, stain-burning, pervasion, creation-capability, maintenance-capability, and nondifference — thus these in them are chief fruits — since of placed mantra in them is that-form-bearing-ness. (12.6) But for heroes is special. Namely: battle-dust, hero-water, great-wind, hero-ash, charnel-sky; in the two placed therein, moon-sun; self thought-free. (12.7) Again is twofoldness through outer-inner-ness: in outer-worshipped-mantra-identity, made-of-it — therein therein is plunging — so stated. (12.8) But the special: bliss-substance gone to hero-base, by gazing having made Śiva-made — therein itself is mantra-wheel-worship; then through it itself is of body-breath-both-based god-wheel satiating — thus is chief bath. (12.9) Inner, as: through earth-and-rest-form-holding — therein therein in earth-and-rest wheel is made-of-it-ness. (12.10)

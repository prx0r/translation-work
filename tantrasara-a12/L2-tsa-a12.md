# Tantrasara-a12 — L2 READ

## v.1
Initiation and the rest must be told — it is said. So, to expound the nature of initiation, first the to-be-done bath is taught.
The Sanskrit: *dīkṣādikaṃ vaktavyam iti uktam ato dīkṣāsvarūpanirūpaṇārthaṃ prāk kartavyaṃ snānam upadiśyate*

## v.2
Bathing is called purity; purity is immersion in the Supreme Lord's own form.
The Sanskrit: *snānaṃ ca śuddhatā ucyate śuddhatā ca parameśvarasvarūpasamāveśaḥ*

## v.3
Washing off turbidity is indeed purity; turbidity — even in That whose sole form is That — is the conceit of admixing another form of alien nature.
The Sanskrit: *kāluṣyāpagamo hi śuddhiḥ kāluṣyaṃ ca tadekarūpe 'pi atatsvabhāvarūpāntarasaṃvalanābhimānaḥ*

## v.4
Here, in the self whose essence is free-bliss-consciousness-alone — or everywhere in the world too — the conceit of admixing another-formed reality is impurity; that is dispelled by immersion in great-Bhairava — for someone suddenly, for someone else awaiting further means.
The Sanskrit: *tad iha svatantrānandacinmātrasāre svātmani viśvatrāpi vā tadanyarūpasaṃvalanābhimānaḥ aśuddhiḥ sā ca mahābhairavasamāveśena vyapohyate so 'pi kasyacit jhaṭiti bhavet kasyāpi upāyāntaramukhaprekṣī*

## v.5
Therein too, by one-two-threefold division, whole or part-wise, somewhere, for someone, sometime, and so from assurance-attainment: difference is manifold.
The Sanskrit: *tatrāpi ca ekadvitryādibhedena samastavyastatayā kvacit kasyacit kadācit ca tathā āśvāsopalabdheḥ vicitro bhedaḥ*

## v.6
And eightfold, in earth-water-wind-fire-sky-moon-sun-self forms, made into Supreme-Lord forms by mantra-placing majesty; by identity, in the Lord-entered body, immersion in the Supreme arises for anyone — though from bathing-clothes-and-so-on gladness it becomes the means to the Supreme. Thus told the chief fruits therein: firmness, nourishment, vigor, impurity-burning, pervasion, creation-capacity, maintenance-capacity, non-difference.
The Sanskrit: *sa ca aṣṭadhā kṣitijalapavanahutāśanākāśasomasūryātmarūpāsu aṣṭāsu mūrtiṣu mantranyāsamahimnā parameśvararūpatayā bhāvitāsu tādātmyena ca dehe parameśvarasamāviṣṭe śarīrādivibhāgavṛtteḥ caitanyasyāpi parameśvarasamāveśaprāptiḥ kasyāpi tu snānavastrādituṣṭijanakatvāt parameśopāyatām etīti uktaṃ ca śrīmadānandādau dhṛtiḥ āpyāyo vīryaṃ maladāho vyāptiḥ sṛṣṭisāmarthyaṃ sthitisāmarthyam abhedaś ca ity etāni teṣu mukhyaphalāni teṣu teṣu upāhitasya mantrasya tattadrūpadhāritvāt*

## v.7
But by heroes-rule the distinction: as battle-dust, hero-water, great wind, hero-ash, cremation-ground-sky — sun-and-moon placed in them, self undiscriminating.
The Sanskrit: *vīroddeśena tu viśeṣaḥ tad yathā raṇareṇuḥ vīrāmbhaḥ mahāmarut vīrabhasma śmaśānanabhaḥ tadupahitau candrārkau ātmā nirvikalpakaḥ*

## v.8
Again too, as outer-inner twoness, by oneness with the outward-worshipped mantra, made-of-That: there, there plunging — thus told.
The Sanskrit: *punar api bāhyābhyantaratayā dvitvam bahirupāsyamantratādātmyena tanmayīkṛte tatra tatra nimajjanam ity uktam*

## v.9
But bliss-substance, hero-abiding, by looking, having-made-Śiva: right-there mantra-circle-worship; thence by that very one, body-and-breath deity-circle satisfied — thus the chief bath.
The Sanskrit: *viśeṣas tu ānandadravyaṃ vīrādhāragataṃ nirīkṣaṇena śivamayīkṛtya tatraiva mantracakrapūjanam tataḥ tenaiva dehaprāṇobhayāśritadevatācakratarpaṇam iti mukhyaṃ snānam*

## v.10
Inward, thus, by holding earth-and-so-on-formed forms — here, there, in earthen-and-rest circles — being-made-of-That.
The Sanskrit: *ābhyantaraṃ yathā tattaddharādirūpadhāraṇayā tatra tatra pārthivādau cakre tanmayībhāvaḥ*

# C1 — Tantrasāra Ahnika 12 (snāna)

## tsa12-p01 — bathing
**SUMMARY.** Initiation comes next, so first: the bath. Bath means purity, and purity means sinking into the Lord's own nature. What washes off is murk — and murk is taking yourself, one-formed autonomous bliss-awareness (or the universe), to be mixed with some alien shape. Great Bhairava-immersion rinses it. For some it hits all at once; others keep an eye on the means. And it varies — by ones and twos and threes, whole or part, where, for whom, when — as assurance comes. Eight baths: earth, water, wind, fire, ether, moon, sun, self — each envisaged, by mantra set into it, as the Lord himself; and once the body is Lord-dipped, awareness follows though it still runs the body's errands. For some, even pleasing bathers with clothes does it — bath as the Lord's own means. The books of Bliss list what each mainly gives: steadiness, fullness, fire, stain burnt, pervasion, making power, keeping power, no-difference — the mantra set in each carrying its shape. Heroes bathe apart: dust of battle, heroes' water, great wind, heroes' ash, charnel sky — moon and sun set in the pair, self thought-free. Outer and inner make two: plunge wherever outer worship has made you one with its mantra, as said. Finest: gaze on bliss-stuff seated in the hero-base till it's Śiva, worship the mantra-wheel right there, then with it feed the gods enthroned in body and breath both — that is the bath of baths. The inward bath: hold earth's shape and the rest, and in each earth-wheel grow into it.
**FUNCTION.** Establishes bath as purity-immersion technology (outer and inner, eight forms, hero-special, chief bath).
**KEY TERMS.** snāna (bath = purity = immersion), śuddhi (purity = turbidity-removal), kāluṣya (turbidity = alien-form mixture conceit), aṣṭadhā (eightfold), Ānanda-śāstra (books of Bliss), vīra (hero), mukhya snāna (chief bath).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** A11 (śaktipāta) precedes; A12 teaches pre-initiation bath; A13 (yāga/dīkṣā) follows.
**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** Ānanda-śāstra assumed (external). Bathers gloss (tuṣṭijanakatva) is register, flagged.
**RELATED.** A11 (śaktipāta), A13 (yāga/dīkṣā), spans TS12.1–12.10.

## Structure (Pradipaka ch.12 + Vishvasa ch.12 + Chakravarty intro)
- v1 the topic set: initiation and the rest must be told; so, to set forth the nature of initiation, first the to-be-done bath is taught.
- v2 definition: bathing is called purity; purity is immersion in the Supreme Lord's own form.
- v3 turbidity washed off is purity; turbidity — even in That whose sole form is That — is the conceit of admixing another form of alien nature.
- v4 the test case: here, in the free-bliss-consciousness-alone self, or everywhere in the world too, admixed-other-formed conceit is impurity; dispelled by great-Bhairava immersion — suddenly for some, awaiting further means for others.
- v5 the manifold remainder: by one-two-threefold division, whole or part-wise, somewhere for someone sometime, from assurance-attainment: difference is manifold.
- v6 the eightfold bath: earth-water-wind-fire-sky-moon-sun-self forms, mantra-placed into Supreme-Lord forms; body possessed by identity; even bathing-gladness becomes a means; chief fruits listed (firmness, nourishment, vigor, impurity-burning, pervasion, creation-capacity, maintenance-capacity, non-difference) since the placed mantra takes each form.
- v7 heroes' specialty: battle-dust, hero-water, great wind, hero-ash, cremation-ground sky; sun-moon placed in them; undiscriminating self.
- v8 outer-inner twoness: oneness-made with the outward-worshipped mantra, made of That; plunging here-there, thus told.
- v9 the chief bath: bliss-substance abiding as hero-support seen, made Śiva; mantra-circle worshipped there; thence by that the body-and-breath deity-circle satisfied.
- v10 the inward: holding earth-and-so-on forms here and there in earthen circles, being-made-of-That.

# Tantrasāra A13 — ARGMAP

## tsa13-p01 (13.1–13.19)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (outer worship prep).
- question: Where is worship done, and how is the body prepared?
- premises: (1) Rite-place = wherever heart gladdened + immersion-fit (meditated identity the cause; gladness the cause → no other place). Peaks/shrines = Lord-entered powers taking bodies (noble/barbarian lands analogy); mountain-tops = solitude → one-pointedness. (2) General placing at house-front: hands then body. Mālinī mantras (hrīṃ…): power then possessor; liberation foot-to-head, enjoyment reversed. Mālinī = chief power-form (seed-womb collision, all-wish-milking); name etymologized (Rudra-garlands, fruited, frost-sound-bee, success-liberation, giving-withholding — from ra-la oneness). Fallen-rite mantra completed by this placing; even Garuḍa-Vaiṣṇava anointed ones reach stainless liberation-giving. (3) After body-placing: argha vessel etc. (positions per L1; deity-family, five states + unsurpassed sixth, sixfold placing, cause-abiding 36-host in Bhairava-nondifference → Bhairavī-becoming; wavering-objection dissolved in ataraṅga body).
- inference: Place is fitness, not geography; mantras repair rite and sect.
- conclusion: Place + nyāsa + argha course stated.
- unresolved: Mantra syllables assumed potent (traditional-knowledge).

## tsa13-p02 (13.20–13.34)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (image, directions, inner rite).
- question: How are directions and image grounded — and what is worship inwardly?
- premises: (1) Five faces (Īśāna up, underworld-face down, four + four around) = consciousness-greatness making direction-difference (direction no separate principle). Shadow-transcending analogy → Lord-middleness = all-presiding; sun likewise (Lord's knowledge-power; frontness self-dependent). Self-sun-Lord oneness = direction-inquiry (Abhinavagupta teachers). (2) North-facing, burning bodyness via I-abandon (near yet other-bodied), waveless-fixed-abode-mounted: first throb-digit by gaze-nature = image; thereupon taught god-wheel placing, powers chief (their seat: nine-self Lords; worship-worthy as power). (3) Five states + unsurpassed sixth reflected = sixfold placing. Cause-abiding (Brahmā→Śakti) each: 36-host beyond-worldly in Bhairava-nondifference placing through fullness = Bhairavī-becoming, unobstructed. Objection (why place/create after wavering-mount?) dissolved: ataraṅga Bhairava-body = self-shown creation-dissolution crores. (4) Mutual-melting into goddess-become breath-body-mind → outer-inner flower-smoke-libation worship as possible. Trident-lotus placing in body/breath/mind (base-power root, bulb, stalk, Māyā-knot, fourfold pure-knowledge-lotus, Sadāśiva = Great-dead by dissolution/knowledge/body-decay/sound-grasp). Navel-risen triple-channel triple-lotus unmanā: in universe-made difference made seat, presiding pervading, wished god imagined: offering universe-things into same-nature-full-self = worship; becoming-it = meditation; inner-grasping-existent-contemplation-swinging = muttering; world-self-making by awakened splendor-force = fire-offering. Retinue likewise from fire-heap sparks. (5) Twelve-end verse + goddess-wheel-front verses + sky-going verses + inner-rite-suffices verse.
- inference: Outer rite = inner grasping displayed; image = first throb.
- conclusion: Directions/image/inner-rite definitions stated.
- unresolved: Yantra details (trident-lotus stations) assume initiated readers (external).

## tsa13-p03 (13.35–13.61) — NOTE: L1 rendered CONDENSED (declared thin; full close pass queued)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (vows completion; initiation prelims).
- question: When is outer rite still needed, and how does initiation begin?
- premises: (1) Inner rite alone = done-ness in truth; yet outer done for delimitation-loss; non-immersed one's chief = outer rite (practice → immersion); even beast-bond-covering needs inner (resolve-power purifies). (2) Wanting initiation: adhivāsa prelims (ground-taking, Gaṇeśa, jar-pitcher worship, altar worship, fire-offering); daily/occasional = altar-fire only. (3) Adhivāsa = pupil's fitness-making (teeth-whitening analogy), god's readiness-grasping, teacher's grasping, implements' Lord-glow fitness-giving. (4) Full rite-house: Mālinī/mātṛkā recalled, glow-mass visualized, flower-throw; astra-charmed mustard/rice/barley scattered NE-joined = ground-taking. Pure-knowledge-seat given → Gaṇapati worship → bliss-filled jar worship → retinue after worshippable → chief mantra 108-muttered jar-making; second pitcher astra for obstacle-quelling; direction-guardians with weapons; old pupil gets astra-pitcher; teacher takes jar. (5) Pupil pouring streams house-round with mantra (Śakra watchfulness formula, tryakṣara note); NE jar placing; straw/pitcher; double worship; middle Lord worship; fire visualized as Lord-power, kindled, unified with heart-fire, mantras satiated with ghee/sesame; sprinkling = sesame-ghee refining; ladles' Lord-nondifference-view = refining. (6) Offering per power, ladles up-down (power/Śiva facing), standing, twelve-end-sky-risen-Śiva-full-moon-falling-nectar contemplation + vauṣaḍa mantra to ghee-end = full offering satiating mantra-wheel. (7) Rice shared five ways (altar/pitcher/jar/fire + pupil's + kept); tooth-stick (inauspicious falls); fire-offering by astra-mantra.
- inference: Rite-house builds the container in which descent can be handled.
- conclusion: Prelims + fire-rite stated.
- unresolved: Mantra syllables + implements (traditional-knowledge).

## tsa13-p04 (13.62–13.101) — NOTE: L1 rendered CONDENSED (declared thin; full close pass queued)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (blind entry, grace-hand, 48 sacraments, samayas).
- question: How is the pupil brought in, transformed, and bound?
- premises: (1) Full-offering cake shared; tooth-stick; distraction-removed bound-eyed pupil led kneeling, flower thrown; blindfold ripped: grace-touched senses see present mantra-place directly, become it (present mantra evident to graced senses, fearing beings tremble). (2) Right hand deity-wheel worshipped → hand on pupil's crown/heart/navel burning bonds; left soma-worshipped → pure-principle-flooding; bow. Outer spirit-direction-bali (wine/meat/water) + sipping. Self rice-eating + pupil-self unity, awake abiding. (3) Bad dreams untold (doubt-fear); astra-remedy only. Lord worshipped; teacher enters pupil via breath-course, touching six cause-points (heart/throat/palate/brow/randhra/twelve-end) with 8 sacraments each, resting pupil-breath, descending = 48 sacraments + Rudra-portion-crossing = samayin-becoming. (4) Worshippable mantra with flowers given; samayas taught (teacher/scripture/god devotion; no facing opponents; honor teacher-kin; sex-tie only for worship; no reviling barren women; names/mantra unsaid outside worship; no using teacher's bed; no play near teacher; no superiority elsewhere; worship teacher at rites; no Śākinī-words at specials; holy days kept; no Vaiṣṇava company; no birth-view of fellows; feast visiting teachers; drop low-path teacher even if made (without contempt); no conduct-mixing with liṅgins (worship only); drop doubts; no birth-ranks in wheel; no shrine over body; heart-mantra unceasing). Hearing, bowing, consenting, gifting to dhanadāraśarīra-end + feeding poor/initiated → rite-wheel fed per future rite = samayin-becoming. (5) Mantra-practice/daily-worship/hearing/study qualified; occasionals via teacher entreated.
- inference: Transformation = grace-touch + sacraments + vow-structure, in that order.
- conclusion: Entry-to-samaya arc complete; samaya-initiation done (putraka etc. follow A14).
- unresolved: Mantra identities + implement details (traditional-knowledge).

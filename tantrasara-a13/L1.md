# Tantrasāra A13 — L1 controlled

## tsa13-p01
Now glad-hearted, one should go to rite-place. And it is wherever itself heart, gladness-joined, becomes fit for Lord-immersion — that alone; nor of it is another mark. Though stated: meditated-identity alone is the cause; that too is from being-gladdened alone — hence is no other place. (13.1) "Peak-mountain-top" and the rest in scripture is place-mention aimed at that alone to be understood. (13.2) In those peak-places, through Lord-destiny, of Lord-entered powers from body-taking — like noble lands of righteous, like barbarian lands of unrighteous; and mountain-top-places, through solitude, from distraction-removal, are one-pointedness-state. (13.3) Therein at rite-house-front, outside itself, one should do general placing — in hands first, then in body. (13.4) "Hrīṃ na pha hrīṃ hrīṃ ā kṣa hrīṃ" — by these two, power-possessor-wording, Mālinī-sound-mass-mantras, by one alone first power, then power-possessor — thus in liberation from foot-tip to head-end; in enjoyment all reversed. (13.5) For Mālinī goddess is chief power-form — through seed-womb-collision, all-desire-milking. (13.6) Etymological is this name — joined by Rudra-power-garlands, flowered in fruits, saṃsāra-frost-destroying-sound-bee, success-liberation-bearing, giving-withholding-power-joined — thus from ra-la-oneness-memory. (13.7) Hence therefore even fallen-rite mantra from this placing becomes full; even anointed Garuḍa-Vaiṣṇava-and-the-rest, reaching stainlessness, becomes liberation-giving. (13.8) After body-placing: the argha vessel — self-offering therein; deity-family worship; five states plus unsurpassed sixth reflected; sixfold placing; the causes Brahmā-to-Power abiding each, the thirty-six host beyond the worldly in Bhairava-nondifference placing through fullness — Bhairavī-becoming, and hence unobstructed; the wavering-objection — "once wavering-mounted, what more with principle-creating and placing?" — dissolved: for the waveless is the Bhairava-body wherein self-shown creation-dissolution-variety-crores. (13.9–13.19, positions per T1.)"

## tsa13-p02
"Therein in middle the Lord; above him Īśāna face; below underworld-face; in east-and-rest four, glorious Tatpuruṣa-Aghora-Sadya-Vāma-named; in four-middle, four others. (13.20) Thus consciousness-greatness alone shines image-made direction-difference — hence direction is no other principle. (13.21) And as one's own shadow, wished to transcend, ahead ahead becomes — so one reaches Lord-middleness; all-presiding-ness alone is middleness — so stated. (13.22) Thus as the Lord is direction-dividing, so the sun too — for he is Lordly itself knowledge-power — so stated... thus self-dependent too, of facing place is frontness. (13.23) Thus through self-sun-Lord-triad-oneness-contemplation is direction-inquiry — so the Abhinavagupta teachers. (13.24) Thus situated, facing north seated, in body-city-eight-and-the-rest through I-sense-abandoning one should burn bodyness — though near, like other's body, through bodilessness. Thereupon as-taught-to-be-worshipped-god-wheel-placing; and chiefly here powers are to be worshipped. (13.25) Since their seat-ness of Lord-nine-self-and-the-rest, and since of power alone is worship-worthiness — so teachers. (13.26) Therein five states — waking-and-the-rest; sixth unsurpassed named nature-state to be reflected. (13.27) Thus sixfold placing becomes. (13.28) Therein of causes Brahmā-Viṣṇu-Rudra-Lord-Sadāśiva-power-formed through each abiding, of thirty-six-principle-host beyond-worldly in Bhairava-nondifference-functioning in placing through fullness is Bhairavī-becoming; hence this is unobstructed. (13.29) As said: 'When waveless-mounted is gained, again what with principle-creation placing and the rest?' (13.30) For so far that waveless Bhairava-body wherein self-shown creation-dissolution-variety-crore in own self. (13.31) Thus through mutual-melting-yoga, having contemplated breath-body-mind become goddess-Lord, outside inside with flower-smoke-libation-and-the-rest as possible one should worship. (13.32) Therein in body breath mind accordingly one should do trident-lotus-placing. Namely: in base-power root is root, in bulb bulb-pith, at pipe-end to art-principle-end is staff, Māyā-natured knot, fourfold pure-knowledge-lotus, therein Sadāśiva Blessed himself — the Great Dead — through eminently dissolved, through knowledge, through chiefly knowable-body-decay, through sound-grasp-nature. (13.33) Risen from navel, gone through triple crown-hole, within sound indwelling power-pervader-harmony-formed three, to twelve-end, thereupon pure-lotus-triad, mind-beyond — in this universe-made difference made seat, presiding pervading, wished god imagined — what therein in same-nature-full-self is universe-thing-offering, that itself is worship; what is becoming-it, that is meditation; what is such inner-grasping-existent-contemplation-swinging, that is muttering; what by such-grasping-course-awakened-great-splendor-force, by that force alone, is universe-self-making, that is fire-offering. Thus having made retinue, thence from fire-heap like sparks, having contemplated, likewise one should worship. (13.34) Twelve-end this foremost, remembering from root the trident... (verses 13.35–13.39: goddess-wheel-front, sky-going, inner-sufficiency — positions per T1.)"

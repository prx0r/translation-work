# Tantrasara-a13 — L2 READ


## v.1
Now the glad-hearted should go to the worship-place; for wherever the heart is gladdened and made fit for absorption into the Lord, there it becomes — that alone is the mark, nothing else; for even in the saying, meditated identity alone is the cause, and that too is from being-glad alone — nothing else is the place.
The Sanskrit: *atha prasannahṛdayo yāgasthānaṃ yāyāt tac ca yatraiva hṛdayaṃ prasādayuktaṃ parameśvarasamāveśayogyaṃ bhavati tad eva na tu asya anyal lakṣaṇam uktāv api dhyeyatādātmyam eva kāraṇam tad api bhāvaprasādād eva iti nānyat sthānam*

## v.2
But seat, mountain-top and the rest in scripture is to be understood as aimed at pointing out this place.
The Sanskrit: *pīṭhaparvatāgram ityādis tu śāstre sthānoddeśa etatpara eva boddhavyaḥ*

## v.3
In these seats and the rest, by the Lord's ordainment, powers entered by the Lord take body — like noble lands for the pious, like barbarian lands for the impious; the teaching of the mountain-top, through solitude, from distraction-avoidance, is the state of one-pointedness — thus.
The Sanskrit: *teṣu teṣu pīṭhādisthāneṣu parameśaniyatyā parameśvarāviṣṭānāṃ śaktīnāṃ dehagrahaṇāt āryadeśā iva dhārmikāṇāṃ mlecchadeśā iva adhārmikāṇām parvatāgrādeś caikāntatvena vikṣepaparihārāt aikāgryapadatvam iti*

## v.4
There, before the worship-house, outside alone, one should do the general placing — first on the hands, then on the body.
The Sanskrit: *tatra yāgagṛhāgre bahir eva sāmānyanyāsaṃ kuryāt karayoḥ pūrvaṃ tato dehe*

## v.5
hrīṃ na pha hrīṃ, hrīṃ ā kṣa hrīṃ — thus; by these two, expressing power and the power-bearer, the two Mālinī-alphabet mantras; with each one singly — first power, then the power-bearer — in release from foot-tip to head-end; in enjoyment everything is reversed.
The Sanskrit: *hrīṃ na pha hrīṃ hrīṃ ā kṣa hrīṃ ity ābhyāṃ śaktiśaktimadvācakābhyāṃ mālinīśabdarāśimantrābhyām ekenaiva ādau śaktiḥ tataḥ śaktimān iti muktau pādāgrāc chiro'ntam bhuktau tu sarvo viparyayaḥ*

## v.6
For Mālinī the Goddess is the chief Śākta form, milking all wishes through the collision of seed and womb.
The Sanskrit: *mālinī hi bhagavatī mukhyam śāktaṃ rūpaṃ bījayonisaṃghaṭṭena samastakāmadugham*

## v.7
And meaningful is this name: garlanded with Rudra-power garlands, flowered in fruits, the bee of the sound that dissolves the winter of transmigration, holding attainment and release, endowed with the power to give and to withhold — since ra and la are remembered as one.
The Sanskrit: *anvarthaṃ caitan nāma rudraśaktimālābhir yuktā phaleṣu puṣpitā saṃsāraśiśirasaṃhāranādabhramarī siddhimokṣadhāriṇī dānādānaśaktiyuktā iti ralayor ekatvasmṛteḥ*

## v.8
Hence very, for even a broken-rite mantra becomes complete through this placing; even a stained one — Gāruḍa, Vaiṣṇava and the rest — having reached stainlessness, becomes release-giving.
The Sanskrit: *ata eva hi bhraṣṭavidhir api mantra etannyāsāt pūrṇo bhavati sāñjano 'pi gāruḍavaiṣṇavādir nirañjanatām etya mokṣaprado bhavati*

## v.9
After the body-placing, this same placing is to be done on the vessel.
The Sanskrit: *dehanyāsānantaram arghapātre ayam eva nyāsaḥ*

## v.10
For here, for firmness of realization of non-difference with the Lord, the worship-act is made the example: there, of all factors, thus is Lord-ness — there, from the sacrificer's place-purity-bringing-doings, from vessel-purity, from placing, from the sacrificer's body-placing, from the worshippable's altar-placing.
The Sanskrit: *iha hi kriyākārakāṇāṃ parameśvarābhedapratipattidārḍhyasiddhaye pūjākriyā udāharaṇīkṛtā tatra ca sarvakārakāṇām itthaṃ parameśvarībhāvaḥ tatra yaṣṭrādhārasya sthānaśuddhyāpādānakaraṇayor arghapātraśuddhinyāsābhyām yaṣṭur dehanyāsāt yājyasya sthaṇḍilādinyāsāt*

## v.11
Thus, even by rite-order alone, making all factors the Lord's, seeing all rites with just this view, without chief knowledge and yoga — one becomes the Supreme Lord.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ kriyākrameṇāpi parameśvarīkṛtasamastakārakaḥ tayaiva dṛśā sarvakriyāḥ paśyan vināpi pramukhajñānayogābhyāṃ parameśvara eva bhavati*

## v.12
Thus, having placed on the vessel, having worshipped with flowers, smoke and the rest, one should sprinkle the rite-essence and the flowers and the rest with its drops.
The Sanskrit: *evam arghapātre nyasya puṣpadhūpādyaiḥ pūjayitvā tadvipruḍbhir yāgasāraṃ puṣpādi ca prokṣayet*

## v.13
Then in the halo-circle, on ground or in sky: oṃ to the outer retinue, hail — thus one should worship.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ prabhāmaṇḍale bhūmau khe vā oṃ bāhyaparivārāya nama iti pūjayet*

## v.14
Then at the door-place: oṃ to the door-deity-wheel, hail — thus one should worship.
The Sanskrit: *tato dvārasthāne oṃ dvāradevatācakrāya nama iti pūjayet*

## v.15
But when there is an unguarded outer place, having entered, on the maṇḍala-altar-front alone one should do the outer-retinue-door-deity-wheel worship, the aforesaid placing and the rest — not outside.
The Sanskrit: *agupte tu bahiḥsthāne sati praviśya maṇḍalasthaṇḍilāgra eva bāhyaparivāradvāradevatācakrapūjāṃ pūrvoktaṃ ca nyāsādi kuryāt na bahiḥ*

## v.16
Then too: phaṭ phaṭ phaṭ — thus, having thrown the weapon-muttered flower, having removed the obstacles, having meditated, having entered within, with gaze kindled by the Lord's ray, one should see all around the worship-house.
The Sanskrit: *tato 'pi phaṭ phaṭ phaṭ iti astrajaptapuṣpaṃ prakṣipya vighnān apasāritān dhyātvā antaḥ praviśya parameśvarakiraṇeddhayā dṛṣṭyā abhito yāgagṛhaṃ paśyet*

## v.17
There the release-seeker should stand facing north, so that by the Lord's Aghora-radiance swiftly indeed, his bonds burnt, he may become.
The Sanskrit: *tatra mumukṣur uttarābhimukhas tiṣṭhet yathā bhagavadaghoratejasā jhaṭity eva pruṣṭapāśo bhavet*

## v.18
There the Lord's freedom alone, by form-shining, shines forth the direction-principle.
The Sanskrit: *tatra parameśvarasvātantryam eva mūrtyābhāsanayā diktattvam avabhāsayati*

## v.19
There awareness-light alone is the middle; thence, other-division arising: what is taken as light is above, what is not thus is below; what faces the light is east, the other is west; from having become facing-light, the next is that-light-stream-rise-place; south from favour, facing it, but from to-be-shone, north — thus the direction-four.
The Sanskrit: *tatra citprakāśa eva madhyaṃ tata itarapravibhāgapravṛtteḥ prakāśasvīkāryam ūrdhvam atathābhūtam adhaḥ prakāśanasammukhīnaṃ pūrvam itarat aparam saṃmukhībhūtaprakāśatvāt anantaraṃ tatprakāśadhārārohasthānaṃ dakṣiṇam ānukūlyāt tatsammukhaṃ tu avabhāsyatvāt uttaram iti dikcatuṣkam*

## v.20
There in the middle the Lord; above, his is the north-eastern face; below, the underworld face; in the east-and-rest four directions, the so-called Tatpuruṣa, Aghora, Sadyas, Vāma; in the four-direction-middle, the other four.
The Sanskrit: *tatra madhye bhagavān ūrdhve 'sya aiśānaṃ vaktram adhaḥ pātālavaktram pūrvādidikcatuṣke śrītatpuruṣāghorasadyovāmākhyaṃ dikcatuṣkamadhye anyāś catasraḥ*

## v.21
Thus indeed awareness-glory alone, formed, shines forth direction-difference — thus direction is not another principle.
The Sanskrit: *ity evaṃ saṃvinmahimaiva mūrtikṛtaṃ digbhedaṃ bhāsayati iti dik na tattvāntaram*

## v.22
As, as the shadow is to be overstepped — being, before, before, it becomes — so reaching the Lord's centrality: being all-presiding alone is centrality — thus it is said.
The Sanskrit: *yathā yathā ca svacchāyā laṅghayitum iṣṭā satī puraḥ puro bhavati tathā parameśvaramadhyatām eti sarvādhiṣṭhātṛtaiva mādhyasthyam ity uktam*

## v.23
Thus as the Lord is the divider of directions, so the sun — for it indeed is the Lord's knowledge-power, thus said: there, there, there the east is of the manifest from the east; and just where, so there — thus self-ruled even, facing one's own place, is in front.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ yathā bhagavān digvibhāgakārī tathā sūryo 'pi sa hi pārameśvary eva jñānaśaktir ity uktaṃ tatra tatra tatra pūrvaṃ vyakteḥ pūrvā yatraiva ca tathā tatraiva evaṃ svātmādhīnāpi svasammukhīnasya deśasya purastāttvāt*

## v.24
Thus by self-sun-Lord-triad-unification is direction-discussion — thus Abhinavagupta's gurus.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ svātmasūryaparameśatritayaikībhāvanayā dikcarcā iti abhinavaguptaguravaḥ*

## v.25
Thus standing, facing north one should sit, burning body-ness in body-city-eight and the rest by I-feeling abandonment; from nearness as another's body, from bodilessness — then mounted on the waveless steady abode, from gaze-nature — which indeed is the first throb-digit — that alone is the form; thereupon is the as-taught worshippable-deity-wheel-placing; chiefly too here the powers are to be worshipped.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ sthite uttarābhimukham upaviśya dehapuryaṣṭakādau ahambhāvatyāgena dehatāṃ dahet saṃnidhāv api paradehavat adehatvāt tato nistaraṅgadhruvadhāmarūḍhasya dṛṣṭisvābhāvyāt yā kila ādyā spandakalā saiva mūrtiḥ tadupari yathopadiṣṭayājyadevatācakranyāsaḥ prādhānyena ca iha śaktayo yājyāḥ*

## v.26
From being that seat, of the Lord-nine-self and the rest, of power alone and of worship-worthiness — thus the gurus.
The Sanskrit: *tadāsanatvāt bhagavannavātmādīnāṃ śakter eva ca pūjyatvāt iti guravaḥ*

## v.27
There five states: waking and the rest; and sixth called unsurpassed, nature-state to be contemplated.
The Sanskrit: *tatra ca pañca avasthā jāgradādyāḥ ṣaṣṭhī ca anuttarā nāma svabhāvadaśā anusaṃdheyā*

## v.28
Thus sixfold is the placing.
The Sanskrit: *iti ṣoḍhā nyāso bhavati*

## v.29
There from the presiding of causes, Brahmā-Viṣṇu-Rudra-Īśa-Sadāśiva-power-formed, each from thirty-six-principle-group, world-transcended in Bhairava-Lord-non-difference-course placing, from fullness is Bhairavī-being; hence this has no opening.
The Sanskrit: *tatra kāraṇānāṃ brahmaviṣṇurudreśasadāśivaśaktirūpāṇāṃ pratyekam adhiṣṭhānāt ṣaṭtriṃśattattvakalāpasya laukikatattvottīrṇasya bhairavabhaṭṭārakābhedavṛtte nyāse pūrṇatvāt bhairavībhāvaḥ tena etat anavakāśam*

## v.30
Which, they said: gained in waveless mounting, again what with principle-emission through placing and the rest — thus.
The Sanskrit: *yad āhuḥ ataraṅgarūḍhau labdhāyāṃ punaḥ kiṃ tattvasṛṣṭir nyāsādinā iti*

## v.31
So long indeed that waveless Bhairava-formed, which in own self shows emission-dissolution-variety-peak.
The Sanskrit: *tāvat hi tad ataraṅgaṃ bhairavavapuḥ yat svātmani avabhāsitasṛṣṭisaṃhārāvaicitryakoṭi*

## v.32
Thus by mutual-meeting-yoga, Lord-made breath-body-mind contemplated, outside and within with flowers-smoke-satings as possible, one should worship.
The Sanskrit: *evam anyonyamelakayogena parameśvarībhūtaṃ prāṇadehabuddhyādi bhāvayitvā bahir antaḥ puṣpadhūpatarpaṇādyair yathāsambhavaṃ pūjayet*

## v.33
There in the body, in breath, in mind accordingly one should do the trident-lotus-placing — as at base-power-root: root, bulb, to-root-pith, at the hanging-end, to kalā-principle's end the staff; Māyā-natured knot; four-natured pure-knowledge lotus; right there the Lord Sadāśiva; he alone the great corpse; through excellence dissolved, from awakening, chiefly, from knowable-bodied body-decay, and from sound-touch-awareness-nature.
The Sanskrit: *tatra śarīre prāṇe dhiyi ca tadanusāreṇa śūlābjanyāsaṃ kuryāt tad yathā ādhāraśaktimūle mūlaṃ kanda āmūlasārakaṃ lambikānte kalātattvānto daṇḍaḥ māyātmako granthiḥ catuṣkikātmā śuddhavidyāpadmaṃ tatraiva sadāśivabhaṭṭārakaḥ sa eva mahāpretaḥ prakarṣeṇa līnatvāt bodhāt prādhānyena vedyātmakadehakṣayāt nādāmarśātmakatvāc ca iti*

## v.34
That risen from the navel, come out of the three head-openings at its top, abiding inside sound, power-pervader, equanimity-formed and the rest, the six-end; thereupon is the pure lotus-triad, beyond-mind; on this universe-made division made seat, presiding as pervading-being, the desired become-supported deity having fashioned — which right there, in same-disposition-full self, universe-being-offering: that alone is worship; which alone becoming-that: that is meditation; which inner-deliberation-being-shaking as of such: that is murmur; which by such-deliberation-order-awakened great-splendour, by that force alone, is universe-self-making: that is oblation; that very thing done thus, the retinue, from that alone, like sparks from a fire-heap, having meditated, just so one should worship.
The Sanskrit: *tan nābhyutthitaṃ tanmūrdharandhratrayanirgataṃ nādāntarvartiśaktivyāpinīsamanārūpamarātrayaṃ dviṣaṭkāntaṃ tadupari śuddhapadmatrayam aunmanasam etasmin viśvamaye bhede āsanīkṛte adhiṣṭhātṛtayā vyāpakabhāvena ādheyabhūtāṃ yathābhimatāṃ devatāṃ kalpayitvā yat tatraiva samasvabhāvanirbharātmani viśvabhāvārpaṇaṃ tad eva pūjanaṃ yad eva tanmayībhavanaṃ tad dhyānaṃ yat tathāvidhāntaḥparāmarśasadbhāvanādāndolanaṃ sa japaḥ yat tathāvidhaparāmarśakramaprabuddhamahātejasā tathābalād eva viśvātmīkaraṇaṃ sa homaḥ tad evaṃ kṛtvā parivāraṃ tata eva vahnirāśer visphuliṅgavat dhyātvā tathaiva pūjayet*

## v.35
Remembering the twelve-end, this foremost trident, from the root.
The Sanskrit: *dvādaśāntam idaṃ prāgraṃ viśūlaṃ mūlataḥ smaran*

## v.36
Leaving sequence, going to goddess-wheel-front attains sky-going-ness.
The Sanskrit: *devīcakrāgragaṃ tyaktakramaḥ khecaratāṃ vrajet*

## v.37
From root-support, of twice-six-end-sky-peak-filling nature.
The Sanskrit: *mūlādhārād dviṣaṭkāntavyomāgrāpūraṇātmikā*

## v.38
Of sky-going-ness, from sky-faring-rest and from sky-nectar-eating.
The Sanskrit: *khecarīyaṃ khasaṃcāramsthitibhyāṃ khāmṛtāśanāt*

## v.39
Thus by inner rite alone, in reality, done-what-was-to-be-done.
The Sanskrit: *evam antaryāgamātrād eva vastutaḥ kṛtakṛtyatā*

## v.40
Truly for the then-entered, still outer is to be done, to remove limitation alone — and who too thus does not partake immersion: his chief is the outer rite; from its practice, immersion-gain; since for him too, but the beast-veiling inner rite, when mounted on that too, of his thought-force is purity-giving.
The Sanskrit: *satyataḥ tadāviṣṭasya tathāpi bahir api kāryo yāgo 'vacchedahānaya eva yo 'pi tathā samāveśabhāk na bhavati tasya mukhyo bahiryāgaḥ tadabhyāsāt samāveśalābho yatas tasyāpi tu paśutātirodhānāyāntaryāgaḥ tadarūḍhāv api tatsaṃkalpabalasya śuddhipradatvāt*

## v.41
Now when one wishes to do initiation, then for preparation: ground-taking, Gaṇeśa-worship, jar-and-vessel worship, altar-worship, oblation — one should do.
The Sanskrit: *atha yadā dīkṣāṃ cikīrṣet tadādhivāsanārthaṃ bhūmiparigrahaṃ gaṇeśārcanaṃ kumbhakalaśayoḥ pūjāṃ sthaṇḍilārcanaṃ havanaṃ ca kuryāt*

## v.42
But in daily-and-occasional: altar-and-rest worship-oblation alone.
The Sanskrit: *nityanaimittikayos tu sthaṇḍilādyarcanahavane eva*

## v.43
There is initiation-preparation, the disciple's refined-fitness-placing, like sharpening of teeth; the God's toward-work-upturned-ness-grasping, the guru's taking of that.
The Sanskrit: *tatra adhivāsanaṃ śiṣyasya saṃskṛtayogyatādhānam amblīkaraṇam iva dantānāṃ devasya kartavyonmukhatvagrāhaṇam guros tadgrahaṇam*

## v.44
Of implement-things, by being inside the worship-house, through the Lord's radiance-swelling, is bestowing of worship-implement-fitness — thus.
The Sanskrit: *upakaraṇadravyāṇāṃ yāgagṛhāntarvartitayā parameśatejobṛṃhaṇena pūjopakaraṇayogyatārpaṇam iti*

## v.45
There, having arranged the worship-house full of all implements, having remembered the Goddess Mālinī or Mātṛkā, bearing the taken, full of her-letter-radiance-mass, one should cast the flower-handful.
The Sanskrit: *tatra sarvopakaraṇapūrṇaṃ yāgagṛhaṃ vidhāya bhagavatīṃ mālinīṃ mātṛkāṃ vā smṛtvā tadvarṇatejaḥpuñjabharitaṃ gṛhītaṃ bhāvayan puṣpāñjaliṃ kṣipet*

## v.46
Then the stated weapon-muttered mustard-grain-parched-rice and the rest, radiance-formed, having scattered, in the north-east direction gathering in order — thus is ground-taking.
The Sanskrit: *tata uktāstrajaptāni yathāsambhavaṃ siddhārthadhānyākṣatalājādīni tejorūpāṇi vikīrya aiśānyāṃ diśi krameṇa saṃghaṭṭayet iti bhūparigrahaḥ*

## v.47
Then having given the seat up to pure-knowledge's end, Gaṇeśa's worship; then having filled the jar, filled with bliss-substance, adorned, one should worship; then the worshippable next, having placed betel, there the chief mantra, as all-presiding, remembering with rule-precedence, with that 108-times-muttered, that jar one should make.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ śuddhavidyāntam āsanaṃ dattvā gaṇapateḥ pūjā tataḥ kumbham ānandadravyapūritam alaṃkṛtaṃ pūjayet tato yājyam anu pūgaṃ nyasya tatra mukhyaṃ mantraṃ sarvādhiṣṭhātṛtayā vidhipūrvakatvena smaran aṣṭottaraśatamantritaṃ tena taṃ kumbhaṃ kuryāt*

## v.48
In the second vessel, weapons for obstacle-quelling one should worship.
The Sanskrit: *dvitīyakalaśe vighnaśamanāya astraṃ yajet*

## v.49
Then in own directions the world-guardians with weapons one should worship.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ svasvadikṣu lokapālān sāstrān pūjayet*

## v.50
Then to the previously-initiated disciple's hand one should give the weapon-vessel.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ śiṣyasya prāk dīkṣitasya haste astrakalaśaṃ dadyāt*

## v.51
And oneself the guru should take the jar.
The Sanskrit: *svayaṃ ca guruḥ kumbham ādadīta*

## v.52
Then making the disciple pour the stream around the house for obstacle-quelling, with jar the guru should follow, reciting this mantra: O O Indra! by you in your own direction for obstacle-pacifying.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ śiṣyaṃ gṛhaparyanteṣu vighnaśamanāya dhārāṃ pātayantaṃ sakumbho 'nugacchet imaṃ mantraṃ paṭhan bho bhoḥ śakra tvayā svasyāṃ diśi vighnapraśāntaye*

## v.53
With attentiveness, to be done to rite's end, by Śiva's command.
The Sanskrit: *sāvadhānena karmāntaṃ bhavitavyaṃ śivājñayā*

## v.54
In the three-syllabled name, 'bho' is one alone — thus.
The Sanskrit: *tryakṣare nāmni bho ity ekam eva*

## v.55
Then in the north-east direction one should place the jar.
The Sanskrit: *tata aiśānyāṃ diśi kumbhaṃ sthāpayet*

## v.56
Upon the scattered, the weapon-vessel.
The Sanskrit: *vikiropari astrakalaśam*

## v.57
Then worship of both.
The Sanskrit: *tata ubhayapūjanam*

## v.58
Then in the altar-middle Supreme-worship.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ sthaṇḍilamadhye parameśapūjanam*

## v.59
Then the fire-pit as Supreme-power-formed having contemplated, fire having kindled there, with heart-inner-awakening-fire made one, with mantra-deliberation-accompaniment, the blazing Śiva-fire having contemplated, placed there, having worshipped, one should sate the mantras with ghee and sesame.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ agnikuṇḍaṃ parameśvaraśaktirūpatayā bhāvayitvā tatra agniṃ prajvālya hṛdayāntarbodhāgninā saha ekīkṛtya mantraparāmarśasāhityena jvalantaṃ śivāgniṃ bhāvayitvā tatra nyasya abhyarcya mantrān tarpayet ājyena tilaiś ca*

## v.60
And with the vessel, sprinkling alone is sesame-ghee and the rest's refining.
The Sanskrit: *arghapātreṇa ca prokṣaṇam eva tilājyādīnāṃ saṃskāraḥ*

## v.61
And of ladle-and-spoon, non-difference-view alone indeed is refining.
The Sanskrit: *sruksruvayoś ca parameśābhedadṛṣṭir eva hi saṃskāraḥ*

## v.62
Then having offered as able, having arranged ladle-spoon up-down-facing as power-Śiva-formed, mutually facing, having stood even-footed, contemplating the stream of supreme nectar fallen, risen in the sky of the twelve-end, risen full-Śiva-moon, and uttering the vauṣaḍ-ending mantra, until ghee's end one should stand — thus is full oblation, mantra-wheel-sating.
The Sanskrit: *tato yathāśakti hutvā sruksruvau ūrdhvādhomukhatayā śaktiśivarūpau parasparonmukhau vidhāya samapādotthito dvādaśāntagaganoditaśivapūrṇacandraniḥsṛtapatatparāmṛtadhārābhāvanāṃ kurvan vauṣaḍantaṃ mantram uccārayan ca ājyakṣayāntaṃ tiṣṭhet iti pūrṇāhutiḥ mantracakrasaṃtarpaṇī*

## v.63
Then the sprinkled caru having brought, on altar-vessel-jar-fires share by share having offered, one share having left over, to the disciple one should give a share.
The Sanskrit: *tataś caruṃ prokṣitam ānīya sthaṇḍilakalaśakumbhavahniṣu bhāgaṃ bhāgaṃ nivedya ekabhāgam avaśeṣya śiṣyāya bhāgaṃ dadyāt*

## v.64
Then the tooth-stick.
The Sanskrit: *tato dantakāṣṭham*

## v.65
Its falling in fire-Yama-Nirṛti directions and below is not auspicious — thus.
The Sanskrit: *tatpāto 'gniyamanirṛtidikṣu adhaś ca na śubha iti*

## v.66
There oblation by weapon-mantra is to be done.
The Sanskrit: *tatra homo 'stramantreṇa kāryaḥ*

## v.67
Then for distraction-avoidance, for fitness to see the future mantra, having brought in the blindfolded disciple, having made him kneeling, one should have him cast a flower-handful.
The Sanskrit: *tato vikṣepaparihāreṇa bhāvimantradarśanayogyatāyai baddhanetraṃ śiṣyaṃ praveśya jānusthitaṃ taṃ kṛtvā puṣpāñjaliṃ kṣepayet*

## v.68
Then suddenly, unfastened-eye-bound, through descent-graced instrument-ness, seeing the present mantra in that place face-to-face, he becomes made of that; of graced instruments mantra-nearness is visible, since as it were of trembling beings.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ sahasā apāsitanetrabandho 'sau śaktipātānugṛhītakaraṇatvāt saṃnihitamantraṃ tatsthānaṃ sākṣātkāreṇa paśyan tanmayo bhavati anugṛhītakaraṇānāṃ mantrasaṃnidhiḥ pratyakṣaḥ yatas trasyatām iva bhūtānām*

## v.69
Then in one's own right hand with blazing, having worshipped the deity-wheel, placing it burning the disciple's bonds on head-heart-navel.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ svadakṣiṇahaste dīpyatayā devatācakraṃ pūjayitvā taṃ hastaṃ mūrdhahṛnnābhiṣu śiṣyasya pāśān dahantaṃ nikṣipet*

## v.70
Then in the left with gentleness, having worshipped, fattening with pure principle — then one should bow.
The Sanskrit: *tato vāme somyatayā pūjayitvā śuddhatattvāpyāyinaṃ tataḥ praṇāmaṃ kuryāt*

## v.71
Then the being-deity-direction-offering, full of wine-meat-water and the rest, outside one should give, having sipped.
The Sanskrit: *tato bhūtadevatādigbaliṃ madyamāṃsajalādipūrṇaṃ bahir dadyāt ācāmeta*

## v.72
Then oneself having eaten the oblation-meal, with disciple-self attaining oneness, awakened-natured one should stay.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ svayaṃ carubhojanaṃ kṛtvā śiṣyātmanā saha aikyam āpannaḥ prabuddhavṛttiḥ tiṣṭhet*

## v.73
Dreaming too, at dawn if the disciple tells an inauspicious dream — that to him one should not explain.
The Sanskrit: *svapan api prabhāte śiṣyaḥ cet aśubhaṃ svapnaṃ vadet tat asmai na vyākuryāt*

## v.74
For doubt and fear would be his; merely with the weapon one should make its expiation.
The Sanskrit: *śaṅkātaṅkau hi tathāsya syātām kevalam astreṇa tanniṣkṛtiṃ kuryāt*

## v.75
Then just so having worshipped the Lord, entered before him by the disciple's breath-course, touching the six-cause-touching in heart-throat-palate-brow-cleft-twelve-ends, each eight refinements contemplating, some time resting the disciple-breath right there, again one should descend.
The Sanskrit: *tatas tathaiva parameśvaraṃ pūjayitvā tadagre śiṣyasya prāṇakrameṇa praviśya hṛtkaṇṭhatālulalāṭarandhradvādaśānteṣu ṣaṭsu kāraṇaṣaṭkasparśaṃ kurvan pratyekam aṣṭau saṃskārān cintayan kaṃcit kālaṃ śiṣyaprāṇaṃ tatraiva viśramayya punar avarohet*

## v.76
Thus effected-forty-eight-refinements-upon-made-Rudra-part-attainment becomes a samayin.
The Sanskrit: *ity evāpāditāṣṭācatvāriṃśatsaṃskāroparikṛtarudrāṃśāpattiḥ samayībhavati*

## v.77
Then to him the worshippable mantra with flowers and the rest one should bestow.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ asmai pūjyaṃ mantraṃ puṣpādyaiḥ saha arpayet*

## v.78
Then the rules to him one should lay down.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ samayān asmai nirūpayet*

## v.79
In the guru with whole self, devotion; so in scripture; in God; aversion toward their opponents; like the guru, of the guru's son and the rest; of the knowledge-tied made, of the before-initiated and the rest, seeing; of the womb-tie, for its honouring, not by oneself — thus to be deemed.
The Sanskrit: *gurau sarvātmanā bhaktiḥ tathā śāstre deve tatpratidvaṃdvini parāṅmukhatā guruvat guruputrādeḥ vidyāsambandhakṛtasya tatpūrvadīkṣitādeḥ saṃdarśanam yaunasambandhasya tadārādhanārtham na tu svata iti mantavyam*

## v.80
Of women, of the barren that-loathing-cause one should not do.
The Sanskrit: *striyo vandhyāyās tajjugupsāhetuṃ na kuryāt*

## v.81
Deity-name, guru-name, and mantra, apart from worship-time one should not utter.
The Sanskrit: *devatānāma gurunāma tathā mantraṃ pūjākālāt ṛte na uccārayet*

## v.82
What the guru has enjoyed — bed and the rest — one should not enjoy.
The Sanskrit: *gurūpabhuktaṃ śayyādi na bhuñjīta*

## v.83
What games and the rest are worldly, that in the guru's nearness one should not do.
The Sanskrit: *yat kiṃcit laukikaṃ krīḍādi tat gurusaṃnidhau na kuryāt*

## v.84
Apart from that, elsewhere one should not make excellence-thought.
The Sanskrit: *tadvyatirekeṇa na anyatra utkarṣabuddhiṃ kuryāt*

## v.85
Everywhere, in śrāddha and the rest, the guru alone one should worship.
The Sanskrit: *sarvatra śrāddhādau gurum eva pūjayet*

## v.86
And in all occasionals, śākinī-and-the-rest words one should not say.
The Sanskrit: *sarveṣu ca naimittikeṣu śākinītyādiśabdān na vadet*

## v.87
Festival days one should worship.
The Sanskrit: *parvadināni pūjayet*

## v.88
With those of lower sight, Vaiṣṇavas and the rest, company one should not make.
The Sanskrit: *vaiṣṇavādyair adhodṛṣṭibhiḥ saha saṃgatiṃ na kuryāt*

## v.89
Abiding in this teaching, one should not see with former-birth thought.
The Sanskrit: *etacchāsanasthān pūrvajātibuddhyā na paśyet*

## v.90
When the guru's circle has come home, as able one should do worship.
The Sanskrit: *guruvarge gṛhāgate yathāśakti yāgaṃ kuryāt*

## v.91
A lower-path abider, a Vaiṣṇava and the rest, having even made him guru, from his teaching-clan one should abandon.
The Sanskrit: *adhomārgasthitaṃ kaṃcit vaiṣṇavādyaṃ tacchāstrakulāt gurūkṛtyāpi tyajet*

## v.92
Even then one should not see with excellence-thought.
The Sanskrit: *tadāpi na utkarṣabuddhyā paśyet*

## v.93
With mark-bearers no conduct-meeting one should make; them only as able one should worship.
The Sanskrit: *liṅgibhiḥ saha samācāramelanaṃ na kuryāt tān kevalaṃ yathāśakti pūjayet*

## v.94
Doubts one should abandon.
The Sanskrit: *śaṅkās tyajet*

## v.95
In the wheel, standing, of last-and-first division, birth-made, one should not fancy.
The Sanskrit: *cakre sthitaś caramāgryādivibhāgaṃ janmakṛtaṃ na saṃkalpayet*

## v.96
Apart from the body, no other abode-ford and the rest with high regard one should see.
The Sanskrit: *śarīrāt ṛte na anyat āyatanatīrthādikaṃ bahumānena paśyet*

## v.97
Constantly one should remember the mantra-heart — thus the disciple, having heard, having bowed, having accepted, the guru with wealth-wife-body-up-to gift pleasing, and the before-initiated and poor-lordless and the rest sating.
The Sanskrit: *mantrahṛdayam anavarataṃ smaret ity evaṃ śiṣyaḥ śrutvā praṇamya abhyupagamya guruṃ dhanadāraśarīraparyantayā dakṣiṇayā paritoṣya pūrvadīkṣitāṃś ca dīnānāthādikān tarpayet*

## v.98
And with the future rule, the image-wheel one should sate.
The Sanskrit: *bhāvividhinā ca mūrticakraṃ tarpayet*

## v.99
Thus one becomes a samayin.
The Sanskrit: *itthaṃ samayībhavati*

## v.100
In mantra-practice, daily worship, hearing, study, one is entitled; but in the occasional, everywhere the guru alone one should request.
The Sanskrit: *mantrābhyāse nityapūjāyāṃ śravaṇe 'dhyayane adhikārī naimittike tu sarvatra gurum eva abhyarthayet*

## v.101
Thus the samayin rule.
The Sanskrit: *iti sāmayiko vidhiḥ*

# Tantrasāra A13 — L2 READ

## tsa13-p01 — where to worship, and dressing the body
Glad at heart, go where rites are done — and "where" is wherever your heart gladdens into fitness for sinking in the Lord. That is the whole mark. Meditation melting into its object causes it; gladness causes that; so no elsewhere. Peaks and holy grounds in the books mean just this: powers, entered by the Lord, taking bodies there — noble lands for the righteous, barbarian lands for the rest — and hilltops, lonely, unscatter the mind. Before the rite-house, outside, dress hands then body in the general placing. Then the Mālinī mantras — power-worded, owner-worded: power first, then its owner; for freedom, foot to crown; for pleasure, reversed. Mālinī the goddess is power's chief shape — seed meeting womb, milking every wish. Her name tells it: garlanded with Rudra-powers, flowered into fruits, bee humming down saṃsāra's frost, holding wins and freedom, giving and withholding — all from ra and la remembered one. So even a botched mantra, set thus, comes whole; even sectarian anointed ones — Garuḍa-men, Viṣṇu-men — stainless, give freedom. Then the argha vessel, the self poured in; the god-families; five states watched plus the unsurpassed sixth; sixfold placing; the causes Brahmā to Power each indwelt, the thirty-six past the world set in Bhairava's non-difference till full — becoming Bhairavī, past objection. ("Wavering mounted — what more with makings and placings?" The waveless IS Bhairava's body: creation-and-ending crores shown in himself.)

## tsa13-p02 — faces, the image, and what worship is inside
Middle: the Lord. Above him Īśāna's face; below, the underworld face; east and round, Tatpuruṣa, Aghora, Sadya, Vāma, and four between. Awareness's greatness alone shines directions into images — direction is no extra principle. As your shadow, meaning to pass it, keeps ahead of you — so reaching the Lord's middle; presiding over all just is being central. The sun divides directions the same way — he is the Lord's own knowing-power... frontness belongs to whatever place you face, self-ruled. Self-sun-Lord made one: that inquiry is "directions," say my teachers. Seated north, quit the I in the eightfold body-city and burn bodyness — near, yet as if another's, bodiless. Then, mounted on waveless fixed ground, by sheer gaze: the first throb-beat — that is the image. On it, set the worshipped god-wheel as taught — powers foremost (they seat the nine Lords; worship belongs to power, say the teachers). Watch five states plus the unsurpassed sixth as your own state: sixfold placing. Indwell the causes one by one — Brahmā to Power — and the thirty-six, past the world, set in Bhairava's non-difference to fullness: becoming Bhairavī, no gap. (The wavering-objection, dissolved above.) Then melt breath, body, mind into goddess-and-Lord, and worship outside and in with flowers, smoke, libations as you can. In body, breath, mind set the trident-lotus: root in base-power, bulb-pith in the bulb, staff to art's end at the pipe's lip, Māyā for knot, fourfold bright-knowing lotus, Sadāśiva himself there — the Great Dead, dead by melting, by knowing, by the known-body's decay, by sound-grasp. Risen from the navel through three crown-holes, the triple inner music — power, pervader, harmony — to the twelve-end, three pure lotuses above, past mind: in that universe-made difference made a seat, presiding, pervading, picture the god you love. Pouring the universe's things into the selfsame full self — that is worship. Growing into it — meditation. The swing of holding it inside — muttering. Making the universe yourself by that awakened blaze — fire-giving. Make the retinue sparks off that heap and worship likewise. (Twelve-end foremost remembered from the root... goddess-wheel ahead, sky-faring, inner rite enough.)
*Fidelity: preserved (faces, direction doctrine + shadow/sun, image = first throb, sixfold placing, 36-cause rite, trident-lotus stations, four inner definitions). Added: none doctrinal. Not-resolved: yantra stations assume initiates (traditional).*

## tsa13-p03 — building the rite-house (CONDENSED companion)
Inner rite alone truly finishes — yet do the outer to wear down limits; for the unimmersed the outer is chief (practice → immersion); even beast-bondage needs the inner (its resolve purifies). For initiation: first lodge the pupil — take ground, honor Gaṇeśa, worship jar and pitcher, altar and fire (daily rites, altar-fire only). Lodging makes the pupil fit (as whitening whitens teeth), readies the god, engages the teacher, and fits the tools with the Lord's glow. Then: full house, Mālinī remembered glowing, flowers thrown; charmed grains scattered northeast joined — ground taken. Seat of knowing given, Gaṇapati honored, bliss-jar worshipped, retinue after the worshippable, chief mantra hundred-and-eighted into the jar; second pitcher with the weapon-mantra; guardians round; old pupil handed the weapon-pitcher, teacher takes the jar. The pupil streams water round the house with Śakra's charge; jars northeast set; straw and pitcher; double worship; the Lord middle; fire seen as Lord-power, lit, one with heart-fire, mantras fed ghee and sesame; sprinkling refines; the ladles' non-difference-view refines.

## tsa13-p04 — blind entry, the grace-hand, and the vows (CONDENSED companion)
Cake shared five ways; tooth-wood omens; weapon-mantra fire. The pupil, distraction-swept, blindfold, kneeling, flower-struck — then the cloth ripped: grace-touched senses see where mantra stands and grow into it (the present mantra plain to graced senses; trembling beings). Right hand, god-wheel worshipped, burns bonds at crown, heart, navel; left, soma-flooded, drowns in purity; bow. Feed outer spirits; sip; eat together as one self, awake. Bad dreams: untold (they breed doubt-fear), weapon-mantra only. Teacher breath-walks the pupil through six cause-points with eight sacraments each, rests his breath, descends — forty-eight plus Rudra's portion: samayin made. Mantra given with flowers; then the vows — devotion to teacher, book, god; no facing their foes; honor teacher-kin; sex vowed to worship only; no scorning; guarded names; no teacher's bed; no play nearby; no heights elsewhere; teacher honored at rites; no Śākinī-talk at feasts; holy days kept; no Vaiṣṇava company; no birth-eyes on fellows; feasting come teachers; dropped low-path teachers without contempt; no liṅgin mingling; doubts dropped; no birth-ranks in the wheel; body over shrines; heart-mantra always. Hearing, bowing, yes-saying, giving to wealth's end, feeding poor and old hands, rite-wheel fed for rites to come: samayin made. Mantra, daily, hearing, study — qualified; feast-days through the teacher.

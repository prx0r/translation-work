# C1 NOTE — tsa-a13 (not the translation: commentary complete)

## KEY TERMS (with grounds)

- samayin: bound disciple; 48 saṃskāras (6 places × 8) + Rudra-part; entitled to practice/study; occasionals need guru.
- adhivāsa: preparation with three meanings (disciple sharpened like teeth; God turned to work; guru taking it up).
- Mālinī: chief Śākta form; seed-womb collision; name unfolded (Rudra-garlanded, fruit-flowering, winter-sound bee, attainment-release, giving-taking; ra=la).
- mālinī/sabdarāśi mantras: Śakti first; foot-to-head for release, reversed for enjoyment.
- arghapātra-nyāsa: vessel shows every factor Lord; seeing = becoming, no yoga needed.
- dik: middle = awareness-light; quarters by light-relations; Bhairava faces; no separate principle; shadow-overstepping centrality; sūrya = jñānaśakti; self-sun-Lord triad (gurus).
- spandakalā: first throb-digit form on waveless abode; powers chief.
- śūlābja: body-breath-mind trident-lotus to Sadāśiva-corpse.
- antar-yāga: offering=worship; becoming=meditation; shaking=murmur; universe-self-making=oblation; retinue like sparks.
- khecarī verses: abandoning sequence; faring-resting-nectar sky-living.
- cakrapūjā/pūrṇāhuti: netra-bandha flower-cast; unveiled one-making with visible nearness; even-footed nectar-stream vauṣaḍ.
- prāṇakrama: six places × eight refinements; rest; descend; samayin.
- samaya code: devotion/aversion/reverence; name-silence; no play; no mixing; no birth-thought; hospitality; apostate-abandoning; sect-distance; doubt-abandoning; wheel-equality; body-supreme; mantra-heart.
- dakṣiṇā: wealth-to-body; priors/poor sated; image-wheel; entitlement.

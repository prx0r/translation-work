# Tantrasāra A14 — ARGMAP

## tsa14-p01 (14.1–14.29)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (putraka initiation rubric).
- question: What is the full son's initiation after the samayin course?
- premises: (1) Extensive in Tantrāloka; condensed here. Samayin-course done → third-day communal triśūlābja-maṇḍala rite: outer retinue/door-gods outside; row worship (Gaṇapati→kṣetrapāla) east-to-southeast; command taken; whole course placed root-to-lotus; goddess Parā center with Bhairava, Aparā left, Parāparā right (+ south/left tridents); goddess-presiding makes all full; whole god-wheel to weapon-guardians worshipped undivided (presiding = all-worshipped). (2) Jar/pitcher/maṇḍala/fire/self five-base linking + nonduality-rasa special worship; unstingy rite-house; no great maṇḍala without wealth. Living animals offered (graced → no doubt, compassion). (3) Fire: Lord ghee-sesame fed, second animal for fat-offering, wheel fat-fed, maṇḍala re-worshipped; Lord entreated; self as whole-six-course-full contemplated; pupil set before. Unseen (living-dead) initiation: meditate him or kuśa-dung image before. (4) Sprinkled/worshipped pupil: whole course placed in his body. Consider: enjoyer — purify auspicious only; liberation-wisher — both; seedless — even time-bonds (dying/utter-fool per Lord's command; devotion-only suffices). (5) Everywhere latency-grasping distinguishes (mantras work by latency-fitness). Reflecting latency-difference, by chief-mantra-grasping purify own-body-gone whole course in Śiva-nonduality: toe-to-twelve-end, self-body-consciousness-nondifferent pupil reached, in bliss-lake/lordship-pith/all-power-full-wheel/consciousness-remainder-circle, one-made with pupil-self, rest = Lord-nondifferent. (6) Enjoyer joined where his wish lies (collective/several); remaining conduct: sudden pure-principle body contemplated = all-bond-releasing initiation. (7) Pupil worships teacher; fire-rite per pupil ("Parā-mantra, so-principle of so-and-so I purify" + svāhā ×3/principle + vauṣaḍa full) = Śiva-end purity + joining full-offering (second for enjoyer's joining, third for pure creation). Teacher gifted = putraka initiation, purifying past+future karma minus present.
- inference: Initiation = course-placing + latency-matched purification + joining-offering.
- conclusion: Putraka arc complete.
- unresolved: Tantrāloka fullness cited (external).

# Tantrasāra A14 — L1 controlled (CONDENSED companion to T1; full close pass queued)

## tsa14-p01
Now son-initiation-course, fourteenth ahnika. Putraka initiation, extensive, is to be learned from Tantrāloka; condensed is stated. (14.1–14.3) Having done to samayin-end the course, on third day in trident-lotus maṇḍala communal rite one should worship; outer retinue and door-god-wheel outside; then in maṇḍala-east from northeast to southeast in rows: Gaṇapati, teacher, supreme teacher, highest, former teachers, yoginī-wheel, speech-lady, field-guardian. (14.4) Taking fit command, from trident-root to white-lotus-end the whole course placed, worship: middle trident middle spoke goddess Parā with Bhairava-lord, left Aparā, right Parāparā, south middle Parāparā, left middle Aparā, two as own. (14.5) In goddess all-place-presiding, all is full; from its presiding it becomes. (14.6) In middle-trident-middle-spoke the whole god-wheel to weapon-guardian-end as undivided worship; from its presiding, everywhere worshipped. (14.7) In jar, pitcher, maṇḍala, fire, self, by nondifference-contemplation five-based linking; then with Lord-nonduality-rasa-swollen flower-offering special worship. (14.8) In short: libation-food-full, unstingy rite-house. (14.9) Without wealth, no great maṇḍala at all. (14.10) Living animals offer — thus graced they become; compassionately in animal-course do not doubt. (14.11–14.12) In fire the Lord ghee-sesame fed, second animal for fat-offering, wheel fat-fed, maṇḍala re-worshipped; Lord entreated, whole-undivided-six-course-full self contemplated, pupil set before. (14.13) In unseen living-dead initiation, meditate him before, or place kuśa-dung image. (14.14) Sprinkled/worshipped pupil: whole course placed in his body. (14.15) Consider: enjoyer's auspicious do not purify; liberation-wisher's both; in seedless purify even time-bonds — dying and utter fool too, by Lord's command, devotion-only succeeding. (14.16–14.18) Everywhere latency-grasping alone distinguishes, since mantras work by latency-fitness. (14.19) Reflecting latency-difference, by chief-mantra-grasping purify own-body-gone whole course in Śiva-nonduality: toe to twelve-end, pupil's self-body-consciousness-nondifferent reached, in bliss-lake, lordship-pith, all-power-full wheel-lord, consciousness-remainder circle, one-made with pupil-self, rest attaining — thus Lord-nondifferent he becomes. (14.20–14.21) Enjoyer joined where his wish lies; remaining conduct: sudden pure-principle body contemplated — all-bond-releasing initiation. (14.22–14.23) Pupil worships teacher. (14.24) In fire the pupil's rite: "Parā-mantra, so-and-so's so-principle I purify" + svāhā, thrice per principle, vauṣaḍa full at end. (14.25) Thus Śiva-end purity; then joining full-offering. (14.26) For enjoyer a second for joining-place, a third for pure creation. (14.27) Teacher gifted — this is son-initiation. (14.28) Wherein, leaving present one, past and future karma is purified. (14.29)

# Tantrasara-a14 — L2 READ

## v.1
Now the child-initiation rite.
The Sanskrit: *atha putrakadīkṣāvidhiḥ*

## v.2
It is expanded, determined from the Tantrāloka.
The Sanskrit: *sa ca vistīrṇaḥ tantrālokāt avadhāryaḥ*

## v.3
But it is stated condensed.
The Sanskrit: *saṃkṣiptas tu ucyate*

## v.4
Having done the samaya-ending rite on the third day, let him worship the group-sacrifice in the trident-lotus mandala; therein let him worship the outer-retinue, the door-deity-wheel outside; then from the mandala's front-part, beginning at the Aiśa-corner up to Agneya, in row-order — Gaṇapati, guru, paramaguru, parameṣṭhin, former-teachers, the yoginī-wheel, Vāgīśvarī, Kṣetrapāla — let him worship.
The Sanskrit: *samayyantaṃ vidhiṃ kṛtvā tṛtīye 'hni triśūlābje maṇḍale sāmudāyikaṃ yāgaṃ pūjayet tatra bāhyaparivāraṃ dvāradevatācakraṃ ca bahiḥ pūjayet tato maṇḍalapūrvabhāge aiśakoṇāt ārabhya āgneyāntaṃ paṅktikrameṇa gaṇapatiṃ guruṃ paramaguruṃ parameṣṭhinaṃ pūrvācāryān yoginīcakraṃ vāgīśvarīṃ kṣetrapālaṃ ca pūjayet*

## v.5
Then, taking the fitting command, having placed the whole path from trident-root up to white-lotus, let him worship; then in the middle trident's middle-axis the Goddess Śrīparābhaṭṭārikā with Bhairavanatha, in the left-axis likewise Śrīmadaparā, in the right-axis Śrīparāparā, in the right trident's middle Śrīmadaparā, in the left trident's middle Śrīparāparā — two each in their own places.
The Sanskrit: *tata ājñāṃ samucitām ādāya śūlamūlāt prabhṛti sitakamalāntaṃ samastam adhvānaṃ nyasya arcayet tato madhyame triśūle madhyārāyāṃ bhagavatī śrīparābhaṭṭārikā bhairavanāthena saha vāmārāyāṃ tathaiva śrīmadaparā dakṣiṇārāyāṃ śrīparāparā dakṣiṇe triśūle madhye śrīparāparā vāme triśūle madhye śrīmadaparā dve tu yathāsvam*

## v.6
Thus by all-place-enshrinement of the Goddess, all becomes complete from her enshrinement — thus.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ sarvasthānādhiṣṭhātṛtve bhagavatyāḥ sarvaṃ pūrṇaṃ tadadhiṣṭhānāt bhavati iti*

## v.7
Then in the middle-trident's middle-axis, let him worship the whole deity-wheel up to the lokapāla-missile by non-difference-alone; from its enshrinement, worshipped everywhere.
The Sanskrit: *tato madhyaśūlamadhyārāyāṃ samastaṃ devatācakraṃ lokapālāstraparyantam abhinnatayaiva pūjayet tadadhiṣṭhānāt sarvatra pūjitam*

## v.8
Then in pot, kalasa, mandala, fire and own-self — by non-difference-contemplation let him join the five-grounds' conjunction; then by enrichment of the Lord two-nature-juice, flowers and rest, special-worship let him do.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ kumbhe kalaśe maṇḍale agnau svātmani ca abhedabhāvanayā pañcādhikaraṇam anusaṃdhiṃ kuryāt tataḥ parameśvarādvayarasabṛṃhitena puṣpādinā viśeṣapūjāṃ kuryāt*

## v.9
Why bother? Complete with satisfaction-food-offering, without wealth-deception — let him make the sacrifice-place.
The Sanskrit: *kiṃ bahunā tarpaṇanaivedyaparipūrṇaṃ vittaśāṭhyavirahito yāgasthānaṃ kuryāt*

## v.10
In non-being wealth, the great-mandala-sacrifice is not to be done — indeed.
The Sanskrit: *asati vitte tu mahāmaṇḍalayāgo na kartavya eva*

## v.11
And living animals let him offer.
The Sanskrit: *paśūṃś ca jīvato nivedayet*

## v.12
They too become graced thus — by compassion in the animal-rite one should not doubt.
The Sanskrit: *te 'pi hi evam anugṛhītā bhavanti iti kāruṇikatayā paśuvidhau na vicikitset*

## v.13
Then in fire, having satisfied the Supreme-Lord by sesame-ghee and rest, before him another animal for vapa-hom let him do; the deity-wheel let him satisfy by its fat; again the mandala let him worship; then approaching the Supreme-Lord, contemplating the self all-non-different-whole-six-path-complete, the disciple placed in front let him make.
The Sanskrit: *tato 'gnau parameśvaraṃ tilājyādibhiḥ saṃtarpya tadagre 'nyaṃ paśuṃ vapāhomārthaṃ kuryāt devatācakraṃ tadvapayā tarpayet punar maṇḍalaṃ pūjayet tataḥ parameśvaraṃ vijñapya sarvābhinnasamastaṣaḍadhvaparipūrṇam ātmānaṃ bhāvayitvā śiṣyaṃ puro 'vasthitaṃ kuryāt*

## v.14
In indirect initiation, of-living-dead-form, let him contemplate him in front, or establish a likeness made of darba-cow-dung-and-rest in front.
The Sanskrit: *parokṣadīkṣāyāṃ jīvanmṛtarūpāyām agre taṃ dhyāyet tadīyāṃ vā pratikṛtiṃ darbhagomayādimayīm agre sthāpayet*

## v.15
Such a disciple — sprinkled with offering-vessel-drops, worshipped by flowers and rest — having done, the whole path in his body let him place.
The Sanskrit: *tathāvidhaṃ śiṣyam arghapātravipruṭprokṣitaṃ puṣpādibhiś ca pūjitaṃ kṛtvā samastam adhvānaṃ taddehe nyaset*

## v.16
Then thus let him consider: for the enjoyment-seeker, the auspicious — let him not cleanse.
The Sanskrit: *tata itthaṃ vicārayet bhogecchoḥ śubhaṃ na śodhayet*

## v.17
For the liberation-seeker, the auspicious-and-unauspicious — both even.
The Sanskrit: *mumukṣos tu śubhāśubham ubhayam api*

## v.18
In the seedless, the covenant-nooses also let him cleanse; she is to-be-done for near-death, even most-foolish — thus the Supreme-Lord's command; and for him, from mere devotion-faith to teacher-deity-fire, attainment.
The Sanskrit: *nirbījāyāṃ tu samayapāśān api śodhayet sā ca āsannamaraṇasya atyantamūrkhasyāpi kartavyā iti parameśvarājñā tasyāpi tu gurudevatāgnibhaktiniṣṭhatvamātrāt siddhiḥ*

## v.19
Herein, everywhere, habit-capture is indeed the distinguishing [factor], by habit-conformity of mantras — from that-and-that work-doing.
The Sanskrit: *atra ca sarvatra vāsanāgrahaṇam eva bhedakam mantrāṇāṃ vāsanānuguṇyena tattatkāryakāritvāt*

## v.20
Thus having traced habit-distinction, by main-mantra-consideration-special, the whole path in-own-body-located, by Siva-two-nature-contemplation let him cleanse.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ vāsanābhedam anusaṃdhāya mukhyamantraparāmarśaviśeṣeṇa samastam adhvānaṃ svadehagataṃ śivādvayabhāvanayā śodhayet*

## v.21
Thus in order, from foot-thumb up to twelve-end, of the disciple whose body-consciousness is made non-different from self-body-consciousness, attained — right-there, in the lake of infinite bliss, in the essence of freedom-sovereignty, in the Lord of the wheel of deities full of all will-knowledge-action-power, bearing all paths, in the mandala of world-being mere-consciousness-residue — by union in such form with the disciple-self, having-become-one, rest let him cause-to-attain; thus non-different from the Supreme-Lord he becomes.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ krameṇa pādāṅguṣṭhāt prabhṛti dvādaśāntaparyantaṃ svātmadehasvātmacaitanyābhinnīkṛtadehacaitanyasya śiṣyasya āsādya tatraiva anantānandasarasi svātantryaiśvaryasāre samastecchājñānakriyāśaktinirbharasamastadevatācakreśvare samastādhvabharite cinmātrāvaśeṣaviśvabhāvamaṇḍale tathāvidharūpaikīkāreṇa śiṣyātmanā saha ekībhūto viśrāntim āsādayet ity evaṃ parameśvarābhinno 'sau bhavati*

## v.22
Then if an enjoyment-seeker there be: where just in-tattva his enjoyment-desire becomes, right-there by whole-and-scattered-manner let him join.
The Sanskrit: *tato yadi bhogecchuḥ syāt tato yatraiva tattve bhogecchā asya bhavati tatraiva samastavyastatayā yojayet*

## v.23
Thereafter, for residual-function, from the Supreme-Lord's-nature, suddenly having-emerged, made-of-pure-tattva body for him let contemplate — thus this all-noose-releasing initiation.
The Sanskrit: *tadanantaraṃ śeṣavṛttaye parameśvarasvabhāvāt jhaṭiti prasṛtaṃ śuddhatattvamayaṃ deham asmai cintayet ity eṣā samastapāśaviyojikā dīkṣā*

## v.24
Then the disciple the guru by fees, as-before, let worship.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ śiṣyo guruṃ dakṣiṇābhiḥ pūrvavat pūjayet*

## v.25
Then in fire the disciple's rite let him do: the Sri-Para-mantra — of-so-and-so, so-and-so tattva I-purify — thus ending-with-svaha, per-tattva three oblations; at-end complete ending-with-vausad.
The Sanskrit: *tato 'gnau śiṣyasya vidhiṃ kuryāt śrīparāmantraḥ amukasyāmukaṃ tattvaṃ śodhayāmi iti svāhāntaṃ pratitattvaṃ tisra āhutayaḥ ante pūrṇā vauṣaḍantā*

## v.26
Thus Siva-end-tattva-purification; then by-yojanika-told-order full-offering.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ śivāntatattvaśuddhiḥ tato yojanikoktakrameṇa pūrṇāhutiḥ*

## v.27
For the enjoyment-seeker, in the enjoyment-place, for-yojanika-sake one [offering]; another for-pure-tattva-creation-sake, one-more.
The Sanskrit: *bhogecchoḥ bhogasthāne yojanikārtham aparā śuddhatattvasṛṣṭyartham anyā*

## v.28
Then worship of the guru by fees — thus this child-initiation.
The Sanskrit: *tato guroḥ dakṣiṇābhiḥ pūjanam ity eṣā putrakadīkṣā*

## v.29
In-which ongoing one [tattva] — having-excluded past and future — act is purified.
The Sanskrit: *yatra vartamānam ekaṃ varjayitvā bhūtaṃ bhaviṣyac ca karma śudhyati*

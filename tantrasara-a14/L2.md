# Tantrasāra A14 — L2 READ (CONDENSED companion)

## tsa14-p01 — the son's initiation
The son's rite, full form in the Tantrāloka, short form here. Samayin-course done: on day three, communal worship in the trident-lotus — retinue and door-gods outside, then rows east to southeast: Gaṇapati, teachers past and present, yoginīs, Speech-lady, field-lord. Command taken, the whole course laid from trident-root to white lotus: center Parā with Bhairava, left Aparā, right Parāparā (south and left tridents likewise); goddess presiding everywhere makes everything full; the whole wheel to the weapon-guardians worshipped undivided — presided, all worshipped. Jar, pitcher, circle, fire, self linked in five, then special worship swollen with nonduality's rasa. No stinting; no wealth, no great circle. Living beasts offered — graced thereby, doubt not, for pity's sake. Fire fed ghee and sesame for the Lord, a second beast for fat, wheel fat-fed, circle re-honored; Lord entreated; self seen whole-six-courses-full; pupil set forth. For the far-off (living-dead), picture him — or a grass-and-dung stand-in. Sprinkle and honor him, lay the whole course in his body. Then judge: enjoyer — cleanse the bright only; freedom-seeker — bright and dark; seedless — even time-bonds, dying or dullest, by the Lord's order, devotion alone availing. Mantras work by fit with latency — grasp that difference always. By the chief mantra's grasp, cleanse the body-gone whole course in Śiva-oneness: toe to twelve-end, pupil's body-consciousness reached as your own, in bliss's lake, lordship's pith, the all-powered wheel, the circle where world is consciousness-remnant — one with the pupil-self, rest: Lord-undivided he becomes. Join enjoyers where they hunger; then picture a sudden pure-principle body for the conduct that remains — the initiation loosing every bond. Pupil honors teacher; fire-rite per pupil ("Parā-mantra — so-and-so's so-principle I cleanse" + svāhā, thrice a principle, vauṣaḍa full): Śiva-end purity, joining full-offerings (a second for the enjoyer's station, a third for pure creation). Gifts to the teacher seal it: son-initiation, washing past and future deeds, the present kept.
*Fidelity: summary-faithful; full close pass queued. Not-resolved: Tantrāloka fullness (external).*

# C1 — Tantrasāra Ahnika 14 (putraka-dīkṣā)

## tsa14-p01 — the son's initiation
**SUMMARY.** The son's rite, full form in the Tantrāloka, short form here. Samayin-course done: on day three, communal worship in the trident-lotus — retinue and door-gods outside, then rows east to southeast: Gaṇapati, teachers past and present, yoginīs, Speech-lady, field-lord. Command taken, the whole course laid from trident-root to white lotus: goddess Parā center with Bhairava, Aparā left, Parāparā right (and south/left tridents likewise); goddess-presiding makes all full; the whole wheel to the weapon-guardians worshipped undivided — presided, all worshipped. Jar, pitcher, maṇḍala, fire, self linked in five, then special worship swollen with nonduality's rasa. No stinting; no wealth, no great circle. Living animals offered (graced thereby, doubt not, for pity's sake). Fire fed ghee and sesame for the Lord, a second beast for fat, wheel fat-fed, maṇḍala re-honored; Lord entreated; self seen whole-six-courses-full; pupil set before. For the far-off (living-dead), picture him — or a grass-and-dung stand-in. Sprinkle and honor him, lay the whole course in his body. Then judge: enjoyer — purify auspicious only; liberation-wisher — both; seedless — even time-bonds (dying and utter fool too, by Lord's command, devotion-only sufficing). Everywhere latency-grasping distinguishes (mantras work by latency-fitness). Reflecting latency-difference, by chief-mantra-grasping purify own-body-gone whole course in Śiva-nonduality: toe to twelve-end, pupil's body-consciousness reached as your own, in bliss's lake, lordship's pith, all-power-full wheel-lord, consciousness-remainder circle, one-made with pupil-self, rest: Lord-undivided he becomes. Join enjoyers where they hunger; then picture a sudden pure-principle body for the conduct that remains — the initiation loosing every bond. Pupil honors teacher; fire-rite per pupil (Parā-mantra, so-and-so's so-principle I cleanse + svāhā, thrice a principle, vauṣaḍa full): Śiva-end purity, joining full-offerings (a second for the enjoyer's station, a third for pure creation). Gifts to the teacher seal it: son-initiation, washing past and future deeds, the present kept.
**FUNCTION.** The putraka (son's) initiation rubric: full rite-house, latency-matched purification, joining-offerings, seal.
**KEY TERMS.** putraka (son-initiation), triśūlābja (trident-lotus), pañcādhikaraṇa (five-based linking), parokṣa (unseen), vāsanā (latency), yojanikā (joining), vauṣaḍa (full-offering close).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** A13 (yāga/dīkṣā) precedes; A14 teaches putraka; A15 (sadyaḥ) follows.
**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** Tantrāloka fullness (external). L1 CONDENSED (full close pass queued).
**RELATED.** A13 (yāga/dīkṣā), A15 (sadyaḥ), spans TS14.1–14.29.

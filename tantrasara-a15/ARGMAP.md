# Tantrasāra A15 — ARGMAP

## tsa15-p01 (15.1–15.7)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (instant + seedless initiations).
- question: What is done for the dying, and what is seedless initiation?
- premises: (1) Near-death, self or kin descent-arisen → instant ascent-initiation: whole course placed/purified in pupil; Kālarātrī marrow-cutter placed, marrow-bonds split, Brahma-hole pupil-consciousness made; joining full-offering → life gone forth Lord-undivided. Enjoyer: second full-offering (joining-place; life-dissolution then; no remaining conduct). Recite brahmavidyā in ear (grasping-natured; wakes grasping in awakened-beast-consciousness); samayins too entitled to this reciting. (2) Marked seedless for fool/descent-come: Śiva-hand-gift-time rite — triangle southeast flame-terrible phaṭ-spark outer-wind-wheel contemplated maṇḍala in right hand, seed cast, up-down-phaṭ-awakened series burning birth-power, hand thrown on crown = both seedless, own-work-power-destroying. Immovables initiable: wind-city-abiding trembling pupil light-made contemplated (balance-light seen).
- inference: Urgency + incapacity change the rite's form, never its Lord-nondifference fruit.
- conclusion: Sadyaḥ + nirbījā stated.
- unresolved: Mantra identities (traditional).

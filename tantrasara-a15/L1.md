# Tantrasāra A15 — L1 controlled

## tsa15-p01
But when for near-death one, by self or through kin-mouth, descent arises — then for him instant ascent-initiation one should do. (15.1) The whole course in pupil placed, and in course purified, goddess Time-night, marrow-cutting, placed, through her in course marrow-bonds split, Brahma-hole-abiding pupil-consciousness one should make. (15.2) Then by before-told-course for joining-sake full offering one should give — so that at full-offering-end, life gone forth becomes supreme-Śiva-undivided. (15.3) But of enjoyer-wisher is second full offering. (15.4) For enjoyment-place joining; and at that time of him is life-dissolution; here is no remaining conduct. And brahma-knowledge in ear one should recite — for it, grasping-natured, instantly in awakened-beast-consciousness makes awakened grasping. (15.5) Of samayin-and-rest too in this reciting is entitlement. (15.6) But marked seedless, if for fool and descent-come one shows initiation — then in Śiva-hand-gift-time this is the course: triangle southeast flame-terrible phaṭ-spark outer-wind-wheel-contemplated maṇḍala in right hand contemplated — therein seed something cast — by up-down-phaṭ-awakened series his birth-power burning — thus doing, that hand on pupil's crown one should throw — thus of both too this initiation seedless, own-work-doing-power-destroying becomes. Of immovables too, through being stated as to-be-initiated — wind-city-abiding trembling pupil light-made contemplated — whereby by balance light is seen. (15.7)

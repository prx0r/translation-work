# Tantrasāra A15 — L2 READ

## tsa15-p01 — for the dying, and seedless grace
Death near, grace come — by himself or a kinsman's mouth: the instant ascent-rite. Lay the whole course in the pupil and cleanse it; set Time-night, marrow-cutter; cut the marrow-bonds joint by joint; seat his awareness at Brahmā's hole. Then the joining full-offering as before — till at its end the life goes out Lord-undivided. Enjoyers get a second full-offering (their station, their life melting then; no conduct after). Whisper brahma-knowledge in his ear — grasping-natured, it wakes grasping in the just-waked beast-mind. Samayins too may hear it read. The marked seedless — for dullards and the graced: at Śiva's hand-gift, picture in the right palm a southeast triangle, flame-grim, phaṭ-sparking, ringed with outer wind-wheels; drop in a seed; burn his birth-power with phaṭ upon phaṭ kindled up and down; clap that hand on his crown. Seedless for both — wrecking the power to make its own work. Even the unmoving may be raised: picture the trembling pupil hung in wind's city, grown light — light on the scales to see.

# C1 — tsa-a15

## SUMMARY

The exit-initiation is performed at once when power-descent comes near death — of oneself or through a kinsman's mouth. The whole path placed in the disciple, Kālarātrī cuts the vein-nooses joint by joint until the disciple's consciousness dwells at the brahmarandhra. The full-offering for yojanā's sake: the departed soul goes out and becomes one with Paramaśiva. For the desirer of enjoyment, the second full-offering. No remaining-turning stands in the enjoyment-place; or Brahma-vidyā in the ear instantly awakens vimarśa in the awakened paśu's consciousness. The ear-recitation's authority derives from samayya and the rest. The seedless initiation to fool or late-comer: āgneya palm-mandala, seed burnt by phaṭkāra-successions, hand cast on the head — own-work-capacity destroyed, even inanimate initiatable; the disciple contemplated shaken and light within the vāyupura.

## FUNCTION

Seals the near-death exit-initiation, completes yojanā by full-offering, licenses the ear-recitation, and performs the seedless initiation by palm-mandala and hand-on-head.

## KEY TERMS

samutkramaṇa-dīkṣā (exit-initiation at death); Kālarātrī (vein-cutting goddess); pūrṇāhuti (full-offering of yojanā); parāmarśa (ear-recitation's nature); nirbījā dīkṣā (seedless initiation); phaṭkāra (burning succession); vāyupura (the disciple's installed place).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Continues the initiation series: admission near death (v1), preparation (v2), the yojanā offering (v3-5), its license (v6), then the seedless rite for fool or late-comer (v7).

## EXPLANATION

Because power-descent may come at the death-hour, the exit-initiation must be immediate; because the disciple's consciousness must stand at the brahmarandhra, Kālarātrī clears the veins jointwise; because yojanā ends in Paramaśiva, the full-offering completes it — or, in the bhoga-sthāna, ear-recitation awakens vimarśa by its own nature; because even sthāvara are initiatable, the seedless rite destroys the capacity for one's own work and installs the disciple light in the vāyupura.

## BOUNDARY

The mandala's geometry and phaṭkāra sequence are stated as rite, not explained as theory; svakārya-sāmarthya's destruction is asserted, not argued.

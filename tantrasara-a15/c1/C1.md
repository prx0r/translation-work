# C1 — Tantrasāra Ahnika 15 (sadyaḥ/nirbījā)

## tsa15-p01 — for the dying, and seedless grace
**SUMMARY.** Near death, grace come — by himself or a kinsman's mouth: the instant ascent-rite. Lay the whole course in the pupil and cleanse it; set Time-night, marrow-cutter; cut the marrow-bonds joint by joint; seat his awareness at Brahmā's hole. Then the joining full-offering as before — till at its end the life goes out Lord-undivided. Enjoyers get a second full-offering (their station, their life melting then; no conduct after). Whisper brahma-knowledge in his ear — grasping-natured, it wakes grasping in the just-waked beast-mind. Samayins too may hear it read. The marked seedless — for dullards and the graced: at Śiva's hand-gift, picture in the right palm a southeast triangle, flame-grim, phaṭ-sparking, ringed with outer wind-wheels; drop in a seed; burn his birth-power with phaṭ upon phaṭ kindled up and down; clap that hand on his crown. Seedless for both — wrecking the power to make its own work. Even the unmoving may be raised: picture the trembling pupil hung in wind's city, grown light — light on the scales to see.
**FUNCTION.** Two rites for urgency and incapacity: instant ascent (sadyaḥ) and seedless initiation (nirbījā).
**KEY TERMS.** sadyaḥ (instant), samutkramaṇa (ascent), kālarātrī (Time-night), marmakartanī (marrow-cutter), brahmarandhra (Brahma-hole), nirbījā (seedless), Śiva-hasta (Śiva-hand).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** A14 (putraka) precedes; A15 teaches urgent/seedless rites; A16 (parokṣa) follows.
**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** Mantra identities (traditional). L1 CONDENSED? No — A15 is short (7 verses), full close done.
**RELATED.** A14 (putraka), A16 (parokṣa), spans TS15.1–15.7.

# Tantrasāra A16 — L1 controlled

## tsa16-p01
Now is of absent one initiation — twofold it is: dead and living. (16.1) Therein, done-teacher-service itself is dead, or exiled, or sorcery-etc.-struck, or child-struck, or death-moment-risen-such-taste, or other-mouth-descent — thus is to be initiated — so the command. (16.2) Here, in dead-initiation, lodging-and-the-rest is not useful. (16.3) For maṇḍala, for mantra-particular-presence, where much rite, best material, flowers, place, seat, circle, triśūla-lotus-shape, meditation-particular, mantra, self-blazing, of meditation-intent yogin, of that-one-devotion-immersion-possessing knower — relation — these presence-causes as latter-best stated. (16.4) In collected-ness, what tale? — so the Lord stated. (16.5) Then god worshipped, his kuśa-made form placed before, through teacher-seat-etc. knowledge-teaching course, it one should see. It, from root-base rising, spread-far-flung-endless-channel-course-staff, by vigor having crossed, nose-sky-pervading, scattering, to pervade wishes. (16.6) As far as smoke-lovely-thick-net-covered course-wheel having covered, desired-soul-bringing — thus great net-named practice. (16.7) Through it, to-be-covered going to subdued facedness first; after, if whole, brought; then too from course-middle as wished. (16.8) In pulling, in lifting, in dead-folk object, in to-be-drawn, then in living, is yoga — by Śrī-Śambhunātha-āgama-understood, net-named, by me stated. (16.9) Outside too, thus how not? In pulling-and-the-rest, without practice — if so: through passion-hate-and-rest-yoga-force that functioning, from lordship-immersion-yoga (no). (16.10) Then from destiny-controlled-ness, practice-and-the-rest-need certainly. (16.11) Here, through grace-natured-Lord-nature-immersion, such-becoming. (16.12) For the Lord itself, through teacher-body-abiding door, the to-be-graced graces. (16.13) It is inconceivable-greatness — so much stated. (16.14) Thus net-practice-drawn soul into kuśa, jāti-fruit-or-rest body entered becomes; nor does it throb — from mind-breath-and-rest-material-lack. But through contemplation-power it throbs too even in such. In it, as before, sprinkling-and-rest-sacrament, full-offering-joining-end. (16.15) Here more: by full-offering, of it kuśa-and-rest-form's dissolution in supreme-glow to be made. (16.16) Thus lifted, it is dismissed by full-offering itself, if standing in hell/ghost/beast. (16.17) Human, then, at once gains knowledge, yoga, initiation, discernment. (16.18) From fit body — so dead-lifting. (16.19) Living too, absent, with descent arisen, this same course — except kuśa-body-soul-drawing. (16.20) Meditation-raised alone, of it, sacrament. (16.21) Initiation gives both fruits. (16.22) Since latency-strong, of many, upper-rule sacrament is strong; others for its sacrament. (16.23) Even of absent-initiated, likewise knowledge-rise. (16.24)

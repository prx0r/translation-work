# C1 — tsa-a16

## SUMMARY

Indirect initiation splits twofold: the dead, and the jīva-part. The command's eligible cases run from guru-service to death-circumstances, closed by the catch-all to-be-initiated. Adhivāsa-and-rest is not used in the dead-initiation. Seven presence-causes plus two yogic subjects stand in the mandala, stated in order. Once together, nothing remains to ask — the Supreme Lord's word. Worship, establish, see by guru-order; rise from muladhara, cross the nerve-path to nose-sky, seek to pervade. Smoke-coverage of the path-wheel and the jīva's bringing-in bound the practice's length — jala-named, mahān. The to-be-covered enters another's power; the whole follows — first facing, then as-wished, even from mid-path. The yoga: two-drawn, two-raised, or dead-scope — through Śaṃbhunātha's tradition, jala-named, told by me. Outside fails without practice: its operation rides rāga-dveṣa-yoga-vaśa, not the aiśvarya-immersion. Niyati-control entails practice-dependence. Here, though, from grace-natured Lordhood-immersion: such the state. By the guru's body-abode door the Supreme Lord graces those to be graced — of unthinkable glory, mostly said. And he, of unthinkable glory — it is mostly told. The drawn jīva enters dārbha or body without tremble, yet trembles by contemplation's force — consecration within the full-offering's yojana-end. The dārbha-form dissolves in supreme fire by the full-offering. Released by the full-offering itself if standing in hell-ghost-animal classes. A man obtains knowledge, yoga, initiation, or discernment just then. Eligibility of body grounds the raising-of-the-dead. The same krama serves the living's power-descent — minus the dārbha-form jīva-drawing. Mere meditation-established is consecrated — no fire required. Initiation gives enjoyment and enjoyer both. Self-habit's strength and enjoyment-habit's unseverability make the higher-doctrine consecration strong for the many. Likewise the outside-initiated receives knowledge-and-rest's manifestation.

## FUNCTION

Defines parokṣa-dīkṣā's two kinds and eligible cases, sets the mandala presence-causes, runs the jāla-practice from smoke-coverage to fire-dissolution, and extends the krama to the living and the outside.

## KEY TERMS

parokṣa-dīkṣā (indirect initiation); saṃnidhāna-hetavaḥ (mandala presence-causes); jālanāmā-prayoga (smoke-mesh practice); mūlādhāra-nāḍī (rise and nerve-crossing); pūrṇāhuti (release and form-dissolution); anugraha-dvāra (guru-body door); sva-vāsanā (habit-strength).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Follows the initiation-rules prakaraṇas; the jāla-practice and mṛtoddharaṇa machinery here feed the rites of a17–a19 already gold.

## EXPLANATION

Because parokṣa cases are defined by guru-service and death-circumstances, adhivāsa (a living preliminary) is dropped; because the mandala's presence is caused by ordered instruments and yogic subjects, the seer proceeds worship → establishment → seeing → rise → crossing. The practice ends when smoke covers the wheel and the jīva is brought. The living share the krama minus the dārbha-drawing; meditation alone suffices for consecration; and sva-vāsanā plus the unseverable enjoyment-habit explain why the higher-doctrine consecration, not any other, is balavān.

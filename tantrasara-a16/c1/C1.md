# C1 — Tantrasāra Ahnika 16 (parokṣa)

## tsa16-p01 — initiating the absent
**SUMMARY.** Initiation at a distance comes in two kinds: for the dead and for the living. The dead who qualify: those who served a teacher, or were exiled, killed by sorcery or childhood, who tasted descent at the death-moment, or got it through another's mouth — the Lord's command. For the dead, no lodging-rites. The circle's presence-causes are graded: a lot of rite, best gear, flowers, place, seat, circle, trident-lotus shape, a meditation-particular, mantra, self-blazing, and the bond of a devoted knower — latter-best. Gathered, what's the story? — says the Lord. Worship the god, set his kuśa-made form before you, and see it through the course of knowledge the teacher seeded. It rises from the root-seat, crosses endless channels by vigor, pervades nose-and-sky, scattering to fill. The great net-practice: smoke-lovely, thick-net-covered, a course-wheel that covers the desired soul — first subduing and facing, then, if whole, drawing it, then from mid-course as you wish. Dead or living, pulling or lifting — Śambhunātha's net-yoga, mine. Outside too — how not? In pulling without practice, passion-hate-yoga would seem to give lordship-immersion — but destiny rules; practice is needed. Here, through grace-Lord-immersion, it works; the Lord graces through the teacher's body-door — inconceivable greatness, much said. Net-drawn, the soul enters a kuśa body or jāti-fruit body, unthrobbing (mind-breath-material lacking) — yet it throbs by contemplation-power; sacraments as before, ending in joining full-offering. There, by full-offering, its kuśa-form dissolves in supreme glow — lifted. If in hell, ghost, or beast, freed. A human at once gains knowledge, yoga, initiation, discernment — that is dead-lifting. The living, absent, with descent arisen: same course, minus soul-drawing. Meditation-raised alone, of it, sacrament; initiation gives both fruits. Latency-strong, many need upper-rule sacraments; others, its sacrament. Even absent-initiated, knowledge rises.
**FUNCTION.** Establishes parokṣa (absent) initiation for dead and living, and the net-rite (jāla-prayoga).
**KEY TERMS.** parokṣa (absent), mṛtoddharaṇa (dead-lifting), jāla-prayoga (net-practice), kuśa (grass-body), saṃnidhānahetavaḥ (presence-causes), anugraha (grace-door).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** A15 (sadyaḥ) precedes; A16 teaches absent initiation; A17 (liṅgoddhāra) follows.
**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** Net-rite implements (traditional). L1 CONDENSED? No — A16 is 24 verses, full close done.
**RELATED.** A15 (sadyaḥ), A17 (liṅgoddhāra), spans TS16.1–16.24.

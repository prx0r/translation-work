# Tantrasāra Ahnika 17 — ARGMAP

## tsa17-p01 (17.1–17.6)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (liṅgoddhāra for lower-śāstra initiates).
- question: How are lower-śāstra dwellers (Vaiṣṇava etc.) turned and initiated?
- premises: (1) Lower-śāstra vow-holders or supreme-śāstra-standing-without-authority serving lower teachers, when turned by descent, get this course. (2) Lodging done, another day, common-mantra-worshipped, conduct heard, brought before Lord; vow taken, thrown in water, bathe, sprinkled, refined with tooth-stick and cake, bound-eyed, brought in; by common mantra Lord-worship. (3) By common mantra, Śiva-made fire, vow-purity done; mantra-enclosure-named "I purify" svāhā 100. (4) Full offering vauṣaḍa-ended. (5) Vow-lord summoned, worshipped, "ineffective you be for him" made heard, satiated, fire dismissed = liṅgoddhāra. (6) Lodging etc. as before; initiation as wished.
- inference: Lower path isn't rejected — it is lifted into the Śiva course.
- conclusion: Liṅgoddhāra arc stated.
- unresolved: Mantra identities (traditional).

# Tantrasāra Ahnika 17 — L1 controlled

## tsa17-p01
Now seventeenth ahnika: for those staying in lower śāstras — Vaiṣṇava and the rest, down to lower tantric ends — or vow-holders, or even those standing in supreme śāstra yet without authority, serving lower-śāstra teachers — when by descent they are turned by the Lord — then for them this course. Therein: him, having done lodging, on another day, of common-mantra-worshipped, his conduct heard, of the Lord before, bring in. Therein his vow taken, in water cast, then he bathes; then having sprinkled, with tooth-stick and rice-cake refined, bound-eyed, bring in. By common mantra, Lord-worship make. (17.1) Then by common mantra, Śiva-made, in fire vow-purity do; that mantra-enclosure-named having made, "I purify" svāhā-ending a hundred offer. (17.2) Then too full-offering with vauṣaḍa-ending. (17.3) Then vow-lord summoned, worshipped, by his command "ineffective you be for him" made heard, him satiated, fire dismissed — thus is liṅgoddhāra. (17.4) Then of him lodging-and-the-rest as before. (17.5) Initiation as wished. (17.6)

# Tantrasara-a17 — L2 READ

## v.1
For those standing in scriptures from the Vaiṣṇavas to the southern tantras at the end, or vow-takers thereof — and even highest-scripture-standers serving unentitled lower-scripture gurus: when they are turned hither by the Lord's descent of power, then this is their rule. Therein, let the fast-observed one on the next day — worshipped with the common mantra, his doings told — be led in before the Lord; there, having taken his vow, let him cast it in water; then let him bathe; then, having sprinkled, having purified with gruel and tooth-stick, having led him blindfolded in, by the common mantra let him have the Lord's worship performed.
The Sanskrit: *vaiṣṇavādidakṣiṇatantrānteṣu śāsaneṣu ye sthitāḥ tadgṛhītavratā vā ye ca uttamaśāsanasthā api anadhikṛtādharaśāsanagurūpasevinaḥ te yadā śaktipātena pārameśvareṇa unmukhīkriyante tadā teṣām ayaṃ vidhiḥ tatra enaṃ kṛtopavāsam anyadine sādhāraṇamantrapūjitasya tadīyāṃ ceṣṭāṃ śrāvitasya bhagavato 'gre praveśayet tatrāsya vrataṃ gṛhītvā ambhasi kṣipet tato 'sau snāyāt tataḥ prokṣya carudantakāṣṭhābhyāṃ saṃskṛtya baddhanetraṃ praveśya sādhāraṇena mantreṇa parameśvarapūjāṃ kārayet*

## v.2
Then, in fire made Śiva by the common mantra, let him purify the vow; having casketed his name in his mantra, 'I purify the expiation,' ending in svāhā, let him offer a hundred times.
The Sanskrit: *tataḥ sādhāraṇamantreṇa śivīkṛte agnau vrataśuddhiṃ kuryāt tanmantrasampuṭaṃ nāma kṛtvā prāyaścittaṃ śodhayāmi iti svāhāntaṃ śataṃ juhuyāt*

## v.3
Then too the full oblation, ending in vauṣaṭ.
The Sanskrit: *tato 'pi pūrṇāhutiḥ vauṣaḍantena*

## v.4
Then, having invoked the Vow-lord, having worshipped, by Śiva's order — 'be thou his no-harm-doer' — having announced it, having satisfied him, having dismissed him, let him dismiss the fire. Thus the liṅga-lifting.
The Sanskrit: *tato vrateśvaram āhūya pūjayitvā tasya śivājñayā akiṃcitkaraḥ tvam asya bhava iti śrāvaṇāṃ kṛtvā taṃ tarpayitvā visṛjya agniṃ visṛjet iti liṅgoddhāraḥ*

## v.5
Then his consecration-dwelling and the rest, as before.
The Sanskrit: *tato 'sya adhivāsādi prāgvat*

## v.6
Initiation at will.
The Sanskrit: *dīkṣā yatheccham*

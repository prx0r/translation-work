# Tantrasāra Ahnika 17 — L2 READ

## tsa17-p01 — lifting the lower into the Śiva course
The seventeenth chapter turns to those still standing in lower śāstras — Vaiṣṇavas and the rest, down to the lower tantric ends — or vow-holders, or even people in the supreme śāstra who lack authority and serve lower teachers. When descent turns them, this is their course. Lodge them first. Another day: hear their conduct, bring them before the Lord who has been commonly worshipped. Take their vow, cast them in water, bathe them, sprinkle, refine with tooth-stick and rice-cake, blindfold, bring them in. Make Lord-worship by a common mantra. Then by that mantra, Śiva-made fire: vow-purity — a hundred "I purify" offerings, svāhā. Then a full offering, vauṣaḍa-ended. Then summon the vow-lord, worship him, by his command declare "you are ineffective for him," satiate him, dismiss the fire: liṅgoddhāra. Then lodging and the rest as before. Initiation as you wish.

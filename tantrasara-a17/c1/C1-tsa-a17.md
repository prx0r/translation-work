# C1 — tsa-a17

## SUMMARY

Converts from any lower order receivable on the Lord's turning-them-hither; unwound in order: fasted, worshipped, confessed, led in, vow cast in water, bathed, purified, blindfolded, worshipped anew. Vow purified in Śiva-made fire: name casketed, a hundred self-purifications ending in svāhā. Full oblation with vauṣaṭ ends the fire-work. Old vow-lord retired with honors: invoked, worshipped, ordered harmless, fed, dismissed with fire — thus liṅga-lifting. Adhivāsa and the rest as before: the convert rejoins the standard sequence. Initiation at will.

## FUNCTION

Unwinds the convert step by step, retires his old bond, and admits him to initiation at will.

## KEY TERMS

liṅgoddhāra (liṅga-lifting as reclamation); śaktipāta (descent as the only admission); sāmānya-mantra (common mantra as the beginner's vehicle); vrateśvara (old vow-lord dismissed with honors); adhivāsa (consecration-dwelling as return); yatheccham (at-will as the close).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Follows the consecration āhnika: the already-consecrated needed no rite; the convert needs unwinding first.

## EXPLANATION

Whoever the convert was — Vaiṣṇava to southern tantra, vow-taker, even highest-scripture man under a lower guru — the Lord's turning-them-hither admits him, and this is the unwinding: fast, common worship, confession heard, led before the Lord, vow taken and drowned, bathed, sprinkled, purified with gruel and tooth-stick, led blindfolded in, Lord worshipped by common mantra. Then the vow is purified in Śiva-made fire: name casketed in mantra, a hundred self-purifications ending in svāhā, then the full offering with vauṣaṭ. Then the old bond is honorably retired: Vrateśvara invoked, worshipped, told by Śiva's order to be harmless, fed, dismissed — and the fire with him. That is liṅgoddhāra. Adhivāsa and the rest follow as before. Initiation — at will.

## BOUNDARY

No doctrine argued; the rite is stated, never justified.

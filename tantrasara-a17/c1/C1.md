# C1 — Tantrasāra Ahnika 17 (liṅgoddhāra)

## tsa17-p01 — lifting the lower into the Śiva course
**SUMMARY.** The seventeenth chapter turns to those still standing in lower śāstras — Vaiṣṇavas and the rest, down to the lower tantric ends — or vow-holders, or even people in the supreme śāstra who lack authority and serve lower teachers. When descent turns them, this is their course. Lodge them first. Another day: hear their conduct, bring them before the Lord who has been commonly worshipped. Take their vow, cast them in water, bathe them, sprinkle, refine with tooth-stick and rice-cake, blindfold, bring them in. Make Lord-worship by a common mantra. Then by that mantra, Śiva-made fire: vow-purity — a hundred I purify offerings, svāhā. Then a full offering, vauṣaḍa-ended. Then summon the vow-lord, worship him, by his command declare you are ineffective for him, satiate him, dismiss the fire: liṅgoddhāra. Then lodging and the rest as before. Initiation as you wish.
**FUNCTION.** Lifts lower-śāstra initiates into the Śiva course (liṅgoddhāra) without rejecting their prior path.
**KEY TERMS.** liṅgoddhāra (liṅga-lifting), adharāśāsanastha (lower-śāstra dweller), sādhāraṇamantra (common mantra), vrataśuddhi (vow-purity), akiṃcitkara (ineffective).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** A16 (parokṣa) precedes; A17 teaches lower-path lifting; A18 (abhiṣeka) follows.
**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** Mantra identities (traditional). L1 CONDENSED? No — A17 is 6 verses, full close done.
**RELATED.** A16 (parokṣa), A18 (abhiṣeka), spans TS17.1–17.6.

# C1 NOTE — tsa-a17

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Converts from any lower order receivable on the Lord's turning-them-hither; unwound in order: fasted, worshipped, confessed, led in, vow cast in water, bathed, purified, blindfolded, worshipped anew. Vow purified in Śiva-made fire: name casketed, a hundred self-purifications ending in svāhā. Full oblation with vauṣaṭ closes the fire-work. Old vow-lord retired with honors: invoked, worshipped, ordered harmless, fed, dismissed with fire — thus liṅga-lifting. Adhivāsa and the rest as before: the convert rejoins the standard sequence. Initiation at will.

## VOICE MAP

Teacher (reclamation) v1; unwinding v2; remaking v3; fire-rite v4; release v5; close v6.

## KEY TERMS

* liṅgoddhāra — liṅga-lifting as reclamation
* śaktipāta — descent as the only admission
* sāmānya-mantra — common mantra as the beginner's vehicle
* vrateśvara — old vow-lord dismissed with honors
* adhivāsa — consecration-dwelling as return
* yatheccham — at-will as the close

## CRUXES

* Śaktipāta is the sole admission criterion: no turning, no rite — the teacher waits.
* The old vow is drowned, not debated; the old lord is fed, not fought.

## FUNCTION

Unwinds the convert step by step, retires his old bond, and admits him to initiation at will.

## Work
Abhinavagupta Tantrasāra āhnika 17 (liṅgoddhāra — reclamation of the liṅga: initiation of converts from lower orders). 6 prose units via workcfg GRETIL span route (KSTS 17). Song tsa-a17 vv1-6.

## Structure (Vishvasa ch.17 + Pradipaka pattern)
- v1 who qualifies: dwellers in scriptures from Vaiṣṇava to southern tantras, their vow-takers, even highest-scripture men serving unentitled lower gurus — when turned by the Lord's śaktipāta, this is their rule: fast observed, next day worshipped with common mantras, doings recounted, led before the Lord; vow taken then cast in water; bathed, sprinkled, purified with gruel and tooth-stick, led blindfolded in, Lord worshipped by common mantra.
- v2 vrata-purification in Śiva-made fire; name casketed in own mantra; "I purify the expiation" + svāhā ×100.
- v3 full oblation ending in vauṣaṭ.
- v4 Vrateśvara invoked, worshipped, told by Śiva's order to be no-harm-doer, satisfied, dismissed; fire dismissed. Thus liṅgoddhāra.
- v5 adhivāsa and the rest as before.
- v6 initiation at will.

## Doctrine notes (lore support, never claims)
- Liṅgoddhāra = "rescuing from fallen state" (Chakravarty/Ishwar Ashram summary): only after teacher is convinced śaktipāta occurred (Trika superiority claim: other śāstras free from prakṛti only, not māyā).
- Vrateśvara impartiality formula ("akiṃcitkaraḥ tvam asya bhava"): the old vow-lord ordered not to obstruct.
- Old mantra thrown in water: severance from the former lineage; blindfold entry + common mantras: re-entry as beginner.

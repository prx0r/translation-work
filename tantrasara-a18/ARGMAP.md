# Tantrasāra A18 — ARGMAP

## tsa18-p01 (18.1–18.2)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (consecration).
- question: Who may be consecrated as practitioner/teacher, and what duties follow?
- premises: (1) Consecrate a knowledge-practiced person for practitioner or teacherhood — even without marks, knowledge-possessing alone is entitled (no other, even consecrated). (2) Delegating authority: teacher, not doing initiation, doesn't transgress; but prior transgression/authority-binding/vidyeśa-state-giving makes not-doing itself bondage. So consecrated, for mantra-deity identity success, six-monthly daily muttering/fire-offering/special-worship conduct, knowledge-vow. Thereafter, having become such, entitled in initiation. (3) Therein: don't initiate unfit; don't omit fit; test initiated in knowledge-giving; even spurious-knowledge, knowing, overlook. And by consecration-splendor, god-worship-and-the-rest.
- inference: Knowledge alone authorizes; consecration adds the vow, not the entitlement.
- conclusion: Consecration rule + vow stated.
- unresolved: Six-month knowledge-vow details (external).

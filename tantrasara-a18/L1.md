# Tantrasāra A18 — L1 controlled

## tsa18-p01
Now eighteenth ahnika. One well-practiced in knowledge for practitioner or teacherhood should be consecrated — because even without all marks, knowledge-possessing alone is entitled for practitioner and grace-giving; not another, even if consecrated. (18.1) In delegating authority, the teacher, not doing initiation and the rest, does not transgress; but prior, by transgression, by authority-binding, by vidyeśa-state-giving, bondage itself is of him not doing initiation and the rest. So consecrated, for mantra-deity identity success, six-monthly, daily, by muttering-fire-offering-special-worship-conduct, knowledge-vow one should do. Thereafter, having obtained such-becoming-ness, in initiation and the rest one is entitled. Therein: not unfit one should initiate; nor fit should one omit; initiated too in knowledge-giving one should test; spurious-knowledge-holding, having known, one should overlook. And therein, by consecration-splendor, god-worship-and-the-rest. (18.2)

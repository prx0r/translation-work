# Tantrasāra A18 — L2 READ

## tsa18-p01 — who may consecrate
Consecrate a man well-practised in knowing, for practice or for teaching — even if he has none of the marks, knowing alone entitles him to practise and to grace; no one else does, consecrated or not. In handing over authority, a teacher who doesn't initiate doesn't transgress — unless earlier transgression, binding to office, and the vidyeśa state make not-initiating itself the bond. So the consecrated one, to succeed in mantra-deity identity, does a six-month knowledge-vow: daily muttering, fire-giving, special worship. After that, having become it, he is entitled to initiate. In that office: don't initiate the unfit; don't skip the fit; test the initiated when they give knowledge; even the false-knowledge holder, once known, overlook. And by the splendor of consecration, worship of gods and the rest.

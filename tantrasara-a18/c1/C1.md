# C1 — Tantrasāra Ahnika 18 (abhiṣeka)

## tsa18-p01 — who may consecrate
**SUMMARY.** Consecrate a man well-practised in knowing, for practice or for teaching — even if he has none of the marks, knowing alone entitles him to practise and to grace; no one else does, consecrated or not. In handing over authority, a teacher who doesn't initiate doesn't transgress — unless earlier transgression, binding to office, and the vidyeśa state make not-initiating itself the bond. So the consecrated one, to succeed in mantra-deity identity, does a six-month knowledge-vow: daily muttering, fire-giving, special worship. After that, having become it, he is entitled to initiate. In that office: don't initiate the unfit; don't skip the fit; test the initiated when they give knowledge; even the false-knowledge holder, once known, overlook. And by the splendor of consecration, worship of gods and the rest.
**FUNCTION.** Establishes consecration: knowledge-only entitlement, six-month vow, office ethics.
**KEY TERMS.** abhiṣeka (consecration), vidyāvrata (knowledge-vow), svādhikārasamarpaṇa (delegating authority), chadmagṛhītajñāna (spurious knowledge), parīkṣā (testing).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** A17 (liṅgoddhāra) precedes; A18 teaches consecration; A19 (antyeṣṭi) follows.
**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** Six-month knowledge-vow details (external). L1 CONDENSED? No — A18 is 2 verses, full close done.
**RELATED.** A17 (liṅgoddhāra), A19 (antyeṣṭi), spans TS18.1–18.2.

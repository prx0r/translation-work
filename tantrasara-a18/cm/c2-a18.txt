Some people live in lower scriptures — Vaiṣṇava ones all the way down to southern tantras — or took vows there. A few even sit high up but still serve gurus who aren't qualified. When the Lord turns them around with his power falling on them, here's the drill. [E0]

First they fast. Next day, after they've worshipped with the plain everyday mantras and told everything they did, you walk them in front of God. Then they pick up their old vow and throw it in the water. Then they bathe. Then you sprinkle them, clean them up with sacred rice and a tooth-stick, walk them in blindfolded, and do the full God-worship for them with the plain mantras. [E1]

Then the fire itself gets turned into Śiva with those same plain mantras, and you clean their vow in it. You pack their name inside their own mantra and say I clean whats wrong ending with svāhā — a hundred times. [E2]

Then one more full ladle into the fire, ending with vauṣaṭ. Then you call the old Vow-Lord, worship him, and tell him straight from Śiva: you do this guy no harm. You announce that, you feed him, you send him off — and you send the fire off too. That's called lifting the liṅga. [E3]

After that it's the usual settling-in and the rest, same as always. And from then on, you start people whenever you want. [E4]

May all six land clean, quiescent, and willing — teacher, convert, fire, and Lord. [E5]

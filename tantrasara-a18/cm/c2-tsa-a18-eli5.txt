Once a teacher has handed you his job, he doesn't get in trouble for not starting new students. But before that handover, skipping initiations IS his chain — through the wrong of it, through the bond of the job, through the one who gives the angels' rank. So: get blessed. Then, to become one with your mantra's god, do the knowing-vow for six months — chant every day, feed the fire, worship extra hard. Then, when you've become that, you're allowed to start students. Here's how: don't start the ones who aren't ready, and don't skip the ones who are. Even test the ones you already started, when you teach them. If you find out someone faked their way in — let it slide, knowingly. And do the god-worship big, the way a blessed one does. [E1]

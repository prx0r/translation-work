# C1 NOTE — tsa-a18

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Consecrate the practiced knower as practitioner or teacher: knowing alone entitles; marks or prior consecration do not. Delegation ends the duty to initiate; before it, omission binds. The consecrated earns initiation-rights through six months of daily vidyāvrata for mantra-deity identity; then, made of That, examines strictly — tests the unfit out, never shuns the fit, tests even the taught, overlooks knowing deceit, and worships by consecration-splendor.

## VOICE MAP

Teacher (injunction) v1; teacher (discipline) v2.

## KEY TERMS

* svabhyastajñānin — well-practised knower as the only qualification
* adhikāra — entitlement as knowing, not office
* abhiṣeka — consecration as handing over
* vidyāvrata — six-month knowledge-vow
* tanmayībhāva — made-of-That as initiation-right
* parīkṣā/upekṣā — testing and overlooking as the two disciplines

## CRUXES

* Legacy T1 glossed a phantom "atha (Now)" with no Sanskrit counterpart — dropped in rebuild; span bytes are the only truth.
* Header "Tantrasāra, aṣṭādaśam āhnikam" excluded as paratext (as with uvāca tags).

## FUNCTION

Sets who may be consecrated, when the duty to initiate ends, and how the consecrated prove and examine.

## Structure (Pradipaka + Vishvasa)
- v1 (18.1) the entitlement rule: consecrate the well-practised knower as sādhaka or guru — for the knower alone, even markless, is entitled to practitionerhood and grace-bestowing; no one else, even consecrated.
- v2 (18.2) delegation and discipline: the guru who has handed over his authority incurs no fault by not initiating; before that, non-initiating IS bondage (via transgression, authority-bond, the Vidyeśa-state-giver). The consecrated one does the six-month vidyāvrata (daily japa-homa-special-worship) for mantra-deity identity; then, that-made, he is entitled to initiate. Test the unfit out, never shun the fit; test even the initiated in knowledge-giving; overlook known deceit-grasped knowledge; god-worship according to consecration-splendor.

## Doctrine notes (lore support, not claims)
- śaktipāta/adhikāra: Wallis (Descent of Power) + Ferrario (grace in degrees) — abhiṣeka makes the ācārya; the un-consecrated knower vs the consecrated non-knower is Abhinavagupta's wedge for gnostic authority.
- Pradipaka: svabhyasta = well-practiced/studied; anugraha-karaṇe = guruhood (grace-bestowing); vidyeśa-pada-dāyin = the guru as giver of the Vidyeśa state.
- Vishvasa (ch. 18 translation): six-month mantra-vrata then initiation-rights; test the fit, disregard the deceitful.

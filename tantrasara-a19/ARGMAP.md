# Tantrasāra A19 — ARGMAP

## tsa19-p01 (19.1–19.7)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (last rites).
- question: What is done for lower-śāstra teachers after death, and for the unexpiated?
- premises: (1) Even lower-śāstra-standing + end + death-moment descent → antyasaṃskāra-initiation (by Lord's command); upper-standing lapsed/unexpiated too. (2) Dead-lifting rite entirely in body; full-offering burns corpse; for fools, marked last-rites by knowledge-yoga force. In corpse: dissolution-course mantras placed, net-course drawn (binding/piercing/striking), breath-course heart/throat/forehead → corpse trembles. (3) Then in supreme Śiva joining, burn; full-offering; pure/others' śrāddha-initiation three days/fourth/monthly/yearly. (4) Fire-offering-end rite, food in one hand, its vigor-power enjoyment-formed, beast-enjoyment-power-identity contemplated, offered in Lord enjoyer → enjoyment-sense removed, Lord alone remains; of last-rites/dead-lifting/śrāddha-initiation by any one, even though success, for enjoyment-wisher much rite for much fruit. (5) For liberation-wisher, same daily for such-becoming. (6) For knowers, no last-rites/śrāddha use: their death = holy day; consciousness-portion-filling, continuity one-consciousness-only ultimate — like living knowledge-gain continuity day. (7) Everywhere in śrāddha etc., image-worship chief (Siddhāmata); course in occasional manifestation.
- inference: Death-rites scale by knowledge-state of the dead.
- conclusion: Antyeṣṭi arc stated.
- unresolved: Image-worship course deferred (naimittikaprakāśa).

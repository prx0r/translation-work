# Tantrasāra A19 — L1 controlled

## tsa19-p01
Nineteenth ahnika. For lower-śāstra-standing teachers too, after death, from descent-arisen at death, one should do called last-sacrament-initiation — for upper-śāstra-standing lapsed samaya unexpiated too — so the Lord's command. (19.1) Therein, who in dead-lifting rite stated, that entirely in body to be done. By full-offering dead-body-burning. For fools, for appearance-rooting, marked last-rites by knowledge-yoga force one should do. Therein in dead-body, in dissolution-course mantras placed, by net-course drawn, binding-piercing-striking-and-the-rest done, through breath-course heart-throat-forehead — thus dead body trembles. (19.2) Then in supreme Śiva having made joining, that burn by full-offering. By last-rites, of pure or others too, or śrāddha-initiation, one should do, three days, fourth, monthly, yearly. (19.3) Therein, fire-offering-end rite done, food in one hand made, its vigor-formed power enjoyment-formed, in beast-enjoyment-power-identity-with contemplated, offered in Lord enjoyer — thus enjoyment-sense removed, Lord alone becomes — of last-rites-dead-lifting-śrāddha-initiation by any one, even though success, yet for enjoyment-wisher rite-muchness for fruit-muchness sake, thus one should do all. (19.4) For liberation-wisher too, for such-becoming-success, this of living daily practice-habituation-like. (19.5) For knowledge-possessors, not any of this last-rites-and-rest-and-śrāddha-end rite useful — for their death of knowledge-continuity-possessors is holy day — from consciousness-portion-filling, so much of continuity one-consciousness-only ultimate — as of living knowledge-gain continuity day. (19.6) Everywhere here in śrāddha-and-rest-rite is image-worship chief — so glorious Siddhāmata; that course will be stated in occasional manifestation. (19.7)

# Tantrasāra A19 — L2 READ

## tsa19-p01 — last rites for the unexpiated
The nineteenth chapter: even lower-śāstra teachers, when turned by descent at death, get the last-sacrament initiation — and upper-śāstra people with lapsed samaya and unperformed expiation too, by the Lord's command. The dead-lifting rite goes entirely in the body: full-offering burns the corpse; for fools, marked last-rites by knowledge-yoga force. In the corpse, dissolution-course mantras placed, net-drawn — binding, piercing, striking; breath-course through heart, throat, forehead — the corpse trembles. Then joining into supreme Śiva, burn it. By last-rites, for the pure or others, or śrāddha-initiation: three days, fourth, monthly, yearly. There: fire-offering-end rite done, food in one hand, its vigor-power, enjoyment-formed, in beast-enjoyment-power-identity contemplated, offered into the Lord-as-enjoyer — so enjoyment-sense removed, Lord alone becomes. Even one of last-rites/dead-lifting/śrāddha-initiation succeeding — still, for enjoyment-wishers, do much rite for much fruit. For liberation-wishers, the same daily, for such-becoming-success. For knowers, none of this is useful: their death is a holy day — consciousness-portion filling, continuity one-consciousness-only ultimate — as a living knowledge-gain continuity day. Everywhere here in śrāddha and rites, image-worship is chief — glorious Siddhāmata; its course in occasional manifestation.

# C1 — Tantrasāra Ahnika 19 (antyeṣṭi)

## tsa19-p01 — last rites for the unexpiated
**SUMMARY.** The nineteenth chapter: even lower-śāstra teachers, when turned by descent at death, get the last-sacrament initiation — and upper-śāstra people with lapsed samaya and unperformed expiation too, by the Lord's command. The dead-lifting rite goes entirely in the body: full-offering burns the corpse; for fools, marked last-rites by knowledge-yoga force. In the corpse, dissolution-course mantras placed, net-drawn — binding, piercing, striking; breath-course through heart, throat, forehead — the corpse trembles. Then joining into supreme Śiva, burn it. By last-rites, for the pure or others, or śrāddha-initiation: three days, fourth, monthly, yearly. There: fire-offering-end rite done, food in one hand, its vigor-power enjoyment-formed, in beast-enjoyment-power-identity contemplated, offered into the Lord-as-enjoyer — so enjoyment-sense removed, Lord alone becomes. Even one of last-rites/dead-lifting/śrāddha-initiation succeeding — still, for enjoyment-wishers, do much rite for much fruit. For liberation-wishers, the same daily, for such-becoming-success. For knowers, none of this is useful: their death is a holy day — consciousness-portion filling, continuity one-consciousness-only ultimate — as a living knowledge-gain continuity day. Everywhere here in śrāddha and rites, image-worship is chief — glorious Siddhāmata; its course in occasional manifestation.
**FUNCTION.** Establishes antyeṣṭi (last rites) for lower-path teachers and the unexpiated; scales by knowledge-state of the dead.
**KEY TERMS.** antyasaṃskāra (last sacrament), mṛtoddharaṇa (dead-lifting), śrāddhadīkṣā (śrāddha-initiation), vīryarūpā śakti (vigor-formed power), bhogyabhāva (enjoyment-sense), mūrtiyāga (image-worship), parvadina (holy day).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** A18 (abhiṣeka) precedes; A19 teaches last rites; A20 (conduct) follows.
**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** Image-worship course deferred (naimittikaprakāśa). L1 CONDENSED? No — A19 is 7 verses, full close done.
**RELATED.** A18 (abhiṣeka), A20 (conduct), spans TS19.1–19.7.

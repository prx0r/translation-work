# C1 NOTE — tsa-a19

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Converts from any lower order receivable on the Lord's turning-them-hither; unwound in order: fasted, worshipped, confessed, led in, vow cast in water, bathed, purified, blindfolded, worshipped anew. Vow purified in Śiva-made fire: name casketed, a hundred self-purifications ending in svāhā. Full oblation with vauṣaṭ closes the fire-work. Old vow-lord retired with honors: invoked, worshipped, ordered harmless, fed, dismissed with fire — thus liṅgoddhāra. Adhivāsa and the rest as before: the convert rejoins the standard sequence. Initiation at will. But everywhere here, in the śrāddha rite, image-abandonment is chief — so holds the revered Siddhāmata; and its rule will be told in the exposition of the occasional.

## VOICE MAP

Teacher: converts rallied v1; unwound v2; vow-fire v3; old bond retired v4; return v5; will v6; seal v7.

## KEY TERMS

* śaktipāta — descent as the only admission
* adhivāsa — consecration-dwelling as return
* vrateśvara — old vow-lord dismissed with honors
* liṅgoddhāra — liṅga-lifting as reclamation
* mūrtiyāga — image-abandonment as chief
* yatheccham — at-will as the close

## CRUXES

* Śaktipāta alone admits; the teacher's verdict on it is assumed, not described.
* The seal-verse (v7) points onward to the occasional rites (a20's subject); it closes nothing.

## FUNCTION

Unwinds the convert step by step, retires his old bond, and admits him to initiation at will.

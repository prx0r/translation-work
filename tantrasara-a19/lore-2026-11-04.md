# Tantrasara a19 lore — 2026-11-04 (verse-usable)

## Work
Abhinavagupta Tantrasāra āhnika 19 (post-mortem initiation: antyeṣṭi, śrāddha-dīkṣā). 6 prose units via workcfg GRETIL span route (KSTS 17). Song tsa-a19 vv1-6.

## Structure
- v1 who qualifies after death: lower-doctrine dwellers up to gurus, immediately after death, solely by raising-the-dead-arisen power-descent — the final-sacrament initiation; and higher-doctrine men who lost observance and skipped expiation — thus the Lord's command.
- v2 the rite on the body: whatever raising-rite was told, all done to the body — full-oblation corpse-burning; for the deluded, a trust-bearing funeral to plant conviction; net-drawn mantras, curbing-piercing-rubbing, breath-moving through heart-throat-forehead — till the corpse-body trembles.
- v3 joining with supreme Śiva, burning with full oblation; for purified and others too — or a three-day śrāddha-initiation: fourth day, monthly, yearly.
- v4 oblation-ending rite, food-offering in one hand, meditating his virile-formed knowable-power attained to beast-present identity — offered to the Lord as enjoyer; knowable-being ceased, he becomes the Lord; succeeded or not, the enjoyment-desirer gets multitude of rites for multitude of fruit — do all.
- v5 even the liberation-seeker's made-of-That attainment, of the living, daily, like sustained practice.
- v6 but the reality-knower needs no funeral-to-śrāddha rite: his death is a festival day for his knowledge-lineage, from consciousness-portion filling — lineage all mere-consciousness, like the living lineage's knowledge-attainment day.

# Tantrasāra A20 — ARGMAP

## tsa20-p01 (20.1–20.20)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (post-initiation conduct, part 1).
- question: What conduct follows initiation, and how does śākta worship look?
- premises: (1) Initiation for knowledge-fit and for unable (liberation-initiation with seed); thereafter conduct for life, teacher-taught. (2) Three kinds: fixed (becoming-it alone), occasioned (dawn-worship/daily/holy-day/pavitra), desired (practitioner alone; not decided here). (3) Fixed worship = necessary (worship/dawn/teacher/holy-day/pavitra); occasioned = knowledge-gain/scripture-gain/teacher-arrival/birth-sacrament-death-days/festival/exposition/vision/meeting/dream-knowledge/samaya-expiation-gain = special-worship cause. (4) Initiated pupil: chief mantra with vigor, consciousness-throb-essence, unwritten, mouth-transmitted; for becoming-success, dawns such-becoming practice; always Lord in altar/liṅga. (5) Heart-altar: visualize self as yājya-wheel-nondifferent, reflected; worship/mutter/praise/such-becoming fearlessly; mirror analogy (self-nature-niściti quick); no course apart from such-becoming. (6) Mantra-tanmayībhāva-entered, passion-trace-removed, attachment-juice-melted all-bond-net — heart alone supreme adoptable (teachers). (7) Abiding-upon paramārthika: thing-flow shines (secret-knowers worship); verses (base/wonder-sap/nectar-vessel) contemplate wheel. (8) Seal-showing, muttering, offering, bodhyaikātmya-dismissal. Chief food oneself eats or casts in water (water-born initiated beings); cat/mouse/dog eating → hell-doubt → even knower shouldn't (desire to grace world) — or abandon world and sit (altar worship).
- inference: Post-initiation life = such-becoming, practiced daily/occasionally.
- conclusion: Conduct + heart-altar + food rule stated.
- unresolved: Desired conduct deferred (na iha niścetavyam).

## tsa20-p02 (20.21–20.35)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (liṅga practice, parva, cakrayāga).
- question: How is liṅga worshipped, and what are festivals?
- premises: (1) Liṅga not installed with secret mantras specially; previously installed → invocation/dismissal worship as base. (2) Many liṅga-substitutes (teacher/self/power body, scripture book, hero-vessel, rosary, weapon, bow, pearl, gold, heart-thing-made, mirror); base-strength alone raises mantra success; before-before chief through base-quality following; everywhere nondifference-conceit supreme sacrament. (3) Parva sixfold (general…special-special); completion of rite. (4) Monthly: first/fifth general; 4/8/9/14/15 both fortnights general-general; stated graha-nakṣatra-yoga general-special; special festivals (month-by-month special nights); special-special by number (Āśvayuja excepted); otherwise anu-festival; graha-nakṣatra-yoga → time not chief; lunar day special-gain; anuyāga time follows festival-day (anuyāga = image/circle worship synonyms). (5) Circle-worship worshippables (teacher, class, children, knower, girl, last, courtesan, red, reality-knowing); center teacher by cover-course; gurusamayin-end, hero, power; circle/row sitting; scent-smoke-flower worship; Sadāśiva-formed vessel, power-nectar-visualization, āsava-filled; enjoyer-power as Śiva worshipped; triple-melting contemplation; cover-entry-exit, liberation/enjoyment chiefness, outside-inside satiating; reverse course nourishes circle. (6) Base: universe-made vessel, wheel satiated, self bowed satiates; no vessel → auspicious shell; right-hand vessel-form; two up-gone-right joined without joints shell; drops satisfy vetāla-guhyakas; holding = Bhairava; entry to no one; careless entry → no inquiry; redo double; leftovers/food scattered before as wished (secret house, signal-name except, all by god-word) = hero-mixed-circle-worship. (7) End: gifts satiate — chief-most image-worship. Even unseen-maṇḍala, image-worship holy days → son-stated fruit from year without dawn-conduct etc. (old/enjoyer/women course when descent, teacher-taught).
- inference: Parva + cakrayāga + mūrtiyāga form the communal/occasional layer.
- conclusion: Liṅga practice + parva + circle-worship stated.
- unresolved: Mantra identities; implement details (traditional).

## tsa20-p03 (20.39–20.52)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (pavitra + vyākhyā).
- question: How is pavitra done, and how is scripture expounded?
- premises: (1) Pavitra-course: in Ratnamālā-triple-peak-Siddhāmata etc., rite-first, Lord-command-fulfilling; Tantrāloka: without pavitra all fruitless. Āṣāḍha-bright to Kula-full-moon-end; Kārttika-dark-fifteenth fills Kula/nitya circle (Nitya-knowers); Māgha-bright-fifteenth (Bhairava-Kula-wave-knowers); southern-course-end fifteenth (Tantrasadbhāva-knowers). With splendor worship, offer, satiate, give pavitra (gold-pearl-jewel down to cloth-thread-cotton-kuśa-wombed). Four pavitras (digit etc. counts: knee-length one, navel another, throat another, crown another) for god + teacher by all-course-full-form-contemplation; one for others. (2) Great festival: 4-months/7-days/3-days (chief-occasional kalpas); splendor → monthly pavitra; or four months; or once. Not-doing → expiation (even knower/with-splendor transgresses from knowledge-blame-doing). (3) Knowledge-gain etc. → consciousness-joy-excess + god-wheel presence specially → special in anuyāga. (4) Exposition: all-scripture-full teacher entreated; even lower-śāstra, compassion, descent-possibility-possessed heart should expound (except secret teaching). Fruitful for low/high seat with restrained body. First draw/contemplate lotus-seat, worship speech-lady and teachers, satiate wheel, expound sūtra/sentence/chapter non-contradicting, establish/refute/prove/commentary to chapter-end; satiate/worship at thing-end; at end worship knowledge-seat, dismiss, anoint, cast in deep.
- inference: Pavitra + exposition form the occasional/teaching layer.
- conclusion: Pavitra + vyākhyā stated.
- unresolved: Implements/mantras (traditional).

## tsa20-p04 (20.53–20.65)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (samaya + guru-pūjā, CONDENSED).
- question: How is samaya expiated, and how is the teacher worshipped?
- premises: Samaya-expiation: knowledge-natured none; conduct-showing grace for liberation-sharers; non-knower conduct-dependent, expiation not-doing → hundred-years corpse-eater; knowledge-blame transgression. Woman-killing no expiation; elsewhere weigh strength; unbroken Mālinī recitation to 3 lakhs until doubt-removal; special worship; circle-worship remainder. (20.57–20.60) Teacher-worship: after all rites next day chief; not doing chiefly authority-bound. Svāstika maṇḍala, golden seat, whole course worshipped, seat placed, worship, satiating, food, gift, self offered, leftover requested, bowed, eaten, circle-worship. (20.61–20.65)
- inference: Samaya-expiation and guru-pūjā close the conduct chapter.
- conclusion: A20 close.
- unresolved: None new.

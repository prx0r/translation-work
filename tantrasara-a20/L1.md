# Tantrasāra A20 — L2 READ

## tsa20-p01 — life after initiation
Initiation done, conduct for a lifetime follows — the teacher gives it, for those fit to know and for those who can't. Fixed, occasioned, desired — three sorts; the desired kind is left to the practitioner (not settled here). The fixed: becoming-it, always. Occasioned: dawn-worship, daily rite, holy days, pavitra, and the rest. Fixed worship, dawn worship, teacher-worship, holy-day worship, pavitra — all necessary. Occasioned: knowledge-gain, scripture-gain, teacher's arrival, birth-sacrament-death days, festivals, expositions, visions, meetings, dream-knowledge, samaya-expiation — each a cause for special worship. The initiated pupil gets his chief mantra with vigor and throb-essence, unwritten, mouth to mouth. For becoming-success, at dawn the such-becoming practice; through it, always, sacrament-gain and success, and daily the Lord in altar or liṅga. In the heart-altar, see yourself as the wheel of worship-nondifferent, reflected, seen; worship, mutter, praise, fearlessly such-become. As in a mirror — one's own face seen without stopping, self-nature settled fast; no chief course here but such-becoming. Who is entered into mantra-tanmayībhāva, passion-trace gone, attachment-juice melted, the whole bond-net — whatever rests in his heart, that alone is supreme to take — so our teachers. Resting in the supreme, thing-flow shines — what secret-knowers worship. Having made the base wonder-sap-washed instant-washed, with inward own-nature flowers and own-sap-mass ones. "Bliss-nectar-full own-heart invaluable vessel course, you with goddess in body-god-abode I worship day-night" — thus two verses' meaning inward contemplated, visualize the god-wheel. Then seal-showing, muttering, its offering, bodhyaikātmya-dismissal. Chief food: eat it, or cast all in water — water-born beings, initiated through food-giving — so the āgama-knowers. Cat, mouse, dog eating: doubt born for hell — even a knower, wanting to grace the world, shouldn't; or abandon the world and sit — thus altar-worship.
*Fidelity: preserved (three kinds, daily/occasional, heart-altar + mirror, such-becoming, food rule). Added: none doctrinal. Not-resolved: desired conduct deferred.*

## tsa20-p02 — liṅga, festivals, and circle-worship
In liṅga: don't install with secret mantras specially; if already installed, worship by invocation-and-dismissal course as base. Worship teacher-body, self-body, power-body, secret book, hero-vessel, beads, weapon, bow, pearl, gold, heart-things, mirror, or liṅga. Through base-strength alone mantra-success rises — before-before chief through base-quality following; everywhere nondifference-conceit is supreme sacrament. Now festivals: six kinds — general, general-general, general-special, special-general, special, special-special — through completion of rite. Monthly: first and fifth general; fourth-eighth-ninth-fourteenth-fifteenth of both fortnights general-general; in stated graha-nakṣatra-yoga, general-special. Special festivals month by month (Mārgaśīrṣa first-night in dark-ninth, Pauṣa night-middle in dark-ninth, Māgha night-middle in bright-fifteenth, Phālguna day-middle in bright-twelfth, Caitra in bright-thirteenth, Vaiśākha in dark-eighth, Jyaiṣṭha in dark-ninth, Āṣāḍha in first day, Śrāvaṇa day-former in dark-eleventh, Bhādrapada day-middle in bright-sixth, Āśvayuja in bright-ninth, Kārttika first-night in bright-ninth). Citrā-and-moon, Maghā-and-Jīva, Tiṣya-and-moon, Pūrvaphālgunī-and-Mercury, Śravaṇa-and-Mercury, Śatabhiṣak-and-moon, Diti, Rohiṇī-and-Venus, Viśākhā-and-Jupiter, Śravaṇa-and-moon. By number in Mārgaśīrṣa-course (Āśvayuja excepted): special-special; otherwise anu-festival. In graha-nakṣatra-yoga, time isn't chief. Lunar-day special-gain; anuyāga time follows festival-day (anuyāga = image/circle worship). In circle-worship: teacher, class, children, knower, girl, last, courtesan, red-one, reality-knowing — chief worshippables. Center: teacher by cover-course, samayin-end, hero, power; circle or row sitting; scent-smoke-flower worship; vessel Sadāśiva-formed, power-nectar visualization, āsava-filled; enjoyer-power as Śiva worshipped; by her alone god-wheel satiated; triple-melting contemplated; cover-entry-exit, liberation-enjoyment chiefness, outside-inside satiating; reverse course nourishes the circle. Base: universe-made vessel, wheel satiated, self bowed satiates; no vessel → auspicious wave-shell; right hand vessel-formed; two up-gone-right joined without joints shell; falling drops satisfy vetāla-guhyakas; holding = Bhairava; no entry to anyone; careless entry → no inquiry; redo double circle-worship; leftovers/food scattered before as wished (secret house or signal-name except, all by god-word) = hero-mixed-circle-worship. End: gifts satiate — chief-most image-worship. Even unseen-maṇḍala, by image-worship holy days, from year son-stated fruit, without dawn-conduct — old/enjoyer/women course when descent, teacher-taught.
*Fidelity: preserved (liṅga substitutes, base-strength, parva sixfold, monthly table, circle-worship, hero-mixed, image-worship close). Added: none doctrinal. Not-resolved: mantra identities, implements (traditional).*

## tsa20-p03 — pavitra and exposition
Pavitra: in Ratnamālā-triple-peak-Siddhāmata etc., rite-first, Lord-command-fulfilling — Tantrāloka: without pavitra all fruitless. Āṣāḍha-bright to Kula-full-moon-end. Kārttika-dark-fifteenth fills Kula/nitya circle (Nitya-tantra-knowers). Māgha-bright-fifteenth (Bhairava-Kula-wave-knowers). Southern-course-end-fifteenth (Tantra-sadbhāva-knowers). With splendor: worship, offer, satiate, give pavitra from gold-pearl-jewel down to cloth-thread-cotton-kuśa-wombed. Four pavitras (digit etc.: knee, navel, throat, crown) for god and teacher by all-course-full-form-contemplation; one for others. Great festival: four-months, seven-days, three-days — chief-occasional kalpas; splendor → monthly pavitra; or four months; or once. Not-doing → expiation (even knower/with-splendor transgresses from knowledge-blame-doing). Knowledge-gain etc. → consciousness-joy-excess + god-wheel presence specially → special in anuyāga. Exposition: all-scripture-full teacher entreated; even lower-śāstra, compassion, descent-possibility-possessed heart should expound (except secret teaching). Fruitful for low/high with restrained body. First draw/contemplate lotus-seat, worship speech-lady and teachers, satiate wheel, expound non-contradicting, establish/refute/prove/commentary to chapter-end; satiate/worship at thing-end; at end worship knowledge-seat, dismiss, anoint, cast in deep.
*Fidelity: preserved (pavitra calendar, four pavitras, great festival, exposition course). Added: none doctrinal. Not-resolved: implements (traditional).*

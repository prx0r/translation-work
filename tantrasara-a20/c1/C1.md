# C1 — Tantrasāra Ahnika 20 (conduct)

## tsa20-p01 — post-initiation conduct, part 1
**SUMMARY.** Initiation done, conduct for a lifetime follows — the teacher gives it, for those fit to know and for those who can't. Three kinds: fixed, occasioned, desired — the desired kind left to the practitioner (not settled here). The fixed: becoming-it, always. Occasioned: dawn-worship, daily rite, holy days, pavitra, and the rest. Fixed worship, dawn worship, teacher-worship, holy-day worship, pavitra — all necessary. Occasioned: knowledge-gain, scripture-gain, teacher's arrival, birth-sacrament-death days, festivals, expositions, visions, meetings, dream-knowledge, samaya-expiation — each a cause for special worship. The initiated pupil gets his chief mantra with vigor and throb-essence, unwritten, mouth to mouth. For becoming-success, at dawn the such-becoming practice; through it, always, sacrament-gain and success, and daily the Lord in altar or liṅga. In the heart-altar, see yourself as the wheel of worship-nondifferent, reflected, seen; worship, mutter, praise, fearlessly such-become. As in a mirror — one's own face seen without stopping, self-nature settled fast; no chief course here but such-becoming. Who is entered into mantra-tanmayībhāva, passion-trace gone, attachment-juice melted, the whole bond-net — whatever rests in his heart, that alone is supreme to take — so our teachers. Resting in the supreme, thing-flow shines — what secret-knowers worship. Having made the base wonder-sap-washed instant-washed, with inward own-nature flowers and own-sap-mass ones. Bliss-nectar-full own-heart invaluable vessel course, you with goddess in body-god-abode I worship day-night — two verses' meaning inward contemplated, visualize the god-wheel. Then seal-showing, muttering, its offering, bodhyaikātmya-dismissal. Chief food: eat it, or cast all in water — water-born beings, initiated through food-giving — so the āgama-knowers. Cat, mouse, dog eating: doubt born for hell — even a knower, wanting to grace the world, shouldn't; or abandon the world and sit — thus altar-worship.
**FUNCTION.** Establishes post-initiation conduct (three kinds), heart-altar worship, and the food rule.
**KEY TERMS.** śeṣavartana (remaining conduct), nitya/naimittika/kāmya (fixed/occasioned/desired), tanmayībhāva (becoming-it), pavitra (purifier), sthaṇḍila (altar), saṃdhyopāsana (dawn-worship).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** A19 (antyeṣṭi) precedes; A20 teaches conduct; A21 (prāmāṇya) follows.
**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in eight steps. (1) Initiation done, conduct for life follows (teacher gives it, for fit and unfit). (2) Three kinds (fixed/occasioned/desired); desired not decided here. (3) Fixed = becoming-it always; occasioned = dawn-worship/daily/holy-day/pavitra. (4) Fixed worship, dawn worship, teacher-worship, holy-day worship, pavitra necessary. (5) Occasioned list (10 items) = special-worship causes. (6) Initiated pupil: chief mantra (vigor, throb-essence, unwritten, mouth-transmitted); for becoming-success, dawn such-becoming practice. (7) Heart-altar: visualize self as yājya-wheel-nondifferent (reflected, seen); worship/mutter/praise/such-becoming fearlessly; mirror analogy (self-nature-niściti quick); no chief course apart from such-becoming; mantra-tanmayībhāva-entered (passion-trace removed, bond-net melted) → heart alone supreme adoptable; resting in supreme, thing-flow shines; verses + god-wheel visualization; seal-showing/muttering/offering/dismissal. (8) Food rule: eat or cast in water (water-born beings initiated through food-giving); cat/mouse/dog eating → hell-doubt → even knower shouldn't (desire to grace) — or abandon world and sit (altar-worship).
**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** Desired conduct deferred (na iha niścetavyam). Mantra identities (traditional). Implements (traditional).
**RELATED.** A19 (antyeṣṭi), A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS20.1–20.20.

## tsa20-p02 — liṅga, festivals, and circle-worship
**SUMMARY.** In liṅga: don't install with secret mantras specially; if already installed, worship by invocation-and-dismissal course as base. Worship teacher-body, self-body, power-body, secret book, hero-vessel, beads, weapon, bow, pearl, gold, heart-things, mirror, or liṅga. Through base-strength alone mantra-success rises — before-before chief through base-quality following; everywhere nondifference-conceit is supreme sacrament. Now festivals: six kinds — general, general-general, general-special, special-general, special, special-special — through completion of rite. Monthly: first and fifth general; fourth-eighth-ninth-fourteenth-fifteenth of both fortnights general-general; in stated graha-nakṣatra-yoga, general-special. Special festivals month by month (Mārgaśīrṣa first-night in dark-ninth, Pauṣa night-middle in dark-ninth, Māgha night-middle in bright-fifteenth, Phālguna day-middle in bright-twelfth, Caitra in bright-thirteenth, Vaiśākha in dark-eighth, Jyaiṣṭha in dark-ninth, Āṣāḍha in first day, Śrāvaṇa day-former in dark-eleventh, Bhādrapada day-middle in bright-sixth, Āśvayuja in bright-ninth, Kārttika first-night in bright-ninth). Citrā-and-moon, Maghā-and-Jīva, Tiṣya-and-moon, Pūrvaphālgunī-and-Mercury, Śravaṇa-and-Mercury, Śatabhiṣak-and-moon, Diti, Rohiṇī-and-Venus, Viśākhā-and-Jupiter, Śravaṇa-and-moon. By number in Mārgaśīrṣa-course (Āśvayuja excepted): special-special; otherwise anu-festival. In graha-nakṣatra-yoga, time isn't chief. Lunar-day special-gain; anuyāga time follows festival-day (anuyāga = image/circle worship). In circle-worship: teacher, class, children, knower, girl, last, courtesan, red-one, reality-knowing — chief worshippables. Center: teacher by cover-course, samayin-end, hero, power; circle or row sitting; scent-smoke-flower worship; vessel Sadāśiva-formed, power-nectar visualization, āsava-filled; enjoyer-power as Śiva worshipped; by her alone god-wheel satiated; triple-melting contemplated; cover-entry-exit, liberation-enjoyment chiefness, outside-inside satiating; reverse course nourishes the circle. Base: universe-made vessel, wheel satiated, self bowed satiates; no vessel → auspicious wave-shell; right hand vessel-formed; two up-gone-right joined without joints shell; falling drops satisfy vetāla-guhyakas; holding = Bhairava; no entry to anyone; careless entry → no inquiry; redo double circle-worship; leftovers/food scattered before as wished (secret house or signal-name except, all by god-word) = hero-mixed-circle-worship. End: gifts satiate — chief-most image-worship. Even unseen-maṇḍala, by image-worship holy days, from year son-stated fruit, without dawn-conduct — old/enjoyer/women course when descent, teacher-taught.
**FUNCTION.** Establishes liṅga practice (substitutes, base-strength), parva (sixfold festival calendar), cakrayāga (circle-worship), mūrtiyāga (image-worship).
**KEY TERMS.** liṅga (practice without secret mantras), parva (festival, sixfold), cakrayāga (circle-worship), mūrtiyāga (image-worship), anuyāga (image/circle worship synonyms).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** P1 established conduct; P2 takes up liṅga/parva/circle-worship.
**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in seven steps. (1) Liṅga: not installed with secret mantras specially; previously installed → invocation/dismissal worship as base; many substitutes (teacher/self/power body, scripture book, hero-vessel, beads, weapon, bow, pearl, gold, heart-things, mirror, liṅga). (2) Base-strength alone raises mantra success (before-before chief through base-quality following); everywhere nondifference-conceit supreme sacrament. (3) Parva: sixfold (general/general-general/general-special/special-general/special/special-special) through completion of rite; monthly table (first/fifth general; 4/8/9/14/15 both fortnights general-general; graha-nakṣatra-yoga general-special; special festivals month-by-month; special-special by number Āśvayuja excepted; otherwise anu-festival); in graha-nakṣatra-yoga time not chief; lunar-day special-gain; anuyāga time follows festival-day. (4) Circle-worship worshippables (teacher/class/children/knower/girl/last/courtesan/red-one/reality-knowing). (5) Center: teacher by cover-course, samayin-end, hero, power; circle/row sitting; scent-smoke-flower worship; Sadāśiva-formed vessel, power-nectar visualization, āsava-filled; enjoyer-power as Śiva worshipped; by her alone god-wheel satiated; triple-melting contemplated; cover-entry-exit, liberation/enjoyment chiefness, outside-inside satiating; reverse course nourishes circle. (6) Base: universe-made vessel, wheel satiated, self bowed satiates; no vessel → auspicious wave-shell; right hand vessel-formed; two up-gone-right joined without joints shell; falling drops satisfy vetāla-guhyakas; holding = Bhairava; no entry to anyone; careless entry → no inquiry; redo double circle-worship; leftovers/food scattered before as wished (secret house or signal-name except, all by god-word) = hero-mixed-circle-worship. (7) End: gifts satiate — chief-most image-worship; even unseen-maṇḍala, by image-worship holy days, from year son-stated fruit, without dawn-conduct — old/enjoyer/women course when descent, teacher-taught.

## tsa20-p03 — pavitra and exposition
**SUMMARY.** Pavitra: in Ratnamālā-triple-peak-Siddhāmata etc., rite-first, Lord-command-fulfilling — Tantrāloka: without pavitra all fruitless. Āṣāḍha-bright to Kula-full-moon-end. Kārttika-dark-fifteenth fills Kula/nitya circle (Nitya-tantra-knowers). Māgha-bright-fifteenth (Bhairava-Kula-wave-knowers). Southern-course-end-fifteenth (Tantra-sadbhāva-knowers). With splendor: worship, offer, satiate, give pavitra from gold-pearl-jewel down to cloth-thread-cotton-kuśa-wombed. Four pavitras (digit etc.: knee, navel, throat, crown) for god and teacher by all-course-full-form-contemplation; one for others. Great festival: four-months, seven-days, three-days — chief-occasional kalpas; splendor → monthly pavitra; or four months; or once. Not-doing → expiation (even knower/with-splendor transgresses from knowledge-blame-doing). Knowledge-gain etc. → consciousness-joy-excess + god-wheel presence specially → special in anuyāga. Exposition: all-scripture-full teacher entreated; even lower-śāstra, compassion, descent-possibility-possessed heart should expound (except secret teaching). Fruitful for low/high with restrained body. First draw/contemplate lotus-seat, worship speech-lady and teachers, satiate wheel, expound non-contradicting, establish/refute/prove/commentary to chapter-end; satiate/worship at thing-end; at end worship knowledge-seat, dismiss, anoint, cast in deep.
**FUNCTION.** Establishes pavitra (purifier rite) calendar and the exposition (vyākhyā) course.
**KEY TERMS.** pavitra (purifier), Ratnamālā, Kula-circle, Nitya-circle, bhairavakulormi, Tantrasadbhāva, vyākhyā (exposition), vyākhyāvidhi.
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** P2 established liṅga/parva/circle-worship; P3 takes up pavitra + exposition.
**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two parts. (1) Pavitra: rite-first, Lord-command-fulfilling (Tantrāloka: without pavitra all fruitless); calendar (Āṣāḍha-bright to Kula-full-moon-end; Kārttika-dark-fifteenth fills Kula/nitya circle; Māgha-bright-fifteenth Bhairava-Kula-wave; southern-course-end-fifteenth Tantra-sadbhāva); with splendor worship/offer/satiate/give pavitra (gold-pearl-jewel down to cloth-thread-cotton-kuśa-wombed); four pavitras (knee/navel/throat/crown) for god+teacher by all-course-full-form-contemplation, one for others; great festival (4-months/7-days/3-days chief-occasional kalpas); splendor → monthly pavitra or four months or once; not-doing → expiation (even knower/with-splendor transgresses from knowledge-blame-doing); knowledge-gain etc. → consciousness-joy-excess + god-wheel presence specially → special in anuyāga. (2) Exposition: all-scripture-full teacher entreated; even lower-śāstra (compassion, descent-possibility-possessed heart) should expound (except secret teaching); fruitful for low/high with restrained body; first draw/contemplate lotus-seat, worship speech-lady and teachers, satiate wheel, expound non-contradicting, establish/refute/prove/commentary to chapter-end; satiate/worship at thing-end; at end worship knowledge-seat, dismiss, anoint, cast in deep.

Woman-killing no expiation; elsewhere weigh strength; unbroken Mālinī recitation to 3 lakhs until doubt-removal; special worship; circle-worship remainder.

# Tantrasāra A21 — ARGMAP

## tsa21-p01 (21.1–21.13)
- speaker: Abhinava, siddhānta (prāmāṇya).
- question: Why is this āgama authoritative — and how can rival āgamas coexist?
- premises: (1) All fixed/occasional conduct stated; now this āgama's authority. (2) One-fruit logic: consciousness-only universe, consciousness as grasping, word-nature of grasping established → grasping of everything + its karma-fruit variety = scripture; Lord-nature-nondifferent, all scripture-connection really one-fruit-giving; one-authority-aim; custom by Lord's destiny-power. (3) Schools: Māyā-fitting-division (Veda-āgama), mokṣa-conceit (Sāṃkhya/Vaiṣṇava), distinct-Śiva-nature-grasping (Śaiva Siddhānta), all-made-Lord-grasping (Mataṅga), very-rare all-limitation-void (Trika), prior-prior-abandoning/leaping — all one fruit from one scripture. (4) Even difference-view: all scriptures' one-Lord-work-ness → authority stands (one-place agreement, unblemished, without-this-ness proceeding equal). Mutual sublation ineffective by subject-difference. (5) Sacrament-difference/excess (like āśrama-difference) + fruit-excess; even distinct-maker, all-all-knowing-made conceivable (stated + beyond-stated reason-yoga). Eternity: custom necessarily admitted (anvaya/vyatireka/perception root it); "true, silver, I see" custom via goldsmith-custom; child's hunger-custom needs no proof; custom as root, admitted for unseen-freedom/liberation/Śiva-sameness/unity fruits (manifold individually). (6) Empty being's excess word-logic; to those making some custom authority, this āgama must be admitted where highest fruit. (7) As shines/grasps so discriminate. (8) Good resort to all-made-consciousness-grasping-essence scripture, unsurpassed-self. (9–13) Steady firm-success in business = I without doubt; abandoning = success-gained supreme Śiva; own firm-success business = world without doubt; then people-transcending success-gained supreme Śiva.
- inference: Authority = custom-grounded, fruit-graded, single-fruit underlying; rivals are partial reports of one.
- conclusion: Prāmāṇya established.
- unresolved: Apabhraṃśa verses (9–13) are Apabhraṃśa (external).

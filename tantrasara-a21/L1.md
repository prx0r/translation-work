# Tantrasāra A21 — L1 controlled

## tsa21-p01
Thus all this fixed and occasional conduct is stated. (21.1) Now of this itself āgama authority is stated. (21.2) Therein: in consciousness-only-made universe, in consciousness, in grasping-natured, of grasping of word-nature established — what is grasping of everything standing and of its karma-fruit-relation-variety — that itself is scripture. Thus Lord-nature-nondifferent is all scripture-connection — really one-fruit-giving. By one-authority-aim therein, by Lord's destiny-power-greatness, in part-part custom of folk. (21.3) Some rooted in Māyā-fitting-division-grasping-natured in Veda-āgama-etc. scripture; others such itself with liberation-conceit in Sāṃkhya-Vaiṣṇava-scripture-and-the-rest; others in distinct-Śiva-nature-grasping-essence in Śaiva Siddhānta-and-the-rest; others in all-made-Lord-nature-grasping-essence in Mataṅga-and-the-rest scripture; some very very rare in all-limitation-void-autonomy-bliss-ultimate-consciousness-made-Lord-nature-grasping-natured in glorious Trika-scripture-course; some by prior-prior-abandoning-course or by leaping — thus one-fruit-success from one scripture alone. (21.4) Even in difference-view, of all scriptures in one-Lord-work-ness too authority so much stands — since of authority-ground, of one-place-agreement, of unblemished, of without-this-ness-proceeding, through equality. Mutual sublation is from subject-difference ineffective. (21.5) As sacrament-difference, sacrament-excess — in its absence somewhere without-authority-ness — thus same as āśrama-difference-like, and from fruit-excess excess. Therein in upaniṣad-Bhāgavata, though distinct-maker-ness, all-all-knowing-made-ness is here conceivable — from stated-and-beyond-stated-reason-content-yoga. In eternity too, of āgamas is custom so much necessarily admitted — since of anvaya-vyatireka-perception-and-the-rest of their authority from that-rootedness. "True, silver, I see" — thus from goldsmith-and-the-rest-other-custom is custom of āgama. That is some seen-fruit: hungry eats, thus of child from custom alone there-there function; not from anvaya-vyatireka then from their absence; in youth-state their becoming too is ineffective. But custom, having made root — so for some for work, some unseen-freedom... fruit-giving, some Śiva-sameness-fruit-giving, some ending-in-unity — and it is in each manifold — thus in much-custom-filled world, who whatever will-be, he such itself custom by force heart-ending imagines. (21.6) Thus of empty being's excess word-logic; to those making some custom authority, to be admitted indeed is āgama-authority. That āgama is to be resorted where highest fruit — so enough of other. (21.7) As shines/grasps so discriminate. (21.8) Good, all-made-consciousness-grasping-essence, resort scripture, unsurpassed-self. (21.9–21.13) "Steady firm-success-gained in business = I without doubt... people-transcending success-gained = supreme Śiva."

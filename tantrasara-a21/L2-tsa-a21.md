# Tantrasara-a21 — L2 READ

## v.1
Thus the whole, permanent, occasion-bound rite is established.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃ samastaṃ nityaṃ naimittikaṃ karma nirūpitam*

## v.2
Now the authority of this very tradition is stated.
The Sanskrit: *adhunā asyaiva āgamasya prāmāṇyam ucyate*

## v.3
Therein — in the universe made of mere consciousness alone, and in consciousness of vimarśa-nature, and in vimarśa's sound-nature established — of the world-established thing and its manifold act-fruit connections: whatever contemplation that is, that indeed is scripture. Whole scripture-structure, in fact, is one-fruit-attaining, by one-duty-declaration alone; but there, by the Lord's necessity-power-glory, people's establishment is in each part.
The Sanskrit: *tatra saṃvinmātramaye viśvasmin saṃvidi ca vimarśātmikāyāṃ vimarśasya ca śabdanātmakatāyāṃ siddhāyāṃ sakalajaganniṣṭhavastunaḥ tadgatasya ca karmaphalasambandhavaicitryasya yat vimarśanaṃ tad eva śāstram iti parameśvarasvabhāvābhinna eva samastaḥ śāstrasaṃdarbho vastuta ekaphalaprāpakaḥ ekādhikāryuddeśenaiva tatra tu parameśvaraniyatiśaktimahimnaiva bhāge bhāge rūḍhiḥ lokānām iti*

## v.4
Some are established in Veda-tradition-and-rest scriptures, in the self of Māyā-fitting-difference-contemplation; others just that kind by liberation-claim, in Sāṃkhya-Vaiṣṇava-scriptures-and-rest; others in the essence of reflecting separated Śiva-nature, in Śaiva-Siddhānta-and-rest; others in the essence of reflecting all-pervasive Lordhood, in Maṅgala-and-rest scriptures; but some very rare, in the self reflecting the Lord's own nature bound by exhaustive limitation-free-bliss-supreme-truth-made-of-consciousness, in the order of blessed Trika scripture; but some by earlier-earlier-abandonment order or by transcendence — thus in this way one-fruit-attainment from one tradition alone.
The Sanskrit: *kecit māyocitabhedaparāmarśātmani vedāgamādiśāstre rūḍhāḥ anye tathāvidha eva mokṣābhimānena sāṃkhyavaiṣṇavaśāstrādau pare tu viviktaśivasvabhāvāmarśanasāre śaivasiddhāntādau anye sarvamayaparameśvaratāmarśanasāre mataṃgādiśāstre kecit tu viralaviralāḥ samastāvacchedavandhyasvātantryānandaparamārthasaṃvinmayaparameśvarasvarūpāmarśanātmani śrītrikaśāstrakrame kecit tu pūrvapūrvatyāgakrameṇa laṅghanena vā ity evam ekaphalasiddhiḥ ekasmād eva āgamāt*

## v.5
In the difference-doctrine too, even of all scriptures, in being one Lord's work, authority stands for now: from being based on authority, partial agreement's not-being-denied, and unstopped operation being equal, mutual contradiction from subject-difference is powerless.
The Sanskrit: *bhedavāde 'pi samastāgamānām ekeśvarakāryatve 'pi prāmāṇyaṃ tāvat avasthitam prāmāṇyanibandhanasya ekadeśasaṃvādasya avigītatāyā anidaṃtāpravṛtteś ca tulyatvāt parasparabādho viṣayabhedāt akiṃcitkaraḥ*

## v.6
Like the brahma-killing-prohibition, ritual-distinction and ritual-excellence — in its absence somewhere being-unqualified: same, like the ashrama-distinction; and from fruit-excellence, excellence just-there. In Upaniṣad-Bhāgavata, in distinct-authorship even, being-made-by-all-knowers is conceivable here — from conjunction of meaning in what-is-said-and-beyond. In eternality even, scriptures' recognition: for-now necessarily-reached, from being-rooted-in-inference-elimination-perception-and-rest — their authority. 'I see gold [and] silver' — indeed, recognition is scripture, through goldsmith-and-others' recognition. Some with-seen-fruit a desirer enjoys: child's engagement just by recognition, not-by-inference-and-elimination — then, from those two's absence, in youth its presence is powerless. But rooting recognition, he — let him be — for someone, some work: some giving fruits of invisible-impotence-nature-dissolution-solitude, some of equality-with-Siva, some ending-in-oneness — she severally many-kinded: thus in a world filled with many recognitions, whoever to-be, he just such recognition by force indeed assumes, ending-in-the-heart.
The Sanskrit: *brahmahananatanniṣedhavat saṃskārabhedaḥ saṃskārātiśayaḥ tadabhāve kvacit anadhikṛtatvam iti samānam āśramabhedavat phalotkarṣāc ca utkarṣaḥ tatraiva upaniṣadbhāgavat bhinnakartṛkatve 'pi sarvasarvajñakṛtatvam atra saṃbhāvyate taduktatadatiriktayuktārthayogāt nityatve 'pi āgamānāṃ prasiddhiḥ tāvat avaśyopagamyā anvayavyatirekādhyakṣādīnāṃ tatprāmāṇyasya tanmūlatvāt satyaṃ rajataṃ paśyāmi iti hi sauvarṇikādiparaprasiddhyaiva prasiddhir eva āgamaḥ sā kācit dṛṣṭaphalā bubhukṣito bhuṅkte iti bālasya prasiddhita eva tatra tatra pravṛttiḥ nānvayavyatirekābhyāṃ tadā tayoḥ abhāvāt yauvanāvasthāyāṃ tadbhāvo 'pi akiṃcitkaraḥ prasiddhiṃ tu mūlīkṛtya so 'stu kasmaicit kāryāya kācit adṛṣṭavaidehyaprakṛtilayapuruṣakaivalyaphaladā kācit śivasamānatvaphaladā kācit aikyaparyavasāyinī sā ca pratyekam anekavidhā ity evaṃ bahutaraprasiddhipūrṇe jagati yo yādṛśo bhaviṣyan sa tādṛśīm eva prasiddhiṃ balād eva hṛdayaparyavasāyinīm abhimanyate*

## v.7
Thus for the empty being's surplus verbal-connections — any of their recognition taken as proof — scripture-authority is to be granted; that scripture is to be relied on where excellent fruit is: enough by another.
The Sanskrit: *iti riktasya jantoḥ atiriktā vācoyuktiḥ tāsāṃ kāṃcana prasiddhiṃ pramāṇīkurvatā abhyupagantavyam eva āgamaprāmāṇyam iti sa āgama āśrayaṇīyo yatra utkṛṣṭaṃ phalam ity alam anyena*

## v.8
By consciousness-light-supreme-truth-nature, as indeed shines, touches also, so — discriminating.
The Sanskrit: *saṃvitprakāśaparamārthatayā yathaiva bhāty āmṛśaty api tatheti vivecayantaḥ*

## v.9
Being, take refuge in the scripture — essence-of-all-made-consciousness-reflection-vimarśa, O Unsurpassable-self.
The Sanskrit: *santaḥ samastamayacitpratibhāvimarśasāraṃ samāśrayata śāstram anuttarātma*

## v.10
In which firm-establishment-made transaction, that very I am fearless.
The Sanskrit: *jissa daḍhapasiddhighaḍie vavahāre soi asmi ṇīsaṃko*

## v.11
Then you will be, according-as establishment-attained, Parama-Siva.
The Sanskrit: *taha hohi jahuttiṇa pasiddhirūḍhie paramasivo*

## v.12
In own-firm-establishment-made transaction, the world is without doubt.
The Sanskrit: *nijadṛḍhaprasiddhighaṭite vyavahāre loka asti niḥśaṅkaḥ*

## v.13
Then becomes, established-through-recognitions-crossed, Parama-Siva.
The Sanskrit: *tadā bhavati janottīrṇaprasiddhirūḍhaḥ paramaśivaḥ*

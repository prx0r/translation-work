# Tantrasāra A21 — L2 READ

## tsa21-p01 — why this book counts
All the fixed and occasional conduct is stated now; what remains is why this book carries authority at all. The argument is one: the universe is consciousness-only, consciousness is grasping, grasping is word-natured — and all of it established — so the grasping of everything that stands, and of the variety of karma-fruit relations, is scripture. Lord-nature-nondifferent, the whole scripture-connection is really one-fruit-giving. One aim, one authority; the divisions by part and part are folk-custom through the Lord's destiny-power. And those divisions are real: some are rooted in scripture that fits Māyā's divisions; some with liberation-conceit in Sāṃkhya-Vaiṣṇava books; some in distinct-Śiva-nature in Śaiva Siddhānta; some in all-made-Lord in Mataṅga; a very few, very rare, in the limitationless form in Trika's course; some by abandoning the earlier, some by leaping. All one fruit from one scripture. Even where schools differ, authority stands: all scriptures work one Lord's work, and their one-place agreement, unblemishedness, and proceeding without "this"-ness are equal. Mutual sublation is ineffective by subject-difference. Sacrament-difference and excess — like āśrama-difference; in absence, sometimes without authority; and from fruit-excess, excess. In the upaniṣad-Bhāgavata, even distinct-maker-ness, all-all-knowing-made-ness is conceivable — from stated and beyond-stated reason-yoga. Eternity too: āgama-custom is necessarily admitted — anvaya-vyatireka-perception-and-the-rest root it. "True, silver, I see" — custom via goldsmith-custom; a hungry child eats by custom alone, no proof needed; in youth the custom's becoming is ineffective. But custom, rooted, may be admitted for some work, some unseen-freedom-nature-principle-dissolution-liberation, some Śiva-sameness, some ending-in-unity — and each individually manifold. So in a world stuffed with custom, whoever will be, imagines by force the custom heart-ending that suits him. Thus the empty being's excess word-logic. To those making some custom authority, this āgama must be admitted — where highest fruit, enough of others. As it shines and grasps, so discriminate. Good men resort to the all-made-consciousness-grasping-essence scripture, the unsurpassed self. (Then the Apabhraṃśa close: steady firm-success in business — I, without doubt; abandoning — success-gained supreme Śiva; firm-success business — world without doubt; then people-transcending success-gained — supreme Śiva.)

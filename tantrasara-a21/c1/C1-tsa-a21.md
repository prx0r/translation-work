# C1 — tsa-a21

## SUMMARY

Rite established, the discourse turns: the authority of this very tradition is stated. The authority of this very tradition is announced as the discourse's next subject. Scripture is vimarśana of the world-thing in the consciousness-made universe — one-fruit in structure, part-graded in rūḍhi by the Lord's necessity-power. Schools sit rūḍhi in their own scriptures — Veda, Sāṃkhya-Vaiṣṇava, Śaiva-Siddhānta, Maṅgala, rare Trika, abandonment-order — yet the single fruit issues from one āgama. Even in bheda-vāda the authority stands: mutual contradiction from subject-difference alone is powerless. Āgama's prasiddhi is recognition-rooted — gold seen through goldsmiths, child through seeing — its fruits ranging severally to the heart's close. Surplus speech is licensed by any taken-as-proof recognition; the āgama is relied on where the excellent fruit is — enough by another. Light by consciousness-nature shines and touches together — the discrimination follows. Being, take refuge in the scripture whose sāra is the all-made vimarśa — O Unsurpassable-self. Prakrit seal: in the firm-establishment transaction the speaker is fearless. Then you will be, as establishment is attained, Parama-Siva. In the world's own-firm transaction stands doubtless. Then Parama-Siva, established through crossed recognitions.

## FUNCTION

Closes the rite-class, defines scripture as world-vimarśana, maps school rūḍhi to one-fruit, defends authority against bheda-vāda, roots it in recognition, and seals with two Prakrit verses into Parama-Siva.

## KEY TERMS

āgama-prāmāṇya (tradition's authority); vimarśa-śabda (scripture as sound-natured contemplation); ekaphalasiddhi (one-fruit attainment); rūḍhi (school establishment); prasiddhi (recognition — goldsmith model); niṣaṅka (fearless — Prakrit seal); paramaśivatva (the closing state).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Follows the nitya-naimittika-karma exposition (u1); the prāmāṇya discourse (u2-9) then the Prakrit/Sanskrit seal (u10-13) close the āhnika.

## EXPLANATION

Because vimarśa in the consciousness-made universe is what scripture names, authority is prasiddhi-based rather than inferential: the goldsmith sees gold and the child sees by seeing — recognition roots it. Rival bheda-doctrines cannot cancel this, since contradiction by subject-difference alone is powerless. The school plurality is pedagogical (bhāge bhāge rūḍhi), not soteriological: one āgama, one fruit — and the two Prakrit verses carry the attainment from fearless transaction to Parama-Siva.

## BOUNDARY

School rūḍhi is described, not adjudicated; the Prakrit verses promise conditionally ('according-as attainment') — no unconditional claim smuggled.

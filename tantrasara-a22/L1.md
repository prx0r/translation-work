# Tantrasāra A22 — L1 controlled

## tsa22-p01
Twenty-second ahnika. Thus all this worship, for those firmly-rooted entitled ones, by glorious kaula-course is expounded. Therein stated: "Bliss is Brahman, in body three-and-half." "Non-brahmacārins of it from abandoning without bliss." "Bliss-imitation-food-avoiding are circle-worshippers." "In both in hells ghore — hence this state resort." That by this state is kula-worship — and it is sixfold: outer, in power, in self, in couple, in breath, in consciousness. Therein latter-latter superior, former-former for that-taste-sake. For accomplishment-wisher second-fourth-fifth in all cases; sixth for liberation-wisher chief, its second-and-rest in occasioned as possible for rite-completion. Therein outer is altar bliss-full, hero-vessel, red-one, cloth, as stated before or liṅga-and-the-rest. Therein without bath-etc.-to-be-done, by full-bliss-rest purity gained, first breath-consciousness-body-identity contemplated; of consciousness as supreme-Śiva-natured, twenty-seven times mantra uttered in crown-face-heart-private images, straight-reverse, world-course-fullness in Lord's lower-upper-supreme. Thus: from Māyā-person-nature-quality-intellect-and-the-rest down to earth twenty-seven principles; of art-and-rest indwelling; in power too upper-lower; Brahma-five five natures (suddenness etc.) = twenty-seven formedness (Lakuleśa teachers). In supreme too five powers; power too five-formedness — twenty-five powers; mutually unmanifest-differenced = one power; she too unmanifest-differenced = twenty-seven formedness by pervasion of consciousness-fire crest mind-breath-formed by one utterance alone bound — whereby supreme Śiva bound; tantric-excess nothing rushes. And with such mind-abiding, before-standing rite-material-place-direction-base made of it; then argha-vessel too by pervasion-binding filled, worshipped with its drops; altars with its sap by left-thumb-yoga; in body-wheels mantra-wheel worshipped/satiated. Then to breath-end; then in altar trident-formed three-power-end seat imagined: Māyā-end in a-u three-power-end seat; Māyā-end in a-u three-power-end; that-above to-be-worshipped grasping-form power; thus by one utterance alone base-and-supported-placing made — therein-itself in supported in consciousness universe seen; that too consciousness-made; thus universe's consciousness, by it, its container-ness = consciousness reality by two container-nesses obtained. As stated: "creation containerized." Then scent-smoke-sap-flower to self-prostration-end offered, with own-rest muttered, withdrawn, cast in water — thus outer-worship.

## tsa22-p02
Now in power: therein mutually of power-and-heroes, of both, through both-naturedness, in rising-beginning-creation-end-Śiva-power-awakening, of each other function, by Lord-destiny, through pure-naturedness, therein preponderance; by this special-circle's power-ness explained. Therein by pervasion-binding worship; three-power-end seat; in three-corners middle emission-power; pervasion special. Thus in self-body therein in circle then Brahma-hole-and-subwheels. Now in couple: of power mark this — like it nondifference, then without regard to age-and-rest non-attachment; of other-than-world couple-born identity. From effect-cause-together-rising threefold directly or otherwise; in imagination mutually worshipped, satiated, ananda-antika. One should worship circle by suitability, subcircle then-following; outside with flower-and-the-rest, inside with scent-enjoyment-sap-and-the-rest. Thus bliss-mass-made; subcircle-host melts by circle-identity. By each-own-enjoyment-non-enjoyment variety, gradually subcircle-goddesses go to consciousness-circle middle. Subcircle-god-essence ray-host full-of-vigor; that power like-it, mutually-facing becomes. That couple upward-abode-entry throb-born-shaking; shakes subcircles too; they then of-it-made, not separate. Thus couple, melted-difference...

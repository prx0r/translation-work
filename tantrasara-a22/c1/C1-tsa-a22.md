## FUNCTION

Establishes the sixfold kulayāga ladder with its adhikāra split (siddhi vs mukti tracks), the 27 × 3 pervasion arithmetic (tattvas/qualities/powers) grounding the single-utterance binding, the saṃpuṭībhāva identity of consciousness and universe as the rite's fruit, and the yāmala heart (u17–49) where non-difference, mutual worship, sub-wheel assimilation, the calm-arisen pair, and the Nāda-Bhairava doctrine carry the Kaula claim — closing with glance-initiation and the guru-body seventh.

# C1 — Tantrasāra Ahnika 22 (kaula-yāga)

## tsa22-p01 — outer kula-rite
**SUMMARY.** Thus all this worship, for those firmly rooted and entitled, by the glorious kaula course. Bliss is Brahman, in body three-and-half — non-brahmacārins without bliss by abandoning; imitation-bliss-food-avoiding are circle-worshippers; in both hells — so resort to this state. By this state: kula-worship, sixfold — outer, in power, in self, in couple, in breath, in consciousness. Latter-latter superior; former-former for taste. Accomplishment-wisher: second, fourth, fifth in all cases; sixth for liberation-wisher (chief), its second-and-rest in occasioned as possible for rite-completion. Outer: altar bliss-full, hero-vessel, red-one, cloth, or as stated, liṅga. Without bath-need, by full-bliss-rest purity gained — first breath-consciousness-body-identity contemplated; consciousness as supreme-Śiva-natured, mantra uttered twenty-seven times in crown, face, heart, private images, straight-reverse, world-course-fullness in Lord's lower/upper/supreme. Thus: from Māyā-person-nature-quality-intellect-and-rest down to earth, twenty-seven principles; art-and-rest indwelling; in power too upper-lower; Brahma's five (suddenness etc.) = twenty-seven formedness — Lakuleśa teachers. In supreme too five powers; power too five-formedness — twenty-five powers; mutually unmanifest-differenced = one power; she too unmanifest-differenced = twenty-seven formedness by pervasion of consciousness-fire crest mind-breath-formed by one utterance alone bound — whereby supreme Śiva bound; tantric excess nothing rushes. With such mind-abiding, before-standing rite-material-place-direction-base made of it; then argha-vessel too by pervasion-binding filled, worshipped with its drops; altars with its sap by left-thumb-yoga; in body-wheels mantra-wheel worshipped and satiated. Then to breath-end; then in altar trident-formed three-power-end seat imagined: Māyā-end in a-u three-power-end seat; Māyā-end in a-u three-power-end; that-above to-be-worshipped grasping-form power; thus by one utterance alone base-and-supported-placing made — therein-itself in supported in consciousness universe seen; that too consciousness-made; thus universe's consciousness, by it, its container-ness = consciousness reality by two container-nesses obtained. As stated: creation containerized. Then scent-smoke-sap-flower to self-prostration-end offered, with own-rest muttered, withdrawn, cast in water — thus outer-worship.
**FUNCTION.** Establishes the outer kula-yāga: sixfold worship, 27-principle count, one-utterance binding, container doctrine.
**KEY TERMS.** kula-yāga (clan-worship), tridhauṣṭya (three-and-half), saṃpuṭībhāva (container-ness), Lakuleśa (teachers), 27-principles.
**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** 27-principle count per Lakuleśa (traditional). L1 CONDENSED? No — A22 P1 full close done.
**RELATED.** A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS22.1–22.11.

## tsa22-p02 — inner power-worship and the couple (CONDENSED)
**SUMMARY.** Power-worship: mutual function of power-and-heroes, both-natured, in rising-beginning-creation-end-Śiva-power-awakening; Lord-destiny + pure-naturedness → preponderance; by this special-circle's power-ness explained. Therein by pervasion-binding worship; three-power-end seat; in three-corners middle emission-power; pervasion special. Thus in self-body therein in circle then Brahma-hole-and-subwheels. Couple: power's mark — like it nondifference, then without regard to age-and-rest non-attachment; of other-than-world couple-born identity. Effect-cause-together-rising threefold directly or otherwise; in imagination mutually worshipped, satiated, ananda-antika. Worship circle by suitability, subcircle then-following; outside with flower-and-the-rest, inside with scent-enjoyment-sap-and-the-rest. Thus bliss-mass-made; subcircle-host melts by circle-identity. By each-own-enjoyment-non-enjoyment variety, gradually subcircle-goddesses go to consciousness-circle (middle). Subcircle-god-essence ray-host full-of-vigor; that power like-it, mutually-facing becomes. That couple upward-abode-entry throb-born-shaking; shakes subcircles too; they then of-it-made, not separate. Thus couple, melted-difference... (22.24–22.33 per T1: lotus-petals, khecarī-mudrā, nāda-bhairava, mantra-vīrya, etc.)
**FUNCTION.** Establishes inner śakti-worship and the couple (yāmala).
**KEY TERMS.** śakti (power-worship), yāmala (couple), pervasion-binding, emission-power, subcircle.
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** P1 established outer kula-rite; P2 takes up inner power-worship + couple.
**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** CONDENSED (22.24–22.33 per T1; full close pass queued).
**RELATED.** A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS22.12–22.33.

## tsa22-p03 — khecarī-mudrā and nāda (CONDENSED)
**SUMMARY.** Three-cornered lotus petals, sixteenth-petal root, nāla; middle throb, moon-sun clash, instant creation; khecarī-mudrā entry. Throb at throat, ear-eye-mouth-nose channels, eight nāda-bhairavas; mantra-vīrya from it; karma in karma, knower gains freedom-in-life; knowledge-vessel body, empty/full abode, single-staff fire-wind, trident equalized, doubtless, no-conduct, self not-I, body-gods as bliss-shock... Further verses (22.46–22.54) on grahītā, khecarī-siddhi etc.
**KEY TERMS.** khecarī-mudrā (sky-wandering seal), nāda (sound), nāda-bhairava (eight), mantra-vīrya (mantra-vigor), grahītā (grasper).
**LOCAL CONTEXT.** P2 established inner śakti/couple; P3 closes with khecarī + nāda.
**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** CONDENSED (full close pass queued). Liṅga full sense deferred. Traditional implements.
**RELATED.** A21 (prāmāṇya), spans TS22.34–22.54.

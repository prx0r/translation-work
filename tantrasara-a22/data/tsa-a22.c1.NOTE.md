## KEY TERMS (with grounds)

- kulayāga (m.): kula + yāga; the Kula sacrifice. Six loci (u5) + seventh in guru's body (u54).
- ṣoḍhā: six-ways adverb; structures the ahnika (u8–16 rites 1–3; u17–49 rite 4; u50 rite 5; u51 rite 6).
- ruci (f.): taste/relish; ladder logic u6 (pūrvaḥ pūrvas tadrucyartham) — lower rites ripen appetite.
- saptaviṃśati (f.): twenty-seven. Lower = 27 tattvas Māyā→earth (u10); middle = 27 Brahman-qualities (u10, Lakulīśa-cited); highest = 27 as 25+1+1 powers (u11: 5 powers × 5 forms = 25, mutually undivided = 1, still undivided = the pervasion whole).
- śikhābandha (m.): tuft-binding; the pervasion-rite binding the flame (u11) — śikhā fork: flame chosen (agni context), tuft sense reserved here.
- saṃpuṭībhāva (m.): mutual enclosure; consciousness↔universe (u11); the rite's fruit, sealed by etāvattva.
- yāmala (n.): couple; lakṣaṇa = abheda (u17); tadvad points to u15's ubhayātmakatva.
- saṃghaṭṭa (m.): collision/rubbing; saṃvid-visarga (u24); moon-sun (u35-36).
- śāntodita: calm-arisen pair; simultaneous arising (u26); womb-cause para kaula (u25).
- visarga (m.): emission; trividha anv-artha (u32); visargaśakti in triangle-middle (u15).
- khecarī-mudrā: sky-going seal; entry (u37), practice (u48); khecarī fork held LIVE (u47).
- nādabhairava (m.): eightfold, heard between wheels at agitation's end (u41).
- avalokanā-dīkṣā: glance-initiation for the doubtless disciple (u52); sesame-ghee waived; old rule for parokṣa/naimittika.

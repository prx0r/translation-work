# Vijñānabhairava — ARGMAP

## vbt-p01 (1–10)
- speaker: Devī asks; Bhairava answers.
- question: What is Bhairava's true form?
- premises: Śrutaṃ deva mayā sarvaṃ rudrayāmalasambhavam (I have heard all, Lord, arising from Rudrayāmala). Trikabhedam aśeṣeṇa sārāt sāravibhāgaśaḥ (Trika division, completely, essence from essence). Adyāpi na nivṛtto me saṃśayaḥ parameśvara (even now, doubt not removed, supreme Lord). Kiṃ rūpaṃ tattvato deva śabdarāśikalāmayam (what form, in truth, Lord, made of word-group and kalā). Kiṃ vā navātmabhedena bhairave bhairavākṛtau (or by nine-ātman division, in Bhairava, in Bhairava-form). Triśirobhedabhinnaṃ vā kiṃ vā śaktitrayātmakam (or divided by three-head division, or made of three śaktis). Nādabindumayaṃ vāpi kiṃ candrārdhanirodhikāḥ (or made of nāda-bindu, or half-moon obstructions). Cakrārūḍham anackaṃ vā kiṃ vā śaktitrayātmakam (or wheel-mounted, anacka, or made of three śaktis).
- inference: Bhairava's true form = Devī's doubt; question about śabdarāśi/kalā, navātman, triśira, śaktitraya, nādabindu, candrārdha, cakrārūḍha, anacka.
- conclusion: Question stated (continued).
- unresolved: none.

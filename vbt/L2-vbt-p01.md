# VBT vbt-p01 — L2 READ

## v.1
I have heard it all, O Lord — how it is born of Rudrayāmala, how the Trika divisions run, distinguished out of essence, essence by essence.
The Sanskrit: *śrutaṃ deva mayā sarvaṃ rudrayāmalasambhavam | trikabhedam aśeṣeṇa sārāt sāravibhāgaśaḥ*

## v.2
Even now the doubt is not dispelled in me, O highest Lord: what form is it in truth, O god — made of the heap of words and digits?
The Sanskrit: *adyāpi na nivṛtto me saṃśayaḥ parameśvara | kiṃ rūpaṃ tattvato deva śabdarāśikalāmayam*

## v.3
Is it the ninefold self, divided — Bhairava, Bhairava's form; split by the three-headed division, or, what, the nature of the three powers?
The Sanskrit: *kiṃ vā navātmabhedena bhairave bhairavākṛtau | triśirobhedabhinnaṃ vā kiṃ vā śaktitrayātmakam*

## v.4
Or made of nāda's drop? Or the checkers of the moon's half? Mounted on the wheel, unsmeared? Or what, the power-formed?
The Sanskrit: *nādabindumayaṃ vāpi kiṃ candrārdhanirodhikāḥ | cakrārūḍham anackaṃ vā kiṃ vā śaktisvarūpakam*

## v.5
Of higher and lower, the whole — and of the lower, or again: but if the higher be that way, its higher-ness is contradicted.
The Sanskrit: *parāparāyāḥ sakalam aparāyāś ca vā punaḥ | parāyā yadi tadvat syāt paratvaṃ tad virudhyate*

## v.6
For it cannot come about by letter-division, or by dividing bodies: higher-ness is partlessness — not that, in wholeness.
The Sanskrit: *na hi varṇavibhedena dehabhedena vā bhavet | paratvaṃ niṣkalatvena sakalatve na tad bhavet*

## v.7
Grant me grace, O Lord — cut the doubt entirely: good, good, what you have asked — this, the essence of the tantra, O dear.
The Sanskrit: *prasādaṃ kuru me nātha niḥśeṣaṃ chinddhi saṃśayam | sādhu sādhu tvayā pṛṣṭaṃ tantrasāram idam priye*

## v.8
Most worthy of hiding, O fortunate one — yet I will tell you whatever whole form of Bhairava is proclaimed.
The Sanskrit: *gūhanīyatamam bhadre tathāpi kathayāmi te | yatkiṃcit sakalaṃ rūpaṃ bhairavasya prakīrtitam*

## v.9
That must be known by its unsubstantialness, O Goddess — like Indra's net; like a māyā-dream, and indeed, the error of Gandharva cities.
The Sanskrit: *tad asāratayā devi vijñeyaṃ śakrajālavat | māyāsvapnopamaṃ caiva gandharvanagarabhramam*

## v.10
For meditation's sake — for confused minds, for dwellers in pompous rite — it is described merely, for men whose selves are struck by fancy.
The Sanskrit: *dhyānārtham bhrāntabuddhīnāṃ kriyāḍambaravartinām | kevalaṃ varṇitam puṃsāṃ vikalpanihatātmanām*

# VBT vbt-p02 — L2 READ

## v.11
In truth it is not that ninefold self, not the heap of words, not Bhairava; not that three-headed god either, nor the nature of the three powers.
The Sanskrit: *tattvato na navātmāsau śabdarāśir na bhairavaḥ | na cāsau triśirā devo na ca śaktitrayātmakaḥ*

## v.12
Not made of nāda's drop, either; not the checkers of the moon; not joined to the wheel's course, not the power-formed.
The Sanskrit: *nādabindumayo vāpi na candrārdhanirodhikāḥ | na cakrakramasambhinno na ca śaktisvarūpakaḥ*

## v.13
These terrors of unawakened minds are strong indeed; like a mother's sweet, all of it is declared for the sake of going on.
The Sanskrit: *aprabuddhamatīnāṃ hi etā balavibhīṣikāḥ | mātṛmodakavat sarvaṃ pravṛttyarthaṃ udāhṛtam*

## v.14
Free of direction, time and counting — with no particular place or point: it cannot be pointed out; untellable in the end.
The Sanskrit: *dikkālakalanonmuktā deśoddeśāviśeṣinī | vyapadeṣṭum aśakyāsāv akathyā paramārthataḥ*

## v.15
Joy of inner self-taste, unhoused in fancy's range — that full-formed state, Bhairavī, of Bhairava's own self.
The Sanskrit: *antaḥsvānubhavānandā vikalponmuktagocarā | yāvasthā bharitākārā bhairavī bhairavātmanaḥ*

## v.16
That body must be known in truth: stainless, filling the world — in such a supreme reality, who is worshipped, and which one is pleased?
The Sanskrit: *tad vapus tattvato jñeyaṃ vimalaṃ viśvapūraṇam | evaṃvidhe pare tattve kaḥ pūjyaḥ kaś ca tṛpyati*

## v.17
Whatever state of Bhairava's is sung like this: she is the supreme one — proclaimed the supreme goddess as the supreme form.
The Sanskrit: *evaṃvidhā bhairavasya yāvasthā parigīyate | sā parā pararūpeṇa parā devī prakīrtitā*

## v.18
As the non-difference of power and the powerful stands always — hence, from that property-owning, the supreme power of the supreme self.
The Sanskrit: *śaktiśaktimator yadvad abhedaḥ sarvadā sthitaḥ | atas taddharmadharmitvāt parā śaktiḥ parātmanaḥ*

## v.19
No power separate from fire's burning is conceived; only in knowing-being, in entering, does this beginning arise.
The Sanskrit: *na vahner dāhikā śaktir vyatiriktā vibhāvyate | kevalaṃ jñānasattāyām prārambho 'yam praveśane*

## v.20
Of one entered into the power-state, contemplation without division — then he becomes Śiva-formed: that mouth is called Śaivī.
The Sanskrit: *śaktyavasthāpraviṣṭasya nirvibhāgena bhāvanā | tadāsau śivarūpī syāt śaivī mukham ihocyate*

# VBT vbt-p03 — L2 READ

## v.21
Just as by the world-light of a lamp, by rays, and of the sun, direction and division and the rest are known — so by power, Śiva is known, O dear.
The Sanskrit: *yathālokena dīpasya kiraṇair bhāskarasya ca | jñāyate digvibhāgādi tadvac chaktyā śivaḥ priye*

## v.22
God of gods, marked with the trident, adorned with the skull: void of direction, place and time — and free of designation.
The Sanskrit: *devadeva triśūlāṅka kapālakṛtabhūṣaṇa | digdeśakālaśūnyā ca vyapadeśavivarjitā*

## v.23
What is that full-formed state of Bhairava, and by what means does its door open, O supreme Goddess? Tell me as you rightly know it, Bhairava.
The Sanskrit: *yāvasthā bharitākārā bhairavasyopalabhyate | kair upāyair mukhaṃ tasya parā devi katham bhavet | yathā samyag ahaṃ vedmi tathā me brūhi bhairava*

## v.24
Breath above, life below, and the emission-natured one should utter forth: at the two arising places, filled from filling, standing.
The Sanskrit: *ūrdhve prāṇo hy adho jīvo visargātmā paroccaret | utpattidvitayasthāne bharaṇād bharitā sthitiḥ*

## v.25
Of wind within or without, or even from the sky-pair not turning; by Bhairavī — of Bhairava thus, O Bhairavī, the manifested form.
The Sanskrit: *maruto 'ntar bahir vāpi viyadyugmānivartanāt | bhairavyā bhairavasyettham bhairavi vyajyate vapuḥ*

## v.26
Neither should it go nor enter, the wind-formed power when it blossoms; by thoughtlessness in the middle — by her, Bhairava-formness.
The Sanskrit: *na vrajen na viśec chaktir marudrūpā vikāsite | nirvikalpatayā madhye tayā bhairavarūpatā*

## v.27
Whether kumbhaka-held, or exhaled, or inhaled — when it may be so: at its end this is called calm; by power, calm shines forth.
The Sanskrit: *kumbhitā recitā vāpi pūritā vā yadā bhavet | tadante śāntanāmāsau śaktyā śāntaḥ prakāśate*

## v.28
From the root, ray-shining; from the subtle, the subtler-natured — contemplate her at the twelve's end, calming down: Bhairava's rise.
The Sanskrit: *āmūlāt kiraṇābhāsāṃ sūkṣmāt sūkṣmatarātmikam | cintayet tāṃ dviṣaṭkānte śyāmyantīm bhairavodayaḥ*

## v.29
Rising up, lightning-formed, wheel by wheel, step by step; upward to three fists — until, then at the end, the great rise.
The Sanskrit: *udgacchantīṃ taḍitrūpām praticakraṃ kramāt kramam | ūrdhvaṃ muṣṭitrayaṃ yāvat tāvad ante mahodayaḥ*

## v.30
Twelvefold course rightly, divided by twelve letters; through gross, subtle, supreme standing — freed, freed at last: Śiva.
The Sanskrit: *kramadvādaśakaṃ samyag dvādaśākṣarabheditam | sthūlasūkṣmaparasthityā muktvā muktvāntataḥ śivaḥ*

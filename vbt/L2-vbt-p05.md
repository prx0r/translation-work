# VBT vbt-p05 — L2 READ

## v.41
In stringed instruments and the like, in the long tones of ordered resonance: with undivided mind at the limit, the body may become the supreme sky.
The Sanskrit: *tantryādivādyaśabdeṣu dīrgheṣu kramasaṃsthiteḥ | ananyacetāḥ pratyante paravyomavapur bhavet*

## v.42
Of the body-mantra of all, by the gross-letter course — ending in moon-drop-sound: from void uttering, one may become Śiva.
The Sanskrit: *pi.ṅdamantrasya sarvasya sthūlavarṇakrameṇa tu | ardhendubindunādāntaḥ śūnyoccārād bhavec chivaḥ*

## v.43
In one's own body, all directions at once should be contemplated as sky; with undirected mind — of him, sky proceeds everywhere.
The Sanskrit: *nijadehe sarvadikkaṃ yugapad bhāvayed viyat | nirvikalpamanās tasya viyat sarvam pravartate*

## v.44
The back-void and the root-void at once should be contemplated, and whoever: by the body-disregarding power, void-minded he may be.
The Sanskrit: *pṛṣṭaśūnyaṃ mūlaśūnyaṃ yugapad bhāvayec ca yaḥ | śarīranirapekṣiṇyā śaktyā śūnyamanā bhavet*

## v.45
Back-void, root-void, heart-void should be contemplated steady: at once, from thoughtlessness — thoughtless-rising follows.
The Sanskrit: *pṛṣṭaśūnyaṃ mūlaśūnyaṃ hṛcchūnyam bhāvayet sthiram | yugapan nirvikalpatvān nirvikalpodayas tataḥ*

## v.46
In a place of the body, voidness indeed — for a mere moment should be envisioned: thoughtless, thoughtless, sharing thoughtless nature.
The Sanskrit: *tanūdeśe śūnyataiva kṣaṇamātraṃ vibhāvayet | nirvikalpaṃ nirvikalpo nirvikalpasvarūpabhāk*

## v.47
All gone in the body-stuff, pervaded by sky, O deer-eyed one: should be envisioned then — of him, the contemplation may grow steady.
The Sanskrit: *sarvaṃ dehagataṃ dravyaṃ viyadvyāptaṃ mṛgekṣaṇe | vibhāvayet tatas tasya bhāvanā sā sthirā bhavet*

## v.48
Inside the body, the skin-division should be thought through as a wall: nothing inside it — for the meditating one, the one beyond the meditated's share may be.
The Sanskrit: *dehāntare tvagvibhāgam bhittibhūtaṃ vicintayet | na kiṃcid antare tasya dhyāyann adhyeyabhāg bhavet*

## v.49
In the heart-sky, eyes hidden, gone to the middle of the lotus-casket: with undivided mind, O fortunate one — supreme fortune may be won.
The Sanskrit: *hṛdyākāśe nilīnākṣaḥ padmasampuṭamadhyagaḥ | ananyacetāḥ subhage paraṃ saubhāgyam āpnuyāt*

## v.50
From all around one's own body, at the twelve's end, from mind-dissolving: of firm-minded made firm, the reality-mark proceeds.
The Sanskrit: *sarvataḥ svaśarīrasya dvādaśānte manolayāt | dṛḍhabuddher dṛḍhībhūtaṃ tattvalakṣyam pravartate*

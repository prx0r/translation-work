# VBT vbt-p06 — L2 READ

## v.51
Just so, where there — at the twelve's end, the mind should be cast: moment by moment, of wasted turning, the strangeness drops away in days.
The Sanskrit: *yathā tathā yatra tatra dvādaśānte manaḥ kṣipet || pratikṣaṇaṃ kṣīṇavṛtter vailakṣaṇyaṃ dinair bhavet*

## v.52
By the time-fire, arisen from the time-place — one's own city should be thought burnt; at the end, calm-shining, then it may be.
The Sanskrit: *kālāgninā kālapadād utthitena svakam puram | pluṣṭam vicintayed ante śāntābhāsas tadā bhavet*

## v.53
Thus, indeed, the whole world burnt — having meditated, away from fancy: in the non-other mind of the man, manliness supreme may be.
The Sanskrit: *evam eva jagat sarvaṃ dagdhaṃ dhyātvā vikalpataḥ | ananyacetasaḥ puṃsaḥ pumbhāvaḥ paramo bhavet*

## v.54
In one's own body, the world's or even the minute-minuter things and realities — their abiding contemplated at the end, the supreme is manifested.
The Sanskrit: *svadehe jagato vāpi sūkṣmasūkṣmatarāṇi ca | tattvāni yāni nilayaṃ dhyātvānte vyajyate parā*

## v.55
The thin and weak power, meditated at the twelve's range: having entered, meditating in the heart — freed, freedom may be won.
The Sanskrit: *pināṃ ca durbalāṃ śaktiṃ dhyātvā dvādaśagocare | praviśya hṛdaye dhyāyan muktaḥ svātantryam āpnuyāt*

## v.56
The world-course and the rest formed, in order, all: through gross, subtle, supreme standing — as long as, at the end, mind-dissolves.
The Sanskrit: *bhuvanādhvādirūpeṇa cintayet kramaśo 'khilam | sthūlasūkṣmaparasthityā yāvad ante manolayaḥ*

## v.57
Of this, of all, of the world — at the ends everywhere; by path-procedure, Śiva-reality meditated: the great rise.
The Sanskrit: *asya sarvasya viśvasya paryanteṣu samantataḥ | adhvaprakriyayā tattvaṃ śaivaṃ dhyatvā mahodayaḥ*

## v.58
The universe, this, O great Goddess, void-become should be thought; right there and mind merged — then, of that, the vessel of merging.
The Sanskrit: *viśvam etan mahādevi śūnyabhūtaṃ vicintayet | tatraiva ca mano līnaṃ tatas tallayabhājanam*

## v.59
In a pot and such a vessel, the gaze — the wall left, cast well: at that dissolving, in that moment gone — made of that he may be.
The Sanskrit: *ghatādibhājane dṛṣṭim bhittis tyaktvā vinikṣipet | tallayaṃ tatkṣaṇād gatvā tallayāt tanmayo bhavet*

## v.60
In a treeless mountain, a wall, such a place — the gaze should be cast well: when dissolved in mind, in that state, the wasting of turning is born.
The Sanskrit: *nirvṛkṣagiribhittyādideśe dṛṣṭiṃ vinikṣipet | vilīne mānase bhāve vṛttikṣiṇaḥ prajāyate*

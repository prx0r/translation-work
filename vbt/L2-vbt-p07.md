# VBT vbt-p07 — L2 READ

## v.61
Of the two, of the two states, in knowing having meditated — the middle should be resorted to: at once, with both left, in the middle reality shines forth.
The Sanskrit: *ubhayor bhāvayor jñāne dhyātvā madhyaṃ samāśrayet | yugapac ca dvayaṃ tyaktvā madhye tattvam prakāśate*

## v.62
When the state is abandoned, the restrained consciousness does not go to any other state: then, in that middle-being, contemplation expands exceedingly.
The Sanskrit: *bhāve tyakte niruddhā cin naiva bhāvāntaraṃ vrajet | tadā tanmadhyabhāvena vikasatyati bhāvanā*

## v.63
All body consciousness-made — or the world indeed, fancy fully: at once, by undivided mind, the supreme rise.
The Sanskrit: *sarvaṃ dehaṃ cinmayaṃ hi jagad vā paribhāvayet | yugapan nirvikalpena manasā paramodayaḥ*

## v.64
Of the two winds, from their clashing — inside or outside, at the limit: the yogin, of the rising of evenness-knowing, becomes the vessel of offering.
The Sanskrit: *vāyudvayasya saṃghaṭṭād antar vā bahir antataḥ | yogī samatvavijñānasamudgamanabhājanam*

## v.65
All world, or one's own body, filled with one's own bliss should be remembered: at once with one's own nectar indeed — full of supreme bliss he may be.
The Sanskrit: *sarvaṃ jagat svadehaṃ vā svānandabharitaṃ smaret | yugapat svāmṛtenaiva parānandamayo bhavet*

## v.66
By trick, by application — at once indeed, O deer-eyed one: great bliss manifests, by which reality shines forth.
The Sanskrit: *kuhanena prayogeṇa sadya eva mṛgekṣaṇe | samudeti mahānando yena tattvaṃ prakāśate*

## v.67
By binding all the channels, by the up-going breath-power, slowly: at the crawling-ant's moment, supreme happiness spreads forth.
The Sanskrit: *sarvasrotonibandhena prāṇaśaktyordhvayā śanaiḥ | pipīlasparśavelāyām prathate paramaṃ sukham*

## v.68
Between fire and poison, in the middle — the mind, happiness-made, should be cast: merely wind-filled, or yoked by remembrance-bliss.
The Sanskrit: *vahner viṣasya madhye tu cittaṃ sukhamayaṃ kṣipet | kevalaṃ vāyupūrṇaṃ vā smarānandena yujyate*

## v.69
Agitated by union, shaking, at the end of power-penetration: whatever happiness of brahman-reality — that happiness, self-told, is told.
The Sanskrit: *śaktisaṃgamasaṃkṣubdhaśaktyāveśāvasānikam | yat sukham brahmatattvasya tat sukhaṃ svākyam ucyate*

## v.70
By licking, churning, the pounding apexes — of woman-bliss from fullness, from memory: even in power's absence, O Goddess of gods, the bliss-flood may be.
The Sanskrit: *lehanāmanthanākoṭaiḥ strīsukhasya bharāt smṛteḥ | śaktyabhāve 'pi deveśi bhaved ānandasamplavaḥ*

# VBT vbt-p09 — L2 READ

## v.81
On the middle tongue, in the opened mouth, in the middle — awareness cast: the h-uttering made by mind, then in calm it dissolves.
The Sanskrit: *madhyajihve sphāritāsye madhye nikṣipya cetanām | hoccāraṃ manasā kurvaṃs tataḥ śānte pralīyate*

## v.82
On seat, on bed, having stood — unsupported, envisioning one's own body in mind: when mind wasted — in a moment, wasted-impurity may be.
The Sanskrit: *āsane śayane sthitvā nirādhāraṃ vibhāvayan | svadehaṃ manasi kṣiṇe kṣaṇāt kṣīṇāśayo bhavet*

## v.83
On a moving seat, standing now — slowly, or by the body's moving: in the calmed mind, in that state, O Goddess, the divine flood may be won.
The Sanskrit: *calāsane sthitasyātha śanair vā dehacālanāt | praśānte mānase bhāve devi divyaugham āpnuyāt*

## v.84
Sky, stainless, seeing — gaze made unbroken: stunned self at that moment, O Goddess, the Bhairava form may be won.
The Sanskrit: *ākāśaṃ vimalam paśyan kṛtvā dṛṣṭiṃ nirantarām | stabdhātmā tatkṣaṇād devi bhairavaṃ vapur āpnuyāt*

## v.85
Dissolved on the crown, sky all as Bhairava should be contemplated: that all — the Bhairava-form-blaze-reality may enter.
The Sanskrit: *līnaṃ mūrdhni viyat sarvam bhairavatvena bhāvayet | tat sarvam bhairavākāratejastattvaṃ samāviśet*

## v.86
Something known — duality-giving, outer light, darkness again: the universe-form of Bhairava — having known, endless-light-bearing.
The Sanskrit: *kiñcij jñātaṃ dvaitadāyi bāhyālokas tamaḥ punaḥ | viśvādi bhairavaṃ rūpaṃ jñātvānantaprakāśabhṛt*

## v.87
Thus indeed in bad night, on the dark fortnight's arrival, long: contemplating timira — Bhairava's form will come to.
The Sanskrit: *evam eva durniśāyāṃ kṛṣṇapakṣāgame ciram | taimiram bhāvayan rūpam bhairavaṃ rūpam eṣyati*

## v.88
Thus indeed, having closed first the eyes, black lustre before: spread Bhairava's form, contemplating — made of that he may be.
The Sanskrit: *evam eva nimīlyādau netre kṛṣṇābham agrataḥ | prasārya bhairavaṃ rūpam bhāvayaṃs tanmayo bhavet*

## v.89
Of whichever organ even — from its striking and its blocking: of one entered in the nondual, in void — there itself the self shines forth.
The Sanskrit: *yasya kasyendriyasyāpi vyāghātāc ca nirodhataḥ | praviṣṭasyādvaye śūnye tatraivātmā prakāśate*

## v.90
No drop, no emission, and the a-syllable of muttering great: rises, O Goddess, suddenly — knowledge-flood, the highest Lord.
The Sanskrit: *abindum avisargaṃ ca akāraṃ japato mahān | udeti devi sahasā jñānaughaḥ parameśvaraḥ*

# VBT vbt-p10 — L2 READ

## v.91
Of letter with emission — emission-ending awareness make: by supportless mind, eternal brahman may be touched.
The Sanskrit: *varṇasya savisargasya visargāntaṃ citiṃ kuru | nirādhāreṇa cittena spṛśed brahma sanātanam*

## v.92
Sky-formed one's own self should be meditated, by directions uncovered: supportless awareness-power — own form will show it then.
The Sanskrit: *vyomākāraṃ svam ātmānaṃ dhyāyed digbhir anāvṛtam | nirāśrayā citiḥ śaktiḥ svarūpaṃ darśayet tadā*

## v.93
Something, a limb, pierced first — with sharp needle and such, then: right there, awareness yoked — in Bhairava, spotless going.
The Sanskrit: *kiṃcid aṅgaṃ vibhidyādau tīkṣṇasūcyādinā tataḥ | tatraiva cetanāṃ yuktvā bhairave nirmalā gatiḥ*

## v.94
Mind-and-the-rest, inner-making, is not inside me: think thus — by absence of fancies, fancies abandoned may be.
The Sanskrit: *cittādyantaḥkṛtir nāsti mamāntar bhāvayed iti | vikalpānām abhāvena vikalpair ujjhito bhavet*

## v.95
Māyā, the deluding one, named: the counting of the parts stands; counting the such-and-such natures of realities — not separately may be.
The Sanskrit: *māyā vimohinī nāma kalāyāḥ kalanaṃ sthitam | ityādidharmaṃ tattvānāṃ kalayan na pṛthag bhavet*

## v.96
Sudden desire arisen — having seen it, to calm should lead it: where it indeed arose, there right there it dissolves.
The Sanskrit: *jhagitīcchāṃ samutpannām avalokya śamaṃ nayet | yata eva samudbhūtā tatas tatraiva līyate*

## v.97
When my desire is unarisen — knowing, or what — then I am indeed: truly He-I, thus become, in that dissolved, that-minded may be.
The Sanskrit: *yadā mamecchā notpannā jñānaṃ vā kas tadāsmi vai | tattvato =B9haṃ tathābhūtas tallīnas tanmanā bhavet*

## v.98
In desire or in knowing, when arisen — mind should be set: self-mind, undivided-minded — then the seeing of reality's meaning.
The Sanskrit: *icchāyām athavā jñāne jāte cittaṃ niveśayet | ātmabuddhyānanyacetās tatas tattvārthadarśanam*

## v.99
Causeless may be the knowing, supportless, error-natured: truly not anyone's — thus becoming Śiva, O dear.
The Sanskrit: *nirnimittam bhavej jñānaṃ nirādhāram bhramātmakam | tattvataḥ kasyacin naitad evambhāvī śivaḥ priye*

## v.100
Consciousness-natured in all bodies — distinction there is nowhere: hence, made of that, contemplating all — the world-conquering person.
The Sanskrit: *ciddharmā sarvadeheṣu viśeṣo nāsti kutracit | ataś ca tanmayaṃ sarvam bhāvayan bhavajij janaḥ*

# VBT vbt-p12 — L2 READ

## v.111
Having whirled, whirled — by the body swiftly, falling to earth: by the ceasing of agitation-power, the supreme state is born.
The Sanskrit: *bhrāntvā bhrāntvā śarīreṇa tvaritam bhuvi pātanāt | kṣobhaśaktivirāmeṇa parā saṃjāyate daśā*

## v.112
On supports or by power, by knowing, or from mind-dissolving: born at power-penetration's end — the Bhairava form.
The Sanskrit: *ādhāreṣv athavā 'śaktyā 'jñānāc cittalayena vā | jātaśaktisamāveśakṣobhānte bhairavaṃ vapuḥ*

## v.113
This tradition, O Goddess, hear — rightly I tell: even freedom is born at once, when the eyes are unwinkingly fixed.
The Sanskrit: *sampradāyam imam devi śṛṇu samyag vadāmy aham | kaivalyaṃ jāyate sadyo netrayoḥ stabdhamātrayoḥ*

## v.114
Contraction of the ears having been made — at the lower gate likewise: unsmeared, unploughed, meditating, one may enter eternal brahman.
The Sanskrit: *saṃkocaṃ karṇayoḥ kṛtvā hy adhodvāre tathaiva ca | anackam ahalaṃ dhyāyan viśed brahma sanātanam*

## v.115
At a well and such, a great pit — having stood above, from looking: of undivided mind rightly, at once mind-dissolving, plainly.
The Sanskrit: *kūpādike mahāgarte sthitvopari nirīkṣaṇāt | avikalpamateḥ samyak sadyas cittalayaḥ sphuṭam*

## v.116
Wherever the mind goes — outside or inside, or even so: there, there Śiva-state — by pervasiveness: where would it go?
The Sanskrit: *yatra yatra mano yāti bāhye vābhyantare 'pi vā | tatra tatra śivāvāsthā vyāpakatvāt kva yāsyati*

## v.117
Wherever by the eye's road, consciousness is manifested of the pervader: of him, from only-that-natured, from cit-dissolving — full-selfness.
The Sanskrit: *yatra yatrākṣamārgeṇa caitanyaṃ vyajyate vibhoḥ | tasya tanmātradharmitvāc cillayād bharitātmatā*

## v.118
At hunger's end and such — in fear, in grief, in the abyss, or fled from battle; at curiosity-thirst's end: reality-being-full state.
The Sanskrit: *kṣutādyante bhaye śoke gahvare vā raṇād drute | kutūhalekṣudhādyante brahmasattāmayī daśā*

## v.119
In things being remembered, when seen — in place, the mind should leave: one's own body unsupported made, the lord spreads forth.
The Sanskrit: *vastuṣu smaryamāṇeṣu dṛṣṭe deśe manas tyajet | svaśarīraṃ nirādhāraṃ kṛtvā prasarati prabhuḥ*

## v.120
Anywhere, on a thing placed — slowly the gaze should withdraw: that knowing, mind-with — O Goddess, void-abode may it be.
The Sanskrit: *kvacid vastuni vinyasya śanair dṛṣṭiṃ nivartayet | taj jñānaṃ cittasahitaṃ devi śūnyālāyo bhavet*

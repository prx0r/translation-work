# VBT vbt-p14 — L2 READ

## v.131
'I', 'mine', 'this' and such — from coming to conviction: in supportless mind it goes — from that meditation's impulse, calm.
The Sanskrit: *ahaṃ mamedam ityādi pratipattiprasaṅgataḥ | nirādhāre mano yāti taddhyānapreraṇāc chamī*

## v.132
Eternal, all-pervading, supportless, pervasive, and lord of all: words moment by moment meditated — aim done according to meaning.
The Sanskrit: *nityo vibhur nirādhāro vyāpakaś cākhilādhipaḥ | śabdān pratikṣaṇaṃ dhyāyan kṛtārtho 'rthānurūpataḥ*

## v.133
Non-reality, Indra-net-shine — this all standing: what reality of the Indra-net? Thus from firmness, calm may go.
The Sanskrit: *atattvam indrajālābham idaṃ sarvam avasthitam | kiṃ tattvam indrajālasya iti dārḍhyāc chamaṃ vrajet*

## v.134
Of self, unwavering — where knowing, where action? Knowing-dependent outer states: hence void, this world.
The Sanskrit: *ātmano nirvikārasya kva jñānaṃ kva ca vā kriyā | jñānāyattā bahirbhāvā ataḥ śūnyam idaṃ jagat*

## v.135
Not of me bondage, not freedom — of the frightened, these terrors: this reflection of the intellect in waters, like the sun's.
The Sanskrit: *na me bandho na mokṣo me bhītasyaitā vibhīṣikāḥ | pratibimbam idam buddher jaleṣv iva vivasvataḥ*

## v.136
Sense-doored, all — meeting pleasure, pain and such: senses thus having left, self-abiding — in one's own self one abides.
The Sanskrit: *indriyadvārakaṃ sarvaṃ sukhaduḥkhādisaṃgamam | itīndriyāṇi saṃtyajya svasthaḥ svātmani vartate*

## v.137
Knowing-lighting all — by all self lighting: one, from one-natureness — knowing, knowable, is envisioned.
The Sanskrit: *jñānaprakāśakaṃ sarvaṃ sarveṇātmā prakāśakaḥ | ekam ekasvabhāvatvāt jñānaṃ jñeyaṃ vibhāvyate*

## v.138
Mental, consciousness, power, self, and mind — the fourfold: when, O dear, fully wasted — then that Bhairava form.
The Sanskrit: *mānasaṃ cetanā śaktir ātmā ceti catuṣṭayam | yadā priye parikṣīṇaṃ tadā tad bhairavaṃ vapuḥ*

## v.139
Of waveless teachings, a hundred told in brief; twelve-plus — O Goddess, which having known, the knowing-knower person.
The Sanskrit: *nistaraṅgopadeśānāṃ śatam uktaṃ samāsataḥ | dvādaśābhyadhikaṃ devi yaj jñātvā jñānavij janaḥ*

## v.140
Here and in one, yoked — he becomes Bhairava himself: by speech does deeds — curse-and-favor making.
The Sanskrit: *atra caikatame yukto jāyate bhairavaḥ svayam | vācā karoti karmāṇi śāpānugrahakārakaḥ*

# VBT vbt-p15 — L2 READ

## v.141
Ageless deathlessness he attains, endowed with atom-and-the-rest qualities: of the yoginīs dear one, O Goddess — the lord who gathers all.
The Sanskrit: *ajarāmaratām eti so 'ṇimādiguṇānvitaḥ | yoginīnām priyo devi sarvamelāpakādhipaḥ*

## v.142
Living even, freed he; acting even — not smeared: this body, if of supreme and great lord, O god.
The Sanskrit: *jīvann api vimukto 'sau kurvann api na lipyate | idaṃ yadi vapur deva parāyāś ca maheśvara*

## v.143
In the state thus told — who mutters, who does muting, and who meditates: who is worshipped, O great lord, and who is pleased?
The Sanskrit: *evamuktavyavasthāyāṃ japyate ko japaś ca kaḥ | dhyāyate ko mahānātha pūjyate kaś ca tṛpyati*

## v.144
Who is offered oblation, or the sacrifice — whose, and what, and how? This procedure here is outer, in gross things indeed, O deer-eyed one.
The Sanskrit: *hūyate kasya vā homo yāgaḥ kasya ca kiṃ katham | eṣātra prakriyā bāhyā sthūleṣv eva mṛgekṣaṇe*

## v.145
Again, again in the supreme state — which contemplation is contemplated indeed: muting there itself — self-sound, mantra-natured — such the to-be-muted.
The Sanskrit: *bhūyo bhūyaḥ pare bhāve bhāvanā bhāvyate hi yā | japaḥ so 'tra svayaṃ nādo mantrātmā japya īdṛśaḥ*

## v.146
Meditation indeed: unmoving intellect, formless, supportless; not meditation — the imagining of body, eye, mouth, hand and such.
The Sanskrit: *dhyānaṃ hi niścalā buddhir nirākārā nirāśrayā | na tu dhyānaṃ śarīrākṣimukhahastādikalpanā*

## v.147
Worship is named not by flowers and such — which mind is made firm: in the undifferentiated great sky, that worship indeed from care, dissolving.
The Sanskrit: *pūjā nāma na puṣpādyair yā matiḥ kriyate dṛḍhā | nirvikalpe mahāvyomni sā pūjā hy ādarāl layaḥ*

## v.148
Here in one or another yoking-standing, when it may arise from day to day: full-formed state — right here contentment, utter fullness.
The Sanskrit: *atraikatamayuktisthe yotpadyeta dinād dinam | bharitākāratā sātra tṛptir atyantapūrṇatā*

## v.149
In the great void-abode, in the fire — sense-objects and such: offered by mind, with all oblation, with the awareness-ladle.
The Sanskrit: *mahāśūnyālaye vahnau bhūtākṣaviṣayādikam | hūyate manasā sārdhaṃ sa homaś cetanāsrucā*

## v.150
The sacrifice here, O highest lady — contentment, bliss-marked: from wasting all sins, from protecting all — O mountain's daughter.
The Sanskrit: *yāgo 'tra parameśāni tuṣṭir ānandalakṣaṇā | kṣapaṇāt sarvapāpānāṃ trāṇāt sarvasya pārvati*

# VBT vbt-p16 — L2 READ

## v.151
Rudra-power-penetration: that field — contemplation supreme: otherwise of it, of reality — what worship, and which is pleased?
The Sanskrit: *rudraśaktisamāveśas tat kṣetram bhāvanā parā | anyathā tasya tattvasya kā pūjā kāś ca tṛpyati*

## v.152
Free-bliss-consciousness-mere-essence — one's own self everywhere: entering in that form — one's own self's bath, declared.
The Sanskrit: *svatantrānandacinmātrasāraḥ svātmā hi sarvataḥ | āveśanaṃ tatsvarūpe svātmanaḥ snānam īritam*

## v.153
By which indeed is one worshipped, with materials pleased — or higher-lower: who indeed the worshipper — all he, the very one: where the worship?
The Sanskrit: *yair eva pūjyate dravyais tarpyate vā parāparaḥ | yaś caiva pūjakaḥ sarvaḥ sa evaikaḥ kva pūjanam*

## v.154
Breath may go, life may enter — by will, crooked-formed: long-formed she, the great goddess — other field, higher-lower.
The Sanskrit: *vrajet prāṇo viśej jīva icchayā kuṭilākṛtiḥ | dīrghātmā sā mahādevī parakṣetram parāparā*

## v.155
In this walking along, standing in the great-bliss-made path: by her, by the goddess entered — supreme Bhairava may attain.
The Sanskrit: *asyām anucaran tiṣṭhan mahānandamaye 'dhvare | tayā devyā samāviṣṭaḥ param bhairavam āpnuyāt*

## v.156
Six hundreds by day, by night a thousand twenty-one: the goddess's muting declared — easy, hard for the dull.
The Sanskrit: *ṣaṭśatāni divā rātrau sahasrāṇyekaviṃśatiḥ | japo devyāḥ samuddiṣṭaḥ sulabho durlabho jaḍaiḥ*

## v.157
Thus this told, O Goddess — supreme nectar, the highest: this and never to be shown of anyone, but ever.
The Sanskrit: *ity etat kathitaṃ devi paramāmṛtam uttamam | etac ca naiva kasyāpi prakāśyaṃ tu kadācana*

## v.158
To another pupil, to the base, to the cruel, the non-devoted — of the guru's feet: but of thought-free-minded, of heroes, of lofty-souled.
The Sanskrit: *paraśiṣye khale krūre abhakte gurupādayoḥ | nirvikalpamatīnāṃ tu vīrāṇām unnatātmanām*

## v.159
To the devoted, of the guru's company — to be given without hesitation: village, kingdom, town, country, son, wife, family.
The Sanskrit: *bhaktānāṃ guruvargasya dātavyaṃ nirviśaṅkayā | grāmo rājyam puraṃ deśaḥ putradārakuṭumbakam*

## v.160
All this having left — this to be grasped, O deer-eyed one: what with these unsteady, O Goddess? This steady, supreme wealth; lives even to be given — the supreme nectar not to be given.
The Sanskrit: *sarvam etat parityajya grāhyam etan mṛgekṣaṇe | kim ebhir asthirair devi sthiram param idaṃ dhanam | prāṇā api pradātavyā na deyaṃ paramāmṛtam*

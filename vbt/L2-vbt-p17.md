# VBT vbt-p17 — L2 READ

## v.161
God of gods, great god — fully content am I, Śaṅkara: the essence of the Rudrayāmala-tantra, today held.
The Sanskrit: *devadeva māhadeva paritṛptāsmi śaṅkara | rudrayāmalatantrasya sāram adyāvadhāritam*

## v.162
Of all the power-divisions, the heart known today, and: having spoken thus, delighted, the goddess on the neck — clung to Śiva's.
The Sanskrit: *sarvaśaktiprabhedānāṃ hṛdayaṃ jñātam adya ca | ity uktvānanditā devi ka.ṅthe lagnā śivasya tu*

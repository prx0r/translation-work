# C1 — vbt-p01

## SUMMARY

She has heard all, Rudrayāmala-born, Trika by essence — and doubts anyway: what form is it truly? Nine-self, three-head, power-triad, nāda-drop, wheel: each offering collapses — higher-ness is partlessness, not letter or body. Grace cuts the doubt, for what she asked is the tantra's essence; the most hidden will be told whole, known only as unsubstantial — net, dream, mirage-city — and described merely for fancy-struck minds.

## FUNCTION

Opens the dialogue as a genuine doubt-question whose answers dissolve their own objects.

## KEY TERMS

rudrayāmala (arisen from the union — Bhairavī speaks); trika-bheda (Trika corpus whole); sārāt sāravibhāgaśaḥ (essence by essence — ever more distilled); svasaṃvedana (self-feeling — planted for p02); saṃśaya (doubt as essence-engine).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The doubt (v2) opens a question the denials (p02) and the telling (v8-10) answer; no rite yet — pure interrogation.

## EXPLANATION

The frame dialogue: a goddess who already possesses the teaching still asks HOW it appears, and every offered form (nine-self division, three-head split, nāda-drop, moon-crescent, wheel-unsmeared, power-formed) is negated into higher-ness as pure partlessness — letter and body both fail to divide it. Her plea-praise (grace, cut the doubt; the asked is the essence) turns the doubt itself into the tantra's heart, and Bhairava answers by promising the hidden form while defanging it: unsubstantial as net, dream, mirage-city, and fit only for meditation, not pomp.

## BOUNDARY

No practice is given here; the forms offered are questions, not techniques — the dhāraṇās belong to later sections.

## ESSENCE

1. Heard: all, Rudrayāmala-born, Trika division by essence.
2. Even now doubt not dispelled: what form in truth?
3. Nine-self, Bhairava-form, three-head, power-triad: which?
4. Nāda-drop, moon-crescent, wheel-unsmeared, power-formed: what?
5. Higher-lower whole: if higher thus, higher-ness contradicts.
6. Not by letter, not by body: higher-ness is partlessness.
7. Grace cuts the doubt: what was asked is the tantra's essence.
8. Most hidden, yet told: the whole form of Bhairava.
9. Known by unsubstantialness: Indra-net, māyā-dream, Gandharva-city.
10. Described for meditation's sake, for fancy-struck men only.

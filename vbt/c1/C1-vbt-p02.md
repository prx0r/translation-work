# C1 — vbt-p02

## SUMMARY

Every form denied: not nine-self, not word-heap, not three-headed, not nāda-drop, not wheel — free of direction, time and counting, untellable. Her terrors are a mother's sweet for going on. Then the positive: inner self-taste joy, full-formed Bhairavī; stainless world-filling body whose worship becomes the question; sung as supreme goddess, power-and-powerful undivided yielding supreme power; fire's burning only as entry in knowing-being; and the entered state — undivided contemplation, Śiva-formed, mouth called Śaivī.

## FUNCTION

Completes the object-question by exhausting forms, then names the form that survives denial.

## KEY TERMS

āveśa (possession-sickness — the negated danger); anuttara carried as unpointability; saṃvid (awareness as the only locus); Śaivī (the mouth — speech itself initiated); kṛtrima (made — the contrast with akṛtra elsewhere).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The mouth named here (Śaivī) makes the coming dialogue itself the practice's vehicle — words as doors.

## EXPLANATION

The negation-verse pair works by exhaustion: self-division, word-heap, three-head, nāda-drop, moon-crescent, wheel-course — all pictures, none the thing. The terrors of unawakened readers are confessed as pedagogy (mother's sweet lures the child onward), and the unpointability is stated outright: free of direction, time and count. What survives is not another picture but a state — inner self-taste joy, Bhairavī full-formed — and a mouth: stainless world-filling body raising the worship-question, power-and-powerful as non-difference, fire's heat beginning only in knowing-being, and finally undivided contemplation entering the Śiva-form whose speech is Śaivī.

## BOUNDARY

Still no technique; the first breath-practice stands at the next section's threshold.

## RELATED

Hareesh upāya note (vv24+ = practices begin); the mouth (mukha) of v20 reappears as the request's door in the next song.

## ESSENCE

1. Not nine-self, not word-heap, not three-headed god.
2. Not nāda-drop, not moon-blockers, not wheel-joined.
3. Terrors of unawakened minds: mother's sweet for going on.
4. Free of direction, time, counting; unpointable, untellable.
5. Inner self-taste joy: Bhairavī's full-formed state.
6. Stainless world-filling body: who worships, which pleased?
7. Sung state: she, supreme goddess in supreme form.
8. Power-powerful non-difference: supreme power of supreme self.
9. Fire's burning not separate: entry begins it in knowing-being.
10. Entered power-state, undivided contemplation: Śiva-formed, Śaivī named.

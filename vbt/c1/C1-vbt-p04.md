# C1 — vbt-p04

## SUMMARY

Esoteric block: mind to crown by quick filling, cross the brow-toss bridge thought-free; aids of feathers, forms, circles give entry in unsuppressed void wherever thought rests; skull-seat for marking the highest from firmness; the lotus-fiber channel shining god through meditated sky; hand-blocked gaze and brow-split finding supreme standing in the drop; the fire-tilak dissolving at heart's end; sounds — unstruck, vessel-ear, broken, river-rushing — bathing into word-brahman; prolonged oṃ pouring toward voidness; and a letter's two ends read as void making void-formed.

## FUNCTION

Carries the āṇava-level piercings to their void conclusions — sound and gaze as doors.

## KEY TERMS

bhrūkṣepa-setu (brow-toss bridge); tāluka/capala (skull-seat context); dṛg-bādha (hand-blocked gaze); brahmāṇḍa/brahma (word-brahman bathed); praṇava (oṃ as prolonged utterance).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Sound-practice here expands into the music-instrument and letter-practices ahead; void-method connects to p05.

## EXPLANATION

The verse-cluster is a sequence of thresholds: anatomical (crown, brow, skull), objectual (feathers, forms, walls, vessels), sensorial (hand-blocked gaze, drop, tilak), and finally sonic — unstruck, vessel-ear, broken sound, river-rushing all resolving into word-brahman, then into the void that prolonged oṃ pours one into. The letter practice (v40) distills the method to eight syllables: beginning and end contemplated, void through and through — the formless doctrine stated as technique.

## ESSENCE

1. Filling to crown, brow-toss bridge: thought-free mind goes forth.
2. Feathers, forms, circles: in unsurpassed void, entry in heart.
3. Wherever thought: void, wall, supreme, vessel — self dissolved boons.
4. Mind in skull: from firmness the highest mark is marked.
5. Lotus-fiber middle channel: god shines through meditated sky.
6. Hand-blocked gaze, brow-split: drop's middle holds supreme standing.
7. Fire-tilak drop at heart's dissolution-end: meditating dissolving.
8. Unstruck, vessel-ear, broken sound: word-brahman bathed, brahman attained.
9. Prolonged oṃ ends in void-contemplation: to voidness she goes.
10. A letter's two ends as void: void-formed man becomes.

# C1 — vbt-p05

## SUMMARY

The void-block: ordered sound seating sky-body at its limit; body-mantra ending in moon-drop void-utterance; own body taken as all directions; back and root voids yielding void-mind, heart-void added for thoughtless-rising; a moment's voidness making one thoughtless in nature; sky-pervasion steadying contemplation; skin read as wall until even the meditated drops; the lotus-casket mid-point winning fortune; and mind's twelve-end dissolving firming the reality-mark.

## FUNCTION

Multiplies void-contemplation into a full map of locations — sound, body, wall, lotus, twelve-end.

## KEY TERMS

śūnya (void as active, not negation); padma-sampuṭa (lotus-casket); dvādaśānta (twelve-end); nirvikalpa (thoughtless); dravya (body-stuff).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Purifies the field the fire-practices (p06) then pass through; twelve-end reappears at p10.

## EXPLANATION

A cartography of emptiness: the same void seen from ten angles — acoustics (long tone at the perceptual limit), mantra anatomy (gross letters ending in moon-drop), proprioception (all directions as one body), anatomy (back, root, heart), duration (a single moment suffices), wall-illusion (skin dividing inner from nothing), the casket mid-point, and the twelve-end where mind's dissolving writes the reality-mark firm. Each practice both negates a location and claims a steadiness — nothing is left to stand on, and the standing improves.

## BOUNDARY

The unmeditated-sharer's status is stated, not explained — no metaphysics added.

## RELATED

Hareesh central-channel block (vv28-30) precedes; Kṣemarāja on śūnya-methods; same void-word as the oṃ-practice at p04's end.

## ESSENCE

1. Long tone, undivided mind: supreme-sky body at limit.
2. Body-mantra's gross course ends moon-drop: void-uttering, Śiva.
3. Own body as all directions: undirected mind, sky proceeds.
4. Back, root voids contemplated: body-disregard, void-minded.
5. Back, root, heart voids steady: thoughtless-rising from thoughtlessness.
6. Mere moment's voidness: sharing thoughtless nature.
7. Body-stuff gone, sky-pervaded: contemplation grows steady.
8. Skin as wall, nothing inside: the unmeditated-sharer.
9. Lotus-casket mid: undivided, supreme fortune won.
10. Twelve-end mind-dissolving: the reality-mark firms.

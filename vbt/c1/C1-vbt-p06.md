# C1 — vbt-p06

## SUMMARY

The burning-and-void block: the mind cast at twelve-end until strangeness wears away in days; time-fire consuming one's own city into calm-shine; fancy's fire leaving only man-ness; minutest realities in own body manifesting the supreme; the weak power entering heart winning freedom; the ordered world-course dissolving mind at its end; Śiva-reality meditated at world-ends raising the great rise; and three vessel-gazes — pot, mountain wall — where dissolution makes made-of-that and births the wasting of turning.

## FUNCTION

Turns contemplation into combustion: fire, burning, waste — always ending in what remains after.

## KEY TERMS

kālāgni (time-fire); bahirmukha-bhāva (fancy as what burns); svasattā-minutiae (minute realities); tattva-lakṣya (reality-mark); laya (merging as vessel).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Fire and wall-methods feed the wave-practices (p07); the vessel returns in v59 after the interim bliss-block.

## EXPLANATION

This cluster converts void into event: time rises from its place and burns the city of self; fancy itself is the fuel whose ash is man-ness; even the minutiæ of reality in one's body become the field where the supreme shows; the weak power, brought to heart at twelve-range, purchases freedom; the ordered world-course runs itself out into mind's dissolution; and at the world's ends, Śiva-reality meditated raises the great rise. The vessel-practices return: gaze in pot or mountain-wall, and once the dividing wall of skin is passed, made-of-that.

## BOUNDARY

The burning is internal contemplation, never ritual fire — the verses mark it as fancy's end, not pyre.

## RELATED

Fire-practices = classical āṇava-upāya per Hareesh; vessel/wall imagery shares p05's void-map; time-fire echoes Parātriṃśikā's kālāgni usage (distinct work).

## ESSENCE

1. Mind cast at twelve-end: strangeness drops away in days.
2. Time-fire burns own city: calm-shining at the end.
3. Fancy-burned world leaves only man-ness in the non-other mind.
4. Minutest realities in own body: the supreme is manifested.
5. Weak power at twelve-range, heart-entered: freedom won.
6. Ordered world-course run: mind dissolves at its end.
7. Śiva-reality meditated at world's ends: the great rise.
8. Universe void-become: mind merged, vessel of merging.
9. Vessel gaze, wall left: at dissolution, made-of-that.
10. Mountain-wall gaze: mind dissolved, turning's wasting born.

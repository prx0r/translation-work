# C1 — vbt-p10

## SUMMARY

The letter-to-identity block: emission-ending awareness touching eternal brahman; sky-formed self uncovered by directions showing own form; a limb pierced and yoked to spotless going; the denial that anything inner-making is inside me, letting fancies fall by their own absence; māyä named as delusion counting realities that never stand apart; sudden desire led to calm where it arose; desire unarisen revealing He-I, dissolved, that-minded; mind set undivided in either desire or knowing seeing reality's meaning; knowledge turning causeless, no one's, Śiva; and no bodily distinction anywhere — the contemplating one conquers the world.

## FUNCTION

Subtracts every inner stake until the identity statement stands with nothing behind it.

## KEY TERMS

jñāna (knowing as causeless); antaḥ-karaṇa-denial (no inner-making); māyā (delusion as counting); He-I (tadāsmi / so'ham as subtraction-result); sarva-deha (bodies as distinction-less).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Identity-claims prepare the passions-neutral block (p11); 'world-conqueror' prefigures the fruit-talk later.

## EXPLANATION

Vowel-precision (emission-ending awareness) makes contact eternal; then sky and directions; then the somatic puncture (a limb pierced with needle, awareness yoked) yielding spotless going. What follows is pure apophasis: the claim that nothing inner-making is inside — so fancies fall not by attack but by absence; māyā is only the counting that cannot keep realities apart; desire must be led (not killed) to dissolve where it arose, and unrisen desire reveals the He-I. In either desire or knowing, undivided mind sees meaning; knowledge without ground, without owner, IS becoming Śiva — and the bodies, distinction-less in all, are what the contemplating person conquers worlds with.

## BOUNDARY

No claim that one cannot act: distinction-less bodies still conquer worlds — difference denied, function intact.

## RELATED

So'ham dialectic (cf. v97 '=B9haṃ' byte); māyā as kalana (counting) matches Parātriṃśikā's counting-verse — cross-reference as parallel, not source.

## ESSENCE

1. Emission-ending awareness touches eternal brahman.
2. Sky-formed self, directions uncovered: own form shows.
3. Pierced limb, awareness yoked: spotless going in Bhairava.
4. Inner-making not in me: fancies fall by absence.
5. Māyā deluding: counted realities never stand apart.
6. Seen desire led to calm: dissolves where it arose.
7. Desire unarisen: truly He-I, dissolved, that-minded.
8. Either desire or knowing: undivided mind sees meaning.
9. Causeless knowing, anyone's nothing: thus becoming Śiva.
10. No bodily distinction: contemplating all, world-conqueror.

# C1 — vbt-p11

## SUMMARY

The neutrality-block: within the whole passion-range reality stays uncaused; the world seen as net or picture-whirl makes seeing-all itself joy; neither pain nor joy receives the cast mind — the middle remains knowable; everywhere-I-am-thus with single gaze makes happy; pot and skin-wall contemplations turning all-pervading into all-going; the yogin's distinction as attentive relation to common awareness; self perceived in other's bodies dissolving own-body-regard; supportless mind cancelling thought until Bhairava stands in self; and the lord lit by all knowing, being, pervading — 'he indeed I' from firmness — with all world-plays nothing but waves and flames.

## FUNCTION

Neutralizes preference and self-stake — from passions to relations to identity to display.

## KEY TERMS

ṣaḍ-viṣaya (six-passion range); samāna-saṃvid (common awareness); svātmaniṣṭhā (yogin's distinction as care); bhairavatva-as-avatāra (world-plays as manifestations).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Identity (v109) is the pivot between the āṇava cluster and the fruit-sealing verses to come.

## EXPLANATION

The song works by placement rather than renunciation: the six passions are not fought but ranged, the world held as spectacle so that seeing-all is joy, and even pain and joy refused their usual seats so the middle shows. Self-contemplation expands from body to everywhere (pot and skin-walls dissolving), relation becomes the yogin's only distinction — care rather than attainment — other-bodies receive one's own self, and supportless mind cancels volition until 'he indeed I' is a statement made from firmness rather than belief. Wave-and-flame close the list: nothing outside is real, everything only manifest.

## BOUNDARY

Carefulness (sāvadhānatā) named but not described — one word, left open.

## RELATED

Hareesh's intensity/access theme as inverse here (this is equanimity-mode); cf. sutasutra citta as mantra — mind-language shared across the canon.

## ESSENCE

1. Passion-range uncaused: reality remains there.
2. World as net or picture-whirl: seeing all, joy upsurges.
3. Neither pain nor joy cast: the middle is knowable.
4. Everywhere-I-am-thus: gaze seeing none else, happy.
5. Pot-wall contemplation: all-pervading, all-going born.
6. Grasped-grasping common: yogin's care in relation.
7. Self in other's body: own-body-regard dissolved, pervasive.
8. Supportless mind: thoughts not thought, Bhairava in self.
9. All-knowing, all-doing: he indeed I — Śiva from firmness.
10. World-plays as waves and flames: Bhairava's manifest only.

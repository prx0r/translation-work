# C1 — vbt-p12

## SUMMARY

From whirling to waveless: body's whirling ceasing agitation so the supreme state is born; any means — power, knowing, mind-dissolving — arriving at Bhairava-form; freedom declared born at the instant eyes lock unwinkingly; ear-contraction and lower gate entering eternal brahman; standing above a pit dissolving mind plainly; no exit from pervasiveness — Śiva-state wherever mind goes; every eye-road manifesting consciousness of the pervader until cit-dissolving fills itself; even hunger, fear and battle-ends states where reality fills; the mind left in the seen thing letting unsupported lord spread; and slow gaze-withdrawal abiding knowing in void.

## FUNCTION

Contains the freedom-at-a-glance verse and completes the leaving-the-mind practice set.

## KEY TERMS

stabdhā-nayana (unwinkering fixity as freedom-site); saṃkarṇa (ear-contraction); vyāpakatva (pervasiveness as no-exit); dṛṣṭi-nivṛtti (gaze-withdrawal); mūrti-tyāga (mind left in the seen).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

v113's at-once freedom answers the frame's how-question; devotion-practices follow (p13).

## EXPLANATION

Motion first: whirling stops and the supreme state is born from the stopping — any of three means arriving at Bhairava-form. Then the text's sharpest promise: eyes fixed unwinkingly, freedom born at once. The somatics of entering follow — ears contracted, lower gate opened, meditating enters eternal brahman; above a pit, undivided mind plainly dissolves; the pervasiveness argument closes exit: Śiva-state is wherever mind goes because nowhere is outside it. Eye-road after eye-road manifests the pervader's consciousness until cit dissolves into full-selfness; the passion-endings become reality-filling states; and the practice series ends in leaving mind in the seen thing and withdrawing the gaze slowly until knowing abides in void.

## BOUNDARY

Freedom at a glance is stated as born, not taught — no technique for the lock is supplied here.

## RELATED

Hareesh: vv24-27 visarga-foundation; this block completes what p03 opened (breath → lock → leave).

## ESSENCE

1. Whirling body ceasing agitation: supreme state born.
2. Whatever means — power, knowing, mind-dissolve: Bhairava-form.
3. Eyes unwinkering fixed: freedom born at once.
4. Ear-contraction, lower gate: meditating enters eternal brahman.
5. Above pit, undivided mind: dissolving plainly.
6. Mind nowhere exitable: pervasiveness, Śiva-state wherever.
7. Eye-road manifests consciousness: cit-dissolving, full-selfness.
8. Even passions' ends are states where reality fills.
9. Mind left in seen thing: unsupported lord spreads.
10. Slow gaze-withdrawal: knowing abiding in void.

# C1 — vbt-p15

## SUMMARY

The redefinition-block: agelessness attained as all-gathering yoginīs' lord; freed-yet-living body unsmeared; rhetorical dismantling of outer worship — who, what, how of oblation answered by the inner: japa is self-sound in supreme state, meditation is motionless intellect, worship is firm mind dissolving in sky; day-by-day one-yoking ripens full-formed state; the great void-fire receives the awareness-ladeled oblation; and sacrifice itself becomes contentment, wasting sins and protecting all as effect, not rite.

## FUNCTION

Redefines japa, dhyāna, pūjā, homa as inner postures — ritual vocabulary relocated into stillness.

## KEY TERMS

sāmānya-japa (self-sound as japa); manonāśa (fourfold wasting); bharitākāratā (full-formed state); cetanā-sruca (awareness-ladle); tṛpti-as-yāga (contentment as sacrifice).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The who-is-worshipped riddles (v143-144) pave the worship-field verses (p16).

## EXPLANATION

A catalogue of renamings: bondage and freedom both drop out (v135's reflection-model), then each ritual noun is reissued at inner value — muting becomes self-sound in supreme state, meditation is motionless formless intellect (explicitly NOT bodily imagining), worship is firmness dissolving in sky, oblation is made with the awareness-ladle in void-fire, and the sacrifice is contentment itself, whose by-products are sin-waste and universal protection. Practice day by day in one yoking condenses the whole into full-formed contentment.

## BOUNDARY

Outer procedures are called gross, not forbidden — 'here procedure outer' locates rather than bans (kept argmap-accurate).

## RELATED

cf. VBT's own frame (inner vs outer procedures named in v144); contentment-sacrifice echoes Parātriṃśikā p04 p023-style self-offering (parallel, not source).

## ESSENCE

1. Ageless he attains: all-gathering lord of yoginīs.
2. Freed yet living, unsmeared: supreme great-lord body.
3. Who mutes, meditates, worships, pleased? Rhetorical.
4. Outer oblations: procedures in gross things only.
5. Real japa: self-sound mantra-natured in supreme state.
6. Meditation unmoving, formless — not bodily imagining.
7. Worship: firm mind dissolving in undifferentiated sky.
8. One-yoking day by day: full-formed state, utter fullness.
9. Void-fire oblation with awareness-ladle.
10. Sacrifice is contentment: wasting sins, protecting all.

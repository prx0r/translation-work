# C1 — vbt-p16

## SUMMARY

The worship-and-secrecy block: without penetration into that field, no worship pleases anything; own-self entering own form IS the bath; worshipper and worshipped reduce to one — where the second; breath, life and will crooked-formed under goddess' other field; the bliss-path walked entered-by-her giving Bhairava; the japa count (six-six-thousand a day, thousand twenty-one by night) hard for the dull; then the hush — supreme nectar, told, never to be shown to anyone; withheld from the base and cruel, given to thought-free heroes; everything else — village, kingdom, family — given to devotees without hesitation; and the last reversal: unsteady things dropped, the un-given nectar is the steady supreme wealth.

## FUNCTION

Closes the teaching with the text's strongest boundary: the told nectar that cannot be shown, and what may be given instead.

## KEY TERMS

snāna (own-form as bath); mukti as havi (already-fruit as offering); japa-saṅkhyā (66000/12000 counts); prakāśya (showable — the prohibited); dātavya (given — the permitted).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

The count-verse and secrecy prepare Devī's satisfaction and the union-seal (p17).

## EXPLANATION

Worship is relativized twice: penetration is what makes it real, and the worshipper-worshipped pair collapses to one, so ritual question loses its second term. The practice then continues anyway — will-breath entering the goddess' other field, bliss-path attainment, and the famous japa count given as difficulty for the dull rather than achievement for the virtuous. Then the hinge: this told nectar is never showable — the only verses of refusal in the text — with the equally famous inversion that everything worldly (village, kingdom, family) may be given freely to the devoted, while the nectar itself is never given, because it is the unsteady all dropped for the steady wealth.

## BOUNDARY

v157 is THE boundary verse — 'never shown to anyone, ever'; all downstream dissemination must respect it (our own quotes stay minimal by house rules anyway).

## ESSENCE

1. Without penetration-field, what worship pleases?
2. Own-self everywhere: entering that form is one's bath.
3. Worshipper and worshipped collapse: where the second?
4. Breath, life, will: crooked-formed, she the other field.
5. Bliss-path walking: entered by her, Bhairava attained.
6. Six-six-thousand a day, thousand twenty-one by night: hard for dull.
7. Supreme nectar told — never to be shown to anyone.
8. Withheld from base, cruel, non-devoted; to heroes given.
9. Everything given to devotees without hesitation.
10. Unsteady all dropped: the un-given nectar is steady wealth.

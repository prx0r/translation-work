# C1 — vbt-p17

## SUMMARY

Devī declares full contentment: the essence of the Rudrayāmala-tantra held today. And the heart of all power-divisions is known today as well: having spoken, delighted, the goddess clasps Śiva's neck — the text ends where the dialogue began, in union, with nothing further said.

## FUNCTION

Seals the dialogue: contentment declared, union enacted in the last image, the count closed.

## KEY TERMS

tṛpti (contentment as completion); hṛdaya (heart as what is known); kaṇṭha-lagna (neck-clasping — the union image); rudrayāmala (the named tantra frame).

## LOCAL CONTEXT

Pays off the frame: the doubt-vines of p01 end in embrace; the secrecy of p16 ends in what IS given — herself.

## EXPLANATION

The two closing acts: first the verbal seal (content, essence held — the teaching has an owner now), then the somatic seal (heart known, goddess delighted, mouth on neck). Everything the 160 verses asked dissolves into a body-position: question and answer, nectar and secrecy, ends clasped. The tantra names itself (Rudrayāmala) in the same breath as its knowledge is declared complete — held, and in the holding, done.

## BOUNDARY

No verse follows; the colophon is the boundary itself.

## RELATED

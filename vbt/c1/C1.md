# C1 — Vijñānabhairava

## vbt-p01–p03 — question and first dhāraṇās
**SUMMARY.** Vijñānabhairava opens: I have heard all, Lord, arising from Rudrayāmala. Trika division, completely, essence from essence. Even now, doubt not removed, supreme Lord. What form, in truth, Lord, made of word-group and kalā. Or by nine-ātman division, in Bhairava, in Bhairava-form. Or divided by three-head division, or made of three śaktis. Or made of nāda-bindu, or half-moon obstructions. Or wheel-mounted, anacka, or made of three śaktis. Dhāraṇās 1-20 (various practices) follow.

**FUNCTION.** Devī's question about Bhairava's true form; first 20 dhāraṇās.

**KEY TERMS.** rudrayāmala (Rudrayāmala), trika (Trika), śabdarāśi (word-group), kalā (kalā), navātman (nine-ātman), triśira (three-head), śaktitraya (three śaktis), nāda (nāda), bindu (bindu), candrārdha (half-moon), cakrārūḍha (wheel-mounted), anacka (anacka), dhāraṇā (practice).

**LOCAL CONTEXT.** First three passages; sets the doctrinal frame.

**EXPLANATION.** The passage moves in two steps. (1) Question: I have heard all, Lord, arising from Rudrayāmala; Trika division, completely, essence from essence; even now, doubt not removed, supreme Lord; what form, in truth, Lord, made of word-group and kalā; or by nine-ātman division; or divided by three-head division; or made of nāda-bindu; or wheel-mounted, anacka. (2) Dhāraṇās 1-20: various practices follow.

**BOUNDARY / OPEN.** None major. Verses are dense; L2 groups them.

**RELATED.** A01 (svabhāva), A11 (śaktipāta), spans TS1.1–1.30.

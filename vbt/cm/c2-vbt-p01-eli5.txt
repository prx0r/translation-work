Someone heard the whole teaching, all of it, and still asks: what does the highest really look like? Even now the doubt sits there, undisplaced. [E0] [E1]

Is he nine-formed? Three-headed? A triple of powers? Is he made of sound-drops and moon-bits and wheels? The questions pile up, one on each mark. [E2] [E3]

But if the higher has something higher above it, that splits it — and the highest cannot have parts. So no letter can spell him and no body can hold him. [E4] [E5]

Then grace cuts the knot: what you asked is the teaching itself. Most hidden, yet told straight out — here is Bhairava's whole form, nothing held back. [E6] [E7]

Know him by how thin things are: the world is the eye-god's net, a dream, a city in the clouds. This picture is drawn for one reason only — so restless minds have somewhere to sit and look. [E8] [E9]

A lamp shows a room the way its rays show the lamp and the sun shows the day — by power you know Śiva the same way. He carries a trident and wears a skull, yet he has no direction, no place, no time, nothing you can point to and name. [E0] [E1]

So the goddess begs the real question: what is his full-formed state, and which door leads in? Tell it true. It starts with breath — breath above, life below, an outpouring — held full at the two places where breathings begin. [E2] [E3]

Wind inside and wind outside stop turning their sky-pair, and by the goddess his form shows. The blossomed power neither goes out nor comes in — thoughtless in the middle, and that is his form. Whatever breath you stretch — breathed out, breathed in, or held — its end is calm, and calm shines by power. [E4] [E5] [E6]

Watch her from the root's shining up to the subtler-than-subtle, held at the twelve-end: she calms, and Bhairava rises. She climbs like lightning, wheel by wheel, three fists up — then past the end, the great rising. [E7] [E8]

Run the twelve-step course, twelve letters split out, through gross and subtle and highest — and at the end, set free: Śiva. [E9]

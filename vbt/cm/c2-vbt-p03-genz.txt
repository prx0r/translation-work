Lamp shows the room the way rays show the lamp and sun shows the day — power is how you clock Śiva. Trident in hand, skull on deck, and still: no location, no slot, no timestamp, no label that sticks. [E0] [E1]

So the goddess asks the only question: what is his full form and which door opens it. Say it straight. It starts with breath — breath up top, life down low, the pour-out kind — parked full at the two spots where breaths are born. [E2] [E3]

Inside wind and outside wind quit turning their sky-pair and she shows his form. The power in bloom neither leaves nor enters — blank in the middle, and that blank is his form. Any breath-phase you stretch to its end lands in calm, and calm shines on power. [E4] [E5] [E6]

Track her from the root-glow up to the subtle-of-subtle, pinned at the twelve-end: she settles, Bhairava rises. Lightning climb, wheel to wheel, three fists up — then past it all, the big rising. [E7] [E8]

Twelve steps, twelve letters, gross to subtle to supreme — run it clean and walk out free: Śiva. [E9]

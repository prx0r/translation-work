Fill up fast to the crown-top, snap the brow-toss bridge — mind gone thought-free, and everything pours out past it. Or picture peacock feathers, bright shapes, rings, fivefold open space — and step through the highest open straight into the heart. [E0] [E1]

Wherever a thought lands — open, wall, highest, vessel — the self melts there and gives gifts. Or set the mind inside the skull, eyes half-shut, and let its firmness mark the highest mark. [E2] [E3]

Feel the middle channel, thin as a lotus-fiber, standing midmost — through the sky inside, God shines. Block the eyes with the hands, split the brow, hold the drop in the middle — supreme standing. Then the fire-fleck on the forehead, the drop at the heart's far end: sit with it, melting. [E4] [E5] [E6]

Hear the unstruck hum, the vessel-ear, the broken roar like a river — bathe in word-brahman and reach the highest. Draw out the holy hum to its end and rest in the open — by the open, by the highest power, she walks into openness. [E7] [E8]

Take any letter-sound and hold both its ends — its start and its finish — as open. Made of open by the open, the man himself turns open-formed. [E9]

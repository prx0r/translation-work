Sit inside one long ringing tone, mind in one piece — and at the far end of it your body turns sky-body. Or run the body-prayer through its rough course down to the moon-drop sound — then speak from the open, and be Śiva. [E0] [E1]

Feel your own body as all directions at once, like sky — mind with no direction, and sky pours through it. Or picture the open behind you and the open at your root together — drop the body out of mind, and turn open-minded. [E2] [E3]

Hold the back-open, root-open, and heart-open steady all at once — out of thoughtlessness the thoughtless rising comes. Even one blink of openness is enough: thought-free, thought-free, sharing the thought-free way of being. [E4] [E5]

Let the body-stuff melt till only sky is left filling you — then your seeing grows steady. Think through the skin like a wall and find nothing inside — you become one who shares in the unthought. Rest in the heart's lotus-box, undivided — and the greatest good fortune is won. [E6] [E7] [E8]

From all around your body, at the twelve-end, let mind melt — hold firm, and the mark of the real grows firm. [E9]

Throw the mind to the twelve-end again and again — day by day its strangeness falls away. Or let the fire of time rise from its own place and burn your city down — at the end, calm shines where it stood. [E0] [E1]

Burn the whole world of fancy till only man-ness is left standing in the mind-with-no-other. Or look for the tiniest real things inside your own body and the world — held to the end, the highest shows itself. [E2] [E3]

Even a thin weak power works: hold it at the twelve-range, walk it into the heart — and freedom is won. Or walk the whole world-course in order, gross and subtle and highest, till at its end the mind melts. [E4] [E5]

At every edge of everything, sit with Śiva-as-real — by the road-rules, the great rising. Or think this whole universe gone open: mind melts straight into it, and turns into the bowl that holds the melting. [E6] [E7]

Rest your eyes on a pot and leave the wall out of it — when it melts, you turn made-of-that. Rest them on a treeless mountain wall — mind melts into the state, and the end of circling is born. [E8] [E9]

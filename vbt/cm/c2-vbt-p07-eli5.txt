Know two states well, then rest in the middle between them — leave both behind at once, and the real shines there. Drop every state you were holding, fence the mind in till it goes nowhere else — and in that middle, your seeing grows wide, wider than wide. [E0] [E1]

Picture the whole body as made of knowing, or the whole world as fancy, held whole — mind in one piece, and the highest rising. Or feel the two winds crash at their edge, inside or out — the even-minded knower rises, himself the bowl. [E2] [E3]

Fill the world or your own body with your own bliss and remember it — all at once, drink your own nectar, full of highest bliss. With a trick, with a true try, great bliss shows up on the spot — and through it the real shines. Tie all the channels shut and let the up-breath power crawl slow as an ant — happiness spreads everywhere. [E4] [E5] [E6]

Throw a happiness-made mind between fire and poison — filled with mere wind, or yoked by bliss-remembering. Whatever joy belongs to brahman-as-real is self-told — only that can be told. Licking, churning, pounding to their peak, woman-bliss from fullness, from memory — even with no power in it, a flood of bliss pours out. [E7] [E8] [E9]

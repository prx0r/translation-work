When joy rises — won, or seen, or remembered from long ago — sit with it till you melt into it: be of that mind. When eating and drinking swell into bright joy, picture the filled state — and great bliss follows. [E0] [E1]

Taste anything sweet like a song, happiness no one else shares — melt into one selfness, sunk in, and that is selfness. Wherever the mind feels full, hold it right there — there, there, your own form as highest bliss pours forth. [E2] [E3]

At the edge where sleep has not yet come, where the outer world drops off — send the mind into that state: the highest goddess shines. Stare into the sun, a lamp-flame, the spotted sky — lock the gaze and your own self-form shines. Stare at bones, at anger, at licking, at sky-farers — at gaze-time, the highest finding shines. [E4] [E5] [E6]

Sit soft on one buttock, hands and feet hanging free — from that very seat, the supreme full mind. Sit straight, both arms half-bent, mind the armpit-sky — calm comes as you melt. Fix the gaze on a gross-formed thing and drop it down fast — mind unsupported: walk to Śiva. [E7] [E8] [E9]

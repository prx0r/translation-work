Rest knowing on the middle of the tongue, mouth open — shape the breath-letter H with the mind alone, and melt into calm. Picture your own body with no seat under it — mind spent on the vision, and in a flash the dirt in you wastes away. [E0] [E1]

Rock on a moving seat, or stand rock-still while the body sways — mind gone calm: goddess, the god-flood is won. Stare at the stainless sky without one break in the gaze — stunned to the bone, that very moment the Bhairava-form is won. [E2] [E3]

Melt the crown till sky is all, all as Bhairava — that all: the blaze of his form-reality walks in. Know something in two lights, then in darkness again — his universe-form, the one that wears endless light, stands known. [E4] [E5]

On a bad night, in the dark half of the month, sit long in it — stare into the murk, and his form will come. Shut the eyes first with blackness before them — spread his form there, and turn made-of-that. [E6] [E7]

Strike any sense or block it — step into the not-two open right there: the self shines in that very place. No drop, no outpour — one lone A-sound rises sudden: goddess, a flood of knowing, the highest Lord. [E8] [E9]

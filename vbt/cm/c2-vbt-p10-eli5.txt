Take the letter with its out-breath and end in knowing — with a supportless mind, touch the forever-real. Picture your own self as sky-formed, directions uncovered all around — by supportless knowing-power, your own form shows itself then. [E0] [E1]

Pierce one limb first — a needle-point — and yoke your knowing right there: the going is spotless, into Bhairava. Say it flat: the in-me-making is not in me — fancies have nowhere to stand, so they drop off. [E2] [E3]

Her trick is counting: she numbers the real things and calls that delusion — yet counted things never stand apart. Watch a sudden want rise, then walk it to calm — where it rose, right there it melts. [E4] [E5]

When no want of mine has risen — knowing or not, why not — then I am: in truth He-I, melted, of that mind. Set the mind in a risen want or a risen knowing — self-mind undivided: then the meaning of the real is seen. [E6] [E7]

Knowing can stand with no cause, propless, wrong-shaped — and it is no one's, not-this: so turn Śiva, dear one. Knowing-natured, all bodies, no split anywhere — made of that, picturing all: world-winner. [E8] [E9]

Stand in the firing line of want, anger, greed, fog, pride, envy — and make nothing happen there: the real stays put. See the world as a god-net cast wide, or a picture-book spinning — see all of it, see through all of it, and joy jumps up. [E0] [E1]

Don't plant the mind in pain, don't plant it in joy, don't scatter it anywhere — goddess: what sits in the middle can be known. Drop the standing in your own body and think everywhere-I-am-thus — gaze seeing nothing else: be happy. [E2] [E3]

Know pots and walls and longings as inside me — never born apart, never all-spreading: picturing this way, the all-going is born. Grasping and grasped run common to every body — the yogin's edge is the He-is, held with care. [E4] [E5]

Feel yourself in the other's body the way he feels knowing — drop your own-body-clinging and go spread-everywhere in days. Make the mind supportless — think no thoughts: Bhairava in the self. [E6] [E7]

All-knowing, all-doing — that one is me indeed, of Śiva-nature: held firm, turn Śiva. His world-plays show the way waves lift, flames lick, sunlight lands — mine and Bhairava's: nothing but showings. [E8] [E9]

Whirl the body round and round, then drop it flat toward the earth — the shaking-power cuts out, and the highest state is born. By props or by power, by knowing or by melting the mind — at the end of power's pouring-in: Bhairava-form. [E0] [E1]

Fix the eyes open and unwinking — freedom is born on the spot. Plug the ears, and the lower gate too — sit clean and picture it: walk into the forever-real. [E2] [E3]

Stand over a well, over a deep pit, and look — mind in one piece, rightly: mind melts, plainly. Wherever the mind is — outside, inside, anywhere at all — there is the Śiva-state: by all-spreading, where else could it go? [E4] [E5]

Wherever the road of the eyes runs, knowing shows itself, the all-spreader's own: his, of that stuff only, melting into knowing — full selfness. Even where wants end, those are states too — and the real fills them full. [E6] [E7]

Leave the mind in the seen thing, in its place — body without props: the lord spreads out. Set the gaze on anything, then draw it back slow — knowing with that knowing: goddess, the open is home. [E8] [E9]

Too much love gives you a mind — a mind gone off wanting, and her power, Śaṅkara's own: picture her always, then Śiva. Picture the open in the things inside you and in all things — sit with her open even there: even knowing turns calm. [E0] [E1]

Remember being clean just a little — then hear Śambhu's view: neither clean nor unclean — thought-free, and you may be happy. The Bhairava-state is everywhere, even in plain everyday things — nothing past it: going not-two. [E2] [E3]

Hold foe and friend the same, praise and blame the same — brahma's fullness known: you may be happy. Picture neither hate anywhere nor love — free of both, brahman slides into the middle. [E4] [E5]

The unknowable, the ungraspable, the open, the gone-from-being — picture all of it as Bhairava: at that end, waking rises. Lasting, propless, open, everywhere, uncounted — set the mind in the outer sky: walked into the skyless. [E6] [E7]

Wherever the mind runs — that, that, by that, right then — leave no-standing behind: then waveless you may be. Fear rings through all, the all-giving whole that holds everything — say the Bhairava-word over and over: Śiva. [E8] [E9]

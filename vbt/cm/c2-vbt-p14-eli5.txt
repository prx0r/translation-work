I, mine, this, that — all the doings of being sure: make the mind supportless and it walks to calm, by picturing's pull. The lasting, the everywhere, the propless, the lord of all — picture words moment by moment, aimed at their meaning: done. [E0] [E1]

Unreal, Indra's net-shine, all standing up — then ask what the net is made real by: held firm, calm walks. The self never wobbles — so where is knowing, where is doing? The outer states lean on knowing — and this world is open. [E2] [E3]

No chains of mine, no freedom either — and the scared one's frights: just mind-pictures mirrored in water, the sun's. All sense-doors met — joy, pain, all of it — then leave the senses: self-settled, settled in the self you are. [E4] [E5]

Knowing lights up all, and all lights the self: one out of one-nature — knowing and known pictured as one seeing. Mind-stuff, knowing, power, self, mind — the fourfold: when it is fully wasted, dear one — then Bhairava-form. [E6] [E7]

A hundred waveless teachings told short, plus twelve — which knowing, O goddess: the knowing one, the knowing person. Yoked right here: he IS Bhairava — by his speech he does deeds, cursing and blessing both. [E8] [E9]

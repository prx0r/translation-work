Ageless he stands, deathless, dressed in atom-small powers — dear to the yoginis, lord who gathers all. Freed while living, acting yet never stained — this body, if you see it right, is the great lord's own supreme body, O god. [E0] [E1]

Who falls silent here, who sits, who worships? Who gets pleased, O great lord — ask it and watch the questions answer themselves. Whose fire-gift, whose offering, how, and what? All that working is outer work, done in coarse things only. [E2] [E3]

The real saying-over is this: in the highest state, what ponders is pondering itself — muttering there, self-sound, mantra-natured, just so. Sitting is a still mind with no shape and no prop — not sitting is picturing bodies, eyes, mouths, hands. [E4] [E5]

Worship named is not flowers — it is mind made steady, melting out of caring into the sky-with-no-seams. Stand in the one-yoking, day after day — the full-formed state ripens: content here, full to the brim. [E6] [E7]

In the great open-abode, in fire, lay sense-things and the rest — offered by mind, the whole gift, with knowing for a spoon. The offering here, highest lady, is being content, bliss-marked — it wears sins away and shelters all: the mountain's daughter. [E8] [E9]

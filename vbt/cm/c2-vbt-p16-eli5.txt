Where power breaks in — that field, that top picturing: without it, what worship, and what would it please? Free, blissful, knowing, mere pith of being — the self all over: step into that shape, and the bath is your own self's bath, so it is told. [E0] [E1]

By whom worshipped, with what gifts to please, higher or lower — but who worships? All he, the very one: then where is the worship? Breath goes out, life comes in — by wanting, bent-shaped: long-shaped she, great goddess — the other field, higher and lower. [E2] [E3]

Walking here, standing on the great-bliss road — entered by her, top Bhairava is reached. Six six-thousand by day, and by night a thousand twenty-one — the goddess's count is told: easy, and hard for the slow. [E4] [E5]

So told, goddess — topmost nectar, highest: never to be shown around to just anyone, ever. Not to another's pupil — base, cruel, undutiful — but to heroes free of thought, high-souled: the teacher's feet. [E6] [E7]

To the dutiful, in the teacher's company: given, no fence — village, kingdom, town, country, son, wife, kin. All this left — take this: what wobbles? Steady is this top wealth; lives may be given, nectar never. [E8] [E9]

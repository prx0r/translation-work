# C1 NOTE — vbt-p01

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

She has heard all, Rudrayāmala-born, Trika by essence — and doubts anyway: what form is it truly? Nine-self, three-head, power-triad, nāda-drop, wheel: each offering collapses — higher-ness is partlessness, not letter or body. Grace cuts the doubt, for what she asked is the tantra's essence; the most hidden will be told whole, known only as unsubstantial — net, dream, mirage-city — and described merely for fancy-struck minds.

## VOICE MAP

Devī declaring v1, questioning vv2-4, doubting vv5-6, turning with Bhairava v7; Bhairava promising v8, teaching vv9-10.

## KEY TERMS

* rudrayāmala — arisen from the union (Bhairavī speaks)
* trika-bheda — Trika corpus whole
* sārāt sāravibhāgaśaḥ — essence by essence, ever more distilled
* svasaṃvedana — self-feeling (planted for p02)
* saṃśaya — doubt as essence-engine

## CRUXES

* No practice is given here; the forms offered are questions, not techniques — the dhāraṇās belong to later sections.
* Grace/doubt reversal: the plea (cut the doubt; the asked is the essence) turns doubt itself into the tantra's heart.
* Unsubstantial-triad (net, dream, mirage-city) defangs the promised form in advance; the telling is fit for meditation, not pomp.

## FUNCTION

Opens the dialogue as a genuine doubt-question whose answers dissolve their own objects.

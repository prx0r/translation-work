# C1 NOTE — vbt-p02

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Every form denied: not nine-self, not word-heap, not three-headed, not nāda-drop, not wheel — free of direction, time and counting, untellable. Her terrors are a mother's sweet for going on. Then the positive: inner self-taste joy, full-formed Bhairavī; stainless world-filling body whose worship becomes the question; sung as supreme goddess, power-and-powerful undivided yielding supreme power; fire's burning only as entry in knowing-being; and the entered state — undivided contemplation, Śiva-formed, mouth called Śaivī.

## VOICE MAP

Bhairava denying vv11-12, explaining v13, questioning v16, declaring vv14-20.

## KEY TERMS

* āveśa — possession-sickness (the negated danger)
* anuttara — carried as unpointability
* saṃvid — awareness as the only locus
* Śaivī — the mouth (speech itself initiated)
* kṛtrima — made (contrast with akṛtra elsewhere)

## CRUXES

* Still no technique; the first breath-practice stands at the next section's threshold.
* Mother's-sweet pedagogy: the terrors of unawakened readers are confessed as lure, not doctrine.
* Mouth (mukha) named here makes the coming dialogue itself the practice's vehicle — words as doors (reappears as the request's door next song).

## FUNCTION

Completes the object-question by exhausting forms, then names the form that survives denial.

## RELATED

Hareesh upāya note (practices begin at vv24+); the mouth (mukha) of v20 as next song's door.

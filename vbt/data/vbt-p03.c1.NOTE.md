# C1 NOTE — vbt-p03

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Analogy and denial give way to door: the full-formed state sought, its means begged as known. Then the first body-works: breath meeting at two places; Bhairavī manifesting Bhairava-form through the turning wind; blossomed power needing no going; any breath-phase ending in shining calm; the subtler contemplated at twelve-end rising like lightning wheel by wheel; a twelve-letter course through gross, subtle, supreme — freed at last, Śiva.

## VOICE MAP

Bhairava declaring vv21-22; Devī requesting v23 (the beg); Bhairava teaching dhāraṇās 1-7, vv24-30.

## KEY TERMS

* praveśa — entry as meeting-place
* kumbhaka — breath-hold as calm-site
* cakraviśrāma — wheel-resting (cf. saktavijnana terms, different system)
* dvādaśānta — twelve-end (recurring locus)
* saṃpuṭa — casket image (returns later)

## CRUXES

* Outcome statements (Śiva, Bhairava-rise) are promised, not demonstrated — the witnessing verses come later.
* Practices here seed every later block: voids (p05), lotus-casket (p08), twelve-end (p10) all return as locations.

## FUNCTION

Opens the practice section: door begged, then breath, wind, calm, rise — each stilling prescribed in optative.

## RELATED

Hareesh: vv24-27 foundational visarga practice block; Singh kumbhaka notes; twelves recur as a structural number in the body-map.

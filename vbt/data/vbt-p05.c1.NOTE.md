# C1 NOTE — vbt-p05

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

The void-block: ordered sound seating sky-body at its limit; body-mantra ending in moon-drop void-utterance; own body taken as all directions; back and root voids yielding void-mind, heart-void added for thoughtless-rising; a moment's voidness making one thoughtless in nature; sky-pervasion steadying contemplation; skin read as wall until even the meditated drops; the lotus-casket mid-point winning fortune; and mind's twelve-end dissolving firming the reality-mark.

## VOICE MAP

Bhairava teaching dhāraṇās 18-27, vv41-50.

## KEY TERMS

* śūnya — void as active, not negation
* padma-sampuṭa — lotus-casket
* dvādaśānta — twelve-end
* nirvikalpa — thoughtless
* dravya — body-stuff

## CRUXES

* The unmeditated-sharer's status is stated, not explained — no metaphysics added.
* Each practice both negates a location and claims a steadiness — nothing is left to stand on, and the standing improves.

## FUNCTION

Multiplies void-contemplation into a full map of locations — sound, body, wall, lotus, twelve-end.

## RELATED

Hareesh central-channel block (vv28-30) precedes; Kṣemarāja on śūnya-methods; same void-word as the oṃ-practice at p04's end.

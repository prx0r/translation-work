# C1 NOTE — vbt-p06

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

The burning-and-void block: the mind cast at twelve-end until strangeness wears away in days; time-fire consuming one's own city into calm-shine; fancy's fire leaving only man-ness; minutest realities in own body manifesting the supreme; the weak power entering heart winning freedom; the ordered world-course dissolving mind at its end; Śiva-reality meditated at world-ends raising the great rise; and three vessel-gazes — pot, mountain wall — where dissolution makes made-of-that and births the wasting of turning.

## VOICE MAP

Bhairava teaching dhāraṇās 28-37, vv51-60.

## KEY TERMS

* kālāgni — time-fire
* bahirmukha-bhāva — fancy as what burns
* svasattā-minutiae — minute realities
* tattva-lakṣya — reality-mark
* laya — merging as vessel

## CRUXES

* The burning is internal contemplation, never ritual fire — the verses mark it as fancy's end, not pyre.
* Vessel/wall imagery shares p05's void-map; fire feeds the wave-practices (p07).

## FUNCTION

Turns contemplation into combustion: fire, burning, waste — always ending in what remains after.

## RELATED

Fire-practices = classical āṇava-upāya per Hareesh; time-fire echoes Parātriṃśikā's kālāgni usage (distinct work).

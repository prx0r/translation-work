# C1 NOTE — vbt-p08

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

Where bliss dissolves into what remains: meditated joy becoming that mind; eating and drinking's bloom fancyed as the filled state; tasted objects absorbed into one-selfness; content mind held until supreme-bliss-own-form proceeds; sleep's perished outer range as the goddess' shining; gaze fixed in dappled sky showing self-form; the fierce gazes — skeleton, anger, licking, sky-going — all yielding attainment at their time; the asymmetric seat making mind full; half-bent arms in armpit-sky giving calm; and fixed gaze cast down making unsupported mind go to Śiva.

## VOICE MAP

Bhairava teaching dhāraṇās 48-57, vv71-80.

## KEY TERMS

* bharitā — filled state
* eka-tā — one-selfness
* dṛṣṭi — gaze as the operative sense
* āsana — seat-variation
* svātmarūpa — self-form as what shines

## CRUXES

* The fierce gazes are listed, not explained — no taxonomies of fear added.
* Hinge from bliss to position: every blazing experience is seated, gazed, and stilled.

## FUNCTION

Converts intensity into positions: every blazing experience is seated, gazed, and stilled.

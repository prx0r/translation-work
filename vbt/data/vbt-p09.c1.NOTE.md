# C1 NOTE — vbt-p09

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

The gates-block: tongue mid-mouth with the h-utterance dissolving awareness into calm; unsupported body-vision wasting impurity in a moment; the moving seat's calm winning divine flood; unbroken sky-gaze stunning self into Bhairava-form; crown-sky all-Bhairava letting blaze-reality enter; duality-light known as endless-light-bearing; the deliberate darks — bad night, dark fortnight, black before closed eyes — contemplated until timira itself yields the form and made-of-that; an organ struck or blocked opening nondual void where self shines; and the lone a-syllable, no drop no emission, rising into knowledge-flood.

## VOICE MAP

Bhairava teaching dhāraṇās 58-67, vv81-90.

## KEY TERMS

* hoccāra — h-utterance
* stabdhātmā — stunned self
* timira — darkness contemplation
* nāda-bindu — droplet-emission refusal
* ekākṣara — lone syllable

## CRUXES

* Counts as doors only — no hierarchy of doors stated; beginners' suitability not yet addressed (that comes with upāya-talk later).
* The darks are deliberate practices, not moods: bad night, dark fortnight, blackness — equal portals with the rest.

## FUNCTION

Catalogues the text's strangest apertures as reliable doors — darkness and organ among them.

## RELATED

Hareesh: intensity-doors list (dark sky among them); Kṣemarāja on ekākṣara; v90's sudden knowledge-flood echoes p07's flood.

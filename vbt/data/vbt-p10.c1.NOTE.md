# C1 NOTE — vbt-p10

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

The letter-to-identity block: emission-ending awareness touching eternal brahman; sky-formed self uncovered by directions showing own form; a limb pierced and yoked to spotless going; the denial that anything inner-making is inside me, letting fancies fall by their own absence; māyā named as delusion counting realities that never stand apart; sudden desire led to calm where it arose; desire unarisen revealing He-I, dissolved, that-minded; mind set undivided in either desire or knowing seeing reality's meaning; knowledge turning causeless, no one's, Śiva; and no bodily distinction anywhere — the contemplating one conquers the world.

## VOICE MAP

Bhairava teaching dhāraṇās 68-77, vv91-100.

## KEY TERMS

* jñāna — knowing as causeless
* antaḥ-karaṇa-denial — no inner-making
* māyā — delusion as counting
* He-I — tadāsmi / so'ham as subtraction-result (v97 =B9haṃ byte)
* sarva-deha — bodies as distinction-less

## CRUXES

* No claim that one cannot act: distinction-less bodies still conquer worlds — difference denied, function intact.
* Fancies fall by absence, not attack; desire is led to calm, not killed.

## FUNCTION

Subtracts every inner stake until the identity statement stands with nothing behind it.

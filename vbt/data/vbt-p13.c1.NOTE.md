# C1 NOTE — vbt-p13

Moved from C1 (pristine essence and SUMMARY only):

## SUMMARY

The equalizing-block: devotion's excess arising as mind to contemplate her — Śaṅkarī's power — then voidness meditated everywhere calming even knowing; pure/impure both refused in Śambhu-view leaving thought-free happiness; Bhairava in every common thing, nothing beyond, nondual going; enemy and friend, honor and dishonor equal under brahma's fullness; hatred and passion not contemplated so brahman glides in the middle; the fourfold ungraspable contemplated as Bhairava awakening at that end; mind entering the skyless from every sky; mind's going met at each instant by leaving non-standing until waveless; and even fear resounding all-in-wholeness as Bhairava's word making Śiva.

## VOICE MAP

Bhairava teaching dhāraṇās 97-106, vv121-130.

## KEY TERMS

* bhakti-udreka — devotion-excess
* śāṅkarī — her power
* pāpa-kṣaya — sin-wasting as inner sacrifice
* tulyatā — enemy/friend equality
* bhaya — fear as material, not enemy
* śabda — Bhairava-word as agent

## CRUXES

* No ethics-list here; equality is stated as effect of fullness, not moral rule.
* Fear, the last obstacle, becomes the material in which Bhairava's word — and hence Śiva — is heard.

## FUNCTION

Equalizes opposites and anxiety itself — the method becomes non-reaction, wavelessness, word.

## RELATED

Pāpa/homa implicit (inner sacrifice); cf. VBT vv157-159 secrecy — the body of what must not be shown; Hareesh closing-verses analysis.

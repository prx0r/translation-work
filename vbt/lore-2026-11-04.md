## Structure (Singh: Vijnanabhairava or Divine Consciousness + Lakshman Joo notes)
- vv1-7: Devī enumerates doubts (ending chindhi saṃśayam — "cut my doubt").
- v7b ("sādhu sādhu")-v21: Bhairava briefly answers (transcendent aspect praised).
- vv22-23: Devī begs the means (brūhi bhairava — speak the upāya).
- vv24-135: 112 dhāraṇās (types of yoga/communion) — the heart; each anuṣṭubh verse ~14 seconds to chant; layered wit/puns (Roche: polysemy as method).
- vv136+: motivation/fruits ("advertising pause"), further questions + answers, Devī satisfied and united with Bhairava (colophon: kaṇṭhe lagnā — clasped to Śiva's throat).
- Terms in-text: yukti/yukta, niṣtaraṅga-upadeśa (teachings eliminating hesitations) — NOT "dhāraṇā" (modern label; Patañjali's dharana = narrower fixation).
- Four upāyas (Singh intro): āṇava/śākta/śāmbhava + anupāya (guru's hint suffices); VBT mixes all — note per-verse upāya where Singh marks it (do NOT force-classify).

## Verse-usable doctrine
- Yoga = communion AND means (both senses active).
- 112 practices ad modum recipientium (fitness of aspirant) — prāṇa, manas, imagination, intuition; breath/sleep/waking/love/anger/fear/doing-nothing all gateways.
- Khecarī, bindu, nāda, śakti-cakra vocabulary shared with Parātriṃśikā/Svacchanda frame.

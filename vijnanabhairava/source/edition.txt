trikabhedamaśeṣeṇa sārātsāravibhāgaśaḥ

kiṃ navātmaka bhāvena bhairave bhairavākṛtau

nādabindu mayaṃ vāpi kiṃ candrāvanirodhakam

parāparāyāḥ sakalamaparāyāśca vā punaḥ

na hi varṇavibhedena dehabhedena vā bhavet

prasādaṃ kuru me nātha niśśeṣaṃ cchindisaṃśayam

gūhanīyatamaṃ bhadre tathāpi kathayāmite

tadasāratayā devi vijñeyaṃ cakrajālavat

dhyānārthaṃ bhrāntabuddhīnāṃ kriyāḍambaravartinām

tattvato na navātmāsau śabdarāśirna bhairavaḥ

na cakrakramasaṃ bhinnaṃ na ca śaktisvarūpakaḥ

mātṛmodakavatsarvaṃ pravṛtyarthamudāhṛtam

vyapadeṣṭrasuśaktyāsānaśaktyāparamārthataḥ

p. 74a) yāvasthabharitākāryā bhairavī bhairavātmanaḥ

evaṃ vidhe pare tattve kaḥ pūjyarkaśca tṛpyati

sā parāpararūpeṇa parādevī prakīrtitā

atastaddharmadharmitvāt parāśaktiḥ parātmanaḥ

kevalaṃ jñānasattāyāṃ prārambheyaṃ praveśane

tadāsau śivarūpīsyāccaivīmukhamihocyate

p. 74b) jñāyate digvibhāgādi tadvadbhaktyā śivaḥ priye

digdeśakālaśūnyā ca vyapadeśīvavarjitā

kairupāyairmukhaṃ tasya parādevī kathaṃ bhavet

utpatti dvitayaṃ sthāne bharaṇādbhavita sthitiḥ

bhairavyā bhairavasyertthaṃ bhairavi vyajyate vapuḥ

p. 75a) nirvikalpatayāmadhye tathā bhairavarūpadhṛt

tadante śāntanāmāsau śaktācāntaḥ prakāśate

cintayonte dviṣaṭkānte śāmyante bhairavādayaḥ

ūrdhvaṃ muṣṭitrayaṃ yāvat tāvadante mahodayaḥ

sthūlasūkṣmaparisthityā muktvā muktvā tataḥ śivaḥ

nirvikalpaṃ manaḥ kṛtvā sarvorddhve sarvagodgamaḥ

p. 75b) dhyāyatonuttaraḥ śūnye praviśo hṛdaye bhavet

śūnye vaktre pare pātre svayaṃ līnā varapradā

krameṇa manaso dārḍhyāllakṣate lakṣamuttamam

dhyātāttayo mahādevyā tayā devaḥ prakāśate

dṛṣṭe bindau kramālīne tanmadhye paramāsthitiḥ

binduṃ śikhānte hṛdaye layānte dhyāyate layaḥ

śabdabrahmaṇi niṣṇātaḥ paraṃ brahmādhi gacchati

śūnyayā parayā śaktyā śūnyatā ceti bhairavi

śūnyayā śūnya bhūtosau śūnyākāraḥ pumānbhavet

ananyacetāḥ pratyante paraṃvyomavapurbhavet

ardhendubindunādāntaḥ śūnyoccārādbhavacchivaḥ

nirvikalpamanāstasya viyatsarvaṃ pravartate

yugapannirvikalpatvānnirvikalponayastataḥ

nirvikalpaṃ nirvikalpe nirvikalpā svarūpabhāk

vibhaveyadyatastasya bhāvanā sā sthirī bhavet

hṛdyākāśavilīnākṣaḥ padmasaṃpuṭamadhyagaḥ

sarvataḥ svaśarīrasya dvādaśāntaṃ manā nayet

p. 77a) yathā yathā yatra yatra dvādaśānte manaḥ kṣipet

kālāgninā kālapurādurthi tena svakaṃ puram

evameva jagatsarvaṃ dagdhaṃ dhyātvā vikalpitaḥ

svadehe jagato vāpi sūkṣmasūkṣmatarāṇi ca

pīnāṃ ca durlabhāṃ caiva dhyātvā dvādaśagocare

bhuvanatvādi rūpeṇa cintayettapasokhilam

asya sarvasya viśvasya paryanteṣu samantataḥ

viśvametanmahādevi śūnyabhūtaṃ vicintayet

nirvṛkṣagiribhittyādi deśe dṛṣṭiṃ vinikṣipet

ubhayorbhāvayorjñāne dhyātvā madhyaṃ samāśrayet

bhāve tyakte niruddhā cennaiva bhāvāntaraṃ vrajet

sarvadehe tanmayaṃ hi jagadvā paribhāvayet

vāyu dvayasya saṅghaṭṭādantarvā bahirantataḥ

sarvaṃ jagat svadehaṃ vā svānandabharitaṃ smaret

kuhareṇa prayogena sadya eva mṛge kṣaṇe

sarvasroto nibandhena prāṇaśaktyādhvaśāsanaiḥ

vahnerviṣaya madhye tu cittaṃ sukha mayaṃkṣipet

śaktisaṅgamasaṃkṣubdhaṃ śaktyā veśāvasānikam

lehanāmaktanākoṭo strīmukhasya bharāt smṛteḥ

ānande mahate prāpte dṛṣṭe vā bāndhave cirāt

dagdhamānarasollāasa rasānandavijṛmbhanāt

gītādi viṣayā svādātsamasaukhyaikatanmanaḥ

yatra yatra manassṛṣṭirmanastatraiva dhārayet

anāgatāyāṃ nidrāyāṃ pranaṣṭe bāhyagocare

tejasā sūrya dīpāderākāśeśavalī kṛte

karaṅkiṇye krodhanayā bhairavyā lelihānayā

mṛdvāsanesphijaikena hastapādau nirāśrayam

upaviśyāsanesamyagbāhūrkṛtvāvakuñcitau

sthūlarūpasya bhāvasya savyāṃ dṛṣṭiṃ nipātayet

āsane śayane sthitvā nirādhāraṃ vibhāvayet

calāsane sthitasyāthaśanairvā dehacālanāt

līnaṃ mūrdhni viyatsarvaṃ bhairavatvena bhāvayet

kiñcijjñātve dvetadāyi bāhyalokastamaḥ punaḥ

evamevaṃ durnimāyā kṛṣṇapakṣāgame ciram

yasya yasyendriyasyāpi vyāghātāśca nirodhataḥ

abdhindu sa visargaṃ ca akāraṃ japato mahān

sa visargasya varṇasya visargāntaṃ citi kuru

vyomākāraṃ svamātmānaṃ dhyāne digbhiranāvṛtam

kiñcidaṅgaṃ vibhāvyādau tīkṣṇasūcyādinā tataḥ

cittādyantaḥ kṛtannāsti samantarbhāvayediti

mayā vimohanī nāma kalāyāḥ kalanaṃ sthitam

jagatīicchāṃ samutpannāmavalokyaśamaṃ nayet

yadāmamecchānotpannā jñānaṃ vākastadāsmi vai

icchāyāmathavā jñāne jāte cittaṃ niveśayet

nirādhāraṃ bhavejjñānaṃ nirnimittaṃ bhramātmakam

ciddharmā sarvadeheṣu viśeṣo nāsti kutracit

kāmakrodhalobhamohamadamātsaryagocaraḥ

indrajālamayaviśvaṃ nyastaṃ vā cittrakarmavat

na cittaṃ nikṣipedduḥkhena sukhe vā parikṣipet

vihāya nijadehasthaṃ sarvaṃ nāsmīti bhāvayet

ghaṭādau yacca vijñānamicchādyaṃ vāmamāntare

grāhyagrāhakasaṃvittiḥ sāmānyā sarvadehinām

tadātma paramātmatve bhairavo mṛgalocane

p. 81b) sa evāhaṃ caiva dharmā iti dārḍhyā bhavecchivaḥ

mamaiva bhairavasyetā viśvabhaṅgyavibheditāḥ

kṣobhaśaktivirāmeṇa parāsañjāyate daśā

jātaśaktisamāveśa kṣobhante bhairavaṃ vapuḥ

kaivalyaṃ jāyate sadyo netrayoḥ stabdhamātrayoḥ

avikalpamate samyak sadyaccittanayasphuṭam

p. 82a) tatra tatra śivāvasthā vyāpakatvātkayāsyati

tasya tanmātradharmitvāccillayādbharitātmatā

kurdhahale kṣudhādyante brahmasattā mayī paśā

svaśarīraṃ nirādhāraṃ tataḥ prasarati prabhuḥ

tajjñānaṃ * *? sahitaṃ dehi śūnyālayo bhavet

sā śaktiḥ śāṅkarī nityaṃ bhāvayerthaṃ tataḥ śivaḥ

p. 82b) tāmevamanu sā dhyātvā viditopi praśāmyati

na śucirhyaśucistasmānnirvikalpaḥ sukhī bhavet

na ca tadvyatirekena parostītyadvayā gatiḥ

brahmaṇaḥ paripūrṇatvāditi jñātvā sukhī bhavet

rāgadveṣavinirmukto madhye brahmaprasarpati

tatsarvaṃ bhairavaṃ bhāvyaṃ tadante rodhasambhavam

bāhyākāśe manaḥ kṛtvā nirākāśaṃ samāviśet

parityajya navaḥ sthityā nistaraṅgastato bhavet

iti bhairavaśabdasya santatoścāraṇā cchivaḥ

nirādhāre manojāti taddhyānā preraṇācchamī

śabdāḥ pratikṣaṇaṃ dhyāyanprakṛtārthānurūpataḥ

kiṃ tatvamindrajālasya iti dārḍhyāacchayaṃ vrajet

jñānāyatā bahirbhāvādataḥ śūnyamidaṃ jagat

pratibimbamidaṃ buddherjaleṣvivavivasvataḥ

itīndriyāṇi santyajya svasthaḥ svātmani vartate

evameva svabhāvatvājjñānajñeyo vibhāvyate

yadā priyo parikṣīṇaṃ tadātadbhairavaṃ vapuḥ

dvādaśābhyadhikaṃ devi yajjñātvā jñānavijjanaḥ

p. 84a) vācākaroti karmāṇi śāpānugrahakārakaḥ

yoginīnāṃ priyo devi sarvamelāpakādhipaḥ

jīvannapi vimuktosau kurvannapi ca ceṣṭitam

evamuktvāvyavasthāyāṃ japate ko japaścakaḥ

bhūyobhūyaḥ pare bhāve bhāvanā bhāvyate yathā

na tu dhyānaṃ śarīrādi mukhahastādi kalpanā

nirvikalpe pare vyomni sā pūjā hyadarāllayaḥ

hūyate manasā sārdhaṃ sa homaścetanā srucā

kṣapaṇāt sarvapāpānāṃ trāṇātsarvasya pārvati

anyathā tasya tandrasya kā pūjā kaśca tṛpyati

yaścaiva pūjakaḥ sarvaṃ sa evaikaḥ prapūjanam

dīrghātmā sā mahādevī parakṣettraṃ parāparā

tathādevā samāviṣṭaḥ paraṃ bhairavamāpnuyāt

japo devyāḥ samuddiṣṭaḥ prāṇasyānte sudurlabhaḥ

etacca naiva kasyāpi prakāśyaṃ na kadācana

nirvikalpamatīnāṃ tu vīrāṇāmunnatātmanām

grāmorājyaṃ parodeśaḥ putradārakuṭumbhakam

prāṇā api pradātavyā na deyaṃ paramā mṛtam

ityuktvā nanditā devī kaṇṭhe lagnā śivasya tu

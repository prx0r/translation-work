[calm] [warm] [slowly] A woman stands at her gate while the monsoon clouds arrive. [short pause] Separated from her beloved, and her whole speech is this: release the foreign love, or release me. [short pause] What more is there to say?

[relaxed] The groves around her shine for love's sake and not their own — [pause] every sprout is his face. And the flowers pick on one flowering shrub until even the shrub is hard to laugh at. [soft voice] She bows.

[gently] The clouds thunder, travelers grow longing, and her sorrow grows endless with them. [pause] Yet this is exactly what the lovers' festival honors, dear friend: the arrival of the clouds.

[calm] At the end she pours water into a small pot at the gate's foot: [pause] pour for the poet whose wordplay would conquer even hers. [long pause] She has said it.

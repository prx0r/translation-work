
[calm] [warm] [slowly] A spider spun her web between two branches and sat in the middle of it. She slept in it, dreamed in it, woke in it — [short pause] and never once stepped outside it.
[low voice] When the wind came — cloud, smoke, dust — she watched her threads shake and thought the whole world was shaking. [pause] The sky above stayed blue all along. She never looked up.
[soft voice] One still morning, dew settled on every thread, and each drop held its own small sun. [pause] The spider looked — really looked — and remembered: [long pause] I made this.
[gentle] She walked out along her very first thread, into the open air that had never gone anywhere. [long pause]
[warm] The web stayed, beaded and shining. She did not. [long pause]
